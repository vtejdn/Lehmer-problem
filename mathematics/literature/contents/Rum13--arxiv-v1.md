# Rum13: contents of the early author manuscript

Robert Rumely, The Fekete–Szegö Theorem with Local Rationality Conditions on Algebraic Curves, arXiv:1203.1213v1, 6 March 2012.
This transcribes the complete supplied contents on printed iii–iv, PDF 3–4, of the [451-page early manuscript](../pdf/Rum13--arxiv-v1.pdf).
It is not a transcription of the unavailable 2013 published book.
Page labels below are the printed destinations; Arabic printed page n is PDF page n+22 in this artifact.
The source's spelling “Cheybshev” is retained in the corresponding contents entry.
