## Torus objects and the true translation group

Let v_1,...,v_4 be nonzero vectors in Z^r, with v_i distinct from both v_j and -v_j for i different from j, and assume they span Q^r.
On the r-dimensional torus consider

    F_epsilon(theta)=sum_(j=1..4) epsilon_j
        (exp(i<v_j,theta>)-exp(-i<v_j,theta>)),
    epsilon_j in {1,-1}.

Let H be the image in T^4 of theta -> (exp(i<v_1,theta>),...,exp(i<v_4,theta>)).
The pushforward of normalized Haar measure is normalized Haar measure on H: it is a probability measure invariant under every translation in that image.
Consequently the Mahler means depend only on H and the sign choice, not on which full-rank integer parametrization of H is used.
Permuting the four coordinates or inverting any of them permutes the sign choices and preserves their minimum.

For every tau in H intersect {1,-1}^4, translation by tau changes the signs epsilon to epsilon*tau and preserves the mean.
Global negation also preserves every mean.
Thus the subgroup generated by H intersect {1,-1}^4 and (-1,-1,-1,-1) gives exact sign equivalences.
Additional torus automorphisms may identify further orbits.
Translations by half periods in the chosen theta coordinates need not exhaust H intersect {1,-1}^4: a parametrization with an even lattice index can miss available translations.

## The bounded rank-three diagnostic and its limitation

The retained [J134 program](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0134/torus-signs.py) evaluates the nine graph templates with 1<=a<=b<=c<=3, excluding (1,1,1).
The excluded support has a good sign by the exact identity

    sin x+sin y+sin z-sin(x+y+z)
      =4sin((x+y)/2)sin((x+z)/2)sin((y+z)/2),

whose doubled sine sum has Mahler measure one.
There are fourteen translation/negation sign-orbit representatives among these nine graph templates.
The same program additionally evaluates (3e_1,3e_2,3e_3,(2,2,2)) and (3e_1,3e_2,3e_3,(2,3,3)), which represent primitive relation multisets 2223 and 2333 and each have one sign orbit.
Thus the complete run contains eleven templates and sixteen orbit representatives, and covers all seven unbalanced primitive rank-three relation multisets with absolute coefficients at most three.
The actual 48^3 and 96^3 shifted tensor grids use shifts (0.173,0.371,0.619) and clip absolute values below 1e-300.
No sample was clipped, but no integration-error or clipping-error estimate is supplied.
The largest change between these two meshes was approximately 0.02918, which is itself a reason not to read the decimal values as bounds.

At the 96^3 mesh the seven genuinely unbalanced classes gave the following exploratory values.

## A rank-two cross and the remaining circle specializations

The character vectors are e_1,e_2,e_1+e_2,e_1-e_2.
Their exact sign equivalence, quadratic Jensen formula, bounds 3/5<=R<=3 and strict Haar estimate m(F)<log(184/93) are fixed in [C863 v1](../claims/Yin_C_G0863_v1.md#rank-two-cross-margin), with its selected review.
The numerical implementation uses A(y)=1+2cos y and B(y)=sin y, taking R=|A| when B^2<=A^2 and R=|B|+sqrt(B^2-A^2) otherwise.
The square-root transition requires a real quadrature-error argument before a numerical mean could serve as a bound.
The fixed Haar theorem does not give the same bound for every circle specialization.

### The separate exploratory numerical values

The actual shifted one-variable grids used 4096 and 16384 nodes with shift 0.371, giving approximately 0.4812120980 and 0.4812123640.
The program used that fixed Jensen formula directly without clipping, and the smallest sampled R was above 0.60016.
The mesh difference is approximately 2.66e-7, without a certified error estimate.
These values provide no evidence for a mean at least log2, and no exact closed form is asserted.
The positive integer specializations have frequencies (1,N,N+1,N-1) for N>=3.
After sorting, N=10 gives (1,9,10,11), the support already retained as the largest best-sign value in the [older J134 finite screen](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0134/readme.md#binary-sign-screen).
The accepted analytic Haar bound uses the exact torus sign equivalence, Jensen reduction and integral majorant rather than these mesh values.
The finite one-variable specializations retain their separate comparison obligation.

## Continuing boundary

For each retained positive graph triple, the positive integer specializations (1,N,N^2,a+bN+cN^2) have four distinct entries for all sufficiently large N and realize the formal character formulas.
A transfer of logarithmic Mahler means to a Haar limit requires the appropriate separate logarithmic convergence argument; finite quadrature and the character identity alone do not provide it.
Even an eventual good-sign theorem would still require the original-input auxiliary supply described in D13 before it could resolve unrestricted G0001.
The completed bounded calculations do not select a further candidate expansion.
