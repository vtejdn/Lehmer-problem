Use the [power-stable reduction and rotation construction](../ledger/Yin_L_G0445_v1.md#rotated-radicals) to produce many independent integral Taylor series from any input needed for the near-zero infimum.
The source of information is the change in relations between these functions over characteristic zero and over the residue field at 2.
[S8](Yin_S_G0008.md#route) supplies the degree descent, while this route tests divisibility of approximants to the functions themselves.
The [complex disk obstruction](../drafts/Yin_D_G0011.md#neutral-disk) explains why the archimedean dimension theorem alone has low priority and records the unreviewed transfer to actual Salem conjugates.

On 2026-11-04 Yin accepted retaining this route as proposed and redirecting its initial test after the overlap comparison.
The previously selected \(\overline{P_2}=\Phi_\ell\) family and the quartic Salem example are [calibration controls already covered by a uniform gap](../ledger/Yin_L_G0445_v1.md#covered-control-family).
The distinction from S4/S8 lies in the function-field relations and rational approximation criterion, not in another gap for that class or the use of a prime-adic determinant alone.

## Next investigation

Start with actual monic irreducible reciprocal power-stable inputs and the [residue classes of their rotated functions](../ledger/Yin_L_G0445_v1.md#first-binary-layer).
Check the [short scalar-period bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0244_v1.md#balanced-scalar-congruence-gap) and the [bounded-binomial-cover bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0135_v2.md#statement) on each proposed unbounded-degree family before treating it as new coverage.
Not satisfying these sufficient hypotheses does not itself establish small Mahler measure or positive prospects.

Seek a growing space of masks beyond the empty mask and any relation supplied only by the [full rotation product](../ledger/Yin_L_G0445_v1.md#full-rotation-product).
Use the squarefree pole criterion where applicable; repeated reduction factors require their own analysis.
For the surviving masks, determine the actual numerator and denominator degrees, integral lifting precision, Hankel rank and descent to rational integral series in the [paid determinant test](../ledger/Yin_L_G0445_v1.md#paid-lifting).
Charge the original degree, rotation prime, mask size, precision, Galois descent and complex analytic loss together; local rational liftability alone is not the discriminating target.

A reason to increase priority would be a quantitative divisibility gain surviving these costs on an unbounded-degree class not already covered by the cited gap criteria, with a precise statement of what remains needed for the unrestricted inputs.
A full residue rank, only the full-product relation, costs absorbing the gain, or an improvement confined to the covered controls calls for setting aside that family as a route to new coverage.
An inequality combining the finite-place contribution with the actual complex loss and a uniform arithmetic supply are open.
