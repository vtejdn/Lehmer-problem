## Next investigation

Start with the [residue classes of the rotated functions](../ledger/Yin_L_G0445_v1.md#first-binary-layer).
For squarefree reduction \(\overline{P_2}=\Phi_\ell\) with \(\ell\) an odd prime, determine when the actual numerator gives many independent rational first corrections and whether those corrections lift with controlled denominator degrees modulo \(2^s\).
Charge \(\ell\), the original degree, the precision and the return from cyclotomic coefficients to rational series in the [Hankel determinant test](../ledger/Yin_L_G0445_v1.md#paid-lifting).
A favorable test should produce a quantitative divisibility contribution on an unbounded-degree class and identify what additional input could supply it on the remaining core.
A full residue rank, expensive lifting, or a gain confined to one example calls for setting aside that family.
An inequality combining the finite-place contribution with the actual complex loss and a uniform arithmetic supply are open.
