# Corvaja and Zannier on heights at unit points

<a id="arxiv-v2"></a>
## Yin's source edition

The [local preprint](../../../literature/pdf/CZ05.pdf) is the authors' public [arXiv v2 of 22 April 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0311030v2).
It has 18 pages, with printed and PDF page numbers agreeing in the passages used, and supplies no table of contents.
The [journal article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00605-004-0273-0) appeared online in December 2004 and in the 2005 volume.
The proposition and equation numbers in [Yin's source unit](Yin_U001.md#s-unit-gcd) refer to the preprint.
