# Lech's paper on recurring series

<a id="edition"></a>
## The 1953 journal edition

Yin's source is the five-page published article in *Arkiv för Matematik*, volume 2, issue 5, printed pages 417 through 421.
The title page identifies the contribution as number 22 of the volume. [Journal record](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02590997) · [Public journal scan](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/116/6892-11512_2007_Article_BF02590997.pdf)
Printed pages 417 and 418 are PDF pages 1 and 2 and contain the theorem, the exponential-polynomial lemma and the number-field proof sketch. [Journal original](../../../literature/pdf/Lec53.pdf#page=1) · [Yin's source inspection](Yin_U001.md#zero-set)
