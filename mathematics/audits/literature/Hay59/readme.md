# Hayman's lectures on meromorphic functions

<a id="edition"></a>
## TIFR volume 17

W. K. Hayman's lectures, with notes by K. N. Gowrisankaran and K. Muralidhara Rao, were published in Bombay in 1959 as volume 17 in the mathematics series. [Bibliographic record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA0814364X?l=en)
The [publisher's series catalogue](https://mathweb.tifr.res.in/lectures.html) identifies the freely readable electronic volumes as retypeset editions.
In the [publisher's PDF](https://mathweb.tifr.res.in/Documents/Publications/Lectures/tifr17.pdf), the numbered contents occupy PDF page 3 and printed pages 1–4 occupy PDF pages 5–8.
The [disk formula unit](Yin_U001.md#hay59-u001) identifies a typesetting issue in the electronic theorem display and the precise corollary used for the pencil integral.
The [contents transcription](../../../literature/contents/Hay59--tifr17.md) uses the printed section and page labels.
