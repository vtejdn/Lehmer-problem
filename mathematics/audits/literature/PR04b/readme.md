## The Ronkin function

The complete English journal article occupies printed pages 481–507 of *Duke Mathematical Journal* 121, issue 3 (2004), corresponding to PDF pages 1–27.
In Section 1, printed page 482, equation (1), the authors attribute to Ronkin the average of \(\log|F|\) over a torus of prescribed logarithmic radii, with Haar probability on its angular coordinates.
This source supplies the terminology and normalization for the [radial orbit distribution](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v774.md#statement).
The project proof derives the directional second derivative and its individual-orbit interpretation by Jensen's formula and a sparse sublevel estimate.
