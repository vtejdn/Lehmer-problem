## Accepted statements and local application

Proposition1.1.6 states that for an element x of a complex unital Banach algebra, the limit of ||x^n||^(1/n) exists and equals its spectral radius.
Exercise1.1.14 records that quotienting such an algebra by a closed two-sided ideal gives the Banach-algebra quotient norm defined by distance to that ideal.
Theorem3.3.1 identifies compact Hilbert-space operators with operators continuous from the weak topology on the unit ball to the norm topology; the following paragraph records that they form a norm-closed two-sided ideal of B(H).

The complete current [collision-radius proof](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0405_v1.md#core-operator-collision-sandwich) uses B(l2(D)) with counting measure and a bounded original transition operator, so the first statement applies directly.
Its [essential-norm subsection](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0405_v1.md#salem-strip-all-power-essential-norm) uses an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space and the proper compact ideal; the other statements identify the quotient norm and send its orthonormal translates to norm zero under compact operators.
