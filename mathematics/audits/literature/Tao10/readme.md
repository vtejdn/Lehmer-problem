## Author's lecture notes of 28 March 2010

Terence Tao's *254B, Notes 1: Equidistribution of polynomial sequences in tori* is cited from the [author's HTML edition](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2010/03/28/254b-notes-1-equidistribution-of-polynomial-sequences-in-torii/).
The [original HTML](../../../literature/pdf/Tao10/equidistribution-20100328.html) contains the formula transcriptions in the image alt attributes and the numbered statements in the text.
The 19 October 2026 reading covered the qualitative linear and weighted equidistribution passages identified in the [contents](../../../literature/contents/Tao10/equidistribution-20100328.html.md).
The [quadratic progression unit](Yin_U001.md#tao10-u001) covers Proposition 1 and Corollaries 5 and 6 in Section 1, including the application to positive indices in a prescribed arithmetic progression.
