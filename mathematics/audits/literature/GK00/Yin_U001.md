# Two generators for a finite nonabelian simple group

<a id="gk00-u001"></a>
<a id="two-generators"></a>
## Generation and the order of the automorphism group

For a finite almost simple group \(G\), Guralnick and Kantor write \(O_\infty(G)\) for the last term of the derived series.
Their Theorem I gives a conjugacy class \(C\) such that, for each \(1\ne x\in G\), more than one tenth of the \(y\in C\) satisfy \(\langle x,y\rangle\supseteq O_\infty(G)\).
The corollary on the following page states that each nonidentity element belongs to a pair generating a subgroup containing the socle. [Journal copy, Theorem I, p. 744 and corollary, p. 745](../../../literature/pdf/GK00.pdf#page=2)

A finite nonabelian simple group \(T\) is almost simple, and both its socle and its last derived subgroup equal \(T\).
Choose \(x\ne1\) in \(T\).
The positive proportion in Theorem I supplies \(y\in T\) with \(\langle x,y\rangle=T\).
For this fixed pair, the map \(\varphi\mapsto(\varphi(x),\varphi(y))\) injects the set of automorphisms of \(T\) into \(T^2\), since a homomorphism is determined on generators.
Thus \(|\operatorname{Aut}(T)|\le |T|^2\).
The inspection covers the published statement, its notation and corollary on pp. 743–745, and this deduction.
