# WWT+99 source reading

## Yin

<a id="exceptional-generators"></a>
The inspected source is the official online ATLAS of Finite Group Representations, not the separate 1985 printed ATLAS.
The [homepage](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/) gives the ordered author list and records creation of version 2.0 on 4 May 1999; this is the edition basis of the registered key.
Its [local original](../../../literature/pdf/WWT+99/index.html) preserves that source identity.

For the exceptional groups in Jones's full-cycle classification, the relevant version-2 pages are [\(L_2(11)\)](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/lin/L211/#sgens), [\(M_{11}\)](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M11/#sgens), and [\(M_{23}\)](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M23/#sgens), specifically their “Standard generators” sections.
They are retained as [the \(L_2(11)\) original](../../../literature/pdf/WWT+99/v2-l211.html), [the \(M_{11}\) original](../../../literature/pdf/WWT+99/v2-m11.html), and [the \(M_{23}\) original](../../../literature/pdf/WWT+99/v2-m23.html).
The additional version-3 page [M23G1-p23B0](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/permrep/M23G1-p23B0), retained as [its local original](../../../literature/pdf/WWT+99/v3-m23-p23.html), identifies the degree \(23\) action and supplies its cycle-type table.
Its linked [first](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M23/gap/M23G1-p23B0.g1) and [second](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M23/gap/M23G1-p23B0.g2) GAP generator files were also read directly online.
The actual mathematical use, elementary sign checks and uninspected group-certification limits are retained in [Jo13 U001, small-degree evenness](../Jo13/Yin_U001.md#small-degree-even).
