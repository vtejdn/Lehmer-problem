## Proposition 5: coefficient length

Source/version: the registered retained seven-page Numdam digitization of Mignotte's 1978 article.
Locator: Proposition 5 and Lemma 1, printed 39-04 / PDF p.5.
Trigger: the current G0001 Frobenius calculation yielded a bounded-coefficient-length gap, and the existing Smy08-U002 report pointed to this exact original proposition.
Date/reviewer: 2026-10-06, coordinating AI agent for Yin; **audited within this scope**, with the standard prime-existence input below retained externally.

For a nonzero algebraic integer alpha which is not a root of unity, let p be its monic minimal polynomial and S=||p||_1.
Proposition 5 gives

\[
M(\alpha)\ge 2^{1/(2S)},\qquad m(\alpha)\ge\frac{\log2}{2S}.
\]

The nonzero convention from the source's introductory setting is retained; alpha=0 is not covered.
The coordinator freshly read the statement and complete short proof, rendered and visually inspected the whole page, and checked the norm divisibility, nonvanishing, and coefficient estimate.
In detail, a prime ell in [2S,4S] gives a nonzero norm divisible by ell^d and at most S^d M^(ell d).
The function log(y)/y on [2,4] is at least log(2)/2, yielding the stated bound.
Existence of a prime in that interval is the standard Bertrand prime-interval theorem; its proof was not re-audited.
The source does not spell out that elementary input.
Lemma 1's finite-order Galois argument establishing nonvanishing was read and checked.
No proof of the subsequent degree-reduction Lemma 2 or the full Dobrowolski theorem was audited.
