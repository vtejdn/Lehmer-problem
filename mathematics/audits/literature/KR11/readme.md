# Kobayashi and Rieck on a triangulated link exterior of linear size

The [authors' final text](../../../literature/pdf/KR11--arxiv-v1.pdf) is arXiv:1205.2441v1 of 11 May 2012. [Edition and publication information](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.2441v1)
Theorem 1.1 on p. 1 bounds the size of a triangulation of a fixed neighborhood of the thick part linearly in hyperbolic volume.
For a closed manifold the complement consists of neighborhoods of drilled geodesics.
Corollary 1.3 on p. 2 compares volume with the smallest number of tetrahedra in a triangulation of a link exterior inside the manifold, with the empty link allowed.
Fixing the Margulis constant and neighborhood radius gives the universal constant used in the homological volume estimate.
