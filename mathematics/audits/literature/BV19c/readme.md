# Breuillard--Varju: Irreducibility of random polynomials of large degree

Canonical bibliographic identity belongs to [BV19c in references.bib](../../../literature/references.bib).
The selected original is the 55-page published article, Acta Mathematica 223 (2019), printed pp. 195-249, retained as [BV19c--published.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/BV19c--published.pdf) from the [YMSC archive copy](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/11784-ACTA-2019-0223-0002-a001.pdf).
The title, authors, DOI and journal pagination were checked from the original; printed page equals one-based PDF page plus 194.
The related preprint is arXiv:1810.13360, and its inspected online v2 has different pagination; its body does not replace the selected published locators.

Yin text-inspected the title, printed pp. 197-199 and Section 7 on pp. 235-237, and visually checked Proposition 38 on printed p. 235/PDF p. 41.
The selected use is the upper bound for a divisor Q of positive logarithmic Mahler measure below tau/10, under the stated coefficient support and collision-probability hypotheses.
The retained application explicitly requires 1<M(Q), excluding the zero-root factor x and cyclotomic factors.
Section 7's statement and proof outline were read to distinguish this unconditional input from the paper's RH-conditional irreducibility theorems; the proof's original counting and Dobrowolski dependencies were not recursively audited.
The coefficient-box specialization and its difference from the probability for the measure of the entire sampled polynomial are retained in [C870](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0870_v1.md#source-interfaces).
