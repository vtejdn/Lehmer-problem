# Silver and Williams on branched covers of links

The [arXiv v3 text](../../../literature/pdf/SW02--arxiv-v3.pdf) is dated 25 May 2001. [arXiv edition](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0003127v3)
Theorem 2.1 on p. 3 gives a limsup for the logarithm of the torsion order per cover degree in finite abelian branched covers of an oriented link in the three-sphere with nonzero Alexander polynomial, as the shortest nonzero lattice vector tends to infinity.
For a knot the theorem gives a limit.
The [journal version](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0040-9383%2801%2900014-3) appeared in 2002.
The direct cyclic calculation for closed surface bundles uses the abelianization of their own mapping-torus groups.
