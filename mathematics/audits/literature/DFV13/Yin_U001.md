# Large-gap factorization when the two blocks are not reversals

<a id="dfv13-u001"></a>
## The hypotheses on the two coefficient blocks

In Theorem 1.2, \(f,g\in\mathbb Z[X]\) have nonzero constant terms, integer gcd one, and \(g\ne\pm f^*\), where \(f^*(X)=X^{\deg f}f(1/X)\).
Above its explicit threshold in \(f,g\), every normalized irreducible reciprocal divisor of \(X^n f+g\) is cyclotomic.
Corollary 1.3 retains that hypothesis and combines the bound with Theorem 1.1 for nonreciprocal factors, subject to its two power exceptions. [Author manuscript, pp. 3–4](readme.md)

For \(g_s=X^s-X-1\), \(B_s=1-X^{s-1}-X^s\), reversal gives \(g_s=(B_s)^*\), also after substituting \(X^a\).
Hence the reciprocal-section factor question at [large primitive slopes](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v391.md#criterion) falls outside the input class of Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 1.3 for every delay.
