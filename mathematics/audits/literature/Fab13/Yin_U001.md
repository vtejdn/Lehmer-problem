# A disk containing a critical point near two distinct zeros

Let \(K\) be algebraically closed and complete for a nontrivial non-Archimedean absolute value, of characteristic zero and residue characteristic \(\ell\).
For a rational function of degree \(n\ge1\), two distinct zeros in a closed disk of radius \(r\) imply a critical point in the concentric closed disk of radius \(r\gamma\).
Here \(\gamma=1\) when \(\ell=0\) or \(\ell>n\), and \(\gamma=|\ell|^{-1/(\ell-1)}\) when \(0<\ell\le n\). [Application 1 on preprint p. 2](../../../literature/pdf/Fab13.pdf#page=2)

The inspection on 18 November 2026 checked the ground field, both closed disks, the word “distinct” and the two radius factors against the statement on pp. 1–2.
This statement gives the existence and location of one critical point and leaves its multiplicity unspecified.
The proof of the underlying ramification-locus theorem has not been audited here. [Inspected edition](readme.md#edition)
