# Fried, Haran and Völklein on the totally real numbers

The [authors' eight-page rendering](../../../literature/pdf/FHV93.pdf) identifies the 1993 note in volume 317 of the Paris academy proceedings, published on pages 95–99.
The English introduction on PDF page 3 describes the field of totally real algebraic numbers by the involutions fixing it. [Author-hosted edition](https://www.math.uci.edu/~mfried/paplist-cov/QTotallyReal.pdf#page=3)
The [cubic factor calculation](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v394.md#conjugation) uses ordinary complex conjugation in a finite splitting group and proves the needed normal-subgroup statement there.
