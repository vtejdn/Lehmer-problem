# Frączyk and Pham on the bottom of the length spectrum

## Yin's source notes

The [18-page author preprint](../../../literature/pdf/FP23--arxiv-v1.pdf) is [arXiv:2207.02332v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02332v1), dated 5 July 2022.
The [journal article](https://doi.org/10.1090/tran/8886) appeared in 2023 on pages 4745–4764.
The manuscript's [contents](../../../literature/contents/FP23--arxiv-v1.md) give its section and page numbers.

Theorem C on preprint page 4 concerns the **squares** of lengths from a single irreducible arithmetic lattice.
Theorem 5.1 on preprint pages 15–16 bounds the sum of two translation lengths in terms of the trace-field degree when their squares are linearly independent over the rationals.
Theorem A on page 2, Proposition 3.1 on page 8 and the final transfer estimate on page 15 give the fixed-group dependence used in the [geometric comparison](../../../drafts/Yin_D_G0034.md#geometry).
