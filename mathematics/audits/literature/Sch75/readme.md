## Yin's inspected edition

The work is Schinzel's published Addendum in Acta Arithmetica 26 (1975), no.3, printed pp.329--331.
The title and year were checked in the original and against the [publisher's record](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-26-3-329-331).
The [published scan](../../../literature/pdf/Sch75--published.pdf) was obtained from the publisher's download, which resolves to the [ICM PDF](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa26/aa26315.pdf).

This scan has two physical PDF pages containing three printed pages.
Physical page 1 contains printed p.329 on its right half, with a blank left half.
Physical page 2 contains printed p.330 on the left and p.331 on the right.
All three printed pages were actually read visually; text extraction alone did not recover their mathematical text.
There is no contents list in this short contribution, so no contents transcription is supplied.

[Yin_U001](Yin_U001.md#sch75-u001) retains the exact non-self-inversive condition, the field hypotheses, the monic integral specialization of Corollary 1', and the full-embedding logarithmic measure convention.
The relevant theorem and corollary are both on printed p.329.
The Addendum contains brief proof explanations and a list of corrections to the 1973 paper on p.331.
Those passages were read, but the earlier proof steps on which they depend were not audited; the retained input is an accepted established external statement rather than an independent proof verification.
The original 1973 article's publisher requests did not return a usable original during this bounded inspection, and Sch00 Theorem 72 was not used as an inspected replacement.
