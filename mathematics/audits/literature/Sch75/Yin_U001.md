# Non-self-inversive polynomials over Kroneckerian fields: a uniform relative measure gap

Inspection date: 2026-11-06.
The source is the [published 1975 Addendum](readme.md#yins-inspected-edition), specifically Theorem 2' and Corollary 1' on printed p.329.
This ordinary source inspection accepts the monic integral specialization below as an established external input.
The complete source statement and its field hypotheses were read visually in the original scan, rather than inferred from a search excerpt.
The original proof is not independently audited here.

<a id="sch75-self-inversive"></a>
## Field and self-inversive conventions

A Kroneckerian number field K in the source is either a totally real finite extension of Q or a totally complex quadratic extension of a totally real number field, that is, a CM field.
Fix its given embedding into C and put D=[K:Q].
Complex conjugation preserves K; in the totally real case it acts trivially.

**Definition (complex self-inversive polynomial).**
For a nonzero polynomial P in C[z] of actual degree d with P(0) nonzero, let bar(P) mean coefficientwise complex conjugation and define
\[
                    P^\dagger(z)=z^d\overline P(z^{-1}).
\]
The expression bar(P)(z^(-1)) means conjugating the coefficients before substitution, rather than conjugating the resulting value at z^(-1).
The polynomial is self-inversive when P^dagger=cP for some nonzero complex scalar c.
Applying dagger twice shows that such a c has absolute value one.
The source's hypothesis is the negation of this proportionality, exactly as printed in Theorem 2' and Corollary 1'.
It is a condition on the individual polynomial P, not a requirement that P and P^dagger be relatively prime.
A zero beta of P^dagger is the inverse of the complex conjugate of a zero of P, with multiplicity preserved.
This agrees with the [ordinary-reversal formulation already used locally](../../../ledger/Yin_L_G0450_v5.md#moving-map-self-inversive-convention); ordinary reciprocity without conjugating coefficients is a different condition over a CM field.

<a id="sch75-corollary-one-prime"></a>
## The original inequality and the adopted monic specialization

Write
\[
                \varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}.
\]
In the source, p_0 is the leading coefficient of P, not its constant coefficient.
The notation C(P) denotes its coefficient content; in ideal notation this is the fractional O_K-ideal generated by its coefficients.
Corollary 1' on printed p.329, with the hypotheses on K inherited from the immediately preceding Theorem 2', asserts
\[
 \prod_{\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C}
       \prod_{|\alpha_{\sigma,j}|>1}|\alpha_{\sigma,j}|
 \ \ge\ \varphi^{D/2}\,
             N_{K/\mathbb Q}\!\left(C(P)/(p_0)\right),
 \tag{1}
\]
provided P is not self-inversive and P(0) is nonzero.
Here alpha_(sigma,j) runs over every root of sigma(P), with multiplicity.
The outer product runs over all D embeddings separately; both embeddings of each complex-conjugate pair occur.
The source imposes no irreducibility hypothesis on P for this corollary.

<a id="sch75-monic-integral-gap"></a>
Only the following direct specialization is adopted for the present research.
Let P be monic in O_K[z], with P(0) nonzero, and assume P is not self-inversive.
Its leading coefficient is p_0=1 and its coefficient ideal is O_K, because the coefficients are integral and include 1.
Thus the ideal-norm term in (1) is exactly one.
In particular,
\[
 \prod_{\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C}
       \prod_{|\alpha_{\sigma,j}|>1}|\alpha_{\sigma,j}|
                   \ge\varphi^{D/2}.
 \tag{2}
\]
No normalization by an uncharged content or leading coefficient occurs in this specialization.
The general fractional-content case in (1) is recorded to identify the original statement, not adopted for a new denominator argument here.

<a id="sch75-logarithmic-normalization"></a>
## Total logarithmic measure

Use the [accepted total logarithmic Mahler normalization](../Smy08/Yin_U001.md#smy08-height-normalization).
Each sigma(P) is monic, so its logarithmic measure is exactly the sum of log|alpha_(sigma,j)| over its roots outside the unit circle, with multiplicity.
Taking logarithms in (2) gives
\[
       \sum_{\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C}m(\sigma P)
                         \ge\frac D2\log\varphi.
 \tag{3}
\]
By multiplicativity of Mahler measure, the left side is the total measure of the polynomial product over all embeddings, usually denoted N_(K/Q)(P).
Thus the corresponding average relative measure has lower bound (log varphi)/2, independently of D and of deg P.
This source statement concerns the full embedding sum, not one selected embedding or one exterior root.
Identifying that norm polynomial with a particular rational minimal polynomial requires the consuming argument to check the relevant field inclusion and multiplicities.
The local fiber argument and its exceptional phases belong to L458 v2 rather than to this source unit.

## Inspection and proof limits

The actual visual reading covered all three printed pages of the Addendum, including its opening description, the complete statements of Theorem 2', Corollary 1', Theorem 3' and Corollary 2', the short proof explanations, and the corrigenda list on printed p.331.
The adopted result is only the monic integral case of Corollary 1'; the other theorem conclusions are not imported.

Its proof explanation uses earlier formulas and proof steps from the 1973 paper, including formula (28) on p.394; those underlying arguments were not obtained and audited in this inspection.
Reading the Addendum's proof explanation does not establish an independent proof review.
The accepted external-statement scope is sufficient for the direct monic specialization and logarithmic conversion above, and asserts no audit of Sch00 Theorem 72 or of an additional generalization.
