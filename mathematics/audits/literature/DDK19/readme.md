# DDK19

D. S. Dummit, E. P. Dummit and H. Kisilevsky's published article occupies pp. 285–298 of *Pacific Journal of Mathematics* 298, no. 2 (2019).
The publisher's [issue PDF excerpt](https://msp.org/pjm/2019/298-2/pjm-v298-n2-p03-s.pdf) has a cover on file page 1 and the article on file pages 2–15.
In the [retained published PDF](../../../literature/pdf/DDK19.pdf), pages 3–5 contain the signature convention, the prime-power circular unit generators and the odd prime-power sine formula.
[U001](Yin_U001.md#sine-signatures) records those facts and the comparison with the reported rank at conductor 29.
