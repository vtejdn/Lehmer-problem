For a Salem extension K/F with [F:Q]=t, the survey's formulas(4)--(5) give

    R_K/R_F=2^(t-2)log(e)/u.

Here u is2 when K is generated over F by the square root of an F-unit, and1 otherwise.
Its stated Stark unit e, together with O_F^*, generates a subgroup of index2u in O_K^*.
Proposition22 reports that e is Salem and every Salem number in K is e^(j/2) for some positive integer j.
These are exact reported source statements used to compare normalization, not new proofs of Stark or Chinburg.

Conjecture23 uses

    Reg(K/F)=(R_K/R_F)/[O_F^*:mu_F N_(K/F)(O_K^*)]

and proposes absolute positive lower constants depending exponentially only on the RELATIVE UNIT RANK.
For a Salem extension that rank is1, so this proposal is a uniform positive bound for the normalized rank-one regulator.
The survey explicitly presents it as a conjecture.
The large-relative-degree and totally-real results it mentions do not by themselves apply to every mixed-signature quadratic Salem extension.
No contemporary literature-completeness claim or new Salem gap follows from this historical survey reading.
