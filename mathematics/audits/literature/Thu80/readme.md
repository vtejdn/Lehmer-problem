# Thurston's lecture notes on three-manifolds

The [379-page MSRI electronic edition](../../../literature/pdf/Thu80--msri-v1.1.pdf) is version 1.1 of March 2002, based on the 1980 lecture notes distributed by Princeton University. [University of Rochester copy](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/mth/sites/doug-ravenel/otherpapers/Thurston-GT3M.pdf)
The title page identifies the marginal numbers as the pagination of the original notes. [MSRI edition description](https://www.cycle.msri.org/nonmsri/gt3m/)

Theorem 5.12.1 on printed page 119, PDF page 131, gives a finite collection of complete hyperbolic parent manifolds for each fixed upper bound on volume.
Every other complete hyperbolic three-manifold within that bound arises by hyperbolic Dehn surgery from the collection.
