# GHS13 source and edition notes

## Yin

<a id="arxiv-v2"></a>
The [arXiv v2 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.4155v2), dated 26 May 2011, is the edition inspected for the Eichler-transvection formula.
The [local original](../../../literature/pdf/GHS13--arxiv-v2.pdf) has 73 PDF pages; its title page identifies V. Gritsenko, K. Hulek and G. K. Sankaran and the exact arXiv version.
It is a public preprint of the work later published in *Handbook of Moduli*, Vol. I (2013), pp. 459--526, as identified by [Sankaran's publication list](https://people.bath.ac.uk/masgks/gksresearch.html) and the [University of Bath record](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/publications/moduli-of-k3-surfaces-and-irreducible-symplectic-manifolds/).
The shared bibliography uses the formal publication year in its stable key GHS13; the retained filename distinguishes the inspected preprint edition.
The published chapter's body has not been compared with this preprint, so its page numbers are not used for the mathematical inspection.

The formula in Section 8.3, equation (49), and its adjacent inverse identity are on printed page 54, also one-based PDF page 54.
The [searchable contents](../../../literature/contents/GHS13--arxiv-v2.md) transcribe the complete supplied list on printed/PDF page 1.
The inspected formula, local specialization and limits are retained in [L462v13](../../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v13.md#integer-generators), which treats the source as attribution for a self-contained calculation, not as a transferred modular-group theorem.
