# HL02 source and edition notes

## Yin

<a id="report0111"></a>
The inspected public author edition is [Report No. 0111, June 2001](https://www.math.ru.nl/~heckman/Heck_14.pdf), retained locally as [HL02--report0111.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/HL02--report0111.pdf).
It has a report cover followed by the complete paper, printed pages 1--54, for 55 PDF pages.
The title page names Gert Heckman and Eduard Looijenga.
The supplied contents occupy printed page 5, PDF page 6, and have a [complete searchable transcription](../../../literature/contents/HL02--report0111.md).

The [author's publication list, item 41](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~looij101/annotated-publication-list.html) identifies the published contribution in *Algebraic Geometry 2000, Azumino (Hotaka)*, volume 36 of *Advanced Studies in Pure Mathematics*, pages 185--248, Mathematical Society of Japan, 2002.
The key HL02 follows that publication year.
The 2001 report is the inspected edition; its mathematical text has not been compared with the 2002 published edition or the arXiv versions.

[Yin_U001](Yin_U001.md#hl02-u001) records the statement identifying the full automorphism group of the D4 root lattice with W(F4), with its exact passage and limited inspection scope.
