# The full automorphism group of the D4 root lattice

<a id="hl02-u001"></a>
## Source statement and scope

In the [June 2001 report](readme.md#report0111), Appendix A, printed page 45 / PDF page 46, the paragraph beginning with the definition of an n-vector identifies W(F4) as the automorphism group of the underlying D4 root lattice over the integers.
In the usual lattice notation this is
\[
\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb Z}(D_4)=W(F_4).
\]
Here the automorphisms preserve the root lattice and its bilinear form.
The statement concerns the full underlying integer lattice, not merely the subgroup preserving its additional Eisenstein-module structure.
It therefore includes the D4 triality automorphisms beyond W(D4).
