# Dub16-U001: the half-norm construction retains its source power

<a id="dub16-u001"></a>
Use the [published edition](readme.md#published-edition).
On 2026-11-05 the coordinator read Theorem 1.1 and its setup (pp.82--83), the displayed quartic construction (p.86), and Section 4 (pp.86--87), with the last formulas checked in the retained PDF text.

For the special degree-2t Salem inputs constructed there, t odd, the half-norm output has degree 2^(t-1).
Half its conjugates have modulus alpha and half alpha^(-1), so its Mahler measure is alpha^(2^(t-2)), not generally alpha.
In the project's own raw normalization, direct substitution gives M_2(beta)=2^(t-1)log(alpha).
Thus the ratio two to its own total measure does not remove the exponential power cost relative to the original alpha.

This is comparison with the already adopted [monomial-class bound](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0866_v1.md#salem-monomial-orbits), not a new input to the Cayley proofs.
The paper supplies selected families, not a classification of all preimages or output supply for every near-one Salem input.
Its external Galois and half-norm ancestors were not newly audited.
