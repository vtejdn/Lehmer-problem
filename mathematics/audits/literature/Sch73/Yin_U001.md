# Schinzel's bound for the exterior roots of a polynomial over a totally real or CM field

<a id="sch73-u001"></a>
<a id="theorem-2"></a>
## Theorem 2 with the author's corrections

Let \(K\subset\mathbb C\) be a totally real number field or a totally complex quadratic extension of a totally real number field, and write \(D=[K:\mathbb Q]\).
Let \(P\in K[z]\) have degree \(s\), leading coefficient \(p_0\), nonzero constant coefficient \(p_s=P(0)\), and fractional ideal \(C\) generated by its coefficients.
The hypothesis is that \(z^s\overline P(z^{-1})\) is not a scalar multiple of \(P(z)\).
In the original typesetting the bar is over \(P\) and conjugates its coefficients.
This operation sends a root to its inverse complex conjugate.
The opening paragraph of the paper defines ordinary reciprocity, while Theorem 2 uses this conjugated polynomial. [Printed p. 385, lower right of PDF page 1](../../../literature/pdf/Sch73.pdf#page=1)

Put \(\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), \(u=(1+\sqrt{17})/4\), \(a=(1+1/\sqrt5)/2\) and \(b=(1-1/\sqrt5)/2\).
For a nonzero fractional ideal \(I\), let \(N(I)\) be its absolute ideal norm.
Let \(E\) be the product of the absolute values of the roots exterior to the unit circle of \(\sigma(P)\), with multiplicities and with all \(D\) embeddings \(\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C\) counted separately.
With \(A=N(C/(p_0))\) and \(B=N((p_s)/C)\), equation (2) gives the following bounds.

| Additional hypothesis | Lower bound for \(E\) |
| --- | --- |
| \(\lvert p_s\rvert\ne\lvert p_0\rvert\) | \(\varphi^{D/2}A^aB^b\) |
| \(\lvert p_s\rvert=\lvert p_0\rvert\) and \((\overline{p_s})C=(p_0)\overline C\) | \(u^D A^{1/\sqrt{17}}\) |
| \(\lvert p_s\rvert=\lvert p_0\rvert\) and \(P\) is irreducible over \(K\) | \(u^{D/2} A^{1/\sqrt{17}}\) |

The first two cases apply to reducible polynomials as well.
The 1973 printing gives \(1/2\pm1/\sqrt5\) for the first row's exponents.
Schinzel's 1975 corrigenda explicitly replace these by \(1/2\pm1/(2\sqrt5)\), the \(a,b\) used above.
The original hypothesis and all three cases were checked on the rendered journal pages, and the correction was checked on the rendered addendum.
This inspection covers the published statement and its correction. [Theorem 2, printed p. 386, left column](../../../literature/pdf/Sch73.pdf#page=2) · [Corrigenda, printed p. 331, right column](../../../literature/pdf/Sch75--published.pdf#page=2)

## Absolute height of a field element

For \(\beta\in K^*\), take \(P(z)=z-\beta\).
Its conjugate reciprocal \(1-\overline\beta z\) is a multiple of \(P\) precisely when \(\beta\overline\beta=1\).
If \(\beta\overline\beta\ne1\), the first row applies.
Write \((\beta)=\mathfrak a/\mathfrak b\) with coprime integral ideals.
The content \((1,\beta)=\mathfrak b^{-1}\) gives \(A=N(\mathfrak b)^{-1}\) and \(B=N(\mathfrak a)\).
For the absolute logarithmic Weil height, \(\exp(Dh(\beta))=N(\mathfrak b)E\).
Since \(1-a=b>0\), one obtains
\[
 \exp(Dh(\beta))\ \ge\ \varphi^{D/2}\bigl(N(\mathfrak a)N(\mathfrak b)\bigr)^b,
 \qquad h(\beta)\ge\tfrac12\log\varphi .
\]
This includes nonintegral elements and units.
For the related 1975 bound on monic integral polynomials of arbitrary degree, see the [addendum specialization](../Sch75/Yin_U001.md#sch75-monic-integral-gap).
