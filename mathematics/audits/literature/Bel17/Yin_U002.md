Accepted standard statements at this scope: Phi_N is monic irreducible integral of degree phi(N); the cyclotomic integer ring has its power basis, so the displayed field discriminant is also the polynomial discriminant.
Its absolute value is N^phi(N)/product_(p|N) p^(phi(N)/(p-1)).
Bell p.7 attributes the discriminant to Washington, Proposition 2.7, and the power-basis statement to Lang, Proposition 10.2; these remain external established inputs.

Lemma 6 on p.5 gives Phi_(2N)(x)=Phi_N(-x) for odd N>1.
Theorem 9 on p.8 gives Phi_N(1)=1 when N is not a prime power; its displayed Mobius-inversion argument was checked.
Together they give Phi_N(1)=Phi_N(-1)=1 for odd squarefree N with at least two prime factors.
No zero-root or N=1 convention is imported into that restricted application.
