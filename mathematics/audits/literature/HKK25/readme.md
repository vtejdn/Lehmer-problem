# Filling orbits and Dehn twists on a fiber

The [local manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/HKK25--arxiv.pdf#page=2) is version two of arXiv:2208.02977, dated 25 January 2023. [Journal edition](https://doi.org/10.1142/S1793525323500322)

## Boundaries and filling orbits

Section 2 on p. 2 allows the boundary components to move.
Theorem 5(a) on p. 3 treats curves with distinct orbits under the given mapping class and requires a finite segment of their joint orbits to fill the surface.
With this hypothesis, taking each twist exponent sufficiently large in absolute value gives pseudo-Anosov monodromy.
Remark 7 on p. 8 explains how filling by the joint orbits gives a finite filling segment. [Preprint, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.02977v2#page=8)
The authors identify Fathi's Theorem 0.2 as the closed-surface case. [Preprint, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.02977v2#page=3)
