# Fili and Pritsker on heights under local splitting conditions

The page numbers here refer to the eleven-page [arXiv manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/FP17.pdf), version 1 of 1507.01879.
The published article appeared in volume 175 of the *Journal of Number Theory*, pp. 250–264. [Journal edition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2016.11.006)

Theorem 1 on manuscript p. 2 bounds the limit inferior of the absolute logarithmic Weil height for algebraic numbers whose entire Galois orbit lies in the prescribed closed set \(E_p\subseteq\mathbb P^1(L_p)\) at each selected prime.
Here the set of primes is finite and \(L_p/\mathbb Q_p\) is a finite normal extension.
Theorem 3 on p. 3 computes the local energy for the disks \(p^n\mathbb Z_p\).
Applying the height bound for a disk requires containment of every conjugate in that disk, beyond information on the number of conjugates in it. [Theorem statements](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.01879#page=2)
