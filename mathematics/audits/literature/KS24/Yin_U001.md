# Logarithmic vectors from the three quadratic subfields

<a id="ks24-u001"></a>
<a id="biquadratic-units"></a>
## Selected statement and application

Let \(L/\mathbb Q\) be totally real and biquadratic, with its three quadratic subfields and fundamental units \(u_1,u_2,u_3>1\) at the chosen real embedding.
Kim–Song's §3, formula (3.1), Lemmas 3.1–3.2 and the three vectors following them state that the units are multiplicatively independent, their generated subgroup contains the squares of all units of \(L\), and their logarithmic vectors in the indicated order of embeddings are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &a(1,1,-1,-1),\\
 &b(1,-1,1,-1),\\
 &c(1,-1,-1,1),
 \end{aligned}
 \qquad a=\log u_1,\quad b=\log u_2,\quad c=\log u_3 .
\]
The logarithms are of absolute values, so norm \(-1\) in a quadratic subfield introduces no change in these vectors. [ArXiv v2, p.4](../../../literature/pdf/KS24--arxiv-v2.pdf#page=4)
This is the totally real case of the already indexed [unit logarithmic lattice convention](../Smy08/Yin_U004.md#smy08-unit-lattice-convention).

The selected application uses the vectors and finite index.
The three sign patterns are pairwise orthogonal, so the logarithmic vectors are linearly independent.
Dirichlet's theorem then gives finite index.
For any specified order and congruence kernel, sufficiently high positive even powers of these units belong to that subgroup.
Their vectors remain independent and generate a subgroup of finite index there.
The resulting projection criterion and its limits are proved in [C298](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v298.md#biquadratic).
