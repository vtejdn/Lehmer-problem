# Absolute irreducibility after reduction at large primes

<a id="rup99-u001"></a>
## The theorem in the introduction

Let \(f\in\mathbb Z[X,Y]\) be absolutely irreducible with positive degree in each variable.
The theorem on page 1 of the [author preprint](readme.md) gives an explicit upper bound for exceptional characteristics in terms of the two partial degrees and the maximum absolute coefficient.
In particular \(\bar f\) is absolutely irreducible at every sufficiently large rational prime.
The application to primitive plane sections uses this consequence of the statement.
The separate requirement that two support differences remain independent modulo \(p\) can be tested directly on the coefficients and their integer exponent vectors.
