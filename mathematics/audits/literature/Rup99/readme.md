# Ruppert on the reduction of absolutely irreducible plane polynomials

The [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9808021v1) is dated 5 August 1998.
Its [seven-page PDF](../../../literature/pdf/Rup99--arxiv-v1.pdf) gives the theorem in the introduction on page 1.
The [journal edition](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.1999.2381) appeared in 1999.
The [source unit](Yin_U001.md#rup99-u001) records the large-prime consequence.
