# Agol's octahedral volume bound for a path of pants decompositions

The [10-page arXiv edition](../../../literature/pdf/Ago03--arxiv-v1.pdf) is the May 2002 preprint of the article published on pp. 53–64 of *Geometriae Dedicata*. [Preprint record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0205091) [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1023/B%3AGEOM.0000006584.85248.c5)
On preprint p. 2 the main construction starts with a path from a pants decomposition to its image and imposes a common-curve condition.
Lemma 2.3 on p. 3 constructs the hyperbolic drilled manifold, and Corollary 2.4 on p. 4 bounds the filled volume by one regular ideal octahedron per one-holed-torus move and two per four-holed-sphere move.
The [polynomial realization](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0883_v4.md#volume) verifies the common-curve condition after passage to a finite cyclic cover.
