## Source content and exact use

Lemma 1.6 relates rational Gram feasibility to rational polynomial sums of squares.
Its proof diagonalizes a rational quadratic form over the rationals and then invokes the four-square theorem for the nonnegative diagonal entries.
The project only needs the preceding positive rational weighted decomposition and supplies that elementary elimination locally.

Remark 1.7 distinguishes positivity at one real ordering from positivity at every ordering of a number field.
It also distinguishes interior rational approximation from boundary feasibility.
Remark 1.8 explains how a real algebraic zero forces its rational minimal polynomial into the factors of a univariate nonnegative polynomial, permitting a reduction to strict positivity.

These are accepted at their exact inspected scope as attribution for known mechanisms.
The source does not state the project's unit-circle Fourier-Gram capacity ceiling, arbitrary-width or block-minor extension, or its Salem trace-field construction.
Those adaptations need their own complete arguments.
Rational diagonalization does not mean taking rational square roots, and positivity in one embedding does not imply total positivity.
