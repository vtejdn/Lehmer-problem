# Borcherds on the Leech lattice

## Yin's source notes

The [author's text](../../../literature/pdf/Bor85--author.pdf) identifies the article as *The Leech lattice*, published in 1985 on pages 365–376 of volume 398 of the *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London, Series A*.
This [author-hosted edition](https://math.berkeley.edu/~reb/papers/leech/leech.pdf) has its own pagination, 1–12.

Theorem 6.1 on page 9 describes the simple roots of the chosen chamber of the Lorentzian lattice of signature (25, 1).
Corollary 6.3 on page 10 gives the split extension by the affine automorphism group of the Leech lattice and attributes the decomposition to Conway.
The kernel in that statement includes the central sign as well as the root reflections.
Lemma 7.1 on page 10 gives the simple roots in coordinates for the same lattice.
