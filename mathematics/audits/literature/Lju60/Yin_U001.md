# Irreducibility of the noncyclotomic part of a signed trinomial

Ljunggren states Theorem 1 for certain signed quadrinomials and then applies its conclusion to \(X^n+\epsilon X^m+\epsilon'\), with \(n\ge2m>0\) and \(\epsilon,\epsilon'\in\{-1,1\}\).
The trinomial corollary at the foot of [printed p. 65, PDF p. 1](../../../literature/pdf/Lju60.pdf#page=1) gives a single irreducible factor after all root-of-unity zeros have been removed, when a noncyclotomic factor is present.
For \(X^a-X^b-1\) with \(0<b<a\), either \(b\) or \(a-b\) is at most \(a/2\).
Reversal with an overall sign gives the indicated convention in the second case, and carries irreducible noncyclotomic factors to their reversals.

On [printed p. 66, PDF p. 2](../../../literature/pdf/Lju60.pdf#page=2), equations (1)–(5) reverse one factor of a monic factorization and compare the product with its reversal.
The middle coefficient of that product is the sum of squares of the coefficients and therefore restricts the possible supports.
The inspected source calculation treats the four-term polynomial.
The [three-term argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v178.md#factor) verifies the specialization directly, including the coincident differences at \(2b=a\).
The quadrinomial factor classification in the remainder of the article is outside this reading.
