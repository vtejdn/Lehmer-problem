# Sparse polynomials on a finite multiplicative group

The [local author manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/Kel16--arxiv.pdf) is arXiv version three, dated 16 June 2016. [Author's record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00208v3) · [Journal edition](https://doi.org/10.1112/S1461157016000334)
Theorem 2.2 on manuscript p. 2 requires every occupied exponent class to have a partner when a polynomial vanishes on a whole coset.
Theorem 2.3 on p. 3 bounds the number of distinct nonzero roots in the ambient finite field in terms of the largest entire coset.
The [cyclic return argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0904_v10.md#cosets) counts multiplicities at a prescribed time and tracks the degree belonging to the closed polynomial.
