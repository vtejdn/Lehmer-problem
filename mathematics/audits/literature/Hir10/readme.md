# Hironaka on the simplest hyperbolic braid

The [author's arXiv revision](../../../literature/pdf/Hir10--arxiv-v2.pdf) is version two of 16 April 2010 and includes the words “pseudo-Anosov” in the title. [Preprint record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.4517v2) [Published article](https://doi.org/10.2140/agt.2010.10.2041)
Section 3 uses the two meridians as coordinates and gives the Alexander polynomial in equation (3) and the Teichmüller polynomial in equation (4).
Propositions 3.3–3.5 compute boundary orbits, fiber genus and prongs, while Corollary 3.9 identifies when the monodromy extends across the capped boundary.
The [closed-fiber computation](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0883_v5.md#hironaka) uses the Alexander polynomial and the two boundary permutations to recover the entire characteristic polynomial.
