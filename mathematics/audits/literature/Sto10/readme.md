# Stoimenow's realization of Alexander polynomials by hyperbolic links

The [31-page arXiv edition](../../../literature/pdf/Sto10--arxiv-v1.pdf) appeared in 2007 under the title *Alexander polynomials and hyperbolic volume of arborescent links*. [Preprint record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.0866)
The October 2008 revision on the author's site and the 2010 journal article use the title *Realizing Alexander polynomials by hyperbolic links*. [Author's revision](https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/papers/aparb.pdf) [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.exmath.2009.06.003)
Section 2.3 specifies the normalization and admissibility of the one-variable polynomial.
In Theorem 4.1 the volume bound on the two-component exterior is equation (9) in the arXiv edition and equation (10) in the author's revision.
The [closed-bundle application](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0883_v4.md#linear-bound) caps the two boundary components of the fiber and computes the resulting polynomial.
