# Babai, Cameron and Pálfy on primitive group orders

## Yin's source editions

The November 1982 article occupies pp. 161–168 of volume 79 of the *Journal of Algebra*.
The inspected text of the authors' abstract is available both from the publisher and through Cameron's university.
The full article is listed in the publisher's open archive, but the full PDF could not be retrieved here. [Publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0021869382903234) · [St Andrews research portal](https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/on-the-orders-of-primitive-groups-with-restricted-nonabelian-comp/)

Yin's unit separates the [hypotheses in the 1982 abstract and the later linear bound](Yin_U001.md#composition-factors).
