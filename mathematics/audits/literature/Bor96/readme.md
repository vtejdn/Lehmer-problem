# Bor96 source and edition notes

## Yin

<a id="publisher-scan"></a>
The inspected original is the [International Press archive scan](https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cdm/1996/1996/0001/CDM-1996-1996-0001-a001.pdf), retained as [Bor96.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/Bor96.pdf).
The 36-page contribution is Richard E. Borcherds's *Automorphic forms and Lie algebras*, and printed and one-based PDF page numbers agree in the inspected opening pages.
The scan's text extraction is empty, so the selected pages were rendered and inspected visually.
Its opening has an introduction and a prose account of later sections, rather than a supplied contents list.

The publisher's [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.4310/cdm.1996.v1996.n1.a1), read through its deposited Crossref fields, gives *Current Developments in Mathematics* 1996(1), pages 1--36, and publication year 1996.
The [author's publication list](https://math.berkeley.edu/~reb/papers/index.html), item 28, instead describes the volume labelled 1996 as published by International Press in 1998.
The canonical key Bor96 follows the publisher's deposited year, while the bibliography retains this explicit discrepancy; the historical release chronology was not resolved by this bounded preparation.
The author-hosted typesetting linked from that list was not compared with the publisher scan.

[Yin_U001](Yin_U001.md#bor96-u001) retains the root convention, affine Leech chamber quotient and limits of transferring a canonical Coxeter spectral theorem.
The inspection is limited to pages 1--5 and does not audit the original Conway result or the source's later automorphic-form and Lie-algebra arguments.
