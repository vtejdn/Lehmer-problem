# Frobenius densities for a fixed finite Galois extension

<a id="kw22-u001"></a>
## The natural density for a conjugacy class

Let \(L/K\) be a finite Galois extension of number fields with group \(G\), and let \(C\) be a conjugacy class in \(G\).
In Section 1, page 1, first display, of the [author edition](readme.md#author-edition), Kadiri and Wong state the classical Chebotarev density theorem in the counting form
\[
 \#\{\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathcal O_K:N\mathfrak p\le x,\quad
          \mathfrak p\text{ unramified in }L,\quad
          \operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak p}=C\}
       \ \sim\ \frac{|C|}{|G|}\operatorname{Li}(x).
\]
Here the count runs over prime ideals, \(N\mathfrak p\) is their absolute norm, and the Artin symbol is the conjugacy class attached to the unramified prime.
For \(K=\mathbb Q\) this gives natural density \(|C|/|G|\) among rational primes.
Summation gives the corresponding density for any union of classes, and ratios of such densities give relative densities when the denominator is positive.
The statement and its normalization in the original preprint were checked for use with fixed finite splitting fields and their cyclotomic composita.
The classical density theorem is accepted at this stated scope without a new audit of its analytic proof.
