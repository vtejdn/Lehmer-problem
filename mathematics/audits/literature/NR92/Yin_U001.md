# Boyd's degree-eighteen example reported by Neumann and Reid

<a id="nr92-u001"></a>
## The polynomial and the quoted measure

In §4.5 of the [published chapter](readme.md#published), printed page 285 (PDF page 13), Walter D. Neumann and Alan W. Reid attribute to D. W. Boyd the example
\[
        P_2(X)=X^{18}+X^{17}+X^{16}-X^{13}-X^{11}-X^9-X^7-X^5+X^2+X+1 .
\]
They report a nonreal root outside the unit circle and quote \(M(P_2)\simeq1.2013961862\).
Their description of the smallest example then known is historical evidence from the 1992 chapter.
Inspection covered the formula, attribution and reported decimal on the rendered page, in the paragraph between Lemma 4.10 and Theorem 4.11.
