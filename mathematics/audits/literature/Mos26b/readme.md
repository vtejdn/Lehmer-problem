# Small Salem numbers in Mossinghoff's June 2026 table

The [author's catalogue at its fixed revision](https://github.com/mossinghoff/Mahler-Measure-Database/blob/27df0451b18cf711c9ff2a5dacbc46eec33a15a4/SalemList.txt) is dated June 1, 2026.
The [local original](../../../literature/pdf/Mos26b/SalemList.txt) gives 72 known Salem numbers below \(49/37\), ordered by size, with their degrees, decimal approximations and coefficients from the leading through the central term of their reciprocal minimal polynomials.
These coefficients select the positive Salem root; the polynomial for the same measure in a general Mahler-measure table may instead have undergone \(X\mapsto-X\).
The author reports completeness below \(1.3\) through degree 44 and identifies a further known degree-46 input below that cutoff.
The [binary-order computation](../../../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0173/readme.md) uses the exact coefficients and retains this scope for any exhaustive conclusion.
