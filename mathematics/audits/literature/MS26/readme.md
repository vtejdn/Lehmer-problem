# MS26: short Salem polynomials


[U001](Yin_U001.md#ms26-u001) adopts and audits the complete length-five classification with three explicit local corrections, a checked analytic reduction, and an independently reproduced exact terminal computation.
It finds 17 Salem polynomials and 13 Salem numbers, whose minimum is Lehmer's number.
Here Salem polynomials may include repeated cyclotomic factors.

The length-six theorem, sporadic list and all later algorithms remain outside the adopted unit.
In particular, the 126-entry list must not be treated as an unconditional complete list below the source's 1.01 cutoff.
The source is the specified 2026 arXiv preprint; no journal publication or whole-paper approval is claimed.
