# Amoroso and Viada on small points on rational subvarieties of tori

<a id="published"></a>
## Published edition

The [EMS Press article](https://ems.press/journals/cmh/articles/11182) appeared in volume 87, issue 2 of *Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici* in 2012, pages 355–383.
The first page of the [local published PDF](../../../literature/pdf/AV12--published.pdf) is printed page 355.
The [height estimate and its hyperplane example](Yin_U001.md#av12-u001) are in the introduction.
The authors' [2009 Duke article](../AV09/readme.md) treats a different height problem over general fields of definition.
