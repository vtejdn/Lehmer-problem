# Friedman and Skoruppa on relative regulators

## Yin's source notes

The journal article places its main bound for the ratio of regulators on printed page 115, equation (0.2), and defines the relative regulator on page 119, equation (1.2). [Journal edition](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002220050281)
The online transcription preserves those locators but damages the mathematical typesetting, including exponents and the norm index in equation (1.3). [Transcription](https://www.academia.edu/127513127/Relative_regulators_of_number_fields)
The publisher provides a subscription preview, and the download from Skoruppa's public upload returned HTTP 403 during preparation.
Checking the printed formulas and proofs requires a legible original. [Author's upload](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/225603017_Relative_regulators_of_number_fields)
