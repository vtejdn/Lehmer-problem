# Trivial homoclinic group for irreducible toral automorphisms with mixed spectrum

Given an action \(\alpha\) of \(\mathbb Z^d\) by continuous automorphisms of a compact abelian group \(X\), its homoclinic group \(\Delta_\alpha(X)\) consists of those \(x\) for which \(\alpha^n x\to0_X\) as \(\|n\|\to\infty\).
For one automorphism this requires convergence in both time directions. [§1, p. 953](../../../literature/pdf/LS99b.pdf#page=1)

Example 3.4 takes \(A\in\mathrm{GL}(k,\mathbb Z)\) with characteristic polynomial irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\) and some, but not all, eigenvalues on the unit circle.
Its toral automorphism has trivial homoclinic group.
The projected contracting and expanding subspaces meet in the homoclinic group, as in Example 3.3.
Their direct sum is a proper invariant subspace because of the neutral eigenvalues.
A nonzero integer vector there would generate a nonzero rational invariant subspace inside it under iterates of \(A\), contradicting irreducibility.
Thus a common projected point lifts to the same vector in the two subspaces, whose intersection is zero. [§3, Examples 3.3–3.4, pp. 957–958](../../../literature/pdf/LS99b.pdf#page=5)

The matrix hypothesis applies directly to the companion recurrence of a monic irreducible integer polynomial with constant coefficient \(\pm1\) and the indicated mix of roots.
The conclusion concerns two-sided convergence on the torus and supplies no rate of divergence for finite sums.
