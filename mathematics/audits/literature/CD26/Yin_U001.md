# Freeness and bounded division over a saturation field

<a id="cd26-u001"></a>
## Checcoli–Dill, Theorems 2 and 3

For a subgroup \(\Gamma\subset G(\overline K)\), its saturation \(\Gamma_{\rm sat}\) consists of the points some positive multiple of which lies in the subgroup generated by all geometric endomorphism images of \(\Gamma\).
Theorem 2 treats an almost split semiabelian variety \(G\) over a number field \(K\) and a finite-rank subgroup \(\Gamma\).
For every finite extension \(L/K(\Gamma_{\rm sat})\), the quotient \(G(L)/\Gamma_{\rm sat}\) is free abelian.

Theorem 3 assumes \(G=\mathbb G_m^t\times A\) over \(K\), with all geometric endomorphisms of the abelian variety \(A\) defined over \(K\), and \(\Gamma\subset G(K)\) finitely generated.
One integer \(d>0\) depending on \(G,\Gamma,K\) sends every \(P\in G(K(\Gamma_{\rm sat}))\) having a positive multiple in \(G(K)\) into \(G(K)+\Gamma_{\rm sat}\) under multiplication by \(d\).

For a root \(\alpha\) of a torus section in a varying primitive direction, a lower bound on \([\mathbb Q(\alpha):\mathbb Q]h(\alpha)\) requires quantitative control across the resulting roots and fields.
Freeness in Theorem 2 supplies a group structure, and the multiplier in Theorem 3 controls division for fixed \(G,\Gamma,K\).
Neither theorem states this uniform Mahler measure bound.

The proofs were outside the inspection scope.
