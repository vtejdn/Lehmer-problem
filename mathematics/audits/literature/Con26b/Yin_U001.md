For squarefree d!=1, the quadratic integer ring is Z[sqrt(d)] when d is not 1 mod 4, and Z[(1+sqrt(d))/2] otherwise.
The proof uses integral trace and norm and the squarefree-denominator condition.
In this ring, a unit has norm +1 or -1, and either norm sign conversely gives an integral inverse by conjugation.

The current specialization is d=3.
The resulting integer coordinates allow a direct mod 3 obstruction to norm -1; that obstruction, the higher-field norm tower, actual-order consequences, Salem hypotheses and constant-profile exclusion are project deductions, not source statements.
The local argument also reconstructs the elementary quadratic specialization.
