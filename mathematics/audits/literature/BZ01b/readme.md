# Bombieri and Zannier on height in normal fields of finite local degree

The [digitized published edition](../../../literature/pdf/BZ01b--published.pdf) comes from the [Biblioteca Digitale Italiana di Matematica](http://www.bdim.eu/item?fmt=pdf&id=RLIN_2001_9_12_1_5_0).
In §3, [Theorem 2 and Example 1](../../../literature/pdf/BZ01b--published.pdf#page=6) on printed page 8 take a normal algebraic extension \(L/\mathbb Q\) and the set \(S(L)\) of rational primes at which \(L\) embeds into a finite extension of \(\mathbb Q_p\).
Writing \(e_p,f_p\) for the ramification index and residue degree of its local closure, the authors bound the lower limit of absolute logarithmic heights of distinct elements of \(L\) by \(\tfrac12\sum_{p\in S(L)}\log p/[e_p(p^{f_p}+1)]\) from below.
Already one such prime gives the Bogomolov property, including for the field of all totally \(p\)-adic numbers.
The [finite-place Mahler spectrum argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v396.md#local) applies its own height estimate to equal-measure representatives of elements in towers over the locally constrained base.
