# ERT19 source and edition notes

## Yin

<a id="arxiv-v3"></a>
The inspected original is [arXiv:1506.03727v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.03727v3), dated 2 July 2018, with its [retained 26-page PDF](../../../literature/pdf/ERT19--arxiv-v3.pdf).
The title page identifies Vincent Emery, John G. Ratcliffe and Steven T. Tschantz and the exact version.
Printed and one-based PDF page numbers agree.
The arXiv record calls v3 the shortened final preprint, and it is this version's numbering that is used in [Yin_U001](Yin_U001.md#ert19-u001).

The published work is *Salem numbers and arithmetic hyperbolic groups*, *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* 372(1), 2019, pages 329--355, DOI 10.1090/tran/7655.
These publication facts are also listed in the [AMS publication's reference 10](https://www.ams.org/notices/202208/rnoti-p1358.pdf#page=10), and the [journal issue record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/e26704292) identifies the article and page range.
The canonical key ERT19 follows that formal year, not the 2015 initial arXiv submission or 2018 revision.
The journal body was not compared with v3.

[Yin_U001](Yin_U001.md#ert19-u001) retains the selected realization statement, exact companion and integral trace Gram normalization, and the unproved integral splitting/generator bridge.
The inspection used extracted text and visual checks of the construction on printed/PDF pages 12--13; it does not claim visual inspection or proof review of all 26 pages.
