<a id="ep88-u001"></a>
<a id="products"></a>
## Easdown and Praeger, Theorem 3.1, printed p. 219 (PDF p. 13)

For a finite direct product of finite simple groups, the least faithful permutation degree is the sum of those of its factors.
The statement permits repeated isomorphic factors and intransitive faithful actions.
I checked the published definition and theorem statement for this application at statement level. [Published theorem](../../../literature/pdf/EP88.pdf#page=13)
