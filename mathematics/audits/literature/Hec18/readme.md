The inspected public author notes are Gert Heckman's [*Coxeter Groups*](https://www.math.ru.nl/~heckman/CoxeterGroups.pdf), dated October 10, 2018, and retained locally as [Hec18--20181010.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/Hec18--20181010.pdf).
The title page identifies Radboud University Nijmegen and explicitly marks the notes unfinished.
This is a public draft edition, not an asserted published textbook or a checked final version.
The complete retained file has 150 PDF pages and ends with its subject index on printed page 150.
Printed and PDF page numbers agree at the selected mathematical passages.
The supplied contents on printed/PDF pages 3--4 have a [complete searchable transcription](../../../literature/contents/Hec18--20181010.md).

[Yin_U001](Yin_U001.md#hec18-u001) records the finite normalized-root and simple-root interfaces, the finite Coxeter classification statement, the classical coordinate models and the normalized F4 basis.
The inspection does not constitute a complete proof audit of the classification or a review of the remaining chapters.
