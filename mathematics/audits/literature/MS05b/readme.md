# McKee and Smyth on Salem numbers from graphs

The [published article](https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.2005.10128915) appears in *Experimental Mathematics* 14 (2005), 211–229.
The [retained manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/MS05b--arxiv-v1.pdf) is arXiv:math/0503480v1, 23 March 2005, with internal pages 1–28.
For the graph classifications used here, Lemmas 2, 3, 5 and Proposition 7 in that manuscript correspond to Lemmas 2.1, 2.2, 2.4 and Proposition 3.2 in the journal article.
See [Yin's source unit](Yin_U001.md#ms05b-u001) for the hypotheses and scope of inspection.
