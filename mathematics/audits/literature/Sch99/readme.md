# Sch99: Reducibility of lacunary polynomials XII

## Yin's inspected edition

The inspected source is the seventeen-page published article, printed pp.273--289, obtained from the [ICM mathematical library](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa90/aa9035.pdf) and retained as [Sch99--published.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/Sch99--published.pdf).
Physical PDF page 1 is printed page 273; there is no separate cover in this copy.
The [publisher's article page](https://www.impan.pl/pl/wydawnictwa/czasopisma-i-serie-wydawnicze/acta-arithmetica/all/90/3/110781/reducibility-of-lacunary-polynomials-xii) confirms the publication identity recorded in the bibliography.

The actual bounded reading covered the conventions on printed pp.273--274, the complete statements of Theorems 1--4 and Corollaries 1--3 on pp.274--276, and the notation in the proof of Theorem 3 and the beginning of the proof of Corollary 1 on pp.283--284.
In particular, the Laurent monomial normalization J and the all-embeddings condition (10) in Theorem 3 were read in the original.
The mathematical comparison and the precise substitution-before-normalization order belong to [L450 v6](../../../ledger/Yin_L_G0450_v6.md#moving-map-stronger-source-boundary), without a duplicate U.
This reading is an applicability inspection of the 1999 printed statement, not an audit of its proof or an adoption of its conclusion.

A published [2019 corrigendum](../Sch19/readme.md) was identified, but its text was not obtained.
Its scope remains unknown; no assertion that it leaves Theorem 3 unchanged is made.
