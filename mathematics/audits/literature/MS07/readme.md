# Margalit and Spallone on homological criteria for pseudo-Anosov maps

The [journal article](../../../literature/pdf/MS07--journal.pdf) has printed pages 853–863. [Publisher's edition](https://intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/mrl/2007/0014/0005/MRL-2007-0014-0005-a012.pdf)
Page 853 states the surjectivity of the mapping class group onto the integral symplectic group for the closed surface.
Pages 855–856 characterize the symplectic characteristic polynomials, and Proposition 2 on p. 856 gives the closed-surface criterion with the three polynomial hypotheses.
Section 3.2 also constructs a matrix for a prescribed polynomial.
The record application uses Proposition 2 and this unrestricted matrix realization; the sufficient coefficient conditions of the introductory construction are unnecessary.
