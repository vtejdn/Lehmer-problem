# Lin82

The [retained journal scan](../../../literature/pdf/Lin82.pdf) begins on printed p. 49, which is PDF page 1 in the [author's copy](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~lind/Papers/Quasihyperbolic.pdf).
Section 4 on printed p. 55 (PDF page 7) identifies the full periodic point count with the cyclic determinant and gives its limiting logarithmic growth for a fixed ergodic integral toral automorphism, using Gel'fond's estimate for the algebraic eigenvalues of modulus one.
The sentence immediately after the displayed limit reverses the inequality in the exceptional set.
For fixed algebraic \(\lambda\) of modulus one and infinite order, \(|\lambda^k-1|<e^{-\epsilon k}\) occurs only finitely often for each \(\epsilon>0\), as also follows from the [unit-circle logarithmic form estimate](../Wus02/Yin_U001.md#unit-circle).
Lemma 4.1 on printed p. 56 (PDF page 8) gives a neighborhood on which the annihilator of the period subgroup contains only the zero character.
Theorem 3 on printed p. 60 (PDF page 12) gives exponential convergence for Hölder test functions.
