# Boyd on the range of Mahler's measure

The journal edition occupies pp. 453–469 of *Canadian Mathematical Bulletin* 24 (1981), no. 4.
At the end of §6, printed p. 461 (PDF p. 9), Boyd asks whether the minimum Mahler measure of an irreducible polynomial tends to infinity with the dimension of the convex hull of its exponent vectors.
