# Lazami on Pisot numbers below two

<a id="edition"></a>
## Yin's source edition

Faouzia Lazami's exposé 3 in volume 20 of the Séminaire Delange–Pisot–Poitou was given on 23 October 1978.
The printed text records receipt on 24 January 1979.
Its six pages are numbered 3-01 through 3-06, following the archive's cover page in the [Numdam scan](https://www.numdam.org/item/SDPP_1978-1979__20_1_A2_0.pdf).
The author cites her earlier announcements under the name Talmoudi.
The [fixed polynomial families](Yin_U001.md#compact-pisot-families) are the conclusion of the final theorem and its proof.
