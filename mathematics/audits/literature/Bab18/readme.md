# Babai's ICM account of the graph isomorphism problem

## Yin's source editions

The author manuscript of 18 February 2018 places Definition 4.1 and Theorem 4.2 together on p. 6.
The retained 20-page NSF copy has unresolved citation labels in the body, but includes the attribution to Pyber immediately below the theorem and the bibliographic entry on p. 20. [Retained manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/Bab18--nsf.pdf) · [NSF copy](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10179670) · [Author's prepublication version](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~laci/papers/icm18-babai-prepub.pdf)

The typeset ICM copy listed by Babai prints pp. 3303–3320 and places Definition 4.1 on p. 3307 and Theorem 4.2 on p. 3308.
The World Scientific chapter published in 2019 carries pp. 3319–3336 and uses the singular “Group” in its title.
The author's publication list gives the final page range and distinguishes the typeset ICM copy from the publicly licensed manuscript. [Author's publication list](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~laci/papers/) · [World Scientific chapter](https://doi.org/10.1142/9789813272880_0183)
