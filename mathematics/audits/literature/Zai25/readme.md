## Yin's source editions

Toufik Zaïmi's article appeared in *International Journal of Number Theory* 21 (2025), no. 9, 2157–2170. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1142/S1793042125501040)
The original printed title page and Theorem 1.1 are available in a [publisher-supplied first-page preview](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391822541_On_Salem_numbers_which_are_exceptional_units).
The specific fixed-list comparison was read in the author's twelve-page [arXiv version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.07723v2), whose printed and PDF page numbers agree.
The [retained preprint](../../../literature/pdf/Zai25--arxiv-v2.pdf) has no supplied table of contents.
[Yin's source note](Yin_U001.md#zai25-u001) distinguishes the two theorem locators.
