## Cambridge print edition of 2002

The publisher's excerpt contains G. Wüstholz's complete chapter, *One Century of Logarithmic Forms*, on printed pp. 1–10, which correspond to PDF pp. 1–10.
The quantitative theorem for ordinary complex logarithms is in §5, from the bottom of p. 5 through the first part of p. 6. [Chapter DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511542961.002) · [Publisher's PDF](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/07999/excerpt/9780521807999_excerpt.pdf)

[Yin's source note](Yin_U001.md#logarithmic-form) records the modified heights and the explicit constant.
Its [power estimate](Yin_U001.md#unit-circle) applies to algebraic numbers of any degree.
