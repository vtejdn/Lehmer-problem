# A computable level for maximal growth of cyclotomic Kummer fields

<a id="division-fields"></a>
## The rank-one specialization

Fix a number field \(K\) and an element \(a\in K^\times\) of infinite multiplicative order.
For \(g\ge1\) let \(L_g=K(\zeta_g,\gamma)\), with \(\zeta_g\) a primitive \(g\)th root of unity and \(\gamma^g=a\).
The choice of \(\gamma\) gives the same field since it contains all the \(g\)th roots of unity.
Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 yield a computable positive integer \(M\), depending on \(K,a\), for which \(m=\gcd(g,M)\) gives
\[
                  [L_g:L_m]=\frac{\varphi(g)}{\varphi(m)}\,\frac{g}{m}.
\]
For compatible generators use \(\zeta_m=\zeta_g^{g/m}\) and \(\gamma^{g/m}\) in \(L_m\).
The source treats \(r\) generators of rank \(r\) and allows separate radical exponents dividing the cyclotomic exponent.
Taking \(r=1\) and equal exponents gives the displayed identity. [Theorem 1.1](../../../literature/pdf/PST21--author.pdf#page=1) [Theorem 1.2](../../../literature/pdf/PST21--author.pdf#page=2)

The proof of Theorem 1.1 chooses a positive level attaining the maximal degree defect.
The effectivity proof takes the computable level from Theorem 5.4 and combines its Kummer degree calculation with the cyclotomic degree calculation of Lemma 2.4.
The inspection checked these statements and the final specialization, accepting the supporting arithmetic results at their published scope. [Choice of a positive level](../../../literature/pdf/PST21--author.pdf#page=5) [Effectivity argument](../../../literature/pdf/PST21--author.pdf#page=14)

The displayed fields contain roots of unity.
Applying their relative degree to the field generated by an individual radical requires a tower argument.
The constant is attached to the chosen field and generator. [Inspected edition](readme.md#edition)
