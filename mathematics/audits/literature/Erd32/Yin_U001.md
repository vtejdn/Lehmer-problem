# A prime between an integer and its double

For every integer \(a\ge1\) there is a prime \(p\) with \(a<p\le2a\).
For \(a\ge2\) the upper endpoint is composite, so the interval may be taken open at both ends.
Section 4 obtains the prime for \(a\ge4000\), and §5 covers the remaining integers by a finite sequence of primes ending at \(4001\) in which each term after the first is less than twice its predecessor. [Inspected journal edition, pp. 197–198](readme.md#edition)

The inspection checked the statement, both endpoints, the division into ranges and their application at \(a=2D\) for positive integer \(D\).
The prime-existence theorem is accepted at that scope.
The estimates for the central binomial coefficient in §§1–4 were outside the proof inspection.
