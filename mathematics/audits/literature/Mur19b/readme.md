# Murillo on the systoles of principal congruence covers

The [20-page author version](../../../literature/pdf/Mur19b--arxiv-v2.pdf) is [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.03870v2), dated 3 October 2017, and includes the appendix with Cayo Dória.
Formula (2) on page 2 and Theorem 6.1 on page 14 give the spin-group normalization and specify the admissible levels.
The statement fixes the initial group and excludes the prime divisors in a finite set before varying the ideal.
The [journal edition](https://doi.org/10.4171/GGD/515) appeared in 2019 on pages 1083–1102.
