<a id="positive-e8"></a>
## Lattice and reflection convention

**Definition (Section 1).**
With the positive Euclidean dot product, the source's lattice is
\[
\Lambda=\left\{x\in\mathbb Z^8+
  \mathbb Z(1/2,\ldots,1/2):\ \sum_i x_i\in2\mathbb Z\right\}.
\]
It identifies this as the unique positive even unimodular lattice of rank eight.
Its roots are \(\Phi=\{\alpha\in\Lambda:(\alpha,\alpha)=2\}\).
Section 2 defines \(W_8\) as the group generated by their orthogonal reflections, which in this normalization are
\[
s_\alpha(x)=x-(x,\alpha)\alpha.
\]

<a id="full-isometry-and-transitivity"></a>
## Exact selected statements

The opening of Section 2 states
\[
O(\Lambda)=\operatorname{Aut}(\Phi)=W_8,
\]
with order \(696729600\); this is the full integral isometry group, not its determinant-one subgroup.
It attributes the root-system automorphism equality to Humphreys, *Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory* (1972), Section 12.2.
Lemma 1 states that \(W_8\) is transitive on \(\Phi\), citing that book's Section 10.4, Lemma C.
Those cited proofs were not inspected.

The simple-root generators and precise Cartan-basis correspondence needed for J158 are supplied by the separate [JKZ08 passage](../JKZ08/Yin_U001.md#simple-generators-and-gram).
The [adopted J158 model](../../../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0158/coxeter_model.md#integral-quotient) supplies the actual positive, even, unimodular rank-eight Gram block and the norm-two vector \(\theta\).
Thus the source's orbit assertion applies to that vector and each norm-two simple basis vector, without scaling the pairing or changing the lattice.
No orthogonal-sum permutation theorem, indefinite canonical representation or transvection-generation theorem is asserted by this unit.
