# Taylor on the geometry of classical groups

## Yin's source edition

The 1992 book appeared as volume 9 of the *Sigma Series in Pure Mathematics*.
Taylor's 237-page electronic edition is marked “Corrections: April 2021”.
Its supplied contents run from PDF page 5 to PDF page 8 and its numbered body begins on PDF page 9.
For the body passages cited in the unit, add eight to the printed page number.
