# Boyer and Zhang on the canonical Newton polygon

Sections 4, 8, 9 and 10 of the [published article](https://doi.org/10.4310/jdg/1090349281) contain the inputs on norm curves and Newton polygons.
Corollary 8.3 and Proposition 10.1 have the same numbering in the [authors' manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/BZ01--author.pdf), with its own [contents and pagination](../../../literature/contents/BZ01--author.md).
The [source unit](Yin_U001.md#bz01-u001) records the result in eigenvalue coordinates.
