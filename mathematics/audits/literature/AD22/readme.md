## Yin's source edition

The 2022 journal article appeared in volume 13, issue 1, pp. 19–29. [Publisher's record](https://www.rivmat.unipr.it/vols/2022-13-1/02-amoroso.html)
The inspected author manuscript is dated 5 July 2023 and has a University of Turin cover followed by ten numbered manuscript pages.
The conjugate-group notation and Proposition 2.4 are on manuscript p. 4, the latter's proof on pp. 5–6, Lemma 3.1 on pp. 6–7 and Proposition 3.3 on p. 8.
Add one to each manuscript page number to locate it in this PDF. [Institutional manuscript](https://iris.unito.it/retrieve/95b2ede0-adf9-409c-b450-bc8fadef4a0b/LacGal12.pdf) · [Retained copy](../../../literature/pdf/AD22--author.pdf)
