# Voloch on plane curves and \(p\)-adic roots of unity

The [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972700036637) occupies pages 479–482 of volume 60 of the *Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society*.
The [publisher's PDF](../../../literature/pdf/Vol99--published.pdf) has four pages, with the main theorem on printed page 479 and the correction and differential calculation on pages 480–481.
