Definition2.1 uses the usual ordinary regulator: a maximal minor of logarithms of a free unit basis, with real weights1 and complex weights2.
Equivalently it is the Euclidean covolume in the sum-zero hyperplane divided by the square root of the number of archimedean places.
This convention agrees with R_K in [Sun18-U001](../Sun18/Yin_U001.md#sun18-u001), not with an unnormalized lattice covolume.

Theorem3.5 reports that for every extension L/K,

    R_L/R_K >= (c1*c2^[L:K])^[K:Q],
    c1=11.5^(-39), c2=1.15.

It attributes this statement to Friedman and Skoruppa, Relative regulators of number fields, Inventiones135 (1999),115--144, DOI10.1007/s002220050281.
