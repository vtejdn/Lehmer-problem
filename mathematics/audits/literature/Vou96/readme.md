# Voutier's effective lower bound for height

<a id="edition"></a>
## Yin's source edition

The article appeared in *Acta Arithmetica* 74 (1996), no. 1, pp. 81–95. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-74-1-81-95)
Voutier submitted the author version arXiv:1211.3110v1 on 13 November 2012 and identified the 1996 publication in the record.
Its Section 1 gives the height normalization on p. 1, the unnumbered Theorem on p. 3 and Corollary 2 on p. 4. [Author's edition record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.3110v1) · [Retained author PDF](../../../literature/pdf/Vou96--arxiv-v1.pdf)

[Yin's source unit](Yin_U001.md#height) records the two bounds on logarithmic Mahler measure.
