# The quantitative density bound for three-term progressions

Theorem 1 on p. 1 of the [authors' preprint](readme.md) bounds the cardinality of a subset of the first \(N\) positive integers having no three distinct terms in arithmetic progression.
Writing \(r_3(N)\) for the largest such cardinality, the theorem gives, for all sufficiently large \(N\),
\[
                    r_3(N)\le N\exp\bigl(-c(\log N)^{1/9}\bigr)
\]
with an absolute \(c>0\).
Translation gives the same bound on any interval of \(N\) consecutive integers, including \(\{0,\ldots,N-1\}\).
The estimate allows any positive common difference and supplies no numerical value of \(c\) in the statement.
I checked the ambient interval, exponent and constant in the statement of Theorem 1 on the [first page](../../../literature/pdf/BS23--arxiv-v1.pdf#page=1).
The project uses the authors' theorem at statement level.
