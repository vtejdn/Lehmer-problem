# Rivin on mapping tori with prescribed homological action

The [arXiv v4 preprint](../../../literature/pdf/Riv14--arxiv-v4.pdf) is dated 28 April 2014 and has 36 pages. [arXiv edition](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.5736v4)
Its [contents](../../../literature/contents/Riv14--arxiv-v4.md) occupy pp. 1–2.
Corollary 2.2 on p. 5 identifies the first homology of the mapping torus, and Theorem 2.6 on p. 6 recalls Thurston's hyperbolization for pseudo-Anosov bundles.
Corollary 5.4 on p. 9 states linear growth of the Gromov norm along random products in a fixed coset of a nonelementary group, with constants depending on the generators.
Section 6 on p. 13 applies the coset method to a lift of a symplectic matrix and elements of the Torelli group.
The volume comparison in the working draft uses only unboundedness in one such coset at a time.
