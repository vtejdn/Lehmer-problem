# Boyd on Pisot and Salem numbers in real intervals

<a id="editions"></a>
## Yin's source editions

David W. Boyd's paper appeared in *Mathematics of Computation* 32 (1978), no. 144, pp. 1244–1260. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1978-0491587-8)
The [author-posted journal scan](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/247185150_Pisot_and_Salem_Numbers_in_Intervals_of_the_Real_Line) reproduces the published pagination, which is used in [Yin's source note](Yin_U001.md#boy78-u001).
The earlier passages were read online when the publisher's download address returned its journal index and the author's hosting site declined the download.
On 2026-11-18 the direct AMS article PDF supplied the complete journal edition, with printed p. 1256 on PDF page 13.
That page identifies the least nontrivial point at a fixed initial coefficient and distinguishes it from a proposed least limit point.
