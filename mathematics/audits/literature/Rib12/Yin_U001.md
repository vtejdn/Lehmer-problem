### Complete conventions and accepted theorem

A pro-2 group is a profinite inverse limit of finite groups of2-power order.
A finite set topologically generates it when the abstract subgroup it generates is dense.
A free pro-2 group on a finite set X is a pro-2 group with the universal property that every map from X to a pro-2 group extends uniquely to a continuous homomorphism.
Its rank is the size of X.
The free rank-one pro-2 group is the additive group Z_2.
An open subgroup has finite index, but finite index alone in an arbitrary profinite group is not being substituted for openness.

Theorem2.6.1 states that an open subgroup of a free pro-C group is free pro-C when the class C of finite groups is closed under extensions.
Take C to be all finite2-groups, which meets this hypothesis.
It follows that every open subgroup of a free pro-2 group is free pro-2.
The source supplies a Schreier basis; its displayed theorem does not separately print a numerical finite-rank formula, and no such formula is needed in the current application.

This source does not alone assert that every open subgroup of a nonabelian free pro-2 group is nonmetabelian.
The consuming argument must establish its rank is at least2 and supply the required nonmetabelian consequence, rather than inserting that claim into the source theorem.
The cyclotomic/radical Galois comparison and the actual arithmetic field realization are also separate inputs.
