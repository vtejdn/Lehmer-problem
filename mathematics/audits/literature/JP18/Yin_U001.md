# Capelli's criterion for every binomial exponent

For a number field \(K\), a nonzero \(a\in K\) and \(n\ge2\), Theorem 2.2 gives the equivalence
\[
 X^n-a\ \text{is reducible in }K[X]
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 a\in\bigcup_{\substack{p\mid n\\p\ {\rm prime}}}K^p
 \ \text{ or }\ (4\mid n\ \text{and }a\in-4K^4).
\]
Thus the list of prime divisors of \(n\) suffices together with one fourth-power test when \(4\mid n\).
The authors state the result over fields and attribute the characteristic-zero criterion to Capelli in Remark 2.3. [Theorem 2.2 and Remark 2.3](../../../literature/pdf/JP18--published.pdf#page=2)

On the same page Theorem 2.1 states that, for irreducible \(P\in K[X]\) with root \(\alpha\), reducibility of \(P(h(X))\) over \(K\) is equivalent to reducibility of \(h(X)-\alpha\) over \(K(\alpha)\).
The paper quotes these results as preliminaries to its dihedral calculation.
The inspection covers their statements and application to number fields. [Theorem 2.1](../../../literature/pdf/JP18--published.pdf#page=2) [Inspected edition](readme.md#edition)
