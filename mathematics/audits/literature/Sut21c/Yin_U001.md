## Exact interface

Let A be a Dedekind domain with fraction field K, let L/K be a finite separable extension, and let B be the integral closure of A in L.
Suppose alpha is integral, L=K(alpha), B=A[alpha], and f is its monic minimal polynomial.
For a nonzero prime p of A, factor the reduction of f into distinct monic irreducibles gbar_i with exponents e_i.
With any lifts g_i, the ideals q_i=(p,g_i(alpha))B are exactly the primes above p, the factorization of pB is product_i q_i^(e_i), and the corresponding residue degrees equal deg gbar_i.
The explicit condition B=A[alpha] matters; a field generator alone does not satisfy it.
