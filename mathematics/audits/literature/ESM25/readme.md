Read definitions on printed p.605, examples on pp.606–607, and Theorems 2.2–2.3 on p.607; visually checked PDF p.5.
Cyclotomic shortness is precisely the minimum coefficient l1 norm under cyclotomic multiplication, allowing repeated factors: L G0027's L(p) is this established invariant.
Reciprocal shortness permits additional multipliers and need not preserve Mahler measure.

Theorem 2.2 concerns shortness 5 with a fixed positive house separation from 1.
Theorem 2.3 concerns cyclotomic shortness 6, the same separation, and a Mahler upper bound strictly below the smallest Pisot number.
These are statement-level readings only: proofs, algorithms and reported minimality computations were not audited or rerun, and are not adopted as new theorem dependencies.

Applicability conclusion: these statements do not provide the uniform shortness bound missing from [L G0027](../../../ledger/Yin_L_G0027_v1.md#strategy-consequence).
A vanishing positive Mahler sequence also has house tending to 1, since house is at most the multiplicative Mahler measure.
Thus no fixed house cutoff covers that sequence.
This is a direct quantifier check, not a refutation of the source results or Lehmer.
