## Yin's source edition

J. Dufresnoy and Ch. Pisot published the article in *Annales scientifiques de l'École Normale Supérieure*, third series, 72 (1955), no. 1, pp. 69–92. [Journal record](https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.1030)
The Numdam edition includes one cover sheet before printed p. 69, with printed pp. 81–82 on PDF pages 14–15. [Numdam scan](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1955_3_72_1_69_0.pdf) · [Retained journal scan](../../../literature/pdf/DP55.pdf)
