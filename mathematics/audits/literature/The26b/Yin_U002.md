## Statement and accepted use

For an INJECTIVE ring inclusion R subset S such that S is integral over R, the induced map Spec(S)->Spec(R) is surjective.
Thus every prime ideal p of R is the contraction of at least one prime ideal q of S.
Injectivity and integrality are both part of the inspected statement; a general ring map without them is not being substituted.

For the actual original monogenic order R=Z[alpha] in a number field K and its full integer ring O_K, R subset O_K is injective.
Every element of O_K is integral over Z and hence over R by the same monic polynomial, so the theorem applies.
An ACTUAL residue homomorphism R->F_p with prime kernel therefore has a prime of O_K above that kernel.
If specified elements of R are in the kernel, they lie in that prime of O_K as well.
The number-field prime and its localization then use the previously accepted standard Dedekind/DVR environment of [U001](Yin_U001.md#the26b-u001).

This supports only the order-to-full-ring existence step in the [fixed actual coupled-defect realization](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0150_v1.md#actual-extra-five-control).
It does not prove the project's explicit polynomial example, irreducibility, weighted valuation bounds, ideal containments, norm fees or the index cost of contracting an ideal back to R.
None of those deductions is supplied by lying over.
No prime is assumed unramified or of residue degree1 merely because a prime of R has residue field F_p.
