# GM02 source and edition notes

## Yin

<a id="author-copy"></a>
The inspected original is the [author-hosted PDF](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/papers/home/text/papers/unim/unim.pdf), retained as [GM02--author-2002.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/GM02--author-2002.pdf).
The retained copy has 30 PDF pages and displays the title *Automorphisms of even unimodular lattices and unramified Salem numbers*, Benedict H. Gross and Curtis T. McMullen, and 1 January 2002 on its title page.
The edition suffix records that displayed date, rather than certifying when the PDF bytes were first issued or that this is the journal typesetting.
The title/contents page is unnumbered; printed page 1 begins at one-based PDF page 2, and the selected printed pages 10--15 correspond to PDF pages 11--16.
The supplied contents list is transcribed in [the same-copy contents](../../../literature/contents/GM02--author-2002.md).

The [publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021869302005525) identifies the published work as *Journal of Algebra* 257(2), 15 November 2002, pages 265--290, DOI 10.1016/S0021-8693(02)00552-5.
The canonical bibliography uses the publication year in GM02.
The [Harvard DASH record](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/73120378-81ee-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b) describes a separate deposit as revised in 2008; that deposit and the journal body were not compared with the retained author copy.
The inspected statements therefore use only the numbering and content of the linked author copy.

GM02 is the joint paper, distinct from Gross's single-author [Gro02](../Gro02/readme.md), *Unramified reciprocal polynomials and Coxeter decompositions*.
The Gro02 source notes and their earlier inspection do not supply inspection of GM02's realization theorem.
[Yin_U001](Yin_U001.md#gm02-u001) retains the selected theorem, signature hypotheses, fractional-ideal and pairing interface, and limits of changing the fixed trace-form realization.
