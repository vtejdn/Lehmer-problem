## Yin's inspected edition

The AMS journal PDF of *Integer transfinite diameter and polynomials with small Mahler measure* is the published version in *Mathematics of Computation* 75, no. 255 (July 2006), pp. 1527–1540.
Its first page records receipt on 24 November 2004, revision on 8 February 2005 and online publication on 28 March 2006.
I verified the fourteen-page original, including the typeset weights on p. 1534 and polynomial rows on p. 1536.
The journal edition is available from the publisher. [Retained journal edition](../../../literature/pdf/FRS06.pdf) · [Publisher PDF](https://www.ams.org/journals/mcom/2006-75-255/S0025-5718-06-01791-1/S0025-5718-06-01791-1.pdf) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-06-01791-1)

On 19 November 2026 I found no separate corrigendum in the coauthor's publication list or in a search for the title with “erratum” or “corrigendum”.
The Crossref record for the original DOI contained no relation or update entry.
These checks identify the edition inspected and leave the authors' intended correction to the numerical example unresolved.
