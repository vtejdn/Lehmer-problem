# Quotients without abelian normal subgroups

<a id="kp00-u001"></a>
<a id="quotient"></a>
## Kovács and Praeger, Theorem 1, printed p. 312 (PDF p. 2)

For a finite group and a normal subgroup, the least faithful permutation degree of the quotient is at most that of the group whenever the quotient has no nontrivial abelian normal subgroup.
The hypothesis concerns the quotient itself and places no restriction on the kernel.
