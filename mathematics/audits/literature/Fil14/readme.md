# Fili on heights of totally local algebraic integers

The [published edition](../../../literature/pdf/Fil14--published.pdf) is available from [Numdam](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.861.pdf).
In [Definitions 1 and 2](../../../literature/pdf/Fil14--published.pdf#page=2) on printed page 103, totality at a place \(v\) means that all conjugates over the base number field \(K\) lie in the chosen finite Galois extension \(L_v/K_v\), equivalently that the minimal polynomial splits there.
For a set \(S\) of finite rational primes, [Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1](../../../literature/pdf/Fil14--published.pdf#page=3) on printed pages 104–105 give the rational-base lower bound \(\tfrac12\sum_{p\in S}\log p/[e_p(p^{f_p}-1)]\) for the lower limit of heights of distinct algebraic integers totally in the prescribed local fields, whose ramification indices and residue degrees are \(e_p,f_p\).
The [finite-place Mahler spectrum argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v396.md#local) gives its own pointwise height bound for nontorsion representatives, allowing nonintegral elements and a bound on each conjugate's local degree.
