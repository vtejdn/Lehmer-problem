Theorem2.1 states the Gauss-lemma integer-factor property for a monic integer polynomial: a factorization into monic rational polynomials has integer coefficients.
Theorem4.1 states the MONIC Eisenstein criterion: a monic integer polynomial whose nonleading coefficients are divisible by a prime p and whose constant term is not divisible by p^2 is irreducible over Q.
The selected proof reduces putative factors modulo p and uses the incompatible divisibility of their constant terms.

These established statements are accepted at their checked scope.
