# Complex Salem numbers and the same-measure lift of a Salem number

<a id="pt21-u001"></a>
## The definition and Conjecture (G)

In §2.3 of the [arXiv v2 manuscript](readme.md#arxiv-v2), page 5, Lam Pham and François Thilmany call a nonreal algebraic integer \(z\) a complex Salem number when the conjugates of modulus greater than one are exactly \(z,\overline z\), and at least one conjugate has modulus one.
Consequently its multiplicative Mahler measure is \(|z|^2\).
Their Conjecture (G) asserts that there exists \(\varepsilon>0\) such that \(|z|>1+\varepsilon\) for every such number.

The ordinary Salem number \(\alpha\) of §2.2, manuscript page 4, is a real algebraic integer greater than one whose other conjugates have modulus at most one, with at least one on the unit circle.
Writing \(P_\alpha\) for its minimal polynomial, the authors state on page 5 that \(P_\alpha(-X^2)\) is the minimal polynomial of a complex Salem number with the same Mahler measure as \(\alpha\).
Thus Conjecture (G) would imply the uniform gap for ordinary Salem numbers.

Section 2.3, manuscript pages 4–5, identifies (G) with the existence of a universal positive lower bound on closed geodesic lengths in arithmetic hyperbolic \(3\)-orbifolds.
The definition, lift and direction of implication were checked against the manuscript, including the rendered formula on page 5.
The geometric equivalence was inspected at statement level.
