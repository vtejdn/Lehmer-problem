<a id="place-space-and-height-normalization"></a>
## Definition: the place-space model and its normalization

Fix an algebraic closure Qbar and let Y be its places.
For a number field K and a place v of K over the rational place pi, let Y(K,v) be the set of places of Qbar extending v.
The source measure is specified by

    lambda(Y(K,v))=[K_v:Q_pi]/[K:Q].

Each rational-place component Y(Q,pi) has measure one; their whole union Y has infinite measure.
For alpha in Qbar^* define f_alpha(y)=log|alpha|_y, with absolute values extending the usual rational ones.
The functions form the Q-vector space F identified with Qbar^* modulo roots of unity, where multiplication becomes addition and rational powers become rational scalar multiplication.
Every f_alpha has compact support and integral zero by the product formula.
The exact normalization is

    ||f_alpha||_1=2 h_abs(alpha),
    h_p(alpha)=||f_alpha||_p,
    m_p(alpha)=[Q(alpha):Q] h_p(alpha),
    m_1(alpha)=2 m(P_alpha).

Thus the source's m_1 is twice the ORIGINAL TOTAL logarithmic Mahler measure, not the absolute height.
The degree of a chosen representative is not invariant under multiplication by a root of unity, even though its function and height are invariant.
All finite places are included for nonunits.
The subspace U of algebraic units consists precisely of functions supported on the probability space Y(Q,infinity).
It is a Q-vector space: roots of units are still units.
Consequently no vector-space norm can have a positive minimum on all nonzero U or all nonzero F, because rational scaling tends to zero.

<a id="field-and-degree-projections"></a>
## Definitions: field projections, degree components and the Mahler operator

For a number field K, V_K is the Q-span of functions represented by elements of K^*.
Membership in V_K permits taking roots; it does not require a representative in K itself.
The minimal field K_f is the least field with f in V_K, and delta(f)=[K_f:Q] is the finite Galois orbit size of f.
For a nonzero f, define

    d(f_alpha)=min{[Q(zeta alpha):Q]: zeta a root of unity}.

This d(f) is the minimal representative degree and may differ from an original chosen degree.

For H_K=Gal(Qbar/K) with probability Haar measure, the source field projection is

    P_K f = integral_(H_K) L_sigma f d sigma,
    L_sigma f(y)=f(sigma^(-1)y).

This is a finite orbit average on F.
If P_K f_alpha=f_beta, its algebraic meaning is

    beta congruent Norm_(K(alpha)/K)(alpha)^(1/[K(alpha):K])
                                        modulo roots of unity.

It is a NORMALIZED algebraic norm, often requiring a radical representative.
It is a contraction in every Lp norm, preserves U, and is an orthogonal projection in L2.
Its conditional-expectation description averages on the cylinders Y(K,v); it is a probability conditional expectation only after restricting to one rational-place component, in particular to U.

For finite Galois K/Q the source constructs orthogonal components T_K from the P_L with L Galois and L contained in K.
They provide a finite direct-sum decomposition of each f in F by Galois field.
The degree filtration is

    V^(n)=sum_([K:Q]<=n) V_K.

Let P^(n) be its orthogonal projection and put T^(1)=P^(1), T^(n)=P^(n)-P^(n-1) for n>1.
Theorems 1--3 assert that these operators preserve F, give finite expansions there, and T_K commutes with T^(n).
The source does not assert a canonical mutually orthogonal decomposition by ALL nonnormal fields.
Its obstruction is the nontrivial linear dependence between conjugate-field subspaces.

The Mahler operator and norm are

    M f=sum_(n>=1) n T^(n)f,
    ||f||_(m,p)=||M f||_p.

The sum is finite on F, M is invertible there, and M is unbounded in every original Lp norm.
These facts do not give a uniformly bounded operator on the original Lp completion.
The new completion in ||.||_(m,p) is a different Banach space.
For p=2 the source's exact identity is

    ||f||_(m,2)^2=sum_n n^2||T^(n)f||_2^2
                 =sum_(K Galois) sum_n n^2||T_K T^(n)f||_2^2.

This is the Hilbert decomposition asserted by Theorem 6.
It expresses a norm; it provides no positive lower bound for its values on the relevant arithmetic set.

## Definitions: representability, projection irreducibility and the exact reduction

The source's representable set is R={f: d(f)=delta(f)}.
Equivalently f has a representative in its minimal field with that field's degree; a torsion-free representative supplies this property.
Projection irreducibility means

    P_K f=0 for every proper subfield K of K_f.

Write P for that set and S=R intersection P intersection U.
These are arithmetic restrictions; P is not a vector subspace, and R is not the whole rationally divisible unit space.
In particular the letter R here has no relation to a finite coefficient projection in the G0001 computation files.

Proposition 4.5 gives M f=delta(f) f for f in P.
Thus for f in S,

    ||f||_(m,p)=d(f)||f||_p=m_p(f),

where m_p(f) is the minimum of m_p(alpha) over torsion-equivalent representatives.
This equality is not asserted for arbitrary f or an arbitrary original representative alpha.
For p=2 the source proves the general UPPER comparison

    ||f||_(m,2)<=delta(f)||f||_2<=d(f)||f||_2.

A lower bound on raw d(f)||f||_2 cannot therefore be silently transferred to the Mahler 2-norm outside the restricted set.

Theorem 4 identifies the universal lower bound for m_p(alpha) with a uniform positive lower bound for ||f||_(m,p) on nonzero S.
Its reductions retain the arithmetic set: nonunits have a finite-place floor; contraction to a suitable minimal nonzero field projection gives projection irreducibility; a controlled rational-line rescaling gives representability without changing the minimized m_p.
This is an equivalence and reduction theorem, not a proof of the uniform lower bound.
The field contraction need not preserve the original polynomial, its degree or its chosen algebraic value.
It supplies no integer polynomial coefficient vector, fixed original quotient-fiber representative or coefficient l1 bound.

<a id="selected-unit-subgroup"></a>
## Definition and status: the selected subgroup Gamma_p

For each finite Galois K, let W_K=T_K(U) intersection P intersection R.
In every nonzero component, the 2013 construction chooses a NONZERO f_(K,p) of smallest Mahler p-norm in W_K; set it to zero only for a zero component.
The required existence uses a fixed-degree Northcott-type finiteness result, not an already uniform bound across all K.
The choice may depend on p.
The intended group is

    Gamma_p=direct_sum_(K Galois) Z f_(K,p)
           contained in the additive group generated by S.

This is a countable free abelian group, not the whole Q-vector space U and not the whole group generated by S.
Theorem 5 states that the p-Lehmer lower-bound assertion is equivalent to Gamma_p being closed in the completed unit Mahler p-norm space.
The proof also relates this to discreteness: a countable additive subgroup of a Banach space that is closed must be discrete.
Neither closedness nor a uniform shortest-vector bound is proved for p=1 or p=2 by this construction.

The fixed-field minima can tend to zero as K varies unless a new uniform arithmetic argument excludes that behavior.
Orthogonality, integral coefficients in a free group and discrete finite-dimensional pieces do not exclude it.
For example, the abstract subgroup direct_sum_(j>=1) Z(e_j/j) of l2 has those properties but contains nonzero vectors of norm 1/j tending to zero.
This is a logical Hilbert-space comparison, not an algebraic-number or near-one counterexample.
In the source's argument, the arithmetic uniform bound on these component minima is an additional input; the later project p=2 result is recorded separately at C855 v2 below.

## Literal source guards in the inspected version

Proposition 4.6, page 28, has one sentence with the reversed equality h=2h_1.
The definitions, page 3 identity and the same paragraph's m_1=2m fix the actual convention h_1=2h; the reversed sentence is not used.
In equation (4.9), page 30, the final displayed inclusion omits the generated-group brackets around R intersection P intersection U.
The introduction, the preceding paragraph and the construction itself specify a subgroup of the GENERATED group; sums of projection-irreducible elements need not remain projection irreducible.
Likewise the minimization must range over nonzero elements when its component is nonzero, or it would choose zero and have no claimed equivalence.
These are explicit interpretation guards, not a claim of a complete source-proof audit.

The Salem discussion also needs the ordinary norm to be distinguished from P_F's normalized logarithm.
For the relative norm beta=Norm_(K/F)(tau), its logarithm at a real place is the SUM over extending embeddings; division by [K:F] belongs to P_F f_tau, not to beta itself.
To verify the relevant Salem projection claim directly, let tau>1 be Salem with K=Q(tau) and use the source's representability statement.
If the two nonzero real logarithms restrict to the same place of a proper F, their sum is zero and P_F f_tau=0.
If they restrict to distinct places, at the distinguished real place the ordinary relative norm is tau, because all other factors occur in conjugate pairs of modulus one.
Then beta=tau belongs to F, contradicting K=Q(tau).
Thus the needed projection irreducibility holds without transferring a normalized-root value as an element of F.
This local check addresses that used normalization only.

## Exact p=1, p=2 and p=infinity applicability to G0001

On units, M f is supported on a probability space, so

    ||f||_(m,1)<=||f||_(m,2)<=||f||_(m,infinity).

The direction of universal implications is therefore

    p=1 Lehmer gap ==> p=2 gap ==> p=infinity gap.

The p=1 statement is exactly the classical TOTAL Mahler gap, with the factor two already recorded.
The source's p=infinity statement is equivalent to the Schinzel--Zassenhaus house assertion.
Its historical conjecture wording is not current project status: the accepted [Dim19 house input](../Smy08/Yin_U001.md#smy08-house) supplies that endpoint at its already retained statement scope.
No reverse implication from this endpoint to p=2 or p=1 is supplied.
Moreover Gamma_infinity and Gamma_2 use potentially different minimizing choices; they are not a single fixed subgroup with automatically interchangeable norm topologies.

The earlier local radial-support/degree p=2 deduction and its house/Dobrowolski/Salem boundaries remain fixed and unassessed in [C855 v1](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0855_v1.md#statement).
The later [C855 v2 project proof](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0855_v2.md#decision-whole-independent-review-2026-11-04) supplies a universal raw p=2 floor and the operator consequence on S through a separate independently reviewed capacity and norm-descent argument.
That result is project mathematics, not a theorem or proof attributed to FM13, and it does not establish the original p=1 gap.

The bounded primary search for later p=2 resolution found the stated paper and related source references but no verified theorem resolving its general p=2 assertion.
This is the search outcome and the boundary of the accepted inputs, not an exhaustive current-literature status claim.

## Research consequence and limits

The p=2 inequality is a distinct intermediate target, weaker than the classical p=1 target and amenable to a Hilbert identity, so it is not literally another equivalent statement of p=1.
However the inspected source supplies no new arithmetic bound that proves this intermediate target, no automatic bounded Frobenius class and no uniform p=2-to-p=1 comparison.
A uniform p=2 floor alone would yield only a degree-dependent p=1 floor through the elementary comparisons.
An additional uniform radial-flatness estimate such as ||f||_(m,2)<=C||f||_(m,1) on the unresolved arithmetic inputs would be needed for that particular return; no such estimate is supplied here.
Merely having many nonzero radial coordinates does not supply this control.

The existing G0001 finite coefficient projections, fixed 2d quotient-fiber energies and B3 Haar logarithms live in different spaces and have different normalizations.
P_K is a normalized algebraic norm in logarithmic place space, T^(n) isolates arithmetic-degree components, and their output may change the field, representative and radical degree.
None is an identity for the finite coefficient projection or a free replacement of the original auxiliary's full norm, denominator, support or transfer fees.
The source's retained contribution is the precise reduction and normalization; the later project p=2 assessment belongs only to C855 v2.
No source-proof upgrade, uniform p=2-to-p=1 comparison or G0001 completion is claimed by this source unit.
