## Finding: the complete factorwise argument is sufficient

No substantive mathematical or source-interface gap was found.
For every allowed g, each monic rational irreducible factor is integral with constant term plus or minus one.
The only monic rational irreducible factors of measure one are \(z-1\) and \(z+1\).
Every other irreducible factor, and every monic rational divisor with measure greater than one, has complete Mahler measure at least the golden ratio.
The conclusion is uniform in g and the divisor and retains exactly the stated family restriction.

### Integral model and every complex fiber

The actual v1 identities and cell convention match the matrices in the new statement.

It does not require individual fibers to have rational coefficients or be squarefree.
The integral symplectic model gives a monic integral characteristic polynomial of even degree with constant one.

On the unit circle the displayed D is Hermitian, its square is 2u times the identity, and its two eigenvalues are plus and minus the square root of 2u, with the zero case included.
The displayed shift is unitary, squares to the phase times the identity and conjugates D to its negative.
The sign reversal gives the adjoint shear product, so the product order in equation (3) is correct.
Invertibility of the shear product gives strict positive definiteness.

Direct multiplication verifies all entries, determinant one and trace of K(d).
Its two positive eigenvalues are exactly the displayed squared expressions; their logarithms give equation (4) for every characteristic root.
Squaring eigenvalues is valid with algebraic multiplicities and does not require diagonalizability of the original fiber.

### Embeddings and the complete unit budget

For a chosen root outside those two linear factors, its unique phase has an order N greater than one dividing q.
The accepted cyclotomic irreducibility supplies every embedding sending that phase to a primitive power.
