Changing the exterior weights from window \(d-1\) to \(W\) multiplies the determinant by \(e^{-2m(W-d+1)}\).

The stable product bound \(\mathcal D\le(1-e^{-2m})^{d/2}\) gives \(\Delta\ge(2\sinh m)^{-2}\), so \(L_P\) diverges.
