## Whole-proof finding

Since the constant coefficient of the monic minimal polynomial of the unit is a unit, the class of \(X\) is invertible modulo its full reduction.
Coprimality keeps the other coefficient nonzero modulo each coefficient prime, so cancellation gives \(X^N=-\epsilon\), with the difference index for a prime of \(a\) and the sum index for a prime of \(b\).
This proves the stated divisibility of the order even when the reduction has repeated factors.
Representing \(\mathbb Z[\beta]\) as \(\mathbb Z[X]/S\) gives the claimed divisibility by \(\ell\) before taking the norm, and squaring at two gives divisibility by four.
Nontorsion makes both norm arguments nonzero, while multiplying \(|\sigma(\beta)^k-1|\le2\max(1,|\sigma(\beta)|)^k\) over embeddings supplies the matching upper bound for either exponent.

For the upper estimate the reciprocal graph in v869 has one zero inside the circle and its pole outside when \(B>1\).
Its circle degree gives at least \(v-u\) distinct unit-circle roots, leaving at most \(u\) exterior roots by reciprocal pairing with multiplicities.
The squared-modulus identity gives \(B-1\ge(r-1)(s-1)/(r+s)\) for \(r=e^{ut}\), \(s=e^{vt}\).
Writing the quotient in hyperbolic functions and using \(2\sinh(ut/2)\ge ut\) and \(\cosh((v-u)t/2)\le\cosh(vt/2)\) gives the radial bound used in the count.
The two nonzero projective coordinates representing \([a:b]\) give \(\log(H/2)\le (u+v)h(\beta)\) by the local triangle inequalities and the product formula.
Since \(h(\beta)\le t_{\max}\), the hypothesis \(H\ge8\) gives \(\tanh(vt_{\max}/2)\ge1/3\), and hence the total exterior sum is at most \(3(B-1)\).
This bounds the sum over conjugates of the primitive unit and gives the stated inequality for \(L/D\).

For consecutive absolute coefficients the even coefficient selects exactly the claimed index in characteristic two.
The order of a residual irreducible factor is odd, and raising its annihilating binomial to a power of two at least its multiplicity restores the required multiplicity.
Thus the two-part of the full order is at most the smallest power of two at least \(D\), and for \(N=c2^j\) its odd part divides \(c\).
For odd \(p\), a full positive \(p\)-part \(p^r\) of the least common multiple occurs in some root order, so \(p^r\mid 2^f-1\) for a factor degree \(f\le D\).
The binomial valuation formula for odd \(p\) gives \(v_p(2^{dk}-1)=e+v_p(k)\), proving the stated order formula and the bound including \(r\le e\) and \(r=0\).
The part coprime to \(p\) divides the fixed \(c\), so the resulting positive lower bound is uniform in the exponent and all directions and dilations.
