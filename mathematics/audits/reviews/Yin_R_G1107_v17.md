### Exact factors and complete measure

The exact integer factorization returns content one and two monic factors, both with exponent one: \(P=(z^2+1)R\), with \(\deg R=80\).
The program multiplies them back to the fixed \(P\) and checks the full degree with multiplicity.
The regenerated coefficients of \(R\) agree with the entire reciprocal coefficient list specified in candidate_diagnostic.md.
The exact cyclotomic test identifies \(z^2+1=\Phi_4\) and rejects \(R\) as cyclotomic.
Irreducibility is supplied by the documented exact factorization interface; this review did not audit FLINT's internal factoring implementation.

The 512-bit root calculation includes every returned root of each factor, counts root multiplicities inside each factor and factor exponents outside it, and verifies the total degree 82.
For a modulus enclosure below one, the contribution is exactly one; above one, the modulus enclosure is retained.
When it meets one, the ball union with the point one encloses \(\max(1,|\alpha|)\), even though it can be wider than that range.
The root product includes the leading coefficient, and the final product includes content and factor exponents.
For the exact cyclotomic quadratic, replacing its already checked root-product enclosure by one is justified by its known roots on the unit circle.

The quadratic contributes two unresolved modulus balls before that exact classification.
The factor \(R\) contributes three balls strictly inside, three strictly outside, and seventy-four meeting one.
The latter seventy-four are not proved to be exact unit-circle roots by this diagnostic and are still included in its full product.
The counted total is 82, with aggregate regions 3 inside, 3 outside and 76 unresolved before the cyclotomic replacement.
