# Independent review of critical slopes and the distribution at seed zeros

<a id="finding"></a>
## Necessary scales and simultaneous positive proportions

On 2026-11-20 an agent for Sheng Yin independently reviewed the [statement of C345 v507](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#statement) and all three sections of its proof.
The reviewer took no part in developing v507 and received the fixed claim and review scope without development dialogue, author explanations or earlier findings on v507.
The [earlier review of v506](Yin_R_G0562_v639.md#finding) was consulted as evidence about that dependency.
The estimates give all five asserted scales, reciprocity and both positive proportions uniformly for fixed \(B,K>0\).
Existence of factors attaining these scales remains open.

In the [scale comparison](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#scales), the unconditional [short-slope count](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#small) eventually exceeds \(Ka\).
Above its cutoff the [slope bounds](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#statement) force \(r\ge c^2s^3/K^2\), \(k/a\ge c/\sqrt B\), and \(m\ge c/(s\sqrt B)\).
The small-projected-measure alternative would give \(k/a\gg_B s/\log^4(2s)\), again exceeding \(K\).
Hence \(h=am\ge\log(2d)\), and the [individual first moment](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#moments) gives \(h\ll k/s\), so \(m\asymp 1/s\).
The endpoint coefficients make each root an algebraic unit, so [Smyth's theorem](../literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-statement-and-conventions) gives reciprocity once \(m<\log\theta\).
The [sparse divisor bound](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#discriminant) yields \(d\ll rh\ll as^2\), while reciprocity and the [square-moment bound](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#moments) give \(d\gg a^2s/h\gg as^2\).
In particular \(y=\log d\to\infty\), and [Voutier's degree-dependent bound](../literature/Vou96/Yin_U001.md#height) yields \(y\gg s^{1/3}\log y\).
Eventually \(\log y\ge1\), hence \(\log y\ge(\log s)/3-O(1)\), so \(y\gg s^{1/3}\log s\).
The upper degree bound gives \(\log a\ge y-2\log s-O(1)\), preserving that lower bound.

The [collision argument](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#collisions) uses [localization on every disk](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v250.md#poles), applicable since \(r/(s^2\log(2s))\to\infty\) uniformly under the derived lower bound on \(r\).
The disks are disjoint, each branch contributes one root, and the [upper bound for the whole parent](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v230.md#poles) exhausts its exterior roots.
For an assigned \(z=x^{-a}\), write \(L=-\log|z|\).
The disk radius \(O(s^{-4})\), \(|\zeta_j|>1/2\), and \(L_j\gg s^{-2}\) imply \(L_j/2\le L\le2L_j\) even at the shallowest pole.
Taylor's remainder divided by \(|z-\zeta_j|\) is \(O(s^2s^{-4})\), below half the uniform derivative lower bound for large \(s\).
Thus \(|z-\zeta_j|\ll|g_s(z)|\le3e^{-rL_j/2}\).
The denominator of the distance ratio for a pair in that disk is at least \(1-e^{-L_j}\gg s^{-2}\), and \(rL_j\gg s\) absorbs the resulting polynomial factor into \(e^{rL_j/4}\).
The [field and discriminant calculation](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#discriminant) retains degree \(d\) under projection by coprimality of \(a,b\) and the absence of common seed and reversal zeros.
In the squared discriminant each disk contributes \(\binom{k_j}{2}\) unordered distinct pairs, giving logarithmic loss at least \(rL_jk_j(k_j-1)/4\).
Since \(d/r\ll h\) and \(h\ge\log(2d)\), division by \(r\) bounds the factorial second moment by \(O(h^2)\).
The diagonal term is at most \(2h=O(h^2)\) because \(h=\sum L\) over the exterior roots of \(P\).

For the [joint selection](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#spread), the same comparison of radii gives \(H=\sum L_jk_j\ge h/2\).
The lower radial tail costs at most \(uKa/s\), and Cauchy–Schwarz with the [seed tail bound](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#moments) bounds the upper tail by \(O(h\sqrt{(1+U)e^{-U}})\).
Since \(h\asymp a/s\), fixed \(u,U\) leave at least \(h/4\) in the band and thus at least \(c_2a\) roots there.
The lower radius in the band gives \(\sum k_j^2\le C_2a^2/s\) there.
Over its at most \(s\) indices, occupancies below \(va/s\) cost at most \(va\) roots, and occupancies above \(Va/s\) cost at most \(C_2a/V\).
Choosing these two costs below \(c_2a/4\) leaves at least \(c_2a/2\) roots on the same indices, whose number is at least \(c_2s/(2V)\).
Their proportions are positive since \(k\le Ka\) and the seed has degree \(s\).
All cutoffs can be chosen using only \(B,K\) and the absolute constants of the fixed input estimates.
