At 2026-11-18T14:27 UTC an agent outside proof development independently checked the complete [weighted pencil-count theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v381.md#statement) and its several-node extension from the fixed proof without development dialogue.

The center \(b=\alpha^p\) has \(V(b)\ne0\), since a common zero of \(U,V\) would follow from \(F(\alpha)=0\) otherwise.
Thus \(H(b)=U(b)/V(b)=w=\alpha^r\) lies in the open disk, excluding a pencil zero at the center for every unit phase.
For any \(|s|<1\), the indicated linear row transformation has determinant \(1-|s|^2\), and
\[
 A-\xi B=(1+\bar s\xi)(U-tV),\qquad
 t=(s+\xi)/(1+\bar s\xi).
\]
The inverse circle map has absolute angular derivative \((1-|s|^2)/|1-\bar s t|^2=(1-|s|^2)/|t-s|^2\), verifying both the sign and the direction of the Poisson weight.
In the source disk the inverse of \(\psi_b\) takes \(z\) to \((z-b)/(1-\bar b z)\), so its modulus is \(\delta(b,z)\).
The composition is analytic past the closed unit disk, since \(|b|<1\).
Jensen's formula on radius \(R\) counts each interior zero with the weight \(\log(R/\delta)\) and its multiplicity.
A boundary zero contributes zero, also on the unit circle at \(R=1\), by the single-factor identity for the angular logarithmic mean.
The center value stays nonzero for all phases.
The compact-set upper bound on the positive logarithms and the bounded continuous phase mean \(f_s\) give integrability of the negative parts by Tonelli, justifying the interchange of integrations.
These facts give (2) with the stated subtraction of \(f_s(b)\).

Differentiation of \(H(x^p)-x^r\) at its double zero gives \(p\alpha^{p-1}H'(b)=r\alpha^{r-1}\).
In particular the zero of \(A(\psi_b(y))\) at zero when \(s=w\) is simple, even when \(F\) has higher multiplicity there.
Its first derivative equals \(V(b)H'(b)(1-|b|^2)\), while \(|B(b)|=(1-|w|^2)|V(b)|\).
Jensen applied after division by \(y\) therefore gives
\[
 J(b,R;w)\ge\log\!\left(R\frac{r}{p}
   e^{(p-r)u}\frac{1-e^{-2pu}}{1-e^{-2ru}}\right)=\log(RD_b).
\]
The nonnegative sum defining \(J\) supplies the maximum with zero.
Strict increase of \(\sinh(v)/v\) for \(v>0\) gives \(D_b>1\) for all \(p>r\).
For \(R>D_b^{-1}\) the positive finite integral and strictly positive bounded density give a positive-Haar-measure set of phases with an interior root.
The density maximum is \((1+|w|)/(1-|w|)\), giving the claimed unweighted integral bound.

Thus \(f_s(b)=\log|B(b)|\), and retaining the zeros \(b_j\) with \(H(b_j)=s\) in Jensen's formula adds their terms to \(\log\delta(s,w)\) with the stated sign.
