## Finding

All three estimates hold at the stated scales.
For the original witnesses the two nonzero endpoints of \(a\) and the smaller degree of \(b\) ensure \(H\ne0\).
The coefficient product bound places \(I\) between \(J-B\log 2\) and \(J\), and the [reflection identity](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v405.md#identity) bounds \(|m(G)-I|\) by \((2I-m(H))/p\le 2J/p\).
Primitivity gives \(m(R_F)=m(F)-2h\), and the [two-sided lift inequality](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v232.md#measure) applies to \(s=pB\), \(m(G)=O(ph)\) and \(\log(ns+1)=o(ph)\).
The resulting error after subtracting \(J\) is \(o(ph)\) uniformly, because \(e=o(ph)\) and \(p,n\to\infty\).
Writing \(J_*\) for the log of the largest absolute coefficient, one has \(0\le J-J_*\le\log(B+1)\).
The explicit first inequality carries over because its lower bound is monotone in \(J\) for \(p>2\), while \(m(G)\le J_*+\log(2(B+1))\le J_*+B\log2\) for \(B=4e\ge16\).
The [short rows and converse cofactor bound](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v229.md#seed) consequently give the claimed two-witness shrinking criterion in the quantifiers of the [original equivalence](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v403.md#equivalence).

For the continuity estimate, Jensen's formula expresses \(|f|/\exp(m(f))\) as a product of normalized root distances.
On the exceptional set \(E\), a polynomial of degree \(1\le k\le D\) therefore has a root distance at most \(2\delta^{1/(2k)}\le2\delta^{1/(2D)}\).
The chord estimate handles every root uniformly, with the trivial probability bound when this threshold exceeds one.
For each of \(f,g\), integrating the negative logarithmic tail of each normalized distance over \(E\) gives \(O(q\log(2/q))\) per root, where \(q=\mu(E)\).
This also handles roots on the circle.
The two suprema differ in logarithm by \(O(\delta)\), and off \(E\) the relative pointwise change is at most \(\sqrt\delta\le1/2\).
Using \(q\ll D\delta^{1/(2D)}\) and \(q\log(2/q)\ll\sqrt q\) gives the asserted \(O(D^2\delta^{1/(4D)})\), including \(q=0\) and constant polynomials.

For the perturbation, \(Y^e Q_0^2\) vanishes to second order on the powered seed orbit and is reciprocal about \(4e\).
Hence both seed jets and the lifted difference \(A-A^*\) persist.
The original [nonzero rows and coprimalities](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v229.md#coprime) exclude identically vanishing affine evaluations at all nonzero test roots, and the fixed endpoints handle zero.
At most \(4d+B\) parameter choices are forbidden.
Multiples of the positive content of \(b\) retain primitive joint coefficients, and taking the first admissible multiple above the prescribed threshold costs \(O(ph)\) in its logarithm.
The support and all nonvanishing and independence conditions thus survive on the two actual witnesses.

With \(M=\sup|Y^eQ_0^2|\ge\exp(2ph)\), the relative perturbation is at most \(1/(4M)\) for every phase \(w\) and at most \(1/M\) for the reflected polynomial.
The continuity estimate at degrees \(4e\) and \(8e\) therefore gives both errors with \(c=1/16\) and a sufficiently large absolute prefactor.
The phase logarithm has bounded positive part, and the uniform lower bound on its inner integral bounds the inner integral of its negative part independently of the phase.
Tonelli's theorem then gives integrability on the product circle, which justifies the change of integration order.
The reflection formula gives \(0<L_t=O(\eta)\), monic division gives \(m(K_t)=m(H_t)-2ph\), and the [cofactor formulas](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v405.md#four) give the error \(O(\eta+ph/n)\) for the lift since its adjusted seed still has \(m(G_t)=O(ph)\).
For varying seeds, [Voutier's Corollary 2](../literature/Vou96/Yin_U001.md#height) applies at degree \(e\) to the nontorsion unit \(\alpha\).
Its \(h>2/(\log(3e))^3\), together with \(p\ge(\log(3d))^4\) and \(e\le\log(3d)\), makes \(ph/e\) grow faster than \(\log(2e)\) uniformly.
The source theorem was used at its stated scope and its original proof was outside this review.
The angular term can approach zero while preserving both jets, and the minimum attainable coefficient size across admissible rows remains open.
