### Double vanishing before cleanup

Under the degree-preserving substitution \(z=\alpha^p\), the field \(\mathbb Q(z)=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\) has the inverse involution, which fixes \(u,\ell\).
Applying it to both \(g\)-jets gives \(g(z^{-1})=-\alpha u\) and \((\mathcal E g)(z^{-1})=\alpha(\ell+u/p)\).
The centered seed graph \(f(x^p)+x^{-1}g(x^{-p})\) thus has zero value and Euler derivative \(p\ell+u-p(\ell+u/p)=0\) at \(\alpha\).
Since \(\alpha\ne0\), its ordinary polynomial associate contains \(P_0^2\).
This argument works for every integer \(p\ge2\) preserving degree.
The extreme coefficients of \(Y^B(aa^\iota-bb^\iota)\) are both one because \(\deg b<B\).
Elimination between the graph and its reflection and the unramified power map therefore give the nonzero integral multiple of \(Q^2\) needed for \(ph\le\log((B+1)H)\).
This elimination uses neither a row gcd nor a slope condition.
In particular it applies to a minimizing initial row at \(p_e\).

### The two slopes, seed coprimality and the additive constant
