## Exact root-order equivalence and all stated limits

The trace identities give

    C2(xi)=z^2+z^(-2),
    xi^(5^j)=z^(5^j)+z^(-5^j).

For nonzero x,y, equality x+x^(-1)=y+y^(-1) is equivalent to x=y or x=y^(-1), since they solve the same monic quadratic with constant coefficient one.
This remains valid in the repeated-root cases.
Taking x=z^2 and y=z^(5^j) proves both directions of

    C2(xi)=xi^(5^j)
      if and only if 2=5^j or -5^j modulo n.

The residue-order screen therefore has exactly the stated scope.
