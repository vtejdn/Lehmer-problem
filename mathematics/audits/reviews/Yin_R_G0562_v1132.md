**Rank, numerical bounds and endpoint.**
For the compressed conjugates the classes modulo torsion are distinct and span the same rational representation, up to a common nonzero scalar, as the original conjugates.
The dimension of the negative space of complex conjugation is the argument rank.
Each neutral class belongs to that space, and the conjugate classes have the same nonzero length in a group-invariant metric.
A space of dimension at most one can contain at most two points of this fixed length, yielding at most one neutral inverse pair.
This argument applies after torsion compression even when the given polynomial is already exponent-primitive.
The compressed polynomial cannot have the form \(S(X^j)\) for \(j>1\), since distinct roots would then have a torsion quotient.
Compression preserves measure, argument rank and reciprocity, and does not increase degree.
Consequently a rank-at-most-one core anywhere among the 944 rows would give a compressed core in the same catalogue with at most one circle pair, contradicting the count above.
For an arbitrary original degree the separate reflection argument supplies the compressed-degree cap needed before using the catalogue.
