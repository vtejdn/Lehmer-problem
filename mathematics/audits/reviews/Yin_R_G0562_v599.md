## Both signs admit prime-power false positives on a quarter of the degrees

The proof establishes the claimed count, the exact lattice membership after reduction modulo \(p^t\), and valuation one at every target for every stated precision.
The exact exclusion concerns the prescribed pair of minima at the same budget.

For odd prime \(\ell\), an \(\ell\)-th root of \(\alpha\) in its quadratic field would be an integral unit with trace \(\alpha^{1/\ell}+\alpha^{-1/\ell}\) strictly between two and three.
The prime binomial criterion and norm induction therefore give \([\mathbb Q(\gamma):\mathbb Q]=2k\) for every odd \(k\).
Also \((\gamma^2)^k=\alpha^2\) generates the quadratic field, and \(\gamma=(\gamma^2)^{(k+1)/2}/\alpha\) recovers \(\gamma\) from its square.
The two distinct minima are reciprocal and substitution preserves their logarithmic measures \(\log\alpha\) and \(2\log\alpha\). [Prime binomial criterion](../literature/The26/Yin_U001.md#the26-u001) and [odd-order induction](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v387.md#substitutions)

For a residue root of order \(M\) coming from \(\alpha^{\pm a}\), \(a=1,2\), the positive integer \(\alpha^{aM}+\alpha^{-aM}-2\) is divisible by \(p\) and is smaller than \(\alpha^{2M}\).
Hence \(M>\log(p)/(2\log\alpha)\), uniformly over the four roots.
At most \(O(p/M+1)\) shifts in \((-p,p)\) solve \(M\mid 2u\).
Every nonzero inverse class has exactly one odd representative in this interval, and every retained class gives \(D_j/(4p)+O(1)\) permissible odd integers \(k\).
The loss is \(O(D_j/\log p+p)\), which is \(o(D_j)\) under the stated limits.
This counts constructed degrees among all integers in the block.
The retained classes depend only on \(p\), so both signs and every indicated \(t\) use the same degrees.
For the first prime above \(j^2\), the stated limits follow from \(j^2<p<2j^2\) for \(j\ge2\). [Bertrand's postulate](../literature/Erd32/Yin_U001.md#erd32-u001)

Since \(G_0^2\) is monic with constant term one, the integral Laurent quotient has the ordinary degree-below-eight remainder basis.
In characteristic zero the involution interchanges the two double-root factors in each reciprocal pair, including for negative \(u\).
On each pair a signed eigenvector is determined by its two coordinates at the first root, giving the rank bound four.
Modulo \(p\), the derivative at each root forces \(B_4^*(\xi^p)=0\), since \(p\nmid u\).
Inversion and Frobenius permute the four distinct residues, so this condition says exactly that \(B\) is a multiple of \(\bar G_0\), and it also makes all four values vanish.
Thus the rank is exactly four in both characteristics.
In Smith coordinates the four nonzero diagonal entries are units modulo every \(p^t\), leaving one free coordinate supplied by the primitive integral kernel generator.
Its leading coefficient is nonzero modulo \(p\), so it can be normalized to one modulo \(p^t\).
Centering gives a monic row with five nonzero coefficients and one-norm at most \(5p^t/2\).

For a base root \(x\), set \(z=x^p\), \(y=\bar z\), \(\xi=\bar x\) and \(D=Y\,d/dY\).
The Euler derivative of the graph and its value at \(x\) eliminate the reflected value, giving the identity for \((u+4p)\widetilde B(z)\) in the claim.
At a root of the reciprocal quartic, \(y^4(D\bar G_0)(1/y)=-(D\bar G_0)(y)\).
Consequently the first residue digit is \(u^{-1}(D\bar G_0)(y)(1+\varepsilon\xi^u)\), nonzero for every target at both signs.
The discriminant and \(p\nmid k\) give simple nonzero reductions of all inflated roots and hence an unramified splitting field.
Centering changes values there by multiples of \(p^2\) for all \(t\ge2\).
Inflation multiplies the resulting value at \((z_0^k)^p\) by the unit \(z_0^{pw}\) for every inflated root \(z_0\).

The inequalities \(-k<v=(ku-1)/p<k\) follow from \(|u|\le p-1\) and \(k>1\).
Evenness of \(v\) gives integral \(0\le w=(k-1-v)/2\le k-1\).
Writing \(pb+1-2pw-4pk=ku\) proves the claimed graph substitution on an exact integral lift.
Since \(G_0(X^k)\) has unit constant coefficient, Laurent divisibility of the resulting polynomial gives ordinary divisibility.
The five occupied positions \(w+ik\), \(0\le i\le4\), are distinct and at most \(5k-1\).
The strict budget is equivalent to \(\log\|A\|_1+\tfrac12\log(2pb+1)<p(\log 2)/8\).
The centering bound and \(b<5D_j\) leave a margin \(\eta p-O(j+\log p)>0\), uniformly in \(k,t\) and the sign.

For a genuine row the nonvanishing numerators recover both original roots from their \(p\)-th powers by the same rational map.
The two powered minima are therefore distinct, have logarithmic measures \(p\log\alpha\) and \(2p\log\alpha\), and both divide the nonzero elimination polynomial because the two graph contacts are double.
Multiplicativity of Mahler measure and the elimination norm bound force \(3p\log\alpha\le\log((2pb+1)\|A\|_1^2)<p(\log2)/4\), a contradiction that uses no support cap. [Elimination identity](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v417.md#elimination) and [height normalization](../literature/Smy08/Yin_U001.md#smy08-height-normalization)

For \(p=293,\ k=65631\) the inverse shift is \(-1\), giving \(v=-224,\ w=32927,\ b=328154\).
I independently formed each column \(X^{1+293i}+\varepsilon X^{293(4-i)}\) in the integral degree-below-eight remainder basis by successive multiplication by \(X\).
Elimination with unit pivots modulo \(293^2\), with the last coefficient fixed to one, recovered both displayed centered rows.
The eight integer remainder coefficients have positive gcd \(85849\) in each case.
The positive and negative rows have respective norms \(66510,81750\), and the two budget integers on the left have bit lengths \(239,241\), both less than \(293\).
