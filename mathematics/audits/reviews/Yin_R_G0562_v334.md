### The rational certificates and their termination

The chosen polynomial direction \((1,t,\ldots,t^{r-1})\) is primitive, and the nonzero differences of rows exclude finitely many integers \(t\).
The actual occupied width along this direction is between one and \(R\) whenever two rows survive.
The lower bound in v156 decreases with this width, and \(e^{16384}<2^{32768}\), \(\log(3R)<3R\) verify (12).
All quantities through \(H\) are computed before \(\rho\) is needed.
Rounding \(N\mathsf Bx\) moves each fast frequency by at most \(A_1/(2N)\) and each finite test divided by \(N\) by at most \(rH/(2N)\).
Equations (15)–(16) therefore preserve ordering, separation, the bound three on frequency size, and the stated lower bounds on the finite tests.
In (11), (13) and (14) bound the independent and good-interval error by \(g_R/128\), the Fejér error by \(2g_R/128\), the additive width error by \(g_R/128\), and the coefficient of \(I\) lost by \(1/16\).
This gives (17) for unbounded \(I\) as well.

In the new fast coordinate, each occupied coefficient is a single slow row times a Laurent monomial in the other variables.
A common divisor has zero width in each of those other variables, and a common nonzero base root over the algebraic closure would have a minimal polynomial dividing every integral row.
The primitive gcd excludes these base factors and nonunit integer content.
Thus a nontorsion component yields \(I\ge c_R\) and \(\mu>c_R/2\).
In the torsion case the integral factorization and additive widths yield \(\varphi(h)|b|\le2d\) and \(\varphi(h)|v_j|\le W_j\).
The nonzero vectors \(v\) are among the finite tests.
In fact \(|v\cdot q|\ge\rho N/2\ge2J_{\rm mar}d\), so even \(|b+v\cdot q|\ge(2J_{\rm mar}-2)d>J_{\rm mar}d\).
The specialized cyclotomic factors have zero logarithmic measure.
One occupied primitive row gives a unit directly, and general common content adds only its own Mahler measure after removal and recentering.

All region tests are strict rational linear inequalities or finite Boolean combinations thereof.
Emptiness of the complementary closed systems inside \(\Delta_k\) is decidable, including on faces with coincident representatives.
For each \(x\in\Delta_k\) take a \(\mathbb Z\)-basis for the group generated by its coordinates.
The integer coordinate matrix has full column rank, and its first and last rows have the required form.
Choose dyadic separation for its distinct rows, compute \(H\), then choose \(0<\rho<\min_{0<\|h\|_\infty\le H}|h\cdot\beta|\), the absolute-value condition in (15).
The projection error at \(x\) is zero.
This proves coverage by the enumerated open regions, so compactness and exhaustive finite stages prove termination without testing an unknown real relation.

For a large-degree input, the support representatives can include both endpoints because no eligible interval contains both.
