The box argument gives a positive return time at most \(Q^u\), hence error at most a constant times \((n-1)^{-1/u}\).
A bounded set of positive return times has a positive minimum error because a root of unity among the conjugates would make \(P\) cyclotomic.
Thus the periods are unbounded and their neutral denominators tend to the nonzero values \(\beta-1\).
The expanding and contracting logarithmic weights each sum to one, so the full real volume of the multiplier body is independent of the period, counting both embeddings at every complex disk.

The trace character descends to the quotient since multiplying its parameter by the period ideal gives integral traces on the order.

The absolute period norm grows comparably to \(e^{nm}\) by the neutral denominator bounds and the off-circle factors.
This excludes the trivial character, whose conductor is the entire order.

At each off-circle embedding the multiplier radius costs at most the geometric decay over the first \(\lceil\eta\log n/m\rceil\) places from its relevant end.
The additional fixed margin makes the off-circle sum uniformly small and leaves bounded total squared size over the middle interval.
