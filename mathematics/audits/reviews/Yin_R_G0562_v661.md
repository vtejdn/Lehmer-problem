## Uniformity in the sections and the rational powers

On 2026-11-20 at 10:57 UTC a separate reviewing agent for Sheng Yin inspected the [fixed claim](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v527.md#statement) independently of its development.
I find the asserted uniform separation valid for each fixed pair of positive constants \(C,K\).

For the orbit argument, consider any sequence of pairs of distinct exponents, choosing any one pair at each index where such a pair exists.
The corresponding twisted power has height at most the span times \(h(t)\), which tends to zero independently of the chosen pair and twist.
It is a nontorsion unit, so bounded degree on a subsequence would contradict finiteness of monic integer minimal polynomials of bounded degree and bounded height and positivity of its height.
In degree \(D\) its minimal polynomial has nonzero integer discriminant, so the [compact-test estimate](../literature/Pri11/Yin_U002.md#pri11-u002) at radius \(1/D\) bounds the fixed test's discrepancy by a quantity tending to zero with \(h(\beta)+(\log D+4)/D\).
Outside the plateau of the cutoff, \(|\log|\sigma\beta||\) is at least \(\log(4/3)\), and its average over all embeddings is \(2h(\beta)\).
This gives phase cancellation for the chosen sequence of pairs and hence for the largest correlation whenever any pair occurs.
The bounded number of cross terms contributes \(o(1)\) to the mean square, even as the coefficients and fields vary.
At each exponent a nonzero coefficient sum is an algebraic integer whose absolute squared conjugates have geometric mean at least one, also after passing to the common field.
On circle embeddings the phase polynomial vanishes, and on the remaining embeddings its squared modulus is at most \(N^2\).
The latter embeddings have vanishing proportion, ruling out any nonzero collected coefficient eventually.

For a hypothetical sequence of counterexamples, the [critical scales](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#scales) permit inversions and give an off-circle proportion \(2k/d=O(s^{-2})\).
Passing to a root \(t^u=x\) preserves this proportion by equal embedding multiplicities.
After sign and gcd reduction, \(h(x)=u h(t)\) and \(h(x')=v h(t)\) hold exactly.
The [factorwise limit](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v510.md#statement) has the same strictly positive value on every admissible sequence, so \(vb'/(ub)\to1\) even when the reduced powers diverge independently.
Dividing the cleared relation's span by \(ub\) bounds the result by \(sa/b+(vb'/(ub))s'a'/b'+|vb'/(ub)-1|\), which vanishes with no relative growth condition on \(s,s'\).
The [graph calculation](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v517.md#argument) excludes vanishing denominators at nontorsion arguments.
The two products each contain at most nine signed torsion monomials, so the orbit conclusion applies after clearing denominators and gives a rational identity.
At zero the order and constant coefficient give \(vb'=ub\) and \(\omega^{b'}=1\).
The first positive power and its coefficient give \(va'=ua\) and \(\omega^{a'}=1\).
Coprimality of each section's exponents identifies both exponent pairs and kills \(\omega\), and coprimality of the reduced powers makes both one.
The first term in \(R_j-(1+T)\) has degree \(j-1\) for \(j\ge3\), identifying the section indices.
Restoring the original signs and common divisor gives the asserted relation between roots and forces the originally supplied twist to equal one.
Failure of a threshold depending only on \(C,K\) would furnish precisely such a counterexample sequence with \(\min(s,s')\to\infty\).
The conclusion supplies a necessary condition on the two sections and leaves existence open.
