## Finite jet count, density threshold and exponent span

At 13:19 UTC on November 20, 2026, a separate agent reviewing for Sheng Yin independently checked the full argument of version 560.
The finite bound and both asymptotic conclusions hold with the stated strict thresholds.

For jet index \(j\), the coordinate difference within a common box has modulus less than \(1/2\) at a real embedding and less than \(\sqrt{2}/2\) at a complex embedding.
A nonzero integral difference would therefore have absolute norm strictly between zero and one.
An interval \([-R,R]\) meets at most \(4R+2\le8R\) half-unit intervals for \(R\ge1\).
Counting a complex embedding twice gives \(\prod_\sigma\max(1,|\sigma\alpha|)^N=\exp(Nm)\) over all embeddings for each jet.
Summing the exponents \(j\) recovers the quadratic term in (1).
Since \(\alpha\ne0\), equality of the scaled jets gives order at least \(k\) at \(\alpha\), and separability and monicity give divisibility by \(P^k\) in \(\mathbb Z[X]\).
Two distinct equal-weight subsets have the same positive number of slots in each directed difference, at most \(\min(t,s-t)\).
Their difference vanishes at one, supplying the additional coprime factor exactly in the asserted case \(P\ne X-1\).

For the density estimate, restriction to \(\lceil\delta N\rceil\) slots preserves an admissible mask and contributes at most two to the stated length bound after rounding.
For fixed seed, multiplicity and weight fraction, the strict entropy surplus is linear in \(N\), whereas rounding, Stirling's remainder and the jet prefactors cost \(O(\log N)\).
For \(u=km/\delta\downarrow0\) and \(x=cu/\log(1/u)\), one has \(x\log(1/x)/u\to c\).
The choices \(c=2\) and \(c=6\) therefore meet the respective thresholds, with the required ambient degree chosen separately for each seed and multiplicity.

If the mask contains \(s\ge\delta N\) slots, its first and last blocks of \(q=\lfloor\delta N/3\rfloor\) slots have distance at least \(s-2q+1\).
With \(B=\binom qt\), fewer than \(B\) jet values on \(B^2\) inputs force a fiber larger than \(B\).
Such a fiber meets two rows, and if two entries in those rows share a column, a third entry outside that column differs in row and column from one of them.
Thus both blocks contribute to the support and each has coefficient sum zero.
The ratio of length to span is at most \(4\lfloor xq\rfloor/(\delta N/3)\le4x\).
Dividing by the lowest monomial preserves the stated divisibility because every guaranteed factor is coprime to \(X\), and yields ordinary degree equal to the original span.
