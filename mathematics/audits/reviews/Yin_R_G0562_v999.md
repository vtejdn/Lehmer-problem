## Every proposed multiple root is excluded

The argument establishes both assertions in the stated domains.
Modulo two, \(q_3q_4=(U^2+U+1)(U^2+1)=U^4+U^3+U+1=a\), which is reciprocal, and \(Ua'=U^3+U\).
On \(F=0\) in the geometric torus, substitution excludes \(U=1\) and \(V=1\).
For even \(k\) the odd \(l\) contributes just \(U^2V\) to \(DF\).
For odd \(k\) and even \(l\) the derivative is \(U(U+1)^2(V+1)^2\).
For both odd, adding \(F\) cancels the middle monomial and leaves \((U+1)^4(V+1)^2\).
The exclusions are valid over the entire residue field because a power of a nonzero field element is nonzero.

For either sign of each nonzero exponent, extremizing \(ki+lj\) forces each coordinate separately to a unique endpoint of its interval.
All four corners occur with coefficient one, so even when weights coincide in the interior the monomial-cleared polynomial has integral coefficients and two unit end coefficients for every pair of dyadic units.
Dividing by the leading coefficient in this polynomial and in its reversal shows that a root and its inverse are both integral.
Consequently the coordinates at a root reduce in the torus, where a multiple root would give the excluded equations by the chain rule.
At a torus zero, multiplying the section by a Laurent monomial preserves this derivative test.
In characteristic zero a torsion specialization and any of its roots lie in a number field, which embeds in the dyadic algebraic closure.

On the fixed-\(U\) axis the conjugate coefficients give discriminant \(1-4|(t-3)(t-4)|^2\le 1-576\), since \(t\) is purely imaginary.
On the fixed-\(V\) axis the derivative of \(U-U^{-1}\) vanishes exactly at \(U^2=-1\), where both coordinates are dyadic units and the even-\(l\), odd-\(k\) calculation excludes a multiple root.
Away from ramification the coefficient \(14\) gives a simple linear equation when \(V^2=-1\).
Otherwise expanding the discriminant and dividing by \(V^2\) gives \((V+V^{-1})^2-4(V+V^{-1})-196\le 4+8-196<0\) in the complex embedding.
Both primitive signs amount to automorphisms of the moving torus coordinate.
