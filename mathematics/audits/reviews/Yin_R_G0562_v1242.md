For the simple monic integral lift \(S\), the product of \(\lvert z_i-z_l\rvert\) over \(i\ne l\) equals \(\lvert\operatorname{disc} S\rvert\ge1\).
Reflecting the exterior root \(T\) into the unit disk gives \(\lvert1-z_l/T\rvert=\lvert T-z_l\rvert/T\) for every other root.
For two unreflected roots the corresponding inequality follows by subtracting squared moduli on the disk.
The regularized logarithm has an absolutely convergent power series since \(\rho<1\), even for the two coincident reflected points \(T^{-1}\).
Summation over ordered pairs gives the term \(-\binom d2\log\rho\), the reflection cost \(2(d-1)\log T\), and a diagonal contribution at most \(d\log d\).
Here \(-\binom d2\log(1-1/d)\le d/2\) and \(\log T<1/q\), giving the strict bound \(B\).
The reflected sum is real and differs from \(\Theta_j-E_j\) by \(E_j-(T^j+T^{-j})+2T^{-j}\), strictly between zero and three for \(j\le n\) by v892.
Thus each displayed squared distance is bounded by the corresponding squared reflected sum, jointly on every prefix.

At position \(j\) an admissible moment lies in an open interval of length \(3+2\sqrt{jB/\rho^j}\), or a shorter one after the preceding charges are deducted.
The fixed residue of the reciprocal coefficient and Newton's identity give spacing \(jM\) for these moments.
Using \(\rho^j\ge(d-j)/d\ge1/2\) gives the claimed branch cutoff, and \(j(d-j)\ge d-1\) gives the uniform length bound after division by \(j\).
Equality in (2) still permits at most one integer in the prescribed progression because the interval is open.
For \(n\le cM^2/\log M\), monotonicity in \(d\) gives \(B/M^2\le4c+o(1)\), while \(Bd/(d-1)=B+O(\log M)\) uniformly there.
Consequently \(c<1/16\) suffices for uniqueness.
The simple root signature and pole are imposed at the end, so every solution on every grid survives and every passing complete candidate gives a realizing grid by v891.
