### Comparing the trace values

The odd-order roots give distinct simple residue traces indexed by inverse pairs, since the two elements of a pair are the two roots of the same monic quadratic with constant term one.
Excluding order three makes the denominators at odd arguments units, and trace zero would require \(\eta=1\), absent from the binary parent.
The unit derivative of \(C_n+1\) at the reference root gives the displacement \(2\tau C_l(\tau)\bmod4\).
Expanding the product of the displaced linear factors modulo four and dividing \(T-V\) by \(2V\) gives the partial fractions for \(\bar J\).
For \(d=v-u\) of valuation \(r\ge1\), terms of second and higher order in \(J(u+d)\) belong to \(2^{r+1}\mathbb Z_2\).
In the quotient of \(1+2J\) the linear term has valuation at least \(r+1\), so its coefficient at that precision is exactly \(\bar J'(u)\).
Differentiation and the two orientations of each pair give (1), including \(r=1\).
At odd arguments the identity \(g+g^2=\tau/(1+\tau)^2\) and the Frobenius invariance of \(\epsilon_{l/2}\) give (2) after squaring the relevant odd units modulo eight.
At even arguments \(1/\tau=\eta/(1+\eta^2)\) gives (3).
