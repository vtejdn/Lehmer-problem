# Independent review of the alternating extension over the totally real and unmatched compositum

## The commutator in the relative kernel, closed restriction image and the spectrum

At 2026-11-25T00:17 UTC an agent outside the proof development independently reviewed for Yin the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v91.md#statement) and both proof sections of the final C0901 v91.
The reviewer received the fixed proof and its mathematical inputs without development discussion and checked the final specification of faithfulness on the labels.
The simultaneous base change to \(TK\), survival of the full natural alternating subgroup in the non-totally-real branch, exact threshold \(b\), and criterion (1) for bounded-measure elements hold.

The pre-tower step of v87 supplies the rationally normal \(A=B_0K\) and \(E=A P_y\), with \(N=\operatorname{Gal}(E/A)\) finite and mapping isomorphically onto \(D_y\) by restriction.
Rational normality of both possibly infinite fields follows from the constructions by composita of rationally normal fields.
The restriction \(\pi:\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\to G_y\) is onto by extension of embeddings and normality.
If the normal label field is not totally real, its ordinary conjugation is nonidentity because a normal subfield lying in \(\mathbb R\) already has every conjugate real.
The centralizer of the full \(D_y\) in the faithful label action is trivial.
Commuting with each alternating factor preserves its moved set, which is its individual block, and commuting with all three-cycles on any one block fixes every three-element support and hence every label there for \(b\ge5\).
Therefore some \(\pi(g)\) for \(g\in N\) fails to commute with \(\pi(c)\).

Let \(H\) be the closed normal subgroup of \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\) generated by the conjugates of \(c\).
The commutator \(cgc^{-1}g^{-1}\) belongs to \(N\) because \(N\) is normal, and belongs to \(H\) because it is the product of \(c\) and a conjugate of \(c^{-1}\).
Its restriction is nonidentity by the choice of \(g\).
The intersection \(N\cap H\) is normal in the full group, so its nonzero image under the surjection \(\pi\) is normal in \(G_y\) and contained in the minimal normal subgroup \(D_y\).
This makes that image all of \(D_y\), and injectivity on \(N\) gives \(N\subseteq H\).
This commutator locates the relative subgroup supplied by v87 inside the closed normal subgroup that survives passage to the totally real base, within the same infinite field \(E\).

An element of \(E\) is fixed by \(H\) if and only if every rational conjugate is real, since point stabilizers are closed and rational embeddings extend.
Thus \(E^H=E\cap T\).
The image on \(E\) of \(\operatorname{Gal}(ET/T)\) is compact and closed, with fixed field \(E\cap T\), so the infinite Galois correspondence identifies it with precisely \(H\).
In particular every element of \(N\subseteq H\) has a lift fixing \(T\).
Such a lift also fixes \(A\) because its restriction lies in \(N\), hence fixes \(AT\).
Conversely every automorphism over \(AT\) restricts into \(N\), and trivial restriction on \(E\) forces triviality on \(ET\) since \(T\) is already fixed.
It follows that \(\operatorname{Gal}(ET/AT)\) maps isomorphically onto \(D_y\) in its original label action.

Take the full field of permitted towers over \(KT\) and compose it with \(AT\).
It is normal over \(AT\), since the family of towers is stable under conjugation, and its finite Galois subextensions have no section \(A_b\).
Indeed normalizing a finite bounded-degree step contributes groups in subgroups of \(S_{b-1}\), solvable steps contribute solvable groups, and the exclusion is inherited by products, subgroups, quotients and extensions as shown in v87.
Each finite subextension in the directed compositum is contained in a finite compositum of the corresponding normal closures over the starting base.
The intersection with \(ET/AT\) is now finite Galois and its group is a quotient of \(D_y\).
Any nontrivial such quotient contains an isomorphic copy of \(A_b\) from some factor, so the intersection is \(AT\).
Since both extensions are normal, this leaves all of \(D_y\) after the further base change.
Restriction over the smaller tower base over \(KT\) contains these same permutations.
Rational normality of that full tower field excludes \(y\) itself whenever \(P_y\) is excluded, because it would otherwise contain \(L_y\).
The v87 stabilizer chain and solvable kernel reach \(y\) already from \(\mathbb Q\) with single adjunctions of degree at most \(b\), proving sufficiency also after base change.
If \(P_y\subseteq T\), the extension \(L_y/P_y\) is solvable and its compositum with \(TK\) is solvable over \(TK\), proving the other branch.

For \(d_y>N\), all inputs contributing to \(K_{\le N}\) are unmatched with \(y\), because matching forces equality of compressed degrees.
Any permitted tower starting from \(TK_{\le N}\) base-changes to one over \(TK\) without increasing the degree bounds or losing solvability.
When \(P_y\) is not totally real, the lower threshold is consequently at least \(b_y\), uniformly greater than the displayed \(q\) after choosing \(c_M\) strictly smaller than the eventual block-size lower constant and taking \(N\) sufficiently large.
When \(d_y\le N\), membership already holds by the definition of \(K_{\le N}\).
For totally real \(P_y\), solvability of the kernel gives membership over \(T\) at every degree, including the small degrees.
This proves the elementwise equivalence (1) and, by taking their measure values, the stated spectral conclusion.
For an already compressed reciprocal unit the labels are precisely its inverse pairs; their distinct traces identify \(P_y\) with the splitting field of the trace polynomial.
Thus \(P_y\subseteq T\) is exactly total reality of that trace, with no positive uniform gap on this branch asserted.
