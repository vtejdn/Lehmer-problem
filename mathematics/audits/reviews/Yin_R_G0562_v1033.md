## Four equivalent conditions and the obstruction to the trace signature

At 2026-11-23T08:10 UTC an agent acting for Yin independently reviewed the statement, the three proof sections and the relative-unit input of C0345v883, submitted at 2026-11-23T08:02 UTC.
The reviewer had no part in developing the submitted argument and received no exploration notes, development dialogue, author explanations or earlier findings about it.
The four equivalent conditions, their signed norm consequences, the explicit family and the two-moment obstruction all hold in the stated ranges.
The initial display translating the graph to its four-term polynomial had a sign error.

The discriminant \(w(w+4)\) is negative at a nondistinguished trace, so adjoining \(T\) gives precisely the quadratic extension of the totally real field \(\mathbb Q(w)\).
Each trace in \((-4,0)\) yields a unit-circle conjugate pair.
Writing \(A=p-qT\) and \(C=pT-q\) gives \(AC=T\delta\) and \(C-A=L(T-1)\).
Thus \((Y-1)^2/Y=L^2w/\delta\), and \(L^2/\delta=L^2+ML^2w/\delta\) checks the potentially delicate direction of the trace criterion without dividing by an integer prime.
The reverse direction follows by multiplying by \(w\).
The involution sends \(Y\) to \(Y^{-1}\), and the identities \(pA+qC=L\) and \(A-q(1-T)=1\) give both directions of the divisibility criterion in the full ring of integers.

On opposite sides of \(p/q\), the comparisons establishing \(|Y|>T\) reduce respectively to \(q(T^2-1)>0\) and \(q(T+T^{-1})<2p\).
Neither rational pole nor rational zero can equal \(T\), an algebraic unit greater than one.
A bound on the absolute value of the distinguished logarithm of a positive relative unit bounds all its conjugates, including its inverse at the other real embedding.
The bounded-coefficient argument of C0345v75 therefore applies to this one split place.
The logarithms of \(T\) and \(|Y|\) generate a cyclic subgroup with positive generator whose preimage \(\beta\) lies in their multiplicative group.
Their exponents relative to that generator are coprime, have \(0<u<v\), and satisfy \(u=1\) exactly when \(|Y|\in\langle T\rangle\).
The reverse implication of the fourth condition follows from closure of the units under powers and negation.
In quadratic degree for \(T\) the integral reciprocal trace is at least three and violates the strict radial bound.

The degree-\(2n\) monic reciprocal lift evaluated at \(p/q\) gives \(q^{2n}S(p/q)=M^nH(1/M)=\prod_i(1-Mw_i)\).
With product one, each nondistinguished factor exceeds one, forcing \(0<1-Mw<1\).
It follows that \(T<p/q\), \(Y>0\) and \(\delta^{-1}\) is a totally positive Pisot unit.

For the explicit family Eisenstein's criterion applies because the constant term \(-2(n-1)\) has exact 2-adic valuation one and every lower coefficient is divisible by two.
At the \(n-1\) specified angles the correction has alternating sign and absolute value at least \(2(1+M|x_j|)>|x_j|^n\).
The positive root between \(0\) and \(1/M\), the \(n-2\) intervening negative roots and the root below \(-4\) exhaust the degree.
At the left endpoint the sufficient bound \(2M\ge4^{n-1}\) already gives \(2(4M+1)>4^n\).
The sum of the interior roots and the positive root exceeds \(-4(n-2)\), giving the stated upper bound for the exceptional root.
The same quadratic irreducibility argument applies, \(\delta^{-1}\) generates the trace field, and \(A\) is a unit because \(A\iota(A)=\delta\) is a unit.
At \(T=-r<-1\), the two positive differences \((p-q)(r-1)\) and \(q(r^2-1)\) give \(1<|Y|<r\).
A relation \(T^hY^k\) equal to a root of unity would give \(h\log|T|+k\log|Y|=0\) at both real exterior embeddings.
The two different ratios force \(h=k=0\), excluding power sections and their dilations.
The quartic trace case works for all \(q\ge6\), and each fixed even trace degree \(n\ge4\) becomes available as \(q\) increases.

For the moment obstruction \(a=cM\) is divisible by four, so the strict first-moment bound \(a<4(n-1)\) gives \(n\ge n_0=a/4+2\).
Newton's identity gives \(\sum_i w_i^2=a^2-2ab+2c\), whereas Cauchy's inequality gives the lower bound \(a^2/n\).
For \(b\ge 2(n-2)-1\) their difference yields the claimed necessary inequality \(f(n)\le0\).
On \(t\ge n_0\) one has \(a/t<4\), and \(f(n_0)\ge 2a/5-2a/6>0\), so the contradiction includes the minimum \(a=12\).
The Chebyshev recurrence gives \(b=2(n-2)\), and pairing primitive \(m\)-th roots under inversion gives \(b=2(n-2)-\mu(m)\) also for a single pair.
The exclusion extends over the whole stated range of degrees independently of the size condition for the explicit family.
These findings leave the existence question for an admissible unit at unbounded \(q\) open.

## The sign of the polynomial section

In the initially inspected display the substitution \(T=\beta^u,\ Y=e\beta^v\) gave \(q(1+TY)-p(T+Y)=2(q-pT)\ne0\).
The reviewer reported this evaluation and the resulting sign correction.
At 2026-11-23T08:11 UTC the reviewer checked the amended statement and its substitution against the same graph and the already checked common powers.
The amended polynomial evaluates at \(\beta\) to \(q-pT+(p-qT)Y=0\), so the sign in \(q(1+\epsilon X^{u+v})-p(X^u+\epsilon X^v)\) is \(\epsilon=-e\).
In the norm-one case the graph sign is \(e=+1\) and the polynomial sign is \(\epsilon=-1\).
This recheck covers the corrected formula and its interface with the four equivalent conditions.
