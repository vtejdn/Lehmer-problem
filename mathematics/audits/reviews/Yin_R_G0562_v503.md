For fixed \(a,b\), [reflected elimination](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v405.md#identity) gives every \(Q_P^2\) in the same reflected polynomial, nonzero with its two unit endpoints.
The integral of its log modulus is bounded by \(\log(\|a\|_2^2+\|b\|_2^2)\), since the pointwise majorant is \(|a|^2+|b|^2\).
