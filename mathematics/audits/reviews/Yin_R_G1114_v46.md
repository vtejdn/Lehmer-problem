# Review of endpoint ordering for finite measures on torsion traces

<a id="endpoint-order-recheck"></a>
## Finite mass, finite support and the gap above each endpoint

On 2026-11-16T14:11 UTC an agent outside the development of both proofs rechecked the [general ordering theorem](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v46.md#ordered-values) together with the [finite-support supplement](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v48.md#support) for Yin.
The assignment supplied the two fixed arguments and the [original partial finding](Yin_R_G1114_v44.md#trace-endpoint-review) identifying the finite-group issue.
The reviewer received no development discussion or proposed resolution apart from the fixed supplement.
Together these two arguments establish the stated ordering for every finite positive measure concentrated on torsion traces with integral Laurent coefficients and with \(2\) excluded.

### Obtaining finite support before evaluating the endpoint

For the support lemma, write \(\widehat\mu(l)=\int v^l\,d\mu(v)\).
The set of all roots of unity is countable, so finite total mass and concentration there give a finite set \(F\) with \(\mu(\mathbb T\setminus F)<d/2\).
Choose \(N\ge1\) divisible by the orders of all elements of \(F\), taking \(N=1\) if the set is empty.
The integral of \(1-v^N\) vanishes on \(F\), and its modulus on integration over the complement is at most \(2\mu(\mathbb T\setminus F)<d\).
Yet \(\widehat\mu(0)-\widehat\mu(N)\) belongs to \(d\mathbb Z\), so it is zero.
Its real part is the integral of \(1-\Re(v^N)\), nonnegative everywhere and strictly positive off the finite group \(\mu_N\).
Positivity now gives \(\mu(\mathbb T\setminus\mu_N)=0\), including when the original set of candidate orders was unbounded.

For \(|x|>2\), the uniformly convergent geometric expansion of \((x-y)^{-1}\) shows that its integrated Laurent coefficients are precisely the moments \(\int y^l\,d\lambda\), \(l\ge0\).
In the symmetric lift, twice the \(l\)-th Fourier coefficient is the integral of \(v^l+v^{-l}=P_l(y)\), where \(P_0=2,\ P_1=y,\ P_{l+1}=yP_l-P_{l-1}\).
These polynomials are integral, negative indices follow by symmetry and the zeroth coefficient is the original integer mass.
At \(y=-2\) the lift assigns all its mass to \(v=-1\), giving the same doubled moment \(2(-1)^l\) per unit mass.
Thus the support lemma applies with \(d=1/2\).
Exclusion of the trace \(2\) gives zero mass to \(v=1\), the only point above that trace.
Each nonzero lift now has finite support separated from \(1\), so \(s=\int|1-v|^{-2}\,d\widetilde\lambda\) is finite by \(|1-v|^2=2-y\).
The zero measure gives \(s=0\).

### Ordering the endpoint values

Given a strictly decreasing sequence \(s_n\), one integer \(k>2s_1\) makes each measure \(\nu_n\) in L462v46 positive with mass \(k\) and identity mass exactly \(k-2s_n\).
For each input the weighted lift and the added atom both belong to its finite group \(\mu_{N_n}\).
For \(l\ge1\), conjugation symmetry gives
\[
 \widehat\nu_n(0)-\widehat\nu_n(l)
 =\int\frac{2-v^l-v^{-l}}{|1-v|^2}\,d\widetilde\lambda_n
 =l\,\widetilde\lambda_n(\mathbb T)
  +\sum_{i=1}^{l-1}(l-i)\int(v^i+v^{-i})\,d\widetilde\lambda_n.
\]
The second equality expands \(\lvert1+\cdots+v^{l-1}\rvert^2\) on the support.
The integer mass and paired moments make this difference integral, giving integral Fourier coefficients at every index.
The [atom isolation theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v6.md#statement) therefore applies with the same mass bound \(k\) throughout, while allowing \(N_n\) to vary.
Its independently reviewed [finite-group argument](Yin_R_G0562_v6.md#atom-isolation-review) excludes the strictly increasing identity masses \(k-2s_n\).
If admissible values met every interval immediately above \(a\), choosing each next value in \((a,\min(s_n,a+1/(n+1)))\) would yield just such a decreasing sequence.
This proves the asserted gap with its dependence on \(a\).

The supplement discharges the missing finite-group hypothesis in the original partial finding and also justifies finiteness of \(S(2)\) from finite total mass.
The separate rank-one application has its own [finite-support review](Yin_R_G1114_v44.md#trace-endpoint-review).
