## Independent finding

The submitted proof establishes all three assertions, including the contact estimate at primes where the full prime power is retained.
Every direction is primitive because \(l-hk=1\), and the minimum slope tends to infinity, so the [squarefreeness and torsion exclusions](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#statement) apply uniformly.
The finitely many excluded primitive directions have bounded first coordinate.

For an octic orbit of degree \(f\), the [reconstruction](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v812.md#statement) gives two geometric orientations per octic point even if Frobenius interchanges them.
The invertible orientation discriminant excludes \(U=\pm1\), hence also excludes section endpoints \(x=\pm1\) at good primes.
The section parametrization on its subtorus is injective since \(x=v/u^h\).
At positive even \(b\), the [local square-root equation](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v808.md#local) splits after an unramified extension of degree at most two, with root separation of valuation \(b/2\) also in the original disk coordinate.
The derivative weights in the \(2f\) disks therefore sum to \(2fb\log p\), covering the [Smith contribution](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v823.md#ideals) of \(fb\log p\).
Odd contact cannot separate rational factors, since the two branches are conjugate over the unramified disk field.
At bad primes summing all repeated-disk derivative valuations gives the whole discriminant valuation, including any endpoint contributions.
Indeed the resultant of the two quadratic trace fibers over \(y_i,y_j\) is \((y_i-y_j)^2\), while the fiber discriminant is \(y_i^2-4\).
This gives the asserted absolute discriminant identity and bounds the bad part of \(U\).
In each disk the derivative sum counts every cross-factor pair twice, so it dominates the required contribution also for a subset of disks.

Each of the two good-disk roots agrees with the Teichmüller lift modulo \(p\) in an unramified ring, giving the required divisibility by \(p^2\) even for an orbit that never splits between global factors.
The [Teichmüller calculation and intersection bound](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v813.md#lift) then apply to every chosen good pair.
At a bad prime outside the content of the exceptional form, its reduction has at most 56 projective zeros, including infinity, and the [proper tangency intersection](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#ramification) has at most sixteen points per slope.
All omitted fixed primes eventually lie below the initial cutoff.
At a smooth occurring point the tangent equation determines \(k\bmod p\), since vanishing of its coefficient would force both logarithmic partials to vanish.
The independent congruence \(x^k=u\) fixes \(k\bmod g\), with \((p,g)=1\), giving spacing \(pg\).

For \(p>M^{1/20}\), the logarithm ratio in the rank-two estimate stays bounded, while the rank-one height term is \(O_F(g\log(3+k+l)/\log p)\) and the lifting term is bounded.
The torsion case is bounded as well.
The inverse Frobenius orbit bound gives \(g\le p^8+1\), and the thirty-two-root bound absorbs disk multiplicities in the charge per occurrence.
For smaller primes summing the full discriminant bound gives the stated \(O_F(M^{9/20}(\log M)^5)\) per direction.
For larger primes the point counts and spacing give prime sums bounded by \(Y^{10}+MY\) at good primes and \(Y^9+M\log(2Y)\) at bad primes, using \(\sum_{p\le Y}\log p\ll Y\) and partial summation.
Both endpoint powers give at most order \(M^2\).

At fixed direction and order the nonzero cyclotomic norm bounds the sum of distinct contributing prime logarithms by \(O_F(\varphi(g))\).
Thus the small orders cost \(O_F(MG^3)\) in total.
For \(G<g\le T\) and \(pg\le M\), charging at most \(2M/(pg)\) occurrences to each of \(O_F(p)\) possible pairs per prime costs \(O_F(M^2/G)\).
For \(pg>M\), the primitive roots lift distinctly in an unramified extension and each vanishing reduction contributes at least one to the valuation of the norm for its fixed \((a,g)\).
This counts geometric residues with their full Frobenius multiplicity once, since each lifted primitive root occurs once in the product.
The torus pair is uniquely recovered from that root and \(a\), including \(a=0\), and the torsion exclusion keeps the norm positive in all cases.
The remaining cost is bounded by \(O_F(\sum_{g\le T}g^2\varphi(g))=O_F(T^4)\).

The resulting nonnegative charge of order \(M^2\) gives at most \(O_F(M/\log M)\) exceptions at threshold \(M\log M\).
Each discarded prime power is dominated by its discarded disks, while at a retained prime the same charge still bounds individual discarded contacts.
Uniformly \(l\asymp M(\log M)^6\), so the [minimum degree](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v811.md#degrees) exceeds the error by a factor tending to infinity and the [splitting threshold](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v827.md#resultant) does as well.
Subtracting the discarded logarithm gives the asserted threshold for \(U_*\).
