### Exact correspondence

For a Pisot input, the nonzero constant gives \(q=\theta\prod_{\alpha\ne\theta}|\alpha|<\theta\).
There are exactly \(d-1\) strict interior roots, counted with multiplicity, and no unit-circle roots.
On that circle the comparison \(|f^*|<q|f|\) therefore applies to the entire polynomials \(-qf\) and \(f^*\).
Cancellation of the degree-\(d\) term in \(A=f^*-qf\), together with the root count, forces degree exactly \(d-1\).
The sign of \(f(1)\) is negative: its exterior real factor is negative there, while every other real factor and every conjugate pair contributes positively.
Thus \(A(1)>0\), its leading coefficient \(L\) is positive, and the strict root-modulus product gives \(L>N\).
This proves the integral normalization \(L=N+j\), \(j\ge1\).

The degree-sensitive reversal is \(zA^*=f-qf^*\), so eliminating \(f^*\) gives the stated inverse.
In the other direction, that inverse has leading coefficient one, constant \(q\), and intermediate coefficient \(-(qa_i+a_{d-i})/N\).
Consequently (2) is exactly the full integrality condition.
Reversing the inverse also gives \(f^*-qf=A\), so the two constructions are mutual inverses.
The strict boundary comparison for \(qA+zA^*\) gives precisely \(d-1\) interior roots and no boundary root; degree \(d\) supplies the one exterior root.
Since \(f(1)<0\) and \(f(x)\to+\infty\) for positive \(x\to\infty\), that exterior root is real and greater than one.
Any proper monic integral factor omitting it would have nonzero integral constant of absolute value strictly below one, which is impossible.
The monic rational-factor property therefore proves irreducibility.
Finally, multiplying one congruence by \(q\) supplies its partner because \(q^2\equiv1\pmod N\), and the middle congruence reduces exactly to \((q-1)\mid a_{d/2}\).
No Salem input is used in this argument.

### Branch identity and first-stage factorization

Its real denominator lies strictly between \(q-2\) and \(q+2+q^{-1}\) when \(q>2\), proving both strict bounds in (10).

### The descendant and its denominator budget

\[
 LA+NA^*=KzB,\qquad LA^*+NA=KB^*.
