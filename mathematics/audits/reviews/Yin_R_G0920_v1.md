## Decisive verification

### Ambient law, chronology and endpoint ratios

The v2 definition of nu_k is the complete independent digit displacement law on O.
It is not defined by a killed transition at arbitrary x.
For endpoints in D, every joining word remains in the core, so K^ell(y,0)=nu_ell(-beta^ell y) is valid with that ambient law.
The argument of nu_ell need not itself be in D.
C645's Gaussian estimate is explicitly stated for every x in O, and therefore applies to this displacement.
Its neutral modulus is exactly rho(y), because the factor zeta^ell has modulus one.

The chronological reversal of a coefficient word preserves every original product weight.
It identifies the reaching probabilities with nu_k(c) and the continuation probabilities with nu_k(-beta^k y), with no change of length.
The original ternary law is the difference of two independent fair-binary endpoint laws.
Cauchy--Schwarz for this finite autocorrelation gives nu_k(x)<=nu_k(0)=p_k for all ambient x, including k=0.
Thus both full endpoint ratios are at most one.
The all-zero word gives p_k>0, so every displayed normalization exists.

### Exact maximal unions and the Gaussian budget

For each ell, a right zero junction at time k-ell splits the full word into an arbitrary return of length k-ell and an arbitrary reaching word of length ell.
A left junction at time ell splits it into an arbitrary y-to-zero continuation and an arbitrary remaining return.
These are the exact events in the definitions.
Any permissible complete word belongs to the corresponding union, and every word in the union has such a split.
A per-word subprobability or probability-preserving mixture supported on these constructions cannot exceed the union measure.
The maximality assertion is therefore precisely relative to the specified zero-junction class and its original per-word budget.

Markov factorization gives p_(k-ell)nu_ell(c) for the right event and K^ell(y,0)p_(k-ell) for the left event.
Concatenating arbitrary independent zero returns proves p_k>=p_(k-ell)p_ell.
Dividing by the positive p_k therefore gives the upper ratio used in SW2, in the correct direction.
The union bound may overcount visits, but it is used only for an upper bound.
Every true union word itself is still counted once.

For each summand the Gaussian denominator is ell+2kappa.
