## The uniform prefix bound and the improvement for even \(s\)

The coefficient matching is exact: the neutral lift differs from \(F_s/(1-z)\) first at degree \(r>n+1\), whereas the first term of \(z^{n+1}H(z+z^{-1})\) has degree \(n+1-\deg H=L+1\) and coefficient \(\operatorname{lc}(H)\).
I recovered the mass \(4b_j^2\sin^2(s\theta_j)/p\) from the trace and grid derivative.
Matching against degree \(L-2\) follows from \(\deg(HT)\le n-2\); the moment of order two is available even at \(L=4\).
The full angular grid contributes each nonzero half-grid term twice, and the zero-angle sine weight vanishes.
The constant coefficients of \(t^{k+2}(1-\cos(2s\theta))\), \(t=2\cos\theta\), are \(2,0,6-\boldsymbol 1_{s=2}\) for \(k=0,1,2\): only the outer frequencies \(\pm4\) in \(t^4\) contribute the subtraction at \(s=2\), and no frequency aliases on the grid.
Consequently \(\sum_j(b_j-a)(2-b_j)\rho_j=-d_2-4a>0\), since every summand is positive; this separates zero from every permitted cut.
For nonnegative \(D\) with \(\deg D\le L-3\), the two signs of \(\lambda=(b-a)(\rho-\mu)\) have equal \(D\)-mass and the negative part at each node is at most \(4\mu_j\).

I checked that the sixth power in the kernel is a polynomial in \(\sin^2\theta\), hence an even polynomial in \(t\) of degree at most \(6(M-1)\), with value one at zero.
The grid averages of \(t^2D,t^4D\) are exact since their degrees are at most \(L+1<p\).
The circle average uses density \(2/\pi\) for the distance \(v\) to the nearest zero of \(\cos\theta\); splitting at \(\pi/(2M)\) gives the constants \(2\pi^2/3\) and \(6\pi^4/5\).
The half-grid sum is bounded by the full nonnegative sum.
For even \(s\), the additional estimate \(|\sin(s\theta)|\le s|t|/2\) is valid on the entire grid.
These checks give \(A_D\le32C_2/M^3\) and, for even \(s\), \(A_D\le8s^2C_4/M^5\), also when \(M=1\).

At zero the odd grid gives \(|b_j|\ge2/p\) and \(|B_n(0)|=1\).
The test polynomials \((1-D)/t\) and \((1-D)/t^2\) remove the unlocalized contributions to the value and derivative of \(G/B_n\); for \(D=1\) both tests are zero.
If \(A_D<4/p^2\), the value bound forces \(H(0)=0\) by integrality and \(|a|>1\).
Only after that vanishing does the derivative bound give \(|aH'(0)|<1\), so \(t^2\mid Q\).
The integral remainder has degree \(n-1\) and leading coefficient two since \(\deg H\le n-4\); the strict signs and endpoint make the integral all-cut root count applicable and exclude a double real root.
Thus \(A_D\ge4/p^2\); the two stated strict bounds follow from \(L<6M+3\) with exactly the constants claimed.
This proves necessary prefix bounds and supplies neither their optimality nor an integral sequence attaining them.

## Evaluation at a cyclotomic trace, including conductors divisible by its order

The torsion divisibility and conductor exclusion use the cut alone.
I checked the uniform separation needed for the evaluation: the first node contributes positively to the denominator of the weighted mean, and when \(p>2s+2a+3\) the discrete mean equals the angular mean.
If \(2s>2a+3\), the oscillating part contributes zero by orthogonality; increasing \(a\) for the weight \(b^2\) makes the mean tend to \(-2\) because every neighborhood of \(-2\) has positive mass.
Choosing this \(a\) first leaves only finitely many small \(s\), for which the same concentration applies with their own nonnegative weight \(b^2\sin^2(s\theta)\).
Since \(p\ge2L+1\) and \(2s<p/2\), sufficiently large \(L\) ensures every quadrature and degree requirement in this finite choice.
In particular the separator lies a fixed positive distance below the smallest trace of a fixed conjugacy class, uniformly in \(s,p\).

For a primitive trace \(\beta=2\cos\alpha\) of order \(q\), I checked the three bounds on the neutral mass: order two in \(\theta-\alpha\) at noncentral sine zeros, order two at the center for arbitrary \(s\), and order four there for even \(s\).
The nonnegative reflected kernel is a polynomial in the trace of degree at most \(L-3\), normalized to one at \(\beta\).
Its reflected denominator stays bounded away from zero on the upper semicircle for each fixed interior \(\alpha\).
Each shell of width \(1/N\) contains \(O(p/N)\) nodes since \(N\le p\); multiplying the weight and kernel bounds leaves a summable shell factor \(O((1+a)^{-2})\).
This gives \(O_q(A L^{-(2e+1)})\) for each of \(\int k\,d\mu_0\) and \(\int k\,d|\lambda|\).
For the latter, \((b-\tau)\mu\) is positive and has the same kernel mass as \((b-\tau)\mu_0\).

Off the grid, rational-angle separation and the cosine difference identity give \(|b_j-\beta|\gg_q1/p\), while the sine formula gives \(|B_n(\beta)|=O_q(1)\).
The constant annihilation of \(\delta\) and the polynomial test \((1-k)/(\beta-t)\) give the localized transform without any principal value.
On the grid the transform cannot be evaluated this way: the atom at \(\beta\) is bounded directly by the kernel mass of \(|\lambda|\) since \(k(\beta)=1\), and \(|B'_n(\beta)|=p/(4\sin\alpha\sin(\alpha/2))\).
Both cases therefore bound \(|H(\beta)|\) by \(C_q A p/L^{2e+1}\) with uniform dependence on \(s,p\).

Cyclotomic irreducibility gives the conjugates of \(\beta\) by pairing primitive roots with their inverses, and \(H(\beta)\) is an algebraic integer.
Making all those conjugate absolute values less than one forces \(H(\beta)=0\) by its field norm.
Since \(Q_0(\beta)=0\), this forces \(\Phi_q\mid P\); if \(q\mid p\), \(\beta\) is a grid node, contradicting the strict residue there.
The orders of the distinct factors of \(F_s\) are precisely \(q>2\) dividing \(2s\), together with \(q=4\), without double counting when \(s\) is even.
This proves the fixed-\(s\) divisibility and coprimality statements and the two stated uniform refinements.
With the additional endpoint, \(\deg H\le n-2\) preserves the integral remainder required by the all-cut theorem; its simple real traces lift to simple primitive roots since their orders exceed two.
Hence each forced factor then has multiplicity exactly one.
I found no gap in either argument; the accepted cyclotomic and all-cut inputs were checked for applicability without re-auditing their original proofs.
