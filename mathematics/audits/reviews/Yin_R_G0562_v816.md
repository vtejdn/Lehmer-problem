# Cardinality, residue classes and digit projections of returning carries

<a id="finding"></a>
## Independent review of the carry estimates

At 2026-11-20T23:10 (UTC), an independent reviewer for Sheng Yin checked the whole submitted C345 v679 and found all three arguments sound on the stated hyperbolic-unit domain.
The reviewer had no role in developing the proof and received its fixed text and the cited mathematical inputs without development dialogue, author explanations or a prior finding on this Claim.

At each expanding embedding the prefix of a returning path gives a geometric bound for its carry, and at each contracting embedding the returning suffix gives the corresponding bound.
Hyperbolicity covers every embedding, so discreteness of the order makes the set \(S\) of actual returning states finite.
Starting at \(c\), the endpoint after \(N\) digits is \(\alpha^{-N}(c+\sum_{i<N}\delta_i\alpha^i)\).
Every path between two states of \(S\) lies wholly in \(S\), by concatenation with paths from and to zero.
Starting from zero, the conditional probability of surviving the next block given the preceding surviving blocks is at most \(M_N\), by conditioning on the current state.
A return after \(\ell\) blocks requires survival of all of them, giving \(p_{\ell N}\le M_N^\ell\).
The collision probability is at least the reciprocal of the binary support size by Cauchy–Schwarz.
The stated grid bound therefore gives \(M_N\ge e^{-Nm}\) on taking \(\ell\)-th roots with the input and \(N\) fixed.

In blocks of \(d\) digits the displacement has Fourier transform \(\prod_i\cos^2(\pi\theta_i)\) in the power basis.
Multiplication by \(\alpha^{jd}\) acts invertibly on the order and on each quotient by \(qR\), including composite \(q\).
Each transformed block thus has the same \(L^k\) norm on normalized dual Haar measure.
Fourier inversion for an arbitrary atom also has a character of modulus one, so Hölder gives the same upper bound for every displacement and for its affine endpoint image.
The one-coordinate continuous integral is \(4^{-k}\binom{2k}{k}\) by the constant Fourier coefficient, and on the finite dual it is the average over \(j/q\) in (1).
For a maximizing start at \(N=kd\), the mass of all actual surviving paths is at most the endpoint atom bound times the number of possible endpoints, both before and after reduction.
This proves both cardinality bounds with the exponent \(e^{-km}\) after taking \(d\)-th roots.
At \(N=d\) the parity vector of the displacement is uniform on all \(2^d\) vectors, and its endpoint map modulo two is bijective.

With \(k=\lfloor1/(2m)\rfloor\) one has \(km\to1/2\) and \(\sqrt m/a_k\to\sqrt{\pi/2}\), giving the stated real constant.
For a varying integer \(q\ge2\), pairing the cosine powers at residues \(j\) and \(q-j\) bounds the part beyond the zero residue by \(2\sum_{j\ge1}\exp(-\pi^2 k j^2/q^2)\) in \(q a_{k,q}\).
An unpaired middle residue for even \(q\) contributes zero.
The choice \(k=\lceil q/\sqrt m\rceil\) satisfies \(k/q^2\to\infty\) and \(km\to0\) whenever \(mq^2\to0\), so this tail vanishes and \(|S_q|^{1/d}/q\to1\).
These arguments use the power basis of \(\mathbb Z[\alpha]\) and do not require invariance of field degree under powering.

The one maximizing state at \(N=d\) gives the same family \(F\) for all assertions about signed words.
The power basis makes each word a distinct representative, and the endpoint formula places its displacement in \(\alpha^d S-c\).
For the product distribution \(p=(1/2,1/4,1/4)\), Hölder gives
\[
  d^{-1}\log|F|\ \ge\
       -\frac{1+u}{u}m-\frac1u\log\sum_a p_a^{1+u},\qquad u>0 .
\]
Taking the lower limit at fixed \(u\) and then \(u\downarrow0\) gives \(-\sum_a p_a\log p_a=(3/2)\log2\).

Moving each coordinate fiber to an initial segment of the digit order preserves its cardinality and increases or preserves its product mass.
If a projection containing the compressed coordinate was shattered afterwards, each assignment on its other coordinates occurs with the last symbol in some compressed fiber.
That fiber has all three symbols and had all three before compression, so the original projection was already shattered.
Projections omitting the compressed coordinate are unchanged.
The integer sum of symbol ranks decreases at each nontrivial compression, ensuring termination at a family closed under coordinatewise decreases.
Any occurrence of the last symbol on \(j\) coordinates of one word then shatters those \(j\) coordinates.
Thus the compressed mass is at most \(\Pr(\operatorname{Bin}(d,1/4)\le v)\) for the original maximum shattered size \(v\).
Combining this with the mass \(e^{-dm}\) and the binomial lower-tail inequality gives \(v\ge d(1/4-\sqrt{m/2})\), including the range where the right side is nonpositive.
The other digits of a representative can vary with the assigned symbols on the shattered set.
