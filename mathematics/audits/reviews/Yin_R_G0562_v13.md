## Finding on the normalized trace numerator

At 2026-11-16T15:26 UTC a separate reviewer for Yin inspected the statement and all four argument sections of [C345v13](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#statement).
The reviewer had previously reviewed other results in this family and had read the mass-two classification and cutoff methods used here.
The reviewer did not develop the new argument and received its fixed text and the assignment without development dialogue, author explanations, an earlier verdict on this version or a requested result.
The argument passes for varying \(j\) under the stated circle norm hypothesis, including the bounded-support consequence.

Put \(\delta=\theta-q\).
The exact [derivative identity](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#second) gives \(1\le j\le\delta(q+3)\), so \(q\to\infty\) and \(j/(q-1)\le\delta(q+3)/(q-1)\to0\).
This uses the derivative bound valid at each input, independently of \(j\).
The [positive delay-one associate](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v32.md#boyd-return) has the same Mahler measure as \(B^\sharp\), since the [trace factorization](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v7.md#trace) removes at most \(z+1\).
That measure tends to one from above, which already excludes bounded \(h\) by the [fixed-support gap](../literature/DS17/Yin_U001.md#ds17-u001).
The constant-one series \(\Pi/j\) is invertible over the integers modulo \(z^{\lfloor\eta h\rfloor+1}\) whenever the given divisibilities hold.
Thus \(J_q=B^\sharp/(\Pi/j)\) is integral throughout this prefix, regardless of the later rational coefficients.

In the [signed inverse measure](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v49.md#signed-measure) the full negative part is \(j\delta_1/[4(q-1)]\).
Each simple root \(y\) of \(C\) carries positive weight proportional to \(w_y/(4-y^2)\) by the [inverse representation](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v43.md#inverse).
Since all \(y\) are distinct and interior, the two lifts of each give \(2h\) distinct atoms of the positive part.
After extraction along one or two \(o(h)\) indices, the eventual relations form a saturated subgroup because a nonzero integer multiple of an integer sum vanishes exactly when that sum vanishes.
The resulting quotient \(\Gamma\) is free abelian of positive rank, and each of its nonzero characters tests an eventually nonzero index of absolute value \(o(h)\).
All the limiting Fourier coefficients of the doubled positive measure are integers by conjugation symmetry, the integral prefix and the vanishing negative mass.

If an integral Laurent polynomial \(P\ne0\) vanished almost everywhere for the limiting measure, multiplication by \(1-X^a\) for \(a\ne0\) would preserve its nonzero polynomial class and force its value at the identity to vanish.
With any fixed lifts of the finitely many characters, their exponents are eventually distinct, and shifting them into \([0,o(h)]\) changes no circle modulus.
For the resulting \(Q_q\), the constant-difference part of its squared norm against \(2\omega_q\) is twice the sum of the coefficient squares, and every other difference tests an integral coefficient of \(J_q\).
The equality \(Q_q(1)=0\) gives exactly the same integer against \(2\omega_q^+\).
Convergence of the finitely many difference moments forces these nonnegative integers eventually to vanish, irrespective of the sizes of the individual atom weights.
The resulting polynomial would then have \(2h\) distinct roots and degree \(o(h)\), a contradiction.

These facts supply all the hypotheses of the [mass-two classification](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v11.md#classification), including the exclusion of a purely singular measure supported on a finite union of proper annihilator subgroups.
Its symmetric Laurent density of mean two has at most one opposite pair of nonconstant frequencies by the [mean-two theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_G0403_v1.md#integer-mean-two-classification).
In the other case the nonzero nonconstant moments form the signs of a character on a subgroup.
If two distinct positive tested indices both have nonzero moments, their characters remain nonidentical and nonopposite in \(\Gamma\), so the subgroup case gives the product law at their sum and difference and the value one at twice the smaller index.
If any required law failed for arbitrarily small fixed prefix fractions along arbitrarily late inputs, the violating indices could be chosen \(o(h)\).
Their sums, differences and doubles would still lie in the original integral prefix, giving eventual equality with their classified limits.
On the resulting common prefix, [subtraction and addition of the least nonzero index](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v11.md#local) give the stated constant, binomial or reciprocal-binomial model.
The last model is the average of the constant one and the Herglotz function \((1+\epsilon z^r)/(1-\epsilon z^r)\), so its probability normalization holds also for varying \(r\).

The model has numerator and denominator of degree \(O(h)\) with circle suprema at most two.
The two series being subtracted have uniformly bounded representing measures at all positive indices, and the zero prefix occupies a fixed positive fraction of \(h\) along the hypothetical sequence.
The [two-cutoff argument](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v51.md#window) therefore controls the \(L^1\) norm of \(UB^\sharp-V\Pi/j\) even with rational coefficients.
Adding \(V\Pi/j\) preserves the bound by the assumed normalized supremum.
The [integer support estimate](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v50.md#window) now applies to \(UB^\sharp\), and its Mahler measure equals that of \(B^\sharp\) because \(U\) has measure one.
The fixed-support contradiction excludes every sequence with a divisible prefix of positive relative length.
Taking the first failure to be infinite for an everywhere divisible lift gives the stated sequential formulation, including \(j=1\).
Since every trace zero is interior, the lift has only circle zeros and \(M(\Pi)=j\).
For at most \(s\ge2\) terms, the [sparse height bound](../literature/DS17/Yin_U002.md#sparse-height-bound) therefore gives \(\|\Pi/j\|_\infty\le s\,2^{s-2}\), uniformly in the branch.
The conclusion is qualitative and the cited external theorems retain their statement and application scopes.
