# Independent review of eight-term lifts with lower offsets \(d,e,d+e\)

At 2026-11-16T17:58 UTC, a separately assigned Codex reviewer for Yin checked the fixed ledger arguments and the hypotheses of their cited inputs.
The reviewer had no part in developing either argument and received no development discussion or author explanations.
Both findings derive from the ledger proofs and their mathematical dependencies.
The review uses the cited external theorems at statement and applicability depth.

## Coefficient classification and the two reciprocal sign cases

The [eight-term reduction](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v93.md#eight) applies on an escaping branch-two sequence and gives \(d+e=o(h)\), \(e/\gcd(d,e)\to\infty\) and Mahler measure two on the rank-two base.
I checked both Jensen integrals after sign rotation and coordinate interchange.
They bound the two positive linear coefficients by two, and equality requires the affine difference of squared moduli to be nonnegative at both ends of \([-1,1]\).
For \((a,b)=(1,1),(1,2),(2,2)\) the allowed nonzero \(c\)'s are respectively \(\{1,2\},\{1\},\{2\}\).
The last triple gives a completely even lift, contradicting the [first nondivisible index](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#statement).
The strict modulus inequality in the first triple excludes zeros on the closed bidisk.

For the product triple, taking the integral factor \(1+t z^\ell\) leaves the required fixed fractional factor \(1+s w/2\) with its root outside the disk, in either ordering of \(d,e\).
The prefix length \(h\) and bounded circle norm satisfy the [rational-prefix theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v21.md#statement).
For the reciprocal triple I verified \(z^{d+e}A(1/z)=stA(z)\).
With \(s=t\), the trace of \(A(z)A(1/z)\) is an integral polynomial of interval norm at most 36 and endpoint value \((4+2s)^2\).
For the unique primitive nonmonic factor of \(C\), a root cannot be a torsion trace because its primitive minimal polynomial has leading coefficient two.
Its lift must therefore vanish in \(A\), giving integral divisibility of the trace polynomial and contradicting the [multiplier bound](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v7.md#statement).

With \(t=-s\), differentiation of \(\Pi\) at one gives \(C(2)=2(d+e)-s(d-e)\).
The [endpoint identity](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v7.md#strip) gives the stated divisibility.
In the formal example \(s=d=1,\ e=2m\), division by \(1-z\) gives exactly the [primitive irreducible polynomial \(S_{e+1}\)](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v45.md#family), with endpoint \(4+3(e-1)=3e+1\).
Its degree-\(m\) trace is irreducible since any integral factorization lifts, and the complementary quotient \((1+z^{N-e-1})/(1+z)\) is monic reciprocal of even degree.
Thus the primitive nonmonic part has endpoint \(3e+1\), which lower-bounds the interval norm of every admissible integral multiple with nonzero endpoint.
I find the classification, the two exclusions, the divisibility and the formal example valid.
Pisot realization of the remaining opposite-sign pattern is open in this argument.

## Positive excess for \(2+a z^d+b z^e+ab z^{d+e}\)

I checked the argument for all four sign pairs and both original degree parities.
The [derivative identity](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#second) reduces both uniform bounds to exclusion of \(q\to\infty\).
On this sequence the two independent base frequencies follow from \(e/\gcd(d,e)\to\infty\).
The strict inequality \(|2+a x|>|1+a x|\) on the closed unit disk gives a positive circle minimum and an absolutely summable Taylor inverse uniformly after every specialization.
The [signed measure and actual denominator](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v20.md#reduction) have the required moment normalization and Mahler limit.

At base and mixed frequencies the four exponents are \(0,d,e,d+e\), so all fixed shifts are \(O(e)\) and the positive-layer tests at \(kt+rd+se\) are inside the prefix.
I checked the zero-layer normalization and the use of the [coset decomposition and slope extraction](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#extinction).
The coefficients \(2+a x\) and \(b(1+a x)\) generate the unit ideal, and the square support has opposite corners of different coefficient magnitudes.
These facts justify irreducibility and coprimality with reversal.
For each graph slope the integral Laurent quotient then has nonnegative density and integer mean, so any nonzero slope exhausts the probability and its nonconstant integral Fourier coefficients vanish.
The fractional coefficient at a base frequency excludes this Haar marginal.
At a last full-dimensional positive row the conditional \(k\)-th power pushforward gives \(|c_k|\le1/2\), and Jensen gives \(M(A)M(f)/2<1\).
Thus the fixed torsion cosets of the marginal suffice for a [single annihilating product](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#singular) on every intermediate scale.

For comparable offsets the [first-coefficient tests](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v72.md#mixed) never reach \(d+e\).
When \(e-d=o(d)\), any short nonzero model has \(e-d=u\) or \(2u\), so \(2(e-d)\le4u\), and its initial coefficient sum is bounded.
The extra offset and its reflection only add split points to the [interval argument](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v72.md#all-intervals).
For \(d/e\to0\), the [one-offset short tests](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#scales) and [mixed-row tests](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#far-rows) stay below \(e\).
Only the projected mixed row is confined to sixth-root atoms, which suffices for the coefficient difference of period \(p=6d\).
I checked the identity \(k_{-l}=-k_{l+p}\) for \(k_l=m_l-m_{l-p}\), including the possible Taylor constant error in the first \(p+1\) indices.
It controls the absolute diagonal sums of the signed Gram matrix weighted by \(|1-v^{-p}|^2\) on blocks of diameter \(e/(2L_1)\).
On a transition of length at most \(6d\), the initial zero moments instead give identity Gram matrices on blocks of diameter \(\kappa d\) when \(d\) grows.
Bounded \(d\) gives bounded transition length directly.
Testing a fixed number of characters allows the negative-part Gram correction to tend to zero before the Bessel bound is used, so both kinds of blocks give the claimed local absolute sums.

For each moving shift of size \(O(e)\) the corrected negative moments are controlled by the local absolute sum of \(V_iJ_i\).
The comparison \(Q_0/S_i\) has an absolutely summable Taylor series and its agreement with \(U_iV_iJ_i\) through \(\lfloor h/L\rfloor\) gives the required positive-length zero prefix.
Both sides of the polynomial identity have bounded multipliers, and the left side has degree \(O(h)\).
The [two cutoffs](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#cutoff) and uniform circle minimum give bounded \(L^1\) norm for the integer polynomial \(U_iV_iB^\sharp\).
I checked the use of the [prime-grid support bound](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v50.md#window) and the [fixed-support gap](../literature/DS17/Yin_U001.md#ds17-u001) on this polynomial, whose added binomials have measure one.
The contradiction proves the stated qualitative gap for the strictly stable coefficient shape.
Together the two ledger arguments leave the opposite-sign reciprocal shape under the specified exponent relation.
