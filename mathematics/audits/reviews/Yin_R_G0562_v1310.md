In rotation order, the two intersection partners of edge \(a_j\) have indices \(a_j\pm s\).
Every clockwise distance between distinct centers and its complementary distance exceed \(s\), so the twist rows use unchanged input coordinates.
The only relation among the cyclically oriented edges is their sum, and each transvection fixes the all-ones vector.
After the ordinary rows are eliminated, the two cyclic products in the retained determinant contribute \(X^{N-sm}\prod_j(X^s+c_j)\) and \(-\prod_j(1+c_jX^s)\), respectively.
At the smallest gap \(s+1\), the backward neighbor is the preceding retained variable, exactly as the formula requires.
For a single center the two contributions occur in the same entry.
The closed action therefore has polynomial \(P\).
The exponents of the two blocks are disjoint by their residues for \(s>1\) and by \(2m<N\) for \(s=1\).
Counting positive and negative real roots with Descartes' rule gives \(m\le2(b_Q-1)\), enough to pay for all \(m\) twists from the open support.

The closed polynomial meets the irreducibility, noncyclotomicity and power-substitution hypotheses of the closed criterion, and the degree gives genus at least two.

Thus the only new reducing disk could contain both poles, with all vertices in the complementary disk.
The winding residue for such a disk-supported representative is zero, while the rotation contributes \(u\) and each disk-supported half twist contributes zero.
The branched lift fixes both origin lifts and permits filling the branch marks because each lifted foliation there has at least two prongs.
For \(s=1\), the separating curve from the fixed-disk construction has closed-essential image and separates the two marks.
Closed-essential test curves meet its orbit by closed pseudo-Anosov dynamics, and any essential test curve lost on capping bounds a disk around both marks and meets the separating curve.
The filling-orbit criterion thus applies with the extra twist on its own level.
The sector triangulation has bounded neighborhoods for each selected edge in either construction.
In the period-\(a\) cover the common collar costs \(O(N)\) once and drilled layers cost \(O(a(m+1))\), uniformly in twist exponents.
Division by \(a\) and passage to the limit give the asserted volume bound.
The two fixed oriented boundary circles contribute one fixed homology dimension.

Modulo two, an even \(c_j\) contributes a monomial to \(Q\) and an odd one contributes \(Y+1\).
The two factors of the resulting \(\overline F\) have degrees \(H=N-sl\) and \(sl\).

The larger degree is at least \(\lceil N/2\rceil\), and division by \(X-1\) loses at most one.

For the prime examples the translated polynomial is monic, reduces to \(X^{N-1}\) modulo \(N\), and has constant \(N\bmod N^2\).
When \(sm<N/2\), the negative lower block is exhausted before the positive one starts, so the negative partial sums of the coefficients of \(F\) give positive coefficients throughout \(P\).

The proposed choices have \(m\to\infty\), \(m=o(N)\) and \(sm=N/3+O(m)\), and satisfy the gap budget at large primes.

For the deletion argument, removing a power of \(X\) leaves a block with nonzero constant.
The common root multiset of this block and its reversal is inversion stable, so the monic integral gcd \(G_0\) has unit constant and is reciprocal up to sign.
Division by \(G_0\) leaves a monic \(T\) coprime to its reversal.
If \(\deg T>0\), the nonreciprocal measure bound and the comparison for either sign apply, since \(h/\deg T\ge n/r\) and the comparison error tends uniformly to zero as this ratio grows.
Multiplicativity and \(M(G_0)\ge1\) then contradict the ceiling for \(F\).
A constant original block would give only cyclotomic factors, so the remaining binomial has \(h\ge2\).
Since \(P\) is irreducible and noncyclotomic, a primitive \(2h\)-th root in the plus case or a primitive \(h\)-th root in the minus case must come from the boundary periods.
Their total length \(\deg C+1<2h\) excludes the plus case and forces a single period of length \(h\) in the minus case.
A singleton list would give \(P=(X-1)G_0\), contrary to the hypotheses.
Removing the period consequently preserves admissibility and leaves \(G_0\), whose two disjoint translates formed \(F\).
This proves exact halving and yields the minimizing-support conclusion at the degree-ten record ceiling.
