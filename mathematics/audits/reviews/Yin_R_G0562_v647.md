# Independent review of the order window for simultaneous powered residues

## Counting exponent pairs at a single place and detecting all small orders

At 2026-11-20T10:00 UTC a separate reviewing agent for Sheng Yin examined the [order-window claim](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v514.md#statement) independently of its proof development.
I found its statement supported, including the distinction between existence of a common root of all four polynomials and the universal order bound for common roots of the two powered minima outside the window.

For fixed seeds and fixed \(d\), nontorsion makes each of the finitely many resultants against \(Y^u-1\), \(1\le u\le d\), nonzero.
At any other characteristic all original roots therefore reduce to units of order greater than \(d\).
At one prime of a common splitting field the two integral factorizations reduce simultaneously and exhaust both root sets, even in ramified characteristics or with coincident labels.
Raising the roots to the indicated exponents still enumerates the roots of the powered minima, since the conjugates of a power are exactly the powers of the conjugates.
Consequently a collision of order \(t\le d\) at this place forces original orders \(pt,qt\), by the prime-exponent order formula.
For \(p,q>d\) each original order determines its exponent as its unique prime factor exceeding \(d\).
There are at most \(ef\) pairs per characteristic, and fixing \(p\) leaves at most \(f\) possible partners since each second label determines at most one \(q\).

Both interval sizes are \(\asymp L/\log L\) by the [modulus-one estimate](../literature/Ked07/Yin_U001.md#siegel-walfisz).
For \(X\le L\) even \(L\) is negligible against their product, and for \(X>L\) the estimate \(\pi(X)\ll X/\log L\) gives the required little-oh under the stated hypothesis.
At fixed \(p\), a collision of order \(t\) places its characteristic in the support of the resultant for \(u=t\) in \(W_p\).
Multiplication over all first labels bounds \(\log W_p\) by \(de\log 2+pd(d+1)h_1/2\).
Thus the number of candidate characteristics above the upper endpoint is \(O_{P_1,d}(L/(A(L)\log L))\), and the bound of \(f\) partners costs only \(O_{P_1,P_2,d}(1/A(L))\) of the rectangle after summation over \(p\).

At fixed trial width the growing cutoff eventually removes the union of the exceptional prime sets for every listed seed, and the two incidence bounds apply to the finite union of ordered seed pairs.
At every scale, including before this eventual exclusion, factoring \(W_p\) and testing the three-way gcd for each \(u\le j\) detects precisely the forbidden characteristics and nodes.
A common irreducible factor supplies a root whose order divides \(u\), and a root of order \(t\le j\) supplies such a factor at \(u=t\).
The [earlier exact comparison](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v508.md#width) applies with \(Z=\lfloor\log_2 L\rfloor\), which tends to infinity and satisfies \(\log Z=o(\log L)\).
Every fixed trial therefore eventually meets its fraction threshold, proving divergence of the largest passing width and density one without a uniform estimate in the trial width.

For each actual seed choice the [contact and local divisor argument](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v508.md#rows) puts it on the tested list and gives the strict bound with \(N=B_0^*\mid B\).
For every prime factor of this integer the unequal-label valuation comparison or the equal-label derivative identity supplies one common nonzero root of both rows and both powered minima, of degree at most the smaller seed degree.
Outside the window the gcd test excludes small orders for every root of every common irreducible factor of the powered minima, hence also for that witness.
The local argument requires only one witness per divisor prime and allows the entire divisor to lie inside the window.
Finally the two-prime sets have minimum tending to infinity and width divided by cardinality tending to infinity, as required by the [unrestricted limit](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#gap).
A zero infimum therefore gives genuine vanishing-cost rows along each retained sequence, and conversely the positive seed measures in the contact bound force the infimum to vanish along any such sequence.
