Their absolute inner product is bounded by \(\zeta(2)g^2/(d_i d_j)\), hence by \(\zeta(2)\min(d_i,d_j)/\max(d_i,d_j)\).

Thus each output coefficient receives at most \(3w\) summands.

Writing \(T=\max_{0\le j\le N}\|F_j\|_4\), Hölder bounds each such squared norm by \(4T^2 b_i^2\,2^{-s/2}\).
