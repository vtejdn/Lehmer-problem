The two exterior roots have product \(\lambda\bar\lambda=\beta\), so \(\lambda^2=\beta\zeta\) and \(\lambda^{2N}=\beta^N\).
Powers of a real Salem number retain all distinct conjugates by C345v52, giving \(K=\mathbb Q(\beta^N)\subseteq\mathbb Q(\lambda)\).
Thus \(\zeta=\lambda^2/\beta\) lies in the same field.
Among embeddings fixing \(\beta\), the two factors of its product must each have the maximal available modulus \(\sqrt\beta\), so the only possible images of \(\lambda\) are \(\lambda,\bar\lambda\).
Both occur under complex conjugation, with \(\bar\lambda=\beta/\lambda\) in the field.
Consequently its degree over \(K\) is two, its conjugation-fixed field is \(K\), and the nonreal \(\zeta\) generates the extension.
This puts \(c=\zeta+\zeta^{-1}\) in \(K\).
At any neutral embedding of \(K\), complex conjugation agrees with the image of inversion on the generator \(\beta\).
Every totally real subfield of \(K\) is therefore fixed by inversion and contained in \(B\), in particular \(\mathbb Q(c)\).

For \(s=\lambda+\bar\lambda\in K\), its square \(\beta(2+c)\) is nonzero for \(N>2\).
Applying inversion, which fixes \(c\), gives \(s\iota(s)=\pm(2+c)\).
At a neutral embedding the left side is \(|\tau(s)|^2>0\) and the right factor \(2+\tau(c)>0\), fixing the sign.
Then \(t=(\beta+1)/s\) is fixed by inversion and has the claimed square, and \((1+\zeta)/(2+c)=\zeta/(1+\zeta)\) gives the formula for \(\lambda\).
Conversely the squareclass yields two opposite choices of \(t\) with real values in the chosen embedding.
The resulting \(\lambda\) has quotient \(\zeta\), and its square is \(\beta\zeta\) by \((1+\zeta)^2=\zeta(2+c)\).
It is an algebraic unit and generates \(K(\zeta)\) by the power relation and the formula for \(\zeta\).
The extension has degree two since \(K\subset\mathbb R\) in the chosen embedding and \(X^2-cX+1\) has the nonreal root \(\zeta\).
Over each embedding of \(K\) there are two conjugates of \(\lambda\), of modulus the square root of the corresponding modulus of \(\beta\).
This gives the required exterior pair, neutral conjugates and exact measure.
At any neutral conjugate complex conjugation realizes its inverse, which makes the entire orbit invariant under inversion.
The two signs exhaust the possibilities.
Since \([K:B]=2\) and \([\mathbb Q(c):\mathbb Q]=\varphi(N)/2\), the degree \(d=4[B:\mathbb Q]\) is divisible by \(2\varphi(N)\).

For odd \(N\) choose \(2j\equiv1\bmod N\).
The nonzero torsion trace \(\zeta^j+\zeta^{-j}\) in \(\mathbb Q(c)\) squares to \(2+c\), so dividing \(s\) by it gives a square root of \(\beta\) in \(K\).
The positive choice generates \(K\) by squaring and is integral, with precisely one exterior conjugate and neutral conjugates by correspondence of embeddings.
Its value lies strictly between one and \(\beta\), ruling out an odd order at the specified Salem minima.
For \(N=2\) the separate construction in C345v72 gives the purely imaginary square roots of \(-\beta\) and measure \(\beta\).

The lower bound for the lift degree combines \(2\varphi(N)\mid d\) with the minimum real Salem degree four.
For \(N=3,4,6\), the real cyclotomic field has degree one and the construction uses the totally real quadratic field \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\).
All other \(N>2\) use the real cyclotomic field itself.
The first \(f\) powers of the chosen integral generator are independent over \(\mathbb Q\).
With the other \(f-1\) embeddings restricted to boxes of side \(\epsilon\), the number of available sums grows as \(H^f\) and the number of boxes as \(O_{F,\epsilon}(H^{f-1})\).
Their nonzero integral difference has nonzero conjugates and integer norm of absolute value at least one, so its distinguished absolute value is at least \(\epsilon^{1-f}\).
After choosing its sign and shrinking \(\epsilon\), \(T=(2+c)t^2-2\) has one value greater than two and all other values strictly between \(-2\) and two.
Equal embedding multiplicities over the one distinguished value force \(T\) to generate \(F\), and a negative image of \(T^2-4\) rules out a square there.
The quadratic unit defined by \(X^2-TX+1\) has the required Salem signature and degree \(2f\).
Its lift attains \(4f=\max(8,2\varphi(N))\).
The real-power condition is exactly \(\zeta^n=1\).
With \(\zeta=e^{2\pi i/N}\) and \(t>0\), the lift has argument \(\pi/N\) and least positive real power \(2N\), at which the value is \(\beta^N\).

For the size estimate the retained inverse totient bound gives \(N\ll\varphi(N)\log\log(3+\varphi(N))\).

Distinct conjugates of this reciprocal unit have nontorsion quotient because their positive powers never coincide.
The resulting \(\varphi(N)\ll(\log\beta)(\log(3d))^{48}\), combined with degree divisibility, gives the bound in \(X\), with absolute constants also absorbing bounded arguments.
