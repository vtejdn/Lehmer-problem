In a relative norm-one unit the two extensions over each negative real trace give unit modulus, the two distinguished real extensions have reciprocal values, and the four extensions over the nonreal trace pair give two opposite logarithms each counted twice.

The two independent generators map to a rank-two subgroup, which spans the plane by discreteness and has finite index there by reduction into a bounded fundamental parallelogram.
The covolume ratio is therefore exactly \(I\).

A nontorsion unit has one exterior distinguished real embedding.
If there were none above the nonreal trace pair, counting with restriction multiplicities would force \(\mathbb Q(u)=K\).
Equality of \(\sigma\iota\) and \(\overline{\sigma}\) on that generator would then make them equal on \(K\), in conflict with their distinct restrictions to \(B\).
There are consequently three exterior embeddings, giving field index one or three and the stated logarithmic measure after division by that index.
In the index-three case the unique exterior conjugate is real because one of its preimages is a real embedding.
Reciprocity gives the unique interior conjugate, and \(3\mid2n\) gives \(3\mid n\).

After killing the torsion in the radical relation, the norm down to \(F=\mathbb Q(\gamma^r)\) is a unit \(v\) with \(v^r=(\gamma^r)^{[\mathbb Q(\gamma):F]}\).
The absolute height law therefore gives \(m(\gamma)=[F:\mathbb Q(v)]m(v)\).
The relative norm of \(v\) in \(K/B\) is a real root of unity whose value at any neutral real place is a positive squared modulus, so it is one.
This proves the descent to \(U\), and the finite index gives the reverse inclusion in the radical closure.
Finiteness of units of bounded degree and measure gives attainment.

Raising to the \(I\)-th power places each nontorsion unit in \(\{\pm1\}H\), and a sign leaves both degree and height unchanged.
This proves the lower bound \(m(T)/I\).
