## Finding: rational feasibility is verified; optimization conclusions remain numerical

It is not an integer input, and neither \(M(P)>1\) nor an integer completion is certified here.

### Exact complete polynomial and original-domain compatibility

The selected input has \(p=2003,n=1001,x=0,\kappa=1,L=140\), exactly 1001 coefficients and the prescribed coordinates through index 138.
The stored rational strings are interpreted as exact fractions, not as further approximations to a floating solution.
They agree with both saved verification payloads and with the decimal-to-rational conversion of the actual LP tail.

For an independent full lift, the checker uses
\[
P(z)=(1+z^2)\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}z^j
 +\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_i z^{i+2}\sum_{j=0}^{2n-2i-2}z^j.
\]
This follows from the retained remainder-basis identity and the complete lift of each B polynomial.
It retains the last central term and both ends of every geometric block.
It is independent of the author's cumulative division by one minus z.

Exact integer arithmetic after a common coefficient denominator constructs all 2005 coefficients of the original degree-2004 polynomial.
The resulting polynomial is reciprocal, monic and has constant coefficient one.
Its coefficients through index 140 agree exactly with the formal series \((1+z^2)^2/((1-z)^2(1+z))\), whose denominator recurrence is checked separately.
Thus the original coefficient prefix is verified, rather than inferred solely from a label on the input.

Evaluating the entire polynomial at minus one agrees with the separate coordinate endpoint formula and gives
\[
P(-1)=\frac{3027458616443292296351}{10^{19}}>0.
\]
The fixed contribution is 280, so the integer endpoint row is precisely a tail sum at least minus 279.
The real witness satisfies the stronger row used by the LP, and therefore also satisfies the original strict endpoint.

The checker derives the values and derivatives of every B polynomial at zero from the E recurrence.
This verifies the program's two closed jet formulas, including index zero, and gives the actual selected jets
\[
Q(0)=\frac{9747626183577589}{10^{19}},\qquad
Q'(0)=-\frac{4534059508669041771}{10^{19}}.
\]
This selected real witness is not on the later requested integer branch with jets zero and minus one.

### The integrality boundary is exact

All 862 free tail coefficients are nonintegers.
Their common denominator is \(10^{19}\), and the independently constructed full polynomial has the same minimum common denominator.
The checker verifies that minimum using the greatest common divisor of all scaled polynomial coefficients and the denominator.
The endpoint above also has denominator \(10^{19}\) in lowest terms, consistent with the full-polynomial calculation.
Therefore neither h nor the monic P is an integer polynomial.

Replacing h by a denominator multiple would alter the fixed prefix and the original construction.
Multiplying the complete P by that denominator would preserve its roots but multiply its complete Mahler measure by \(10^{19}\).
Neither operation converts this verified real point into the required integer small-measure construction.
