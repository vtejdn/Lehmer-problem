### Which factors enter the search

Dividing all occupied rows by their common factor \(A\) preserves their nonvanishing.
If a noncyclotomic irreducible \(g\) divided \(A\), then \(g(x^p)\) could have no torsion root, so divisibility by \(CP\) would force \(g(x^p)=\pm P\), contrary to the support-difference hypothesis.
Thus the reduced diagonal still has \(P\) with multiplicity one and a cyclotomic cofactor.
Its reciprocity gives \(t\) pairs about the central residue, which itself occurs precisely for odd \(k\).
The centered lift has \(h\le H\) by the box estimate, or by sending the single pair to \(\{\pm1\}\) when \(t=1\).
In particular the strict inequality \(24H<p\) suffices for the hypotheses of v347.

The reciprocal carrier supplied by v347 has \(1\le s\le h\) and normalized diagonal \(J\) of degree at most \(\lfloor Dp/(p-24H)\rfloor\).
Writing \(L\) for this bound minus \(D\), each cyclotomic factor of \(J/P\) has \(\varphi(m)\le L\) and hence \(m\le2L^2\) by the prime-power estimate in the proof.
The degree budget also bounds multiplicity and permits the empty product.
After shifting the minimum fast exponent to zero, translation in the base coordinate changes the minimum diagonal exponent by multiples of \(p\), so some enumerated \(r\) is the required representative.
For each nonzero coefficient in \(J\) the congruence determines at most one \(j\) in the strip and the diagonal equation then gives its unique integral base exponent.
Every monomial of the actual carrier survives specialization by this same injectivity, which proves completeness of the reconstruction.

Fast widths add because the extreme coefficients in a product of Laurent polynomials in \(Y\) are products in the domain \(\mathbb Z[X^{\pm1}]\).
For every remaining irreducible factor, v347 supplies a primitive character, with nonzero fast component by the unit row gcd.
Character reversal lets one choose \(v_i>0\) at the cost of a Laurent unit, even for \(\Phi_1\).
The width budget therefore bounds the sum of \(v_i\varphi(m_i)\), including every repeated factor.
The unrestricted enumeration of \(a_i\) includes primitive characters and also permits nonprimitive ones, whose specializations still have cyclotomic factorizations.

### Solving all collision conditions

For a fixed list, a term coming from \(X^uY^j\) and powers \(q_i\) in the cyclotomic factors has exponent pair \((u+\sum_i q_i a_i,j+\sum_i q_i v_i)\).
Two terms can cancel only at the same pair.
Thus each row partition specifies an equality class for each coefficient sum, and a row survives exactly when some class in it has a nonzero sum.
The equality system for all rows shares the same exponent vector \(a\).
Smith normal form gives either inconsistency or a particular integer solution plus the full integer kernel.
In the resulting free parameters each inequality excludes the zero set of an affine form.
An identically zero form makes the partition impossible.
For positive rank \(r\), substitution of \(N,\ldots,N^r\) gives a nonzero polynomial for every other form because its constant and linear coefficients occupy distinct powers of \(N\).
The finite union of their integral root sets is finite, so successive trials find a simultaneous solution.
In rank zero the particular point decides every inequality, including the case of an empty list of factors.
An actual multiplier gives one of these partitions, with all inequalities realized by its own exponents.

Conversely the constructed product has fast width at most \(H<p\), so distinct surviving rows have different diagonal residues and different base exponents in one row stay distinct.
For each added factor the diagonal exponent \(n_i=pa_i+bv_i\) is nonzero modulo \(p\).
For \(n_i>0\), every root of the monic integral polynomial \(\Phi_{m_i}(x^{n_i})\) is torsion, and inversion gives the same conclusion up to a signed Laurent monomial for \(n_i<0\).
Normalizing the resulting diagonal therefore gives a monic polynomial with precisely the prescribed residue count and divisible by \(P\) with cyclotomic quotient.
Exact polynomial division recovers a witness.

### Prime bounds

In the collinear case the nonzero lattice differences in the carrier support are multiples of one primitive vector \((u,v)\), with \(v\ne0\).
Factoring its one-variable polynomial \(f\) would factor the carrier, so \(f\) is irreducible and a torsion root would make \(f\) cyclotomic.
This would give a torsion-only diagonal, since \(0<|v|\le s<p\) implies \(q=pu+bv\ne0\).
Hence \(f(x^q)\) has no torsion root and its normalized diagonal equals \(P\).
Every support difference of \(P\) is divisible by \(q\), giving \(|q|=1\) and \(D=\deg f\le |v|\deg f=s\).
For a noncollinear support the integer triangle bound and the determinant-\(p\) map \((i,j)\mapsto(pi+bj,j)\) give \(ds\ge p/2\).
Together with the v347 degree inequality this yields \(D\ge p/(2s)-12\) throughout the stated narrow range.
In the smaller shell v347 excludes collinearity, so \(p/(2h)\le D+12\).
For \(t\ge2\) this gives \(\lfloor(p-1)^{1/t}\rfloor\le4(D+12)\), proving the advertised upper bound and finiteness of the search over qualifying primes.
For \(t=1\) direct substitution gives \(p\le4(D+12)\).

For \(k=2,3\) and \(p>48\), the pair \(\{\pm1\}\) gives \(h=2\) in either parity.
If \(d<p\), the exponents in the normalized \(J\) lie in an interval containing at most one exponent per residue, and \(s\le2\) gives at most three terms.
Positive measure excludes a monomial or a binomial with unit endpoints.
Simultaneous reciprocity of the carrier induces reciprocal symmetry up to sign for \(J\).
For three terms the middle term is fixed by reversal, so the sign is positive, its exponent is \(d/2=e\), and \(J=x^{2e}+a x^e+1\).
Power substitution reduces its measure to that of \(z^2+az+1\), whose roots lie on the circle for \(|a|\le2\) and whose larger root modulus is at least \((3+\sqrt5)/2\) for \(|a|\ge3\).
This excludes the assumed interval for \(m(P)=m(J)\), giving \(d\ge p\) and \(D\ge d(1-24s/p)\ge p-48\).
All smaller primes meet the bound directly.
The degree-ten example has constant and linear coefficients one and the cited degree, irreducibility and measure range, giving the stated cutoff \(58\).
The argument settles only the fixed-input questions in its hypotheses and leaves the growing-degree existence question open.
