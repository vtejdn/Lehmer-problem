## Exclusion of the constant limiting reciprocal polynomial

At 2026-11-16T21:49 UTC a separately assigned Codex reviewer for Sheng Yin checked the full C345v50 submission dated 2026-11-16T21:48.
The reviewer had no part in developing C345v50 and received its fixed proof without development dialogue, author explanations or an earlier verdict on it.
The reviewer had previously checked the fixed L462v143 classification, including its allowable torsion orders, all six rows, their atom masses and the first-departure exclusions.
It supplies the negative-sign gap and leaves the two specified positive-sign prefixes.
C345v49 applies to exactly the linear prefix for the same interlacer and degree.
In its fixed proof the squared coefficient norm is at most \(13/6\).
The tests at the primitive sixth and fourth roots then leave only a signed \(1-z^d\) with \(12\mid d\), and the test at angle \(\pi/3+\pi/d\) excludes it.

For the constant prefix, the partial fractions of \(C/S\) give positive residues of sum two and, after the reciprocal substitution, integral coefficients of \(G_i=(1-u^2)C^\sharp/P\).
The exterior trace contributes \(t(\sigma^j+\sigma^{-j})\), and halving each interior weight between its conjugate circle lifts gives the remaining \(2\int u^j\,d\lambda_i\) at positive indices.
In the constant-prefix row the limiting measure of \(\lambda_i+t\delta_1\) has doubled density \(B_0=(4-x^2)(1+x^2)\), so its zero atom at one forces \(t\to0\) by positivity.
Reciprocity bounds the first nonzero intermediate coefficient index by \(m=k+1\), leaving an increasing distance to the reflected numerator at \(2k-2\).
At lag \(n-1\) the vanishing trace moment and \(\|\lambda_i\|\le2\) give \(t\sigma^n\le4\sigma\).
At lag \(n\) this bound also bounds the nonzero integer \(p_i\), since \(g_{i,n}=-2p_i\).

For \(z=u^{-1}\) the \(j\)-th Fourier coefficient of the projection weighted by \(u^n\) tests the neutral moment of order \(n+j\).
The exterior growing term tends to the extracted \(c=\lim t\sigma^n\) at every fixed offset, and the decreasing term tends to zero.
For every negative offset the trace moments vanish eventually, giving \(\widehat U(j)=-c\).
Fourier decay of the density \(U\), dominated by \(B_0\), forces \(c=0\).
Decay at the positive end and integrality of the limiting coefficients then make \(U\) an integer polynomial with constant \(-2p\).
Its bound by \(B_0\) gives zeros of order at least two at \(1\) and \(-1\).
Comparison at each fixed offset in \(G_i(1+u^nF_i)\), using \(G_i(0)=2\), gives convergent integer coefficients for \(F_i\) and \(U=-GF\).
The integral series \(F\) converges at either dyadic root of \(2+z^2\), of norm \(2^{-1/2}\).
Consequently \(2+z^2\) divides \(U\) in \(\mathbb Z[z]\), since it is irreducible and monic.
After both divisions the remaining integer polynomial \(V\) has nonzero constant \(-p\).
The mean-square bound uses \(|2+z^2|^2=1+2x^2\) and the mean of \(x^2\) equal to two.
The mean of \(1/(4(1+2x^2))\) is strictly less than \(1/4\), giving \(\int|V|^2<2\).
Parseval forces \(V=-p\) and \(p^2=1\).

At \(x=2\cos(l\pi/k)\), \(C(x)=(-1)^l(4+2\cos(2l\pi/k))\), so all \(k\) interlacer roots are strictly between \(-2\) and \(2\).
Pairing \(T-y_{j-1}>T-x_j\) in the product for \(t\) leaves \(t\ge2(T-2)/(T+2)\).
It follows that \(t\sigma^{j(n+2)}=O_j((T-2)^{1-j})\).
For the \(j\)-th projected moment with \(w=p u^{n+2}\), a backward difference of order \(2j\) kills the growing exterior term to order \(O_j(T-2)\) since \((\sigma-1)^2=\sigma(T-2)\).
It also sends the decreasing term to zero.
Thus \((1-z)^{2j}B_j\) has integer Fourier coefficients tending to zero at both ends.
The boundedness of \(B_j\) forces the resulting Laurent polynomial to be divisible by \((1-z)^{2j}\) in the integral Laurent ring.
Conjugation covers negative \(j\), and \(|B_j|\le B_0\) gives the two double zeros needed for division by \(|1-z^2|^2\).
The divided positive measure has finite marginal \(3+s+s^{-1}\), where \(s=z^2\), and its first row is \(2+s\) because \(B_1=p z^{-2}U\).

Weighting next by \(|1-w|^2\) gives marginal \(L_0=2+s+s^{-1}\), with double zeros at \(z=\pm i\).
Each Laurent row of this positive measure is bounded by \(L_0\), so division followed by Parseval gives either zero or a signed monomial.
The nonzero conditional moments have modulus one almost everywhere and thus correspond to powers of \(w\) constant on the fiber.
Their indices are closed under subtraction on a common full-measure set.
If only the zeroth moment survived, Haar conditional laws would make the recovered mass off \(w=1\) infinite.
For subgroup \(d\mathbb Z\), all Fourier coefficients give the uniform distribution on the \(d\) roots of \(w^d=W(z)\).
The weighted measure has no mass at \(w=1\), so \(W=1\) can occur only on a null set.
The mean of \((1-\overline w)^{-1}\) on these roots equals \((1-\overline W)^{-1}\), while the projected first difference divided by \(L_0\) equals \(1/(1+s)\).
Hence \(W=-s^{-1}\).
The squared modulus of \(\sum_{l=0}^{d-1}w^l\) has fiber mean \(d\) by orthogonality.
The recovered marginal off \(w=1\) is therefore \(dL_0/|1-W|^2=d\) almost everywhere.
Comparison with the whole marginal \(3+s+s^{-1}\), whose essential infimum is one, gives \(d=1\).

The two support equations \(w=1,-z^{-2}\) give \(u^{n+2}=p\) or \(u^n=-p\).
The product of the corresponding binomials and its reversal as a Laurent polynomial defines the claimed integral polynomial \(A_i(x)\).
For the test \(f=(2-x)^4A_i(x)^2\), the coefficient of \(x^{m-1}\) in the remainder of \(Cf\) modulo monic \(S\) is \(\sum c_jf(x_j)\) by interpolation.
This integer is strictly positive at every index because all summands are nonnegative and the exterior summand is positive.
On the circle \(|u^n+p|=|w+z^{-2}|\), and the neutral integrand in the fixed two-circle coordinates thus vanishes on both limiting graphs.
At the exterior root, \(Q_i(\sigma^{-1})\) is bounded and \(|Q_i(\sigma)|^2=O(\sigma^{4n+4})\).
The contribution is \(O((T-2)^4t^{-3})=O(T-2)\) using \(t\sigma^n=O(1)\) and the lower bound on \(t\).
Both parts tend to zero, contradicting the positive integer lower bound.
The reviewer finds the qualitative positive-sign gap and the common bound for both signs proved as submitted.
