### The integral gap and the gcd

In the complementary case the integral horizontal factors \(\Phi_q(X^aY^b)\), \(b>0\), have lowest \(Y\)-coefficient \(\pm1\).
Their product has a Laurent unit as its lowest row after a monomial shift, identifying the remaining base factor with the gcd including integer content.
