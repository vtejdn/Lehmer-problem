# Independent review of the gap after adjoining Salem trace-comparison radicals

## Signs over the totally real field and all bounded-measure targets

At 2026-11-25T03:31 UTC an agent outside the development of C0901 v137 independently reviewed for Yin its fixed [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v137.md#statement) and all three proof sections.
The reviewer received the fixed proof and its mathematical inputs without development discussion or previous findings on v137.
The reviewer developed the earlier small-conductor rank formula v129 and checked the scope of that comparison in this proof.
The bounds on compressed degree and the finite union of positive unit measures hold uniformly at fixed \(M,N\).
They imply the stated positive gap without a restriction on the second iterate.

The conjugates of each powered inverse-pair trace are still real, and the full admissible set of thresholds is stable under rational conjugation.
Either square root generates the same field, so the compositum \(W\) is rationally normal.
Each finite normal solvable subfield of \(V\) remains a solvable extension after base change to \(T\), and all the other generators are square roots of elements of \(T\).
Thus \(W\subseteq T^{\rm sol}\), also for an infinite set of generators.
If \(\sqrt d\in W\), \(d\in T^\times\), ordinary multiquadratic theory uses only finitely many radicands to make \(z=d/\prod a_j\) square over \(TV\).
Its square root already lies in \(TL\) with finite rational normal solvable \(L\).
Restriction identifies \(\operatorname{Gal}(TL/T)\) with \(\operatorname{Gal}(L/L\cap T)\).
The index-two subgroup for \(T(\sqrt z)\ne T\) therefore cuts out \((L\cap T)(\sqrt c)\subset L\) with \(c\in(L\cap T)^\times\).
Equality after base change gives \(z/c\in T^{\times2}\), while \(c=1\) suffices in the trivial case.
This proves the claimed sign identity at every rational embedding, since a nonzero square from \(T\) is positive at each one.

To apply v106 at any fixed target bound, take an external base consisting of \(V\) and the splitting fields of all possible sources of degree below the relevant cutoff.
The latter compositum is finite and rationally normal by the simultaneous degree and measure bounds.
All the remaining block permutation fields lie in \(V\) by hypothesis.
Any finite normal quotient in the external compositum has its nonabelian composition factors among those of the fixed finite splitting compositum, since its relative extension there is prosolvable.
The label group of a large target has composition factor \(A_{b_y}\) with \(b_y\to\infty\) uniformly.
Thus its label field is not contained in this external base at sufficiently large degree.
The elementwise obstruction in v106 gives every supported target alternating permutation over the compositum of all source splitting fields and \(V\) for an unmatched target.
Applying it with a given large source omitted gives the analogous lifts fixing every other source field and \(V\), because the sources are pairwise unmatched.
These conclusions hold over the entire possibly infinite composita, and the finite field used in the cutoff is independent of the chosen family.

In the marked source block, \(r=C_1\sqrt{(1+M+u(\log(2b)+4))/b}\) tends uniformly to zero by the polylogarithmic bound on \(u\).
With \(C_1\) sufficiently large, both losses of the block disk estimate together are at most \(r/(8\pi)\).
The disk at \(e^{3ir}\) avoids the real axis and contains at least \(2br/(8\pi)\) actual roots, tending to infinity.
These roots are neutral; the upper half-plane contains just one member of each inverse pair.
The angular deviation from \(3r\) is at most \(2\arcsin(r/2)<2r\).
Two different neutral labels therefore have \(0<\theta^2<\Delta_\alpha\) after the indicated absolute choice of \(C\).
The supported three-cycle through them and the marked label fixes all other label traces of this source, independently of any inversion in the lift.
On its three labels the powered traces all strictly exceed the threshold ceiling, at the neutral labels by \(2\cos(h\theta)\ge2-h^2\theta^2\) for every \(h\ge1\).
The lift fixes the other sources and \(V\), hence preserves the sign of every allowed radicand and every scalar \(c\) from the sign identity simultaneously.
It changes the sign of the marked trace discriminant \(t^2-4\); consequently its square root and the source \(\alpha\) do not belong to \(W\).
The conductor comparison follows since the maximum of the conjugates of a primitive conductor-\(p\) trace is \(2\cos(2\pi/p)\) and its distance from two is bounded below by an absolute positive multiple of \(1/p^2\).
Conversely a rational threshold between the largest neutral trace and two makes its trace difference and \(t^2-4\) have identical signs at every embedding.
The quotient then has a totally real square root, verifying the single-source recovery when that threshold is admitted.

For an arbitrary nontorsion unit \(w\in W\), rational normality contains its conjugate-class product \(y\), a unit of the same total measure with distinct classes of conjugates.
At large compressed degree, level zero of v108 applied to \(T\) gives \(P_y\subseteq T\), since \(y\in T^{\rm sol}\) and the relative block orbit has positive integer size.
Ordinary complex conjugation fixes each label.
If \(z\) is a nonreal root, its distinct conjugate \(\bar z\) in that label cannot have torsion quotient with \(z\); their product must be torsion.
As \(z\bar z>0\), it is one.
This makes the minimum reciprocal and leaves only real nonneutral roots; if all the roots were real instead, the real-conjugate bound already contradicts bounded measure at large degree.
For the reciprocal case the trace \(z+z^{-1}\) is totally real at every label.

In a match, transport a power relation to a conjugate of \(y\) of modulus greater than one.
Both it and the corresponding source root are real, and the torsion in the relation reduces to a rational sign.
All nonzero powers of either root have the full degree because distinct conjugates in each orbit have nontorsion quotients.
The common power up to sign identifies the two embedded real fields, which places the forbidden Salem source in the normal field \(W\).
It also identifies the compressed degrees, so bounded-degree sources cannot be matched at large target degree.
If \(y\) is unmatched, every actual square root of the discriminant of a target inverse pair is, up to sign, the difference of its two roots and thus in \(W\).
The sign identity for that discriminant is unchanged by any automorphism fixing all source fields and \(V\).
Applying the independent alternating actions on the target blocks shows that all labels of any block have the same sign.
There is a positive block by Kronecker, and it provides at least \(2b_y\) real conjugates, a fraction at least \(1/u_y\).
The quantitative real-conjugate inequality then gives \(m(y)>d/(100u_y^2\log(8u_y))\), tending to infinity at bounded \(N\) since \(u_y\ll_N(\log(3d))^{212}\).
This provides the required contradiction in both target cases.
Coefficient finiteness now bounds the union of the compressed unit measures at the chosen ceiling over all admissible families.
A positive total log measure below \(\log2\) forces a nonzero algebraic number to be a unit by the two extreme coefficients of its primitive minimum.
The unit conclusion therefore supplies the gap for all the nonzero algebraic numbers asserted.
