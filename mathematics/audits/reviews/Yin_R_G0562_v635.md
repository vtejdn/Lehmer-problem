## Independent review of the intrinsic bound

On 2026-11-20 at 09:11 UTC an agent for Sheng Yin independently reviewed all three mathematical sections of [v502](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v502.md#statement).
The reviewer had no part in developing the proof and received the fixed claim and review scope without the development discussion.
The intrinsic bound, its strict inverse, the numerical consequence and the asymptotic restriction on zero-infimum witnesses all follow under the stated hypotheses.

The two prime incidences give positive logarithmic measures with weighted average at most the row cost \(\eta\), including when both incidences use the same minimum.
Each degree is at most \(b\), and each measure is at most \(3\eta\le0.003<\log(1091/1000)\), since \(\log(1+x)\ge x/(1+x)\) for positive \(x\).
This verifies both the seed measure cutoff and \(H<Q^\Sigma\) at the calibrated width.
Coprimality gives a nonzero resultant, and the positive integer divisor of v500 gives \(\log|\operatorname{Res}(U,V)|\ge(1-1/b)\Gamma\).
Write \(h=\min(h_1,h_2)\le\eta\).
If the two minima are distinct, use \(d_1qh_2\) when \(h=h_1\), and \(d_2ph_1\) when \(h=h_2\).
In either choice, the crossed product is at least \(dph\), also in a tie, and \(3p\ge\Sigma\).
For a repeated minimum, subtracting \(2d\Sigma h\) from \((4dq-2(q-p))h\) leaves \(2(d-1)(q-p)h\ge0\).
Division by \(\log H=\eta\Sigma>0\) gives \(K\ge c_bdh/\eta\) uniformly at the eventual primes of v500.

The chosen root is a nonzero nontorsion unit of degree \(d\ge2\), and its log Mahler measure equals its absolute logarithmic height multiplied by \(d\).
I checked the hypotheses, constants and strict inequalities in the author's Theorem and Corollary 2 on pp. 3–4 of [Voutier's edition](../../literature/pdf/Vou96--arxiv-v1.pdf#page=3), using the published bounds at statement scope.
For \(t=\log d\), the corollary gives \(t>(2/h)^{1/3}-\log3>3\), because \(2000^{1/3}>12\) and \(\log3<2\).
For \(0<s\le10^{-3}\) the equation defining \(T_s\) has its unique large root above 3, by monotonicity of \(T/\log T\) above \(\mathrm e\) and \((4s)^{-1/3}\ge250^{1/3}>3/\log3\).
The theorem gives \(t/\log t>(4h)^{-1/3}\), hence \(d>\exp(T_h)\).
With \(s=(\log T/T)^3/4\), the logarithmic derivative of \(s\exp T\) is \(1-3/T+3/(T\log T)>0\) for \(T>3\).
Since \(T_h\ge T_\eta\), we obtain \(dh>h\exp(T_h)\ge\eta\exp(T_\eta)\).
Thus \(L=\log(K/c_b)>T_\eta\), and strict decrease of \((\log T/T)^3/4\) above \(\mathrm e\) gives the claimed strict lower bound on \(\eta\).

The three-term lower sums for \(\log 2+2\log 3\) total \(28039/9720>72/25\).
Consequently \(18/\log18<25/4\), whose cube is \(15625/64<250\), proving \(T_\eta>18\) throughout the closed upper endpoint of the cost range.
The degree-eight Taylor sum at 3 is \(89641/4480>20\), so \(\exp(18)>20^6=64{,}000{,}000\), and \(c_b\ge1\) from \(b=4\) onward.

As \(A=(4\eta)^{-1/3}\to\infty\), the large root tends to infinity and \(\log A/\log T_\eta=1-\log\log T_\eta/\log T_\eta\to1\).
Thus \(T_\eta=A\log T_\eta\sim 4^{-1/3}\eta^{-1/3}\log(1/\eta)/3\).
The bounded term \(\log c_b\to\log(4/3)\) contributes zero after normalization along \(p\to\infty\), yielding the asserted lower limit for every such sequence.
The equivalence in v501 supplies a vanishing-cost sequence conditional on the unrestricted infimum being zero, and the bound here constrains every possible witness.
Existence of such sequences and the value of the unrestricted infimum remain open.
