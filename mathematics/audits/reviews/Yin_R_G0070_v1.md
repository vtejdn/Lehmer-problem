  Zero would require zeta_j=1, and modulus one would require a_j=-1, hence zeta_j^2-zeta_j+1=0.
  Either contradicts the irreducible Salem minimal polynomial.

- In the norm expansion, an expanding conjugate contributes n log|u| plus an exponentially small logarithm, and a contracting conjugate contributes only such a logarithm.
