### The first coefficient pair

I checked the local degree normalization on p. 7 and Theorem 2.1 on p. 8 of the [retained Xiao preprint](../../literature/pdf/Xia24--arxiv-v3.pdf#page=7).

Normality gives the required coefficient in \(K\), and \(\sum_{v\mid\infty}d_v=1\).
The sum in Theorem 2.1 is exactly \(2\log B-\log|p-cq|-\log|q|\), with projective height \(\log B\) since each finite-place maximum is one.
If \(|p-cq|<B^{-1-\epsilon}\) then the sum is at least \((2+\epsilon)\log B\), absorbing the theorem's bounded term at excess \(\epsilon/2\) for sufficiently large \(B\).
