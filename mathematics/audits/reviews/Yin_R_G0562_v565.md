### Multiplicities and costs in both directions

At a counted point the first signed graph is \(\varepsilon V(X^n)(X-f(X^n))\), and its second counterpart has the same form with \(g,T,\sigma,n+k\).
All the local denominators are nonzero by the two graph values and the genuine numerator condition.
In the first composition \(Y=X^n\) is an unramified parameter and \(Yf(Y)^k=X^{n+k}+O((X-c)^t)\).
Holomorphy of \(g\) transfers this order to \(g(Yf(Y)^k)-f(Y)\).
The factor converting this difference to the forward polynomial is \(V(Y)^{kd+1}T(Yf(Y)^k)\), a local unit.
For the reverse polynomial the substitutions in the degree-\(b\) homogenizations of \(U,V\) are \(ZT(Z)^k,S(Z)^k\), with outside multipliers \(T(Z),-S(Z)\).
Its factor relative to \(f(Z/g(Z)^k)-g(Z)\) is \(T(Z)S(Z)^{kb}V(Z/g(Z)^k)\), also nonzero at the counted node.
The reverse argument is unramified because \(Z=X^{n+k}\) and \(c\ne0\).
Thus both multiplicity assertions hold for the full minimum of the two orders.

In the forward polynomial each homogeneous summand before the outside multiplier has degree at most \(d(kb+1)\) and one-norm at most \(H_A^{kd}\) times the absolute value of its scalar coefficient.
Both coefficient lists used in summation have total absolute value \(H_B\).
Adding the two terms and using the outside row norm gives \(2H_BH_A^{kd+1}\), with degree at most \(d(kb+1)+b\).
The reverse calculation gives \(2H_AH_B^{kb+1}\) and degree \(b(kd+1)+d\).
Zeros in a row or leading positions of a formal reversal do not alter these upper bounds.

### Exceptional rational identities

Write \(\beta=c^n,\ \zeta=c^{n+k}\).
Differentiation of the two local graph equations gives \(\beta f'(\beta)/f(\beta)=1/n\) and \(\zeta g'(\zeta)/g(\zeta)=1/(n+k)\).
Thus the logarithmic derivatives of \(F(Y)=Yf(Y)^k\) and \(J(Z)=Z/g(Z)^k\) at these nodes are respectively \((n+k)/n\) and \(n/(n+k)\), both nonzero.
Identical vanishing of either polynomial therefore gives a rational composition equal to the identity, since all the factors used to clear denominators are nonzero at the counted point.
Multiplicativity of the degrees of nonconstant rational maps forces \(F,J\) both to be Möbius maps over \(\mathbb Q\).

For \(F(Y)=(pY+q)/(rY+s)\) with \(ps-qr\ne0\), the logarithmic-derivative equation puts \(\beta\) in the zero set of
\[
             n(ps-qr)Y-(n+k)(pY+q)(rY+s).
\]
An identity would force \(pr=qs=0\), so \(F\) would be a constant multiple of \(Y\) or \(Y^{-1}\), both excluded by the prescribed logarithmic derivative.
This polynomial has degree at most two, hence \(c=f(\beta)\) has degree at most two as well.
A rational unit is \(1\) or \(-1\).
A nonreal quadratic unit has norm \(|c|^2=1\) and is torsion.
A real quadratic nontorsion unit has a real conjugate of modulus greater than one, at which all equations still hold.

Since \(f(1/Y)=1/f(Y)\), \(F\) commutes with inversion and permutes \(1,-1\).
Conjugating by \((Y-1)/(Y+1)\) therefore gives \(u\mapsto au\) or \(u\mapsto a/u\).
Taking the logarithmic differential gives the two possible signs in the claimed identity for \(F'(Y)/(F(Y)^2-1)\).
At the expanding real conjugate neither \(\beta\) nor \(F(\beta)=\zeta\) is \(0,1,-1\).
The absolute logarithmic derivative is \(|\zeta-\zeta^{-1}|/|\beta-\beta^{-1}|\), valid for negative conjugates as well.
Strict increase of \(\sinh(x)/x\) on \(x>0\) makes this ratio greater than \((n+k)/n\).
This contradiction excludes either identity for every counted pair.

### Distinct factors, exact recovery and the example

If two powered nodes belong to the same Galois orbit, transport them to the same \(\beta\).
The value \(c=f(\beta)\) is then the same, and \(c^n=c^{n'}\) forces \(n=n'\) since \(c\) is nontorsion.
The same proof with \(g\) applies to the reverse nodes.
The equality of the powered and original fields follows from the value equation in each case.
Degree times absolute height consequently gives logarithmic measures \(nm_c\) and \((n+k)m_c\).
The repeated irreducible factors for the distinct pairs have these weights with multiplicity at least \(t\).
Remaining integral factors of each nonzero elimination have Mahler measure at least one.
This proves both measure inequalities and the degree bound, also when the counted set is empty.

For each repeated monic irreducible noncyclotomic unit factor of the forward polynomial the candidate \(c=f(\beta)\) and then \(n=c/(\beta f'(\beta))\) are forced.
Testing the nonzero values, exact power equality, integer exponent and both value and first-derivative conditions in the factor field exactly recovers the double zeros.
If \(c^n=\beta\) is a unit, integrality of \(c\) and \(c^{-1}\) follows from the monic equations with constant terms \(-\beta\) and \(-\beta^{-1}\).
Noncyclotomicity of \(\beta\) excludes torsion of \(c\), and the two original graph polynomials give the exact vanishing orders.
A zero forward polynomial permits an immediate empty answer by the identity argument.
In the square specialization field equality preserves the degree while \(m(c^2)=2m_c>m_c\).
The two distinct orbits give at least \(4D_c\) and \(6nm_c\) in the indicated sums.
All bounds concern two fixed rows.
