## First conductor, unique completion and whole measure

I found exactly one strict completion through coefficient index four at the 152 conductor and cut pairs for odd \(7\le p\le37\).
It occurs at \(p=37,\ c=17\) and has the vector given in J155 v2.
The integer exclusions include unbounded initial choices of coefficient height for the following reason.
Writing \(\nu_j=4\sin^2(2\pi j/p)/p\), the cyclic sine orthogonality formula gives \(\sum_j\nu_j E_i(b_j)E_k(b_j)=\delta_{ik}\) for \(0\le i,k<n\).
The sum on the half grid is half that on the full nonzero grid, and the positive sine frequencies \(i+1,k+1\) lie in \(1,\ldots,n\), so distinct ones never coincide up to sign modulo \(p\).
It follows that \(a_i=\sum_j\rho_j E_i(b_j)\).
In particular \(\sum\rho_j=2\) and \(\sum b_j\rho_j=0\).
At the arithmetic midpoint \(u\) of a strict cut all terms \((b_j-u)\rho_j\) are positive, with sum \(-2u<4\).
Writing \(\Delta=b_c-b_{c+1}\), this yields \(\sum_j|\rho_j|<8/\Delta\) and \(|a_i|<8(i+1)/\Delta\).
The sine chord bound on both gap factors gives \(\Delta\ge32/p^2\), since \(\min(2c+1,p-2c-1)\ge2\).
This proves the native search box for every strict solution.
