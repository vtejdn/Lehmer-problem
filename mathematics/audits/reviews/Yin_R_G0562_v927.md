# Marginal entropy loss and joint concentration for masked line records

## Independent whole-proof review

At 2026-11-21T06:56 UTC an agent working for Sheng Yin independently reviewed [C345 v786](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v786.md#statement) and recommends acceptance of (1)–(4) and their stated consequences.
The reviewer took no part in developing the proof and received the fixed version and the requested scope without development discussion or author explanations.
The reviewer consulted R562 v925 for its declaration format and checked the mathematical inputs directly.

On a masked line, any occupied subset of a block of length at most \(d\) is independent over \(\mathbb Q\), since a relation divided by the lowest power would have degree less than \(d\).
For integer columns of full column rank every nonzero character of the target torus pulls back to a nonzero character, so the joint phase of those columns has product Haar law.
The collision integral on one line uses the squared absolute characteristic function.
Its block with \(b\) occupied positions contributes \(I_k^{b/k}\) to the Hölder bound, including \(b=0\) and the shorter final block.
The recurrence \(I_{k+1}/I_k=(2k+1)/(2k+2)\) gives \(I_k\le(k+1)^{-1/2}\) from \(I_0=1\).
Jensen's inequality gives \(H(Y_\ell)\ge-\log\sum_y\mathbb P(Y_\ell=y)^2\).
Summing the occupied positions counts every allowed bit \(J\) times, while \(H(Y)\le |S|\log2\) counts its input entropy once.
This proves both bounds in (1), including the \(\log J\) lower bound on the excess per allowed bit for \(k\asymp J\).
A positive difference requires \(k>J\log(k+1)/(2\log2)\), which first gives \(k>J/2\) and then \(L>(k-1)d\gg Jd\log(2J)\) as \(J\to\infty\).

Distinct coefficient vectors label disjoint unit cubes up to boundaries, so smoothing gives precisely the Shannon entropy even when several bit patterns give the same vector.
The real embedding map has absolute determinant \(2^{-r_2}\sqrt\Delta\) for the power basis of the order \(\mathbb Z[\alpha]\).
Independence gives the variance bound by adding the \(s_\ell\) bit variances and the \(d\) cube-coordinate variances, with the same factor \(\max(1,|\beta|)^{2(L-1)}\) at each real coordinate of an embedding.
The Gaussian entropy bound counts complex places twice in \((L-1)m\).
Using \(s_\ell\le L,\ d\le L,\ r_2\le d/2\) gives the stated factor \(4\pi\mathrm e L/(3\Delta^{1/d})\) inside the logarithm.
There are \(Jn/L\) lines, so the measure contribution is at most \(Jmn\).
For \(x\ge A_\kappa T\), one has \(T/x\le1/A_\kappa\) and the supremum of \((\log(4\pi\mathrm e/3)+\log x)/x\) over \(x\ge A_\kappa\) tends to zero as \(A_\kappa\to\infty\).
Thus the separate entropy subtraction itself leaves at least \(\kappa n/2\).
For the uniform input its conditional entropy given the record is the average logarithmic fiber size, which proves (2).
Under \(\Delta^{1/d}\ge c_0d\) the discriminant term is bounded solely in terms of \(c_0\), giving the claimed order for fixed \(\kappa,c_0\).

For a fixed layer \(h\), take a rational dependence and extend its coefficients by zero off the layer.
At a cell with \(q\) coordinates in block \(h\), vary one of those coordinates.
Earlier blocks on the resulting line lie outside the layer.
Later blocks also lie outside when \(q=1\), and have \(q-1\) such coordinates in the layer when \(q>1\).
Induction therefore leaves a relation supported inside one block of at most \(d\) powers, forcing the coefficient at the chosen cell to vanish.
This proves independence within each layer for every choice of orders, including a short block in any position and arbitrary deletion by the mask.
For each fixed allowed cell its block ranks in the \(J\) coordinate orders are independent uniform integers from \(1\) to \(k\), regardless of the block lengths.
Taking expectations gives the claimed number of retained cells for at least one set of orders.
In the joint Fourier inversion bound all factors have modulus at most one.
Hölder over the \(t\) retained layers uses independence only within each layer and gives the exponent \(1/t\) for each retained column, proving (3).

For \(t=\lfloor z\rfloor\) and \(z\ge2\) the covered fraction is at least \(1-\exp(-t/z)\ge1-\exp(-1/2)\).
Writing \(b=\lfloor t/2\rfloor\ge1\), we have \(a_t\le I_b\le(1+b)^{-1/2}\) and \(1+z<2b+3\le(1+b)^3\).
Consequently (4) holds, for example with \(c=(1-\exp(-1/2))/6\), since \(1/t\ge1/z\) and \(|\mathcal F_y|=2^{|S|}\mathbb P(Y=y)\).
The resulting compulsory deficit per allowed bit tends to zero with \(\log(1+z)/z\).
It bounds every common fiber from above and supplies no corresponding lower construction.
The earlier dimension bound uses \(n=L^J\) as its denominator and implies \(L\gg Jd\) at fixed positive rate on that normalization.
The positive-rate existence question on every fixed-density mask at \(L\asymp Jd,\ J\asymp1/m\) remains open in this argument.
