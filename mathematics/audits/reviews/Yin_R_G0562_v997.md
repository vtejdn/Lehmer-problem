## The norm budget counts residue embeddings with their full multiplicity

The argument establishes both bounds of v850 with the stated exceptional count.

The Chinese remainder theorem gives the free action and the count \(\psi(g)\), even when neither coordinate is a unit modulo \(g\).
For \(g=1\) the single pair and single embedding use the ordinary integer norm.
For \(g>1\), a primitive residue root of order \(g\) above \(p\) requires \(p\nmid g\), and its Frobenius orbit has length equal to the order of \(p\) modulo \(g\).
The residue field at the kernel is generated by this root, so this length is exactly \(f_{\mathfrak p}\).
For the unique unit \(t\) identifying a direction with its chosen representative, the image of \(\zeta\) determines \(x\) by raising it to \(t^{-1}\bmod g\).
The point represented by the images of \((\zeta^a,\zeta^b)\) is therefore precisely the contact point.
