# Review of the small-measure spectrum of complex Salem numbers

## Finding on the arithmetic spectrum and the geometric equivalence

At 2026-11-16T23:00 UTC a separate reviewer for Yin checked all three mathematical sections of [C345v72](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v72.md#statement), submitted at 22:57 UTC, and their declared inputs.
The reviewer did not develop this argument and received the fixed proof and its scope without proof-generation dialogue, a prior verdict or a requested conclusion.
The equality of irreducible spectra, the degree cost, and the two gap equivalences pass.

For the interval one may take its upper endpoint below the minimum of 2, [Smyth's nonreciprocal bound](../literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-u001), the exponential of the positive [three-exterior logarithmic gap](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v65.md#statement), and the first positive multiplicative measure above one in degrees at most four.
The last exclusion needs only the finite set of monic polynomials of those degrees and measure at most two, by the elementary symmetric coefficient bound.
A polynomial in the interval has leading coefficient of modulus one, and a nonreciprocal irreducible with nonzero constant is excluded by Smyth's bound.
In [torsion-class compression](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v61.md#fibers) each class has the same size \(f\), each new exterior logarithm equals \(f\) times its old value, and the product of exterior moduli is preserved.
Thus the rank-one hypothesis survives and the exclusions apply to the compressed degree and exterior count.
With a single exterior root, sign change gives a positive real exterior root and the degree cutoff guarantees neutral conjugates.
With two real exterior roots, a rational log ratio gives equality of positive even powers, and their equal positive absolute heights make the two exponents equal.
This contradicts compression, as in the [residual signature test](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v65.md#reduction).
In the remaining case the two exterior roots form a nonreal conjugate pair, their inverses give the only two interior roots, and degree greater than four again supplies a neutral conjugate.

For a real Salem number \(\alpha\), the field \(\mathbb Q(\alpha)\) in its positive embedding lies in \(\mathbb R\), so \(-\alpha\) is not a square there.
Since \(-\beta^2=\alpha\) for \(\beta=i\sqrt\alpha\), the tower degree is exactly \(2\deg\alpha\), proving irreducibility of the monic normalized substitution.
The two roots over each original conjugate \(u\) have modulus \(\sqrt{|u|}\) and product \(u\), so their contribution to the measure is \(\max(1,|u|)\).
Only the pair over \(\alpha\) is exterior, and the neutral conjugates of \(\alpha\) give neutral lifts.
The quotient \(-1\) of the exterior pair is allowed by the definition.
These verifications give the same measure at cost at most twice the original degree, and every complex Salem minimal polynomial already has commensurable exterior logarithms.

For a reducible input of measure in the interval, multiplicativity and the lower bound one for the measures of its primitive integer factors put each factor of measure greater than one in that same interval, with content of modulus one.
Every such factor inherits the rational commensurability property.
Replacing them individually with their multiplicities and omitting factors of measure one preserves the product measure and the degree bound.
If products tend to one from above, one may choose a factor from each product whose measure lies strictly above one and is bounded by the product measure.
The converse implication uses a single complex Salem minimal polynomial per input.

The reviewer read §§2.2–2.3, pages 4–5 of the [retained Pham–Thilmany arXiv v2 edition](../literature/PT21/readme.md#arxiv-v2), and checked the definition, the substitution and the equivalence on the rendered page 5 against [PT21-U001](../literature/PT21/Yin_U001.md#pt21-u001).
Conjecture (G) bounds \(|\beta|\) away from one, which is equivalent to a positive gap for \(M(\beta)=|\beta|^2\).
The source identifies (G) with the three-dimensional arithmetic hyperbolic orbifold short-geodesic conjecture.
The geometric input was verified at the statement and application level.
Its original proof and the truth of either equivalent conjecture lie outside this finding.
