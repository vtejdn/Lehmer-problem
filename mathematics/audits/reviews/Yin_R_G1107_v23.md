### Exact existence and the complete polynomial interpretation

The mean-value bound on the convex box makes \(G(\theta)=\theta-A\Phi(\theta)\) a contraction with constant at most \(q<1\).
Its displacement from the center is at most \(\delta+rq<r\), so it maps the closed box into itself.

The defect bound also makes \(A\Phi'(\widehat\theta)\) nonsingular: a nonzero null vector would violate \(\|I-A\Phi'(\widehat\theta)\|<1\).
Since the matrices are square, \(A\) is nonsingular, and therefore \(\Phi(\theta_*)=0\).
This checks the necessary step from a preconditioned fixed point to all seven original equations.

The 9840 roots lie in disjoint open cells and are distinct.
Adding \((t-s)^2\) gives a real monic polynomial of degree 9842; a possible coincidence of \(s\) with a selected root merely retains the appropriate multiplicity.

### Endpoint value, original constraints and finite limits

Counting the signs in \(Q_*(-2)\) and \(B(-2)\), and cancelling every paired pole, gives exactly
\[
 -\frac{Q_*(-2)}{B(-2)}
 =\frac{(2+s)^2}{2+c}
       \prod_{i\ne1991}\frac{2+u_i}{2+x_i}.
\]
All factors are positive.
Taking logarithms produces the displayed integral of \(w/(2+t)\) with its negative sign.
The first selected root is strictly greater than \(-2\), so the integral is finite.

At the exact solution, the already verified polynomial part \(t+2\) makes this ratio equal to the original residue functional \(\sum r_j/(2+b_j)\).
Away from that solution the same product is the natural endpoint ratio of the parameterized polynomial; it need not be identified with that residue functional to enclose its value at the solution.
The native endpoint calculation covers the entire same box, including \(\theta_*\), and its positive-argument assertions justify every logarithm.

The default returned the printed enclosures
\[
 \log U\in[-3.798\pm2.68\cdot10^{-4}],
 \qquad U\in[0.02241\pm8.70\cdot10^{-6}],
\]
and directly proved \(0.022<U<0.023\).
The independent reconstruction multiplied all 9840 positive ratios directly, without using logarithms, and obtained the same printed \(U\) enclosure and strict coarse bounds.
Thus endpoint failure is proved for this certified configuration, rather than inferred from a small numerical residual or a center evaluation.

The nonintegral rational root \(s\) independently prevents \(Q_*\) from being monic integral.
The required resultant or norm has not been imposed, and the original endpoint value is explicitly false.
The result establishes simultaneous compatibility of seven polynomial moments and strict terminal-interval geometry for this one real configuration only.
It gives no exclusion or existence statement for another configuration with \(U=1\), for the other four residual triples, or for the original integer branch.
