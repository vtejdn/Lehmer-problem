## Conditional parent law and implementation

The finding is **PASS for the stated conditional class, exact sampling law and repaired implementation**.
The supplied core must remain monic reciprocal of positive even degree, have constant coefficient one, no cyclotomic factor, exponent gcd one and the specified sign normalization.
The constructor checks the structural and sign interfaces but does not certify the absence of cyclotomic factors.
Irreducibility is unnecessary for this conditional argument.

For an even-degree reciprocal cyclotomic product with constant coefficient one, the multiplicity of the factor at \(1\) is even.
The other nonreal cyclotomic factors have even degree, so the multiplicity at \(-1\) is even as well.
Thus the two squared real factors and the irreducible \(\Phi_m\), \(m\ge3\), are independent multiset types with no missing even-degree products.
The elementary prime-power calculation underlying \(\varphi(m)^2\ge m/2\) gives the stated complete finite order bound.
The constant product is handled separately at neutral degree zero.
No intermediate height pruning is present.

The substituted core \(F(\varepsilon z^k)\) has no root of unity, since such a root would map to a root of unity of \(F\).
Removal of every cyclotomic factor therefore recovers exactly the substituted core from its parent.
Its nonzero exponent gcd is \(k\), so two equal parents first have the same substitution index.
After compression, exponent gcd one ensures a nonzero odd coefficient and hence distinguishes \(F(z)\) from \(F(-z)\).
Cancellation and unique factorization then recover the same neutral multiset.
This establishes the claimed bijection without irreducibility or a duplicate correction.

The suffix recurrence counts each multiset once by the multiplicity of its first available factor.
The implementation's ascending residual-degree recurrence, unranking blocks and inverse ranks agree with that partition.
The sieve is bounded before allocation, and the table has the stated number of integer cells.
Factor polynomials are constructed only when used by an attempt.
These are cell-count and implementation statements, with no claim that an integer cell has constant bit size or that rejection costs are bounded uniformly over parameters.

Each raw parent has probability \(N(r_k)/W\cdot1/2\cdot1/N(r_k)=1/(2W)\) under independent uniform integer choices.

It is independent of the current fresh random choice even when earlier construction costs or the factor cache depend on those past outcomes.
The equal summands \(\Pr(B_i)/(2W)\) therefore give the same return probability for every accepted parent.
Conditional uniformity requires a positive probability of returning and a normally completed call under the documented callback contract.
