## The cofactor supplies more than one fortieth of the signed binomial

All three assertions hold with the stated degree and measure hypotheses.
For the companion matrix \(B\), multiplication by \(sX^N-1\) on \(\mathbb F_p[X]/(\bar P)\) has nullity \(k=\deg\gcd(\bar P,X^N-s)\), even when \(\bar P\) has repeated factors.
Each auxiliary factor vanishing at one modulo \(p\) therefore has at least \(k\) Smith entries divisible by \(p\) upon evaluation at \(sB^N\).
Its determinant is nonzero since \(P\) has no root of unity.
Taking products gives the exponents \(5k,2k,k\) in the respective characteristics.
The degree-six auxiliary polynomial has circle norm \(16/(3\sqrt3)\) by maximizing \(8v^2\sqrt{1-v^2}\) on \([0,1]\).
The degree-three and linear polynomials each have circle norm two.
Bounding the determinant by the product of the scalar bounds at the conjugates gives all three inequalities with the actual \(k\), independently of any integral representation of \(PC\).
I checked all three strict power comparisons by integer arithmetic after clearing denominators, including \(p=5\), the weakest case among the larger primes.
Thus \(N-k>N/40\) also for \(d=2N\).
At each irreducible residue factor the exponent still needed by the binomial must occur in \(\bar C\), giving the asserted lower bound on the gcd.
For the record application, the source identifies the positive exterior root of the degree-ten competitor as its measure, and direct substitution gives \(16^{10}L(19/16)=330753263801>0\) with \(L(1)=-1\). [Mossinghoff, Rhin and Wu, p. 2](../../literature/pdf/MRW08--author.pdf#page=2)

For \(p\nmid m\), Frobenius in the cyclotomic quotient gives exponent \(p^a-p^{a-1}\) for \(a\ge1\), while \(a=0\) gives exponent one.
The \(\varphi(m)\) primitive roots of each such order are simple and disjoint from all other orders.
Removing the \(p\)-part of \(N=p^e M\) leaves multiplicity \(p^e\) throughout the signed binomial.
For the positive sign the eligible orders are all divisors of \(M\).
For odd \(p\), a root satisfies \(z^M=-1\) exactly when its order divides \(2M\) and does not divide \(M\).
In characteristic two the signs coincide.
Taking the smaller multiplicity order by order gives the displayed sum, including \(e=0\).

For a compact connected oriented fiber with all boundary circles capped, the relative homology sequence identifies the kernel on \(H_1\) with the free module on the capping disks modulo the image of the closed fundamental class, the invariant sum of the disks.
An orientation-preserving monodromy permutes their induced orientations, giving the stated kernel polynomial and the factorization of the open polynomial as \(PC\).
A nonempty intersection of the root sets for \(X^{h_i}-1\) and \(X^N-s\) is a coset of a cyclic group whose cardinality is the prime-to-\(p\) part of \(\gcd(h_i,N)\).
Restoring the common multiplicity gives gcd degree \(\gcd(h_i,N)\) in this case and zero for an empty intersection.
The gcd degree of a product is subadditive at every irreducible factor, so some \(\gcd(h_i,N)>N/(40t)\).
Taking \(q=N/\gcd(h_i,N)\) gives the claimed divisor and strict bound.
The realization statement assumes that the open polynomial contains the signed binomial modulo \(p\) and that the closed polynomial satisfies the initial hypotheses.
