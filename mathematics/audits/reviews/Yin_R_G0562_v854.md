The inspection checked the dyadic classification, every selected twist and power, both finite and asymptotic estimates, and the field and measure in the unrestricted application.

The nested intersections with \(K\) can increase strictly only when their degrees increase, so their union is attained.
At \(A=1,2\) minimality gives the full level.
At \(A\ge3\) the kernel of descent by one level is generated by \(u=1+2^{A-1}\), and minimality excludes \(u\) from \(H\).
Writing a unit as \(\pm5^i\), any element of order at least four has a power equal to the unique involution in \(\langle5\rangle\), namely \(u\).
Thus \(H\) is inside the four-element group of elements of order dividing two and has at most two members.
In the order-two case its nonidentity member is \(-1\) or \(-u\), and its difference from 1 has 2-adic valuation one.
The action on \(\mu_w\) therefore fixes just \(1,-1\).
Restriction from \(KE/K\) has fixed field \(K\cap E\) in \(E\), identifying its group with \(H\), including when \(K/\mathbb Q\) is nonnormal.

For a twist of order \(t\), the \(t\)-th power of \(\zeta\alpha^b\) generates \(K\), and division by \(\alpha^b\) then recovers \(\zeta\).
Thus for \(h=1\) every twist generates \(K=L\), and for \(h=2\) every nonfixed twist generates the quadratic extension \(L/K\).
An embedding identifying two twists at level \(b\) fixes \(\alpha^{bw}\), a generator of \(K\), hence belongs to the indicated subgroup on \(L\).
This gives \(w=2s\) distinct minimal polynomials in the first case and \((w-2)/2=2s-1\) in the second.
Extending the embeddings of \(K\) to \(L\) yields total logarithmic measure \(hbm\), which separates all levels because \(m>0\).
These distinctions give the pairwise coprime polynomials required by the packing argument.
The proof does not require multiplicative independence of the algebraic numbers being twisted.

In characteristic two every dyadic root of unity reduces to one, and iterating Frobenius permutes the reduced root multiset of \(P\) with its multiplicities.
Each embedding of \(K\) occurs precisely \(h\) times in the common field of the selected twists.
The resulting common residue polynomial is exactly \(\overline P^h\), even if \(\overline P\) has repeated roots.
For \(v=[E:\mathbb Q]=sh\), the derivative discriminant of \(X^v+1\) is \(v^v\) in absolute value at \(A\ge2\).
No odd prime divides the index of this order.
At two the shifted Eisenstein generator \(\pi\) has valuation \(1/v\), and terms \(a_i\pi^i\) in the power basis have distinct fractional valuations whenever nonzero.
An integral sum therefore has only integral terms, forcing \(v_2(a_i)\ge0\) because \(0\le i<v\).
This verifies the local maximality and root discriminant \(v\), with \(A=1\) giving root discriminant one directly.
The field discriminant in the tower \(L/E/\mathbb Q\) and the power-basis index for each integral generator yield \(\log|\operatorname{disc} P_i|\ge hd\log(sh)\).
In v707's inequality divide by total degree \(kJhd\), use these \(kJ\) discriminants, and sum the measures \(kh(2^J-1)m\).
The term \(\log(kJhd)-\log(sh)=\log((k/s)Jd)\) and the measure coefficient \(2-2/(kJhd)\) give (1).

Write \(X=\log(1/m)\) and \(J=\lfloor X/\log2\rfloor\) on a shrinking sequence.
Then \(J\log2>X-\log2\), \(2^Jm\le1\), and the right-side errors beyond \(\log d\) in the estimate for \(kX\) are \(O(\log(2J)+k)\).
Their ratio to \(kX\) tends to zero uniformly in \(k\ge1\).
It follows that \(d\to\infty\), \(\limsup kX/\log d\le1\), and \(s/\log d\to0\) since \(s\le k\) and \(X\to\infty\).
For \(s\ge\delta\log(2d)\), the weaker \(\log d\le k/\delta\) suffices.
Choosing \(J\log2\ge1+\delta^{-1}+\log(4J)\) makes the left side of (1) minus its logarithmic term at least \(k+(k-1)\log(4J)\ge k\).
The remaining multiplier of \(m\) is at most \(4k(2^J-1)\), giving the claimed effective gap for each fixed \(\delta>0\).
