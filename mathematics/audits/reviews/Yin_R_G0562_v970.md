## Independent proof finding

The fixed-cover conclusions follow for every positive integer \(k\).
If a positive \(u\in H_j\) has \(u^e=\beta_j\), then \(u\) and its inverse are integral, \(e\) is odd and the other embedding sends \(u\) to \(-u^{-1}\).
Hence \(u-u^{-1}\) is a positive integer and \(u\ge\phi\).
The inequalities \(\beta_3<\phi^3=\beta_4\) give the asserted possibilities for \(e\), including the realized cube root \(\phi\) of \(\beta_4\).
For a positive \(k\)-th root \(c\), the product of its \(d\) conjugates over the real field has absolute value \(c^d\) in that field.
Consequently \(c^{\gcd(k,d)}\) is a positive radical there of exponent \(k/\gcd(k,d)\).
This forces \(d=k\) in the indicated irreducible cases and gives degree \(h\) over \(H_4\) for the positive \(h\)-th root of \(\phi\).

For the remaining orbit with \(k=3h\), the relative norm of the nonzero constant coefficient from \(E=H_4(\zeta_3)\) is positive at both real embeddings and equals \(\phi^{2d/h}\) at the chosen embedding.
Writing \(2d/h=p/q\) in lowest terms, Bézout gives \(\phi^{1/q}\in H_4\), so \(q=1\), and total positivity then forces \(p\) even.
Since \(1\le d\le h\), this gives \(d=h\).
The root recovers \(H_4\) from its \(3h\)-th power and \(\zeta_3\) from its \(h\)-th power, so its rational degree is \(4h\).
Its exterior centers are exactly the \(2h\) roots with \(h\)-th power \(\phi\zeta_3^{\pm1}\), none real.

On the fixed cover the simple zero of the original short coordinate at \((0,i)\) gives the Eisenstein extension used in v790.
The quadratic coefficients are coprime, the support has dimension two, and the leading and constant endpoint scalars are one.
The two irreducible quadratics have disjoint simple nonzero roots, hence their horizontal pullbacks give a squarefree face.
For an orbit with \(t\) exterior centers of common modulus \(r>1\), its measure is \(t\log r\).
The theorem in v789 gives \((l/\deg S)c\log r=t\log r/2+o_k(1)\) uniformly over the monic factors, all noncyclotomic by v793.
Thus the finitely many candidate fractions \(c/t\) for a given factor coincide across orbits once \(l\) is large enough for this \(k\).
Each is positive, and when \(k=3h\) the counts on the three orbits are consequently \((3a,a,2a)\).
For fixed \(k\) the localized roots converge uniformly to their centers, one per disk and with no other exterior roots, giving the claimed factor measure by summing their logarithms.
The \(k\) disks above \(\beta_3\) supply at most \(k/g\) factors counted with multiplicity.
For \(k=3\) the bound is one and v793 gives six exterior roots, two real, and \(2l\) circle roots, also when \(3\mid l\).

For \(n\) equally spaced roots of a positive real number, complex conjugation fixes one center for odd \(n\) and two for even \(n\).
Pairs realize every even count up to twice the number of pairs, one fixed center gives the intervening odd counts, and the full set also supplies the largest count for even \(n\).
This gives every prescribed size on the unsplit orbits and any \(a\) on the orbit over \(\phi\).
Selecting \(a\) pairs among the \(h\) pairs of the other orbit supplies its required \(2a\) centers.
Complementation gives the simultaneous second allocation whenever the first is proper.
This verifies the formal splitting assertion at \(b=g\) for \(k/g>1\), with factorization in those cases left open by these equations.
