## Pole weights and the first failing coefficients

Fix \(-4=t_0<\cdots<t_{n-1}=0\), \(M=q(q+1)\), \(h=1/M\), and an integer \(K>0\).
For every integer \(q\ge2\) there is at most one monic integral polynomial \(H\) of degree \(n\) with \((-1)^{n-i}H(t_i)>0\) for all \(i\) and \(M^nH(h)=K\).
The argument gives an exact decision and reconstruction procedure on rational grids.

Indeed, for \(Q=\prod_i(x-t_i)\), the signs give \(r_i=-H(t_i)/Q'(t_i)>0\).
Evaluating \(H/Q\) at \(h\) fixes the mass of the weights \(b_i=r_i/(h-t_i)\) at \(D=1-K/\prod_i(1-Mt_i)\).
Necessarily \(0<D<1\).
Let \(\beta_j=\sum_i b_it_i^j\) and \(\mu_j=\sum_i r_it_i^j\).
At coefficient \(c_j=[x^{n-j}]H\), the quotient expansion gives \(\mu_{j-1}=a-c_j\), where the previous coefficients determine \(a\).
The identity \(\beta_j=h\beta_{j-1}-\mu_{j-1}\) then gives coefficient \(+1\) on \(c_j\) in the weighted sum of the monic shifted Chebyshev polynomial \(2T_j((x+2)/2)\).
Its bound by 2 on the grid gives a closed interval of width \(4D<4<M\).
The pole equation fixes the residue modulo \(M\) at each step, and fixes the last coefficient exactly.
The program's endpoint rounding includes both endpoints of the weighted interval, and its final sign tests distinguish an actual solution from a vector surviving the necessary bounds.

For a signed pole value the same interval has width \(4D\), which can exceed \(M\).
With a prescribed upper bound \(2/q\) on the positive root, the original coefficient bounds still give uniqueness for \(q\ge4\).
For smaller \(q\) the interval intersection can decide individual signed cases.
Existence of an interlacing grid for a given trace polynomial supplies no common grid for different polynomials.

I independently evaluated the 200 deformations using the first three power sums of their nodes for the coefficients of \(Q\).
The weighted tests for \(c_2,c_3\) use \(x^2+4x+2\) and \(x^3+6x^2+9x+2\), respectively, together with the original strict moment bounds and the successive pole residues.
At \(q=5,6,40\) the numbers first failing at \((c_2,c_3)\) were \((99,8),(69,17),(7,0)\), respectively.
These exclusions cover exactly the listed deformations.
Running the supplied program reproduced the two control vectors in both modes.
As additional positive-pole checks at \(q=2,3\), the five-node control grid recovered the same degree-five polynomial for \(K=55399,623221\), respectively.
