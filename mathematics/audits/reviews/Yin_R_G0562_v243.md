## The optimized period and the rational bound for even \(l\)

At 2026-11-17T13:26 UTC an agent for Yin independently reviewed the [v243 submission](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v243.md#decision-reciprocal-two-prime-binary-obstruction-submitted), including the explicit even-\(l\) qualification on (3).
The reviewer had previously reviewed the local order calculation in v235 and the one-prime comparison in v239, took no part in developing this construction, and received its fixed text and scope without development discussion or previous findings on v243.
The modular construction and Hensel lift apply at all \(l\), and the stated rational bounds hold under the even-\(l\) hypothesis used in their proof.

The parent reduces to \(X^{2n}+X^n+1\), with \(n\) prime to six.
Dividing the separable \(X^{3n}-1\) by \(X^n-1\) therefore gives exactly the simple roots of orders \(3t,\ t\mid n\).
For odd \(3t\), reduction of \(\Phi_{3t}\) gives every primitive root of that order once, as follows by factoring \(X^{3t}-1\) in characteristic two and using induction over divisors.
All selected prime exponents fit in \(n\).
The inequality \(2\log_75>1\) makes the integer parts \(b_a\) decrease strictly between consecutive even indices, while the five-exponents increase.
Hence all selected orders are pairwise incomparable.
The floor also gives \(L/7<t_a\le L\) and \(L^2/7<n\le L^2\).
There are \(\lfloor A/2\rfloor+1\) selected orders, each contributing \(2\varphi(t_a)\asymp L\) with absolute constants, so \(d\asymp AL\asymp A\sqrt n\).

Squarefreeness makes the gcd count the whole totient of each order dividing \(D\) exactly once, including for even \(D\).
If the extreme represented indices in this gcd are \(a\le a'\), their lcm is \(3t_a5^{a'-a}\), so \(D>(3L/7)5^{a'-a}\).
This also handles a single represented order.
It bounds the number of covered indices by \(1+\log_5(7D/(3L))\).
For \(D\le Kd\), summing the individual degrees gives the asserted \(O_K(\log(2A)/A)\) fraction.

Above the cutoff \(L\exp(\sqrt A)\), the objective is at most \(d\log2/D\), uniformly in \(i\).
Below that cutoff a positive gcd has \(D>3L/7\) and \(r_A(D)/d=O(A^{-1/2})\).
A positive contribution therefore needs \(i+1\gg\sqrt A\), and is at most \(O(A(i+1)2^{-i})\).
Both bounds are \(\exp(-c\sqrt A)\) for a fixed \(c>0\) and large \(A\).
Conversely the first \(s=\lfloor\sqrt A\rfloor\) even indices fit in the stated interval.
Their period \(D=3\cdot5^{2s-2}7^{b_0}\) captures at least \(csL\) degrees and satisfies \(D\le3L5^{2s}\).
At \(i=\lceil 2d/r_A(D)\rceil=O(A/s)\), the numerator before the \(\log2\) factor is at least \(d\).
Thus this period gives at least a constant times \(A5^{-2s}2^{-O(A/s)}\), proving the opposite exponential bound.
These are bounds on the best numerical right side of v166's inequality for the specified reduction, conditional on a suitable monic noncyclotomic lift.

The selected and complementary factors of \(\bar P\) are coprime, giving the unique monic \(\mathbb Z_2\)-divisor with the required reduction by Hensel factorization.
Its normalized reciprocal is another such divisor, since inversion preserves the primitive roots of every order in \(B_A\) and the parent is reciprocal.
If the constant of the lift were \(-1\), reciprocal symmetry up to sign would force it to vanish at one.
Since \(P(1)=-1\), its constant is \(1\) and the lift is reciprocal.

For a cyclotomic divisor with conductor \(2^js\), \(s\) odd and \(j\ge1\), reduction gives the \(2^{j-1}\)-th power of \(\bar\Phi_s\).
The squarefree parent therefore requires \(j\le1\), and the odd part must be \(s=3t\) for \(t\mid n\).
At a root of order \(3t\) or \(6t\), powering by \(n\) gives order three or six respectively, hence traces \(-1\) and \(1\).
The parent equation would then require the trace of \(\zeta^l\) to be respectively zero or one, forcing order four or six.
For even \(l\) it has odd order, excluding both possibilities.

The even-\(l\) qualification on (3) is essential.
For odd \(A\), set \(l=n/5\).
This exponent is odd and divisible by every selected \(t_a\), since the largest even index is \(A-1\).
For each primitive root of order \(6t_a\), both its \(n\)-th and \(l\)-th powers have order six, so the two traces are one and the normalized parent value is \(1-2+1=0\).
Consequently the product of the \(\Phi_{6t_a}\) over the selected indices is an actual rational divisor with reduction \(B_A\) and zero logarithmic measure.

At an exterior root of the parent, bounding all four terms below the leading one by their coefficient magnitudes times \(|x|^{n+l}\) gives \(|x|^k\le6\).
Reciprocity of the parent then bounds the absolute value of every logarithmic modulus by \(t=(\log6)/k\).
For even \(l\) each irreducible constituent \(S\) of a possible monic rational divisor \(Q\) is a nontorsion unit polynomial.
Writing \(b=\deg S\), the radial floor gives \(1/10\le b\sum(\log|x|)^2\), whose right side is at most each of \(b^2t^2\) and \(2btm(S)\).
Since \(b\le d\) and \(m(S)\le m(Q)\le e(Q)t\), these bounds prove all three inequalities in (3), including the count with multiplicities.
For \(k\equiv1\bmod70\) one has \(l=n-k\) even and \(\gcd(k,n)=1\).
The proposed rounding produces \(0<k<n\) and \(k\sim n^\gamma\) in both regimes.
For \(\gamma=3/4\), \(k/d\asymp n^{1/4}/A\to\infty\), which rules out the rational divisor.
For \(\gamma=1/2\), the necessary count is \(e(Q)\gg\sqrt n/A\), with existence of a rational lift unresolved.
