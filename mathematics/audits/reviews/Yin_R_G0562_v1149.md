# Review of small-measure transfer from a bounded radial rank to bounded exterior places

<a id="finding"></a>
## Degree minimality inside the class and the oriented block constants

At 2026-11-25T00:15 UTC an agent outside the development of v89 independently reviewed its final [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v89.md#statement) and all three proof sections, including the growing-\(R\) threshold, for Yin.
The reviewer received the fixed claim and scope without its development dialogue or prior findings on v89.
The representative has positive total measure at most \(m(w)\), degree at most \(\deg w\), and the claimed neutral and exterior signatures for sufficiently small \(m(w)\) at each fixed \(R\).
The input witness in \(D(w)\) requires no bound on its own measure or degree.

**Choice of representative.**
The rank hypothesis on \(D(w)\) is equivalent to the presence of a rational irreducible constituent with \(c\)-fixed dimension at most \(R\), by the minimum-rank formula.
The minimization set in v89 consists of nontorsion elements of \(D(w)\) with total measure at most \(m(w)\) for which this property continues to hold in their own divisible groups.
Since \(w\) is itself eligible, taking the least positive integer degree gives \(z\) with both required bounds.
No comparison of the original witness's height with \(h(w)\) is needed.
Torsion-class compression takes products over equal-size classes, preserves total measure and rational conjugate span, and hence preserves the entire divisible group when torsion and all roots are allowed.
The products lie in \(D(w)\), so any degree loss would contradict this particular minimization.
This verifies that \(z\) has distinct conjugates modulo torsion, independently of any global record property.

For fixed \(R\), first take the block and sign theorem thresholds at the fixed total-measure ceiling one.
For the compressed reciprocal input their bounds give \(b=d/(2u)\) and \(u\ll(\log(3d))^{212}\).
Thus at sufficiently large \(d\), uniformly for all such inputs, \(b/2-1>R\) and \(d>4R\).
Smyth's strict lower bound supplies reciprocity if \(\varepsilon_R\) is small enough.
The nonzero measures of nontorsion units of degree below the selected threshold and measure at most one have a positive lower bound by finiteness of their monic minimal polynomials.
Choosing \(\varepsilon_R\) below that bound enforces the large-degree conditions on \(z\).
This order of choices only uses \(R\) and the fixed measure ceiling.

For the uniform extension write \(m=m(w)\), \(d=\deg z\), \(t=\log d\), and \(A=(4m)^{-1/3}\).
Bounded-degree finiteness implies \(t\to\infty\) uniformly over all chosen \(z\) with \(0<m(z)\le m\).
At this actual degree Voutier's theorem gives \(m\ge m(z)>\tfrac14(\log t/t)^3\), hence \(t>A\log t>A\) for small \(m\), and then \(t>A\log A\).
From \(b=d/(2u)\) and the uniform block bound, \(\log b\ge t-212\log(t+\log3)-O(1)\).
The right side increases for large \(t\), so it may be evaluated at \(A\log A\).
The loss \(O(\log(A\log A))\) is negligible compared with \(m^{-1/3}\log(1/m)\), and
\[
 A\log A=\frac{m^{-1/3}}{3\sqrt[3]4}\bigl(\log(1/m)-\log4\bigr).
\]
Thus for each fixed \(\eta<1/(3\sqrt[3]4)\) in the positive range, \(\log(b/R)\to\infty\) uniformly for all integers \(1\le R\le\exp(\eta m^{-1/3}\log(1/m))\).
Since \(d\ge2b\), both required strict size inequalities hold for all sufficiently small \(m\) depending on \(\eta\), with no dependence on the witness.

**The two actions and the block products.**
In the independent inversion case, signs supported on two labels of a block distinguish any two coordinate characters by using an unused third label of the appropriate block.
Character projection and transitivity make the signed permutation module absolutely irreducible.
Its equivariant map to the actual root classes is nonzero, hence an isomorphism.
This argument uses the sign theorem for compressed reciprocal units, whose group alternative has no degree-record hypothesis.
The fixed dimension of complex conjugation counts one for a real exterior inverse pair and one for the two labels exchanged at a nonreal conjugate exterior pair.
A neutral pair contributes zero.
The available low-rank constituent must be this module, so the number of exterior places is at most \(R\).

In the oriented alternative let \(U\) be the sum of the zero-sum spaces on the blocks, and \(B_0\) the block-constant space.
The supported pure alternating factors act independently on the block zero-sum spaces.
Over \(\mathbb C\) each is irreducible by double transitivity of \(A_b\), and they are inequivalent as representations of the product because they have different active factors.
Full-group transitivity on the blocks then gives absolute irreducibility of \(U\).
The map from \(U\) to \(V_z\) is nonzero since a difference of classes of two chosen roots in the same block could vanish only for a torsion quotient, excluded by compression.
Thus \(U\) embeds, and its image meets \(B=\operatorname{im}B_0\) trivially by irreducibility and \(\dim B\le u<u(b-1)\).
This establishes \(V_z=U\oplus B\) before drawing any conclusion about torsion of the block products.

Writing \(\beta\) for the product of one oriented half, \(B=V_\beta\) because its conjugates run over the half-block products, allowing coincidences and inverses.
At most \(2u\) such products occur, and \(h(\beta)\le b h(z)\).
Thus \(\deg\beta\le2u<d\) and \(m(\beta)\le2ub\,h(z)=m(z)\).
If \(B\) contained an irreducible constituent of fixed dimension at most \(R\), then \(\beta\) would be nontorsion and \(D(\beta)\) would still contain a low-rank direction by the same constituent formula.
This would make \(\beta\in D(w)\) an admissible lower-degree competitor.
This is the precise step where the class-specific minimality suffices.
Consequently the available small-fixed-dimension constituent is \(U\).

If \(c\) exchanged two blocks, its fixed graph on their zero-sum spaces would contribute \(b-1>R\) dimensions.
On a fixed block with positive sign the number of cycles of the induced involution is at least \(\lceil b/2\rceil\), and removing its constant line leaves more than \(R\) fixed dimensions.
All blocks must therefore be individually preserved with negative uniform sign.
In particular \(c=-I\) on \(B\).
Every conjugate \(y\) of a block product has class in \(B\), so \([y\bar y]=0\).
Since \(y\bar y\) is positive real it equals one, and all conjugate moduli of the block product are one.
It is an algebraic unit, so Kronecker's theorem makes it torsion and its conjugate span \(B\) vanishes.

With negative sign on a block, a fixed label is an actual neutral inverse pair.
A two-cycle cannot instead be real or neutral, since either would make its label fixed.
Its four roots give precisely one nonreal conjugate pair outside and the corresponding inverse pair inside.
The negative signed action has one fixed vector per two-cycle, and these vectors lie in the zero-sum space, the constant line having sign minus.
Thus the exterior-place count equals \(\dim U^c\le R\).

**Measure and gap implications.**
In both alternatives \(V_z\) is irreducible, so every nontorsion element in \(D(z)\) spans the same module and has the same radial rank.
For \(z\) itself the positive logarithms span the full radial space by reciprocity.
One for each exterior place gives a spanning list with length equal to the dimension, proving rational independence.
At most \(2R\) exterior roots and their inverses occupy at most \(4R\) embeddings, and the larger degree supplies a neutral conjugate.
If a lower bound \(\eta_R>0\) holds for the stated reciprocal class, then \(\min(\varepsilon_R,\eta_R)\) is a lower bound on all original inputs under the \(D(w)\) hypothesis.
Conversely each reciprocal unit with at most \(R\) exterior places supplies its own radial-rank-at-most-\(R\) witness.
For \(R=1\), a real exterior value becomes a positive real Salem number after choosing sign and conjugation, and its quadratic lift \(\sqrt{-\alpha}\) is complex Salem of the same total measure in \(D(w)\).
A nonreal exterior conjugate pair already gives a complex Salem number.
The quadratic lift may double the degree of the representative, as allowed in this consequence.
For the last assertion a subsequence with bounded minimum radial rank would give neutral reciprocal units with at most \(2R\) exterior roots and measures tending to zero, contradicting the corresponding fixed-count gap.
The result preserves the upper measure budget and leaves the existence of those gaps open.
