Since \(t_0=0>t_1>\cdots>t_{n-1}=-4\), the signs of \(H(t_j)\) and \(R'(t_j)\) are opposite at every node.

Thus the residue sum is \(s=w_0+\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}(w_j-t_j)\), and summing the gap lengths gives \(0<s<4+1/M\).
For the exact mesh the endpoint factors have multiplicity two in the reciprocal transform of \(R\), while each interior angle supplies the two simple roots \(e^{\pm i\theta_j}\).
