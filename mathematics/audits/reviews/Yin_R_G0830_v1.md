### Exact law, strong lumping and conductance factors

For every positive pair (y,c), the return equation forces theta(beta)=y-beta^4c.
Two possible four-digit words with that value would differ by a polynomial of degree at most three vanishing at beta; irreducibility of degree four excludes this unless their digits agree.
Thus the middle word is unique, its actual integer weight is w(theta_e), and summing over all tail representations gives W_e=L_y R_c w(theta_e).
These fibers partition the complete return event, so sum W_e=Z and the conditional law within a label is exactly alpha_y times gamma_c.
Neither a uniform representation law nor a selected witness is used.

For a fixed full left word A of label y, summing the original weights over the variable middle/right block gives label probability p_(y,c')/r_y.
Conditional on that label, the right word has law gamma_(c'), independently of the representation of A.
The analogous full-right-word calculation gives p_(y',c)/s_c and alpha_(y').
Averaging the two heat-bath choices therefore yields the displayed Kbar, including both self-loop contributions q_A+q_C.
Every denominator is positive because only positive current fibers and their marginals are used.
Negation preserves all integer weights and sends the full conditional factors to their corresponding negative-word laws.

For a distinct-label edge sharing y, multiplying the full source mass by its H_A transition gives

    (p_e p_(e')/(2r_y)) alpha_y(A) gamma_c(C) gamma_(c')(C').

The leading factor is exactly Qbar, proving (7).
The common-c formula is obtained in the same way with alpha and gamma interchanged.
Distinct labels cannot share both coordinates.
Within one label, a nonloop C change receives only H_A and has conductance p_e q_A alpha_y gamma_c(C)gamma_c(C'), exactly (8).
The A-changing formula uses q_C.
A same-label nonloop step cannot change both tails, because neither full-tail conditioning operation permits that change.
These edge classes are disjoint, a fact used later when taking a maximum instead of summing load bounds.

### Commuting conditional expectation and selected odd energy

Write g(e)=Rf=E[f|e].
For a full left word of label y, H_A Rf equals sum_(c') p_(y,c') g(y,c')/r_y and depends only on y.
Averaging H_A f over alpha_y gives the same expression, since the conditional right-label weights do not depend on A and its conditional law at each y is alpha_y.
Thus RH_A=H_A R, and the common-c calculation proves RH_C=H_C R.
Original sign symmetry also gives RS=SR.
Consequently the label subspace and its orthogonal complement reduce K and I-K, and R preserves oddness.

For h in ker R, H_A h is a function of the left word with alpha_y mean zero for each y.
Conditional on any right word, the left-word law is a mixture of those complete alpha_y laws, so H_C H_A h=0.
Similarly H_A H_C h=0.
The restricted conditional expectations are orthogonal projections with mutually orthogonal ranges, so their sum is a projection and

    <h,Kh><=||h||_2^2/2,
    E(h)>=||h||_2^2/2.

This verifies the factor two for all residual modes, including the interaction residuals, without assuming that the label graph is connected.

For odd f=g+h with g=Rf, the energy splits as Ebar(g)+E(h).

### Randomized lift and correlation with the prescribed terminal word

The load calculation is performed after averaging the actual source weights abar_e mu_e, not after fixing a source representation and replacing its mass by a uniform one.
Conditional on source label e, the stored A0,C0 therefore have independent laws alpha_y,gamma_c.

The first refresh draws a fresh C and the second a fresh A; after these two proposals, the current tails have product law mu_e independently of both stored original words.
Identity proposals need not be performed as edges, but the independent random draws and the remembered words remain defined.

Fix the entire sampled label path, not only its current vertex.
Induction over its transitions preserves the current product law and independence from the stored source words: the tail whose label remains fixed is retained with its correct complete marginal, and the changing tail is a fresh independent draw at its new label.
This conditioning also makes ell fixed when evaluating a particular proposed traversal.

During the final A correction, the held -C0 is independent of the old current A and target -A0, because the stored original tails are independent of each other and the current tails are independent of both.
