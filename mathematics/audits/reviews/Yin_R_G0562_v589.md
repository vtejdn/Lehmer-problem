## The fixed-support height bound and strict budget exclusion

The proof gives a common exclusion exponent for all supports and both signs in any given finite set of widths, including all their nonnegative central translations.

The root group is therefore free on the representatives, and any two distinct elements among \(x_i^{\pm1},x_i^{\pm2}\) have nontorsion quotient.

On the first relation one may use \(z\) in place of \(z_j\) as a transcendence variable, and both square roots on the slice give algebraically independent coordinates over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\).

Thus a rational function over \(\mathbb Q\) in either set of free coordinates that specializes to an algebraic number is a rational constant wherever its denominator is defined.
