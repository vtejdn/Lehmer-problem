## Complete trace-factor and Sturm verification

On 2026-11-18T15:23 a separately assigned agent for Yin inspected the [census program](../../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0170/census.py) and independently reproduced all 1,108 directions in the [finite census](../../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0170/readme.md#census).
The reviewer had no role in developing the submitted census and received the program, its results and the review scope without development discussion or prior findings about the computation.
The independent computation agreed with every reported factor coefficient, cyclotomic order, multiplicity, root signature, control variation and domain summary.

The submitted program forms the monic parent by adding contributions at coincident exponents, factors it over the integers and multiplies back all factors with multiplicity.
Monicity permits rational factorization by Gauss's lemma.
The prime-power inequality \(\varphi(m)^2\ge m/2\) makes its cyclotomic search complete through the degree cap.
Its Chebyshev recurrence recovers the trace, whose binomial lift reproduces the coefficient list.
The exact isolating intervals separate the simple real trace roots and the sign comparison determines the side of either cutoff for a straddling interval.
The cutoffs themselves would give \(x=\pm1\), which have already been excluded.
The signed pseudo-remainders in the four original controls correct the sign of the leading-coefficient power before division by positive content.

I used a [separate checker](../../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0170/verify_traces.py) with Python 3.14.3 and SymPy 1.14.0.

The checker enumerates by total degree \(D\), taking \(4\le D\le104\), \(1\le a\le(D-1)/3\), \(b=D-3a\) and \(\gcd(a,D)=1\).
This gives every primitive positive direction once since \(\gcd(a,D)=\gcd(a,b)\).
It builds \(-g(x^a)-x^bB(x^a)\) directly and divides out the factors prescribed by the [primitive-direction congruences](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v172.md#cyclotomic).
The respective occurrence counts for orders \(2,6,8,10,12,18,30\) were \(734,94,90,62,44,29,16\).
For each nonempty residual polynomial \(P\), the checker subtracts the centered binomial lifts of the trace monomials in descending degree, verifying every coefficient.
It checks rational irreducibility of the resulting trace \(T\) using exact integer polynomial arithmetic.
Thus the two computations use the same algebra library on different factorization problems.

An independently implemented integer Euclidean algorithm gives a Sturm chain for every trace, scaling each elimination by positive constants and negating the final remainder.
Every trace is irreducible and has a real root in \((-2,2)\).
At that real embedding of its root field, \(t^2-4\) is negative, so \(x^2-tx+1\) is irreducible there over the embedded field.
Since \(t=x+x^{-1}\), the degree of a lifted root doubles the trace degree, proving irreducibility of the entire nonempty core \(P\).
The Sturm counts also give at least one exterior root in every nonempty case, so these 1,103 factors are nontorsion.
Together with the exact divisions and the coefficient comparison, this verifies the original rational factorization and simplicity.

For the strict domain \(b>3a\), the checker found 552 directions, the two stated empty cores, the two stated minimizers of degree 8, and maximum degree 104.
The complementary domain had 556 directions, its three stated empty cores, the unique minimizer \((1,3)\) of degree 6, and maximum degree 104.
All 550 nonempty strict-domain cores have nonreal exterior roots only, and exactly 266 have \(a\) conjugate pairs outside the circle.
