The proof and exact computations establish that for every primitive positive direction and every nontorsion root \(\alpha\), none of \(3,5,7\) is a power exponent for \(\alpha\) in \(\mathbb Q(\alpha)\).

If \(\gamma^p=\alpha\) in that field, then \(\gamma,\gamma^{-1}\) are integral by transitivity of integrality, and \(\mathbb Q(\gamma)=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\).

Indeed reduction sends its single local orbit onto the Frobenius orbit of \(x\), with equal fiber sizes.
It divides the integral monic parent locally, giving \(e\le m_x\) even if the parent has other local factors over the same residue.
Writing \(u=\gamma^a,v=\gamma^b\), when \(m_x<p\) the equality \(F(u^p,v^p)=0\) gives \(v_p(F(u,v))\ge1/e\) and hence \(v_p(F(u,v)^p/p)>0\).

The graph Bézout identity excludes zeros of \(B\) there, and Frobenius preserves the zero set when passing from \(\bar u\) to \(t=\bar u^p\).
