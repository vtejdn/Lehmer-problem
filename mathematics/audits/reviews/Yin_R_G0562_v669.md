The fixed-seed consequence of the residue theorem therefore applies with the stated width and slot growth, including complete intervals.

Thus a subsequence yields distinct positive compressed seed measures tending to zero.

This proves convergence without a uniform row estimate across varying seeds.

Since the spectrum in question contains only positive values, any attained infimum is positive.

If the uncorrected case is unresolved by this argument, some exceptional seed attains \(\lambda\) and eventually meets the degree cap.
