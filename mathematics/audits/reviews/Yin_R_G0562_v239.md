At 2026-11-17T13:10 UTC an agent for Yin independently checked the [fixed v239 submission](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v239.md#decision-reciprocal-five-binary-period-submitted).

Because \(S\) is monic integral and divides the integral parent, its reduction has degree \(d\) and divides \(\bar P\).
Thus every represented class has order \(3^{v+1}t\), multiplicity at most \(N\), and orbit length \(f_t\le d\).
For \(N<2^{w+4}\) this gives (3) with an absolute constant.
In the other range, coprimality ensures that raising a root to \(l\) preserves its whole \(t\)-part and, if \(v\ge1\), its whole three-part.

Either condition precludes \(y+y^{-1}\in\mathbb F_2\).
Dividing the constant at its Teichmüller representative by \(2^w\) when \(2^w\mid c-a\) now precludes cancellation with the scalar term, including for \(c=a\).
When \(v_2(c-a)<w\), that smaller valuation determines the constant.
Consequently every root in these classes satisfies the v235 ramification estimate with initial index zero.
Multiplication by the full Frobenius degree gives \(d\ge f_t(J-2)/w\) for each such represented order.

The omitted order can only be three and then has \(f_1=2\).
Any other represented order has Frobenius degree at least two, and is subject to the full estimate.
When order three occurs alone, its two classes have at most four exceptions in all, including exact zeros at the lifts and repeated roots.
Thus \(d>4\) gives a nonexceptional root in one of the classes and, by Frobenius, roots of the same ramification in both.
Since \(J-2\ge 3N/2^{w+3}\), this proves (3) with the maximum over all represented orders.
In degrees at most four the radial floor gives \(m(S)\ge1/(2\sqrt{10d})\).

For a chosen nonempty \(\mathcal U\), the odd number \(M=3^{v+1}T\) is divisible by the order of every selected class, so \(X^{NM}-1\) has multiplicity precisely \(N\) at each.
The gcd contains the full multiplicity supplied by \(\bar S\) at these classes and may also contain classes with unselected orders.
Its degree is therefore at least \(\delta d\).
For \(\delta=1\) one also gets divisibility of \(X^{NM}-1\) by \(\bar S\) and, by monic polynomial division, an even remainder in \(\mathbb Z[X]\) before reduction.
The monic irreducible nontorsion factor satisfies v166's cyclotomic exclusion.
At \(i=\lceil1/\delta\rceil\) the numerator in its estimate is at least \(\delta d\log 2\), since \(i\delta\ge1\).
Substituting (3) into this inequality cancels \(N\).
The maximum \(f_t\) controls the degree of the entire rational factor and need not correspond to an order selected for the gcd.

For full support let \(i\) be the largest represented \(q\)-valuation, allowing \(i=0\).
Then \(T\mid Hq^i\), and the same represented order realizing \(i\) has \(3^v\mid f_t\) and \(q^{(i-e_q)_+}\mid f_t\).
For \(i\ge1\) the latter follows by writing \(f_t=km\) with \(k=\operatorname{ord}_q(2)\) and \(v_q(2^{km}-1)=e_q+v_q(m)\).
The binomial expansion at a power prime to \(q\) preserves the valuation, and raising to \(q\) increases it by one for odd \(q\).
The two divisibilities involve distinct primes and give \(f_t/(3^{v+1}T)\ge 1/(3Hq^{\min(i,e_q)})\).
Together with \(\delta=1\) this gives the absolute constant claimed in (1).
Nonunit endpoints and nontrivial content cost at least \(\log2\), so the bounded-degree bound and additivity cover every positive-measure integral divisor.
