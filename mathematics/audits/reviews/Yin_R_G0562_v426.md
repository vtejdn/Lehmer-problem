## Irreducibility, progression densities and three finite certificates

On 2026-11-18T15:51 UTC a separately assigned reviewer for Yin checked the fixed [criterion](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v190.md#criterion), [density formula](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v190.md#density) and [examples](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v190.md#examples).
The reviewer had previously inspected the individual trace-group theorem used as input and did not participate in developing L190 or receive its development discussion or previous review findings.
All three sections hold at their stated scopes.

After removing the listed cyclotomic factors, simplicity and reciprocity hold for every remaining rational factor in the stated range.
For a root \(\alpha\), the conjugation carrying it to its distinct inverse gives an automorphism of its own root field that fixes \(t=\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\).
Since \(\alpha\) satisfies a quadratic over \(\mathbb Q(t)\), the relative degree is exactly two.
Different rational factors give disjoint trace orbits because \(t\) determines its unordered pair of roots.
The fixed cubic graph is irreducible by linearity in its second variable and coprimality of \(g,B\), and its support differences span rank two.
The primitive factor-degree lower bound therefore makes every trace-factor degree at least two uniformly for sufficiently large \(b/a\).
If the trace polynomial has two or more such rational factors, monicity preserves their degrees on reduction and every irreducible modular factor has degree at most \(n-2\).

In the one-factor case the natural trace action contains \(A_n\), with \(n\ge5\).
For odd \(n\) an \(n\)-cycle is even, and for even \(n\) an \((n-1)\)-cycle with one fixed letter is even.
These are exactly the cycle types detected by a modular factor of the specified degree at a prime away from the trace discriminant, since reduction identifies the Frobenius orbits on distinct roots with the residue factors.
The number of these permutations is \((n-1)!=n!/n\) in the first case and \(n(n-2)!=n!/(n-1)\) in the second.
Both alternating conjugacy classes are included whenever a symmetric class splits.

In the splitting field \(K\) the Vandermonde transforms by the sign on the trace labels.
For \(G=A_n\) its square root of the discriminant is rational and perfectness makes the maximal abelian subfield \(\mathbb Q\).
For \(G=S_n\) the commutator is \(A_n\), whose fixed field is the quadratic discriminant field \(D\).
As every subfield of the abelian extension \(B_m=\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)\) is abelian, \(K\cap B_m=D\cap B_m\) in both cases.
The image of the compositum group in the product consists of the pairs with common restriction to \(K\cap B_m\), by injectivity and the degree formula.
Fixing the second coordinate to \(\sigma_c\) therefore gives every element of \(G\) if the intersection is trivial, and exactly one sign coset in \(S_n\) if the intersection is quadratic.

I checked the first display of Section 1 on page 1 of the [Kadiri–Wong author preprint](../../literature/pdf/KW22--arxiv-v2.pdf).
It states the counting asymptotic \((|C|/|G|)\operatorname{Li}(x)\) for each conjugacy class in a fixed finite Galois extension, over unramified primes counted by absolute norm.
Applied over \(\mathbb Q\), it gives the natural densities of the entire fiber and its conjugation-invariant subset by finite sums.
The fiber has positive density, so division gives the required relative density, disregarding the finite set dividing \(m\operatorname{disc}H\).
The proportions are \(2/h\) in \(A_n\), \(1/h\) in \(S_n\), \(2/h\) in the even coset and zero in the odd coset.
The progression \(c=1\) always permits an even cycle, which completes the prime-existence direction.
The source was used at statement level for fixed fields.

For the finite checks I reconstructed the integral coefficients of \(H(Y)=\sum_j c_jY^j\) directly from the six coefficients of \(-A\).
At descending \(j=n,\ldots,0\) I took the current coefficient of \(X^{n+j}\) as \(c_j\) and subtracted \(c_j X^n(X+X^{-1})^j\) by the binomial formula.
The remainders vanished in all three cases.
I then factored each reduction over its finite field and independently computed the degrees of the gcds of its largest factor \(U\) with \(Y^{p^j}-Y\).

All three pairs are coprime and fail the conditions for each of the two possible odd-odd torsion orders \(8,30\).
In the third case the linear factor modulo eleven is \(Y-6\); any proper rational factorization would have a monic linear factor, whose root must divide the constant term two.
The exact evaluations exclude every such root.
The first two trace polynomials are irreducible from their full-degree reductions.
Each row has opposite signs at two arguments in \([-2,2]\), giving a real trace embedding strictly between the endpoints.
There \(t^2-4\) is negative and therefore cannot be a square in the trace field, so each original polynomial is irreducible of degree \(2n\).
These finite results use no asymptotic threshold, and the densities for fixed inputs give no count as \((a,b)\) varies.
