## The separator, the exact cancellations and the uniform gap

A reviewer outside proof development checked the full fixed argument of C894 v1 for Yin on 2026-11-16T09:26 UTC.
The reviewer received the claim and scope without development discussion, author explanations or an earlier finding on C894.
The prior [odd-grid review](Yin_R_G1106_v4.md#odd-grid-review) and [fixed-weight review](Yin_R_G1105_v2.md#weighted-lift-review) cover the inherited block estimates and variation theorem.

In the full odd-grid chamber the integral remainder \(C\) has a unique expansion in the monic integral basis \(B_{n-i-1}\) of degrees \(n-i-1\).
The congruence \(B_{n-1}E_i\equiv B_{n-i-1}\bmod B_n\) gives \(C(b_j)=B_{n-1}(b_j)h(b_j)\) for the same integral coefficients.
Together with \(B_{n-1}(b_j)/B'_n(b_j)=(4-b_j^2)/p\), this proves the asserted normalization even for a general integral quotient shift.
Since \(E_i(-t)=(-1)^i E_i(t)\), multiplication by \(4-t^2\) preserves the two coordinate parities.

The strict cut and endpoint with \(1<M(P)\to1\) satisfy the hypotheses in L464 v5 for \(\kappa=o(p)\) and \(\kappa\to\infty\).
The midpoint lies strictly in the gap, so \(\mathbb E_p X=(p/n)\sum_j(b_j-a)\rho_j\) is bounded by the two bounded residue moments in C880 v2.
On \(b_j\ge-1\) one has \(b_j-a\ge1/2\) eventually and \(Z(b_j)>0\).
Thus this part of the mean absolute value of \(Z\) is bounded uniformly along the sequence.

For \(b_j<0\), write \(j=n-\ell\), so \(p-2j=2\ell+1\) and \(2\cos(2\pi(2\ell+1)/p)=b_j^2-2\).
The last \(\kappa\) indices have \(0\le\ell<\kappa\), giving \(\phi_j\le\alpha-\delta\), while the earlier negative traces have \(\phi_j\ge\alpha+\delta\).
The normalized squared geometric sum is nonnegative, has circle integral one and contains only frequencies through \(m-1\).
Integrating it over the moving interval preserves the degree and gives an even trigonometric polynomial in \(\phi\), hence a polynomial in \(2\cos\phi\).
The interval has length at most \(2\pi\) eventually, so the rescaled function has absolute value at most one everywhere.
The sine bounds give \(I\ge4m/(\pi^2p)\), whereas \(J\le\pi/(2m\alpha)\to0\) since \(m\alpha\) grows proportionally to \(\kappa\).
Thus one can use, for example, the fixed margin \(\gamma=1/(2\pi^2)\) once \(p\) is sufficiently large.
The inner window contains \([-\delta,\alpha]\), of mass \(I+1/2-J\).
For the outer window the portion below \(\pi\) has mass at most \(1/2-I\), and any excess above \(\pi\) reflects into \([\pi-\alpha,\pi]\subseteq[\alpha,\pi]\), of mass at most \(J\).
These give the required opposite signs with the same margin at every negative trace, including the nodes adjacent to the cut.

The composed polynomial \(W_p(t^2-2)\) is even of degree at most \(2(m-1)\).
Each integrand in the two cancellations is odd and has degree at most \(n+2+2(m-1)+\deg D<p\) for fixed \(D\) and large \(p\).
Its Laurent expansion on \(t=z+z^{-1}\) contains only odd exponents, all strictly between \(-p\) and \(p\).
The full cyclic average therefore vanishes at composite and prime conductors alike.
Both integrands vanish at the omitted point \(t=2\) because each parity part retains \(4-t^2\), so the half-grid cancellation is exact.

For fixed even \(D\ge0\), the sign margin on the negative traces makes their mean of \(WDZ\) dominate \(\gamma\) times their mean of \(|DZ|\).
The nonnegative-trace contribution is bounded, and cancellation of the odd part in the full mean leaves an upper bound by \(\mathbb E_p|DZ_e|+O_D(1)\).
For the second estimate, \(-b_jWDZ\) is nonnegative on the negative traces and dominates \(\gamma |DZ|\) wherever \(b_j\le-1\).
On \(b_j>-1\) the mean of \(|DZ|\) is bounded, and the nonnegative traces contribute bounded error even after multiplication by \(b_j\).
Cancellation of the even part in the full mean now bounds \(\gamma\mathbb E_p|DZ|\) by \(2\mathbb E_p|DZ_o|+O_D(1)\).

For any fixed nonzero \(A\in\mathbb Z[t]\), \(D=(A(t)A(-t))^2\) is a nonzero even nonnegative integral polynomial.
The total weighted mean is \((p/n)\sum_j|D(b_j)\rho_j|\to\infty\) by C880 v2.
The two estimates force both parity means with \(D\) to diverge.
The pointwise bound \(D(t)\le\|A\|_{[-2,2]}^3|A(t)|\) transfers this to each requested mean, including when \(A\) vanishes on the grid.
In a bounded degree \(d\), the coefficients of these monic integral lifts satisfy \(|[z^i]P|\le\binom di M(P)\), leaving finitely many lifts of measure at most two.
Thus failure of a uniform positive gap under either parity bound yields an increasing-conductor subsequence contradicting the two divergences, even if the bounded parity varies with the input.
For even \(h\), the odd mean vanishes identically, which applies to every coefficient height.
The cited conductor-55 vector has zero entries at all odd indices from 1 through 25 and so illustrates this subfamily.
A second reviewer checked the indexing and these thirteen entries against the [native witness](../../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0155/versions/v3/long_prefix.py) and both saved output copies, without rerunning the enumeration.
The theorem proves a uniform gap at each fixed weight and budget and leaves the case of unbounded variation in both parities open.
