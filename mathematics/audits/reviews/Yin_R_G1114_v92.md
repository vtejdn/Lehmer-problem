## Four sublinear offsets in bounded-support trace lifts

At 2026-11-16T18:29 UTC, a separately assigned Codex reviewer for Yin inspected all five sections of fixed L462v103 and the mathematical inputs used there.
The reviewer had previously checked several of its dependencies but took no part in developing this proof and received no development discussion, author explanation or previous verdict on it.
The external theorems were checked at the level of their retained statements and applicability.

I checked the extraction with a missing fourth coefficient throughout a fixed fractional prefix, including the case where the lift has only three lower coefficients in total.
The [three-offset theorem](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v93.md#three-offsets) places all three at \(o(h)\).
The fixed support bound and \(M(\Pi)=2\) bound every coefficient by the [sparse height estimate](../literature/DS17/Yin_U002.md#sparse-height-bound), and the [first nondivisible index](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#statement) forces an odd coefficient in this prefix.
In the [stabilized lattice](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v93.md#face), the limiting normalized exponents separate all later and reflected terms from the three early terms and the constant.
Rational approximation preserves the strict inequalities and all three vanishing conditions, so Jensen gives the face upper bound \(M(A)\le2\).
A late positive evaluation exposes the constant coefficient two and gives the reverse bound.

I checked all ranks in the group generated by the early characters.
The [common-phase argument](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v98.md#common-phase) excludes rank three and the rank-two case with the three nonconstant points on an affine line.
In rank one the odd coefficient, constant two, Jensen equality and exact fractional prefix meet the hypotheses of the [rational-prefix theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v21.md#statement).
In rank two, a pair collinear with the origin gives a trinomial of measure two and minimum at least one by Jensen in the independent variable, contrary to the [minimum classification](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v101.md#minimum).
The [extreme-ray slices](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v23.md#slices) and the [diagonal contradiction](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v23.md#diagonal) use just the four points, their positive evaluation and measure two for the nonparallel geometries.
They therefore apply to the exposed face by itself.

I checked the [parallel-side table](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v100.md#trapezoids) and the [equal-side coefficients](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v94.md#shapes) against all four effective polynomials.
The all-even case contradicts the odd coefficient, and the equal-side product is excluded with its integral binomial allowed in the rational-prefix theorem.
For the unequal-side product, division by the integral unit-constant factor yields an integral formal series \(X_i\).
Its two remaining characters have exponents \(e,e+d\) with the same gcd as the original pair, and the first two nonconstant coefficients in \(S_i\) are signed halves in every case.
At each sufficiently late evaluation the exponents within each effective polynomial remain distinct and positive away from the constant.
Every phase specialization therefore has measure at least two, fixed degree and fixed leading modulus.
Its mean logarithmic measure equals \(\log2\) under [phase translation](../../claims/Yin_C_G0900_v1.md#endpoint), giving \(M(A_i)=2\) exactly by continuity.

The ordered base tests and mixed-row tests of \(X_i\) only require indices \(o(h)\).
I checked the application of [triangle slope extinction](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#extinction), the [strictly exterior square](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v95.md#two-circles), and the [irreducible trapezoid](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v99.md#rows).
For the reciprocal square the two coefficients in \(y\) have equal modulus on the first circle and are relatively prime for every sign choice.
The [transverse zero-graph argument](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v96.md#rows) forces divisibility of the integral slope numerator and kills the mass on the zero graph in every weighted row, including when the linear signs agree.
Thus the singular cosets give a fixed annihilating product after extraction, and the coefficient window can be shortened inside the fractional prefix.
For comparable offsets the [first-index tests](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v72.md#mixed) use only the initial two signed halves whenever a term is active.
Any third term is at least \(p+b\), safely beyond \(p+o(p)\).
For separated offsets the [sixth-root filter and weighted Gram estimate](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v95.md#separated) use only the single signed half below \(b\).
In both regimes the bounded partial multipliers on the finitely many pieces give the needed coefficient sums through \(Cb\), also for \(X_i\) obtained by formal division.
The geometric expansion for \(1/S_i\) has only \(1+\lfloor C\rfloor\) potentially contributing powers of the part starting at \(b\) in that interval.

For the denominator comparison I rechecked the two-sided moment differences, including the correction at the Taylor constant and the identity at negative indices.
The resulting initial absolute sums cover every shift in \(S_iU_i\), and the vanishing window leaves \(Q_i\) of degree \(O(b)\) with bounded absolute coefficient sum.
Since \(\Pi J_i=2B^\sharp\), the polynomial identity against the actual denominator holds globally, while agreement of \(X_i\) with \(S_iJ_i\) is only needed through \(\lfloor h/L\rfloor\).
The [two cutoffs](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v51.md#window) and the [integer support bound](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v50.md#window) give bounded support for \(A_iU_iV_iB^\sharp\).
Its constant coefficient is two and its measure is exactly \(2M(B^\sharp)>2\), tending to two by the [actual denominator representation](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#integrality).
The [endpoint gap at two](../../claims/Yin_C_G0900_v1.md#statement) excludes this sequence.
I find the fourth-offset conclusion and its equivalent qualitative uniform bounds valid for bounded-support branch-two inputs of both degree parities.
