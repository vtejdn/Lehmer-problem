# Review of the interpolated row ideal and its prime divisors

<a id="finding"></a>
## The integer certificate and both directions of the support formula

At 2026-11-20T10:23 UTC a separate reviewing agent for Sheng Yin checked the [construction and cost assertions](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v518.md#statement) from the fixed proof and its cited mathematical inputs, without participation in their development or access to their development discussion.
The integer divisibility, radical equality, width, height and zero-infimum existence assertion all follow.

For a fixed exponent, degree preservation separates its nodes.
Across exponents the powered monic minima have distinct logarithmic measures \(ph\), and reciprocal pairing gives constant term one in \(Q\).
The Galois action permutes the summands of \(R,T\) within each exponent, so the weighted sums have integral coefficients.
In the notation of the [rational interpolation](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v518.md#rows), division of \(-WK\) by \(V\) gives \(R/D+QT/(JYD^2)-Q(R'D-RD')/D^3\).
At \(z\), multiplication of the correction in parentheses by \(Q'(z)=D(z)\) cancels \((R/D)'(z)\) and leaves \(\alpha_i/(pz)\).
The added numerator is a multiple of \(Q^2\), and \(V(z)\ne0\).
Consequently \(X-(-U/V)(X^p)\) has value and derivative zero at \(\alpha_i\), since \(p\alpha_i^{p-1}\cdot\alpha_i/(pz)=1\).
Monic division yields the square divisibility in \(\mathbb Z[X]\).

One has \(\deg K\le3N-2\), and the leading coefficient of \(Y^{N+1}WQ^2\) is \(N\) at degree \(4N+1\).
With \(U(0)=1\), this also gives its primitive content and the uncancelled coefficient \(N\) of \(Y^{4N+1}\) in the reflected difference.
The bounds \(\|A_z\|_1\le A/(1+|z|)\) and \(|\alpha_i|\le1+|z|\) give the two weighted sum bounds directly.
In particular \(\|K\|_1\le (3N^3+N^2)JA^3\), so \(\|U\|_1\le4JN^4A^4+A^2+NA^3\), while \(\|V\|_1\le JN^4A^4\).
Both fit the stated \(7JN^4A^4\).
At fixed \(P\), \(N/s\le e/\min S\), \((\log J)/s\le\sup_{x\ge\min S}(\log x)/x\), and \(\log N/s\to0\).
Only finitely many primes can identify two fixed conjugates under powering, and \(4N+1\le b\) eventually.
The resulting \(\limsup(\log H)/s\le4h\) permits successive fixed reciprocal seeds with \(h\downarrow0\) when the unrestricted infimum vanishes.

In the [integral contraction](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v518.md#ideal), the relation \(Q(0)=1\) gives an integral inverse for \(Y\bmod Q\).
Writing \(E\) as a combination of \(Q,W\) gives \(E^2\in(Q^2,W)=(U,W)\), as \(U\equiv Q^2\bmod W\).
Taking fourth powers modulo \(U\) and multiplying by \(J\) yields \(JE^8\in(U,V)\) through \(JW^4=Y^2V\).
This proves coprimality over \(\mathbb Q[Y]\) and bounds the generator of the original integral ideal's contraction without dividing either row by its content.
The discriminant is nonzero, and its absolute value belongs to \((Q,D)\) by the resultant identity.
Every discriminant prime gives a common residual zero of \(Q,D\) and hence of both rows, including when the derivative reduces identically to zero.
At a divisor of \(J\), \(V\) reduces identically to zero.
When \(\bar W=0\), \(\bar U=\bar Q^2\) has positive degree.
Otherwise the term of degree \(3N+1+\deg\bar W\) exceeds both \(2N\) and \(3N-2+\deg\bar W\), so \(\bar U\) again has a root over the algebraic closure.
Evaluation forces every such prime to divide \(B\), and \(B\mid JE^8\) and \(E\mid|\operatorname{disc} Q|\) exclude all other primes.

## A fixed seed in the interval obstruction

The initial statement described a seed-uniform \(O(s)\) bound for \(\log B\) as open.
The reviewer identified a fixed-seed counterexample to this description and supplied the comparison with the dyadic theorem.
The check here of the [revised final assertion](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v518.md#statement) therefore includes a contribution to its argument.
For \(4h<\log2\), the preceding limit bound gives \(H<2^s\) eventually and \(j(L)\to\infty\) gives \(4e|S|+1\le j(L)|S|\).
Thus these rows satisfy the [dyadic hypotheses](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v513.md#statement) with \(k=2\).
The class is nonempty even without a zero-infimum assumption.
For the [degree-ten polynomial \(Q_8\)](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v3.md#minus-delay-progression), the unique expanding root equals its measure, \(Q_8(1)=-1\), and \(Q_8(19/16)=330753263801/16^{10}>0\), while \((19/16)^4<2\).
For large \(L\), the prescribed deleted primes include all divisors of \(J\), as \(2L\le L\sqrt{\log L}\).
If \(E_{\mathrm{out}}\) denotes the full part of \(E\) outside the deletions, then \(B_{\mathcal A}\mid E_{\mathrm{out}}^8\), prime by prime.
The [divergence after deletion](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v513.md#remove) therefore gives \((\log E_{\mathrm{out}})/s\to\infty\).
This verifies the changed conclusion using the proved dyadic lower bound at its stated scope.
