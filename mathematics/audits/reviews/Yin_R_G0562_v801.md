Since \(\mathbb Q(\alpha)/\mathbb Q(\alpha^k)\) is separable of degree \(e\), each embedding of the smaller field extends in exactly \(e\) ways.

The roots of \(P_k(X^k)\) are nonzero and simple, so its monic integral quotient \(D\) by \(P\) vanishes at precisely the \(k-e\) unselected points in each fiber.
For an integral Laurent polynomial, multiplying by a power of \(X\) clears the negative exponents without changing vanishing at any of these nonzero roots.
Division by the monic minimal polynomial then shows that vanishing at one conjugate is equivalent to Laurent divisibility.
Collecting \(AD\) modulo \(k\) therefore gives integral Laurent numerators, and the convergent Laurent series on an annulus around the circle splits their coefficients into the stated progressions.
Parseval with Haar probability measure yields the sum of their squared norms.

At every root of \(P_k\) the Fourier transform has support \(I\) of size \(e\), while the support \(S\) of its input consists of the indivisible numerators and is independent of the chosen root.
Finite Parseval and Cauchy–Schwarz give \(k\|v\|_2^2\le e|S|\|v\|_2^2\) with \(v\ne0\).
At equality the summands \(v_r\omega^{ir}\) agree for all \(r\in S\) in each row \(i\in I\).
The two difference sets are consequently orthogonal.
With \(H=\langle I-I\rangle\), the two supports lie in cosets of \(H\) and \(H^\perp\), respectively.
Since both products of cardinalities equal \(k\), both inclusions are equalities.
The cyclic group has a unique subgroup of size \(e\), so \(e\mid k\) and every root fiber is a multiplicative coset of the \(e\)-th roots of unity.
Its factor \(X^e-c\) has only exponents divisible by \(e\), and the product over fibers has integral coefficients because it equals \(P\).
Charging each of the at least \(k/e\) surviving sections its unrestricted infimum gives the normalized comparison, including the case \(e=1\) in which all \(k\) sections survive.
