## From the two coordinates to the signed section

The sign and both directions in the trace criterion hold as written.
At the distinguished embedding let \(A=p-qT\) and \(C=pT-q>0\).
The unit \(T>1\) differs from the rational number \(p/q\), and \(Y=C/A\) has sign \(e=\operatorname{sgn}(A)\).
For \(A>0\), the inequality \(C>TA\) follows from \(q(T^2-1)>0\).
For \(A<0\), \(C>T(-A)\) is equivalent to \(T+T^{-1}<2p/q\), supplied by \(w<2/q\).
Hence \(|Y|>T>1\) in either case.
When \(Y\) is integral, inversion under the trace-field involution makes it a unit.
Every product of powers of \(T\) and \(|Y|=eY\) has modulus one at all neutral places and reciprocal moduli at the remaining pair.
A bounded distinguished logarithm thus bounds all conjugates, giving only finitely many such products by the coefficient bounds in fixed degree.
The positive generator of the discrete logarithm group of these two units gives \(T=\beta^u,\ |Y|=\beta^v\) with \(0<u<v\) and \(\gcd(u,v)=1\).
In particular \(u=1\) exactly when \(|Y|\in\langle T\rangle\).

Substitution gives \(q-p\beta^u+e(p-q\beta^u)\beta^v=0\), so the original section sign is \(\epsilon=-e\) for \(a=q,\ b=-p\).
Conversely a real unit \(\beta>1\) satisfying that parent recovers \(Y=e\beta^v\) on setting \(T=\beta^u\), since the same denominator is nonzero.
Every \(g\)-th root of \(\beta\) is a nontorsion unit root of the dilated parent, and taking the \(g\)-th power maps a unit root of the dilated equation back to its primitive parent.
In the signed norm-one case all other conjugates of \(1-pqw\) exceed one, so \(0<w<1/(pq)\).
As \(w=T+T^{-1}-2\) increases on \(T>1\), this gives \(T<p/q,\ e=1,\ \epsilon=-1\).
