The least positive multiplicative measure in the stated class is \(M(P)\), and the characterization by irreducible substitutions holds in both directions.

At the cutoff \(c=601/500\) one has \(500^3(c^3-c-1)=-58168199<0\), so \(c\) is strictly below the real root \(>1\) in Smyth's theorem.
The minimum of a nontorsion unit below \(c\) is therefore reciprocal, and an odd-degree reciprocal minimum would have \(-1\) as a root.
The antireciprocal convention similarly contributes only the linear root \(1\).
This accounts for every odd degree, including \(55\).
Passing from an irreducible minimum \(R(X^k)\) to \(R\) preserves irreducibility of the core and preserves its Mahler measure since the \(k\) lifts of each nonzero root have the same modulus.
Iteration terminates at an exponent-primitive core, and the author's choice between \(R(X)\) and \(R(-X)\) exhausts the remaining sign ambiguity.
For each resulting root one has \(w^k=\pm u\) with \(u\) a root of the listed core.
In a common normal field \(k f_w=f_u\), so a zero or nonzero Salem projection is preserved in both directions.

The paper's Theorem 1, Table 1 and §3 establish the two external search scopes used here.
The full list below \(1.3\) applies through degree \(44\).
At each even degree from \(46\) through \(54\) the theorem supplies the polynomial attaining the minimum among exponent-primitive irreducible noncyclotomic polynomials.
Comparing the five published half-vectors coefficient by coefficient identifies them at lines \(1115,1223,1304,1381,1448\), respectively, of the pinned author table.
The exact cut below puts every one of these witnesses strictly above \(c\).
The classification uses the published enumeration as an external theorem at these precise scopes. [Mossinghoff, Rhin and Wu](https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.2008.10128872)

Three of the four surviving rows have a single exterior real trace, with the remaining traces in \((-2,2)\).
The positive exterior root of the polynomial, or of its sign substitution when the trace is negative, is therefore a Salem number.
It is a positive power of the primitive generator of its Salem field.
Its log function has nonzero inner product with that generator's log function, and all its conjugates are detected after conjugating the direction.
The other survivor, at line \(196\), is exactly the irreducible reciprocal lift in v55.
For this input v55 excludes Salem numbers from its splitting field.

The independent [review of v55](Yin_R_G0562_v1112.md#finding) covers the group argument and irreducibility used at this interface.
Thus \(P\) supplies attainment below \(c\), and every orthogonal unit below \(c\) in the degree range arises from its row.
Conversely an irreducible \(P(\pm X^k)\) with \(18k\le55\) is a monic unit minimum of measure \(M(P)\), and its roots have the required vanishing projections by the same log identity.
This argument covers \(k=1,2,3\) subject to the stated irreducibility condition.

For the independent calculation the first header field \(d\) identified the start of the data, the integer degree and half-vector determined each polynomial, and neither numerical data column was used.
All \(1339\) inspected rows had distinct half-vectors, leading coefficient one, and the prescribed reciprocal length.

For the independent root-squaring implementation write the current polynomial as \(\sum_{i=0}^d a_i X^i\).
The coefficient of \(X^{2t}\) in its product with the polynomial at \(-X\) is
\[
 (-1)^t a_t^2+2\sum_{i=\max(0,2t-d)}^{t-1}(-1)^i a_i a_{2t-i}.
\]
Computing these diagonal sums through the middle and reflecting them gives the monic reciprocal polynomial on the squared roots.
After \(j\) steps put \(b=\lfloor601^{2^j}/500^{2^j}\rfloor\).
With exact integers, \(\sum_i a_i^2\le b^2\) certifies the lower side by the circle geometric-mean bound and Parseval.
An entry \(|a_i|\ge(b+1)\binom di\) certifies the upper side by the elementary-symmetric bound.
These comparisons are strict at \(c\) since \(c^{2^j}\) is never an integer.
Both the retained verifier and this independent implementation decided every row, using at most fourteen squarings.
There were precisely \(4\) below and \(940\) above through degree \(44\), and \(107,80,76,66,66\) above at the five higher degrees, in increasing order.

The Gaussian integers \(B_k=\sum_{j\ge k}a_j\binom jk A^{j-k}D^{18-j}\) give
\[
 \left\lfloor
 \frac{\max(|\Re B_1|,|\Im B_1|)}

\]

\[
 \frac{1201393424050}{10^{12}}<M(P)=|z|^2<

\]
Both endpoints lie strictly inside the claimed interval, whose upper endpoint is below \(601/500\).
