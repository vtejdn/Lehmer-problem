Therefore both real roots belong to one connected component of the displayed complement, including the repeated-root case.
This argument is uniform over every allowed choice of the mandatory roots.

### Localization under the complete measure

The reciprocal lifts of the selected roots all lie on the unit circle.
The two residual reciprocal pairs therefore have exterior radii \(\rho_1,\rho_2\ge1\) with product equal to the entire \(M(P)\), including multiplicities.
