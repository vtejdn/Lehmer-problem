### Crossing count and exhaustion of the rows

For \(z=e^{i\theta}\), \(-\pi<\theta<\pi\), one has \(1+z=2\cos(\theta/2)e^{i\theta/2}\) with positive cosine.
Thus \(z^s=h(1+z)\), \(0<h\le1\), requires \(\theta=4\pi m/(2s-1)\) with \(|m|\le(2s-1)/6\), and equality is impossible because \(2s-1\) is odd.
The endpoint \(-1\) never solves the equation.
The reciprocal of \(s-z/(1+z)\) has strictly positive real part since \(\Re(z/(1+z))=1/2\).
It follows both that the root is simple at each crossing and that the derivative of \(\log|z|\) with respect to \(\log h\) is positive.
The \(s\) roots begin inside the circle, and precisely \(1+2\lfloor(2s-1)/6\rfloor\) leave before \(h=1\).
Reversal gives the asserted exterior pole count \(t_s\) and excludes circle poles.
On \(|X|=1\) the two reciprocal blocks have equal nonzero modulus, so Rouché for \(X^b B_s(X^a)+v g_s(X^a)\), \(0<v<1\), gives exactly \(at_s\) exterior roots.
Taking disjoint disks strictly outside the circle at the limiting exterior roots gives the claimed upper bound with multiplicities at \(v=1\).

One has \((t_3,t_4,t_5)=(2,1,2)\) and \(t_{s+3}=t_s+1\).
Hence \(at_s\le2\) gives precisely the five displayed rows.
At large slope the [exterior allocation](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v391.md#allocation) attains this count, and the [real-root classification](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v210.md#real) makes the size-two sets on these rows nonreal conjugate pairs.

In the remaining size-two cases an exterior nonreal pair must belong to the same rational factor and cannot occur twice.
Every nontorsion rational factor receives an exterior root by monicity, the unit constant coefficient and Kronecker's theorem.
This proves irreducibility and simplicity of each nonempty core, without asserting reducibility on any other row.

### Simultaneous divisibilities and the measure bound

Fix \(p\mid c\), \(e=p^{v_p(N)}\) and \(M=N/e\).
Reduction gives \((X^M+1)^e\) with \(X^M+1\) separable since \(p\nmid M\).
An unramified lift \(\xi^M=-1\) of any residue root has \(\xi^N=-1\) since \(e\) is odd.
The residue order has exact two-part \(2^{j+1}\) since \(v_2(M)=j\), and the same is true for \(u=\xi^a\) because \(a\) is odd.
Direct evaluation gives \(F(\xi)=-cu^{-s}(1+u^t)(u^{2s-t}-1)\).
The exact two-part of the first power \(u^t\) is at least eight, and the second power has even order by \(j\ge v_2(2s-t)\).
The two binomials are therefore units, so the valuation is exactly one by squarefreeness of \(c\).
Every monic rational irreducible factor is integral and each occurrence meeting the residue root has positive integral valuation at \(\xi\) in the unramified ring.
Consequently exactly one factor occurrence receives the whole multiplicity \(e\) at that root.
The degree of any rational factor is thus a multiple of \(e\), including when the valuation of \(N\) at \(p\) is zero.
Independence of these odd prime powers gives the asserted product.

For a reciprocal irreducible factor at \(p_0\), the multiset of its residue roots admits inversion and Frobenius with constant multiplicities on each joint orbit.
The power \(M/2^j\) sends each point to a primitive \(2^{j+1}\)-st root.
For \(w=\pm p_0\equiv5\bmod8\), \(v_2(w^{2^h}-1)=h+2\) gives order \(2^{j-1}\) modulo \(2^{j+1}\).
Together with \(-1\) this generates the full unit group.
Thus the image of every orbit is the full set of \(2^j\) primitive roots and every fiber on that orbit has the same size.
Counting multiplicities proves the independent \(2^j\)-divisibility.
On the upper semicircle the real normalized section has values \(2-4c\) and \(2\) at \(1\) and the specified \(\zeta\).
Indeed \(\zeta^{at}=-1\), \(\zeta^{N/2}=1\), and these identities cancel both signed pairs.
This supplies a nonreal circle root strictly between the endpoints and hence a reciprocal rational factor.
When all odd prime divisors of \(N\) divide \(c\), that factor has degree at least \(N\), exhausting the monic parent.
The real sign change to the right of \(1\) ensures positive measure.
The parity threshold is \(2\) at \(t=1\), and \(a\equiv1\bmod 2c\) within \(2c\) of \(\sqrt N\) is coprime to every such \(N\), giving the claimed growing primitive directions.

The seed denominator is Eisenstein at \(p_0\), hence separable in characteristic zero.
A common root with its reversal would make \(z,z^{-1}\) algebraic integers and force the nonintegral rational value \(z^t+z^{-t}=c^{-2}-2\).
This also rules out unit-circle roots by complex conjugation.
Checking the substitution \(X=\xi\exp(u/a)\) at each simple exterior pole as in the [lift estimate](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v401.md#poles) gives radial error \(O(|\tau|^{-b/a}/a)\) in each logarithm.
There are \(a\) disjoint lifts per pole, so summing yields \(m(F)\ge m(B)-o(1)\ge\log c-o(1)\) uniformly at fixed \(s,t,c\) as \(b/a\to\infty\).
On the irreducible smooth degrees this is the measure of the irreducible polynomial itself.
The coefficient change and the high-slope bound give no shrinking sequence.
