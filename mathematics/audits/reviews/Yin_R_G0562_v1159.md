The reviewer previously developed v70 and v95 and checked how v99 applies their specified interfaces.
Both index divisibilities, the attained minima, the classification strictly below the coordinate value and the scope of the large-degree requirement hold.

The two exterior conjugates of modulus \(R\), their inverses and the remaining neutral roots have radial rank one.
Thus the integer indices from v95 apply to every nontorsion element of the divisible group.
For a primitive unit on the coordinate line the stabilizer of its actual value fixes the class of the original root and therefore the root itself by compression.
The unit already lies in the root field, so the two degrees agree.
Its logarithmic measure is \(m(\alpha)/e\) by homogeneity of height, giving the positive integer \(k=h/e\) in the v95 spectrum.
The \(h\)-th power map from the conjugates of \(\eta\) onto those of \(\beta\) is bijective because their fields coincide.

In the independent case the signed product orbit gives both \(a_i a_j\) and \(a_i/a_j\) on each eligible pair.
Their unique lifts give the unit \(z_{ij}\) with \(h\)-th power \(a_i^2\).
The stable derived subgroup of the preimage of the supported \(A_{b-1}\) still surjects onto that perfect group and has trivial action both on the two-element inverse pair at \(i\) and on \(\mu(L)\).
On \(z_{ij}\) it acts by a character into \(\mu(L)\), which is trivial by perfectness.
The unique lifts and transitivity on the remaining labels therefore make the products independent of \(j\), including lifts that interchange the two factors by reversing the sign at \(j\).
The entire stabilizer of \(a_i\) preserves the resulting unit, placing it in \(K_i\) and giving \(h\mid2e\).
In the oriented case the stabilizer preserves the selected half and permutes the other positions there.
The product of the lifted ratios is thus in \(K_i\) and has \(h\)-th power equal to \(a_i^b\) divided by the half product.
All half products are torsion under the one-pair hypothesis of v94, so the primitive lattice in \(K_i\) gives \(h\mid be\).

For any permitted radical or torsion twist \(x\), its class stabilizer contains the stabilizer of the actual \(x\) and the subgroup fixing \(L\).
Its fixed field \(F_x\) is consequently in both \(\mathbb Q(x)\) and \(L\).
The relative norm \(v\) multiplies the class by \([\mathbb Q(x):F_x]\), preserving the class stabilizer.
The field of \(v\) is exactly \(F_x\), its output orbit separates classes, and degree times height is unchanged, since the norm differs from that power of \(x\) by torsion.
This descent justifies using \(d=\deg v\) throughout the orbit estimates and transferring any surviving class line back to \(x\).

For the stabilizer of the signed root at \(i\), independent even flips kill every other coordinate in the group average.
In the centered model, other blocks average to zero by their own alternating groups and the entries at the remaining positions of the distinguished block average to \(-q_i/(b-1)\).
The average thus represents \(bq_i[\rho_i]/(b-1)\), incorporating the block constant relation.
In each case the norm of \(v\) from \(\mathbb Q(v)K_i\) to \(K_i\) is the average times the relative degree.
For a supported signed root its orbit size under the stabilizer of \(v\) is \(s_i=[\mathbb Q(v)K_i:\mathbb Q(v)]\), so that relative degree over \(K_i\) is \(ds_i/(2n)\).
Primitivity gives a positive integer for each orbit after taking the absolute coefficient.
Their sum is \(J\), and \(J=1\) forces one orbit.
With coordinates divided by \(e\) in the height formulas of v94 and \(\lambda=2\log R/e\), I obtain \(m(v)/\lambda=dS/(2n(b-1))\).
The bounds \(2S\ge(b-1)\sum|q_i|\) and \(2S\ge b\sum|q_i|\) in the respective models give \(m(v)\ge J\lambda/2\).

In the independent case a subcoordinate value can only occur for \(k=2\), with \(J=1\) and equality in the pairwise maximum bound.
This equality requires constant absolute coefficients on each block, and the single supported orbit gives a common nonzero size \(a\) across all \(t\) occupied full blocks.
The kernel of the label permutation has at most \(2^u\) classes modulo the independent even signs in the commutator.
The label image modulo its perfect alternating product has size at most \(2^u u!\), so \(\varphi(T)\le4^u u!\) for \(T=|\mu(L)|\).
The prime-power totient inequality gives \(T\le2\varphi(T)^2\).
The fixed measure bound and \(b=n/u\), \(u\ll_M(\log(3n))^{212}\), give \(\log T=o(b)\) uniformly.
Two supported double flips isolate \(4q_i[\rho_i]\), and taking a \(T\)-th power removes the torsion ambiguity over \(K_i\), so \(a\ge1/(4T)\).
The support orbit has at least \(u/t\) members and the independent even flips contribute \(2^{t(b-1)}\) images per member.
These bounds give \(J\ge2^{t(b-1)}/(8T)>1\) eventually, ruling out the putative value.

In the oriented case \(m(v)<\lambda\) gives \(J=1\), so every occupied block has \(j\) entries \(a\), \(j\) entries \(-a\) and the rest zero by transitivity of the supported orbit and centering.
A double transposition on one block exchanges distinct entries at \(i,l\) and equal entries at two other positions.
The difference unit \(v/(sv)\) has class \((q_i-q_l)([\rho_i]-[\rho_l])\).
The supported \(A_b\) fixes any unit of \(L\) whose class vanishes on this block and belongs to the conjugate span: it fixes the class and the roots of unity, and a character on a perfect group is trivial.
The same argument for the supported \(A_{b-2}\) shows that it fixes the difference unit exactly.
For an element \(g\) of the ordered-label, half-preserving stabilizer, there is \(p\) in this supported \(A_{b-2}\) matching \(g\) on the block pattern of \(v\), using equal remaining entries to correct parity.
Then \(gv/pv\) is fixed by \(s'=gsg^{-1}\), and \(s'pv\) and \(psv\) have identical centered entries on every block.
Both are conjugates of the compressed \(v\), so they coincide as actual units.
This verifies fixation of the difference unit by \(g\), including possible actions on other blocks.
The full ratio stabilizer allows only one further coset, corresponding to simultaneous interchange of labels and reversal of the half.
Norming to the ratio field thus costs at most two and gives a nonzero integral coefficient on the primitive ratio line, of absolute value at most \(4ak\).
This yields \(a\ge1/(4k)\ge1/(4b)\).

For a fixed support the independent alternating actions attain all \(D_j^t\) placements in the proof, with a repeated entry available for parity correction.
Together with at least \(u/t\) supports the bound gives \(J\ge jD_j^t/(4b(b-1))\).
For \(j=2\) its lower bound is already \((b-2)(b-3)/8\); for \(3\le j\le b/2\) it is at least \(3(b-2)/24\).
For \(j=1,t\ge2\) it is at least \(b(b-1)/4\).
All three exceed one at \(b\ge12\), leaving \(j=t=1\).
The surviving vector is a nonzero rational multiple of a ratio vector with distinct conjugate classes.
Its class field is the ratio field, where \(v\) lies on the primitive line transported from \(\eta\).
The degrees of \(v\) and that transported generator are equal by their class stabilizers, so its logarithmic measure is a positive integer multiple of \(m(\eta)=(b-1)\log\eta\).
The primitive coordinate attains \(k\log\eta\), and \(\eta\) itself realizes the competing value.
A proper divisor \(k\) of \(b\) is at most \(b/2\), whereas \(k=b\) allows precisely one positive multiple of \(b-1\) below \(k\).
This gives all the claimed minima and the class-line necessity without an existence assertion for any specified primitive index.
