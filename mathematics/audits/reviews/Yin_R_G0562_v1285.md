## Cyclic position costs and closed braid fibers

The cyclic-position bound and the independent closed-fiber formula both follow with the stated hypotheses.
In the symplectic factorization the prefix of switches takes the \(i\)-th handle to handle zero after renumbering, while the other handle in that coefficient factor has label \(i\).
Transporting the marked axes around the full shift leaves a single quarter-turn power at the return.
Irreducibility implies symplectic irreducibility, and the noncyclotomic and proper-power exclusions give the other two hypotheses of Proposition 2 for this closed surface of genus at least two. [Margalit and Spallone, Proposition 2, Theorem 9 and Lemmas 10–11](../literature/MS07/readme.md)

After placement by a unit \(u\), a shortest arc of length \(m=\|ui\|_g\) allows \(m-1\) exchanges avoiding handle zero, followed by the indicated local operation and the inverse exchanges.
The three disjoint twists have the required action because one can band parallel copies of the two signed axes through their connected complement.
The rotation permutes an identical marked piece in each sector, including its refinements at the boundaries of the supported maps.
Thus the number of factors for a noncentral position is \(2m-1\), and neither the individual supporting triangulations nor the cost of the middle coefficient depends on \(g\).
The fixed-genus bound covers \(g=2\).
If \(I=\{i\}\), a common divisor of \(i,g\) divides every exponent, so the compression hypothesis permits the choice \(ui=1\bmod g\).
The several-position bound sums the distances for the same unit.

The taper identifies the two faces along their common boundary and leaves a collar of the whole fiber after attaching the local piece.
In the coordinate marking of its exposed face, the next supported triangulation is again a subcomplex of the marked model, even when the preceding homeomorphism moved its support.
The annulus around a horizontal curve times an interval is a solid torus with that curve as core.
With a drilled core, filling along the meridian plus \(k\) fiber longitudes changes the relative annular marking by \(k\) twists up to the chosen sign convention.
Disjoint cores realize the required signed powers at once.
Only the initial full collar costs \(O(g)\).

In the universal cover of the filled manifold a component over a solid torus has cyclic stabilizer, the image of the solid-torus fundamental group up to conjugacy.
If nontrivial, it acts on its invariant geodesic, and free deck action permits an equivariant image on that geodesic for each vertex orbit.
The trivial case permits a single point.
The disjoint filling components therefore contribute zero signed straight volume, irrespective of the triangulations extending the boundary across them.
Geodesic interpolation gives degree one, and the other tetrahedra each contribute at most \(v_{\rm tet}\) in absolute value.
Dividing by the cyclic covering degree removes the \(O(g)\) collar cost in the limit with \(g\) fixed.

For the braid exterior, the primitive cone is given just before equation (3), which yields the five-term numerator \(F=\Delta(t^a,t^b)\).
The cusp cycles have lengths \(e_1,e_2\), the smallest possible prong number under \(b\ge6\) is two, and the closed genus is \(b+1-(e_1+e_2)/2\ge5\).
Thus capping gives a closed pseudo-Anosov bundle by filling the two boundary tori, and strict hyperbolic volume decrease applies. [Hironaka, preprint pp. 8–11, Propositions 3.1 and 3.3–3.5 and Corollary 3.9](../literature/Hir10/readme.md) [Thurston, Theorem 6.5.6](../literature/Thu80/readme.md)

Over the two-variable Laurent ring the abelian boundary entries generate the augmentation ideal, whose gcd is one.
The vector of signed maximal minors is therefore the boundary row times the link Alexander polynomial up to a unit.
After specialization the nonzero leading term of \(F\) and a nonzero specialized boundary entry give an acyclic complex over \(\mathbb Q(t)\) with torsion \(F\) up to a signed monomial.
The fiber mapping cone gives \(\chi_{H_1(\mathrm{open})}=(t-1)F\) in monic normalization.
Its boundary submodule is the direct sum of the two cyclic permutation modules modulo the line generated by the sum of all boundary circles.
Dividing by its characteristic polynomial gives exactly the displayed closed polynomial, of degree \(2b+2-e_1-e_2\) as required by the genus.
The removed factor is \(1\) or \(\Phi_3\), since coprimality precludes both \(e_i=3\).
