## At most half of the horizontal dimensions remain

At 2026-11-24T19:07 an agent outside the development of this proof independently checked all four fixed sections for Yin.
The reviewer had previously reviewed the horizontal quotient construction and received the present claim without its development dialogue, author explanations or previous findings on this argument.

Over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\), an irreducible Laurent factor defines an irreducible divisor of dimension \(N-1\) on the quotient torus.
All four row functions are nonzero at the specified point by coprimality and reciprocal contact, so they are also nonzero in the divisor's function field.
A repeated factor makes the differential of the restricted determinant zero on the divisor.
In logarithmic coordinates the annihilator of the quotient torus's tangent space is the constant span of its \(\ell=n-N\) defining character vectors.
The ambient gradient therefore belongs to that span after extending scalars to the function field.
Extending each vector to the two pivot slots by zero row sums is injective and preserves dimension.
These pivot entries are the actual missing derivatives because each row times its inverse is invariant under simultaneous scaling of all its slots.

Put \(\tau=-P/Q\) and adjoin \(\omega\) with \(\omega^2=P/P^\iota\).
In the first row the normalized logarithmic derivative is \(c_i(z_i/\omega-\omega/z_i)\).
In the second row the minus sign in the determinant gives \(c_i(\tau z_i/\omega-\omega/(\tau z_i))\), using \(Q^\iota/P^\iota=P/Q=-\tau\).
Thus \(w_i=c_i(T_i-T_i^{-1})\) in both rows and \(w\) belongs to the extended relation space \(L\).
Each \(T_i\) satisfies a monic quadratic with coefficients in the field generated by the \(w_i\).
Dividing \(T_i\) by the \(T\)-value of its own pivot recovers every original horizontal coordinate.
Those coordinates generate the function field of the divisor, since the images of the original characters generate the quotient character lattice.
The \(T_i\) and \(w_i\) themselves lie in a finite extension of that function field.
Both inequalities in transcendence degree therefore give \(\operatorname{trdeg}_{\overline{\mathbb Q}}\overline{\mathbb Q}(w)=N-1\le\ell\).

In the equality case \(N-1=\ell\) with \(N\ge2\), the image of \(w\) is dense in \(L\), so nonzero coordinate forms \(u_i=w_i/c_i\) are genuinely nonconstant homogeneous linear forms there.
The row sums of \(w\) vanish and \(\sum_i c_iT_i=(P+\tau Q)/\omega=0\).
Consequently \(\sum_i c_i(T_i+T_i^{-1})=0\).
For a nonzero form \(u\), the two affine factors \(u\pm2\sqrt{-1}\) are distinct.
Two forms from different groups modulo sign have no common affine factor, since proportional affine factors would have proportionality constant \(1\) or \(-1\) by their constant terms.
Valuation at any one of these unique linear factors proves independence of the squareclasses \(u^2+4\) for the different groups in the rational function field of \(L\).
Each \(T_i+T_i^{-1}\) in such a group is one of the two signs of the corresponding square root.
Independent sign-changing automorphisms of the multiquadratic field separate all groups and the constant term.
The cancellation argument thus also works for proportional forms whose proportionality constant differs from both signs and in dimension one.

On the open subset of the irreducible divisor where all four row values are invertible, the algebra given by adjoining \(\omega\) is finite and \(\omega\) is a unit.
The component corresponding to the chosen generic square root is finite over the base, and its defining prime contracts to zero in the base coordinate ring.
Lying over makes that component surjective onto the open set, including its possibly singular specified point.
The \(T_i\) and their inverses are regular there, so their identities of functions hold on a lift of that point.
For a first-row slot \(a_i=pj_i\) one then has \(T_i=\alpha^{p(j_i-j_0)}/\omega\).
For a second-row slot \(a_i=1+pj_i\), the factor \(\tau=\alpha^{1+p(j_1-j_0)}\) converts \(\alpha^{p(j_i-j_1)}/\omega\) to the same formula \(T_i=\alpha^{a_i-pj_0}/\omega\).
Distinct slots thus have specialized \(T_i\) distinct even up to sign, since the occupied exponents are distinct and the unit is nontorsion.

A zero form would give \(T_i=\pm1\), allowing at most one such slot.
Its single nonzero constant contribution cannot cancel, so there are no zero forms.
If \(u_j=\sigma u_i\), the two solutions are \(T_j=\sigma T_i\) and \(T_j=-\sigma/T_i\).
For distinct slots specialization excludes the first, and two partners choosing the second would specialize equal up to sign.
Every group therefore has exactly two members because one nonzero form alone cannot cancel.
The second root gives \(T_iT_j=-\sigma\) and \(T_j+T_j^{-1}=-\sigma(T_i+T_i^{-1})\), so \(c_j=\sigma c_i\).
Comparing the products of two pairs on the same lift gives an integral power of \(\alpha\) equal to a sign.
This forces all paired exponent sums to agree and all pair signs to agree.
Hence the original polynomial is proportional to its reflection with that single sign.
For every integer \(p\ge3\) the reflection must exchange the two occupied residues, since preserving each would require \(p\mid2\).
If \(a\) is its exponent sum, then \(a\equiv1\bmod p\) and \(A(Y)=\sigma Y^{(a-1)/p}C(Y^{-1})\), which would make the reflected determinant zero.
This rules out equality and proves \(N\le\ell\) on every terminal divisor of dimension \(N-1\ge1\).

On a terminal rank-one quotient the earlier construction bounds the degree of the power field solely in terms of \(k,K\).
On every other terminal branch the preceding estimate gives \(2\le N\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\).
Each successive primitive relation lifts to an original horizontal-lattice vector independent of the earlier ones because its class in their quotient is nonzero.
The finite lists of integral basis changes at bounded depth yield a finite list of such lifts depending only on \(k,K\).
There are \(n-N\ge\lceil n/2\rceil\) independent relations.
For \(k=7\) the terminal repeated-factor quotient necessarily has dimension two.
The two-variable repeated factor through the point is the remaining case at that support.
