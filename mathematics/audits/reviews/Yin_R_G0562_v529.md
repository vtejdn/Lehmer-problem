## Outcome of the full numerical review

For the seed \(P=X^8-X^5-X^4-X^3+1\), all 53 reported uncapped minima at width nine are correct.
Only \(p=17\) meets \(H\le2^p\), with least cost \(91\,698\).
For the test allowing every qualifying seed this gives \(M_{17,9}\le91\,698\).
The exclusions at the other primes concern this single octic and the stated range.

**Independent lattice calculation.**
Using exact SymPy polynomial and integer arithmetic separately from the J177 functions, I constructed the signed remainder matrices by binary modular powering modulo \(P^2\).
The two matrices had rational rank eight at every prime in a separate enumeration of the primes in \([3,251]\).
I cleared denominators in rational nullspace bases to form integral \(2\)-by-\(10\) matrices \(N\).
With \(G\) a column Hermite basis for \(N\mathbb Z^{10}\), the rows of \(G^{-1}N\) have integral coordinates, lie in the kernel and generate its whole integer lattice.
Indeed the columns generate \(\mathbb Z^2\), so any rational linear combination of the two rows with integral coordinates has integral multipliers.
The stored bases also span the same rational kernels and have column Hermite form \(I_2\).
Independent Gauss reduction gave both reported successive squared lengths at all 106 signs and primes.
For \(a=\|u\|^2\le c=\|v\|^2,\ |u\cdot v|=|b|\le a/2\), the shortest squared length at transverse coefficient \(|j|=1\) is \(c\), and \(|j|\ge2\) gives at least \(4(c-b^2/a)\ge3c\).

**Exhaustion of the uncapped and capped searches.**
For each stored minimum \(H\), I enumerated both orientations of every nonzero vector \(iu+jv\) of one-norm at most \(2H\) in each saturated signed lattice.
The exact quadratic bound \((ai+bj)^2+(ac-b^2)j^2\le a(2H)^2\) supplies closed integer endpoints for \(i,j\).
Every genuine pair of height at most \(H\) occurs in this enumeration because \(U+V^*\) and \(U-V^*\) are both nonzero and their one-norms are at most \(2H\).
I reconstructed all parity-compatible pairs, evaluated their one-norms, ordinary rational gcds and reciprocal products, and compared the least passing costs against the stored figures.
Across the 53 optimum ceilings this gave 2,460 signed oriented vectors and 8,992 candidate pairs before filtering.
Every stored witness occurred and no lower cost qualified.
Independent searches at the cutoff confirmed the counts and span dimensions for each sign and the counts of compatible, affordable and genuine combinations.
Only primes \(3,7,17\) have a nonzero eligible signed vector in either plane.
At 51 primes a shortest Euclidean squared length already exceeds the necessary bound in at least one plane.

The original program's rational elimination imposes every remaining coordinate integrality congruence in the two free coordinates by a Bézout change of basis and the exact resulting index.
Its choice of one orientation per signed vector preserves the least possible cost because negating one component swaps the two reversed rows up to sign.
A common zero at the origin introduced by formal reversal is removed by a common monomial, preserving both norms and divisibility since \(P(0)=1\).
Common nonzero roots under reversal correspond by inversion.
The original uncapped ceilings contain verified witnesses for every input, and the exhaustive lower search within each ceiling therefore suffices.
I also reran the native per-prime and width-eight control routines in memory and obtained every stored record exactly.
The largest uncapped candidate-count product in that implementation is \(9\cdot565\) at \(p=109\), and direct integer comparison gives \(H^4>2^{5p}\) for every listed \(p\ge19\).

**The minimum at seventeen.**
At \(H=91\,697\) there are two oriented plus vectors and eight oriented minus vectors, and eight of their sixteen pairs have matching coefficient parities.
None of those eight pairs meets the height bound.
At \(H=91\,698\) the same two counts and eight compatible pairs give four oriented genuine witnesses.
The displayed witness has respective norms \(91\,698\) and \(79\,938\), polynomial gcd one, zero remainder modulo \(P^2\) and outer Laurent coefficient \(u_0u_9-v_0v_9=8790\).
At the cutoff \(131\,072\), accounting for the four orientations recovers the nine compatible combinations and the two representative costs \(91\,698\) and \(114\,378\).
At width eight both rational kernels are lines, and their primitive generators are congruent modulo two.
The signed edge expression has absolute value \(69\,599\,886\).
The minimum-width formula and a direct calculation give \(H_{17,8}(P)=13\,902\,464\).
