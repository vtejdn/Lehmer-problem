## Two reductions of the integral trace

Writing the centered Laurent polynomial as a sum of reciprocal pairs gives the indices \(129600,129961,130322,130683,131044\) with weights \(1,7,10,-7,1\), together with the constant one.
I constructed the Chebyshev pairs \((C_j,C_{j+1})\) recursively from \((2,T)\) by doubling and evaluated their weighted sum over each of \(\mathbb F_{19}\) and \(\mathbb F_3\).
Both polynomials were monic of degree 131044 and coprime to their derivatives.
At both primes I factored the polynomial with python-flint 0.9.0, multiplied the monic factors back to recover it, and obtained the degrees subsequently recorded in J0183.
Evaluating over the integers at \(-2,-1,0,1,2\) gave \(25,-29,-15,13,25\).
As a separate check through degree 722, successive gcds with \(T^{19^j}-T\), removing earlier factors, returned the six degrees \(1,3,5,8,89,625\).

## Nine modular divisors

A monic integral factor of positive degree at most 65522 would have the same degree at both primes.
Modulo three the degree 115545 exceeds this bound, and the six smaller degrees have 63 distinct nonzero subset sums.
I compared all sixteen recorded pairs of factor evaluations modulo 19 with my recomputation and checked the output of the fixed certificate program against the table in J0183.
Enumerating the monic products gives the nine degree and residue triples
\[
 (1,10,11),\ (5327,15,9),\ (7172,4,10),\ (7173,2,15),\ (8302,5,2),
 \ (8303,12,3),\ (12202,18,7),\ (12524,4,5),\ (15474,16,2).

The signed divisors of 25 reduce to \(1,5,6,13,14,18\) modulo 19.
Only degrees 8302 and 12202 pass at \(-2\), with residues 5 and 18, respectively.
Their residues 2 and 7 at \(-1\) both lie outside \(\{1,9,10,18\}\), the residues of the signed divisors of 29.
This exhausts the proper degrees required by Gauss's lemma.

Since \(130322\ge2\cdot361\), the circle-root theorem gives a root \(z\) with \(t=z+z^{-1}\in(-2,2)\) and exactly 722 exterior roots counted with multiplicity.
The trace is irreducible, so \([\mathbb Q(t):\mathbb Q]=131044\).
The negative discriminant \(t^2-4\) at this real embedding makes \(X^2-tX+1\) irreducible over \(\mathbb Q(t)\), giving \([\mathbb Q(z):\mathbb Q]=262088\), the degree of the whole section.
