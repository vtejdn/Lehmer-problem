## Independent finding

The proof establishes both exclusions for the stated sequence.
Expanding the section from v805 and pairing opposite Laurent powers gives the five trace terms and the constant in v829.
I constructed the degree-fourteen template directly from the coefficient polynomials and verified irreducibility over the rationals with SymPy.
The trace recurrence gives the displayed septic in v827, with values 167 and −179 at 3 and 4, so the section has a root off the circle.
Exact discriminant factorization gives \(d=19\cdot3137\cdot343830539219\) in absolute value for the septic and \(5^2\cdot23\cdot d^2\) for the section.

For a multiple trace root, either preimage under the trace map is a multiple section root, including at a ramification point.
Its Frobenius orbit degree is bounded by the section gcd degree for either primitive direction.
Over its residue field the companion matrix has eigenvalues of order dividing \(p^j-1\), and one additional factor \(p\) kills any unipotent part.
In the truncated ring, \(I^4=0\) follows from \(p^2=u^3=0\), and three successive \(p\)-th powers lift identity modulo \(I\) to exact identity, also at two.
The determinant is one, so the exponent period covers negative subscripts as well.
The constant and linear coefficients detect multiple roots in both directions, and the quadratic coefficient transfers exact double multiplicity.
At each ramified prime the template Sylvester matrix nullity is positive and bounded by its determinant valuation one, so precisely one linear factor repeats, with multiplicity two.
Its lifted quadratic and complement have unit cross-resultant, and the complement has unit discriminant.
For the translated quadratic, \(p\mid a,b\) gives \(a^2-4b\equiv-4b\pmod {p^2}\) at those odd primes, and the full value at the lift differs from \(b\) by a unit factor.
This proves the required valuations throughout \(p\le Y\).

The logarithm of the period costs at most \(\sum_{j=1}^{16}(4+j)\sum_{p\le Y}\log p\).
The nonzero cyclotomic resultants cost \(O(H^2)\) in total, and the remaining lcm costs at most \(\log(H!)\).
Since \(5\mid M\), \(\gcd(1+M,5+(1+M)M)=1\), and the unbounded period gives steepness.
At a prime above the cutoff, a small-order root evaluates identically on the template because its order divides \(M\).
That order is prime to the residue characteristic, so the root annihilates the corresponding cyclotomic polynomial, forcing \(p\mid M\) through the resultant.
The logarithmic derivatives then agree by exponent congruences modulo both the order and the characteristic.
The template is separable above the chosen cutoff, excluding a shared reduction there.
At smaller primes, eventual reciprocity and the square resultant identity exclude contacts because the trace discriminant valuations are at most one.
Finally \(\log l\le C Y\) gives both thresholds after reducing the common constant, including the floor in \(H\) since the contact order is integral.
