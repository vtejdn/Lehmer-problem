For any \(q\ge2\), monic division gives \(q^{4d}\) residues, and the unit constant term of \(U\) makes \(x\) invertible.
Its order is a positive integer at most \(q^{4D}\), so the stated least common multiple works simultaneously for every eligible pair of minima.

For \(p\equiv-1\), multiplication by \(X^b\) gives \(A^{*b}+\sigma XA=0\).
Both expressions have degree strictly below \(4d\), so monic division makes them coefficientwise zero over \(\mathbb Z/q\), even for composite \(q\).
Reading the second equality in Laurent polynomials shifts the reflection center to \((b-1)/2\), while the first has center \((b+1)/2\).
Unpaired coefficients vanish modulo \(q\), as do fixed plus coefficients when \(2\) is invertible.
Division by row content preserves the contacts by monic division over \(\mathbb Z\).

The retained Dirichlet theorem applies separately to each fixed modulus since both classes are reduced, and the primes in the growing-modulus example can be chosen successively.

The opposite signs give \(D_A\equiv0\bmod q\) even when the coefficient ring has zero divisors.
For a genuine pair the graph equation recovers each of the two target roots from its \(p\)-th power with the same rational function on both orbits.

Eliminating the shifted terms makes \(D_A(X^p)\) vanish twice at both orbits, and the power map has nonzero derivative there.
For a transverse pair \(D_A/q\) is nonzero, and monic division by \(Q^2\) gives an integral cofactor of measure at least one.
Multiplicativity of measure and the coefficient one-norm bound give (2), with one factor of \(q\).

In a radical presentation, recovery of \(z=\gamma,\gamma^2\) gives \([\mathbb Q(z^p):\mathbb Q]=fk\).
If \(p\mid k\) then \(S_1(X^{k/p})\) would annihilate \(\gamma^p\) in strictly smaller degree.
The elements \((z^p)^k\) each have degree at least \(f\) by the tower bound and at most \(f\) since they are powers of \(\xi,\xi^2\), respectively.
Their positive absolute heights differ by a factor of two, so their minima are distinct.
In the component of index \(pr\bmod k\), a term from the reversed row requires an original index congruent to \(b+p^{-1}-r\bmod k\).
Without that class, divisibility of this component by \(U_0\) would force \(A_r\) to have both powered base minima as double factors, of total degree \(4f\), although \(\deg A_r\le\lfloor b/k\rfloor<4f\).
This proves (3) for occupied classes, including the single-class interpretation at \(k=1\).
For \(k\ge3\) odd, the reflection fixes just one class.
In a nonempty ordered sample the first entry must lie there or have its partner represented by some later draw.
