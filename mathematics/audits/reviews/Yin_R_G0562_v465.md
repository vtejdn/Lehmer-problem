### Reconstruction and Hensel lifts

Here \(893\) is odd, prime to 3 and congruent to \(3\bmod10\).
The quartic torsion table gives exactly \((X+1)\Phi_{10}(X)=X^5+1\), with both factors simple.
I divided the six-term polynomial \(H_{893}\) by \(X^5+1\) over \(\mathbb Z\) and checked that the remainder vanishes and the degree-900 monic quotient \(R\) is reciprocal.
To obtain \(T\bmod9\), I eliminated the coefficients of \(R\bmod9\) from the highest exponent down against \(X^{450-j}(1+X^2)^j\), \(j=450,\ldots,0\), leaving zero remainder.
This reconstructs the same trace as the Chebyshev expansion since the eliminated polynomial is \(X^{450}(X+X^{-1})^j\).

Over \(\mathbb F_3\) I formed the Frobenius matrix with columns \(Y^{3i}\bmod\bar T\) on the basis of powers of \(Y\) and extracted the monic factors by increasing orbit degree.
Their degrees were 199 and 251 and their product was exactly \(\bar T\).
I also checked that the gcds against the full \(\bar T\) at steps \(1,199,251\) have degrees \(0,199,251\) as displayed.
Both positive degrees are primes, so each of the latter two gcds is irreducible by the finite-field factorization of \(Y^{3^j}-Y\) and the exclusion of linear factors.
They are distinct, establishing squarefreeness.

Using coefficients \(0,1,2\) for the initial lifts \(F_0,G_0\), I computed \(\Delta=(T-F_0G_0)/3\bmod3\) from the coefficients modulo nine.
I obtained the two corrections independently as \(\Delta g^{-1}\bmod f\) and \(\Delta f^{-1}\bmod g\), using the polynomial Euclidean algorithm.
Their actual degrees are 198 and 250, respectively.
The second correction also equals the ledger's exact quotient \((\Delta-sg)/f\).
I multiplied the lifted factors over \(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\) and recovered all coefficients of \(T\bmod9\).
Monicity fixes the top coefficients, and the two correction congruences with their strict degree bounds prove uniqueness even over this composite modulus.

### Signed determinants and the rational conclusion

For each computed lift \(U\) of degree \(k\) I formed \(P_U=X^kU(X+X^{-1})\bmod9\) directly and reduced it modulo \(D=X^{12}+X^9-1\).
I used the columns \(X^iP_U\bmod D\), \(0\le i<12\), to compute the determinant of multiplication on the original pole algebra.
Taking the integer determinant of representative columns and reducing it modulo nine gives \(\operatorname{Res}(D,P_U)\) since \(D\) is monic.
The answers in increasing degree are **7 and 4**.
This obtains the sign directly from the \(X\)-variable, independently of the odd-degree sign adjustment in the trace calculation.

I also checked the listed \(W\bmod9\) by the trace recurrence and all twelve coefficients of both remainders.
In ascending order they are
\[
\begin{split}
 F\bmod W&=(1,4,5,4,8,3,5,4,1,8,7,5),\\
 G\bmod W&=(0,3,7,8,6,3,8,5,3,0,4,8).
\end{split}
\]
The two degrees are odd, so the trace-algebra determinant acquires a minus sign in the formula for the pole norm.
The two signed residues both reduce to one modulo three, and their product is one modulo nine.
Each of the two nonempty proper subsets is a singleton and has residue different from one modulo nine.
A monic proper rational factor of \(T\) would be integral and, with its complementary factor, would reduce to one such unique pair of lifts.
Its reciprocal lift would divide \(H_{893}/(X+1)\), forcing signed pole norm exactly \(+1\) by L211.
Thus neither subset can be the reduction of a proper rational factor.

Finally the quartic phase count gives \(893+6=899\) simple circle roots.
Removing the five torsion roots leaves 894, all distinct from \(1,-1\), hence at least one trace \(y\in(-2,2)\).
Its irreducible trace polynomial has degree 450, and the strictly negative discriminant \(y^2-4\) at this real embedding makes its quadratic lift irreducible over \(\mathbb Q(y)\).
This proves the full degree 900 and the strict gain from modulus three to modulus nine for the specified section and test.
