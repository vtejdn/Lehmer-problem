## Arbitrary prescribed residues and the fixed-exponent Lehmer criterion

At 2026-11-24T19:21 UTC an agent outside the proof development independently reviewed for Yin the [fixed-exponent theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v9.md#statement) and all four argument sections of C0901 v9.
The reviewer received the fixed proof and its mathematical inputs, without development discussion, author explanations or previous findings about v9.
The exact rate is \(\rho(S)m(\beta)\) for the stated width schedules, with the claimed dichotomy for a nonzero row value and the unrestricted signed-row criterion at every fixed \(q\ge2\).

On the occupied classes \(J\), the evaluations of a \(K\)-basis under all embeddings of \(E/K\) form a nonsingular matrix by separability.
Thus the matrix of evaluations of \(\alpha^s\), and hence the matrix with entries \(\zeta_i^s\) after column scaling, has rank \(r(J)\).
The Euler Wronskian is a monomial times an integral polynomial in \(X^q\), whose highest coefficient is nonzero by the Vandermonde on the distinct top exponents.
The determinant one-norm is at most \(l!(1+L)^{\binom l2}\|F\|_1^l\), since each original row has one-norm at most the norm of the whole graph on disjoint classes.
Factoring \(D=(X^q-\beta)^c\) from every column polynomial gives the Wronskian factor \(D^l\) by the triangular Leibniz expansion of the derivative rows.
Among the new columns one may take \(r\) independent rotations \(F(\zeta_iX)/D\), since the remaining columns can complete an invertible constant change of basis.
At \(\alpha\) each rotation retains at least \(a-c\) zeros after the division when \(a\ge c\), because \(\zeta_i\alpha\) is a conjugate and \(X^q-\beta\) has a simple zero there.
The first \(l\) derivative orders lose at most \(l-1\), giving the stated lower bound on \(\operatorname{ord}_\beta W\).
Monic divisibility over \(\mathbb Z\) and the one-norm estimate imply (2).
There are finitely many \(J\), so \(\log(B+2)=o(a)\) makes the loss sublinear uniformly over all candidate graphs at this fixed seed.
When \(r=l\), inverting the rotations directly shows each occupied row vanishes to order at least \(a\) at \(\beta\).

In the jet count, multiplication of the \(j\)-th divided derivative by \(\alpha^j\) leaves precisely \(\binom{qi+s}{j}\beta^i\alpha^s\) per monomial.
One denominator suffices for all coordinates of the fixed powers in a \(K\)-basis of their span, independently of \(i,j,t\).
The coordinate values become algebraic integers, distinct ones separated by the integral norm in simultaneous embedding boxes of side \(1/2\).
Multiplying the embedding radii gives the factor \(\exp(rbt\,m(\beta))\) because \(\beta\) is integral and the embeddings are counted with their archimedean multiplicities.
The remaining terms give the stated binomial product and \(rd_0t\) powers of \(C'NH\).
For \(f=b/t\to\infty\), the sum of the logarithms of the binomials is \(O_q(t^2\log(2+f))\) by the increasing bound \(j\log(\mathrm e L_0/j)\).
Dividing by \(l(b+1)-rd_0t\) yields (3), including the \(O(t/f)\) correction to the leading term, and \(b\le t^2\) controls the one-norm factor \(N\).
The two colliding coefficient vectors differ on an actual exponent, so the resulting graph is nonzero.
With \(t=a\) the width \(\min(B(a),a^2)\) gives the required upper limit.

When \(\rho(S)<1\), a minimizing \(J\) of least size has strictly larger rank ratio on each proper nonempty subset.
Together with \(m(\beta)>0\), (2) and (3) therefore force all \(l\) rows to be occupied eventually.
Since \(l-r>0\), the same comparison bounds \(x=c/t\) by the two error terms stated in the proof.
For \(f\le t\), multiplying that bound by \(\log(2+f)\) gives a quantity tending to zero, as both \((\log(2+f))^2/f\) and \((\log(2t))^2/t\) tend to zero.
Hence \(\log\binom{L_0}{c}/t\le x\log(\mathrm e L_0/(tx))\to0\) for \(c>0\), with no cost at \(c=0\).
The operation \(X^c F_0^{(c)}/c!\) preserves exponents of surviving monomials and integrality.
At a row of order exactly \(c\), it gives a nonzero value at \(\alpha\), hence a nonzero row value at \(\beta\).
The order of the whole graph is at least \(t-c\), which exceeds or equals \(a\) eventually for fixed positive slack \(t/a\to1+\epsilon\).
Letting \(\epsilon\) decrease after this limit proves the assertion for every allowed schedule.

Writing \(d=[E:K]\), the height identity gives \(m(\beta)=q m(\alpha)/d\).
If \(d=1\), every nonempty subset has rank one and the minimum ratio is \(1/|S|\).
For the full set, the \(q\) consecutive powers span \(E\) over \(K\), so the use of the full set gives upper rate \(d\,m(\beta)/q=m(\alpha)\).
The \(a\)-fold divisibility by the minimum of \(\alpha\) supplies the matching lower rate.

For the finite criterion, at any admissible root \(U(\beta)\) and \(V(\beta)\) are nonzero and \(\alpha=-U(\beta)/V(\beta)\) recovers its field.
The integral elimination polynomial \(qY(U'V-UV')-UV\) is nonzero because its lowest coefficient has multiplier \(q(\operatorname{ord}_0U-\operatorname{ord}_0V)-1\ne0\).
The derivative identity transfers at least \(a-1\) zeros to \(\beta\) for composite \(q\) and for \(q=2\) as well.
Gauss's lemma applies also to a nonmonic primitive minimum, and field recovery gives \(m(\beta)=q\,m(\alpha)\) for this step.
The norm bound and the fallback therefore give the pointwise lower inequality, and the width error divided by \(a\) tends to zero.
For the reverse limit, all-power reciprocal representatives preserve the original measure, yield rank one in the two selected classes, and permit the nonzero-value construction with \(b\le B-1\).
Reversal at degree \(qb+1\) preserves reciprocal divisibility, and the two possible signed first rows sum to \(2U\), so one retains a nonzero value.
The multiplier \(Y^{B-b}+\sigma\delta\) has reversal sign \(\sigma\delta\), has nonzero value at nontorsion \(\beta\), and costs a factor at most two on the row norm.
This proves the upper bound for either prescribed sign at the exact formal width.
Taking the infimum over the representatives establishes (4) for the unrestricted \(\lambda\), with finite searches for computable \(B\).
Its positivity remains open.
