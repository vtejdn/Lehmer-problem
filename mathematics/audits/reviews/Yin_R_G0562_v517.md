### Seed degrees, multiplicity and uniformity

For a genuine row pair, coprimality gives \(V(\alpha^p)\ne0\), since otherwise the graph equation would give a common zero of the rows.
Thus \(\alpha=-U(\alpha^p)/V(\alpha^p)\) and \(\alpha^p\) has the full degree \(e\).
Reversing the graph and eliminating its two rows retains the square of the powered minimum in \(Y^b(UU^\iota-VV^\iota)\), since \(X\mapsto X^p\) is unramified at a unit.
This nonzero polynomial has degree at most \(2b\), while on the circle its absolute value is bounded by \(H^2\).
Taking degree and logarithmic measure gives \(e\le b\) and \(p\log M(P)\le\log H\).
In particular \(M(P)>2\) is ruled out already at equality in the coefficient cutoff.
Inversion has no fixed point among the conjugates of a nontorsion reciprocal irreducible, so \(e\) is even.
The small-degree classification excludes degree two and leaves exactly the two positive quartics and their variable-sign transforms in degree four.
Both quartics have \(\rho^{3/2}>2\), and the quartic lower rate at width eight in C345 v411 already exceeds the cutoff.

For a sextic with \(g\) exterior roots, sums over opposite signs and over pairs with a zero log modulus give \(D(P)\ge2(6-g)h\).
Using the supplied width nine for every smaller actual width is valid in the norm inequality.
Its lower rate is at least \((4(6-g)-7)h/6\), giving \(13h/6\) and \(9h/6\) in the two census groups.
Integer subtraction gives
\[
 7^{13}-64\cdot5^{13}=18\,764\,010\,407,\qquad
 17^9-64\cdot10^9=54\,587\,876\,497 .
\]
Both comparisons are strict, so the fixed-seed sublinear error can be absorbed even for \(H=2^p\).
The variable-sign transform preserves all log moduli.
Taking the maximum of the finitely many onsets handles a putative sequence choosing different sextics or quartics at different primes.
For \(b\le7\) these are all possible degrees and the initial integer \(2^p\) is the only qualifying candidate.
At widths eight and nine the conclusion for all sextic seeds uses no classification of octics.

### The octic and the scope of the comparison

Expanding \(X^4T(X+X^{-1})\) gives the stated octic.
Over \(\mathbb F_2\), reduction of \(Z^4+Z+1\) modulo \(Z^2+Z+1\) leaves remainder one, and evaluation at either residue gives one.
This eliminates every possible proper factor of degree at most two.
The values of the integral \(T\) at \(-2,-1,0,1,2,3\) are \(3,-1,1,-3,-1,43\).
The four disjoint sign-change intervals give four simple real trace roots, exactly three inside \((-2,2)\).
At an interior embedding of the trace field, the lift discriminant is negative, so it cannot be a square in that field.
The lift therefore has degree eight over \(\mathbb Q\) and one exterior root, on the positive real axis.
Direct integer evaluation gives \(P(1)=-1\) and \(7^8P(9/7)=552451\), so the unique exterior root satisfies \(1<\rho<9/7\).
In \((4e-2-b)/e\), the choices \(e=b=8\) give \(11/4\).
The exact difference \(16\cdot7^{11}-9^{11}=256\,168\,279\) shows that this lower rate is strictly below \(\log2\).
