Hence the reduction of its local minimal polynomial is a power of one irreducible residue polynomial of degree \(g\), and comparison of degrees gives exponent \(e_w f_w/g\).
In particular \(g\mid f_w\) even when the residue of the root generates only a proper subfield of the residue field of the completion.
