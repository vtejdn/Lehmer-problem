If \(\theta^{q^a}=\beta\) in \(K=\mathbb Q(\beta)\), then \(\theta,\gamma=\theta^{q^{a-1}}\) and their inverses are integral, and the fields generated by \(\theta,\gamma,\beta\) have the same degree \(d\).
In the \(d\) embeddings of \(K\), the conjugates of \(\theta\) give exactly the conjugates of \(\gamma\) and of \(\beta\) on taking the corresponding powers.
The characteristic polynomials of the two powers of the companion matrix of \(\theta\) are therefore the minimal polynomials of \(\gamma\) and \(\beta\), each with simple roots.

For an integral matrix \(T\), replacing its entries by nonnegative representatives preserves every trace modulo \(q^a\).
In the resulting directed multigraph, rotation of a based closed walk of length \(q^a\) through \(q^{a-1}\) edges fixes exactly the repetitions of based closed walks of length \(q^{a-1}\).
Every other orbit has size \(q^a\), since each proper divisor of \(q^a\) divides \(q^{a-1}\).
This counts walks with their edge labels and also works for loops and \(a=1\).
Applying the trace congruence to the integral exterior powers gives every signed coefficient of the characteristic polynomial, including the constant term.

The resulting lower bound \(q^{ad}\) combines with the upper bound \(\ell^d\exp(nB/q)\), since \(\gamma^q=\beta\) in all embeddings and all three numbers are units.
