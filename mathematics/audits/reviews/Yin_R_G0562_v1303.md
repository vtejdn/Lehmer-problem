## The shorter closure has strictly smaller Mahler measure

The [example](../../claims/Yin_C_G0904_v8.md#example) gives the stated irreducible degree-twenty Salem polynomial and a shorter closure of measure strictly between one and its Salem root.
For \(Q=x^5-x^3-1\), reversal gives \(Q^*=1-x^2-x^5\).
Expanding \(x^{15}Q+Q^*\) and pairing exponents about ten yields \(x^{-10}P=D_{10}-D_8-D_5\), with \(D_k=x^k+x^{-k}\).
The recurrence in \(y=x+x^{-1}\) gives the displayed \(H\) coefficient by coefficient.
At the ten listed points its reduced numerators in order are \(2,-3834357299,1175501,-8410076,1,-14735899,613,-1,1229,-3154157299\).
The nine disjoint intervals thus contain distinct roots in \((-2,2)\), and \(H(2)=-2\) forces a tenth root above two by monicity.

In \(\mathbb F_3[y]/(H)\), repeated cubing gives \(y^9\) at step two, \(-y^9+y^8+y^7-y^6+y^4+y^2+1\) at step five, and \(y\) at step ten.
After subtracting \(y\), the respective Euclidean divisions against \(H\) end with monic remainders \(y^2-1,y,1\) and \(y^2-y,y+1,1\).
Hence \(H\bmod 3\) divides the squarefree polynomial \(y^{3^{10}}-y\), and its factor degrees divide ten.
The two gcds exclude degrees one, two and five, so \(H\) is irreducible modulo three and over \(\mathbb Q\).
The nine interior trace roots provide real embeddings of \(\mathbb Q(y)\) at which \(y^2-4<0\), excluding a square in the trace field.
The quadratic lift therefore has degree twenty over \(\mathbb Q\).
Its nine interior traces give eighteen conjugates on the unit circle, and the trace above two gives a positive reciprocal pair.
Consequently \(P\) is an irreducible Salem polynomial and its measure is its sole exterior root.

Direct substitution gives \(4^{20}P(5/4)=-891643099999\), \(10^{20}P(13/10)=2200540778275984425901\), and \((13/10)^3-13/10-1=-103/1000\).
The unique exterior root is thus between \(5/4\) and \(13/10\), below the plastic number since \(t^3-t-1\) is increasing for \(t\ge1\).

Multiplying the [degree-ten polynomial](../../claims/Yin_C_G0904_v5.md#record) by \((x-1)(x^6+x^3+1)=\Phi_1(x)\Phi_9(x)\) gives \(x^{17}-x^{15}-x^{12}+x^5+x^2-1=x^{12}Q-Q^*\).
Independently, \(x^{-5}L=y^5+y^4-5y^3-5y^2+4y+3\) takes the values \(-1,3/32,-1,3,-1\) at \(-2,-3/2,-1,0,1\), respectively, and takes the value \(-1\) at two.
It has four roots in \((-2,2)\) and one above two, so \(L\) has exactly one exterior root and this root is positive.
Since \(L(1)=-1\) and \(16^{10}L(19/16)=330753263801>0\), the shorter closure has measure \(M(L)\) strictly between one and \(19/16<5/4\).
Here \(5<12<15\), and the degree-ten competitor already excludes a strict record for \(P\).
This finding covers the explicit example and the stated degree-ten input.
