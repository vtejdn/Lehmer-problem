### Elimination with a reflected Laurent polynomial

Write \(Y=x^p\) and \(G_{\rm ref}(x)=x^rG(1/x)=V^\iota(Y)+x^rU^\iota(Y)\).
Reciprocity gives \(P(1/x)=x^{-\deg P}P(x)\), and the powers of \(x\) are units in the Laurent ring, even for negative \(r\).
Thus \(P^t\) divides both \(G\) and \(G_{\rm ref}\).
The exact elimination is
\[
 U^\iota(Y)G(x)-V(Y)G_{\rm ref}(x)=H(Y).
\]
Every conjugate \(\gamma\) has \(\gamma\ne0\), so neither the Laurent monomials nor the substitution derivative \(p\gamma^{p-1}\) change its local order.
Consequently \(H\) has order at least \(t\) at each \(\gamma^p\).
The hypothesis on the degree makes these powered values distinct and makes their monic product over conjugates the polynomial \(Q\), with \(m(Q)=ph\).
Shifting the least exponent of \(H\) to zero preserves its measure and these orders.
Division by the monic \(Q^t\) gives an integral nonzero \(K\) and \(m(H)=tph+m(K)\).
The quotient can contain additional copies of \(Q\).

For example take \(P=x^2-3x+1,\ p=4,\ r=-1,\ Q=Y^2-47Y+1\).
At a root \(\gamma\), the identity \(\gamma^4=21\gamma-8\) preserves degree and gives the displayed \(Q\) since the two conjugates have product one and fourth-power trace \(47\).
With \(U=21Q,\ V=-(Y+8)Q\), one factor \(P\) comes from \(Q(x^4)\) and another from \(21-x^{-1}(x^4+8)\).
Expansion gives \(Y^3H=-8Q^3\), so \(t=2\) gives \(K=-8Q\).
This checks the distinction between the imposed multiplicity and the actual quotient with a composite power, negative offset and a common row factor.

### Finiteness and size of the excess

On the circle \(H=|U|^2-|V|^2\), with possible equality confined to the finite zero set of the nonzero Laurent polynomial \(H\).
The function \(\log a\), \(a=\max(|U|,|V|)\), is integrable by its constant upper bound and the integrable lower bound \(\log|U|\).
Outside finitely many zeros of the rows and \(H\), \(0<\rho<1\) and \(-\log(1-\rho^2)=2\log a-\log|H|\).
The right side is integrable, so the circle average \(L\) is finite and strictly positive, including when the two rows share roots.

In the fiber \(x^p=Y\), the phases from \(x^r\) run through all \(p\) possible values because \((p,r)=1\).
The product of the values of \(G\) there is \(U^p-(-1)^pY^rV^p\).
The ordinary and reverse triangle inequalities bound its modulus divided by \(a^p\) between \(1-\rho^p\) and \(1+\rho^p\).
For \(0<\rho<1\) one has \(\log(1+\rho^p)\le-\log(1-\rho^p)\le-\log(1-\rho^2)\).
Uniform averaging over the fibers followed by circle integration bounds \(|p(m(G)-I)|\) by \(L\), where \(I=\int\log a\).
Substituting \(2I=tph+m(K)+L\) proves both sides of (1), with a strictly positive lower excess since \(p>2\).
Here \(m(K)\ge0\) by integrality, and \(h>0\) for a nontorsion algebraic unit.
For any sequence of eligible inputs with fixed \(t\) and \(p\to\infty\), the lower bound on the excess above \(tph/2\), divided by \(ph\), is the sum of \(m(K)/(2ph)\) and \((1/2-1/p)L/(ph)\).
The latter coefficient is eventually bounded away from zero, so convergence to \(t/2\) forces both normalized contributions to vanish.
Their simultaneous vanishing suffices by the upper bound, even when \(h\) varies towards zero.

### The four occupied classes and both actual cofactors

For the four-class seed the choice \(r=-v,\ U=a,\ V=\sigma Y^B b(1/Y)\) gives exactly the indicated \(G\).
One has \((v,p)=1\) since \(p\) is prime and \(1\le v<p\).
Both rows are nonzero, and \(VV^\iota=bb^\iota\).
The extreme exponents of the difference are \(\pm B\), with the same coefficient \(a(0)\operatorname{lc}(a)\ne0\) since \(\deg b<B\).
Thus the unique monomial normalization is \(T^B\) and \(\deg\widetilde H=2B=8e\).
The seed multiple contains \(P_0^2\).
Powering preserves the degree \(e\) of a seed root, and the powered minimum \(Q_0\) is reciprocal with constant coefficient one.
Monic division by \(Q_0^2\) leaves exact degree \(6e\), with constant coefficient \(a(0)\operatorname{lc}(a)\).
Reversing the factorization in these exact degrees and cancelling \(Q_0^2\) proves that \(K\) is reciprocal.

The four summands in \(F=A(x^p)+\sigma x A^*(x^p)\) occupy the pairs \((0,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,0)\) of residues modulo \(p,n\), respectively, when listing \(a,b,a^\iota,b^\iota\) in that order.
Indeed \(pu\equiv1\bmod n\) and the extra \(x\) in the reflected row adds one to its exponent.
All four components are nonzero and their classes are distinct.
With \(T=Y^n\), let \(c=(a(T),\sigma Y^{nB-u}b(1/T))\) and \(d'=(Y^ub(T),\sigma T^B a(1/T))\).
Rotation \(j\) evaluates the two rows at \(\zeta^{jp}Y\) and multiplies the second entry by \(\zeta^j\), giving exactly \(c+\zeta^jd'\).
The oriented determinant for \(i<j\) is \((\zeta^j-\zeta^i)\det(c,d')\), where
\[
 \det(c,d')=\sigma T^B(aa^\iota-bb^\iota)=\sigma Q_0(T)^2K(T).
\]
The normalized product over \(N=\binom n2\) pairs is therefore \(\sigma^NQ_0^{2N}K^N\) in the seed variable.
The full powered minimum is \(Q_0(Y^n)\), so the quotient prescribed by the orbit determinant is exactly \(\sigma^NK^N\), with normalized logarithmic measure \(m(K)\).

The disjoint classes preserve primitivity for both seed and lifted diagonals.
The double divisors have logarithmic measure \(2h\) in both polynomials, and deleting cyclotomic factors from the two integral primitive quotients has zero measure cost.
Any extra nontorsion copies remain in their respective cofactors.
Using \(t=2\) and subtracting \(2h\) gives the seed term \((p-2)h\), with the remaining contribution \(m(K)/2+L/2+\epsilon\) and \(|\epsilon|\le L/p\).
The strictly positive circle contribution gives \(m(R_G)>(p-2)h\).
For the lift, the two-sided seed comparison has \(s=pB,\ m(G)=O(ph)\) and \(\log(npB+1)=o(ph)\).
It follows that \(m(R_F)-m(R_G)=O(ph/n)\) for each witness uniformly, proving its stated lower bound and applicability for either sign.
Along a sequence with \(h\to0\), division by \(p\) makes the base term and the transfer error tend to zero.
The upper estimate on the remaining terms is \((1/2+1/p)(m(K)+L)/p\), so a positive uniform lower bound for \(m(R_F)/p\) would require an order-\(p\) lower bound for \(m(K)+L\).
The decomposition leaves that lower bound open.
