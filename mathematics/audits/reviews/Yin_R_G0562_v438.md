## Endpoint, projection and mixed splitting results

From there one can use any endpoint \(A\).
Indeed \(r(N)\le r(AN)=r(A)\), and equality at \(N\) is equivalent to every factor there remaining irreducible under all substitutions.
Applying the coprime pullback with either of two endpoints in the role of \(A\) shows their gcd is again an endpoint.
Taking the gcd of the endpoint divisors of \(A\) thus gives an endpoint dividing every other one.
For \(k\mid A\), descent to \(A/k\) is possible precisely when each terminal support consists of multiples of \(k\).
In that case a split in any descended factor under substitution by \(X^n\) would give a split in the corresponding terminal factor under the same substitution by applying \(X\mapsto X^k\).

Distinct seeds cannot acquire a common root under substitution, so their positive multiplicities do not change the lcm rule.

For projection, the embeddings of \(\mathbb Q(\beta)\) above each embedding of \(\mathbb Q(\beta^k)\) give the common repetition \(e\) in (2).
