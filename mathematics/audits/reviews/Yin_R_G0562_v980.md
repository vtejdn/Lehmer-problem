## Independent assessment of the degree and density formulas

The proof establishes the stated formulas throughout their respective ranges.
At a zero of \(A\), writing \(t=u-u^{-1}\) gives \(t=3,4\) and \(v=-(t+3)(t+4)\), impossible for a unit.
The absolute coefficient sum of both quartics is 26, and dividing the equation by \(v\) bounds \(|A(u)v|\) by \(|u|^2+|A^*(u)|\) when \(|v|>1\).
Averaging the displayed estimate over embeddings subtracts a nonnegative logarithmic norm and yields the claimed height bound since \(u,v\) are units.
For \(d=[\mathbb Q(\alpha):\mathbb Q]\le 2t\le W\), v795 covers both \(d\ge16\) and the smaller nontorsion degrees.
For \(l>4k\), substitution gives \(t>(l-4k)(\log\log W/\log W)^3/(8\log 27)\), which suffices with the stated rounding.
Every smallest proper rational factor contributes its degree to each subset-sum set by monicity and squarefree reduction.
For an irreducible trace polynomial the upper comparison with \(n\) follows from the definition of the minimum.

On the steep sequence, \(B^2/n\to\infty\) gives \(r<B\) for all sufficiently late sections.
In the first pattern the singleton cycles contribute at most \(r\), so every admissible size below \(m\) must use a cycle of length \(m-1\), possible only if \(m\) is even.
The second pattern excludes the odd sum \(m-1\) because every odd cycle there has length at least \(m+1\).
For \(m=n\) the default value in the minimum gives equality even though \(n\) lies outside the search interval.
The product cycle classes are stable under the joint group, as each rational block is preserved.
Even when a symmetric class splits in an alternating group, the entire prescribed class belongs to the alternating group and its full size counts.
The distinct even cycle lengths on blocks of size at least six give the stated centralizer orders, bounded by \(2n\) per block.
The kernel obtained by imposing splitting in a cyclotomic field contains the full alternating product, so both proportions retain the same lower bound.
With fixed section and congruences, omitting finitely many bad primes and repeated coordinates has limiting cost zero.

In the irreducible case the cyclotomic fiber projects to a trace coset of size at least \(n!/2\) with equal multiplicities, even if the residue class forces a sign.
The joint law of four independent trace permutations therefore gives each event at most sixteen times its unrestricted symmetric probability.
Proposition 2.1 on page 3 of the [revised paper](../../literature/pdf/EFG17--arxiv-v2.pdf) covers common invariant sizes in \((j/2,j]\) for each integer \(j\le n/2\).
Writing \(N=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), start at \(j=B\), double through \(j\le N\), then use \((N/2,N]\) for the terminal remainder.
This covers the endpoint \(B\) and sums to \(O(B^{-c})\) with absolute constants, independently of the cyclotomic moduli.
The interval is empty if \(B>N\).
A reducible section in the eventual reciprocal range has a proper trace factor and hence cannot pass the four-prime test.

Finally the roots of each eventual rational factor occur in inverse pairs, so its degree is twice its trace degree.
The uniform error in \(2l h(\alpha)=C+o(1)\) bounds every factor measure between \((C\pm o(1))n_i/l\), and taking the minimum preserves these relative bounds since \(C>0\).
Replacing \(l\) by \(n\sim l\) gives the claimed equivalence along steep sequences.
