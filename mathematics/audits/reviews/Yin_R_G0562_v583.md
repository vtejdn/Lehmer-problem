### The additional equation at a proposed numerator zero

Exchanging the evaluation and Euler slots at the repeated root gives the same multiplicative base and opposite determinant signs, leaving their nonzero index difference.
Every other permutation has a lower weight, apart from this exchange.

Their relative scalar then has the form \(\pm c\xi\) for a root of unity \(\xi\), whereas the two nonzero index differences are rational integers.
The unit \(c\xi\) is irrational, since a rational algebraic unit is \(\pm1\) and \(c\) is nontorsion.
This calculation also works when the target is the only inverse pair.
