## The cyclotomic completion and the cyclic homology ranks

The support and Mahler measure bounds hold for every stated bundle, and one integral symplectic action attains all the stated homology minima for each polynomial satisfying the realization hypotheses.

For a closed hyperbolic manifold, Corollary 1.3 bounds the least number of tetrahedra in a compact link exterior by \(K V\), with the empty link allowed.
The constants are absolute once the three-dimensional Margulis constant and neighborhood radius are fixed. [Kobayashi and Rieck, p. 2](../literature/KR11/readme.md)
Subdivision in dimension three and the cells attached for the boundary cylinders cost only a bounded number of cells per tetrahedron.
In the cellular argument, a primal spanning tree and one edge of nonzero character give one end binomial.
The internal dual graph surjects on the fundamental group of the exterior, which surjects on that of its filling, so it also contains a cycle of nonzero character.
Attaching the boundary prisms as leaves in the dual minor preserves its binomial up to a Laurent unit.
The infinite cyclic cover has the homology of the closed oriented fiber, with orders \(t-1,P,t-1\) in degrees zero, one and two.
The complementary determinant therefore has the two end binomials times \(P/(t-1)^2\) up to a signed Laurent monomial, including when some core characters vanish. [Cellular construction and integral torsion](../../claims/Yin_C_G0883_v2.md#bounded-volume)

Each retained target-core row occurs only in columns for its own boundary edges.
For a nonzero core character \(c\), scaling that row by \(t^c-1\) yields the difference of two monomials on the core and leaves the three other incidences in the column untouched.
For \(c=0\) the entire core path contributes a single integer coefficient times a monomial, also for negative winding.
Choosing one incidence summand per column gives at most \(5^s\) choices because each choice determines the rows and hence at most one determinant permutation.
The two end binomials divided by \((t-1)^2\) give the two stated geometric sums, and each scaled row gives one additional binomial after removing Laurent units.
There are at most \(s=O(V)\) such rows.
These factors are monic cyclotomic products, so collecting terms and taking the minimum over completions proves the support bound.
Descartes' rule applies separately to the completion at \(t\) and \(-t\), since multiplication preserves at least the multiplicities of the original real zeros.

After removing a signed Laurent unit the completion is monic and has nonzero constant term.
If \(M(P)>1\), it has at least two terms, its measure is \(M(P)\), and Dobrowolski's Theorem 1 applies to the entire completion. [Dobrowolski, p. 202](../literature/Dob06/Yin_U001.md#sparse-measure)
For \(k\ge2\), \(\log(a_0 3^{\lfloor(k-2)/4\rfloor}k^2\log k)\le C k\) with an absolute \(C\).
Thus \(k\le e^{bV}\) yields the claimed triple exponential after increasing the absolute constant, and continuity gives the same bound at the infimum.
Taking three logarithms in the indicated range gives the stated lower bound for volume.

For the homology assertion, reversal followed by negation preserves the recurrence and its initial values once \(u_g=1\) is computed.
This proves alternation, and reciprocity gives the recurrence in each argument of the form.
In the block \((u_{g+j-i})_{0\le i,j<g}\) the entries below the diagonal vanish and the diagonal entries equal one, which makes the alternating form unimodular.
The shift preserves the form because simultaneous translation leaves \(j-i\) fixed, and the unit constant coefficient of \(P\) makes the shift invertible over the integers.
Its cyclic power basis gives characteristic polynomial \(P\).
Genus \(g\ge2\), irreducibility over the integers, noncyclotomicity and the exclusion of \(Q(x^k)\) supply all hypotheses for the homological criterion after lifting through the surjective mapping class action.
Hyperbolization applies to the resulting closed pseudo-Anosov bundle. [Margalit and Spallone, pp. 853, 855–856](../literature/MS07/readme.md) · [Thurston's theorem in Rivin, Theorem 2.6](../literature/Riv14/readme.md)
For the indicated strict degree records, a proper-power substitution would produce a lower-degree polynomial of equal Mahler measure, contradicting strict improvement. [Record realization](../../claims/Yin_C_G0883_v2.md#mapping-tori)

The mapping-torus homology sequence adds one base dimension to the dimension of the fiber coinvariants.
For this cyclic lattice the reduction is cyclic at every prime, so the latter dimension is exactly \(\deg\gcd(\bar P,x^j-1)\).
For any competitor the lengths in an \(h\)-primary part sum to \(v_h(\bar P)\).
Quotient by \(x^j-1\) truncates each length at \(v_h(x^j-1)\), so their sum is at least the corresponding single truncated length.
This proves simultaneous attainment including inseparable reductions and \(p\mid j\).
The gcd degree is bounded by \(j\), giving the asserted ceiling on the normalized excess.
