## Clearing denominators and transferring the period valuations

On 2026-11-20 at 10:57 UTC a separate reviewing agent for Sheng Yin examined the [fixed statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v528.md#statement) and both parts of its proof independently of their development.

On a column basis, restriction to the rows of a nonzero maximal minor is injective, so its adjugate clears the coordinates of the entire integral output with a single positive denominator.
Restriction to these rows can only decrease the Euclidean norm of each column, and all omitted integer columns have norm at least one.
Hadamard's bound therefore gives the asserted cost even if the original translates are dependent or have widely separated supports.

At each lifted component, [sum-period divisibility](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v504.md#statement) applies before multiplication by any Laurent shift, which evaluates to a unit there.
The degree restriction excludes every retained cyclotomic factor of the primitive output.

For each order, weighting these individual inequalities by the residue degrees uses at most \(e\) copies of the same resultant valuation.
Across all orders the total residue degree is again at most \(e\), so the subtraction charges the clearing integer at most once with weight \(e\), as in the [component count](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v524.md#mass).
The [circle bound](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v524.md#circle) incurs its error once per root of the output, hence with its actual span \(d\) even when this exceeds the input width.
Dividing by \(eT\) proves (1), and \(d\le4L\) makes the circle error negligible uniformly because \(w/K\) dominates \(\sqrt L\log L\).

For primitive monomial output the cyclotomic resultants are all one, so the transfer inequality directly gives (2), with no loss in its constant.
The bounds \(T\gg K\Sigma/g\) and \(Kh/e\gg j\) then imply (3), and \(H>1\) follows from the positive lower bound for the monomial clearing cost whenever such a representation exists.
In the ordinary full prime interval the [order layers](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v513.md#layers) constrain every represented integer by the same local valuations, since Laurent multipliers are integral at the witnessing units after clearing.
The [remaining mass estimate](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v513.md#remove) with the calibrated \(h/e\gg j/\log L\) and \(W/\Sigma\asymp\log L\) gives the asserted coefficient count there as well.
