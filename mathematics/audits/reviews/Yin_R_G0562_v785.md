This is the nullity contribution for multiplication by \(\Phi_{p^a}(X^q)\), since its reduction is \((X^q-1)^{p^{a-1}(p-1)}\).
Each such kernel dimension bounds the valuation of its nonzero integral determinant from below by Smith normal form.
The factors indexed by the distinct prime powers dividing \(h\) are separate integer factors of \(X^{hq}-1\), so their logarithmic contributions add to that of \(X^q-1\), even when \(p\mid q\).

For fixed \(p\), summing the valuations through each \(t<n\) gives the displayed digit-sum correction to \(pC_n/(p-1)\).
It is nonpositive and contributes \(O_p(\log n/n^2)\) to the objective, independently of \(k\).
With \(b=p\log p/(p-1)\), positivity implies \(n-1>k\log n/b\), hence \(n>k\log 2/b\) and then \(n>k\) for all sufficiently large \(k\), uniformly over all positive candidates.
