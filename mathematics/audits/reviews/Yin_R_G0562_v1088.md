This gives \(m(P)\ge\lambda_{\rm exc}/\varphi(b)\), and \(\log\|P^aC\|_1\ge a\,m(P)\) by integral-cofactor Mahler multiplicativity.

That maximum is independent of \(P\), so taking the outer infimum preserves it.
