## Integer measure bounds, uniform divergence and the degree-eighteen spectrum

At 2026-11-24T22:56 UTC an agent outside the development of v70 independently reviewed the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v70.md#statement) and all five proof sections for Yin.
The reviewer received the fixed claim and scope without development dialogue or previous conclusions about v70.
The bounds hold for arbitrary torsion twists and roots of products, the mixed measures diverge uniformly under the large-group hypotheses, and the three listed measures exhaust the open interval \((1,2)\) for the degree-eighteen example.

**Normalization on coordinate lines.**
For \(n\ge3\) the even sign subgroup separates each pair of coordinate characters by flipping one coordinate and a third.
Character projection and transitivity make the signed module irreducible, and its nonzero image in the multiplicative classes of the roots is isomorphic to it.
In particular the signed root classes are all distinct, so the conjugates remain distinct under any nonzero integral power.
The primitive generator of the saturated line in \(\mathcal O_{K_i}^{\times}/\mu(K_i)\) has index \(e\) for \(\alpha_i\), the same by conjugation for all labels.
After killing torsion, a positive power of this generator is a nonzero power of \(\alpha_i\), so it generates \(K_i\) and has total measure \(\lambda\).
Changing its torsion representative does not affect its class or height.
Every element of the divisible group is a unit since a positive power is a product of units up to torsion.

In a common normal field the subgroup fixing \(K_i\) averages the \(i\)-th class to itself and averages every other coordinate to zero.
For the latter assertion a flip at any chosen other coordinate and at a third is available while fixing the actual root \(\alpha_i\).
Norm transitivity therefore gives \([N_{E_i/K_i}w]=[E_i:K_i]q_i[\rho_i]\).
The left side lies in the unit lattice of \(K_i\), giving the integer \([E_i:K_i]q_i\) with its sign.
Reorienting positive coefficients makes the stabilizer of the actual \(w\) permute its selected oriented roots while preserving their weights.
For an orbit \(O\) with representative \(i\), its cardinality is \([E_i:\mathbb Q(w)]\), and \(d|O|=2n[E_i:K_i]\).
Summing over orbit representatives proves \(J\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), even when the degree of \(w\) exceeds the size of its orbit modulo torsion.

**Heights at the actual degree.**
The absolute log moduli of the oriented primitive generators sum to \(\lambda\) and have \(k\) nonzero entries.
Over each coset of the even sign subgroup, any two sign coordinates are independent for \(n\ge3\).
Thus \(\mathbb E|S|\ge|\mathbb E(\epsilon_i S)|=|u_i|\) for the signed sum \(S\).
At most \(\ell\) terms are nonzero, so the sum of their absolute values costs at most \(\ell\,\mathbb E|S|\).
Transitivity in the permutation average and the product formula \(m(w)=(d/2)\mathbb E|\log|g(w)||\) give (1).
For \(n\ge5\), any four signs are independently uniform since an unused label can restore parity.
The second and fourth moment calculations and interpolation give \(\mathbb E|S|\ge\|u\|_2/\sqrt3\), including the zero-vector case by direct evaluation.
The support size supplies \(\|u\|_2\ge\|u\|_1/\sqrt\ell\).
Taking the better bound proves the \(B_\ell\) refinement.
On a coordinate line the first sign-average bound is an equality, and \(\rho_i\) realizes \(J=1\).
The competitor of degree \(2n\) and measure \(\lambda\) gives the two strict inequalities for a record of greater degree, using first attainment as well as minimality.

**Removing denominators without losing measure.**
In the common normal field the class-stabilizer fixed field \(F\) lies in \(\mathbb Q(w)\), since the actual stabilizer fixes the class.
Put \(c=[\mathbb Q(w):F]\).
All factors of \(v=N_{\mathbb Q(w)/F}w\) are torsion-equivalent to \(w\), so \([v]=c[w]\), \(h(v)=c h(w)\) and the rational coefficient vector of \(v\) is \(Q=cq\).
Any automorphism fixing \(v\) restricts to a class stabilizer on \(L\).
As \(v\in F\), this proves \(\mathbb Q(v)=F\), giving \(m(v)=m(w)\) and degree \(b\) for this representative.
This descent places the representative inside \(L\) even when the initial radical or torsion factor is outside \(L\).

A double flip with a zero coordinate isolates \(2Q_i[\rho_i]\) in proper support.
For full support, applying \(1-f_{ij}\) and \(1-f_{il}\) with distinct \(i,j,l\) leaves \(4Q_i[\rho_i]\).
The representatives are quotients of actual conjugate units in \(L\).
The subgroup fixing \(K_i\) changes each representative only by roots of unity in \(L\), so its \(t\)-th power is in \(K_i\).
Saturation of the line in that field gives the asserted denominators \(2t\) and \(4t\) for \(Q_i=cq_i\).
On an axis the class stabilizer is the stabilizer of the corresponding oriented root because its conjugates have distinct classes, so \(F=K_i\) and \(Q_i\) is already integral.

Transitivity of \(A_n\) on each size of subset and all supported sign choices from an unused label give \(b\ge2^s\binom ns\) for \(s<n\).
For \(2\le s<n\) its lower endpoint bound is \(2n(n-1)\), by the decreasing successive ratios and comparison with \(n2^{n-1}\).
Averaging any fixed supported component gives \(\mathbb E|S|\ge\lambda/(2tn)\), whence \(m(v)\ge b\lambda/(4tn)\).
For full support even flips give \(b\ge2^{n-1}\) and every permutation contributes \(\mathbb E|S|\ge\max_i a_i/(4t)\ge\lambda/(4tk)\).
For \(s=2\), averaging the two signs gives the larger absolute summand.
Each of the \(\binom n2\) target pairs occurs equally often, yielding \(m(v)\ge t^{-1}\sum_{i<j}\max(a_i,a_j)\).
Pairs meeting exactly one nonneutral label contribute \((n-k)\lambda\), and pairs meeting two contribute at least \((k-1)\lambda/2\), as stated.

**Roots of unity and uniformity.**
All double flips have the same image in the abelianization and have order dividing two.
The triangle of three double flips has product one, so their common image is trivial.
The remaining image of the sign kernel has order at most two.
For trace action \(A_n\) the quotient by that image is trivial, and for \(S_n\) it is generated by the image of a lift of a transposition.
The square of this lift is an even sign vector.
Thus the abelianization has exponent dividing two, as does the quotient acting on \(\mathbb Q(\mu_t)\).
Reduction to odd primes forces their only possible divisor to be three, and the unit groups modulo nine and sixteen exclude those prime powers.
This verifies \(t\mid24\).
At \(n=50\) the proper-support multiplier is at least \(49/48>1\), and \(2^{n-1}/(192n)>1\) from that point on.
Both mixed multipliers grow at least linearly for large \(n\).
At fixed height rank two, distinct primitive coordinate units are multiplicatively independent, have individual height \(\lambda/(2n)\), and have joint field degree \(D\le4n^2\).
Amoroso–David Theorem 1.6 gives a lower bound for their product of heights with \(\kappa(2)=3(3!)^2-2=106\), hence exponent \(212\) before the square root.
Substitution yields \(\lambda\gg(\log(12n^2))^{-106}\) with an absolute constant.
Consequently the **total** mixed measures tend uniformly to infinity.
The axis competitor and the actual-degree bounds prove the record and attainment assertions for \(n\ge50\).

**The measure spectrum in the example.**
For the fixed trace polynomial, direct evaluation gives \(Q(2)=Q(-2)=1\).
The product of the nine oriented square roots of \(t_i^2-4\) is therefore \(\pm1\), fixing the parity of sign changes to be even.
With the double flips already present the sign kernel is exactly the even subgroup and dies in the abelianization, which is thus \(S_9^{\rm ab}\).
The sole quadratic subfield has nontrivial Frobenius at thirteen since the trace cycle pattern \((2,7)\) is odd.
The prime splits in both \(\mathbb Q(i)\) and \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\), excluding both subfields.
Together with \(t\mid24\) and \(-1\in L\) this gives \(t=2\).
The degree-eighteen unit \(\rho_i\) in the same group has radial rank two, so the established degree-55 minimum gives \(\lambda\ge m(\alpha)\) and forces \(e=1\).
The support-two multiplier is \(7/2\).
For \(3\le s\le8\) the smallest orbit bound is \(672\), giving multiplier at least \(672/72>7/2\), while full support gives \(16/3>7/2\).
The exact differences \(12193^7-4\cdot10000^7\), \(12193^4-2\cdot10000^4\) and \(2\cdot2500^3-3049^3\) are all positive.
The verified rational cuts thus place every mixed measure above \(\log2\) and exactly the first three positive axis multiples below it.
Every such multiple is attained by an ordinary power since all powers generate the original field.
