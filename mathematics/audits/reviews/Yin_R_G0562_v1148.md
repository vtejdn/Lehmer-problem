## Common phase correction, the height of a shortest class and all finite thresholds

At 2026-11-25T00:09 UTC an agent outside the proof development independently reviewed for Yin the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v88.md#statement) and all four proof sections of C0901 v88.
The reviewer received the fixed proof and its mathematical inputs without development discussion, author explanations or previous findings about v88.
The reduction inside \(D(w)\), the bound \(b\le18\), the reducible bound \(d\le1152\), inequality (1) and the strict uniform bound \(d<9\,000\,000\) hold.
They establish a finite search domain, with the original measure allowed to decrease in the chosen representative.

Choose the least possible degree with \(0<m(w_0)\le m(w)\) inside the given divisible group, and then a least measure at that degree by coefficient finiteness.
The negative conjugation eigenspace on its submodule has dimension at most two, and the plastic minimum excludes dimensions zero and one below \(59/50\).
The v80 fiber product for any nonzero rational equivariant quotient has positive measure at most \(m(w_0)\), lies in the original divisible group, and has degree exactly the number of distinct projected roots.
Thus every such quotient separates the \(d\) roots, the original classes are distinct, and every nonzero constituent is faithful because a kernel element would fix all projected points and thereby all original roots.
For a pair of distinct roots the submodule generated by their difference of classes cannot be proper, since its nonzero quotient would identify the pair.
For \(l\) equivalent irreducible summands identify their projections equivariantly with one \(W\).
If \(s\ne1\), take a root \(a\) moved by \(s\).
The \(l\) projections of \([a/sa]\) lie in \((1-s)W\), and any dependence over \(\mathbb Q\) gives a nonzero equivariant coordinate combination onto \(W\) annihilating that difference.
This contradicts separation and proves the multiplicity bound.
The negative dimensions add to two, and the fixed-dimension floor of v76 rules out any constituent with zero negative dimension.
Thus the reducible case has two inequivalent constituents, each of negative dimension one.
For negative dimension two and \(r\ge19\), or negative dimension one and \(r\ge10\), the real fraction in v76 is at least \(11/19\).
Its height floor exceeds \(1/6>\log(59/50)\), confirming \(b\le18\).

For a nonzero lattice class \(u\) with stabilizer \(P\), any conjugate \(s\) of complex conjugation in \(P\) sends a representing monomial \(\beta\) to \(\eta_s\beta\) with \(\eta_s\in\mu(L)\).
Each such \(s\) inverts the roots of unity, so \(st\) fixes torsion and multiplies \(\beta\) by \(\eta_s/\eta_t\).
The order of this ratio divides \(\operatorname{ord}(st)\).
The common fixed subspace of \(s,t\) has codimension at most four and is fixed by \(st\).
Since the action is rational, finite and faithful, the order of \(st\) is the least common multiple of orders of its nontrivial cyclotomic eigenvalues, using at most four dimensions.
An order of totient four uses the entire budget and is \(5,8,10\) or \(12\).
With only eigenvalue orders \(2,3,4,6\), the only additional possible least common multiple is 12.
This recovers the complete list \(1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,12\).

I checked the joint conclusion for the phases, which is stronger than a bound on individual pairwise orders.
If two phases differ at 5, they have equal 3-primary parts and equal 2-primary parts modulo \(\mu_2\), since the only allowed orders divisible by five are five and ten.
Any third phase differing at either of these shared components would violate the order list with at least one endpoint of the pair.
All phases therefore belong to a single translate of \(\mu_{10}\).
In the absence of 5-variation, an order-eight pair agrees at three and has distinct 2-primary parts modulo \(\mu_4\).
A third phase differing at three would have order divisible by 24 against at least one endpoint, so this case gives a translate of \(\mu_8\).
In the remaining case every quotient has order dividing twelve.
With a faithful reflection constituent, integral pairings of equal-length primitive normals on its lattice restrict products of distinct reflections to orders two and three, and pairwise orders cannot use both against one base phase since that would force order six.
This gives the smaller common exponent \(j\le3\).

For the first use of the correction, choose \(\lambda^2=\eta_{s_0}\) in the roots of unity and a finite normal field containing it and \(L\).
Any conjugate of complex conjugation there above a counted \(s\) inverts \(\lambda\), so its ratio on \(\lambda\beta\) is \(\eta_s/\eta_{s_0}\), killed by the same \(j\).
Uniform conjugation restricts with the correct multiplicity and gives height \(j h(\beta)\ge F(p)/2\).
For the second use, \(t\in P\) sends \(\beta\) to \(\xi\beta\), and \(s_1=t s_0t^{-1}\) belongs to the same counted set.
The two factors \(\xi\) cancel in \(t(\beta s_0\beta)=\beta s_1(\beta)\), and the remaining ratio is killed by \(j\).
Consequently the actual stabilizer of \(z=(\beta s_0\beta)^j\) contains \(P\), and its class \(2ju\ne0\) forces the reverse inclusion.
This proves \(\deg z=[G:P]\) and \(h(z)\le2j h(\beta)\) in the original splitting field.

The quotient metric on \(V\) from \(\mathbb Q^d\) is rational and invariant, with \(\sum_i x_i\otimes x_i=I\) and all \(\|x_i\|^2=b/d\).
The finitely generated full lattice admits a shortest nonzero \(v\) of length at most \(\sqrt{b/d}\).
In its rational frame expansion, Cauchy's inequality gives \(\sum_i|(v,x_i)|\le\sqrt{d\sum_i(v,x_i)^2}=\sqrt d\,\|v\|\).
Any integral monomial representative of \(v\) differs, after a common positive power, by torsion from the monomial supplied by this expansion.
Height homogeneity and subadditivity give \(h(\beta)\le\sqrt b\,m/d\) without a new degree or denominator factor.

For the random conjugate involution \(s\), the squared displacement is four times the quadratic form of its negative projection on \(v\).
The conjugation average is scalar \(j_i/r_i\) on each rational constituent; the operator is orthogonally diagonalizable in the invariant metric.
A displaced shortest vector moves by at least its length, so the fraction fixed is at least \(1-4\max_i(j_i/r_i)\).
For irreducible \(b\ge9\) this is at least \(1-8/b>0\).
The common phase exponent at most twelve now yields \(d\le24\sqrt b\,m/F(1-8/b)\).
With two constituents of dimensions at least five the corresponding fraction is at least \(1/5\), and their faithful reflection action permits \(j\le3\), giving \(d<700\).
If one faithful reflection constituent has dimension at most four, its group embeds in the full automorphism group of its normalized simply laced root system with transitive component action.
The possible systems are the single classical \(A\)- and \(D_4\)-types, \(A_1^s\) and \(A_2^2\) in the stated dimensions.
Their order bounds include diagram automorphisms, with maximum \(2^3\,4!\,6=1152\) at \(D_4\).
Thus \(d\le1152\) throughout the reducible case.

For the remaining irreducible small dimensions, if no conjugation fixes \(v\), they cannot all send it to \(-v\).
Otherwise \((g\beta)\overline{g\beta}\) is positive and torsion for every \(g\), contradicting nontorsion by Kronecker.
Choose one \(s\) with \(sv\ne-v\) and set \(u=v+sv\).
This is a nonzero fixed class of \(s\) with \(\|u\|^2=4\|v\|^2-\|v-sv\|^2\le3\|v\|^2\) since \(sv\ne v\) in this case.
The case of a fixed \(v\) uses \(u=v\) directly.
In both cases a representative has height at most \(2\sqrt b\,m/d\), and distinct orbit points of \(u\) are separated modulo \(4\Lambda\) since their distances are at most \(2\sqrt3\,\|v\|<4\|v\|\).
The exact-stabilizer unit therefore has degree at most \(4^b\) and height at most \(48\sqrt b\,m/d\).
If \(d>48\sqrt b\,4^b\), its degree and total measure would both be smaller, contradicting the original degree choice in \(D(w)\).
Independent rational comparisons using \(y-y^3/6\le\operatorname{arsinh}y\) confirmed the lower bounds on \(F(11/19),F(1/9),F(5/9),F(1/5)\), the cutoffs \(85\,000\), 107 and 700 in their respective branches, and \(48^2\cdot8\cdot4^{16}<9\,000\,000^2\).
These cover every allowed constituent configuration and require no enumeration of candidate units.
