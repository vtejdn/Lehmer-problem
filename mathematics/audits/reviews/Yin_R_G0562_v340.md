### Sign of the clipping error

When \(m(P)<L\), reciprocity up to sign gives \(P=U+\sigma X^nU^*\) with \(n=N-a\).
On the unit circle \(|U|=|U^*|\), and linear Jensen gives \(m(U)\) for the independent mean before division by the common row gcd.
Dividing by the integral Laurent gcd \(h\) subtracts \(m(h)\) from that mean and the diagonal mean.
The quotient rows have joint width at most \(a\) by width additivity, and a common Laurent shift leaves both means invariant.
Thus \(d=a\) is an admissible support bound, including when the quotient rows are constant.
For \(a\ge1\), the primitive independent logarithmic mean \(I=m(U)-m(h)\) is nonnegative and at most \(m(U)\).

Let \(b\) be the integral of the logarithmic normalization \(\log A\), and let \(I_u,\mu_u\) denote the clipped relative means of the quotient.
The two inequalities \(I_u\ge I-b\) and \(\mu-b\ge\mu_u-C_*\{u+(1+I)/D\}\) keep the two losses in their respective directions.
At a fixed fast angle the \(n\) slow preimages have equal weights.
Comparing that shifted grid with the full circle costs at most the quotient variation divided by \(n\), giving \(\mu_u\ge I_u-512\log(2/u)/D\).
Combining these inequalities cancels \(b\), and restoring \(m(h)\) and using \(I\le m(U)\) gives the one-sided inequality in the claim.
Only the diagonal clipping loss is needed.

For \(u=1/D\), the error is \(\{C_*(2+m(U))+512\log(2D)\}/D\).
The assumed \(D\ge2^{218}\) is above \(D_*\), and increasing \(C_*\) to \(2^{217}\) leaves a positive coefficient of \(m(U)\) on the lower side.
If the blocks differ up to translation and sign, \(U^*\ne\pm U\) for a mirrored polynomial, so v337 gives \(m(U)\ge L\).
This proves (2) in the subplastic case, and \(m(P)\ge L\) gives (2) directly.
For \(a=0\), subplastic reciprocity itself forces a signed pair of monomials.

### The rational threshold

Any cover by two intervals of length \(W<N/2\) must cover zero and \(N\) with distinct intervals, placing all exponents in the prescribed end bands.
For \(W\le2^{-226}N\) and \(a\ge1\), \(D=N/a-1\ge2^{226}-1\ge2^{225}\).
Because \(L<1\), the two power terms in (2) divided by \(D\) are less than \(3/256\).
The function \(\log(2D)/D\) decreases on this range, and the logarithmic term is bounded above by \(512\cdot226/2^{225}<1/1024\).
The total error is strictly less than \(13/1024\).
At \(33/25\), the plastic cubic has value \(-313/15625\) and is strictly increasing above one, so \(\theta>33/25\).
Integration of \(1/t\) from \(1\) to \(66/65\) gives \(\log(66/65)\ge1/66>13/1024\), the latter comparison using \(13\cdot66=858<1024\).
Thus \(L-\log(13/10)\) exceeds the entire error.
This proves the claimed implication even at \(M(P)=13/10\).
For a signed copy, a nonconstant low block gives a factorization into two nonunits, while a constant low block at this cutoff has absolute value one and gives zero logarithmic measure.
The strict irreducible lower bound follows.
