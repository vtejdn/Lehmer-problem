## Finding: the complete obstruction is supported

No unresolved mathematical gap was found in the complete statement, argument, source applicability or stated consequences.
The claimed nonexistence holds for every integer \(r\ge2\) under the exact same-space natural-representation hypothesis.

Every individual coordinate sign change is an actual D4 lattice isometry, so its associated coordinate projection preserves any invariant real subspace.
The argument therefore identifies such a subspace with the span of some coordinate axes, rather than assuming that an arbitrary invariant subspace is a sum of whole D4 blocks.
The within-block and whole-block permutations are transitive on those axes, forcing the subspace to be zero or the whole space.
Irreducibility of a containing group follows in the stated direction: a subspace invariant under the containing group is invariant under the subgroup.

The hypothesized group's reflecting normals span the entire space because a complementary fixed space would contradict that irreducibility and the nontrivial sign changes.
Taking both normalized normals of every reflection mirror supplies a finite root set closed under the actual reflection action by conjugation.
It generates the given group, since that group is assumed to be generated by reflections.
The accepted simple-root correspondence thus applies in its full essential rank \(4r\), without assuming crystallography, integrality of the containing group, or that the original coordinate basis is a simple-root basis.
If the diagram were disconnected, the orthogonal spans of its simple-root components would give nontrivial invariant subspaces.
The diagram is consequently connected, so the irreducible finite classification is the correct one to use.

For rank \(4r\ge8\), the complete list leaves only \(A_{4r},B_{4r},D_{4r}\), together with \(E_8\) when \(r=2\).
No noncrystallographic family is omitted: the dihedral systems have rank two and the exceptional H systems have ranks three and four.
The A model acts on its sum-zero subspace of dimension n and has order \((n+1)!\); confusing that dimension with its coordinate count would change the subsequent comparison, but the argument uses the correct convention.
The B and D orders count all signed permutations and those with even sign parity, respectively.

The faithful block action has order \(1152^r r!\), with no central quotient or overlap between independent block factors and their permutation action.
The factorial valuation formula follows from the displayed factor count and digit expansion, and yields
\[
v_2(|G_r|)=8r-s_2(r),\qquad
v_3(|G_r|)=2r+v_3(r!).
\]
Multiplication by four shifts the binary digits and adding one appends a final one, giving the two digit identities used in the A and D cases.
The binary valuation for \(A_{4r}\) is smaller than the required value by \(4r\), and that for \(D_{4r}\) is smaller by exactly one.
These are direct failures of the necessary subgroup order divisibility, independent of the relative total sizes of the groups.

For \(B_{4r}\), the binary valuations do agree, as the argument acknowledges.
The ternary difference is instead
\[
v_3((4r)!)-v_3(r!)
=\frac{3r-s_3(4r)+s_3(r)}2
\le\frac{4r-1}{2}<2r.
\]
Both digit bounds hold for every positive integer r: its base-three digit sum is at most r, and the digit sum of the nonzero integer \(4r\) is at least one.
This strict inequality contradicts the additional \(2r\) powers of three in the block-group order.
The comparison is uniform in r and does not infer an infinite-range conclusion from finite checks.

The accepted exceptional order factors exactly as
\[
696729600=2^{14}3^5 5^2 7.
\]
At \(r=2\), the subgroup's binary valuation is 15 and the E8 group's is 14, excluding the only remaining exceptional case.
For \(r=1\), the direct D4 generators furnish the indicated finite F4 reflection group, so that endpoint is correctly excluded from the nonexistence statement.

The conclusion concerns a finite reflection group containing the entire natural positive block action on the same \(4r\)-dimensional Euclidean space.
The proof neither assumes nor proves that every possible improvement of the canonical-cover permutation cost must have that form.
Larger ambient spaces, different positive representations, neutral extensions, radical quotients, indefinite constructions and mechanisms that need only a smaller subgroup remain outside the obstruction.
The statement therefore strengthens the earlier coordinate-mixing failure at its actual mechanism scope while preserving the existing D4 cover and leaving the unrestricted Lehmer Task unresolved.
