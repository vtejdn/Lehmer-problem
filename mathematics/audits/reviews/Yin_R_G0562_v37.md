Write \(\gamma_\ell=bw_\ell\) at the neutral embeddings.
The product of the \(m-1\) positive summands \(\gamma_\ell/(2-x_\ell)\) is \(N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\gamma)u/(\gamma|S(2)|)\), including repetitions if \(\gamma\) lies in a proper subfield.

\[
 \frac{\gamma}{mu}\ge

The norm is a positive integer whenever \(\gamma\) is integral.
Since \(u<1\) eventually, the neutral logarithm estimate gives \(0\le m^{-1}\log|S(2)|\le m^{-1}\sum_{\ell<m}\log(2-x_\ell)\) eventually, and the right side has upper limit zero.
The prefactor tends to one, independently of the inverse and of the positive multiplier \(b\), which may vary with the input.

It follows that \(e\to\infty\) along every subsequence of bounded normalized traces.
