## Uniform power windows, irreducible traces and endpoint exclusions

At 2026-11-17T02:33 UTC a separately assigned Codex reviewer for Sheng Yin checked the entire C345v126 submission fixed at 2026-11-17T02:27 UTC.
The reviewer had no role in producing this version and examined the fixed claim, its cited inputs and the scope of each conclusion.

For a fixed Salem number of degree \(2m\), Galois transport of a representative of one neutral inverse pair to the exterior forces its exponent in any multiplicative relation modulo torsion to vanish.
It keeps all other representatives on the circle, since inversion commutes with transport.
This proves independence, and hence distinction of the powered conjugates and product-arcsine equidistribution as in C345v52.
All torsion nodes to be avoided arise from orders bounded in terms of \(m\), since their trace degrees are \(\varphi(q)/2\) for \(q\ge3\), and \(\varphi(q)^2\ge q/2\).
For distinct representatives \(v,w\), the distance between the powered traces is exactly \(|(v/w)^n-1|\,|(vw)^n-1|\).
For a fixed torsion node of order \(q\), the two analogous factors each exceed or equal \(|v^{nq}-1|/q\) by the geometric sum.
Each nontorsion base involved is fixed, its angular modulus is one, and an algebraic \(a\)-th root scales its absolute logarithmic height by \(1/a\).
That root remains nontorsion, cannot be rational on the circle and meets the required height bound for a fixed sufficiently large \(a\).
Dim16-U001 applies with exponent \(an\) or \(anq\) at least two.
It gives the polynomial lower bound on the minimum distance after absorbing constants and the two factors into one exponent.
This uses the audited statement of the logarithmic-form estimate as an external input.

Independently, write the neutral deviations from a tested endpoint \(x_i\pm d_n/3\) as the product of its distance \(d_n/3\) from \(x_i\) and its distances at least \(2d_n/3\) from every other neutral root.
The endpoint lies strictly inside \((-2,2)\), so the remaining factor has modulus at least \(T_n-2\).
Each marked interval therefore retains its sign change after every positive shift in the displayed strict window, simultaneously.
The additional change between \(2,T_n\) exhausts degree \(m\), including the one-neutral-root case \(m=2\).
A proper factorization would leave an integral monic neutral factor, whose reciprocal lift has unit constant and, by Kronecker, only torsion roots.
Its trace roots lie in the avoided finite set, so the full trace is irreducible.
At a neutral real conjugate \(r^2-4<0\), forcing the quadratic extension of its totally real trace field and giving the claimed Salem degree.
Choosing \(C>(m-1)K\) absorbs the fixed constants in the root window.
For fixed \(\delta>0\), the excluded neighborhoods of the torsion nodes and pair diagonals have boundary of product measure zero, and their measures tend to zero with \(\delta\).
Off these neighborhoods \(H_n=o(T_n)\) eventually fits the entire window.
Thus the upper density of the failures is zero for the prescribed sequence, without requiring one set of exponents for every sequence.
Finally \(h=o(T_n)\) puts the exterior trace in \((T_n-1,T_n)\) by the sign at \(T_n-1\), even when \(m=2\).
Each neutral factor at that trace is \(T_n(1+O(T_n^{-1}))\) uniformly in \(h\), giving the relative error claimed for its displacement.

In the growing-degree family the \(q=p+1\) midpoint angles sample \(\sin(q\theta)\) at alternating values \(\pm1\), and the factor \(N-x\) strictly exceeds \(N-2\) there.
The \(q-1\) sign changes and the negative value at two therefore localize all \(q\) roots, also when \(|c|=N-2\).
For every root \(u\) of a hypothetical all-neutral factor, each conjugate of the nonzero algebraic integer \(V_p(u)\) would have modulus \(|c|/(N-u')<1\).
Its norm cannot be a nonzero integer, proving full irreducibility.
Evaluation at the exterior trace for the smaller parameter, together with the negative sign at two, gives strict ordering.
At fixed positive distance on either side of \(N\) within \(x>2\), the hyperbolic expression for \(V_p\) grows to positive infinity, proving the bounded limit.
The zero shift of the unperturbed product has only the rational exterior trace \(N\) and the explicit \(q-1\) torsion nodes.
The parent \(F_{-1}\) therefore gives no genuine Salem factor at level \(-1\), and at level \(-2\) gives the full smaller Salem output.

For the rational-node obstruction, any rational solution at an integer level is integral by monicity.
When \(6\mid q\), the nodes \(-1,0,1\) all lie at the unsuccessful level \(-1\).
For the endpoints it suffices to compare \(g(x)=(N-x)V_p(x)\) with \(g(-2)=-(N+2)q\) and \(g(2)=(N-2)q\).
Inside the interval, \(|V_p|<q\) and \(N-x<N+2\) give the strict lower bound.
For \(x\ge0\), factoring \(\sin(q\theta)\) relative to \(\sin(2\theta)\) gives \(|V_p|\le qx/2\), and \((N-x)x/2<N-2\) for \(0\le x<2,\ N\ge4\), also at \(N=4\).
For \(x=-2\cos\theta<0\) where \(V_p(x)>0\), oddness forces \(\theta>\pi/q\).
The chord bound \(\sin y\ge3y/\pi\) on \([0,\pi/6]\) implies \(|V_p(x)|\le q/3\).
Here \(N-x<N+2\le3(N-2)\) provides strictness even at \(N=4,q=6\).
Hence neither endpoint level has any open neutral trace, which every genuine Salem factor would require.
As a separate exact check, the recurrence at \(N=4,\ p=5,11\) gave the five node levels \((35,-1,-1,-1,-13)\) and \((71,-1,-1,-1,-25)\).
For each of the four endpoint fibers, Sturm counting found one root in the closed interval, the corresponding endpoint itself.
