## Mahler measure two on a quadrilateral with parallel sides

I checked the side orientation, the Haar projection for two independent characters and the necessity and sufficiency of each row of the table.
Jensen integration in the transverse coordinate reduces equality to pointwise domination of the opposite binomial by \(2+a z^m\).
For \(|a|=2\), the \(m\) zeros force \(m\mid n\), equal opposite coefficient magnitudes \(B\) and the stated relative sign.
The norm of the geometric sum of length \(n/m\) then gives \(B(n/m)\le2\).
For \(|a|=1\), the squared mean restricts the opposite magnitudes to \(\{1,2\}\) or \(\{1,1\}\).
At equality of squared means the nonconstant Fourier coefficient forces \(n=m\) and \(bc=2a\).
In the unit-magnitude case averaging over \(z^m=-\operatorname{sgn}(a)\) gives divisibility, and evaluating \(3-4\cos\phi+2\cos(k\phi)\) at \(\pi/k\) excludes every \(k=n/m\ge3\).
At \(k=1,2\) the two displayed nonnegative expressions give sufficiency with the listed signs.
The basis claim is in the lattice generated by the support, and a finite-index embedding preserves its torus measure.

I checked the translation of the table to the positive exponents of an [escaping eight-term sequence](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v93.md#eight).
The [sum-of-offsets result](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v96.md#reciprocal) excludes equal sides.
For unequal sides the two displayed polynomials give the actual coefficients on expansion, for either ordering of \(d,e\).
The first is primitive and irreducible by coprimality of \(2+s x\) and \(1-x^2\) and linearity in \(y\).

For the second shape, \(\gcd(e,e+d)=\gcd(d,e)\) and \(e+d\ge(e+2d)/2\) give the required unbounded reduced pair.
Dividing the actual denominator by the integral unit-constant binomial gives an integral formal series \(X_i\).
The [base and mixed-row tests](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#integer), [slope extinction](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#extinction) and [annihilation](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#singular) use only coefficients of \(X_i=S_iJ_i\) inside the common prefix and the actual signed inverse measure.
For bounded \(v/p\), I checked the first-index congruences, including \(v-p=o(p)\), and the three bounded partial multipliers in the [block estimates](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v72.md#all-intervals).
For \(v/p\to\infty\), the [first-marginal tests](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#hierarchy) at \(v+lp\) and the intermediate-scale tests all stay below \(h\).
The resulting [local bounds](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#support) suffice for the \(O(v)\) shifts in both representing measures.
The geometric expansion of \(1/S_i\) contributes at most two to the local absolute sum at each of its first \(1+\lfloor C\rfloor\) powers of the upper term.
The [comparison](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v87.md#return) uses the positive real part of \(1/S_i\) in the disk and the long Taylor agreement.
Its global identity is polynomial in the actual \(B^\sharp\), even if \(X_i\) is an infinite series.
I verified the [two-cutoff bound](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#cutoff) and the passage by \(|W_i|^2\le32|S_i|\) to bounded \(L^1\) norm for \(U_iW_i^3B^\sharp\).
The [integer support estimate](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v50.md#window), [fixed-support gap](../literature/DS17/Yin_U001.md#ds17-u001) and [branch identity](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#second) yield the two asserted qualitative bounds.
The argument excludes the reducible unequal-side shape and leaves the first irreducible shape as a candidate within the scope of this ledger.

## Classification at minimum \(m-1\), uniform separation and pairwise exponent growth

For a nonconstant one-variable specialization with constant \(m\), Jensen excludes interior zeros of \(T\), and the positive minimum excludes boundary zeros.
The strong maximum principle applied to \(1/T\) gives the strict interior inequality needed for \(E=T-(m-1)\).
Its constant one and integral leading coefficient force all roots onto the circle and give leading coefficient \(\sigma=\pm1\).
I checked that the Cayley transform has positive real part and that approaching a boundary pole over all inward angles forces order one and positive real coefficient of \((1-z/\zeta)^{-1}\).
Consequently \(\zeta E'(\zeta)\) is a nonzero real number.
Differentiating \(z^nE(1/z)=\sigma E(z)\) and using conjugation at each root gives \(\zeta^n=-\sigma\).
Simplicity gives \(E=1+\sigma z^n\), and both resulting forms for \(T\) satisfy the required bounds.

For an arbitrary marked vertex, the strict exposing cone contains an integral functional avoiding all collisions.
Its translated specializations have constant \(m\) at every phase, logarithmic measure at least \(\log m\), continuous logarithmic measure and Haar mean exactly \(\log m\) by the [phase-translation argument](../../claims/Yin_C_G0900_v1.md#endpoint).
At the trivial phase the circle minimum is at least the torus minimum, so the one-variable classification applies with every term and coefficient preserved.
This inequality for the minima also transfers an upper bound for the specialized minimum to the original polynomial.

For the bounded-support separation I checked that the [height bound](../literature/DS17/Yin_U002.md#sparse-height-bound) fixes a coefficient tuple after extraction, and the [stabilized lattice](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v97.md#translations) preserves distinct terms and the exposed constant.
[Lawton convergence](../literature/Den09/Yin_U002.md#den09-u002) gives \(M(F)=m\) for the fixed limit polynomial.
The integrals of characters on the specializing circles converge to their Haar integrals because each nonzero frequency eventually has nonzero evaluation.
Weak convergence therefore forces the circles to meet every open set of positive Haar measure eventually.
If the minima tended to \(m-1\), continuity would force \(\mu(F)\ge m-1\), contradicting the classification for the retained three or more terms.

Finally, on a subsequence with bounded reduced ratio a fixed pair of lower exponents spans one rational direction, and the remaining one is independent by the [rank-two reduction](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v93.md#eight).
I checked the positive multiples in that direction and the Haar projection giving \(T(x)+cy\).
Jensen in \(y\) bounds its log measure below by \(\log M(T)\ge\log2\).
Equality forces \(\mu(T)\ge |c|\), so the classification excludes the trinomial at \(m=2\).
The converse about a zero entry of the unique relation follows by reducing the fixed two-coordinate relation to coprime entries.
The [nonzero coefficient-sum result](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v98.md#relation) excludes collinearity of the three nonconstant points, while pairwise independent directions exclude collinearity of triples containing zero.
The same Jensen argument proves the final generalization to an independent term with \(|c|\ge m-1\).
I find the stated classification, qualitative separation and necessary exponent conditions valid.
