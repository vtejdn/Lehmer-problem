## Uniform irreducibility conditional on the rational conjecture

At 2026-11-20T12:20 UTC, a separate reviewing agent for Sheng Yin checked the full argument of [Claim v544](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v544.md#statement).
I received the candidate and review scope, without participating in its development or receiving earlier findings or exploration notes concerning this proof.
The uniform single-factor implication and its exclusion of the critical sequence follow with the rational specialization conjecture for the one displayed polynomial as their hypothesis.

[Conjecture 2.1 in the published article](../../literature/pdf/AS18--published.pdf), pp. 364–365, supplies two finite sets depending on the input Laurent polynomial.
Outside the hyperplanes specified by the first set, some nonsingular matrix from the second set factors the direction and gives the irreducible rational factorization after removing the cyclotomic parts, with the exponents inherited from the pullback factorization.
Theorem 2.4 concerns a function field and removes constant factors.
The three differences between the exponent of the term \(-U\) and those of \(-U^2,UV,UW\) are independent, and direct reversal gives \(U^2VW H(U^{-1},V^{-1},W^{-1})=H\).
Thus the fixed input is noncyclotomic and self-inversive.

In the Laurent coefficient ring, every prime of the diagonal constant coefficient divides \(D=V^m-U^m-1\) exactly once because \(U\) and \(\partial_V D\) are units.
A prime common to both coefficients would divide \(U^{2m}+U^m+1\), hence be associated to some \(U-c\), whereas substituting \(U=c\) leaves a polynomial monic in \(V\) in \(D\).
Primitivity and Eisenstein therefore give irreducibility for every diagonal pullback, including \(m=1\), and localization in \(W\) preserves it.
For a general integral isogeny matrix \(M\), its signed adjugate takes any factorization of its pullback to one of the diagonal pullback at \(m=|\det M|\).
The induced map is injective on exponent vectors, so both nonunit factors would still have at least two support points.
This proves irreducibility over \(\mathbb C\), hence also over \(\mathbb Q\), with exponent one.

For each nonzero relation \(a(u+sv)=-bw\), coprimality forces either \(|w|\ge a\) or \(w=0\) and \(|u|=s|v|\ge s\).
Taking \(L\) to be the maximum of 24 and the norms of all listed normals works for every coprime positive offset \(b\).
The identity \(H(T^a,T^{as},T^b)=T^a F_{s,a,b}(T)\) introduces only a Laurent unit.
At a common seed zero \(t\), the equations would give \(t^s=1+t\) and \(t^{s-1}=-t\), forcing \(t^2+t+1=0\) and \(t^s=-t^2\), with incompatible orders.
Thus the [two-row estimate](../../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v148.md#two-row) applies with \((p,S,n)=(a,b,s)\), including multiplicities.
The unique highest term has coefficient \(-1\) and degree \(N=as+b\), and the constant term is \(-1\).
The normalized quotient consequently has positive degree in \([N-24s,N]\), and the conjectural factorization contains exactly one irreducible factor with multiplicity one.

Along a [critical sequence](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#statement), the stated lower bound on \(\log a\) ensures that the threshold is eventually crossed.
Since \(24s/N\le 24/a\to0\), the sole noncyclotomic factor would have degree asymptotic to \(N\asymp as^3\), incompatible with the required degree \(\asymp as^2\).
Conversely, a sequence with reducible quotients and \(\min(a,s)\to\infty\) would disprove this fixed instance even without the critical assumptions.
The two-row bound and the critical estimates were checked for applicability at their stated scopes.
The geometric irreducibility and degree estimates are unconditional, while the rational irreducibility and critical exclusion depend on Conjecture 2.1.
