A reciprocal minimal polynomial with constant term a unit has nonzero residue roots, and inversion preserves its roots at each prime.
Consequently an exact Laurent relation gives both residue evaluations even when translation puts a surviving exponent below zero.
For a fixed term sum the equal reciprocal products give equal values of \(x+x^{-1}\) for \(x=\beta^u,\beta^v\), since \(ce\) is invertible.
The roots of the resulting quadratic coincide as unordered pairs, also in characteristic two and at a double root.
Each order \(s\) therefore divides one of \(u-v,u+v\).
Every \(s\) divides the same integer \(u^2-v^2\), so its joint least common multiple divides that integer even for overlapping orders with different choices of sign.

For equal gaps put \(h=i-i'\).
If either of \(c+e\beta^{u}\) and \(c+e\beta^{-u}\) is nonzero, its corresponding evaluation forces \(s\mid h\).
If both vanish then \(\beta^{u}=\beta^{-u}\) and \(c^2=e^2\), giving \(s\mid 2u\).
Thus \(M\mid 2uh\), equivalently \(M/\gcd(M,2u)\mid h\).
Distinct coefficient squares at every selected prime force \(M\mid h\).
For odd \(M\) the factor two can be canceled, and all orders at primes of characteristic two are odd because they divide \(2^f-1\) for some \(f\).

The positive gaps give \(\lvert u^2-v^2\rvert\le W^2-1\).
The position \(i\) lies in the same one of \([-W,-1]\) and \([1,W]\) as \(i'\) because its label has fixed order relative to zero.
Hence \(\lvert h\rvert\le W-1\).
The two strict inequalities of the general criterion first force equal gaps and then force \(h=0\), including when the divisor of \(2uh\) is even.
The weak endpoint \(M=W^2\) suffices with odd \(M\) because \(\lvert uh\rvert\le W(W-1)<W^2\), and with distinct squares because \(\lvert h\rvert<W^2\).
For characteristic-zero recovery a vanishing two-term sum would give a nonzero rational power value \(\alpha^k\), \(k>0\), fixed by inversion, whence \(\alpha^{2k}=1\).
The prescribed order and nonzero sum therefore determine both erased positions by the reciprocal recovery formula.
