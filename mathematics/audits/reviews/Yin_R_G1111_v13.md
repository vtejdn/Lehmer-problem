### Neutral trace multiplicities and groups

For \(m\ge3\), the primitive \(m\)-th roots occur in distinct conjugate pairs and give \(\varphi(m)/2\) distinct trace values in \((-2,2)\).
Different orders cannot share one of those trace values, since a trace determines its reciprocal pair and hence its order.
Thus an irreducible cyclotomic multiplicity \(e_m\) becomes the same trace multiplicity, contributing \((e_m-1)\varphi(m)/2\) to the excess interior multiplicity count.
At the endpoints, \((z-1)^2=z((z+z^{-1})-2)\) and \((z+1)^2=z((z+z^{-1})+2)\).
Consequently the actual cyclotomic multiplicities at orders one and two are twice the trace endpoint multiplicities.
The DP interface stores squared endpoint atoms, so its recorded powers already give the endpoint trace multiplicities.
The implementation's conversion, \(B\), endpoint fields and three repetition classes agree with these facts.

For \(C=1+z^r\), the orders dividing \(2r\) but not \(r\) give the exact squarefree cyclotomic product.
Here every direct residual degree is even, so neither endpoint occurs and all ten direct neutral templates have \(B=0\).
