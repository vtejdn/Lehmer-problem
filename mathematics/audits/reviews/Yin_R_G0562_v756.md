## Trace interpolation, irreducibility and equality of conjugate moduli

At 2026-11-20T18:55 UTC a Codex reviewer for Sheng Yin checked the full argument of C0345v619 and its application to the length-504 binary prefix.
The reviewer was outside proof development, read the fixed claim and the two cited prefix tests, and received no development discussion or earlier verdict on this claim.
The construction realizes all the prescribed integral prefixes at arbitrarily large common degrees, including \(k=1\), when interpolation below degree \(k\) is empty.

For \(1\le j<k\), the residues \(sj\) for \(0\le s\le r\) are distinct modulo each chosen prime.
Consequently the difference of the two \(p\)-traces in the proposed element is \(p\) precisely at the selected coordinate and vanishes elsewhere, while the \(q\)-trace is \(-1\) throughout.
Interchanging the primes gives a second multiple of the same basis vector, coprime to the first.
A prime divisor \(t\) of \(\Phi_{4pq}(4pq)\) is prime to \(4pq\), and separability of \(X^{4pq}-1\) modulo \(t\) makes its cyclotomic factors disjoint, giving exact order \(4pq\) for \(4pq\bmod t\).
In the split residue ring the trace is the sum of the coordinates, and complex conjugation exchanges coordinates at inverse roots.
Raising primitive \(pq\)-th roots to \(j\) permutes them, so after deleting the selected inverse pair the remaining Vandermonde columns have rank \(r\).
The integral trace correction by a multiple of \(t\) preserves both prescribed zero coordinates.

In degree \(j\) only a single linear trace contribution can involve the as yet undetermined \(a_j\).
The other contribution is a Galois-invariant algebraic integer, as required for the integral lifting step.
Avoiding the \(r\) forbidden residues modulo each prime ensures that \(t\omega^{b+sk}\) generates \(K\) at every twist.
The prime \(t\) is \(1\bmod4\), so \(t+1\) has an odd prime divisor \(\ell\), and a primitive \((t+1)\)-st root has inverse Frobenius conjugate of degree two.
For \(h=\ell^a\), if an element raised to \(h\) has order \(t+1\), all prime-to-\(\ell\) factors of its order are unchanged and its \(\ell\)-adic order exponent is \(v_\ell(t+1)+a\).
The order of \(t\) modulo \((t+1)h\) must be even, since it is two modulo \(t+1\).
For an even exponent \(2v\), the valuation \(v_\ell(t^{2v}-1)=v_\ell(t+1)+v_\ell(v)\) then gives least exponent \(2h\), also when \(a=0\).
This proves irreducibility of the degree-\(2h\) reduction and hence of \(F_h\) over \(K\), using integrality of coefficients of monic factors and the two zero coordinates for the low and reflected coefficients.

The inequality \(k<h<2h-k\) separates the prescribed coefficients, the central term and the reflected coefficients.
Each twisted conjugate factor has constant one, so two associates must coincide, and their degree-\(k\) coefficients distinguish the embeddings.
The Galois group permutes these distinct irreducible factors transitively, forcing any nonconstant rational divisor of their product to contain them all.
The norm of \(\omega\) is one by pairing inverse embeddings, giving both monicity and cancellation of the scalar in the reciprocal identity.
Each twisted root lies in \(K(\beta)\) and has rational degree \(2hD=[K(\beta):\mathbb Q]\), proving equality of its generated field with \(K(\beta)\).
The twists disappear on taking the \(pq\)-th power and preserve absolute values in each common field embedding.
The same fixed \(c\) dominates the two outside blocks for every embedding and every \(h\), giving circle modulus greater than two in each factor and the stated uniform upper bound by the triangle inequality.
Integrating the factor estimates proves both bounds on the total logarithmic Mahler measure.

For the example I counted 120 distinct positive differences among the sixteen positions in C0345v613.
Writing the binary multiplier as \(J_0(X^2)+XJ_1(X^2)\), the square test requires \(J_1=R J_0\bmod X^{252}\).
The constant term of \(J_0\) contributes all sixteen positions, and each further monomial of \(J_0\) cancels at most one of them.
The two parts of the multiplier therefore require at least seventeen nonzero coefficients in total, whereas the constant prefix passes with multiplier one.
Taking the integer lifts with constant one supplies the contrasting test outcomes at identical conjugate moduli.
The lower bound \(D\log 2\) holds throughout this family and leaves open the existence of excluded-prefix inputs whose total measure tends to zero.
