### Independent construction and Sturm verification

The checker extracts the trace polynomial by descending triangular subtraction of the binomial lifts of \(t^j\), independently of the author's \(z^k+z^{-k}\) recurrence.
The remainder vanishes and the complete reciprocal lift reconstructs every quotient coefficient.
This verifies the original polynomial, trace normalization and degree, rather than only a root-count surrogate.

For the independent Sturm calculation, an integer pseudo-remainder step replaces a current remainder R by
\[
 |\operatorname{lc}(B)|R
 -\operatorname{sgn}(\operatorname{lc}(B))
   \operatorname{lc}(R)t^s B,
\]
where s cancels the leading degree against B.
Each step multiplies the congruence class modulo B by a positive scalar, and every intervening content division is positive.
The final remainder is therefore a positive scalar multiple of the ordinary rational remainder.
Negation gives the next Sturm element with its correct sign.
This uses neither the author's rational remainder operation nor an imported counting function.

The author's positive-denominator and positive-content normalization satisfies the same sign requirement.
Its squarefree product reconstruction, derivative gcds and pairwise gcds justify summing all component counts with their recorded multiplicities.
For each of the three actual inputs, the independent chain terminates in a nonzero constant and proves squarefreeness directly.
