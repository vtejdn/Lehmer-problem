# Independent review of the all-degree measure bound at argument rank at most one

## Reflection normals, the common phase correction and the finite degree cutoff

At 2026-11-24T23:09 UTC an agent outside the proof development independently reviewed for Yin the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v74.md#statement) and all four proof sections of the final C0901 v74.
The reviewer received the fixed proof and its mathematical inputs without development discussion, author explanations or previous findings about v74.
The strict lower bound \(M(w)>13/10\) holds in every degree for all nontorsion units of argument rank at most one.
Every value below the plastic number \(\theta\) on this class has a representative of compressed degree at most 42, and a minimum is attained in \((13/10,\theta]\).

Below the plastic number Smyth gives reciprocity.
Torsion-class products preserve total measure and argument rank, separate conjugate classes and commute with inversion as a permutation of the reciprocal roots.
The action on the resulting lattice is faithful, since any element fixing the class of each actual conjugate fixes each conjugate itself.
If a rational constituent has no negative direction for \(c\), every conjugate of \(c\) fixes that constituent pointwise.
The product selected from the actual root orbit in v68 then gives a totally real nontorsion unit of height at most \(m/2\).
Garza at real fraction one gives \(m\ge\operatorname{arsinh}(1/2)=\log\phi>\log\theta\).
Below \(\log\theta\) complete reducibility thus leaves a single rational irreducible constituent, with negative dimension one.
If its rank were one then \(a\overline a\) would be positive and torsion for every original conjugate, forcing \(|a|=1\) throughout the orbit.
Kronecker excludes this case.

The rational negative line of each conjugate reflection meets the conjugate-class lattice in a primitive rank-one lattice.
All lines are conjugate, so the primitive normals have equal lengths in a common invariant metric, allowing one global normalization to squared length two.
Their rational span is invariant and nonzero, hence all of \(V\).
The reflection difference for any lattice vector \(x\) is \((x,v)v\), integral in the primitive line, so \((x,v)\in\mathbb Z\).
The finite set of normals is closed under its own reflections, and distinct nonopposite normals have scalar products \(0,\pm1\).
In a simple basis the only negative product is therefore \(-1\), so the normalized finite classification gives precisely \(A,D,E\) components.
Transitivity on normal lines gives isomorphic components and their Weyl groups lie inside the actual Galois action.
The average of the normal-line projectors commutes with every reflection and consequently preserves its one-dimensional negative line.
Transitivity gives one scalar on all these lines, which span \(V\), and trace one makes that scalar \(1/b\).
Against a prescribed line, the traces of overlap are one once, zero on perpendicular lines, and \(1/4\) on all others.
Solving \(1/b=1/C+(1-p-1/C)/4\) gives (1).
Every neutral root class lies on the negative line of \(c\), and all root classes have equal length.
After compression at most the two antipodal classes there can occur, hence at most one actual neutral inverse pair.

The integer \(K=\max_v |(x,v)|\) is positive since the normals span.
For a primitive normal \(v\) attaining it, any unit representing \(v\) has height \(h(w/s_v(w))/K\le2m/(dK)\).
This applies to representatives at every normal by conjugation, inversion and torsion invariance.
Fixing the normal of \(c\), perpendicular reflections act on a representative \(\beta\) by the phases \(\eta_s\).
Their product \(st\) acts on \(\beta\) by the phase \(\eta_s/\eta_t\) and fixes all torsion, because each restriction of a conjugate of complex conjugation inverts torsion.
For distinct such reflections their product has order two or three by the scalar products just proved, and faithfulness gives the same order in the splitting field.
Thus each phase quotient has order dividing two or three.
Relative to a fixed \(t\), quotients of exact orders two and three cannot coexist, since their own quotient would have order six.
One exponent \(j\le3\) therefore kills all phase quotients, with \(j\le2\) when the perpendicular lines are mutually orthogonal.
After adjoining \(\lambda^2=\eta_t\) with \(\lambda\) torsion, each lifted conjugation above one of the counted reflections fixes \((\lambda\beta)^j\) as an actual unit.
Restriction of uniform conjugation to the original splitting group preserves the uniform distribution, hence at least the fraction \(p\) of embeddings of this new unit are real.
The height bound becomes \(2jm/(dK)\) and Garza gives (2).
For any reflection fixing \(x\), compression already guarantees fixation of the original \(w\), yielding (3) without a phase correction.

At rank \(b\ge7\), (1) gives \(p\ge3/7\).
At rank six, \(A_6,D_6\) have at most 30 lines and the equal-component products \(A_1^6,A_2^3,A_3^2\) have at most twelve.
For \(E_6\), the six given roots have arms of lengths \(2,2,1\) in their Gram diagram, and satisfy \(x_6=x_7=x_8\).
The integral \(E_8\) lattice used for this embedding has 40 integer norm-two vectors in that subspace, supported in the first five positions, and 32 half-integral vectors by the equal-last-three and parity constraints.
Reflections in the six simple roots preserve the lattice and subspace, so there are at most 36 lines and \(p\ge5/12\).
The remaining reducible systems of ranks at most five are the equal \(A_1\) products and \(A_2^2\), with \(p\ge1/2\).
The full \(A_2\) root automorphism group has order twelve.
For \(A_3\), the subgroup permuting three of the four coordinates fixes the displayed nonzero direction.
Averaging the spanning orbit thus gives a nonzero orbit product of height at most \(6m/d\), fixed by three of the six conjugate reflections.
This gives \(d\le12m/F(1/2)\).

At rank five \(7/20\) is the smallest perpendicular fraction, attained in \(D_5\).
For \(K\ge2\) the resulting \(dK<71\) bounds \(d\) by 35.
For \(K=1\), an \(A_5\) weight has two coordinate levels and at most twenty coordinate permutations, with at most one further factor two from the diagram automorphism.
In \(D_5\) all coordinates are integral or all half-integral since every pairwise sum and difference is integral.
The two largest absolute values sum to at most one, leaving a single nonzero integral coordinate of magnitude one or all magnitudes \(1/2\).
All root automorphisms act by signed permutations in this rank, giving \(d\le32\).
The chamber argument for these automorphism bounds uses the Weyl action on chambers and the fact that an automorphism fixing a chamber permutes the simple basis and preserves its Gram diagram.

For \(A_4\), \(C=10\) gives \(p=3/10\).
When \(K=1\) two coordinate levels give \(d\le20\).
When \(K=2\) the five entries have at most three levels, so the possible permutation orbit sizes are \(1,5,10,20,30\).
The actual orbit is either one such orbit or the union of two of the same size, since the diagram quotient has order at most two.
The only resulting size above 40 is 60, which contradicts \(dK<115\).
At \(K\ge3\) the same inequality gives \(d\le38\).
For \(D_4\), the three normals perpendicular to \(e_1-e_2\) are mutually perpendicular, giving \(j\le2\) and \(p=1/4\).
For the torsion-corrected normal unit \(z\), a conjugation over the splitting field fixes \(z\) whenever its restricting reflection has line perpendicular to the chosen normal.
Conversely a real image fixes the oriented class after conjugating back, so the restricting reflection must have perpendicular line.
This holds for every lift to the field containing the torsion twist and gives precisely a quarter of the embeddings.
The log modulus functional is \(c\)-invariant on \(V\), so in the classical coordinates its dual vector has first two entries equal and can be written \((A,A,B,C')\).
The absolute evaluations are constant on the two orientations of any line, each line occurs equally often, and multiplication by \(j\) applies to every evaluation.
The sum for the three perpendicular lines is \(S=2|A|+2\max(|B|,|C'|)\), and the chosen line contributes zero.
The sum for the eight remaining lines is \(4(\max(|A|,|B|)+\max(|A|,|C'|))\), which is at least \(2S\) because the two maxima together are at least \(|A|+\max(|B|,|C'|)\).
Hence the real embeddings contribute at most one third of the unconditional mean \(2H\) for \(H=h(z)\).
The conditional mean of \(|\log|u||\) there is at most \(8H/3\), giving at most \(16H/3\) for the mean normalized factor \(|1-u^2|/\max(1,|u|^2)\).
The factors are at most two at the other archimedean places and one at finite places, and their full weighted product is \(e^{-2H}\) by the product formula at \(1-z^2\ne0\).
Arithmetic-geometric mean on the real quarter therefore gives \(e^{-8H}\le128H/3\).
Together with \(1-8H\le e^{-8H}\) this yields \(H\ge3/152\), and \(H\le4m/(dK)\) gives \(dK\le608m/3<58\).
In particular \(K\ge2\) gives \(d\le28\).
For \(K\le2\), integral coordinates either have a lone two in absolute value, or \(s\) ones, with respectively six or \(\binom{s}{2}+2\binom{4-s}{2}\ge3\) perpendicular lines.
In the half-integral case at least three absolute entries equal \(1/2\), also giving three distinct perpendicular lines.
Hence \(p_x\ge1/4\) and (3) gives \(d<37\), allowing the full diagram automorphism group in \(D_4\).
I checked the strict limits \(41,44,28,71,115,58,37\) by rational comparisons using the polynomial lower bound for \(\operatorname{arsinh}\), the listed rational lower bounds for \(2^{-1/p}\), and \(\log\theta<0.282\).
Rank six has \(d<44\), and in every other case the bound on \(d\) itself is at most 40.
The compressed nontorsion reciprocal minimum has even degree, so \(d\le42\) for all \(m<\log\theta\).

As a separate finite check I read degrees and coefficients directly from the native 944-row catalogue range, reconstructed traces by subtracting the centered lifts \(X^n(X+X^{-1})^j\) in decreasing \(j\), and counted the roots between \(-2\) and \(2\) by signed integer pseudo-remainder Sturm chains. [Catalogue edition and sign convention](../literature/Mos26/readme.md#complete-small-cores)
This computation used neither J0189 nor its imported J0188 routines, and agreed with the complete histogram for counts three through twenty-one.
The minimum was three at exactly lines 152, 158 and 159, of degrees eight, ten and ten.
Compression excludes a common exponent divisor greater than one because any such substitution introduces distinct roots with torsion quotient.
The published exhaustive range below \(1.3\) through degree 44 therefore covers every possible compressed core through degree 42 and contradicts its at most one neutral inverse pair.
The exterior root set is closed under complex conjugation, so the multiplicative measure of an algebraic unit is the product of its exterior roots up to a sign, hence an algebraic integer.
It cannot equal the nonintegral rational number \(13/10\), which proves strictness without using completeness at the catalogue endpoint.
This check uses the published completeness result and verifies the new count from the supplied coefficients.
The plastic unit has just one nonreal pair, hence argument rank at most one.
If that pair had torsion quotient a nonzero positive power would drop in degree, and in its cubic field would have to be a rational unit, forcing torsion.
This proves exact rank one and supplies the upper endpoint.
All possible lower values have compressed degree at most 42, giving a finite list by the elementary coefficient bound.
Together with the attained endpoint this proves existence of a minimum while leaving its precise value in the stated interval open.
