## Exceptional divisors and the two cutoffs

The fixed claim establishes (1), (2) and the graph assertions under their stated hypotheses.
For a prime outside the fixed set, the projective zeros of the reduced nonzero form number at most 56, including infinity.
The degree-sixteen slope map bounds the number of geometric torus points over each such zero by sixteen, even if some other points of the fiber lie on the boundary.
Monicity and unit constant term put every section root in a torus residue disk.
The chain rule identifies the tangent condition there since at least one exponent is a unit, and the primitive parametrization gives exactly one residue coordinate for every compatible torus point.

When the denominator \(l\) is a unit, the equation \(rUF_U+VF_V=0\) implies \(F_U\ne0\) on the smooth residue curve, including at \(r=0\).
Thus \(V\) is a parameter and unramifiedness means precisely that \(ds/dV\) is a unit for \(s=Vf'/f\).
Inverting this parameter lifts the point and gives \(V'(r)\) a unit constant term.
The binomial substitution from \(V\) to the section coordinate has unit derivative because \(1/l\) is a unit.
Neither this substitution nor the identity \(H''=-V'/V\) requires the numerator to be a unit.
The ordinary preparation therefore gives shallow valuation one or two deep centers, with multiplicities, at every prime under consideration.
Interchanging the coordinates gives the same argument at projective infinity.
At the simple ramification points the finite \(l/k\) chart of v822 has unit denominator and the prepared cubic of v848 gives shallow valuation two or an exact sum over three centers.

At most \(56\cdot16\cdot3\) centers are needed at each prime outside the fixed set.
Two primitive directions closer to one center than the prescribed threshold have determinant divisible by a prime power exceeding \(2KL\), whereas its absolute value is at most \(KL\).
This applies also to algebraic centers, by the ultrametric inequality in a common extension, and includes equality of a tested slope with its center.
Each remaining compatible disk contributes at most \(3\log(2KL)\), using \(p\le2KL\) for the shallow disks.
Distinct residue disks contribute zero cross-distance and a simple disk costs zero, so this accounts for every discriminant power.
A nonzero integer of absolute value at most \(C_F(K+L)^{56}\) has at most \(\log_2(C_F(K+L)^{56})\) distinct prime divisors, including none when its absolute value is one.
The primes of bad reduction and those annihilating the content lie in a fixed finite set, where the sum of \(p^{16}\log p\) is an \(F\)-dependent constant in v819.
Its exceptional directions and the rational projective zeros of the two nonzero forms add only a constant to the omissions, absorbed by \(\pi(Y)\ge1\).
