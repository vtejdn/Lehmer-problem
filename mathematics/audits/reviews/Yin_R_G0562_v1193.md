## Delay one gives the minimum over both signs and suffices for shrinking sequences of prescribed signs

The fixed-seed minimum, both strict monotonicities, their common limit and the same-sign reduction along a shrinking sequence follow for all the specified nondegenerate seeds.

At the marked real embedding, monicity and the single simple real root above one give the sign of the seed on either side of that root.
The degree of \(X^kP+eQ\) is \(k+\deg P\) at either sign.
The strict circle inequality and the exterior-root argument in v128 give precisely one exterior root counted with multiplicity, with positive real value above one and no boundary zero.
Thus each delayed polynomial also has negative sign between one and its exterior root and positive sign to the right.
These statements allow shared interior roots of the two polynomials in the construction.

Evaluation at \(\beta\) gives the sign of \(eQ(\beta)\) independently of the delay.
If nonzero, this places the sequence for each fixed sign entirely on one side of \(\beta\), with the two signs on opposite sides.
At \(x=R_{k,e}\), subtracting the previous equation from that at delay \(k+1\) leaves \((x-1)x^kP(x)\).
Since the prefactor is positive, the lower sequence has its old root strictly below the new root and the upper sequence has its old root strictly above the new root.
For any fixed test point strictly between one and \(\beta\), the seed term tends to minus infinity with increasing delay; at any fixed point above \(\beta\) it tends to plus infinity.
These pointwise signs bracket the two roots arbitrarily closely to \(\beta\).
When \(Q(\beta)=0\) the root \(\beta\) itself is present for both signs at every delay, and uniqueness excludes any other exterior value.
It follows in each case that the two-sign infimum is attained at delay one.

For the rational norm polynomial the marked exterior modulus contributes \(R_{k,e}\).
The marked interior moduli have product \(1/R_{k,e}\) by the unit constant term, so the opposite place contributes the same exterior product and the complex places contribute one.
This is the norm-polynomial measure \(R_{k,e}^2\), even if characteristic-zero factors repeat or the norm is reducible.
It gives the stated minimum for these norm polynomials without requiring equality with the Mahler measure of an individual root.

For variable seeds the uniform inequality from v128 uses the nondegeneracy at the complex places and coefficient trace degree at least two.
It gives \(\beta\ge B_2>1\) regardless of the seed degree, the coefficient field and the delay.
Hence a sequence of exterior roots tending to one eventually lies on the lower side, excluding both the upper-side and shared-root alternatives there.
The same sign at delay one then has a root greater than one and no larger than the original, so it also tends to one.
Conversely delay one belongs to the family, giving the gap reduction within the prescribed construction.
This yields no unconditional gap for that family or for unrestricted integer polynomials.
