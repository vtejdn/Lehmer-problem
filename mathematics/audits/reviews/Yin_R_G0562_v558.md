### Exhaustion of the box

The integral Euler exponents of the polynomial give these congruences, and Möbius inversion recovers integral Euler exponents from them.
Each admissible vector thus determines its integer coefficients through the center and a unique reciprocal polynomial.
At \(s_1>0\) the sign representative is already determined.
At \(s_1=0\), I computed the total for each \(s_2\), subtracted a second count with every odd-indexed variable set to zero, and divided by two.
These are exactly the exclusions and sign orbits used by the program, since an odd \(j>16\) has proper divisors at most 16 and \(B_j<j\), forcing \(s_j=0\) when the earlier odd sums vanish.
