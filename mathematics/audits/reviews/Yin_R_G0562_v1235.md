# Review of integer evaluations on five neighborhoods of interlacing grids

## The pole congruence excludes every grid in each of the five boxes

At 2026-11-25T09:26 UTC a reviewer outside the development checked the two sections of the integer evaluation certificate and its Python implementation for Yin.
The review used the fixed inputs and their stated mathematical dependencies, without development dialogue, author explanations or earlier findings on the certificate.

Write \(s=1+aM\).
In \(\mathbb Z/s\mathbb Z\), \(M(-a)=1\), so evaluating the integral polynomial \(H\) at \(-a\) gives \(M^nH(-a)\equiv M^nH(1/M)=K\pmod s\).
In particular a zero of \(H\) at \(-a\) would imply \(s\mid K\) and hence \(s\mid aK-4s=a-4\), which is impossible for \(a=1,2,3\) and \(q\ge2\).

On a strictly increasing grid the prescribed sign of \(H(t_i)\) is opposite to that of \(Q'(t_i)\).
Thus the partial fractions of \(H/Q\) have residues \(-r_i<0\) and constant term one.
Evaluation at \(h=1/M\) gives \(\sum_i b_i=1-\delta\) for \(b_i=r_i/(h-t_i)>0\) and \(\delta=K/\prod_i(1-Mt_i)>0\).
Using \(x-h=(x-t_i)-(h-t_i)\) now gives \(H=\delta Q+\sum_i b_i(x-h)Q_i\) as a polynomial identity, including at any node equal to \(-a\).
It suffices to bound each entry of this convex combination.

I checked the native certificate's scaling by \(MB^n\) for \(B=2^{40}\), its sign counts after omitting each factor, and its ceiling and floor operations.
For independent arithmetic I used the same rational centers and propagated signed intervals of integers.
Starting with \(A=[1,1]\), let \(A\) enclose the full product of the factors processed so far and let \(D\) enclose all products omitting one of those factors.
For a new factor interval \(I=B(-a-t_i)\), replace \((A,D)\) by \((AI,\operatorname{hull}(A,DI))\), taking \(D=[1,1]\) after the first factor.
Each interval product uses the minimum and maximum of its four endpoint products.
Each interior factor ranges over the full central interval of radius \(2^{24}\) in this scaling, and both endpoint factors are fixed.
The final hull of \(MA\) and \(-BsD\), divided by \(MB^n\), encloses all entries even if the independent factor intervals permit node orderings outside the allowed grid.
I obtained the residues separately by inverting \(M^n\bmod s\) with the Euclidean algorithm.
