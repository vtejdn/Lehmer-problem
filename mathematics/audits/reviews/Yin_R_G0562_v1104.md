Writing \(I=K_1\cap K_2\), a basis of \(K_2/I\) spans the compositum over \(K_1\), so \(D\le d_1d_2/[I:\mathbb Q]\).

Consequently the choices for different traces can be made over the same embedded \(B\), even when their exceptional places differ.
