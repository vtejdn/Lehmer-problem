### The tower, offsets and all lifted row conditions

Fix a reciprocal nontorsion unit seed of even degree \(e\ge4\).
Choosing an odd prime \(t\) for which the seed root is not a \(t\)-th power in its field makes every \(t^j\)-th root have the full relative degree \(t^j\).
The induction uses the norm of the preceding root, which equals the seed root because the binomial degree is odd.
The complete set of lifts preserves the logarithmic Mahler measure \(h\).
At \(n=t^j,\ d=ne\), divergence of the prescribed prime function implies \(p>\max(t,e+1)\) eventually.
For \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\), \(L=\mathbb Q(\alpha^p)\), the degree \(l=[K:L]\le e\) is coprime to \(p\).
The identity \(N_{K/L}(\alpha)^p=(\alpha^p)^l\) and Bézout give a \(p\)-th root of \(\alpha^p\) in \(L\).
Its ratio to \(\alpha\) is a \(p\)-th root of unity in \(K\), and a nontrivial one would require \(p-1\le e\).
This proves precisely the seed degree preservation used by C407.
Since \(\gcd(p,n)=1\), the offset \(1\le r<n\) has \(pr=1+vn\), with \(1\le v<p\), independently of which of \(p,n\) is larger.

At \(w=-v\) the seed theorem supplies both rows, their common nonzero value, independent derivatives, \(b_0(0)=0\), unit endpoints and the required seed gcd.
Thus \(Y^{-r}b_0(Y^n)\) is an ordinary nonzero polynomial of lowest exponent at least \(n-r>0\) and degree less than \(4d\).
Its formal reversal is \(Y^{n+r-1}b_0^\flat(Y^n)\), coprime to \(a_0(Y^n)\) by the substituted Bézout identity and the constant endpoint one.
The upper endpoint of \(a_0(Y^n)\) is one at exactly \(4d\).
At \(\beta=\gamma^p\), the multiplier \(\beta^{-r}\alpha^v=\gamma^{-1}\) gives the required value of the other centered row.
Differentiation multiplies the seed derivative by \(n\) and adds \(ru\) inside its signed bracket, yielding \(n\ell_0+(r-nv/p)u=n\ell_0+u/p\).
Both first-row entries remain in the full trace field because \(C_n(\gamma+\gamma^{-1})=\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\).
Scaling each derivative by \(n\) preserves irrationality of the individual ratios and multiplies the joint determinant by \(n\).
Finally \(pB-1+pr=n(4ep+v)\), which makes the entire lifted graph exactly \(G_0(x^n)\), with the chosen sign and the required double factor.

Substitution and a single monomial shift are injective on each individual support.
The four lists therefore use at most \(2((4e+1)+(4e-1))=16e\) nonzero coefficients in total and preserve their actual magnitudes.
The seed coefficient logarithm is bounded by \(3ph+3e\log2+O(\log(2ep))\) uniformly in the offset.
For a fixed seed the last two terms are \(o(p)\), and the minimum of the two support schedules eventually exceeds \(16e\) along the tower.

### Lower bound, quantifier order and the finite decision

For an arbitrary passing row the signed value relation gives \(\gamma=-\sigma f(\gamma^p)/g(\gamma^p)\), since \(u\ne0\).
Thus the power preserves the entire input degree even when \(p\) is small relative to \(d\).
The powered minimum \(Q\) has measure \(p\,m(P)\).
Eliminating the two reflected graphs and using the unramified power map yields \(Q^2\mid Y^B(aa^\iota-bb^\iota)\) over the integers.
The outer coefficients from \(aa^\iota\) are both one and \(bb^\iota\) has smaller span.
Monic division gives a nonzero integer quotient with nonnegative measure.
On the circle the difference of the squared row moduli is bounded in absolute value by the greater squared modulus, which is at most \((t_* H_*)^2\).
This proves \(\log H_*\ge p\,m(P)-\log t_*\) on each actual row.
The support condition gives \(t_*\le p\) without a degree-dependent error.

Given a uniform positive gap, choose \(k\) with \(\log(2)/k\) strictly below it.
The loss \(\log p/p\to0\) then excludes every candidate for all sufficiently large even \(d\).

That margin also absorbs the loss of at most \(\log 2\) from the integer floor.
This supplies arbitrarily large counterdegrees for each proposed \(k\).
