## Conditional cycle counts and the two measure criteria

The fixed inputs establish the claimed one-prime estimate and both sequence criteria.
Indeed a repeated monic rational factor of the section polynomial would contribute its full degree to the gcd with the derivative after reduction, contradicting the v805 bound of 16 once all factor degrees exceed 16.
Its reciprocal factors give disjoint trace blocks, since the two preimages of a trace are inverse to one another.
Thus the block sizes sum to \(n\).
Since \((l-4k)/n\to1\) and \(W=2n\) eventually, rounding the cutoff preserves \(B/n\sim (\log\log n/\log n)^3/(8\log27)\).
In particular \(B^2/n\to\infty\), and all \(t_i=n_iB/(4n)\) simultaneously exceed four by \(n_i\ge B\).

For a fixed remainder \(j\in[2,\lfloor t_i\rfloor]\), the distinguished cycle uses more than half the block and is unique.
Each sign has exactly \(\binom{n_i}{j}(n_i-j-1)!j!/2\) admissible elements because a transposition of the remaining letters interchanges their parities.
Dividing by \(n_i!/2\) gives \(1/(n_i-j)\).
Distinct admissible lengths are disjoint events, so their combined probability in either sign is at least \((\lfloor t_i\rfloor-1)/n_i\ge t_i/(2n_i)=B/(8n)\).
In the compositum, the restrictions fixing the cyclotomic field contain the product of the alternating actions on traces by v806 and the inclusion of the cyclotomic field in the maximal abelian field.
Uniform measure on the prescribed cyclotomic fiber projects uniformly to its image, by equality of restriction-fiber sizes.
Partition that image into cosets of the product of alternating groups.
Each coset consists of the full Cartesian product of its individual sign classes, giving the product bound in every coset and hence in their mixture.
The abelian cyclotomic quotient makes the prescribed fiber conjugacy-invariant, and the rational factor blocks and their cycle types are preserved under conjugation.
The Chebotarev ratio therefore gives the stated conditional natural density for each fixed section, modulus and invertible class, with finitely many exclusions harmless.

The total number of letters off the long cycles is at most \(\sum_i t_i=B/4\), so any eligible sum uses at least \((1-B/(4n))m\) letters.
The default \(n\) satisfies the same lower bound.
For reducible \(Q\), the smallest rational factor gives an admissible sum at every good prime since \(B\le m\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor\) and its monic reduction has degree \(m\).
For irreducible \(Q\), the definition itself gives \(b(p)\le n=m\).
The full event is a union of cycle types, so its relative density exists independently of the sufficient long-cycle event.

If \(m/n\ge\delta>0\) eventually, then \(r\le R=\lfloor1/\delta\rfloor\) and the lower estimate exceeds \(\delta n/2\) at all sufficiently late sections.
Since \((\log n)^3 B/(8n)\sim (\log\log n)^3/(64\log 27)\to\infty\), the relative density is at least \((\log n)^{-3R}\) uniformly in the selected class.
Conversely, positive relative density furnishes a good prime in each late section, and the universal upper bound forces \(m/n\ge d\).
For any selection of classes the maximum exists because the values form a finite nonempty set, and (1) squeezes \(b_{\max}/m\) to one.
Hence \(b_{\max}/n\to0\) exactly when every fixed positive threshold eventually has no witness in the selected class.
