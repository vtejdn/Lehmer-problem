An embedding taking \(V\) of order \(N\) to \(e^{2\pi i/N}\) extends to the common cyclotomic field.

For the remaining orders \(\varphi(h)^2/h\ge1/2\), since only the first power of the prime two can supply a factor below one.
Hence every possible torsion zero of a nonzero resultant of degree \(b\) has order dividing the specified \(M_b\).

For an independent exact computation let \(C_N\) be the companion matrix for multiplication by \(V\) on \(\mathbb Q[V]/(\Phi_N)\).

Thus the gcds with the indicated nonzero reductions exclude every remaining torsion pair.
This also excludes torsion zeros after both allowed translations.

Both factors of \(a(t)\) have a periodic argument, since their real parts are negative constants.
The logarithmic derivative bounds \(|\psi'|\) and \(|(\log|a|)'|\) by \(7/6\).
Writing \(u=1/(2|a|)\) in the derivative of \(\arccos(-u)\) gives \(|b'|\le (7/6)/\sqrt{575}\).
The sum of these derivative bounds is strictly less than two.
Each translated phase \(l\theta+\arg\eta+\psi(k\theta+\arg\xi)\pm b(k\theta+\arg\xi)\) therefore increases strictly even when \(l=2k\) and has winding number \(l\).

At a zero \(\beta\) of \(A\) the contour \(|u-\beta|=b/(1+s)\) gives \(|A(u)|\gg_F(1+s)^{-1}\) and \(|u^{-s}|=O_F(\beta^{-s})\).
For \(|t|\le R=c\beta^s/(1+s)\), the perturbations in (5) are \(O_F(c/(1+s)+c^2/(1+s)^2)\), so one fixed sufficiently small \(c>0\) works for both poles.
Rouché's theorem counts a unique root with multiplicity one on the interior for every parameter in the disk.
This uniqueness glues the local holomorphic implicit branches across the whole parameter disk.
The power map is locally invertible at every lifted \(X\).
Equality of two lifts would force equality of their \(u=X^k\) values and then equality of their labels, since the logarithm on each center disk is fixed.
These lifts exhaust the \(2k\) exterior roots, and the real ones correspond exactly to the labels fixed by conjugation.
This gives \(2\gcd(2,k)\) real exterior roots for sufficiently large \(s\).

The derivatives at zero of \(u(t)\) and \(\log(u(t)/\beta)\) are respectively \(-\beta^{2-s}/A'(\beta)\) and \(-\beta^{1-s}/A'(\beta)\).
Their nonconstant Taylor coefficients satisfy the claimed Cauchy bound because \(u(t)-\beta\) and its logarithmic displacement stay \(O_F((1+s)^{-1})\) throughout the disk.
At \(|t|=1\), summing orders \(h\ge2\) therefore gives \(O_F((1+s)\beta^{-2s})\).
The ordinary section is monic, so each center contributes \(\log\beta\) plus the mean of the real logarithmic displacements.
The image of \(\nu\mapsto\nu^{-l}\) has order \(k/g\) and every image has exactly \(g\) preimages.

The resulting geometric bound is \(O_F(\exp(-(\log\beta)l/(2g)))\), which proves (2) in its large-slope regime.

For a two-exterior factor on escaping coprime directions, the section roots are units of infinite order and their powered pairs leave every finite set, since their primitive relation modulo torsion determines the slope.
