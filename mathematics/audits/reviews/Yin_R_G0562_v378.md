## Independent finding

At 2026-11-18T12:15 UTC a separate reviewer for Yin examined the whole [grid-inverse submission](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v374.md#decision-explicit-grid-inverse-submitted) and the dependency passages listed below.
The reviewer had no role in developing the proof and received or consulted no development discussion, author explanation or previous verdict on this submission.
The construction works for every stated pair \(q,d\), and the uniform threshold and the lower bound for every inverse of the specified return follow under their stated hypotheses.

**Construction.** In the rounding argument the \(h\) lower trace-basis coefficients contribute independent centered errors of range at most two at each old node.
The exponential bound gives failure probability at most \(1/(2h)\) per node, so rounding preserves all the prescribed signs with \(|R_0(x_i)|\le3B_h\).
For \(k>0\), the choice \(q_0=q+1\) gives leading coefficient \(-q\) for \(BR_0\), and \(Q_0D_k\) restores the required \(1-q\) without changing any old residue.
At a new node \(2\cos t_j\), differentiation of \(2\cos(kt)\) gives \(B'=k\sin(kt_j)/\sin t_j\), and \(\sin((k-1)t_j)=\sin(kt_j)\cos t_j\).
Consequently the new contribution to the weighted sum is \(\sum_j(T-y_j)/(2k)=T/2\).
Its unweighted sum is \(T\) for the sole node \(y_1=0\) at \(k=1\), and \(T-1\) for \(k\ge2\) by the second moment \(\sum_j y_j^2=2k\).
All signs are strict, the old and new roots are disjoint by nontorsion of the Salem roots, and the weighted total is below \(q+T/2\).
For \(k=0\) direct rounding at \(q\) gives the old-node bounds with no shift.

**Pisot criterion.** In the deformation from \(p_q(z)z^{d/2-1}H(z+z^{-1})\), the real part of \(e^{-it/2}p_q(e^{it})\) is \(\cos(t/2)(2\cos t-T)<0\) on the open upper semicircle.
The initial radial displacement therefore has sign opposite to the residue.
At positive deformation parameter the constant return restricts circle crossings to its unit roots and \(-1\).
The nonzero residues exclude the former and strict positivity at both ends of the path at \(-1\) excludes the latter.
The one remaining exterior root is positive by \(f(1)=P(1)<0\), and any monic integral factor omitting it would have absolute constant term strictly between zero and one.
The identity at this root follows by substituting the partial fractions for \(R/H\) into \(f(\theta)=0\).
Since \(\theta>q\) by the root product, each neutral denominator exceeds \(q-2\), yielding the claimed upper bound.
The positive grid factor has value two at both endpoints and only roots of unity.

**Uniform threshold.** The absolute Vandermonde determinant of the roots of \(A=(z+1)P_0\) is at least one by its nonzero integral discriminant.
Deleting the row of a neutral root \(\xi\) and the highest power leaves the \(m\)-root Vandermonde determinant of absolute value \(|\det V|/|A'(\xi)|\).
Hadamard's inequality bounds the smaller determinant by \(m^{m/2}\sigma^{m-1}\), since only the exterior root contributes a modulus greater than one.
At \(\xi=e^{it}\) direct differentiation gives \(|A'(\xi)|=2\sin(t/2)(T-x)(2+x)|H_0'(x)|\).
These estimates give the stated \(K_*\), which also bounds \(T\).
For bounded \(\sigma\) the nonleading terms in \(\log K_*\) are \(O(m+\log m)\).
Under the given \(m\log m\) bound one has \(m=o(\log d)\), so the leading term leaves a margin of \((\varepsilon/2)\log d\) below \(2\log d\).

**Necessity and sharp order.** Antisymmetry gives the integral numerator for every inverse of this simple return, independently of factorization.
For a Pisot inverse the absence of unit roots forces all residues nonzero, and even degree and the root locations give \(f(-1)>0\).
The same deformation therefore counts one exterior root plus two for each negative residue, forcing all signs positive.
At the grid nodes the nonzero integer resultant has absolute value at least one, while \(\prod_j|H_0(y_j)|=\prod_i|2\cos(kt_i')|\le2^h\) for \(x_i=2\cos t_i'\).
The endpoint product gives \(\prod_j(2\sin t_j)=2\), hence \(\prod_j|B'(y_j)|=2^{k-1}k^k\), including \(k=1\).
Taking the \(k\)-th root and applying the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives the claimed \(2^{-1+(1-h)/k}\) lower bound for the sum of grid residues.
The two-point inequality gives \(q<\theta\le q+T+2\) uniformly, so both denominators in (5), divided by \(q\), tend to one for fixed \(P_0\).
For each fixed \(d,q\) this root bound and strict interiority of the other conjugates bound all integer coefficients, so a nonempty fiber attains the minimum of \(\theta-q-(T-2)\).
At \(q=d^2\) the positive limiting lower coefficient \(T-2+1/2\) and the constructed upper bound give order \(d^{-2}\) for that minimum.
The lower coefficient concerns this single grid and the argument assumes the specified Salem input throughout.
