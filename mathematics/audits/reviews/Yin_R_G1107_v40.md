## Finding: the full variable-reference bound is sufficient

No mathematical gap was found in C881 v9.
The theorem establishes
\[
1\le M(P)\le(8q+H+3)^{2/q},\qquad q=\lfloor L/2\rfloor,
\]
at exactly its stated hypotheses.

The monic degree-\(n+1\) trace polynomial lifts to a monic reciprocal degree-\(2n+2\) polynomial with constant coefficient one, independently of integrality.
The two same-sign blocks have \((n-\kappa-1)+(\kappa-1)=n-2\) interior gaps.
The endpoint supplies one more distinct selected root, also when \(n=2\).
Selecting one copy in each interval leaves exactly a real monic quadratic and retains every other multiplicity.
The matched right poles are all except \(b_{n-\kappa}\).
Their intervals have disjoint interiors, so their combined variation cost is one total variation, without a factor of \(n\).

Normalization \(U(0)/V(0)=1\) permits the displayed unit-root factorization even for constant factors.
Prefix agreement is through coefficient index \(L\), so the formal logarithms agree through precisely that index.
Their derivatives give the signed inverse-root sums, and reciprocity identifies the polynomial's inverse-root multiset with its root multiset.
Every numerator and denominator multiplicity contributes to the valid bound \(H\); cancellation only makes that estimate weaker.
The grid sum is \(-1\) because every tested frequency lies below \(p\), with no primality assumption.
Thus the trace-difference cost is \(H+1\), the unmatched-pole cost is two, and the variation is \(4j\), giving exactly \(4j+H+3\).

For nonreal residual traces, conjugate exterior lifts have one common radius greater than one.
The frequencies \(q,2q\) both lie in the inspected prefix, and the double-angle identity forces one absolute cosine to be at least one half.
This gives the claimed exponent \(2/q\) for the complete measure.
For real residual traces, the even frequency \(2q\) gives a lower contribution \(\rho^{2q}-2\) for the residual sum when an exterior trace is present.
Consequently \(M(P)\le(8q+H+5)^{1/q}\), which is bounded by the displayed theorem for \(q\ge1,H\ge0\).
Unit-circle and coincident residual roots are included.
No previous measure bound, discarded factor or simplicity assumption is used.

Since \(q\) is comparable to \(L\), the stated condition \(\log(L+H)=o(L)\) makes the upper logarithmic bound tend to zero.
For the sufficient integer subcase, the hypotheses identify exactly the \(x=0,a_0=2,\kappa=1\) domain of C881 v8.
