**Ambient minimum.**

The unordered rational class \(\{\pm[w]\}\) of the resulting unit has a stabilizer with nontrivial sign because an actual conjugate of \(w\) equals its inverse.
The equality \(\tau g[w]=-g[w]\) means that \(|gw|^2\) is a positive real root of unity, hence equals one.

**One exterior conjugate and transfer.**
