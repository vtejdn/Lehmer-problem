## Decisive mathematical checks

### 1. Same-current-time lower counts

The two kernels are distinct throughout: K_orig acts chronologically on order states, whereas K_n resamples complete blocks of an already returned word.
Every path between actual core endpoints stays inside the core, since actual zero-reaching and zero-returning endpaths can be attached.
Thus the killed-core convention does not discard a complete original path between the endpoints used in the count identities.

For a right label c, prepend each complete (k-ell_c)-return to the fixed actual ell_c-word from zero to c.
For a left label y, follow its fixed actual ell_y-continuation to zero by each complete (k-ell_y)-return.
These maps are injective on the original digit words and use disjoint unconditioned positions.
The fixed-word probabilities are at least 4^-ell, and all remaining-return probabilities are kept.
The binary-difference proof of C169 gives p_(k-ell)>=p_k, so both resulting probabilities are at least 4^-T p_k.
The cases ell=0 and ell=k retain the empty-word convention p_0=1 and cause no exception.

Reversing iid chronological coefficients preserves every digit weight and gives exactly

    K_orig^k(0,c)=nu_k(c),
    K_orig^k(y,0)=nu_k(-beta^k y).

Dividing by p_k proves the asserted lower normalized counts at this same k.
No later tail length or limiting harmonic ratio is substituted.
Binary autocorrelation and Cauchy--Schwarz give nu_k(z)<=p_k for every z, proving the upper normalized counts globally.
This upper bound is never reversed; the lower bound has its separate explicit injection.
The flow later retains all word representations rather than restricting source mass to these witnesses.

### 2. Complete row denominators and the factor 131072

The complete original middle-word weight is (1+2+1)^4=256.

The degree-four basis makes the middle word unique.

    t_k alpha_k(y) beta_k(c) beta_k(c')
