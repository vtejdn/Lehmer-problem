## Valuation count and individual-factor gap

At 2026-11-17T09:54 UTC an agent outside proof development reviewed the [fixed v201 submission](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v201.md#decision-local-delay-submitted) for Sheng Yin.
The reviewer received the fixed mathematics without development discussion, exploration notes, author explanations or previous findings on this submission.
The local count holds for every allowed prime and both signs; the individual-factor conclusions hold in the stated large-delay range with bounded \(m\).

For a nonseed characteristic, multiplication by \(\prod_i(-p_i)\) sends \(\overline A\) to \(\overline {A^*}\), since \(p_i^2\equiv1\).
With a seed equal to \(\ell\), the same identity for the other seeds instead gives \(X^\Delta\overline{A^*}=\prod_{p_i\ne\ell}(-p_i)\overline A\).
Thus \(H\) is zero modulo \(\ell\) with the specified \(b\), including \(b=1\) in characteristic two.
Evaluating at an exterior zero of \(A\) proves \(H\ne0\); its coefficient sum in absolute value is bounded by \(K=2\prod_i(1+p_i)\), so \(\ell^h\le K\).
With \(\Delta>0\), the constant term has valuation one because the seeds are distinct primes.
The identity \(\overline F=(X^m-b)^M\overline A\) follows by Frobenius, as \(b^M=b\).
The roots of the monic \(X^m-b\) are units with distinct residues by separability, even if \(\overline A\) vanishes at some of those residues.
At most \(D\) roots of \(F\) reduce elsewhere, counting multiplicity.
Inside a class the expansion in \(y=\alpha/\zeta-1\) has \(v(\alpha^m-b)=v(y)\) since its linear coefficient is a unit; the equality also holds at \(y=0\).

Translation and scaling by a unit and the inverse substitution preserve integral coefficients, so the translated \(H\) has some coefficient of valuation \(h\) in degrees at most \(B\).
For every positive contributing binomial index \(r\le i<M/\ell^h\), one has \(v_\ell(r)<v_\ell(M)-h\); the second summand cannot cancel that coefficient.
Write \(j\le B\) for its index.
For \(B<i<L\) the binomial estimate gives \(v(c_i)\ge h\).
In the upper bands indexed by \(e=1,\ldots,h-1\) it gives \(v(c_i)\ge e\), and for \(i\ge M\) integrality gives the same assertion with \(e=0\).
For these bands \(i\ge \ell^{h-1-e}L\ge(h-e)L\), hence \(i-B\ge(h-e)(L-B)\).
For \(t>1/(L-B)\), every \(i>B\) therefore has strictly greater weight than \(j\), including at a band endpoint and when \(h=1\) and only the last band remains.

In multiplying two polynomials the sum of the first minimizing indices has just one contributing pair of minimum weight; at every smaller index no pair has that weight.
The first minimizing index for a linear factor is one exactly when its root has valuation greater than \(t\), including a zero root.
Thus at most \(B\) roots in each translated polynomial have valuation greater than \(t\); finiteness permits taking all valuations strictly above \(1/(L-B)\) in the count.
Every remaining root inside the chosen classes has \(0<v(\alpha^m-b)\le1/(L-B)\).
Two terms of the rational minimal polynomial of \(\alpha^m-b\) must tie for minimum valuation.
Their index difference is at most \([\mathbb Q(\alpha):\mathbb Q]\) and their coefficient valuations are integers, so the positive valuation is at least the reciprocal of that degree.
Every root of every rational factor of degree less than \(L-B\) is exceptional; removing the artificial \(X^\Delta\) cannot increase their total multiplicity.

For noncyclotomic factors the reviewed allocation in v198 gives \(s\ge1\), hence \(d/n>1/g-1/(3(D+1)^2)\ge1/(2g)\).
Since \(m\le m_0,\ \ell^h\le K,\ B\le2D\) and \(g\le D\), a constant depending only on the fixed primes and \(m_0\) ensures both \(M/\ell^h>B\) and \(n/(2g)>mB+D\).
Every such factor consequently has \(d\ge L-B\).
The relative degree differs from \(s/g\) by \(o(1)\) uniformly: the bound \(1/(3(D+1)^2)\) suffices for large \(D\), and for each bounded range of \(D\) the explicit v198 error tends to zero uniformly as \(n\) grows.
Also \(B/n\to0\) and the v198 measure error tends to zero uniformly, so the stated leading fraction follows from \(M=(n+\Delta)/m\).
Its denominator is at most \(m_0K\), and every residual finite range has positive noncyclotomic measures by Kronecker's theorem.
For \(m=h=1\), two degrees of size \(n-D\) exceed \(n+D\) in total, and the v198 partition forces existence and full share for the unique factor.
For the examples with primes \(2,3\), both choices of \(\ell\) give \(\Delta=g,\ m=h=1\) at \(n=\ell^a-g\), with \(\gcd(n,g)=1\); arbitrarily large \(a\) meet the stated threshold for every admissible \(g\).
