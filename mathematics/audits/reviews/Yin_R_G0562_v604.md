Write \(y=x^n\) for a retained pair.
Reduction modulo the prime \(q\) identifies \(U(Y^q),V(Y^q)\) with the \(q\)-th powers of \(U,V\), respectively, so division of the difference by \(q\) preserves integrality.
Each product has degree at most \(2qb\) and one-norm at most \(H^{q+1}\).

At \(x\) the two contact orders, the second pulled back through \(X\mapsto X^q\), give \(f(X^{nq})-f(X^n)^q=O((X-x)^t)\).

Consequently all the indicated powered minima occur as distinct factors in the same polynomial.

The two distinct positive powers of a nontorsion seed thus cannot both be double contacts.
