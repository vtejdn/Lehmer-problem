Independent application on the disjoint pair choices gives an orbit of exactly \(q^h\) reciprocal selections, also for composite \(q\).

At a separating \(b\), the orbit gives degree \(N=q^h\) over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}(T)\) and hence also over \(\mathbb Q(T)\).

Searching integer \(b\) and testing the discriminant of the computed \(H\) consequently terminates.

On the positive real ray, complex conjugation and inversion take each neutral branch to the same branch over the opposite constant, by simple-root uniqueness.
