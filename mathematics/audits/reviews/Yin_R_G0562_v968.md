## Independent whole-proof finding

The splitting threshold, its two variants, the finite criterion and the degree-fourteen example hold with the stated hypotheses.
For a reciprocal splitting into even palindromic factors, each inverse pair from the first factor contributes the square of the second trace polynomial evaluated at that pair’s trace, since the power-of-root prefactors cancel.
Consequently the absolute complementary resultant equals the square of the trace resultant and divides the absolute trace discriminant by its product formula.
Its valuations at exceptional primes are bounded by the largest even valuations allowed by that discriminant.
At the other primes [v823](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v823.md#statement) applies to this same squarefree reciprocal splitting and supplies the eight-integer divisor, with precisely the failing orbits removable by [descent](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v823.md#descent).
These primewise divisibilities justify both entries of the gcd and both proposed alternatives.
The nonzero homogeneous [exceptional form](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v812.md#statement) excludes only finitely many slopes, and the [circle and polar root counts](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#statement) give eventual simplicity on a steep sequence, including the interior by inversion.

For a smallest irreducible factor of degree \(d\), [v805](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#statement) gives eventual reciprocity and the uniform mean log derivative \(\log l+L+o(1)\).
Subtracting the Hadamard discriminant bound per root costs at most \(\log d+2(1-1/d)m(S)\).
The [individual height estimate](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v799.md#statement) makes the second term at most \(Cd/l+o(1)\) uniformly because \(d/l\le1+2k/l\).
Since \(|A|\ge12\) on the circle, \(L-C\ge\tfrac12\log575>2\), supplying the slack in the claimed lower bound.
The function \(d(2+\log(l/d))\) has positive derivative \(1+\log(l/d)\) throughout the required range for late indices.
At the uniform [degree lower endpoint](../../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v811.md#degrees) \(d_0=cl(\log\log l/\log l)^3\), the logarithmic multiplier is \(3\log\log l-3\log\log\log l-\log c\) before adding 2.
Monotonicity now gives the stated scale on every reducible subsequence, hence the little-oh criterion.

For the finite test, v793 gives a circle root and excludes both endpoints when \(l\ge2k\).
Its irreducible factor is fixed by inversion because rational conjugation sends that root to its inverse.
If the section is reducible, squarefreeness leaves at least one further inversion orbit of factors, even when an orbit interchanges two nonreciprocal factors.
Each orbit product groups distinct inverse root pairs, giving an even monic palindromic factor, and the smallest orbit has degree at most half the section degree.
The derivative identity and Hadamard bound give the asserted inequality since the Mahler measure of the orbit product is the product of its exterior pair moduli.
The smallest pair products over the entire section thus furnish a lower bound for every possible orbit of that size.

I reproduced the finite example using integer polynomial arithmetic and rational interval endpoints.
Expanding the defining section and using \(D_0=2,D_1=Y,D_{j+1}=YD_j-D_{j-1}\) for \(X^j+X^{-j}\) gives exactly the printed trace polynomial.
Its discriminant is \(20493331629070057=19\cdot3137\cdot343830539219\), and trial division through \(4,56,586370\), respectively, found no divisors.
The left endpoint signs in ascending order are \(-,+,-,+,-,+,-\), each reversed at the corresponding right endpoint.
The seven disjoint brackets therefore exhaust the roots and avoid both endpoints of the trace circle interval, certifying squarefreeness of the section.
The differentiated trace identity gives the pair derivative product \(|4-y^2|\,|Q'(y)|^2\).
Using the minimum endpoint value of \(|4-y^2|\) in each bracket and the squared product of its six minimum distances to the other brackets gives lower bounds with integer parts \(22272,25819,27239,20035,10256,2019068,36941588\).
For the last two brackets the upper endpoints are less than \(r+r^{-1}\) at \(r=3.31,4.24\), respectively, bounding the exterior moduli by monotonicity.
