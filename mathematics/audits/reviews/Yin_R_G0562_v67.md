The class products of C345v61 preserve the measure and remove torsion quotients between distinct roots.
An element of the splitting-field Galois group acting trivially on the quotient span thus fixes each actual root and is the identity.
The positive products \(xc(x)\) have logarithms \(2\log|x|\), with the negative logarithms supplied by inverse roots.
Their rank is exactly \(k\) and their image spans \((1+c)V=U\), since the original root classes span \(V\).
Positivity makes the map from this product group to the torsion-free quotient injective.

With the invariant inner product, \(U\) and \(U^\perp\) are the two eigenspaces of the involution \(c\).
Preserving \(U\) preserves its orthogonal complement and hence commutes with the operator \(c\), and faithfulness gives commutation in \(G\) itself.
A Galois element fixing any chosen \(k\) independent products fixes their classes spanning \(U\), so belongs to this centralizer and acts trivially on \(U\).
Conversely an element in the kernel of the centralizer action on \(U\) sends \(xc(x)\) to another positive product with the same class.
The quotient is a positive real root of unity and equals one.
The stabilizer of the chosen tuple and the pointwise stabilizer of all the products are therefore the same kernel.
This proves equality of their fields even when the tuple has finite index greater than one in the lattice of positive products.

The degree of that field is \(B\) times the order of the finite image of the centralizer on the rank-\(k\) lattice.
For the reduction of this image modulo three, a nontrivial torsion element in the kernel would give a nontrivial prime order element \(I+3^\ell A\).
At prime order \(p\ne3\) the linear term survives modulo \(3^{\ell+1}\).
At order three its first term has valuation \(\ell+1\) at an entry prime to three in \(A\), while the higher terms vanish modulo \(3^{\ell+2}\) even at \(\ell=1\).
Thus reduction embeds the image into \({\rm GL}_k(\mathbb F_3)\) and gives both \(B\le[F:\mathbb Q]\) and \([F:\mathbb Q]\le3^{k^2}B\).
