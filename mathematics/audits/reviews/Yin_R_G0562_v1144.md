## The two block actions, absolute irreducibility and uniformity over radicals

At 2026-11-25T00:05 UTC an agent outside the proof development independently reviewed for Yin the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v84.md#statement) and all three proof sections of C0901 v84.
The reviewer received the fixed proof and its mathematical inputs without development discussion, author explanations or previous findings about v84.
At each fixed upper bound on total measure and sufficiently large original record degree, the absolute irreducibility, exact dimensions, linear argument-rank bounds and exterior-count assertions hold in both block alternatives.
The conclusion about the neutral class uses the stated neutral conjugate of the original record.

The torsion-class product would otherwise give an algebraic unit with the same positive measure and strictly lower degree, contrary to first attainment.
Thus each compressed conjugate class corresponds to a single actual root.
Two proportional nonzero classes among equal-height conjugates can only differ by a factor \(1\) or \(-1\), by scaling of absolute height.
Reciprocity consequently identifies the projective labels with precisely the \(n=d/2\) inverse pairs.
The bounded-measure action from v270 supplies independent natural alternating factors on these labels, with \(u\ll_M(\log(3d))^{212}\) and \(b=n/u\) tending to infinity.
The signed lifts of v274 apply to this same reciprocal, torsion-separated unit.

In the first alternative there are no relations among representatives of the inverse pairs modulo torsion.
The full signed coordinate representation therefore maps isomorphically to \(V_w\).
Its diagonal subgroup of even block flips separates every two coordinate lines by negating one chosen label and a spare label of its block while leaving the second chosen label alone.
This remains valid when both chosen labels are in the same block.
Character averages over these flips extract individual coordinates after complexification, and transitivity forces every nonzero invariant subspace to be the entire space of dimension \(n\).

In the oriented alternative v274 provides supported lifts acting as pure permutations on the canonically chosen halves block by block, with all other actual roots fixed.
The product on each half has degree at most \(2u<d\) and measure at most \(m(w)\).
If it had positive measure it would contradict either minimality through degree \(d\) or first attainment, so all these products are torsion.
The \(u\) block-sum relations are independent by disjoint support, and the v270 lower bound \(n-u\) leaves no additional kernel.
Over \(\mathbb C\) the quotient thus splits over \((A_b)^u\) into \(u\) ordinary sum-zero spaces, the other factors acting trivially on each one.
The permutation commutant for a doubly transitive action on \(b\) letters has dimension two, and the constants form one copy of the trivial representation.
Complete reducibility gives an irreducible complex sum-zero complement.
Different blocks give inequivalent summands for the product because their nontrivially acting factors differ.
A subrepresentation is consequently a sum of entire block summands, and the full group's block transitivity leaves only zero and the full quotient of dimension \(n-u\).
The common orientation sign on a transported block preserves its sum-zero space.
This proves absolute irreducibility in the oriented case.

For any nontorsion \(x\in D(w)\) a positive multiple of its class is an integral combination of the original classes, hence its conjugate span is a nonzero invariant submodule of \(V_w\).
It equals \(V_w\), even when actual roots and torsion twists require larger splitting fields.
The divisible group is recovered by taking representatives of the entire rational class space, so equality of spans gives \(D(x)=D(w)\).
The positive and negative eigenspace dimensions of the same complex conjugation give the radial and argument ranks, respectively, and therefore agree for all these \(x\).

The signed space before quotient has \(q+s\) negative dimensions: each neutral label is inverted, each of the \(s\) off-circle nonreal quartets exchanges two labels with square one, and each real pair is fixed.
The quotient loses at most \(u\) dimensions, which gives \(k(x)\ge d/4+q/2-a/2-u\) because \(n=a+2s+q\).
Exactly \(2a\) original conjugates are real, so v65 gives \(a=O_M(\sqrt{d\log d})\) at bounded measure and \(a=o(\sqrt{d\log d})\) on shrinking sequences.
The bound for \(u\) is little-oh at this scale.
When the conjugations commute on \(V_w\) and \(m(w)\to0\), v82 makes the fixed dimension fraction tend to zero and the whole dimension is \(d/2-O(u)\), giving \(k(x)=(1-o(1))d/2\).
Every estimate here uses the original degree.

Reciprocity means that the \(a+s\) exterior \(c\)-orbits supply the positive log moduli spanning the full radial space.
The application of v271 has \(R\ge a+s-f\), where the number \(f\) of wholly exterior block orbits is at most \(u\).
When \(a+s\ge2u\) this gives \(R\ge(a+s)/2\).
Otherwise all these \(a+s\) radii are independent by the separate small-subset assertion of v271, since at sufficiently large degree \(2u\le\lfloor(b-1)/2\rfloor\).
The latter bound follows uniformly from \(u^2/n\to0\).
In either case \(R\ge(a+2s)/4=l(w)/4\), including the cases of very few exterior roots or no neutral roots.

For a noncoordinate vector on \(t\ge2\) active blocks there are at least \(u/t\) support sets, by transitivity on blocks and coverage.
In an oriented active block every nonzero orbit under \(A_b\) spans its irreducible \((b-1)\)-dimensional space and has sum zero, hence contains at least \(b\) vectors.
In an inversion block any \(j<b\) nonzero coordinates give at least \(2^j\binom bj\) possibilities, and full support gives at least \(2^{b-1}\).
Here the independent label factors transport the supports, and the independently supported even flips fill each required sign fiber, using a zero coordinate when necessary.
Thus the count needs only the proven label quotient in this case, without a pure unsigned splitting of that quotient.
The joint orbit over each block support has at least \(b^t\) points, and \((u/t)b^t\ge ub(b-1)/2\) for \(b\ge2,\ t\ge2\).

Within a single inversion block noncoordinate support \(j\ge2\) gives at least \(\binom b2\) points from these same estimates, including \(j=b-1,b\) at large \(b\).
Within a single oriented block the images of the original coordinate lines are exactly the one-exceptional-entry lines in the sum-zero model.
For any other nonzero vector, a value class of size \(j\) with \(2\le j\le b-2\) occupies all \(\binom bj\) possible position sets under \(A_b\).
If no value repeats the orbit already has \(b!/2\) points.
Transporting to all \(u\) blocks therefore gives \(\deg x\ge ub(b-1)/2\) in the one-block case as well, since each distinct class image is realized by a distinct actual conjugate.

For reciprocal \(x\), the fixed-space degree inequality of v64 gives \(2l(x)\dim V_w\ge(\deg x)R\).
Using \(\dim V_w\le ub\), the noncoordinate orbit bound and \(R\ge l(w)/4\) gives exactly \(l(x)\ge(b-1)l(w)/16\).
On a coordinate line, the class is a nonzero rational multiple of an original conjugate class.
Its \(d\) different class images have equal actual conjugate fiber sizes by transitivity.
Torsion contributes zero log modulus, scaling by a positive rational preserves signs, and negative scaling interchanges exterior and interior, which have equal sizes by reciprocity of \(w\).
Thus \(l(x)\) is that fiber size times \(l(w)\), with neutral conjugates present exactly when they are present for \(w\).
At sufficiently large degree \(b-1>16\), so the minimum exterior count among all reciprocal nontorsion elements is \(l(w)\), attained by \(w\).
If \(w\) has a neutral conjugate, the minimum rules out every \(k<l(w)\) while \(w\) realizes membership for every \(k\ge l(w)\).
This one degree threshold is independent of \(k\), without asserting the same neutral-class equivalence for an original record without neutral conjugates.
