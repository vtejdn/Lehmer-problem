## Findings

No mathematical defect was found in the selected asymptotic argument.
Writing \(m=d+3=N+1\), the phase is \(mt+b(t)\) with a fixed smooth periodic \(b\), so its derivative is positive for all sufficiently large \(d\).
The cubic has two roots inside the unit disk and one at \(\theta>1\), giving the stated winding and exactly \(m\) simple circle roots of the numerator.
For consecutive phase indices the root equation gives \(t_k=2\pi k/m-b(t_k)/m\).
Thus cyclic matching to an equally spaced grid has error \(O(m^{-1})\); summing the Lipschitz errors for a fixed smooth function gives \(O(1)\), as does its grid Riemann-sum error.
Removing the five simple roots of \(Z^5-1\) leaves exactly \(d-2\) circle roots.
There is no missing multiplicity or factor of two in the phase count.

For the exact formula, put \(a=Z^2f\) and \(Q=Z^5-1\).
At a root of \(P_h\), the identities \(B_h=-1/a\), \(z^d=f^*/a\) and \(W=zV\) give
\[
 P_h'(z)=\frac{-W(z)/a(z)+d f^*(z)/z}{Q(z)}
         =\frac{zH_d(z)}{a(z)Q(z)}.
\]
The factors divided out are nonzero by the fixed family and its fifth-order evaluations.
The products of \(z\) and of \(a(z)\) over the roots of \(P_h\) are both one.
The remaining denominator is exactly \((1-12h)(144h^2+36h+1)^2\), and the discriminant sign multiplier is one because \(d=60h\) is divisible by four.
This independently verifies the precise resultant interface used by C345v288.

On the circle, \(ff^*\) is bounded away from zero, so the error in \(\log|H_d|=\log d+\log|ff^*|+O(d^{-1})\) is uniform.
Jensen's formula applies to the fixed cubic and gives circle mean \(2\log\theta\) for \(\log|ff^*|\).
The phase estimate consequently yields \((d-2)\log d+2d\log\theta+O(1)\) for the circle contribution.
At the exterior root, boundedness of \(f^*\) and \(\beta_h\ge\beta_1>1\) give \(f(\beta_h)=O(\beta_1^{-d-2})\).
Since \(f'(\theta)>0\), the same exponential bound holds for \(\theta-\beta_h\).
The identity \(V(\theta)=\theta f'(\theta)(\theta^5-1)>0\) therefore gives \(H_d(\beta_h)\to-V(\theta)\ne0\).
Reciprocity supplies the inverse-root value, so both remaining logarithms stay bounded.

The exact denominator contributes \(5\log d+O(1)\), leaving
\[
 \log|\operatorname{disc}P_h|=(d-7)\log d+2d\log\theta+O(1).
\]
For \(n=30h\), reciprocity gives \(S_h(2)=1-12h\), \(S_h(-2)=1\) and
\[
 |\operatorname{disc}P_h|=(12h-1)(\operatorname{disc}S_h)^2.
\]
Subtracting the additional \(\log d+O(1)\) and dividing by two yields exactly \((n-4)\log(2n)+2n\log\theta+O(1)\).
The trace discriminant is positive because its roots are distinct and real.
The normalized root limit and the deficit \(7\log d+O(1)\) follow, using \(d(\log\theta-\log\beta_h)\to0\).

The error bounds are uniform along the full integer sequence \(h\to\infty\), and hence along every unbounded subsequence supplied by C345v287.
This checks compatibility with its local conditions, while the existence and ramification properties of those subsequences remain the separate mathematical content of C345v287.
The asymptotic attainment assertion has the normalized-root meaning explicitly given in equation (2); the discriminant-to-Hadamard-bound ratio itself has logarithmic deficit \(7\log d+O(1)\).
This review concerns polynomial discriminants and does not identify them with number-field discriminants.
