After all torsion factors are removed, C345v102 gives a squarefree monic \(Q_0\) with constant of absolute value one.
Thus \(\sum t_i=2v\), and the \(f=0\) case is immediate.

Moving the angle by at most \(t\) and replacing \(t_i\) by \(t\) changes this lower bound by at most an absolute factor, uniformly even across the choice of angular representative.
The union \(U_t\) therefore lies in a superlevel set of the positive convolution at scale \(t\).
Its integral is precisely \(\mu(\mathbb T)\) with normalized angular measure, giving \(\nu(U_t)\ll Y/(dt)\).

Each root in the band gives a summand in \(H_t\) bounded below by a constant times \(1/t\) on its own arc of radius \(t\), whose normalized length is \(t/\pi\).
Summing the integrals of these individual nonnegative summands bounds \(N_t\) by a constant times \(\int_{U_t} H_t\,d\nu\), with all overlapped contributions retained.
Subtracting the mean \(f\) and applying Cauchy–Schwarz on \(U_t\) gives \(t N_t\ll fY/d+\sqrt{YB_t/d}\) with \(B_t=t\|H_t-f\|_2^2\).
The empty band and empty union also satisfy this inequality.

The nonzero Fourier coefficients of the smoothed count have modulus \(e^{-tl}|\sum_i w_i^l|\) at both frequencies of absolute value \(l\).

The number of eligible \(t\) is \(O(\Delta)\) because \(t\ge C_0Y/d\ge1/d\).
A root with \(t_i=0\) contributes nothing, and every \(0<t_i<1\) in an unselected band satisfies \(t_i<2C_0Y/d\).
This also bounds every \(t_i<1\) when no band is selected.
At \(t_i=1\) or greater the original Poisson kernel has bounded supremum and yields \(t_i\ll Y/d\).
Thus both exceptional ranges contribute \(O(fY/d)\).
The half-open band convention counts every other root exactly once, including those with a dyadic \(t_i\).
Cauchy–Schwarz over scales supplies \(\sqrt{\Delta\sum_t B_t}\), and the squarefree energy bound gives the first inequality of (1).
Separating \(\sqrt{v+\Delta}\le\sqrt v+\sqrt\Delta\) and absorbing the \(\sqrt v\) term by the elementary quadratic inequality gives the second with absolute constants.

Writing \(x=f/d\), the second bound divided by \(Y\) is \(O(x\Delta+\Delta\sqrt{x/Y})\).
It tends to zero for \(x=o(\Delta^{-2})\) since \(Y\ge1\).
For \(x\le K/\Delta^2\) it is \(O(K/\Delta+\sqrt{K/Y})\), yielding relative transfer at divergent whole measure and bounded \(Y\) whenever the remaining measure stays bounded.
Any positive shrinking divisor sequence has positive parent measures, and bounded \(d\) would give only finitely many integral divisors of bounded measure and degree.
Under the little-oh bound, bounded \(Y\) then forces \(v\to0\), in contradiction to a parent gap.

At the critical scale take \(x\le c/\Delta^2,\ c\le1\).
On a hypothetical shrinking divisor sequence, boundedness of \(Y\) follows first from the full-scale result with \(K=1\).
For some absolute \(C\), division of the second bound of (1) by \(\sqrt Y\) gives
\[
              v/\sqrt Y\le C\bigl(c\sqrt Y/\Delta+\sqrt c\bigr).
\]
The first term vanishes.
On the other hand,
\[
         \frac{v}{\sqrt Y}
             = \frac{m(G)}{\sqrt{1+m(G)}}-\frac{m(F)}{\sqrt Y}
             \ \ge\ \frac{\gamma}{\sqrt{1+\gamma}}-o(1)
\]
because \(m(G)\ge\gamma\) and the first function is increasing.
Taking \(c\le1\) with \(C\sqrt c<\gamma/\sqrt{1+\gamma}\) gives the required strict contradiction.
Only the parent gap and absolute constants enter this choice.

For \(x=O(\Delta^{-2})\) and bounded remaining measure, (1) gives \(v\ll_{B,K}\Delta\sqrt x\).
Any noncyclotomic irreducible factor of \(Q_0\) is monic with unit constant, hence has degree \(g>1\) and \(v\ge m(J)>0\).
For sufficiently large \(g\le d\), Dobrowolski's total logarithmic measure bound and the decreasing function \((\log y)/y\) at \(y=\log g>e\) give \(m(J)\gg(\log\Delta/\Delta)^3\).
Boundedly many smaller degrees have a positive gap by coefficient finiteness below \(M(J)=2\) and Kronecker's theorem, with measures above two already sufficient.
For \(f=o(d(\log\Delta)^6/\Delta^8)\) the upper bound for \(v\) is \(o((\log\Delta/\Delta)^3)\), forcing \(Q_0=1\).
The special bound \(Kd/\Delta^8\) meets this condition for every fixed \(K\).
The normalized-height factor \(1/g\) does not enter the lower bound for \(m(J)\).

At full scale in the stated multiplicative-interval class, bounded \(Y\) bounds \(\log|a|,\|L\|_1\) and hence the total absolute binomial exponent by C345v98.
The C345v101 annulus bound gives \(|\log|\alpha||=O(\Delta/d)\) for every parent root.
For \(g\le f\le Kd/\Delta^2\) this gives \(g|\log|\alpha||=O(1/\Delta)\), contradicting Dimitrov's house lower bound on one root of any noncyclotomic \(J\).
Thus all remaining cofactor roots are torsion there, giving eventual equality and transfer of the whole-polynomial gap.
Replacing \(f\) by the larger \(\deg Q\) in any upper hypothesis only strengthens it.
