## Verification of tangency and simultaneous squarefreeness

Centering the section divides it by \(X^{2k+l}=X^{(k+1)^2}\), so evaluation of the normalized equation equals evaluation of the stated trace.
Modulo 19 the polynomial \(X^2-11X+1\) has discriminant 3, a nonsquare since \(3^9=-1\) in that field.
Frobenius thus takes either root \(v\) to its inverse, and \(v^{20}=1\).
The residue 360 modulo 380 gives \(k=0\bmod20\) and \(k=-1\bmod19\), hence the section passes through \((1,v)\) with direction \((-1,2)\) in characteristic 19.
At this point \(t=0\), \(Dt=2k\), \(D^2t=0\), \(Dc=ld\), and \(Dd=lc\).
The first derivative is \((12l-14k)d=38d=0\), while the second is \((8k^2+12l^2-28kl)c=112\cdot11\ne0\bmod19\).
Since \(v\ne0\) and \(2\ne0\), these are the exact order-two conditions, and \(1-v^{-2}\ne0\) preserves that order in the trace.
This excludes the prime from the cited good-reduction test on the progression, consistently with the separate census in which 19 alone certified 94 of 128 sections.

For a trace of degree \(j\le h\), adjoining a lift requires at most a quadratic extension of \(\mathbb F_{p^j}\).
The lift has order dividing \(p^{2j}-1\), so the two powers of the lift specialize to 1 and the lift itself.
Locally both substitutions agree with these values as functions, respectively constant 1 and the coordinate \(X\), modulo the \(p^2\)-th power of the maximal ideal, by Frobenius applied to \(X^k-1\) and \(X^{k^2}-1\).
All inverted coordinates are units there, so the same congruence holds after substitution in the normalized Laurent polynomial.
The trace has local degree one or two by \(X+X^{-1}-z=(X-x)(X-x^{-1})/X\).
The trace polynomial consequently agrees with \(12T+1\) to order at least two at each tested trace, even when both lifts coincide.
This gives just one simple root for \(p>3\), including the ramified traces at 5 and 23, and none for 2 or 3 throughout the tested fields.

A hypothetical repeated trace root pulls back to a repeated root of the centered section, and its lift's minimal polynomial then divides the section's gcd with its derivative.
The cited degree bound applies because \(\gcd(k,k^2+1)=1\), putting the lift and its trace in an extension of degree at most 16, already covered.
For any one prime in the nonempty set, \(k\ge p^{2h}-1\), while multiplying all entries of the least common multiple bounds \(\log k\) above by \((2+h(h+1))\sum_{p\in S}\log p\).
Thus \(n\) is unbounded and \(\sqrt{\log n}=O_S(h)\).
Each nonexceptional factor has degree at least \(h+1\), giving at most \(1+n/(h+1)\) factors in total.
These checks establish both claims at their stated scope and leave the least subset sum at the height cutoff open.
