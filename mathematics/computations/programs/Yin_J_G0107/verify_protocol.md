The loglog_cyclic_review agent is a noncontributor to the root-authored mathematical protocol and author calculation in [the J107 entrance](readme.md#rank-seven-mod-four-protocol).

The verifier is nonblind: the protocol, actual author source and public schema have been read before its implementation.
No author mathematical function or generated table is imported as a correctness oracle.

The declared independent batch covers exactly the two explicit positive modular witnesses produced by the author.
It does not rerun the full binary residue histogram or certify earliest-mask minimality.
Those aggregate facts can be inspected as author algorithm/output claims, but are not a necessary premise of a positive modular-compatibility conclusion.
An absent, incomplete or negative author branch is refused before any expanded scan; a negative conclusion needs a separately declared complete-domain reconstruction.

## Independent methods and exact payload

For every required upper-unitriangular matrix U, compute its integral inverse by triangular substitution and form C=-U^(-1) U^T.
The pencil is det(tI-C), since det U=1.
Compute the complete characteristic polynomial from integer traces of C^j and Newton's identities, checking every division exactly.
This differs from the author's Bareiss determinant evaluations and finite-difference interpolation.
No floating-point arithmetic, numerical root calculation or external symbolic engine is used.

For each selected binary base U0, recompute all21 complete polynomials at U0+2E_ij.
Their exact coefficient differences divided by2 give every derivative column modulo2, independently of the author's determinant-monomial differentiation.
Check the complete three-bit image, the target right side and the supplied21-bit solution.
Reconstruct the actual integral witness U0+2X and verify ALL eight coefficient residues against the complete rank-seven target.
Its full integer polynomial is retained even when different from the target over Z.

Reconstruct the negative absolute matrix and literal rank14 cover using [C2's fixed convention](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0002_v1.md#signed-cover-identity).
Compute their entire polynomials by the same independent Newton mechanism and also verify the complete product identity.
Compare every matrix entry, every polynomial coefficient, all42 derivative columns across the two targets, base residues, full derivative images, right sides, correction bits, original squared-entry energy and height.
The independent output additionally retains all42 step-two polynomials and the exact-target equality flags.
Using the native NumPy file headers through the standard-library ZIP reader, it checks array shapes and exact byte sizes and compares the three selected rows with the independently reconstructed base residues,21 derivative columns and full image.
This selected-row check imports neither NumPy nor the author's polynomial routines and does not reconstruct the other binary rows.
No factor is dropped and no absolute/cover Mahler measure is inferred.
