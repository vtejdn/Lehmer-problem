Context: [continuous G0001 authority](../../../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#decision-continue-until-stop-2026-10-18), [C157v2 accepted gate](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0157_v2.md#decision-whole-reviewed-2026-10-18) and its complete [R378 independent review](../../../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0378_v1.md#buffered-shear-whole-independent-finding).

## Question and scope

Compute exactly two new component logarithms J_G=integral log|H35/30375|^2, where H35=250P(t^3)^5-243P(t^5)^3, on the same original quartic Salem and degree-ten Lehmer inputs as J99.
Their ascending coefficients are the quartic_Salem_seed and Lehmer_degree10 entries of [J97 input](../Yin_J_G0097/input.json).
The other two J97 entries are not inputs of this test.

The exact buffered family is B_(s,q)=q[(E+sG)^2+2G^2]+q^(-1)F_0^2 from C157v2.
On the circle, C157v2 proves for every real s and every real q>0

    I_(s,q)>=3log2/2+(J_G+J_V)/2
           =log2+(J_G+J_V+log2)/2.

The arithmetic full-norm theorem has the different domain s in Z and integer q>=1.
The sign S=J_G+J_V+log2 is a sufficient all-parameter screen; a negative S supplies neither a successful parameter nor an attained minimum.
No parameter grid is part of this diagnostic.

The author explicitly reuses J_V from J99 author output (`mathematics/computations/outputs/Yin_J_G0099/results.json`); the checker independently reuses J_V from J99 verification output (`mathematics/computations/outputs/Yin_J_G0099/verification.json`).
Both are actual previously certified enclosures at the scope of [R377](../../../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0377_v1.md#two-input-component-finding), not decimal guesses or new integrations.
The retained prior complete flags, original inputs, normalization and enclosing balls are checked before use.
