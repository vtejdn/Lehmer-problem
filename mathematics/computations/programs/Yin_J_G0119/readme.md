# Exact full-gate support certificate for odd orders coprime to five

Human author Yin.
This is a bounded exact algebra test under the current G0001 continuation.
The same_trace contributor derived the reduction and authored main.py and verify.py.
A separate reviewer subsequently inspected the entire fixed C608 proof and these native programs in an isolated context, with the actual scope recorded in [R810](../../../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0810_v1.md#whole-support-obstruction-finding).

## Question and scope

Can a product of complete odd-order trace groups whose orders are coprime to5 satisfy BOTH the normalized A_0=2 low-X condition and the full depth-eight C199v5 gate, for either original cyclotomic layer parity?
The fixed ring is R=F_2[X]/M with M=X^4(X^2+X+1)^8, of dimension20 over F_2.
The exact finite certificate tests the32 odd residue classes n modulo80 with5 not dividing n, and exactly two prescribed target classes.
There is no varying degree cutoff, floating point, random seed or unbounded numerical search.
The 80-period and divisor-product reduction are fixed in the [C608 argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0608_v1.md#argument) before any all-order interpretation.

A prospective same-T coefficient search was conditional on obtaining a support that passes this complete gate.
Its support selection rule is the first lexicographic product witness supplied by the fixed finite unit-group calculation; no support may be substituted after a failure.
The currently retained certificate instead separates both targets from the entire support subgroup, so that selection has no output and the actual monic coefficient/Gamma linear systems are not entered.
No actual T, divided quotient triple or integer Gamma is fabricated from a missing support.
A later test involving orders divisible by5 beyond5 is outside this finite allocation.

## Mathematical reduction

Put phi=X^2+X+1, chi=X phi^2, Psi=phi(chi), H=X^2Y, C=X^2Y(chi)Psi^14 and V=X^5Y.
The C199v5 branch supplies Y=1+X^2 moduloX^4 and the exact normalized gate Z(V+C^3)+HV epsilon X^4(X+1)^8 phi^4=0 modulophi^8, where Z^11=HC^2 modulophi^6 and epsilon is0 or1.
The complete proof reduces these conditions to Y=X+epsilon phi^4X^7 modulophi^8.
Combined with the low-X condition, these are precisely the two target elements tested in R.

For odd n, B_n is the monic trace polynomial of all nonidentity n-th roots, with B_1=1, B_3=X+1 and B_(n+2)=X B_n+B_(n-2) in characteristic two.
The recurrence matrix A satisfies A^8=I moduloX^4 and A^40=I modulophi^8, hence B_(n+80)=B_n moduloM.
When5 does not divide n, B_n is a unit in R.
Write Omega_d for the complete set of irreducible exact-order trace factors and W_d=product_(psi in Omega_d)psi for its polynomial.
The complete-order factor identity B_n=product_(d|n,d>1) W_d implies by induction that every product of the W_d with5 not dividing d belongs to the subgroup generated by these32 classes, including arbitrary multiplicities.
Conversely every such B_n is a product of precisely those allowed complete groups.
Finite group inverses introduce no additional attainable classes, because each inverse is a nonnegative power in the same finite unit group.

Since M'=0, D(u)=u'/u is a well-defined homomorphism from R units to the additive group of R.
The certificate supplies the explicit linear functional Lambda(f)=[X^13]f+[X^15]f.
It checks Lambda(D(B_n))=0 for all32 residue classes and Lambda(D(Y_target))=1 for each target.
Thus subgroup exclusion needs no BFS output or exhaustive membership oracle.
A separately implemented verifier uses coefficient sets, polynomial long division and inverse-by-linear-system instead of the generator's bit-polynomial Euclidean arithmetic.
Both programs are author work, not independent agent review.

## Results and limits

Both author commands completed successfully.
Every one of the32 generators has character0, both full-gate target classes have character1, and their logarithmic derivatives are identically X^15+X^7.
The generator verifies the complete recurrence matrix identity A^40=I, and the verifier reconstructs it with separate polynomial arithmetic.
For both original parity choices, the verifier also reconstructed the CRT target, C=X^2, the eleventh root, the entire normalized gate and the earlier support condition at their required precision.
The certificate's ambient unit count is393216; no BFS group-size result is needed or assumed.
These are two author implementations, not two independent agent reviews.
The separate isolated reviewer's independent verifier also completed successfully, checking every certificate field, all32 displayed C608 rows, both CRT targets, three exact polynomial matrix identities and all32 lifts of the two root classes from phi^6 to phi^8.
Its direct Laurent reconstruction of B_n does not use the author programs or their recurrence implementation.
Four in-memory corruption controls rejected an altered generator coefficient, a missing row, an altered target and an altered Claim-table derivative; the retained inputs were unchanged.
The complete analytical target, period, complete-group argument and original-domain application are covered by the [R810 whole finding](../../../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0810_v1.md#whole-support-obstruction-finding), which requires no substantive proof correction.
Claim adoption remains with C608's selected decision.
Failure of this complete restricted support family prevents its proposed same-T construction; the character also excludes supports in which every group of order divisible by5 beyond5 has even normalized multiplicity.
It does not exclude a support with an odd normalized multiplicity at such an order.
It does not decide whether C624 FE5 is forced on the unrestricted full gate, does not construct a characteristic-zero original input and gives no unrestricted Mahler estimate.
