# Exact separation tree for the fixed degree-56 cell

This is a separate bounded proof-generation attempt for the same fixed cell and height-four box as [trace56_cell.json](trace56_cell.json).
It does not repeat or extend the numerical MIP experiment, and none of its prefixes are identified with the MIP solver's reported nodes.
The precise question is whether every integer vector satisfying all 29 strict signs belongs to the complete `qL` or `qH` divisibility fiber.
The fixed cell, root configuration, trace basis and proof that the complete Mahler measure lies strictly between one and `6/5` remain in [the original analytic reduction](trace56_cell.md).
No new root calculation, parent, separator, coefficient range or target threshold is selected here.

## Exact enclosure and branch justification

The argument adapts the integer separation and coordinate bounds in [C876v2](../../../claims/Yin_C_G0876_v2.md#argument), [exact_cell_hull.py](exact_cell_hull.py) and [exact_cell_unbounded.py](exact_cell_unbounded.py).
Their degree-64 exclusion itself does not apply to this degree-56 cell.
For the current 29 rational nodes, write

\[
S(t)=\prod_{i=0}^{28}(t-s_i),\qquad V_i(t)=S(t)/(t-s_i).
\]

In the descending free coordinates of the basis `1,C1,...,C28`, the strict cell is the open simplex generated by the 29 vectors of `Vi`.
The proof uses its closed convex hull, which contains every strict-cell point and may also contain boundary points that must be rejected at a leaf.
[trace56_tree.py](trace56_tree.py) independently reconstructs the rational products, their monic Chebyshev expansions, every cleared integer sign row and both affine remainder maps from the fixed input.
This reconstruction uses only Python integer arithmetic and `Fraction`, without FLINT or numerical root routines.
It checks equality with all corresponding retained mathematical arrays.

Taking the least common multiple of the vertex-coordinate denominators gives the positive integer

\[
D=70835497243044678205440.
\]

Write each free-coordinate vertex as `v/D`, with `v` integral.
The initial coordinate box is the intersection of height four with the exact closed-hull extrema:

\[
L_j=\max\left(-4,\left\lceil\min_v v_j/D\right\rceil\right),
\qquad
U_j=\min\left(4,\left\lfloor\max_v v_j/D\right\rfloor\right).
\]

Its first seven intervals are `[-2,1]`, `[0,4]`, `[-4,1]`, `[-1,4]`, `[-4,2]`, `[-3,4]` and `[-4,3]`; the other 21 intervals are `[-4,4]`.
This exact box is checked against the frozen input before generation and verification.

For an integer normal `h` and a prefix `p` of length `k`, define the exact box maximum

\[
B(h,p)=\sum_{j<k}h_jp_j+\sum_{j\ge k}\max(h_jL_j,h_jU_j).
\]

If `min_v h·v > D B(h,p)`, the prefix box and closed hull have disjoint projections along `h`, so no feasible point extends that prefix.
The strict inequality is recomputed using integers for every recorded separating normal.
Otherwise, for any positive integer `a` and either sign `sigma`, convexity gives

\[
\sigma x_k\le
\left\lfloor\frac{D B(h,p)+\max_v(\sigma a v_k-h\cdot v)}{aD}\right\rfloor
\]

for every feasible integer extension of that prefix.
Indeed `sigma*a*x_k-h·x` is bounded by its maximum on the vertices, while `h·x` is bounded by `B(h,p)` on the prefix box.
The integer floor is valid because `sigma*x_k` is integral and `aD` is positive.
The program obtains a separate bound for each sign, intersects them with the current coordinate's fixed box interval and visits every remaining integer child in ascending order.
An empty interval is an exact exclusion, independent of the origin or quality of the two suggested normals.
The traversal is depth first in the fixed order `a1,...,a28`.

At every full leaf, the verifier first evaluates all 29 original cleared sign rows exactly and then computes both complete remainder vectors modulo the fixed monic `qL` and `qH`.
It records the exact prefix, every failed sign index, both remainder arrays and every zero-remainder fiber.
A strict-cell leaf with either zero remainder belongs to a known fiber; a leaf failing a strict sign is outside the selected cell.
A strict-cell leaf with both remainders nonzero is retained as a surviving integer point with the complete degree-56 polynomial and all strict sign values, and generation stops immediately.
That outcome does not itself perform or assert a new full-root certificate.

A missing, nonfinite or unhelpful suggestion falls back to an exact bound with zero normal; no floating infeasibility result deletes a prefix.
