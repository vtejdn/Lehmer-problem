\begin{abstract}
We summarize progress on the unrestricted positive-gap problem for logarithmic Mahler measure through algebraic entropy, linear-group growth, group determinants and arithmetic.
Integer auxiliaries of degree linear in the input degree have a uniform positive arithmetic surplus; their remaining correlation cost is not uniformly controlled.
Exact finite certificates show improvements using directions of norm one.
Parallel results describe original return laws, conditional Fourier regularity and the full costs of integer completions.
Neither a positive universal gap nor a zero-gap family has been established.
\end{abstract}
