# Neighboring translates of strict record measures and the cost of multiplicative descent

2026-11-25.
A research agent for Yin investigated whether multiplying translates from an [adjacent record row](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v163.md#statement) can give a smaller-degree witness against the record property.
The [single-target norm estimate](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v165.md#statement) controls one translate at a time.
The following derivation uses ideal valuations to compare a growing window of translates.
The [independent review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0562_v1228.md#finding) checks the window, the height and field conclusions, and the degree obstruction under their respective hypotheses.

<a id="window"></a>
## Distinct prime ideal divisors in a growing window

Assume the unrestricted logarithmic gap is zero.
Take consecutive strict records, write \(A=A_i=M(w_i)\), \(B=A_{i+1}=M(w_{i+1})\), \(m=\log A\), \(b'=\log B<m\), \(K=\mathbb Q(A)\), \(F=K(B)\) and \(D=[F:\mathbb Q]\).
The parameters and field may vary with the row.
Fix an integer \(t\ge2\), take \(a_0=\lfloor(\log t-b')/m\rfloor\) and use the window \(a_0-R,\ldots,a_0-1\) with \(R=R_i\) a positive integer.
Write \(z_a=t-A^aB\).
If \(R\sqrt{m\log(1/m)}\to0\), then all these exponents are positive and admissible eventually, by the [row endpoints](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v163.md#rows) and \(Rm\to0\).
For every vector of integers \(k_a\), let \(\|k\|_1=\sum_a|k_a|\).
Uniformly over all such vectors,
\[
 \frac1D\sum_{\mathfrak p}
  \left|v_{\mathfrak p}\!\left(\prod_a z_a^{k_a}\right)\right|\log N\mathfrak p
       =(\log t+o(1))\|k\|_1.                                      \tag{1}
\]
The sum ranges over the prime ideals of \(F\), with \(v_{\mathfrak p}\) normalized to give integral exponents of principal ideals.
The window therefore has \(R\) pairwise coprime integral ideals, one divisor of each \((z_a)\), all of norm \(\exp((\log t+o(1))D)\).
This concerns prime ideals in a varying common field; multiple prime ideals can lie above one rational prime.

First, each \(z_a\) has normalized log ideal norm \(\log t+o(1)\), uniformly.
Indeed \(A^aB\) generates \(F\) by the [strict Perron product argument](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v155.md#fields), and the deficit \(s=\log(t/(A^aB))\) lies between \(m\) and \((R+1)m\).
The [uniform exterior estimate](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v165.md#energy) and the [relative-degree bounds in its application](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v165.md#rows) apply with \(\log^+(1/s)\le\log(1/m)\), giving the assertion.
Each \(z_a\) is coprime to \(t\) since \(A,B\) are units.

For any two distinct indices \(a,c\), the ideal \((z_a,z_c)\) divides \((A^{|a-c|}-1)\), by subtraction and the unit property.
Put \(\lambda_j=D^{-1}\log|N_{F/\mathbb Q}\Phi_j(A)|\), with \(\Phi_j\) the \(j\)-th cyclotomic polynomial.
These quantities are nonnegative and \(\sum_{j\mid r}\lambda_j=D^{-1}\log|N_{F/\mathbb Q}(A^r-1)|\).
Take \(N=\lceil\sqrt{\log(1/m)/m}\rceil\), so \(R\le N/2\) eventually, and \(h(A)\le m\) since \(A\) is a strict Perron unit.
For \(j\le N/2\) the weights in the [simultaneous cyclotomic norm inequality](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v730.md#statement) satisfy \(E_j(N)/\binom N2\ge1/(4j)\).
Its right side \(U_N(h(A))\) is \(O(\sqrt{m\log(1/m)})\).
Consequently
\[
 \sum_{r=1}^R\frac{\log|N_{F/\mathbb Q}(A^r-1)|}{D}
   =\sum_{j=1}^R\lfloor R/j\rfloor\lambda_j
   \le4R\,U_N(h(A))=o(1).
\]
Each positive difference occurs at most twice for fixed \(a\), so the sum over \(c\ne a\) of the normalized log norms of the common ideals is \(o(1)\), uniformly in \(a\).

Assign each prime ideal occurring in the window to an index where its valuation is greatest, breaking ties arbitrarily.
For each \(a\), the contribution of its valuations at primes assigned elsewhere is bounded by that sum of common ideals.
Keep the full \(a\)-valuations at its assigned primes to obtain the claimed pairwise coprime divisors.
On these disjoint sets, the triangle inequality from below bounds the left side of (1) by the sum of the retained masses weighted by \(|k_a|\), less the sum of the discarded masses weighted by \(|k_a|\).
The triangle inequality from above uses the total mass at each index.
This proves (1), including coefficients that grow arbitrarily fast with \(i\).
Multiplication by any algebraic unit preserves the finite valuation expression, using normalized log norms in any common overfield.

<a id="descent"></a>
## Multiplication by first-field elements and units

For the shorter window satisfying \(R^3m\log(1/m)\to0\), take arbitrary \(\lambda\in K^\times\), an arbitrary algebraic unit \(u\), and a nonzero integer vector \(k\).
For
\[
 \gamma=u\lambda\prod_a(t-A^aB)^{k_a}
\]
one has, uniformly in these choices,
\[
 h(\gamma)\ge\bigl(\tfrac12\log t-o(1)\bigr)\|k\|_1,
 \qquad K(\gamma)\supseteq F.                                        \tag{2}
\]
The field inclusion holds also when \(u\) lies outside \(F\).
In particular the total log measure of \(\gamma\) cannot improve the preceding record \(m\) in sufficiently late rows.

In a finite common normal field containing \(F\) and \(u\), choose any automorphism \(\tau\) fixing \(K\) but moving \(B\), and put \(B'=\tau B\).
Such automorphisms exist eventually because the [relative degree tends to infinity](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v159.md#orbits).
Use the two lists \(t-A^a B\) and \(t-A^a B'\) together, with log ideal norms divided by the degree of the common normal field.
Each individual entry has the same estimate as before, by conjugacy.
For a pair from different lists the common ideal divides the ideal of \(\xi-1\), with \(\xi=A^{a-c} B/B'\), by subtraction and division by a unit.
The ratio has infinite order.
For \(a\ge c\), strict Perron dominance gives \(|A^{a-c}B/B'|>1\) at the distinguished embedding.
For \(a<c\), apply \(\tau^{-1}\) to the inverse ratio, putting the larger exponent and the distinguished \(B\) in its numerator.
Its absolute height is at most \(Rm+2b'\), by conjugacy, the triangle inequality for heights and \(h(B)\le b'\).
The [one-factor cyclic norm bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v730.md#statement) therefore bounds each cross overlap by \(O(\sqrt{Rm\log(1/m)})\) in normalized log norm.
The sum of the cross overlaps for each entry is \(O(\sqrt{R^3m\log(1/m)})=o(1)\).
All constants are independent of \(\tau\) and the overfield degree.
Within-list overlaps still satisfy the preceding bound.
In particular the two conjugate entries at any fixed exponent have identical rational absolute norms, although their common ideal has normalized log norm \(o(1)\).
This same construction explains why ideal separation cannot by itself give a bound for the gcd of rational norms.
The same prime allocation proves (1) for the combined lists.
Applied with coefficients \(k\) and \(-k\), it gives normalized finite absolute valuation mass \((2\log t+o(1))\|k\|_1\) for \(\gamma/\tau\gamma\), since \(\lambda\) cancels and \(u/\tau u\) is a unit.
For any algebraic number, twice its absolute height bounds this finite mass from above by the product formula.
Since \(h(\gamma/\tau\gamma)\le2h(\gamma)\), the height estimate in (2) follows.
The quotient has a nonzero finite valuation for every such automorphism in all sufficiently late rows.
Consequently every automorphism fixing \(K\) and \(\gamma\) fixes \(B\), proving the field assertion.

## A degree obstruction when the later record has two exceptional positions

Suppose the exterior product \(B\) has at least two exceptional positions in the [signed or oriented block representation](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v149.md#statement).
Write \(d_{i+1}=2s b\) for the later source degree, with \(s\) blocks of \(b\) inverse pairs each.
The [relative block actions](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v159.md#orbits) yield \([F:K]\ge\binom b2\) for all sufficiently late such rows.
Indeed two occupied blocks give at least \(b^2\) images by their independent alternating actions.
In a single oriented block, the positions outside a most frequent color number at least two and at most \(2b/3\).
Their position sets alone have at least \(\binom b2\) images for large \(b\), since the alternating group acts transitively on subsets of each given size.
Subtracting the block mean preserves color distinctions.
In a single independent signed block with \(2\le e\le b-2\) occupied positions the same subset count works.
For \(e=b-1,b\), even sign flips on the block already give at least \(2^{b-1}\) images.
The relative action fixes the earlier record field.
Distinct exterior coefficient classes give distinct conjugates, so the counts lower-bound the relative degree.
Since \(s\ll(\log(3d_{i+1}))^{212}\), one has \(\binom b2>d_{i+1}\) eventually.
It follows from (2) that \([\mathbb Q(\gamma):\mathbb Q]\ge[K(\gamma):K]\ge[F:K]>d_{i+1}\).

These obstructions apply to multiplicative expressions in the specified window, including arbitrary first-field multipliers and units.
They leave open a descent using additive combinations or a different polynomial of the record inputs.
No bound excluding \(A^aB\to2\) from below is obtained.
