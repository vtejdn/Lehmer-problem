# Rational terminal supports at Lehmer's unit in a window of length twenty-two

<a id="counts"></a>
## Exact counts at the degree-ten unit

Let \(\tau>1\) be the Salem root of \(P(X)=X^{10}+X^9-X^7-X^6-X^5-X^4-X^3+X+1\).
The window is \(0\le t<22\), with the least exponent translated to zero and its coefficient fixed to \(+1\).
Among 4159 nonzero ternary relations with these conventions there are 557 inclusion-minimal supports.
The sign choice on each minimal support is unique with this normalization, since subtracting two different choices and dividing by two gives a nonzero ternary relation on a proper subset.
The third column counts survivors under the [two minimal supports with a shared missing in-span exponent](Yin_L_G0462_v295.md#splitting) rule.
The fourth column counts survivors under the [full rational splitting rule](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#statement), which permits any nonzero rational coefficient and any integer exponent.
The last column divides each surviving support by its exponent gcd before counting, retaining the mark at its least exponent.

| 5 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 |

Of the full survivors, twelve distinct primitive weight-seven lists share the same fixed outgoing signs \((-1,-1,1,-1,-1,1)\) in increasing exponent order, with output \(-1\) at the marked position.
The six additional eliminations all use only coefficients of absolute value one at exponents outside the parent span.
The following table lists **all** intermediate exponents for each such support, allowing either sign and all rational coefficients.

In particular the defining relation \(P(\tau)=0\) splits because \(\tau^{10}-\tau^3-\tau^4-\tau^5=-\tau^{-2}\), an identity checked by division after multiplication by \(X^2\).
In contrast, the [squared progression](Yin_L_G0462_v305.md#period) on \(0,2,6,8,10,12,14,18,20\), with positive positions \(0,8,12,20\), survives even the full test.
Its primitive exponent support equals the support of the defining polynomial, so terminality can differ on the same primitive support at different dilations and signs.
These counts concern one fixed unit, one window and the marked ternary source relations themselves.

## Why the enumeration exhausts the allowed carriers

Use degree-less-than-ten remainder coordinates in \(\mathbb Z[X]/(P)\).
For a partial sum \(z\), an identity \(z=c\tau^h\) with rational \(c\ne0\) forces \(c\) to be a rational algebraic integer, since \(c=z\tau^{-h}\) and \(\tau\) is a unit.
Indeed multiplication by \(\tau^h\) is unimodular on this order, so the gcd of the coordinates of \(z\) equals \(|c|\).
At a unit-circle conjugate the triangle inequality gives \(|c|\le w\) for weight \(w\).
At \(\tau\) and \(\tau^{-1}\) it gives \(\tau^h\le w\tau^{\max S}\) and \(\tau^{-h}\le w\), respectively, using \(|c|\ge1\).
The [verified lower bound](Yin_L_G0462_v289.md#density) \(\tau>7/6\) and \(7^{22}>22\cdot6^{22}\) therefore place \(h\) in \([-22,\max S+22]\) throughout this window.
The total zero sum allows checking just subsets missing the mark, because a complementary partial sum has the opposite sign.
A support is split under the older rule precisely when two translated minimal supports within its span meet in one absent exponent and their symmetric difference is the given support.

The Python 3 calculation below constructs all signed relations by matching signed sums in two halves and tests inclusion against every translate of previously retained smaller supports.
The signed radix encoding is linear and injective for every comparison made, since the base exceeds twice the largest possible absolute coordinate of a partial sum or of a tested carrier.
A second enumeration chose the first twelve ternary digits and computed the unique possible integral quotient by \(P\) from the constant coefficient upward, then rejected nonternary final digits.
It reproduced every full signed relation, and independent translated-support pairing and half-subset carrier matching reproduced the survivor counts and the six full carrier lists above.

```python
from collections import defaultdict,Counter
from math import gcd
N=22
P=[1,1,0,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,0,1,1]
def step(v): return [a-v[-1]*b for a,b in zip([0]+v[:-1],P)]
def back(v): return [v[i+1]-v[0]*P[i+1] for i in range(9)]+[-v[0]]
vs={0:[1]+[0]*9}
for i in range(N+22): vs[i+1]=step(vs[i])
for i in range(22): vs[-i-1]=back(vs[-i])
assert all(step(vs[i])==vs[i+1] for i in range(-22,N+22))
B=1+2*(N*max(abs(x) for v in vs.values() for x in v)+sum(max(map(abs,vs[i])) for i in range(N)))
def enc(v):return sum(x*B**i for i,x in enumerate(v))
V={i:enc(v) for i,v in vs.items()}
def words(lo,hi):
    rows=[(0,0,0)]
    for i in range(lo,hi):
        bit=1<<i
        rows += [(v+V[i],m|bit,p|bit) for v,m,p in rows]+[(v-V[i],m|bit,p) for v,m,p in rows]
    return rows
left=defaultdict(list)
for v,m,p in words(0,N//2):
    if p&1:left[v].append((m,p))
relations=defaultdict(list)
for v,m,p in words(N//2,N):
    for a,b in left.get(-v,()):relations[a|m].append(b|p)
mins={};trans=set()
for s in sorted(relations,key=int.bit_count):
    if any(t&s==t for t in trans): continue
    assert len(relations[s])==1
    mins[s]=relations[s][0]
    trans.update(s<<j for j in range(N+1-s.bit_length()))
print('relations/minimal',sum(map(len,relations.values())),len(mins),flush=True)
carrier={c*V[h]:(c,h) for h in V for c in range(-N,N+1) if c}
counts=Counter(); exceptions={}; signs=Counter(); primitive=defaultdict(set)
for s,p in mins.items():
    idx=[i for i in range(N) if s>>i&1]; w=len(idx); mx=idx[-1]
    orig=any((u^s) in trans for u in trans if u.bit_length()<=mx+1 and (u&~s).bit_count()==1 and (s&u).bit_count()>=2 and (s&~u).bit_count()>=2)
    # u^s contains the shared intermediate and the complementary leaves.
    parts=[(0,0)]
    for i in idx[1:]:
        x=V[i] if p>>i&1 else -V[i]
        parts += [(v+x,b|1<<i) for v,b in parts]
    hits=set()
    for v,b in parts:
        if 2<=b.bit_count()<=w-2 and v in carrier:
            c,h=carrier[v]; hits.add((h,abs(c)))
    assert not orig or hits
    counts[w,'all']+=1;counts[w,'old']+=not orig;counts[w,'full']+=not hits
    if not hits:
        g=gcd(*idx);primitive[w].add(tuple(i//g for i in idx))
        signs[tuple(1 if p>>i&1 else -1 for i in idx[1:])]+=1
    if not orig and hits: exceptions[tuple(idx)]=sorted(hits)
for w in sorted({w for w,k in counts}):
    print(w,counts[w,'all'],counts[w,'old'],counts[w,'full'],len(primitive[w]))
print(exceptions,signs.most_common(1))
```
