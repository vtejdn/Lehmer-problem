# A disk sum that bounds the Mahler measure of the original unit

<a id="input-bound"></a>
## Adjacent rows at one admissible prime

Let \(P\) be the minimal polynomial of a nontorsion algebraic integer unit \(\alpha\) of degree \(d\) and put \(h=\log M(P)\).
Take \(d\) sufficiently large for the [finite witness comparison](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v363.md#statement).
Fix any degree-preserving prime \(y<p\le2y\), where \(y=\lceil(\log(3d))^4\rceil\), and let \(Q\) be the minimal polynomial of \(\alpha^p\).
Select finitely many primes \(\ell\ne p\), and at each \(\ell\) choose finite positive-depth disks \(D\) with unit centers and distinct center residues at that prime.
At each disk one may choose its own integral expression \(A,B\in\mathbb Z[X]\) with \(B(\alpha^p)\ne0\) and \(\alpha B(\alpha^p)=A(\alpha^p)\).
Put \(T=(A^p-XB^p)/Q\) and require \(T(\alpha^p)\ne0\).
Write \(v_D\) for the scaled coefficient Gauss valuation at depth \(s_D>0\), with \(v_\ell(\ell)=1\), and set
\[
 a_D=\min(v_D(A),v_D(B)),\qquad c_D=v_D(T),\qquad
 u_D=\big[\min((p-1)a_D,v_D(Q))-c_D\big]_+ .
\]
With \(C=\int_{\mathbb T}(2/|1-z|)^{1/2}\,d\mu(z)\) for normalized Haar measure, one has
\[
 (2p+2)h+\frac{2}{p}\log\!\big(Cp(16d+1)\big)
 \ \ge\ \frac{1-4/p}{32d+1}
          \sum_{\ell\ne p}(\log\ell)\sum_D\frac{u_D^2}{s_D}.             \tag{1}
\]
The disk choices and the rational expressions on the right refer only to the original unit and its power.

Indeed take the integral witness \(F\) on the prescribed classes \(0,1\) provided by the finite comparison, divide out its content, and let \(R\) be the product of its noncyclotomic factors left after two copies of \(P\), omitting monomials and taking all multiplicities.
The comparison gives \(m(R)\le2p h\), and the polynomial \(F\) has coprime Laurent rows and degree at most \(p(16d+1)\).
For its stripped rows the degree sum \(N\) is positive and at most \(32d+1\).
The [two congruences](Yin_L_G0462_v157.md#identities) at row difference one give common valuation at least \(u_D\) on each chosen disk of each auxiliary prime.
The same witness satisfies every bound, even when different expressions for the unit establish the contributions at different disks.
The [disjoint-center determinant](Yin_L_G0462_v154.md#resultant) adds the weights at each prime, then prime factorization adds their logarithms in the nonzero integer row resultant.
Apply the [arithmetic row inequality](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v366.md#statement) to this witness and use the upper bound for \(m(R)\), the degree sum and the exponent span.
This proves (1).

<a id="small-cofactor"></a>
## Where the other powered factors have small valuation

On any disk where \(c_D\le a_D\), the eliminant gives \(v_D(Q)\ge pa_D-c_D\ge(p-1)a_D\).
Thus \(u_D\ge(p-2)a_D\), and (1) controls the simpler quantity
\[
                W(P,p)=\sum_{\ell}(\log\ell)
                   \sum_{D:\,c_D\le a_D}a_D^2/s_D .
\]
For varying \(P\) of degree tending to infinity and an admissible \(p\) for each input, if \(pW(P,p)/d\) has lower limit at least \(\kappa>0\), then \(\liminf h\ge\kappa/64\).
This follows by inserting \((p-2)^2W(P,p)\) in (1) and dividing by \(2p+2\), since \(\log(p(16d+1))/p^2\to0\).
Conversely, if \(h\to0\), then \(pW(P,p)/d\to0\) for every such choice of prime, expressions and finite collections of disks, by the same inequality.
The limits here use natural logarithms in \(h,W\).

For the [Salem family at three](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v375.md#disks), write \(m=d/p,\ q=3^{v_3(m)}\).
There are \(m/q\) disks of depth \(1/q\) with \(a_D\ge1,\ c_D\le1/q\), so \(W(P,p)\ge(d/p)\log3\).
Formula (1) consequently gives \(\liminf h\ge(\log3)/64\) along that family in the stated window using the finite-place data alone as its input bound.
Its [known actual limit](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v371.md#salem) is \(\log2\).
For general units it remains to find an admissible prime and disks with \(pW(P,p)/d\) bounded below, or to control the witnesses by another contribution when this quantity vanishes.
