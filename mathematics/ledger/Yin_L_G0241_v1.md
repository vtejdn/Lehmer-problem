### 2. The sharp determinant bound for every auxiliary collection

Let C be an N-by-d integer coefficient matrix.
Its columns are d integral polynomials F_1,...,F_d of degree at most N-1.
Assume VC is nonsingular.
In particular no F_j is a P multiple, so irreducibility guarantees Res(P,F_j)!=0 for each column.
Define the full joint ratio

    J_N(C)=|det(F_j(alpha_i))_(i,j=1)^d|^2
                          /det(C^T C).               (3)

The denominator is positive because VC has rank d.
It is the full Gram determinant, not the product of individual coefficient norms.
The square and complex absolute value in the numerator are indispensable.

The orthogonal projection onto the complement of the P-multiple space is

    R=V^T W^(-1)V.                                    (4)

Indeed R is real symmetric and idempotent, its image is the row space of V and its kernel is ker V.
Thus it is precisely I-Pi from L237 in the degree window N.
For B=VC, decompose C into RC+(I-R)C.
The two coefficient subspaces are orthogonal, and

    (RC)^T(RC)=B^T W^(-1)B,
    C^T C=B^T W^(-1)B+C^T(I-R)C.                     (5)

The second summand is positive semidefinite.
For a positive definite M and positive semidefinite H, det(M+H)>=det M: factor out M^(1/2), and all eigenvalues of I+M^(-1/2)HM^(-1/2) are at least one.
Equality holds precisely when H=0.
Applied here,

    det(C^T C)>=det(B)^2/G_N,                         (6)

with equality if and only if (I-R)C=0.

Let E=(alpha_i^j)_(i=1,...,d; j=0,...,d-1).
Monic remainder evaluation gives the exact matrix of ALL original-root values as EVC=EB.
The roots are distinct, so E is invertible and |det E|^2=Delta.
Therefore(3)--(6) give

    J_N(C)<=Delta G_N,
    equality iff every coefficient column of C
                         lies in im R.               (7)

No archimedean root or polynomial factor has been omitted.
The proof applies as a real coefficient inequality as well; integrality and irreducibility ensure that the actual auxiliary interpretation has nonzero individual resultants.

The upper bound is attained by actual integer polynomials, not just as a real supremum.
Choose

    C_*=V^T adj(W),       VC_*=G_N I_d.                (8)

Its columns are integral, lie in im R, and have values G_N alpha_i^j,0<=j<d, at the original roots.
Hence each has full absolute resultant G_N^d.
For N>d, remove each column's positive coefficient content c_j.
The actual-order argument of L237 gives c_j|G_N because alpha^j is a unit in O=Z[alpha].
Thus the primitive column has exact root value (G_N/c_j)alpha_i^j and every individual p-adic depth v_p(G_N/c_j).
The columns remain jointly independent and(7) remains an equality.
Constants or a zero income in an individual normalized column are not needed for the determinant identity; the full-rank primitive collection and its nonzero root values suffice.
At N=d the projection is I and the trivial basis already attains Delta, with G_d=1.
No spurious all-root income is claimed in that boundary case.

Consequently the sharp arithmetic statement is

    max_(integral C, det(VC)!=0) J_N(C)=Delta G_N.     (9)

Columnwise primitive normalization does not alter the maximum or its value: numerator and denominator are both multiplied by product_j c_j^(-2).
Similarly a nonsingular rational change C->C A multiplies both by det(A)^2; after clearing any denominators the ratio is unchanged.
These operations cannot improve(9), even when the individual resultants or displayed coefficient contents change substantially.

### 3. Every rounded monomial ensemble pays exactly the kernel error

To see the relation to L237 explicitly, choose a d-element subset J of coefficient positions0,...,N-1 for which V_J is nonsingular.
Let E_J be the N-by-d column selector and let Q be a positive diagonal matrix of integer scales.
Exact rationally scaled residuals have coefficient matrix R E_J Q.
Their Gram determinant is

    det(Q R[J,J] Q)=det(Q)^2 det(V_J)^2/G_N.          (10)

Their whole root-value matrix is E V_J Q.
Thus the ratio is exactly Delta G_N, independently of the chosen full-rank positions and scales.
Choosing several individually small residuals does not guarantee that their V_J is nonsingular; the rank condition is retained rather than hidden.

For integral approximations C=R E_J Q+H obtained by rounding the P-multiple coefficients, every column of H lies in ker V.
Therefore

    C^T C=Q R[J,J] Q+H^T H,
    det(F_j(alpha_i))=det(E V_J Q).                  (11)

The first matrix on the right is positive definite.
Any nonzero rounding error H strictly increases its determinant, by the equality condition used in(6).
It follows that such rounding can only reduce J_N(C), with equality exactly when H=0.
This is a complete finite-dimensional charge, not a coefficientwise estimate or an assumption that rounding errors have favorable signs.
Independent dyadic choices, common scaling, primitive normalization and any subsequently cleared rational basis change leave this conclusion valid.
No further p-adic floor can be kept after a content division except the actual values of the normalized polynomials.

Thus a simultaneous determinant cannot multiply the separate low-energy improvements while discarding their coefficient correlations.
Keeping those correlations exactly returns(9); discarding them replaces the Gram determinant by a different object and requires its own justified inequality and full cost.

### 5. Endpoint columns and the inherited finite-window issue

For k=N-d>=1, the relation Gram matrix in(2) is Toeplitz.
Its two corner cofactors are D_(k-1).
The first row of the relation matrix is a_0 e_0^T and its last row is e_(k-1)^T.
Since a_0^2=1, the two endpoint residual norms in R satisfy the exact identities

    R_(0,0)=R_(N-1,N-1)
       =1-D_(k-1)/D_k=1-1/r_k,
    r_k=D_k/D_(k-1).                                 (15)

This is a direct cofactor computation in the same projection, not a new form of the Szego theorem.

    lim_(k->infinity) ||R_N e_0||_2^2
       =1-exp(-2m),

The second expression is the exact-rational projected scalar margin when its denominator is cleared as in L237; normalization changes neither side.

### 6. Actual radical blocks and the scope of support growth

Let p be a fixed monic irreducible nontorsion unit of degree d_0, let P(t)=p(t^ell), and take N=ell N_0 with N_0>=d_0.
Suppose P is actually irreducible; this is checked below for the control family, not presumed for every ell.
Since C[t]/(p(t^ell)) decomposes by exponent residue modulo ell, a coefficient polynomial has the unique form

    F(t)=sum_(a=0)^(ell-1) t^a F_a(t^ell),
                    deg F_a<N_0.

It is divisible by P exactly when each F_a is divisible by p.
Polynomial division, or the independent remainder coordinates t^(a+ell b), proves both directions without a root selection.
Permuting coefficients by their residue class is an orthogonal permutation.
The full relation subspace is the orthogonal direct sum of ell copies of the base relation subspace.
Hence the residual projection itself, not merely its determinant, obeys

    R_N(P) is permutation-conjugate to
             direct_sum_(a=0)^(ell-1) R_(N_0)(p).    (17)

In particular the column at index a+ell b is t^a times the base column at b with t replaced by t^ell.
Its full S_1,S_2, rho and exact-rational projected margin are unchanged.
The content of an integral lifted column is unchanged as well.
A base dyadic rounding F_0(x) with exact value2^r alpha^b lifts to t^a F_0(t^ell), whose value at every lifted root beta is2^r beta^(a+ell b).
It retains depth r, coefficient energy and effective support; its full norm is2^(r d_0 ell), not the base norm.
Its actual degree is ell deg(F_0)+a.
Thus every increase in original degree and root multiplicity is charged, while no artificial ell factor is inserted into the support.

L4 already retains the corresponding Gram/discriminant dilation identities

    G_(ell N_0)(P)=G_(N_0)(p)^ell,
    Delta(P)=ell^(ell d_0) Delta(p)^ell.              (18)

For completeness, the first also follows from the block relation matrix.
For the second use P'(beta)=ell beta^(ell-1)p'(beta^ell), multiply over all root fibers, and use the unit constant term to remove the absolute-value product of beta.
All roots are distinct since p is separable with nonzero roots.
It follows that

    log(Delta(P)G_(ell N_0)(P))-ell d_0 log(ell N_0)
       =ell[log(Delta(p)G_(N_0)(p))-d_0 log N_0].     (19)

The determinant certificate's original degree penalty cancels the new ell logarithm exactly.
Neither scalar growth nor block repetition creates a new fixed positive surplus absent in the base.
