# A powered trace discriminant divides the sum of the two compressed jets

<a id="different"></a>
## An integral identity in the powered seed trace order

Let \(P_0\) be the monic reciprocal minimal polynomial of a nontorsion unit \(\alpha\), of degree \(e=2m\), and write \(q_0=\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\), \(K_0=\mathbb Q(q_0)\).
Take a positive integer \(p\) for which \(z=\alpha^p\) still has degree \(e\), put \(s=z+z^{-1}\), and let \(T_p\) be its monic trace polynomial, the minimal polynomial of \(s\).
The involution \(\alpha\mapsto\alpha^{-1}\) on \(\mathbb Q(\alpha)=\mathbb Q(z)\) exchanges \(z\) and \(z^{-1}\).
Its fixed field is both \(\mathbb Q(q_0)\) and \(\mathbb Q(s)\), since each of the two quadratic equations for \(\alpha,z\) over the respective trace field has its two distinct roots exchanged.
In particular \(\deg T_p=m\).

For a positive integer \(n\), suppose that \(a\in\mathbb Z[Y]\) and \(u,w\in K_0\) satisfy the two equations for \(a\) in the [compressed seed jets](Yin_L_G0462_v229.md#space).
Explicitly, with \(\mathcal E=Y\,d/dY\), \(J=4pne+1\), and \(f(Y)=Y^{-2e}a(Y)\), these are
\[
       f(z)=u,\qquad 2pn\,(\mathcal E f)(z)=u+w.                       \tag{1}
\]
Write \(S=Y+Y^{-1}\) and \(f^\iota(Y)=f(Y^{-1})\).
There is a unique \(Z\in\mathbb Z[S]\) such that
\[
       f-f^\iota=(Y-Y^{-1})T_p(S)Z(S),\qquad
       u+w=pn(s^2-4)T_p'(s)Z(s).                                      \tag{2}
\]
Indeed each \(Y^j-Y^{-j}\), \(j\ge1\), is \(Y-Y^{-1}\) times an integral polynomial in \(S\), by the recurrence with initial quotients \(1,S\).
Thus \(f-f^\iota=(Y-Y^{-1})H(S)\) for \(H\in\mathbb Z[S]\).
At \(Y=z\), the left side is zero because \(u\) is fixed by the involution.
Since \(z^2\ne1\), this gives \(H(s)=0\), and monic division gives \(H=T_p Z\) over \(\mathbb Z[S]\).
The substitution in the transcendental \(Y\) is injective, giving uniqueness.
The Euler derivative of the left side at \(z\) is \((\mathcal E f)(z)+(\mathcal E f)(z^{-1})=2(\mathcal E f)(z)\), since (1) makes \((\mathcal E f)(z)\) fixed by inversion.
On the right only the derivative of \(T_p\) survives there, and \(\mathcal E S=Y-Y^{-1}\).
This proves (2) integrally, including at primes dividing \(2pn\).

For either final row of [L229](Yin_L_G0462_v229.md#rows), \(u\ne0\) and \(w/u\notin\mathbb Q\), so \(u+w\ne0\).
With \(N=N_{K_0/\mathbb Q}\), the identity gives
\[
 (pn)^m\big|T_p(2)T_p(-2)\operatorname{disc}T_p\big|
                       \ \mid\ |N(u+w)|.                              \tag{3}
\]
In fact the quotient equals \(|N(Z(s))|\), a positive integer, by the root formulas for the monic \(T_p\), its values at \(2,-2\), and its discriminant.
For a general pair satisfying (1) one needs to assume \(u+w\ne0\) to get a positive lower bound.
The [odd-span trace residue](Yin_L_G0462_v189.md#odd) applies to the derivative remainder after differentiating a reduced value representative in the original trace variable.
Here the separate \(a\)-equations put both the value and the Euler derivative in \(K_0\), which gives a divisor of \(u+w\) in the powered trace order itself.

## The exponent of the divisor for a fixed seed

Choose one conjugate \(\rho_j\) of \(\alpha\) from each inverse pair with \(|\rho_j|\ge1\), and number them by
\[
  0\le\lambda_1\le\cdots\le\lambda_m,\qquad
  \lambda_j=\log|\rho_j|,\qquad \sum_{j=1}^m\lambda_j=h=m(P_0)>0.
\]
When \(p\) preserves the degree, the roots of \(T_p\) are the \(m\) distinct numbers \(s_j=\rho_j^p+\rho_j^{-p}\).
For fixed \(P_0\), through all such positive integers \(p\ge2\),
\[
 \log\big|T_p(2)T_p(-2)\operatorname{disc}T_p\big|
             =2p\sum_{j=1}^m j\lambda_j+O_{P_0}(\log(2p)).           \tag{4}
\]
In particular the quotient by \(p\) tends to \(2\sum_j j\lambda_j\).
For prime \(p\), the excluded primes are exactly the orders which are prime among the root-of-unity quotients of two distinct conjugates of \(\alpha\).
Indeed powering identifies a pair exactly when its quotient has order dividing \(p\), and a root can never coincide with its own inverse after powering since it is nontorsion.
Hence there are only finitely many excluded primes for a fixed seed.

For each fixed algebraic \(\xi\) with \(|\xi|\le1\) and \(\xi^p\ne1\), one has \(\log|1-\xi^p|=O_\xi(\log(2p))\).
For \(|\xi|<1\) one has \(|1-\xi^p|\to1\).
For a root of unity the nonzero distances have a positive minimum.
For a nontorsion point of the unit circle the lower estimate is the [Baker–Wüstholz distance bound](../audits/literature/Wus02/Yin_U001.md#unit-circle), and the upper bound is \(\log 2\).
The factors in
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 s_j^2-4&=\rho_j^{2p}(1-\rho_j^{-2p})^2,\\
 s_j-s_i&=\rho_j^p
   \big(1-(\rho_i/\rho_j)^p\big)\big(1-(\rho_i\rho_j)^{-p}\big)
   \qquad(i<j)
 \end{aligned}
\]
are consequently nonzero at every allowed exponent, even for tied radii or neutral pairs.
The resulting logarithmic absolute values are \(2p\lambda_j+O_{P_0}(\log(2p))\) and \(p\lambda_j+O_{P_0}(\log(2p))\), respectively.
The product of \(|s_j^2-4|\) over \(j\) is \(|T_p(2)T_p(-2)|\), and the product of \(|s_j-s_i|^2\) over \(i<j\) is \(|\operatorname{disc}T_p|\).
The \(j\)-th radius occurs once in the first sum and \(j-1\) times in the squared differences, proving (4).

## Lower bounds on the compressed trace coefficients

For each L229 row, (3) and (4) give
\[
   \frac{\log|N(u+w)|}{m}
      \ \ge\ \log(pn)+p\,\frac{2}{m}\sum_{j=1}^m j\lambda_j
                                     -O_{P_0}(\log(2p)).               \tag{5}
\]
The same lower bound up to an additive seed-dependent constant holds for
\(\log\max(\|U\|_C,\|V\|_C)\), where \(u=U(q_0), w=V(q_0)\) and the two reduced polynomials use the basis \(1,C_1,\ldots,C_{m-1}\) of [L229](Yin_L_G0462_v229.md#space).
Indeed every archimedean image of \(u+w\) has absolute value at most this maximum coefficient size times \(2\max_j(1+\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}|\rho_j^k+\rho_j^{-k}|)\).
Multiplying over all \(m\) trace embeddings proves the bound.
The weight \(2\sum j\lambda_j/m\) lies between \((1+1/m)h\) and \(2h\), since pairing the indices \(j,m+1-j\) gives \(\sum_j(j-(m+1)/2)\lambda_j\ge0\), while \(j\le m\) gives the upper endpoint.
For the fourth-logarithmic window of a growing representative at fixed seed, \(\log(pn)=o(p)\) and (5) applies throughout the window.
Its error constant depends on the seed, and the estimate controls the compressed traces of the indicated witnesses without supplying a seed-independent positive lower bound for \(h\).
