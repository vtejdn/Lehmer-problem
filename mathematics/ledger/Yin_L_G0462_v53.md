# An exact finite exclusion on the leading-two Chebyshev path

<a id="exclusion"></a>
## The unit divisor for degrees 74 through 90

For \(C_h(x)=2U_h(x/2)-U_{h-1}(x/2)\), the [positive-constant Pisot inverse test](Yin_L_G0462_v43.md#inverse) has no solution with \(b=1\) and \(74\le h\le90\).
Here \(U_h\) denotes the Chebyshev polynomial of the second kind, \(C_h(2)=h+2\), and the choice \(b=1\) fixes \(q=h+3\).
The [integer enumeration](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0162/README.md#prefix-certificate) proves this finite statement by exhausting coefficient prefixes of the monic trace denominator \(B\).

<a id="moments"></a>
## Positive moment matrices before the reflected coefficients meet

Write \(m=h+1\), \(B^\sharp=z^m B(z+z^{-1})=\sum_{k=0}^{2m}b_kz^k\), where \(b_0=1\) and \(b_k=b_{2m-k}\) are integers.
The [endpoint requirements](Yin_L_G0462_v43.md#endpoints) are
\[
 B(2)=-1,\qquad (-1)^h(q+1)B(-2)\le -(3h+2),
\]
since \(C_h(-2)=(-1)^h(3h+2)\).
Reciprocity gives \(B(2)=b_m+2\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}b_i\) and \(B(-2)=b_m+2\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}(-1)^{m-i}b_i\).

The [signed measure](Yin_L_G0462_v49.md#signed-measure) \(\omega_q\) of \(J_q=2/K_q\) has total mass one, negative mass \(-1/[2(q-1)]\) at \(1\), and mass at least \(1/[2(q+1)]\) at \(-1\).
It assigns strictly positive mass to both circle points above each simple trace root of \(C_h\), since the inverse requires every \(w_i>0\).
The \(h\) trace roots are distinct and interior: at \(x=2\cos(k\pi/(h+1))\), \(1\le k\le h\), the values of \(C_h\) alternate with sign \((-1)^k\), while \(C_h(2)>0\).
These \(h\) sign changes account for its whole degree.
For an interval \(q_{\min}\le q\le q_{\max}\), put
\[
 c=\frac1{2(q_{\min}-1)},\qquad d=\frac1{2(q_{\max}+1)},\qquad
 \mu=\omega_q+c\delta_1-d\delta_{-1}.
\]
Then \(\mu\) is positive and still charges all \(2h\) interior lifts.
With \(s_k=\int v^k\,d\mu\), its real Toeplitz matrix \((s_{|i-j|})_{0\le i,j\le n}\) is strictly positive definite whenever \(n\le2h-1\).
Indeed its quadratic form integrates the squared modulus of a polynomial of degree at most \(n\), which cannot vanish at all \(2h\) points unless it is zero.

The [path identity](Yin_L_G0462_v49.md#path) gives \(B^\sharp=(1-z/2)J_q+O(z^{2h+1})\).
Writing \(j_k=[z^k]J_q\), the moments are therefore determined successively by
\[
 j_0=1,\qquad j_k=b_k+j_{k-1}/2,\qquad
 s_0=1+c-d,\qquad s_k=j_k/2+c-(-1)^k d\quad(1\le k\le 2h).
\]
For \(2\le k\le 2h\), the identity gives \(b_k=\int(2v^k-v^{k-1})\,d\omega_q\).
Hence \(|b_k|\le3(1+1/(q-1))<4\) for \(q\ge6\).
Also \(|b_1|\le2(1+1/(q-1))+1/2<4\), so every integer choice needed here lies between \(-3\) and \(3\).

<a id="recursion"></a>
## Exact interval for the next coefficient

Take \(L=8\operatorname{lcm}(2(q_{\min}-1),2(q_{\max}+1))\), write \(y_i=Ls_i\), and let \(T_n=(y_{|i-j|})_{1\le i,j\le n}\) with \(D_n=\det T_n\), \(D_0=1\).
At order \(n\) all moments through \(y_n\) are known and \(T_{n+1}\) is positive definite.
Store \(p=D_n T_n^{-1}(y_1,\ldots,y_n)^{\mathsf T}\), empty at \(n=0\), and form
\[
 X=D_n y_{n+1}-\sum_{i=1}^n p_i y_{n+1-i}.
\]
Eliminating the middle \(n\) indices of \(T_{n+2}\) gives a two by two Schur complement whose diagonal entries are both \(D_{n+1}/D_n\) by reversal symmetry, and whose off-diagonal entries are \(X/D_n\).
Thus the necessary and sufficient condition for the next positive definite matrix is \(-D_{n+1}<X<D_{n+1}\), and then
\[
 D_{n+2}=(D_{n+1}^2-X^2)/D_n,\qquad
 p_{\rm new}=\left((D_{n+1}p-Xp^{\rm rev})/D_n,\ X\right).
\]
For the second identity solve \(T_{n+1}u=(y_1,\ldots,y_{n+1})^{\mathsf T}\) in blocks.
Eliminating its first \(n\) coordinates leaves \(u_{n+1}=X/D_{n+1}\), and substitution gives the first \(n\) coordinates \((p-u_{n+1}p^{\rm rev})/D_n\).

The coefficient of \(b_{n+1}\) in \(X\) is \(D_nL/2>0\).
The program intersects the strict interval above with the integers in \([-3,3]\), starting from \(D_0=1,D_1=y_0\).
It stores \(2^ky_k\) and \(2^k j_k\) to handle dyadic moments and checks every division in the determinant and predictor recurrence for zero remainder.
At \(n=m\) it tests the two endpoints, then appends only the coefficient forced by reciprocity at each later index.
For \(h=74\) with the single value \(q=77\), there are 1,461 prefixes of length \(m+1\) including \(b_0\), of which 64 pass both endpoints.
Four survive through index 98, and none survives index 99.
Using one common measure correction for \(74\le h\le90\) gives an empty level in every branch before the proved strict positivity range ends.
The certificate records these levels individually.
