# Low-index changes of both coefficient parities on the odd grid

<a id="parity-count"></a>
## Counts below a fixed fraction of the conductor

Consider a sequence in the [full integral odd-grid chamber](../claims/Yin_C_G0894_v1.md#statement) with \(p=2n+1\to\infty\) and \(1<M(P)\to1\).
Write \(b_j=2\cos(2\pi j/p)\) for \(1\le j\le n\), \(Z(t)=(4-t^2)h(t)\), \(h=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_iE_i\), and \(Z(b_j)=p\rho_j\).
The final \(\kappa\) residues are strictly negative, the preceding residues are strictly positive, and the chamber requires \(Q(-2)/B_n(-2)<0\).
For \(\epsilon>0\) and \(\sigma\in\{0,1\}\), let \(N_\sigma(\epsilon,p)\) count the nonzero \(a_i\) with \(i<\epsilon p\) and \(i\equiv\sigma\bmod 2\).
Put \(\lambda=\min(\epsilon/4,1/8)\) and define
\[
       \Gamma_\lambda=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_0^{\pi\lambda}
                           \left(\frac{\sin u}{u}\right)^2du .
\]
For each parity, with arbitrary coefficient heights,
\[
        \liminf_{p\to\infty}\frac{\kappa}{p}N_\sigma(\epsilon,p)
                  \ \ge\ \frac{\Gamma_\lambda}{32}
                  \ \ge\ \frac{\lambda}{4\pi^2}.                         \tag{1}
\]
In particular both counts tend to infinity since the [negative block estimates](Yin_L_G0464_v5.md#endpoint-index-exponent) give \(\kappa/p\to0\) and \(\kappa\to\infty\).
The same lower bound holds for the number of discrepancies from each [neutral coefficient profile](../claims/Yin_C_G0881_v3.md#prefix-coordinates), counted separately in each parity below \(\epsilon p\).
On 2026-11-16 the construction contributor for [Yin's Lehmer Task](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#contract) obtained (1) by retaining exactly the frequencies at which the doubled-angle separator pairs with a coordinate difference.
The [independent proof check](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G1107_v62.md#low-index-parity-review) verifies the two frequency sets, the constants and the comparison with each profile.

<a id="coordinate-differences"></a>
## Coordinate differences detected by the separator

Let \(m=\lfloor\lambda p\rfloor\), \(\alpha=4\pi\kappa/p\), and extend \(a_i\) by zero outside \(0\le i<n\).
For \(1\le r<m\) define two sets of frequencies
\[
  R_0=\{r:a_{2r}\ne a_{2r-2}\},\qquad
  R_1=\{r:a_{2r+1}\ne a_{2r-3}\}.
\]
Each satisfies the more precise estimate
\[
 \liminf_{p\to\infty}
 \frac{4}{\pi}\sum_{r\in R_\sigma}
       \left(1-\frac rm\right)\frac{|\sin(r\alpha)|}{r}
       \ \ge\ \Gamma_\lambda .                                         \tag{2}
\]

Write \(\mathbb E_p\) for the uniform mean on the \(n\) traces and \(T_k(z+z^{-1})=z^k+z^{-k}\), including \(T_0=2\) and \(T_{-k}=T_k\).
Take \(G_0=Z\) and \(G_1=-tZ\).
The [first two residue moments](../claims/Yin_C_G0880_v2.md#moving-cut) bound both signed means \(\mathbb E_p G_\sigma\), since \(p/n\) is bounded.
The same estimates give \(\mathbb E_p(|Z|{\bf1}_{b_j\ge-1})=O(1)\) as the cut tends to \(-2\).
The [total variation gap](../claims/Yin_C_G0880_v2.md#statement) with constant weight gives \(\mathbb E_p|Z|\to\infty\).
It follows that \(V_\sigma=\mathbb E_p|G_\sigma|\to\infty\) for both parities, since \(|t|>1\) on the remaining traces \(t<-1\).

In the [doubled-angle separator](../claims/Yin_C_G0894_v1.md#separator) use this choice of \(m\).
The Fejér kernel \(K_m(v)=(2\pi m)^{-1}(\sin(mv/2)/\sin(v/2))^2\) gives
\[
  W_p(2\cos\phi)=1-2\int_{\phi-\alpha}^{\phi+\alpha}K_m(v)\,dv ,
  \qquad W(t)=W_p(t^2-2).
\]
The interval estimates in that construction give \(|W|\le1\) on \([-2,2]\) and \(W(b_j)Z(b_j)\ge\gamma_p|Z(b_j)|\) whenever \(b_j<0\), with
\[
    \gamma_p=2(I_p-J_p),\qquad
    I_p=\int_0^{2\pi/p}K_m(v)\,dv,\qquad J_p=\int_\alpha^\pi K_m(v)\,dv .
\]
Here \(J_p\le\pi/(2m\alpha)\to0\), and substituting \(u=mv/2\) shows that \(I_p\to\Gamma_\lambda/2\) since \(m\sin(u/m)\to u\) with relative error uniform for \(0<u\le\pi/8\).
Thus \(\gamma_p\to\Gamma_\lambda>0\).
Multiplying by \(-b_j>0\) on the negative traces gives the same sign estimate for \(G_1\).
Both \(G_\sigma\) have bounded absolute mean on \(b_j\ge0\), so
\[
                   \mathbb E_p(WG_\sigma)\ge\gamma_p V_\sigma-O(1).     \tag{3}
\]

The geometric-sum expansion of \(K_m\) and integration over the interval give
\[
 W=w_0+\sum_{r=1}^{m-1}w_rT_{2r},\qquad
 w_0=1-2\alpha/\pi,\qquad
 w_r=-\frac{2}{\pi}\left(1-\frac rm\right)\frac{\sin(r\alpha)}r .
\]
At \(t=2\) the polynomial \(G_\sigma T_{2r}\) vanishes, and its degree is at most \(n+2+2(m-1)<p\).
Hence its half-grid mean equals \(p/(2n)\) times the constant term of its Laurent expansion at \(t=z+z^{-1}\), by averaging on the complete \(p\)-point grid and pairing opposite angles.
The identities
\[
    (4-t^2)E_i=T_i-T_{i+2},\qquad
    t(T_i-T_{i+2})=T_{i-1}-T_{i+3}
\]
follow from the recurrence for \(E_i\) and from \(tT_i=T_{i-1}+T_{i+1}\).
For \(r\ge1\) the coefficient of \(T_{2r}\) in \(Z\) is \(a_{2r}-a_{2r-2}\), and that in \(tZ\) is \(a_{2r+1}-a_{2r-3}\).
Only this coefficient contributes to the constant term after multiplication by \(T_{2r}\).
Consequently \(\mathbb E_p(G_\sigma T_{2r})=0\) for \(1\le r<m\) outside \(R_\sigma\).
Using \(\lVert T_{2r}\rVert_\infty\le2\) on the trace interval, we obtain
\[
       \mathbb E_p(WG_\sigma)
          \le O(1)+2 V_\sigma\sum_{r\in R_\sigma}|w_r| .
\]
Division by \(V_\sigma\) and (3) give (2).

Since \(2|w_r|\le4\alpha/\pi=16\kappa/p\), (2) gives \(\liminf (\kappa/p)|R_\sigma|\ge\Gamma_\lambda/16\).
Every \(r\in R_\sigma\) uses at least one nonzero coordinate of parity \(\sigma\) with index at most \(2m-1<\epsilon p\), and each such coordinate occurs in at most two of the displayed differences for that parity.
Thus \(|R_\sigma|\le 2N_\sigma(\epsilon,p)\).
The sine chord bound on \([0,\pi/2]\) gives \(\Gamma_\lambda\ge8\lambda/\pi^2\), completing (1).

More generally compare the even coordinates to any constant and the odd coordinates to any two constants, one for each odd residue class modulo four.
These reference values may vary with \(p\).
For \(r\ge2\), both indices in each difference are nonnegative and have the same reference value.
Each \(r\in R_\sigma\) other than \(r=1\) therefore requires a discrepancy at one of its two indices.
The same counting argument, with one exceptional frequency, proves (1) for discrepancies from these references.
Each neutral profile has \(a_i=x^2+4\) for even \(i\ge2\) and \(a_i=-4x\) for odd \(i\), with the exceptional \(a_0=x^2+2\).
Changing that one comparison affects the count by at most one, which vanishes after multiplication by \(\kappa/p\).
