# An explicit integral prefix bound from three geometric sums

<a id="explicit-bound"></a>
## The matching length

Under the [integer hypotheses of C892](../claims/Yin_C_G0892_v1.md#statement), let \(s\ge2\) and let \(L\) be any index through which the reciprocal lift agrees with \(F_s(z)/(1-z)\).
Every strict terminal cut satisfies
\[
              L<(2s+3)(20\,000\,000-1)+3.                       \tag{1}
\]
This bound is uniform in the conductor, the cut and all coefficients of the integral perturbation and requires no endpoint condition.
The [independent finite-grid review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G1107_v52.md#explicit-prefix-review) verifies (1), including the estimates used for the numerical constant.
For Yin's [unrestricted Lehmer Task](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#contract), the main researcher proposed taking a regularized polynomial inverse of each of the three binomial factors on 2026-11-16.
The main researcher and the construction contributor developed the explicit comparison, with the latter checking the discrete products and numerical margins below.

Use \(p=2n+1\), \(b_j=2\cos(2\pi j/p)\), the perturbed residues \(\rho_j\), and the neutral residues \(\rho_j^0=(4-b_j^2)b_j^2E_{s-1}(b_j)^2/p\) of the [moment calculation](../claims/Yin_C_G0892_v1.md#moments).
Here \(E_{s-1}(2\cos\theta)=\sin(s\theta)/\sin\theta\).
Use the longest matching index \(L=n-\deg H\), since bounding that index suffices for (1).
For a cut after \(c\), set \(a=2\cos((2c+1)\pi/p)\), and write \(\mathbb E\) for the mean over \(1\le j\le n\).
Put
\[
 X_j=p(b_j-a)\rho_j,\qquad G_j=p(b_j-a)\rho_j^0,\qquad
 f(t)=(t+2)(4-t^2)t^2E_{s-1}(t)^2 .
\]
The strict signs give \(X_j>0\), and \(G_j\le f(b_j)\) since \(a>-2\) and the neutral residues are nonnegative.
For every real polynomial \(V\) of degree at most \(L-3\), the [moment and resultant identities](../claims/Yin_C_G0892_v1.md#saturation) give
\[
              \mathbb E XV=\mathbb E GV,\qquad \mathbb E\log X\ge0 .
 \tag{2}
\]
In this notation \(V\) is evaluated at \(b_j\).
For \(L\ge3\) the [first changed integer moment](../claims/Yin_C_G0892_v1.md#rigidity) further gives
\[
                    \mathbb E|X-G|\ge\frac{p}{2n}>1 .           \tag{3}
\]
Indeed \((t-a)T_{L-2}(t)\) is monic of degree \(L-1\), with \(T_k(2\cos\theta)=2\cos(k\theta)\).
Its sum against \(\rho_j-\rho_j^0\) is the nonzero integer leading coefficient of \(H\), whereas \(|T_{L-2}|\le2\) on the grid.

<a id="three-inverses"></a>
## Positive polynomial approximations

On \(|z|=1\) put \(t=z+z^{-1}\).
The factors \(|1+z|^2=2+t\), \(|1+z^2|^2=t^2\), and \(|1-z^{2s}|^2=4\sin^2(s\theta)\) at \(z=e^{i\theta}\) give
\[
                  f(t)=|(1+z)(1+z^2)(1-z^{2s})|^2\le64 .
\]
Use the three choices \((k,\sigma)=(1,-1),(2,-1),(2s,1)\), and in each put \(x=\sigma z^k\) and \(f_k=|1-x|^2\).
Take \(\lambda=1-10^{-6}\), \(N=20\,000\,000\), and \(u=\lambda^N\le e^{-20}<3\cdot10^{-9}\).
Define
\[
 w_k(t)=\frac{\lambda}{(1+u)^2}
         \left|\sum_{\ell=0}^{N-1}(\lambda x)^\ell\right|^2,
 \qquad w(t)=w_1(t)w_2(t)w_{2s}(t).
\]
Each \(w_k\) is strictly positive, since its geometric sum has numerator \(1-u x^N\) and denominator \(1-\lambda x\).
It is a real symmetric Laurent polynomial in \(z\), hence a polynomial in \(t\) of degree at most \((N-1)k\) by the cosine recurrence.
Thus \(\deg w\le(N-1)(2s+3)\).

Set \(\epsilon=(1-\lambda)^2/\lambda\) and \(q=((1+u)/(1-u))^2\).
Since \(|1-\lambda x|^2/\lambda=f_k+\epsilon\) and \(1-u\le|1-u x^N|\le1+u\), one has
\[
                   f_k\le\frac1{w_k}\le q(f_k+\epsilon).
\]
Numerically \(\epsilon<2\cdot10^{-12}\) and \(q-1=4u/(1-u)^2<2\cdot10^{-8}\).
Consequently \(0\le1/w_k-f_k<10^{-7}\), using \(f_k\le4\).
All these factors are below five, so telescoping their products gives
\[
              fw\le1,\qquad 0\le1/w-f<3\cdot5^2\,10^{-7}<10^{-4},
              \qquad 1/w<65 .                                   \tag{4}
\]

<a id="logarithmic-mean"></a>
## Discrete logarithmic averages and the contradiction

For any one of the three pairs let \(g=\gcd(p,k)\).
The \(k\)-th powers of the full set of \(p\)-th roots of unity give every \((p/g)\)-th root exactly \(g\) times.
Therefore
\[
 \prod_{z^p=1}|1-\lambda\sigma z^k|
            =|1-(\lambda\sigma)^{p/g}|^g\le2^g .
\]
The omitted point \(z=1\) contributes at least \(1-\lambda\), and all other points are in equal conjugate pairs.
Writing \(z_j=e^{2\pi i j/p}\) gives
\[
 \mathbb E\log|1-\lambda\sigma z_j^k|
 \le \frac{k\log2+\log(1/(1-\lambda))}{p-1}.
\]
The formula for the finite geometric sum and \(|1-u x^N|\ge1-u\) now imply
\[
 -\mathbb E\log w
 \le -3\log\lambda
      +6\log\frac{1+u}{1-u}
      +\frac{2\big((2s+3)\log2+3\log(1/(1-\lambda))\big)}{p-1}.
 \tag{5}
\]

Suppose \(L\ge(N-1)(2s+3)+3\), so (2) applies to \(w\).
Also \(p-1=2n\ge2L\) and \(2s+3\ge7\).
The first term in (5) is below \(3.00001\cdot10^{-6}\) by \(-\log\lambda\le(1-\lambda)/\lambda\).
The second is below \(4\cdot10^{-8}\) by \(\log((1+u)/(1-u))\le2u/(1-u)\).
The final term is at most \((\log2+3\log(10^6)/7)/(N-1)<3.4\cdot10^{-7}\), using \(\log2<0.7\) and \(\log(10^6)<14\).
Thus \(-\mathbb E\log w<4\cdot10^{-6}\).

Let \(Y=Xw\) and \(\psi(y)=y-1-\log y\).
Equations (2) and (4) give \(\mathbb EY=\mathbb EGw\le\mathbb Efw\le1\) and
\(\mathbb E\psi(Y)\le-\mathbb E\log w<4\cdot10^{-6}\).
The [elementary entropy inequality](../claims/Yin_C_G0892_v1.md#rigidity) gives \((y-1)^2/(y+1)\le2\psi(y)\) for \(y>0\).
By Cauchy–Schwarz, \(\mathbb E|Y-1|\le\sqrt{2\mathbb E(Y+1)\mathbb E\psi(Y)}<0.004\).
Using (4) yields
\[
                 \mathbb E|X-f|<65\cdot0.004+10^{-4}<0.261.
\]
Since \(G\le f\) and \(\mathbb EX=\mathbb EG\), one has \(\mathbb E|f-G|=\mathbb E(f-X)\le\mathbb E|f-X|\).
It follows that \(\mathbb E|X-G|<0.522\), contradicting (3) and proving (1).
