# A norm-sensitive radial bound at delays two and three

<a id="radial"></a>
## The product over the interior conjugates

Let \(f\) be the monic minimal polynomial of a Pisot number \(\theta>1\), let \(q=|f(0)|\), and put \(g(z)=z^{\deg f}f(1/z)\).
For \(N\ge1\) and either sign write \(D_N(z)=z^Nf(z)\pm g(z)\), and denote its multiplicative Mahler measure by \(M_N\).
For \(1<r<\theta\) one has
\[
 \left|\frac{g(r)}{f(r)}\right|
 \ \le\ \frac{\theta r-1}{\theta-r}\,
                 \frac{\theta+r q}{r\theta+q}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Whenever the right side is strictly less than \(r^N\), the polynomial \(D_N\) has a positive real exterior root greater than \(r\), for both choices of sign.
On 2026-11-17 the main researcher proposed retaining the norm in the [radial comparison](../claims/Yin_C_G0336_v3.md#cofactor) for [Yin's Lehmer Task](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#contract).
Agent-S obtained the resulting trace inequality, explicit exclusions for nonunit seeds and quadratic examples at the delay-two boundary.
[Boyd's fixed-delay enumeration](../audits/literature/Boy78/Yin_U001.md#fixed-delay-enumeration) reports all the seeds producing Salem numbers for his choice of sign in \((1,1.3]\) at delays four through eight.

Write \(a=|\beta|<1\) for the modulus of an interior conjugate of \(\theta\).
The squared modulus of \((1-r\beta)/(r-\beta)\) is
\[
 \frac{1+r^2a^2-2r\Re\beta}{r^2+a^2-2r\Re\beta}.
\]
It decreases as \(\Re\beta\) increases because the denominator exceeds the numerator by \((r^2-1)(1-a^2)>0\).
The modulus thus has upper bound \(h(a)=(1+ra)/(r+a)\), attained at \(\beta=-a\).
For \(0\le a,b\le1\),
\[
 h(ab)-h(a)h(b)=
 \frac{r(r-1)(1-a)(1-b)(1-ab)}
      {(r+a)(r+b)(r+ab)}\ge0 .
\]
Multiplication gives the interior contribution at most \(h(q/\theta)\), since the product of the moduli is \(q/\theta\).
The empty product for an integer seed gives \(h(1)=1\).
The exterior conjugate contributes \((\theta r-1)/(\theta-r)\), proving (1).
Since \(f(r)<0\) for \(1<r<\theta\), the stated strict test makes \(D_N(r)<0\), while \(D_N\) is monic.
In particular \(M_N>r\) whenever the strict test holds.

<a id="two"></a>
## An explicit trace bound and its sharp boundary examples

For every \(N\ge2\) and either sign,
\[
                   M_N+M_N^{-1}\ge\max\{2,\theta-q/\theta\}.
 \tag{2}
\]
In fact the difference between the numerator of the right side of (1) and \(r^2\) times its positive denominator is
\[
 (\theta r-1)(\theta+r q)-r^2(\theta-r)(r\theta+q)
      =r\theta(r^2-1)(r+r^{-1}-\theta+q/\theta).
\]
If \(\theta-q/\theta>2\), let \(R>1\) satisfy \(R+R^{-1}=\theta-q/\theta\).
Then \(R<\theta\), and (1) gives \(M_N>r\) for every \(1<r<R\) since \(r^N\ge r^2\).
This proves (2), including the equality permitted at \(R\).

For \(q=1,2\), the integer polynomial \(f=z^2-2z-q\) is irreducible with Pisot root \(\theta=1+\sqrt{q+1}\) and sole interior conjugate \(1-\sqrt{q+1}<0\).
Thus (1) is an equality for every \(1<r<\theta\), and \(\theta-q/\theta=2\).
The actual delay-two minus associate is
\[
                   z^2 f-g=z^4-2z^3+2z-1=(z-1)^3(z+1).
\]
These examples attain the boundary with measure one.

<a id="nonunit"></a>
## A gap above \(3/2\) for nonunit seeds

Every seed with \(q\ge2\) at every \(N\ge3\) gives \(M_N>3/2\), except \(f=z-2,\ N=3\) with the minus sign, which gives \(M_N=1\).
Every seed with \(q\ge3\) at \(N=2\) also gives \(M_N>3/2\), except \(f=z-3\) with the minus sign, which gives \(M_N=1\).

For the exclusion of nonlinear seeds near their norm, take \(q\ge2\), \(\deg f\ge2\) and \(\delta=\theta-q>0\).
The [first-coefficient calculation](Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#second) works with either constant sign after taking \(F=\operatorname{sgn}(f(0))f/g\).
Indeed \(g(0)=1\) gives \(F(0)=q\) and \(u_1=[z]F\in\mathbb Z\).
With \(B=(1-\theta z)F/(\theta-z)\), the disk pole cancels, \(|B|\le1\) by the boundary modulus, and \(B(0)=q/\theta\).
Thus \(H=(1-B)/(1-q/\theta)\) has positive real part on the disk and \(H(0)=1\).
Applying Schwarz's lemma to \((H-1)/(H+1)\) gives \(|H'(0)|\le2\).
Differentiation gives
\[
      u_1-q^2+1=\delta(q+\theta^{-1}-H'(0))\in\mathbb Z_{>0},\qquad
      \delta\ge\frac{1}{q+\theta^{-1}+2},
 \tag{3}
\]
where positivity uses \(q\ge2\).
In particular \(\theta>20/9\) at \(q=2\) and \(\theta>51/16\) at \(q=3\).

For \(r=3/2\) the coarse part of (1) already satisfies \(r^3>(\theta r-1)/(\theta-r)\) if \(\theta>r+r^{-1}=13/6\), by factoring \(r^4-1=(r^2+1)(r^2-1)\).
This includes every nonlinear seed with \(q\ge2\), and every integer seed \(\theta=q\ge3\).
For \(f=z-2\), \(r^4=81/16>(2r-1)/(2-r)=4\) covers \(N\ge4\).
At \(N=3\) its minus associate is \((z-1)^3(z+1)\) and its plus associate \(z^4-2z^3-2z+1\) is negative at \(3/2\).

At \(N=2\), a nonlinear seed with \(q=3\) satisfies
\(\theta-3/\theta>51/16-16/17>13/6\).
If \(q\ge4\), then \(\theta-q/\theta\ge q-1\ge3\).
Equation (2) gives \(M_N>3/2\) in both cases.
For \(f=z-3\), \(z^2 f-g=(z-1)^3\) and \(z^2 f+g=z^3-3z^2-3z+1\) is negative at \(3/2\).

## Necessary seed ranges for positive measures tending to one

On a sequence at a fixed delay \(N=2\) with \(1<M_N\to1\), eventually \(q=1\) or \(q=2\), and
\[
                     \theta\le1+\sqrt{q+1}+o(1).
\]
Indeed the preceding exclusions remove \(q\ge3\), and (2) gives \(\theta^2-(2+o(1))\theta-q\le0\).
For \(q=2\) the only degree-one seed has fixed output for each sign, so (3) also gives \(\theta>20/9\) eventually on that subsequence.

At \(N=3\) the same positive-measure escape requires \(q=1\) eventually, and
\[
                     \limsup\theta\le (2+\sqrt{13})/3 .
\]
To see the additional upper bound, at \(r=1\) the right side of (1) has value one and derivative
\[
  A_q(\theta)=\frac{\theta+1}{\theta-1}-\frac{\theta-q}{\theta+q}
            =\frac{2\theta(q+1)}{(\theta-1)(\theta+q)} .
\]
At any limiting \(\theta_*>1\) with \(N>A_q(\theta_*)\), a fixed \(r>1\) sufficiently close to one gives a strict test, first at \(\theta_*\) and then throughout its neighborhood by continuity.
The delay-two inequality already bounds \(\theta\) above, so every limit above the claimed threshold would have to pass \(3\le4\theta_*/(\theta_*^2-1)\), an impossibility.
In fact \((2+\sqrt{13})/3\) is not an algebraic integer, since its monic irreducible polynomial is \(X^2-(4/3)X-1\).
By the [closedness of the Pisot numbers](../audits/literature/Smy15/Yin_U002.md#closedness-and-delay-one), a whole open neighborhood of this endpoint contains no Pisot number, so the upper bound can be decreased by a positive constant.
These restrictions retain both signs and leave open the existence of the proposed sequences within the remaining seed ranges.
