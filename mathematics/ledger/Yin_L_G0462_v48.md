# Finite torsion support from discrete Fourier coefficients

<a id="support"></a>
## From finite mass to a common finite group

Let \(\mu\) be a finite positive measure concentrated on roots of unity.
If \(\int v^k\,d\mu(v)\in d\mathbb Z\) for every integer \(k\), for one fixed real \(d>0\), then \(\mu\) is supported on a finite cyclic group.
Indeed take a finite set of roots whose complement has mass less than \(d/2\).
Let \(N\ge1\) be a common multiple of their orders.
Then \(\left|\int(1-v^N)\,d\mu(v)\right|<d\), while this integral is a difference of two elements of \(d\mathbb Z\).
It vanishes, so \(\int(1-\Re v^N)\,d\mu(v)=0\).
The integrand is positive off \(\mu_N\), proving the assertion.

For the [endpoint ordering theorem](Yin_L_G0462_v46.md#ordered-values), take the symmetric circle lift \(\widetilde\lambda\) of the original finite measure on torsion traces.
Its doubled Fourier coefficients are integers at all indices by the trace moments and the integral recurrence for \(v^k+v^{-k}\).
The lemma with \(d=1/2\) therefore places this lift in one finite group even when only finite total mass was assumed at the outset.
Its support excludes 1 and has a positive distance from 1 unless the lift is zero, so the endpoint integral \(s\) is finite.
Each weighted lift and its added identity atom in L462v46 belong to the same finite group for that input.
This supplies the missing input to the atom isolation theorem identified by the [original independent review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G1114_v44.md#trace-endpoint-review).
