# One minimal ternary support at every weight for the golden ratio

<a id="supports"></a>
## The complete list of patterns

Let \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\) and \(P=X^2-X-1\).
Up to translation there is exactly one inclusion-minimal ternary relation support at each weight \(j\ge3\).
Writing \(\ell=j-2\), it is
\[
            \{0,2\ell\}\ \cup\ \{1,3,\ldots,2\ell-1\}.
\]
Each has exponent gcd one, so the generating series for either normalization in the [general counting theorem](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v576.md#series) is \(z^3/(1-z)\).
This is a fixed input where infinitely many different weights occur and the number of patterns stays one at every weight.

A carry lies in \(\mathbb Z[\phi]\) and changes by \(x\mapsto\phi x+e\) for \(e\in\{-1,0,1\}\), reading the digits from high to low.
Write \(x=a+b\phi\) and \(x'=a+b\phi'\) at the conjugate \(\phi'=-1/\phi\).
For a carry along a finite zero-to-zero word, bounding the future in the expanding embedding and the past in the contracting one gives \(|x|<\phi\) and \(|x'|<\phi^2\).
Therefore \(|b|<(\phi+\phi^2)/\sqrt5<2\).
When \(b=0\) one has \(a=0,1,-1\).
When \(b=1\) the first bound gives \(-2\phi<a<0\), while the second gives \(a> -2\), leaving only \(-1\).
The case \(b=-1\) follows by negation.

Up to sign the two possible nonzero carries are \(A=1\) and \(B=\phi-1\).
A nonzero word starts at \(A\).
From \(A\) it must go to \(B\) with a nonzero digit to stay in the returning graph.
From \(B\) a zero digit takes it to \(A\) and a nonzero digit can finish at zero.
All these transitions exist with the displayed signs or their simultaneous negatives.
Thus the support of any word from its first nonzero digit to its first subsequent return is a translate of the displayed pattern, which is invariant under reflection.
Every dependent set contains such a pattern.
The one with \(\ell=1\) is minimal since two exponents cannot give a relation.
For \(\ell\ge2\) there is no triple of consecutive positions, and its only adjacent pairs are at its two ends.
A contained pattern of weight at least four would have to use both of these pairs, hence the full span, which determines the same \(\ell\).
This proves minimality and completeness.

## Finite maxima and all root substitutions

For \(N\ge1\) the largest admissible size is \(a_N(P,1)=1+\lfloor N/2\rfloor\).
Indeed the nonzero carry subsets reachable on admissible masks are the empty set, \(\{A\}\), and \(\{A,B\}\).
Their respective successors on bit zero are the empty set, the empty set, and \(\{A\}\).
On bit one their successors are \(\{A\}\), \(\{A,B\}\), and rejection.
Assign the three states potentials \(0,1,2\) in that order.
Every legal transition on bit \(z\) increases the potential by at least \(2z-1\), giving \(2|S|-N\le2\) from the initial state.
Starting with bits \(11\) and then alternating zeros and ones attains the bound after taking integer parts for each \(N\), with the initial one alone covering \(N=1\).

For every \(q\ge1\) the polynomial \(P(X^q)\) is irreducible.
Indeed the smallest positive log measure of a unit of degree at most two is \(\log\phi\).
A nontorsion quadratic unit must have real conjugates, since a nonreal quadratic unit has both conjugates on the circle and is torsion.
Change its sign if necessary so the larger modulus \(R>1\) occurs at a positive root.
Its integral trace is either \(R-1/R\ge1\) or \(R+1/R\ge3\), and both give \(R\ge\phi\).
Apply the [norm argument for record units](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v574.md#lifts).
Every minimal support for \(P(X^q)\) lies in a single residue class modulo \(q\) by [division in residue classes](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v574.md#reduction), and is a translate of \(q\) times a pattern from the list.
For \(N<q\) every exponent can therefore be taken.
For \(N\ge q\), writing \(N=2qb+r\) with \(0\le r<2q\), the same division formula gives
\[
             a_N(P(X^q),1)=q+qb+\max(0,r-q).
\]
Thus every substituted input has density \(1/2\), including those that survive many prescribed tests.
