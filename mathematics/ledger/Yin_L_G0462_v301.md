# The two defining Salem excursions cover only two thirds of the critical deficit

<a id="excursions"></a>
## First returns for a Salem family approaching two

For \(n\ge4\) take the [Salem root](Yin_L_G0462_v296.md#barrier) \(r=r_n\in(1,2)\) of \(F_n(X)=X^n+1-\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}X^j\).
Under the recurrence \(y\mapsto ry+d\) starting at zero, give \(d=0\) weight two and \(d=\pm1\) weight one, as furnished by the difference of two independent fair binary digits after multiplying probabilities by four.
The one-digit zero word and the two coefficient words of \(\pm F_n\) read from highest degree are first returns to zero.
Indeed, for the positive leading sign, the state after the initial digit and \(j\) successive negative digits is
\[
 y_j=\frac{1-(2-r)r^j}{r-1}\qquad(0\le j\le n-1).
\]
Since \((2-r)r^n=2r-1\) and \(2r-1<r^2\), all \(y_j\) with \(j\le n-2\) are positive.
The next state is \(y_{n-1}=-1/r\), and the final positive digit returns it to zero.
Negation gives the other excursion, independently of any cyclotomic factors of \(F_n\).

<a id="deficit"></a>
## The missing first-return weight

With \(x=r/4\), the critical weight of the three excursions is \(T(x)=2x+2x^{n+1}\).
The fraction of the deficit \(1-2x\) covered by the two longer excursions is exactly
\[
 \frac{2x^{n+1}}{1-2x}=\frac{r}{2r-1}(r/2)^{2n}<1.
\]
Thus \(T(x)<1\) for every \(n\ge4\).
First-return decomposition gives the generating series \(1/(1-T)\) for arbitrary concatenations of the three words, and this series converges at a positive argument strictly greater than \(x\) by continuity.
The restricted weighted returns therefore have exponential rate strictly below \(4/r\).

As \(r_n\to2\), eventually \(r_n\ge3/2\), and the same root identity gives \(2-r_n\le3(3/2)^{-n}\).
Consequently \(n\log(r_n/2)\to0\) and \((r_n/2)^{2n}\to1\).
The displayed fraction tends to \(2/3\), while \(2^n(2-r_n)=(2r_n-1)(2/r_n)^n\to3\).
In particular the exact missing mass satisfies
\[
 1-T(r_n/4)\sim 2^{-n-1}.
\]
Any finite collection of additional distinct first returns must contribute strictly more than \(1-T(r_n/4)\) at \(x=r_n/4\) for its concatenations with the three displayed excursions to certify a strict collision-entropy deficit.
This criterion includes the weight \(2\) for each zero digit of every added word.
