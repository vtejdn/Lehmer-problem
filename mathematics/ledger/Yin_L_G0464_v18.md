# Positive-unit square classes and the full odd-grid moment-volume boundary

This author-checked exploration for Yin's [unrestricted Lehmer contract](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#contract), developed on 2026-11-09, extends the [power-of-three moment-ellipsoid comparison](Yin_L_G0464_v10.md#moment-ellipsoid) across all positive-unit square classes at any odd conductor.
It retains a possible change of arithmetic input and shows why the number of such classes alone supplies no positive integer-count argument.
There is no independent proof review or adoption, no new computation, and no claim of a shrinking positive Mahler sequence.

<a id="full-order-classes"></a>
## Arithmetic classes in the original order

Let \(p=2n+1\ge5\), let \(b_j=2\cos(2\pi j/p)\) for \(1\le j\le n\), and use the full odd-grid polynomial \(B=B_n\) and order \(\mathcal O_p=\mathbb Z[t]/B\).
Fix an integral element \(h_0\) strictly positive above a cut and strictly negative below it, with no zero grid value.
A totally positive unit \(u\in\mathcal O_p^{\times,+}\) gives \(h=h_0u\) with the same strict signs, principal ideal and absolute resultant
\[
                      \Delta=|\operatorname{Res}(B,h_0)|\ge1.
\]
At \(p=3^m\), [v10's square-class argument](Yin_L_G0464_v10.md#positive-squares) proves \(\mathcal O_p^{\times,+}=(\mathcal O_p^\times)^2\).
There, every positive-unit change is already an integral change of square-root coordinates.

Outside that family, a nontrivial class in \(\mathcal O_p^{\times,+}/(\mathcal O_p^\times)^2\) could change the moment form beyond this multiplication-by-a-unit coordinate orbit while retaining its determinant.
This adds no resultant cost, unlike the [paired nonunit filters](Yin_L_G0462_v7.md#paired-filter-supply).
It need not produce a form inequivalent under every integral change of basis.
For composite \(p\), the required unit lies in the full order, not merely in the product of its field-component integer rings.
Separate field units may fail to glue integrally, and an even power used to obtain gluing may destroy the proposed nonsquare class.
An actual full-order representative is therefore necessary.

<a id="coset-ellipsoids"></a>
## Moment ellipsoids with an unchanged determinant

Use the [full odd-grid quadrature](Yin_L_G0464_v5.md#odd-grid-product), with \(\nu_j=(4-b_j^2)/p\), and the monic Chebyshev basis \(E_i\) of [v10](Yin_L_G0464_v10.md#moment-ellipsoid).
Multiplication by \(t\) has the symmetric integer Jacobi matrix \(J\) with adjacent entries one and diagonal zero except for its last entry \(-1\).
Its recurrence and the quadrature apply to every odd conductor independently of the power-of-three square-class conclusion.

For \(x\in\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}\), retain the necessary profile moments
\[
 K_0=x^2+2,\qquad K_1=-4x,\qquad
 \sum_j\nu_jh(b_j)=K_0,\qquad
 \sum_j\nu_jb_jh(b_j)=K_1.
\]
Choose one positive-unit representative \(w\) of each positive class modulo squares.
Every unit in that class is \(wv^2\) for an integral unit \(v\), represented by its coefficient vector \(\mathbf v\) in the \(E_i\) basis.
The same quadrature as in v10 gives
\[
 H_w=h_0(J)w(J),\qquad
 \mathbf v^{\mathsf T}H_w\mathbf v=K_0,\qquad
 \mathbf v^{\mathsf T}JH_w\mathbf v=K_1.
\]
For a separator \(c\) strictly inside the cut, \(G_w=(J-cI)H_w\) is positive definite because its grid eigenvalues are \((b_j-c)h_0(b_j)w(b_j)>0\).
Consequently every retained vector satisfies
\[
 \mathbf v^{\mathsf T}G_w\mathbf v=S:=K_1-cK_0,
 \qquad \det G_w=|B(c)|\Delta.
\]
The determinant identity uses the positive full norm of \(w\), which is one.
If \(S\le0\), there is no unit solution.
For \(S>0\), choose the angular-midpoint separator, for which [v5](Yin_L_G0464_v5.md#odd-grid-product) gives \(|B(c)|\ge1\).
Since \(|c|<2\), each of the five targets has \(S\le20\).
The Gaussian volume estimate used in v10 therefore gives, uniformly in the class,
\[
 \operatorname{vol}\{\mathbf y:\mathbf y^{\mathsf T}G_w\mathbf y\le S\}
 \le\frac{(2\pi\mathrm e S/n)^{n/2}}{\sqrt{|B(c)|\Delta}}
 \le(40\pi\mathrm e/n)^{n/2}.                              \tag{1}
\]
This solid ellipsoid relaxes the equality, the other moment equation, the unit condition and the endpoint.

<a id="all-class-volume"></a>
## Summing over every positive-unit square class

There are at most \(2^n\) unit square classes in \(\mathcal O_p\).
Here is the needed upper bound without a maximal-rank assertion.
Write the rational algebra \(\mathcal O_p\otimes\mathbb Q\) as a product of \(\ell\) totally real fields of total degree \(n\).
The norm of a unit on each component is \(\pm1\), so the logarithms of its absolute embeddings lie in a real space of dimension \(n-\ell\).
The evaluation image of the order is a full lattice, since the grid Vandermonde matrix is invertible.
A bounded set of logarithms bounds every absolute evaluation and hence contains only finitely many evaluated order elements.
Thus the logarithmic image is a discrete free abelian group of rank \(r\le n-\ell\).
Its kernel has at most \(2^\ell\) elements: on each field component an element whose conjugates all have absolute value one is \(1\) or \(-1\), because the component is totally real.
Lifting a basis of the free image shows that the unit group modulo squares has at most \(2^\ell2^r\le2^n\) elements.
The quotient of the positive units by all unit squares is a subgroup of that quotient and has no more elements.

Summing (1) over all positive classes, even when their number grows with the conductor, gives
\[
 \sum_{[w]}\operatorname{vol}
     \{\mathbf y:\mathbf y^{\mathsf T}G_w\mathbf y\le S\}
 \le2^n(40\pi\mathrm e/n)^{n/2}
   =(160\pi\mathrm e/n)^{n/2}\longrightarrow0.             \tag{2}
\]
This is not an exclusion of integer points or of a shrinking Mahler sequence.
A small ellipsoid can contain exceptional arithmetic vectors.
It shows that multiplying the existing random-translate volume estimate by the entire possible square-class count still does not supply a positive count on the actual unshifted integer lattices.
A useful change of class must exploit particular short vectors or a correlation between the forms and the exact moment targets.

<a id="arithmetic-bridge"></a>
## The unresolved arithmetic bridge

[C881 v3](../claims/Yin_C_G0881_v3.md#statement) supplies the analytic upper bound tending to one from exact growing prefixes, strict grid signs and the endpoint.
A new unit class still has to produce integer completions satisfying those conditions and strictly positive complete measure.
The two moments alone are insufficient, and (2) supplies neither existence nor a probability lower bound.
Growing perturbation width matters only if it provides a mechanism for those simultaneous arithmetic constraints.

The first discriminating question is whether an explicit conductor and strict-cut base admit a genuine nonsquare positive unit in the full order whose exact moment problem supplies a prefix-and-endpoint completion unavailable in the base square orbit.
Failure of integral gluing removes that candidate before a magnitude search.
A successful class representative warrants only a bounded exact comparison at the same resultant, checking the endpoint and complete measure on each retained input.
A favorable finite completion must still be accompanied by a mechanism persisting as the prefix grows.
Extra class labels, two-moment hits or repeated fixed measures do not resolve that bridge.
No developed alternative to the integer-prefix supply problem emerged from this comparison.
