# A long forced progression in the denominator when the Chebyshev offset grows

<a id="progression"></a>
## Reading every \(d\)-th coefficient

Consider the positive-constant inverse construction on \(j=2\) with \(C(x)=2U_h(x/2)\pm U_{h-d}(x/2)\), \(1\le d\le h\).
Write \(B^\sharp(z)=z^{h+1}B(z+z^{-1})=\sum_i b_i z^i\) for the lifted monic trace denominator.
There are absolute constants \(c>0,\ q_0,\ N_0\) with the following property.
Put \(N=\lfloor(2h+1-d)/d\rfloor\).
If \(q\ge q_0\) and \(N\ge N_0\), then for some row \(U_0,V\) of the [nine-profile table](Yin_L_G0462_v44.md#profiles),
\[
        b_{kd}=[w^k]\,V(w)/U_0(w)
                \qquad (0\le k\le \lfloor cN\rfloor)
\]
for the minus sign.
For the plus sign the corresponding formula uses \(V(-w)/U_0(-w)\).
Thus even for \(d\to\infty\) with \(d=o(h)\), one arithmetic progression of coefficients through a positive fraction of the degree is forced into one of nine explicit series.
The constants obtained by compactness are ineffective.

<a id="prefix"></a>
## A rigidity statement for integer prefixes of signed circle moments

Let \(\omega\) be a conjugation-invariant real signed measure on the circle of mass one.
Write \(J\) for its Herglotz transform, with coefficients \(j_0=1\) and \(j_l=2\int v^l\,d\omega(v)\) for \(l>0\).
There exist absolute constants \(\delta,c>0\) and \(N_0\) such that, whenever the negative part of \(\omega\) has mass at most \(\delta\), \(N\ge N_0\), and \(B=(1-w/2)J\) has integer coefficients through degree \(N\), its coefficients through \(\lfloor cN\rfloor\) agree with a single \(V/U_0\) in the nine-profile table.

If no such constants exist, take examples with negative mass at most \(1/i\), \(N_i\ge i\), for which no table entry agrees through \(\lfloor N_i/i\rfloor\).
Pass to a weakly convergent subsequence of the measures.
Its limit is a symmetric probability with transform \(J_\infty\) and \(J_\infty(0)=1\).
The analytic function \(B_\infty=(1-w/2)J_\infty\) has integer coefficients, each eventually equal to those of \(B_i\) by convergence.
Moreover \(K=2/J_\infty=(2-w)/B_\infty\) has positive real part, integer Taylor coefficients, constant two and an odd coefficient at degree one.
The [integer Fourier–Stieltjes argument](Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#periodic) makes \(K\) rational with a squarefree cyclotomic denominator and its continuous doubled density \(\rho=K(w)+K(1/w)\) a nonnegative Laurent polynomial.
The [rational reciprocal argument](Yin_L_G0462_v44.md#cyclotomic) gives \(K=(2-w)U_0/V\) with the squarefree coprime products used there.
In particular \(\rho\) is not identically zero: the rational extension at \(w=2\) gives \(K(2)=0\) and \(K(1/2)>0\).
The [phase calculation](Yin_L_G0462_v44.md#phase) therefore applies without any realization hypothesis on \(K\).
It follows that \(B_\infty=V/U_0\) belongs to the nine-entry list.

Let \(n_i\) be the first coefficient of \(B_i\) differing from this particular \(B_\infty\).
Then \(n_i\to\infty\) but \(n_i\le N_i/i\).
At each fixed \(k\ge1,\ l\in\mathbb Z\), both \(kn_i+l\) and \(kn_i+l-1\) are eventually strictly inside the integer prefix.
The [two-circle comparison](Yin_L_G0462_v52.md#harmonics) therefore gives a single family of conditional probability moments with integral Laurent polynomials \(H_k=(1-w/2)\sigma c_k\), \(H_1\ne0\), where \(\sigma=2\Re J_\infty\) away from atoms.
Indeed the positive parts of \(2\omega_i\) have bounded mass, and discarding the negative parts changes every moment by \(o(1)\), uniformly in its index.
At the first discrepancy all negative offsets have zero limiting difference in the \(J\) coefficients.
After extraction of fixed phases \(v^{n_i}\) on the finitely many torsion atoms of the limiting marginal, the [atomic cancellation](Yin_L_G0462_v49.md#departure) identifies the conditional first moment at every atom with that phase.
It has modulus one there, hence all its conditional harmonics are those of the same point mass.
The remaining continuous projections give integral coefficients of \(H_k\) by the two prefix indices.
No endpoint, degree or root-location property of the original \(B_i\) enters this comparison.

For all nine profiles these rows are impossible by the [bracket bounds](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v9.md#brackets), [exceptional envelope bounds](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v9.md#exceptions) and [prime specialization](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v9.md#torsion) at fixed offset one.
Those arguments use just the profile \(K\), the integral rows, their conditional positivity and \(H_1\ne0\).
This proves the stated uniform prefix rigidity.
Rotating the circle by \(-1\) gives the corresponding assertion for \(B=(1+w/2)J\).

<a id="projection"></a>
## Applying the rigidity statement to the inverse construction

Push the [signed measure for \(J_q=2/K_q\)](Yin_L_G0462_v49.md#signed-measure) forward by \(v\mapsto v^d\).
Its mass is one, its negative part has mass at most \(1/[2(q-1)]\), and its Herglotz series is \(\widetilde J(w)=\sum_{k\ge0}([z^{kd}]J_q)w^k\).
The exact lift gives
\[
     B^\sharp=(1\pm z^d/2)J_q+O(z^{2h+2-d}).
\]
Consequently \((1\pm w/2)\widetilde J\) has integral coefficient \(b_{kd}\) at every \(k\le N\).
Choosing \(q_0\) so that \(1/[2(q_0-1)]\le\delta\) gives the progression assertion.
The coefficients with index outside the progression and the case of bounded \(h/d\) still require other tests.
