## Retaining the atom in the remaining moment problem

Here \(y=-161/100\), \(\sigma_j=(2-b_j)(b_j-y)h(b_j)/p>0\), and \((R_0,\ldots,R_5)=(3,4,7,O,V,W)\).
Write \(s_i=\sum_jb_j^i\sigma_j\).
All six known moments follow from
\[
 s_0=261/100,\qquad s_{i+1}=R_{i+1}-yR_i-2s_i\quad(0\le i\le4).
\]
For either candidate atom value the remaining moments are \(s_i'=s_i-183h(-1)(-1)^i/(100p)\).
For all five triples and both candidates, the bounded native test evaluated
\[
 [s_{i+j}']_{0\le i,j\le2},\quad
 [2s_{i+j}'+s_{i+j+1}']_{0\le i,j\le2},\quad
 [2s_{i+j}'-s_{i+j+1}']_{0\le i,j\le2},\quad
 [4s_{i+j}'-s_{i+j+2}']_{0\le i,j\le1}.
\]
These are necessary conditions on the actual remaining positive support, using the known moments through \(s_5\).
At \(h(-1)=2048\) the second matrix fails for every triple.
At the forced value \(32\), every leading principal minor of all four matrices is strictly positive for every triple.
Thus these explicitly joint tests exclude no further triple.
They do not imply existence on the discrete remaining support or of an integer polynomial.
The [native record](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0151/rational_pole.md#result) retains the exact finite scope, matrices, reproduction command and completed result.
