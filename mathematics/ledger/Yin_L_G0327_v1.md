### 2. The complete scalar original law and its exact metric

For n>=1 define

    S_n=sum_(j=0)^(n-1) 2^j eta_j,
    M_n=2^n,  q_n=sum_(j=0)^(n-1)4^j=(4^n-1)/3.              (1)

Set S_0=0,q_0=0 and M_0=1 for empty classes.
Let epsilon_j,epsilon'_j be independent fair binary bits.
The difference epsilon_j-epsilon'_j has exactly the original ternary law, independently at every j.
The map from n binary bits to sum 2^j epsilon_j is a bijection onto the integers0,...,M_n-1: induction adds the disjoint intervals0,...,2^(n-1)-1 and2^(n-1)...,2^n-1.
Counting pairs of these uniform integers therefore proves the ENTIRE endpoint law

    Pr(S_n=s)=(M_n-|s|)/M_n^2  if |s|<=M_n-1,
    Pr(S_n=s)=0 otherwise,
    Pr(S_n=0)=1/M_n.                                        (2)

There is no selection of a representative word in(2): all original fibers and their multiplicities are included by the binary-pair count.
Normalization follows either from the original pair construction or from sum_(s=-(M-1))^(M-1)(M-|s|)=M^2.

For the real scalar constraint sum 2^j u_j=s, Cauchy--Schwarz gives the minimum squared preimage norm

    E_n(s)=s^2/q_n=3s^2/(M_n^2-1),                          (3)

attained at u_j=s2^j/q_n.

### 3. A uniform strict three-quarters estimate after two binary places

**Lemma 1.** For every n>=1 and every endpoint s in(2),

    Pr(S_n=s)/Pr(S_n=0) <= exp[-gamma_n E_n(s)],
    gamma_1=log2,  gamma_n=3/4 for n>=2.                    (4)

*Proof.* For n=1 the ratios at s=0,+/-1 are1,1/2,1/2 and E_1=s^2, giving exact equality.

    -log(1-u)=u+integral_0^u t/(1-t) dt,
    integral_0^u t/(1-t) dt
       >= (integral_0^u t dt)^2 / integral_0^u t(1-t)dt
       =u^2/[2(1-2u/3)].                                  (5)

Every denominator is positive.

    [-log(1-u)]/u^2
       >= 1/u+1/[2(1-2u/3)]

### 4. Actual irreducible inputs, all windows and unavoidable relation overlap

Fix d>=1 and epsilon in{-1,1}, and let alpha be any root of

    P_(d,epsilon)(t)=t^d-epsilon 2.                          (8)
