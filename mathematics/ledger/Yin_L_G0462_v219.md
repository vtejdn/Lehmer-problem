# Even sign changes and unramified class group quotients for the negative quartic factor

<a id="kernel"></a>
## Real traces of both types determine the sign kernel

Let \(P=X^n f(X+X^{-1})\) be monic, irreducible and reciprocal over \(\mathbb Z\), with \(n\ge5\).
Write \(y_1,\ldots,y_n\) for the distinct roots of \(f\), \(T\) for their rational splitting field and \(L\) for the rational splitting field of \(P\).
Assume \(\operatorname{Gal}(T/\mathbb Q)\) contains the natural \(A_n\).
Suppose at least three \(y_i\) are real, including one in \((-2,2)\) and one outside \([-2,2]\).
Set \(E_n=\{v\in\mathbb F_2^n:\sum_i v_i=0\}\).
On orienting the inverse pairs, the kernel on traces is exactly
\[
 \operatorname{Gal}(L/T)=
 \begin{cases}
 E_n,& f(2)f(-2)\in T^{\times2},\\
 \mathbb F_2^n,& f(2)f(-2)\notin T^{\times2}.
 \end{cases}                                                        \tag{1}
\]

In the Salem case the even flips already follow from transitivity using the conjugates of complex conjugation, as in the [Salem kernel calculation](../claims/Yin_C_G0383_v1.md#salem-kernel-class-population) and [Christopoulos and McKee, Proposition 2.3](../audits/literature/CM10/readme.md#cm10-salem-kernel-attribution).
The [endpoint product test](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v272.md#flips) distinguishes the two kernels there.
For mixed signatures we use the [three-cycle commutator](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v315.md#signs), originally applied in a prime-degree setting with at least four fixed labels.
Three fixed labels suffice in arbitrary degree under the stated alternating action.

Indeed write complex conjugation as \(c=(u,\pi)\) in \(\mathbb F_2^n\rtimes S_n\), where the sign at a fixed label is one for a circle pair and zero for two real roots.
Take a three-cycle \(\gamma\) on fixed labels having both signs and choose any lift \(h=(v,\gamma)\).
Since \(\gamma\) commutes with \(\pi\), the kernel contains the vector of \(hch^{-1}c^{-1}\), namely \((1+\pi)v+(1+\gamma)u\), where permutations act by permuting coordinates.
On the chosen three coordinates it has exactly two ones and one zero, regardless of the action on other labels.
For any nonconstant kernel vector, adding its translate by a three-cycle on coordinates carrying both values produces a weight-two vector.
The kernel is invariant under \(A_n\), transitive on unordered pairs, so it contains all of \(E_n\).
With \(\delta_i=y_i^2-4\), select \(x_i+x_i^{-1}=y_i\) and take \(\sqrt{\delta_i}=x_i-x_i^{-1}\).
Their product squares to \(\prod_i\delta_i=f(2)f(-2)\), and a kernel element changes its sign precisely when it has odd weight.
The fixed-field criterion for this product proves (1).

<a id="base"></a>
## Adjoining \(i\) preserves the even kernel

Suppose the first case of (1) holds and put \(M=T(i)\).
Then \(\operatorname{Gal}(LM/M)\simeq E_n\).
When \(i\notin T\), containment \(T(i)\subseteq L\) would give a nonzero homomorphism \(E_n\to\mathbb F_2\) invariant under \(\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)\), because \(T(i)/\mathbb Q\) is normal and its order-two group over \(T\) has trivial automorphism group.
The weight-two generators \(e_i+e_j\) all have the same image under an \(A_n\)-invariant homomorphism.
The sum of the three edge vectors on any triangle is zero, so this common image is zero.
Hence \(L\cap T(i)=T\) when \(i\notin T\), and the conclusion also holds directly when \(i\in T\).

If \(|f(2)|=|f(-2)|=1\), every \(\delta_i\) is an algebraic integer unit because their product is a unit.
The polynomial \(X^2-y_iX+1\) then has unit discriminant, so its roots over any finite completion of a field containing \(y_i\) lie in an unramified extension, including at primes above two.
Indeed its distinct residue roots lift after a finite residue field extension.
Taking composita shows that \(L/T\) and \(LM/M\) are unramified at every finite place, as in the [unit-discriminant argument](Yin_L_G0462_v218.md#classes).
Since \(M\) is totally imaginary, the [ordinary class group Artin map](../audits/literature/GM02/Yin_U001.md#artin-sign) gives a quotient \(\operatorname{Cl}(M)\twoheadrightarrow E_n\).
Thus its ordinary class group has 2-rank at least \(n-1\), where 2-rank means \(\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{Cl}(M)/2\operatorname{Cl}(M)\).
If \(T\) contains a nonreal trace, normality makes \(T\) totally imaginary and the same quotient already occurs over \(T\).

For any one trace let \(F_i=\mathbb Q(y_i)\).
Its real embeddings include a place where \(\delta_i>0\) and another where \(\delta_i<0\), because \(f\) is irreducible and the real traces have both types.
Neither \(\delta_i\) nor \(-\delta_i\) is a square in \(F_i\).
Squaring \(s+it\) with \(s,t\in F_i\) shows that \(\delta_i\) remains a nonsquare in \(F_i(i)\), since its imaginary part forces \(st=0\).
Under the unit endpoint condition this yields a nontrivial quadratic extension of \(F_i(i)\) unramified at all finite places, so \(\operatorname{Cl}(F_i(i))\) has even order.

## The factor carrying the negative exterior root at even \(b\)

Take coprime positive \(a,b\) with \(a\) odd and \(b\) even, \(r=b/a\), and \(g(U)=U^4-U-1,\ B(U)=1-U^3-U^4\).
The monic parent is \(-g(X^a)-X^bB(X^a)\).
At all sufficiently large \(r\) its individual nontorsion factors are reciprocal and their trace groups contain the full alternating group by the [quartic trace group theorem](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v397.md#trace).
Let \(P=X^nf(X+X^{-1})\) be the irreducible factor carrying the [unique negative real exterior root](Yin_L_G0462_v210.md#real), and \(k\) its number of exterior roots.
The sole real exterior root gives one real trace below \(-2\), each of the other \(k-1\) exterior roots gives a nonreal trace, and the remaining \(n-k\) traces are in \((-2,2)\).
Indeed each inverse pair outside the circle contains exactly one exterior root, and an inverse pair has a real trace exactly when it consists of real roots or of circle roots.
The [individual degree comparison](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v399.md#counts) gives \(2n=rk(1+o(1))\) uniformly.
In particular \(n-k\ge2\) and \(n\ge5\) for sufficiently large \(r\), including when \(k\) varies.

For completeness the [even endpoints and parity](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v400.md#parity) give \(f(2)=f(-2)\in\{1,2\}\).
In fact the parent values at \(1,-1\) are \(2,-2\), and the only possible [cyclotomic factor](Yin_L_G0462_v212.md#orders) is \(\Phi_4\), present precisely if \(4\mid b\).
After its removal the full trace \(H\) has odd degree in both even residue classes of \(b\bmod4\).
Thus \(H(2)=H(-2)=h\), with \(h=1\) for \(4\mid b\) and \(h=2\) otherwise.
Every monic irreducible trace factor \(f_i\) has \(f_i(2)>0\) since all its real roots are below two.
The congruence \(f_i(-2)\equiv f_i(2)\bmod4\) and \(|f_i(-2)|\mid h\) force \(f_i(-2)=1\) whenever \(f_i(2)=1\).
If \(h=2\), the sole factor with \(f_i(2)=2\) also has \(f_i(-2)=2\) by multiplication to \(H(-2)=2\).
In particular the negative factor has square endpoint product, and (1) gives the sign kernel \(E_n\) of order \(2^{n-1}\) and rank \(n-1\).

When \(4\mid b\) the ordinary 2-rank of \(\operatorname{Cl}(T(i))\) is therefore at least \(n-1\) and \(\operatorname{Cl}(F_i(i))\) has even order, both when \(k=1\) and when \(k\ge3\).
In the latter case \(T\) itself has the same lower bound on its ordinary 2-rank.
Thus these lower bounds permit either exterior allocation.
The [signed endpoint and degree criterion](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v400.md#statement) distinguishes them at sufficiently large slopes because the sign change is Salem exactly when the negative factor's trace degree \(n\) is less than \(b/a\).
For \(b\equiv2\bmod4\) the exact sign kernel still follows, while the unit-discriminant class group conclusions apply to this negative factor if both its endpoint values are one.
