# A finite certificate for the plastic-number ternary density

<a id="density"></a>
## Exact density

Let \(P=X^3-X-1\) and let \(a_N(P,1)\) denote the maximum size of a set \(S\subseteq\{0,\ldots,N-1\}\) whose binary subset sums of powers of \(X\) are distinct modulo \(P\).
The [earlier finite computations](Yin_L_G0462_v285.md#thresholds) gave \(1/3\le D_1(P)\le 11/30\).
For every \(N\ge1\),
\[
 \lceil N/3\rceil\le a_N(P,1)\le\lfloor(N+6)/3\rfloor,
 \qquad D_1(P)=\lim_{N\to\infty}a_N(P,1)/N=1/3.
\]
Indeed the positive root \(r>1\) of \(P\) satisfies \(r^3=r+1>2\).
Among the powers with exponents \(0,3,6,\ldots<N\), each exceeds the sum of its predecessors, giving the lower bound.

## A certificate with seven nonzero carries

Read the indicator word of \(S\) from highest to lowest exponent.
At a position with bit \(z\), a signed difference of two subset sums can use any digit \(d\in\{-z,\ldots,z\}\).
The update on a partial difference \(a+bX+cX^2\) takes its coefficient triple to \((c+d,a+c,b)\) modulo \(P\).
A nonzero digit word ending at zero proves a collision.
It suffices for an upper bound to detect words whose nonzero intermediate carries belong, up to sign, to the following seven triples.
The digit columns give the update on the displayed representative, and a dash denotes a carry outside the retained list and zero.
For a negative representative, negate the digit and the new carry, which gives the same possible classes at either mask bit.

| Class | Triple | \(-1\) | \(0\) | \(+1\) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | (1,0,0) | C | B | – |
| B | (0,1,0) | E | – | – |
| C | (1,-1,0) | – | D | F |
| D | (0,1,-1) | – | F | D |
| E | (1,0,-1) | – | A | 0 |
| F | (1,1,-1) | G | E | – |
| G | (2,0,-1) | – | C | B |

Begin in the empty set of classes.
For bit \(z\), take the union of all permitted digit columns over the current classes, discard dashes, and include A if \(z=1\) to start a new nonzero word at this position.
A zero output rejects the mask because the corresponding relation extends by zero digits over all remaining positions.
Induction on the prefix length shows that every retained class is realized by a partial signed difference using a nonzero digit.
Thus every admissible mask follows a path in the next table, where a dash denotes rejection and the numbers identify the destination rows.
Each row applies the union rule above, so the table also verifies closure starting from the empty set.

On every displayed transition \(i\xrightarrow{z}j\), the last column satisfies \(h(j)\ge h(i)+3z-1\).
Summing along a length-\(N\) admissible mask gives \(3|S|-N\le h(j)-h(0)\le6\), as required.
