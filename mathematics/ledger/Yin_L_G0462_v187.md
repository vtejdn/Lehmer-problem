# The rational splitting group of the primitive cubic factors

<a id="quotient"></a>
## The two quadratic characters on each factor

Let \(a,b\) be coprime positive integers with \(\max(a,b)\) sufficiently large, and let \(A(X)=g(X^a)+X^bB(X^a)\), where \(g(T)=T^3-T-1\) and \(B(T)=1-T^2-T^3\).
Write \(P_i(X)=X^{n_i}H_i(X+X^{-1})\) for the distinct monic nontorsion rational irreducible factors of \(-A\).
Reciprocity gives a monic \(H_i\in\mathbb Z[T]\) of degree \(n_i\), by expanding the symmetric Laurent polynomial \(X^{-n_i}P_i(X)\) in \(X+X^{-1}\).
Let \(M\) be the joint splitting field of these \(P_i\), and set
\[
 d_i=\operatorname{disc}H_i,\qquad
 s_i=H_i(2)H_i(-2)=(-1)^{n_i}P_i(1)P_i(-1),\qquad
 E=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{d_i},\sqrt{s_i}:i).
\]
All these square classes are nonzero since the inverse pairs have distinct traces and no root is \(1\) or \(-1\).
Let \(\rho\) be the dimension of their joint span in \(\mathbb Q^\times/\mathbb Q^{\times2}\).
Then \(E=M\cap\mathbb Q^{\rm ab}\), and the rational splitting degree is
\[
 [M:\mathbb Q]=2^\rho\prod_i 2^{n_i-1}\frac{n_i!}{2}.
\]

In each signed permutation group \(W_i=\mathbf F_2^{n_i}\rtimes S_{n_i}\), take the sign of the permutation of the labels and the parity of the flips, in that order.
For representatives \(z_j\) and traces \(t_j=z_j+z_j^{-1}\) of one factor, the Vandermonde in the \(t_j\) is a square root of \(d_i\) with the label sign as character.
The product \(\prod_j(z_j-z_j^{-1})\) has the flip character, since permutations reorder its factors and each flip changes one sign.
Its square is \(\prod_j(t_j^2-4)=H_i(2)H_i(-2)\).
Thus all the displayed square roots lie in \(M\), and the two characters are independent of the initial choice of representatives and ordering.

By the [root group over the maximal abelian field](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v384.md#statement), the kernel of all parities in \(\prod_i W_i\) is already \(\operatorname{Gal}(M/(M\cap\mathbb Q^{\rm ab}))\).
Its fixed field in \(M\) is also \(E\), since fixing these square roots is exactly the parity condition.
Consequently \(\operatorname{Gal}(M/\mathbb Q)\) is the **full inverse image** in \(\prod_iW_i\) of the sign patterns realized by \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\).
Equivalently a pattern occurs exactly when its signs multiply to \(+1\) on every rational square-class relation among the \(d_i,s_i\).
The multiquadratic field \(E\) has degree \(2^\rho\), giving the stated degree with all correlations included.
If \(T\) is the compositum of the splitting fields of the cyclotomic factors, the splitting field of \(A\) is \(MT\).
Since \(T\subset\mathbb Q^{\rm ab}\), its degree over \(\mathbb Q\) is \([M:E][ET:\mathbb Q]\).

## Endpoint restrictions when \(b/a\) is sufficiently large

The [exterior partition](Yin_L_G0462_v169.md#partition) gives simple roots and only nonreal exterior roots.
Reciprocity and the absence of \(1,-1\) from the nontorsion factors then give \(P_i(1),P_i(-1)>0\), by grouping conjugate pairs.
For every \(i\),
\[
 P_i(1)=H_i(2)\equiv H_i(-2)=(-1)^{n_i}P_i(-1)\pmod4 .
\]

For odd \(a,b\) we have \(-A(1)=-A(-1)=2\).
The [torsion table](Yin_L_G0462_v172.md#cyclotomic) allows only \(\Phi_8\) and \(\Phi_{30}\), with endpoint values \((2,2)\) and \((1,1)\), respectively.
If \(b\equiv a\pmod8\), then \(\Phi_8\) occurs and every \(P_i\) has endpoints \((1,1)\).
Otherwise exactly one \(P_i\) has endpoints \((2,2)\), since the endpoint products are both two and each factor's endpoint values agree modulo two.
Every factor with \((1,1)\) has \(n_i\) even by the congruence modulo four.
The two possible cyclotomic half-degrees are \(2,4\), so \(\sum_i n_i\equiv(3a+b)/2\equiv(b-a)/2\pmod2\).
Hence when \(b\not\equiv a\pmod4\), the unique factor with endpoints \((2,2)\) has odd \(n_i\) and flip field \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-4})=\mathbb Q(i)\).
Every other flip character is trivial, and when \(b\equiv a\pmod4\) all flip characters are trivial.

For opposite parity \(\Phi_2=X+1\) consumes the entire endpoint value \(-A(1)=2\), so \(P_i(1)=1\) for every \(i\).
Write \(\epsilon_m=1\) if \(\Phi_m\mid A\), and zero otherwise.
At \(-1\) the value of \(-A/(X+1)\) is \(-A'(-1)\), which differentiation gives as \(b+7a\) for even \(a\) and \(b-a\) for odd \(a\).
The remaining possible cyclotomic factors for even \(a\) are \(\Phi_6,\Phi_{10}\), with values \(3,5\) at \(-1\).
For odd \(a\) they are \(\Phi_{12},\Phi_{18}\), with values \(1,3\).
Consequently
\[
 \prod_i P_i(-1)=
 \begin{cases}
 (b+7a)/(3^{\epsilon_6}5^{\epsilon_{10}}),&a\text{ even},\\
 (b-a)/3^{\epsilon_{18}},&a\text{ odd}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Each \(P_i(-1)\) is a positive odd divisor of this product, and the flip square class is represented by \(s_i=(-1)^{n_i}P_i(-1)\equiv1\pmod4\).
The degree formula still uses the joint rank with the trace discriminants, including any relations between trace and flip characters.
