# Automorphisms of a field realizing a strict degree record

<a id="norms"></a>
## Norms to proper subfields

Let the algebraic unit \(a\) generate \(K\), and suppose its positive log Mahler measure \(m(a)\) is strictly smaller than every positive measure of a unit of smaller degree.
For every proper subfield \(F\subset K\), the unit \(b=N_{K/F}(a)\) is torsion.
Indeed, the triangle inequality for positive parts of logarithms gives
\[
 [F:\mathbb Q(b)]m(b)=\sum_{\tau:F\hookrightarrow\mathbb C}\log^+|\tau b|
 \le \sum_{\sigma:K\hookrightarrow\mathbb C}\log^+|\sigma a|=m(a).
\]
If \(b\) were nontorsion, its degree would contradict strict minimality.
In a sequence of record measures tending to zero as in the [primitive pattern argument](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v575.md#scale), taking the first degree at which each new value occurs gives precisely this strict hypothesis.

<a id="automorphisms"></a>
## A representation on which every nonidentity automorphism acts without fixed vectors

Put \(G=\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbb Q}(K)\), allowing \(K/\mathbb Q\) to be nonnormal.
In the complexification of \((\mathcal O_K^\times/\mu(K))\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q\), take the nonzero subspace \(V\) spanned by the classes of \(ga\) for \(g\in G\).
For each nontrivial cyclic subgroup \(H\le G\), its group sum annihilates every generator of \(V\).
Indeed \(\prod_{h\in H}hga=g N_{K/K^{g^{-1}Hg}}(a)\) is torsion by the previous calculation and the fixed-field theorem for the finite group \(g^{-1}Hg\).
The average over \(H\) projects onto its invariant vectors, so each nonidentity element of \(G\) has zero fixed space on \(V\).
In particular this representation is faithful.
Every involution therefore acts as minus the identity, giving at most one involution in \(G\).
Every abelian subgroup is cyclic, since a simultaneous eigenline gives a faithful character into the finite roots of unity.
For a reciprocal generator the involution sending \(a\) to \(a^{-1}\) is consequently central in \(G\).

For example, two distinct field involutions exclude a strict record even when they do not commute.
Explicitly, for any nontorsion generating unit in such a field, some cyclic subgroup has a nontorsion relative norm, since otherwise the argument on \(V\) would identify the two involutions.
This produces a smaller-degree unit with at most the original measure.
A field with only the reciprocal involution already passes the representation test, so this descent alone leaves the unrestricted gap open.
