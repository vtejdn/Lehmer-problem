### 1. The preserved Walsh core requires enough independent effective couplings

Let r=2^h>=8, let H be the r-by-r integer Walsh matrix, and put

    V_r=[[I,-H],[0,I]],   H H^T=rI,
    Q_r(t)=t^2-(r-2)t+1.                               (1)

Its full determinant is Q_r^r.
Write lambda>1 for the positive root of Q_r and K=Q(lambda).
The polynomial Q_r is irreducible and lambda is simple, by the exact L234 discriminant argument.

Keep this ENTIRE ordered core unchanged, and append any s vertices with arbitrary integral upper-unitriangular block W and arbitrary integer coupling C:

    U=[[V_r,C],[0,W]],
    C=[[C_L],[C_R]],     C_L,C_R in M_(r,s)(Z).         (2)

All new vertices are after the core.
The entries of C and W, internal external-graph cycles, number of appended vertices and their heights are unrestricted.
This scope does not include changes to the retained core's entries or a different interleaving that changes its pencil.
Define the actual effective coupling matrix and its field rank by

    D_lambda=(lambda+1)C_L+H C_R,
    rho=rank_K D_lambda,      0<=rho<=r.               (3)

Then the COMPLETE determinant obeys

    Q_r^(r-rho) divides det(tU+U^T) in Z[t],
    m(det(tU+U^T))>=(r-rho) log lambda.                (4)

To prove this, at t=lambda the kernel of the core pencil is exactly

    v(x)=(x, H^T x/(lambda+1)),     x in K^r.          (5)

Indeed its block equations are (lambda+1)x-lambda H y=0 and -H^T x+(lambda+1)y=0, and (lambda+1)^2=r lambda.
Thus the kernel has dimension r.
The full pencil evaluated on(v(x),0) vanishes precisely when C^T v(x)=0.
This last equation is D_lambda^T x=0, after multiplication by the nonzero lambda+1.
Hence the full pencil has kernel dimension at least r-rho.

Since det U=1, its determinant polynomial is the characteristic polynomial of -U^(-1)U^T.
Algebraic multiplicity is at least geometric multiplicity, so lambda is a root with multiplicity at least r-rho.
Equivalently one can put its pencil at lambda in constant row-echelon form: every row that vanishes there contributes a factor t-lambda to the determinant.
Conjugation in K gives the same rank and multiplicity at lambda^(-1).
The monic irreducible Q_r therefore divides to the stated power over Q, and monic division gives the integer quotient.
Every remaining monic integer factor has nonnegative logarithmic Mahler measure, proving the full-measure lower bound in(4).
No degree normalization or cancellation with an external factor is possible.

In particular rho<=rank_K C<=s, and rho is also at most the number of nonzero rows of C.
If the coupling has rank at most q<r, the full determinant retains at least Q_r^(r-q), regardless of how long, complicated or high-weight the appended graph is.
For complete irreducibility in degree greater than2, a NECESSARY condition is rho=r.
Thus at least r independent effective coupling columns, and at least r touched core coordinates, are required in this preserved-core architecture.
They are not sufficient: the remaining determinant can still factor for other reasons.

The L238 single path has only one nonzero coupling column, of effective rank1.
Its Q_r^(r-1) factor is therefore forced by(4), not an accident of the path recurrence.
Finitely many independent paths through q<r coupling columns cannot remove the complete positive background either.
This obstruction remains only at the precise block-extension scope(2); changing the signed core, its order or its spectrum is a legitimate new mechanism.
The construction below does so.

### 2. A sparse height-one core with no positive-measure background

Take the explicit integer matrix

    B=[[ 0, 1, 1, 1],
       [-1, 0, 1,-1],
       [-1,-1, 0, 1],
       [-1, 1,-1, 0]].

Each row has squared norm3.
The six distinct row scalar products are respectively0,0,0,0,0,0, as direct multiplication shows; also B^T=-B.
Thus

    B B^T=B^T B=3I_4,   |B|=J_4-I_4.                 (6)

Define V_0=[[I_4,-B],[0,I_4]] in the displayed standard integer basis.
Its diagonal entries are all one, its height is1, its rank is8 and its energy is12.
The block determinant gives

    p_0=det((t+1)^2 I_4-t B B^T)
       =(t^2-t+1)^4=Phi6^4.                         (7)

The symmetric integer Gram matrix V_0+V_0^T is positive definite: its eigenvalues are2+sqrt3 and2-sqrt3, each with multiplicity4, and its determinant is1.
These are direct spectral facts about the displayed lattice; no classification of positive integral lattices is used.
In particular this core has no positive Mahler factor at all.

Choose one vertex in the first block and append k>=0 vertices as a unit-edge path after the core.
Denote the actual matrix by V_k.
The first new vertex is coupled only to the chosen core vertex; each next vertex is coupled only to its predecessor.
All those new upper entries can be chosen plus one.
Deleting the chosen core vertex removes one row of B; the remaining three rows still have Gram3I_3.
The rectangular block formula therefore gives the COMPLETE deletion determinant

    q_0=(t+1)Phi6^3.                                  (8)

Using the exact L203 leaf recurrence with(7)--(8) yields

    p_(V_k)=Phi6^3 F_k,
    F_k(t)=[t^(k+2)(t-2)-(1-2t)]/(t-1).              (9)

This is a polynomial identity for every k>=0.
The numerator has the written factor t-1; after division F_k is monic integral reciprocal of degree k+2 and constant term one.
All matrices have rank8+k, height1 and energy12+k.
The fixed factor Phi6^3 is now genuinely neutral, in contrast with L238's positive Q_8^7.
It has degree6 and is retained, not canceled from the actual determinant.

The first cases distinguish the threshold exactly:

    F_0=Phi6,
    F_1=(t+1)(t-1)^2,
    F_2=t^4-t^3-t^2-t+1.                             (10)

Thus k=0,1 have zero original measure.
At k=2 the actual full determinant is Phi6^3 times the irreducible quartic Salem polynomial already fixed in [L169](Yin_L_G0169_v1.md#quartic-infinite-core-theorem).

### 3. Complete positive-root count and growing irreducible complexity

Reuse the L203 exterior-root lemma at its proved scope: a fixed monic real seed D with no circle root gives at most as many exterior roots in t^N D-D^* as D has, counted with multiplicity; its exterior roots are approached with multiplicity as N tends to infinity.
Its short proof uses Rouche on the circle for t^N D-epsilon D^*,0<=epsilon<1, passage to epsilon=1, and disjoint exterior disks.
The division by t-1 removes only a unit root.
This classical mechanism is already attributed to Hir06 in the responsible fixed proof.

The seed t-2 has exactly one exterior root and no circle root.
Therefore every F_k has at most one exterior root.
Differentiating its numerator at1 gives

    F_k(1)=1-k.                                      (11)

For k>=2 this is negative, while at t=2 the numerator is3>0.
Thus there is a unique exterior root lambda_k in(1,2), and it is real, positive and simple.
Reciprocity supplies its unique strictly contracting root lambda_k^(-1); all other k roots are on the unit circle.
In particular

    m(p_(V_k))=m(F_k)=log lambda_k,      k>=2.          (12)

The sequence of these roots is strictly increasing.
Write N=k+2 and L_N(t)=t^N(t-2)-(1-2t).
At t=lambda_k one has t^N(t-2)=1-2t<0, so

    L_(N+1)(lambda_k)=(lambda_k-1)(1-2lambda_k)<0.

Since there is just one exterior root for the next polynomial and its value at2 is positive, lambda_(k+1)>lambda_k.
The exterior-disk lemma also gives convergence to the seed root2.
If beta_4 is the actual L169 quartic root, then

    beta_4=lambda_2<=lambda_k<2,
    lambda_k increases to2,
    log beta_4<=m(p_(V_k))<log2.                     (13)

The full original total measure is uniformly bounded and is strictly smaller than the large fixed-background cost in L238.
It is still bounded away from zero.

Let R_k be the monic irreducible factor of F_k containing lambda_k.
Every monic irreducible factor of F_k has constant plus or minus one because their constant terms multiply to one.
To have absolute root product one, the factor containing the sole exterior root must contain the sole contracting root.
Its reciprocal therefore shares a root and coincides after monic normalization.
It has constant one: the positive reciprocal pair and all remaining complex-conjugate circle pairs have positive product; rational unit roots cannot occur in this irreducible factor.
Consequently R_k is reciprocal, occurs once, and contains every off-circle root of F_k.
Every remaining factor is cyclotomic by the same monic-integral circle-root criterion used in L203.
Thus the COMPLETE original determinant is

    F_k=R_k C_k,    p_(V_k)=Phi6^3 R_k C_k,
    C_k is the full cyclotomic cofactor with multiplicities,
    deg C_k=k+2-deg R_k.                             (14)

No formula for the individual orders or a uniform bound on deg C_k is assumed.
The actual cyclotomic padding has degree6+deg C_k.
At k=2, R_2=F_2 and C_2=1 by the retained quartic result.

The R_k are pairwise distinct.
If an exterior root z belonged to two F_k with different k, the two identities(9), with t-2 and1-2t having no common root, would give z^(k-l)=1.
This is impossible for |z|>1.
A shared positive irreducible factor would provide such a root and is therefore excluded.
Since exp(m(R_k))=lambda_k<2, the elementary coefficient bound |c_j|<=binomial(D,j) exp(m(R_k)) leaves only finitely many integer polynomials of any bounded degree D.
Pairwise distinctness then proves

    deg R_k tends to infinity as k tends to infinity. (15)

There is no repeated positive background to delete here.
There is still a neutral cofactor, and the full determinant is not claimed irreducible.
All factors of degree greater than two in this positive sequence are Salem polynomials at their stated root configuration.
This constructed family supplies no arbitrarily prescribed Salem input or near-one sequence.

### 4. The whole absolute companion has a different, higher cost

The actual absolute core replaces B by J_4-I_4.
Its squared Gram is I_4+2J_4, with eigenvalues9 once and1 three times.
Put Q_j(t)=t^2-(j-2)t+1, so Q_1=Phi3.
Its complete core determinant is therefore

    p_0^-=Phi3^3 Q_9.

Deleting the chosen first-block vertex leaves three rows of J_4-I_4, whose Gram is I_3+2J_3.
Its eigenvalues are7,1,1.
The full deletion has one extra dimension and hence

    q_0^-=(t+1)Phi3^2 Q_7.                            (16)

The same exact leaf recurrence now uses

    Phi3 Q_9-(t+1)Q_7=t J(t),
    J(t)=t^3-7t^2-t-2.

Reversal in J^* is at degree3.
Define the full growing factor

    A_k(t)=[t^(k+2)J(t)-J^*(t)]/(t-1).

Then, with no omitted factor,

    p_(V_k^-)=Phi3^2 A_k,
    deg A_k=k+4,    A_0=Phi3 Q_9.                    (17)
