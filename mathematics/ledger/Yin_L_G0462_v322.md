# A real half-degree numerator with a pointwise bound

<a id="square"></a>
## Testing integrality of the extremal polynomial

On 2026-11-20 the G0001 inquiry compared the [cyclotomic matching construction](Yin_L_G0462_v320.md#matching) with a real polynomial obtained directly from the interior roots.
Let \(P\) be monic irreducible reciprocal of degree \(2h>0\), with no unit-circle roots.
Let \(p\) be the real monic polynomial of the interior roots, \(a=p(0)\), \(t=|a|=e^{-m}\), \(s=\operatorname{sign}(a)\) and \(p^*(z)=z^h p(1/z)\).
As in the [short-square estimate](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v630.md#energy), \(|P|=|p^*|^2/t\) and \(I=p/p^*\) is inner.
Put \(H=(p+s p^*)/(1+t)\).
This polynomial is monic of degree \(h\), has constant \(s\), and satisfies
\[
                      \frac{|H|^2}{|P|}
                  = \frac{t|I+s|^2}{(1+t)^2}
                  \le \frac{4t}{(1+t)^2}<1 .
\]
It provides a pointwise bounded real numerator for every such input.

For any real monic polynomial \(B\) of degree \(h\) with \(B(0)=s\), the functions \((B-H)/p^*\) and \(H/p^*\) are orthogonal on the circle.
Indeed \(H/p^*=(I+s)/(1+t)\).
The analytic difference has mean zero, and its inner product with \(I\) vanishes because \((B-H)/p\) has only negative Laurent powers at infinity.
The inner function has mean \(a=st\), so the exact excess is
\[
        \int_{\mathbb T}\frac{|B|^2}{|P|}\,d\mu
                      =\frac{2t}{1+t}
                       +t\left\|\frac{B-H}{p^*}\right\|_2^2 .
\]
Consequently \(H\) uniquely minimizes this mean over the prescribed real endpoint coefficients.
If \(H\) has integer coefficients, the [nonnegative decomposition bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v635.md#statement) applied to the pointwise inequality forces \(M(P)\ge(3+\sqrt5)/2\).
This restriction is sharp at \(P=X^2-3X+1\), where \(H=X-1\).

For any integral \(B\) with these endpoints the [logarithmic mean bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v632.md#statement) forces the weighted squared distance from \(H\) in the excess identity to grow at least as \((1/26-o(1))\log(1/m)\) along a shrinking sequence.
The identity specifies the metric needed to test integer approximations, including ones whose roots are moved collectively.
For a test allowing a subsequent correction by a Laurent multiple of \(P\), use the [two-ended energy bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v638.md#statement) on the resulting representative.
