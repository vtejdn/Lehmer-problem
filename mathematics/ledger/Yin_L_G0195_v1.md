### 3. Quantitative normalized margin and the remaining obstruction

The cost of the finite correction is visible.
Suppose an ordinary finite strict control family has, for every annular real x and every unit direction u, a chosen word satisfying

    u.c_w<=-a,    ||c_w||<=C_*,    a>H sqrt(s).

Compose with (1), and put K=H sqrt(s).
The composite vector z=c_w+A^(-length(w))c_v satisfies

    u.z<=-(a-K),    ||z||<=C_*+K.

It is nonzero, and hence

    u.(z/||z||^2)<=-(a-K)/(C_*+K)^2.

After the finite real-cell refinement described in Section1, the centered normalized convex hull therefore contains a ball of radius at least

    r_cert=(a-K)/(C_*+K)^2.                          (3)

This is an explicit transfer bound for a supplied ordinary control table.
L180's arbitrary-negative result ensures existence of finite tables with any fixed a>K after shrinking strict neighborhoods, but gives no upper bound on the corresponding C_* in terms of a, the ideal or degree.
Formula(3) alone consequently supplies positivity, not the L187 threshold.

In fact the crude correction estimate (3) cannot reach the required threshold for the present small ideal, irrespective of how the ordinary table is chosen.
Cauchy--Schwarz gives a<=C_*, so for C_*>K,

    r_cert<=(C_*-K)/(C_*+K)^2<=1/(8K).             (4)

The last inequality is equivalent to(C_*-3K)^2>=0.
Here s=2 and H=4, so K=4 sqrt(2).

### 4. Why a full inverse attractor alone would be insufficient

An earlier tempting shortcut can be made exact at its proper scope.
For any algebraic unit beta with1<beta<3 and any nonzero ideal I, let Q=O/I and B=1/(beta-1).
On tuples of nonempty compact real sets define

    (F K)_r = union_(eta=-1,0,1) (K_(beta r+eta)-eta)/beta.

Iterating F prepends original digits.

    dist_H((F^n K^(0))_r,[-B,B])<=2B beta^(-n)

This density does not show that they cover every real point, nor that a finite family covers[-1,1].
