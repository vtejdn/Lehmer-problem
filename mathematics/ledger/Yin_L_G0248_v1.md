### 1. Exact two-parameter overlap on the whole order

Let alpha be an actual algebraic unit, O=Z[alpha] of actual degree d, and X its compact additive dual, with Haar probability H.
Let S(theta)(x)=theta(alpha x).
Use the ORIGINAL difference digits0,+1,-1 with probabilities1/2,1/4,1/4.
Let p_n be the full zero-return probability, p_0=1, let R be the radius of U, and write

    U(t)=sum_(n>=0) p_n t^n=1/(1-F(t)),  0<t<R,
    eta_t=1-F(t),    eta_R=1-F(R)>=0.                 (1)

All sums and derivatives at R mean increasing limits.
The full first-return series has nonnegative coefficients and F(R)<=1.
For a nontorsion unit,1<R<=2 by the already retained sparse-position proof; the formulas also apply to a torsion unit whenever their stated parameter interval is nonempty.

Use the actual L240 functions and measures

    w(theta)=cos^2(pi theta(1)),
    W_n=product_(j=0)^(n-1) w composed with S^j,
    V_m=product_(j=1)^m w composed with S^(-j),
    a_s=eta_s sum_(n>=0) s^n W_n H,
    b_t=eta_t sum_(m>=0) t^m V_m H.                   (2)

Their whole-order Fourier coefficients are A_s(x),B_t(x) in[0,1], nonnegative and increasing in their own parameter through R.
L240 proves their unique critical weak limits.
Define c_(s,t)=a_s*b_t.
For0<s,t<R,

    K(s,t):=sum_(x in O) A_s(x) B_t(x)
           =[s U(s)-t U(t)]/[(s-t)U(s)U(t)]
           =[s eta_t-t eta_s]/(s-t),   s!=t,
    K(s,s)=eta_s+s F'(s).                             (3)

Here and below the zero coefficient is exactly one.

To prove the full mass identity, expand the finite trigonometric products.
The coefficient identity gives

    sum_x mu_n(x) mu_m(alpha^m x)
         =integral_X W_n V_m dH=p_(n+m).              (4)

Indeed composing W_n V_m with S^m changes its exponents from -m,...,n-1 to0,...,n+m-1, so Haar invariance gives the last equality.
This keeps every digit and uses no independence between a selected prefix and a return event.
All coefficients in the double sum are nonnegative.
Thus Tonelli yields

    K(s,t)=eta_s eta_t sum_(n,m>=0) s^n t^m p_(n+m).

This is finite because, with v=max(s,t)<R, it is bounded by a constant times sum_(k>=0)(k+1)v^k p_k.
The latter converges strictly inside the power-series radius.
Grouping terms of total length k uses the exact geometric identity sum_(n=0)^k s^n t^(k-n)=(s^(k+1)-t^(k+1))/(s-t), proving(3).
On the diagonal it is(U(s)+sU'(s))/U(s)^2, which equals the stated expression.
This diagonal is precisely L236/L240 reuse, not another new stationary identity.

The absolutely summable coefficients provide the continuous convolution density

    C_(s,t)(theta)=sum_x A_s(x)B_t(x) chi_(-x)(theta),
    0<=C_(s,t)(theta)<=C_(s,t)(0)=K(s,t).             (5)

Equality of Fourier coefficients identifies this series with the positive convolution measure.
Positivity everywhere follows from continuity and positivity as a measure.
The triangle inequality gives the exact supremum at zero.
Thus this is a full density norm, not merely the formal value of two potentially unbounded individual densities at a point.

The norm K(s,t) is symmetric in its two parameters.
The density itself need not be symmetric under their exchange.
At a reciprocal input, L240's actual dual involution J^vee gives J^vee_* c_(s,t)=c_(t,s), by b_t=J^vee_*a_t and convolution commutativity.
No ordinary equality a_s=b_s is inserted.

### 2. A critical factor still has a bounded mixed convolution

Fix0<s<R and increase t to R.
Nonnegative coefficient monotonicity and(3) give

    K(s,R)=sum_x A_s(x)B_R(x)
          =[R eta_s-s eta_R]/(R-s)
          =eta_R+R[F(R)-F(s)]/(R-s)<infinity.          (6)

The same total holds for A_R B_s.
This includes the transient case eta_R>0; setting it to zero without recurrence would be incorrect.
The remaining factor R-s is strictly positive and eta_s<=1, so

    1<=K(s,R)<=R/(R-s).                              (7)

Consequently a_s*b_R and a_R*b_s both have continuous bounded densities, with norm exactly K(s,R).
The convergence C_(s,t)->C_(s,R) as t increases to R is uniform, since the nonnegative Fourier coefficient differences have l1 norm K(s,R)-K(s,t).
This argument does not presume that b_R has a Haar density or that it is square-integrable.
At nontorsion recurrence, it is the actual singular continuous measure from L244.

At this stage no boundedness has been proved with BOTH parameters equal to R.
The upper bound in(7) can diverge as s increases to R.
The distinction is precisely the unresolved critical derivative, not a removable normalization constant.

### 3. Complete Fourier tails and Holder regularity from actual word length

Identify O with Z^d in its power basis.
Let N be the integer matrix of multiplication by alpha on coefficient columns, and set

    Lambda=max{2, ||N||_infinity}.                    (8)

The norm here is the maximum absolute row sum on the ACTUAL coefficient space.
It need not equal the norm of the transposed dual matrix.
For a length-n word from zero to x, the original recurrence gives

    x=sum_(j=0)^(n-1) e_j N^j e_0,
    ||x||_infinity<Lambda^n,  |e_j|<=1.              (9)

For n>=1 this follows from sum_(j<n)Lambda^j=(Lambda^n-1)/(Lambda-1)<Lambda^n.
If x!=0, every word reaching x has n>=1.

The whole-order last-zero identity in L240 is important here: for x!=0, A_s(x) is the sum of s^n times original probabilities of zero-to-x paths with no further zero after departure.
It is a genuine nonnegative word series, even outside the core.
Therefore for kappa>0 and u=s Lambda^kappa<R,

    ||x||_infinity^kappa A_s(x)<=A_u(x), x!=0,
    sum_(x!=0) ||x||_infinity^kappa A_s(x)B_R(x)
             <=K(u,R)-1.                            (10)

Every degree, coefficient-space norm and change of Abel parameter is charged.
There is no assertion that one coordinate or one real embedding controls all words.
Multiplying the first pointwise inequality by B_R and summing is valid by nonnegativity and the finite total in(6).
In particular, for H>=1,

    sum_(||x||_infinity>H) A_s(x)B_R(x)
               <=H^(-kappa)[K(u,R)-1].              (11)

This is a tail of the full Fourier coefficient sum of the mixed convolution, not a conditional tail for the countable original return chain.

For0<kappa<=1 and u<R, let rho be the coordinate sup distance between two torus points.
The elementary character inequality gives

    |chi_x(theta+h)-chi_x(theta)|
       <=2 min{1,pi d ||x||_infinity rho}
       <=2(pi d rho)^kappa ||x||_infinity^kappa.

Using the absolutely convergent series and(10),

    |C_(s,R)(theta+h)-C_(s,R)(theta)|
          <=2(pi d rho)^kappa [K(u,R)-1].           (12)

Thus every one-critical mixed convolution is Holder continuous with any0<kappa<=1 satisfying s Lambda^kappa<R.
This phrase means exactly the displayed power modulus; no differentiability or universal exponent is asserted.
The swapped convolution has the analogous estimate using ||N^(-1)||_infinity and the first-hit word expansion for B_s, or using the actual reciprocal involution and its coordinate norm when appropriate.

For the direct first-hit proof of that swapped assertion, a length-n path from x to zero gives x=-sum_(j=1)^n e_(n-j)N^(-j)e_0.
With Lambda_-=max(2,||N^(-1)||_infinity), its norm is at most Lambda_-^(n+1), rather than necessarily Lambda_-^n.
This yields the fully charged factor Lambda_-^kappa in the analogue of(10)--(12).
We do not silently reuse the forward constant for the inverse word convention.
This inverse estimate is sufficient but is not claimed sharp.

### 4. Exact approach to criticality and the missing moment

For0<s<u<R, monotonicity in the word parameter makes every Fourier coefficient of C_(u,R)-C_(s,R) nonnegative.
Their series is absolutely summable, so

    ||C_(u,R)-C_(s,R)||_infinity=K(u,R)-K(s,R).        (13)

This follows from the triangle bound with equality at zero; it does not say the difference is a nonnegative function at every torus point.

Let q_k be the coefficient of t^k in F(t), multiplied by R^k, for k>=1.
Then sum q_k=F(R)<=1.
It is a probability law exactly at critical recurrence.
Put r=s/R.
Formula(6) becomes the completely nonnegative expression

    K(s,R)=eta_R+sum_(k>=1) q_k sum_(j=0)^(k-1) r^j.
    K_R:=eta_R+sum_(k>=1) k q_k=eta_R+R F'(R).       (14)

If K_R is infinite, the norms in(6) tend to infinity, and no bounded critical convolution follows.
If K_R is finite, L240 supplies the continuous density C_(R,R), and monotone l1 convergence gives the exact error

    ||C_(R,R)-C_(s,R)||_infinity
          =sum_(k>=1) q_k sum_(j=0)^(k-1)(1-r^j)
          =R F'(R)-R[F(R)-F(s)]/(R-s).              (15)

Both formulas retain the transient correction eta_R through(14); it cancels only in this difference.
Thus continuity at full criticality is controlled by the actual first-return secant, not by the individual factor norms.

For0<beta<=1, if M_(1+beta)=sum k^(1+beta) q_k is finite, Bernoulli's inequality gives1-r^j<=min(1,j(1-r))<=(j(1-r))^beta.
Since sum_(j=0)^(k-1)j^beta<=integral_0^k x^beta dx,

    ||C_(R,R)-C_(s,R)||_infinity
          <=[(1-s/R)^beta/(1+beta)] M_(1+beta).      (16)

This is a proved sufficient rate with the full actual moment.
It is not a converse, and neither that moment nor its input-uniform bound is supplied.
At recurrence it is the(1+beta)-moment of the complete tilted first-return length.
Fixed spatial-box mass at one time does not establish it, and arbitrarily long low-height words prevent identifying word length with height without a separate argument.

The finite-mean iff bounded-convolution equivalence itself is old L240.
The new information in(13)--(16) is the exact unequal-parameter regularization and its full uniform-error formula and moment cost.

    861/500<beta<1723/1000<125/64=(5/4)^3.           (17)
