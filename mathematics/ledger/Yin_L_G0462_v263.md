# Excluding residue factors of the first two-value candidate by cyclotomic resultants

<a id="resultant"></a>
## Intersections with small-measure test polynomials at 101

Suppose a row \(A\) at \(p=101\) passes the [two-value test](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#statement).
Write \(F=P_1P_2\) for its two qualifying primitive minima.
The [degree and height restrictions](Yin_L_G0462_v262.md#frontier) give \(H=\|A\|_1\le58,\ b=57,\ D=\deg F\in\{112,114\}\).
Put \(l=b-D/2\), so that \(l=1\) or \(0\), and \(S=\operatorname{rad}(\bar F)\) in \(\mathbb F_{101}[Y]\).
Bars denote reduction of integer coefficients.
The graph and its derivative modulo 101 imply \(S\mid\bar A\) and \(S\mid\overline{A_b^*}\).

For any monic irreducible integer polynomial \(T\) reciprocal up to sign and of degree \(t>l\) with nonzero constant term, one has
\[
          101^{\deg\gcd(S,\bar T)}
                   \le |\operatorname{Res}(T,A)|\le 58^t M(T)^{57}.       \tag{1}
\]
In fact \(T\mid A\) would also put \(T\) into \(A_b^*\).
Dividing both rows by \(T\) preserves every contact because \(A(\alpha^p)\ne0\) there.
The resulting signed graph at width \(b-t\), with the sign adjusted if \(T\) is antireciprocal, would have to satisfy \(D\le2(b-t)\) by [derivative divisibility](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v419.md#product).
This is impossible for \(t>l\), and when \(t>b\) even the initial divisibility is impossible.
Thus the determinant of multiplication by \(A\) on \(\mathbb Z[Y]/(T)\) is a nonzero integer.
Its nullity after reduction is \(\deg\gcd(\bar T,\bar A)\ge\deg\gcd(\bar T,S)\), giving the first inequality by integer Smith form.
The second follows by bounding each evaluation of \(A\) by \(58\max(1,|\gamma|)^{57}\) at a root \(\gamma\) of \(T\).

In particular if \(M(T)^{57}<(101/58)^t\) then \(\bar T\) cannot be squarefree and divide \(S\).
For \(T=\Phi_n\) with \(101\nmid n\) and \(\varphi(n)>l\), (1) gives the stronger numerical bound
\[
          \deg\gcd(S,\overline{\Phi_n})\le
                         \left\lfloor\varphi(n)\log(58)/\log(101)\right\rfloor .
\]
Thus a candidate residue factorization can be rejected from its intersections with these known polynomials before constructing the integer factors or the signed row.

## Forty-seven orders excluded in both degree pairs

Every nonzero root of \(\bar F\) has finite multiplicative order coprime to 101.
If it has order \(n\), all its Frobenius powers and their inverses are roots of the same \(\bar P_i\), by rationality and reciprocity.
When \(101\) and \(-1\) generate \((\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times\), this orbit comprises all primitive \(n\)-th roots.
In that event \(\overline{\Phi_n}\mid S\) and \(\varphi(n)\le d_i\le58\).
For \(n\ge3\) one has \(\varphi(n)\ge2>l\) and \(M(\Phi_n)=1\).
Inequality (1) would give \(101^{\varphi(n)}\le58^{\varphi(n)}\).
Consequently no such order can occur.

Among \(n\ge3\) with \(101\nmid n\) and \(\varphi(n)\le58\), the orders excluded by this single-orbit test are precisely
\[
\begin{gathered}
3,4,6,7,8,9,11,12,14,16,18,19,22,23,27,28,29,32,36,38,41,43,44,46,\\
47,49,53,54,57,58,59,64,69,81,82,86,87,94,98,106,108,114,116,118,138,162,174 .
\end{gathered}
\]
To reproduce the finite list, enumerate integers from 3 to \(2\cdot58^2\), discard multiples of 101 and compute \(\varphi(n)=n\prod_{q\mid n}(1-1/q)\).
The range suffices because \(\varphi(n)^2/n\) is multiplicative and its prime-power factors \(q^{e-2}(q-1)^2\) are at least one except for \(q^e=2\), where the factor is one half.
For each remaining \(n\) with \(\varphi(n)\le58\), start at one and multiply by 101 modulo \(n\) until returning to one, collecting the residues in \(C\).
Retain \(n\) exactly when \(C\cup(-C)\) has size \(\varphi(n)\).
This yields the 47 displayed entries and applies separately to each possible factor.

At the endpoints, a root \(1\) or \(-1\) of \(\bar F\) forces \(A(1)=0\) or \(A(-1)=0\) over \(\mathbb Z\), since the integer evaluation is divisible by 101 and has absolute value at most 58.
The corresponding common linear factor can be removed from both signed rows.
Thus when \(D=114\) neither endpoint can occur, and when \(D=112\) at most one endpoint can occur, since two removals would leave width 55.
Orders outside the listed test remain possible subject to (1) and the [discriminant restrictions](Yin_L_G0462_v262.md#frontier).
No two-value row at 101 is established or excluded by these conditions alone.
