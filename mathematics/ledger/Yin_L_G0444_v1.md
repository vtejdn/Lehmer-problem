    P_4(t)=t^4-t^3-t^2-t+1,
    P_10(t)=t^10+t^9-t^7-t^6-t^5-t^4-t^3+t+1.

    E(t)=-t^(-3d) H23(t)/72,
    F(t)=-t^(-5d) H25(t)/800,
    G(t)=-t^(-15d/2) H35(t)/30375.

Their reality follows from reciprocity and the respective even degrees 6d,10d,15d.

On the circle F(t)^2=|H25(t)/800|^2=V(t).
For every real s, the proposed shear symbol

    B_s(t)=(E(t)+s G(t))^2+F(t)^2+G(t)^2

therefore satisfies B_s>=V pointwise.
The nonzero polynomial H25 has only finitely many circle zeros and log V is integrable, by the exact Jensen normalization already proved in [C156](../claims/Yin_C_G0156_v1.md#logarithmic-screen).
B_s is bounded above and has the same integrable logarithmic lower bound almost everywhere, so log B_s is integrable and

    integral log B_s >= integral log V = J_V.              (1)

The independently checked [J99/R377 component values](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0377_v1.md#two-input-component-finding) are

    Lehmer_degree10:   J_V=1.991241720952,

with certified absolute error below 1e-10, whereas log2=0.693147180560 at that error bound.
Thus (1) is strictly greater than log2 for every real s on both retained inputs.
This excludes the whole real shear family from the intended Haar-below-log2 test without invoking an arithmetic singleton gate.
It does not refute that arithmetic gate or exclude the same family on other P.

More generally the same argument applies to any positive semidefinite real quadratic expression R(E,G) and any coefficient c>0:

    B=R(E,G)+c F^2,       integral log B >= log c+J_V.     (2)

Any candidate of this form reaching integral log B<log2 must have c<2 exp(-J_V) on the actual input.

## A residual component bound for genuinely mixed positive forms

For a fixed real symmetric form write x=(E,G)^t and

    B=x^t A x+2F b^t x+cF^2,

where A is positive definite and rho=c-b^t A^(-1)b>0.
Direct completion of the square gives

    B=(x+A^(-1)b F)^t A(x+A^(-1)b F)+rho F^2
     >=rho V.

Thus integral log B>=log rho+J_V, with the same zero-set and integrability argument as above.
The relevant unavoidable coefficient is this residual rho, not merely the displayed diagonal coefficient c.
Cross terms involving F can reduce rho even if c remains 1, whereas cross terms restricted to E,G cannot do so.
This elementary identity is a useful necessary screen for positive definite real matrix candidates; it establishes no arithmetic norm income.
