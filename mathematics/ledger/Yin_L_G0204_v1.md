### 1. The exact module carried by a common-length block alphabet

Let beta be an algebraic unit, O=Z[beta], g a nonzero member of O, L>=1 and theta=beta^L.
Suppose a finite family of original-digit words W_j, all of length L, has translations D_j=g delta_j with delta_j in O.
At block endpoints the substitution x=g y gives exactly

    x -> theta x+g delta_j,
    y -> theta y+delta_j.                              (1)

Write R=Z[theta] and M=sum_j R delta_j, an additive R-submodule of O.
Since theta is an algebraic unit, theta^(-1) belongs to R.
Thus multiplication by theta is an automorphism of O/M.
Each block acts on this quotient by [y] -> theta[y], independently of its chosen digit delta_j.
Consequently

    y outside M cannot reach0 by any finite sequence of these blocks,
    and it cannot be reached from0 by such a sequence.             (2)

This conclusion is purely algebraic and does not assume intermediate real legality.
In particular it remains an obstruction after imposing real guards.
It concerns the chosen block alphabet only.
The original single-digit maps need not preserve this quotient and can bypass it.

If w is an original return word for a nonzero generator a, of length L, then D_w=-theta a.
Set g=theta a; since theta is a unit, gO=aO.
The three length-L blocks w,-w,0^L induce delta in{-1,0,1}, so M=R.
This natural attempt to transfer the original ternary law to the entire principal ideal is therefore restricted by the exact suborder Z[beta^L].
If deg(theta)=deg(beta), L59 identifies its index by the determinant of the power-coordinate matrix; otherwise R has smaller rank and the obstruction is even stronger.
Principal equality gO=I alone says nothing about equality gR=I.

For a larger block library, a necessary algebraic repair is to supply additional actual block translations whose divided digits enlarge M to cover the desired states.
This does not itself prove representability with the finite alphabet, real legality, pointwise control or compact termination.
It identifies the precise module that must be checked before those analytic steps.

### 2. The actual degree-six generator blocks

Keep P(t)=t^6-t^5-t^3-t+1 and its Salem root beta in(3/2,151/100).
Put

    a=1-beta^(-3),    L=6,    theta=beta^6,
    g=theta a=beta^5+beta-1.

The fixed six-digit word w=(-1,0,0,0,-1,1) returns a to zero.
The exact power-coordinate matrix for1,theta,...,theta^5, in the basis1,beta,...,beta^5, is

    C6 = [1 -1  -7  -81  -951  -11097;
          0  1   2   27   320    3729;
          0  0   2   18   212    2476;
          0  1   8   93  1092   12741;
          0  0   6   62   724    8460;
          0  1  10  123  1432   16713].

The initial exact algebra gives det(C6)=1024 and

    beta = (7+41theta+14theta^2+14theta^3-13theta^4+theta^5)/16.

Thus theta has degree six and beta does not belong to R=Z[theta].
J41 reconstructs these finite integer identities by multiplication modulo P, integer determinant elimination and exact rational linear solving; it does not accept the displayed coordinates as a certificate without reconstructing them.
This remains an author computation, not a separately organized independent review.
The obstruction is an actual suborder of index1024 inside O, not a different ambient fractional module.

For example x1=a(beta-1)=beta+beta^2-beta^3 belongs to I and is narrow at both real embeddings.
Indeed 0<x1_+<51/100, while |x1_-|=(beta^3-1)(1-beta^(-1))<124590501/151000000<1<S, where S=beta/(beta-1).
Its scaled state y1=x1/g=theta^(-1)(beta-1) lies outside R: otherwise the unit theta would give beta-1 in R.
Therefore (2) forbids every return using only w,-w,0^6.
Yet direct use of P gives

    beta^6 x1=beta^3+beta^2-beta,

so the original six-digit word(0,0,-1,-1,1,0) returns x1 exactly to zero.
This word already reaches zero after its fifth digit and then appends zero; no minimal-length claim is intended.
It is a decisive original-alphabet bypass, not a counterexample to the already proved L199 theorem.

The arithmetic obstruction also occurs strictly inside the smaller macro law's own necessary real windows.
Set

    y2=beta^(-3)(beta-1)^2=1-beta+beta^2-2beta^3+beta^4,
    x2=g y2=(beta^3-1)(beta-1)^2.

Exact solving in the theta basis gives

    y2=(-1+73theta+114theta^2+130theta^3+23theta^4-3theta^5)/16,

so y2 is outside R.
For the ternary macro law at theta, its natural two real windows are |y_+|<1/(theta-1) and |y_-|<theta/(theta-1).
They both hold here, since

    0<y2_+<(51/100)^2/(3/2)^3=289/3750
            <1/((151/100)^6-1)<1/(theta-1),
    0<y2_-=beta(beta-1)^2<(151/100)(51/100)^2<1.

Equivalently the corresponding x windows are

    |x_+|<beta^3/(beta^3+1),
    |x_-|<1/(beta^3+1).                              (3)

In particular x2 is in the original narrow domain N and in I, and L199 proves its original-law return.
But every return in the three-block system is forbidden by its nonzero O/R class.
Thus the module obstruction does not merely restate exclusion from the macro law's real windows.

### 3. A second obstruction already visible at the real boundary

Let beta>1, h>=1, a=1-beta^(-h), and suppose w is an original return word for a of length L.
For the three-block alphabet w,-w,0^L, set theta=beta^L and g=theta a>0 at the expanding embedding.
Any finite macro return from a real x satisfies

    |x| <= g sum_(j=1)^n theta^(-j)
         <g/(theta-1).                              (4)

If L>=h, then g/(theta-1)<=1.
More explicitly, for x>=1 every block gives

    theta x+D >= theta x-g >= theta-g=beta^(L-h)>=1,

and induction proves that no nonempty concatenation has a strictly narrow endpoint when it starts at x=1.
For the degree-six generator of Section2 the lower endpoint after one block is beta^3>1, so it immediately escapes even farther in that direction.

This is an obstruction to a full real-cell control cover on the closed annulus [1/2,1], as required by the existing compact-descent interface.
It is not an assertion that1 belongs to the proper ideal I.
The actual-order states x1,x2 in Section2 supply the separate arithmetic counterexamples.
Nor is this a claim that no other original-digit library can control the same ideal: the already proved L199 theorem precludes that inference in the fixed degree-six case.

### 4. Cyclic rotations repair the algebraic module, but not automatically the real overlap

Let beta be an algebraic unit which is not a root of unity, h>=1, theta=beta^h and g=theta-1.
Suppose an original-digit word w=(eta_1,...,eta_h) has translation D_0=1-theta=-g, equivalently T_w(1)=1.
Define its prefix states

    s_0=1,
    s_j=beta s_(j-1)+eta_j
       =beta^j+sum_(i=1)^j eta_i beta^(j-i).

Let w_j be its j-fold left cyclic rotation, for0<=j<h, with translation D_j.
Moving its first digit to the end gives the exact identity

    D_(j+1)=beta D_j+eta_(j+1)(1-theta).

Starting with D_0=-g, induction proves

    D_j=-g s_j.                                    (5)

Every rotation therefore preserves the actual principal ideal I=gO at its block endpoints.
For R=Z[theta], the divided translations delta_j=-s_j generate the whole O as an R-module.
Indeed delta_0=-1; successively delta_j has leading term -beta^j with an integer combination of1,...,beta^(j-1) below it.
Induction gives1,beta,...,beta^(h-1) in their R-span.
Every nonnegative power beta^k is theta^q beta^r with0<=r<h, so these elements span O over R.
This argument does not require degree preservation for theta.
Since beta is a unit, no missing negative powers enlarge O.
Consequently

    sum_(j=0)^(h-1) R delta_j=O.                   (6)

This is a constructive symbolic repair of Section1's module obstruction using only genuine original-digit words.
It does not assert that arbitrary R-coefficients can be represented by the finite digit set in a finite Horner expansion.
Real legality and neutral geometry remain separate obligations.

There is a general supply of such cyclic libraries.
If v=(nu_1,...,nu_ell) returns1 to0, then for every h>=ell+1 the word

    w_h=v 0^(h-ell-1) 1                            (7)

has T_(w_h)(1)=1 and translation1-beta^h.
It also returns a_h=1-beta^(-h) to zero.
The rotations, their negatives and the zero block all have length h and preserve I_h=(beta^h-1)O.
There are at most2h+1 such words, and a concatenation of n blocks has exactly hn original digits.

For every Salem beta in(1,2), the premise that such a v exists follows from the already fixed [L91 height-one relation](Yin_L_G0091_v1.md#effective-first-height-one-relation).
With d=deg(beta), delta=log2-log(beta)>0 and

    N=ceil[(2d/delta)log(8d/delta)],

that result supplies a monic relation Q of degree ell<=N-1 with coefficients in{-1,0,1}; its coefficients after the leading1, read from high to low, form v.
Thus one may take h=N.
This only transfers L91's explicit length cost and existence.
L91 gives no bound |s_j,+|<=1, no full neutral control margin and no terminated residue certificate for the cyclic libraries.

For the degree-six beta above, there is a much more explicit infinite symbolic family.
The word

    v=(-1,0,-1,0,-1,1)

returns1 to0 directly by P(beta)=0.
Its prefix states are

    s_0=1,
    s_1=beta-1,
    s_2=beta^2-beta,
    s_3=beta^3-beta^2-1,
    s_4=beta^4-beta^3-beta,
    s_5=beta^5-beta^4-beta^2-1=-beta^(-1),
    s_6=0.                                        (8)

The first six states form a Z-basis of O: their coefficient matrix is triangular with all diagonal entries1.
Their expanding values lie in[-1,1].
To check this without a numerical root approximation, beta in[3/2,151/100] gives the respective enclosures

    [1,1], [1/2,51/100], [3/4,7701/10000],
    [1/8,162851/1000000], [3/16,24590501/100000000],
    [-2/3,-100/151], [0,0].                        (9)

For every h>=7, use w_h=v 0^(h-7)1.
Its first h prefix states consist of s_0,...,s_5 and repeated zeros.
Hence the distinct divided translations of its rotations, with signs and zero added, are exactly{0,+/-s_0,...,+/-s_5}.
Their Z-span already equals O, a stronger statement than(6).
Nevertheless every physical translation at the expanding embedding satisfies

    |D_j,+| <= beta^h-1.

Every permitted block, including its negative and zero, therefore preserves the half-line x_+>=1:

    beta^h x_+ +D_j,+ >= beta^h x_+-(beta^h-1)>=1.  (10)

No nonempty concatenation from real1 ends strictly inside(-1,1).
Thus an explicit family of module-complete cyclic-ideal libraries fails the full real-cell pointwise-control condition.
Equation(10) is an all-h proof, not extrapolation from a finite search.
It does not exclude a state-dependent library using additional original words, variable block lengths or a different real entry mechanism.
It also does not assert failure at every actual ideal point or every interior real point.

### 5. The joint neutral geometry retains the generator's singular values

For clarity, the geometric transfer below is conditional on actual control words and on the correct rescaled real domains; principalization alone supplies neither.
Let beta be Salem, with selected neutral embeddings zeta_1,...,zeta_s, and regard C^s as a real Euclidean space with squared norm sum_j|z_j|^2.
Set A=diag(zeta_j) and G=diag(sigma_j(g)).
Since g is nonzero in the number field, G is invertible.
Write

    m_g=min_j |sigma_j(g)|>0,
    M_g=max_j |sigma_j(g)|.

For n common-length blocks with divided translations delta_i, the neutral displacement in the rotating frame is

    c_y=sum_(i=1)^n A^(-Li) sigma_neutral(delta_i),
    c_x=sum_(i=1)^n A^(-Li) sigma_neutral(g delta_i)
       =G c_y.                                    (11)

This equality includes all neutral coordinates.
In particular

    m_g||c_y|| <= ||c_x|| <= M_g||c_y||.

Suppose a finite set of nonzero divided displacements c_y has the following normalized directional margin: for every real unit q, some member obeys

    <q,c_y>/||c_y||^2 <= -r_y,     r_y>0.

For a real unit u put q=G* u/||G* u||, where G* is the Euclidean adjoint.
Choosing its corresponding member gives

    <u,Gc_y>/||Gc_y||^2
     <= -r_y ||G* u|| ||c_y||^2/||Gc_y||^2
     <= -r_y m_g/M_g^2.                            (12)

Thus a sufficient transferred margin is r_x=r_y m_g/M_g^2.
If the divided squared displacements have minimum mu_y>0, the physical minimum is at least m_g^2 mu_y.
Every word of n macro digits costs Ln original digits.
There is no assertion that(12) is optimal.

The domain also changes.
If the original narrow domain is |x_+|<=1, |x_-|<=S=beta/(beta-1), multiplication x=g y gives exactly

    |y_+|<=1/|g_+|,    |y_-|<=S/|g_-|.            (13)

These are not in general the standard narrow strips for the base theta.
Endpoint strictness and all originally required real guards must be verified on(13), using the actual original words; macro scaling does not do this.
Residue preservation is at block endpoints, and intermediate original digits can leave the ideal.

For the cyclic choice g=beta^h-1 the singular values are |zeta_j^h-1|.
The ideal index satisfies the exact product identity

    [O:gO]=|N_(K/Q)(g)|
           =|g_+g_-| product_j |sigma_j(g)|^2,      (14)

since multiplication by g is an injective integer matrix on the actual order.
Neither principality nor this product formula eliminates the individual factors in(12).
Indeed, when s>=2, the fixed [joint phase-density theorem](Yin_L_G0176_v1.md#salem-joint-phase-density) supplies arbitrarily large h with zeta_1^h arbitrarily near1 and zeta_2^h arbitrarily near-1.
Along a chosen sequence m_g tends to0 while M_g tends to at least2 in the sense liminf M_g>=2, and M_g<=2; hence m_g/M_g^2 tends to0.
Thus this general sufficient transfer estimate can degenerate even at one fixed input.
This is a statement about the bound, not a proof that the optimal physical control margin degenerates.
The cited density proof supplies no effective first such h, and no uniformity in beta, degree or the target accuracy is imported.
For s=1 this particular two-coordinate argument does not apply.
