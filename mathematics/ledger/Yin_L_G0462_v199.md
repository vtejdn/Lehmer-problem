# Small nonzero trace remainders on the original reciprocal input

<a id="obstruction"></a>
## Simultaneous bounds for two reduced traces and their remainder

Let \(P\) be a monic irreducible reciprocal nontorsion unit polynomial of degree \(d=2m\ge4\), \(h=m(P)\) its logarithmic Mahler measure, and \(p\ge5\) a degree-preserving prime.
For a root \(\alpha\) write \(\beta=\alpha^p\), \(Q=\min_{\mathbb Q}(\beta)\), \(q=\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\), \(R=\mathbb Z[q]\), \(P(x)=x^mH(x+x^{-1})\), and \(I=[\mathbb Z[\alpha]:\mathbb Z[\beta]]\).
Write \(N\) for the norm from \(\mathbb Q(q)\).
For each \(\sigma=\pm1\) there is a primitive integral row \(A\) of degree \(2d\) with nonzero constant term and coprime to its reversal such that, for \(J=2pd+1\),
\[
 P^2\mid F(x)=A(x^p)+\sigma x^J A(x^{-p}).
\]
These are the adjacent classes within the [original degree cap](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v363.md#statement).
Define \(u,v\in R\) by
\[
 \beta^{-d}A(\beta)=(1-\sigma\alpha)u,\qquad
 \beta^{-d}\bigl(2p\beta A'(\beta)-J A(\beta)\bigr)=(1+\sigma\alpha)v.
\]
Let \(U,V\) be their unique integral representatives of degree \(<m\), and let \(\|\cdot\|_C\) be the largest absolute coefficient in the monic integral basis \(1,C_1,\ldots,C_{m-1}\), where \(C_j(x+x^{-1})=x^j+x^{-j}\) for \(j\ge1\).
The row can be chosen with \(u\ne0\), \(v/u\notin\mathbb Q\), and nonzero [derivative remainder](Yin_L_G0462_v189.md#odd) \(D=v+u-2(2\sigma-q)U'(q)\), satisfying
\[
 \log\max(1,\|U\|_C,\|V\|_C)\ll ph+\log(dp),\qquad
 \frac{\log|ND|}{pm}\ll h+\frac{\log(dp)}p .                         \tag{1}
\]
The constants are absolute.
In the fourth-logarithmic prime window, the right side of the second estimate tends to zero along any sequence \(d\to\infty,\ h\to0\).
Thus an absolute lower bound for the normalized norm on the nonzero, nonproportional branch would need an additional restriction on the row, such as small cofactor measure.

## Integral lifts and simultaneous approximation

In \(O_2=\mathbb Z[x]/P^2\) let \(B_2=\mathbb Z[X]/Q^2\) be the suborder under \(X=x^p\), with [index](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v376.md#layers) \(p^d I^4\).
Both variables are units in their orders.
For \(u,w\in R\) take their integral representatives \(U,W\) of degree \(<m\) and write \(t=x+x^{-1}\).
The kernel \(\Lambda\) of the additive map
\[
 R^2\longrightarrow O_2/B_2,\qquad
 (u,w)\longmapsto x^{pd}(1-\sigma x)\bigl(U(t)+H(t)W(t)\bigr)\bmod B_2
\]
has index at most \(p^d I^4\).
For a pair in this kernel choose its powered representative \(T(X)\) of degree \(<2d\).
Each \(A_M=T+M Q^2\) has the required double zero in its diagonal since the displayed target cancels with its signed reversal about \(J\) and reciprocity preserves the Laurent ideal \((P^2)\).
Evaluation and one Euler derivative, as in the [odd-span congruence](Yin_L_G0462_v189.md#odd), give the prescribed \(u\) and
\[
 v=-u+2(2\sigma-q)U'(q)+\gamma w,\qquad
 \gamma=2(2\sigma-q)H'(q),\qquad N_0=|N\gamma|=2^m|P(\sigma)\operatorname{disc}H|.
\]
The additive triangular map \((u,w)\mapsto(u,v)\) has absolute determinant \(N_0\), by the determinant of multiplication by \(\gamma\) on \(R\).
The image \(\Gamma\) of \(\Lambda\) therefore has index at most \(J_*=p^d I^4 N_0\) in \(R^2\).

Put \(B=\lfloor(3J_*)^{1/(m-1)}\rfloor\).
In the indicated integral basis, some coset of \(\Gamma\) contains more than \(3(B+1)^{m+1}\) points of \([0,B]^{2m}\), since \((B+1)^{m-1}>3J_*\).
Fix one such point and subtract it from each of the others and itself.
At most \((B+1)^m\) differences have \(u=0\), and at most \((B+1)^m\) have \(D=0\), since the latter determines \(v\) from \(u\).
For \(u\ne0\) and \(v\in\mathbb Q u\), fixing \(u\) and the value of any coordinate of \(v\) at which \(u\) is nonzero determines \(v\).
This excludes at most another \((B+1)^{m+1}\) differences.
One remaining pair gives \(u\ne0,\ v/u\notin\mathbb Q,\ D\ne0\) and \(\max(\|U\|_C,\|V\|_C)\le B\).

For any such lift with \(u\ne0\), \(T(\beta)=\beta^d(1-\sigma\alpha)u\ne0\).
The value of \(T-X^{2d}T(1/X)\) at \(\beta\) is \(-\sigma\beta^d(\alpha-\alpha^{-1})u\ne0\), by inversion in the quadratic extension of \(\mathbb Q(q)\).
Every common root of \(A_M\) and its reversal must be a root of this fixed nonzero difference.
No root of \(Q\) is a root of \(T\), and each candidate away from \(Q\) can annul \(A_M\) for at most one \(M\).
Exclude also \(M=0,-T(0)\), and take \(M\) coprime to the content of \(T\).
Then \(A_M\) is primitive since any common coefficient divisor divides its leading coefficient \(M\) and hence also \(T\).
The two residue classes are disjoint, so the diagonal is primitive too.

## The scale of the norm and the coefficients on the same row

In each inverse pair of conjugates choose \(z_i\) with \(|z_i|\ge1\), and write \(a_i=\log|z_i|\), \(q_i=z_i+z_i^{-1}\).
Then \(\sum_i a_i=h\).
At \(q_i\) the defining Laurent expression gives \(|C_j(q_i)|\le 2e^{ja_i}\), while \(|C_j'(q_i)|\le j^2 e^{(j-1)a_i}\) by expanding \(j(z_i^j-z_i^{-j})/(z_i-z_i^{-1})\) into \(j\) terms.
Also \(|2\sigma-q_i|\le4e^{a_i}\).
For any odd-span row with the traces of [L189](Yin_L_G0462_v189.md#odd), let \(B_A=\max(1,\|U\|_C,\|V\|_C)\).
At every embedding \(|D_i|\le12m^3 B_A e^{(m-1)a_i}\), so every nonzero remainder satisfies
\[
 \frac{\log|ND|}{m}\le\log(12m^3 B_A)+(1-1/m)h .                    \tag{2}
\]
In particular \(\log|ND|\ge\eta pm\) requires \(B_A\ge e^{\eta p-(1-1/m)h}/(12m^3)\) on the same row.

The evaluation matrix of the monic basis at the \(q_i\) has row lengths at most \(2\sqrt m\,e^{(m-1)a_i}\) and determinant equal up to sign to the Vandermonde determinant.
Thus \(|\operatorname{disc}H|\le(4m)^m e^{2(m-1)h}\).
The root product gives \(|P(\sigma)|\le 2^d e^h\), so \(\log N_0/m\le\log(32m)+(2-1/m)h\).
The ordinary power-basis Vandermonde bound for \(Q\) gives \(|\operatorname{disc}Q|\le d^d e^{2(d-1)ph}\).
Since \(\operatorname{disc}Q=I^2\operatorname{disc}P\), we obtain \(\log I\le(d/2)\log d+(d-1)ph\).
Substitution in \(\log B\le(\log3+d\log p+4\log I+\log N_0)/(m-1)\), followed by (2), proves (1).
The contribution \((\log N_0)/(pm)\) from the fixed divisor is \(o(h)\) uniformly in the fourth-logarithmic window, since there \(ph/\log d\to\infty\) by the [finite comparison](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v363.md#finite).
For fixed \(P,p\) and the chosen pair, \(|M|\) may increase indefinitely along the admissible integers while leaving both traces fixed.
Indeed \(F_M/M\) converges coefficientwise in fixed degree to \(Q(x^p)^2(1+\sigma x)\), so continuity and primitivity give \(m(R_{F_M})=\log|M|+(2p-2)h+o(1)\) for the cofactor of the original comparison.
Controlling the measure for a minimizing row requires a further estimate.

## The exact minimum of the nonzero norm at index one

When \(I=1\), the minimum of \(|ND|\) among the primitive coprime witnesses above with \(D\ne0,\ v/u\notin\mathbb Q\) equals \(p^mN_0\).
Indeed \(P\) has [squarefree reduction](Yin_L_G0462_v162.md#support-prime) at \(p\).
This forces \(\bar H\) squarefree and \(\bar H(\pm2)\ne0\), since a repeated trace root lifts to a repeated root of \(\bar P\), while \(x+x^{-1}\mp2=(x\mp1)^2/x\).
It follows that \(p\nmid N_0\).
The odd-span congruence gives \(u,v\in pR\), so \(U\in p\mathbb Z[T]\), \(D\in pR\), and \(D=\gamma w\) with \(w\in pR\) since multiplication by \(\gamma\) is invertible modulo \(p\).
Conversely the [index-one additive quotient](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v376.md#denominator) is killed by \(p\), so every \((u,w)\in pR^2\) admits the integral lift above.
Take \(w=p\) to give \(D=p\gamma\).
If \(\gamma\notin\mathbb Q\), take \(u=p\), giving \(v/u=-1+\gamma\).
If \(\gamma\in\mathbb Q\), take \(u=p(q+c)\) with \(c\in\{0,1\}\) chosen so \(4\sigma+\gamma+2c\ne0\).
Then \(v/u=-3+(4\sigma+\gamma+2c)/(q+c)\) is irrational since \(m\ge2\).
The coprimality and primitivity argument applies to both choices.
These index-one hypotheses occur [at prescribed primes in the original window](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v371.md#lift).
