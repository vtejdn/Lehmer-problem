### 2. A basis-independent extra condition

Let A be an integral bilinear form on a free integer lattice, and let v generate its primitive rank-one alternating kernel.
Consider the condition

    x^T A x = x^T A v (mod 2) for every integer x.       (1)

The sign of v does not matter modulo2.
Condition(1) is invariant under any unimodular congruence: if A'=Q^T A Q and v'=Q^(-1)v, substitute x=Qy in(1).
All integer x are covered because Q is unimodular.
Thus(1) is an invariant of the form together with its intrinsic primitive alternating-kernel line; it is not attached to one displayed choice of coordinates.
This is the elementary characteristic parity condition used here, with the BILINEAR pairing on its right explicitly specified.
No general theorem about characteristic vectors is needed.

**Lemma 1.** Every proper integral rank-three form whose primitive alternating-kernel generator has norm minus one satisfies(1).

*Proof.* L312 gives, in its actual upper-unitriangular coordinates,

    V=V(a,b,c), a,b,c divisible by4,
    v=(c,-b,a)/4, with every coordinate of v odd.        (2)

Diagonal signs do not change these assertions modulo2.
Consequently V is the identity modulo2, and v is(1,1,1) modulo2.
For any integer x=(x0,x1,x2),

    x^T Vx = x0+x1+x2 = x^T Vv (mod 2),               (3)

since each integer square has the parity of that integer.
The invariant argument preceding the lemma covers every integer basis.
The norm-minus-one assumption and primitivity are essential; the complete scalar polynomial alone is not substituted for them.

Now let B be any integral2-by-2 matrix with det B=-1 and nonzero alternating part, and put

    G=[-1] direct-sum B, v=(1,0,0).                    (4)

The alternating kernel is exactly Zv, v is primitive and has norm minus one.
For x=(n,w), with w an integer binary vector,

    x^T Gx=-n^2+w^T Bw,  x^T Gv=-n.                   (5)

Hence G satisfies(1) if and only if w^T Bw is even for every integer w.
Call this latter condition B being even.
If B=[[r,s],[t,u]], it is equivalent to

    r,u,s+t all even.                                 (6)

Necessity follows by evaluating at the two coordinate vectors and their sum, and sufficiency by expanding the quadratic value.
At |s-t|=4, the third condition is automatic, so evenness is simply r and u both even.

**Theorem 2.** If [-1] direct-sum B admits a proper positive upper-unitriangular integral basis, then B is even.
In the nonsymmetric determinant-minus-one binary class, its alternating content must also be4 by L312.
In particular EVERY odd B with det B=-1 and |s-t|=4 is excluded in ALL integer bases, with no height or ordering restriction.

*Proof.* A congruence to a proper form carries the intrinsic primitive kernel to its primitive kernel, preserving norm minus one.
Lemma1 transfers(1) to G.
Equations(5)--(6) then prove the assertion.
The result is about the whole integral lattice, not a failed attempt to find a first positive pivot.

The evenness has an equivalent complement description.
In the proper coordinates, any y in the two-sided orthogonal complement of v has V(y,v)=0, and(1) makes V(y,y) even.
The exact integral splitting in L312 identifies this complement with B in(4).
This supplies an independent formulation of the same obstruction.
It does not prove that every even content4 complement is realizable; that classification remains outside this result.

### 3. An actual other class with all old scalar invariants preserved

Take

    B_o=[[1,2],[-2,-5]],
    G_o=[[-1,0,0],[0,1,2],[0,-2,-5]].                (7)

Then det B_o=-5+4=-1, its alternating content is4, and det G_o=1.
The primitive alternating kernel of G_o is the displayed negative unit line.
Yet the binary coordinate vector(1,0) has norm1, so B_o is odd.
Theorem2 therefore proves that G_o has NO proper integral upper-unitriangular basis.

This is a different binary congruence class from the successful even B_e=[[2,7],[3,10]] of L312: parity of all quadratic values is invariant under any unimodular binary change of coordinates.
More strongly, G_o and [-1] direct-sum B_e are themselves not integrally congruent, since their intrinsic kernel lines give different answers to(1).
Both full rank-three forms are odd because of the negative unit summand; ordinary parity of the whole form alone would not distinguish them.
The required information is the kernel-relative condition(1).

Their complete determinant polynomials coincide.
Direct expansion of the binary pencil for(7) gives

    p_(B_o)(t)=-5(t+1)^2+4(t-1)^2
              =-(t^2+18t+1),
    p_(G_o)(t)=(t+1)(t^2+18t+1)=F(t).               (8)

In particular they have the same scalar endpoints, not just the same positive logarithmic measure.
Their primitive alternating content and kernel norm also agree.
The symmetric part G_o+G_o^T is diag(-2,2,-10), of inertia(1,2); for the successful even example, B_e+B_e^T has determinant-20 and inertia(1,1), so its negative-line stabilization also has inertia(1,2).
Thus these endpoint, determinant, content, primitive-norm and real-inertia tests collectively still do not decide this actual integral-basis problem.

The proper V(4,4,4) realizes F, as L312 proves.
Consequently the new obstruction is to this specified integral bilinear form G_o, not to F as a scalar target.
There is an actual positive unit vector in G_o, namely(0,1,0), but no full positive upper-unitriangular integer basis.
The presence of a first pivot is insufficient.

### 4. Whole relaxed companion and literal lift costs

For the nonproper G_o use the explicit full-entry operation A^-=-|A|; it changes the diagonal as well and is distinct from D9's proper diagonal-one companion.
Here

    B_o^-=[[-1,-2],[-2,-5]],
    G_o^-=[-1] direct-sum B_o^-.                      (9)

The binary absolute form is symmetric, has determinant1 and is negative definite.
Thus its whole pencil is (t+1)B_o^- and

    p_(B_o^-)(t)=(t+1)^2,
    p_(G_o^-)(t)=-(t+1)^3.                           (10)

These are full polynomials, including leading signs and every neutral factor.
Put Q_-(t)=t^2+18t+1 and ell18=log(9+4sqrt5).
The original binary and ternary polynomials both have logarithmic measure ell18; both full-entry companions have measure0.

For completeness define the literal two-sheet lift using I=I2 and S=[[0,1],[1,0]].
Each entry x<=0 is replaced by xI, while x>0 is replaced by -xS.
Applied to G_o it is the actual integer block matrix

    W_o=[[-I,0,0],[0,-S,-2S],[0,-2I,-5I]],           (11)

of rank six.
Symmetric sheets carry G_o^- and antisymmetric sheets carry G_o.
The same orthogonal sheet splitting works for the transpose, so the WHOLE pencil satisfies

    p_(W_o)(t)=p_(G_o)(t)p_(G_o^-)(t)
              =-(t+1)^4 Q_-(t).                    (12)

The analogous binary lift has rank four and polynomial -(t+1)^2 Q_-(t).
Both relaxed lifts have measure ell18, exactly the original measure, not twice that measure.
The leading sign minus is retained and has zero logarithmic contribution.
No factor is deleted to obtain this calculation.

The full quantitative accounting is:

| B_o | 2 | 5 | 34 | -Q_- | ell18 |

The integer sums follow directly from1+4+4+25=34, adding the negative unit's square, and the fact that each lift block has two nonzero entries of magnitude|x|.
They are FULL squared-entry sums, not the strict-upper energy of a proper matrix.
The diagonal blocks in(11) include -I, -S and -5I, so the lift is manifestly not upper-unitriangular with diagonal ones.
It is never supplied as a proper realization or proper signed cover.

For the same full scalar F, the L312 theorem says that EVERY proper rank-three realization has height at least4, strict-upper energy at least48, companion measure at least ell110=log(55+12sqrt21), and proper-cover measure at least ell18+ell110.
V(4,4,4) attains all these bounds; its full squared-entry sum is51, and its rank-six proper cover has strict-upper energy96 and full squared-entry sum102.

### 5. Scope, failed implication and complete self-check

This is an unbounded all-integer result for the stated one-negative-line/rank-three operation.
