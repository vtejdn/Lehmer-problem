### 1. The actual integer quotient core is the reversed original core

Let alpha be any algebraic unit, with actual monic irreducible polynomial

    P(t)=sum_(i=0)^d p_i t^i, p_d=1, p_0 in{-1,1},
    O=Z[alpha], mu(0)=1/2, mu(1)=mu(-1)=1/4.

Use chronological quotient states q=(q_0,...,q_(d-1))=(h_(j-d)...,h_(j-1)) and the EXACT L235 update

    h_j=p_0(e-sum_(i=1)^d p_i h_(j-i)),
    S_e(q)=(q_1,...,q_(d-1),h_j), e in{-1,0,1}.       (1)

Let Q_act be the set of integer states lying on finite zero-to-zero words under(1), and D_act the original core for x -> alpha*x+eta.
Both include zero.
To compare these actual domains, define

    ell_j=-sum_(k=0)^j p_(d-k) alpha^(j-k+1),
                          0<=j<=d-1,
    Phi(q)=sum_(j=0)^(d-1) ell_j q_j,
    Psi(q)=alpha^(-1) Phi(q).                         (2)

By P(alpha)=0, ell_(d-1)=p_0.
For j<d-1, ell_j is -alpha^(j+1) plus integer lower positive powers.
Relative to the integer basis alpha,...,alpha^(d-1),1, the ell_j therefore have triangular change-of-basis determinant +/-1.
This assertion includes d=1, when the sole basis element is p_0.
Multiplication by alpha^(-1) also preserves O bijectively because alpha is a unit.
Thus Phi and Psi are genuine Z-module isomorphisms from Z^d onto O; there is no enlarged fractional module or omitted coset.

The identities

    alpha^(-1) ell_0=-1,
    alpha^(-1) ell_j=ell_(j-1)-p_(d-j) for1<=j<=d-1,
    p_0 ell_(d-1)=1

give directly, coordinate by coordinate in(1),

    Phi(S_e q)=alpha^(-1)Phi(q)+e,
    alpha Psi(S_e q)-e=Psi(q).                       (3)

Consequently a quotient edge q -> S_e q corresponds to the REVERSED original edge Psi(S_e q) -> Psi(q), with original digit -e.
Reversing an entire zero-to-zero word and negating its digits preserves the alphabet and is a bijection of closed words.
Equation(2) therefore restricts to a bijection Q_act -> D_act.
The quotient zero-digit companion has inverse-root orientation; assuming it has eigenvalues alpha rather than alpha^(-1) would be incorrect for general units.
Reciprocity was not needed for(2)--(3).

Let T_theta be precisely the WHOLE integer Markov chain of L239, at a fixed0<=theta<1.
Its digit probabilities are

    T_theta(q,S_0 q)=(1+(-1)^tau(q) theta)/2,
    T_theta(q,S_+ q)=T_theta(q,S_- q)
                               =(1-(-1)^tau(q) theta)/4,
    delta_theta=(1-theta)/4>0,                        (4)

where tau is L239's specified state parity.
In particular all three original digits remain possible and the support core is Q_act for every such theta.
At theta=0, mu(e)=mu(-e), so the matrix of T_0 restricted to Q_act is, after the permutation Psi, exactly K_act^T for the original weighted operator K_act.
Hence its counting-l2 norms also agree with those of K_act.
At positive theta there is only the support/core bijection: no original weighted transpose identity is asserted.
The L239 closed-path and observation factors remain necessary whenever the auxiliary law is used.

For a zero-to-zero quotient word of length n, all coordinates appearing during that word are precisely quotient coefficients, together with initial and terminal zero padding.
Thus the maximum of |q_i| over the full state path is the height of E/P for E(t)=sum_(j=0)^(n-1)e_j t^j.
Our tail below concerns this MAXIMUM OVER THE ENTIRE RETURN WORD, not a terminal state (which is zero), a single midpoint, or an occupation distribution.

### 2. A missing first-return word strictly increases complete return pressure

Fix alpha and theta as above and write

    b_n=T_theta^n(0,0), b_0=1,
    r_theta=lim_(n->infinity) b_n^(1/n)
                          =sup_(n>=1) b_n^(1/n).    (5)

The limit and supremum follow also directly from b_(n+m)>=b_n b_m and b_n>=((1+theta)/2)^n>0.
For a nonnegative integer height H, let C_H be the states reachable from zero and co-returnable to zero by paths confined to [-H,H]^d.
This is the zero strongly connected component of that finite killed graph.
Let A_H be its matrix with the ORIGINAL T_theta edge weights, without row renormalization, and put

    b_n^H=A_H^n(0,0), r_H=r(A_H).

Every boxed return stays in C_H and every path counted by A_H is genuine.
Thus b_n^H is exactly the mass of the returned words with maximum quotient height at most H.
The finite nonnegative matrix A_H is irreducible and has a positive loop at zero.
Finite Perron--Frobenius gives a positive right vector v with A_H v=r_H v, where0<r_H<=1.
The radius bound follows from substochasticity.

Here the diagonal has precisely this exponential rate.
Indeed

    b_n^H v(0)<= (A_H^n v)(0)=r_H^n v(0),

so b_n^H<=r_H^n.
From every state of C_H choose a path to zero and pad at zero to a common positive length L.
The minimum a of their finitely many A_H path weights is positive.
Therefore

    b_(n+L)^H >= a sum_i A_H^n(0,i)
                >= a r_H^n v(0)/max_i v(i).

The upper and lower bounds show lim_n(b_n^H)^(1/n)=r_H.
Nothing about an infinite l2 radius or positive eigenvector was used.

Let f_(H,j) be the mass of the FIRST positive returns at time j within the box and F_H(z)=sum_(j>=1)f_(H,j)z^j.
The finite Doob matrix

    Q_H(i,j)=A_H(i,j)v(j)/(r_H v(i))                 (6)

is stochastic and irreducible.
Its first-return masses are f_(H,j)r_H^(-j), since the v factors telescope along a closed word.
They sum to one and have a finite mean m_H.
For completeness, choose a common positive length L_0 and positive lower bound a_0 for Q_H paths from every state to zero, padding at zero.
At each L_0-step block the probability of a hit is at least a_0.
The first positive return therefore has tail at most(1-a_0)^k at k L_0 and mean at most L_0/a_0.
This proves finite normalization and mean without an infinite-chain recurrence assumption.
With z_H=1/r_H,

    F_H(z_H)=1,
    m_H=sum_(j>=1)j f_(H,j)z_H^j<infinity.          (7)

Suppose there exists a first-return word w that exits the height-H box.
Write its length as ell>=1 and its COMPLETE T_theta path probability as omega>0.
Set

    kappa=omega z_H^ell,
    epsilon_H=kappa/[2(m_H+ell*kappa)],
    z_*=z_H(1-epsilon_H).                           (8)

All quantities are fixed-input and box dependent; omega>=delta_theta^ell.
Also0<epsilon_H<=1/(2ell)<1.
Bernoulli's inequality(1-epsilon)^j>=1-j epsilon, summed against the probability distribution in(7), gives

    F_H(z_*) >=1-m_H epsilon_H,
    omega z_*^ell >=kappa-ell*kappa epsilon_H.

The full first-return series F(z), which may be infinite at a given argument, contains all the boxed first returns and additionally this distinct word.
Thus

    F(z_*) >=F_H(z_*)+omega z_*^ell
             >=1+kappa/2>1.                        (9)

Decomposing every closed word at successive visits to zero gives the formal nonnegative renewal identity

    sum_(n>=0)b_n z^n=sum_(k>=0)F(z)^k.

It holds as an extended nonnegative equality for real z>=0, by summing the unique finite decompositions; there is no need to exchange conditionally convergent sums.
At z_* the right side diverges by(9).
Cauchy--Hadamard and(5) consequently imply the quantitative STRICT comparison

    r_theta >=1/z_*=r_H/(1-epsilon_H)>r_H.          (10)

This uses a normalized first-return law ONLY for the finite matrix A_H.
In particular F(1/r_theta)=1 or finite full critical mean was neither assumed nor deduced.
The mechanism is a finite strict-pressure extension proved here directly, not a claim of a new general Perron theorem.

### 3. Infinite actual core forces fixed-height relative escape

Combining its two actual limits gives

    lim_(n->infinity)(b_n^H/b_n)^(1/n)
          =r_H/r_theta<=1-epsilon_H<1,
    Pr_theta(maximum quotient height>H | q_n=0)
          =1-b_n^H/b_n ->1.                        (11)

No statement of a uniform exponential onset or an input-uniform epsilon_H is included.
The extra word, its length and probability, the finite Perron vector and m_H carry the entire cost.
In particular no estimate epsilon(H)->0 can bound the omitted RETURN-CONDITIONED mass from above simultaneously for all n at a fixed infinite-core input.

    lim_(H->infinity)r_H=r_theta,
    r_H<r_theta for each finite H.                  (12)

The corresponding fractions have different iterated limits:

    lim_(H->infinity)lim_(n->infinity)b_n^H/b_n=0,
    lim_(n->infinity)lim_(H->infinity)b_n^H/b_n=1.    (13)

Thus fixed-box pressure convergence does not mean that a fixed box captures a fixed proportion of all sufficiently long returned paths.
Growing H=H(n), a tail of a single-time state, or stationary occupation tightness are different questions and are not excluded by(11)--(13).
If the actual core is finite, a sufficiently large box captures every return and(11) can fail completely; infinitude is an essential hypothesis.

### 4. A complete-state norm cost makes finite original returns usable at all times

Return to the ORIGINAL iid law mu, on any fixed algebraic unit alpha.
Use the complex counting-measure space l2(D_act) and the actual killed-core operator

    (K_act f)(x)=sum_eta mu(eta)1_(D_act)(alpha*x+eta)
                                        f(alpha*x+eta).

No finite box is imposed.
Let p_n=K_act^n(0,0), p_0=1.
Exactly L173's adopted inequality says

    p_n<=||K_act^n||_(2->2)<=C_alpha(n+1)^q p_n,
    ||K_act||_(2->2)<=1,                            (15)

where q=r_0+2s_0 counts real neutral embeddings plus twice neutral complex pairs.
For nonneutral places, counted as real places and ONE representative of each complex pair, set

    R_v=1/(|v(alpha)|-1) if |v(alpha)|>1,
    R_v=1/(1-|v(alpha)|) if |v(alpha)|<1,
    C_alpha=2^r_0 8^s_0
       product_(real nonneutral v)(2R_v+2)
       product_(complex nonneutral v)(2sqrt(2)R_v+2)^2. (16)

The cost in(15) controls both rows and columns on the full state space.
Its ingredients, explicitly checked at the L173 interface, are that the original difference law's zero displacement is a largest atom, nonneutral embeddings of actual core states are bounded by the relevant R_v, and a translated norm grid bounds every row AND column support by C_alpha(n+1)^q.

For a positive integer N define A_N=C_alpha(N+1)^q p_N.

    p_n<=||K_act^n||
        <=||K_act^N||^k ||K_act^r||
        <=A_N^k,
    p_n<=min{1,A_N^floor(n/N)}.                      (17)

All intermediate states and their full correlations are retained in the operator product.
No independence of adjacent conditioned blocks, forced zero seam, deleted boundary mass, or finite-memory reset is assumed.

    h_2(alpha)>=-log A_N/N.

In the other direction, concatenating actual zero-to-zero words gives p_(kN)>=p_N^k and therefore h_2(alpha)<=-log p_N/N.
