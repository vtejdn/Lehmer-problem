# The least radical modulus for the quadratic five-term example

<a id="orders"></a>
## Odd and even orders give different measures

For the unit \(\alpha=(3+\sqrt5)/2=\phi^2\), \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\), from the [five-term modular example](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v467.md#example), the integer \(M=2\) suffices for the [radical factorization](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v475.md#statement).
The reciprocal radicals having the same degree as their squares occur exactly at odd orders \(g\).
Their logarithmic Mahler measure is \(2\log\phi\); at every even order all radicals have measure \(\log\phi\).

Indeed the [positive radical of \(\phi\)](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v195.md#count) has degree \(2g\) at every order \(g\).
When \(g\) is odd, put \(y=\phi^{2/g}\).
Since \(y^g=\alpha\) and \(\phi=\alpha-1\), the field of \(y\) contains \(\phi\).
Bézout's identity for \(2,g\) then puts \(\phi^{1/g}\) in that field as well.
Consequently \(T^{2g}-3T^g+1\) is the minimum of \(y\).
It is reciprocal and has an odd-power term, so the negative of \(y\) is not conjugate to \(y\); squaring preserves its degree.
Every \(g\)th root of \(\alpha\) is a root of this same irreducible polynomial.

For \(g=2k\) the exact factorization is
\[
       T^{4k}-3T^{2k}+1=(T^{2k}-T^k-1)(T^{2k}+T^k-1).
\]
The first factor is the known minimum of \(\phi^{1/k}\); the second is its normalized reciprocal and is also irreducible.
Each contains roots whose \(g\)th power is \(\alpha\), since \(T^k=\phi\) gives a root on the first and \(T^k=-\phi\) on the second.
Each factor has measure \(\log\phi\), and inversion interchanges the two factors.
These odd and even descriptions yield full residual degree \(g/\gcd(2,g)\) above either choice of the corresponding base root.
The choice \(M=1\) already fails at \(g=2\), since \(T^2-\alpha\) splits in \(\mathbb Q(\alpha)\).

On 2026-11-19 the coordinating agent for Sheng Yin compared these formulas with exact rational polynomial factorization at every order \(1\le g\le24\).
The calculation agrees at all twenty-four orders; the argument above establishes the remaining orders.
This example distinguishes reciprocal degree classes and the distinct measures carried by them before applying the signed-graph or budget conditions.
