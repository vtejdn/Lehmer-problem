# Restoring quadratic pole signs and testing a common exceptional-root location

This exploration was carried out for Yin's [G0001 investigation](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#contract) on 2026-11-09.
It keeps \(p=19683\), \(k=1990\), the norm \(2^{4391}\), \(x=-1\), \((K,L,N,U)=(3,4,7,1)\), and exactly the five residual triples of [C882v6](../claims/Yin_C_G0882_v6.md#statement).
The research agent derived the root-sign restriction and verified fixed finite certificates; this authoring record does not supply an independent review or select a Claim assessment.

## The root restriction missing from the distinct-atom models

The [previous two-unit-atom models](../claims/Yin_C_G0882_v7.md#statement) restored two individual weights but did not require their residual quadratic to have the signs of the actual quotient after mandatory roots were removed.
For an actual input choose the \(n-1\) mandatory real roots as in the complete-root pairing, and set \(Z=Q/\prod(t-u_i)\).
The new [exact sign argument](../claims/Yin_C_G0882_v8.md#quadratic-signs) gives \(Z(b_j)>0\) at every pole and \(Z(-2)>0\).
Its root-count proof is independent of which root is chosen inside each mandatory interval and retains all multiplicities.
Consequently two real remaining roots lie in one component of \(\mathbb R\setminus\{-2,b_n,\ldots,b_1\}\).

This additional condition rules out the five particular distinct rational atom pairs stored for C882v7.
All ten atoms are in \((-2,0)\), and the separation within each recorded pair is at least \(171/4096>1/25\).
Every bounded component between \(-2\) and \(b_1\) has width at most \(4\pi/19683<16/19683<1/1000\).
Thus each stored pair crosses an actual pole, where its monic quadratic is negative, and cannot be the actual remaining quadratic.
This does not refute C882v7, whose exact claim explicitly concerns a relaxation without these pole signs.
It identifies a concrete additional constraint that its particular witnesses fail.

The [complete location argument](../claims/Yin_C_G0882_v8.md#root-location) also accounts for the two exterior components and the nonreal case.
Under \(M(P)\le R\), the left exterior separation is at most \(R+R^{-1}-2\), while the right exterior separation is at most \(R+R^{-1}-b_1\).
The left exterior component is impossible at \(R=1\).
A nonreal conjugate pair shares its real part and has imaginary height at most \(\sqrt R-1/\sqrt R\).
Double roots are allowed, including coincidence with a chosen root; strict positivity only forbids collisions with the poles or \(-2\).

## The fixed 129-position common-location experiment

The next test fixed both atomic weights at one and required their locations to coincide at
\[
                      s_j=-2+j/32,\qquad j=0,\ldots,128.
\]
For each of the five triples, the LP varied only the 256 occupancies in \([0,1]\).
It used the same seven nonconstant trace equations and the same cut bracket as C882v6; no conductor, degree or further moment was added.
The authorized stopping rule was the first sign-admissible exact model in each group, 645 LPs in total, or 60 seconds.

The actual tested prefixes and outcomes were:

| \((O,V,W)\) | Tested grid indices | LP count | Retained \(s\) | Extra exact but inadmissible hit |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| \((10,18,27)\) | 0 through 34 | 35 | \(-15/16\) | None |
| \((11,20,33)\) | 0 through 33 | 34 | \(-31/32\) | None |
| \((11,20,34)\) | 0 through 33 | 34 | \(-31/32\) | \(s_{32}=-1\) |
| \((11,21,36)\) | 0 through 30 | 31 | \(-17/16\) | None |
| \((11,21,37)\) | 0 through 30 | 31 | \(-17/16\) | None |

The total was 165 LPs: 159 numerical infeasibility reports and six numerical positive candidates, each of the latter passing exact occupancy-control verification.
The \(s=-1\) candidate had to be discarded because \(b_{6561}=2\cos(2\pi/3)=-1\), giving \(Z_s(b_{6561})=0\).
This was an exact model for the weaker moment system but not for the newly restored strict pole signs.
The same group then passed at its next prescribed grid point \(-31/32\), requiring one additional LP rather than a new search window.
No continuity perturbation or off-grid location was needed.

No group exhausted all 129 positions, and the untested suffixes are not reported as covered.
Numerical infeasibility at the tested points is not an exact exclusion of those points, a continuous interval, or the original integer branch.
The useful retained result is the five exact positive certificates, not the numerical failure reports.

## Exact certificates and the remaining gap

The fixed [J151 program](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0151/diagonal_atom_scan.py) now has a standard-library default verifier and a separate optional `--explore` mode for that bounded grid scan.
Its `MODELS` table stores the five locations, fixed-one occupancy ranges and seven free occupancy columns per group.
For each group, all eight degree-seven Bernstein controls across the entire cut bracket are solved as exact \(7\times7\) rational systems.
All forty systems pass direct substitution, and every free control lies strictly between \(1/10\) and \(999/1000\).
The default run also verifies \(s\ne-2\), nonvanishing of \(B(s)\) by the exact scaled recurrence, and exact coverage of the five residual triples.
The completed default run passed in about 0.45 seconds without a numerical solver.

The [complete mathematical statement and proof](../claims/Yin_C_G0882_v8.md#diagonal-certificate) show that these controls give, for every cut \(c\) in the inherited interval and every real polynomial \(f\) of degree at most seven,
\[
        \mathcal D(f)+f(c)=2f(s)-\int_{-2}^2w_c(t)f'(t)\,dt,
        \qquad 0\le w_c\le1.
\]
Each \(Z_s=(t-s)^2\) has the required strict pole and endpoint signs, has two coincident real roots in one permitted component, and has unit-circle reciprocal lifts.
Thus restoring the quadratic signs and even zero exceptional-root separation still leaves exact models in this moment-and-occupancy relaxation.

The decisive remaining distinction is between fractional cell occupancy and the actual collection of root-to-pole intervals, together with the global integral polynomial and resultant conditions.
In fact the retained \(s\) are nonintegral rationals, so they cannot themselves be roots of a monic integral \(Q\); the certificate does not claim otherwise.
No original residual branch is proved realizable or impossible.
Continuation must use further original structure if it is to exclude these models; this finite experiment alone does not prescribe or execute a larger search.
