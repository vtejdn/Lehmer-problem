### 1. A complete integer recurrence classification

First use the degree-two seed P_1=t^2-t-1.
Let H=sum_j h_j t^j be any integer polynomial, extended by h_j=0 outside its finite support.
Its product coefficients are

    (P_1 H)_j=h_(j-2)-h_(j-1)-h_j.                  (3)

Then coefficient height ht(P_1 H)<=1 holds if and only if all three following conditions hold:

* every h_j belongs to{-1,0,1};
* consecutive coefficients cannot both be nonzero;
* if h_j and h_(j+2) are both nonzero, their signs are the same.

Here is a proof of necessity which excludes ALL unbounded integer excursions, rather than scanning a finite box.
Write a=h_(j-2), b=h_(j-1), c=h_j and e=a-b-c in{-1,0,1}, so c=a-b-e.
If a and b are nonzero with opposite signs, c has the sign of a and |c|>=|a|+|b|-1>=1.
Thus the next pair (b,c) again consists of opposite nonzero signs.
This alternating-sign region is forward invariant and can never return to the zero pair required beyond the end of H.

Start from(0,0) before the support.
Consider the five states

    (0,0), (0,1), (1,0), (0,-1), (-1,0).             (4)

Every allowed transition out of(0,0) remains in this set.
From(0,s), s=+/-1, the possible next coefficients are0,-s,-2s; the latter two immediately enter the invariant alternating region.
From(s,0), the possibilities are0,s,2s.
The last produces(0,2s), whose next coefficient necessarily has the opposite nonzero sign to2s and therefore enters that invariant region.
Every exit from the five states consequently prevents finite termination, even when coefficients outside the five states are allowed to have arbitrary size.
A finitely supported admissible H stays in(4).
This proves the first two conditions.
From(s,0) the next nonzero coefficient in a returning path can only be s, giving the third.

Conversely these three conditions make |a-b-c|<=1 at every triple: if b is nonzero then a=c=0; if b=0 and a,c are both nonzero they have the same sign and cancel; if at most one of a,c is nonzero the assertion is immediate.
This proves the complete classification in both directions, with all initial and final zero coefficients included.

For P_r, write uniquely

    H(t)=sum_(a=0)^(r-1) t^a H_a(t^r).

Multiplication by P_r acts separately on these residue classes.
Thus the same three conditions apply independently to the coefficients of each H_a.
This is a polynomial identity over Z, without passing to a fractional module or assuming field independence for an integer coefficient calculation.

### 2. Exact integer lift counts and their sign weights

Let u be a binary polynomial of degree<N.
In each residue class modulo r, read its coefficients as a finite zero-one string.
If any such string has adjacent ones, then a_P(u)=0 by Section1.
Otherwise let s(u)=wt(U) and let c(u) be the total number of nonempty clusters obtained by connecting two occupied positions when their exponents differ by exactly2r.
Distinct clusters in one residue string are separated by at least two zero positions.
Define c(0)=0.

For such an admissible support, every cluster must have one common sign, and different clusters may choose signs independently.
There are exactly2^c(u) integer quotients H.
Moreover

    a_P(u)=2^c(u),
    wt(GU)=s(u)+2c(u),
    a_P(u)2^(-wt(GU))=2^(-s(u)-c(u)).               (5)

For the weight identity, a cluster of l occupied coefficients at indices0,2,...,2l-2 in a residue string multiplies 1+t+t^2 modulo2 to a polynomial supported at0,2l and the l odd indices1,3,...,2l-1.
Its weight is l+2.
The output supports of distinct clusters are disjoint, including when their starting coefficients are separated by exactly three steps.
Different residue classes are disjoint as well.
Summing proves the second identity, and the others follow from Section1.
At u=0 all three conventions give the single zero quotient with weight one.

These are genuine nonlocal lifts: a cluster may have arbitrarily many occupied positions and length, while there are exactly two sign choices for the entire cluster.
The resulting product still has coefficient height one.
Their sign probabilities have not been discarded; an l-term quotient cluster contributes2^(-l-1), rather than merely being counted as an allowed binary vector.

### 3. A weighted deficit for EVERY actual selector

The rows of F=1+t^r are the equations u_j+u_(j-r)=0 at selected indices, with u=0 outside the N internal positions.
For each residue class modulo r this is a path: consecutive internal vertices are joined by the selected equations, and the first and last have a possible selected edge to a fixed zero boundary.
An empty residue string has no internal vertex and at most a zero row, which imposes no condition.

Connected components touching a fixed zero boundary are forced to zero.
Every other component contributes exactly one freely chosen bit, constant along that component.
Their total number is precisely k_A.
Let J_A consist of the internal vertices which alone form a free component, and put s_A=|J_A|<=k_A.
If an actual integer lift contributes to(2), its binary u has no adjacent ones within a residue string by Section1.
Hence it must be zero on every free component of length at least two: taking the constant value one there would violate that condition.
Boundary components are already zero.
All nonzero quotient-parity coefficients therefore lie in J_A.

For any permitted subset B of J_A, equation(5) gives weight2^(-|B|-c(B))<=2^(-|B|).
Dropping its remaining restrictions only increases the sum.
Consequently

    W_P(A)<=sum_(B subset J_A)2^(-|B|)
           =(3/2)^s_A <=(3/2)^k_A,
    Q_n(A)<=2^(-(k_A-s_A))(3/4)^s_A
             <=(3/4)^k_A.                           (6)

This holds for every selector A and every n>=2r, not merely with high probability over selectors.
In L227's notation it proves its actual integer-lift deficit with

There is an explicit all-length bound with every degree cost.
Let N_a be the number of internal positions in residue class a, so sum_a N_a=N.
Its path has N_a+1 potentially observed edges.
If m_a is the number of unobserved edges, its number of free components is max(m_a-1,0), including N_a=0.
The independent selector makes m_a binomial with parameters N_a+1 and1-theta, independently across residue classes.

### 4. An exact three-state formula for the observation experiment

       =(1+theta)(c_theta')^N Z_N(z)-theta^(N+1).   (10)

To prove it, the N+1 rows of F=1+t are the edges of a path with N internal vertices and both endpoints fixed to zero.

    E_A[2^(rank L_A) 1_(u in ker L_A)]
        =(1+theta)^(N+1-|S|)(1-theta)^|S|.          (11)

The complete integer classification supplies a finite expression for Z_N.

    Z_N(z)=[1,0,0] T(z)^N v(z).                    (12)

Here is the full weight check.

After N freely chosen quotient coefficients, the next two coefficients must be zero.
Their combined terminal weights are respectively1,z/4,1/2, giving v.
