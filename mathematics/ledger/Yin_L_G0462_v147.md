# Testing cyclotomic multiples by root multiplicities at the support prime

2026-11-18T05:46.

<a id="multiplicities"></a>
## A necessary condition on each order of roots of unity

Consider the [prime-residue coverage question](Yin_L_G0462_v146.md#coverage) for a monic polynomial \(P\in\mathbb Z[x]\) with constant term \(\pm1\).
Fix a prime \(p\).
For \(n\) prime to \(p\), let \(E_n\subset\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\) be the set of residues of the multiplicities of \(\overline P=P\bmod p\) at **all** primitive \(n\)-th roots in \(\overline{\mathbb F}_p\).
A primitive root at which \(\overline P\) does not vanish contributes zero.
Let \(w(E_n)\) be the least positive \(k\) for which a translate of \(\{0,\ldots,k-1\}\) in \(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\) contains \(E_n\).

Every product \(C\) of cyclotomic polynomials satisfies
\[
       \#\{a\bmod p:[x^a]CP\ne0\}\ \ge\ \max_{(n,p)=1} w(E_n),
\]
where the set on the left ranges over all nonnegative exponents with nonzero integer coefficient.
Only orders of roots of \(\overline P\) can give a bound greater than one.
No bound on the degree or the multiplicities of \(C\) enters the test.

To see this, write the nonzero reduction \(F=\overline{CP}\) in its \(k'\) nonzero residue classes as \(F(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{k'}x^{r_i}V_i(x^p)\), \(0\le r_i<p\).
Monicity ensures \(F\ne0\), and reduction can only decrease the number of occupied classes.
Fix \(\xi\in\overline{\mathbb F}_p^\times\), let \(\lambda=\min_i\operatorname{ord}_{\xi^p}V_i\), and write \(V_i(Y)=(Y-\xi^p)^\lambda W_i(Y)\) over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_p\).
At least one \(W_i(\xi^p)\ne0\).
After dividing \(F\) by \((x-\xi)^{p\lambda}\), the coefficient of \(y^j\) in the Taylor expansion at \(x=\xi+y\), for \(0\le j<k'\le p\), is
\[
       \xi^{-j}\sum_{i=1}^{k'}\binom{r_i}{j}\xi^{r_i}W_i(\xi^p).
\]
Indeed \((\xi+y)^p=\xi^p+y^p\), so the nonconstant terms from \(W_i\) enter only from degree \(p\) onward.
The binomial coefficient polynomials in \(r_i\) have respective degrees \(j\) and nonzero leading coefficients \(1/j!\) for these \(j\).
Evaluation at distinct \(r_i\) therefore gives an invertible Vandermonde matrix up to invertible changes of basis.
The displayed coefficients cannot all vanish.
Consequently the multiplicity of \(F\) at \(\xi\) is congruent modulo \(p\) to an integer in \(\{0,\ldots,k'-1\}\).

For each fixed \(n\) the reduction of \(C\) adds the same multiplicity \(c_n\) at all primitive \(n\)-th roots.
Indeed \(\overline\Phi_b\) has simple roots of exact order \(b\) for \(p\nmid b\), by the separable factorization of \(x^b-1\).
For \(a\ge1\), the identity \(\Phi_{p^a b}(x)=\Phi_b(x^{p^a})/\Phi_b(x^{p^{a-1}})\) gives \(\overline\Phi_{p^a b}=\overline\Phi_b^{\,p^{a-1}(p-1)}\).
It follows that \(E_n+c_n\subseteq\{0,\ldots,k'-1\}\), as required.
Conversely, any finitely supported choice of the translations \(c_n\bmod p\) can be realized at once by multiplying by \(\prod_n\Phi_n^{\,c_n}\), using representatives from \(0\) through \(p-1\).
This realizes the translations of multiplicities alone.
It provides no control on the number of nonzero coefficient rows after multiplication.

## A reciprocal irreducible family requiring linearly many classes

For every sufficiently large odd prime \(p\), choose the odd integer \(e\) nearest \(p/2\) and define
\[
 Q(Y)=(Y-3)^e+p,\qquad
 P(x)=x^eQ(x+x^{-1})=(x^2-3x+1)^e+px^e .
\]
This \(P\) is a monic reciprocal irreducible unit polynomial of degree \(2e\).
Its constant coefficient is one and its linear coefficient is \(-3e\) for \(e>1\), so the gcd of its support differences is one.
Irreducibility of \(Q\) follows from Eisenstein at \(p\) after translation.
Its real root \(\beta=3-p^{1/e}\) lies in \((-2,2)\) for large \(p\), since \(e=p/2+O(1)\) and \(p^{1/e}\to1\) from above.
Over this real embedding of \(\mathbb Q(\beta)\) the discriminant of \(X^2-\beta X+1\) is negative.
Hence the quadratic is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q(\beta)\) and its roots have absolute degree \(2e\), proving irreducibility of \(P\).

Modulo \(p>5\), the two distinct roots of \(x^2-3x+1\) are reciprocal, have a common multiplicative order \(n(p)\), and both have multiplicity \(e\) in \(\overline P\).
Their orders tend to infinity as the primes tend to infinity.
In fact a bound \(n(p)\le B\) forces \(p\) to divide the fixed integer \(\prod_{j=1}^B\operatorname{Res}(x^2-3x+1,x^j-1)\), which is nonzero since the two real roots are off the unit circle.
In particular \(\varphi(n(p))>2\) for all sufficiently large \(p\).
Some primitive \(n(p)\)-th root then has multiplicity zero and \(E_{n(p)}=\{0,e\}\).
The shortest cyclic interval containing these residues has \(1+\min(e,p-e)=(p+1)/2\) classes.
Thus even after arbitrary cyclotomic multiplication, at least \((p+1)/2\) classes remain occupied at this prime.

The measures in this construction grow with \(p\).
At least \((e-1)/2\) roots of \(Q\) have real part at least \(3\), since they have the form \(3+p^{1/e}\exp((2j+1)\pi i/e)\), \(0\le j<e\).
If such a trace is \(x+x^{-1}\) and the larger root modulus is \(R\), then \(3\le R+R^{-1}\), so \(R\ge(3+\sqrt5)/2\).
Consequently
\[
           m(P)\ \ge\ \frac{e-1}{2}\log\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2}.
\]
The example rules out low row counts at a specified growing prime under irreducibility, reciprocity and the support-gcd condition alone.

## Which primes the test can distinguish

At every prime \(p\nmid\operatorname{disc}(P)\) for a monic separable \(P\), the reduction is squarefree.
All \(E_n\) then lie in \(\{0,1\}\) and the test gives at most two classes.
Thus the possibility of choosing another prime is essential even for the displayed family.
For [coverage of a sequence approaching measure one](Yin_L_G0462_v146.md#coverage), large \(w(E_n)\) can discard a particular prime before a search for a multiplier.
Small \(w(E_n)\) leaves both the existence of a multiplier with few classes and its required [total term count](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v343.md#statement) unresolved.
