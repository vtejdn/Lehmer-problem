## 3. Exceptional unit count is insufficient; their actual mass and energy are decisive

**Proposition 2.** Such a bundle satisfies

    nu>=[1-(1+t)/S]_+,
    E_exc<=1/S,
    e_exc<=1/(S-1-t) whenever S>1+t.              (7)

    t>=S-1-1/E_*.

*Proof.* For a given inverse orbit, its weighted trace contribution is a^2c+b^2c^-1.

    a^2c_j+b^2/c_j
      =2ab+(a c_j-b)^2/c_j.                      (12)
