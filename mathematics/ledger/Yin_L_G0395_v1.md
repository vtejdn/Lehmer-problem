## 4. A bounded-cover induction from individual support is false

The following proof-scheme obstruction is kept separate from the original density-capture question.

There are dyadic intervals(Y,2Y] with Y arbitrarily large containing an unbounded number k of primes.
This follows from the elementary [L17 prime-reciprocal divergence](Yin_L_G0017_v1.md#discriminant-divergence): if every dyadic interval above some point contained at most a fixed M primes, their reciprocal sum would be bounded by a finite initial part plus sum_j M/2^j, a contradiction.
For each requested k choose an interval with at least k primes and retain ALL its primes.
Along such a sequence their counts k tend to infinity and Y tends to infinity.
No quantitative lower bound or prime-distribution asymptotic is used.

For Y>4, let S be these primes, let A=max S and X=A^2, and take full weights w_q=phi(q)=q-1.
Every order has individual support1, every selected order is at most A, and every pair has lcm at most A^2=X.
Hence the oriented star anchored at A is ALL S, with pair density1.

But an integer n<=X<=4Y^2 cannot be divisible by THREE distinct selected primes: their product exceeds Y^3>4Y^2.
Every such integer covers at most TWO of the k selected labels.
Any divisor-set cover of the full star therefore uses at least ceil(k/2) different times.
This tends to infinity, disproving every bound depending only on the individual support count, independently of the chosen weights.

    H_X/W<=4Y/[k(Y-1)]->0.                            (10)
