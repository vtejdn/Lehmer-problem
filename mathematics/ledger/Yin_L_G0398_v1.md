## 1. Complete reduction to the old mass theorem

Let alpha be an actual nontorsion algebraic integer unit of degree d and positive TOTAL logarithmic measure m=d h_abs(alpha).
Let L be its normal closure, G=Gal(L/Q), X its d roots, p a rational prime, Pfrak a prime of O_L above p and sigma a residue-Frobenius lift in its decomposition group.
Set x=alpha, y=sigma(alpha), and use the exact C162 definitions

    Omega=G.(x,y), |Omega|=s d,
    b=#{z in X:(z,sigma z) belongs to Omega},
    gamma=b/d,
    Kpair=Q(x,y), rho=x^p/y,
    E=Q(rho), D=[E:Q].

L397 gives [Kpair:Q]=s d, E contained in Kpair, and a constant fiber size

    f=sd/D

for the map Omega to the D distinct conjugates of rho, (z,w) maps to z^p/w.
Every ratio is an algebraic integer unit.
For completeness, if rho^v=1 then sigma(alpha)^v=alpha^(pv); iteration through the finite order q of sigma gives alpha^((p^q-1)v)=1, a contradiction.
Thus rho is nontorsion, so its primitive minimal polynomial P_rho is monic irreducible with unit constant term and positive total measure.

For each of the b selected Frobenius edges, sigma z reduces to z^p at Pfrak, and both entries are units.
Its ratio therefore reduces to1.
At most f selected pairs can map to any one distinct conjugate of rho.
Consequently at least

    b/f=(gamma/s)D

distinct original rho-conjugates reduce to1, with the actual integer count at least the ceiling of this expression.
There is no distinct-reduction hypothesis: different rho-conjugates may all reduce to the same element1.
Their multiplicity is precisely retained in the reduction of the monic minimal polynomial P_rho.
In particular the linear factor T-1 of that reduction has multiplicity at least (gamma/s)D, so

    W_(p,1)(P_rho)/D>=delta, delta=gamma/s.             (2)

Here W_(p,1) is L205's total degree weight of ALL linear reduction factors, including their polynomial multiplicities.

No assertion about the ramification degree of the ambient splitting field is made or required.

The two coordinate projections of Omega onto X have constant fiber size s.
