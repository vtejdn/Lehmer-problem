# The squarefree part of the reduced trace controls the final derivative remainder

<a id="obstruction"></a>
## An individual bound on each of the two coprime rows

Use the [two reciprocal witnesses](Yin_L_G0462_v206.md#statement) for the same input \(P,\alpha,p\), with \(d=2m\ge4,\ h=m(P),\ \beta=\alpha^p,\ q=\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\) and \(L_l=(d/l)(ph+\log(2dp))\), \(1\le l<m\).
Here \(m(B)\) denotes logarithmic Mahler measure, \(h_{\rm W}\) denotes absolute logarithmic Weil height, and \(N_+\) is the norm from \(\mathbb Q(q)\).
For each **final primitive coprime row** let \(u,v\) be its [integral traces](Yin_L_G0462_v206.md#remainders) and \(U_0\in\mathbb Z[t]\) the representative of \(u\ne0\) of degree \(r<m\).
Write \(D=v+u-2(2\epsilon-q)U_0'(q)\) in odd span, and \(D=v-U_0'(q)\) in either even chart.
In particular the positive even chart uses the remainder after removing \(q^2-4\).
Let \(S_u\) be the product of the **distinct primitive irreducible factors** of \(X^rU_0(X+X^{-1})\), chosen with positive leading coefficients, with empty product one.
Then on each of the two rows, simultaneously with \(m(R_i)\ll L_l,\ D\ne0,\ v/u\notin\mathbb Q\), one has
\[
  h_{\rm W}(v/u)\ll L_l,\qquad
  \frac{\log|N_+u|}{m},\ \frac{\log|N_+v|}{m}\ll L_l,\qquad
  \frac{\log|N_+D|}{m}\ll L_l+m(S_u).                                \tag{1}
\]
All constants are absolute.
In the [fourth-logarithmic prime window](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v363.md#statement), take \(l=\lfloor m/2\rfloor\).
Along any supplied sequence \(d\to\infty,\ h\to0\), each final row with \(m(S_u)=o(p)\) has both \(m(R_i)/p\to0\) and \(\log|N_+D|/(pm)\to0\).
In particular this holds for entirely cyclotomic \(S_u\), with unrestricted orders, multiplicities in the original trace polynomial, and scalar content.
Conversely a fixed positive lower bound for the latter normalized norm on a sequence of these rows forces \(m(S_u)\gg p\) there, with the implied lower constant depending on that bound.

## Logarithmic derivatives after removing arbitrary factors

Suppose an integral polynomial \(B\) of degree \(n\ge1\) vanishes at each distinct root of an integral polynomial \(T\) of positive degree \(e\).
Put \(E=X\,d/dX\) and \(C=B(ET/T)\).
For each primitive irreducible factor \(f\) of \(T\) of multiplicity \(k_f\), its contribution is \(k_f Xf'(B/f)\), integral by Gauss's lemma.
For any root \(z\) of \(T\), each coefficient of \(B/(X-z)\) has magnitude at most \(n\|B\|_\infty\).
Indeed synthetic division from the leading coefficient sums at most \(n\) terms with powers of \(z\) when \(|z|\le1\), and synthetic division from the constant coefficient uses inverse powers when \(|z|>1\).
Summing these quotients with multiplicities gives \(\deg C\le n\) and \(\|C\|_\infty\le en\|B\|_\infty\).
Thus at any algebraic \(x\) with \(B(x)\ne0\),
\[
 h_{\rm W}((ET/T)(x))
 \le n h_{\rm W}(x)+\log\big((n+1)\max(1,en)\|B\|_\infty\big).       \tag{2}
\]
Indeed use the projective coordinates \([C(x):B(x)]\), bounding at each infinite place by the coefficient sums and at each finite place by integrality.
For constant \(T\) the logarithmic derivative is zero.

Each final row \(A\) divides an integral polynomial \(B\) of degree at most \(4d\), with \(B(\beta)\ne0\) and \(\log\|B\|_\infty\ll L_l\).
Take the original short row of [L206](Yin_L_G0462_v206.md#division), or its formal reversal if the orientation changed.
Division by the fixed form, a monomial and scalar content gives precisely the required divisibility.
The reversal is nonzero at \(\beta\) by conjugacy under inversion.
Apply (2) with \(T=A,\ x=\beta\) and \(h_{\rm W}(\beta)=ph/d\).
It gives \(h_{\rm W}(w)\ll L_l\) for \(w=2p(EA/A)(\beta)-J\), where \(J\le4pd+1\) is the final span.
Set \(\Delta=\alpha-\alpha^{-1}\).
In odd span \(v/u=w(1-\epsilon\alpha)/(1+\epsilon\alpha)\).
In even span \(v/u=w/(2\Delta)\) for \(\epsilon=-1\) and \(v/u=(\Delta w/2-q)/(q^2-4)\) for \(\epsilon=1\).
Since \(h_{\rm W}(\alpha)=h/d\), the height inequalities for sums, products and inverses give the first bound of (1).
The [first evaluation bound](Yin_L_G0462_v206.md#statement) gives the stated norm bound on \(u\), since \(A(\beta)\) is \(u\) times a unit power of \(\alpha\) times \(1-\epsilon\alpha,\ 1,\ \Delta/2\), respectively.
Each denominator introduced when solving for \(u\) is a nonzero algebraic integer up to the displayed factor of two.
For nonzero \(z\in\mathbb Q(q)\), \(\log|N_+z|/m\le h_{\rm W}(z)\), so \(v=u(v/u)\) gives the norm bound on \(v\).

## A short multiple of the squarefree trace polynomial

Let \(S\in\mathbb Z[X]\) be primitive and squarefree of degree \(s\le d\), with \(S(\alpha)\ne0\).
There exists an integral multiple \(B\) of \(S\) of degree at most \(N=2d\) with \(B(\alpha)\ne0\) and
\[
                   \log\|B\|_\infty\ll\log(2d)+m(S).                  \tag{3}
\]
For constant \(S\) take \(B=1\).
For each primitive irreducible \(f\mid S\) with positive leading coefficient \(a_f\) and degree \(s_f\), evaluate the polynomials with coefficients in \(\{0,\ldots,K\}\) of degree at most \(N\) at the roots of \(f\).
At a root \(z\) the values are bounded by \((N+1)K\max(1,|z|)^N\).
Partition each real coordinate and both coordinates for one member of each complex pair into intervals of length \(a_f^{-N/s_f}/3\).
Two polynomials in the same box have difference \(V\) divisible by \(f\).
Otherwise the nonzero integer \(|\operatorname{Res}(f,V)|=a_f^{\deg V}\prod_{f(z)=0}|V(z)|\) would be smaller than one.
Over all factors the number of boxes is at most
\[
              (K+1)^s \exp\{s\log(12(N+1))+N\,m(S)\}.
\]
Here the coordinate scales contribute \(a_f^N\) for each factor, accounting also for nonmonic \(S\).
Choose the least positive integer \(K\) for which \((K+1)^d>\exp\{s\log(12(N+1))+N\,m(S)\}\).
Some box contains more than \((K+1)^{N+1-s-d}\) points.
Their differences from one of them are integral multiples of \(S\) of coefficient magnitudes at most \(K\).
If they all vanished at \(\alpha\), the original points would lie in a translate of the space of multiples of \(SP\), of dimension \(N+1-s-d\) by coprimality.
Projection to that many independent coefficient coordinates bounds the number of points in such a translate by \((K+1)^{N+1-s-d}\).
This proves (3).

Take \(T=X^rU_0(X+X^{-1})\) and \(S=S_u\), so \(s\le2r<d,\ S(\alpha)\ne0\).
The multiple in (3) covers all the roots needed in (2), even when \(T\) has high multiplicities.
At \(\alpha\) one has
\[
            (ET/T)(\alpha)=r+\Delta\, U_0'(q)/u .
\]
Thus \(h_{\rm W}(U_0'(q)/u)\ll \log(2d)+h+m(S_u)\), also valid for constant \(U_0\).
Substitute this into the expression for \(D/u\) in the applicable chart and use \(\log|N_+D|/m\le\log|N_+u|/m+h_{\rm W}(D/u)\).
This proves (1).

## Fixed trace norms and a growing squarefree measure

Take \(q=1+\sqrt2,\ q'=1-\sqrt2\), and let \(\alpha>1\) solve \(\alpha+\alpha^{-1}=q\).
Its minimal polynomial is \(P=X^4-2X^3+X^2-2X+1\), since \(q'^2-4<0\) excludes a square discriminant in \(\mathbb Q(q)\).
The two real reciprocal conjugates and the two nontorsion circle conjugates stay distinct after taking fifth powers, so take \(p=5\).
Fix the positive odd-span sign and take a positive integer \(b\) annihilating the finite quotient \(R^2/\Gamma\) for \(R=\mathbb Z[q]\) and the [jet lattice of L199](Yin_L_G0462_v199.md#lattice).
For every \(j\ge1\) there is a primitive coprime row of degree \(2d\) realizing \(u=bq^j,\ v=bq^{j+1}\), by the lift and coprimality argument there.
Both absolute trace norms equal \(b^2\) and the irrational slope equals \(q\).
Write \(q^j=a_j+c_jq\) with integers \(a_j,c_j\), so \(c_j=(q^j-q'^j)/(q-q')\).
The derivative remainder satisfies
\[
                 D_j/b=(q+1)q^j-2(2-q)c_j.
\]
The two real values of \(D_j\), each divided by the same positive real number \(bq^j\), tend to \(q+1-2(2-q)/(q-q')>0\) and \(-2(2-q')/(q-q')<0\), respectively.
Hence \(\log|N_+D_j|/2=j\log q+O(1)\).
The integers \(a_j,c_j\) are coprime since \(q^j\) is a unit.
As \(-a_j/c_j\to q'\in(-2,2)\), the primitive polynomial \(c_jX^2+a_jX+c_j\) eventually has distinct circle roots.
It is then the squarefree part up to sign, with \(m(S_u)=\log c_j=j\log q+O(1)\).
This realizes the order of the squarefree term with fixed input, fixed slope and fixed trace norms.
Its lifts have unrestricted cofactor cost.
For the two low-cofactor choices on a varying input, an upper bound \(m(S_u)=o(p)\) remains open.
