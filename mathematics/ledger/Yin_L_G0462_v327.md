# Relative traces of adjacent record measures and the cost of normalizing them

2026-11-25.
Take consecutive strict degree records \(w_1,w_2\) of degrees \(d_1<d_2\) in a sequence on which the unrestricted gap fails.
Write \(A=M(w_1)\), \(B=M(w_2)\), \(m_1=\log A\), \(b=\log B<m_1\), \(K=\mathbb Q(A)\), \(F=K(B)\) and \(q=[F:K]\).
Write \(N_i\) for the rational splitting field of \(w_i\).
All asymptotic assertions concern sufficiently late rows, with \(m_1\to0\).
Use \(h\) for absolute logarithmic height and \(m(x)=[\mathbb Q(x):\mathbb Q]h(x)\) for total logarithmic Mahler measure.

<a id="filtration"></a>
## The minimum already attainable in the earlier field

Every relative trace from \(F\) to \(K\) lies in \(N_1\).
At any fixed positive ceiling containing the record measures, \(N_1\) is contained in the compositum of the splitting fields of inputs through compressed degree \(d_1\).
The [bounded-measure filtration](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v87.md#filtration) shows that every positive total measure below this ceiling in that compositum already occurs at degree at most \(d_1\).
Consequently no element of \(K\) can have positive total measure below \(m_1\), regardless of its own degree.
The same restriction persists after the solvable and bounded-degree tower steps specified in that theorem.
This settles the proposed use of relative traces to improve the earlier record.
The estimates below distinguish the actual sizes of particular additive constructions within that restriction.

<a id="denominators"></a>
## The finite denominator of the normalized trace

For an element \(z\in K\) let \(\|z\|_2^2\) be the mean of \(|\sigma z|^2\) over all embeddings of \(K\) into \(\mathbb C\).
Set \(\epsilon=b+\log(2q)/q\) and \(V_j=q^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}_{F/K}(B^j)\) for nonzero integers \(j\).
Then
\[
          \|V_j\|_2\ll\sqrt{|j|\epsilon}\exp\bigl(|j|(b+1/q)\bigr).
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
Indeed all conjugates of the exterior product \(B\) lie in modulus between \(B^{-1}\) and \(B\), by the [radial bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v159.md#moments).
For \(j>0\) apply the [relative power-series energy estimate](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v165.md#energy) to the relative conjugates of \(B\) with damping \(\rho=\exp(-1/q)\).
There the mean energy is \(O(\epsilon)\) by the integral nonzero relative discriminant.
Its term at \(j\) is \(\rho^{2j}e^{-2bj}|\sigma V_j|^2/j\).
For \(j<0\) apply the same disk and discriminant calculation to \(B^{-1}\), a unit generating the same relative field.
The [relative-degree growth](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v165.md#rows) gives \(\log(2q)/q=o(b^k)\) for each fixed \(k>0\).

Consider a finite sum \(V=\sum_{j\ne0} f_jV_j\) with \(f_j\in K\), possibly depending on the row.
Writing \(H_j=\max_\sigma|\sigma f_j|\), (1) gives
\[
       \delta:=\|V\|_2\ll \sqrt{\epsilon}
            \sum_{j\ne0}H_j\sqrt{|j|}\exp\bigl(|j|(b+1/q)\bigr).
                                                               \tag{2}
\]
For \(V\ne0\), let \(\mathfrak d(V)\) be its denominator ideal, with exponent \(\max(0,-v_\mathfrak p(V))\) at each prime ideal.
Taking the geometric mean over embeddings and using the numerator ideal norm at least one gives
\[
        [K:\mathbb Q]^{-1}\log N\mathfrak d(V)\ \ge\ \log(1/\delta).
\]
Indeed \(|N_{K/\mathbb Q} V|^{1/[K:\mathbb Q]}\le\delta\).
For any integral \(t\), the finite denominator of \(t-V\) is exactly that of \(V\).
Its normalized log norm is unchanged by extending the field and multiplying by any algebraic unit.
Thus whenever the right side of (2) tends to zero, every nonzero such \(V\) gives \(h(u(t-V))\ge\log(1/\delta)\) for every algebraic unit \(u\).
This includes \(V=q^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}_{F/K}(A^aB)\) whenever \(A^a\) stays bounded, and each single power \(j\) with \(|j|b\to0\) and bounded \(H_j\).
If the centered trace vanishes, its unscaled translate is simply \(t\).

<a id="scalings"></a>
## Arbitrary rational rescalings of the translate

Fix a rational integer \(t\ge2\) and suppose \(0<\delta<t/2\).
Put \(p=1-4\delta^2/t^2\).
For every nonzero rational \(r\), either \(x=r(t-V)\) is rational, or
\[
            h(x)\ \ge\ \frac{p}{2p+1}\log\frac{t}{8\delta}.             \tag{3}
\]
At least a fraction \(p\) of the embeddings have \(|\sigma V|\le t/2\), so \(h(x)\ge p\log(|r|t/2)\).
If \(x\) is nonrational choose an automorphism \(\tau\) of a normal closure with \(\tau x\ne x\).
The embedding norm in (2) is unchanged when calculated in the normal closure or after applying \(\tau\).
Since the constants cancel, \(\|x-\tau x\|_2\le 2|r|\delta\).
The product formula and the geometric mean bound imply \(h(x-\tau x)\ge-\log(2|r|\delta)\).
Also \(h(x-\tau x)\le2h(x)+\log 2\).
Eliminating \(|r|\) gives (3).
As \(\delta\to0\), the absolute heights of all nonrational rescalings therefore diverge uniformly.
A rational rescaling that is itself rational has either zero measure or positive total measure at least \(\log 2\), for nonzero outputs.
Since \(q(t-V)=\operatorname{Tr}_{F/K}(t-\sum_{j\ne0} f_j B^j)\), the same conclusion applies to every nonzero rational multiple of this translated input's ordinary relative trace.

<a id="centered"></a>
## Removing the constant and the exceptional nesting of the fields

For every \(y\in N_2^\times\) and integer \(a\ge1\), if \(A\notin N_2\) then
\[
            m(A^a y)\ \ge\
              a\sum_{A'\ {\rm over}\ N_2}\log^+|A'|
              \ \ge\ a m_1.                                            \tag{4}
\]
The sum uses the distinct relative conjugates of \(A\), including the chosen positive \(A\).
The earlier record's [space of conjugate classes](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v84.md#modules) modulo torsion is irreducible and is spanned by the conjugates of the nonzero class of \(A\).
Work in \(N_1N_2\) over \(\mathbb Q\).
The subgroup fixing \(N_2\) is normal, so its fixed subspace in the earlier module is either zero or the whole space.
In the latter case it fixes each class of an earlier source root, hence fixes each actual root by the record's separation modulo torsion.
This would fix \(A\), contrary to \(A\notin N_2\).
The average of the relative orbit of \([A]\) is therefore zero, and the numbers \(\log|A'|\) sum to zero.
The distinct relative conjugates of \(A\) still have distinct \(a\)-th powers, since its strict Perron property gives \(\mathbb Q(A^a)=\mathbb Q(A)\).
Thus the logarithms \(a\log|A'|+\log|y|\) are attached to distinct conjugates of \(A^ay\).
If \(\log|y|\ge0\), their total positive part is at least \(a\sum\log^+|A'|\).
If \(\log|y|\le0\), their total negative part in absolute value is at least the same quantity by the zero sum before translation.
The total Mahler measures of \(A^ay\) and its inverse coincide and bound those respective sums.
This proves (4) without a height or integrality assumption on \(y\).
For instance, every nonzero rational multiple of \(\operatorname{Tr}_{F/K}(B^j)\) lies in \(N_2\), being a sum of conjugates from that normal field.
In a row with \(A^a B\to t\), (4) bounds the total measure of the corresponding centered product from below by \(\log t-o(1)\).

In the exceptional case \(A\in N_2\), normality puts all conjugates of \(A\) in \(N_2\).
They span the earlier module, so fixing \(N_2\) fixes that module and all the actual earlier roots by the same separation.
Hence \(N_1\subseteq N_2\).
Write \(u_i,b_i\) for the number and size of the canonical inverse-pair blocks, with \(d_i=2u_i b_i\).
The two records are unmatched since their compressed degrees differ.
Apply the [single-input quotient theorem](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v279.md#quotients) to the later source with the earlier source as target.
The input size condition \(b_2>u_2\) holds eventually by the [block count bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v150.md#statement).
The earlier source lies in \(N_2\), and its large-degree label field has trivial solvable radical.
Since the sources are unmatched, that earlier label field lies in the later field of the action on the \(u_2\) blocks.
Its Galois group contains the independent \(u_1\) alternating groups on \(b_1\) labels.
Consequently \((b_1!/2)^{u_1}\) divides \(u_2!\).
Counting factors of two in the factorials, \(v_2(k!)=\sum_{l\ge1}\lfloor k/2^l\rfloor=k-s_2(k)\) with \(s_2(k)\) the binary digit sum.
It follows that
\[
 u_2\ge u_1\bigl(b_1-s_2(b_1)-1\bigr)+1=(1-o(1))d_1/2,\qquad
 u_2<16 b^2(29920\log(8d_2))^{36}.
\]
In particular nesting requires \(\log(8d_2)\gg(d_1/b^2)^{1/36}\).
The field filtration applies even at this rate of degree growth.
Neither these inequalities nor the trace estimates exclude the adjacent-record approach to the chosen target in the unrestricted gap problem.
