## 1. Exact finite theorem, including attainment of the least defect

Let Omega be a finite set with strictly positive probability pi and a reversible Markov kernel K.
Let S:Omega->Omega be an involution satisfying

    pi(Sx)=pi(x), K(Sx,Sy)=K(x,y).                     (1)

Write D_K(f)=<f,(I-K)f>_pi for real functions.
The involution permutes the complete communicating components.
Call C self-sign when S(C)=C, and put

    s=sum_(self-sign components C) pi(C).              (2)

This includes every such component, not merely one distinguished component or states fixed pointwise by S.
Every other component occurs in a distinct pair C,S(C) with equal original masses.
A function is odd if f(Sx)=-f(x).
Then E_pi f=0 and osc(f)=2||f||_infinity, unless f=0, when both sides vanish.
On a self-sign component its conditional mean is also zero.

For a self-sign C, let B_C be the least nonnegative coefficient such that

    E_(pi|C) f^2 <= B_C D_(K|C)(f)

for every odd function on C.
This coefficient is finite.
If the odd subspace is zero, take B_C=0.
Otherwise the conditional Dirichlet form has positive minimum on its odd unit sphere: zero energy forces a function to be constant on C, and an odd constant on a self-sign C is zero.
Compactness of that finite-dimensional sphere proves the positive minimum without an infinite-chain spectral assumption.
Let B_self=max_C B_C, or0 if there is no self-sign component.
Since complete components are closed, conditional component energies sum with their original masses to the full energy.
For every odd f, with a=||f||_infinity,

    Var_pi(f)=E_pi f^2
       <= B_self D_K(f)+(1-s)a^2
       =B_self D_K(f)+(1-s)osc(f)^2/4.                (3)

Only the self-sign components use an energy estimate.
On all exchanged component pairs the ENTIRE second moment, including within-component fluctuations, is bounded by their mass times a^2.
No mixing or spectral coefficient on those paired components is required.

Conversely, if s<1 choose one component from each exchanged pair, put f=+1 there and f=-1 on its partner, and put f=0 on every self-sign component.
This is an odd component-constant function with zero energy, variance1-s and oscillation2.
Hence any inequality

    Var_pi(f)<=B D_K(f)+epsilon osc(f)^2

for ALL odd f and some finite B>=0 requires epsilon>=(1-s)/4.
Equation(3) attains equality at that defect with a finite coefficient.
For s=1 the least NONNEGATIVE defect is0 and is also attained by(3), including the zero-dimensional odd case.
Thus this is a MINIMUM, not merely an unattained infimum:

    epsilon_odd,min=(1-s)/4.                          (4)

A selected subcollection of self-sign components with total original mass s_sel also gives(3) with1-s_sel and the maximum B_C only over that subcollection.

The odd restriction is substantively weaker than the all-function condition.
