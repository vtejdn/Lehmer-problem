# Archimedean energy on reciprocal inputs with small resultant

<a id="separation"></a>
## A reciprocal family with energy of order \(kp\)

On the [reciprocal factors at prescribed primes](Yin_L_G0462_v171.md#window), \(h\) stays bounded above and away from zero, and \(\log I_p/(pd)\to0\).
Write \(B\) for the absolute degree-\(k\) homogeneous row resultant and \(\mathcal D(g)\) for the [Dirichlet energy of the row maximum](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v379.md#identity), with \(k\) the maximum stripped row degree.
For every prescribed pair use the [short-denominator witnesses](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v378.md#rounding) for multiplicity two.
Put \(x=(\log p)/p+4\log I_p/(pd)\), and take \(j=\lceil d\sqrt{x}\rceil\) once \(0<x<1\).
In that construction \(\log J_2=dpx\), \(\deg V\le j\), \(k=\deg U=2d+\deg V\), and
\[
   |\operatorname{lc}U|\le3\|V_{\rm initial}\|_1\le6jJ_2^{1/j}.
\]
Here \(V_{\rm initial}\) precedes scalar content removal.
The polynomial \(Q\) is monic and the top term of \(U\) comes from \(wQ^2V_{\rm initial}\) with \(1\le w\le3\).
Thus \(\log|\operatorname{lc}U|/p\to0\) since \(\log d=o(p)\) and \(dx/j\le\sqrt x\).
The [finite resultant bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v378.md#statement) gives \(\log|\operatorname{Res}(U,V)|/(kp)\to0\) since \(j/d\to0\), \(h\) is bounded and the stripped degree sum is at most \(2k\).
The [homogeneous resultant formula](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v379.md#identity) now gives \(\log B/(kp)\to0\).
Nevertheless every such witness has \(m(R)/p\ge h/3\).
The [upper circle comparison](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v379.md#cofactor) and the Dirichlet identity imply
\[
                  \frac{\mathcal D(g)}{2kp}\ge \frac h3-o(1).
\]
This realizes a positive archimedean contribution on actual reciprocal inputs with negligible homogeneous resultant, uniformly in the prescribed residue pair.
The [annular count](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v379.md#radial) gives a lower bound for the energy.
How much of this contribution is visible in the radial distribution for these double roots remains open.
For general integral rows the [unit-resultant example](Yin_L_G0462_v175.md#rows) has negligible radial contribution at every annular width.
