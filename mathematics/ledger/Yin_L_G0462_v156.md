# Making the first admissible primes of the actual degree window exceptional

<a id="first-primes"></a>
## Fixing the integer lower endpoint before choosing a Salem degree

The [finite double-root comparison](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v363.md#statement) allows the least degree-preserving prime of its window to be used.
That prime always gives the measure comparison, but its isolated-pair weight need not give a lower bound.
The [Salem construction with a prescribed prime list](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v371.md#salem) can make the first several primes of the actual window all exceptional for this weight.

Let \(Y\) range over sufficiently large positive integers, set
\[
       K=\left\lfloor\frac{Y^{1/4}}{3\log(2Y)}\right\rfloor,
       \qquad T=\frac13\exp(Y^{1/4}),
\]
and take any \(K\) primes in \((Y,2Y]\), for instance the first \(K\).
The [dyadic prime bound](Yin_L_G0462_v54.md#all-units) supplies at least a constant times \(Y/\log(2Y)\) primes in this interval.
Write \(n\) for the product of the selected primes and choose
\[
                   d=6n\left\lfloor T/(6n)\right\rfloor .
\]
Here \(n\le(2Y)^K\le\exp(Y^{1/4}/3)\), so \(d\) is positive, \(T-6n<d\le T\), and \(\log(3d)=Y^{1/4}+O(n/T)\).
Since \(Y^{3/4}n/T\to0\), taking fourth powers and using the upper bound \(d\le T\) gives
\[
                 Y-1<(\log(3d))^4\le Y,\qquad
                 \left\lceil(\log(3d))^4\right\rceil=Y .
\]
Thus the interval is exactly \((Y,2Y]\) for the constructed input.

Since \(6n\mid d\), the [power-index calculation](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v371.md#salem) applies to \(S_d=x^d-\sum_{j=1}^{d-1}x^j+1\) at every selected \(p\).
It uses only \(6\mid d,\ p\mid d,\ p>3\), and the [irreducibility and Salem root theorem](../claims/Yin_C_G0022_v1.md#salem-first-window-precise-statement).
The power preserves degree and
\[
             I_p=3^{(d-2)(p-1)/2},\qquad E_p=0 .
\]
For the first \(K\) primes these are also the first \(K\) degree-preserving primes, because each of them preserves degree.
Even choosing other lists by their positions in the integer interval before constructing \(S_d\) gives the same conclusion.
The [specified two-group bound](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v371.md#collision) for the double-root criterion satisfies \(B/N\le\log p+4\log(3)/p\) at every one of those primes.

The log measures of these genuine Salem inputs tend to \(\log 2\).
The construction does not determine which primes work for units of shrinking log measure.
It isolates the limitation of selecting the first admissible primes using their positions alone, and leaves both the other primes and additional contributions to the actual row resultant available.
