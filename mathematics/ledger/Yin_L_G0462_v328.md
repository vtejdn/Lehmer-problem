# What an obstruction or a construction establishes about the Lehmer gap

2026-11-25.
This synthesis compares the logical scope of the current approaches for Yin and relates the trace experiments to their [fixed-grid realization problem](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v891.md#statement).

<a id="gap"></a>
## Equivalent formulations and sufficient constructions

The [unrestricted question](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#contract) is whether \(\mathfrak L=\inf_{m(P)>0}m(P)\) is positive for integer polynomials of arbitrary degree and coefficients.
In \(m(P)=\log|a_d|+\sum_i\log^+|\alpha_i|\), all exterior conjugates and all multiplicities contribute to the same total.
A putative sequence with \(m(P)\downarrow0\) must have positive measure at every term.
Fixed-degree coefficient finiteness forces its degrees to escape along a subsequence.

The [algebraic entropy infimum](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#algebraic-entropy-contract) equals \(\mathfrak L\).
For the [two-dimensional affine family](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#linear-growth-contract), the comparison constants are uniform in the algebraic input and give equivalence of the gap questions.
Both formulations retain the uniformity required by the original problem.
An estimate for a restricted class contributes to the positive-gap proof once there is an argument taking every possible failure of the gap into the class with the needed bounds.

Coverage and a uniform numerical bound are separate issues even within the arithmetic route.
The [auxiliary polynomial construction](../claims/Yin_C_G0570_v1.md#uniform-auxiliary-margin-fixed-scope) supplies a fixed positive difference between the arithmetic gain and the log coefficient norm for every monic irreducible nontorsion unit.
Applying that difference to the original measure still requires control of the combined error over the conjugates as the degree grows.

There is such a reduction to [adjacent strict degree records](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v163.md#statement).
Put \(A_i=M(w_i)\) at successive strict improvements in the minimum positive measure through degree \(d_i\).
For allowed integer exponents \(1\le a\le\lceil(\log(3d_i))^3\rceil\), failure of the unrestricted gap is [equivalent](../claims/Yin_C_G0901_v165.md#statement) to an admissible sequence \(A_i^aA_{i+1}\to2\) strictly from below.
Ruling out that entire limiting configuration would settle the positive-gap direction.
The [relative-trace obstruction](Yin_L_G0462_v327.md#scalings) rules out one way of obtaining a smaller record within that configuration; the configuration itself remains possible under the present results.

The first nonunit trace branch supplies a sufficient construction in the opposite direction.
Each [integral realization](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v891.md#return) yields an actual Salem unit with total log measure strictly between zero and \(\log(1+1/q)\).
Thus constructing points for unbounded integer \(q\) would settle the zero-gap direction.
Excluding every point on this branch would close the construction.
A theorem that every possible shrinking sequence yields such points would be needed to infer an unrestricted positive gap from that exclusion.

## Sharing observations and realizing one polynomial

For fixed \(q,n\) and separating nodes \(\tau\), the [sign simplex and pole lattice](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v891.md#simplex) live in one coefficient hyperplane.
Every grid with \(n\ge3\) has rational polynomials satisfying the pole and signs, and the pole equation always has integral polynomials.
The whole intersection has at most one point per grid.
The question concerns its existence as the grid, degree and height vary.

A common value of an observation \(\pi\) proves that a sign-compatible polynomial and an integral pole-compatible polynomial can agree on those data.
The strongest such construction in the [five-value trial](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v890.md#statement) shares the exact pole, five integer values and up to \(q/2\) leading coefficients for every \(q=(9^s-1)/2\) at infinitely many degrees.
At \(q=40,\ n=3282851794363\) even a shared four-coefficient prefix and those five values leave disjoint possibilities at the next reciprocal coefficient: the real moment bound permits only \(-2,-1,0,1\) as integer choices, while the pole requires \(33\bmod41\). [Exact calculation](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0562_v1236.md#finding)
Thus a projection can have a common point although its two preimages contain no common polynomial.
Even a growing prefix concerns only the specified coordinates at its actual degree.

Different observations can detect different failures.
At \(q=5,\ n=981\), sixteen coefficient steps pass on an adapted grid.
Throughout a box around it, an integral witness would have \(H(-1)=0\) from interpolation and \(H(-1)\equiv3\bmod31\) from the pole. [Point-evaluation exclusion](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0185/integer_grid_values.md#trials)
Conversely a common five-value vector leaves the [integer quotient](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v890.md#completion) to determine the remaining signs.
For comparing further attempts, the decisive information is which polynomial carries all conditions, which observed data were shared, and over which grids and degrees the conclusion holds.
