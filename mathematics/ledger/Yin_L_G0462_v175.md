# Unit-resultant rows with large angular energy and small radial displacement

<a id="rows"></a>
## The radial part can be small at every annular width

For integers \(a\ge2,\ k\ge1\), put
\[
 U(z)=a z^k+a-1,\qquad V(z)=(a+1)z^k+a.
\]
These primitive coprime integral row pairs have absolute homogeneous resultant \(B=1\), since \(\operatorname{Res}(aX+a-1,(a+1)X+a)=1\) and substitution \(X=z^k\) raises its absolute value to the \(k\)-th power.
With \(g=\log\max(|U|,|V|)-k\log^+|z|\), their [Dirichlet integral](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v379.md#identity) is
\[
                             \mathcal D(g)=2k\log(a+1).
\]
Indeed on the circle \(|V|^2-|U|^2=4a(1+\Re z^k)\ge0\), and \(m(V)=\log(a+1)\).

For each \(|t|=1\), every root of \(U-tV\) has
\[
 |z|^{2k}=\frac{1+2a(a-1)(1-\Re t)}{1+2a(a+1)(1-\Re t)} .
\]
The fraction decreases as \(1-\Re t\) increases, giving
\[
            \left(\frac{2a-1}{2a+1}\right)^{1/k}\le |z|\le1 .
\]
The derivative in the logarithm of the radius of the angular mean of \(g\) therefore vanishes almost everywhere outside the interval from \(-k^{-1}\log((2a+1)/(2a-1))\) to zero, and its modulus is at most \(k\) everywhere it exists, by the [radial count formula](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v379.md#radial).
Thus its entire squared integral is at most \(k\log((2a+1)/(2a-1))\).
This bounds \(\tau(n_-^2+n_+^2)\) for every positive width \(\tau\), even if the width is chosen separately for each pair.
With \(a=\lceil e^p\rceil\) the total energy divided by \(kp\) tends to two while the radial contribution divided by \(kp\) tends to zero, uniformly in \(k\).
This example concerns the analytic estimate on integral rows.
For a Lehmer cofactor test the rows must additionally meet the double-root interpolation conditions on an actual nontorsion input.

## Exterior angular frequencies

For \(|z|>1\), \(V\) strictly dominates \(U\).
Indeed for \(r=|z|^k>1\) the difference of their squared moduli is at least
\((2a+1)r^2-4ar+2a-1=(r-1)((2a+1)r-(2a-1))>0\).
Writing \(q=a/(a+1)\), one obtains
\[
 g(z)=\log(a+1)+\Re\sum_{j\ge1}\frac{(-1)^{j-1}q^j}{j z^{kj}} .
\]
For the normalization in the Dirichlet identity, \(\Re(z^{-m})\) has exterior energy \(m/2\), by integration in polar coordinates, and distinct positive angular frequencies are orthogonal.
Summing the absolutely convergent energy series gives
\[
 \frac1{2\pi}\int_{|z|>1}|\nabla g|^2\,dA
    =\frac k2\sum_{j\ge1}\frac{q^{2j}}j
    =\frac k2\log\!\left(\frac{(a+1)^2}{2a+1}\right).
\]
This accounts for a proportion tending to \(1/4\) of the full energy as \(a\to\infty\).
For any fixed \(J\), the first \(J\) nonconstant exterior modes together cost at most \((k/2)\sum_{j\le J}1/j\), a vanishing fraction of the exterior contribution.
