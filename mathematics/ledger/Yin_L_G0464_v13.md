# The full two-moment chamber at conductor 6561 for the negative-one profile

A delegated research agent derived and self-checked these filters for Yin's [G0001 investigation](../tasks/Yin_T_G0001_v1.md#contract) on 2026-11-08.
Take the [full grid and logarithmic cut](Yin_L_G0464_v8.md#small-norm-terminal-block) at \(p=6561=3^8\), \(n=3280\), and \(k=746\).
At the decreasing nodes \(b_j=2\cos(2\pi j/p)\), let \(r_j=(4-b_j^2)h(b_j)/p\) with \(h\in\mathbb Z[t]\), positive on the first 2534 nodes and negative elsewhere.
Fix the absolute resultant \(\Delta=\prod_j|h(b_j)|=2^{1502}\), the shift \(a=-2\), and the strict endpoint \(P(-1)>0\) of the complete reciprocal lift.
Impose the two integer moments \(K=\sum_j r_j=3,\ L=\sum_j b_jr_j=4\) of the profile \(x=-1\).
These hypotheses encompass every positive-unit orbit with this total resultant.

## A third-moment exclusion from integral layer traces

Put \(y=-68/45\), \(N=\sum b_j^2 r_j\in\mathbb Z\), and \(U=\sum r_j/(2+b_j)\).
The [conductor and separator check](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0151/readme.md#next-conductor) gives \(p|B(y)|>2\) and places \(y\) between the negative and positive nodes.
The [integral left endpoint](Yin_L_G0464_v11.md#endpoint-moment-test) gives \(U\ge1\).
Positivity of \((4+y)K-L-4(2+y)U\) gives \(U<(8+3y)/(4(2+y))<2\), so \(U=1\).
The [positive sum](Yin_L_G0464_v12.md#negative-one) \(W=(2+y)L-2yK-N=8-2y-N>1/2\) gives \(N\le10\).

Consider the integral cubic \(H(t)=t^3-2t^2-2t+5\).
Its discriminant is \(-107\), so it has only one real root.
At \(y_0=-151157/100000\) and \(y=-68/45\) its values are \(-249170666893/10^{15}\) and \(433/91125\).
Both rationals have exactly 2534 grid poles above them by the [integer variation recurrence](Yin_L_G0464_v11.md#finite-exclusions), and neither is a pole.
Thus \(H(b_j)h(b_j)>0\) at every node.
For \(m_i=p^{-1}\sum_j(2-b_j)b_j^i h(b_j)\) one has \(m_0=U=1\) and \(m_{i+1}+2m_i=\sum b_j^i r_j\).
In particular \((m_0,m_1,m_2,m_3)=(1,1,2,N-4)\), and
\[
 Z=\frac1p\sum_j(2-b_j)H(b_j)h(b_j)=N-5 .
\]

Use the [layer valuations and integral normalized traces](Yin_L_G0464_v11.md#layer-trace-test).
On a layer of degree \(d=3^{\ell-1}\) write \(h=2^{t_\ell} f_\ell\), where \(f_\ell\) is an integral unit on that layer, and let \(F_\ell=1+T_d\) be its trace polynomial.
The normalized trace \(I_\ell=d^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}_\ell((2-b)H(b)f_\ell(b))\) is a positive integer.
Its arithmetic-geometric mean bound is \(I_\ell\ge(3|\operatorname{Res}(F_\ell,H)|)^{1/d}\), since \(F_\ell(2)=3\).
The first three absolute resultants are \(4,17,1297\), and the fourth is \(1669767859\).
The binary lengths of all eight absolute resultants, in increasing layer order, are \(3,5,11,31,92,280,831,2507\).
For an exact check with polynomials of degree at most two, start with \(v=t\), evaluate \(|\operatorname{Res}(H,1+v)|\), and replace \(v\) by the remainder of \(v^3-3v\) modulo \(H\) between layers.
This works by the trace identity \(T_{3d}=T_d^3-3T_d\).
Each binary length is at least \(d+1\), so \(I_\ell>2\) and hence \(I_\ell\ge3\).
Since \(\sum d\,t_\ell=1502\) and \(2^{t_\ell}\ge 1+t_\ell\), we obtain
\[
 N-5=Z=\frac1p\sum_\ell d\,2^{t_\ell}I_\ell
       \ \ge\ \frac{3(3280+1502)}{6561}>2 .
\]
Consequently \(N\in\{8,9,10\}\) on the entire fixed-resultant chamber.

The exclusion of \(N=6\) also has a shorter check valid at every nonzero resultant.
Let \(s=\sqrt2\) and form the positive sum \(A=p^{-1}\sum(2-b_j)(b_j-y)(b_j-s)^2h(b_j)\).
Here \(|B(s)|=1\) by the odd-grid sine formula and \(p\equiv1\pmod{16}\).
The full-grid product bound gives \(A\ge(n/p)(p\Delta |B(y)|\,|B(s)|^2)^{1/n}>1/2\).
On the other hand \(A=N-4-2y-2s(2-y)+s^2(1-y)\), by the four moments \(m_i\).
At \(N=6\) this is \((452-316\sqrt2)/45<1/2\).

## A conjugate trace pair and its complete exterior cost

For \(f(t)=-t^4+(5/3)t^3+(3/2)t^2-(9/4)t\) write \(\mathcal D(f)\) for the sum over zeros of \(Q\) minus the sum over poles of \(B\), counting multiplicities.
The [two-moment expansion](Yin_L_G0464_v12.md#negative-one) gives \(\mathcal D(f)=73/6+4(N-8)\).
The derivative of \(f\) vanishes exactly at \(-3/4,1/2,3/2\).
In increasing order its values at the two endpoints and three critical points are \(-113/6,387/256,-29/48,9/16,-7/6\).
Thus its negative variation on \([-2,2]\) is \(V_-=2953/768\) and its maximum even on all of \(\mathbb R\) is \(387/256\).
Let \(b_*\) be the smallest pole with positive residue.
The [disjoint gap pairing](Yin_L_G0464_v12.md#pairing) leaves two trace roots \(v_1,v_2\) and gives
\[
 f(v_1)+f(v_2)\ge\mathcal D(f)-V_-+f(b_*)>
 B_N:=4389777869/1049760000+4(N-8).
\]
The strict lower bound uses \(y<b_*<-1\), since its index 2534 exceeds \(p/3\), and \(f\) is strictly increasing to the left of \(-3/4\).
As \(B_8-2\max_{\mathbb R} f=1215894119/1049760000>0\), the two remaining trace roots form a nonreal conjugate pair.
All the matched roots lie in \((-2,2)\), so \(M(P)=\rho^2\), where \(\rho>1\) is the exterior radius of either exceptional trace lift.

For \(R>1\), the ellipse \(R e^{i\theta}+R^{-1}e^{-i\theta}\) has the real part of \(f\) on its boundary given by
\[
 g_R(t)=-3+\frac12\sum_{j=1}^4 c_j(R^j+R^{-j})T_j(t),\qquad
 t=2\cos\theta,\qquad(c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4)=(11/4,-5/2,5/3,-1).
\]
This follows by expanding \(f=-3+\sum c_jT_j\).
The maximum principle for the harmonic function \(\operatorname{Re}f\) bounds its value throughout the filled ellipse by \(\max_{[-2,2]}g_R\).
If \(B_N-2g_R(t)>0\) on \([-2,2]\), the two actual roots must lie outside that ellipse, giving \(M(P)>R^2\).
