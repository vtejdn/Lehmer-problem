# Small integral multiples of the powered minimum preserve two short coprime rows

<a id="rows"></a>
## An alternative bound for both cofactors

Let \(P\) be a monic irreducible reciprocal nontorsion unit polynomial of degree \(d=2m\ge4\), let \(\alpha\) be a root, and put \(h=m(P)\), its logarithmic Mahler measure.
Let \(p\ge5\) be a prime with \(\beta=\alpha^p\) still of degree \(d\), and put \(Q=\min_{\mathbb Q}(\beta)\) and \(L=ph+\log(2dp)\).
Suppose there is a finite family of nonzero integral multiples \(H_i\) of \(Q\) with
\[
 \deg H_i\le2d,\qquad \gcd_{\mathbb Q[X]}(H_i)_i=Q,\qquad
 \max_i\log\|H_i\|_1\le B,                                             \tag{1}
\]
where the gcd is monic and the norm is the sum of absolute coefficients.
For either fixed \(\sigma=\pm1\), there are two primitive diagonals \(F_i(x)=A_i(x^p)+\sigma x A_i^*(x^p)\) divisible by \(P^2\), with \(A_i(0)\ne0,\ \deg A_i=4d,\ A_i^*(X)=X^{4d}A_i(1/X)\), and \(\gcd(A_i,A_i^*)=1\).
Their span is \(4pd+1\).
Write \(R_i\) for the noncyclotomic part of \(F_i/P^2\) with multiplicities and \(q=\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\).
For \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\), \(K^+=\mathbb Q(q)\), let \(N,N_+\) be their rational norms.
Write \(\mathcal E=X\,d/dX\) and \(W=A_1(\beta)(\mathcal EA_2)(\beta)-A_2(\beta)(\mathcal EA_1)(\beta)\).
In the [odd trace chart](Yin_L_G0462_v189.md#odd), write \(u_i,v_i\) for the traces and \(D_i\) for the derivative remainder.
Let \(U_i,V_i\) be the degree-\(<m\) Chebyshev representatives of \(u_i,v_i\), and let \(S_{u_i}\) be the [primitive squarefree part of the lift of \(U_i\)](Yin_L_G0462_v215.md#obstruction).
Then \(A_i(\beta),W,u_i,D_i\ne0\), \(v_i/u_i\notin\mathbb Q\), and, with absolute constants,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 m(R_i)&\ll L+B,\\
 \log\max(1,\|U_i\|_C,\|V_i\|_C),\quad m(S_{u_i}),\quad
 \frac{\log|N_+D_i|}{m},\quad
 \frac{\log|NA_i(\beta)|}{d},\quad \frac{\log|NW|}{d}
 &\ll L .                                                            \tag{2}
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\|\cdot\|_C\) is the largest absolute Chebyshev coefficient.
On any sequence with \(d\to\infty,\ h\to0\), a degree-preserving prime in the [fourth-logarithmic window](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v363.md#statement) satisfying (1) with \(B=o(p)\) suffices for all the quantities in (2) to be \(o(p)\).
The existence of such families for general shrinking irreducible inputs remains open.

## Preserving the two jets while removing common roots

Start with the two [short original rows](Yin_L_G0462_v206.md#space) of formal degree \(n=4d\) before fixed-factor division, chosen with the [nonzero determinant and individual remainders](Yin_L_G0462_v206.md#remainders).
Their coefficients and those of their reduced traces have logarithmic size \(O(L)\) on taking \(l=\lfloor m/2\rfloor\).
For each original row \(A\), set \(A^\#=X^nA(1/X)\) and \(E=A-A^\#\).
The [centered value identity](Yin_L_G0462_v222.md#perturbation) gives \(\beta^{-n/2}E(\beta)=-\sigma(\alpha-\alpha^{-1})u\ne0\), so \(E\) is coprime to \(Q\).
For every nonzero root \(z\) of \(E\), its inverse is also a root since \(X^nE(1/X)=-E(X)\).
Consequently neither \(z\) nor \(1/z\) is a common zero of the family in (1).

Choose a basis from that family for its rational linear span, containing at most \(2d+1\) members and having the same gcd.
Adjoin to the list the shift \(X^{2d-\deg H_i}H_i\) of each basis member.
The common monic gcd remains \(Q\), every entry has coefficient norm at most \(e^B\), and at least one member has nonzero coefficient in degree \(2d\).
At least one has nonzero constant term since \(Q(0)=1\).
Let \(H\) be a linear combination of the enlarged list.
Its constant and degree-\(2d\) coefficients, and its evaluations at \(z,1/z\) for every distinct nonzero root of \(E\), are all nonzero linear forms in the parameters.
Their product has degree at most \(2n+2\).
By successive univariate interpolation it is nonzero at a point of the integer grid \(\{0,\ldots,2n+2\}\) in each parameter.
There are at most \(4d+2\) summands, so this choice gives
\[
               \log\|H\|_1\le B+\log((4d+2)(2n+2)).
\]
The argument may be applied separately to the two original rows.

Write \(H^\dagger=X^{2d}H(1/X)\) and \(T=HH^\dagger\).
Then \(T\) has degree \(n\), nonzero constant coefficient, and is invariant under reversal about \(n\).
It is divisible by \(Q^2\), since \(Q\mid H\) and \(Q\) is reciprocal with constant term one.
At each nonzero root \(z\) of \(E\), \(T(z)=z^{2d}H(z)H(1/z)\ne0\).
In \(A+kT\), at most one value of \(k\) kills either specified endpoint or the evaluation at any specified nonzero root of \(E\).
There are at most \(n+2\) exclusions, so an integer \(0\le k\le n+2\) gives both endpoints nonzero and avoids all these roots.
The difference from its reversal is still \(E\), proving coprimality.
The added multiple of \(Q^2\) preserves the value and Euler derivative at \(\beta\), hence both centered trace representatives and divisibility of the diagonal by \(P^2\).

Divide each row by its positive scalar content, which also makes its diagonal primitive since the two coefficient classes modulo \(p\) are disjoint.
Each individual trace, evaluation and derivative remainder is divided by that same scalar, and \(W\) is divided by the product of the two scalars.
The trace representatives after division remain integral by the odd chart and their uniqueness.
The [original determinant and value norm bounds](Yin_L_G0462_v206.md#remainders) and the [squarefree lift and individual remainder estimates](Yin_L_G0462_v222.md#perturbation) give the second line of (2).
The coefficient sum of the perturbed row is at most \(\|A\|_1+(n+2)\|H\|_1^2\).
Hence the logarithm of the coefficient sum of the primitive diagonal is \(O(L+B)\).
This bounds its logarithmic measure, and deleting \(P^2\) and all cyclotomic factors gives the first line of (2).

## Short multiples at conductors with large angular fluctuations

Choose a squarefree conductor \(N\) with \(s\) prime factors in \([X,2X]\), \(X=e^{Cs}\), as in the [large-fluctuation construction](Yin_L_G0462_v225.md#conductors), and put \(d_N=\varphi(N)\).
For each prime \(q\mid N\) set
\[
                   H_q(X')=\frac{(X')^N-1}{(X')^{N/q}-1}
                          =\sum_{j=0}^{q-1}(X')^{jN/q}.
\]
In the squarefree factorization of \((X')^N-1\) into cyclotomic polynomials, the factors of \(H_q\) have orders dividing \(N\) and divisible by \(q\).
An order divisible by every prime divisor of squarefree \(N\) must be \(N\), giving \(\gcd_q H_q=\Phi_N\).
Moreover \(N/d_N=\prod_{q\mid N}(1-1/q)^{-1}=1+O(s/X)\), so eventually \(\deg H_q<N<2d_N\).
One has \(\log\|H_q\|_1=\log q=O(\sqrt{\log d_N})\) since \(\log d_N\asymp s^2\).
For every prime in the fourth-logarithmic window of \(d_N\), eventually \(p\nmid N\), so the powered minimal polynomial is again \(\Phi_N\).
The same small family therefore satisfies (1) at every one of these primes even though \(J(\Phi_N)=\int(\log|\Phi_N|)_+\,d\mu\ge\exp(c\sqrt{\log d_N})\) for an absolute \(c>0\). [First absolute moment](Yin_L_G0462_v225.md#moments)

For the [positive-measure controls](Yin_L_G0462_v225.md#positive), fix a nontorsion unit minimal polynomial \(P_0\) of degree \(e\) and use the squarefree product \(P_0\Phi_N\).
For its large degree-preserving primes \(p\nmid N\), write \(P_{0,p}\) for the minimal polynomial of the \(p\)-th power of a root of \(P_0\).
The powered product \(P_{0,p}\Phi_N\) is the monic gcd of \(P_{0,p}H_q\) over \(q\mid N\).
Their degrees are at most \(2(d_N+e)\), and
\[
 \log\|P_{0,p}H_q\|_1
 \le e\log2+p\,m(P_0)+O(\sqrt{\log d_N}).
\]
Indeed \(\|P_{0,p}\|_1\le\prod_\gamma(1+|\gamma|)\le2^e\exp(m(P_{0,p}))\), over its roots \(\gamma\), and degree preservation gives \(m(P_{0,p})=p\,m(P_0)\).
Thus a positive-measure reducible control can also have large angular fluctuation and a family of multiples of this size.
For fixed \(P_0\) the bound includes its fixed positive \(m(P_0)\) times \(p\).
The row conclusion (2) requires the original irreducible nontorsion input, and the control only compares the two costs.
