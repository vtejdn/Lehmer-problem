# Unit Pisot realizations of all first departures at the pole two

Every constant or binomial in the [first-departure classification at \(F_0=1/(1-2z)\)](Yin_L_G0462_v117.md#departure) occurs for a sequence of actual unit Pisot minimal polynomials.
The matched delay-two polynomial has Mahler measure one in each construction.

<a id="seeds"></a>
## Exact unit Pisot realizations of all constant and binomial profiles

Choose signs \(a,b,c\in\{-1,1\}\), integers \(n\ge2,\ k\ge1\), and set \(D=n+1+k\).
Put
\[
 P=a+bz^k+2cz^D,\qquad Q=cP^*,\qquad V=P/Q.
\]
The polynomial \(P\) has no exterior zero since for \(|z|>1\), \(2|z|^D>1+|z|^k\).
Every circle zero satisfies \(z^k=ab\), by equality in the triangle inequality, and is a root of unity.
Thus \(Q\) has no disk zero, and after canceling any common circle zeros \(V\) is Schur and has circle modulus one.
Define
\[
 A=\frac{Q+(z-2)z^n P}{2},\qquad
 B=\frac{(1-2z)Q+z^{n+1}P}{2},\qquad p=cA .
\]
Since \(Q\equiv z^{n+1}P\bmod 2\), the polynomials \(A,B\) are integral.
Both have degree \(n+1+D\), constant one and leading coefficient \(c\).
Direct reversal gives \(B=cA^*=p^*\), and \(p\) is monic with constant \(c\).
In particular its normalized quotient is
\[
  \frac{c\,p}{p^*}=\frac{A}{B}
       =\frac{1+(z-2)z^nV}{1-2z+z^{n+1}V}.                         \tag{1}
\]

The denominator \(1-2z+z^{n+1}V\) has at most one disk zero counted with multiplicity, by Rouché for \(1-2z+t z^{n+1}V\) with \(0\le t<1\), followed by \(t\uparrow1\).
It has a positive zero between \(1/3\) and \(2/3\) since it has opposite signs there for \(n\ge2\), using \(|V|\le1\).
Since \(Q\) has no disk zero, \(p^*=B\) has precisely this one simple disk zero.
Its only possible circle zeros outside the roots of \(Q\) occur at \(1\), because \(|1-2z|>1\) elsewhere on the circle.
Thus \(p\) has a unique exterior root \(\theta>1\), and all its circle zeros are roots of unity.
The irreducible monic factor \(f\) containing \(\theta\) is a Pisot minimal polynomial, since a conjugate of \(\theta\) cannot be a root of unity.
The remaining monic factor \(C\) has measure one, so is cyclotomic by Kronecker's theorem.
Since \(p=Cf\) has constant \(c=\pm1\) and \(C^*=C(0)C\), (1) equals \(f(0) f/f^*\) for the actual Pisot unit.

Solving (1) for its centered quotient gives exactly \(V\), with first discrepancy \(-2V(0)=-a\) at index \(n\).
Indeed \((1-2z)A/B-1=-2(1-z)^2 z^n V/(1-2z+z^{n+1}V)\).
Along \(n\to\infty\) with fixed \(k\), (1) tends meromorphically on compact subsets of the disk to \(F_0\), and \(V\to(a+bz^k)/2\) there.
For \(k\to\infty\) as well, \(V\to a/2\).
These limits follow immediately from \(Q=2+cb z^{n+1}+ca z^{n+1+k}\) and the formula for \(P\).
At the pole \(r=1/\theta\), the denominator in (1) gives the [signed scale formula](Yin_L_G0462_v118.md#side), whose derivation uses only the pole and the compact profile convergence.
Thus \(2^n(\theta-2)\) tends to \(-(a+b2^{-k})/2\) for fixed \(k\), and to \(-a/2\) when \(k\to\infty\).
The two sides of two correspond to the signs of the actual first discrepancies \(-a\).

## The matched output has measure one

The exact delay-two polynomial satisfies
\[
           B+z^2 A=(1-z)^2\,\frac{Q+z^{n+1}P}{2}.
\]
It has no disk zero since \(Q\) has none and \(|z^{n+1}V(z)|<1\) in the disk.
It is reciprocal up to sign with constant one and leading coefficient of modulus one, so its Mahler measure is one.
Replacing \(p\) by its Pisot factor preserves the measure, since \(B+z^2 A=C(0)C\big(f^*+z^2 f(0)f\big)\).
In fact \(f(0)=-1\) for all these seeds.
If \(f(0)=1\), then the polynomial in parentheses is positive at zero and has value \(2f(1)<0\) at one, which would give a disk zero.
Here \(f(1)<0\) follows from the unique positive exterior root and the other roots in the open disk.

For completeness the two size-two discrepancies give \(V=t\) exactly for the [trinomial completions](Yin_L_G0462_v117.md#extreme), \(t=\pm1\), by (1) with \(V=t\).
Their corresponding delay polynomial before cyclotomic cancellation is \((1-z)^2(1+t z^{n+1})\), also of measure one.
Thus every profile in the first-departure classification is realized by actual Pisot units, while all realizations constructed here have zero matched delay-two excess.
