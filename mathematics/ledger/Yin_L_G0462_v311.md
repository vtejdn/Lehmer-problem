# Radial spread at the house scale for small logarithmic Mahler measure

<a id="spread"></a>
## A near-neutral conjugate in each small-degree fiber

Let \(\alpha\) be a nontorsion reciprocal algebraic unit of degree \(d\), let \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\), and write \(m=\log M(\alpha)\), \(H=\max_\sigma\log|\sigma(\alpha)|\), and \(c=(\log 2)/4\).
The logarithmic product formula and integrality give \(\sum_\sigma|\log|\sigma(\alpha)||=2m\), summing over the \(d\) complex embeddings of \(K\).
[Dimitrov's house inequality](../audits/literature/Smy08/Yin_U001.md#smy08-house) gives \(dH\ge c\) ([Dim19 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12545v1) and [accepted Annals statement](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/articles/22975)), since the minimal polynomial is reciprocal with constant coefficient one.
For any subfield \(F\subseteq K\) of degree \(b\) over \(\mathbb Q\), each embedding of \(F\) admits exactly \(d/b\) extensions to \(K\).
In each such fiber there is therefore an extension \(\tau\) with \(|\log|\tau(\alpha)||\le 2bm/d\le (2bm/c)H\).
More generally, for \(0<\theta\le1\) the fraction in that fiber satisfying \(|\log|\tau(\alpha)||\ge\theta H\) is at most \(2bm/(c\theta)\), by summing their contributions to \(2m\).
In particular this applies to the fiber of an embedding attaining the house, even when the subfield generated by a bounded-degree output varies with the dilation.

Taking \(F=\mathbb Q\) and \(h_0=\min_\tau|\log|\tau(\alpha)||\), the spread \(\delta=H-h_0\) satisfies
\[
 d\delta\ge c-2m,\qquad 1-2m/c\le\delta/H\le1.
\]
Thus along any sequence with \(m\to0\) the relative spread tends to one, including when no conjugate lies on the unit circle.
If all conjugates lie on two reciprocal circles, then \(m\ge c/2\).
These conclusions require no strict record assumption.

## Uniform dilation cost for fixed rational coefficients

For \(m\le c/4\) we have \(\delta\ge c/(2d)\).
The [two fixed-coefficient estimates](Yin_L_G0462_v308.md#spread) therefore bound the number of rational-output dilations of any one nonconstant primitive marked list of weight \(r\) with distinct positions and fixed rational outgoing coefficients by
\[
 (r-1)\left\lceil (2d/c)\log(64r^2)\right\rceil.
\]
For asymmetric coefficients the estimate uses \(H\ge\delta\), and for symmetric coefficients it uses \(\delta\) directly.
Dividing the [terminal realization lower bound](Yin_L_G0462_v308.md#patterns) by this cost preserves the exponent \((1-o(1))(r-2)\log(1/m)\) whenever \(r\to\infty\) and \(\log d=o((r-2)\log(1/m))\).
This gives a degree criterion without a coefficient height cutoff or an additional radial hypothesis.
The finite-height argument in [C588](../claims/Yin_C_G0345_v588.md) gives a separate restriction on the dilations of its constructed witnesses.
For outputs of degree greater than one the near-neutral comparison embedding above may change with the output, so this count for fixed rational coefficients does not directly extend to that setting.
