# Undilated power schedules with overlapping exterior radii

<a id="statement"></a>
## Sharp cost on the original slots

Fix an integer \(d\ge2\), constants \(C>1,\delta>0\), and arbitrary sets \(I_N\subset[N,CN]\cap\mathbb Z\) with \(|I_N|\ge\delta N\).
Prescribe \(S=\{n^d:n\in I_N\}\), \(r=|S|\), \(L=N^d\), \(\Sigma=\sum_{p\in S}p\), and integer widths \(b/r\to\infty\).
At every fixed compressed reciprocal nontorsion unit minimum \(P\), the minimum log cost of the [coprime double-contact rows](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#statement) for this seed is \(\Sigma\log M(P)+o(\Sigma)\).
No restriction on its exterior radii is needed.
Allowing all reciprocal seeds and using the ordinary cutoff and fallback \(2^\Sigma\), the normalized log minimum tends to the unrestricted infimum \(\lambda\) of positive logarithmic Mahler measures.
In particular one can prescribe all squares from \(N^2\) through \(4N^2\) before choosing any seed, including all their contacts in the final pair.

<a id="scales"></a>
## Separation away from exact coincidences

For each exterior conjugate \(c\) write \(a_c=\log|c|\).
The logarithmic depth of its node \(c^{-n^d}\) is \(a_c n^d\).
Fix a constant \(B>0\), to be chosen below depending on the seed.
For fixed labels \(c,c'\), two depths within \(B\log(2L)\) have
\[
 |m-\alpha n|\ll_{P,C,d,B}\log(2L)/N^{d-1},\qquad \alpha=(a_c/a_{c'})^{1/d}.
\]
This follows by factoring the difference of the two positive \(d\)-th powers, with both roots comparable to \(N\).
For rational \(\alpha\) such a near coincidence must be exact for large \(N\), by its fixed denominator.
For irrational \(\alpha\), equidistribution of \(n\alpha\) modulo one shows that only \(o(N)\) indices can qualify, first bounding the distance to an integer by fixed \(\epsilon>0\) and then letting \(\epsilon\downarrow0\).
There is eventually at most one partner per such index.
Mark both ends of every inexact near coincidence in the full interval of indices, taking the union over finitely many labels.
This marks \(o(N)\) indices.

<a id="phases"></a>
## Polynomial inverse bounds on almost all tied blocks

Group nodes by exact depth.
Each group uses at most one index per exterior label.
For any group or any nonempty subset of it, its index tuple can be written \(n_i=A_i k\), with fixed primitive positive integer vector \((A_i)\) and positive integer \(k\asymp N\).
Indeed the label list fixes every ratio \(n_i/n_j\), and only rational ratios occur within a group.
There are only finitely many such patterns for the seed, including all subsets.
In one pattern set \(s=\#\{i\}\), \(u_i=(|c_i|/c_i)^{A_i^d}\), \(x_i=u_i^{k^d}\), and \(t=k^{-d}\).
The nodes at equal depth differ only by their phases \(x_i\).
These phases are distinct for every positive \(k\): equality of powered conjugates implies equality of their exponents by positivity of their common absolute logarithmic height, and compressedness then forces the same label.
On this equal-depth block the [weighted residue system](Yin_C_G0345_v531.md#system) has diagonal entries \(tc_i/A_i^d\) and off-diagonal entries \(-(c_i-c_j)x_j/(x_i-x_j)\).
The diagonal weighting is constant inside the block.

Let \(G\) be the closure of the cyclic group generated by \(u=(u_i)\) in the compact torus and let \(K\) be the order of its finite component group.
Along any residue class \(k\equiv a\pmod K\), the sequence \(x=u^{k^d}\) is Haar equidistributed in \(u^{a^d}G^0\).
Indeed \(u^K\) generates a dense subgroup of the connected torus \(G^0\), as the union of the \(K\) translates of its closure is \(G\).
A nontrivial character on \(G^0\) therefore takes an irrational angle at \(u^K\).
On writing \(k=a+Kv\), its phase on the translated sequence has irrational leading coefficient, by expanding \(((a+Kv)^d-a^d)/K\).
The polynomial character test follows by repeated fixed-lag differencing down to the geometric sum, as in [Tao's equidistribution criterion](../audits/literature/Tao10/Yin_U001.md#tao10-u001).

Multiply the block determinant by \(V(x)=\prod_{i<j}(x_i-x_j)^2\) to obtain a polynomial \(E(t,x)\) in \(t\) with polynomial coefficients in \(x\).
Each pair of indices contributes at most two factors to any determinant denominator.
The coefficient of \(t^s\) is \(V(x)\prod_i c_i/A_i^d\), nonzero at every sampled point and hence not identically zero on any sampled component.
On that component let \(j\le s\) be the least index whose coefficient \(e_j(x)\) is not identically zero.
The zero sets of \(e_j\) and \(V\) have Haar measure zero there, by the zero-set property of a nonzero trigonometric polynomial on a torus (successive one-variable slicing proves this by induction).
Outside \(o(N)\) values of \(k\asymp N\), both absolute values exceed \(1/\log(2L)\).
Indeed equidistribution bounds the upper density of a fixed closed sublevel set by its Haar measure, and these measures tend to zero with the threshold.
All coefficients are bounded, so \(|E(t,x)|\gg t^j/\log(2L)\) for large \(N\).
The uniform upper bound on \(|V|\) gives the same lower bound up to constants on the determinant, while the lower bound on \(|V|\) bounds every reciprocal difference by \(O_P(\log(2L))\).
Cofactors thus give an inverse norm \(O_P(L^{s+1})\), allowing the implicit constant to depend also on the fixed interval and power.
Mark all indices \(A_i k\) from an exceptional \(k\) in any pattern, including the patterns for subsets, within the full interval.
Only \(o(N)\) more indices have been marked.

<a id="endpoint"></a>
## Endpoint recovery and restoration of every slot

Write \(s_0\) for the number of exterior conjugates and now take \(B=2s_0+12\).
The phase estimates were independent of this choice.
Let \(D_N=o(r)\) be the total number of marked indices in \(I_N\).
For any subset of the unmarked indices, partition the weighted residue system into exact-depth blocks.
The off-block entries are \(O_P((2L)^{-B/2})\) by the [weighted entry bound](Yin_C_G0345_v531.md#inverse), and there are \(O_P(r)\) per row.
The block inverse has norm \(O_{P,C,d}(L^{s_0+1})\), including after arbitrary deletions, so a Neumann series gives the same bound for the full inverse.
The integral endpoint recovery in [the same argument](Yin_C_G0345_v531.md#inverse) now costs \(O_{P,C,d}(L+r)\) in the logarithm: undoing the diagonal weight costs \(\exp(O_{P,C,d}(L))\), and the polynomial part on the right costs at most \(2^{O_P(r)}H\).
The product of all retained node moduli is \(\exp(-h\sum_{\rm retained} p)\), where \(h=\log M(P)\).
Consequently on all original slots, or after deleting any one slot, every nonzero homogeneous pair has log cost at least \(h\sum p-O_{P,C,d}((1+D_N)L+r)\), with the sum taken before discarding marked indices.

Use the [jet count and packing](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#counts) on all original slots with its same \(q=\min(\lfloor b/r\rfloor,\lfloor\sqrt L\rfloor,r)\), \(w=rq\), and the increased multiplicity
\[
 t_*=2+\left\lceil r/q^{1/3}+\sqrt{r(1+D_N)}\right\rceil.
\]
It is \(o(r)\), tends to infinity, and \((1+D_N)L+r=o(t_* L)\).
All the cited parameter inequalities still hold, since the original lower bound on the multiplicity only increased.
In the packing comparison the added endpoint error times \(w+1\) is \(o(wt_*L)\), and in [designated-slot division](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#protect) it is \(o(t_*L)\), uniformly in the designated slot.
Compressedness gives full degree at every power and different powers have distinct minima by height.
The [integral combination](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#combine) therefore gives the required coprime pair on every original slot with log cost \(h\Sigma+o(\Sigma)\).
The endpoint estimate gives the matching lower bound.
Finally [compression, reciprocal reduction and the pointwise tangency inequality](Yin_C_G0345_v535.md#spectrum) supply the unrestricted lower bound and permit a fixed compressed comparison seed arbitrarily close to \(\lambda<\log 2\) before the schedule grows.
