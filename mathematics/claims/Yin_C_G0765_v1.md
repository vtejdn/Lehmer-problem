## Statement

### MB1. Exact source, complete factor and height domains

Fix one actual C759 starting source Q,m,Y_1,H and its order integer K_odd, and vary only its permitted s>=s_min unless stated otherwise.
Thus Q=2^ell with ell>=82, m=Q/4-2, phi=X^2+X+1, h=2d_1-4m, and

    M=Y_1 phi^(14m),
    d_M=deg M=deg Y_1+28m=d_1-2m=h/2,
    N=1+2B_step Delta_crit s>h.               (MB1)

The actual positive odd K_odd is the least common multiple of5 and ALL primitive orders present in the complete Y_1, not the order of one selected factor.
It is fixed for this starting source and satisfies gcd(K_odd,N)=1.
Its possible size is paid as the actual C759 finite least common multiple; it is not assigned an unproved polynomial-in-Q bound.

For ANY actual added-passing copied-block output of C762, retain its monic reciprocal integer K, constant coefficient1, with

    deg K=7N+h-3,
    K=P_(T_K), T_Kbar=X^2Y_1 B_N^7 phi^(14m),
    P=H_big(1-z^2)(1-z^(2n))K,
    m(P)=m(K).                                (MB2)

Here P_f(z)=z^(deg f)f(z+z^(-1)) denotes the reciprocal transform when f is a trace polynomial, and m denotes logarithmic TOTAL Mahler measure with natural logarithms.
All three removed factors in MB2 are the actual cyclotomic factors with all their multiplicities from C762; no residual precision is passed to T_K or to a factor of K.

Let E_nc be the FULL monic noncyclotomic part of K, retaining every noncyclotomic irreducible integer factor with its actual multiplicity, and put D_nc=deg E_nc.
Set E_nc=1 and D_nc=0 if no such factor occurs.
Since MB2 removes only cyclotomic factors, E_nc is also the full noncyclotomic part of P with exactly the same multiplicities.
Neither K nor E_nc is assumed irreducible.

Choose q_0 to be the LEAST dyadic integer at least max(8,d_M), and put

    L=q_0 K_odd N,
    c_0=0.427800=2139/5000.                   (MB3)

Then the unconditional, possibly zero, bound for every such actual K is

    m(P)=m(K)>=c_0 D_nc/(q_0 K_odd N).        (MB4)

The constant c_0 is the accepted historical published evaluation for precisely C246's auxiliary expression; it is not newly certified, optimized or asserted currently best.

For a sequence at the SAME fixed starting source, m(K_s)->0 necessarily implies D_nc(s)/N_s->0 and D_nc(s)/deg K_s->0.
A proportional lower bound D_nc>=eta N with fixed eta>0 instead gives the fixed-source gap c_0 eta/(q_0 K_odd); neither such proportionality nor a nearzero sequence is asserted.
If, additionally, the ORIGINAL full T has its OWN X_(epsilon,T) coefficientwise even for either sign, then the separately applicable C752 gives D_nc>=48m and m(K)>=48m c_0/(q_0 K_odd N)>0.
That additional global condition is not asserted for every C762 member and is not inherited by T_K or its factors.
The same factor-summation inequality also holds with the exact C246 auxiliary expression c_exact defined in MB3, at its retained numerical-inspection boundary.
All these statements, the varying-source limitation and the absence of a proportional degree conclusion are proved in MB2--MB5 below.

## Complete argument

### MB2. A full reciprocal period, with every multiplicity paid

Write Sigma_N=(z^N+1)/(z+1) and Phi_5(z)=z^4+z^3+z^2+z+1 in F_2[z].
The exact binary reciprocal polynomial in MB2 is

    Kbar=(z+1)^4 P_(Y_1)(z) Sigma_N(z)^7
                              Phi_5(z)^(14m). (MB5)

Indeed the trace X^2 gives (z^2+1)^2=(z+1)^4, the complete trace B_N gives Sigma_N, and phi gives Phi_5.
This distinguishes the trace X multiplicity2 from the reciprocal root1 multiplicity4.
Every nonidentity root of Sigma_N is retained, including every proper divisor order when N is composite.

Because K_odd N is odd, z^(K_odd N)-1 is squarefree in characteristic2: its derivative is z^(K_odd N-1), a unit at all its roots.
Its root set contains1, every root of P_(Y_1), every nonidentity N-th root, and every primitive fifth root.
The first Y_1 assertion uses ALL of its actual primitive orders in K_odd; the fifth roots are included because5 divides K_odd.
The sets arising from P_(Y_1), Sigma_N, Phi_5 and root1 are pairwise disjoint, by the actual source gcds gcd(Y_1,Xphi)=gcd(B_N,Y_1phi)=1 and by the unramified reciprocal trace map away from root1.

For an irreducible factor psi^e of Y_1, each of its reciprocal roots has multiplicity exactly e.
This follows from the derivative1+z^(-2) of z+z^(-1), which is nonzero away from z=1; Y_1 is prime to X, so none of these roots is1.
Each e is at most deg Y_1 and hence at most d_M.
The remaining multiplicities in MB5 are4,7 and14m, each at most max(8,d_M).
Therefore Frobenius gives the full POLYNOMIAL divisibility

    Kbar divides (z^(K_odd N)-1)^(q_0)
                  =z^L-1 in F_2[z].          (MB6)

This is full divisibility with every repeated primary, not only vanishing at the reduced root set.
The endpoint bounds are explicit: d_M>=28m>8, so

    d_M<=q_0<2d_M=h,
    L<h K_odd N,
    deg K=7N+h-3<8N<L.                       (MB7)

For the last inequality q_0 K_odd>=8*5=40.
Both q_0 and K_odd are fixed after the starting source is fixed, whereas N and L grow linearly with the actual s.

### MB3. Genuine integer division and the accepted height transfer

Perform actual monic integer division of z^L-1 by K.
Reduction of its unique remainder modulo2, using MB6, gives zero, so there are actual integer polynomials Q_K,R_K with

    z^L-1=K Q_K+2R_K, deg R_K<deg K.         (MB8)

This finite identity requires no effective evaluation of a huge root or a numerical factorization.
For EVERY complex algebraic root alpha of K it gives

    (alpha^L-1)/2=R_K(alpha) in Z[alpha].     (MB9)

Because K is monic, alpha is an algebraic integer; the right side is integral, and the same identity holds at every embedding of its number field.
Since K(0)=1, no alpha is zero and all of them are algebraic units.
For example the monic equation divided by alpha shows alpha^(-1) is an integer polynomial in alpha, up to the constant coefficient1.
Unitness is available here, although C246's dyadic integral-input theorem does not require it.

Take any noncyclotomic monic irreducible integer factor P_i of K, a root alpha_i and d_i=deg P_i.
Its root is nontorsion: a nonzero algebraic integer that is a root of unity has a cyclotomic minimal polynomial.
Thus MB9 meets the exact adopted C246 hypothesis with exponent a=L.
That theorem gives

    h_abs(alpha_i^L)>=c_0,
    h_abs(alpha_i^L)=L h_abs(alpha_i),
    m(P_i)=d_i h_abs(alpha_i)>=c_0 d_i/L.     (MB10)

Here h_abs is the absolute logarithmic Weil height, distinct from the fixed integer h=deg H in MB1.
The d_i counts ALL original embeddings of alpha_i, and the height equality remains true if alpha_i^L has smaller degree.
There is no replacement of d_i by a powered degree or by the degree of K.
No residual condition on P_i, its trace, or its own canonical remainders is assumed.

The original GIM+10 theorem for an all-odd-coefficient degree-n polynomial is not directly being applied to K and no odd-coefficient completion is constructed.
The consumed result is C246's separately proved integral-congruence transfer of its exact seven-factor auxiliary calculation.
The numerical value c_0 is accepted at GIM+10-U001's recorded statement-level boundary: the original supremum computation, code and interval margin are not reproduced or independently audited here.
C246 also retains the exact alternative

    c_exact=[16407 log2-log L_aux]/24020,

where L_aux is its specifically displayed nonnegative circle-expression maximum.
Replacing c_0 by that retained expression in MB10 and the subsequent algebra gives the corresponding exact-expression inequality without a new numerical certification.
The symbol L_aux is unrelated to the actual period L in MB3.

### MB4. Full factor summation and the fixed-source consequence

Factor K over Z into its full cyclotomic part K_cyc and distinct monic noncyclotomic irreducibles with their actual positive multiplicities:

    K=K_cyc product_i P_i^(e_i),
    E_nc=product_i P_i^(e_i), D_nc=sum_i e_i d_i.

Additivity of logarithmic TOTAL Mahler measure, and the zero measure of every cyclotomic factor, give

    m(K)=sum_i e_i m(P_i)
         >=c_0 sum_i e_i d_i/L=c_0 D_nc/L.   (MB11)

This proves MB4, including the empty product where both D_nc and m(K) are zero.
No selection of a single factor drops its conjugates or multiplicity.

For a sequence of these base outputs with the SAME Q,Y_1,H,K_odd and hence the SAME q_0, if m(K_s) tends to0, then

    D_nc(s)/N_s
       <=(q_0 K_odd/c_0)m(K_s) ->0.          (MB12)

Since deg K_s/N_s=7+(h-3)/N_s>=7, the fraction D_nc(s)/deg K_s also tends to0.
These are necessary conditions only; no converse or actual nearzero sequence is obtained.
If instead D_nc(s)>=eta N_s throughout a subfamily for some fixed eta>0, then MB4 gives the genuine fixed-source uniform bound

    m(K_s)>=c_0 eta/(q_0 K_odd)>0.           (MB13)

If the starting source itself changes, the conclusion D_nc/N->0 does not follow from this argument: the factor q_0 K_odd is then changing too.
Only the weighted necessary ratio D_nc/(q_0 K_odd N)->0 follows directly.
No cost is hidden by calling the modular condition free or by treating K_odd as a uniformly bounded integer.

### MB5. Separate global-X corollary and the remaining degree-fraction question

Suppose, in addition to the actual C762 domain, that the ORIGINAL full trace T has its OWN X_(epsilon,T) coefficientwise even for either sign.
This is an extra global premise, not a conclusion of C762 and not an inherited conclusion of the separate C764 ZC3/ZC9 strong target.
C759 supplies the full profile

    Tbar=X^A Y_out phi^(7A), A=2m,
    Y_out=Y_1 B_N^rho B_n^2,
    gcd(Y_out,Xphi)=1,
    Y_out divides Y_out(lambda), lambda=X^3+X,

with all multiplicities retained.
These are exactly the hypotheses of [adopted C752 CY3/CY7](Yin_C_G0752_v1.md#decision-whole-reviewed-2026-10-25).
That theorem gives strict positivity and FULL noncyclotomic reciprocal degree at least24A=48m for P.
The explicit cyclotomic removal MB2 identifies that full part with E_nc, so

    D_nc>=48m,
    m(P)=m(K)>=48m c_0/(q_0 K_odd N)>0.      (MB14)

This applies C752 to the full T, not to T_K, whose binary X multiplicity is only2 and whose canonical precision is not inherited.
It bounds the total degree of ALL noncyclotomic factors rather than the degree of each irreducible factor.
For a fixed starting source the numerical right side of MB14 tends to zero as N grows, so this fixed minimum degree does not by itself prove the proportional condition in MB13.
Conversely, if the additional global-X premise holds along a hypothetical fixed-source nearzero sequence, MB12 and D_nc>=48m together force N->infinity and D_nc=o(N); they do not contradict each other.
Determining a positive proportional lower bound for D_nc, or constructing a sequence satisfying the necessary ratio with a matching Mahler upper estimate, remains a separate unresolved problem.
The present result asserts no irreducibility of K, no factor precision inheritance, no uniformly bounded source cost, no current-best analytic constant and no unrestricted Task completion.
