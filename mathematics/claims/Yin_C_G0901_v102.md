# A height bound on the primitive measure generator of a complex exterior pair

<a id="statement"></a>
## The measure of that generator grows with the source degree

Take a compressed reciprocal algebraic unit \(\alpha\) of degree \(d=2ub\) whose only exterior conjugates are the nonreal pair \(\alpha,\bar\alpha\), under the [large-block hypotheses](Yin_C_G0901_v94.md#statement).
Write \(m=\log M\) for total logarithmic measure, \(\beta=M(\alpha)=\alpha\bar\alpha\), and \(\eta>1\) for the primitive positive unit on the line of \(\beta\) in \(F=\mathbb Q(\beta)\).
Take \(\rho\) primitive on the line of \(\alpha\) modulo torsion in \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\), and use
\[
     \lambda=m(\rho)=k\log\eta,\qquad k\in\mathbb Z_{>0}.
\]
There are absolute positive constants \(c,C\) such that, in either signed block action,
\[
          m(\eta)\lambda\ \ge\ c\,\frac{d}{(\log(3d))^{212}},\qquad
          k\ \le\ C\,\frac{\lambda^2(\log(3d))^{212}}{u}.                \tag{1}
\]
In particular the least unit greater than one on the measure line at the chosen embedding of \(F\) has diverging total logarithmic measure at large source degree and bounded \(m(\alpha)\), including when \(\eta\) approaches one in that embedding.

In the oriented alternative \(k\mid b\).
If every prime factor of \(b\) exceeds \(C\lambda^2(\log(3d))^{212}/u\), then \(k=1\).
In that case the entire nontrivial multiplicative measure spectrum of \(D(\alpha)\), without a ceiling, is
\[
                         \{\eta^j:j=1,2,\ldots\}.
\]
This holds in particular when \(b\) is prime and the degree is sufficiently large in terms of a fixed bound on \(m(\alpha)\).
In the independent even-inversion case \(k\mid2\), and the second bound in (1) still applies.

<a id="degree"></a>
## Two independent primitive line units in a small relative extension

The field \(E=\mathbb Q(\alpha,\bar\alpha)\) contains \(K\) and \(F\) and satisfies \([E:F]=2\). [Exterior product field](Yin_C_G0901_v94.md#orbit)
The unit \(\rho\) generates \(K\): fixing it fixes the class of \(\alpha\), hence its actual value by compression.
Likewise \(\eta\) and all its positive powers generate \(F\). [Common measure field](Yin_C_G0901_v95.md#powers)
Write \(D=[F:\mathbb Q]\); the two explicit values in v94 give \(D\le d(b-1)\le d^2\).

The classes of \(\rho\) and \(\eta\) are independent.
It suffices to check the lines of \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\).
In the independent case they are the coordinate line and the sum of two distinct signed coordinate vectors in a free module.
In the oriented case \(\alpha\) is a coordinate class and \(\beta\) is the difference of two coordinates in its block, with only block constants as relations. [Exterior pair and relation space](Yin_C_G0901_v94.md#orbit)
They cannot be proportional modulo a block constant when \(b\ge3\), by examining the second and a third coordinate.
Thus the [rank-two height product theorem](../audits/literature/AD99/Yin_U001.md#ad99-u001) applies to the actual units \(\rho,\eta\).
Their joint field is contained in \(E\) of degree \(2D\).
For an absolute \(c_0>0\), its bound in absolute logarithmic heights gives
\[
              h(\rho)h(\eta)\ \ge\
                     \frac{c_0}{2D(\log(6D))^{212}} .
\]
Multiplication by \(dD\), using \(D\le d^2\), gives the first inequality in (1).

<a id="index"></a>
## The saturation index and complete attainment

The common iteration exponent satisfies \(M(\eta)=\eta^t\); the two values are \(t=2b-3\) and \(t=b-1\). [Iteration at one exterior pair](Yin_C_G0901_v95.md#iteration)
Since \(\lambda=k\log\eta\), this gives \(m(\eta)=t\lambda/k\).
Substitute into (1) and use \(t/d\le1/u\) to obtain its second inequality with an absolute constant.
The divisibilities \(k\mid2\) and \(k\mid b\) are the [coordinate saturation bounds](Yin_C_G0901_v99.md#indices).
Any \(k>1\) in the oriented case has a prime factor of \(b\), proving the claimed sufficient criterion.
When \(b\) is prime at bounded measure, \(b=d/(2u)\) exceeds the right side of (1) for large \(d\), since \(\lambda\le m(\alpha)\).
If \(k=1\), \(\rho\) already has \(M(\rho)=\eta\), and its \(j\)-th power attains \(\eta^j\) because all its powers have degree \(d\).
Every value in the larger group belongs to this list by the [rank-one field theorem](Yin_C_G0901_v95.md#statement).
