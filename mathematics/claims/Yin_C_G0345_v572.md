# An integer threshold for universal ternary relations and the unrestricted Lehmer gap

<a id="statement"></a>
## A finite obstruction at each cardinality

Fix integers \(t,k\ge1\).
Let \(R_k(t)\) be the largest \(N\ge t\) for which some nontorsion algebraic unit \(\alpha\) has the following property.
Every \((t+1)\)-element subset of \(\{0,\ldots,N-1\}\) supports a nonzero ternary polynomial divisible by \(P_\alpha^k\), where \(P_\alpha\) is the monic minimal polynomial and ternary means coefficients in \(\{-1,0,1\}\).
The condition at \(N=t\) is vacuous.
Then \(R_k(t)\) is finite and computable from \(t,k\).
In fact, putting \(\lambda(x)=(\log\log y/\log y)^3\) for \(y=\max(16,x)\), one has
\[
              t\ \le\ R_k(t)\ \ll\ t\max\{1,1/(k\lambda(t/k))\}
\]
with an absolute implied constant.

If \(D_k(P)\) is the [limiting maximal density of dissociated exponents](Yin_C_G0345_v570.md#statement), then
\[
             \lim_{t\to\infty}R_k(t)/t
             =1/\inf_{P}D_k(P)
\]
in the extended reals, over the minimal polynomials of all nontorsion units.
In particular Lehmer's unrestricted gap is equivalent to \(R_1(t)=O(t)\).
Failure would give \(R_1(t)/t\to\infty\).
There is also a finite certificate for an actual small measure.
If the property holds for \(N>128t\), every witnessing minimum has degree at most \(t/k\) and has log measure at most \(128t/(kN)\).

<a id="constant"></a>
## An explicit density constant

The [Gaussian and shattering argument](Yin_C_G0345_v570.md#matrix) permits the absolute constant \(1/128\) in
\[
         D_k(P)\ \ge\ \tfrac1{128}\min(1,k\log M(P)).
\]
To verify it, write \(I(s)=h(s\xi+G)-h(G)\) in bits, with \(\xi\) a fair binary digit and \(G\) an independent standard normal.
The Gaussian maximizes entropy at fixed variance, so \(I(1/2)\le\tfrac12\log_2(1+1/16)<1/16\).
At \(s=8\) classify the received value by threshold four.
The error probability is at most \(1/16\) by Chebyshev's inequality.
The input bit is determined by the received value and the binary error indicator, so \(I(8)\ge1-H_2(1/16)>5/8\).
Here \(H_2(v)\le v(\log_2(1/v)+\log_2 e)\) for \(0<v<1\).
Telescoping between \(1/2\) and \(8\) by four doublings gives \(\Phi(2)=\sup_{s>0}(I(2s)-I(s))>1/8\).
Since \(H_2(1/64)<1/8\), the shattering step applies with density \(1/64\) whenever \(k\log M(P)\ge1\).
The [dyadic power transfer](Yin_C_G0345_v570.md#powers) loses at most a factor two at smaller positive log measure.

<a id="polynomial"></a>
## A finite polynomial test with no seed supplied

For \(N>t\) and a \((t+1)\)-element mask \(S\), form the product \(L_S\) of the distinct monic noncyclotomic irreducibles whose \(k\)-th powers divide at least one nonzero ternary polynomial on \(S\), omitting \(X\).
An empty product is one.
These are integer polynomials with unit constant term as each ternary input, after removal of a monomial, has unit endpoints.
Compute the monic greatest common divisor of the \(L_S\) over all these masks.
Its irreducible factors are exactly the witnesses for this \(N\), since one and the same minimum must serve every mask while the multiple may change with the mask.
The initial mask \(\{0,\ldots,t\}\) bounds the candidate degrees by \(t/k\) and gives a finite list independent of \(N\).

Denote by \(a_N(P,k)\) the maximum size of an admissible mask as in the density theorem.
The property means \(a_N(P,k)\le t\), since admissibility passes to subsets.
Subadditivity gives \(D_k(P)\le a_N(P,k)/N\), so \(t/N\ge \min(1,k\log M(P))/128\).
This gives the finite small-measure certificate.
It also shows termination of the search in \(N\), since each member of the finite initial list has positive measure and eventually fails.
The test is decreasing in \(N\) by restriction to the shorter interval.
For \(k>t\) the list is empty.
Otherwise the [degree-dependent lower bound of Voutier](Yin_C_G0345_v250.md#cuts) gives \(\log M(P)\gg\lambda(t/k)\) for \(2\le\deg P\le t/k\), with bounded degrees absorbed by Northcott's theorem.
This gives the asserted uniform upper bound for \(R_k(t)\).

<a id="limit"></a>
## The limiting threshold

Put \(D_*=\inf_P D_k(P)\).
For \(D_*>0\), the inequality \(a_N(P,k)\ge N D_*\) gives \(R_k(t)/t\le1/D_*\), also at the vacuous value \(N=t\).
For the reverse bound fix any one eligible \(P\) and \(\varepsilon>0\).
For all sufficiently large \(N\) one has \(a_N(P,k)\le N(D_k(P)+\varepsilon)\).
Taking \(N=\lfloor t/(D_k(P)+\varepsilon)\rfloor\) gives a witness when \(N>t\), and when \(N\le t\) the lower bound \(R_k(t)\ge t\) suffices.
Let \(t\to\infty\), then \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), and finally take the supremum over \(P\).
This also gives divergence when \(D_*=0\).
At \(k=1\) the two-sided density comparison identifies \(D_*>0\) with a uniform positive Mahler gap on nontorsion units.
Every positive-measure integer polynomial outside the unit-factor problem has a positive-measure unit factor or a contribution of at least \(\log2\) from content or a nonunit endpoint after removing monomials.
