# A fixed Schinzel specialization test for the varying six-term sections

<a id="statement"></a>
## A single rational factorization conjecture for all seed degrees

For integers \(s\ge3\), \(a\ge2\), \(b\ge1\) with \(\gcd(a,b)=1\), put
\[
 F_{s,a,b}(T)=T^{as}-T^a-1+T^b(1-T^{a(s-1)}-T^{as}),\qquad N=as+b.
\]
Write \(C\) for its complete monic cyclotomic part, with multiplicities.
Consider the fixed Laurent polynomial
\[
 H(U,V,W)=U(V-U-1)+W(U-V-UV).
\]
If [Schinzel's rational specialization conjecture](../audits/literature/AS18/Yin_U001.md#as18-u001) holds for this one polynomial, then there is an absolute threshold \(L\) such that for every \(\min(a,s)>L\) the polynomial \(-F_{s,a,b}/C\) is monic irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\) of degree between \(N-24s\) and \(N\).
The same threshold works for all coprime offsets \(b\), including those outside the critical slope range.
Thus a sequence with \(\min(a,s)\to\infty\) and reducible nontorsion quotient would refute this specific instance of the conjecture, even without any bounds on the measures of the factors.
For a [critical sequence](Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#statement) the predicted irreducible degree is asymptotic to \(as+b\asymp as^3\), whereas the factor with at most \(Ka\) exterior roots would have degree \(\asymp as^2\).
This excludes such a sequence conditionally.
The argument proves no unconditional irreducibility for the rational univariate quotient.

<a id="lift"></a>
## Every isogeny pullback is geometrically irreducible

Write \(A=U(V-U-1)\), \(B=U-V-UV\), so that \(H=A+WB\) and \(H(T^a,T^{as},T^b)=T^a F_{s,a,b}(T)\).
Fix \(m\ge1\) and work over the Laurent unique factorization domain \(R=\mathbb C[U^{\pm1},V^{\pm1}]\).
The polynomial \(D=V^m-U^m-1\) is squarefree there, since its derivative in \(V\) is a Laurent unit, and is a nonunit.
No prime divisor of \(D\) divides \(B(U^m,V^m)\): modulo \(D\) the latter equals \(-(U^{2m}+U^m+1)\), whose irreducible factors over \(\mathbb C\) are all of the form \(U-c\), and none divides \(D\).
It follows that \(A(U^m,V^m)+W^m B(U^m,V^m)\) is primitive in \(R[W]\) and Eisenstein at any prime divisor of \(D\).
It stays irreducible after inverting \(W\), since it has nonzero constant term and positive \(W\)-degree.
For any nonsingular integral matrix \(M\), composition with the signed adjugate gives the diagonal pullback for \(m=|\det M|\).
Nonsingular monomial substitution takes nonunits to nonunits since it separates support points.
This proves absolute irreducibility under every isogeny.
The support differences span three dimensions, so \(H\) is noncyclotomic.
Direct reversal gives \(U^2VW H(U^{-1},V^{-1},W^{-1})=H(U,V,W)\), putting this input outside the proved non-self-inversive case of the conjecture.

<a id="directions"></a>
## Exceptional directions and the degree left after division

If \((u,v,w)\ne0\) is an integral relation on \((a,as,b)\), then \(a\mid w\) by coprimality.
For \(w\ne0\) its maximum norm is at least \(a\), and for \(w=0\) the equality \(u=-sv\) gives a lower bound \(s\).
Consequently all the finitely many exceptional hyperplanes in Conjecture 2.1 are avoided once \(\min(a,s)\) exceeds the maximum norm of their specified normals.
In the other alternative the isogeny pullback has just the one simple irreducible factor proved above.
The conjectural factorization with the cyclotomic parts removed therefore leaves at most one simple rational irreducible factor.

For the unconditional degree estimate let \(g(T)=T^s-T-1\) and \(g^*(T)=1-T^{s-1}-T^s\).
At a common zero \(t\) the equations imply \(t^2+t+1=0\) and \(t^s=1+t=-t^2\), which is impossible since \(t^s\) has order dividing three and \(-t^2\) has order six.
The [two-row cyclotomic degree bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v148.md#two-row) now gives \(\deg C\le24s\) with multiplicities for every allowed offset.
For \(a>24\) a positive nontorsion degree remains, proving the assertion.
This uses the conjecture only for \(H\), extending the [fixed cubic single-factor criterion](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v185.md#schinzel) to a moving seed.
The [general sparse obstruction](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v193.md#obstruction) detects shrinking factors by passing to a suitable subtorus and irreducible component, while here the fixed lift and its entire nontorsion quotient are explicit.
