# Ordinary short intervals and the finite exceptional seed spectrum

<a id="statement"></a>
## Sharp costs without a dilation on shrinking relative intervals

Let \(L\to\infty\) through integers and take any sets \(S_L\subset[L,L+o(L)]\) of distinct positive integers with \(r=|S_L|\to\infty\), \(\Sigma=\sum_{p\in S_L}p\) and integer widths \(b/r\to\infty\).
For example, take every integer from \(L\) to \(L+\lfloor L/\log(2L)\rfloor\).
Use the [common double-contact rows](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#statement) at the exponents themselves, and let \(\eta_L\) be their least normalized log cost allowing any single reciprocal nontorsion unit seed, with cutoff and fallback \(2^\Sigma\).
Write \(\lambda\) for the unrestricted infimum of positive logarithmic Mahler measures.

Call a reciprocal nontorsion unit minimum compressed if distinct conjugates have nontorsion quotient.
Among compressed minima with \(M(P)\le2\), form \(\mathcal E\) from those for which two different complex-conjugation orbits of exterior roots have the same modulus.
Then \(\mathcal E\) is finite.
For every fixed compressed seed with no such equality, at any positive measure, the minimum normalized log cost for this seed without a cutoff converges to \(\log M(P)\) on all the prescribed schedules.

If the unrestricted infimum is approached by compressed seeds outside \(\mathcal E\) with \(M(P)\le2\), then \(\eta_L\to\lambda\).
This holds in particular if \(\lambda\) is unattained, or is an accumulation point of distinct positive measures, or is attained by a compressed seed with no exterior modulus shared between different orbits.
Consequently failure of convergence to \(\lambda\) forces a positive, attained, isolated minimum in the unrestricted spectrum, with every compressed reciprocal minimizer in \(\mathcal E\).

There is also an exact limit without any spectral assumption.
Retain the finite integer row minimum \(N_L\) defining \(\eta_L=\log N_L/\Sigma\), and admit the additional integers
\[
          \lceil M(P)^{\Sigma}\rceil
        \quad (P\in\mathcal E,\ \deg P\le L).                           \tag{1}
\]
The logarithm of the resulting minimum divided by \(\Sigma\) converges to \(\lambda\).
The extra polynomials can be found by finite tests at each stage, and only finitely many different polynomials ever enter (1).
These statements concern undilated exponents and include complete consecutive intervals.

<a id="orbits"></a>
## Separating complex-conjugation orbits

The [exterior-radius independence theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v271.md#statement) applies to compressed reciprocal seeds with bounded positive logarithmic measure and sufficiently large degree.
It gives multiplicative independence of the radii of any two distinct exterior complex-conjugation orbits, since the underlying block size is then at least nine.
In particular those two radii differ.
Thus membership in \(\mathcal E\) forces bounded degree, with the bound taken at the fixed log measure ceiling \(\log 2\).
The elementary coefficient bounds \(|P_i|\le2\binom{\deg P}{i}\) now give finiteness.
At any specified degree cap the integer coefficients, irreducibility, positive unit measure, reciprocity and compression are decidable as in the [finite seed selection](Yin_C_G0345_v487.md#packets).
Comparing the real algebraic numbers \(c\bar c\) across different exterior conjugation orbits is an exact test.
This proves the claimed stagewise enumeration without requiring a numerical upper bound for the last exceptional degree.

For any fixed compressed seed with distinct orbit moduli, each exterior modulus carries either one real conjugate or one nonreal conjugate pair.
Choose \(C_P>1\) close enough to one that the intervals \([\log R,C_P\log R]\) are disjoint for its different exterior moduli \(R\).
There is no restriction on this choice if there is only one orbit.
Eventually \(S_L\subset[L,C_P L]\), and every positive power preserves the seed degree.
Thus the [uniform residue estimates and protected-slot construction](Yin_C_G0345_v531.md#statement) give sharp cost for this seed on the entire supplied schedule.
If its measure is below the cutoff rate \(\log2\), the same rows eventually qualify for the capped minimum.

<a id="spectrum"></a>
## Infima, isolated minima and the finite correction

The [tangency inequality](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#tangencies) gives \(\eta_L\ge\lambda\) for integer exponents by the [degree and exponent recovery](Yin_C_G0345_v523.md#width), and the fallback also exceeds \(\lambda\).
The [reciprocal reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v408.md#lower) and [class compression](Yin_C_G0345_v264.md#labels) preserve the infimum and give \(\lambda<\log2\).
In fact every positive measure sufficiently close to \(\lambda\) that comes from a single irreducible polynomial is the measure of a compressed reciprocal unit, since the nonreciprocal and nonunit lower bounds exceed the known degree-ten value.

The distinct measures of integer polynomials need not all arise from individual irreducibles.
If \(\lambda>0\), then any measure below \(2\lambda\) comes from a single positive-measure irreducible counted once, after discarding zero-measure factors and using the same nonunit bound on the scalar content near \(\lambda\).
If \(\lambda=0\), an infimizing positive sequence always yields positive-measure irreducibles tending to zero by factoring and using nonnegativity.
It follows that if \(\lambda\) is unattained or is accumulated by distinct measures, there exist distinct compressed seed measures approaching \(\lambda\) below the cutoff.
The finite set \(\mathcal E\) accounts for only finitely many distinct measures.
Taking the fixed-seed upper limit for the others proves convergence in these cases, and the same fixed-seed argument applies to a nonexceptional minimizer.
Taking the contrapositive proves the assertion about failure.
Here isolation refers to distinct spectrum values, irrespective of how many polynomials share one value.

Finally all original and added candidates in (1) have normalized log cost at least \(\lambda\).
If no nonexceptional seeds approach \(\lambda\), the reciprocal reduction and finiteness force some \(P\in\mathcal E\) to attain \(\lambda\).
Its integer candidate has normalized log cost at most \(\lambda+(\log2)/\Sigma\) once \(L\ge\deg P\).
This gives the exact corrected limit also in the exceptional case.
For the [critical six-term factors](Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#statement), the large-\(s\) compression follows from [power-field preservation](Yin_C_G0345_v517.md#statement).
Their unbounded degrees and bounded measures put every sufficiently large such factor in the undilated sharp-cost class, conditionally on existence.
