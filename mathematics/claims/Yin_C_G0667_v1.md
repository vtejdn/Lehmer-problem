### FC1: exact forward multiplicities in the actual finite source rows

For odd d>1 with5 not dividing d, the whole primitive-group identities over F_2 are

    W_d(chi)=W_d W_(5d),
    W_(5d)(chi)=W_(25d).                                 (FC1)

They follow by taking all inverse-paired primitive roots z and solving for the order of z when z^5 has the prescribed order.
In the first case the possible orders are d and5d; in the second case the only order is25d.
No multiplicity is lost: chi'=phi^2, but a preimage root of either nonzero primitive trace group is not a primitive fifth trace, since chi sends the latter to0.
Thus the pullbacks are squarefree, and the complete root counts or monic degrees give exactly the stated factors.

For odd n coprime15, the ordinary row B_n contains exactly W_d for d|n,d>1, each once.

    D_(5ell)=B_(5ell)/phi=B_ell(chi)
               =product_(d|ell,d>1) W_d W_(5d).          (FC2)

The d=1 contribution is precisely the removed W_5, with no W_1/X contribution.
Consequently every nonnegative product of C664's chosen rows has the complete factorization

    Y=product_(d>1,gcd(d,15)=1) W_d^(u_d) W_(5d)^(v_d),
    0<=v_d<=u_d, with finite support.                    (FC3)

There are no higher-five factors in this PARTICULAR representative, even though all higher-five images were covered before the finite representative was chosen.
Equation FC1 gives

    Y(chi)=product_d W_d^(u_d) W_(5d)^(u_d) W_(25d)^(v_d),
