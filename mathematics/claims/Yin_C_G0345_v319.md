# Quadratic twists of both Boyd–Mossinghoff trace families have growing measure

<a id="statement"></a>
## Uniform ramification control even when the specialized polynomials split

Use either of the following reciprocal integer polynomials \(H\).

- In the [adjacent family](Yin_C_G0345_v311.md#specialization), let \(H=F(z,-z^n)\), \(n\ge3\), with \(F(x,y)=(1+x)+(1+x+x^2)y+x(1+x)y^2\), and put \(k=1\).
- In the [balanced family](Yin_C_G0345_v313.md#balanced), let \(H=(1-z^a)(1-z^b)(1+z^{a+b})-z^{a+b}\), \(a>b>0\), \(\gcd(a,b)=1\), and put \(k=a-b\).

Write \(H=z^h Q(z+z^{-1})\).
For every monic irreducible trace factor \(T\) of \(Q\), of degree \(d\), and every rational prime \(\ell\),
\[
                \deg\gcd(\overline T,\overline T')\le3k.            \tag{1}
\]
Bars denote reduction modulo \(\ell\).
Let \(K\) be a field generated by a root of \(T\), \(D\) any quadratic number field not contained in \(K\), and \(u\) an algebraic unit generating \(KD\) over \(\mathbb Q\) with \(N_{KD/K}(u)=1\).
With \(r=\max(d-6k,0)\), every rational prime \(\ell\) ramified in \(D\) satisfies
\[
 \frac{r(r-1)}4\log\ell
              \le 2d\log(2d)+2(2d-1)\log M(u).                    \tag{2}
\]
In particular if \(k=o(d)\) these units have \(\log M(u)\ge(\log2/16-o(1))d\).
This conclusion holds for the trace field of every noncyclotomic irreducible factor in the adjacent sequence as \(n\to\infty\), and in the balanced sequence with \(b=420\lfloor p/840\rfloor\), \(a=p-b\), as primes \(p\to\infty\).

For \(n=p-1\) in the adjacent sequence, or this explicit balanced sequence, at every sufficiently large prime **where \(H\) is irreducible**, the minimum exterior count among nontorsion radicals of splitting-field units with \(M(y)\le M(H)\) is the number \(e(H)\) of exterior roots of \(H\) itself.
For the adjacent family this is \((\delta+o(1))p\), with \(\delta=2\arcsin((\sqrt2-1)/2)/\pi\).
The two input measures tend to the common \(\lambda\) reported as about \(1.255433\).
The square-root alternative at reducible primes with no cyclotomic factor persists, with upper limiting measure at most \(\sqrt\lambda<1.121\).
This does not decide which prime branches are infinite.

<a id="elimination"></a>
## A bounded-degree polynomial divisible by the gcd at every prime

Work in Laurent polynomials over \(\mathbb F_\ell\).
If \(H=G(z^k,z^b)\), then \(zH'(z)\) specializes the logarithmic derivative \(D_G=kXG_X+bYG_Y\).
The resultant \(\operatorname{Res}_Y(G,D_G)\) belongs to their ideal over \(\mathbb Z[X]\), by the Sylvester determinant identity.
After substitution and reduction, the gcd of \(H,zH'\) divides this resultant evaluated at \(z^k\), up to Laurent units.
The same argument for the adjacent family uses \(G=F\), \(X=z,Y=-z^n\) and \(D_F=XF_X+nYF_Y\).

In the adjacent case, after a monomial and sign are removed, the resultant is the reciprocal sextic whose coefficients in descending order are
\[
 [A,-1,-8A-2,-14A-1,-8A-2,-1,A],\qquad A=n(n+1).
\]
It remains nonzero modulo every prime.
For the balanced case let \(G=(1-XY)(1-Y)(1+XY^2)-XY^2\), so that \(H=G(z^k,z^b)\).
After removing \(-X^5\), its resultant is reciprocal of degree at most six.
Its degree-six and degree-five coefficients are
\[
 U=b(k+b)(k+2b)^2,\qquad
 V=k^4+16k^3b+63k^2b^2+94kb^3+47b^4 .
\]
If \(b=0\) in the residue field, \(V=k^4\ne0\) by coprimality.
If \(b\ne0\) and \(U=0\), then \(k=-b\) or \(k=-2b\); correspondingly \(V=b^4\) or \(V=-b^4\).
Thus reduction never annihilates the two coefficients simultaneously, including in characteristics two and three.
The following exact determinant calculation verifies the polynomial identities used in both cases.

~~~python
from sympy import Poly, diff, expand, resultant, symbols
X,Y,n,k,b = symbols("X Y n k b")
F = 1+X+(1+X+X**2)*Y+X*(1+X)*Y**2
A = n*(n+1)
terms = [A,-1,-8*A-2,-14*A-1,-8*A-2,-1,A]
E = sum(t*X**j for j,t in enumerate(terms))
assert expand(resultant(F,X*diff(F,X)+n*Y*diff(F,Y),Y)+X*E) == 0
G = (1-X*Y)*(1-Y)*(1+X*Y**2)-X*Y**2
R = Poly(resultant(G,k*X*diff(G,X)+b*Y*diff(G,Y),Y),X)
co = [-R.nth(j+5) for j in range(7)]
assert R.degree()==11 and all(R.nth(j)==0 for j in range(5))
assert co==co[::-1]
assert expand(co[6]-b*(k+b)*(k+2*b)**2)==0
V = k**4+16*k**3*b+63*k**2*b**2+94*k*b**3+47*b**4
assert expand(co[5]-V)==0
assert expand(V.subs(k,-b))==b**4 and expand(V.subs(k,-2*b))==-b**4
~~~

Consequently the Laurent width of any common divisor of \(H,zH'\) is at most \(6k\) in both cases.
If \(g=\gcd(\overline T,\overline T')\), then \(g\) also divides \(\overline Q,\overline Q'\) by the product rule, even if \(T\) occurs repeatedly.
Its pullback \(g(z+z^{-1})\) divides \(H,zH'\) by the chain rule in the Laurent ring.
The pullback width is exactly \(2\deg g\) because the extreme coefficients come from the leading power alone.
This proves (1).

<a id="local"></a>
## Generating units in a quadratic compositum

Over the perfect finite field, the sum of the degrees of the repeated irreducible factors of \(\overline T\), counted with full multiplicities, is at most \(2\deg\gcd(\overline T,\overline T')\).
A root of multiplicity \(m\ge2\) costs at least \(m-1\ge m/2\) in the derivative gcd, including in inseparable characteristic.
The simple factors thus have total degree at least \(r\).
Each lifts by Hensel factorization to the corresponding unramified local field factor of \(K\otimes\mathbb Q_\ell\).
At any \(\ell\) ramifying in \(D\), these unramified base components ramify in \(KD/K\).
Since \(u\) generates the compositum and has relative norm one, the involution over \(K\) takes \(u\) to \(u^{-1}\); its inverse-pair trace field is exactly \(K\).
The [ramified-cluster discriminant bound](Yin_C_G0345_v275.md#cluster) applies at absolute ramification index one to at least \(2r\) embeddings, giving (2), as in the [five-term argument](Yin_C_G0345_v318.md#cluster).
Using \(\log\ell\ge\log2\) proves the asserted growth when \(k/d\to0\).

Every large adjacent noncyclotomic irreducible factor is reciprocal, with degree comparable to \(n\), by [the factor bounds](Yin_C_G0345_v311.md#statement).
Its trace factor is irreducible of half that degree: a reciprocal nontorsion root has degree exactly two over its inverse-pair trace.
The [balanced factor bounds](Yin_C_G0345_v313.md#statement) give the same conclusions there with degree comparable to \(p\) and \(1\le k\le839\).
These facts put every indicated trace field within (2).

## The exact ambient minimum on each irreducible prime branch

Both prime-indexed families have exactly one real exterior conjugate.
For the adjacent family the [factorwise signature](Yin_C_G0345_v312.md#distribution) gives \(c/p\to\delta/2\) along the irreducible primes.
For the balanced family the [half-grid count](Yin_C_G0345_v313.md#neutral) gives at least \((1-\arccos(3/4)/\pi)p-O(1)\) neutral pairs, and the [factor count](Yin_C_G0345_v313.md#statement) gives \(c\gg p\).
In particular both families have \(0<c<(p-1)/4\) for all sufficiently large irreducible indices, with \(c\) the number of nonreal exterior conjugate pairs.
They have the same limiting measure \(\lambda<1.256\) by the [adjacent limit certificate](Yin_C_G0345_v312.md#bound) and [balanced specialization](Yin_C_G0345_v313.md#balanced).
Thus every nontorsion output in the budget is reciprocal by [Smyth's theorem](../audits/literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-u001).

The [twist criterion](Yin_C_G0345_v316.md#twists) leaves only the possibility of reducing the count from \(2c+1\) to \(2c\) by a primitive norm-one unit in \(KD\), for some imaginary quadratic \(D\).
Here \(d=p\) and \(k\) is bounded throughout.
Formula (2) excludes every such unit in the input budget for sufficiently large \(p\), uniformly in \(D\).
The input realizes \(2c+1\), as claimed.
At reducible primes the [adjacent](Yin_C_G0345_v312.md#alternative) and [balanced](Yin_C_G0345_v313.md#dichotomy) results give the separate factor alternative; the adjacent statement requires its specified cyclotomic-free congruence classes.
