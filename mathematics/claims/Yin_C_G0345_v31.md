# A positive-constant Pisot inverse at a selected norm controlled by the Salem degree

<a id="statement"></a>
## A delay-one seed in each prescribed range

Let \(P\) be the monic minimal polynomial of a Salem number \(1<\sigma\le2\), of degree \(d=2m\), and put \(e=\sigma+\sigma^{-1}-2\).
For every real \(L\ge16d\) there exists a monic Pisot minimal polynomial \(f\) of degree \(d\), with positive constant coefficient \(q\) and exterior root \(\theta\), such that
\[
 \begin{gathered}
      3L<q<10L,\qquad zf(z)+f^*(z)=(z+1)P(z),\qquad
      f^*(z)=z^d f(1/z),\\
      0<\theta-q-e<\frac{36d}{q-2}.
 \end{gathered}                                                       \tag{1}
\]
The constant and the error bound are uniform over all these Salem polynomials.
In particular \(q<160d\) suffices for this exact return.
For each \(0<\varepsilon\le1\), one can choose \(q<160d/\varepsilon\) and obtain \(e<\theta-q<e+\varepsilon\).

For any fixed function \(\psi(d)\ge1\) with \(\psi(d)\to\infty\), there exist Salem numbers tending to one if and only if there exist positive-constant Pisot minimal polynomials of degrees \(d\) with
\[
       48d\psi(d)<q<160d\psi(d),\qquad \theta-q\longrightarrow0 .
\]
In the construction from a Salem sequence the degrees agree term by term after omission of any initial \(\sigma>2\).
At relative precision \(1/d\) there is a choice satisfying
\[
       q<2560d^2\left(\frac{\log d}{\log\log d}\right)^6,\qquad
       0<\frac{\theta-q}{e}-1<\frac1d .                               \tag{2}
\]
The norms in (1) and (2) are selected from the indicated ranges.
The [earlier fixed-output theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v2.md#statement) applies at every sufficiently large prescribed \(q\), with constants that can depend on \(P\).
An earlier [degree-dependent threshold](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v33.md#degree-uniform) gives relative accuracy \(1/d\) at every prescribed \(q\ge(2d)^4(32\sqrt d)^d\).

<a id="construction"></a>
## Choosing the residues of an integral trace polynomial

Write \(P=z^m Q(z+z^{-1})\) and \(T=e+2\).
The roots of \(Q\) other than \(T\) are \(x_j=\zeta_j+\zeta_j^{-1}\) for the \(m-1\) conjugate pairs on the unit circle.
The \(\zeta_j\) are nontorsion since \(P\) is irreducible and has an exterior root.
Let \(k\) be the least positive integer such that \(X=\sigma^k\ge L\), and define \(D_k(z+z^{-1})=z^k+z^{-k}\).
The recurrence \(D_0=2,\ D_1=x,\ D_{j+1}=xD_j-D_{j-1}\) gives integral coefficients.
Put \(g=(x+2)(D_k-2)\) and let \(p\) be the remainder of \(gQ'\) modulo the monic \(Q\).
By the [partial fractions of this remainder](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v126.md#seed) its leading coefficient in degree \(m-1\) is \(q-1=g(T)-\sum_j w_j\), where \(w_j=-g(x_j)\in(0,16)\).
Here \(g(T)=(T+2)(X+X^{-1}-2)\), \(4<T+2\le9/2\) and \(L\le X<2L\), giving \(3L<q<10L\).

Take \(f=P-(z-1)z^{m-1}p(z+z^{-1})\), a monic degree-\(d\) integer polynomial with constant \(q\) and the exact return in (1) by reversal.
Its residues in the [Pisot inverse criterion](Yin_C_G0345_v4.md#simplex) are \(b_j=(T-x_j)w_j>0\), and their endpoint sum is
\[
      \sum_j\frac{b_j}{2+x_j}
       =\sum_j(T-x_j)(2-D_k(x_j))<18m<q+T/2 .
\]
The criterion gives a Pisot minimal polynomial, so the interior conjugates have product of moduli \(q/\theta<1\).
The [root equation](Yin_C_G0345_v4.md#second-order) yields
\[
   \theta-q-e
       =\frac e{\theta-1}
        +\sum_{j=1}^{m-1}\frac{(T-x_j)w_j}{\theta+\theta^{-1}-x_j}.
\]
Since \(e\le1/2,\ T-x_j<9/2,\ \theta>q\), the [uniform estimate](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v126.md#cost) is strictly between zero and \(72m/(q-2)\).

<a id="degree"></a>
## Selecting the scale for a varying Salem input

Set \(L=16d/\varepsilon\) for the additive accuracy assertion.
For the sequence equivalence take \(L=16d\psi(d)\).
If \(\sigma\to1\) its degrees tend to infinity by finiteness of bounded-degree monic integer polynomials of bounded Mahler measure; hence (1) gives \(\theta-q\to0\).
The converse follows from the [positive delay-one return and its Salem consequence](Yin_C_G0345_v2.md#optimum).
For (2) use \(\varepsilon=e/d\).
The [explicit height bound of Voutier](../audits/literature/JT26/Yin_U001.md#external-inputs) applies to \(\sigma\) of degree \(d\ge4\), with \(d h(\sigma)=\log\sigma\).
It gives \(e\ge(\log\sigma)^2>\frac1{16}(\log\log d/\log d)^6\), which proves the [relative accuracy estimate](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v126.md#degree).
