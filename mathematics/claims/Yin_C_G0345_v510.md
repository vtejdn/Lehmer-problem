# The degree share of a critical six-term factor determines its measure

<a id="statement"></a>
## A limiting constant for an individual rational factor

Fix positive constants \(C,K\) and let \(s\to\infty\).
Take coprime positive integers \(a,b\) with \(a\ge2\), write \(r=b/a\), and assume \(s\le r\le Cs^3\).
Put \(g(T)=T^s-T-1\), \(B(T)=1-T^{s-1}-T^s\), and let \(P\) be a monic nontorsion irreducible rational factor of \(F(X)=g(X^a)+X^b B(X^a)\) with at most \(Ka\) roots outside the unit circle.
With \(d=\deg P\) and \(m=\log M(P)\), every such sequence satisfies
\[
 \frac{b m}{d}\longrightarrow \mu,
 \qquad \mu=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\log^+|1+e^{it}|\,dt>0 .       \tag{1}
\]
Thus the log measure, even when it tends to zero, is asymptotic to the explicit constant \(\mu\) times the factor's share of the parent degree \(sa+b\).
For the circle conjugates \(x\) of this individual factor, the points \((x^a,x^{as})\), counted with weight \(1/d\), converge to normalized Haar measure on the two-dimensional unit torus.
Their mean of the logarithm with singularities satisfies
\[
             \frac1d\sum_{P(x)=0,\ |x|=1}\log|g(x^a)|\longrightarrow\mu .       \tag{2}
\]
The conclusions give necessary conditions for a factor with these parameters.

<a id="circle"></a>
## Two angular coordinates of a single factor

Write \(k\) for the exterior root count and \(h=am\).
The [critical slope and degree estimates](Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#statement) give reciprocity, \(r\asymp s^3\), \(d\asymp as^2\), \(h\asymp a/s\), \(k\asymp a\), and \(\log a\gg s^{1/3}\log s\).
For any root \(x\), the [degree-preserving projection](Yin_C_G0345_v506.md#discriminant) gives \(y=x^a\) of degree \(d\) and absolute logarithmic height \(h/d\ll s^{-3}\).
Exactly \(2k=O(d/s^2)\) conjugates are off the circle.
For a fixed nonzero character \((i,j)\in\mathbb Z^2\) put \(n=i+js\), eventually nonzero with \(|n|=O_{i,j}(s)\).
The unit \(y^n\) has height at most \(|n|h/d=O_{i,j}(s^{-2})\) and degree \(D\ge d/|n|\), since adjoining \(y\) to its power costs at most \(|n|\).
Each conjugate of \(y^n\) is counted \(d/D\) times by the embeddings of \(\mathbb Q(y)\).
Apply [Pritsker's compact-test bound](../audits/literature/Pri11/Yin_U002.md#pri11-u002) to its monic integral minimum using \(\epsilon=D^{-1}\) and dropping the nonpositive discriminant term.
For the real and imaginary parts of \(z/|z|\) times a piecewise linear radial cutoff supported on \([1/2,2]\) and equal to one at radius one, the tests are compactly supported Lipschitz functions with bounded Dirichlet integral and circle mean zero.
Their bound tends to zero as \(D\to\infty\) and the height tends to zero.
Discarding the off-circle conjugates costs \(O(k/d)\) in these bounded tests, leaving the character mean for the specified circle conjugates.
The trivial character has mean tending to one.
Uniform approximation by trigonometric polynomials proves the asserted joint Haar limit.

<a id="tails"></a>
## Uniform control near the logarithmic singularities

For \(|z|=1\), put \(v=|g(z)|\) and \(S=\{e^{2\pi i/3},e^{-2\pi i/3}\}\).
Since \(\big||1+z|-1\big|\le v\) and \(|1+z|-1\) has two simple angular zeros, \(\operatorname{dist}(z,S)\ll v\) whenever \(v\) is small, with an absolute constant.
At either \(\omega\in S\), \(1+\omega=-\omega^2\) and \(\omega^s\) is a cube root of one, so \(|g(\omega)|\ge1\).
The unit disk derivative bound \(|g'|\le s+1\) now implies \(v\ge c/s\) everywhere on the circle for an absolute \(c>0\).

For \(0<u\le u_0\) small and fixed \(u_0>0\), the proportion with \(|x|=1\) and \(|g(y)|\le u\) is \(O(u+s^{-1/2})\), uniformly.
To see this take the function which equals one within distance \(C_1 u\) of \(S\), decreases linearly to zero over an additional distance \(s^{-1/2}\), and vanishes beyond it.
Here \(C_1\) is the preceding distance constant.
This nonnegative compact test has circle integral \(O(u+s^{-1/2})\), Lipschitz constant \(s^{1/2}\) and planar Dirichlet integral \(O(s)\) by the bounded area of its support.
Apply the same discrepancy inequality to \(y\), now with \(\epsilon=d^{-1}\).
Its additive error is \(O(s^{1/2}/d+s^{1/2}\sqrt{s^{-3}+(\log d)/d})=O(s^{-1})\), using the lower growth of \(d\).
Positivity permits counting only the circle conjugates under this majorant.

For \(0<\delta\le u_0\), replacing \(\log|g(y)|\) by \(\log\max(\delta,|g(y)|)\) in (2) costs at most \(O(\delta+s^{-1/2}\log(2s))\) by integrating this proportion against \(du/u\) from \(c/s\) to \(\delta\) when the interval is nonempty.
At fixed \(\delta\) the joint limit applies to the bounded continuous function \(\log\max(\delta,|w-z-1|)\).
For Haar measure in \((z,w)\) the proportion where \(|w-z-1|\le u\) is \(O(u)\) by the same angular restriction on \(z\).
Consequently the cost of removing the truncation in that integral is \(O(\delta)\).
Letting \(\delta\downarrow0\) proves convergence to the torus mean of \(\log|w-z-1|\), equal by the one-variable Jensen integral in \(w\) to \(\mu\).
The integrand defining \(\mu\) is positive in a neighborhood of \(t=0\).

<a id="norm"></a>
## The integral norm and the remaining conjugates

The resultant of the parent with \(g(X^a)\) has absolute value \(|\operatorname{Res}(g,B)|^a\) by evaluation at the roots of \(g(X^a)\), which are units.
At a zero \(\zeta\) of \(g\) one has \(B(\zeta)=-(\zeta^2+\zeta+1)/\zeta\).
Therefore \(|\operatorname{Res}(g,B)|=|g(e^{2\pi i/3})g(e^{-2\pi i/3})|\) belongs to \([1,4]\).
The complete norm \(|\operatorname{Res}(P,g(X^a))|\) is a positive integer divisor of this parent resultant, so its logarithm divided by \(d\) is \(O(a/d)=o(1)\).

For each reciprocal off-circle pair take the inside member \(x\) and put \(z=x^a\), \(L=-\log |z|\).
Then \(g(z)=-x^b B(z)\) and \(g(1/z)=z^{-s}B(z)\), whence the pair contributes exactly \(-(r-s)L+2\log|B(z)|\) to the norm logarithm.
By [localization at every occupied zero](Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#collisions), \(z\) lies within \(O(s^{-4})\) of an interior zero \(\zeta\) of \(g\).
Each such zero is at distance at least \(1/(s+1)\) from each \(\omega\in S\), by the same derivative bound and \(|g(\omega)|\ge1\).
The two cube roots are separated by \(\sqrt3\), so \(|\zeta^2+\zeta+1|\gg1/s\).
The expression for \(B(\zeta)\), \(|\zeta|<1\), and \(|B'|\le2s\) throughout the disk imply \(c'/s\le |B(z)|\le3\) with an absolute \(c'>0\) for large \(s\).
Thus the total error from these logarithms is \(O(k\log(2s))=o(d)\).
Since the sum of \(L\) over these pairs is \(h\), subtracting the off-circle contributions from the norm identity gives \((r-s)h/d\to\mu\) by (2).
Finally \(s/r\to0\) and \(rh=bm\), which yields (1).

An agent for Sheng Yin obtained the factorwise limiting constant by separating the integral norm into its circle and off-circle contributions.
