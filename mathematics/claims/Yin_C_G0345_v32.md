# A semicircle trace-residue criterion for Salem numbers tending to one

<a id="statement"></a>
## Prescribing the distribution and the integer branch

Fix \(\lambda\ge0\).
For every sequence of Salem numbers \(\sigma_i\to1\) with minimal polynomials \(P_i\) of degrees \(d_i\), there are monic Pisot minimal polynomials \(f_i\) of the same degrees, with roots \(\theta_i\), positive integer constants \(q_i\), and
\[
     z f_i+f_i^*=(z+1)P_i,\qquad
     q_i/d_i\to\infty,\qquad \theta_i-q_i\to0 .
\]
With \(T_i=\sigma_i+\sigma_i^{-1}\) and \(j_i=[z](f_i/f_i^*)-q_i^2+1\), these representatives satisfy
\[
            q_i(T_i-2)/d_i\to\lambda,\qquad j_i/d_i\to2+\lambda .
\]
The [positive trace residues](Yin_C_G0345_v7.md#trace) of the seed have total mass \(j_i\).
After division by \(d_i\) their measures converge weakly to \(2\mu_{\rm sc}+\lambda\delta_2\), where the semicircle probability \(\mu_{\rm sc}\) has density \(\sqrt{4-x^2}/(2\pi)\) on \((-2,2)\).
In particular, the existence of Salem numbers tending to one is equivalent to the existence of positive-constant Pisot minimal polynomials with \(\theta_i-q_i\to0,\ q_i/d_i\to\infty,\ j_i/d_i\to2\) and normalized trace-residue probabilities tending to \(\mu_{\rm sc}\).
This statement does not assert that either sequence exists.

<a id="argument"></a>
## The two phases of the exact inverse seed

Write \(P=z^{d/2}S(z+z^{-1})\) and \(D_k(z+z^{-1})=z^k+z^{-k}\).
All sufficiently late terms have \(\sigma\le2\), and the [fixed-output inverse](Yin_C_G0345_v2.md#statement) supplies the same degree and reciprocal identity at any earlier terms.
The [integer inverse construction](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v126.md#seed) uses \(g(x)=(x+2)(D_k(x)-2)\) and the remainder of \(gS'\) modulo \(S\).
The [exact partial fractions](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v129.md#residues) give weight \((x^2-4)(D_k(x)-2)\) at each root \(x\) of \(S\).
For \(u=T-2\), the nonzero integer \(S(2)\) and one-dimensional equidistribution give \(\log(1/u)=o(d)\) by truncating \(\log(2-x)\) from below on the circle traces.
Choose \(\sigma^k\sim \lambda d/(4u)\) for \(\lambda>0\), or \(\sigma^k\sim d\min(\log(d+2),u^{-1/2})\) for \(\lambda=0\).
Then \(q\sim4\sigma^k,\ q/d\to\infty,\ qu/d\to\lambda\) and the [root excess bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v126.md#cost) gives \(\theta-q\to0\).

The [joint application of Bilu's theorem](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v129.md#limit) uses \(k\to\infty\) and \((1+k)\log(\sigma)/d\to0\).
Each fixed nonzero character is eventually nontrivial on \((\sigma,\sigma^k)\), so the phases converge in distribution to the product of two circle Haar probabilities over the full Galois orbit.
The two real conjugates can be discarded for the bounded tests on the circle part; their exterior trace residue is computed separately.
Averaging the neutral weights yields \((4-x^2)dx/(\pi\sqrt{4-x^2})=2\,d\mu_{\rm sc}(x)\) per unit degree, accounting for the two circle phases above each interior trace.
The exterior trace contributes \(u g(T)/d\to\lambda\) at \(T\to2\).
Taking total mass gives the branch ratio.
The converse at \(\lambda=0\) follows from the [positive-constant Pisot criterion](Yin_C_G0345_v2.md#statement) on the same seeds.
