# All degrees represented by a bounded common support

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact progressions and a bound for the first passing degree

Fix a prime \(p>3\), a positive integer cap \(C\) on the normalized span of the union of the two supports, and the width \(b=5d/2-1\) at each even positive \(d\).
Use either coefficient budget of the [narrow-width tests](Yin_C_G0345_v473.md#statement), including nonzero determinant in the product test.
One may additionally charge the full width cost \((2pb+1)^4\) in that budget.
The two signs must have genuine double contacts at the same reciprocal nontorsion unit \(\gamma\) and its square, both of degree \(d\).

For each coefficient-only test the set of *all* passing degrees is an effectively computable finite union of one-sided arithmetic progressions.
Its density is a computable rational number.
With the width cost the progressions are truncated at explicit upper endpoints.
Each presentation on a progression has one fixed positive logarithmic Mahler measure, computable from a bounded radical at its base.
Thus one can also list the distinct measures that actually occur, with duplicate presentations identified by equality of measures.

If \(C\le p\), define the integers
\[
 B_C=\max(4,1+\lceil\log^3(6C)\rceil),\qquad
 M_C=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\dots,B_C)^{\,B_C\lceil\log_2 B_C\rceil}.
\]
Every individual progression before truncation can be chosen to have step at most \(4p C M_C\) and first degree at most \(8p C M_C\).
In particular, for *either* budget convention existence at any degree is equivalent to existence at some degree \(d\le8p C M_C\).
For the coefficient-only budget one passing degree gives lower asymptotic density at least \(1/(4p C M_C)\).
These bounds are uniform in the two rows and their widths for the chosen \(p,C\).
They can be very large even at moderate caps.

<a id="fields"></a>
## Uniform control of the radical orders

Use the [common compression](Yin_C_G0345_v474.md#compression), writing \(c\le C\) for the actual joint normalized span.
Enumerate the finitely many possible pairs of coefficient lists, the displacement \(u\), the stride quotient \(\ell\), and the unit \(x\) with its square on the two compressed graphs.
Keep the exact prescribed numerator, multiplicity and determinant conditions.
The compressed exponent is \(p\ell\), \(1\le\ell\le2,\ |u|\le3pc\), and \(e=[\mathbb Q(x):\mathbb Q]\le2c\).
In this paragraph \(m(x)\) denotes logarithmic Mahler measure.
For the row of smaller coefficient one-norm \(H\), both coefficient budgets give \(H^2<2^{p/4}\).
The [compressed derivative polynomial](Yin_C_G0345_v474.md#compression) is nonzero, has norm at most \((2p\ell c+|u|)H^2\le7pcH^2\), and contains the minimum of \(x^{p\ell}\).
Because that power retains the field of \(x\),
\[
                   m(x)\le\frac{\log(7pc)+2\log H}{p\ell}.
\]
For \(c\le C\le p\) the right side is less than \(2\), since \(\log(7p^2)/p\) decreases for \(p\ge5\) and \((\log2)/4+\log(175)/5<2\).
The [uniform radical modulus](Yin_C_G0345_v475.md#uniform) therefore gives the displayed \(M_C\) simultaneously for every compressed \(x\).
Without \(C\le p\) one can compute a modulus separately for each \(x\) by the [fixed-field theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v475.md#statement), which suffices for the exact sets and densities.

There is a separate source for the existence and computability of a modulus at a fixed \(x\).
The [cyclotomic Kummer degree theorem](../audits/literature/PST21/Yin_U001.md#pst21-u001), specialized to the one nontorsion element \(x\) in \(F=\mathbb Q(x)\), gives a computable \(M\) with the maximal relative degree from \(m=\gcd(M,g)\) to \(g\).
For \(k=g/m,\ \beta^m=x,\ \gamma^k=\beta\), set \(L_m=F(\zeta_m,\beta)\).
The equality \([F(\zeta_g,\gamma):L_m]=(\varphi(g)/\varphi(m))k\) attains both degree upper bounds in the tower through \(L_m(\zeta_g)\).
Hence \(T^k-\beta\) is irreducible over \(L_m(\zeta_g)\) and over \(\mathbb Q(\beta)\).
The numerical bound uniform in \(C\) uses the elementary height estimate above.

<a id="degrees"></a>
## Keeping exactly the radical degrees that pass

For a modulus \(M\) valid for \(x\), run over \(m\mid M\) and take the roots \(\beta^m=x\) up to conjugacy over \(\mathbb Q(x)\).
Write \(f=[\mathbb Q(\beta):\mathbb Q]\).
Keep \(\beta\) exactly when it is reciprocal and \([\mathbb Q(\beta^2):\mathbb Q]=f\).
It is then a nontorsion unit and \(f\) is even.
The [factor and square-degree identities](Yin_C_G0345_v475.md#square) say that the required radicals occur exactly at
\[
         k\ge1\text{ odd},\qquad \gcd(k,M/m)=1,\qquad
         g=mk,\qquad d=fk .
\]
Indeed \(m=\gcd(g,M)\) is equivalent to the displayed coprimality at \(g=mk\), and the minimum of every such \(\gamma\) with \(\gamma^k=\beta\) is \(P_\beta(T^k)\).
It follows directly from its roots that \(m(\gamma)=m(\beta)\), including all exterior roots.
The multiplicative measures of algebraic units are positive real algebraic numbers by the conjugate product, so equality of the listed logarithmic measures is decidable.

The [remaining placement constraints](Yin_C_G0345_v474.md#progressions) for these data reduce to three tests.
Put \(A=5f/2-m\ell c\).
Then \(g\ell c\) is the actual span before translation, \(b-g\ell c=Ak-1\), and the common translation \(w\) is given by \(2p w=1-p+k(pA-mu)\).
Thus the tests for integer \(w\) between zero and \(b-g\ell c\) are exactly
\[
 \begin{split}
 k(pA-mu)&\equiv p-1\pmod{2p},\\
 k(pA-mu)&\ge p-1,\qquad k(pA+mu)\ge p+1 .
 \end{split}
\]
Both coefficients of \(k\) in the inequalities must be positive.
When they are, their common lower cutoff for integer \(k\ge1\) is at most \(p+1\).
The parity, coprimality and congruence tests are periodic in \(k\) with period \(T=2pM/m\).
Take each allowed residue class from its first integer above or equal to the cutoff.
The reverse substitution in the compression theorem proves that *every* degree \(fk\) in this list passes.
A root with the requisite fields and graph contacts exists at each of those degrees.
Conversely each genuine pair produces such a presentation.
The degree-dependent budget, when used, is monotone in \(k\) for fixed compressed rows and therefore truncates each class at its last passing integer.

If \(M=M_C\) then \(f\le em\le2Cm\), so \(fT\le4pCM_C\).
The first degree on an accepted residue class is at most \(f(p+1+T)\le8pCM_C\).
A nonempty truncated progression retains its first degree.
For the coefficient-only test any one progression gives the stated density lower bound.
Computing the union over a common period counts a degree only once, even when multiple coefficient pairs or different measures occur there.
All these enumerations are finite regardless of the size of their periods.

<a id="criterion"></a>
## Exclusion at slowly increasing spans

In the [gap criterion with a joint span cap](Yin_C_G0345_v473.md#gap), one may use this exact count of all placements in each block.
One may also test emptiness at all degrees at any sequence of primes \(p\to\infty\) and any caps \(C_p\to\infty\) with \(C_p\le p\).
If the full coefficient test, or the test charging width, is empty at infinitely many of these primes, then \(\lambda\ge(\log2)/120\).
Indeed any strictly smaller positive measure yields a fixed base whose exact-width witnesses meet the relevant budget at every late prime, with joint normalized span bounded by its fixed base width.
The test can be decided at each prime using only the bounded search or the progressions above.
An exclusion for an unbounded sequence of primes, or a sequence of positive measures tending to zero, remains to be established.
