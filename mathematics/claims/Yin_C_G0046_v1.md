## 2. Full-signature norm floor for every actual half-profile

Let P be ANY ACTUAL monic irreducible reciprocal nontorsion integer-unit polynomial of degree d>=2.
Put K=Q(alpha), sigma(alpha)=alpha^(-1), O0=Z[alpha], m=m(P)>0, and n=d-1.
Take ANY finite family of nonzero actual u_b in O0 and positive normalized rational weights lambda_b with

    sum_b lambda_b u_b sigma(u_b)=1/2 in K.             (1)

Use their unique ordinary integer representatives f_b of degree at most n, and put

    G(z)=sum_b lambda_b |f_b(z)|^2 for |z|=1,
    C=max_(|z|=1)G(z),
    N_b=Norm_(K/Q)(u_b) in Z minus{0},
    Lambda=(1/d)sum_b lambda_b log|N_b|>=0.            (2)

There is no assumption of constant circle covariance, no restriction on individual coefficient heights, finite family size, minimum positive weight, ideal inventory or unit status.
Finiteness makes every sum and logarithm below well-defined.
Irreducibility and u_b!=0 imply f_b(alpha_i)!=0 at EVERY original root.

Let h be the number of original roots on the unit circle and k=d-h.
Then k>0 and

    k log C >= h log2 +2d Lambda -2n m.               (3)

Consequently, for the FULL C43 profile infimum, attained or not,

    c_infty(P)>=max{1, exp[(h log2-2(d-1)m)/(d-h)]}.  (4)

Only the finite class already defined in C43 is optimized.
Formula(4) includes all reciprocal trace signatures, without assuming that the trace field is totally real.
The factor h counts actual original roots, including both members of every circle pair; it is not a trace degree.

**Proof.**
The coefficient mean is sum_b lambda_b sum_j f_(b,j)^2>=1, since each f_b is a nonzero integer polynomial.
Thus C>=1.

For 0<=r<1, C10's positive Poisson kernel K_r has normalized integral1 and reproduces each finite polynomial at rz from its boundary values.
Its Cauchy--Schwarz inequality, multiplied by lambda_b and summed, gives

    sum_b lambda_b |f_b(rz)|^2
       <=integral K_r(theta)G(z exp(i theta))dtheta/(2pi)
       <=C,                 |z|=1.

Continuity covers the closed disk.
Each reversed polynomial z^n f_b(z^(-1)) is an ordinary polynomial even when f_b has smaller degree.
Real coefficients give the SAME weighted boundary square as G.
Applying the same disk bound to that family yields, for every complex z,

    sum_b lambda_b |f_b(z)|^2
                    <=C max(1,|z|)^(2n).             (5)

This uses a boundary UPPER bound, not lag cancellation; no exact complementary packet is inferred.
Finite concavity of log now gives at any off-circle root alpha_i

    sum_b lambda_b log|f_b(alpha_i)|
                      <=(1/2)log C+n log^+|alpha_i|. (6)

At a circle root alpha_i, evaluating the exact field identity(1) gives instead

    sum_b lambda_b |f_b(alpha_i)|^2=1/2,
    sum_b lambda_b log|f_b(alpha_i)|<=-(1/2)log2.    (7)

Here sigma corresponds to alpha_i^(-1), which equals its complex conjugate at exactly these circle roots.
Summing (6)--(7) over ALL original roots, with m=sum_i log^+|alpha_i| and the nonzero full norm in(2), gives

    d Lambda <=(k/2)log C+n m-(h/2)log2.

If k=0, then also m=0 and the right side is negative, contradicting Lambda>=0.
Hence k>0 for every actual profile, independently of any separate invocation of Kronecker.
Rearrangement proves(3).
Dropping Lambda>=0 and taking the infimum preserves the weak inequalities in(4), even if the infimum is unattained.
