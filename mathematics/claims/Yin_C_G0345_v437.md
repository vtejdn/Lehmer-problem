# A prime-width criterion for simultaneous residue multiplicities

<a id="statement"></a>
## Divisibility of a passing row modulo its exponent

Let \(p\) be an odd prime, \(b\ge1,\ a\ge2\) integers, and \(A\in\mathbb Z[Y]\) primitive of formal width \(b\).
Suppose \(((2pb+1)\|A\|_1^2)^4<2^{(a-1)p}\).
Fix either sign, and select at least two distinct monic reciprocal irreducible minima \(P_i\) of nontorsion units that divide \(A(X^p)+\varepsilon X A_b^*(X^p)\) to order at least \(a\) and have \(A(c^p)\ne0\) at their roots.
Write \(F=\prod_iP_i\), \(D=\deg F\), and \(E(\xi)=\operatorname{ord}_\xi\bar F\) for the multiplicity in the residue field at \(p\), extended to a splitting field.
If
\[
 \frac{2b}{a-1}\le p+1,\qquad
 (a+1)^2 b\le (a-1)p\log_2 p ,
 \tag{1}
\]
then
\[
    \prod_\xi(Y-\xi)^{\lfloor aE(\xi)/2\rfloor}\mid\bar A(Y),\qquad
    \sum_\xi\lfloor aE(\xi)/2\rfloor\le b .
 \tag{2}
\]
In particular \(\lfloor a/2\rfloor D\le b\).
The second inequality of (1) suffices on its own if the maximum multiplicity of each individual \(\bar P_i\) on every disk is already known to be less than \(p-1\).

At every prime \(p\ge257\), (2) holds in the [original count](Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#statement) with its prescribed \(a,b\).
For the [fixed-double test](Yin_C_G0345_v423.md#statement) it gives \(\bar F\mid\bar A\) and \(D\le b\) for every \(b\le(p+1)/2\) at those primes.
Scalar content can be removed before applying the assertion.

<a id="energy"></a>
## Using the budget to control every occupied disk

Put \(H=\|A\|_1\), \(B=(\log 2)/4\), and \(e_\xi=\max_i\operatorname{ord}_\xi\bar P_i\).
The [product divisibility for the derivative polynomial](Yin_C_G0345_v419.md#product) and the strict budget give
\[
       (a-1)D\le2b,\qquad m(F)<B,\qquad \log H<(a-1)p\log(2)/8 .
\]
Here \(m(F)=\log M(F)\); each individual measure is at most \(m(F)\).
A rational unit is torsion, and an odd-degree reciprocal irreducible unit polynomial of degree greater than one cannot occur because of its root \(-1\).
A degree-two reciprocal nontorsion unit minimum has the form \(X^2-sX+1\) with integral \(|s|\ge3\), hence multiplicative measure at least \((3+\sqrt5)/2>e^B\).
Thus every selected degree is at least four.
There are at least two selected factors, so \(e_\xi\le D-4\le p-3\) by the first inequality of (1).
The alternative hypothesis gives \(e_\xi<p-1\) directly.

Use the [powered resultant inequality](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v264.md#energy) at order \(a\) for every selected factor.
It gives
\[
 (a-1)\log p\sum_\xi E(\xi)^2
 \le 2D\log H+2pb\,m(F)<pb\log 2 .
\]
Indeed the degree estimate bounds the first term strictly by \(pb\log(2)/2\), and the measure estimate does the same for the second.
The second inequality of (1) now yields \((a+1)E(\xi)<p\) on every occupied disk.
Consequently \(k_\xi=\lfloor a E(\xi)/2\rfloor\) satisfies \(2k_\xi-1<p-e_\xi\).
The [local coefficient divisibilities](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v264.md#digits) give multiplicity at least \(k_\xi\) in \(\bar A\) at \(\xi^p\).
The list and its weights are permuted by Frobenius, so the same product divides \(\bar A\) at the stated residues.
Primitivity makes the reduction nonzero, proving (2), including the stronger local exponent when \(a\) is odd and several roots coincide.

<a id="widths"></a>
## The natural widths at all primes from 257

For \(a=2\) and \(b\le(p+1)/2\), the first inequality of (1) holds directly.
Also \(9b\le9(p+1)/2<8p\le p\log_2 p\).
For the original \(a=\lfloor p^{1/8}\rfloor\ge2,\ b=1+\lfloor p^{7/8}\rfloor\), one has \(p^{7/8}<p/2\), so \(b\le(p+1)/2\).
Moreover \(ab/p\le1+a/p\), and \(a/p\le1/4\) since \(p\ge257\).
Therefore
\[
   \frac{(a+1)^2 b}{(a-1)p}
      \le \frac{(a+1)^2}{a(a-1)}(1+a/p)
      \le \frac92\cdot\frac54 <8\le\log_2 p .
\]
This verifies both conditions without a separate residue calculation for each new prime.
The [small-budget reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#upper) gives reciprocal unit minima for all qualifying factors in the original test.
A row of nontrivial scalar content becomes primitive after division and still passes, with the same genuine contacts.

For the particular fixed-double pair \(c,c^2\) with all powers generating \(\mathbb Q(c)\), the two degrees agree and the positive measures are \(m_c,2m_c\).
If \(c\) is a reciprocal unit, a passing row therefore forces \(2[\mathbb Q(c):\mathbb Q]\le b\) throughout the half-prime width range and \(M(c)<2^{1/12}\).
This degree restriction still permits every fixed degree as \(b\) increases.
