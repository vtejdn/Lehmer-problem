# Degree bounds from four choices per coefficient on moving trace grids

<a id="statement"></a>
## Specifying only the first grid moments

Let \(q\ge2\) be an integer, \(p=q+1,\ M=qp,\ L=2q+1,\ h=1/M,\ \rho=q/p\).
Let \(H\) be monic integral of degree \(n\ge4\), with \(M^nH(h)=L^2\).
At \(n\) nodes \(t_i=2\cos\theta_i-2\), \(0=\theta_0<\cdots<\theta_{n-1}=\pi\), require \(H(0)<0\) and strictly alternating signs.
Write \(U(z)=\prod_i(1-2z\cos\theta_i+z^2)\).
Fix any \(V\in\mathbb Z[[z]]\) with \(V(0)=1,\ [z]V=0\).

* If \(U\equiv V\bmod z^{k+1}\) and \(1\le k\le(n-2)/2\), the reciprocal polynomial \(S(z)=z^nH(z+z^{-1}-2)\) has at most four choices for each successive coefficient through index \(k\) given its predecessors.
  For grids varying with \(n\) and agreeing with this same \(V\) through \(k=\lfloor\log_M n\rfloor\), the number of possible degrees \(n\le X\) is \(O_q(X^{\log 4/\log M})\), uniformly in \(V\).
* At \(q=40\) no degree \(23\le n\le10^{24}\) occurs if the grid is within total angular displacement \(1/256\) of any reference grid with \(U_{\rm ref}\equiv(1-z^2)^e\bmod z^{11}\), for either \(e=1\) or \(2\).
  Total displacement is \(\sum_i|\theta_i-\theta_i^{\rm ref}|\) in angular order.
  The [integer census](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0185/grid_moment_digits.md#census) certifies the finite assertion.

The first reference class includes the shifted Chebyshev extrema from [the earlier exclusion](Yin_C_G0345_v887.md#statement).
The second includes the two endpoints together with any disjoint union of complete internal level sets \(\cos(m_i\theta)=a_i\), with integer orders \(m_i\ge11\) and real levels \(-1<a_i<1\), where \(n=2+\sum_i m_i\).
Indeed each level set contributes the reciprocal factor \(1-2a_i z^{m_i}+z^{2m_i}\), and the endpoints contribute \((1-z^2)^2\).
Individual levels, orders and their number can vary between instances.

<a id="moments"></a>
## A moment with only one exterior contribution

Put \(Q(x)=\prod_i(x-t_i)\), \(r_i=-H(t_i)/Q'(t_i)>0\), \(b_i=r_i/(h-t_i)\), and \(\delta=H(h)/Q(h)=L^2/\prod_i(1-Mt_i)>0\).
By the [pole weights](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0185/trace_grid.md#pole-weights), \(\sum b_i=1-\delta\) and
\[
 H(x)/Q(x)=\delta+(x-h)\sum_i b_i/(x-t_i).
\]
For \(A(z)=1-(2+h)z+z^2\), define \(F_U=(1-z^2)S/(A U)\).
Substitute \(x=z+z^{-1}-2\) in the preceding identity.
The geometric series \((1-z^2)/(1-2cz+z^2)\) has coefficient \(2\cos(j\theta)\) at \(j\ge1\) when \(c=\cos\theta\).
The exterior denominator \(A=(1-\rho z)(1-\rho^{-1}z)\) gives coefficient \(\rho^j+\rho^{-j}\) instead.
Thus \(F_U(0)=1\), and
\[
 -2<[z^j]F_U=\delta(\rho^j+\rho^{-j})+2\sum_i b_i\cos(j\theta_i)
 \le 2+\delta(\rho^j+\rho^{-j}-2).
\]
The lower bound is strict even at maximal alignment, since \(\delta>0\).

When \([z]U=0\), the node sum is \(-2n\).
Concavity of \(\log(1-Mt)\) gives \(\log(1-Mt)\ge(-t/4)\log(1+4M)\) on \([-4,0]\).
Hence \(\delta\le L^{2-n}\).
For \(2j\le n-2\), multiplication of the excess in the upper bound by \(M^j\) gives less than \(L^{2-n}p^{2j}<1\).
If \(U\equiv V\bmod z^{j+1}\), the coefficient of \(F_V=(1-z^2)S/(A V)\) agrees with that of \(F_U\), and is an integer multiple of \(M^{-j}\), by inversion of the unit-constant series at \(Mz\).
Consequently \(-2<[z^j]F_V\le2\).
Since \(c_j=[z^j]S\) enters this coefficient with multiplier one, exactly four consecutive integer choices pass this necessary interval.

<a id="degrees"></a>
## The even degree classes

Let \(D=2n\).
At \(b=q,-p\), reciprocity gives \((1+b)^D S(b/(1+b))=L^2\).
Two degrees with the same prefix through \(k\) therefore satisfy \((1+b)^{|D-D'|}\equiv1\bmod |b|^{k+1}\), as the truncated \(S\) is a unit there.
The [local order argument](Yin_C_G0345_v887.md#digits) applies at odd primes and whenever \(4\mid b\).
If the even \(b\) is \(2\bmod4\) and \(d=|D-D'|>0\), write \(d=2s\).
Since \((1+b)^2-1=b(b+2)\) is divisible by eight, successive squaring and taking odd powers give \(v_2((1+b)^d-1)=v_2(b+2)+v_2(d)\).
Here \(b+2\ne0\) for the allowed \(q\).
It follows that \(M^k\mid C_q(D-D')\), with \(C_q=1\) if \(4\mid M\) and \(C_q=2^{v_2(b+2)-1}\) otherwise.
On the block \(M^k\le n<M^{k+1}\), there are at most \(4^k\) prefixes and \(O_q(1)\) degrees per prefix.
The finitely many small degrees outside \(1\le k\le(n-2)/2\) contribute \(O_q(1)\).
Summing the geometric bound over blocks proves the count.

<a id="perturbation"></a>
## Displacement of a reference grid at height forty

For the reference grids in the finite assertion let \(\eta=\sum_i|\theta_i-\theta_i^{\rm ref}|\le1/256\).
Their reference node sum is \(-2n\), and the actual sum is at most \(-2n+2\eta\) by the cosine Lipschitz bound.
The same chord estimate gives \(\delta\le L^{2-n+\eta}\).
For \(n\ge23,\ 1\le j\le10\), one has \(\rho^{-j}\le(41/40)^{10}<2\), and hence \(|[z^j]F_U|<201/100\).
The [logarithmic perturbation estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v887.md#fourier) bounds the sum of absolute nonconstant coefficients through degree ten of \(U/U_{\rm ref}\) by \(\exp(20\eta)-1<1/12\).
Through that degree \(F_V=F_U(U/U_{\rm ref})\) for \(V=(1-z^2)^e\), so
\[
 |[z^j]F_V|< (201/100)(1+1/12)<9/4 .
\]
Using the enclosing closed interval and the two degree congruences, the calculation exhausts all ten-digit prefixes for each \(V\).
Both lists are empty at the tenth position.
No bound on degrees for arbitrary interlacing grids follows from this exclusion.
