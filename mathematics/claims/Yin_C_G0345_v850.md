# Residue orders in a two-dimensional family of steep sections

<a id="statement"></a>
## The certificate after imposing prime and order cutoffs

For sufficiently large integers \(M\), set \(h=\lceil(\log M)^6\rceil\), \(A=M^2h\), \(Y=\sqrt A\) and \(T=\lfloor(A/\log M)^{1/3}\rfloor\).
Take the primitive integer directions \(M\le k<2M,\ Mh\le l<2Mh\) and the [integer certificate](Yin_C_G0345_v827.md#statement) \(U\) for \(P=F(X^k,X^l)\).
These sections are squarefree and the exceptional form \(B(k,l)\) is nonzero throughout the box by the [steep-box argument](Yin_C_G0345_v849.md#box).
At a good prime \(p\nmid B(k,l)\) choose the [geometric critical disks of positive even contact](Yin_C_G0345_v833.md#weights), and at a bad prime \(p\mid B(k,l)\) choose all multiple geometric residue roots.
Let \(U_*\) retain the full \(p\)-part of \(U\) if some chosen section coordinate \(x\) has multiplicative order \(>T\) and the prime exceeds its size cutoff (\(Y\) for a good prime and \(A\) for a bad prime).
Outside \(O_F(A/\log M)\) primitive directions,
\[
                      \log(U/U_*)\ll_F Y.                              \tag{1}
\]
For every factorization into complementary monic reciprocal integer factors at the same directions, the actual logarithmic resultant contribution from contacts failing either cutoff is also \(O_F(Y)\), including low-order contacts at primes retained in \(U_*\).
The bound is \(o(d)\) for every irreducible factor degree \(d\), and each remaining reducible section satisfies
\[
                \log U_*\gg_F l(\log\log l)^4/(\log l)^3.               \tag{2}
\]
Here \(T\asymp M^{2/3}(\log M)^{5/3}\) holds with exceptions counted in the whole box.
On the particular graph \(l=hk+1\), the [graph estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v847.md#statement) gives the order cutoff \(M^{1/2}\) with its own exceptional count.

<a id="classes"></a>
## Cyclotomic norms for projective exponent classes

Fix an order \(g\) and a primitive complex \(g\)-th root \(\zeta\).
Primitivity of \((k,l)\) makes its reduction modulo \(g\) a pair generating the unit ideal.
Group such pairs into classes under simultaneous multiplication by a unit modulo \(g\), choosing one representative \((a,b)\) per class.
There are \(\psi(g)=g\prod_{q\mid g}(1+1/q)\) classes, since \(g^2\prod_{q\mid g}(1-1/q^2)\) pairs split into free orbits of size \(\varphi(g)\), where the products run over primes.
For \(g=1\) there is one class.
The algebraic integer \(c=F(\zeta^a,\zeta^b)\) is nonzero by [torus torsion exclusion](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#torsion), and every complex conjugate has absolute value at most the sum of the absolute coefficients of \(F\).
Consequently, writing \(f_{\mathfrak p}\) for the residue degree of a prime ideal of \(\mathbb Z[\zeta]\) above \(p\),
\[
 \sum_{\mathfrak p\mid (c)} f_{\mathfrak p}\log p
 \le \log |N_{\mathbb Q(\zeta)/\mathbb Q}(c)|\ll_F\varphi(g).        \tag{3}
\]

At an occurrence with section coordinate \(x\) of order \(g\), necessarily \(p\nmid g\).
Write \((k,l)\equiv t(a,b)\pmod g\) for its unit \(t\).
Sending \(\zeta\) to \(x^t\) defines a residue homomorphism into \(\overline{\mathbb F}_p\), since the primitive \(g\)-th roots are precisely the zeros of \(\Phi_g\) in this characteristic.
Its kernel \(\mathfrak p\) divides \((c)\), and the images \((u,v)\) of \((\zeta^a,\zeta^b)\) are \((x^k,x^l)\).
For a fixed direction and residue homomorphism, there is at most one such \(x\), since raising to \(t\) permutes the primitive \(g\)-th roots.
There are \(f_{\mathfrak p}\) residue embeddings for this ideal, permuted transitively by Frobenius.

<a id="spacing"></a>
## The number and weight of occurrences at one prime ideal

For \(p>Y\), the [smooth torus reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v813.md#density) gives \((uF_U,vF_V)\ne(0,0)\) for all sufficiently large \(M\).
Multiple contact forces \(k uF_U+l vF_V=0\), so the point determines a unique projective direction modulo \(p\) over the algebraic closure.
An occurrence makes this direction rational over \(\mathbb F_p\), since \((k,l)\) is primitive, and then Frobenius fixes the direction for all residue embeddings of \(\mathfrak p\).
Thus for any two occurring directions of the same class and ideal, \(kl'-k'l\) is divisible by both \(p\) and \(g\), hence by \(pg\).
Distinct primitive positive pairs have distinct slopes, so the spacing between their ratios \(l/k\) is at least \(pg/(4M^2)\).
These ratios lie between \(h/2\) and \(2h\), giving \(O(1+A/(pg))\) directions at the ideal.

Charge each multiple geometric residue disk of order \(g\) the weight \((\log p)\sum_{z\bmod p=x}v_p(P'(z))\) over the roots of \(P\), with \(v_p(p)=1\).
The [order-sensitive valuation argument](Yin_C_G0345_v825.md#valuation) gives \(v_p(P'(z))\ll_F g\) here since \(\log(3+k+l)/\log p\) is bounded throughout the box and its slopes eventually avoid the fixed exceptional directions.
In rank two its bound is \(O_F(g(1+\log(3+k+l)/\log p)^4)\), and in rank at most one the height and exponent estimates give the same conclusion as in the [large-prime calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v833.md#spacing).
At most [32 roots](Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#ramification) belong to multiple disks at a prime, so each occurrence costs \(O_F(g\log p)\).
For a given class and ideal the number of geometric disks per occurring direction is at most \(f_{\mathfrak p}\) by the embedding count above.
Multiplying the spacing bound by this disk count and the weight, and then applying (3), costs \(O_F((g+A/Y)\varphi(g))\) for one class over all \(p>Y\).
Thus for any integer \(H\ge1\) the total weight at \(p>Y\) in multiple disks of orders \(g\le H\), summed over the whole primitive box, satisfies
\[
 \ll_F\sum_{g\le H}(g+A/Y)\psi(g)\varphi(g)
 \ll_F H^4+AH^3/Y,                                                       \tag{4}
\]
using \(\psi(g)\varphi(g)=g^2\prod_{q\mid g}(1-q^{-2})\le g^2\).

<a id="loss"></a>
## Loss at the discarded contacts

Outside \(O_F(A/\log M)\) directions, (4) gives a pointwise loss of at most \(O_F((\log M)(T^4/A+T^3/Y))\) at orders through \(T\) and primes above \(Y\).
The second term with its logarithm is at most \(A/Y=Y\), and the first is at most \(T\le Y\).
The [two prime cutoffs in the box](Yin_C_G0345_v849.md#box) already cost \(O_F(Y+\log^3 M)=O_F(Y)\) in full discriminant weight outside another \(O_F(A/\log M)\) directions.
At each prime the chosen disks dominate both the certificate exponent and the actual complementary contacts by the [disk weight comparison](Yin_C_G0345_v833.md#weights).
If the size condition holds and the prime power is omitted, all chosen disks have order at most \(T\), proving (1).
At a retained prime, (4) still counts the low-order disks, giving the asserted bound on actual contacts.
Finally the [degree estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v811.md#degrees) gives \(d\gg_F M(\log M)^3(\log\log M)^3\), whereas \(Y\asymp M(\log M)^3\).
Subtracting the loss (1) from the [necessary certificate size](Yin_C_G0345_v827.md#statement) proves (2).
