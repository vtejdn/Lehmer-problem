# Conditional covariance in the neutral embeddings of Salem collision carries

<a id="statement"></a>
## Bounds for an exact finite bridge

Let \(\alpha>1\) be Salem, let \(d\) be its degree, set \(m=\log\alpha\) and \(s=d-2=2b\), and choose one neutral conjugate \(\zeta_l\) from each of the \(b\) complex pairs.
Let \(\sigma_l\) take \(\alpha\) to \(\zeta_l\) and write \(\pi(x)=(\sqrt2\operatorname{Re}\sigma_l(x),\sqrt2\operatorname{Im}\sigma_l(x))_{l=1}^b\in\mathbb R^s\).
Use the [exact collision bridge](Yin_C_G0345_v683.md#statement) at \(T=Lkd\), with \(N=kd\), past \(\mathcal F_{i-1}\) through the start of block \(i\), and carry \(C_{iN}\) at its end.
Let \(Q_T\) denote conditioning on \(C_T=0\).
Write \(\mathcal H=\mathcal H_R(k,L)\) for the averaged conditional carry entropy divided by \(d\).
All expectations and covariances in the following definitions use \(Q_T\).
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 K&=\frac1L\sum_{i=1}^L\mathbb E\,\operatorname{Cov}
                        (\pi(C_{iN})\mid\mathcal F_{i-1}),\\
 E_l&=\frac1L\sum_{i=1}^L\mathbb E\left|
       \sigma_l(C_{iN})-\mathbb E[\sigma_l(C_{iN})\mid\mathcal F_{i-1}]
                                      \right|^2 .
 \end{aligned}
\]
Let \(V_s\) be the Euclidean unit ball volume in dimension \(s\) and put
\[
 r=\left((\alpha-1)(1-\alpha^{-1})/4\right)^{1/s},
 \qquad \tau=\frac{s r^2}{4(s+2)},\qquad c_s=\frac{sV_s^{2/s}}{8\pi\mathrm e}.
\]
At every finite horizon,
\[
 \det(K+\tau I_s)^{1/s}\ge c_s r^2 e^{2d\mathcal H/s},
 \qquad
 \left(\prod_{l=1}^b E_l\right)^{1/b}+\tau
                        \ge c_s r^2 e^{2d\mathcal H/s}.                \tag{1}
\]
Thus on any Salem sequence with \(m\to0\), at \(k=\lfloor1/(2m)\rfloor\) and \(\log(4T)=o(L)\),
\[
 \liminf m\,\det(K+\tau I_s)^{1/s}\ \ge\ \frac{\pi}{8\mathrm e},
 \qquad
 \liminf m\left(\prod_{l=1}^bE_l\right)^{1/b}
                                \ \ge\ \frac{\pi}{8\mathrm e}.        \tag{2}
\]
The second inequality bounds the geometric mean of the optimal squared prediction errors in the individual complex embeddings.
Both bounds use the true conditional distribution given every earlier difference digit.

<a id="separation"></a>
## Separation of the neutral projections

Any state on a finite path from zero back to zero has expanding real coordinate of absolute value less than \(1/(\alpha-1)\) and contracting real coordinate of absolute value less than \(1/(1-\alpha^{-1})\), by the [past and future bounds](Yin_C_G0212_v1.md#carry-state-bound).
The real strips also enter the [stationary neutral radius estimate](Yin_C_G0508_v1.md#low-information-neutral-radius-cost).
For two distinct returning states \(x,y\), their difference is a nonzero algebraic integer, so its absolute field norm is at least one.
The two real differences are bounded by twice the respective radii, giving
\[
                   \prod_{l=1}^b|\sigma_l(x-y)|^2\ge r^s .
\]
The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives \(\|\pi(x)-\pi(y)\|\ge\sqrt{s}\,r\).
The same separation holds after rescaling both real coordinates of the \(l\)-th complex pair by any \(w_l>0\) with \(\prod_l w_l=1\), since the product bound is invariant.
In particular balls of radius \(a=\sqrt{s}\,r/2\) about these points have disjoint interiors, for every such choice of weights.

<a id="conditional"></a>
## Entropy and covariance of the neutral coordinates

Let \(D\) be any of these rescalings, and add an independent random vector uniform on the Euclidean ball of radius \(a\) to the scaled neutral projection at a cut.
For fixed past of positive bridge probability, the resulting distribution is a mixture of uniform distributions on disjoint ball interiors.
Its differential entropy is exactly the conditional discrete entropy of the carry for that past plus \(\log(V_s a^s)\).
Its covariance is the conditional covariance of the scaled projection plus \(\tau I_s\), since the uniform ball has covariance \(a^2 I_s/(s+2)\).
The Gaussian maximum entropy inequality and concavity of \(\log\det\), averaged over pasts and over cuts, yield
\[
 d\mathcal H+\log(V_s a^s)
       \le \tfrac{s}{2}\log(2\pi\mathrm e)
                +\tfrac12\log\det(D K D^{\mathsf T}+\tau I_s).       \tag{3}
\]
All mixtures have finite support before adding the independent noise.
Exponentiation at \(D=I_s\) gives the determinant bound in (1).

The arithmetic mean of the eigenvalues on the right of (3) is \(\tau+b^{-1}\sum_l w_l^2 E_l\).
Its infimum over the rescalings is \(\tau+(\prod_lE_l)^{1/b}\), by the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality with equality by equalizing the summands when positive, or by taking limits if some vanish.
This proves the second inequality in (1).

As \(m\to0\) the degrees tend to infinity by boundedness of integer coefficients at bounded degree and bounded measure.
The [degree bound for total logarithmic measure](../audits/literature/Vou96/Yin_U001.md#height) gives \(\log(1/m)=O(\log\log(3d))\), hence \(r\to1\).
Stirling's formula gives \(c_s\to1/4\) and \(\tau\to1/4\).
The [bridge entropy bound](Yin_C_G0345_v683.md#scales) gives \(\liminf \sqrt m\,e^{\mathcal H}\ge\sqrt{\pi/(2\mathrm e)}\).
Since \(d/s\ge1\) and \(\mathcal H\ge0\), these estimates prove (2).

<a id="gram"></a>
## An upper bound and its deterministic limitation

Put \(v_j=(\sqrt2\operatorname{Re}\zeta_l^{\,j-N},\sqrt2\operatorname{Im}\zeta_l^{\,j-N})_{l=1}^b\) and \(W_N=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}v_jv_j^{\mathsf T}\).
The carry recursion in a block expresses its neutral projection as the previous projection rotated pairwise by \(\zeta_l^{-N}\), plus \(\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}\delta_{(i-1)N+j}v_j\).
The rotated projection predicts the next carry from the past.
For any direction \(u\in\mathbb R^s\), apply the [exact zero-endpoint exponential moment bound](Yin_C_G0419_v1.md#original-endpoint-conditioned-exponential-moments) to the coefficients \(u^{\mathsf T}v_j\) in this block and zero elsewhere in the length-\(T\) word.
The integer-vector endpoint is the full binary difference sum in the power basis, whose vanishing is exactly \(C_T=0\).
By sign symmetry the linear form is centered under the bridge, and the moment bound at zero quadratic order gives squared prediction error at most \(u^{\mathsf T}W_Nu\).
Conditional expectation minimizes squared error, so \(K\preceq W_N\) in the positive semidefinite order.

The phase matrix by itself satisfies a stronger asymptotic determinant lower bound than the one obtained by replacing \(K\) with \(W_N\) in (2).
Indeed form the \(d\)-by-\(d\) matrix of the full power basis at exponents \(0,\ldots,d-1\), with the two real evaluations and the scaled real neutral coordinates.
Its absolute determinant is \(\sqrt{|\operatorname{disc}P|}\ge1\) for the minimal polynomial \(P\) of \(\alpha\), since the change from each pair of conjugate complex rows to scaled real and imaginary parts is unitary.
The two real row norms have product at most \(d e^{m(d-1)}\).
The full row volume is at most this product times the neutral row volume, by orthogonal projection when appending the real rows.
Consequently the Gram matrix \(G\) of the neutral rows at these exponents satisfies \(\det(G)^{1/s}\ge(d e^{m(d-1)})^{-2/s}\).
Each consecutive block of \(d\) columns in \(W_N\) differs from this neutral matrix by an orthogonal rotation of the row coordinates.
Concavity of \(\log\det\) for their \(k\) Gram matrices gives
\[
                  \det(W_N)^{1/s}\ge k(d e^{m(d-1)})^{-2/s}.
\]
At the scales of (2) this yields \(\liminf m\det(W_N)^{1/s}\ge1/2\).
A contradiction from (2) would require a smaller upper estimate for the actual conditional covariance or coordinate prediction errors on the same bridges.
