# One prescribed prime per degree and sparse rows with two genuine double factors

<a id="statement"></a>
## Two finite tests for the unrestricted infimum

Let \(p_d\) be any computable sequence of primes tending to infinity as even \(d\to\infty\).
Fix a sign at each degree by any computable rule, and allow an optional support cap \(s_d\) given by any computable sequence of positive integers tending to infinity.
At degree \(d\) put \(p=p_d\) and search only widths \(2d\le b<3d\) and nonzero integral polynomials \(A\) of degree at most \(b\), one-norm \(H\le2^p\), and at most \(s_d\) nonzero coefficients when a cap is imposed.
Set \(V=\varepsilon Y^bA(1/Y)\) and \(F=A(X^p)+X V(X^p)\).
Keep a row if \(F\) has genuine double factors at the distinct degree-\(d\) minima of a reciprocal nontorsion algebraic unit and its square.
Genuine means that \(A\) does not vanish at either powered node.
Put
\[
 W=pY(A'V-AV')-AV,\qquad W_{\rm pr}=W/\operatorname{cont}(W),
\]
where scalar content is positive.
Let \(S_d\) be the minimum of \(\log2\) and the following scores of kept rows:
\[
   \frac{(b-2d+1)\log2}{d}
          +\frac{\log H}{p}+\frac{\log\|W_{\rm pr}\|_1}{3p}.               \tag{1}
\]
The finite numbers \(S_d\) are computable and \(S_d\ge\lambda\), the unrestricted infimum of positive logarithmic Mahler measures.
For every such prime and support schedule,
\[
           \lambda=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
              \liminf_{\substack{d\to\infty\\2\mid d}}S_d=0.              \tag{2}
\]
Thus a zero infimum supplies genuine rows at widths \(2d+o(d)\) with \(\log H=o(p_d)\), even when both the prescribed prime and the permitted number of coefficients grow arbitrarily slowly.

For \(p_d/\log(2d)\to\infty\) the same assertion holds for \(T_d\) obtained by replacing (1) with the original score
\[
   \frac{(b-2d+1)\log2}{d}
                 +\frac{2\log H+\log(2pb+1)}{3p}.                        \tag{3}
\]
This uses a single specified prime in place of the [degree-dependent prime window](Yin_C_G0345_v463.md#statement).
For example, with \(L_d=\lceil\log_2(2d)\rceil\) one may take the first prime above \(L_d^2\), or above \(L_d f(d)\) for any computable positive integer function \(f(d)\to\infty\).
By [Bertrand's postulate](../audits/literature/Erd32/Yin_U001.md#erd32-u001) each such prime is at most twice its threshold.
At this prime growth rate, excluding the original strict budget at the single width \(3d-1\) for all sufficiently large even degrees would imply \(\lambda\ge(\log2)/144\), with the precise conditions in the [fixed-width test](#budget).

<a id="tower"></a>
## Raising the degree while preserving both minima

Fix a reciprocal nontorsion algebraic unit \(\alpha\) of even degree \(e\), of measure \(h=\log M(\alpha)>0\), whose positive powers all generate \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\).
Write \(P_{0,a}\) for the minimum of \(\alpha^a\) and \(G_0=P_{0,1}P_{0,2}\).
These are two different minima of degree \(e\) and measures \(h,2h\).
Choose an odd prime \(\ell\) with \(\alpha\notin K^\ell\).
Such a prime exists by the [bounded-degree root argument](Yin_C_G0345_v403.md#powers).
For \(k=\ell^j\) put \(\gamma^k=\alpha\).
The [tower of irreducible binomials](Yin_C_G0345_v403.md#powers) gives \([\mathbb Q(\gamma):\mathbb Q]=d=ek\).
Since \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha^2)\), the field \(\mathbb Q(\gamma^2)\) contains \(\alpha\), and \(\gcd(2,k)=1\) gives \(\gamma\in\mathbb Q(\gamma^2)\).
The degree-\(d\) minima of \(\gamma,\gamma^2\) are therefore \(P_{0,1}(X^k),P_{0,2}(X^k)\).
Substitution preserves their measures and the product of the minima is \(G(X)=G_0(X^k)\).

Call \(p>3\) good for \(G_0\) if \(p\nmid\operatorname{disc}(G_0)G_0(1)G_0(-1)\).
For such \(p\ne\ell\) the reduction of \(G\) is squarefree, since every base residue is nonzero and simple and \(kX^{k-1}\) is nonzero at every lift.
Also \(G(\pm1)=G_0(\pm1)\) because \(k\) is odd.
Only finitely many primes are excluded throughout the tower.
Consequently for every sufficiently large \(j\), the single prescribed \(p_{e\ell^j}\) is good for both products and coprime to \(k\).
The inflated unit need only preserve the degree of its square.

<a id="shift"></a>
## A short base row uniform in the displacement of the second class

For any good \(p\) for \(G_0\), take an integer \(1\le u<p\) and consider
\[
    G_0(X)^2\mid B(X^p)+\varepsilon X^u B_c^*(X^p),
             \qquad B_c^*(Y)=Y^c B(1/Y).
\]
Fix \(2\le r\le e/2,\ c=2e+r-1,\ q=\lfloor r/2\rfloor\).
There is an integral row \(B\) with nonzero values at \(\alpha^p,\alpha^{2p}\) such that, uniformly in \(u\) and the sign,
\[
     \frac{\log\|B\|_1}{p}
       \le \frac{(3c+4)h}{q}
              +O_{e,h}\left(\frac{\log(2p)}p\right).                      \tag{4}
\]

To prove this, use the integral linear map taking the graph remainder modulo the monic \(G_0^2\).
Over characteristic zero the signed reciprocal involution of width \(pc+u\) on the quotient exchanges the two local double jets at each inverse pair.
Its signed eigenspace has dimension \(2e\), bounding the rank of the map.
Modulo \(p\), the derivative of the graph at each residue \(\xi\) is \(\varepsilon u\xi^{u-1}B_c^*(\xi^p)\).
Since \(u\) is a unit, double vanishing at every residue is equivalent to \(\bar G_0\mid\bar B\), using reciprocity and the permutation of roots by Frobenius.
Thus the reduction has rank \(2e\) and so does the integral map.
Its saturated kernel has rank \(r\), and its reduction is exactly \(\bar B=\bar G_0 C,\ \deg C\le t_0=r-1\), by equality of dimensions.

For each root \(x\) of \(G_0\) in the unramified splitting field, write \(z=x^p,\ y=\bar z,\ \xi=\bar x,\ D=Y\,d/dY\).
Combining the value and Euler derivative equations gives
\[
 (u+pc)B(z)=p\{(DB)(z)-\varepsilon x^u z^c(DB)(1/z)\}.
\]
Here \(B(z)/p\) is integral.
Since \((D\bar G_0)(1/y)=-y^{-2e}(D\bar G_0)(y)\), reduction after division by \(p\) gives
\[
   u B(z)/p\bmod p=(D\bar G_0)(y)
                  \big\{C(y)+\varepsilon\xi^u C_{t_0}^*(y)\big\}.          \tag{5}
\]
For each of the two targets separately, one of the signs \(\eta=\pm1\) has \(1+\eta\varepsilon\xi^u\ne0\) on at least \(e/2\) of its \(e\) distinct residues.
Indeed the vanishing sets for the two signs are disjoint.
On \(C_{t_0}^*=\eta C\), which has dimension at least \(q\), evaluation on these residues is injective because \(t_0<e/2\).
The characteristic-zero vanishing subgroup for that target is saturated in the integral kernel and reduces into the kernel of (5).
Thus each forbidden rational subspace has codimension at least \(q\).

The [explicit determinant and successive-minimum argument](Yin_C_G0345_v463.md#height) applies to the \(e\) inverse-pair representatives, each used twice for the value and derivative rows.
In that argument replace the absolute-value prefactor \(2(1+pc)\max(1,|x|)\) for each entry by \(2(u+pc)\max(1,|x|)^u\).
The weighted column distances are unchanged, with total contribution bounded by \(p(3c-2)h\) after averaging.
The new site factors cost at most \(6uh\), since the sum of positive logarithmic moduli at the representatives is at most \(3h\).
The remaining factorial, dimension and Euclidean factors have logarithms \(O_e(\log(2p))\), uniformly for \(u<p\).
Taking the successive minimum of index \(r-q+1\) and combining two rows to avoid both vanishing subspaces proves (4).

<a id="lift"></a>
## Substitution removes the degree factor from the primitive derivative

At a sufficiently large tower level, take \(1\le u<p\) with \(ku\equiv1\bmod p\) and put \(t=(ku-1)/p\).
Then \(0\le t<k\), and for a base row from (4) set
\[
             A(Y)=B(Y^k),\qquad b=kc+t .
\]
The signed graph for this row at width \(b\) is the base graph with \(X\) replaced by \(X^k\), because \(pt+1=ku\).
It has the two prescribed genuine double factors by the tower identities.
Its norm is \(\|B\|_1\), its support has cardinality at most \(c+1\), and
\[
     2d\le b<3d,\qquad 0<\frac{b-2d+1}{d}\le\frac r e .
\]

Writing \(V_0=\varepsilon B_c^*\), we have \(V(Y)=Y^t V_0(Y^k)\), hence
\[
 W(Y)=kY^t W_0(Y^k),\qquad
 W_0=pY(B'V_0-BV_0')-u B V_0 .
\]
The polynomial \(W_0\) is nonzero since \(pY(B/V_0)'/(B/V_0)\) cannot equal the constant \(u\), as its limit at infinity is an integral multiple of \(p\).
Removing the scalar content therefore gives
\[
    \|W_{\rm pr}\|_1\le\|W_0\|_1\le(2pc+u)\|B\|_1^2.                      \tag{6}
\]
This estimate involves the base width even when \(p_d\) is smaller than \(\log d\).

<a id="limit"></a>
## Uniform recovery and a diagonal choice of tower levels

For every retained row the [derivative product and rational recovery](Yin_C_G0345_v419.md#product) prove that the powered minima divide \(W\) with total logarithmic measure \(3ph'\), where \(h'\) is the measure of the recovered degree-\(d\) unit.
The same product divides \(W_{\rm pr}\) by Gauss's lemma.
The derivative polynomial is nonzero for every nonzero row by the same limit-at-infinity argument with shift one.
Thus \(3ph'\le\log\|W_{\rm pr}\|_1\le 2\log H+\log(2pb+1)\).
The norm box is finite, the two minima are decidable by recovery and factorization as in the [degree-window test](Yin_C_G0345_v463.md#finite), and all logarithmic score comparisons can be made by rational arithmetic after clearing denominators.
Both scores bound \(\lambda\) from above on each kept row and the fallback has that property as well.
Consequently a zero lower limit for either score forces \(\lambda=0\).

Conversely, if \(\lambda=0\), the [small-measure factorization](Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#upper) and [all-power norm](Yin_C_G0345_v421.md#norm) give base seeds with \(0<h\to0,\ e\to\infty\).
For each choose \(r=\max(2,\lceil\sqrt e\rceil,\lceil e\sqrt h\rceil)\).
Eventually \(2\le r\le e/2,\ r/e\to0\) and \((3c+4)h/q=O(\sqrt h)\).
For each base choose a tower level as large as necessary to make \(d\) exceed all earlier chosen degrees, to have \(s_d\ge c+1\) when applicable, and to make both the error in (4) and \(\log(2pc+u)/p\) smaller than \(1/e\).
These choices exist since \(e,c,h\) are now fixed and \(p_{e\ell^j}\to\infty\).
The lifted norms satisfy \(\log H/p\to0\), so \(H\le2^p\) eventually, and (6) gives \(\log\|W_{\rm pr}\|_1/p\to0\).
The width estimate proves (2).
For the raw score (3), \(\log(2p_db+1)/p_d\to0\) whenever \(p_d/\log(2d)\to\infty\), proving its additional assertion.
Conversely the positive degree term in (3) itself requires \(\log d/p_d\to0\) on any successful sequence of raw scores tending to zero.

<a id="budget"></a>
## Excluding an interval of measures at the single width \(3d-1\)

Assume \(p_d/\log(2d)\to\infty\).
At each even degree consider the same genuine pair test with the single prescribed prime, at width \(b=3d-1\), allowing all integer rows with the original strict budget \(((2p_db+1)H^2)^4<2^{p_d}\).
Suppose that at every sufficiently large even degree at least one sign, possibly depending on the degree, has no passing row.
Then \(\lambda\ge(\log2)/144\).

Indeed any smaller positive irreducible measure yields a base seed of the same measure \(h\) by the preceding reduction.
Pass to all sufficiently large levels of its tower, at which each \(p_d\) is good.
Use the [good-prime codimension and height argument](Yin_C_G0345_v463.md#digits) on the inflated pair with \(r=d,\ q=d/2\).
Its proof at a good prime requires only distinct reciprocal unit minima of degree \(d\), with measures \(h,2h\) and maximum single exterior logarithm at most \(2h\).
It uses no degree condition on further powers or comparison between \(p_d\) and \(d\).
Here the simple disjoint reductions, interpolation of the reciprocal quotient at \(d\) residues and the full determinant estimate all apply.
Since \(b=3d-1\), the finite error \(E\) in [the height bound](Yin_C_G0345_v463.md#statement) is \(O(d\log(2p_d d))\) for \(h\le1\).
For each sign there is therefore a genuine row satisfying
\[
 \limsup_{j\to\infty}
       \frac{\log H+\tfrac12\log(2p_db+1)}{p_d}\le 18h ,
          \qquad d=e\ell^j .
\]
As \(18h<(\log2)/8\), both signs pass throughout the late tower, a contradiction.
The exclusion is finite at each degree, and the required statement about all sufficiently large degrees remains open.
