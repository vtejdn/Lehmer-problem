## 1. Positive density with bounded relative divisor cores

    b_o:=G/g_o in{1,...,B} for every selected order.       (1)

The prime support and magnitude of G may be arbitrarily large, as may the union of the free order primes in a_o.

**Proof.** The elementary totient product formula gives, for every pair of positive integers g,a,

    phi(ga)<=g phi(a).                                  (4)

But an integer n<=X<=4Y^2 cannot be divisible by THREE distinct selected primes: their product exceeds Y^3>4Y^2.
