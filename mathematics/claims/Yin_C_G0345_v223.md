# Irreducible factors and the frequency of small measures at subexponential prime gaps

<a id="statement"></a>
## Uniform ranges for the gaps

Put \(\tau=(1-\log2)/2\), using natural logarithms.
Fix \(0<\gamma<\tau\).
There are absolute positive constants \(C_0,c_0,c_1,c_2\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(x\) and every real \(H\) with
\[
               C_0\log x\le H\le \exp((\log x)^\gamma),
\]
at least \(c_1 xH/(\log x)^2\) pairs of primes \(x\le r<q\le2x\) satisfy \(c_0 H\le g=q-r\le H\), \(\gcd(q-1,r-1)=2\), and the following irreducibility assertion.
For each choice \(\varepsilon=\pm1\) the entire noncyclotomic part of
\(X^r(X^g-2)(X^g-3)+\varepsilon(1-2X^g)(1-3X^g)\) is the single irreducible polynomial
\[
 Q_\varepsilon=\frac{X^r(X^g-2)(X^g-3)+\varepsilon(1-2X^g)(1-3X^g)}
                          {X+\varepsilon}.
\]
Its logarithmic Mahler measure is \(\log6+O(g\exp(-c r/(2g+1)^2))\) for an absolute \(c>0\).
At least \(c_2x/\log x\) of these pairs can be chosen with all endpoints distinct.

Among all pairs \(x\le r<q\le2x\) of gap \(g\le H\), the number admitting a monic irreducible rational factor of either \(Q_\varepsilon\) with logarithmic Mahler measure at most \(\eta>0\) is
\[
              O\!\left(\eta\,\frac{xH}{(\log x)^2}\right)
                  +o_\gamma\!\left(\frac{xH}{(\log x)^2}\right).                  \tag{1}
\]
The error bounds are uniform in \(H\) in the stated range and in \(\eta\).
In particular for each \(\eta=\eta(x)\to0\) the proportion of all such prime pairs admitting a factor of logarithmic measure at most \(\eta\) tends to zero, uniformly in \(H\).

<a id="orders"></a>
## Rank-one orders and the error in an exterior share

Choose \(\gamma<\beta<\tau\).
In the [unconditional order bound](../audits/literature/Pap96/Yin_U001.md#rank-one-orders), take \(\psi(p)=(\log\log p)^{1/4}\).
Writing \(L=\log\log p\), the exponent in the multiplicative gain over \(\sqrt p\) is \(\exp(\tau L-L^{3/4})\).
Its ratio to \((\log p)^\beta\) tends to infinity.
It follows by separate applications to \(2,3,5\) that apart from a set of \(o_\gamma(x/\log x)\) exceptional prime endpoints in \([x,2x]\), their three orders all exceed \(\sqrt{2x}\exp((\log x)^\beta)\).
The exceptional set is independent of \(H\).

Take two nonexceptional endpoints and \(g=q-r\le H\).
Here \(g\) is even and \(r\ge A(2g+1)^9\) for every fixed \(A\) once \(x\) is sufficiently large, uniformly in \(g\).
Thus the [cyclotomic classification](Yin_C_G0345_v212.md#torsion) and the [positive exterior shares](Yin_C_G0345_v198.md#integers) apply.
For a monic irreducible divisor \(S\) of \(Q_\varepsilon\) let \(d=\deg S\) and \(s\) be its number of exterior roots near the roots of each of \(X^g-2,X^g-3\).
Put \(W=2g+1\).
The unsimplified bound at [the integer allocation step](Yin_C_G0345_v198.md#integers) gives
\[
   |d-rs/g|\ll rW e^{-c r/W^2}+W^{3/2}\sqrt{d\,\{W^2+\log(2d)\}}+W^4.
\]
Let \(N=q-1,q-1,r-1\) for the primitive grids in characteristics \(2,3,5\), respectively, and let \(\delta\) count the roots in the reduction of \(S\) outside the corresponding grid, with multiplicity.
By [the orbit divisibility argument](Yin_C_G0345_v221.md#rounding), \(g(d-\delta)-Ns\) is always divisible by its grid orbit size \(t\).
Its absolute value is at most \(g|d-rs/g|+3g^2\).
Since \(d\le r+2g\le3x\), \(g\le\exp((\log x)^\gamma)\), and \(r\ge x\), this bound is \(O(\sqrt{x}\exp(O((\log x)^\gamma)))\).
Indeed \(rW e^{-c r/W^2}=o(1)\), and every fixed power of \(\log x\) can be absorbed into \(\exp((\log x)^\gamma)\) for large \(x\).
The orbit size is at least the relevant order, greater than \(\sqrt{2x}\exp((\log x)^\beta)\).
The integer is therefore zero at all three grids.

As in the [three exact degree equations](Yin_C_G0345_v221.md#rounding), the reduced denominator of \(s/g\) divides \(\gcd(J_2,J_3,J_5,g)\), where \(J_\ell=N/t\) at each grid, and hence divides \(\gcd(q-1,r-1)\).
For both signs the degree and exceptional counts obey
\[
                  d=\frac{q-1}{g}s+\delta_2,\qquad
                  \delta_3=\delta_2,\qquad \delta_5=\delta_2+s .
\]
These equations extend to arbitrary monic divisors by additivity.
For irreducible \(S\) the [measure estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v198.md#statement) gives
\[
             m(S)=\frac{s}{g}\log6+O(W e^{-c r/W^2})
                              \ \ge\ \frac{s}{2g}\log6,                         \tag{2}
\]
since \(s\ge1\) and the error is uniformly \(o(1/g)\).

<a id="counting"></a>
## Sieve counts over the larger interval of shifts

In the [upper bound for two linear forms](Yin_C_G0345_v222.md#sieve), the restriction on the sizes of \(m,j\) was used only to absorb the absolute remainder \(O(x^{3/10})\) into the main estimate.
With \(z=x^{1/20}\) the same weights and the same uniform lower bound for their sum give
\[
 \#\{n:x\le mn+1\le2x,\ mn+1,\ m(n+j)+1\ \mathrm{prime}\}
                      \ll \frac{x}{m(\log x)^2}F(m)F(j),\qquad
 F(n)=(n/\varphi(n))^2 ,
\]
uniformly for \(m,j\le2H\).
Indeed \(2H=x^{o(1)}\) and \(F\ge1\).
The [mean bound for \(F\) and the common divisor sum](Yin_C_G0345_v222.md#common-divisors) now give, for \(1\le D\) and \(1\le h\le H\),
\[
 \#\{(r,q):q-r\le h,\ \gcd(q-1,r-1)\ge D\}
                       \ll \frac{xh}{D(\log x)^2}.                            \tag{3}
\]
All endpoint counts here and below are restricted to primes in \([x,2x]\).

For each prime \(p\) in this interval let \(D_H(p)\) count its other prime neighbors within distance \(H\).
As in the [three-form proof](Yin_C_G0345_v222.md#exceptional-endpoints), the weighted count for shifts \(1\le i<j\le2H\) is \(O(x V(6ij(j-i))/(\log x)^3)\), where \(V(n)=(n/\varphi(n))^3\).
The same \(O(x^{3/10})\) remainder is absorbed here as well.
Hölder's inequality and the bounded mean of \(V^3\) give \(\sum_{1\le i<j\le2H}V(6ij(j-i))\ll H^2\) independently of \(H\).
Adding the pair and triple estimates gives
\[
        \sum_p D_H(p)^2\ll \frac{xH}{(\log x)^2}+\frac{xH^2}{(\log x)^3}
                         \ll \frac{xH^2}{(\log x)^3}                           \tag{4}
\]
when \(H\ge\log x\).
Cauchy–Schwarz applied to (4) and the \(o_\gamma(x/\log x)\) exceptional endpoints from the order bound shows that they meet \(o_\gamma(xH/(\log x)^2)\) pairs.
This little-oh is uniform in \(H\) since the count of exceptional endpoints was independent of \(H\).
For every remaining pair at which \(m(S)\le\eta\), (2) and the denominator divisibility force \(\gcd(q-1,r-1)\ge(\log6)/(2\eta)\).
Apply (3) with this lower threshold or with \(D=1\) if it is smaller.
This proves (1).

<a id="matching"></a>
## Many pairs and a matching on the same scale

Use primes \(p\equiv3\bmod4\) such that \(p\not\equiv1\bmod\ell\) for every odd prime \(\ell\le B\).
For each fixed \(B\) their density \(\Delta_B\) among the primes satisfies \(B\Delta_B^2\to\infty\) as \(B\to\infty\), by the [finite congruence calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v222.md#supply).
After removing the exceptional endpoints there are \(N\ge\Delta_B x/(2\log x)\) of these primes in the given interval for all sufficiently large \(x\).
Partition the interval into at most \(2x/H\) consecutive subintervals of length at most \(H\), assigning each boundary point just once.
If \(n_j\) counts the remaining primes in the \(j\)-th subinterval, the number of prime pairs lying in a common subinterval is
\[
             \sum_j\binom{n_j}{2}\ge\frac{N^2H}{4x}-\frac N2
                             \ge\frac{\Delta_B^2 xH}{32(\log x)^2}
\]
whenever \(H\ge8\log x/\Delta_B\).
Indeed \(NH\ge4x\) implies the subtraction uses at most half of the first term.
These pairs have gap at most \(H\) and a gcd exactly divisible by two.
If the gcd exceeds two, it exceeds \(B\).
By (3) at most \(O(xH/(B(\log x)^2))\) pairs fail the gcd-two requirement.
The constant is absolute, so fix \(B\) sufficiently large that this discards at most one quarter of the lower bound above.
Set \(C_0\ge\max(1,8/\Delta_B)\) once \(B\) is fixed.
Discard also the pairs with gap below \(c_0H\), taking \(c_0>0\) small enough that (3) at \(D=1\) removes at most another quarter, uniformly in \(H\).
This leaves \(E\gg xH/(\log x)^2\) distinct pairs satisfying the stated gcd and gap conditions.

For these pairs \(J_2=(q-1)/\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_q(2))\) is odd since \(q\equiv3\bmod4\).
The share denominator divides two and also \(J_2\), so \(s/g\) is an integer for every noncyclotomic constituent of either numerator.
Each share is positive and the shares sum to one after division by \(g\).
There is exactly one constituent, with \(s=g\), giving the asserted measure by (2) and the exact factorization by the cyclotomic classification.

View the remaining pairs as the edges of a finite graph on the prime endpoints and take an inclusion-maximal matching.
Its endpoint set \(V_0\) meets every edge, so \(E\le\sum_{p\in V_0}D_H(p)\le(|V_0|\sum_pD_H(p)^2)^{1/2}\).
Equation (4) gives \(|V_0|\gg x/\log x\), and the matching has \(|V_0|/2\) pairs of the required scale.
All selection constants here are absolute, while the onset for the order bound and exterior allocation can depend on \(\gamma\).
