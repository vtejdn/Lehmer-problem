# Every finite convolution has a collision-entropy deficit at quartic Salem parameters

<a id="statement"></a>
## A strict deficit for each convolution power

Let \(\beta\in(1,2)\) be a Salem number of degree \(d\) with minimum \(P\), and put \(m=\log\beta\).
For each integer \(k\ge1\) let \(T_{n,k}\) be the sum of \(k\) independent copies of \(\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\xi_j\beta^j\), with all digits independent fair bits.
For an independent copy \(T'_{n,k}\), put \(p_{n,k}=\Pr(T_{n,k}=T'_{n,k})\) and \(h_{k,2}=\lim_n -n^{-1}\log p_{n,k}\).
If \(P\) is either of the two quartic Salem minima in this interval,
\[
 X^4-X^3-X^2-X+1,\qquad X^4-2X^3+X^2-2X+1,
\]
then \(\delta_k=m-h_{k,2}>0\) for every fixed \(k\ge1\).
The same conclusion holds at any fixed Salem input in this interval satisfying the [packet sequence condition ST4](Yin_C_G0798_v2.md#statement).
The positive lower bound may depend on both the input and \(k\).

<a id="integral"></a>
## Collision probability on the companion torus

On \(\mathbb T^d\) use the companion shift \(T\) of \(P\) and let \(y_j(x)\) be the centered first coordinate of \(T^jx\).
The character vectors \((T^j)^{\mathsf T}e_0\) obey the recurrence \(P\) and the first \(d\) of these vectors form the standard basis, so an integer linear combination vanishes precisely when the corresponding polynomial vanishes at \(\beta\).
The Fourier factor for the difference of two independent bits is \((2+e^{2\pi it}+e^{-2\pi it})/4=\cos^2(\pi t)\).
Expanding the product for all independent bits and integrating against normalized Haar measure therefore gives
\[
 p_{n,k}=\int_{\mathbb T^d}\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}\cos^{2k}(\pi y_j(x))\,dx.
\]
In particular the action in the integrand is \(k\sum_j V(y_j)\), where \(V(t)=-\log\cos^2(\pi t)\) and a zero factor corresponds to infinite action.
Combining independent exact collisions on consecutive blocks gives \(p_{n+r,k}\ge p_{n,k}p_{r,k}\), so the defining entropy limit exists by subadditivity.

<a id="packets"></a>
## Packing the same boxes for each fixed power

Use the [localized packets](Yin_C_G0798_v2.md#multiple-neutral-packet-definition) with neutral amplitude \(\Delta\), core length \(\ell\), core margin \(2\eta\), tail constant \(h\), and localized part \(H\).
Suppose a sequence of these packets has common \(\eta,h\), \(\Delta\to0\), \(\Delta\ell\to0\), and \(E=\sum_i V(H_i)=o(\log(1/\Delta))\).
For both quartics such sequences are supplied by the [inspected reciprocal packet construction of Cheng, Fang, Ma and Zhang](../audits/literature/CFMZ26/Yin_U002.md#cfmz26-u002), specifically their Definition 3.2 and Theorem 5.2 with its subsequent estimates.
Their single outgoing wave has \(\Delta=2|\gamma|\), exactly the one-mode instance of the packet definition used here.
In higher degrees existence of this sequence is the stated hypothesis.

For one such packet put \(N=\lceil\Delta^{-1}\rceil\), and use \(b_N,u_N,C_N\) of [ST2 and MP4–MP6](Yin_C_G0798_v2.md#multiple-neutral-packet-argument).
Here \(b_N=\lfloor N/L\rfloor\) for the fixed steering length \(L=L(P)\), and
\[
 C_N=E+u_N\sum_i|V'(H_i)|+2\pi^2\csc^2(\pi\eta)Nu_N^2.
\]
Since \(\ell/N\to0\), eventually \(N>2\ell+d\), the geometric tail error in \(u_N\) is \(o(\Delta)\), and \(u_N=O_P(\Delta)<\eta\).
The common margin and tail bound give \(\sum_i|V'(H_i)|=O_{P,\eta,h}(\ell+1)\), so \(C_N=E+o(1)\).
For fixed \(k\), choose a packet sufficiently far in the sequence that \(b_N\ge1\) and \(\Theta_{N,k}=\log b_N-kC_N>0\), possible because \(\log b_N=\log(1/\Delta)+O_P(1)\).
Keep this packet and \(N\) fixed from now on.

For every integer \(M\ge2\), the placement and box construction in MP4–MP5 gives a common length \((M-1)N+\ell+d\le n<2NM\) and at least \(b_N^{M-1}/(MN)\) pairwise disjoint boxes in \(\mathbb T^d\).
Each has Haar volume \(c_0(P,\eta)\beta^{-n}n^{-d}\) with the same \(c_0>0\), and every point of each box satisfies \(\sum_{j<n}V(y_j)\le MC_N+1\).
These are bounds on the sets and the observations before integration, so they apply directly to the integrand for \(p_{n,k}\).
Integration over their union yields
\[
 \beta^np_{n,k}\ge c_0(P,\eta)n^{-d}\frac{b_N^{M-1}}{MN}\exp[-k(MC_N+1)].
\]
Its logarithm is bounded below by \(M\Theta_{N,k}-O_{P,k,N,\eta}(\log M)\), which is positive for large \(M\).
The common lengths tend to infinity and are smaller than \(2NM\), so the entropy limit gives \(\delta_k\ge\Theta_{N,k}/(2N)>0\).

<a id="moments"></a>
## Exponential moments of uniformly small coefficients

Let \(O\) be the ring of integers of \(\mathbb Q(\beta)\), \(G_n=O/(\beta^n-1)O\), and \(A_n(\chi)=|\mathbb E\chi(T_{n,1})|\).
Write \(\mathfrak a_\chi=\{z\in O:\chi(zy)=1\text{ for every }y\in O\}\).
For either quartic, every fixed \(k\ge1\), and every \(0<\kappa<\delta_k/m\), the [conductor cutoff for Fourier moments](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v300.md#cutoff) gives
\[
 \sum_{\log N\mathfrak a_\chi>\kappa mn} A_n(\chi)^{2k}=\exp(n\delta_k+o(n)).
\]
Yet the largest amplitude in this sum tends to zero because its conductor condition eventually excludes all characters trivial on the digit order by the [order-index bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v300.md#neutral), and every remaining amplitude is bounded by the [neutral digit-order maximum](Yin_C_G0345_v579.md#statement).
The same argument applies to each Salem input satisfying ST4.
