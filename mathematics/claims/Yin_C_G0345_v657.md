# Radial equidistribution at the unrestricted energy threshold

<a id="statement"></a>
## Which root angles can support equality

Let \(P\in\mathbb Z[X]\) be monic with \(|P(0)|=1\), no zero on the unit circle, and \(m=\log M(P)>0\).
Let \(A\in\mathbb Z[X,X^{-1}]\) be indivisible by \(P\), and put \(K=5/2+\log2-2\sqrt2\).
Suppose \(m\to0\) and \(m\|A/P\|_2^2\to K\), with Haar probability measure \(\tau\) on the circle.
Give each root \(\rho\) mass \(\ell_\rho/(2m)\) at \(z_\rho=\rho/|\rho|\), where \(\ell_\rho=|\log|\rho||\) and multiplicities count.
Write \(\mu\) for that probability.
For every sequence of positive integers \(g\) with \(gm\to0\), the image of \(\mu\) under \(z\mapsto z^g\) converges weakly to Haar measure.
Equivalently, for every positive integer window \(G=o(1/m)\),
\[
                      \max_{1\le g\le G}|\widehat\mu(g)|\to0.          \tag{1}
\]
In particular \(\max_\rho\ell_\rho/(2m)\to0\), and the numbers of roots both inside and outside the circle tend to infinity.

For each fixed \(\epsilon>0\) there exist \(\eta>0,\ m_0>0\) such that
\[
 0<m<m_0,\qquad \max_\rho\frac{\ell_\rho}{2m}\ge\epsilon
       \quad\Longrightarrow\quad m\|A/P\|_2^2\ge K+\eta .             \tag{2}
\]
These constants are not evaluated.
This is a strict improvement of the uniform energy estimate on the indicated signature classes.
A separate argument would be needed to give a positive lower bound on \(m\) itself.

<a id="decomposition"></a>
## The two projected functions at a balanced cut

Use a [balanced division](Yin_C_G0345_v638.md#division) \(F=X^{-j}A/P=Q+H\), \(H=R/P\), with \(R\ne0\) short.
Write \(Q_-,Q_+\) for the negative and nonnegative parts of \(Q\) and \(P_-,P_+\) for the corresponding Fourier projections.
Use the [inner factors](Yin_C_G0345_v638.md#projections) \(U=P/O,\ V=O^*/P\), where \(O\) is exterior, and put \(u=U(0),v=V(0),\ t=|u|=|v|=e^{-m}\).
With \(c=(1+\sqrt2)/2\), the [projection reconstruction](Yin_C_G0345_v651.md#reconstruction) gives
\[
 \sqrt m\|H+c DQ\|_2\to0,\qquad
 DQ=\bar U P_+ y_-+V P_- y_+,\quad
 y_-=(U-u)Q_-,\quad y_+=(\bar V-\bar v)Q_+.                        \tag{3}
\]
Indeed \(D=I-\bar U P_- U-VP_+\bar V\).
The positive inputs to \(P_- UQ\) and negative inputs to \(P_+\bar VQ\) contribute zero, and the constant factors vanish under the complementary projections in (3).

Put \(x=(\log2)/2\) and \(a=\sqrt2 x\).
The [support counts and integer excess](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#statement) give \(m\|Q_\pm\|_\infty\le m\sum_j|[X^j]Q_\pm|\le x+o(1)\).
In fact each support count times \(m\) tends to \(x\), and each sum of \(|q_j|-1\) over a support is bounded by its sum of \(q_j^2-1\).
Consider the probabilities
\[
       d\gamma_-=\frac{|U-u|^2}{1-t^2}d\tau,\qquad
       d\gamma_+=\frac{|V-v|^2}{1-t^2}d\tau .
\]
For each side choose any lag \(g_\pm\ge1\) and sign \(\epsilon_\pm=\pm1\), let \(\Delta_\pm=X^{g_\pm}-\epsilon_\pm\), and put \(d_\pm^2=\int|\Delta_\pm|^2d\gamma_\pm\).
The mass and frequency estimates give
\[
 \sqrt m\|y_\pm\|_2\le a+o(1),\qquad
 \sqrt m\|\Delta_\pm y_\pm\|_2\le(a+o(1))d_\pm .                    \tag{4}
\]
Here \((1-t^2)/m\to2\).

<a id="orders"></a>
## A simultaneous concentration obstruction at arbitrary orders

For either Fourier projection \(P_\pm\), the commutator with \(X^g\) selects a block of \(g\) coefficients, up to sign and translation.
The [block variation bound](Yin_C_G0345_v651.md#arbitrary-lags) implies
\[
 \|\Delta P_\pm y\|_2\le\|\Delta y\|_2+
               \sqrt{\|y\|_2\,\|\Delta y\|_2},\qquad \Delta=X^g-\epsilon.
\]
This estimate is uniform even for \(g\) much larger than \(1/m\).
Multiplication by either inner boundary factor commutes with both differences and preserves norms.
Apply \(\Delta_-\Delta_+\) to (3), using the designated difference first on each of its two terms and bounding the other by two.
All zeros of the two binomials lie on the circle, so their product times \(R\) remains indivisible by \(P\).
The [unrestricted lower bound](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#statement) applies to that product even when its degree varies.
Thus
\[
 \sqrt K\ \le\ 2ca\liminf
        \big(d_-+\sqrt{d_-}+d_++\sqrt{d_+}\big).                     \tag{5}
\]
In particular the two probabilities cannot each concentrate on its own signed period, even when those two periods and signs differ.
If both differences coincide, applying it once in (3) replaces \(2ca\) by \(ca\) on the right.
This step uses the lower bound on an integer multiple of the actual remainder, whose nonzero class is preserved.

<a id="angles"></a>
## Passing from the two inner functions to radial mass

Let \(\mu_-\) and \(\mu_+\) be the probabilities formed with weights \(\ell_\rho/m\) from the interior and exterior roots respectively.
Their mean is \(\mu\).
Uniformly for \(1\le g\le G=o(1/m)\), we have
\[
             \widehat\gamma_\pm(g)=\widehat\mu_\pm(g)+O(gm).          \tag{6}
\]
For \(W=U\) or \(V\) with \(w=W(0)\) and corresponding radial probability \(\mu_W\), the positive Fourier coefficients of \(|W-w|^2/(1-t^2)\) are \(-\bar w[X^g]W/(1-t^2)\).
The [reflected product expansion](Yin_C_G0345_v639.md#angles), with the [uniform moving-order estimates](Yin_C_G0345_v654.md#coefficients), gives
\([X^g](W/w)=-2m\widehat\mu_W(g)+O(gm^2)\).
Using \(2mt^2/(1-t^2)=1+O(m)\) gives (6).

Now \(m\|H\|_2^2\to1/2\), by the balanced equality conditions.
The [moving integer word](Yin_C_G0345_v654.md#statement) says that the moments \(\widehat\mu(g)\) through \(G\) are uniformly within \(o(1)\) of digits in \(\{-1,0,1\}\).
If (1) failed, there would be a subsequence, a lag \(g\le G\), and a fixed sign \(\epsilon\) such that \(\epsilon\widehat\mu(g)\to1\).
Since the signed moments of \(\mu_-\) and \(\mu_+\) are both at most one, both would tend to one as well.
Equation (6) would give \(d_-^2,d_+^2\to0\) at the same signed lag, contradicting (5).
This proves (1) and eliminates the nonzero period allowed by the short-remainder theorem when it comes from an extremizing unrestricted quotient.

For every \(g\) with \(gm\to0\), (1) applies also to each fixed positive multiple \(kg\).
All nonconstant Fourier coefficients of the pushed-forward probability tend to zero, proving weak convergence to Haar.
At \(g=1\), an individual root of mass bounded below would have a subsequence whose angles converge to a point and would force an atom at that point in the weak limit.
Each of the interior and exterior sets has mass \(1/2\), proving the count assertion.
Finally if (2) failed for some fixed \(\epsilon\), choose inputs with \(m<1/n\) and \(m\|A/P\|_2^2<K+1/n\) under that radial condition.
The unrestricted lower bound forces convergence to \(K\), contradicting the vanishing maximum mass.
For example, bounding the number of exterior roots by \(s\) gives the hypothesis in (2) with \(\epsilon=1/(2s)\).
