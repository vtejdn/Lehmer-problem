# An explicit increment in the unrestricted integral energy bound

<a id="statement"></a>
## A numerical lower bound for all numerator widths

Let \(P\in\mathbb Z[X]\) be monic of positive degree, with \(|P(0)|=1\), no root on the unit circle and \(m=\log M(P)>0\).
Let \(A\) be an integral Laurent polynomial indivisible by \(P\), and use Haar probability measure on the circle.
There is an absolute \(m_0>0\) such that whenever \(m<m_0\),
\[
 m\|A/P\|_2^2\ \ge\ K+10^{-8},\qquad K=\tfrac52+\log 2-2\sqrt2 .
\]
This quantifies the increment in the [strict energy bound](Yin_C_G0345_v659.md#statement) for arbitrary numerator widths and reducible denominators, including repeated roots.
The proof does not evaluate \(m_0\) or give a lower bound on \(m\) itself.

<a id="slack"></a>
## The errors in the balanced projection estimate

Write \(\eta=10^{-8}\).
If the claim fails, choose a sequence with \(m\to0\) along which \(m\|A/P\|_2^2\to L\le K+\eta\), passing to a subsequence.
Use a [balanced division](Yin_C_G0345_v638.md#division) \(X^{-j}A/P=Q+H\), \(H=R/P\), with \(R\ne0\) short.
Write \(n_\pm\) for the two support counts, \(t=e^{-m}\), \(s=\sqrt{1-t^2}\), and \(D\) for the [complementary projection](Yin_C_G0345_v651.md#reconstruction), so \(DH=H\).
The [prefix localization](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#localization) applies with this slack: a count above \(\lfloor a/m\rfloor\), \(a=\log(3/2)\), along a subsequence would give \(L\ge2a-4/9>K+10^{-3}\).
The last inequality follows from the rational estimates for \(\log(9/8)\) and \(\sqrt2\) there.
The determinant bound gives \(m\|Q\|_2^2=O(1)\), and \(m\|H\|_2^2=O(1)\) follows by the triangle inequality.

After taking a further subsequence write \(x=\lim mn_\pm\), \(r=\lim\sqrt m\|H\|_2\), \(w=\lim\sqrt m\|DQ\|_2\) and \(B=\lim m\|Q\|_2^2\).
Let \(b_\pm\) be the limits of \(m\sum(q^2-1)\) over the occupied integer coefficients \(q\) on each side, and put \(b=b_-+b_+=B-2x\).
The [complementary norm estimates](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#defect) give
\[
 w\le W=2(1-e^{-x})+\sqrt2\,b/3,\qquad w^2\le5L/4 .
\]
Indeed their row iteration requires only \(2(e^{2a}-1)L=5L/2<1\), which follows from \(L<0.37\).
The [short remainder bound](Yin_C_G0345_v644.md#statement) gives \(r\ge r_0=1/\sqrt2\).

Set \(J=L-(r^2+B-2rw)\ge0\) by Cauchy–Schwarz and \(f(x)=1/2+2x-2\sqrt2(1-e^{-x})\).
The exact identity
\[
 L-f(x)=b/3+J+(r-r_0)(r+r_0-2w)+2r_0(W-w)
\]
expresses the slack as nonnegative terms, since \(w\le\sqrt{5L/4}<0.69\).
On \([0,a]\) one has \(f''\ge1\) and \(f\) has minimum \(K\) at \(x_0=(\log2)/2\).
It follows that
\[
 |x-x_0|\le\sqrt{2\eta},\quad b\le3\eta,\quad J\le\eta,\quad
 r-r_0\le 40\eta,\quad W-w\le\eta/\sqrt2 .
\]
In particular \(0.346<x<0.347\), \(r<0.708\), \(W<0.6\), and \(w\ge 2(1-e^{-0.346})-\eta/\sqrt2>0.584\).
For \(c=r/w<1.22\), expansion using the exact energy identity now gives
\[
          \lim m\|H+cDQ\|_2^2=(r-cw)^2+cJ=cJ\le1.22\eta .          \tag{1}
\]

<a id="prefix"></a>
## The loss at a prefix of each end

Use the [actual future Fourier output](Yin_C_G0345_v659.md#state) at each end.
After reflection on the positive end, the input is \(B_h=\sum_{i=1}^h q_iX^{e_i}\) with strictly increasing \(e_i<0\), and the causal inner multiplier \(I\) has \(|I(0)|=t\).
Write \(B_j\) for the first \(j\) inputs and \(L_j\) for the norm of the modes above \(e_j\) of \(I B_j\).
The recurrence and its iterated form there give, for \(S_j=s\sum_{i\le j}t^{j-i}|q_i|\),
\[
  0\le t^{h-j}(S_j-L_j)\le S_h-L_h .
\]
Its one-step hypothesis holds uniformly because \(\lim s^2\|B_h\|_2^2=2(x+b_\pm)<1\), and every occupied input has magnitude at least one.

Set \(M=\sqrt2(1-e^{-x})>0.4\), \(p_\pm=M+\sqrt2\,b_\pm/3\) and \(Z_\pm=\lim\sqrt m L_h\) on the two ends, taking a subsequence.
The integer inequality \(|q|-1\le(q^2-1)/3\) gives \(\limsup\sqrt m S_h\le p_\pm\) on the respective ends.
By the [orthogonal splitting](Yin_C_G0345_v659.md#saturation), the parts of \(DQ\) have norms \(\|P_+ I B_h\|_2\) in their respective orientations.
These are at most \(L_h\), since \(e_h<0\).
Thus \(w^2\le Z_-^2+Z_+^2\), whereas the Euclidean triangle inequality gives \(\|(p_-,p_+)\|_2\le\sqrt2 M+\sqrt2 b/3=W\).
Consequently
\[
 0\le (p_\pm-Z_\pm)M
 \le \sum_{\sigma=\pm}(p_\sigma^2-Z_\sigma^2)
 \le W^2-w^2\le\sqrt2 W\eta .
\]
As \(M>0.4\) and \(W<0.6\), each terminal deficit satisfies \(\limsup\sqrt m(S_h-L_h)<3\eta\).

Fix \(u=4\cdot10^{-4}\) and \(k=\lfloor u/m\rfloor\), which lies between \(1\) and \(h\) on both ends eventually.
Since \(t^{k-h}\to e^{x-u}<3/2\) and \(\sqrt m\,s\sum_{i=1}^k t^{k-i}\to\sqrt2(1-e^{-u})\), the recurrence gives
\[
 \liminf\sqrt m L_k\ge\sqrt2(1-e^{-u})-5\eta,\qquad
 \limsup m\sum_{i\le k}|q_i|\le u+\eta .                            \tag{2}
\]
The second bound uses \(b_\pm/3\le\eta\).

<a id="product"></a>
## Signed tests from two distinct prefix coefficients

Define \(d\gamma=|I-I(0)|^2d\tau/s^2\) for circle Haar probability \(\tau\).
As in the [prefix Fourier test](Yin_C_G0345_v659.md#contradiction), one has \(L_k\le s\|B_k\|_{L^2(\gamma)}\), since the constant multiplier contributes no modes above \(e_k\).
Hence, with \(M_k=\sum_{i\le k}|q_i|\), (2) gives
\[
 \liminf \|B_k/M_k\|_{L^2(\gamma)}
 \ge \frac{\sqrt2(1-e^{-u})-5\eta}{\sqrt2(u+\eta)}
 \ge 1-u .
\]
For the last inequality use \(1-e^{-u}\ge u-u^2/2\) and \((5+\sqrt2)\eta<\sqrt2 u^2/2\).

Draw two indices independently with weights \(|q_i|/M_k\).
The expected squared \(L^2(\gamma)\) distance of their signed monomials is \(2(1-\|B_k/M_k\|_{L^2(\gamma)}^2)\), of limit superior at most \(4u\).
Their coincidence probability is \(O(m)\), since \(\sum_{i\le k}q_i^2=O(1/m)\) and \(M_k\ge k\).
Conditioning on distinct indices therefore gives a pair with some positive lag \(g\), sign \(\epsilon\), and \(d=\|X^g-\epsilon\|_{L^2(\gamma)}\le0.041\) eventually, since \(\sqrt{4u}=0.04\).
Reflecting the circle preserves the signed distance because \(|X^{-g}-\epsilon|=|X^g-\epsilon|\).
Choose these tests independently on the two ends and denote the binomials by \(\Delta_\pm\).

In the original coordinates write \(U=P/O,\ V=O^*/P,\ y_-=(U-U(0))Q_-\), \(y_+=(\bar V-\overline{V(0)})Q_+\) as in the [projection splitting](Yin_C_G0345_v657.md#decomposition).
The same integer inequality on each full end gives \(\limsup m\sum|q_i|\le x+b_\pm/3\).
It follows that \(\limsup\sqrt m\|y_\pm\|_2\le\sqrt2(x+\eta)<1/2\) and \(\limsup\sqrt m\|\Delta_\pm y_\pm\|_2\le 0.041/2\).
The [block estimate for a signed binomial](Yin_C_G0345_v657.md#orders) then bounds the scaled norm of its application after the relevant Fourier projection by \((0.041+\sqrt{0.041})/2\) in the limit superior.
Apply the other binomial with norm at most two to each term in \(DQ=\bar U P_+y_-+V P_-y_+\).
Using (1) with \(T=\Delta_-\Delta_+\) gives
\[
 \limsup\sqrt m\|TH\|_2
 \le 4\sqrt{1.22\eta}+2.44(0.041+\sqrt{0.041})<0.596 .
\]
Both binomials have roots only on the unit circle, so \(TR\) remains indivisible by \(P\), with all multiplicities allowed.
The [unrestricted energy floor](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#statement) gives \(\liminf\sqrt m\|TH\|_2\ge\sqrt K>0.6\), a contradiction.
