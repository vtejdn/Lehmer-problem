# Prescribing the integer output by reduction at a short period

<a id="statement"></a>
## A sufficient characteristic for simultaneous coefficient counts

Let \(\alpha\) be a nontorsion algebraic unit of degree \(d\), \(m=\log M(\alpha)\to0\) along a sequence, and \(L=\log(1/m)\).
Use the [finite witness](Yin_C_G0345_v575.md#scale) with \(T=\lceil64d\log(2d)\rceil\), \(N=\lfloor T\log(2)/(4m)\rfloor\) and \(3\le j\le T+1\) at which there are \(A_j\ge(\log(2)/(32m))^{j-1}\) minimal supports up to translation.
Write \(Y=\log j+Nm/d\) and \(H=\lfloor e^Y\rfloor\).
For a positive integer \(D\) let \(l_D\) be the additive order of one in the finite ring \(\mathbb Z[\alpha]/(\alpha^D-1)\), with \(l_D=1\) in the zero ring, and put
\[
                    E_D=D^2(1+\lfloor 2H/l_D\rfloor).
\]
Suppose \(D\) can be chosen at the successive inputs with \(\log E_D=o(L)\).
Then for a weight \(r\to\infty\) a single tuple of nonzero rational integers, **including the output**, realizes at least \((c/(mE_D))^{r-2}\) sorted marked rational-terminal exponent lists, with an absolute \(c>0\).
All coefficients have magnitude at most \(H\), all marked exponents have magnitude at most \(4Yd/m\), and \(r\le j\).
If \(E_D=O(1)\), this gives the full constant-base lower bound \((c'/m)^{r-2}\) for one prescribed tuple and output.
If all powers of the input preserve degree or the unit has a real conjugate, the number \(V\) remaining after division by the common positive exponent gcd satisfies \(\log V\ge(1-o(1))(r-2)L\).
These conditions use no neutral conjugate.
One may test them without knowing \(j\) by replacing \(H\) in \(E_D\) by \(\lfloor (T+1)\exp(Nm/d)\rfloor\).

## The characteristic of a cyclic quotient

Write \(P\) for the minimal polynomial.
The ring is also \(\mathbb Z[X]/(X^D-1,P)\), and multiplication by \(P\) on the free rank-\(D\) group \(\mathbb Z[X]/(X^D-1)\) presents its additive group.
The determinant has absolute value \(\prod_{z^D=1}|P(z)|\ne0\), by complex evaluation at the distinct roots of unity.
The additive exponent of the quotient equals the order of its identity, since any integer killing the identity kills every element by multiplication.
Consequently \(l_D\) is exactly the largest Smith invariant of this integer matrix, and \(l_D\ge(\prod_{z^D=1}|P(z)|)^{1/D}\) by the product of the invariant factors.
For \(v\mid D\), evaluation at a primitive \(v\)-th root gives a surjection to \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_v]/(P(\zeta_v))\); its characteristic divides \(l_D\) and is at least \(|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_v,P)|^{1/\varphi(v)}\).
Furthermore the [cyclotomic remainder content](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v310.md#test) \(k_v\) divides that characteristic, since \((P(\zeta_v))\subseteq k_v\mathbb Z[\zeta_v]\).
In particular \(l_1=|P(1)|\), and \(l_2\) is a multiple of both \(|P(1)|\) and \(|P(-1)|\).
It suffices for the constant-base conclusion that \(D\) remain bounded and that any of these certified lower bounds on \(l_D\), including their least common multiple when they are known divisors, be at least a fixed positive multiple of \(H\).

## Reading a fixed output from the child subtrees

Split each ternary relation with rational carriers and reconstruct it from the leaves in its [ordered plane tree](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#trees).
For any original or intermediate edge \(z=c\alpha^u\), the cut identity gives \(h(z)\le Y\), and integrality gives \(c\in\mathbb Z\).
The norm \(N_{\mathbb Q(\alpha)/\mathbb Q}(z)=\pm c^d\) yields \(h(c)\le h(z)\) and \(|u|m/d\le2Y\), as in the [rational edge estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v595.md#near).
Thus every local output and child coefficient has magnitude at most \(H\), and subtracting the parent exponent puts all local positions in the stated window.
Let \(W_s\) maximize the count in these windows over fully prescribed integer coefficients of every term, requiring [rational terminality](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#statement).
At a vertex of degree \(s\), fix the constructed child subtrees and their now known outgoing coefficients \(a_i\).
There are at most \(D^{s-1}\) possible residue lists for their relative exponents \(t_i\bmod D\), since the parent position is zero.
For each list the equation \(\sum a_i\alpha^{t_i}=b\) prescribes the element of the quotient represented by \(b\), since its image of \(\alpha\) is a unit with \(D\)-th power one.
The map from rational integers into this quotient has kernel \(l_D\mathbb Z\), so an attaining list permits at most \(1+\lfloor 2H/l_D\rfloor\) possible outputs in the allowed interval.
For each such output at most \(W_s\) choices of the actual exponent list remain, even before imposing its prescribed residues.
This argument permits the characteristic and the chosen residues to involve primes dividing \(D\).

There are at most \(j-2\) internal vertices, \(\sum_v(s_v-2)=j-2\), and \(\sum_v(s_v-1)\le2(j-2)\).
The plane tree and leaf signs have at most \(32^j\) possibilities by the same reconstruction.
Consequently
\[
 A_j\le 32^j E_D^{j-2}\Big(\max_{3\le s\le j}W_s^{1/(s-2)}\Big)^{j-2}.
\]
For \(m\) sufficiently small comparison with the lower bound gives \(\max W_s^{1/(s-2)}\ge c/(mE_D)\), using \(j/(j-2)\le3\).
Taking a maximizing arity gives the stated count with one full coefficient tuple.
The maximizing arity grows because the free-output bounds at every fixed weight hold uniformly for rational coefficients as the minimum positive-power degree tends to infinity. [Fixed arity and power fields](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#trees)
The primitive conclusions follow with logarithmic dilation loss \(O(L+\log(2r))\) from the [fixed-polynomial determinant or real-root argument](Yin_C_G0345_v595.md#primitive), as \(r\to\infty\) and \(\log E_D=o(L)\).
The extent of arithmetic growth of these characteristics at shrinking hyperbolic records remains open.
