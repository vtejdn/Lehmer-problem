# Bounding all powered pairs on one signed row

<a id="statement"></a>
## One polynomial for every graph exponent

Fix a nonzero integral polynomial \(A\) of degree at most \(b\), a sign \(\varepsilon\), and a prime \(q\).
Write \(H=\|A\|_1,\ U=-\varepsilon A,\ V=Y^b A(1/Y)\), and \(f=U/V\).
For each integer \(n\ge2\) the signed graph has equation \(XV(X^n)-U(X^n)=0\), up to the nonzero scalar \(\varepsilon\).
Retain a pair \((x,n)\), up to conjugacy in \(x\), if \(x\) is a nontorsion algebraic unit, both \(x,x^q\) are roots of this graph, and both values of \(V\) at \(x^n,x^{qn}\) are nonzero.
Let \(t(x,n)\) be the smaller of the two orders of vanishing on the graph, \(d_x=[\mathbb Q(x):\mathbb Q]\), and \(m(x)\) the logarithmic Mahler measure of the monic minimum of \(x\).
If at least one pair is retained, the integral polynomial
\[
       E_q(Y)=\frac{U(Y^q)V(Y)^q-V(Y^q)U(Y)^q}{q}
\]
is nonzero, has degree at most \(2qb\), and satisfies \(\|E_q\|_1\le 2H^{q+1}/q\).
Across all retained pairs at all exponents on this row,
\[
 \sum_{(x,n)}t(x,n)d_x\le\deg E_q,\qquad
 \sum_{(x,n)}t(x,n)n\,m(x)\le\log M(E_q)
                         \le(q+1)\log H+\log(2/q).                         \tag{1}
\]
In particular any genuine double contact for \(x,x^2\) satisfies \(2n\,m(x)\le3\log H\), with no dependence on the graph width.
All double-contact pairs on a fixed row can be enumerated from \(E_q\) using exact operations.

<a id="proof"></a>
## Value recovery, multiplicity and finite enumeration

Frobenius on the coefficients modulo \(q\) proves integrality, and the triangle and convolution inequalities give the stated one-norm.
If \(E_q=0\), the rational function \(f\) satisfies \(f(Y^q)=f(Y)^q\).
Write its Laurent expansion at zero as \(aY^s\) times a power series with constant term one.
Comparison of leading coefficients gives \(a^{q-1}=1\).
If the series had a first nonconstant term in degree \(j>0\), its coefficient after raising to the \(q\)th power would be \(q\) times the original coefficient in degree \(j\), while substitution \(Y^q\) contributes zero there.
Thus \(f=aY^s\), and \(x=f(x^n)\) would imply \(x^{1-ns}=a\), impossible for a nontorsion \(x\) because \(n\ge2\).

At \(X=x\), put \(t=t(x,n)\).
The two graph equations give \(f(X^n)=X+O((X-x)^t)\) and \(f(X^{qn})=X^q+O((X-x)^t)\), using the nonzero denominators and the nonvanishing derivative of \(X^q\) at \(x\).
Consequently \(E_q(X^n)\) vanishes to order at least \(t\) there.
Since \(X^n\) is unramified at \(x\), the minimum of \(y=x^n\) divides \(E_q(Y)\) to order at least \(t\).
The equality \(x=f(y)\) gives \(\mathbb Q(y)=\mathbb Q(x)\), and hence \(m(y)=n\,m(x)\) by the product over the conjugates of \(x\).
If two powered nodes are conjugate, recovery transports one seed to the other under the same conjugation.
After transport the recovered seed has equal powers at the two exponents, so its infinite order forces equality of the exponents.
The resulting distinct monic irreducible factors and their multiplicities give (1), since every nonzero integral cofactor has nonnegative logarithmic measure and \(M(E_q)\le\|E_q\|_1\).

For double contacts factor \(E_q\) and take a root \(y\) of each irreducible factor, omitting poles and zeros of \(f\) and roots of \(V\).
The double-contact identity \(n y f'(y)=f(y)\) determines the only possible \(n\), since \(f(y)\ne0\).
Test this quotient for integrality and \(n\ge2\), put \(x=f(y)\), and check \(x^n=y\), unit and nontorsion status, both denominators and both exact graph multiplicities.
If \(E_q=0\) the preceding Laurent argument returns the empty list.

<a id="binomial"></a>
## Two occupied positions exclude two distinct positive powers at every height

Suppose the support of \(A\) consists of the two positions \(w,w+k\), with coefficients \(a,c\ne0,\ k>0\).
Writing \(T=Y^k\), the function \(f\) is a signed monomial times \((a+cT)/(c+aT)\).
If \(a^2=c^2\) it is itself a signed monomial, which admits no genuine nontorsion node.
Otherwise its logarithmic derivative is
\[
 \frac{Yf'(Y)}{f(Y)}
 =2w+k-b+\frac{k(c^2-a^2)}
                 {ac(T+T^{-1})+a^2+c^2}.
\]
At two genuine double contacts \(x^i,x^j\) for the same exponent \(n\) this expression must have the same value \(1/n\) at \(Y=x^{in},x^{jn}\).
For distinct positive \(i,j\) this would force \(x^{ink}=x^{jnk}\) or \(x^{ink}=x^{-jnk}\), both impossible.
A one-term row gives a monomial \(f\) as well.
Thus at least three occupied positions are necessary even without a coefficient budget.

<a id="counts"></a>
## Coefficient cutoffs for arbitrarily slowly diverging primes

Let \(\lambda\) be the unrestricted infimum of positive logarithmic Mahler measures.
Use the [common-prime dyadic tests](Yin_C_G0345_v466.md#statement) with any computable primes \(p_j\to\infty\) and \(D_j=2^j\).
Let \(N_j(\tau)\) count even degrees in \([D_j,2D_j)\) with genuine double contacts at \(x,x^2\) at one prescribed sign, \(2d\le b<3d\), \(H\le2^{p_j}\) and
\[
        \frac{(b-2d+1)\log2}{d}+\frac{3\log H}{2p_j}<\tau.               \tag{2}
\]
Retain the degree and reciprocity conditions of that test and, optionally, any computable support cap \(s_d\to\infty\).
One may instead require both signs for the same seed and width with (2) at each sign.
For either count, \(\lambda=0\) if and only if \(\liminf_j N_j(\tau)/D_j>0\) for every positive rational multiple \(\tau\) of \(\log2\).
In the forward direction the [fixed-base construction](Yin_C_G0345_v466.md#density) makes the width contribution and \(\log H/p_j\) arbitrarily small on a positive proportion of the degrees at both signs, uniformly over each late block.
In the reverse direction (1) yields an actual positive measure below the cutoff at every counted degree.
The integer coefficient box and the exact graph and factor tests make the counts computable, including the strict comparisons after clearing denominators.

At the single width \(b=5d/2-1\) take \(p_j\to\infty,\ p_j=o(D_j)\).
Write \(K_j\) for the number of even degrees in the block with common genuine double contacts at both signs and \(H^8<2^{p_j}\) on each row, with the same optional cap.
Then \(\liminf_j p_j K_j/D_j=0\) implies \(\lambda\ge(\log2)/120\).
Indeed a positive measure \(h<(\log2)/120\) gives an all-power base unit of the same measure by the [irreducible reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#upper) and [norm construction](Yin_C_G0345_v421.md#norm).
The [fixed-width construction](Yin_C_G0345_v466.md#narrow) has \(\log H/p_j\le(15-10/e)h+o(1)\) for a fixed base degree \(e\), uniformly on the retained substitutions \(k\equiv1\bmod p_j\).
The correction to \(H\) costs at most a factor of two, independently of the inflated degree.
Thus both signs pass the coefficient cutoff for those degrees, and the [arithmetic progression count](Yin_C_G0345_v466.md#count) gives a positive lower limit for \(p_jK_j/D_j\).
No estimate for this lower limit from above is established here.
