### Exact compensated transition

Write x=a0+a1 beta+a2 beta^2+a3 beta^3 and retain

    K(x)=-(a0 a1+a1 a2+a2 a3+a0 a2+a1 a3+a0 a3),
    ell(x)=a0+a1+a2+3a3.

The order is stable under beta and beta^(-1).
Since beta^4=beta^3+beta^2+beta-1, multiplication by beta sends the coefficient vector to

    (-a3,a0+a3,a1+a3,a2+a3).

C300 gives rho(x)^2=sqrt13 K(x)+x_+x_- on the actual order.
Multiplication by beta rotates the neutral image and preserves x_+x_-, so their difference gives K(beta x)=K(x) directly.
On the actual core, |x_+x_-|<BS=(3+sqrt13)/2<sqrt13; integrality and rho(x)^2>=0 therefore imply K(x)>=0, by the same algebra fixed in C780.
This proof uses the existing C300 identity directly and does not depend on adoption of C780.
Adding an integer e to the constant coefficient changes K by minus e times the sum of the other three coordinates.
Thus for every x in O and every integer e,

    K(beta x+e)-K(x)=-e ell(x).                         (LD1)

In particular the actual unrestricted iid original law satisfies

    E[K(X_(j+1))|X_j]=K(X_j),
    E[(K(X_(j+1))-K(X_j))^2|X_j]=ell(X_j)^2/2.         (LD2)

The finite-time variables have finite support, so these expectations are legitimate without an integrability hypothesis.
This is the elementary martingale identity itself, not an optional-stopping inference.
The form K is indefinite on the full order; its nonnegativity applies only to the actual return core.
Consequently the unconditional equality E K(X_j)=0 cannot be combined with core nonnegativity to assert K=0 under a bridge.
For example x=beta+1 has K(x)=-1 and is outside the strict real core.
The nonzero original length-six returned states in C780 already have K=1.

Since beta<2, B>1 and S>1.
Choose

    d_+=(B-1)/(2 beta), d_-=beta(S-1)/2=S/2.

If |x_+|<d_+ and |x_-|<d_-, then x is in D and every beta x+e, e=-1,0,1, is in D, because

    beta d_++1=(B+1)/2<B,

### A quantitative same-level cost for the actual harmonic function

At every LD4 row, the actual harmonic equation H=R P_D H has all three legal original digit probabilities and gives

    sum_(e=-1)^1 p_e H(beta x+e)=H(x)/R.              (LD7)

Therefore at least one of these same-level successors has H(beta x+e)<=H(x)/R.
All values are strictly positive, and hence the ratio of the maximum to the minimum H value among x and its three successors is at least R.
The construction supplies such configurations at arbitrarily large K levels.
Every fixed level is finite, because the real strips and rho^2=sqrt13 K+x_+x_- bound all four real embeddings.
Consequently the true harmonic function has within-level max/min ratio at least R on an unbounded sequence of occupied integer levels.
This statement applies in either critical-mean branch.

In particular H cannot be any positive function of K alone.
More quantitatively, suppose a positive function h(k), a number 0<=epsilon<1 and a cutoff k0 obeyed

    (1-epsilon)h(K(x))<=H(x)<=(1+epsilon)h(K(x))
                    for EVERY x in D with K(x)>=k0.      (LD8)

Choose an LD4 row above that cutoff.
All four states have the same level, so LD7 implies

    1+epsilon >= R(1-epsilon),
    epsilon >= (R-1)/(R+1).                            (LD9)

The adopted [C776 true-radius bound](Yin_C_G0776_v1.md#decision-whole-reviewed-2026-10-26) is R>1687/1000, and the displayed rational function is strictly increasing for R>0.
Thus LD8 necessarily has

    epsilon>687/2687.                                  (LD10)

This excludes small uniform relative-error replacement of H by an arbitrary level-only profile, even one that is irregular or nonmonotone.
The bound is necessary only; no h or error above the threshold is constructed, and no sharp attainable error for the actual H is claimed.
It neither excludes a profile depending on the other actual coordinates nor controls the actual finite bridge imbalance in LD6.
No free Perron normalization, displacement-preserving translation or stationary surrogate is used.
