# A mixed-signature Mahler gap without a prescribed coefficient discrepancy

<a id="statement"></a>
## A growth condition on the relative degree

Let \(\alpha\) be a reciprocal nontorsion algebraic unit of logarithmic Mahler measure \(m\), and let inversion on \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\) induce a nonidentity automorphism \(\tau\) on a stable subfield \(F\).
Write \(r>0\) and \(s>0\) for the counts of its real and nonreal embeddings, with each nonreal embedding counted separately, and set \(b=[K:F]\), \(t=s/r\).
For fixed \(A,C>0\), there is a uniform positive lower bound for \(m\) on all such inputs satisfying
\[
 t\le A,\qquad
 \max\left\{[b\log(2b)]^{t/2},\ (b/r)^t\right\}\le Cr.            \tag{1}
\]
In particular, when \(s=r\), the single condition \(b\log(2b)\le C_0 r^2\) suffices for each fixed \(C_0>0\).
The conclusion holds for both torsion and nontorsion relative constants and requires no restriction on the first differing coefficient.
For \(s=r\) and \(b\) of order \(r^2/\log(2r)\), a first difference at the coefficient of degree \(b-1\) leaves the factor \(2b\) at every nonreal embedding in the [single coefficient bound](Yin_C_G0345_v624.md#statement).
At these parameter sizes that bound alone permits vanishing measure in the torsion-constant case.

<a id="finite"></a>
## Estimate at the actual first differing coefficient

Let \(q\) be the monic minimum of \(\alpha\) over \(F\), let \(j\) be the first coefficient index of disagreement of \(q,q^\tau\) from their common leading coefficient, and let \(\mathcal D\) be the absolute norm of the relative discriminant ideal of \(F/F^\tau\).
Write \(e=r+s\), \(\rho=\exp(-1/b)\), and put
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W={}&2(b-1)m+
       e\left[b(b-1)\log(1/\rho)+b\log(1/(1-\rho^2))\right],\\
 B_j={}&2\rho^{-j}\sqrt{\frac{W}{js}}+\frac{4m}{s}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Then \(1\le j\le b\) and
\[
 \mathcal D\le [4\sinh(m/r)]^r
    \min\left\{\left(\frac{2b}{j}\right)^s e^{2m},\
                         B_j^s e^{2jm}\right\}.                     \tag{2}
\]
The first entry is the [first-moment discriminant estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v624.md#statement).

<a id="energy"></a>
## Reflected power sums in the conjugate factors

For a complex embedding \(\sigma\) of \(F\), including a real one, write \(m_\sigma=m(q^\sigma)\), and let \(\Delta_\sigma\) be the polynomial discriminant of \(q^\sigma\).
Its roots \(\lambda_i\) are simple.
Put \(\beta_i=\lambda_i/\max(1,|\lambda_i|)^2\) and
\[
 E_\sigma=\sum_{\ell\ge1}\frac{\rho^{2\ell}}{\ell}
                    \left|\sum_{i=1}^b\beta_i^\ell\right|^2.
\]
We apply the [reflected disk inequality](Yin_C_G0345_v108.md#phases) separately to each complex factor, retaining its actual discriminant.
Indeed on the closed disk \(|v-w|\le |1-\bar v w|\).
For every pair of roots this gives
\[
 |\lambda_i-\lambda_k|\le
 \max(1,|\lambda_i|)\max(1,|\lambda_k|)|1-\bar\beta_i\beta_k|.
\]
For two exterior roots apply the disk inequality to their reflected values, and for roots on opposite sides the displayed inequality is equality.
Squaring gives \(|1-z|\le\rho^{-1}|1-\rho^2z|\) for \(|z|\le1\).
Use the two bounds for \(i\ne k\), and use \(1-\rho^2|\beta_i|^2\ge1-\rho^2\) on the diagonal.
The absolutely convergent logarithmic expansion gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
  E_\sigma
    &=-\sum_{i,k}\log|1-\rho^2\bar\beta_i\beta_k|\\
    &\le 2(b-1)m_\sigma-\log|\Delta_\sigma|
          +b(b-1)\log(1/\rho)+b\log(1/(1-\rho^2)).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The conjugate factors partition the roots of the rational minimum, so \(\sum_\sigma m_\sigma=m\).
Their discriminants have nonzero integral product, since they are the conjugates of a single nonzero algebraic integer in \(F\), as in the [discriminant-product estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v539.md#energy).
It follows that \(\sum_\sigma E_\sigma\le W\).

For any \(j\ge1\), the \(j\)-th term alone bounds \(|\sum_i\beta_i^j|\) by \(\rho^{-j}\sqrt{jE_\sigma}\).
An exterior root of log modulus \(h\) contributes an error \(|\lambda_i^j-\beta_i^j|=e^{jh}-e^{-jh}\le2jh e^{jh}\).
All other roots have zero error.
Hence
\[
 \frac1j\left|\sum_i\lambda_i^j\right|
       \le \rho^{-j}\sqrt{E_\sigma/j}+2m_\sigma e^{jm_\sigma}.      \tag{3}
\]

<a id="product"></a>
## Taking the product over the embeddings

Put \(\mu_\sigma=m_\sigma+m_{\sigma\tau}\), where right composition by \(\tau\) permutes each signature class.
At the first differing coefficient, the [power-sum identity](Yin_C_G0345_v624.md#coefficient) expresses its value \(w_\sigma\), up to sign, as the difference of the two \(j\)-th power sums divided by \(j\).
By (3) it satisfies
\[
 |w_\sigma|\le
  \left[\frac{\rho^{-j}}{\sqrt j}
              (\sqrt{E_\sigma}+\sqrt{E_{\sigma\tau}})
                      +2\mu_\sigma\right]e^{j\mu_\sigma}.
\]
Over the \(s\) nonreal embeddings the geometric mean of the bracket is at most its arithmetic mean.
The sum of the two square roots over them is at most \(2\sqrt{sW}\), and the sum of the \(\mu_\sigma\) there is at most \(2m\), so that arithmetic mean is at most \(B_j\).
At the real embeddings the [reflected coefficient bound](Yin_C_G0345_v616.md#discriminant) has product at most \([4\sinh(m/r)]^r\exp(\sum_{\sigma\ {\rm real}}\mu_\sigma)\).
Since \(j\ge1\) and \(\sum_\sigma\mu_\sigma=2m\), the combined exponential factor is at most \(e^{2jm}\).
The same relative order bounds \(\prod_\sigma |w_\sigma|\) below by \(\mathcal D\), proving the second entry of (2).

<a id="uniform"></a>
## Removing the coefficient index

Suppose for contradiction that a sequence satisfies (1) and \(m\to0\).
We may take \(m\le1\).
If \(j>r\), the \(r\)-th root of the first entry of the minimum in (2) is at most
\[
 (2b/r)^t e^{2m/r}\le 2^A Cr e^{2m/r}.
\]
For \(j\le r\), use the second entry.
Since \(j\le b\), we have \(\rho^{-j}\le\exp(1)\).
Also \(1/(1-\exp(-2/b))\le 1+b/2\), by \(\exp(2/b)\ge1+2/b\).
Consequently \(W\le b[2m+e(1+\log(1+b/2))]\).
Writing \(e/s=1+1/t\), we obtain for an absolute constant \(c\ge1\)
\[
 B_j\le c\sqrt{b\log(2b)}\,(1+1/t)^{1/2}
\]
using \(j,s,b\ge1\) and \(m\le1\).
The inequality \(t\log(1+1/t)\le1\) gives
\[
 B_j^t e^{2jm/r}
 \le c^A \exp(1/2)\,[b\log(2b)]^{t/2}e^{2m}
 \le c^A \exp(1/2)\,Cr e^{2m}.
\]
In both cases (2) and \(\mathcal D\ge1\) give \(1\le O_{A,C}(r\sinh(m/r)e^{2m})=O_{A,C}(m)\), a contradiction.
If \(t=1\) and \(b\log(2b)\le C_0r^2\), both terms in (1) are \(O_{C_0}(r)\), as required.
