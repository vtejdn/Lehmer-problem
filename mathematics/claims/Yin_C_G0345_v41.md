# Empty gaps in displaced Chebyshev grids for polynomials of small Mahler measure

<a id="statement"></a>
## The fraction of gaps missed by a reciprocal target

Define \(D_k\) by \(D_k(z+z^{-1})=z^k+z^{-k}\).
Fix a real polynomial \(B\) of degree \(s\ge1\) with \(s\) distinct roots \(2\cos\phi_j\), where \(0<\phi_1<\cdots<\phi_s<\pi\).
Set \(\phi_0=0,\ \phi_{s+1}=\pi\), \(v_j=(\phi_{j+1}-\phi_j)/\pi\) for \(0\le j\le s\), and
\[
          u_0=u_s=\frac1{2s},\qquad u_j=\frac1s \quad(0<j<s).
\]
Let \(P(z)=z^m S(z+z^{-1})\) run through monic irreducible reciprocal integer polynomials with \(M(P)>1\) tending to one, where \(M\) is multiplicative Mahler measure.
Let integers \(k\to\infty\) satisfy \(m/(sk)\to1\).
Of the \(sk-1\) open internal gaps between the roots of \(B(D_k)\), let \(E_k\) count the gaps containing no real root of \(S\).
Then
\[
             \liminf \frac{E_k}{sk}
                   \ge \frac12\sum_{j=0}^s |u_j-v_j|.
\]
Thus \(o(k)\) empty gaps require \(B\) to be a scalar multiple of \(D_s\).
For \(B(x)=2x+a\) with \(0<|a|<4\) the lower bound is \(|\arcsin(a/4)|/\pi\).
This includes all four primitive integral displacements \(a=-3,-1,1,3\) of the leading-two grid \(2D_k+a\).

In particular fix \(H\ge0\).
If \(0\le m-sk-1\le H\) and there are real roots of \(S\) on both sides of all the nodes and one in every internal gap, there is a positive uniform gap above one for \(M(P)>1\) on this class, depending only on \(B,H\).
This also holds for \(B\) proportional to \(D_s\).
At \(a=0\) the nodes are torsion traces, while \(a=\pm4\) does not give \(k\) distinct interior nodes and \(|a|>4\) gives none.

<a id="folding"></a>
## The phase of a moving power counts the available trace roots

Choose a root \(\alpha\) of \(P\).
It is nontorsion and has absolute logarithmic height \(\log M(P)/(2m)\).
The powers \(\alpha^k\) have height tending to zero since \(k=O(m)\).
They form a strict sequence in the one-dimensional torus since none is a root of unity.
By the [one-dimensional small-points theorem](../audits/literature/Bil97/Yin_U001.md#strict-small-points-and-orbit-measures), the arguments of their conjugates are equidistributed on the circle.
The pushforward of the full orbit of \(\alpha\) under the power map gives those orbit masses, since each image has the same number of preimages.
Consequently the folded angle \(\arccos(\cos(k\arg z))\) for the \(2m\) conjugates \(z\) is asymptotically uniform on \([0,\pi]\).
Interval endpoints have limiting mass zero.

Let \(N_j\) count the roots \(x=2\cos\theta\) of \(S\) in \((-2,2)\) whose folded angles \(\arccos(\cos(k\theta))\) lie in \((\phi_j,\phi_{j+1})\).
Each contributes two phases to this interval among the \(2m\) conjugates.
The remaining conjugates can only add to the phase count, so \(\limsup N_j/m\le v_j\) even if some traces of the target are nonreal.

As \(0<\theta<\pi\) varies, the folded angle makes \(k\) monotone passages across \((0,\pi)\), with successive passages traversed in opposite directions.
Each interior bin \(0<j<s\) produces \(k\) separate gaps between consecutive nodes of \(B(D_k)\).
Each endpoint bin produces \(k/2+O(1)\) internal gaps, as the two neighboring passages join at the corresponding extremum, and at most two truncated end components are discarded.
These numbers divided by \(sk\) tend to \(u_j\).
A target root in any occupied open gap contributes to the corresponding bin count unless its folded angle is the extremum \(0\) or \(\pi\).
Such extrema cannot occur for a target root, since \(z^k=\pm1\) would make its circle lift torsion.
Thus at least the positive part of the difference between the gap count and \(N_j\) counts empty gaps in that bin.
Summing and using \(\sum u_j=\sum v_j=1\) gives \(\sum (u_j-v_j)_+=\frac12\sum |u_j-v_j|\) as asserted.

Equality \(u_j=v_j\) for all \(j\) requires \(\phi_j=(2j-1)\pi/(2s)\), precisely the roots of \(D_s(2\cos\theta)=2\cos(s\theta)\).
For \(B=2x+a\) the single threshold is \(\phi_1=\arccos(-a/4)\), giving the stated discrepancy for both signs.
If the fixed-degree-defect uniform gap failed, the resulting sequence would have \(k\to\infty\), since bounded degree and bounded Mahler measure allow only finitely many monic integer polynomials.
Full occupation then forces the exceptional profile \(B\) proportional to \(D_s\).
In that case \(D_s(D_k)=D_{sk}\) is a monic integer polynomial with distinct torsion trace roots, and the [partial torsion interlacing gap](Yin_C_G0345_v38.md#statement) applies with the stated end roots and defect bound.

<a id="sign"></a>
## Alternating signs for a fixed modulated grid

Let \(Q(z,w)\) be a fixed nonzero Laurent polynomial that is real on the two-dimensional unit torus.
In a sequence of targets \(P\) as above, suppose \(k\to\infty,\ k=O(m)\), and \(Q(z,z^k)=A_k(z+z^{-1})\) for a real polynomial \(A_k\).
Suppose \(A_k\) has \(g=m+o(m)\) simple roots, all in \((-2,2)\), with only \(o(m)\) empty internal gaps, and has no other roots.
Then for almost every \(z\) on the circle,
\[
                   \int_{\mathbb T}\operatorname{sgn}Q(z,w)\,dw=0,
\]
where \(dw\) is normalized arclength.
In particular this excludes every profile \(A_k(x)=a(x)D_k(x)+b(x)\) satisfying the stated node hypotheses with fixed real polynomials \(a,b\) and \(b\ne0\).

To see this, select one target trace root in each occupied gap of \(A_k\).
Their number is \(m-o(m)\), so they account for all but \(o(m)\) of the traces, and all the selected roots lie in \((-2,2)\).
The sign of \(A_k\) alternates on consecutive gaps because its roots are simple.
Within any fixed subinterval of \(0<\theta<\pi\), the sum of \(\operatorname{sgn} A_k(2\cos\theta)\) over the selected trace roots \(2\cos\theta\) is \(o(m)\).
Indeed a full block of consecutive gaps has imbalance at most one, deleting the empty gaps costs at most their number, and at most two gaps meet the ends of the interval without being contained in it.
Adding any omitted traces changes this count by \(o(m)\) for bounded sign weights.

For \((\alpha,\alpha^k)\) the sum of the coordinate heights tends to zero and any fixed nonzero integer character \((a_0,b_0)\) is nontrivial eventually, since \(\alpha^{a_0+k b_0}\ne1\) for all large \(k\).
The [two-dimensional small-points theorem](../audits/literature/Bil97/Yin_U001.md#strict-small-points-and-orbit-measures) therefore gives independent Haar arguments on the full torus.
Test the upper-half phase interval in the first variable and the sign of \(Q\) in both variables.
The discontinuities have product Haar measure zero, since a nonzero Laurent polynomial vanishes on a Haar-null subset of the torus, by one-variable zero counting in the successive coordinates.
The count just established makes the limiting signed integral zero on each such interval.
The lower semicircle has the same conclusion by counting the conjugate circle lift of every selected trace, using the same identity \(Q(z,z^k)=A_k(z+z^{-1})\).
Taking intervals with rational endpoints proves the stated conditional integral.
For \(Q=a(z+z^{-1})(w+w^{-1})+b(z+z^{-1})\), the real variable \(w+w^{-1}\) has a continuous symmetric distribution with unique median zero.
At every base point where \(a\ne0\) and \(b\ne0\), the conditional sign integral is therefore nonzero.
If \(a\) is identically zero and \(b\ne0\), it is likewise nonzero almost everywhere.
This proves the exclusion.
