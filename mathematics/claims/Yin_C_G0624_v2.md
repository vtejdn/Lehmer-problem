    v_5(T(C5(delta_*)))=J,

## Equivalent root-order screen and exact limits

    z^2+z^(-2)=z^(5^j)+z^(-5^j),

### 2. The C3 derivative, maximal-distance comparison and full residual contradiction

Choose a prime attaining maximal t_p and one actual root delta_*=eta+w above xi with t=v_5(w)=max_p t_p>0.

Both C3 and C5 commute with C_(5^h), so their images of eta are the unique periodic lifts of their image residues.
The C5 image has residue xi^5 in the same cluster, and (C3a) puts the C3 image in that same cluster as well.
The exact expansions are

    C5(eta+w)-C5(eta)
      =5(eta^4-3eta^2+1)w+5(2eta^3-3eta)w^2
         +5(2eta^2-1)w^3+5eta w^4+w^5,
    C3(eta+w)-C3(eta)=3(eta^2-1)w+3eta w^2+w^3.

Every C5 term has valuation greater than t.

Thus, with A=v_5(a), B=v_5(b), C=v_5(c) at this SAME actual conjugate,

    C=J-1.                                       (C3g)

The C3 displacement has valuation exactly t, because its linear coefficient is a unit and its higher terms have larger valuation.

    B>=J=C+1.                                    (C3h)

The original values are nonzero integral, so A,B,C are finite and nonnegative.

    2B>=2C+2=5A+2>3A,

### 4. Full residue-field reconstruction and ninth roots of unity

Use the actual residue field k_p=F_(5^h), whose full degree was just proved.
The residues abar,cbar are nonzero and bbar=0.
The two original residual equations become

    cbar^2=-abar^5,
    cbar^3=s abar^3.                              (C3i)

The first identity uses 3/2=-1 in characteristic 5; no identity abar^5=abar is assumed.
Dividing the second equality by the first gives

    cbar=-s abar^(-2).

Squaring this and comparing with the first gives abar^9=-1.
Set rho=-abar; then rho^9=1 and cbar=-s rho^(-2), proving (C3d), including Tbar(C2(xi))=2abar=-2rho.
Conversely, substitution of any rho^9=1 into these formulas satisfies both equations (C3i) with nonzero a,c and b=0.
This converse concerns only those residue equations, not an original input or a lift to characteristic zero.

The roots in mu_9(k_p) are the common roots of X^9-1 and X^(5^h-1)-1.
The Euclidean identity for these two binomials gives their polynomial gcd X^g-1, where g=gcd(9,5^h-1).
Since 5 does not divide 9, all these roots are distinct, so their number is g.
The successive powers of 5 modulo 9 are

    5,7,8,4,2,1.

Hence the order of 5 modulo 9 is 6.
For odd h there is no factor 3 in 5^h-1; for h=2 or 4 modulo 6 it has gcd 3 with 9; for h=0 modulo 6 the gcd is 9.
This proves (C3e).
In particular the cube map cannot be treated as bijective for general even h, and the actual residue of a need not generate k_p.

### 5. Exact root-order criterion and the three examples

Let z be a nonzero root of Z^2-xi Z+1 in the algebraic closure of F_5 and let n be its finite multiplicative order.
Then n is prime to 5, and z,z^(-1) have the same order.
The trace identity and Frobenius give

    C3(xi)=xi^(5^j)
      iff z^3+z^(-3)=z^(5^j)+z^(-5^j).

The two terms z^3,z^(-3) are precisely the roots of the same monic quadratic with constant coefficient 1 as z^(5^j),z^(-5^j) when these traces agree.
Thus the equality is equivalent to z^3=z^(5^j) or z^3=z^(-5^j), giving exactly (C3f).
This argument includes a repeated quadratic root.
If 3 divided n, the left residue 3 modulo n could not equal a unit plus or minus 5^j; thus 3 does not divide n.
There is no parity restriction on n from this criterion.

For n=2 one has 3=5^0 modulo 2, and the trace is xi=-2 in F_5.
For n=4 one has 3=-5^0 modulo 4, and xi=0 in F_5 because z^2=-1.
These have h=1 and therefore rho=1, giving the first two target values in the statement.
They agree with the already fixed rational trace-cluster conclusions of C622v3, without extending those F_5 arguments to higher residue degree.

For n=8 one has 3=-5 modulo 8.
Since z^2 has order 4, z^2+z^(-2)=0, so xi^2=2.
The element 2 is not a square in F_5, whose square residues are 0,1,4, so xi has degree h=2 and C2(xi)=0.
The target value Tbar(0) lies in F_5, and (C3d) consequently forces rho in F_5 intersect mu_9(F_25).
The nonzero elements of F_5 have order dividing 4, while rho has order dividing 9, so rho=1.
Thus Tbar(0)=-2=3, with abar=-1,cbar=-s in this specific degree-two example.
This rational-target argument, not a general bijective cube map in F_25, produces the stronger conclusion.

For n=13, the subgroup generated by 5 and -1 is {1,5,8,12}, which does not contain 3.
Primitive order13 traces are therefore not C3-preserved either, and the new preservation theorem supplies no conclusion for those trace orbits.
The present result supplies no realization or general classification for such non-preserved cycles.
