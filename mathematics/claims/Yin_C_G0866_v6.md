## All-place normalization

All logarithms are natural unless a base is written.
For a nonzero algebraic number z of actual degree D_z and own field K_z, use the usual absolute values and local degrees n_v at its places.
At a finite place over p, write integer ord_v, ramification e_v and residue degree f_v, so n_v=e_v f_v and log|z|_v=-(ord_v(z)/e_v)log p; at an infinite place n_v is 1 or 2 and the usual modulus is used.
Set

    h_abs(z)=(1/D_z)sum_v n_v log^+|z|_v,
    m(z)=D_z h_abs(z),
    M_2(z)^2=D_z sum_v n_v(log|z|_v)^2.                 (N1)

Thus m(z) is the total logarithmic Mahler measure of its primitive irreducible polynomial, including the leading coefficient.
For an actual unit beta of own degree D, every finite-place term vanishes.
Uniform averaging E over a containing finite Galois field has constant restriction multiplicity over its D actual embeddings and gives

    h_beta=m(beta)/D=E log^+|sigma(beta)|,
    M_2(beta)=D sqrt(E[(log|sigma(beta)|)^2]),
    2m(beta)<=M_2(beta).                                (N2)

The actual unit hypothesis is retained in every use of these equalities.
No formal norm-one expression substitutes for integrality and invertibility, and no normal-closure degree replaces D.

## Statement

### A. The full-use odd even-kernel setting

Let alpha be a genuine Salem number of degree d=2t>=6 with t odd, splitting field L, G=Gal(L/Q), and actual inverse-pair kernel V exactly the even-weight flip group.
Put E_0=L^V and choose one oriented root u_i from every inverse pair, 1<=i<=t.
For arbitrary positive rational weights a_i define

    X_i=(u_i+1)/(u_i-1),
    Z=sum_i a_i X_i, W=sum_i a_i,
    beta=(Z+1)/(Z-1),
    K_beta=Q(beta), D=[K_beta:Q],
    Gamma=Norm_(L/E_0)(beta).                            (S1)

Assume beta is an actual algebraic integer unit.
Integrality alone is not asserted to imply that condition in this domain.
The norm Gamma is a totally positive unit, E_0(beta)=L and D>=2^(t-1).
If Gamma!=1, then

    M_2(beta)>=log(phi), phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2.               (S2)

### B. Actual coordinate norms when Gamma=1

Assume Gamma=1.
For each i put

    K_i=Q(u_i), H_i=Gal(L/K_i),
    V_i=V intersect H_i, E_i=L^(V_i)=E_0(u_i),
    N_i=Norm_(L/E_i)(beta),
    F_i=K_beta(u_i), Gamma_i=Norm_(F_i/K_i)(beta).

The group V_i is normal in H_i and has index two in V.
Every embedding taking u_i to a neutral root has |N_i|=1 and |Gamma_i|=1 at that embedding.
At the two real K_i-embeddings, Gamma_i is positive, strictly expanding and strictly contracting respectively, and it has actual degree d.
Write alpha=alpha_0^A with its smallest Salem field generator, coherent conjugates v_i^A=u_i, and m_0=log alpha_0=log(alpha)/A.
Then

    Gamma_i=v_i^(n_i), n_i>=1 integer.                  (S3)

Let H=Gal(L/K_beta), and let its orbits on the selected oriented roots have sizes q_O and count kappa.
The rational weight is constant on each orbit, n_i=n_O is constant there, and for i in O the exact degrees are [F_i:K_beta]=q_O and [F_i:K_i]=D q_O/d.
Consequently

    m(beta)=m_0 sum_O n_O=:N m_0,
    M_2(beta)^2>=2d m_0^2 sum_O n_O^2/q_O.               (S4)

These statements are proved here in the actual even kernel under Gamma=1; they are not a domain extension by citation of v4's other coordinate-norm theorem.

### C. Square-root-degree cost and the two distinct branches

Use the adopted v5 constants and define

The last ratio tends to infinity with d, with the actual source power A paid before the numerical height input.
After enlarging the degree threshold if necessary, all actual units in the full-use odd even-kernel setting with m(alpha)<=1 satisfy

    M_2(beta)>=min{log(phi), k_1 sqrt(d)m_0}.             (S7)

The constant Gamma!=1 branch supplies no degree-divergent ratio for all sequences of merely bounded positive source measure.
No assertion is made that Gamma=1 is impossible or that an actual unit solution of that equation exists.

### D. The complete rational linear Cayley construction class

For this combined conclusion let alpha be any genuine Salem number of degree d=2t>=4, with its actual inverse-pair kernel and the same smallest-generator notation m_0=m(alpha)/A.
Combine the new branch with the adopted v5 result for r<t or an actual full flip kernel.

### The actual even-kernel norm-one bridge and full-use Cayley costs

    beta=(Z+1)/(Z-1),
    K_beta=Q(beta), D=[K_beta:Q],
    Gamma=Norm_(L/E_0)(beta).                            (EK1)

Assume beta is an actual algebraic integer unit.

The [adopted C866 v4 full-use setup](Yin_C_G0866_v4.md#statement) already proves that the output is defined, nonzero and nontorsion, that X_i are E_0-linearly independent, and that

    E_0(beta)=L,

    M_2(beta)>=log(phi), phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2.              (EK3)

#### The actual index-two partial norms

Assume henceforth Gamma=1.
For each i define

    K_i=Q(u_i), H_i=Gal(L/K_i),
    V_i=V intersect H_i={v in V:v(u_i)=u_i},
    E_i=L^(V_i),
    N_i=Norm_(L/E_i)(beta).                             (EK4)

The subgroup V_i has index two in V: the i-th sign takes both values among the even flips when t>=3.
It is normal in H_i because V is normal in G and V_i=V intersect H_i.
Also K_i is contained in E_i, and E_i=E_0(u_i): the root satisfies T^2-(u_i+u_i^(-1))T+1 over E_0, while an even flip moving u_i shows u_i is not in E_0.
Thus E_0(u_i) is contained in E_i and both have degree two over E_0.
Consequently all displayed norm towers are actual field norm towers, with

    Norm_(E_i/E_0)(N_i)=Gamma=1.                        (EK5)

Fix any g in G for which g(u_i) is neutral.
Let c be ordinary complex conjugation on L in the chosen complex embedding, and put c_g=g^(-1)c g.
It is the actual Salem conjugation c_j fixing the pair whose g-image is real and inverting all other pairs.
Because g(u_i) is neutral, j!=i, so c_g belongs to V but not V_i.
Its restriction to E_i represents the nontrivial automorphism of E_i/E_0.
Equation EK5 therefore reads

    Gamma=N_i c_g(N_i),
    1=g(Gamma)=g(N_i) conjugate(g(N_i))=|g(N_i)|^2.

Hence

    |g(N_i)|=1 whenever g(u_i) is neutral.              (EK6)

This is an actual conjugation in the even kernel and an actual degree-two norm identity.
No missing odd sign pattern has been inserted into the Galois distribution.

Now use the legal tower K_i subset E_i subset L.
With representatives h of the cosets hV_i in H_i/V_i,

    Theta_i:=Norm_(L/K_i)(beta)
      =Norm_(E_i/K_i)(N_i)
      =product_(hV_i in H_i/V_i) h(N_i).                (EK7)

At any g with g(u_i) neutral, every gh also takes u_i to the same neutral root, because h fixes u_i.
Every factor in g(Theta_i) therefore has modulus one by EK6.
Thus all K_i-embeddings of Theta_i corresponding to neutral u_i have modulus one.

#### Descent to the actual coordinate fields and positive Salem norms

Define the actual coordinate field and norm

    F_i=K_beta(u_i),
    Gamma_i=Norm_(F_i/K_i)(beta).

The field contains K_i and beta, and beta is a unit there.
The norm tower retains the exact multiplicity

    Theta_i=Gamma_i^[L:F_i].                            (EK8)

It follows from EK7--EK8 that every neutral K_i-embedding of Gamma_i has modulus one.
At the embedding taking u_i to alpha, every extension to F_i has Re Z=a_i coth(log(alpha)/2)>0 and hence log|beta|>0.
At u_i=alpha^(-1), every such logarithm is negative.
All these relative output embeddings are nonreal, as noted after EK2, so their complex-conjugate pairs have positive products.
The two real values of Gamma_i are therefore positive, one greater than one and the other less than one.
Their product is one because Gamma_i is a unit and all other complex-conjugate pairs have modulus-product one.

The unique expanding K_i-embedding proves that Gamma_i has its own degree d: a value in a proper subfield would repeat each of its embeddings with the nontrivial extension multiplicity.
Under the identification taking u_i to alpha, Gamma_i is consequently a genuine Salem number in Q(alpha).
Write alpha=alpha_0^A with the actual smallest Salem field generator, put m_0=log(alpha_0)=log(alpha)/A, and choose coherent conjugates v_i with v_i^A=u_i.
The exact accepted [Smy15-U001 interface](../audits/literature/Smy15/Yin_U001.md#smy15-u001), at its retained statement/displayed-proof scope, gives

    Gamma_i=v_i^(n_i), n_i>=1 integer.                  (EK9)

The original Salem source proofs and regulator bounds are not newly adopted.
The positive coordinate norms have now been proved in the even-kernel norm-one branch; they are not an extrapolation of v4's unused-pair/full-kernel hypothesis.

#### Original Mahler exponent and all actual multiplicities

Let H=Gal(L/K_beta).
The E_0-linear independence of X_i makes the stabilizer of beta exactly the stabilizer of its rational coefficient vector under the actual signed-permutation action.
Since all a_i are positive, H permutes the selected oriented roots without inversion and preserves each weight.
Let O be its orbits, q_O their sizes and kappa their number, so sum_O q_O=t.
For i in O the field degrees are exactly

    q_i=[F_i:K_beta]=|H.u_i|=q_O,
    e_i=[F_i:K_i]=D q_i/d.                              (EK10)

An h in H carrying u_i to u_j carries F_i,K_i,Gamma_i,v_i to the corresponding j objects while fixing beta.
Thus n_i in EK9 is constant on each O; write it n_O.
If ell_i is the actual Galois average of log|beta| conditional on u_i mapping to alpha, each relative F_i-embedding has the same extension multiplicity in L, and

    ell_i=n_i m_0/e_i=d n_i m_0/(D q_i).

The mean conditional on u_i=alpha^(-1) is -ell_i.
Each positive event has probability 1/d, these are exactly the positive radial events, and all selected pairs are used.
Therefore

    m(beta)=D sum_i ell_i/d
           =m_0 sum_i n_i/q_i
           =m_0 sum_O n_O=:N m_0,
    M_2(beta)^2>=2d m_0^2 sum_O n_O^2/q_O.              (EK11)

The second inequality is conditional Cauchy--Schwarz on both radial signs at the actual own degree D.
Its finite terms vanish because beta is an actual unit.
In particular, with B=M_2(beta)/m_0,

    B>=2N>=2kappa>=2,
    q_O>=2d n_O^2/B^2>=2d/B^2.                         (EK12)

These recover the necessary norm accounting while retaining the correct degree bound D>=2^(t-1), not 2^t.
