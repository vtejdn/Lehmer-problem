# Unit-circle fibers and conjugates off the circle on sections approaching an exceptional slope

<a id="statement"></a>
## When a point mass at zero controls the actual signature

Let \(F\) have the rank-two, primitive integral and rational irreducibility hypotheses of the [archimedean orbit theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v774.md#statement).
Fix a signed primitive integer direction \((p,q)\) and put \(u=(p,q)/\|(p,q)\|_\infty\).
Choose integers \(r,s\) with \(pr+qs=1\), and use the unimodular characters
\[
                   U=X^qY^{-p},\qquad V=X^rY^s.
\]
Up to a Laurent monomial the equation in these coordinates is
\[
                 G(U,V)=\sum_{j=0}^{D} A_j(U)V^j=0,\qquad
                 A_0A_D\ne0,\qquad D>0 .
\]
The two extreme face polynomials for \(u\) are \(A_0,A_D\) up to monomials in these coordinates.
Write \(\nu_u\) for the limiting distribution of scaled archimedean logarithmic radii.
The following conditions are equivalent.

1. \(\nu_u=\delta_0\).
2. Both \(m(A_0)\) and \(m(A_D)\) equal \(m(F)\).
3. At every \(|U|=1\), the fiber polynomial has degree \(D\) and all \(D\) roots counted with multiplicity have modulus one.

Under these conditions neither \(A_0\) nor \(A_D\) has a unit-circle zero.
The map \(U\) on the smooth projective normalization of each geometric component is unramified above the unit circle.
For a section \(F(\xi z^a,\eta z^b)=0\) with roots of unity \(\xi,\eta\), put
\[
                 k=qa-pb,\qquad l=ra+sb,\qquad N=\max(|a|,|b|)>0.
\]
Thus \(U=\xi' z^k\), \(V=\eta' z^l\) for roots of unity \(\xi',\eta'\).
If \(k=0\), all complex conjugates of \(z\) have modulus one, and an algebraic unit solution must be torsion.

If \(k\ne0\) and \(|l/k|\) is sufficiently large, there are constants \(0<c<C\) depending only on the fixed curve and coordinates with
\[
      c\le |k|\,\big|\log|\sigma z|\big|\le C
           \quad\hbox{whenever }|\sigma z|\ne1 .                         \tag{1}
\]
Let \(d=[\mathbf Q(z):\mathbf Q]\), and let \(h_{\rm off}\) count its complex embeddings off the unit circle.
For every nontorsion solution in this range \(h_{\rm off}/d\ll_F |k|/N\).
For nontorsion algebraic units one has the two-sided estimates
\[
             h_{\rm off}\asymp_F |k|\,m(z),\qquad
             \frac{h_{\rm off}}d\asymp_F\frac{|k|}{N}.                  \tag{2}
\]
Here and below the constants may also depend on the chosen coordinates for the fixed direction.
In particular the actual neutral fraction tends to one on all sequences approaching this ray with \(k\ne0\) and \(|l/k|\to\infty\).

There are \(c_1,C_1>0\) with the following finer conclusion for every nonneutral embedding.
Extend the embedding to the phases and the powered point.
Its \(U\)-coordinate lies within \(C_1e^{-c_1|l/k|}\) of a nonzero root \(\lambda\) of \(A_D\) if \(|V|>1\) and of \(A_0\) if \(|V|<1\).
Consequently, for a Salem number \(\tau\) arising as \(z\), there is such a root with
\[
          \big|\,|k|\log\tau-|\log|\lambda||\,\big|
                 \le C_1 e^{-c_1|l/k|}.                                \tag{3}
\]
Thus these directions give a measure gap at bounded \(|k|\), and a shrinking Salem sequence on them requires \(|k|\to\infty\).
The estimates allow \(\log\tau\) to have order \(1/|k|\) when \(|k|\) grows.

<a id="fibers"></a>
## Equality at both faces and every fiber on the circle

The [one-sided first moments](Yin_C_G0345_v774.md#jensen) prove the equivalence of the first two conditions.
The unimodular change preserves Haar probability on the torus.
For all circle parameters \(U\) except the finitely many zeros of \(A_0A_D\), denote the \(D\) roots of \(G(U,V)\) by \(\rho_j(U)\), with multiplicities.
Jensen's formula in \(V\) and then in \(V^{-1}\) gives
\[
 \begin{split}
   m(F)-m(A_D)&=\int_{\mathbb T}\sum_j \log^+|\rho_j(U)|\,dU,\\
   m(F)-m(A_0)&=\int_{\mathbb T}\sum_j \log^+|\rho_j(U)|^{-1}\,dU .
 \end{split}
\]
The logarithms of the fixed nonzero Laurent polynomials are integrable, for instance by the [sparse sublevel estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v774.md#continuity).
If both integrals vanish, all roots have modulus one at almost every circle parameter.
There \(|A_0|=|A_D|\) and \(|A_j|\le\binom Dj |A_D|\) by elementary symmetric functions.
Continuity extends these inequalities over the circle.
A common zero of the \(A_j\) in \(\mathbf C^\times\) would be algebraic, and its rational minimal polynomial would divide every coefficient after clearing Laurent monomials, contrary to irreducibility and rank two.
It follows that \(A_0\) and \(A_D\) never vanish on the circle.
Continuity of the roots of the resulting monic fiber polynomial now gives condition 3 everywhere, including repeated roots.
Conversely condition 3 makes both Jensen integrals zero.

For an exact direction \(k=0\), \(U\) and all its conjugates are roots of unity, hence every \(V\) above them and every conjugate of \(V\) has modulus one.
Since \(l\ne0\), the equality \(|V|=|z|^{l}\) gives the same conclusion for all conjugates of \(z\).
Kronecker's theorem applies if \(z\) is an algebraic unit.

<a id="annulus"></a>
## Uniform control on the normalization

Fix a geometric component and work on its compact complex normalization \(C'\).
Both \(U,V\) are nonconstant meromorphic functions because [no component has a constant nontrivial monomial](Yin_C_G0345_v769.md#geometry).
Near a parameter on the unit circle both extreme coefficients stay nonzero, which bounds the sizes of the roots of the fiber and their reciprocals.
Every point \(P_0\) with \(|U(P_0)|=1\) therefore has \(0<|V(P_0)|<\infty\).
The full-fiber condition gives \(|V(P_0)|=1\).
Choose a complex local parameter \(w\) at \(P_0\) with \(\log(U/U(P_0))=w^e\), where \(e\) is the ramification index.
Write \(H(w)=\log(V/V(P_0))\) with \(H(0)=0\).
Its real part vanishes whenever \(\Re w^e=0\).
On the rays \(\arg w=(\pi/2+j\pi)/e\), each coefficient \(c_m\) of the power series \(H(w)\) satisfies
\(\Re(c_me^{im(\pi/2+j\pi)/e})=0\).
Two consecutive rays show that \(c_m=0\) unless \(e\mid m\).
Thus \(H(w)=H_0(w^e)\) with \(\Re H_0=0\) on an interval of the imaginary axis.
If \(e>1\), nearby \(w\) differing by a factor \(e^{2\pi i/e}\) would have identical \((U,V)\).
This contradicts generic injectivity of the normalization into its plane component, so \(e=1\), including above a singular plane image.
Comparing \(H_0(v)\) with \(H_0(i\Im v)\) and bounding its derivative on a smaller disk gives
\[
                 \big|\log|V|\big|\le C\,\big|\log|U|\big|
\]
near \(P_0\).
Compactness of the inverse image of the circle and of the complement of these neighborhoods gives common constants over an annulus \(|\log|U||<c\).
There are only finitely many components.

At the zeros and poles of \(U\), write both meromorphic coordinates as powers of a parameter times holomorphic units.
The ratio \(\log|V|/\log|U|\) stays bounded there, since the order of \(U\) is nonzero.
Compactness therefore gives another bound \(|\log|V||\le C'\,|\log|U||\) whenever \(|\log|U||>C''\).
Now at any complex embedding off the circle,
\[
                    \log|V|/\log|U|=l/k .
\]
For sufficiently large \(|l/k|\), it follows that \(c\le|\log|U||\le C''\), proving (1).
The bounds are valid for all extensions of every embedding to the torsion phases.

<a id="counts"></a>
## Embedding counts and convergence to edge roots

The global sum of absolute logarithmic values at the infinite places of \(z\), counted over the \(d\) embeddings, is at most \(2m(z)\) by the product formula.
For an algebraic unit it equals \(2m(z)\), since all finite contributions vanish.
Summing (1) gives the upper bound \(h_{\rm off}\ll_F |k|m(z)\) for every nontorsion solution and the matching lower bound for units.
Here \(\gcd(|a|,|b|)=\gcd(|k|,|l|)\) by unimodularity.
As \(|l/k|\to\infty\), the primitive direction length therefore tends to infinity, while \(N\asymp_F |l|\).
The [individual-height theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v769.md#statement) gives \(h(z)\asymp_F 1/N\) for all these solutions, since \(m(F)>0\) and the width is comparable to \(N\).
This proves (2).
The estimate was obtained directly at actual embeddings before counting them.

On the compact annulus given by (1) all \(A_j(U)\) are bounded.
If \(|V|>1\), division of the equation by \(V^D\) gives \(|A_D(U)|\ll_F |V|^{-1}\ll_F e^{-c|l/k|}\).
For \(|V|<1\), the same argument applied to the reversed polynomial gives \(|A_0(U)|\ll_F |V|\ll_F e^{-c|l/k|}\).
After removing a Laurent monomial, the relevant face polynomial has fixed leading coefficient and degree.
Factoring it shows that the distance from \(U\) to some nonzero root is \(O_F(e^{-c_1|l/k|})\), since \(U\) lies in a compact annulus away from zero.
If that face polynomial is a monomial, its positive lower bound on the annulus rules out the corresponding case for sufficiently large slope.
The absolute logarithm is Lipschitz there after a small enlargement of the annulus.
Applying the estimate to an embedding with \(z=\tau>1\) gives (3).
Together with [the projected-width height limit](Yin_C_G0345_v769.md#statement), (3) also shows that along a subsequence with fixed limiting root \(\lambda\),
\[
        \frac{[\mathbf Q(\tau):\mathbf Q]\,|k|}{w(a,b)}
               \longrightarrow \frac{|\log|\lambda||}{m(F)}
                  \qquad (|l/k|\to\infty),
\]
where \(w(a,b)\) is the width of the original support projected on \((a,b)\).

## The measure and signature restrictions

The [untwisted edge theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v171.md#statement) already forces a shrinking untwisted Salem sequence towards a real edge root under an intermediate power.
The [critical four-term result](Yin_C_G0345_v759.md#statement) counts neutral embeddings on compatible Möbius components under its shrinking small-value hypotheses.
For a totally real pole the [whole-graph count](Yin_C_G0345_v750.md#statement) gives an exact exterior multiplicity.
Here (1)–(3) control every individual orbit of a full-circle algebraic fiber, including different geometric components and arbitrary external torsion translations.

Infinite Salem families can occur in this regime without their measures tending to zero.
For example take \(G=(U-2)V-(2U-1)\), the integer pole \(2\) and negative sign in the whole-graph result.
It is irreducible, and \(|2U-1|=|U-2|\) on \(|U|=1\).
With \(U=z,V=z^n\) and \(n>3\) the monic reciprocal section is \(z^{n+1}-2z^n-2z+1\).
The whole-graph count gives exactly one root outside the unit circle, and its reciprocal is the only root inside.
The outside root \(\tau_n\) lies in \((2,3)\) by the signs at these endpoints.
It satisfies \(0<\tau_n-2=(2\tau_n-1)/\tau_n^n<5/2^n\).
Its minimal polynomial contains the reciprocal root because every monic factor has constant term \(\pm1\).
The \(\tau_n\) are distinct, since two exponents at the same positive root would give a root of unity.
Their heights are bounded and their degrees tend to infinity by Northcott, so for large \(n\) these are Salem numbers tending to \(2\).
Their measures tend to \(\log2\), in agreement with the bounded transverse exponent in (3).
