# A total-measure transfer to units of totally real trace for commuting conjugations

<a id="statement"></a>
## Extracting individual sign characters at the cost of their orbit

Let \(w\) be a nontorsion algebraic unit of degree \(d\), of total logarithmic measure \(m<\log\phi\), where \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\).
As in the [real-trace generation theorem](Yin_C_G0901_v82.md#statement), suppose all conjugates of complex conjugation \(c\) commute on the rational span \(V\) of the conjugate classes.
Write \(G\) for the splitting group, \(C\) for the normal closure of \(c\) in \(G\), and \(C_0\) for its elementary abelian image on \(V\).
For each character \(\chi:C_0\to\{\pm1\}\) let \(e_\chi\) project onto its common eigenspace.
Take \(O=Cw\), an orbit of actual roots, \(D=|O|\), and put
\[
 S=\{\chi:e_\chi[w]\ne0\},\qquad
 s=|S|,\qquad H=\{g\in G:gO=O\},\qquad a_\chi=|H\cdot\chi|.
\]
The set \(S\) is nonempty, excludes the trivial character and is stable under \(H\).
For each \(\chi\in S\) there is a product \(y_\chi\) of \(D/2\) distinct elements of \(O\) which is a nontorsion reciprocal unit of totally real trace, with both real exterior and neutral conjugates, such that
\[
     \deg y_\chi\le\frac{2d}{D}a_\chi,\qquad
     0<m(y_\chi)\le a_\chi m,\qquad a_\chi\le s .                       \tag{1}
\]
In particular, if \(\min_{\chi\in S}a_\chi\) is bounded along a sequence with \(m\to0\), there is a shrinking sequence of reciprocal units with totally real trace.
This criterion covers arbitrarily large \(D\).
For a strict degree record in this class, if \(H\) fixes any active character, then \(D=2\) and \(w\) itself is reciprocal with totally real trace.

<a id="product"></a>
## Halving the block and controlling its conjugates

For the trivial character the block product \(b=\prod_{u\in O}u\) is fixed by \(C\), and so are all its conjugates up to permutation by \(G\); in particular \(b\) is totally real.
Indeed for every \(g\), \(g^{-1}cg\in C\) fixes \(b\).
It belongs to the divisible conjugate group of \(w\).
The [sharp height transfer for a totally real subfield](Yin_C_G0901_v73.md#products) and \(m<\log\phi\) force \(b\) to be torsion, hence \(b=\pm1\).
Thus \(e_1[w]=0\).
For any \(t\in C\), \(e_\chi[t w]=\chi(t)e_\chi[w]\); in particular the active set is constant on \(O\) and is permuted by \(H\).

Choose \(\chi\in S\).
Every element of \(C\) fixing \(w\) has sign \(+1\) under \(\chi\).
Exactly half of \(O\) can therefore be written \(u=tw\) with \(\chi(t)=+1\); their product is \(y_\chi\).
The other half has product \(b/y_\chi\).
The signed orbit projection in the [integral generation formula](Yin_C_G0901_v82.md#generation) gives \(2[y_\chi]=D e_\chi[w]\ne0\).
Some conjugate of \(c\) acts with sign \(-1\), since those conjugates generate \(C\).
It sends \(y_\chi\) to \(b/y_\chi\).
Transporting that conjugation to the chosen complex embedding sends \(b\) to a positive squared modulus, so \(b=1\).
Consequently every \(t\in C\) acts on \(y_\chi\) by exponent \(\chi(t)\) with no torsion correction.
This implies reciprocity, a totally real trace and at least one neutral conjugate.
Kronecker's theorem gives a nonneutral conjugate, which is real since its trace is real; its inverse is also a conjugate.

The pair \((O,\chi)\) has orbit size \((d/D)a_\chi\).
Above each such pair the two halves of the block are intrinsic up to exchange: changing the starting root within the block either keeps the positive half or exchanges it with the negative half.
Therefore there are at most twice this many conjugates of \(y_\chi\), and \(h(y_\chi)\le D h(w)/2\) by the product construction.
This proves (1).
If \(a_\chi=1\) at a strict record and \(D>2\), (1) contradicts its minimal degree at its measure.
Nontriviality of the active character forces \(D\) even, hence \(D=2\) in the remaining case.
The positive half then consists only of \(w\).

<a id="signs"></a>
## Odd relations and the proportion of off-circle conjugates

For every character \(\chi\) occurring on \(V\), let \(p_\chi=\Pr_g(\chi(g^{-1}cg)=1)\) with \(g\) uniform in \(G\).
It is positive, since an entire orbit of nonzero rational classes on which every conjugate of \(c\) acted by \(-1\) would have all absolute logarithms zero and would represent only torsion.
For \(\chi\in S\) it is exactly the proportion of real conjugates of \(y_\chi\): an inverted nonreal neutral conjugate cannot equal \(\pm1\).
From the [real-place determinant estimate](Yin_C_G0901_v65.md#statement) and the height of the half-block product,
\[
         m>\frac{d}{D}\frac{p_\chi^2}{50\log(8/p_\chi)} .
\]
Every occurring character belongs to a conjugate of \(S\), since the original root classes span \(V\).
Both \(D\) and \(p_\chi\) are preserved on transporting blocks and characters, so the estimate holds for all occurring characters.
The fraction \(\delta\) of nonneutral conjugates of \(w\) equals the probability of the union of the positive-sign events for \(\chi\in S\).
Indeed \((1+g^{-1}cg)[w]\) is zero exactly when all those signs are negative; its image under \(g\) is the class of \(|g(w)|^2\), positive real and torsion only when it equals one.
Thus for some \(\chi\in S\) one has \(p_\chi\ge\delta/s\).
This gives the explicit necessary inequality
\[
             m>\frac{d}{D}\frac{(\delta/s)^2}{50\log(8s/\delta)} .        \tag{2}
\]

If an odd number \(h\) of occurring characters has trivial product, their signs at any conjugate of \(c\) cannot all be negative.
Hence one of their positive-sign proportions is at least \(1/h\).
The same bound implies \(m>(d/D)/(50h^2\log(8h))\).
In particular as \(m\to0\), the lengths of all such odd relations must diverge, uniformly on every input, including characters from different blocks.
If there is no odd relation, an element of \(C_0\) acts as \(-I\) on all of \(V\).
Indeed assigning the binary value one to each occurring character then respects every linear relation in the dual vector space over \(\mathbb F_2\), and the resulting functional extends to the full dual and is evaluation at an element of \(C_0\).
Conversely \(-I\) rules out every odd relation by evaluation.
These tests give no lower bound for inputs escaping the stated character-orbit and sign conditions.
