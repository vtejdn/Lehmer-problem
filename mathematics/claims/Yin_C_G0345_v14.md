# A positive excess for two Chebyshev terms across all Pisot branches

<a id="statement"></a>
## Uniform in the leading coefficient, offset and degree

Let \(f\) be a Pisot minimal polynomial with positive constant \(q\ge2\), exterior root \(\theta\), and [trace numerator](Yin_C_G0345_v7.md#trace)
\[
               C(x)=jU_h(x/2)+aU_{h-d}(x/2),\qquad
                  j\ge1,\quad 1\le d\le h,\quad 0\ne a\in\mathbb Z .
\]
If \(|a|\) divides \(j\), there is an absolute positive lower bound for \(\theta-q\).
In particular both unit coefficients \(a=\pm1\) are covered simultaneously for all branch indices \(j\).
The conclusion bounds the excess and permits the constant \(q\) to grow with \(j\).

We also prove a fixed-ratio perturbation statement.
Write \(\Pi=(1-z^2)z^h C(z+z^{-1})\) for an arbitrary trace numerator on branch \(j\).
Fix \(k\ge1\) integral, \(M<\infty\) and \(\eta>0\).
If \(\|\Pi/j\|_\infty\le M\) on the circle and \(\Pi/j\) agrees through degree \(\lfloor\eta h\rfloor\) with \(1+\tau z^d/k\), \(\tau=\pm1,\ 1\le d\le h\), then \(\theta-q\) has a positive lower bound depending only on \(k,M,\eta\).
No upper bound on the number of terms is required for this fixed-ratio assertion.
The main researcher extended the two-scale argument to the normalized coefficient ratio.
Agent-S obtained the eight projected series at denominator \(k\ge3\), and a separate contributor developed the large-denominator grid argument proposed by the main researcher.

<a id="small"></a>
## A vanishing coefficient ratio rounds the inverse measure to a finite grid

For the exact two-term input set \(N=2h+2,\ k=j/|a|\), and \(\tau=\operatorname{sgn}(a)\).
Then
\[
          \Pi/j=1-z^N+(\tau/k)(z^d-z^{N-d}).
\]
On an assumed sequence with \(\theta-q\to0\), \(k\to\infty\) is impossible by the [endpoint-dominance bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v64.md#dichotomy).
Here are the steps of that bound.
Write \(\Pi/j=1-z^N+E\) with sum of absolute coefficients \(e\), equal to \(2/k\) in this case.
At every support point \(v\) of the [signed inverse measure](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v49.md#signed-measure), the zero of \(\Pi\) gives \(|1-v^N|\le e\).
For small \(e\), mapping \(v\) to its nearest \(N\)-th root moves the argument by at most \(\pi e/(2N)\).
The total variation of twice the inverse measure is bounded since its negative mass is \(j/[4(q-1)]\to0\) by the [derivative identity](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#second).
The Fourier transform of the doubled grid measure thus approximates the doubled original moments uniformly through order \(N\), with error \(O(e)\).
The identity \(B^\sharp=(\Pi/j)J_q\) for \(B^\sharp=z^{h+1}B(z+z^{-1})\) shows that at \(1\le l<N\) these moments differ from \([z^l]B^\sharp\) by \(O(e)\).
The coefficient at residue zero modulo \(N\) is \(b_0+b_N=2\), exactly the doubled mass.

The integer coefficient function of the folded denominator on \(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z\) consequently lies \(O(e)\) from a finite Fourier transform of bounded algebra norm.
The [near-integer Fourier-algebra bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0464_v7.md#rounding) makes it a sum of boundedly many signed coset indicators once \(e\) is small enough.
The polynomial of a coset with step \(t\mid N\) is \(z^r(1-z^N)/(1-z^t)\) for its least representative \(0\le r<t\).
Multiplying by the product of the binomials \(1-z^t\) for these boundedly many cosets leaves boundedly many integer terms, also after restoring \(B^\sharp\) from its folded polynomial by adding \(z^N-1\).
This cyclotomic multiple has measure \(M(B^\sharp)>1\) tending to one, contrary to the [fixed-support gap](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v32.md#boyd-return).
It remains to rule out a fixed \(k\), since a bounded sequence of integer ratios has a constant subsequence.

<a id="seam"></a>
## Vanishing and bounded support near the offset for a fixed ratio

Assume \(\theta-q\to0\) under the fixed-ratio perturbation hypotheses.
Then \(h\to\infty\), \(j/(q-1)\to0\), and the signed measures of \(J_q=j/K_q\) have negative mass tending to zero, as in the [divisibility theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#integrality).
For \(k=1\) the normalized prefix is integral and that theorem immediately gives a contradiction.
For \(k\ge2\), the same theorem gives \(d=o(h)\), since the portion strictly below \(d\) of the given prefix is integral.
On the common prefix,
\[
             b_l=[z^l](1+\tau z^d/k)J_q\in\mathbb Z .                \tag{1}
\]
For each fixed \(L\), the number of nonzero \(b_l\) on \([0,Ld]\) is bounded along the sequence, after extraction.
This is immediate for bounded \(d\), so consider \(d\to\infty\).
The following generalizes the [two-coordinate seam test](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#seam).
For any positive \(t=o(d)\) take the limit probability of \((x,y)=(v^t,v^d)\) under the signed measures.
Let \(M(r,l)\) denote twice its \((r,l)\)-th moment.
By (1), \(M(r,0)\) is integral, \(M(r,1)\) is integral for \(r<0\), and
\[
        M(0,1)+\tau/k\in\mathbb Z,\qquad
        M(r,1)+(\tau/k)M(r,0)\in\mathbb Z\quad(r>0).
\]
Thus \(kM(r,1)\) is an integer Fourier–Stieltjes transform and its periodic part takes values divisible by \(k\), by the negative tail.
In particular \(M(r,0)\) is divisible by \(k\) for all sufficiently large positive \(r\).

The Herglotz series \(J_x\) of the first marginal and its reciprocal have integer Taylor coefficients and positive real part, hence are rational with squarefree cyclotomic denominators by [integer Fourier decomposition](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#periodic).
A purely atomic marginal would be supported on finitely many torsion points and make \(M(r,1)\) periodic, violating its nonintegral value at zero.
The [cyclotomic factor count](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#haar) therefore gives \(J_x=1,\ 1\pm x^\ell\) or \(1/(1\pm x^\ell)\).
The reciprocal binomials violate the eventual divisibility by \(k\).
If \(J_x=1+\epsilon x^\ell\), projecting \(2ky\) times the probability onto \(\bar x\) gives a density \(Q\) with Fourier coefficients \(kM(r,1)\).
They are integral and tend to zero at infinity, so \(Q\) is an integer Laurent polynomial.
The marginal density gives \(|Q|\le k|1+\epsilon x^\ell|^2\), forcing \((1+\epsilon x^\ell)^2\mid Q\).
But the displayed moment relations yield \(Q\equiv-\tau(1+\epsilon x^\ell)\pmod k\).
Reduction modulo any prime divisor of \(k\) contradicts that divisibility in the Laurent polynomial ring.
Thus \(J_x=1\).
Consequently \(b_t=0\) throughout \(1\le t\le c_0d\) eventually for some \(c_0>0\) on the sequence, by diagonal extraction as in the [Haar-marginal proof](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#haar).

Characters from that difference range are orthogonal against the signed measure, and on any fixed collection the Gram matrix against its positive part tends to the identity.
For \(0<l<d\) the coefficient \(b_l\) is the moment of the constant multiplier 2 against \(v^l\,d\omega_q\).
For \(d<l\le Ld\) the multiplier is \(2+(2\tau/k)v^{-d}\), bounded by three.
Thus more than ten nonzero coefficients in an interval of diameter \(c_0d\) in either range would contradict [Bessel's inequality for this Gram matrix](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#sparse): take eleven nonzero integers, whose corrected squared inner products sum to at least \(11-o(1)\), while the squared multiplier norm is at most \(9+o(1)\).
Partitioning the two ranges and counting 0 and \(d\) separately proves the bounded support on \([0,Ld]\).

<a id="period"></a>
## Periodicity between the offset and the trace degree

Let \(d/t\to0,\ t/h\to0\) with \(t>0\).
Project the doubled signed measures by \(v\mapsto(w,u)=(v^d,v^t)\) and take a positive weak limit.
From (1) on the multiples of \(d\), the first marginal is twice a probability with a profile from the [nine-entry list for \(k=2\)](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v57.md#prefix) or the [eight-entry list for \(k\ge3\)](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v65.md#prefix), with the indicated sign rotation.
These are statements about signed measures with a vanishing negative part and an integer prefix, so their parameter is the integer ratio \(k\), independently of the actual leading coefficient \(j\).
All atoms of this marginal have orders dividing six.
For each harmonic \(m\ge1\), weight by \(u^m\), project and reflect the first coordinate, and multiply by \(1+\tau w/k\).
The Fourier coefficients of this measure at every integer shift \(l\) are the limits of \(b_{mt+ld}\), by (1).
Its continuous part is an integer Laurent polynomial density by the [integer-transform row argument](Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#far-rows).
The densities are \((1+\tau w/k)\sigma c_m\) for a single family of conditional probability moments, where \(\sigma\) is the continuous density of the doubled marginal.
All these rows vanish by [prime specialization for \(k=2\)](Yin_C_G0345_v9.md#torsion) with the nine [envelope bounds](Yin_C_G0345_v9.md#brackets), or by [row extinction for the eight profiles](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v65.md#extinction) when \(k\ge3\).
At \(m=1\) the remaining atomic measure has equal coefficients at shifts zero and six.
Thus \(b_{t+6d}=b_t\) eventually.
As in the [uniform window extraction](Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#comparison), one fixed \(L\) gives this period throughout \(Ld\le t\le h/L\) eventually, with \(1/L<\eta\).

<a id="cutoff"></a>
## An integer cyclotomic multiple with bounded support

Set \(U=1-z^{6d}\), let \(Q\) be the truncation of \(UB^\sharp\) through \(\lfloor h/L\rfloor\), and put \(A=1+\tau z^d/k\).
The periodicity and the low support bound imply that \(Q\) has boundedly many terms of bounded size, each of degree at most \((L+6)d\).
Its coefficients are bounded since (1) uses moments of bounded measures and \(1/k\le1/2\).
Now \(J_*=Q/(AU)\) agrees with \(J_q\) through the truncation degree.
Its positive-index coefficients have a uniformly bounded representing Fourier measure by the [congruence-class expansion](Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#comparison), replacing \(2^{-m}\) by \(k^{-m}\) in its absolute convergence estimate.
The identity
\[
           AUB^\sharp-Q\Pi/j=AU(\Pi/j)(J_q-J_*)
\]
has left side of degree \(O(h)\), bounded circle factors on the right before the series, and a zero series prefix of length comparable to \(h\).
The [two bounded cutoffs](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v51.md#window) bound the circle \(L^1\) norm of the left side.
The bound on \(Q\Pi/j\) and \(|A|\ge1-1/k\) yield \(\|UB^\sharp\|_1=O(1)\).
This integer polynomial then has bounded support by the [prime-grid bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v50.md#window), contradicting the [delay-one return and fixed-support gap](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v32.md#boyd-return).
This proves the fixed-ratio assertion.
For the exact two-term family the normalized lift has norm at most four and the prefix is available through \(h\), completing the uniform excess bound.
