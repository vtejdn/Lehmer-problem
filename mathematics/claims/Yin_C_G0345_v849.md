# Discriminant weights at exceptional primes in primitive rectangles

<a id="statement"></a>
## A bound for the whole exceptional-prime contribution below a cutoff

Let \(P=F(X^k,X^l)\) be the [two-pole section](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#statement), where \(1\le k\le K,\ 1\le l\le L\) and \(\gcd(k,l)=1\).
Call a prime bad when it divides the [degree-56 binary form](Yin_C_G0345_v812.md#statement) \(B(k,l)\), and good otherwise.
For \(2\le Y\le2KL\), one can omit \(O_F(\pi(Y))\) primitive directions, including all primitive zeros of \(B\), so that every remaining squarefree section satisfies
\[
 \sum_{\substack{p\le Y\\p\mid B(k,l)}}v_p(\operatorname{disc}P)\log p
 \ll_F \log(2KL)\log(2+K+L)+\log^3(3+K+L).                       \tag{1}
\]
The sum includes the full powers of all bad primes through \(Y\), including the primes of bad reduction of the curve or its slope map.
Squarefreeness is required wherever the discriminant weight is used.

<a id="centers"></a>
## A bounded number of centers across the exceptional slopes

Let \(S\) contain the fixed bad-reduction primes of the [branch classification](Yin_C_G0345_v822.md#map), the prime divisors of the content of \(B\), and all primes below 17.
For \(p\notin S\) the reduction of \(B\) has at most 56 distinct projective zeros, and the logarithmic slope map on the smooth projective curve has degree sixteen.
Each exceptional slope therefore has at most sixteen geometric torus points in its fiber, counting even the points of fibers above the coordinate axes.
The chain rule puts every multiple residue root of the section above one of these points, since the section has unit constant term and \((k,l)\) is primitive.
At a point \((u,v)\) the condition \(u^l=v^k\) determines whether a section coordinate exists, and Bézout's identity gives its uniqueness.
The classification gives ordinary double contacts at compatible unramified points and triple contacts at compatible simple ramification points.

At an unramified point choose the chart \(r=k/l\) when \(p\nmid l\), interchanging \((U,k)\) and \((V,l)\) when the other chart is needed.
In the displayed chart the slope equation is \(rUF_U+VF_V=0\).
Smoothness forces \(F_U\) to be a unit there, since its vanishing would force \(F_V\) to vanish as well.
Thus \(U=f(V)\) on the disk and \(s(V)=Vf'(V)/f(V)\) is the slope coordinate.
Unramifiedness gives \(s'\) a unit, even at a point where the residue of \(r\) is zero.
The resulting unit Jacobian gives a unique unramified lift of the pair at an integral lift \(r_0\) of its residue slope, and inversion of \(s\) gives \(V(r)\) on that slope disk.
In particular, the [ordinary critical-value calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v847.md#disk) applies with the same change of section coordinate, whose inverse exponent \(1/l\) lies in \(\mathbb Z_p^\times\).
For \(U(r)=f(V(r))\), its critical value \(H=\log(U(r)/[u])-r\log(V(r)/[v])\) satisfies \(H''=-V'/V\) with unit constant at \(r_0\), where brackets are Teichmüller lifts.
The [quadratic preparation](Yin_C_G0345_v847.md#spacing) consequently gives disk discriminant valuation one in the shallow case, and \(\sum_{i=1}^2 v_p(r-\lambda_i)\) for two fixed centers in the deep case.
The interchanged chart has the identical derivation for \(l/k\).

At a ramification point the [cubic critical-value calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v848.md#centers) gives disk discriminant valuation two in the shallow case and \(\sum_{i=1}^3v_p(l/k-\lambda_i)\) in the deep case.
These branch slopes are finite in the \(l/k\) chart by the [branch classification](Yin_C_G0345_v822.md#map).
Thus there are at most \(56\cdot16\cdot3\) centers per prime across all exceptional projective slopes, independent of the rectangle.
Each center uses the chart in which it was constructed.

<a id="spacing"></a>
## Spacing of primitive directions and support of the sum

For each of those centers omit the directions of its residue slope for which the chart coordinate differs from the center by an element of valuation greater than \(\log_p(2KL)\), allowing infinite valuation.
If two directions satisfied this test at the same center, their unit denominators would give \(v_p(kl'-k'l)>\log_p(2KL)\).
Distinct primitive directions have a nonzero integer determinant of absolute value at most \(KL\), so at most one direction is omitted per center.
For \(p\le Y\) outside \(S\), every remaining compatible deep disk costs at most \(3\log(2KL)\), and every shallow disk costs at most \(2\log p\le2\log(2KL)\).
Differences between roots in distinct disks are units, so summing these bounds over the fiber controls the full discriminant at \(p\).
For \(B(k,l)\ne0\) there are \(O_F(\log(2+K+L))\) such prime divisors, since \(|B(k,l)|\ll_F(K+L)^{56}\).
The [fixed-prime valuation bound](Yin_C_G0345_v819.md#statement) contributes \(O_F(\log^3(3+K+L))\) in total at the finitely many primes in \(S\), outside its fixed finite set of primitive directions.
Include these directions and the rational projective zeros of \(B\) and of the [branch form](Yin_C_G0345_v822.md#statement) among the omissions, each rational projective point giving at most one positive primitive pair.
This proves (1) with the stated exceptional count.

<a id="box"></a>
## Two prime cutoffs for reducible sections in a steep box

Put \(h=\lceil(\log M)^6\rceil\) and take \(M\le k<2M,\ Mh\le l<2Mh\).
For sufficiently large \(M\) every such section is squarefree by the [steep-root count](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#statement) and reciprocity, and the interval of slopes avoids the finite projective zeros of \(B\).
In the [integer certificate](Yin_C_G0345_v827.md#statement) \(U\), retain the full prime powers at good primes \(p>M\sqrt h\) and bad primes \(p>M^2h\), and denote their product by \(U'\).
Outside \(O_F(M^2h/\log M)\) primitive directions, the full logarithmic discriminant weight of all discarded primes together is \(O_F(M\sqrt h+\log^3 M)\).
Indeed take \(K=2M,L=2Mh,Y=M^2h\) in (1), and apply the [good-prime rectangle estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v847.md#applications) at \(M\sqrt h\).
Every bad prime retained for large \(M\) divides a nonlinear irreducible factor of \(B\), since each fixed primitive rational linear factor has nonzero integer value of size \(O_F(Mh)<M^2h\) and the content is fixed.

The [trace-discriminant identities](Yin_C_G0345_v833.md#weights) bound both \(\log(U/U')\) and the discarded weight of any monic complementary resultant by this discriminant weight.
Every irreducible factor degree satisfies \(d\gg_F l(\log\log l/\log l)^3\) by the [degree bound](Yin_C_G0345_v811.md#degrees).
Since \(l\asymp M(\log M)^6\), the discarded weight is \(o(d)\) and also \(o(l(\log\log l)^4/(\log l)^3)\).
The [reducibility criterion](Yin_C_G0345_v827.md#statement) now gives, for every remaining reducible section,
\[
                 \log U'\gg_F l(\log\log l)^4/(\log l)^3.            \tag{2}
\]

<a id="graphs"></a>
## Graphs of polynomial growth

For any graph \(1\le l=l(k)\le(2M)^A\) with fixed \(A>0\), restrict to primitive points over \(M\le k<2M\).
Using \(Y=M\) in (1) gives full bad-prime weight \(O_{F,A}(\log^3 M)\) through \(M\) at its squarefree sections outside \(O_F(M/\log M)\) indices.
On the same graph the [ordinary rectangle bound](Yin_C_G0345_v847.md#rectangle) gives full good-prime weight \(O_A(\sqrt M)\) through \(\sqrt M\) with the same order of exceptions.
On the particular graph \(l=hk+1\), the [order-filtered certificate](Yin_C_G0345_v847.md#statement) also removes low-order contacts at larger primes, with its original total error \(O_F(M\log M)\).
The polylogarithmic bad-prime estimate here concerns all contacts through \(M\) on each of the polynomially bounded graphs.
