# Irreducible representatives below base two for any finite forbidden set

<a id="statement"></a>
## A uniform upper bound on the coefficient rate

Let \(L=X^{10}+X^9-X^7-X^6-X^5-X^4-X^3+X+1\) be Lehmer's polynomial.
For a finite set \(B\) of positive orders, let \(N\ge1\) be any common multiple of its members, with arbitrary \(N\ge1\) if \(B\) is empty.
The polynomial \(P_N(X)=L(X^N)\) is monic, reciprocal and irreducible of degree \(10N\), and \(m(P_N)=m(L)\) in total logarithmic Mahler measure.
Recall the [rate for allowed cyclotomic cofactors](Yin_C_G0901_v18.md#rate)
\[
 \gamma_B(P)=\inf_{a\ge1,C}\frac{\log\|P^a C\|_1}{a},
\]
where \(C\) ranges over monic products of cyclotomic polynomials of orders outside \(B\).
The same infimum is the limit of the minima as \(a\to\infty\).
Uniformly in \(B,N\),
\[
 m(L)\le \gamma_B(P_N)
 \le \frac{\log H}{160}
 < \log 2-\frac{\log(10/7)}{160},\qquad
 H=1021447200076579342416613392732943233813204502112.                   \tag{1}
\]
The limiting upper bound still holds if at each multiplicity \(a\) the search is restricted to total degree at most \((5508/1600)a\deg P_N\).
For every \(b\in B\) one has \(|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_b,P_N)|=1\).
In particular, even when \(4\in B\), changing from \(L\) to one of these equal-measure irreducible polynomials gives the upper bound (1), whereas avoiding just \(\Phi_4\) on the original \(L\) has [rate \(\log3\)](Yin_C_G0901_v16.md#statement).

<a id="witness"></a>
## An integral multiple avoiding order one

Take the monic cyclotomic cofactor \(C\) specified by the [twenty nonzero multiplicities](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0186/readme.md#below-two), all at orders \(n\ge2\).
Direct coefficient multiplication gives
\[
    \deg C=3908,\qquad C(1)=2^{104}3^{15}5^{12},\qquad
    \deg(L^{160}C)=5508,\qquad \|L^{160}C\|_1=H .
\]
The comparison \(10H<7\cdot 2^{160}\) follows by integer arithmetic.
In particular the whole circle modulus of \(L^{160}C\) is strictly below \(2^{160}\).
The exact value of \(\gamma_{\{1\}}(L)\) within the resulting bounds remains open here.

<a id="representatives"></a>
## Irreducibility and transfer of the bound

Let \(\alpha>1\) be the real Salem root of \(L\), and \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\).
The [power certificates for the first Salem catalogue entry](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0174/readme.md#powers) give \(\alpha\notin K^p\) for every prime \(p\).
Under the chosen real embedding all elements of \(-4K^4\) are nonpositive, so \(\alpha\notin-4K^4\).
For every \(N\ge2\) the [binomial criterion](../audits/literature/JP18/Yin_U001.md#jp18-u001) gives irreducibility of \(Y^N-\alpha\) over \(K\).
For an \(N\)-th root \(\beta\) of \(\alpha\), its \(N\)-th power generates \(K\), so \([\mathbb Q(\beta):\mathbb Q]=10N\) and \(L(X^N)\) is its minimum.
For \(N=1\) this is the original minimum.
Substitution preserves reciprocity and the positive log moduli sum, since each exterior root gives \(N\) lifts with log modulus divided by \(N\).
Thus the representatives remain nontorsion reciprocal units of exactly the original measure.

Every primitive \(b\)-th root \(\zeta\), \(b\in B\), has \(\zeta^N=1\) and \(P_N(\zeta)=L(1)=-1\), proving the resultant assertion.
Also \(C(\zeta^N)=C(1)\ne0\), so the substituted cyclotomic product \(C(X^N)\) has no forbidden order.
Writing \(a=160j+r\) with \(0\le r<160\), use the multiple
\[
          \bigl((L^{160}C)(X^N)\bigr)^j\, L(X^N)^r .
\]
It has total degree \(N(5508j+10r)\le(5508/1600)a\deg P_N\).
Substitution preserves the one-norm, so the one-norm of this multiple is at most \(H^j9^r\), since \(\|L\|_1=9\).
Division of its logarithm by \(a\) and passage to the upper limit prove (1).
The lower bound follows from \(m(P_N^aC')=a\,m(P_N)\) for every monic cyclotomic cofactor \(C'\) and \(m(P_N^aC')\le\log\|P_N^aC'\|_1\).
Equivalently the [exact power-substitution formula](Yin_C_G0901_v18.md#pullback) gives equality with \(\gamma_{\{1\}}(L)\) for the forbidden set of all divisors of \(N\), and deleting forbidden orders can only lower the rate.
The degree and input polynomial can vary with \(B\), while the measure and upper bound remain fixed.
