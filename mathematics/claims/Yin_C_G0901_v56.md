# The least Salem-orthogonal Mahler measure through degree fifty-five

<a id="statement"></a>
## An exact algebraic minimum on the second branch

For a nontorsion algebraic unit \(w\), let \(M(w)=\exp(m(w))\) and impose the condition that its archimedean logarithms are orthogonal to all primitive Salem conjugates, as in the [unrestricted infimum decomposition](Yin_C_G0901_v52.md#infimum).
Among these units of degree at most \(55\), the least multiplicative Mahler measure is exactly \(M(P)\) for
\[
       P(X)=X^{18}+X^{17}+X^{16}-X^{13}-X^{11}-X^9-X^7-X^5+X^2+X+1 .
\]
In particular
\[
                   1.2013475236 <M(P)<1.20143521 .
\]
Every orthogonal unit of this degree range with \(M(w)<601/500\) already has this same measure.
Its monic minimum is an irreducible sign and power substitution \(P(\pm X^k)\) up to leading sign, \(18k\le55\).
Conversely each irreducible substitution of that form in the degree range gives orthogonal units attaining the same minimum.
The result uses the published completeness and per-degree minima of Mossinghoff, Rhin and Wu; it does not certify a global minimum at unrestricted degree.

<a id="exhaustion"></a>
## Passing from the primitive catalogue to all inputs

At \(M(w)<601/500\), [Smyth's theorem](../audits/literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-statement-and-conventions) makes the irreducible minimum reciprocal.
It has even degree since a reciprocal polynomial of odd degree has a root at \(-1\) (an antireciprocal polynomial has a root at \(1\)).
If all exponents are divisible by \(k>1\), write the minimum as \(Q(X^k)\); \(Q\) is irreducible, and \(M(Q(X^k))=M(Q)\) by the root moduli.
Continuing and allowing the sign of the variable restores the normalization of the [published primitive search](../audits/literature/MRW08/Yin_U001.md#degree).
For \(w^k=\pm u\), \(f_w=f_u/k\) in every common normal closure, so this operation also preserves zero or nonzero Salem projection.

Mossinghoff's [full author table](../audits/literature/Mos26/readme.md#complete-small-cores) includes every primitive irreducible core below \(1.3\) through degree 44.
The [integer comparisons](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0187/readme.md#cut) put four of those 944 rows below \(601/500\) and all others above.
Each of the five remaining possible primitive degrees \(46,48,50,52,54\) has its proved minimizing witness in the author's list, and every listed row at these degrees was verified to exceed the same cutoff.
This use of the higher-degree result needs only the individual minima.
The exhaustive search establishing the external classification was not rerun.

<a id="witness"></a>
## Distinguishing the Salem and orthogonal cases

Three survivors have [exactly one exterior real trace and all remaining traces interior](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0187/readme.md#signatures).
Their roots are conjugates of Salem units up to sign.
Such a root has nonzero projection onto the corresponding primitive generator because taking its nonzero power multiplies its nonzero logarithm function by that exponent.
The remaining row is \(P\).
The [splitting-field calculation](Yin_C_G0901_v55.md#statement) excludes every Salem number from its normal closure.
The [algebraic detection criterion](Yin_C_G0901_v53.md#algebra) therefore gives zero projection onto every Salem conjugate.
The [rational trace-disk certificate](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0144/trace_control.py) gives its displayed strict measure interval, below \(601/500\).
This supplies attainment and excludes all other values in the asserted range.
