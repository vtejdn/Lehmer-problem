## 5. The last-occurrence lemma for ancestors that remain unabsorbed

Continue with Y_h>M.

    q_j|u_j(a) for EVERY i<=j<h.                      (8)

In addition every distinct prime in the suffix q_i,...,q_(h-1) divides u_i(a), so that suffix has at most K distinct primes.

Order the LAST occurrences of the distinct primes in the full finite path word q_0,...,q_(h-1).
