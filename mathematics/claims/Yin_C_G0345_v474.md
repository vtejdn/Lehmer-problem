# Recovering a bounded pair of graphs from every common support

<a id="statement"></a>
## A finite search for all necessary small-degree data

Take the two genuine signed rows of the [joint degree test](Yin_C_G0345_v466.md#statement) for a reciprocal nontorsion unit \(\gamma\) and its square.
They have distinct minima of the same degree \(d\), a common integer exponent \(p>3\) prime and common width \(2d\le b<3d\).
Write \(w\) for the smallest point of the union of the two supports, \(K\) for the gcd of the differences in that union, and \(C\) for its normalized span.
A genuine row has at least two terms.
Write the two rows with the same shift as \(A_i(Y)=Y^wB_i(Y^K)\), taking formal width \(C\) for both \(B_i\), even if one endpoint is missing.
Use \(B_i^*(Y)=Y^CB_i(1/Y)\), and keep the original signs \(\varepsilon_i\).
Put
\[
 R=1+p(b-2w-KC),\qquad g=\gcd(K,R),\qquad
 \ell=K/g,\qquad u=R/g,\qquad x=\gamma^g .
\]
Then \(\ell\in\{1,2\},\ \gcd(p\ell,u)=1,\ |u|\le3pC\).
For each sign the Laurent polynomial
\[
                B_i(X^{p\ell})+\varepsilon_i X^u B_i^*(X^{p\ell})       \tag{1}
\]
has double contact at \(x,x^2\) with both numerators nonzero.
Its two actual target degrees \(e_1=[\mathbb Q(x):\mathbb Q]\) and \(e_2=[\mathbb Q(x^2):\mathbb Q]\) satisfy
\[
             d/g\le e_2\le e_1\le2e_2,\qquad e_1+e_2\le2C .
\]
If the original product of the two minima is squarefree modulo \(p\), or \(p>10C/3+1\), then \(\ell=1\).
The original pair is transverse exactly when \(B_+B_-^*+B_-B_+^*\ne0\).

Thus, for fixed prime, finite bounds on the row norms and the joint normalized span, all the data \((B_+,B_-,\ell,u,x)\) needed by any genuine witness can be found in a finite search whose graph degrees are at most \(5pC\).
This covers pairs with different intrinsic spacings or missing endpoints as well as the [common substitutions from bounded bases](Yin_C_G0345_v473.md#lift).
At the single width \(b=5d/2-1\), these data give a computable finite union of progressions and finite sets containing every passing degree, together with one exact radical check for each proposed presentation.

<a id="compression"></a>
## Degree control for a common formal width

Substitution gives the original graph as \(X^{pw}\) times (1) at \(X^g\).
The power substitution is unramified at both nonzero targets, proving both contact orders and nonvanishing.
The original determinant equals \(Y^{b-KC}\) times the determinant in the statement at \(Y^K\).
One has \(\gcd(pK,R)=g\) since \(R\equiv1\bmod p\).
At either \(t=x,x^2\), the value equation recovers \(t^u\) as a rational function of \(t^{p\ell}\), hence \(\mathbb Q(t^{p\ell})=\mathbb Q(t)\) by Bézout.
The double-contact equation puts both powered nodes on the single polynomial
\[
              p\ell Y(B_i'B_i^*-B_i(B_i^*)')-uB_iB_i^* .
\]
It is nonzero, even with common endpoint zeros, since \(Y(B_i/B_i^*)'/(B_i/B_i^*)\) at infinity tends to an integer and \(u/(p\ell)\) is not integral.
The polynomial has degree at most \(2C\), and the two minima dividing it are distinct by their positive absolute heights.
This bounds \(e_1+e_2\), while the inclusions from squaring bound \(e_1/e_2\).
Also \(\gamma^2\) is a root of \(T^g-x^2\) over \(\mathbb Q(x^2)\), giving \(d/g\le e_2\le C\).
Now \(K C\le b<3d\) gives \(\ell<3\), and \(|b-2w-KC|\le b\) gives \(|u|\le1/g+pb/g<1+3pC\).

For row \(i\) let \(w_i,k_i,c_i\) be its individual shift, spacing and normalized span.
Then \(K\mid k_i,\ K\mid(w_i-w),\ c_i\le C\).
In either of the stated prime regimes the [individual stride result](Yin_C_G0345_v469.md#statement) gives \(k_i\mid 1+p(b-2w_i-k_i c_i)\).
Thus \(K\mid R\) and \(\ell=1\).
To enumerate (1), clear its negative powers by a monomial.
The resulting degree is at most \(p\ell C+|u|\le5pC\); its two summands occupy disjoint residues modulo \(p\) and it is nonzero.
Factor the graphs, verify the two double contacts and the original nonvanishing conditions at their powered nodes, and retain reciprocal nontorsion unit roots \(x\).
One can take the union over all allowed \(C\), requiring the joint support of the two \(B_i\) to have endpoints \(0,C\) and gcd of differences one.

<a id="ratios"></a>
## A finite list for the ratio of degree to radical order

For each retained \(x\), write \(F=\mathbb Q(x)\) and \(h_{\rm W}\) for absolute logarithmic Weil height.
If \(\gamma^g=x\), put \(v=N_{\mathbb Q(\gamma)/F}(\gamma)\) and \(l=[\mathbb Q(\gamma):F]\le g\).
Then \(v^g=x^l\).
Reduce \(l/g=a/q\) to lowest terms, with \(1\le a\le q\).
The element \(v^q/x^a\) is a root of unity.
Choose integers \(i,j\) with \(ia+jq=1\).
The unit \(v^i x^j\in F\) has absolute height \(h_{\rm W}(x)/q\), because its \(q\)th power is \(x\) times a root of unity.
In particular \(q\) is bounded effectively from \(x\).
To compute an upper bound, enumerate monic unit polynomials of degree at most \(e_1\) with coefficient one-norm at most an integer strictly above \(2^{e_1}\exp(e_1 h_{\rm W}(x))\).
Every eligible unit of height at most \(h_{\rm W}(x)\) and degree at most \(e_1\) occurs by the elementary coefficient bound from Mahler measure.
Discard roots of unity; the remaining roots include \(x\), and one can compute a positive rational lower bound \(\mu\) for all their heights.
Thus \(q\le h_{\rm W}(x)/\mu\), which yields an effective finite list of rational candidates for \(\rho=d/g=e_1 a/q\).
This strengthens the [finite norm spectrum for fixed coefficients](Yin_C_G0345_v470.md#norm) here by retaining a rational degree ratio for the same compressed pair.

<a id="progressions"></a>
## Linear restrictions followed by the exact radical test

For each candidate \(\rho\) restrict \(g\) to positive integers satisfying
\[
 d=\rho g\ \text{even and positive},\qquad b=5d/2-1,\qquad
 w=\frac{1+p(b-g\ell C)-gu}{2p}\in\mathbb Z,\qquad 0\le2w\le2(b-g\ell C).
\]
The conditions are linear inequalities and congruences in \(g\) after denominators are cleared.
They define an effectively computable finite union of one-sided progressions and finite sets, as do their images in \(d\).
The original width restriction \(2d\le b<3d\) can also be imposed directly.
A width-dependent norm budget can truncate each progression by its monotone inequality at \(b\), using the unchanged two row norms.
A degree represented by any surviving \(g\) and data is realized by that presentation if and only if \(T^g-x\) has a reciprocal unit root \(\gamma\) of degree \(d\) whose square also has degree \(d\).
This is an exact finite factor and degree check at each \(g\).
Indeed each necessity was proved above; conversely put \(K=g\ell\), use the displayed \(w,b\), and substitute \(X^g\) in (1).
The two resulting signed rows at \(\gamma,\gamma^2\) satisfy the prescribed double contacts, nonvanishing and budget at those widths.
Their determinant has the same nonvanishing status as the compressed one.
For these recovered data \(\gcd(g\ell,gu)=g\) as required.
The arithmetic progressions before the radical check give an upper count, complementing the [lower count from genuine base lifts](Yin_C_G0345_v473.md#statement).
Their exact accepted densities for arbitrary compressed data remain open.

The coordinating agent for Sheng Yin developed this common compression to compare the two counts at fixed normalized span.
