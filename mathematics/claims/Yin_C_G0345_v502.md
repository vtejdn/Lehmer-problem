# An intrinsic resultant bound at small normalized coefficient cost

<a id="statement"></a>
## The bound and its inverse

Take the [computable growing width](Yin_C_G0345_v500.md#width) \(b=b(p)\) for \(Q=(1091/1000)^{1/3}\).
For every sufficiently large prime \(p\), every prime \(p<q\le2p\), and every polynomial pair \(U,V\) passing the [two-prime test](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#common) at this width, put
\[
 \Sigma=p+q,\quad H=\max(\|U\|_1,\|V\|_1),\quad
 \eta=\frac{\log H}{\Sigma},\quad K=\frac{\log|\operatorname{Res}(U,V)|}{\log H},
 \quad c_b=\frac43(1-1/b),
\]
where we restrict throughout to \(0<\eta\le10^{-3}\), so \(1<H<Q^\Sigma\).
For \(0<s\le10^{-3}\), let \(T_s>\mathrm e\) be the larger positive solution of
\[
                    T_s/\log T_s=(4s)^{-1/3}.
\]
Then every such pair satisfies
\[
       K>c_b\exp(T_\eta),\qquad L=\log(K/c_b)>T_\eta,\qquad
       \eta>\frac14\left(\frac{\log L}{L}\right)^3.                 \tag{1}
\]
In particular, once \(b\ge4\), every row in this cost range has \(K>\mathrm e^{18}>64{,}000{,}000\).
Along every sequence of passing rows at this width with \(p\to\infty\) and \(\eta\to0\),
\[
       \liminf\frac{\log K}{\eta^{-1/3}\log(1/\eta)}
                       \ge\frac{4^{-1/3}}{3}.                       \tag{2}
\]

<a id="contact"></a>
## The degree of the contact of smaller measure

Choose a qualifying nontorsion reciprocal unit minimum at each exponent, and let \(h_1,h_2\) be their positive logarithmic Mahler measures.
Set \(h=\min(h_1,h_2)\) and let \(d\) be the degree of a seed attaining this minimum, choosing either seed in case of equality.
The [weighted contact bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#tangencies) gives \(\Sigma h\le p h_1+q h_2\le \eta\Sigma\).
For distinct seeds, the [mixed divisor bound](Yin_C_G0345_v500.md#divisors) gives \(\Gamma\ge4\max(d_2p h_1,d_1q h_2)\), where \(d_i\) is the degree at the corresponding prime.
One of these products pairs \(d\) with the other seed's measure and an exponent at least \(p\ge\Sigma/3\), whence \(\Gamma/\Sigma\ge(4/3)d h\).
For equal seeds the [diagonal estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v501.md#resultant) gives \(\Gamma/\Sigma\ge2dh\).
The [uniform divisor](Yin_C_G0345_v500.md#statement) thus gives \(K\ge c_b d h/\eta\) in both cases.

<a id="inversion"></a>
## Inverting the logarithmic height bound

Write \(t=\log d\), with \(d\ge2\) since a rational algebraic unit is torsion.
[Voutier's Theorem and Corollary 2](../audits/literature/Vou96/Yin_U001.md#height) give
\[
     h>\tfrac14(\log t/t)^3,\qquad t>(2/h)^{1/3}-\log 3>3
\]
for \(h\le10^{-3}\).
The function \(T/\log T\) increases strictly from \(\mathrm e\) to infinity on \((\mathrm e,\infty)\), and \((4h)^{-1/3}\ge250^{1/3}>3/\log 3\).
Hence \(T_h\) exists, \(T_h>3\), and the first strict height bound forces \(t>T_h\).
Writing \(s=(\log T/T)^3/4\) for \(T=T_s\), one has
\[
     \frac{d}{dT}\log(s\mathrm e^T)=1-\frac{3(\log T-1)}{T\log T}>0
                        \qquad (T>3).
\]
Since \(T_s\) decreases as \(s\) increases, \(h\mathrm e^{T_h}\ge\eta\mathrm e^{T_\eta}\).
Together with \(d>\mathrm e^{T_h}\), this proves the first inequality in (1).
The other inequalities follow by taking logarithms and using strict decrease of \(\log T/T\) for \(T>\mathrm e\).
For the numerical bound, \(18/\log18<25/4<250^{1/3}\), so \(T_\eta>18\).
Indeed the first three positive terms of \(\log x=2\sum_{j\ge0}((x-1)/(x+1))^{2j+1}/(2j+1)\) at each of \(x=2,3\) give \(\log18=\log2+2\log3>2.88\).
The exponential series through degree eight gives \(\mathrm e^3>20\), and \(c_b\ge1\) for \(b\ge4\).

Finally set \(A=(4\eta)^{-1/3}\).
As \(\eta\to0\), the identity \(T_\eta=A\log T_\eta\) yields \(\log A=\log T_\eta-\log\log T_\eta\sim\log T_\eta\), and hence \(T_\eta\sim A\log A\).
Substituting into (1) gives (2).
Thus a sequence with bounded \(K\), or with a limiting upper ratio in (2) strictly below the displayed constant, cannot witness vanishing normalized cost at this width.
The [unrestricted sequence criterion](Yin_C_G0345_v501.md#gap) leaves open whether there exist small-cost witnesses with the required growth of \(K\).

The coordinating agent for Sheng Yin proposed the inverse bound from Voutier's Theorem, and a collaborating agent verified the two contact cases, strict inversion, numerical threshold and limiting coefficient.
