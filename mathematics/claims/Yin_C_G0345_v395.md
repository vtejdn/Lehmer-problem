# Small Mahler measures over solvable extensions of the totally real numbers

<a id="statement"></a>
## The label field and the least degree in a tower

Let \(T=\mathbb Q^{\rm tr}\) be the field of all totally real algebraic numbers in a fixed algebraic closure inside \(\mathbb C\).
Let \(B\) be its maximal solvable extension, the union of all finite solvable Galois extensions of \(T\).
Both fields are normal over \(\mathbb Q\), and \(B\) contains every Salem splitting field by [quadratic lifting of its traces](Yin_C_G0345_v394.md#statement).
For an integer \(q\ge1\), let \(B_q\) consist of the elements reachable from \(B\) by finite towers of single algebraic adjunctions of relative degree at most \(q\) and arbitrary adjunctions with solvable relative normal closure.
There is no bound on the number or order of the steps.

For a nontorsion \(x\ne0\), write \(m(x)=[\mathbb Q(x):\mathbb Q]h(x)\) for its logarithmic Mahler measure.
Let \(\gamma\) be the product of the rational conjugates of \(x\) whose quotient by \(x\) is a root of unity.
Put \(d=[\mathbb Q(\gamma):\mathbb Q]\).
The [class-product identity](Yin_C_G0345_v264.md#labels) gives \(m(\gamma)=m(x)\).
On the conjugates of \(\gamma\), identify two whose quotient or product is a root of unity.
Write \(P\) for the field fixed in the rational splitting field of \(x\) by the kernel of the action on these labels.

Fix \(M>0\) and suppose \(0<m(x)\le M\) with \(d\) sufficiently large in terms of \(M\).
Let \(u\) and \(b\) be the number and size of the [minimal blocks of labels](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement), with \(v=ub\) labels in total.
Then:

* If \(P\) is totally real, the full rational splitting field of \(x\) lies in \(B\).
* Otherwise \({\rm Gal}(PB/B)\) contains the entire product \((A_b)^u\) acting separately on those blocks, and the least \(q\) for which \(x\in B_q\) is exactly \(b\).
  In particular
  \[
      \log [PB:B]\ \ge\ u\log(b!/2)
           \ \ge\ v\log d-O_M(d\log\log(3d)).
  \]

<a id="base-change"></a>
## Conjugation and normal closures over the enlarged field

Put \(G={\rm Gal}(P/\mathbb Q)\) and \(D=(A_b)^u\).
The [alternating action](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement) makes \(D\) the unique minimal nontrivial normal subgroup of \(G\), with \(b\gg_M d/(\log(3d))^{212}\).
The rational splitting field \(L\) of \(x\) is solvable over \(P\) by the [two solvable kernels](Yin_C_G0345_v267.md#normal).
If \(P\subseteq T\), the compositum \(LT\) is a finite solvable Galois extension of \(T\), proving the first case.

If \(P\) is not totally real, normality of \(P/\mathbb Q\) implies that ordinary complex conjugation acts nontrivially on \(P\).
It fixes \(T\), so \(H={\rm Gal}(P/(P\cap T))\) is a nontrivial normal subgroup of \(G\) and contains \(D\).
Identify \(H\) with \({\rm Gal}(PT/T)\).
The quotient obtained by restriction to \(PT\cap B\) is solvable, since this finite normal extension of \(T\) lies in a finite compositum of solvable Galois extensions of \(T\).
The perfect group \(D\) is in the kernel, which identifies with \({\rm Gal}(PB/B)\) in its label action.
The degree bound follows from \(ub=v\), the stated lower bound on \(b\), and \(\log(b!)\ge b\log b-b\).

For \(q<b\), the normal closure over \(B\) of a permitted tower has no subgroup admitting \(A_b\) as a quotient.
Indeed this exclusion passes to subgroups, quotients, finite products and extensions, while each new relative normal closure after taking conjugates has group inside a product of solvable groups or groups in \(S_q\).
Here \(|S_q|<|A_b|\); the induction is the [simple-section tower argument](Yin_C_G0345_v281.md#towers).
If such a tower contained \(x\), take one rational conjugate of the tower for each conjugate of \(x\), extending the corresponding embedding to the algebraic closure.
Every conjugate tower still starts at \(B\), since \(B/\mathbb Q\) is normal, and satisfies the same degree and solvability bounds.
The compositum of their normal closures over \(B\) still excludes the section \(A_b\), but contains \(BL\) and hence \(BP\).
Its quotient on \(BP\) contains \(D\), a contradiction.
Conversely the [attaining tower over the rationals](Yin_C_G0345_v281.md#towers) reaches \(x\) with maximum additional degree \(b\).
Base change to \(B\) preserves the upper bounds on the degrees and solvability of the steps.

<a id="spectrum"></a>
## Which measures near zero occur in these fields

For each fixed \(q\ge1\) there is an \(\epsilon_q>0\) such that for **every** algebraic number \(x\) with \(0<m(x)<\epsilon_q\) the following are equivalent:

1. \(x\in B_q\).
2. The full rational splitting field of \(x\) is contained in \(B\).
3. Every conjugate of its class product \(\gamma\) is real or has absolute value one.

In this interval the log-measure spectrum of \(B_q\) equals the spectrum of reciprocal units with this last root condition.
Every such unit has degree at most two over \(T\).
Equivalently the spectrum in the interval consists precisely of the positive sums
\[
     \sum_{\substack{t'\ {\rm conjugate\ of}\ t\\ |t'|>2}}
                   \operatorname{arcosh}(|t'|/2)                       \tag{1}
\]
there, for totally real algebraic integers \(t\) such that \(Z^2-tZ+1\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q(t)\).

To prove the equivalence, take \(M=1\) and choose a threshold \(d_0(q)\) beyond which the first result applies with \(b>q\).
The bounded degree and measure conditions on \(\gamma\) with \(d\le d_0(q)\) allow only finitely many minimal polynomials, by the usual coefficient bounds in terms of \(d\) and \(e^M\).
Choose \(\epsilon_q\le 1\) below all their positive log measures, below \(\log 2\), and below the [nonreciprocal lower bound of Smyth](../audits/literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-u001).
The leading and constant coefficients of the primitive polynomial of \(\gamma\) have absolute value bounded by \(e^{m(\gamma)}<2\), so it is a monic unit polynomial up to sign.
Smyth's theorem gives reciprocity.
Each label now consists of exactly \(z,z^{-1}\), as there are no torsion quotients between distinct compressed conjugates.
Consequently \(P\) is the splitting field of the trace \(t=\gamma+\gamma^{-1}\).
For \(z=r e^{i\theta}\) the imaginary part of this trace at the corresponding embedding is \((r-r^{-1})\sin\theta\).
Thus \(P\) is totally real precisely under condition 3, and the first result gives the equivalences.

The measure equality \(m(x)=m(\gamma)\) supplies a representative satisfying the root condition for every value in \(B_q\) in this interval.
Conversely a reciprocal unit having only real or unit-circle conjugates has totally real trace, so lies in a quadratic extension of \(T\) or in \(T\) itself and belongs to \(B\).
For such a nontorsion reciprocal unit the inverse is a distinct conjugate fixing the trace, so its quadratic over its rational trace field is irreducible.
Conversely if \(t\) is as specified in (1), both lifts of every conjugate occur exactly once among the conjugates of either root.
The pair contributes zero when \(|t'|\le2\), and \(\operatorname{arcosh}(|t'|/2)\) otherwise, proving (1).
All exterior real inverse pairs contribute, with no bound assumed on their number.
The unrestricted Lehmer question still includes the nonreal-label case, as well as the possibility of such positive sums tending to zero.
