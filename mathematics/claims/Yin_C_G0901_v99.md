# The first attained measure index for a single exterior complex pair

<a id="statement"></a>
## The minimum in the full divisible conjugate group

Fix \(M>0\) and let \(\alpha,\bar\alpha\) be the only exterior roots of the minimum of a reciprocal algebraic unit of positive total logarithmic Mahler measure \(m(\alpha)\le M\).
Suppose these two roots are nonreal, distinct conjugates of \(\alpha\) have nontorsion quotients, and \(2n=\deg\alpha\) is sufficiently large in terms of \(M\).
Write \(n=ub\) for the [canonical blocks](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement) of \(b\) inverse pairs, and \(L\) for the rational splitting field.
Let \(\beta=\alpha\bar\alpha=M(\alpha)\), where \(M(x)=\exp(m(x))\).
Let \(\eta>1\) generate the positive real units on the line of \(\beta\) in \(F=\mathbb Q(\beta)\), and write \(\beta=\eta^h\).
The [rank-one measure theorem](Yin_C_G0901_v95.md#statement) gives \(M(x)=\eta^{I(x)}\) with \(I(x)\) a positive integer for every nontorsion \(x\) in the divisible conjugate group \(D(\alpha)\).
This group allows arbitrary roots and torsion twists of monomials in the conjugates.

In \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\) let \(\rho\) be primitive on the unit line of \(\alpha\) modulo roots of unity, with \([\alpha]=e[\rho]\), \(e>0\), in additive notation for these classes.
Set \(k=h/e\) and \(\lambda=m(\rho)=k\log\eta\).
In the [two actual-root actions](Yin_C_G0345_v274.md#statement) the integer \(k\) and the first attained index satisfy
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 &\text{coordinate index}&\min_{x\in D(\alpha),\,m(x)>0}I(x)\\ \hline
\text{independent even inversions}&k\mid 2&k\\
\text{canonical oriented halves}&k\mid b&\min(k,b-1).
\end{array}
\]
In the second case a value strictly below \(\lambda\) in logarithmic normalization occurs exactly when \(k=b\).
The only such value is then \((b-1)\log\eta\), attained by \(\eta\); every input attaining it has class on the line of a conjugate of \(\beta\).
If \(k=1\) in either case the full index set is the positive integers by [closure under positive multiples](Yin_C_G0901_v95.md#powers).
The minimum formula requires no assumption that \(\alpha\) is a strict degree record and makes no existence assertion for an input with a specified \(k\).

<a id="indices"></a>
## Comparing the two primitive unit lines

Choose one root \(a_i\) on each inverse pair, using one canonical half per block in the oriented alternative.
The corresponding primitive classes satisfy \([\rho_i]=[a_i]/e\) by conjugation, inverse orientations allowed.
The [unit lattice on a rational line](Yin_C_G0901_v43.md#primitive) gives a representative \(\rho_i\) in \(K_i=\mathbb Q(a_i)\).
An automorphism fixing \(\rho_i\) fixes the class of \(a_i\), hence the actual \(a_i\) by the torsion-quotient hypothesis.
Thus \(\mathbb Q(\rho_i)=K_i\), \(m(\rho_i)=m(\alpha)/e\), and the common measure field theorem applied to \(\rho_i\) gives \(k=h/e\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\).

Each distinct conjugate of \(\beta\) has a unique corresponding conjugate of \(\eta\) whose \(h\)-th power is that value, since \(\mathbb Q(\eta)=\mathbb Q(\beta)\).
In the independent case every product \(a_i a_j^{\pm1}\) on distinct labels of one block occurs by the [product orbit computation](Yin_C_G0901_v94.md#orbit).
Let \(z_{ij}\in L\) be the product of the corresponding two conjugates of \(\eta\), so \(z_{ij}^h=a_i^2\).
For fixed \(i\), take the full preimage in the splitting group \(G\) of the alternating group on the other \(b-1\) labels of this block, supported there.
Its derived series stabilizes in a perfect subgroup \(P\) still mapping onto \(A_{b-1}\), since \(A_{b-1}\) is perfect.
The group \(P\) fixes \(a_i\), since its action on that inverse pair is abelian.
It also fixes every root of unity of \(L\), whose Galois action is abelian.
As \(g z_{ij}/z_{ij}\) is torsion for \(g\in P\), these quotients form a character of \(P\), hence are trivial.
Transport of the two factors of \(z_{ij}\) sends it to \(z_{i,g j}\), regardless of the sign at \(j\).
Transitivity shows that \(z_{ij}\) is independent of \(j\).
Every element fixing \(a_i\) preserves its block and permutes the other labels, so the common unit lies in \(K_i\).
Primitivity there gives \(h\mid 2e\), hence \(k\mid2\).

In the oriented case every \(a_i/a_j\) on distinct labels of one selected half occurs.
Write \(\eta_{ij}\) for its corresponding root of \(\eta\)'s minimum.
The stabilizer of \(a_i\) preserves its half, so \(z_i=\prod_{j\ne i}\eta_{ij}\), with \(j\) in the same block, belongs to \(K_i\).
Its \(h\)-th power is \(a_i^b/\prod_j a_j\), and the half product is torsion by [its conjugate moduli](Yin_C_G0901_v94.md#orbit).
Thus \(h\mid be\), giving \(k\mid b\).

<a id="budget"></a>
## Coordinate norms and a lower bound for every attained measure

For \(x\in D(\alpha)\) of positive measure, write its rational class in the \([\rho_i]\) coordinates.
In the oriented alternative use the unique vector centered separately on every block, since the full relation space consists of the block constants.
The coordinate action factors through \(G\).
In a finite normal extension containing \(x\) and \(L\), the field fixed by the stabilizer of the class of \(x\) is a subfield \(F_x\subseteq L\cap\mathbb Q(x)\).
The norm \(v=N_{\mathbb Q(x)/F_x}x\) has class \([\mathbb Q(x):F_x][x]\), since its relative conjugates are torsion multiples of \(x\).
This nonzero scalar multiple has the same class stabilizer, giving \(\mathbb Q(v)=F_x\) and \(m(v)=m(x)\).
The actual orbit of \(v\) therefore maps bijectively onto its class orbit. [Norm descent](Yin_C_G0901_v70.md#denominators)

Write \(d=\deg v\), \(q_i\) for its coordinates and \(H=\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q(v))\).
Count the nonzero coefficients by the actual signed roots \(a_i^{\operatorname{sgn}q_i}\), one per supported label.
The stabilizer \(H\) permutes these roots with \(|q_i|\) constant on each orbit.
Put \(\kappa=1\) in the independent case and \(\kappa=b/(b-1)\) in the oriented case.
Averaging the vector of \(v\) over the stabilizer of \(a_i\) gives \(\kappa q_i[\rho_i]\).
In the independent case a double flip avoiding \(i\) can reverse each other coordinate, using an additional label in its own block different from \(i\).
In the oriented case the supported alternating group kills the mean of every other centered block, and \(A_{b-1}\) averages the other entries of the block of \(i\) to \(-q_i/(b-1)\).
The block constant relation then gives the displayed projection.

For an \(H\)-orbit of supported signed roots containing the choice at \(i\), its size \(s_i\) equals \([\mathbb Q(v)K_i:\mathbb Q(v)]\).
The norm from \(\mathbb Q(v)K_i\) to \(K_i\) of \(v\) has class \([\mathbb Q(v)K_i:K_i]\kappa q_i[\rho_i]\) by the averaging formula and norm transitivity.
Since \(\rho_i\) is primitive, the absolute coefficient \(d s_i\kappa|q_i|/(2n)\) is a positive integer.
Summing one such integer per orbit gives
\[
                  J=\frac{d\kappa}{2n}\sum_i|q_i|\in\mathbb Z_{>0}.
\]
In particular \(J=1\) forces all the supported signed roots to belong to one \(H\)-orbit.

The [two height formulas](Yin_C_G0901_v94.md#heights), with coordinates now relative to the primitive units, give \(m(v)/\lambda=dS/(2n(b-1))\).
Here \(S\) sums \(\max(|q_i|,|q_j|)\) or \(|q_i-q_j|\) over all within-block pairs in the respective alternatives.
In the first case \(2S\ge(b-1)\sum_i|q_i|\), with equality exactly when the absolute coefficients are constant within each block.
In the second case each centered block satisfies \(\sum_j|q_i-q_j|\ge|\sum_j(q_i-q_j)|=b|q_i|\).
Consequently in both cases
\[
                              m(v)\ge J\lambda/2.                         \tag{1}
\]

<a id="inversions"></a>
## Full sign supports cannot attain half the coordinate measure

For \(k=1\) the primitive coordinate already attains the first index.
For \(k=2\) a positive measure below \(\lambda\) must equal \(\lambda/2\) by the integral measure indices.
Then \(J=1\) and equality in the sign estimate forces every occupied block to be entirely supported.
The single \(H\)-orbit forces a common absolute coefficient \(a>0\) on all these blocks.

Put \(T=|\mu(L)|\).
The kernel on labels has image of order at most \(2^u\) in \(G^{\rm ab}\), since all even flips supported on one block belong to \(G'\).
The label action modulo its perfect subgroup \(A_b^u\) has order at most \(2^u u!\).
It follows that \(\varphi(T)\le |G^{\rm ab}|\le4^u u!\).
The elementary bound \(T\le2\varphi(T)^2\) follows prime power by prime power, with only \(2^1\) needing the factor two.
Hence \(\log T=O(u\log(2u))=o(b)\) because \(u\ll_M(\log(3n))^{212}\) and \(b=n/u\).

For a supported \(i\), take distinct \(j,l\) in its block away from \(i\), and denote the two pair flips by \(f_{ij},f_{il}\).
In additive class notation \((1-f_{ij})(1-f_{il})[v]=4q_i[\rho_i]\) is represented by a unit of \(L\).
Its \(T\)-th power is fixed by the stabilizer of \(a_i\), so \(4Tq_i\) is integral by primitivity in \(K_i\).
In particular \(a\ge1/(4T)\).
If \(t\ge1\) blocks are occupied, the orbit of their support has at least \(u/t\) members by transitivity on blocks.
For each fixed support the even flips give at least \(2^{t(b-1)}\) distinct vectors.
Consequently \(J=d t a/(2u)\ge 2^{t(b-1)}/(8T)>1\) for sufficiently large input degree, contradicting \(J=1\).
This proves the first minimum.

<a id="oriented"></a>
## Below the coordinate measure only a ratio line can occur

Suppose \(m(v)<\lambda\) in the oriented alternative.
By (1), \(J=1\).
All nonzero coefficients have the same absolute value \(a\), and \(H\) is transitive on the occupied blocks because it is transitive on the supported signed roots.
Centering therefore gives the same pattern in every occupied block, with \(j\) entries \(a\), \(j\) entries \(-a\), and \(b-2j\) zeros, for \(1\le j\le b/2\).
There are \(t\ge1\) occupied blocks.

Fix one such block \(B\), choose positions \(i,l\) with different entries, and choose two further positions with equal entries.
The latter are available since there are at most three different entries and \(b\ge9\).
Let \(s\) be the supported pure alternating permutation interchanging \(i,l\) and interchanging the two further positions in the canonical halves. [Supported lifts](Yin_C_G0345_v274.md#cycles)
The unit \(\delta=v/(s v)\) has class
\[
                     [\delta]=(q_i-q_l)([\rho_i]-[\rho_l]).
\]
We show that \(\delta\) belongs to the field fixed by the subgroup \(H^+\) preserving the selected half of \(B\) and fixing the ordered labels \(i,l\).
First, a unit \(z\in L\) in the conjugate span modulo torsion whose centered \(B\)-coordinates vanish is fixed as an actual unit by the supported \(A_b\) in \(B\).
Indeed this perfect group fixes its class and every root of unity of \(L\), so \(g\mapsto gz/z\) is a trivial character.
The same character argument shows that the supported \(A_{b-2}\) fixing \(i,l\) fixes \(\delta\), since it fixes the class displayed above.

For \(g\in H^+\) the \(B\)-pattern of \(g v\) is obtained from that of \(v\) by a permutation fixing \(i,l\).
There is a supported \(p\in A_{b-2}\) with the same effect on that pattern, since two of the other entries are equal and their interchange can correct parity.
The unit \(z=(gv)/(pv)\) has zero \(B\)-coordinates, so \(s'=gsg^{-1}\), a member of the supported \(A_b\), fixes \(z\).
It follows that \(g\delta=(pv)/(s'pv)\).
On \(B\), the effect of \(s'\) on \(pv\) interchanges the entries at \(i,l\) and two equal entries elsewhere, just as \(psp^{-1}\) does.
Both permutations fix all other blocks.
Thus \(s'pv\) and \(psv\) are conjugates of \(v\) with the same class and are equal by the bijection in the norm descent.
This gives \(g\delta=p\delta=\delta\) as claimed.

Let \(F_{il}=\mathbb Q(a_i/a_l)\).
The stabilizer of that ratio can preserve the selected half and fix \(i,l\) or reverse the half and interchange \(i,l\), by the distinct ratio vectors proved in the [oriented product orbit](Yin_C_G0901_v94.md#orbit).
Hence \([L^{H^+}:F_{il}]\le2\).
On the unit line in \(F_{il}\) the class \([\eta_{il}]=([\rho_i]-[\rho_l])/k\) is primitive by transport of \(\eta\) from \(F\).
The norm of \(\delta\) from \(L^{H^+}\) to \(F_{il}\) has class \([L^{H^+}:F_{il}](q_i-q_l)k[\eta_{il}]\).
Its nonzero coefficient is an integer, since the ratio stabilizer fixes the line elementwise.
As \(|q_i-q_l|\le2a\), this proves \(a\ge1/(4k)\ge1/(4b)\).

In a prescribed occupied block the supported alternating permutations give all
\[
                         D_j=\binom bj\binom{b-j}{j}
\]
placements of this pattern, since a repeated entry permits parity correction.
Independence across these blocks and the support orbit give \(d\ge (u/t)D_j^t\).
Thus
\[
                 J=\frac{d t j a}{u(b-1)}
                     \ \ge\ \frac{jD_j^t}{4b(b-1)} .
\]
For \(j=2\) already \(D_2=b(b-1)(b-2)(b-3)/4\).
For \(3\le j\le b/2\) one has \(D_j\ge\binom b3\), and for \(j=1\) one has \(D_1=b(b-1)\).
For \(b\ge12\) the right side exceeds one whenever \(j\ge2\) or \(t\ge2\).
Hence \(J=1\) in this situation forces \(j=t=1\).

The surviving vector is a rational multiple of one \(e_i-e_l\), so its class stabilizer fixes \(F_{il}\) as its fixed field, again by distinctness of the ratio classes.
The compressed unit \(v\) belongs to \(F_{il}\), where it is an integral multiple of \([\eta_{il}]\) modulo torsion.
All conjugates of \(\eta_{il}\) remain distinct modulo torsion because their \(h\)-th powers are the distinct ratio classes.
Thus a nonzero multiple represented by a compressed unit has measure at least \(m(\eta)\), an integral multiple of that measure, and \(\eta\in D(\alpha)\) attains the lower value itself.
The [exact iteration exponent](Yin_C_G0901_v95.md#iteration) gives \(m(\eta)=(b-1)\log\eta\).
Together with the available coordinate value \(k\log\eta\) and \(k\mid b\), this proves the stated minimum and the claim about all smaller positive values.
