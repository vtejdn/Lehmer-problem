# Conditional entropy of intermediate carries on exact binary collision paths

<a id="statement"></a>
## The finite return law

Let \(\alpha\) be any algebraic unit of degree \(d\), let \(R=\mathbb Z[\alpha]\), and put \(m=\log M(\alpha)\).
Start at \(C_0=0\) and put \(C_{t+1}=\alpha^{-1}(C_t+\delta_t)\), where the difference digits of two independent fair binary words have probabilities \(1/4,1/2,1/4\) on \(-1,0,1\).
Fix integers \(k,L\ge1\), set \(N=kd\), \(T=LN\), and let \(Q_T\) be the original law conditioned on \(C_T=0\).
Earlier visits to zero are allowed.
For \(i=1,\ldots,L\), let \(\mathcal F_{i-1}\) reveal every digit before time \((i-1)N\).
Let \(Y_i=C_{iN}\) in \(G=R\), or its image in \(G=R/qR\) for any integer \(q\ge2\).
With natural logarithms, define
\[
 \mathcal H_G(k,L)=\frac1{dL}\sum_{i=1}^L H_{Q_T}(Y_i\mid\mathcal F_{i-1}),\qquad
 a_k=4^{-k}\binom{2k}{k},\qquad
 a_{k,q}=\frac1q\sum_{j=0}^{q-1}\cos^{2k}(\pi j/q).
\]
Conditional entropy here includes expectation over the past under \(Q_T\).
With \(a=a_k\) in \(R\) and \(a=a_{k,q}\) in \(R/qR\), one has
\[
                \mathcal H_G(k,L)\ \ge\
                   -\log a-km-\frac{\log(4T)}{L}.                    \tag{1}
\]
This includes Salem inputs and every other conjugate signature.
The estimate averages all the specified cuts of the same bridge, including the deterministic terminal cut.

Along any sequence with \(0<m\to0\) and \(\log(4T)=o(L)\), the following choices give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 k=\lfloor1/(2m)\rfloor\!:\quad&
    \liminf \sqrt m\,\exp(\mathcal H_R(k,L))
                  \ge\sqrt{\pi/(2\mathrm e)},\\
 mq^2\to0,\quad k=\lceil q/\sqrt m\rceil\!:\quad&
       \log q-\mathcal H_{R/qR}(k,L)\longrightarrow0 .
 \end{aligned}                                                       \tag{2}
\]
For the second choice, fix \(0<\lambda\le1\) and \(\gamma>0\).
At cut \(i\) let any list \(A_i\subset R/qR\) of cardinality at most \(q^d e^{-\gamma d}\) depend on \(\mathcal F_{i-1}\).
Uniformly in these rules, the bridge probability of hitting the lists at least \(\lambda L\) times satisfies
\[
 \limsup\frac1{dL}\log Q_T\!\left\{\sum_{i=1}^L
           \mathbf1_{\{Y_i\in A_i\}}\ge\lambda L\right\}
               \le-\lambda\gamma .                                   \tag{3}
\]
We use \(\log0=-\infty\).
This strengthening of the [returning-carry count](Yin_C_G0345_v679.md#statement) describes the conditional law on paths of a prescribed length.
It gives a necessary property of any family of units with shrinking positive measure.

<a id="cost"></a>
## Entropy lost to the endpoint condition

Write \(P\) for the original digit law and \(p_T=P(C_T=0)\), which is positive because the all-zero path returns.
The return is exactly the equality of two independent binary sums in \(R\).
By Cauchy–Schwarz and the [support estimate](Yin_C_G0281_v1.md#norm-grid-support-bound),
\[
              p_T\ge(4T)^{-d}e^{-(T-1)m}.
\]
Thus \(D(Q_T\Vert P)=-\log p_T\le d\log(4T)+(T-1)m\), with all distributions taken on the finite set of length-\(T\) difference words.

Let \(B_i\) be the \(N\) digits of block \(i\).
Given the full past \(w\), denote by \(Q_i^w\) its conditional law under \(Q_T\) and by \(P_N\) its unconditioned product law.
The chain rule gives the exact equality
\[
           \sum_{i=1}^L\mathbb E_{Q_T}
                  D(Q_i^{\mathcal F_{i-1}}\Vert P_N)=-\log p_T .
                                                                    \tag{4}
\]
For each past \(w\) of positive bridge probability the next carry is a fixed function of the block word.
The unrestricted output law from this past has maximum atom at most \(a^d\) by the [degree-block calculation in the order and its quotients](Yin_C_G0345_v679.md#blocks).
That calculation needs only the power basis and the fact that multiplication by \(\alpha\) is an automorphism.
It applies at every signature and starting state, since the map from displacement to endpoint is an invertible affine map in both groups.
Data processing bounds the output relative entropy by \(D(Q_i^w\Vert P_N)\).
The cross entropy with respect to the unrestricted output law is at least \(-d\log a\), so
\[
       H_{Q_T}(Y_i\mid\mathcal F_{i-1}=w)
                   \ge -d\log a-D(Q_i^w\Vert P_N).
\]
Summation, (4) and the bound on \(p_T\) prove (1).
In the quotient, \(d\log q\) is the maximum possible entropy at each cut.

<a id="scales"></a>
## Block lengths and a common horizon

For the first choice in (2), \(km\to1/2\) and Stirling's formula gives \(a_k\sim1/\sqrt{\pi k}\).
For the second, \(km\to0\), \(k/q^2\to\infty\), and the [finite-dual moment estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v679.md#blocks) gives \(q a_{k,q}\to1\).
These limits in (1), together with the maximum entropy in the quotient, yield (2).
In either regime one can take \(L=\lceil(\log(2+kd))^2\rceil\), since \(kd\to\infty\).
Both conclusions can also be read from one and the same conditioned law at different cuts.
With the two block parameters denoted by \(k_1,k_2\), take
\[
       L_0=\big\lceil(\log(2+dk_1k_2))^2\big\rceil,\qquad
       T=d k_1 k_2 L_0 .
\]
The respective block counts are \(k_2L_0\) and \(k_1L_0\), so the required logarithmic error vanishes for both.

<a id="lists"></a>
## Probability of repeated predictions from the past

Use \(G=R/qR\), and set \(b=\min\{1,(q a_{k,q})^d e^{-\gamma d}\}\).
For every past, the unrestricted conditional probability of hitting its chosen list in the next block is at most \(b\), by the atom bound.
A rule given only on pasts of positive bridge probability can be extended by empty lists on all other pasts.
Success at any specified \(h\) cuts has unrestricted probability at most \(b^h\), by conditioning successively from the last of those cuts.
Taking \(h=\lceil\lambda L\rceil\), a union bound over their choices and conditioning on the actual return give the finite estimate
\[
 Q_T\!\left\{\sum_{i=1}^L\mathbf1_{\{Y_i\in A_i\}}\ge h\right\}
 \le \min\{1,\ p_T^{-1}\tbinom{L}{h} b^h\}.                         \tag{5}
\]
In the modular regime of (2), \(-\log p_T/(dL)\le km+\log(4T)/L\to0\) and \(\log b/d\to-\gamma\).
Also \(L\to\infty\) and \(d\to\infty\).
Indeed bounded degree and bounded measure give bounded integer coefficients of the minimal polynomials, preventing their positive logarithmic measures from tending to zero at bounded degree.
Thus \(\log\binom{L}{h}/(dL)\le(\log2)/d\to0\), and \(h/L\to\lambda\).
Equation (5) now gives (3), with the same bound for all predictable lists.
No finiteness of the full returning-carry set is needed because every random variable here comes from a finite digit horizon.
A positive Mahler gap would require a separate obstruction to this behavior.
