## Statement

### UJ1. Explicit local domain and bounded global consequence

Fix an integer j>=2 and a sign epsilon in{1,-1} separately.
The letter j denotes the dyadic level here, so it cannot be confused with the residual sign.
Let A be a positive integer divisible by4, Phi=X^2+X+1, phi=Phibar and chi=Xphi^2.
Use actual integer polynomials H,K,W,C with H monic and H_0=Hbar prime to phi, and let

    T=H Phi^(7A)+2Phi^AK+4W,
    degT=d, degK<d-2A, degW<d,
    U=Tbar=H_0 phi^(7A), k=Kbar,
    gcd(k,phi)=1.                                      (UJ1)

For C_2=X^2-2, C_3=X^3-3X and C_5=X^5-5X^3+5X, define the ACTUAL canonical integer polynomials

    a=rem_T T(C_2)/2,
    b=rem_T T(C_3)/3, c=rem_T T(C_5)/5,
    N_F=2c^2-3a^5, N_X=b^2-a^3+epsilon(c^3-2b^5),
    N_F=TQ_F+F(T), N_X=TQ_X+X_epsilon(T).              (UJ2)

The prime divisions are actual coefficient divisions after monic remainder.
Define actual integer Q_D,Q_3,Q_5 by

    Delta(T)=TQ_D+a, Delta(T)=(T(C_2)-T^2)/2,
    T(C_3)=TQ_3+3b, T(C_5)=TQ_5+5c.                  (UJ3)

The two quotient conventions Q_D and Q_3,Q_5 are different and remain explicit throughout.
Require the following canonical entrances, at this SAME T:

    b=0 modulo2 as a polynomial,
    a^2=Phi^(4A)K^4 modulo4 in the phi-primary quotient,
    c^2=Phi^(4A)C^2 modulo4 in the phi-primary quotient,
    phi^(3A)|Q_Fbar and phi^(3A)|Q_Xbar,
    F(T)=0 modulo2^(j+2),
    X_epsilon(T)=0 modulo2^(j+1) coefficientwise.      (UJ4)

The quotient orders are orders of the reductions of the actual full polynomial quotients, not freely chosen local coordinates.
The square entrances follow, for example, from the actual normalized modulo4 representative formulas in C716 AV7: a=Phi^(2A)K^2+2Xi-TQ_a and c=Phi^(2A)C+2P(C_5)-TQ_c modulo4.
Their squaring gives precisely UJ4 in the old quotient.
The source normalization of adopted [C730 J512.2](Yin_C_G0730_v1.md#two-earlier-source-jets-for-next-low-five-twelve) supplies these squares, globally even b, and both quotient orders; the latter also follow from the actual C689/C716 polynomial quotient identities and retained root relation.
No value or vanishing of the old error X_epsilon(T)/2^(j+1) is included in UJ4.

Choose actual integer R,V with

    X^(4A)|R, X^A|V,
    degR<d-18A, degV<d-6A,
    r=Rbar, v=Vbar,
    v=kr/H_0 modulo phi^A.                            (UJ5)

Put

    L=Phi^(9A)R, M=Phi^(3A)V,
    D=2^j L+2^(j+1)M,
    T_new=T+D,
    K_new=K+2^(j-1)Phi^(8A)R,
    W_new=W+2^(j-1)Phi^(3A)V.                        (UJ6)

These are same-degree integer changes, and T_new is monic with the same binary divisor U.
All forthcoming reductions use the ACTUAL new divisor T_new, with H inverted only on its phi-primary component.
At coefficient modulus2^(j+2) this component is a finite free module over Z/2^(j+2), obtained from the monic quotient by its phi-primary idempotent.
Its reduction is B_phi=F_2[X]/phi^(7A).
Thus a class divisible by2^(j+1) has a well-defined binary quotient in B_phi; this local division is not declared to be a coefficientwise division of a global polynomial unless all other primary components have also been checked.
For the old error, reduction of the actual polynomial X_epsilon(T)/2^(j+1) in this new component gives its usual binary class because T_newbar=Tbar.

The local conclusions are

    F(T_new)=0 modulo2^(j+2) in the phi component,
    X_epsilon(T_new)=0 modulo2^(j+1) there,
    E_new+E_old=phi^(4A)(k^5/H_0)v^2
                                      modulo phi^(6A),       (UJ7)

where E_old is the old actual divided error and E_new is the just-defined local binary quotient of the new residual.
In particular UJ7 holds for every j>=2 under the explicit local entrances and actual correction conditions, independently of any power-root construction.

For a GLOBAL consequence, use the full adopted C730/C728 normalized original source with q>=2^17, the complete forward support and integer factors

    J_K=H_b^(15q/16)|K,
    J_W=H_b^(7q/8)|W,
    J_K|R, J_W|V.                                    (UJ8)

Here H=H_b^q and all original multiplicities and composition hypotheses of the generic C728 theorem remain in force.
Its generic full-primary theorem applies to T_new as well as T, because UJ6 retains S, degree and UJ8.
It supplies both residuals modulo2^17 on every full H_0 component without an extra error hypothesis.
Consequently, for2<=j<=15, UJ7 gives the coefficientwise global preservation

## Complete argument

### UJ2. Exact dyadic difference and its uniform coefficient precision

Because T_new=T modulo4, both actual old square identities in UJ4 transport to the new coefficient-four quotient.
Moreover K_new=K modulo2, hence K_new^4=K^4 modulo4, so the first transported square may use K_new.
In the new quotient the actual identity Delta(T)=TQ_D+a and T=-D give

    delta a=D(C_2)/2-DQ_D+D^2/2
       =2^(j-1)L(C_2)+2^j M(C_2)
        -2^j LQ_D-2^(j+1)MQ_D+2^(2j-1)L^2
                                        modulo2^(j+2).       (UJ10)

The omitted terms2^(2j+1)LM and2^(2j+1)M^2 have the required coefficient divisibility for every j>=2.
This equality compares the old canonical a evaluated at T_new with the new canonical a; it includes the old dyadic quotient carry.

The integer difference Phi(C_2)-Phi^2 is a2-multiple.
Since4|A, the binomial expansions give

    Phi(C_2)^(9A)=Phi^(18A) modulo8,
    Phi(C_2)^(3A)=Phi^(6A) modulo4.                 (UJ11)

For the first congruence the first binomial correction has coefficient18A divisible by8, the second has coefficient4*binom(9A,2) divisible by8, and all later corrections have coefficient8.
The second congruence needs only even3A.
These fixed coefficient moduli suffice in UJ10 because their outside factors are2^(j-1) and2^j respectively.
No composition at modulus2^(j+2) is being inferred from a binary local representative.

Use the SAME actual new divisor relation

    H Phi^(7A)=-2Phi^AK_new-4W_new.                 (UJ12)

In particular

    Phi^(18A)=4Phi^(6A)K_new^2/H^2
                                              modulo16,
    Phi^(9A)=-2Phi^(3A)K_new/H-4Phi^(2A)W_new/H.

If j>=3 the coefficient2^(2j-1) kills L^2 already modulo2^(j+2).
For j=2 the remaining8Phi^(18A)R^2 is zero modulo16 after even one application of UJ12.
Substituting UJ11--UJ12 into UJ10 therefore yields

    delta a=2^j Phi^(6A)V(C_2)
       +2^(j+1)Phi^(6A)K_new^2 R(C_2)/H^2
       +2^(j+1)Phi^(3A)(K_new R/H-V)Q_D
                                        modulo2^(j+2).       (UJ13)

Thus delta a is a genuine2^j-multiple in the new local module and (delta a)^2=0 modulo2^(j+2), including the endpoint j=2.
This calculation is uniform because the precise integer scalar precedes every composition and divisor substitution.

### UJ3. The dyadic cube term and every odd canonical term

The transported square a^2=Phi^(4A)K_new^4 modulo4 and UJ13 show that only its first summand can contribute to -3a^2 delta a after division by2^(j+1).
Indeed its actual product is

    -3*2^j Phi^(10A)K_new^4 V(C_2)
       =3*2^(j+1)Phi^(4A)K_new^5 V(C_2)/H
                                        modulo2^(j+2),       (UJ14)

where the W_new part of UJ12 has an additional coefficient2 and disappears.
After the actual coefficient division, V(C_2)bar=v(X^2)=v^2, so UJ14 has binary value phi^(4A)k^5v^2/H_0.
The second summand of UJ13 meets a^2 at Phi-order10A after its factor2^(j+1), and the third meets it at order7A.
Both therefore vanish in B_phi after that coefficient division.
The quadratic and cubic delta a terms are zero modulo2^(j+2) by UJ13, so the full change of -a^3 has precisely UJ14's divided contribution.

The actual odd quotient identities UJ3 give

    delta b=(D(C_3)-DQ_3)/3,
    delta c=(D(C_5)-DQ_5)/5                       (UJ15)

in the new quotient.
Both are2^j-multiples, using the odd denominators only as units.
Since b is globally even, 2b delta b and(delta b)^2 are zero modulo2^(j+2).
The change of2b^5 is also zero: its linear term has coefficient10b^4 delta b and every higher term has at least2^(2j+1).
This checks both b contributions to N_X without a local composition of b or of an error class.

For c^3 only3c^2 delta c survives at this precision, because(delta c)^2 is zero modulo2^(j+2).
Use the actual transported square c^2=Phi^(4A)C^2 modulo4 and inspect all four terms of UJ15.
First, C_5=XPhi^2+2E_5 for an actual integer E_5.
The full factor X^(4A)|R and even exponent4A imply

    R(C_5) in(4,Phi^(8A)).                         (UJ16)

This follows by substituting the complete integer polynomial before reduction; the first binomial correction in C_5^(4A) has coefficient8A and all later corrections have coefficient4.
In the contribution2^j Phi^(4A)C^2 L(C_5)/5, the4-part of UJ16 dies modulo2^(j+2), while its Phi^(8A) part produces Phi^(12A).
One application of UJ12 changes that term into a2^(j+1)-multiple whose divided binary order is at least6A.
The other composition term has coefficient2^(j+1), and V(C_5)bar=v(chi) has order at least2A because of the full X^A factor of V.
Together with Phi^(4A) it too starts at6A after division.
The LQ_5 subtraction has Phi-order13A before one UJ12 substitution, hence order7A after its factor2^(j+1).
The MQ_5 subtraction already has coefficient2^(j+1) and Phi-order7A.
Both are zero in B_phi at the divided precision.
All unit factors3/5 and Phi(C_5) powers are retained in these order estimates; no vanishing of those units is presumed.
Thus the complete change of epsilon c^3 is a2^(j+1)-multiple and contributes zero modulo phi^(6A) to the divided second error.
The sign disappears only in the final binary reduction.

### UJ4. Actual old-numerator carries and first-residual preservation

The canonical equality N_X=TQ_X+X_epsilon(T) gives, in the new quotient,

    X_epsilon(T_new)=X_epsilon(T)+delta N_X-DQ_X.

The same-divisor relation UJ12 rewrites the ACTUAL correction as

    D=2^(j+1)Phi^(3A)(V-K_new R/H)
                                        modulo2^(j+2).       (UJ17)

Its divided product with the actual old Q_X therefore reduces to

    phi^(3A)(v-kr/H_0)Q_Xbar.                     (UJ18)

The quotient order in UJ4 puts UJ18 at order at least6A without the matching condition, and UJ5 improves it to7A.
All raw second-numerator changes in UJ3 are divisible by2^(j+1), and the old residual is a coefficient2^(j+1)-multiple.
This proves the legitimate local divisibility of the new residual before forming its binary quotient.
The old divided residual has unchanged binary class when read in the new component, because the reduced divisor is still U.
Combining UJ14, UJ16 and UJ18 proves the full second formula UJ7 modulo phi^(6A).
There is no assumption that E_old is zero and no replacement of its actual integer quotient by a formal local error.

For F, the change of2c^2 is zero modulo2^(j+2): its terms are4c delta c and2(delta c)^2.
In the fifth-power change of a, all quadratic and higher delta a terms vanish by UJ13.
In the linear term -15a^4 delta a, the first summand of UJ13 meets a^4=Phi^(8A)K_new^8 modulo4 and produces Phi^(14A) after its factor2^j.
Squaring UJ12 makes Phi^(14A) a4-multiple in the same local quotient, supplying the full coefficient2^(j+2).
The other two summands already have coefficient2^(j+1); their product with a^4bar=phi^(8A)k^8 is zero in B_phi.
Consequently delta N_F=0 modulo2^(j+2).
The only remaining canonical change is the actual old-divisor carry -DQ_F.
Its divided reduction is phi^(3A)(v-kr/H_0)Q_Fbar, which is zero in B_phi by UJ4--UJ5 because its order is at least7A.
The old F is already a coefficient2^(j+2)-multiple.
This proves the whole local first-residual preservation in UJ7, rather than only preservation below phi^(6A).

### UJ5. Retained normalized data and exact scope of reuse

Under the C730 source domain the new binary k,w are exactly unchanged by UJ6, since j>=2.
Hence both original root congruences, the binary X^(3A/2) factor of k, the phi^A factor of w and all reduced canonical/source data retain their old meanings.
For B=Phi^(8A)R the exact integer identity is

    Delta(K_new)-Delta(K)
       =2^(j-2)B(C_2)-2^(j-1)KB-2^(2j-3)B^2.

At j=2 its binary value is phi^(16A)r^2; for every j>=3 its binary value is zero.
Thus phi^(4A)|Delta(K)bar is retained in every case, with the exceptional lowest value checked rather than discarded by a high-level estimate.
The actual strong factors are retained by UJ8, and the degree inequalities UJ5 give degK_new<d-2A and degW_new<d.
The SAME original S,H,C and full multiplicities remain in place.
The W change has an actual Phi^(3A) factor, so every actual remainder of W modulo Phi^A is unchanged, including any legitimate existing halves, quarters or higher coordinate; no new value of those coordinates is thereby created.
No assertion that the full integer K_new is a single q/512-th power is made.

The adopted C728 generic theorem, with its own q>=2^17 and full-forward hypotheses, supplies exactly the bounded global implication UJ9.
The purely local proof UJ10--UJ18 uses only4|A and the explicit UJ1--UJ5 entrances and is not restricted to j<=15.
