# A ternary Mordell–Weil sieve for untwisted trace factors

<a id="statement"></a>
## Three modular orbits that must occur together

Let \(Q_{k,l}\) be the monic trace polynomial of the untwisted two-pole section at positive coprime exponents \((k,l)\), of degree \(2k+l\).
At the good prime \(p=919\), the three irreducible modular factors of \(Q_{1,4}\) have degrees \(1,2,3\) and all have character \(2\) in an additive quotient of order three of the second elliptic quotient.
Any rational factor must have character zero, so all three modular orbits occur together and \(Q_{1,4}\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\).
At the same prime the factors of \(Q_{1,10}\) have degrees \(2,10\) and characters \(1,2\), respectively, giving rational irreducibility there as well.
The ordinary modular degree test alone admits every degree from one through five in the first case and degrees two and ten in the second.
A single additive character of order two vanishing on the full sextic cannot by itself force these three orbits together because at least one of three binary values summing to zero is zero.

For \(h\ge12\) put \(M_h=p\operatorname{lcm}_{1\le j\le h}(p^{2j}-1)\).
At any positive primitive direction congruent coordinatewise modulo \(M_h\) to \((1,4)\), every rational factor of positive degree at most \(h\) has degree six.
In the class of \((1,10)\) its degree must be twelve.
For instance taking \(k=1\) and appending any positive multiple of \(M_h\) to the given \(l\) gives infinitely many directions in each class.

## The rational image in the quotient of order nine

Use the [rank-one elliptic quotient](Yin_C_G0345_v858.md#models) \(E:Y^2=X^3+1550X^2+597425X\), with rational torsion of order two.
Its equation at \(p\) has coefficients \(a=631,b=75\), with \(b(a^2-4b)\ne0\bmod p\).
The group has order \(936=9\cdot104\) by the counting formula \(1+\sum_{x=0}^{918}(1+\chi_p(x(x^2+631x+75)))\), with the quadratic character zero at zero.
For an integer-arithmetic verification of the sum, tabulate a counter at \(y^2\bmod919\) for each \(0\le y<919\).
Summing the counters at the displayed cubic values gives 935 affine points.
The rational point from the [quartic certificate](Yin_C_G0345_v858.md#descent) reduces to \(G=(906,618)\), using the ordinate \(-23\cdot783965\cdot184438601605/132828^3\).
Set \(A=104G=(495,876)\) and \(B=(904,353)\).
Both have order three and their abscissas differ, so they generate \(E[3](\mathbb F_p)\).
The exponent of the 3-primary subgroup is three by the group order.
Multiplication by 104 kills the prime-to-three part and induces an isomorphism \(E(\mathbb F_p)/3E(\mathbb F_p)\to\langle A,B\rangle\).
Define \(\lambda(P)=j\in\mathbb F_3\) by \(104P=iA+jB\).
The rank and rational torsion give \(|E(\mathbb Q)/3E(\mathbb Q)|=3\).
Since \(G\) contributes \(A\ne O\), the reduced rational image under multiplication by 104 is precisely \(\langle A\rangle\).
Consequently each rational point has character zero, without having to decide whether the chosen rational point generates the free part globally.

## Signed coordinates of the finite-field trace points

Put \(r=s+s^{-1},\ U=s^k,\ V=s^l,\ t=U-U^{-1},\ Z=U+U^{-1}\), and use the [inversion invariant](Yin_C_G0345_v857.md#cover) \(W=t(2(t-3)(t-4)V+1)\).
Write \(B_j=s^j+s^{-j}\), \(C_j=(s^j-s^{-j})/(s-s^{-1})\) as polynomials in \(r\), each satisfying \(D_{j+1}=rD_j-D_{j-1}\) with initial pairs \((2,r)\) and \((0,1)\).
The quadratic in \(V\) gives \((t-3)(t-4)V+(t+3)(t+4)V^{-1}=-1\).
Subtracting the two terms instead gives \(2(t-3)(t-4)V+1\), so on the section
\[
 v=Z^2-4,\quad Z=B_k,\quad W=(v+12)(r^2-4)C_k C_l-7v B_l,\qquad
 X=-575 Z^2/v,\quad Y=2300 ZW/v^2 .
\]
These coordinates keep the ordinate determined by the section even when \(s\) itself is only defined in a quadratic extension of the trace field.
For each modular factor \(f\), sum its associated elliptic points over the Frobenius orbit to obtain \(P_f\in E(\mathbb F_p)\).
The [rational divisor interpretation](Yin_C_G0345_v857.md#factors) makes the sum of the characteristic-zero points for a monic rational factor a rational elliptic point.
When the modular factors are simple and \(v\) is nonzero there, specialization commutes with this sum by good reduction and the displayed regular coordinates, so the sum of their \(\lambda(P_f)\) is zero.

The factors below are given by descending coefficient lists modulo 919.
A row of degree at most two gives the computed orbit sum.
The remaining character in each section follows by applying the zero-sum rule to the whole rational trace polynomial.

For verification multiply the indicated factors and compare with \(1+10B_l+B_{l+2k}+B_{|l-2k|}-7B_{l+k}+7B_{|l-k|}\).
For each list of degree \(d\), successive powering of \(r\) to the 919th power modulo the list returns \(r\) at step \(d\), with \(\gcd(r^{919^{d/q}}-r,f)=1\) for each prime \(q\mid d\).
The distinct lists therefore give the full simple factorization.
At \(r=2,-2\) both base polynomials have value 25, so the denominators are units at every root since \(k=1\) in the table.
The linear orbit has \(W=731\).
The two quadratic points before summation are respectively
\[
  (331+4r,350+414r),\qquad (588+33r,122+476r).
\]
Their conjugates are obtained by substituting \(15-r\) and \(92-r\), respectively.
Adding each pair on the displayed cubic gives the table.
All point checks may use \(x_3=m^2-a-x_1-x_2,\ y_3=m(x_1-x_3)-y_1\) with secant slope or tangent slope \((3x_1^2+2ax_1+b)/(2y_1)\).
The nonzero proper subset sums of the three equal characters, and of the two opposite characters separately, exclude every proper rational factor of either polynomial.

## Bounded degrees on the congruence classes

For a potential trace root of degree \(j\le h\) modulo \(p\), its lift \(s\) with \(s+s^{-1}=r\) lies in \(\mathbb F_{p^{2j}}^\times\).
All section monomials at this lift agree with the base direction throughout its stated exponent class, because its order divides \(M_h\).
Their derivatives in \(s\) agree as well because the exponents are congruent modulo \(p\).
Both values \(r=\pm2\) are excluded by the base equations, so the trace change has nonzero derivative and the short roots are still simple.
The signed values of \(t,Z,V,W\) coincide with the base values, preserving the table of orbit characters.
A monic rational factor of degree at most \(h\) reduces to a product of distinct short orbits, hence to a subset of the corresponding base table with character sum zero.
The two degree restrictions follow.
