# Split denominator mass at the first integer evaluation of a reversed block

<a id="statement"></a>
## Bounds uniform in the size of the block

Use the signed polynomial \(f\) of the [reversed real-root signature](Yin_C_G0345_v809.md#statement), put \(g=\tau f\), \(n=[K:\mathbb Q]=2u\), and write \(R=e^{m/2}\).
For any integer \(q>R\), put \(w=f(q)/g(q)\), let \(D\) be the integral denominator ideal of \((w)\) in lowest terms, and set \(L=\log w\) at the chosen real place.
Then
\[
 ND\ge \frac{(q/2)^n}{\sinh^2(L/2)},\qquad
 ND\ge \frac{q^{n/2}}{\sinh L}\quad(q\text{ even}).
\]
In particular if \(m\to0\) on a sequence of these witnesses, the fixed argument \(q=2\) satisfies
\[
 ND(2)\gg \max\{m^{-2},2^u/m\}
\]
with absolute implied constant.
This forces a nontrivial denominator supported above split primes already at the first integer above one, uniformly in the size \(b\) of the block.
The [resultant bound](Yin_C_G0345_v818.md#denominator) contains a factor \((2b)^{-nb}\) and gives no corresponding conclusion there.
The estimate controls a norm and permits powers of split primes already present.

<a id="congruence"></a>
## Comparing the finite and infinite places of the same element

The [oriented block product](Yin_C_G0345_v274.md#statement) is torsion, and its image at the chosen real embedding lies in \(\{1,-1\}\).
Signing the roots multiplies it by a rational sign, so \(f(0)=g(0)=\pm1\).
At every prime ideal dividing \((q)\), both evaluations are local units and \(w-1\) belongs to \(q\mathcal O_{K,\mathfrak p}\).
For even \(q\), the same primes above two also satisfy \(w+1\in2\mathcal O_{K,\mathfrak p}\), since \(w+1=(w-1)+2\).
Thus \(w^2-1\) belongs locally to \((2q)\) at every prime dividing \((2q)\) when \(q\) is even.
For \(h=1,2\), the ideal \((w^h-1)D^h\) is integral: a negative valuation of \(w\) contributes exactly \(h\) times that valuation to \(w^h-1\).
It is nonzero by the [strict positivity of \(L\)](Yin_C_G0345_v809.md#expansion).
Its norm is therefore at least \(q^n\) for \(h=1\) and at least \((2q)^n\) for \(h=2\) and even \(q\).

The two real embeddings give reciprocal values of \(w\), and every other embedding gives modulus one by the [signature and involution](Yin_C_G0345_v809.md#ideals).
The real pair contributes exactly \(4\sinh^2(hL/2)\) to \(|N_{K/\mathbb Q}(w^h-1)|\), while each remaining embedding contributes at most two.
Consequently this absolute norm is at most \(2^n\sinh^2(hL/2)\).
Comparing the bounds proves both inequalities.
Finally the [series estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v809.md#expansion) gives \(0<L\le 2mR/(2-R)=O(m)\) at \(q=2\) as \(m\to0\), and the involution exchanges numerator and denominator prime ideals.
