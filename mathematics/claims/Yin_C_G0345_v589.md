# A lower bound on the number of terms forced by a small Mahler measure

<a id="statement"></a>
## A degree-independent lower bound on the witness weight

For a nontorsion algebraic unit \(\alpha\) write \(m=\log M(\alpha)\), \(d=\deg\alpha\), and \(d_*=\min_{q\ge1}\deg(\alpha^q)\).
Use the [terminal relation count \(W_r(\alpha,B)\)](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#statement), which fixes the entire ordered list of outgoing coefficients and permits an output of degree at most \(B\) and arbitrary height.
There is an absolute \(C\) such that for every \(1\le B\le d_*/4\) and \(r\ge3\),
\[
                  \log\max(1,W_r(\alpha,B))
                  \le \log(2B)+ C r^5\log(2r).
\]
Along any sequence on which \(m\to0\) and \(Bm\to0\), put \(L=\log(1/m)\).
Every witness weight \(r\) of the [fixed-coefficient lower bound](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#statement) satisfies, for an absolute \(a>0\) and all sufficiently late inputs,
\[
                       r\ge a\left(\frac{L}{\log(2+L)}\right)^{1/4}.
\]

At strict degree records this yields a lower bound for the number of primitive marked exponent lists on a range described entirely by \(m\) and \(d\).
Specifically suppose \(\log B=o(L)\) and put \(D=\log\log(3d)\).
For the fixed coefficient tuple and the weight supplied by the [finite witness](Yin_C_G0345_v588.md#statement), let \(V_r\) count the distinct lists after division by their exponent gcd at the same marked origin, as in that result.
If
\[
                   D=o\!\left(\frac{L^{5/4}}{(\log(2+L))^{1/4}}\right),
\]
then \(\log V_r\ge(1-o(1))(r-2)L\).
This includes every sequence with \(L\ge D^{4/5+\epsilon}\) for a fixed \(\epsilon>0\).
Conversely, if \(V_r\le A^r\) at the indicated witnesses for a fixed \(A\ge1\), then along the sequence
\[
                     L=O\!\left(D^{4/5}(\log(2+D))^{1/5}\right).
\]
These implications apply to all conjugate configurations.
The exponential upper bound for primitive lists is an open assumption in the converse.

<a id="blocks"></a>
## How many exponent lists a partition can represent

Let \(s=r-1\), and fix an embedding \(\sigma\) for which \(\beta=\sigma(\alpha)\) and \(\alpha\) are multiplicatively independent.
For an exponent list counted at this embedding the equality between the outgoing sum and its conjugate is a labeled equation on \(2s\) terms.
As in the [embedding argument](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#embeddings), it admits a partition into minimal vanishing blocks, each meeting both sides.
Choose the least labeled term on the conjugate side of each block as its pivot.
In a block of length \(\ell\), division by its pivot gives \(n=\ell-1\) nonzero coordinates in the \(n\)-fold Cartesian product of \(\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle\), of rank at most \(2n\).
Its coefficients are fixed by the chosen lists, labels and embedding.
Minimal vanishing of the block makes this an inhomogeneous equation with right side one and with no nonempty left zero subsum.
For fixed pivots the full collection of coordinate tuples recovers every original exponent by multiplicative independence.

We use Amoroso and Viada's Theorem 6.2 as an external theorem at the level of its checked statement.
For \(n\) nonzero fixed coefficients over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\) and tuple-group rank \(\rho\), its upper bound on the number of nondegenerate tuples is \((8n)^{4n^4(n+\rho+1)}\). [Source and hypotheses](../audits/literature/AV09/Yin_U001.md#torino-recurrence)
Here \(n\ge1\), so \(\rho\le2n\) bounds the logarithm of the number of choices by \(16n^5\log(8n)\).
For a partition with all block sizes at most \(k\le2s\) we have \(\sum n\le2s\), yielding the bound \(32s k^4\log(8k)\) on the logarithm of the product of the block counts.
At most \((2s)^{2s}\) partitions of the labeled positions occur.
Thus for one fixed independent embedding, the total number of lists that have some partition with all block sizes at most \(k\) is at most
\[
                E(s,k)=(2s)^{2s}\exp\bigl(32s k^4\log(8k)\bigr).
\]
Taking \(k=2s\) includes every list at this embedding.
Every allowed output has at least \(d/(2B)\) independent fixing embeddings by the [power-degree incidence estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#embeddings).
Double counting over at most \(d\) embeddings gives \(W_r\le 2B E(s,2s)\), as claimed.

<a id="arity"></a>
## Comparing the upper and lower bounds

For a witness with \(W_r\ge(c_0/m)^{r-2}\) we have
\[
              (r-2)(L+\log c_0)\le \log(2B)+Cr^5\log(2r).
\]
The witness weights tend to infinity by the [fixed-arity bound](Yin_C_G0345_v584.md#trees).
Since \(\log B\le L\) eventually, this forces \(L\ll r^4\log(2r)\).
If \(r\ge L\), the claimed lower bound follows immediately; otherwise \(\log(2r)\le\log(2L)\), giving it again.
In particular \(rL\gg L^{5/4}/(\log(2+L))^{1/4}\).
The restricted finite-window count is at most \(W_r\) and satisfies the same lower bound at its chosen weight, so this estimate applies there as well.

At a strict record the finite-window estimate gives the asserted primitive lower count whenever \(D=o(rL)\). [Height and dilation bound](Yin_C_G0345_v588.md#polynomial)
For \(L\ge D^{4/5+\epsilon}\) the required little-oh follows because \(L^{5/4}/(\log(2+L))^{1/4}\) eventually increases and dominates \(D\) already at the lower endpoint.
Under \(V_r\le A^r\), the same finite-window result gives \(r=O(1+D/L)\).
As \(r\to\infty\), this implies \(rL=O(D)\).
Together with the lower bound for \(rL\), this gives \(L^5\ll D^4\log(2+L)\).
We also have \(L=O(D)\), so the last logarithm is \(O(\log(2+D))\), proving the converse.
The resulting restriction on the decay of \(m\) still permits positive measures tending to zero.

<a id="large-blocks"></a>
## Relations whose conjugate comparisons require a large block

For each sufficiently late witness let \(\mathcal I\) consist of all pairs of a counted list and an independent embedding fixing its output, using one fixed outgoing tuple realizing the lower bound.
For the finite-window variant restrict to its counted lists.
At least \(\#\mathcal I\ge (d/(2B))(c_0/m)^{r-2}\) pairs occur.
The proportion admitting any partition with maximum block size at most \(k\) is bounded by
\[
                2B E(r-1,k)(m/c_0)^{r-2}.
\]
In particular, when \(\log r=o(L)\) and \(k^4\log(8k)=o(L)\), this proportion tends to zero.
Indeed its logarithm is at most \(-(r-2)L+o(rL)\), using \(\log B\le L\).
Thus almost all of these incidences require a longer mixed minimal block in every partition.
The fraction concerns pairs of lists and fixing embeddings and allows a list to occur at more than one embedding.
