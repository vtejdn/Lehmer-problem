# Salem units below the plastic number with no valuation gain at prescribed finite places

<a id="statement"></a>
## A growing torsion window with zero local valuations

Let \(f(Z)=Z^3-Z-1\), \(f^*(Z)=1-Z^2-Z^3\), and let \(\theta>1\) be the root of \(f\).
For every integer \(h\ge1\), put
\[
 P_h(Z)=\frac{Z^{60h+2}f(Z)-f^*(Z)}{Z^5-1}
       =1+Z^2 f(Z)\sum_{j=0}^{12h-1}Z^{5j}.                            \tag{1}
\]
This is the irreducible monic minimal polynomial of a Salem number \(\beta_h\), of degree \(d_h=60h\) and coefficient height one.
The numbers \(\beta_h\) strictly increase to \(\theta\) from below.
For \(m\ge1\) set \(R_m(h)=|\operatorname{Res}(P_h,\Phi_m)|\), including \(\Phi_1=Z-1\).
For every \(m\mid60h\),
\[
 R_m(h)=
 \begin{cases}
  12h-1,&m=1,\\
  (144h^2+36h+1)^2,&m=5,\\
  1,&m\ne1,5.
 \end{cases}                                                         \tag{2}
\]
In particular \(P_h(1)=1-12h\) and \(P_h(-1)=1\).

For any prime \(p\mid h\) and any order \(m\) whose prime-to-\(p\) part divides \(60h\), \(P_h(\zeta_m)\) is congruent to one at every prime above \(p\).
Consequently, for arbitrary bounds \(J,B\ge1\), one may choose arbitrarily large \(h\) such that
\[
 v_\mathfrak p(P_h(\zeta_m))=0
 \quad\text{for all }m\le J,\ p\le B,\text{ and every }\mathfrak p\mid p.       \tag{3}
\]
Here \(p\) ranges over rational primes and the valuations are those of the relevant cyclotomic integer ring.
For each selected \(p\), the same conclusion holds after multiplying the tested order by an arbitrary power of \(p\).
Choose \(h\) divisible by \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,J)\) and every prime at most \(B\).

For \(J\ge5\) and this choice of \(h\), the entire unweighted sum of logarithmic norms through the tested window is exactly
\[
 \sum_{m=1}^J\log R_m(h)
   =\log(12h-1)+2\log(144h^2+36h+1)=5\log h+O(1).                   \tag{4}
\]
Thus its ratio to the degree tends to zero.
Taking \(h=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,H)\), \(J=B=H\to\infty\), makes the local zero-valuation conclusion simultaneous for windows with
\[
             \log d_h=\log60+\psi(H),\qquad
             \psi(H)=\sum_{p^a\le H}\log p .
\]
The elementary estimates proved below give \(H\asymp\log d_h\) along powers of two.
The measures here approach the positive value \(\log\theta\).
The family gives a concrete obstruction to requiring a positive valuation contribution uniformly from these prescribed finite tests on all Salem inputs below \(\theta\).

More generally, fix finitely many integers \(A_i\ge2\) and monic \(G_i\in\mathbb Z[Z]\) of positive degrees \(g_i\), with \(\gcd(G_i(0),A_i)=1\).
There are arbitrarily large \(h\) for which the same Salem minimal polynomial satisfies
\[
                            P_h\equiv1\pmod{(A_i,G_i)}
                                  \quad\text{for every }i.             \tag{5}
\]
Let \(e_i\) be the order of \(Z^5\) in the finite unit group of
\((\mathbb Z/A_i\mathbb Z)[Z]/(G_i)\).
Every positive multiple of
\[
 H_0=\operatorname{lcm}_i\frac{A_i e_i}{\gcd(12,A_i e_i)},
 \qquad H_0\le\prod_i A_i^{g_i+1},
\]
is an admissible \(h\), giving explicit degree \(60h\).
Powers of the \(G_i\) are allowed, so these congruences include arbitrary fixed orders of vanishing of \(P_h-1\) at the chosen nonzero residue nodes.
A diagonal sequence of these Salem inputs converges to the constant polynomial one under every such fixed finite local test.
This supplies a test-dependent degree cost while the Mahler measures still tend to \(\log\theta\).

## The actual irreducible factors on this progression

Write \(N=60h+2\).
The [negative plastic completions](Yin_C_G0345_v131.md#minus) show that \(Z^Nf-f^*\), for \(N\ge8\), has one simple exterior root in \((1,\theta)\), its inverse, and a cyclotomic complement.
Those exterior roots strictly increase with \(N\) to \(\theta\).
The [torsion calculation for even delays](Yin_C_G0345_v131.md#torsion) gives the only possible cyclotomic orders \(1,3,5,12,30\), with orders \(3,5,12,30\) occurring respectively at
\[
                 N\equiv1\pmod3,\quad 2\pmod5,\quad4\pmod{12},
                                  \quad6\pmod{30}.
\]
At \(N=60h+2\), the only such factors are \(Z-1\) and \(\Phi_5\).
The identity \(Z^2f-f^*=(Z-1)\Phi_5=Z^5-1\) proves both forms in (1).
At \(Z=1\) the displayed geometric sum gives \(P_h(1)=1-12h\ne0\), so the factor \(Z-1\) is simple in the numerator.
At a primitive fifth root \(\zeta\), it gives
\[
                    P_h(\zeta)=1+12h(1-\zeta^2-\zeta^3).              \tag{6}
\]
The two conjugate values of \(1-\zeta^2-\zeta^3\) are \((3+\sqrt5)/2\) and \((3-\sqrt5)/2\).
Both are positive, so (6) is nonzero and the factor \(\Phi_5\) is also simple.
There are therefore no remaining cyclotomic factors.
The single noncyclotomic factor of the completion is exactly \(P_h\), proving its irreducibility and Salem signature.
The geometric sum in (1) has terms only in exponent classes zero, two and three modulo five after multiplication by \(Z^2f=Z^5-Z^3-Z^2\).
There are no overlaps among these three classes, so the coefficient height is one.

## Exact unit values and the two exceptional norms

Suppose \(m\mid60h\).
If a primitive \(m\)-th root \(\zeta\) satisfies \(\zeta^5\ne1\), then
\[
             \sum_{j=0}^{12h-1}\zeta^{5j}
                          =\frac{\zeta^{60h}-1}{\zeta^5-1}=0 .
\]
Equation (1) gives \(P_h(\zeta)=1\).
The two excluded orders are \(1\) and \(5\).
The endpoint value at one was already computed.
For order five, the two values in (6) each occur twice in the four embeddings of the cyclotomic field.
Their product is
\[
 \left(1+12h\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2}\right)
 \left(1+12h\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\right)=1+36h+144h^2.
\]
This proves (2), and summation gives (4).

## Prime-power inflation and relative ramification

Fix \(p\mid h\), a primitive \(m\)-th root \(\zeta\), and a prime above \(p\).
If the prime-to-\(p\) part of \(m\) divides \(60h\), its reduction \(\bar\zeta\) has \(\bar\zeta^{60h}=1\).
Indeed reduction removes the \(p\)-power part of a root of unity and preserves its prime-to-\(p\) order.
If \(\bar\zeta^5\ne1\), the same geometric sum identity shows that the sum in (1) reduces to zero.
If \(\bar\zeta^5=1\), that sum reduces to \(12h=0\) in the residue field.
In both cases \(P_h(\zeta)\) reduces to one, proving (3) and its extension to all \(p\)-power multiples of the tested orders.
No coefficient denominator or nonintegral input occurs in this calculation.

Let \(A_h=\mathbb Q(\beta_h)\) and \(F_h=\mathbb Q(\beta_h+\beta_h^{-1})\).
The [relative quadratic discriminant bound](Yin_C_G0345_v73.md#relative) says that the norm of the relative discriminant ideal divides the absolute trace endpoint product.
For the present even trace degree \(30h\), that product is \(12h-1\).
Thus \(A_h/F_h\) is unramified at every prime of \(F_h\) above every rational prime dividing \(h\).
This includes all primes at most \(B\) in (3).

For the least common multiple choice, its logarithm is exactly \(\psi(H)\).
The integer \(\binom{2n}{n}\) divides \(\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,2n)\), since its valuation at \(p\) is a sum of zero-one terms, one for each power \(p^a\le2n\).
Thus \(\psi(H)\ge H\log2-\log(H+1)\) for even \(H\), using the largest term in the binomial expansion of \(2^H\).
Conversely, the product of the primes corresponding to powers \(n<p^a\le2n\) divides \(\binom{2n}{n}\).
Every such power contributes a term one to that prime's binomial valuation, so
\(\psi(2n)-\psi(n)\le2n\log2\).
Summing at powers of two gives \(\psi(H)\le2H\log2\) there.
This proves the asserted logarithmic window scale without a prime-distribution asymptotic.
The [earlier plastic comparison](Yin_C_G0345_v145.md#plastic) uses a family approaching \(\theta\) from above and a selected cyclotomic product with unit resultant.
Here the progression lies below \(\theta\), covers every order in a growing window with exactly two computed exceptions, and makes even these exceptions units at all selected finite places.
The [integrality criteria](Yin_C_G0345_v284.md#statement) and [full-value criterion](Yin_C_G0345_v285.md#statement) hold for these actual integral inputs.
They yield no positive valuation contribution at the places in (3).

## All fixed polynomial congruences and their degree cost

In the ring \(R_i=(\mathbb Z/A_i\mathbb Z)[Z]/(G_i)\), the class of \(Z\) is a unit since the constant coefficient of \(G_i\) is a unit.
The ring has \(A_i^{g_i}\) elements, so \(e_i\) exists and is at most \(A_i^{g_i}\).
If \(A_i e_i\mid12h\), partition the geometric sum in (1) into blocks of length \(e_i\).
The blocks have the same value, and their number is divisible by \(A_i\).
Their total is zero in \(R_i\), proving (5) and the bound on \(H_0\).
For \(A_i=1\) or \(G_i=1\) the condition is vacuous and may simply be omitted.
Enumerate the countably many allowable pairs \((A_i,G_i)\), and let \(H_r\) be the least common multiple of the corresponding divisibility conditions for the first \(r\).
The choice \(h_r=r!H_r\) increases to infinity and satisfies every fixed condition eventually.
This proves the diagonal assertion.

The reciprocal identity also supplies a necessary period for exact matching.
If a real rational reciprocal polynomial \(P\) of degree \(d\) has \(P(\zeta_m)=1\), then conjugation gives \(P(\zeta_m^{-1})=1\), and reciprocity forces \(\zeta_m^d=1\).
Hence \(m\mid d\).
For \(J\ge10\), demanding these values at every order \(2\le m\le J\) except five forces
\[
                            \operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,J)\mid d,
\]
since order ten already supplies the factor five.
The present construction, with \(h=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,J)\), realizes all these exact values at degree \(60\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,J)\).
Thus its degree is within a fixed factor of this necessary cost.

There is a corresponding period for the local polynomial tests.
If the ideal \((G_i)\) in \((\mathbb Z/A_i\mathbb Z)[Z,Z^{-1}]\) is invariant under \(Z\mapsto Z^{-1}\), as for a self-reciprocal \(G_i\) up to a unit, then \(P\equiv1\) and reciprocity imply \(Z^d=1\) in \(R_i\).
The order of \(Z\) in \(R_i^\times\) must therefore divide \(d\).
Orders incompatible with the input degree prevent these exact values, while a nontrivial unit value can still have zero local valuation.
The coordinator supplied the finite-ring extension, its degree cost, the reciprocal period obstruction and the least-common-multiple choice of windows.
