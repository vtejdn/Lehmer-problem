# Geometric monodromy and real branch values of the two-pole curve

<a id="statement"></a>
## The parameter of a translated section

On the smooth projective model \(C\) of the [two-pole curve](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#statement), let \(T=V^k/U^l\) for coprime positive integers \(k,l\).
There are constants \(s_0,c>0\), depending only on the curve, such that for \(l/k>s_0\) the cover \(T:C\to\mathbb P^1\) has degree \(n=2l+4k\) and geometric monodromy \(S_n\).
It has exactly sixteen branch values in \(\mathbb C^\times\), all real, with distinct absolute values and \(\big|\log|T|\big|>cl\).
Each has a single ramified point of index two.
A turn around the unit circle in the parameter plane has cycle lengths \(l,l,k,k,k,k\).
The fiber at \(T=1\) parametrizes the ordinary section \(F(X^k,X^l)=0\) by Bézout, and simultaneous inversion of \(U,V\) acts on this fiber since it sends \(T\) to \(T^{-1}\).
The theorem leaves rational irreducibility of that section open.

<a id="critical"></a>
## Real critical points at small positive slope

Write \(r=k/l\), \(t=U-U^{-1}\), \(a=(t-3)(t-4)\) and \(b=(t+3)(t+4)\), so the torus equation is \(aV^2+V+b=0\).
The [projective intersection calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v808.md#critical) gives geometric irreducibility and exactly sixteen critical intersections with multiplicity in the torus for \(kl\ne0\).
At a point where \(U=f(V)\), the logarithmic derivative of \(T\) vanishes exactly when \(Vf'(V)/f(V)=r\).
At \(r=0\) there are eight torus solutions coming from the discriminant \(1-4ab=-4t^4+100t^2-575\).
Indeed its roots have \(t^2=(25\pm5\sqrt2)/2\), and each of the four distinct nonzero real \(t\) has two distinct real preimages under \(U-U^{-1}\).
Both coefficients \(a,b\) are nonzero there and the quadratic in \(V\) has a double real root \(v_0=-1/(2a)\).
The discriminant has a simple zero as a function of \(U\), so \(F_U\ne0\) and the local graph \(U=f(V)\) has \(f'(v_0)=0\), \(f''(v_0)\ne0\).
Consequently the slope function has nonzero derivative there.

At \(V=0\) the four roots of \(b=0\) in \(U\) are real and simple, as are the four roots of \(a=0\) at \(V=\infty\).
In the coordinates \(w=V\) and \(w=1/V\), respectively, write \(U=f(w)\) with \(f(0)=u_0\).
The coefficient of \(w\) in the affine equation is one in both charts, whence \(f'(0)\ne0\).
The critical equation becomes \(r=\epsilon wf'(w)/f(w)\), with \(\epsilon=1\) at zero and \(-1\) at infinity, and its right-hand side has a simple zero.
The real inverse function theorem now gives sixteen distinct real analytic branches for small \(r>0\), all in the torus and all simple zeros of \(dT\) since the same slope equations give its logarithmic derivative up to nonvanishing factors.
These exhaust the intersection count.
The projective boundary has four points with \(V=0\), four with \(V=\infty\), and two each with \(U=0,\infty\) where the other coordinate is \(V=\pm i\).
The relevant edge polynomial has simple roots everywhere, so the vanishing coordinate or reciprocal is a local parameter.
Thus the zero and pole divisors of \(T\) each have multiplicities \(l,l,k,k,k,k\), giving degree \(n\).

<a id="separation"></a>
## Separation of branch values

The limiting \(U\)-coordinates \(u_0\) are real, nonzero and different from \(\pm1\) since every limiting \(t\) is nonzero.
The boundary has \(t=\pm3,\pm4\), where the interior discriminant equals one.
In each locus the only distinct limits of equal modulus are the sign partners \(u_0,-u_0\), corresponding under \((U,V)\mapsto(-U,1/V)\) since this changes \(t\) to \(-t\) and interchanges \(a,b\).
At an interior point \(V=v_0+O(r)\) and \(U=u_0+O(r^2)\) by the criticality of the graph, so
\[
 \log|T|=-l\log|u_0|+k\log|v_0|+O(kr).
\]
The sign partners have inverse limiting \(V\)-coordinates and \(|v_0|\ne1\), since a real double root at \(\pm1\) would give \(a=b\) by the product of the roots, hence \(t=0\).
The two corrections therefore differ by \(2k\log|v_0|+O(kr)\).
At a boundary point \(w=c_0r+O(r^2)\) with \(c_0\ne0\) and \(U=u_0+O(r)\), giving
\[
 \log|T|=-l\log|u_0|+\epsilon k\log r+O(k).
\]
Each sign pair has opposite \(\epsilon\), so these corrections also separate the values.
For distinct leading moduli both corrections are \(o(l)\) uniformly as \(r\to0^+\), since \(r|\log r|\to0\) and there are finitely many branches.
Taking \(c\) smaller than \(\min_{u_0}|\log|u_0||\) gives the asserted annulus and all sixteen distinct absolute values after increasing \(s_0\).

<a id="group"></a>
## Permutations of the sheets

The geometric monodromy group \(G\) is transitive because the curve is geometrically irreducible.
A small positive loop around each nonzero finite branch value acts by a transposition because precisely one simple critical point lies above it.
Form the graph on the sheets whose edges are the pairs exchanged by all \(G\)-conjugates of these transpositions.
The edge transpositions generate the full symmetric group on each connected component, since along any path successive conjugations give the transposition of its endpoints.
The components are permuted transitively by \(G\).
In this action every finite nonzero branch loop is trivial, so the action factors through the fundamental group of the twice-punctured sphere, generated by a loop about zero.
On \(m\) components the loop must therefore act by a single \(m\)-cycle.
Every cycle length of the same loop on the original sheets is divisible by \(m\), because returning to a sheet returns to its component.
These lengths include \(k,l\) by the zero divisor, so coprimality gives \(m=1\) and the edge transpositions generate \(S_n\).

<a id="circle"></a>
## The permutation over the unit circle

On \(|U|=1\) the [two phase graphs](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#circle) have the form \(U=e^{i\theta}, V=e^{i\phi_\pm(\theta)}\), with smooth periodic real phases.
On each graph the argument of \(T\) is \(k\phi_\pm(\theta)-l\theta\), strictly decreasing for large \(l/k\) and decreasing by \(2\pi l\) per turn.
Each graph thus contributes one cycle of length \(l\).
The two boundary points with \(V=\infty\) and \(|u_0|>1\) have \(u_0=(j+\sqrt{j^2+4})/2\) for \(j=3,4\).
Near each use the [holomorphic polar solution](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#lifts) \(u(h)\) of \(A(u)+u^2h u^{-l/k}+A^*(u)h^2u^{-2l/k}=0\), for \(|h|\le1\), with the indicated local logarithm.
Taking \(U=u(h)\) and \(V^{-1}=h u(h)^{-l/k}\) gives \(T=h^{-k}\).
For \(|h|=1\) the lift is injective since its coordinates recover \(h=V^{-1}U^{l/k}\) using that logarithm.
Each point thus supplies one \(k\)-cycle, and simultaneous inversion supplies two further disjoint \(k\)-cycles with \(|U|<1\).
These six circles account for all \(2l+4k\) sheets.
