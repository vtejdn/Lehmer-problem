## Statement

### A. Original complete cyclotomic input and canonical conventions

Let T be ANY monic integer polynomial of degree d such that the COMPLETE reciprocal polynomial

    P_T(z)=z^d T(z+z^(-1))

is a product of cyclotomic polynomials, retaining every actual factor and multiplicity.
There is no irreducibility or nontorsion assumption.
At this SAME original divisor define, using actual integer divisions and monic remainders,

    C_2=X^2-2, C_3=X^3-3X, C_5=X^5-5X^3+5X,
    a=rem_T(T(C_2))/2,
    b=rem_T(T(C_3))/3, c=rem_T(T(C_5))/5,
    F=rem_T(2c^2-3a^5), G=rem_T(c^3-2b^5),
    X_epsilon=rem_T(b^2-a^3+epsilon G),
    epsilon in{1,-1}.

Assume coefficientwise F=0 modulo4 and X_epsilon=0 modulo2 for one, hence either, sign.
For odd j>1 let the full integer primitive trace factor F_j be defined by

    z^(varphi(j)/2)F_j(z+z^(-1))=Phi_j(z),
    f_j=F_jbar in F_2[X].

Indexed F_j is distinct from the canonical residual F.
The complete binary factorization is

    U=Tbar=X^A product_(j>1 odd) f_j^(E_j).

Each f_j is squarefree, possibly reducible over F_2, and its full exponent E_j is uniform on all its irreducible components.
All different f_j and X are pairwise coprime.
Let delta=deg rad(U), where rad(U) is the product of ALL distinct monic binary irreducible factors.
For T=1 the zero quotient satisfies all vanishing conclusions trivially.

### B. Exact normalized obstruction at every nonzero G component

#### Square structure forces both parity restrictions

#### The complete derivative forces a high-order zero at the lower odd order

Equivalently, J'=0 would make J a polynomial square, contradicting its nonconstant squarefreeness.
The even exponents E_* and B_* show that J is prime to both complete f_h and f_k.

At a primitive h trace x with C_5bar(x)=y a primitive k trace, unramified substitution preserves the local order of J' at y.
The order of the right side of FD10 is therefore

    3B_*+ord_y J'.

It must be at least E_*, so ord_y J'>=g.
Every primitive k trace arises from a primitive h trace under C_5: a primitive exponent modulo k can be lifted modulo5k while staying prime to5, and it is already prime to every other prime divisor.
If5|k the prime-to5 condition is automatic; if5 does not divide k, four of the five residue lifts satisfy it.

Thus the inequality holds at EVERY root of the full squarefree f_k, even when f_k splits over F_2, and

    f_k^g divides J'.                           (FD11)

The full other-order factors of U_* remain inside S and J throughout this argument; their derivative contributions are not treated as independent freely chosen local units.
