## 1. Actual pair orbits, ratio collisions and full norms

Let alpha_1,...,alpha_d be ALL distinct roots of the actual irreducible unit P, let L be its normal closure and G=Gal(L/Q), and fix a rational prime p.
For ordered labels(i,j), put rho_(i,j)=alpha_i^p/alpha_j.
Every ratio is an algebraic integer unit.
It is also nontorsion: if rho_(i,j)^M=1, choose an actual g in G carrying alpha_i to alpha_j.
Then g(alpha_i^M)=alpha_i^(pM), and iteration through the finite order of g forces alpha_i to be torsion.
This is the same global nonvanishing mechanism already retained in C146; it is not merely a nonzero check in a short cyclic window.

Partition the ordered pair set into its actual G-orbits O.
For a representative(i,j), set

    F_O=Q(alpha_i,alpha_j), M_O=[F_O:Q]=|O|,
    E_O=Q(rho_(i,j)), D_O=[E_O:Q], mu_O=M_O/D_O.        (1)

The stabilizer of the ordered pair fixes exactly F_O, so the orbit degree equality is exact.
The inclusion E_O subset F_O proves that mu_O is a positive integer.
The map from O to distinct conjugates of its ratio has every fiber of size mu_O.

If Q_O is the minimal polynomial of rho_(i,j), then

    product_((i,j) in O)(T-rho_(i,j))=Q_O(T)^(mu_O),

           =Norm_(E_O/Q)(rho_(i,j)^n-1)^(mu_O)         (2)

for every integer n>=1.

There is no nonintegral coefficient orbit product hidden in(2).

Each coordinate projection O-> {1,...,d} is equivariant and onto by transitivity of G on roots.

    sum_O s_O=d,
