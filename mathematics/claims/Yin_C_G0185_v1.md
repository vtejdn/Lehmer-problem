# Actual nontorsion dyadic units outside every fixed radical field

Human author Yin.
Root authored the unit-generation argument and its arithmetic input consequence below on 2026-10-20T10:49.
It is a new consequence of the accepted field-level noncontainment, with its own proof and independent-review obligation.
No unit algorithm, field enumeration or numerical computation is performed.

## Statement

Let B_infinity be the compositum of all finite Galois extensions of Q of2-power degree unramified at every odd finite rational prime.
For every fixed number field K and every fixed finitely generated subgroup Gamma of the nonzero algebraic numbers, there exist a finite Galois2-power extension B/Q unramified at every odd finite prime and a nontorsion algebraic integer unit alpha in B such that

    alpha is not in K(mu,Gamma^div).                    (U1)

Here mu contains all roots of unity and Gamma^div allows all positive division orders.
Both K and Gamma are fixed before B and alpha are selected.
Every such alpha nevertheless satisfies the accepted bound

    m(alpha)>=log2/12,                                  (U2)

and belongs to the broader C181 family obtained by adjoining any finite abelian extension unramified at2.
This is an actual original-unit noncontainment statement, not just a field-level difference or an abstract group model.
It supplies no small-measure sequence, numerical extremizer or global literature-priority claim.

## Complete argument

### Unit generation in a field containing i

Let L be any number field containing i, and let E=Q(O_L^times) be the subfield generated by all its algebraic integer units.
As a subfield of the finite extension L/Q, E is a number field.
The element i and its inverse are integral units, so i belongs to E.
Both E and L therefore have no real embeddings.
The unit groups agree exactly:

    O_E^times=O_L^times.                               (U3)

Indeed every L-unit belongs to E by definition, and it and its inverse are integral over Z, so it is an E-unit.
Conversely every E-unit and its inverse remain integral in L.
The accepted all-signature Dirichlet theorem [Con26-U002](../audits/literature/Con26/Yin_U002.md#con26-u002) gives the free rank r1+r2-1, with r2 counting complex-conjugate pairs and finite root-of-unity kernel.
Thus the two equal groups in(U3) have ranks [E:Q]/2-1 and [L:Q]/2-1.
Equality of ranks forces [E:Q]=[L:Q], hence E=L.
This also covers degree2, where both ranks can be zero: no positive-rank assumption is inserted.
Consequently every number field containing i is generated as a field over Q by its actual integral units.
The proof needs the complex-place convention of U002, not merely U001's totally real scope.

### From actual field noncontainment to an original unit

Fix arbitrary K,Gamma in(U1), and put R=K(mu,Gamma^div).
Assume for contradiction that every integral unit in every finite allowed B lies in R.
For each such B, its compositum L=BQ(i) is again finite Galois of2-power degree and unramified at odd finite primes.
The field Q(i) has this ramification property, and the unramified-compositum interface is the exact accepted one used in [C181](Yin_C_G0181_v1.md#unramified-lifting-source-interface).
The preceding unit-generation argument gives L=Q(O_L^times).
The assumed containment of all those units in R therefore gives B contained in L contained in R.
Taking the directed union over all actual finite B would give B_infinity contained in R, contradicting [C183](Yin_C_G0183_v1.md#statement) at these same fixed K,Gamma.
Thus an actual unit alpha outside R exists in one actual finite allowed B.
Since every root of unity is already in mu and hence in R, this alpha is nontorsion.
All integrality and inverse integrality are those of its original field; no abstract multiplicative element is substituted for a unit.
The bound(U2) is exactly [C181](Yin_C_G0181_v1.md#statement) with A=Q, applied to this original alpha; its degree need not equal [B:Q].
No normal-closure degree is inserted into its total measure.

## Exact input and comparison boundaries

The [all-signature unit source](../audits/literature/Con26/Yin_U002.md#con26-u002) has its actual retained edition, inspected lattice proof and classical-foundation limits.
Only its rank and finite-kernel consequence is used here; no effective heights, generators or regulator bounds are assumed.
The actual dyadic field noncontainment is independently accepted in [C183](Yin_C_G0183_v1.md#decision-whole-reviewed-2026-10-20) through [R455](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0455_v1.md#dyadic-fixed-radical-noncontainment-whole-finding).
The automatic original-measure theorem is independently accepted in [C181](Yin_C_G0181_v1.md#decision-whole-reviewed-2026-10-20) through its whole R600 and actual source-declaration readback.
These accepted results are reused, not independently re-reviewed by their contributing author.

For each fixed K,Gamma this result provides original nontorsion units outside that fixed radical field, not merely a larger field containing possibly no new units.
Changing K or Gamma after choosing alpha does not give a uniform fixed-parameter theorem for the whole C181 class.
The result does not refute the fixed-radical height theorem or exclude every more general infinite-base theorem in its source.
The B-only units also satisfy the old original-residue criterion at2; the proof does not claim these individual witnesses escape every old gap criterion.
The new broad automatic class includes arbitrary abelian A unramified at2, whose original residue degrees need not remain bounded, as C181's separate exact controls show.
Combining these distinct coverage boundaries is a scoped comparison of whole classes, not a claim that one exhibited unit simultaneously defeats every earlier sufficient condition.
