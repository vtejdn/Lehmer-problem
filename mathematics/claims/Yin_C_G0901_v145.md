# Recovery at unbounded powers or conductors and the exact small spectrum of the comparison fields

<a id="statement"></a>
## What the comparison radicals recover

Write \(T=\mathbb Q^{\rm tr}\), \(V=\mathbb Q^{\rm sol}\).
Fix a genuine real Salem number \(\alpha>1\), put \(t=\alpha+\alpha^{-1}\), \(F=\mathbb Q(t)\), \(n=[F:\mathbb Q]\), and mark the real embedding where \(t>2\).
Set \(t_h=\alpha^h+\alpha^{-h}\), and let \(h\) run through the positive integers of any fixed infinite arithmetic progression.
Fix any totally real algebraic number \(\lambda\) all of whose conjugates belong to \((-2,2)\), put \(L=F(\lambda)\), \(k=[L:F]\), and form
\[
                        D=T(\sqrt{t_h-\lambda}:h).
\]
Then \([D:T]=2^{(n-1)k}\).
Its quadratic characters are precisely the square classes in \(T^\times/T^{\times2}\) coming from elements of \(L^\times\) positive at all embeddings above the marked place of \(F\).
Here the character space is identified with its Kummer radicands.
The positive real Salem numbers in \(D\) are **exactly** those whose marked embedded trace field is contained in \(F\).
In particular even the single threshold \(\lambda=0\), the trace of a primitive fourth root of unity, recovers the original source without a power cutoff.
At this threshold the individual exponents with
\[
                    T(\sqrt{t_h})=T(\alpha)
\]
have relative natural density \(2^{1-n}\) in the chosen progression.

There is an alternative at the original power \(h=1\).
For any finite lower bound on the conductors, adjoining the comparison roots belonging only to cyclotomic traces of exact conductors **above** that bound already recovers \(\alpha\) over \(T\).
This assertion uses all such conductors, which may be composite.
The [one-prime criterion](#conductors) below is an exact test at an individual odd prime satisfying its disjointness and parity hypotheses.

For an arbitrary source family as in the [threshold gap theorem](Yin_C_G0901_v137.md#statement), the small positive Mahler spectrum after either recovery is exactly the union of the positive integer multiples of the primitive source measures in that range.
The cutoff is uniform over families at fixed source measure bound.
The more general [intermediate-field formula](#spectrum) identifies precisely which progressions enter if only some original sources are recovered.
Thus removal of either threshold can restore a shrinking family if the eligible Salem sources themselves shrink; the existence of such sources is not asserted.

<a id="signs"></a>
## Kummer signatures in the field of one trace and one comparison point

For nonzero totally real elements the quotient of two elements is square in \(T\) exactly when it is positive at every embedding: all conjugates of either square root of a totally positive element are real.
Thus the image of \(L^\times\) in the square-class space over \(T\) is its ordinary signature space.
Every sign assignment occurs by weak approximation, or by the density of rational linear combinations of a rational basis in the Minkowski embedding.

Write \(x_i=e^{i\theta_i}\), \(0<\theta_i<\pi\), for one root over each unmarked trace.
The \(x_i\) are multiplicatively independent modulo torsion, because sending one of them to \(\alpha\) by a rational automorphism sends every other one in this list to a neutral conjugate.
Thus the phase vectors \((h\theta_i\bmod 2\pi)_i\) are uniformly distributed with independent circle coordinates, also along any fixed infinite progression: the average of every nonconstant torus character tends to zero by a geometric sum. [Neutral phase independence](Yin_C_G0901_v127.md#signed)
All unmarked traces of \(t_h\) therefore jointly sample the product arcsine law on \([-2,2]^{n-1}\), whose marginals give every open subinterval positive probability and give no point positive mass.

Over a fixed unmarked embedding of \(F\), the \(k\) extensions to \(L\) give \(k\) distinct comparison values in \((-2,2)\), since the image of \(\lambda\) distinguishes these extensions.
When the sampled trace decreases past these ordered cuts, the negative-sign vector on this fiber visits the empty prefix and every successive prefix through the whole fiber.
Every simultaneous choice of prefixes on the \(n-1\) fibers occurs, using open intervals and the joint distribution.
At the marked embedding \(t_h>2\), so no negative entry occurs above it.
Differences of adjacent prefixes on one fiber, with the other fibers fixed, span every individual unmarked coordinate over \(\mathbb F_2\).
The radicands are nonzero because \(t_h>2\) in the marked embedding.
This proves the square-class and degree formulas by elementary multiquadratic theory.

If \(\beta>1\) is a real Salem number, write \(v=\beta+\beta^{-1}\), \(B=\mathbb Q(v)\) and \(d=v^2-4\).
Membership in \(D\) is equivalent to membership of the square class of \(d\) in the indicated Kummer space.
If it belongs, \(d=c s^2\) for \(s\in T^\times\) and \(c\in L^\times\) positive above the marked place.
Every rational absolute automorphism fixing \(F\) sends \(c\) to a positive value, so it sends \(d\) to a positive value.
This forces it to fix \(v\), since only its distinguished conjugate lies outside \([-2,2]\).
Hence \(B\subseteq F\).
Conversely if \(B\subseteq F\), then \(d\in L\) is positive at every embedding over the marked place, proving membership.
At \(\lambda=0\) the class of \(t_h\) equals the nontrivial class of \(t^2-4\) exactly when every unmarked trace is negative.
Each circle coordinate has probability one half of negative cosine, giving the displayed density with no claim on its first hitting time.

## Recovery without powering the source

Write \(\Psi_d(X)\) for the monic minimum of a primitive \(d\)-th root of unity plus its inverse, \(d\ge3\).
For any odd \(j>1\) put
\[
                     U_j(t)=\prod_{d\mid j,\ d>1}\Psi_d(t).
\]
The reciprocal cyclotomic identities give \(U_j(t)=\alpha^{-(j-1)/2}(\alpha^j-1)/(\alpha-1)>0\).
At a neutral trace \(2\cos\theta_i\) its value is \(\sin(j\theta_i/2)/\sin(\theta_i/2)\).
The half phases are jointly uniformly distributed along any fixed progression by the same character argument, since squaring takes any putative torsion relation among their exponentials to one among the original neutral roots.
Thus the exponents with every unmarked sine negative have relative density \(2^{1-n}\) in any fixed progression of odd positive integers.
On these exponents \([U_j(t)]=[t^2-4]\) in \(T^\times/T^{\times2}\).
Given any lower bound \(B\ge2\) on all conductors to be used, choose \(j\equiv1\pmod{2\lceil B\rceil!}\).
Every divisor \(d>1\) of \(j\) exceeds \(B\).
The square root of \(U_j(t)\) is, up to sign, a product of comparison square roots for the primitive traces of just these conductors.
Consequently the field formed with all conductors above \(B\) contains \(\alpha\), with no change of the trace \(t\).

For an individual prime \(p\equiv3\bmod4\) put \(C_p=\mathbb Q(2\cos(2\pi/p))\), and assume \([FC_p:F]=(p-1)/2\).
With \(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{(p-1)/2}\) the primitive trace conjugates, there is an exact test
\[
   \alpha\in T(\sqrt{t-\lambda_j}:1\le j\le(p-1)/2)
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
      \sin(p\theta_i/2)<0\ \text{for every unmarked }i.                  \tag{1}
\]
If membership holds, write \([t^2-4]\) as a sum of comparison classes.
Lift each member of \(\operatorname{Gal}(FC_p/F)\) to a rational absolute automorphism, apply it to this equality over the normal field \(T\) and sum in characteristic two.
The left side stays the same because the group order is odd.
Each selected comparison class contributes the whole column sum, since this group acts regularly on the primitive traces.
As the left class is nonzero, one obtains \([t^2-4]=[\Psi_p(t)]\).
The sign rule just proved gives the right side of (1).
Conversely that sign pattern gives the same class and the product of the radicals supplies \(\alpha\).
The progression density above is for integer exponents, with no use of a distribution theorem along primes.

## What remains small in the enlarged comparison fields

Use the [unmatched-source hypotheses and notation](Yin_C_G0901_v137.md#statement): the sources are pairwise unmatched, have sufficiently large degrees at bounded \(\log\alpha\le M\), and their canonical block permutation fields are solvable.
Let \(K\) be \(V\) times the compositum of their full rational splitting fields, and form the rationally normal field
\[
                    U_*=TK(\sqrt c:\ c\in(K\cap T)^\times).
\]
For any rationally normal \(U\subseteq U_*\) and all sufficiently small positive measures in terms of \(M\) alone, one has the equality of spectra
\[
  \{m(w):w\in U,\ 0<m(w)<\varepsilon_M\}
    =(0,\varepsilon_M)\ \cap\!
      \bigcup_{\alpha\ {\rm a\ source},\ \alpha\in U}
                m(\sigma_\alpha)\mathbb Z_{>0},                          \tag{2}
\]
where \(m\) denotes total logarithmic measure and \(\sigma_\alpha>1\) is the primitive positive Salem generator in its marked real field.
The fields formed over \(TV\) by adjoining the comparison radicals for all conjugate rows at the single threshold zero and unbounded powers in the given progression, or at power one with all conductors above any prescribed finite lower bound for each source, are among these \(U\).
Both contain every source by the preceding arguments, so in both fields every source progression enters (2).

Here are the details needed to apply the [bounded-degree exclusion for unmatched units](Yin_C_G0901_v137.md#gap) in this larger field.
First \(TK/T\) is prosolvable: \(TV/T\) is obtained from solvable finite extensions, and the splitting field of a Salem number over \(T\) is generated by square roots of its totally real trace discriminants.
Adding square roots of elements of \(K\cap T\) likewise keeps \(U_*\subset T^{\rm sol}\).
Second, if \(d\in T^\times\) is a square in \(U_*\), its class over \(T\) has a representative in \(K\cap T\).
Indeed multiquadratic theory first removes a finite product of the displayed radicands, leaving a square in \(TK\).
Its square root belongs to \(TL_0\) for a finite rational Galois \(L_0\subset K\).
As in the [quadratic restriction argument](Yin_C_G0901_v137.md#signs), the quadratic subfield over \(T\), if nontrivial, descends to a quadratic extension of \(L_0\cap T\) inside \(L_0\).
Writing that extension with a radicand in \(L_0\cap T\) gives the asserted representative.

For \(0<m(w)<\log2\), the two extreme coefficients make \(w\) a unit.
Its torsion-class product \(y\) lies in \(U\), is compressed and has the same positive measure.
The degree of such a representative tends uniformly to infinity as the measure tends to zero, by coefficient finiteness.
At these degrees its label field is totally real by the [solvable closure criterion over \(T\)](Yin_C_G0901_v108.md#statement); it is reciprocal by Smyth's bound, after decreasing \(\varepsilon_M\).
If \(y\) were unmatched, for every trace conjugate \(v_i\) its discriminant \(v_i^2-4\) would have a square root in the normal field \(U\), and its signs would be invariant under every automorphism fixing \(K\) by the preceding square-class descent.
The [supported alternating actions over all source fields](Yin_C_G0901_v137.md#signs) would make the nonempty collection of its exterior inverse-pair labels a union of whole blocks.
Indeed total reality of the label field makes the exterior roots real, and these labels are exactly those of positive discriminant.
The resulting real-conjugate proportion is at least one divided by the number of blocks.
This contradicts the [bounded-measure real-conjugate estimate in this situation](Yin_C_G0901_v137.md#gap) at unbounded degree, uniformly over all source families.

A match can be transported to a real exterior conjugate of \(y\), where the matched source conjugate is either \(\alpha\) or \(\alpha^{-1}\).
The torsion in the resulting real power equality is a rational sign.
Positive powers have full degree after compression on both sides, so the two root fields coincide at those embeddings and \(\alpha\in U\).
In its root field a positive Salem number is a power of the unique primitive norm-one unit; the saturated multiplicative line meets the units there just in its signed integral powers. [Primitive Salem line](Yin_C_G0901_v44.md#units)
For clarity, the relative norm of the matched unit on this line is torsion.
At any nonsplit place its value is a positive squared modulus and hence one; injectivity forces relative norm one.
Thus \(m(w)=m(y)\) is a positive integral multiple of \(m(\sigma_\alpha)\).
Conversely \(\alpha\in U\) puts its whole root field and its primitive generator in \(U\); every positive integral multiple in (2) is realized by the corresponding power.
This proves the claimed equivalence without supplying new shrinking Salem sources.
