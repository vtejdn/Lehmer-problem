# Independent inversions for every prime in the five-term Salem family

<a id="statement"></a>
## The sign kernel at every prime index

For every prime \(q\ge5\), let \(P_q=X^{2q}-2X^{2q-1}+X^q-2X+1=X^qW_q(X+X^{-1})\).
Its trace polynomial \(W_q\) is separable modulo five.
Consequently all \(2^q\) choices of inversion of its reciprocal root pairs occur over the splitting field of \(W_q\).
In particular the full signed symmetric group occurs for every sufficiently large prime \(q\equiv5,7\bmod8\), by the [binary trace calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v272.md#trace).

<a id="argument"></a>
## Elimination in characteristic five

Set \(F=(x-2)y^2+xy-2x^2+x\), so \(F(x,x^q)=xP_q(x)\).
For \(a=q\bmod5\) set \(D=xF_x+ayF_y\).
A repeated nonzero root \(x\) of \(P_q\bmod5\) gives \(F(x,x^q)=D(x,x^q)=0\).
Up to a nonzero scalar, the following are the resultants of \(F,D\) in \(y\) over \(\mathbb F_5[x]\).

| \(a\) | Resultant up to scalar |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | \(x^2(x-2)(x+1)^2(x+2)\) |
| 1,4 | \(x^2(x+1)^2(x^2+x+1)\) |
| 2 | \(x^2(x-1)^2(x+1)^2\) |
| 3 | \(x^3(x+1)^2\) |

The entries follow by substituting the coefficients of \(F,D\) into the quadratic resultant \((AF_0-CD_2)^2-(AE-BD_2)(BF_0-CE)\), for polynomials \(Ay^2+By+C,D_2y^2+Ey+F_0\).
Here the second leading coefficient \(x+2a(x-2)\) is never identically zero modulo five.
At \(x=1\) the value of \(P_q\) is \(-1\).
At a primitive cube root the trace is \(-1\), and \(W_q(-1)=-2C_{q-1}(-1)\) is either \(2\) or \(-4\), where \(C_j(x+x^{-1})=x^j+x^{-j}\).
The row \(a=0\) occurs only at \(q=5\), and at \(x=\pm2\) the trace is zero with \(W_5(0)=1-2C_4(0)=-3\).
Thus \(-1\) is the only possible repeated root of the parent.
A multiple trace root would lift to a repeated parent root, necessarily over the trace \(-2\).
But \(W_q'(-2)=q^2+2(q-1)^2\ne0\bmod5\), since the quadratic \(3q^2-4q+2\) has nonsquare discriminant \(-8\bmod5\).
This proves separability.
The [Salem sign-kernel argument](Yin_C_G0345_v272.md#flips) now applies at every \(q\): the trace splitting field is unramified at five, while the full splitting field contains \(\sqrt5\) from \(W_q(2)W_q(-2)=5\).
Even sign changes and an odd one therefore generate the full kernel.
