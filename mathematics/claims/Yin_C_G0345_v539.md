# Small disks in every Galois block of a compressed reciprocal seed

<a id="statement"></a>
## Disk occupancy and simultaneous prescriptions

Let a monic separable integral polynomial of degree \(d=un\) have a Galois-invariant partition of its roots into \(u\) sets of size \(n\ge2\), and let \(m\) be its logarithmic Mahler measure.
For every one of these sets \(B\), every \(|z|=1\) and every \(0<r\le1\), its fraction of roots in the open disk of radius \(r\) about \(z\) is at least
\[
 {r\over4\pi}-{1\over nr}-\sqrt{\frac{2m+u(\log n+4)}{2n}}.                 \tag{1}
\]
In particular an empty disk forces
\[
 m\ \ge\ n\left({r\over4\pi}-{1\over nr}\right)_+^2-\tfrac12u(\log n+4).
\]

Now take a compressed reciprocal nontorsion unit of degree \(d\) and logarithmic Mahler measure \(m\le M\), for fixed \(M\).
At sufficiently large degree take the [minimal blocks](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement) of its inverse-pair labels, with \(b\) labels per block, \(u\) blocks and \(n=2b\).
Suppose \(0<r\le1\) depends on the seed and \(r/\sqrt{(2M+u(\log n+4))/n}\to\infty\) as \(d\to\infty\).
This includes every \(r=d^{-1/2+\epsilon}\) with fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), by the polylogarithmic bound on \(u\).
In each block prescribe up to \(\lfloor br/(8\pi)\rfloor\) distinct source labels and attach an arbitrary unit-circle target to each.
There is a single splitting-field automorphism preserving all blocks and taking each listed inverse pair to a pair containing a root within distance \(r\) of its own target.
In particular its trace lies within \(3r\) of the target trace when \(r\le1/2\).
The estimate excludes persistent holes in even one minimal block of any growing-degree bounded-measure sequence, while leaving open the unrestricted positive gap.

<a id="energy"></a>
## The product of the discriminants

Let \(P_B\) be the monic product over the roots of \(B\), \(m_B=\log M(P_B)\), and \(\Delta_B\) its polynomial discriminant.
The product of all \(\Delta_B\) is a nonzero rational integer because it is a Galois-invariant algebraic integer and the original roots are simple.
The [Dirichlet discrepancy inequality](../audits/literature/Pri11/Yin_U002.md#pri11-u002) for the complex polynomial \(P_B\) with smoothing radius \(1/n\) has radicand
\[
 H_B=2m_B/n-n^{-2}\log|\Delta_B|+(\log n+4)/n.
\]
Each radicand is nonnegative by the source energy bound.
Since \(\sum_B m_B=m\) and \(\sum_B\log|\Delta_B|\ge0\), each \(H_B\le\sum_C H_C\le(2m+u(\log n+4))/n\).
Use the compact test \(\phi(w)=(1-|w-z|/r)_+\), of Lipschitz constant \(1/r\) and planar Dirichlet integral \(\pi\).
Its Haar mean on the circle is at least \(r/(4\pi)\), since \(ze^{it}\) for \(|t|\le r/2\) has test value at least one half.
Its mean on the roots of \(B\) is bounded above by their open-disk fraction.
Substitution proves (1), and setting the fraction to zero gives the asserted obstruction.

<a id="matching"></a>
## Lifting simultaneous assignments

Take the inverse images of the label blocks in the set of conjugate roots.
This gives a Galois-invariant partition with exactly two roots per label.
Under the stipulated scale both error terms of (1) are \(o(r)\), uniformly in the block and target.
Thus every disk contains at least \(nr/(8\pi)\) roots from that block at large degree, belonging to at least \(br/(8\pi)\) labels.
In each block greedily match the prescribed labels to unused labels from their respective disks.
Every partial injection extends to a permutation of the block, and swapping two unused images if necessary makes it even, since at least two labels remain unused at large degree.
The independent alternating groups act on the respective blocks, so their product lifts to the required splitting-field automorphism.
If \(|w-z|<r\le1/2\) and \(|z|=1\), then \(|w^{-1}-z^{-1}|<2r\), giving the trace bound for either orientation of the pair.
