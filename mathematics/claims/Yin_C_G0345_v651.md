# Translation restrictions on quotients at the unrestricted energy bound

<a id="statement"></a>
## Near equality and signed translations

Let \(P\) be a monic integral polynomial of degree \(d\ge1\) with \(|P(0)|=1\) and no zero on the unit circle.
Let \(A\) be an integral Laurent polynomial indivisible by \(P\), put \(m=\log M(P)>0\), and use Haar probability measure for all circle norms.
Suppose along a sequence that
\[
 m\longrightarrow0,\qquad m\|A/P\|_2^2\longrightarrow
 K=\tfrac52+\log 2-2\sqrt2 .
\]
Take any [balanced division](Yin_C_G0345_v638.md#division) \(F=X^{-j}A/P=Q+R/P\), with \(0\ne R\in\mathbb Z[X]\) of degree less than \(d\).
For a numerator of width less than \(d\), translate it into the short window and take \(Q=0\).
Write \(S\) for multiplication by \(X\) on circle \(L^2\).

For every choice of positive integer lags \(g\) with \(mg\to0\), and signs \(\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}\), one has
\[
 \liminf m\|(S^g-\epsilon)Q\|_2^2\ \ge\ K,\qquad
 \limsup m|\langle S^gQ,Q\rangle|\ \le\ \log 2-K/2.                 \tag{1}
\]
There is also a restriction at arbitrary lags.
For any fixed \(s\ge1\), the quotient cannot admit approximations
\[
 \sqrt m\left\|Q-\sum_{\ell=1}^{s}q_\ell\right\|_2\to0,\qquad
 \sup\sqrt m\|q_\ell\|_2<\infty,\qquad
 \sqrt m\|(S^{g_\ell}-\epsilon_\ell)q_\ell\|_2\to0                  \tag{2}
\]
with \(q_\ell\in L^2\), even if the positive integer lags \(g_\ell\) and signs \(\epsilon_\ell=\pm1\) vary independently along the sequence.
In particular (2) excludes a bounded number of arithmetic progressions of unit coefficients, constant or alternating in sign on each progression, when each progression has length \(O(1/m)\) and the total approximation error is \(o(m^{-1/2})\) in norm.
The two ends may have different steps.
These conclusions strengthen the [support and norm constraints](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#statement) at \(K\).
These restrictions enter the [subsequent proof of a strictly larger uniform bound](Yin_C_G0345_v659.md#statement).

<a id="reconstruction"></a>
## Recovering the quotient function from the balanced polynomial

Use the [inner factors](Yin_C_G0345_v638.md#projections) \(U=P/O\), \(V=O^*/P\), and \(J=UV\), so \(|U(0)|=|V(0)|=e^{-m}\).
Write \(P_-\) and \(P_+\) for projection onto strictly negative and nonnegative Fourier indices, respectively.
Multiplication by \(U\) carries the space of short quotients into the orthogonal complement of \(H^2_-\oplus JH^2\).
On pulling that complementary projection back by \(U\), one obtains the orthogonal projection
\[
 D x=x-\bar U P_-(Ux)-V P_+(\bar Vx),\qquad D(R/P)=R/P .
\]
Put \(H=R/P\) and \(w_m=\sqrt m\|DQ\|_2\).
The [equality conditions](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#optimization) give \(\sqrt m\|H\|_2\to r=1/\sqrt2\) and \(m\|Q\|_2^2\to B=\log2\).
The [bound on the complementary projection](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#defect) gives \(\limsup w_m\le w=2(1-1/\sqrt2)\), by substituting \(m\max(n_-,n_+)\to(\log2)/2\) and \(B=\log2\) into its subsequential estimate (5).
Since \(DH=H\), the identity \(\|F\|_2^2=\|H\|_2^2+\|Q\|_2^2+2\operatorname{Re}\langle H,DQ\rangle\) gives
\[
 m\operatorname{Re}\langle H,DQ\rangle\longrightarrow
 (K-r^2-B)/2=-rw .
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz gives the reverse bound \(\liminf w_m\ge w\).
With \(c=r/w=(1+\sqrt2)/2\), expansion of the squared norm now yields
\[
   \sqrt m\|H+cDQ\|_2\to0,\qquad
   \sqrt m\|F-(I-cD)Q\|_2\to0 .                                   \tag{3}
\]
The operator \(I-cD\) has norm at most one because \(D\) is an orthogonal projection and \(0<c<2\).

<a id="commutator"></a>
## Moving the Fourier cut across a block

For a positive integer \(g\), commuting \(S^g\) past either \(P_-\) or \(P_+\) selects, up to sign and the shift, precisely the input Fourier indices \(-g,\ldots,-1\).
Write \(B_g\) for projection onto this interval.
Multiplication by \(S^g\) commutes with the inner factors, so
\[
 \|[S^g,D]x\|_2\le \|B_g Ux\|_2+\|B_g\bar Vx\|_2,                  \tag{4}
\]
where \([S^g,D]=S^gD-DS^g\).

First take \(x=Q\).
For \(W=U\) or \(W=\bar V\), the nonconstant Fourier coefficients have squared sum \(1-e^{-2m}\).
Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(|[X^n](WQ)|\le |[X^n]Q|+\sqrt{1-e^{-2m}}\|Q\|_2\) at every index.
The second term is bounded along the sequence.
The [excess of the integer squared coefficients over their support count](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#statement) is \(o(1/m)\), hence \(m\|B_g Q\|_2^2\le mg+o(1)\).
Thus \(\sqrt m\|[S^g,D]Q\|_2\to0\) whenever \(mg\to0\).
By (3), for \(\Delta=S^g-\epsilon\) we obtain
\[
 \sqrt m\|\Delta F\|_2\le \sqrt m\|\Delta Q\|_2+o(1).
\]
Here \(\|\Delta\|\le2\) uniformly.
Every zero of \(X^g-\epsilon\) has modulus one, so this polynomial is coprime to \(P\), including when \(P\) has repeated factors.
Thus \((X^g-\epsilon)A\) remains indivisible by \(P\), by monic division or Gauss's lemma.
The [unrestricted energy bound](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#statement) for this integer numerator proves the first inequality of (1).
Choosing the sign of the real autocorrelation and expanding the squared norm proves the second.

<a id="arbitrary-lags"></a>
## Different periods in finitely many parts of the quotient

For any \(x\in L^2\), \(g\ge1\), and \(\epsilon=\pm1\), one has
\[
                 \|B_g x\|_2^2\le \|x\|_2\|(S^g-\epsilon)x\|_2 .     \tag{5}
\]
Indeed split the Fourier sequence into successive length-\(g\) vectors \(v_k\), using \(-g,\ldots,-1\) for one block.
The block energies tend to zero at both ends, so their total variation bounds twice the energy of the selected block.
Each difference of energies in absolute value is at most
\(\|v_k-\epsilon v_{k-1}\|(\|v_k\|+\|v_{k-1}\|)\).
Summing and applying Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the total variation by \(2\|(S^g-\epsilon)x\|_2\|x\|_2\), as claimed.
Apply (5) in (4) to \(Ux\) and \(\bar Vx\), since both multipliers are unitary and commute with \(S^g\).
It follows that
\[
 \|\Delta(I-cD)x\|_2\le \|\Delta x\|_2+
                       2c\sqrt{\|x\|_2\|\Delta x\|_2}               \tag{6}
\]
uniformly in \(g\) and the sign.

If (2) held, (6) would imply \(\sqrt m\|\Delta_\ell(I-cD)q_\ell\|_2\to0\) for each \(\Delta_\ell=S^{g_\ell}-\epsilon_\ell\).
Form \(T=\prod_{\ell=1}^s\Delta_\ell\).
Its norm is at most \(2^s\), and all factors commute.
Applying \(T\) to each \((I-cD)q_\ell\), using the corresponding factor first, gives \(\sqrt m\|T(I-cD)q_\ell\|_2\to0\).
Now (2), the contraction bound and (3) give \(m\|TF\|_2^2\to0\).
All factors of \(T\) are coprime to \(P\), so the unrestricted bound for \(TA\) gives \(\liminf m\|TF\|_2^2\ge K>0\), a contradiction.
For a signed progression of length \(L\) and step \(g\), its norm is \(\sqrt L\) and its signed difference at that step has norm \(\sqrt2\) when \(L\ge1\).
This proves the stated exclusion with no restriction on the locations of the progressions.
