# Congruence obstructions and isogeny pullbacks for the two-pole quadratic curve

<a id="statement"></a>
## Degree and exterior-root bounds for primitive sections

Let \(A(U)=(U^2-3U-1)(U^2-4U-1)\), \(A^*(U)=U^4A(1/U)\) and
\[
 F(U,V)=A(U)V^2+U^2V+A^*(U),\qquad P_{k,l}(X)=F(X^k,X^l)
\]
for positive integers \(k,l\).
For \(p=2,3\) and \(D\ge1\) write \(L_p(D)=\operatorname{lcm}_{1\le j\le D}(p^j-1)\).
Every irreducible rational factor of \(P_{k,l}\) has degree greater than \(D\) under either of the following hypotheses.

1. \(L_3(D)\mid 2k\).
2. For each \(1\le j\le D\), at least one of \(k,l\) is divisible by \(2^j-1\).

Consider any sequence of positive coprime \(k,l\) with \(k\to\infty\) and \(s=l/k\to\infty\).
Uniformly over its rational irreducible factors of degree \(d\), the number \(H\) of exterior roots satisfies
\[
                              H=(1+o(1))d/s .                              \tag{1}
\]
Consequently, if either congruence hypothesis holds with \(D/s\to\infty\), then the minimum exterior count over all factors tends to infinity.
This occurs on explicit sequences both for \(k\equiv1\bmod 6\) and for even \(k\) prime to three.

Every pullback of \(F\) by a nonsingular integral monomial substitution is absolutely irreducible in the Laurent polynomial ring.
If [Schinzel's rational specialization conjecture](../audits/literature/AS18/Yin_U001.md#as18-u001) holds for this particular \(F\), then \(P_{k,l}\) itself is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\) outside finitely many positive primitive directions.

<a id="congruences"></a>
## Reductions at two and three

Since \(A(1)=A(-1)=A^*(1)=A^*(-1)=12\), we have
\[
             F(\pm1,V)=12V^2+V+12\equiv V\pmod{p}
                         \qquad(p=2,3).
\]
Expanding \(A\) gives \(F(U,1)=2U^4+21U^2+2\equiv U^2\pmod 2\).
For positive \(k,l\) the section is monic with constant coefficient one.
If it had a monic rational irreducible factor of degree at most \(D\), that factor would have integer coefficients and unit constant coefficient.
Its reduction at either prime would have a nonzero root \(x\) in some \(\mathbb F_{p^j}\) with \(1\le j\le D\), since any irreducible factor of the reduction has degree in this range.
In characteristic three the first hypothesis gives \(x^{2k}=1\), so \(x^k=\pm1\) and \(F(x^k,x^l)=x^l\ne0\).
In characteristic two the second gives \(x^k=1\) or \(x^l=1\), and hence \(F(x^k,x^l)\) equals \(x^l\) or \(x^{2k}\), both nonzero.
Each contradiction proves the corresponding degree bound.

<a id="growth"></a>
## Exterior counts on two sequences of primitive directions

All factors are noncyclotomic by the [torsion exclusion](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#torsion).
For any sequence of factors let \(b_j\) count the exterior roots whose \(k\)-th powers visit the fixed small disk about the positive root \(\beta_j\) of \(U^2-jU-1\), for \(j=3,4\).
The [two pole mass limits](Yin_C_G0345_v790.md#allocation) give \(s b_j/d\to1/2\) before any boundedness assumption on either count is used.
Indeed each local logarithm divided by \(s\) is \(\log\beta_j+O(e^{-c s})\), with \(\log\beta_j>0\), and the marked height limit after division by \(d\) is \(\frac12\log\beta_j\).
All exterior roots visit one of the two disks, so \(H=b_3+b_4\).
Adding the two limits proves (1), uniformly since the chosen sequence of factors was arbitrary.

Let \(D\) tend to infinity through positive integers and put \(r=2^{\lfloor(\log_2 D)/2\rfloor}\), so \(r\asymp\sqrt D\).
For even \(k\) choose \(k=L_3(D)\), \(l=rk+1\).
Every \(3^j-1\) is prime to three, and the term \(3-1\) makes \(k\) even.
For odd \(k\) take \(k=6L_2(D)+1\), \(l=r(k-1)\).
Here \(k\equiv1\bmod6\), and \(\gcd(k,l)=1\) since \(k\) is odd and \(r\) is a power of two.
These two choices meet respectively the first and second congruence hypotheses, with \(s\sim r\) in both cases.
Every factor then has \(H\ge(1-o(1))D/s\gg\sqrt D\).
This growth still permits a factor to carry a vanishing fraction of the \(2k\) exterior roots of the full section.

<a id="isogenies"></a>
## Two simple valuations and all monomial pullbacks

Let \(K\) be the complex function field of the [absolutely irreducible parent](Yin_C_G0345_v789.md#example).
For each \(m\ge1\) the [horizontal pullback argument](Yin_C_G0345_v790.md#pullback) gives \([K(T):K]=m\) for \(T^m=U\), by Eisenstein at the simple zero of \(U\) above \(U=0,V=i\).
There is a simple pole of \(V\) above \(U=\beta_3\), with uniformizer \(w=1/V\).
In the completed local ring \(\mathbb C[[w]]\), \(U\) is a unit with constant term \(\beta_3\ne0\).
Each of its \(m\) distinct constant-term radicals lifts to a power series \(T\) with \(T^m=U\) by successive coefficient matching, since \(mT(0)^{m-1}\ne0\).
Choosing one expansion embeds \(K(T)\) into \(\mathbb C((w))\) over \(K\), so the extended valuation still gives \(w\) order one.
Eisenstein for \(Y^m-w\) now gives degree \(m\) over \(K(T)\).
Equivalently adjoining an \(m\)-th root of \(V\) after \(T\) gives total degree \(m^2\) over \(K\).

The coordinate ring of the pulled-back curve \(F(X^m,Y^m)=0\) in the torus is free of rank \(m^2\) over the original coordinate ring, with basis \(X^aY^b\) for \(0\le a,b<m\).
After localization at its base function field \(K\) it is the degree-\(m^2\) field just constructed.
Freeness gives an injection into that field, so the pulled-back ring is a domain and its equation is irreducible in the complex Laurent ring.
If a nonsingular integral monomial substitution with matrix \(M\) gave a factorization into nonunits, composing with the signed adjugate of \(M\) would give one for \(m=|\det M|\).
Indeed a nonsingular substitution preserves distinct support exponents and hence preserves nonunits.
This proves absolute irreducibility for all the claimed substitutions.

<a id="conditional"></a>
## The conditional rational irreducibility conclusion

The support of \(F\) spans a two-dimensional polygon, so \(F\) is noncyclotomic.
In the [published formulation of Conjecture 2.1](../audits/literature/AS18/Yin_U001.md#as18-u001), exceptional directions are orthogonal to one of finitely many nonzero integral vectors.
Each such line contains at most two primitive vectors in \(\mathbb Z^2\).
Outside these lines the conjecture gives a nonsingular monomial pullback whose rational factors, after the prescribed specialization and removal of their cyclotomic parts, produce the irreducible factorization of the cyclotomic-free section.
The pullback has one irreducible factor of multiplicity one by the preceding argument.
The specialized section is nonconstant, monic and has no torsion zero, giving the claimed conditional irreducibility.
The unconditional non-self-inversive case omits this reciprocal \(F\), and the function-field analogue requires a polynomial nonconstant in its extra parameter.
