# Higher circle moments and concentration for short integral numerators

<a id="statement"></a>
## A bound up to the supremum norm

Let \(P\in\mathbb Z[X]\) be monic of degree \(d\ge1\), with \(|P(0)|=1\), no root on the unit circle, and \(m=\log M(P)>0\).
Write \(C=1/(2\sinh(2m))\).
Let \(T\ne0\) be an integral Laurent polynomial of width less than \(d\), and use Haar probability measure \(\mu\) on the unit circle.
Translate \(T\) so that its least exponent is zero and put \(H=T/P\).
Let \(u_0=0<u_1<\cdots\) be the occupied Taylor exponents of \(H\) at zero and \(q_i\) their nonzero integer coefficients.
For every \(2\le p\le\infty\), \(N\ge1\), with \(1/\infty=0\), we have
\[
 \|H\|_p\ \ge\
 \big(\sqrt C-\sqrt{(N-1)/2}\big)_+
 N^{-1/p}\left(\sum_{i<N}q_i^2\right)^{1/2}.                       \tag{1}
\]
In particular \(\|H\|_p\gg_p m^{-(1-1/p)}\) along every sequence with \(m\to0\).
For \(p=\infty\) this gives
\[
              \liminf_{m\to0}m\|T/P\|_\infty\ \ge\ \frac1{8\sqrt2}.  \tag{2}
\]
For reciprocal \(P\) and \(T=1\) the [reciprocal square estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v630.md#statement) gives the stronger circle-minimum bound \(\min|P|\le2\sinh m\).
Here the numerator can be any nonzero short integral vector.

<a id="weighted"></a>
## Testing all remainders against one evaluation vector

The [stripping identity](Yin_C_G0345_v644.md#remainders) gives short integral polynomials \(T_i\) with nonzero constant, \(T_i(0)=P(0)q_i\), and
\[
          T_i/P=X^{-u_i}\left(H-\sum_{j<i}q_jX^{u_j}\right).
\]
It constructs the infinite sequence since each stripping step gives a nonzero polynomial of degree less than \(d\).
The [prescribed-constant minimizer](Yin_C_G0345_v621.md#evaluation) determines a single unit vector \(e\) in the space of short quotients with
\(\langle S/P,e\rangle=\sqrt C\,S(0)\) for every \(\deg S<d\), with the inner product linear in its first argument.
Indeed the constant-one minimizer has squared norm \(C\) and is orthogonal to the constant-zero subspace.

Put \(V=\sum_{i<N}q_i X^{-u_i}\), \(b^2=\sum_{i<N}q_i^2\).
Weight the stripping identities by \(q_i\), pair with \(e\), and take the absolute value of the term involving \(\sum q_i T_i/P\).
This term equals \(\sqrt C\,b^2\) because \(|P(0)|=1\).
The subtracted prefixes have total norm at most
\[
 \sum_{i<N}|q_i|\left(\sum_{j<i}q_j^2\right)^{1/2}
 \ \le\ \sqrt{N-1}\left(\sum_{j<i<N}q_i^2q_j^2\right)^{1/2}
 \ \le\ \sqrt{(N-1)/2}\,b^2 .                                      \tag{3}
\]
The first bound on their norm is triangle inequality and monomial orthogonality; the second skips the empty first prefix.
It follows that \(\|HV\|_2\ge(\sqrt C-\sqrt{(N-1)/2})b^2\).
For \(1/r=1/2-1/p\), orthogonality gives \(\|V\|_2=b\), and Cauchy–Schwarz gives \(\|V\|_\infty\le\sqrt N b\).
Thus \(\|V\|_r\le N^{1/p}b\), including \(p=\infty\), so Hölder's inequality proves (1).
For large \(C\), take \(N=\lfloor C/2\rfloor\) and use \(b\ge\sqrt N\).
This gives \(\liminf\|H\|_p/C^{1-1/p}\ge 2^{-3/2+1/p}\) and (2), since \(mC\to1/4\).

<a id="concentration"></a>
## Where the short energy must lie

Define the trigonometric polynomial \(D_N=V/(b\sqrt N)\) and the set \(U_N(\tau)=\{|D_N|\ge\tau\}\) for \(0<\tau<1\).
Its size and the energy captured on it satisfy
\[
 \mu(U_N(\tau))\le\frac1{N\tau^2},\qquad
 \int_{U_N(\tau)} |H|^2\,d\mu\ \ge\
 \frac{(\sqrt C-\sqrt{(N-1)/2})_+^2-\tau^2\|H\|_2^2}{1-\tau^2}.    \tag{4}
\]
Indeed \(\|D_N\|_\infty\le1,\ \|D_N\|_2^2=1/N\), and (3) gives \(\|HD_N\|_2\ge(\sqrt C-\sqrt{(N-1)/2})_+\) since \(b\ge\sqrt N\).
Split this weighted integral over \(U_N(\tau)\) and its complement to obtain (4).

For any fixed \(D\ge1\), when \(\|H\|_2^2\le DC\) and \(C\) is sufficiently large, choose \(N=\lfloor C/2\rfloor\) and \(\tau^2=1/(8D)\).
Then \(U_N(\tau)\) has Haar measure \(O_D(m)\) and carries at least \(C/8\) of the squared norm.
If \(m\|H\|_2^2\to1/2\), every \(N\to\infty\) with \(N=o(C)\) gives a set \(U_N(N^{-1/4})\) of measure at most \(N^{-1/2}\) carrying at least \(1/2-o(1)\) of the entire energy.
Here \(\|H\|_2^2/C\to2\) and the first term of the numerator of (4) is asymptotic to \(C\).
For these same prefixes every \(q_i\) for \(i<N\) is eventually \(1\) or \(-1\).
Indeed the bound on the maximum stripped norm in [the finite iteration](Yin_C_G0345_v644.md#remainders) applies with the starting norm \(\|H\|_2\), so \(q_i^2\le(\|H\|_2^2/C)(1-\sqrt{(N-1)/C})^{-2}=2+o(1)<4\).
Consequently \(D_N\) is then the signed average of the actual monomials \(X^{-u_i}\).
These conclusions apply to the balanced short remainder at the [unrestricted limiting energy](Yin_C_G0345_v643.md#statement), whose scaled energy must approach \(1/2\).
The set in (4) describes the concentration of the remainder; estimating the quotient on that same set requires control of the polynomial removed in the balanced division.
