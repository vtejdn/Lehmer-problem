# Cyclotomic overlap in the signed contraction and a bound for consecutive Mahler values

<a id="statement"></a>
## Weighted contraction and simultaneous double contacts

Fix a reciprocal nontorsion algebraic unit \(\delta\) such that \(\mathbb Q(\delta^k)=\mathbb Q(\delta)\) for every positive integer \(k\).
Let \(P_a\) be the minimal polynomial of \(\delta^a\), and write \(h=\log M(P_1)\).
For a nonempty finite set \(I\) of positive integers take \(F=\prod_{a\in I}P_a^{t_a}\) with positive integral \(t_a\), and fix \(\sigma\in\{1,-1\}\).
Let \(\mathcal D\) be the set of orders of the roots of the polynomials \(Y^a-\sigma\), \(a\in I\), and put
\[
      m_d=\max_{\substack{a\in I\\ \Phi_d\mid Y^a-\sigma}}t_a,\qquad
      K_\sigma(t)=\sum_{d\in\mathcal D}\varphi(d)m_d,\qquad
      M=\operatorname{lcm}_{d\in\mathcal D}d .
\]
Let \(L\) be the splitting field of \(P_1\).
For each \(n\ge2\) discard the rational primes dividing \(2nM F(1)F(-1)\) or the discriminant of \(\prod_{a\in I}P_a\), and those ramified in \(L\).
Define the positive integer \(B_n\) by \(v_\ell(B_n)=\max_{z^n=\sigma}v_\ell(F(z))\) at every retained prime, taking the nodes in \(\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\) with \(v_\ell(\ell)=1\), and by valuation zero elsewhere.
Then along all positive integer exponents tending to infinity,
\[
                        \frac{\log B_n}{n}\longrightarrow \frac h2 K_\sigma(t).
                                                                        \tag{1}
\]

In the [fixed-double-factor test](Yin_C_G0345_v423.md#statement) write \(B=(\log2)/4\).
For \(r\ge1\) set \(T=r(r+1)/2,\ K=\sum_{a=1}^r\varphi(2a)\) and \(C_r=4T-2K\).
If \(C_rh<B\), the original minima \(P_1,\ldots,P_r\) all occur with multiplicity at least two in a single passing graph \(A(X^n)+\varepsilon X A_b^*(X^n)\), with \(A(\delta^{an})\ne0\) for each \(a\).
This holds for either prescribed sign \(\varepsilon\) at every sufficiently large \(n\), at one fixed width and also along every prescribed \(b(n)\to\infty\) with \(\log b(n)=o(n)\).
Here \(A_b^*(Y)=Y^bA(1/Y)\) and passing requires \(((2nb+1)\|A\|_1^2)^4<2^n\).
Writing \(\lambda\) for the unrestricted infimum of positive logarithmic Mahler measures of integer polynomials, an infinite subsequence of those growing widths on which each passing row has fewer than \(r\) distinct qualifying values for one sign implies \(\lambda\ge B/C_r\).
For \(r=1,2,3,4,5\) the denominators are \(2,6,14,22,34\).

At odd exponents the three minima of \(\delta,\delta^2,-\delta^3\) admit either signed graph with double contacts and nonzero numerator at all three powered nodes.
The least logarithmic row one-norm divided by \(n\) tends to \(6h\) when \(b\to\infty,\ b=o(n)\), and each fixed positive excess over \(6h\) is attainable at a sufficiently large fixed width.
An infinite odd-exponent exclusion of three distinct qualifying values on the stated growing widths therefore implies \(\lambda\ge B/12\).

<a id="contraction"></a>
## Prime-by-prime weights

Take one representative \(x\) of every inverse pair of conjugates of \(\delta\).
Different representatives are multiplicatively independent modulo torsion.
Indeed their equal positive absolute heights would force the two exponents of a primitive dependence to have equal absolute values, so a product or quotient would be torsion.
After possible inversion this would contradict injectivity of all powers on distinct conjugates.
The same field condition gives \(\log M(P_a)=ah\) and gives full degree for every \(P_a\).
The minima are thus distinct, and all roots of their squarefree product have distinct residues at a retained prime.

At a prime \(\mathfrak l\) of \(L\) above a retained \(\ell\), put \(w_{x,d}=\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak l}\Phi_d(x^n)\).
At most one \(d\) contributes for a given \(x\), since the factors of \(Y^M-1\) have disjoint reductions.
For such a \(d\), a matching level \(a\) has \(\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak l}(x^{an}-\sigma)=w_{x,d}\).
This valuation is realized at the unique node \(z^n=\sigma\) with the same residue as \(x^a\), since the nodes have distinct residues.
Every other root of the squarefree product gives a unit difference there, hence \(v_\ell(F(z))=t_a w_{x,d}\) by unramifiedness.
Inversion preserves \(w_{x,d}\), and every node of positive valuation arises this way.
It follows that
\[
                            v_\ell(B_n)=\max_{x,d}m_d w_{x,d}.                \tag{2}
\]
In particular the exponents are integral and have finite support by the resultant product.

Sum (2) with weights \(\log N\mathfrak l/[L:\mathbb Q]\).
The sum of the left side is \(\log B_n\).
The excess on replacing a maximum by the sum is bounded by the sum of pairwise minima.
Different \(d\)'s at one \(x\) have disjoint support at retained primes.
For \(x\ne y\), the common valuations are bounded by those of \(x^{Mn}-1,y^{Mn}-1\).
The normalized logarithmic norm of this last gcd is \(o(n)\) by [Corvaja and Zannier, Proposition 2](../audits/literature/CZ05/Yin_U001.md#s-unit-gcd) for the distinct pairs of independent units of heights \(O(n)\).
The fixed multiplicities and the finite number of pairs preserve that error.
The [exponent-prime bound](Yin_C_G0345_v427.md#bad) applied to \(x^{2Mn}-1\) bounds the discarded valuations by \(o(n)\).
Inversion pairs the valuations equally, so the complete weighted sum of \(w_{x,d}\) over \(x,\mathfrak l\) equals half the logarithm of \(\lvert\operatorname{Res}(P_1,\Phi_d(X^n))\rvert\).
By Möbius inversion of the [signed resultant estimate](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v42.md#substitution) at the finitely many exponents \(en\) with \(e\mid d\), this logarithm is \(nh\varphi(d)+O_{\delta,d}(\log(2n))\).
Summation with the weights \(m_d\) proves (1).

<a id="witnesses"></a>
## A graph for each required nonzero value

At the negative endpoint for \(I=\{1,\ldots,r\}\), the occurring orders are exactly \(2j,\ 1\le j\le r\), since an order \(2j\) occurs at level \(a\) precisely when \(a/j\) is an odd integer.
Thus \(D=T-K\ge0\) is the total degree lost when passing from the product of \(Y^a+1\) to their least common multiple.
Fix a target \(a\) and prescribe order \(2+k\) there and order two elsewhere, where \(k=\lfloor 2D/a\rfloor\).
For any fixed \(\eta>0\), the [integral jet estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v423.md#construction) gives a nonzero graph with integral rows \(U,V\) of degree at most \(w\) satisfying these orders and
\[
              2\log\max(\|U\|_1,\|V\|_1)/n\le(2T+ka)h+\eta
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\), at one sufficiently large width \(w\) common to all targets.
Write \(Q_a\) for the minimum of \(\delta^{an}\), with logarithmic measure \(anh\).
If \(Q_a^{k+1}\) divides both rows, their quotients give a nonzero graph containing \(P_a\prod_{j\ne a}P_j^2\).
Indeed the powered minima are distinct and substitution is unramified at the original roots.
For this last product the endpoint weight is at least \(2K-a\), since only the factors of \(Y^a+1\) can each lose one unit of multiplicity.
The [inverse-node argument](Yin_C_G0345_v427.md#argument) bounds the larger quotient row norm below by \(B_n/2^w\).
In detail, remove the common power of \(Y+1\), preserving all contacts, and evaluate the graph at a maximizing node and its inverse.
At every prime divisor of \(B_n\) these two nodes have unit difference, so \(B_n\) divides both remaining row evaluations at \(-1\).
At least one is nonzero, and each is a divided derivative of an original quotient row of magnitude at most \(2^w\) times its one-norm.
For the larger quotient row the Mahler measure is at least its one-norm divided by \(2^w\).
Restoring \(Q_a^{k+1}\) and using (1) gives a lower bound \((2K-a+2(k+1)a)h-o(1)\) on the doubled logarithmic norm divided by \(n\).
Its excess over the interpolation main term is \(((k+1)a-2D)h>0\).
Choose \(\eta\) smaller than every such gap and than \(B-C_rh\).

At most \(k\) common copies of \(Q_a\) occur.
Removing these copies preserves each double contact, and both resulting rows are nonzero at \(\delta^{an}\) by the graph equation.
Division increases the norm by at most \(2^w\) by multiplicativity of Mahler measure and the coefficient bound.
The [two signed projections](Yin_C_G0345_v419.md#retention) give a row \(A_a\) at width \(w+1\) of the prescribed sign, preserving all double contacts and the chosen nonzero value at bounded additional cost.
For each \(j\), the polynomial \(\sum_{a=1}^r s^{a-1} A_a(\delta^{jn})\) in \(s\) is nonzero by its coefficient at \(a=j\).
The \(r\) polynomials exclude at most \(r(r-1)\) integers, so some \(0\le s\le r(r-1)\) gives a single linear combination retaining all values.
The norm factor is bounded in terms of \(r\), and \(2T+ka\le 2T+2D=C_r\), proving the strict passing budget.
The [width multiplier](Yin_C_G0345_v423.md#construction) extends that budget to the stated growing widths.
If \(\lambda<B/C_r\), factorization supplies a positive irreducible logarithmic measure \(h<B/C_r\), with a reciprocal unit [all-power representative](Yin_C_G0345_v421.md#norm) by the [small-measure reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#upper).
The resulting consecutive measures \(h,\ldots,rh\) contradict the proposed exclusion.

<a id="triple"></a>
## The three lowest levels

For the original triple at order two, (1) gives slope \(5h\) at the negative endpoint from orders \(2,4,6\), and \(4h\) at the positive endpoint from orders \(1,2,3\).
At the negative endpoint dropping one copy at level one leaves weight \(10\), and dropping one at level two leaves weight \(8\).
Restoring the respective powered minima gives only \(6h\) in each comparison, equal to the interpolation upper slope.
These comparisons leave the optimal coefficient rate of the three original minima unresolved.

For the signed triple \(\delta,\delta^2,-\delta^3\), the negative-endpoint conditions at odd \(n\) use \(Y+1,Y^2+1,Y^3-1\) at \(Y=x^n\).
Within matched powers the pair of levels \(1,3\) has primitive phase \(-1\) and even exponent sum, while \(1,2\) and \(2,3\) have phase \(+1\) and odd exponent sum.
Other pairs of chosen inverse-pair representatives come from independent base conjugates, and the full degrees and distinct absolute heights ensure injectivity of powering.
The [sharp signed-row theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v427.md#statement) now gives slope \(6h\) and the fixed-width excess assertion.
If \(\lambda<B/12\), choose an all-power small representative with \(12h<B\) as above and use the fixed excess and padding to contradict an odd-exponent exclusion.
The [original exploration](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v275.md#rate) and its [independent review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_G0562_v559.md#rates) cover the contraction, the separate witnesses and both count bounds.
