# The minimum degree of a base field for an expression by radicals

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact minimum for an individual algebraic number

Fix \(M>0\) and a nonzero algebraic number \(x\) with positive logarithmic Mahler measure \(m(x)\le M\).
Let \(\gamma\) be the product of the conjugates of \(x\) whose quotients by \(x\) are roots of unity, and let \(d=[\mathbb Q(\gamma):\mathbb Q]\).
Among the conjugates of \(\gamma\), identify two if their product or quotient is a root of unity.
These [multiplicative labels](Yin_C_G0345_v264.md#labels) all have the same size \(w\in\{1,2\}\), and there are \(v=d/w\) labels.
For all \(d\) sufficiently large in terms of \(M\) alone,
\[
 \min\bigl\{[K:\mathbb Q]: x\text{ belongs to a finite tower of radical adjunctions over }K\bigr\}=v .
\]
The minimum ranges over all number fields inside a fixed algebraic closure, including nonnormal fields.
It is attained inside \(\mathbb Q(x)\) by the fixed field of the stabilizer of the label containing \(\gamma\).
In particular every such radical construction satisfies \(d\le 2[K:\mathbb Q]\), with the exact bound \(d\le[K:\mathbb Q]\) for single-root labels.
For paired labels equality in the factor-two bound is attained for that individual input.

There is also an exact minimum if the base is built in stages from \(\mathbb Q\).
Allow unrestricted radical adjunctions interspersed with adjunctions of single algebraic elements of degree at most \(q\) over the field already reached.
The least positive integer \(q\) for which such a finite tower contains \(x\) is the common size \(b\) of the minimal blocks of labels.
The bound is independent of the number and order of the steps.

<a id="attainment"></a>
## Construction over one label

Write \(L\) for the rational splitting field of \(x\), \(P\subset L\) for the field fixed by the kernel of the label action and \(E\subset P\) for the field fixed by the stabilizer of the distinguished label.
Transitivity gives \([E:\mathbb Q]=v\).
An automorphism fixing \(x\) preserves its torsion-quotient class and its product \(\gamma\).
Similarly, fixing \(\gamma\) preserves its label.
Thus \(E\subseteq\mathbb Q(\gamma)\subseteq\mathbb Q(x)\) and \([\mathbb Q(\gamma):E]=w\le2\).
If \(f\) is the size of the class of \(x\), its product is \(\gamma=\zeta x^f\) for a root of unity \(\zeta\).
Over \(E\), adjoin \(\gamma\) by at most a quadratic extension, then \(\zeta\) as a radical of unity and \(x\) as an \(f\)-th root of \(\gamma/\zeta\).
This gives the upper bound even when the uncompressed degree greatly exceeds \(d\).

<a id="lower"></a>
## The normal closure of every base contains the label field

Suppose \(x\) belongs to a finite radical tower over \(K\), and write \(H\) for the rational normal closure of \(K\).
After base change to \(H\), take all rational conjugates of the tower and their compositum.
Adjoining roots of unity of the finitely many radical orders first makes the extension at each successive level abelian after adjoining all conjugate radicals of that level.
This constructs a finite rational normal extension \(T\) containing \(H,L\) with \(\operatorname{Gal}(T/H)\) solvable.
Its image in \(G=\operatorname{Gal}(P/\mathbb Q)\) is solvable and normal.
For large \(d\), the [label group has trivial solvable radical](Yin_C_G0345_v267.md#statement), so \(P\subset H\).

Let \(\mu(U)\) denote the least degree of a faithful permutation representation of a finite group \(U\).
Restriction gives \(G\) as a quotient of \(\operatorname{Gal}(H/\mathbb Q)\), which acts faithfully on the \([K:\mathbb Q]\) embeddings of \(K\).
Since \(G\) has no nontrivial abelian normal subgroup, [Kovács and Praeger, Theorem 1](../audits/literature/KP00/Yin_U001.md#quotient), gives
\[
 \mu(G)\le\mu(\operatorname{Gal}(H/\mathbb Q))\le[K:\mathbb Q].
\]
By the [alternating actions on all minimal blocks](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement), \(G\) contains \(D=(A_b)^u\) with \(b\ge9\) and \(ub=v\).
The natural representation of \(A_b\) has degree \(b\), and its order \(b!/2>(b-1)!\) excludes a smaller faithful action.
The factors are simple, so [Easdown and Praeger, Theorem 3.1](../audits/literature/EP88/Yin_U001.md#products), gives \(\mu(D)=ub=v\).
Restricting a faithful action of \(G\) to \(D\) yields \(v\le\mu(G)\).
Together with the construction over \(E\), this proves the asserted minimum.

<a id="towers"></a>
## The largest necessary degree in a tower

The field \(B\) in \(P\) fixed by the stabilizer of the distinguished label's block lies inside \(E\).
Writing \(u=v/b\) for the block count gives \([B:\mathbb Q]=u\) and \([E:B]=b\).
The [block bounds](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement) give \(u\ll_M(\log(3d))^{212}\), hence \(u<b\) eventually because \(ub=v\ge d/2\).
By the primitive element theorem one algebraic adjunction at each of these two stages suffices, after which the radical construction above reaches \(x\).

For the converse, call a quotient of a subgroup of a finite group a section.
If a group has a simple section, every normal subgroup either has that simple section or the corresponding quotient group has it.
Indeed, for a surjection from a subgroup onto the simple group, the image of its intersection with the normal subgroup is either the whole simple group or trivial.
In the latter case the map descends to a subgroup of the quotient.
Consequently a subgroup of a finite product has no section \(A_b\) if none of the coordinate groups has that section.

Suppose \(q<b\) and take rational normal closures successively along any allowed tower.
At an algebraic step of degree at most \(q\), the new normal closure over the preceding one is the compositum of splitting fields of the conjugates of the step's minimal polynomial.
Its relative group embeds by restriction in a product of symmetric groups of degrees at most \(q\).
Their orders are at most \((b-1)!<|A_b|\).
At a radical step with exponent \(n\), first adjoining the \(n\)-th roots of unity and then the \(n\)-th roots of all conjugate radicands gives a normal extension of the preceding closure with solvable relative group.
The new normal closure at this step is a subextension with solvable relative group as well.
Induction by the simple-section observation excludes \(A_b\) as a section of the final normal closure group, irrespective of how often the operations alternate.
If the resulting tower contained \(x\), its rational normal closure would contain \(L\) and thus \(P\).
Restriction would then give a quotient containing \(A_b\) by the supported alternating actions, a contradiction.
