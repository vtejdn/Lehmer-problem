#### Reciprocal identities and all regions

The integer trace identity and derivative reciprocity used in C798 give

    xi_i= sigma xi_(j_*-i)+chi_i modulo Z,
    chi_(j_*-i)=-sigma chi_i.

The source identity for Kplus follows from 1-X^L=-X^L(1-X^(-L)); even L keeps its new center integral.
Its expanding sequence is xiPlus_i=xi_i-xi_(i-L)=(1-r^L)xi_i, and its neutral wave is chiPlus_i=chi_i-chi_(i-L).
None of its root values vanishes, since a Salem conjugate is not a root of unity.
The exact neutral norm is DeltaPlus, while |chi_i|<=A and |chiPlus_i|<=Aplus.

On the first core [a,b], the shifted real term has magnitude at most rho, and the canonical identity is

    Yplus_i=Y_i-xi_(i-L).

On the second core, write i=L+j_*-k with a<=k<=b.
Using both reciprocal identities gives

    Yplus_i=sigma[-Y_k+xi_(k-L)+chiPlus_k].              (MM3)

The right sides are canonical under the assumed margin inequalities.
The first core loses at most rho of boundary margin, and the second loses at most rho+Aplus.

On the gap i=b+j, 1<=j<=g, one has

    Yplus_(b+j)=x_j+z_j+chi_(b+j),
    x_j=sigma xi_(a-j), z_j=-xi_(a-(g+1-j)),
    |x_j|=h0 r^j, |z_j|=h0 r^(g+1-j).

Its absolute value is at most U<1/2.
The exterior left tail is xiPlus, and the exterior right canonical tail follows from the new reciprocal identity and Aplus+h0r<1/2.
Removing its outgoing neutral wave leaves the exact localized tails hplus r^distance, proving MM1.

The original forcing vanishes before a and the left tail equals xi, so the monic recurrence propagates Y_i=xi_i through a+d-1.
The first-core formula preserves this identity for xiPlus at those d initial coordinates; hence the new forcing vanishes for i<a.
The right tail is homogeneous for i>b+L, so no forcing occurs there either.
Centering makes the forcing integer-valued.
It cannot be identically zero, since a homogeneous sequence agreeing with nonzero xiPlus on the left would equal that expanding mode everywhere and contradict bounded canonical observations.
Thus source symmetry, finite support and nonzero forcing are all retained.

### Exact limitation of the basic simultaneous estimate

The finite simultaneous-return pigeonhole argument already retained in C318, applied to zeta_j^2, gives for large integer T

    e(q(T))<=4pi T^(-1/s).

Choose Q=floor(T^(1/s)); a return q<=Q^s<=T has error at most 2pi/Q, and Q>=T^(1/s)/2 eventually.
This guarantees the qualitative preparatory return in every fixed degree, but for s>=2 its exponent is at most 1/2.
The displayed power schedule needs both alpha nu>1 and alpha<2; substituting nu=1/s would require alpha>s and alpha<2 simultaneously.
At the s=2 endpoint alpha=2, the available amplitude-log multiplier is only two, so the bound E_k=O(2^k) does not prove the required little-o comparison; the strict-best-return improvement is not supplied by that worst-case estimate either.
These are failures of this sufficient cost certificate, not lower bounds on actual returns, actual action, or all possible packet constructions.
The exact weighted amplitude in MM7 can be much smaller than its maximum-phase upper bound, and different constructions or sharper action estimates remain possible.
No impossibility theorem for higher-degree ST4 or strict radius is asserted.
