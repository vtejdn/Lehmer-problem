# A single Pisot limit for delay-two Salem polynomials of measure tending to one

<a id="statement"></a>
## A uniform gap for nonunits and convergence for units

Let \(f\in\mathbb Z[z]\) be the monic minimal polynomial of a Pisot number \(\theta>1\), let \(q=|f(0)|\), and put \(f^*(z)=z^{\deg f}f(1/z)\).
For every nonunit seed, both signs and every integer \(N\ge2\), there is an absolute \(c>0\), common to all these choices, such that
\[
              M(z^N f\pm f^*)=1
       \quad\hbox{or}\quad M(z^N f\pm f^*)\ge 1+c .
\]
Here \(M\) is the multiplicative Mahler measure.
Suppose a sequence of delay-two inputs has \(M_i=M(z^2 f_i\pm f_i^*)>1\) with \(M_i\to1\).
Eventually its seeds have norm of absolute value one and its polynomials have the sign \(z^2 f_i+e_i f_i^*\), with \(e_i=\operatorname{sgn} f_i(0)\).
For the entire sequence,
\[
             \theta_i\longrightarrow 2,\qquad
             F_i=e_i f_i/f_i^*\longrightarrow F_*=\frac1{1-2z}
\]
meromorphically on compact subsets of the open unit disk.
In particular each fixed Taylor coefficient at zero eventually equals the corresponding power of two.
If \(s_i\) is the first index at which these coefficients differ, then \(s_i\to\infty\) and
\[
       (M_i+M_i^{-1}-2)\big(s_i(s_i-1)-3\big)\le6
\]
on the eventual sequence.
The constant \(c\) is qualitative.
Boyd previously identified \(F_*\) as a second derived Pisot function in his discussion of the [delay-two search](../audits/literature/Boy78/Yin_U001.md#fixed-delay-enumeration).
The concentration proved here does not decide whether such a sequence exists.

<a id="reduction"></a>
## Classification of all possible subsequential limits

From any escaping sequence at delay two, the [delay bounds](Yin_C_G0345_v27.md#statement) extract a subsequence with fixed \(q\in\{1,2\}\) and a bounded convergent seed parameter separated from one.
Every fixed seed supplies only finitely many measures at this delay, so we can take distinct seeds.
The [sign and root count](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v114.md#input) give the asserted sign and a single simple positive exterior root \(M_i\).
Compactness of the [Pisot class](../audits/literature/BM96/Yin_U001.md#bm96-u001) gives a further subsequence with derived quotient \(F=A/Q\) of modulus strictly less than one on an open arc and a single disk pole at the reciprocal of the limiting seed.
For \(T_i=M_i+M_i^{-1}\), the [positive-real function](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v114.md#positive) removes the two off-circle poles of \((1-z^2F_i)/(1+z^2F_i)\).
Its limiting function \(K\) has integer Taylor coefficients, \(K(0)=1\), \(K'(0)=q-1\), and an absolutely continuous component of positive mass in its representing measure.
The [classification and inversion](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v114.md#limit) yield
\[
 K=1,\quad 1+\epsilon z^m,\quad(1+\epsilon z^m)^{-1},
 \qquad
 F=\frac{K-1+z}{z(1-z-zK)},\qquad \epsilon=\pm1,\quad m\ge1 .
\]
The point of retaining the circle modulus loss is that the [integral Herglotz classification](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#haar) only gives this list when the limiting measure has a continuous part.

<a id="exclusion"></a>
## Elimination of the nonconstant profiles

For each fixed nonconstant \(K=k/l\) on this list, write \(A=(k-(1-z)l)/z,\ Q=(1-z)l-zk\).
On the circle the defect \(|Q|^2-|A|^2\) equals \(|1-z|^2|1\pm z^m|^2\).
Its multiplicative measure is one, including after any common cyclotomic factor is removed.
Thus \(F\) has [Boyd width one](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v116.md#width).
By [Boyd's uniqueness after a sufficiently late first departure](../audits/literature/Boy78/Yin_U001.md#boy78-u001), each nearby input in the extracted sequence agrees with one of the two signed reciprocal completions of this pair at its departure index.
The [completion argument](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v116.md#completions) exhibits the actual seed minimal polynomial times a cyclotomic product as a single signed sum of the fixed numerator and a shifted reversal of the fixed denominator.
The corresponding delay polynomial therefore has a cyclotomic multiple of [at most ten terms at \(q=2\), or fourteen at \(q=1\)](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v116.md#delay-two).
The [fixed-support gap](../audits/literature/DS17/Yin_U001.md#ds17-u001) excludes the proposed convergence to one on this subsequence.
The size of the coefficient neighborhood can depend on \(m\) because the argument applies after a specific limiting quotient has been extracted.

Consequently every extracted cluster quotient has \(K=1\), \(q=1\), \(F=F_*\), and \(\theta=2\).
An infinite nonunit subsequence or a subsequence staying outside a fixed neighborhood of this seed or germ would admit another extracted subsequence and give a contradiction.
This proves convergence for the whole sequence and a uniform positive gap at delay two for nonunit seeds.
The [uniform result at all larger delays](Yin_C_G0345_v27.md#statement) finishes the nonunit assertion.
The [finite-prefix estimate](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v114.md#energy) applies to the actual positive measure at each late input and gives the stated inequality.
