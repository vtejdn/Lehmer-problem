# A Mahler gap for a fixed number of residue classes at prime moduli

<a id="statement"></a>
## A necessary term count in the original cyclic projection

Fix \(k\ge2\) and put \(t=\lfloor k/2\rfloor\).
Let \(P\in\mathbb Z[x]\) have nonzero constant term and occupy exactly \(k\) residue classes modulo an odd prime \(p\).
Write
\[
 P(x)=\sum_{j=1}^k x^{r_j}U_j(x^p),\qquad 0\le r_j<p,
 \qquad \gcd(U_1,\ldots,U_k)=1\quad\text{in }\mathbb Z[X^{\pm1}].
\]
The gcd includes integer content and is taken up to a signed monomial.
Let \(\#V\) count the nonzero terms of \(V\), and write
\[
 T(P,p)=\min_{0\ne H\in\mathbb C[X]}\sum_{j=1}^k\#(HU_j).
\]
There are \(a_k,b_k>0\) depending only on \(k\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(p\),
\[
 0<m(P)<a_k\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T(P,p)\ge b_k p^{1/t}/\log p. \tag{1}
\]
Here \(m\) denotes total logarithmic Mahler measure.
The row-gcd hypothesis holds for irreducible \(P\) with at least two occupied classes, since a nonunit common divisor would give a nonunit factor in \(x^p\) and a quotient with at least two occupied classes.
In particular three occupied classes require order at least \(p/\log p\) terms after every common multiplication along a sequence of positive measures tending to zero.
Row heights and degrees are unrestricted.

<a id="lift"></a>
## Short representatives for reciprocal support

Let \(\theta>1\) solve \(\theta^3-\theta-1=0\).
For \(m(P)<\log\theta\) the [reciprocity threshold](Yin_C_G0345_v337.md#rigidity) gives \(P^*=\pm P\).
Choose an integer \(c\) with \(2c\equiv\deg P\bmod p\).
The occupied set translated by \(-c\) consists of exactly \(t\) opposite nonzero pairs, together with zero for odd \(k\).

Choose representatives \(v_1,\ldots,v_t\) of the pairs and put \(B=\lfloor(p-1)^{1/t}\rfloor\).
Place the \(p\) vectors with coordinates \(\{iv_j/p\}\), \(0\le i<p\), in the unit cube, where braces denote fractional part.
Two belong to one of the \(B^t<p\) equal boxes with side length \(1/B\).
Their index difference \(a\ne0\bmod p\) makes each \(av_j\) congruent to an integer of absolute value at most \(p/B\).
Taking signed representatives for opposite residues gives distinct \(e_j\equiv a(r_j-c)\bmod p\) with span \(h=\max e_j-\min e_j\le2p/B\).
For \(t=1\) one can send the sole pair to \(\pm1\) and take \(h=2\).

Choose an inverse \(b\) of \(a\bmod p\) and form
\[
 F(X,Y)=\sum_j X^{(r_j-c-b e_j)/p}\,U_j(X)Y^{e_j}.
\]
This integral Laurent polynomial has \(k\) occupied powers of \(Y\) and unit row gcd.
It satisfies the exact identity \(P(x)=x^c F(x^p,x^b)\).

<a id="comparison"></a>
## Comparing the circle with the two-dimensional torus

Choose a minimizing \(H\), which exists since all the counts are positive integers, and put \(T=T(P,p)\).
Write \(G=H(X)F(X,Y)\) and let \(L\) be its largest coefficient modulus.
All \(T\) monomials remain distinct on the diagonal \(Q(x)=G(x^p,x^b)\), because the rows occupy different residues and the base exponents within each row remain distinct.
Thus \(\sup_{\mathbb T}|Q|\ge L>0\) by the coefficient integral, and \(|G|\le TL\) everywhere on the torus.

At fixed \(X\), clip \(\log|G|\) from below at \(\log(uL)\) for \(0<u\le1\).
The squared modulus is a trigonometric polynomial of degree at most \(h\) in the angle of \(Y\).
If nonconstant, its derivative has at most \(2h\) zeros on the circle.
Clipping preserves monotonicity on the intervening arcs, so the variation is at most \(2h\log(T/u)\).
Constant fibers have variation zero, including fibers on which \(G\) vanishes.
Averages on any shifted \(p\)-point grid differ from the circle mean by at most the variation divided by \(p\), by integrating the oscillation on each mesh interval.
At fixed \(X=x^p\) the \(p\) choices of \(x\) give such a grid in \(x^b\).
Integrating over \(X\) gives a clipped diagonal mean at least \(m(G)-(2h/p)\log(T/u)\).

For normalized circle measure \(\mu\), the [measurable-set Turán–Nazarov inequality](../audits/literature/BS13/Yin_U001.md#bs13-u001) gives \(\mu\{|Q|<vL\}\le A v^{1/(T-1)}\), with an absolute \(A\), by applying it to the \(T\) distinct purely imaginary frequencies on \([0,2\pi]\).
Integrating at \(v=u e^{-s}\) over \(s\ge0\) bounds the diagonal clipping loss by \(A(T-1)u^{1/(T-1)}\).
The measures of \(H(x^p)\) and \(H(X)\) agree and cancel.
Taking \(u=p^{-(T-1)}\) gives
\[
 m(P)\ge m(F)-
 \frac{2h\{\log T+(T-1)\log p\}+A(T-1)}p.                          \tag{2}
\]
The [two-row phase argument](Yin_C_G0345_v342.md#grid) motivates this use of the cyclic grid.
The present comparison uses one absolute clipping level and works for unequal row moduli.

<a id="gap"></a>
## Transferring the gap to the original polynomial

The [projected-support theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v338.md#statement) gives either \(m(F)\ge\eta_k>0\) or a factorization of \(F\), up to a signed monomial, into cyclotomics in Laurent monomials.
In the latter case each factor specializes to \(\Phi_j(x^v)\) for some integer \(v\).
A nonconstant monomial gives logarithmic measure zero.
A constant monomial gives an integer value, nonzero because \(P\ne0\).
The specialized product therefore has logarithmic measure zero or at least \(\log2\).

Set \(a_k=\min(\log\theta,\log2,\eta_k/2)\).
When \(0<m(P)<a_k\), (2) must lose at least \(\eta_k/2\).
For fixed \(k\), \(h/p=O_k(p^{-1/t})\) and \(\log T\le T\), so that loss is at most \(C_k T\log(p)/p^{1/t}\).
This proves (1).

<a id="scope"></a>
## A four-class obstruction and the remaining coverage question

For the four reciprocal residues \(\{\pm1,\pm s\}\) with \(s=\lfloor\sqrt p\rfloor\) and \(p\) sufficiently large, no invertible affine dilation puts all four in an interval of width \(W<\sqrt p/4\).
Indeed, if \(a\) is its slope, both \(2a\) and \(2as\) would have representatives of absolute value at most \(W\) by taking differences.
For the least absolute representative \(v\) of \(2a\) we would have \(0<|v|\le W\) and \(s\le |sv|<p/2\).
Hence the least absolute representative of \(2as\) would exceed \(W\).
This example explains a loss in the span estimate alone.
The Mahler discrepancy on such supports could still be smaller.

It remains to control dense primitive rows, growing numbers of occupied residues, or to find another representation with a justified return to the measure of an arbitrary irreducible polynomial.
