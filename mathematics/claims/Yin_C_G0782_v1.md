This holds for every C767 target, without any extra choice of a C778 first-eight seed, sign correction, source row, larger s, dyadic raising or free trace coefficient.

On the full Y_1 primary components the stronger intermediate statement holds: if c_T=2d locally, the reduction of d is divisible by the corresponding factor of Y_half=product_(f|Y_1)f^ceil(e_f/2).

## Complete argument

### NF1. Full actual local rings and a fifth-power estimate

Write T_base=F^e H_f and T=T_base+2V using C767's actual factor-lift reference.
In the actual f-primary localization S_16, H_f is a unit and F^e=-2V/H_f belongs to2S_16.
Thus

    F^(4e)=0, 2F^(3e)=0, 8F^e=0 in S_16.

Binary order at least t of the ACTUAL a_T permits a_T=F^t D+2E in this ring.
The complete fifth-power expansion modulo16 is

    a_T^5=F^(5t)D^5+10F^(4t)D^4E
                         +40F^(3t)D^3E^2 modulo16.

The remaining integer coefficients are80,80,32 and vanish modulo16.
Consequently a_T^5=0 if5t>=4e,4t>=3e and3t>=e.
At X, C762 gives t=A-4,e=A; all three inequalities hold for A>=20.
At each f|B_N, C762 gives t=2A-10,e=2A-1; all three hold for A>=23.
Every original A satisfies these conditions.
On every full Y_1 or B_n^2 component, C767 already proves the actual a_T is even, so a_T^5=0 modulo16 directly.
The remaining issue for F16 is therefore 2c_T^2 at those same actual components.

### NF2. Actual copied factor kills the old odd square

    C_(2^k)(x)=x^(2^k)+4x^(2^(k-1))+2 modulo8.

### NF3. Rounded forward multiplicities and the local reference expansion
