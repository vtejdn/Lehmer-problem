# Relative block degrees and a single field containing all small measures with few nonreal exterior pairs

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact membership over a variable normal base

For any normal algebraic base \(A/\mathbb Q\) in the fixed algebraic closure and any integer \(h\ge0\), let \(U_h(A)\) be the compositum of all finite Galois extensions of \(A\) whose nonabelian chief factors have rank at most \(h\).
The rank of a chief factor \(S^j\) is \(j\).
Fix \(M>0\) and a nonzero algebraic input \(y\) with positive total log measure \(m\le M\) and sufficiently large compressed degree \(d\) in terms of \(M\).
Use the rational label field \(P\), \(u\) canonical blocks of size \(b\) and \(D=(A_b)^u\) as in the [absolute chief-factor calculation](Yin_C_G0901_v107.md#level).
Write \(E\subseteq P\) for the fixed field of the setwise stabilizer of one block, and \(B\) for its rational normal closure (the block permutation field).
Put \(v_A=[AE:A]\), independent of the chosen block since \(A/\mathbb Q\) is normal.
Then for all \(A,h\) with the same original degree cutoff,
\[
                    y\in U_h(A)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
                  P\subseteq A\quad\text{or}\quad v_A\le h.               \tag{1}
\]
When \(P\not\subseteq A\), the nonabelian chief factors in \(\operatorname{Gal}(AP/A)\) coming from \(D\) are exactly \(u/v_A\) factors of rank \(v_A\).
All other nonabelian chief factors have strictly smaller rank.
If \(P\not\subseteq A\), the preceding level isolates the full product:
\[
       \operatorname{Gal}(U_{v_A-1}(A)P/U_{v_A-1}(A))=D.                 \tag{2}
\]
Under the same hypothesis \(P\not\subseteq A\), for every \(h<v_A\), even after adjoining arbitrary solvable steps over \(U_h(A)\), the least allowed bound on single adjunction degrees that can reach \(y\) is exactly \(b\).

<a id="relative"></a>
## Computing the chief ranks from block orbits

The finite-extension and subdirect-product arguments for [the defining class of groups](Yin_C_G0901_v107.md#formation) apply over any base \(A\).
Thus \(U_h(A)\) contains precisely the finite normal extensions of \(A\) with the defining property, and it is closed under adjoining solvable extensions.
It is normal over \(\mathbb Q\), since \(A\) is normal and conjugation transports the whole defining class to itself.
Let \(H=\operatorname{Gal}(AP/A)\) act on \(P\) by restriction.
If \(P\subseteq A\) then the actual input is reachable by a solvable extension by the [solvable kernel above the label field](Yin_C_G0345_v267.md#normal).
Otherwise \(H\) is nontrivial and normal in \(\operatorname{Gal}(P/\mathbb Q)\), so it contains that group's unique minimal normal subgroup \(D\).
Its orbits on the \(u\) blocks all have the same size \(v_A\): the orbit at the chosen block has size \([AE:A]\) by the stabilizer description, and transitivity of the original group transports \(H\)-orbits.
On each such orbit the product of the corresponding \(A_b\)'s is minimal normal in \(H\).
Indeed any normal subgroup in this product is invariant under the inner action of \(D\), hence is a product of individual simple factors; the transitive \(H\)-action requires all or none.
These orbit products commute, and adjoining them one at a time gives the claimed chief factors up through \(D\).

Above \(D\) the kernel of the block permutation action is abelian.
The permutation image of \(H\) embeds as a subdirect product of its images on its individual orbits.
Each image is a subgroup of \(S_{v_A}\); all its nonabelian chief factors have rank \(<v_A\) by the prime-divisor bound for \(v_A!\).
The finite subdirect-product calculation transfers this strict inequality to the entire permutation image.
Thus \(v_A\) is the maximum nonabelian chief-factor rank, proving (1) by normality and the solvable kernel for the full splitting field.

The quotient above \(D\) lies in the defining class for \(U_{v_A-1}(A)\).
Consequently the restriction group on \(P\) over that field lies in \(D\).
It is nontrivial by (1), and is normal in the *rational* label group because \(U_{v_A-1}(A)/\mathbb Q\) is normal.
It therefore contains every factor of \(D\), proving (2) even when \(D\) has several \(H\)-orbits.
Isolating this pure product and applying the [tower intersection and size argument](Yin_C_G0901_v107.md#level) proves the degree assertion.

## The totally real base and the exterior roots of a reciprocal unit

Set \(T=\mathbb Q^{\rm tr}\) and \(\mathcal F=U_1(T)\).
This is a single normal, solvably closed field, obtained by adjoining qualifying *Galois extensions over \(T\)*.
For every large compressed reciprocal unit at bounded measure,
\[
                   y\in\mathcal F\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad B\subset T.
                                                                            \tag{3}
\]
In fact \(P\subset T\) already implies \(B\subset T\), and \(v_T=1\) means \(E\subset T\), hence \(B\subset T\) by normality.
Let \(s\) be the number of complex-conjugate pairs of nonreal exterior conjugates of the compressed unit.
If \(s<b\), then \(B\subset T\) by the [block conjugation count](Yin_C_G0901_v106.md#real).
Thus this fixed field contains every such input, even if its label field is nonreal, its number of blocks is unbounded or its individual trace field is nonreal.
In particular all sufficiently large compressed Salem and complex Salem inputs of bounded measure belong to \(\mathcal F\).

More quantitatively, outside \(\mathcal F\) complex conjugation must move at least two whole blocks, exchanging them.
It moves at least \(b\) pairs of labels in transpositions, so \(s\ge b\).
The radial rank \(r(y)\) of the compressed input also satisfies \(r(y)\ge b-1\).
In the independent signed action the two exchanged coordinate blocks contribute \(b\) fixed dimensions, since the fixed subspace on an exchanged pair is the graph of an isomorphism.
In the oriented alternative the same calculation on the two sum-zero subspaces gives \(b-1\).
These subspaces inject into the actual multiplicative module even when block constants survive. [Signed alternatives](Yin_C_G0345_v274.md#statement) · [Oriented module](Yin_C_G0901_v89.md#oriented)
The fixed dimension of complex conjugation is [the radial rank](Yin_C_G0901_v62.md#modules).
In particular an unrestricted shrinking sequence, if there is a positive gap in \(\mathcal F\), eventually has
\[
           s\ge b,\qquad r(y)\ge b-1,\qquad
                  b\gg\frac{d}{m(y)^2(\log(3d))^{212}}                  \tag{4}
\]
on its compressed inputs.
Here sufficiently small positive measures permit reduction to a reciprocal unit by the elementary extreme-coefficient bound and [Smyth's nonreciprocal lower bound](../audits/literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-statement-and-conventions).
Compression preserves positive total measure and radial rank and its degree then diverges.
The last bound in (4) is the [two-label height estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v264.md#block).

Consequently the full Lehmer gap is equivalent to the conjunction of a gap on \(\mathcal F\) and a gap for compressed reciprocal units with a nonreal block field and satisfying (4) in the large-degree regime.
The condition about nonreal block fields concerns the normal field of the block permutation action.
An unrestricted proof still has to control both classes, including the real Salem branch inside \(\mathcal F\).
