# A single specialization conjecture controls every shrinking factor with growing seed degree

<a id="statement"></a>
## Arbitrary first exponents and offsets

For coprime positive integers \(a,b\) and \(s\ge3\), let \(F=g_s(X^a)+X^b B_s(X^a)\) be the [six-term section](Yin_C_G0345_v544.md#statement).
Write \(\lambda_0(a,s,b)\) for the least maximum norm of a nonzero integer vector orthogonal to \((a,as,b)\).
For every fixed \(L\) there are \(\epsilon_L>0\) and \(s_L\) such that at \(s\ge s_L\), every section with \(\lambda_0\le L\) has \(m(P)\ge\epsilon_L\) for each nontorsion irreducible rational factor \(P\), taken monic.
In particular along **any** sequence \(s\to\infty,\ m(P)\to0\), the shortest relation length tends to infinity, and
\[
                  m(F)\longrightarrow\mu
             =\int_{\mathbb T}\log^+|1+u|\,du>0
\]
with normalized arclength \(du\).
The complementary factors of \(F/P\) then carry total log measure tending to \(\mu\).

Assume Schinzel's rational specialization conjecture for the single fixed polynomial \(H(U,V,W)=U(V-U-1)+W(U-V-UV)\).
There are constants \(c>0\), \(s_0\) such that \(m(P)\ge c\) for every \(s\ge s_0\), **every** coprime \(a,b\ge1\) and every nontorsion irreducible \(P\) of \(F\).
In addition, for all sufficiently large \(s\) and every \(b\ge s(s+1)\), the nontorsion quotient is irreducible whenever it is nonconstant, uniformly even at bounded \(a\).
Along all sequences with \(s\to\infty,\ b/(as^2\log(2s))\to\infty\), that quotient is eventually nonconstant and its logarithmic measure tends to \(\mu\) unconditionally.
The exclusions on the fixed-relation directions and the limit for the parent along a shrinking sequence are unconditional.

<a id="exception"></a>
## Factoring on each fixed relation

Suppose \((u,v,w)\) gives a relation of maximum norm at most \(L\).
At \(s>L\), the possibility \(w=0\) would force \(u=-sv\), hence \(u=v=0\).
Otherwise \(a\mid w\), so \(a\le L\) and \(b=-a(u+vs)/w\).
There are finitely many choices of \(a,u,v,w\) under these restrictions.
For each choice split \(s\) into residue classes modulo \(|w|\), writing \(s=s_*+|w|n\) with \(0\le s_*<|w|\).
In a class containing an admissible section put \(b_*=-a(u+vs_*)/w\in\mathbb Z\) and \(h=-av|w|/w\in\mathbb Z\), so that \(b=b_*+hn\).
Every exponent of \(F\) is now of the form \(c+en\) with \(c,e\) fixed integers.
Replacing \(X^{c+en}\) by \(X^cY^e\) in the six terms gives one fixed integral Laurent polynomial for the class.
It is nonzero on every admitted specialization because \(F(0)=-1\).
The [factor gap for \(G(X,X^n)\)](Yin_C_G0345_v168.md#statement) gives a positive bound for every positive-measure divisor in this class at \(n\ge1\).
Taking the minimum over the finitely many nonempty classes proves the first assertion for \(s>L\) with \(n\ge1\).
It follows in particular that a shrinking sequence eventually leaves each bounded set of relations.

<a id="parent"></a>
## The limiting parent and the conditional gap

By [Lawton's specialization theorem](../audits/literature/Den09/Yin_U002.md#den09-u002), whenever \(\lambda_0\to\infty\) one has \(m(F)\to m_3(H)\), since the specialization differs from \(F\) by the monomial \(X^a\).
For \(|U|=|V|=1\) the two coefficients of \(H\) in \(W\) have the same modulus, namely \(|V-U-1|\), by reversal and complex conjugation.
Jensen integration first in \(W\) and then in \(V\) gives \(m_3(H)=\int_{\mathbb T}\log^+|1+U|\,dU=\mu\).
The simultaneous zeros of the two coefficients on the torus have measure zero, and the logarithms of these nonzero Laurent polynomials are integrable.
Additivity now gives the asserted complement limit.

Under the stated conjecture the [isogeny irreducibility and factorization argument](Yin_C_G0345_v544.md#lift) leaves at most one simple nontorsion factor for directions outside a fixed finite list of rational hyperplanes.
If a uniform positive gap at large \(s\) failed, there would be \(s\to\infty\) with \(m(P)\to0\).
These directions eventually avoid the finite list.
The only nontorsion factor would then be \(P\) with multiplicity one, so that \(m(F)=m(P)\to0\), contrary to the positive limit just proved.
The monic endpoints of the parent ensure there is no contribution from integer content.

For a direct quantitative bound on relation length, when \(w\ne0\) the equality \(a|u+sv|=b|w|\) and \(a\mid w\) give \(\max(|u|,|v|)\ge b/(s+1)\) and \(|w|\ge a\).
When \(w=0\) the norm is at least \(s\).
Thus \(\lambda_0\ge\min(s,\max(a,b/(s+1)))\).
For \(b\ge s(s+1)\) this suffices to avoid the same finite list for large \(s\), irrespective of \(a\).
In the stated long-slope limit it also gives \(m(F)\to\mu>0\), ensuring a nonconstant nontorsion quotient.
Under the conjecture this quotient is irreducible and accounts for the whole measure.
