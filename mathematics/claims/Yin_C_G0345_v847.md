# A square-root cutoff for ordinary good primes on steep sections

<a id="statement"></a>
## The remaining certificate on the steep family

Let \(P_{k,l}=F(X^k,X^l)\) be the [two-pole section](Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#statement), and call \(p\) good for a direction if \(p\nmid B(k,l)\) for the [degree-56 exceptional form](Yin_C_G0345_v812.md#statement).
Take \(M\le k<2M\), \(h=\lceil(\log M)^6\rceil\) and \(l=hk+1\).
For sufficiently large \(M\) the sections are squarefree and their [integer certificates](Yin_C_G0345_v827.md#statement) \(U\) are defined.
Form \(U_{**}\) from the full prime powers of \(U\) by the following tests.

* Retain a good prime if \(p>\sqrt M\) and some critical residue disk of positive even contact has section coordinate of multiplicative order \(>\sqrt M\).
* Retain a bad prime if \(p>M\), \(p\nmid B_{\rm lin}(k,l)\) and some multiple geometric residue root has order \(>C_F M\), with the rational linear part and constant from the [bad-prime cutoff](Yin_C_G0345_v834.md#statement).

Outside \(O_F(M/\log M)\) indices the full discriminant weight at good primes \(p\le\sqrt M\) is \(O_F(\sqrt M)\), and
\[
      \log(U/U_{**})\ll_F M\log M.
\]
For every monic reciprocal factorization, the same \(O_F(M\log M)\) bounds the total logarithmic complementary resultant from contacts failing the applicable prime or order test, including low-order contacts at retained primes.
This error is \(o(d)\) uniformly for irreducible factor degrees \(d\).
Consequently a reducible section among these indices requires
\[
                \log U_{**}\gg_F l(\log\log l)^4/(\log l)^3.
\]

<a id="rectangle"></a>
## Discriminant weights in a rectangle of primitive directions

For \(1\le k\le K,\ 1\le l\le L,\ \gcd(k,l)=1\), fix a prime \(p\le KL\).
After omitting at most \(32(p+1)\) primitive directions, each remaining squarefree section for which \(p\) is good satisfies
\[
              v_p(\operatorname{disc}P_{k,l})\log p\le48\log(2KL).       \tag{1}
\]
Hence, for \(2\le Y\le KL\), outside \(O(Y^2/\log(2Y))\) directions the full weight of all good primes \(p\le Y\) is \(O(\pi(Y)\log(2KL))\) at every squarefree section.
On any primitive graph \(l=l(k)\le(2M)^A\) with fixed \(A>0\) over \(M\le k<2M\), taking \(Y=\sqrt M\) gives the asserted \(O_A(\sqrt M)\) weight outside \(O(M/\log M)\) indices.

<a id="disk"></a>
## The critical value as the slope varies

Fix a good projective slope modulo \(p\), meaning \(\bar B\ne0\) there.
The factor \(2kl\) in \(B\) makes \(p\) odd and both coordinates nonzero, so write \(r=k/l\) and fix an integral lift \(r_0\) of this residue ratio.
The [octic reconstruction](Yin_C_G0345_v812.md#elimination) gives sixteen transverse geometric intersections of \(F=0\) with \(rUF_U+VF_V=0\).
At any such pair \((u,v)\), smoothness and the slope equation force both partial derivatives of \(F\) to be units modulo \(p\).
Let \(\mathcal O\) be the unramified integer ring with residue field \(\mathbb F_p(u,v)\), and use brackets for Teichmüller lifts.
The unit Jacobian lifts the pair uniquely to \((U_0,V_0)\) at \(r_0\) by Hensel's lemma.
The integral implicit function theorem gives a graph \(U=f(V)\) with coefficients integral in \(V-V_0\) on its open residue disk.
In this graph put \(s(V)=Vf'(V)/f(V)\).
Since the second intersection equation on the graph is \(UF_U(r-s(V))=0\), transversality says that \(s'(V_0)\) is a unit.
Integral power-series inversion gives \(V(r)\) in \(r-r_0\) with \(V(r_0)=V_0\), \(s(V(r))=r\) and \(V'(r_0)\) a unit.
Write \(U(r)=f(V(r))\) and set
\[
             H(r)=\log(U(r)/[u])-r\log(V(r)/[v]).
\]
These logarithms converge for \(r-r_0\in p\mathbb Z_p\).

A section residue coordinate \(x\) above \((u,v)\) exists exactly when \(u^l=v^k\), and is then unique by Bézout's identity.
On its disk the coordinate change \(X=[x](V/[v])^{1/l}\) and its inverse preserve distances by their integral expansions with unit linear terms.
The equation of the section there is \(J(V)=0\), where \(1+J(V)=f(V)/([u](V/[v])^r)\).
Its logarithmic derivative is \((s(V)-r)/V\), so \(J'(V(r))=0\) and \(J''(V(r))=(1+J(V(r)))s'(V(r))/V(r)\) is a unit.
Also \(\log(1+J(V(r)))=H(r)\) and the two quantities \(J(V(r)),H(r)\) have the same valuation on these unramified principal units at odd \(p\).
Writing \(w=V-V(r)\) gives \(J(V)=J(V(r))+w^2 D(w)\) with integral coefficients and \(D(0)\) a unit.
The [square-root change of variable](Yin_C_G0345_v845.md#distances) \(w\sqrt{D(w)/D(0)}\) preserves distances, giving two roots of mutual distance valuation \(v_p(H(r))/2\).
For a squarefree section the value is finite and the disk contributes exactly \(v_p(H(r))\log p\) to the full logarithmic discriminant.
Every multiple residue root belongs to one of these compatible pairs by the chain rule, and simple residue roots contribute zero.

<a id="spacing"></a>
## Preparation and spacing of rational slopes

Differentiating the critical value cancels the terms in \(V'\) because \(s(V(r))=r\), giving
\[
     H'(r)=-\log(V(r)/[v]),\qquad H''(r)=-V'(r)/V(r).
\]
Thus \(H(r_0),H'(r_0)\in p\mathcal O\), while \(H''\) has integral power-series coefficients and a unit constant at \(r_0\).
For the coefficient \(a_n\) of \((r-r_0)^n\) in \(H\) this gives \(n(n-1)a_n\in\mathcal O\) for \(n\ge2\).
After substituting \(r=r_0+pT\), all nonconstant terms are divisible by \(p^2\), and the valuations of the coefficients of degree \(n\ge2\) after division by \(p^2\) are at least \(n-2-v_p(n(n-1))\).
This lower bound tends to infinity and is positive for \(n\ge4\), since at most one of \(n,n-1\) is divisible by \(p\) and \(\log_3 n<n-2\) for \(n\ge4\).
For \(n=3\) it is positive when \(p\ge5\) and zero when \(p=3\).
If \(v_p(H(r_0))=1\), the disk therefore has weight \(\log p\) whenever compatible.
Otherwise \(H(r_0+pT)/p^2\) is an integral restricted series whose reduction has degree \(m=2\) for \(p\ge5\), and \(m\in\{2,3\}\) for \(p=3\), since its quadratic coefficient is a unit.

This series factors as a unit reducing to a nonzero scalar times a monic polynomial of degree \(m\).
Indeed take the scalar and monic factors of the reduction, then at each precision divide the reduced error polynomial by the monic factor modulo \(p\).
The quotient corrects the unit and the remainder divided by the scalar corrects the monic factor below its leading term, lifting the product one digit at a time in the complete restricted series ring.
For its integral polynomial roots \(t_i\), counted with multiplicity in an algebraic closure, set \(\lambda_i=r_0+pt_i\).
On the slope disk the valuation is exactly
\[
                 v_p(H(r))=2-m+\sum_{i=1}^m v_p(r-\lambda_i).
\]
For each center omit directions in the disk satisfying \(v_p(k/l-\lambda_i)>\log_p(KL)\).
Two distinct such primitive directions have nonzero determinant \(kl'-k'l\) of absolute value at most \(KL\) and valuation greater than \(\log_p(KL)\), which is impossible.
For \(p\ge5\) at most \(16\cdot2(p-1)\) centers occur across the good slopes, since both projective coordinates are nonzero.
At \(p=3\) at most \(16\cdot3\cdot2\) centers occur, also within \(32(p+1)\).
On all remaining directions each compatible disk costs at most \(3\log(2KL)\), including the shallow case since \(p\le KL\).
Summing over at most sixteen disks proves (1).
The union bound and \(\pi(Y)\ll Y/\log(2Y)\) give the simultaneous estimate.

<a id="applications"></a>
## The steep interval and a two-dimensional family

On the steep interval take \(K=2M,\ L=2Mh+1\).
The full discriminant dominates both the certificate and every actual complementary resultant prime by prime by the [trace and derivative identities](Yin_C_G0345_v833.md#weights).
Discarding all good primes up to \(\sqrt M\) therefore adds only \(O_F(\sqrt M)\) to the discarded weight outside the stated exceptional set.
Use the [low-order estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v833.md#order) for good contacts of order at most \(\sqrt M\) and the [bad-prime estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v834.md#statement) for the other discarded contacts.
Their exceptional sets and errors add within the claimed bounds, and the [degree estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v811.md#degrees) and [certificate lower bound](Yin_C_G0345_v827.md#statement) give the conclusions for factor degrees and reducibility.

In the box \(M\le k<2M,\ Mh\le l<2Mh\), take \(Y=M\sqrt h\) in the rectangle bound.
Outside \(O(M^2h/\log M)\) primitive directions, the good primes up to \(Y\) contribute \(O_F(M\sqrt h)\) to the full logarithmic discriminant at squarefree sections.
Here \(d\gg l(\log\log l/\log l)^3\gg M(\log M)^3(\log\log M)^3\) uniformly for irreducible factor degrees by the same degree estimate, so the contribution is \(o(d)\).
