## 1. All isolated union edges at any fixed overlap

Fix an integer b>=1, put h=2b, k=n-b>=1 and retain n>=3.
Normalize the left endpoint by x=beta^-k a and keep the right endpoint c.
The full middle endpoint equation is

    x+theta+beta^h c=0,
    theta=sum_(j=0)^(h-1)eta_j beta^j.                  (1)

For h>4 different middle words can ordinarily have the same endpoint; no basis uniqueness is assumed.
As k varies, the bipartite union has vertex sets two copies of D and has an edge(x,c) exactly when an original h-step path takes c to -x.
Necessity follows by attaching the two tails of any full returned word.
Conversely choose actual finite paths x to zero and zero to c, extend them to the same sufficiently large k by zero digits, reverse/negate the first into its ascending tail and reverse the second into its ascending tail.
Equation(1) then makes the full word return.
Every path between core endpoints stays in D because reachability and co-return words can be attached.
Arbitrarily long paths via zero show that both copies of each core vertex occur.
Thus this is the complete original incidence graph, not a real-strip relaxation.

A core state has exactly one legal successor if and only if |q_+|>b0.
The forward implication outside that strip is L319.
Inside it, the zero digit and at least one digit of opposite sign are both legal: if0<=beta q_+<B, then beta q_+-1 lies in(-B,B), and the negative case is symmetric.
Every original digit preserves the stable strip.
The equality |q_+|=b0 is not attained in D, since it would force q=+/-B/beta with stable magnitude beta S>S.
On the one-successor region the forced map is INJECTIVE: its positive branch beta q-1 has positive expanding value in(B-1,B), its negative branch beta q+1 has negative value in(-B,1-B), and each branch is injective.

Let S_i be the finite nonempty set of core endpoints reachable from c in i steps.
If S_(i+1) is a singleton, every member of S_i has exactly one successor, and injectivity on that region makes S_i a singleton too.
It follows by backward induction that a singleton h-step endpoint set has exactly one entire h-digit path.
Every step is forced, and the sign is preserved throughout the forced segment.
Therefore this word is either(+1)^h or(-1)^h.
This excludes hidden merging of several middle words even when h exceeds the polynomial degree.

The exact minus-word isolated edges are obtained from the core corner

    C_h^-={c in D:c_+>B-beta^-h, c_-<beta-S},
    y=beta^h c-(1+beta+...+beta^(h-1)), x=-y.           (2)

Indeed the first inequality is equivalent to all first h forward states remaining in the positive forced region.
For that path, q_i=B+beta^i(c-B) and

    q_(i,-)=-S+beta^-i(c_-+S).

A core state q has exactly one predecessor with digit-1 whenever q_-<-B: the predecessor has stable value beta(q_--eta), which is below-S for eta=0 or+1, while the actual-1 predecessor exists.
More generally unique incoming states/paths are the corresponding forward statements under the accepted reciprocal involution J=beta^-1 iota.
Thus all h reverse steps are forced if and only if q_(1,-)<-B, equivalently c_-<beta-S.
For necessity, an isolated union edge has one h-step predecessor as well as one successor; the same no-merging lemma applied through J forces every reverse step, so this condition is necessary too.
The plus-word corner is exactly C_h^+=-C_h^- with global sign reversal of its edge.
These two corners classify ALL isolated edges of the union.

## 3. A uniform ORIGINAL mass bound for the entire isolated collection

At a fixed actual n,b let I_(n,b) be the union of full-word components whose endpoint edge is isolated in the INFINITE union above.
An edge isolated only in this finite graph is not silently included.
Put M=pi_(n,b)(I_(n,b)), using the complete returned law.
Section1 shows that every word in this collection has its middle h digits all+1 or all-1.

Let Z be the original outside endpoint sum over the PARTICULAR gapped support consisting of the k digits on either side of that middle block, in their unchanged original positions.
Write r(v)=Pr(Z=v).
Every ternary digit is a difference of two independent fair binary digits, so if mu is the binary endpoint law on this same support, then

    r(v)=sum_q mu(q)mu(q-v)<=sum_q mu(q)^2=r(0),
    r(v)=r(-v), r(0)>0.                                (9)

This is the existing L353 gapped-autocorrelation mechanism, with every outside word counted.
No replacement by p_(2k) or an iid spatial approximation occurs.
Set v=beta^k(1+beta+...+beta^(h-1)).

They are disjoint events.
Consequently the COMPLETE conditional mass of the two constant words, and hence M, satisfies

    M<= [2*4^-h r(v)]/[2^-h r(0)+2*4^-h r(v)]
      <= alpha_b:=2/(2^h+2), h=2b.                    (10)

This applies at EVERY allowed finite n, even if no isolated union edge has yet appeared.
It bounds the combined mass of all persistent isolated components simultaneously, not just individual selected-word probabilities.

    every b>=1: alpha_b<=1/3, alpha_b->0 as b->infinity. (11)

The last limit is a bound on the isolated mass only; it supplies neither a variable-overlap sampler nor a favorable growing-b midpoint-energy budget.

## 4. Their internal energy and component defect are both paid

For each isolated endpoint edge the middle word is unique, and the complete left/right tail word fibers have a PRODUCT of their original normalized endpoint laws.
C170's two conditional expectations resample exactly one of these tail fibers while the other is fixed.
On this product component they commute, their intersection is constants, and the residual one-tail modes have eigenvalue1/2 while interaction modes have eigenvalue0.
Equivalently the elementary two-factor variance decomposition gives

    Var_(pi_i)(f)<=2 D_i(f)                             (12)

on every isolated full-word component, with zero variance when its support is a singleton.
The coefficient2 is uniform in n,b and in both endpoint multiplicities for the AUXILIARY update clock.
Its digit-work normalization divides energy by n+b and changes the coefficient to2(n+b); it does not provide a free chronological operation.

At the given n,b partition the whole original word space into these individual isolated components and ONE remaining block J.
The latter is nonempty because the zero edge is not isolated in the union: zero has at least two legal successors, and the no-merging lemma prevents a later singleton endpoint set.
The remaining block is a union of actual full-word components and is closed under the original K_(n,b).
Let the isolated component masses be m_i, sum_i m_i=M, and let pi_J be the conditional original law on J.
By L410's exact component-mean calculation, this COARSENED partition has least defect

    chi_iso=M(1-M)<=alpha_b(1-alpha_b)<1/4.             (13)

Indeed every union not containing J has mass at most M<=1/3, and its variance q(1-q) increases with q there; the union of all isolated components attains M(1-M).
A union containing J has the same variance as its isolated complement.
This chi_iso is a lower bound on the FULL graph's chi, not an upper bound on it.
Other components can increase the latter.
For a fixed constructed sign pair already present, let q_x,q_c be its chosen endpoint-word probabilities from(8) and put ell_(k,b)=4^-h q_x q_c/D_k.
Each member has mass at least ell_(k,b), while their combined mass is at most M<=alpha_b<1/2.
The actual full component defect therefore has the paid lower bound

    chi_full>=2 ell_(k,b)(1-2 ell_(k,b))>0.             (14)

This follows by taking that pair's union and using monotonicity of u(1-u) below1/2.
It is an all-late bound for this fixed pair, but retains the unknown original D_k rather than claiming a mean-independent positive numerical constant.
It neither forces the quarter threshold nor controls the total mass of other components.

Let D_I=sum_i m_i D_i and D_J be the energy conditional on J, so D=D_I+(1-M)D_J.
Full variance decomposition and(12)--(13) give the UNCONDITIONAL actual-law bound

    Var_pi(f)<=2D_I(f)+(1-M)Var_(pi_J)(f)
                         +M(1-M)osc(f)^2.             (15)

Thus all internal energies and all between-block variation contributed by the persistent isolated collection have explicit costs.
Only the actual remainder term is left unestimated.
For b=2 the paid defect is at most8/81, which is strictly below1/8 and below1/4.
The persistence construction alone therefore does not force L410's defect to its trivial ceiling.

For clarity, if a separately proved remainder inequality had constants B_J and epsilon_J, then(15) would yield

    Var_pi(f)<=max{2,B_J}D(f)
        +[epsilon_J+alpha_b(1-alpha_b)]osc(f)^2.        (16)

This follows by multiplying the remainder inequality by1-M, without renormalizing any original mass.
At b=2, any fixed epsilon_J<49/324 would leave a strict margin below1/4.
No such uniform remainder inequality is assumed or proved here.
Formula(16) is the precise consumer of the costs already established, not a replacement of the actual operator by a refreshed or uniform law.
