# A square-root degree bound for prime-residue representations in a small Mahler shell

<a id="statement"></a>
## Prime modulus, support lattice and original degree

Let \(P\) be a monic irreducible unit polynomial of degree \(D\) whose support differences have gcd one, and write \(m=m(P)>0\) for its logarithmic Mahler measure.
Fix \(k\ge2\), let \(\eta_k>0\) be the [free projected-support gap](Yin_C_G0345_v338.md#statement), and let \(\theta>1\) solve \(\theta^3-\theta-1=0\).
Assume \(m<\min(\log\theta,\eta_k)\).
Suppose a product of \(P\) with cyclotomic polynomials occupies exactly \(k\) classes modulo an odd prime \(p\).
Use the [centered lift](Yin_C_G0345_v343.md#lift) after [removing common row content](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v146.md#coverage), and suppose its fast width \(h\) satisfies \(24h<p\).
This holds for all sufficiently large \(p\) at fixed \(k\).

Let \(B\) be the [factor of the lift carrying \(P\)](Yin_C_G0345_v347.md#statement) under the diagonal \((x^p,x^b)\) with \(0<b<p\).
Write \(s\) for its fast width and \(d\) for the exponent span of its diagonal.
Its support differences generate a rank-two lattice \(\Lambda\), of index \(I=[\mathbb Z^2:\Lambda]\).
Set
\[
                       Z=\frac{I p}{s}>24.
\]
There are absolute constants \(A\ge1\), \(c>0\) for which every such representation satisfies
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \eta_k-m
 &\le \frac{2}{Z}\left(\frac{2D}{Z-10}+1\right)
                        \bigl(2+\log(AZ/2)\bigr),                       \tag{1}\\
 m&\ge
 \frac{c(1-10/Z)}{\bigl(2D/(Z-10)+2\bigr)^2}.                         \tag{2}
 \end{aligned}
\]
These estimates use the degree and measure of the original input and allow cyclotomic multipliers of arbitrary degree.

Put \(t=\lfloor k/2\rfloor\).
Throughout the shell \(m\le\eta_k/2\) under the above hypotheses,
\[
                  I p^{1/t}\ll_k \sqrt{D\log(2D)} .                    \tag{3}
\]
Along a sequence of these representations with \(m\to0\) there is also the bound
\[
                  I p^{1/t}\ll_k D\sqrt m.                            \tag{4}
\]
In particular, at two or three occupied classes the admissible primes in the half-gap shell satisfy \(p\ll_k \sqrt{D\log(2D)}/I\).
The inequalities restrict primes at which a representation can occur.
They leave the supply of such representations at smaller growing primes unresolved.

<a id="lattice"></a>
## An enlarged grid in the lattice generated by the support

The [carrier theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v347.md#factor) gives \(m(B)\ge\eta_k\), \(1\le s\le h\), and a diagonal \(J\) that after multiplication by a signed monomial equals \(P C_0\) with \(C_0\) a product of cyclotomics.
All its exponents \(pi+bj\) for support points \((i,j)\) of \(B\) are distinct because \(s<p\) and \(p\nmid b\).
If the support were collinear, its one-variable character would specialize to a nonconstant power, giving \(m(B)=m(J)=m\), contrary to \(m<\eta_k\).
This establishes rank two.

The gcd \(g\) of all exponent differences of \(J\) is one.
Otherwise the monomial-normalized \(J\) has the form \(J_0(x^g)\) with \(g>1\), where \(J_0\) is monic integral with unit constant term and \(m(J_0)=m>0\).
A monic irreducible noncyclotomic factor \(W\) of \(J_0\) then has \(W(x^g)\mid P C_0\).
Every root of \(W(x^g)\) is nontorsion, so the factorization on the right forces \(W(x^g)=P\), contradicting the support-gcd assumption.
Thus the image of \(\Lambda\) under the homomorphism \((i,j)\mapsto pi+bj\) is \(\mathbb Z\).

Choose a lattice basis \((a,0),(c_0,v)\) with \(a,v>0\).
Here \(v\) generates the projection of \(\Lambda\) onto its second coordinate and \(a\) generates its horizontal kernel.
Then \(a v=I\), and, up to a Laurent monomial,
\[
    B(X,Y)=G(X^a,X^{c_0}Y^v),\qquad
    J(x)=G(x^q,x^\ell),\qquad
    q=pa,\quad \ell=p c_0+b v,\quad \gcd(q,\ell)=1 .
\]
The gcd is one by the preceding image calculation.
Substitution in these lattice coordinates preserves the independent Mahler measure because the surjective homomorphism \((X,Y)\mapsto(X^a,X^{c_0}Y^v)\) of compact tori sends Haar probability to Haar probability.
The support differences of \(G\) span \(\mathbb Z^2\), and its fast width \(w=s/v\) gives \(q/w=Z\).
The integer \(q\) may be composite.
For fixed \(x^q\), the \(q\) possible values of \(x^\ell\) form a full rotated \(q\)-point grid because of the gcd condition.

<a id="area"></a>
## From the cyclotomic cofactor to a term budget

Let \(V\) be the Newton area and \(T\) the number of terms of \(G\).
The map \((i,j)\mapsto(qi+\ell j,j)\) has determinant \(q\) and sends its Newton polygon into a rectangle of widths \(d,w\), hence \(V\le d/Z\).
The support of \(G\) can be triangulated using all \(T\) support points as vertices.
Start with a triangulation using the extreme vertices of the polygon and insert the other points on edges or in triangles.
Each insertion increases the number of triangles by at least one, so there are at least \(T-2\) triangles.
Every nondegenerate lattice triangle has area at least \(1/2\), proving \(T\le2V+2\).
All \(T\) terms survive on the diagonal, since any two equal diagonal exponents would differ by a multiple of \(q\) in the fast direction whereas \(w<q\).

The Laurent polynomial \(G\) is rationally irreducible, since a factorization would pull back to a factorization of \(B\), preserving the terms of each factor.
Its diagonal at the coprime weights \(q,\ell\) has precisely \(P\) and the cyclotomic cofactor \(C_0\).
An expanding root of \(P\) exists because \(m>0\) and \(P\) is monic.
The [three-intersection torsion estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v349.md#statement) therefore gives \(d-D=\deg C_0\le10V\).
It applies also to negative \(\ell\), by its Laurent formulation.
Together these estimates give
\[
                d\le\frac{D}{1-10/Z},\qquad
                T\le\frac{2d}{Z}+2\le\frac{2D}{Z-10}+2.              \tag{5}
\]

<a id="clipping"></a>
## The lower comparison uses the oscillation of the carrier

Take \(L>0\) to be the largest coefficient modulus of \(G\).
On the torus \(|G|\le TL\), while its univariate diagonal has supremum at least \(L\) since each of its coefficients survives.
At fixed first variable clip \(\log|G|\) from below at \(\log(uL)\), \(0<u\le1\).
As in the [cyclic clipping estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v343.md#comparison), the variation in the fast angle is at most \(2w\log(T/u)\).
Indeed the derivative of its squared modulus before clipping has at most \(2w\) zeros unless it is identically zero, and clipping preserves monotonicity on each intervening arc.
The discrepancy between a rotated grid mean and the circle mean of the clipped function is at most this variation divided by \(q\).
For the actual diagonal, the [measurable-set Turán–Nazarov bound](../audits/literature/BS13/Yin_U001.md#bs13-u001) gives normalized sublevel measure at most \(A y^{1/(T-1)}\) at threshold \(yL\), with an absolute \(A\ge1\).
The \(T\) distinct integer frequencies, the surviving coefficient of size \(L\), and \(T\ge3\) verify the inputs.
Integrating this bound on the levels \(y=u e^{-r}\) for \(r\ge0\) pays at most \(A(T-1)u^{1/(T-1)}\) to undo diagonal clipping.
The clipped full-torus log majorizes the unclipped one.
Consequently
\[
 m(B)-m
 \le \frac{2}{Z}\log(T/u)+A(T-1)u^{1/(T-1)} .
\]
Use \(u=(2/(AZ))^{T-1}\), which lies in \((0,1)\).
The resulting right side is \((2/Z)\{\log T+(T-1)(1+\log(AZ/2))\}\).
Using \(\log T\le T-1\), \(m(B)\ge\eta_k\), and (5) proves (1).

For (2), let \(\alpha\) be a root of \(P\).
This nontorsion algebraic unit has degree \(D\) and total log measure \(m\).
The actual \(J\), shifted to minimum exponent zero, is a nonzero rational annihilator of \(\alpha\), of width \(d\) and \(T\) terms.
For its nonzero coefficients \(c_i\), set \(\rho=(\sum|c_i|)^2/\sum|c_i|^2\le T\).
The [quadratic sparse-divisor bound](Yin_C_G0345_v186.md#statement) gives \(d m /D\ge c/\rho^2\ge c/T^2\) for an absolute \(c>0\), allowing exactly these rational coefficients and unrestricted cofactor.
Substituting both bounds from (5) proves (2).
This second estimate uses the original vanishing at \(\alpha\), in addition to the cofactor and area constraints.

<a id="consequences"></a>
## The prime window for shrinking inputs

When \(m\le\eta_k/2\), the left side of (1) is at least \(\eta_k/2\).
For \(Z\) sufficiently large in terms of \(k\), the \(+1\) on its right contributes at most \(\eta_k/4\).
Thus \(D\gg_k Z^2/\log(2Z)\).
This implies \(Z\ll_k D\) first, then \(Z\ll_k\sqrt{D\log(2D)}\), enlarging constants for bounded \(Z\).
Also (2), with \(Z>24\), implies \(2D/(Z-10)+2\gg 1/\sqrt m\).
For all sufficiently small \(m\) the left side's additive two can be absorbed, giving \(Z\ll D\sqrt m\).

The centered construction has \(s\le h\ll_k p^{1-1/t}\), and hence \(Z\gg_k I p^{1/t}\).
Combining these inequalities gives (3) and (4).
For \(k=2,3\), the [opposite representatives](Yin_C_G0345_v343.md#lift) give \(h\le2\).
In either case the conclusion applies even if unboundedly many cyclotomic factors were used to construct the original multiple.
