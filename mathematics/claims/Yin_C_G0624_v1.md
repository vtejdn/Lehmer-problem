## Statement

Use the actual trace domain of [C153v2](Yin_C_G0153_v2.md#exact-reciprocal-phase-and-trace-domain): P is monic irreducible reciprocal nontorsion integral with constant coefficient1 and degree d=2r, K=Q(alpha), delta=alpha+alpha^(-1), F=Q(delta) is totally real of degree r, and P(z)=z^r T(z+z^(-1)) for its actual monic integral minimal T.

Assume only f=2c^2-3a^5=0.
No second residual equation, canonical Haar condition or unsaturation assumption is used in this question.

Let phi be an irreducible factor of T modulo5, of degree h, and let xi be any of its geometric roots in an algebraic closure of F5.
Suppose C2(xi) is a Frobenius conjugate xi^(5^j) for some j>=0.
Then such a phi cannot occur.
Equivalently, no actual residue orbit of T modulo5 can be preserved by C2.
This includes the rational fixed trace classes 2 and -1 from C644, and also nonrational classes.


Equivalently, for any actual residue xi of delta at a prime over5, choose a nonzero root z of Z^2-xi Z+1 in an algebraic closure of F5 and let n be its multiplicative order.
The finite order n is prime to5 and is unchanged by z to z^(-1).
Then no such actual residue order can satisfy

    2=+5^j or -5^j modulo n for any j>=0.

The criterion excludes n=1,3,7,11, among other possible orders.
It does not exclude n=13, whose subgroup generated by5 and-1 is {1,5,8,12}.
An excluded residue condition is not an assertion that any other original input exists.
The criterion forces n odd; no even-order exclusion is inferred from it.
All actual primes, repeated reduced roots and original residue degrees are retained, with no unramifiedness or power-order maximality assumption.

## All actual primes, roots and multiplicities

For each actual prime p of F over5 let E_p=v_p(5), f_p=[k_p:F5], and let phi_p be the minimal polynomial of delta_bar over F5, with degree h_p dividing f_p.

    T=product_(p|5) T_p,
    T_p modulo5=phi_p^(E_p f_p/h_p).

The proof is precisely the complete lattice-filtration argument of C191: multiplication by delta on the free Z5 lattice O_(F_p), followed by its E_p maximal-ideal quotients, each of dimension f_p over F5.

Thus for a cluster of actual primes with the same phi of degree h, put m_p=f_p/h and n_p=E_p m_p.
Each geometric root of phi occurs among the local polynomial roots from p exactly n_p times.
Every actual prime and repeated reduced root is retained.

Use H_h(X)=C_(5^h)(X)-X, with the usual integral trace polynomials C_n(z+z^(-1))=z^n+z^(-n).
The recurrence proves C_5 composed h times equals C_(5^h), and modulo5 H_h=X^(5^h)-X with derivative -1.
At every prime in this cluster, the accepted complete-field Hensel theorem gives a unique integral eta_p congruent to delta and satisfying H_h(eta_p)=0.
The actual delta differs from eta_p, since equality would give alpha^(5^h)=alpha or alpha^(-1), contradicting nontorsion.
Consequently

    j_p=v_p(delta-eta_p)>0,
    t_p=j_p/E_p>0,
    J=sum_(p in cluster) n_p t_p
     =sum_(p in cluster) m_p j_p

are finite, and J is a positive integer.
Every Q5 embedding preserves the extended absolute value.
By simple-root uniqueness in a common finite complete field, all local roots with a fixed geometric residue xi have the SAME corresponding periodic lift eta_xi, and a root arising from p has distance exactly t_p from that lift.

No unramifiedness of the actual primes is assumed.

## Maximal distance and the contradiction

Choose p with maximal t_p in the cluster, and one actual conjugate delta_*=eta+w above the geometric residue xi, where t=v_5(w)=max t_p>0.
Both C2 and C5 commute with C_(5^h), so their values on eta are the unique periodic lifts of their corresponding residues.
C5(eta) has residue xi^5 in the same phi cluster.
By the assumed C2 orbit preservation, C2(eta) also has residue in that same cluster.
The assumption implies xi!=0, since C2(0)=-2 is not in the Frobenius orbit of0.
Thus eta is a5-adic unit.

The exact expansion is

    C5(eta+w)-C5(eta)
     =5(eta^4-3eta^2+1)w
      +5(2eta^3-3eta)w^2
      +5(2eta^2-1)w^3+5eta w^4+w^5.

Every term has valuation greater than t.
All roots of T with residue xi^5 have distances t_p<=t from C5(eta), while every nonmatching factor is a unit.
The complete root product therefore gives

    v_5(T(C5(delta_*)))=J,
    C=v_5(c)=J-1.

At C2, the displacement is 2eta*w+w^2.
Its first coefficient is a5-adic unit, so its valuation is exactly t.
Every root matching the residue of C2(eta) has distance at most t from that lift.
The complete product inequality consequently gives

    A=v_5(a)=v_5(T(C2(delta_*)))>=J,

because the divisor2 is a unit.
The equation f=0 is valid at this same original conjugate, and its5-adic valuation is 2C=5A because2 and3 are units.
Hence

    2(J-1)=5A>=5J,

which is impossible for positive J.
This excludes every such actual residue cluster without dropping original multiplicities or changing the underlying field.

## Equivalent root-order screen and exact limits

For any residue xi choose a nonzero root z of Z^2-xi Z+1 in an algebraic closure of F5, and let n be its multiplicative order.
The order n is finite and prime to5, and the two roots z,z^(-1) have the same order.
The equality C2(xi)=xi^(5^j) is equivalent to

    z^2+z^(-2)=z^(5^j)+z^(-5^j),

which, by the same monic quadratic with constant coefficient1, is equivalent to z^2=z^(5^j) or z^2=z^(-5^j).
Thus the exact condition is

    2=+5^j or -5^j modulo n for some j>=0.

Every actual residue order n with that property is excluded under f=0.
The condition itself forces n odd; no even-order exclusion is inferred from it.
For example n=1,3,7,11 satisfy the condition, by the exact congruences modulo1, 5=2 modulo3, 5^4=2 modulo7 and -5^4=2 modulo11.
For n=13, the subgroup generated by5 and-1 is {1,5,8,12}, so this criterion does not exclude that order.
The latter is only a limit of this test, not an existence claim for an original input.
Different phi factors permuted nontrivially by C2, higher residue-degree behavior outside this condition, original realization and the unrestricted Mahler gap remain unresolved.
