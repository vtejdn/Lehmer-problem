# Thin families of integer Salem seeds with uniform factor measures at every delay

<a id="statement"></a>
## Factor measures and irreducibility

For integers \(a\ge3,\ n\ge7\) and either sign \(\epsilon\), let \(r=r_{a,n,\epsilon}>1\) be the exterior Salem root of \(G_a(X)=X^{n+1}-aX^n+\epsilon(aX-1)\), and let \(P_{a,n,\epsilon}\) be its minimal polynomial.
Write \(R=R_{a,n,\epsilon}\) for the largest integer \(q\ge1\) such that the positive radical \(r^{1/q}\) belongs to \(\mathbb Q(r)\).
Set \(H(a)=\operatorname{lcm}_{i=-1,0,1}((a+i)/\operatorname{rad}(a+i))\), where the radical of an integer is the product of its distinct prime divisors.

For \(Q=6^j\) or \(Q=2^j3^{6j}\), \(j\ge1\), define three nested sets of seeds.
Let \(\mathcal S_Q\) consist of all \(a=Qb,\ 1\le b\le256Q\), for which no prime \(v\ge5\) has \(v^2\mid(a-1)a(a+1)\).
Retain in \(\mathcal A_Q\) those \(a\in\mathcal S_Q\) with \(R_{a,n,\epsilon}\mid H(a)\) for every \(n\ge7\) and both signs.
Put \(\mathcal A_Q^\circ=\{a\in\mathcal A_Q:a+1\text{ is not a square}\}\).
Along both sequences all three sets have cardinality asymptotic to
\[
                 256Q\prod_{v\ {\rm prime},\,v\ge5}(1-3/v^2).          \tag{1}
\]
Moreover \(|\mathcal S_Q\setminus\mathcal A_Q|\le\exp(O(\sqrt{\log Q}))\) and \(|\mathcal A_Q\setminus\mathcal A_Q^\circ|\le136\).

For \(Q=6^j\), all sufficiently large \(j\) and every \(a\in\mathcal A_Q\), one has \(R\le C\) at every delay and sign for an effective absolute constant \(C\).
Consequently every noncyclotomic irreducible factor of every \(G_a(X^k)\), \(k\ge1\), has logarithmic Mahler measure at least \(\log(a-1)/C\).
For \(a\in\mathcal A_Q^\circ\) one has \(R\mid3\) at every delay and sign, so one can use \(C=3\).
The earlier divisor bound \(\log r/H(a)\) tends to zero uniformly on \(\mathcal S_Q\).

For \(Q=2^j3^{6j}\), all sufficiently large \(j\) and every \(a\in\mathcal A_Q^\circ\), one has \(R=1\) at **every** delay and both signs.
Thus \(P_{a,n,\epsilon}(X^k)\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\) for every \(k\ge1\).
It is the unique noncyclotomic irreducible factor of \(G_a(X^k)\) and its logarithmic Mahler measure is exactly \(\log r>\log(a-1)\).
Here too \(\log r/H(a)\to0\) uniformly on \(\mathcal S_Q\).

For the balanced sequence \(Q=6^j\), call \((a,n)\) exceptional if \(P_{a,n,\epsilon}(X^k)\) is reducible for at least one sign \(\epsilon\) and at least one integer \(k\ge1\).
For \(\mathcal B_Q\) equal to any of the three displayed sets, let \(D_Q(M,N)\) count the exceptional pairs with \(a\in\mathcal B_Q,\ M<n\le M+N\).
Then
\[
 \lim_{\min(j,N)\to\infty}\sup_{M\ge6}\frac{D_Q(M,N)}{N|\mathcal B_Q|}=0,
 \qquad
 \lim_{j\to\infty}\sup_{N\ge1}\frac{D_Q(6,N)}{N|\mathcal B_Q|}=0.      \tag{2}
\]
The translates and lengths here are integers.
At every nonexceptional pair each dilation has the exact measure and unique noncyclotomic factor described above.

<a id="seeds"></a>
## Counting the seeds and removing possible square radicals

Write \(K=256Q\).
For each prime \(v\ge5\) the three integers \(Qb+i\), \(i=-1,0,1\), have distinct zero classes modulo \(v\).
Hence \(v^2\) divides their product precisely on three classes of \(b\bmod v^2\), since \(Q\) is invertible modulo \(v^2\).
For primes through a fixed \(Y\), the Chinese remainder theorem gives the product of their individual surviving fractions, with an error tending to zero as \(K\to\infty\) independently of the residues of \(Q\).
Primes \(v>Y\) can eliminate at most a further fraction
\[
                   3\sum_{v>Y}v^{-2}+3\pi(17Q)/K
\]
by the [square-divisor sieve](Yin_C_G0345_v712.md#seeds), since only \(v\le\sqrt{QK+1}<17Q\) occur.
Here \(\pi(x)=o(x)\) follows already by counting primes larger than a fixed \(z\) among residues coprime to \(\prod_{p\le z}p\).
The upper limiting fraction is at most \(\prod_{p\le z}(1-1/p)\), whose reciprocal dominates \(\sum_{m\le z}1/m\).
Letting \(Y\to\infty\) proves (1) for \(\mathcal S_Q\), with positive product by convergence of the sum of \(v^{-2}\).
The [all-delay exceptional-seed count](Yin_C_G0345_v703.md#seeds) through \(256Q^2\) gives the claimed bound on \(\mathcal S_Q\setminus\mathcal A_Q\).

An integer \(a+1=w^2\) in the progression gives \(1\le w<17Q\) and \(w^2\equiv1\bmod Q\).
Modulo the exact power of three in \(Q\) there are two possible residues, since three cannot divide both \(w-1\) and \(w+1\).
Modulo \(2^j\) there are at most four, since for \(j\ge3\) one of the even neighbors of odd \(w\) has valuation exactly one, forcing \(w\equiv\pm1\bmod 2^{j-1}\).
The cases \(j\le2\) satisfy the same count.
There are at most eight residues modulo \(Q\), each contributing at most seventeen positive \(w\) in this range.
This proves the bound \(136\), completes (1), and the [necessary square condition at even seeds](Yin_C_G0345_v716.md#squares) now excludes \(2\mid R\) for every \(a\in\mathcal A_Q^\circ\), at every delay and sign.

<a id="powers"></a>
## Bounding the radical index and excluding powers of three

Every \(a\in\mathcal S_Q\) has \(Q/6\mid a/\operatorname{rad}(a)\mid H(a)\), while all primes of \(H(a)\) belong to \(\{2,3\}\).
The [root and cofactor classification](Yin_C_G0345_v681.md#roots) gives \(a-1<r<a+1\) and \(d=[\mathbb Q(r):\mathbb Q]\ge n-1\).
It follows that \(\log r/H(a)\le6\log(256Q^2+1)/Q\).
For \(Q=6^j\) and \(p=2,3\) one has \(v_p(a)\log p\ge j\log2\ge\frac18\log(a+1)\) for all sufficiently large \(j\).
The [prime-power bound (3)](Yin_C_G0345_v715.md#statement) therefore bounds any power of \(p\) dividing \(R\) by an effective absolute constant.
Taking the product for \(p=2,3\) gives the claimed uniform \(C\) on \(\mathcal A_Q\).
More generally, whenever \(R\mid H(a)\), all primes of \(H(a)\) lie in a fixed finite set \(S\), and each such \(p\) has \(p^{v_p(a-c)}\ge(a+1)^\theta\) for some \(c\in\{0,1,-1\}\), the identical argument gives \(R\le C(\theta)^{|S|}\).

For \(e=1,2\), suppose \(3^e\mid R\) and set \(\gamma=r^{1/3^e}\in\mathbb Q(r)\).
The [prime-power Frobenius congruence](Yin_C_G0345_v698.md#frobenius) at \(3\mid a\) gives \(Y=\gamma^{n+1}\equiv\epsilon\bmod3\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(r)}\), since the reduced minimal polynomial divides \(X^{n+1}-\epsilon\).
For every valuation \(v\) over three normalized by \(v(3)=1\), cubing a unit of the form \(\epsilon+x\) with \(v(x)\ge1\) gives \(v((\epsilon+x)^3-\epsilon)=1+v(x)\) by the binomial theorem.
Applying this \(e\) times and using \(Y^{3^e}-\epsilon=r^{n+1}-\epsilon=a(r^n-\epsilon r)\) gives \(v(Y-\epsilon)\ge v_3(a)-e\).
If \(v_3(a)\ge e\), the nonzero algebraic integer \(Y-\epsilon\) consequently belongs to \(3^{v_3(a)-e}\mathcal O_{\mathbb Q(r)}\).
As in the [odd-prime norm bound](Yin_C_G0345_v716.md#odd), precisely one conjugate of \(\gamma\) is exterior.
Thus \(|\operatorname{Norm}(Y-\epsilon)|\le2^d\gamma^{n+1}\), and \((n+1)/d\le4/3\) gives
\[
                   3^{v_3(a)-e}
                       <2(a+1)^{4/(3^{e+1})}.                         \tag{3}
\]
For \(Q=6^j\), (3) at \(e=2\) would imply \(3^{j-2}<2(256\cdot6^{2j}+1)^{4/27}\), impossible for all sufficiently large \(j\) since \(27\log3>8\log6\).
For \(Q=2^j3^{6j}\), (3) at \(e=1\) would imply \(3^{6j-1}<2(256Q^2+1)^{4/9}\), again impossible for all sufficiently large \(j\) since \(6\log3>8\log2\).
These exclusions hold over the entire progression before the seed sieve.
After the two seed exclusions they leave \(R\mid3\) on the first sequence and \(R=1\) on the second.
The [least factor measure formula](Yin_C_G0345_v205.md#statement) gives \(\log r/\gcd(k,R)\) at dilation \(k\) and irreducibility exactly when \(\gcd(k,R)=1\).
The removed factors of \(G_a\) are linear with roots \(1\) or \(-1\) and contribute only roots of unity after substitution.

<a id="rectangles"></a>
## Translating the delay interval on the balanced sequence

The seeds outside \(\mathcal A_Q^\circ\) in \(\mathcal S_Q\) contribute \(o(Q)N\) pairs in every interval.
On \(\mathcal A_Q^\circ\) it suffices to sieve for \(3\mid R\), separately at the two signs.
Fix \(u\ge2,\ m=3^u\), and take any finite set \(\Lambda\) of primes \(\lambda\ge5\) congruent to \(1+m\bmod 3m\).
The [fixed-prime rectangle argument](Yin_C_G0345_v720.md#sieve) gives the following local data at each \(\lambda\).
For each class \(n\equiv t\bmod m\) with \(t\not\equiv1\bmod3^{u-1}\), one residue of \(a\bmod\lambda\) and all but one residue of \(n\bmod\lambda\) give a simple root whose preimages under cubing have Frobenius orbit length three.
Each such pair excludes \(3\mid R\).
Since \(Q\) is invertible modulo \(\lambda\), the required seed residue becomes exactly one residue of \(b\bmod\lambda\).
Among unrestricted \(1\le b\le K\) and translated delay intervals, distinct \(\lambda\)'s impose independent conditions in residue counting by the Chinese remainder theorem.
For fixed \(m,\Lambda\) the errors tend to zero uniformly in \(M\) and in the varying residues of \(Q\), since the counts of each residue in intervals of fixed length differ by at most one.
The upper limiting fraction of unrestricted pairs with \(3\mid R\) at the given sign is at most
\[
            3^{1-u}+\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
                        \left(1-\frac{\lambda-1}{\lambda^2}\right).
\]
For fixed \(u\), the [divergence of prime reciprocals in this reduced progression](../audits/literature/Sut21/Yin_U001.md#sut21-u001) makes the product tend to zero as \(\Lambda\) increases.
Letting \(u\) tend to infinity and taking the union over the two signs gives \(o(KN)\) pairs.
Restriction to \(\mathcal A_Q^\circ\) only reduces this numerator, and (1) provides a positive lower bound for each denominator divided by \(KN\).
This proves the first limit in (2).
For the second, any sequence of unbounded initial lengths has a subsequence of lengths tending to infinity, while bounded initial lengths involve only finitely many delays.
Each of those delays has only finitely many exceptional seeds by the [fixed-delay theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v694.md#statement), and every seed on the progression satisfies \(a\ge Q\).
