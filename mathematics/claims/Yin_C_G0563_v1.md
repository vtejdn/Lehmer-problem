### 1. Whole-order correlations and exact support

Let alpha be any algebraic unit, including a root of unity; O=Z[alpha] is its actual additive order, isomorphic to Z^d.
Use exactly the original difference digits eta=0,+1,-1 of probabilities1/2,1/4,1/4 and X_(j+1)=alpha X_j+eta_j.

    U(z)=sum_(n>=0) p_n z^n=1/(1-F(z)),  0<z<R.       (1)

Here F counts all complete original first returns.
It has F(z)<1 on this interval.
Let nu_n be the actual fair-binary sum law on O and let

    mu_n(x)=sum_y nu_n(y)nu_n(y-x).
    A_z(x)=U(z)^(-1) sum_(n>=0) z^n mu_n(x),
    B_z(x)=U(z)^(-1) sum_(n>=0) z^n mu_n(alpha^n x),

A function f on O is positive definite here when every finite quadratic form sum_(i,j)c_i conjugate(c_j) f(x_i-x_j) is nonnegative.

This support assertion concerns the product on the WHOLE order.

### 2. Exact occupation identity, including the zero correction

For x in D minus{0}, let H_z(x) be the sum of z-weighted original first-hit paths from x to0, and let L_z(x) be the sum of z-weighted paths from0 to x which have no further visit to0 before their endpoint.

Every ambient path between two core endpoints stays in the core, since fixed paths from0 and back to0 complete it.
Decomposition at the first hit to0 and at the last visit to0 respectively gives

    sum_(n>=0) z^n K^n(x,0)=H_z(x)U(z),

The same endpoint probabilities are mu_n(alpha^n x) and mu_n(x), using the digit symmetry in the first case.

    q_z(w)=p(w)z^tau/F(z),  mu_z=zF'(z)/F(z).          (6)

    zF'(z) pi_z(x)=sigma_z(x), x!=0,

    sum_x sigma_z(x)=T(z):=zF'(z)+1-F(z)>=1.          (8)
