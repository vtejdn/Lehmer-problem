# Which new small-measure fields survive base change to all totally real numbers?

<a id="statement"></a>
## A dichotomy over the combined infinite base

Use the bounded measure \(M\), target \(y\), unmatched compositum \(K\), compressed degree \(d=d_y\), label field \(P_y\), and alternating group \(D_y=(A_b)^u\) of the [unmatched-field theorem](Yin_C_G0901_v87.md#statement).
Let \(T=\mathbb Q^{\rm tr}\) inside the fixed complex algebraic closure.
For all sufficiently large \(d\) in terms of \(M\), the following statements hold.

* If \(P_y\) is totally real, \(y\) is reachable over \(TK\) by a solvable extension alone.
* Otherwise, even after arbitrary towers of degree-at-most-\((b-1)\) adjunctions and solvable steps over \(TK\), the rational label extension of \(y\) still has relative degree at least \((b!/2)^u\).
  Its restriction group contains \(D_y\) in the natural label action.
  The first sufficient bound on the single non-solvable adjunction degrees is exactly \(b\).

The base \(K\) contains the full splitting fields, including arbitrary torsion twists and radical fibers, of *every* unmatched input through the target's compressed degree at measure at most \(M\).
Allowing both \(T\) and \(K\) simultaneously requires a separate argument: independence over each base alone would not imply it over their compositum.

For the [field \(K_{\le N}\) of all bounded-measure inputs through compressed degree \(N\)](Yin_C_G0901_v87.md#filtration), there is consequently an exact description of the bounded-measure elements after adjoining \(T\).
For some \(c_M>0\), all sufficiently large \(N\) and all positive integer step bounds \(q\le c_M N/(\log(3N))^{212}\), the resulting field, allowing arbitrary solvable steps as well, contains precisely the following elements in \(0<m(y)\le M\):
\[
                     d_y\le N\qquad\text{or}\qquad P_y\subseteq T .       \tag{1}
\]
For a compressed reciprocal unit the second condition says its trace \(y+y^{-1}\) is totally real.
The uniform gap on that branch, which includes Salem numbers, is still open.

<a id="commutator"></a>
## Commuting an independent alternating factor with complex conjugation

Assume \(P_y\not\subseteq T\).
The [independence argument before the tower steps](Yin_C_G0901_v87.md#independence) gives a normal base \(A=B_0 K\) containing \(K\), with \(E=A P_y\) Galois over \(\mathbb Q\) and \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/A)\) mapping isomorphically to \(D_y\).
Here \(A,E\) may be infinite, while this relative group is finite.
The label group \(G_y=\operatorname{Gal}(P_y/\mathbb Q)\) acts faithfully on the labels, partitioned into blocks of size \(b\ge5\).
The centralizer of \(D_y\) in \(G_y\) is trivial.
Indeed commuting with each independent alternating factor preserves its support, and the centralizer of the natural \(A_b\)-action in \(S_b\) is trivial.
An element commuting with each three-cycle preserves every three-element support, hence fixes each label.

Let \(c\in\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\) be complex conjugation and \(\pi\) its restriction map to the label group, also writing \(\pi\) for restriction of other automorphisms.
As the label field is normal, \(\pi(c)\ne1\).
Choose \(g\in\operatorname{Gal}(E/A)\) such that \([\pi(c),\pi(g)]\ne1\), using the centralizer calculation.
Then \([c,g]\) is a nontrivial member of \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/A)\) and belongs to the closed normal subgroup \(H\) generated by all conjugates of \(c\) on \(E\).
The intersection of this finite relative group with \(H\) is normal in \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb Q)\).
Restriction to \(P_y\) maps it to a nontrivial normal subgroup of \(G_y\) contained in \(D_y\), since \(\pi\) is surjective.
The [minimal normal alternating product](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement) forces this image to be \(D_y\).
Thus \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/A)\subseteq H\).

The fixed field of \(H\) is \(E\cap T\): a point is fixed by all conjugates of complex conjugation exactly when all its rational conjugates are real.
Restriction of \(\operatorname{Gal}(ET/T)\) onto \(E\) has that fixed field, hence image \(H\) (a closed image of a profinite group).
To impose the additional condition of fixing \(A\subset E\), intersect this image with \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/A)\).
It follows that \(\operatorname{Gal}(ET/AT)\) still restricts to the entire \(D_y\).

<a id="towers"></a>
## Towers and the bounded-measure spectrum

Over \(AT\), adjoining any of the indicated degree-at-most-\((b-1)\) and solvable towers over \(KT\) cannot remove any of \(D_y\).
Indeed the resulting common base can be taken normal over \(AT\), and all finite Galois subextensions have no \(A_b\) section by the [extension and tower argument](Yin_C_G0901_v87.md#independence).
Every nontrivial quotient of \(D_y\) contains \(A_b\).
Intersection with \(ET/AT\) therefore leaves the relative group unchanged.
Descending from the enlarged base with \(A\) to the one with \(K\) preserves at least the stated restrictions on \(P_y\).
The full tower field over \(KT\) is normal over \(\mathbb Q\), so it cannot contain \(y\) and omit \(P_y\).
The [label stabilizer chain](Yin_C_G0901_v87.md#degree) reaches \(y\) with bound \(b\) already over \(\mathbb Q\).
If instead \(P_y\subseteq T\), the solvable extension \(L_y/P_y\) gives the first alternative by base change, as in the [totally real base theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v395.md#statement).

Finally suppose \(d_y>N\) and \(P_y\not\subseteq T\).
Then \(K_{\le N}\subseteq K\), since no input of smaller compressed degree can match \(y\).
The lower bound just established applies over \(T K_{\le N}\), while \(b\gg_M d_y/(\log(3d_y))^{212}\) eventually exceeds the indicated range for \(q\).
This proves necessity in (1).
Every input of compressed degree at most \(N\) lies in the base by its definition, and each input with \(P_y\subseteq T\) is solvably reachable from \(T\).
These two observations prove sufficiency for all degrees.
