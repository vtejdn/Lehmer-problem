# Sparse multiples near rational multiples of a critical section exponent

<a id="statement"></a>
## When a multiple of one factor contains the whole section

Fix \(C,K>0\) and use the [critical six-term hypotheses](Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#statement) with \(s\) sufficiently large in terms of \(C,K\).
Write \(g(Z)=Z^s-Z-1\), \(B(Z)=1-Z^{s-1}-Z^s\), \(F(X)=g(X^a)+X^bB(X^a)\), and let \(P\) be a retained factor of degree \(d\).
Fix integers \(q\ge1,\ J\ge0\).
Let \(A(X)=\sum_{i=1}^h c_iX^{n_i}\) be a nonzero complex Laurent polynomial with distinct exponents, all coefficients nonzero, divisible by \(P\).
Suppose that for some integer \(n_0\) the exponents admit representations
\[
                q(n_i-n_0)= j_i b+e_i,\qquad 0\le j_i\le J,\quad |e_i|\le E
\]
with integers \(j_i,e_i\).
If \(F\) does not divide \(A\) in \(\mathbb C[X^{\pm1}]\), then
\[
 h^q\left(\sqrt{\frac{E+Jas}{b}+\frac{\log(2d)}d}+s^{-2}\right)
                       \gg_{C,K,q,J}1.                                      \tag{1}
\]
In particular for any fixed term bound and fixed \(q,J\) there is \(\eta>0\) for which \(E\le\eta b\) forces divisibility by the entire parent for all sufficiently large \(s\).
This remains true for arbitrarily large coefficients and preserves every factor of the parent with its multiplicity.
For a multiple \(A\) of span \(W\) that misses part of the parent, the choice \(q=1,\ J=0,\ E=W\) gives the sharper short-span bound directly from (1).
In particular \(P\) itself has \(\gg_{C,K}\sqrt{s}\) nonzero coefficients, since \(d/b\asymp 1/s\).

<a id="gram"></a>
## A sparse relation on a radical of the root

Choose \(t\) with \(t^q=x\), a root of \(P\), and average over the rational embeddings of \(\mathbb Q(t)\).
The [critical scales](Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#scales) give an off-circle fraction \(\delta\ll s^{-2}\), also for \(t\) by equal extension multiplicities.
The [factorwise height bound](Yin_C_G0345_v510.md#statement) gives \(h_{\rm abs}(t)\ll 1/(qb)\).
For every nonzero \(l\), the degree \(D\) of \(t^l\) is at least \(d\), since \((t^l)^q=x^l\) and [every power preserves the root field](Yin_C_G0345_v517.md#statement).
Apply the [compact discrepancy estimate](../audits/literature/Pri11/Yin_U002.md#pri11-u002) to the real and imaginary parts of the complex phase times a fixed Lipschitz radial cutoff supported on \([1/2,2]\) and equal to one at radius one.
The tests have bounded Dirichlet energy and zero circle mean.
The smoothing radius \(1/D\), integrality of the nonzero discriminant and \(D\ge d\) bound the full cutoff average by \(O_{C,K}(\sqrt{|l|/(qb)+\log(2d)/d})\).
Restricting to circle embeddings changes that average by \(O(\delta)\).

Suppose a nonzero Laurent polynomial of span \(W'\) with \(T\) nonzero complex coefficients vanishes at every conjugate of \(t\).
On circle embeddings the Gram matrix of its monomials, normalized by the total number of embeddings, has diagonal \(1-\delta\).
Its off-diagonal entries have absolute value at most \(O_{C,K}(\sqrt{W'/(qb)+\log(2d)/d}+\delta)\) by the preceding estimate.
The coefficient vector lies in its kernel.
Taking a row corresponding to a coefficient of largest absolute value gives
\[
              1\ll_{C,K} T\left(\sqrt{W'/(qb)+\log(2d)/d}+s^{-2}\right).       \tag{2}
\]

<a id="elimination"></a>
## Eliminating a rational fraction of the long exponent

Over \(\mathbb C[T^{\pm1},Y]\) form
\[
      G=B(T^{qa})Y^q+g(T^{qa}),\qquad H=\sum_i c_iT^{e_i}Y^{j_i}.
\]
The polynomials \(g,B\) have no common zero by the [section graph calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v532.md#divisors).
Every zero of \(g\) is simple and nonzero.
Indeed a common zero with its derivative would satisfy \(Z=-s/(s-1)\) and \(sZ^{s-1}=1\), inconsistent in absolute value.
Thus \(g(T^{qa})\) has a simple zero away from zero where \(B(T^{qa})\) is a unit in the local ring.
Eisenstein at that zero proves irreducibility of \(G\) over \(\mathbb C(T)\), including the linear case.
Its coefficients are coprime in \(\mathbb C[T^{\pm1}]\), so divisibility of \(H\) by \(G\) over the fraction field gives divisibility in the Laurent coefficient ring by Gauss's lemma.
Specialization at \(Y=T^b\) would then give \(F(T^q)\mid T^{-qn_0} A(T^q)\).
Dividing a monomial translate of \(A(X)\) by \(F(X)\) and substituting \(X=T^q\) shows that \(F\mid A\), since a nonzero remainder has smaller degree and \(F(0)\ne0\).

When \(F\nmid A\), the resultant \(U(T)=\operatorname{Res}_Y(G,H)\) is consequently a nonzero Laurent polynomial.
At every conjugate \(t'\) of \(t\), both polynomials vanish at \(Y=(t')^b\), since \(P((t')^q)=0\) and \(P\mid A\).
Write \(j=\deg_Y H\le J\).
In the Sylvester determinant each product uses \(q\) entries from \(H\) and \(j\) entries from \(G\).
It follows that \(U\) has at most \((q+J)!\,h^q3^J\) terms and span at most \(2qE+Jqas\), also for \(j=0\) when the resultant is \(H^q\).
Applying (2) gives (1).
For the fixed term corollary choose \(\eta\) small enough in (1) and use \(as/b\to0\).
For \(A=P\), the [degree estimates](Yin_C_G0345_v507.md#scales) give \(d\asymp as^2\), \(b\asymp as^3\) and \(\log(2d)/d=o(1/s)\), so \(F\nmid P\) for large \(s\) and (1) gives the asserted support bound.
