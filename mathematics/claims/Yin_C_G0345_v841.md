# Independent trace splitting fields for distinct steep directions

<a id="statement"></a>
## Simultaneous reductions over a range of primitive directions

Consider the [two-pole sections](Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#statement) in positive coprime directions \((k,l)\) satisfying
\[
                      \log l\le k\le l/(\log l)^2 .                         \tag{1}
\]
Write \(Q_{k,l}\) for the trace polynomial, \(L_{k,l}\) for its splitting field, and \(n_i\) for the degrees of its irreducible rational factors.
There is a threshold \(l_0\) such that over \(F=\mathbb Q^{\rm ab}\) the fields \(F L_{k,l}\) for all distinct directions in (1) with \(l\ge l_0\) are linearly disjoint as a family.
On every finite collection the joint group is the full product of the natural \(A_{n_i}\) over all factors of all its sections.
In particular the family can include all eligible horizontal exponents at every sufficiently large prime \(l\).

For a finite selection use the section-specific notation \(B,n,r,m,b(p)\) of the [one-prime estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v838.md#statement).
In every invertible residue class modulo any fixed integer, the primes simultaneously satisfying \((1-B/(4n))m\le b(p)\le m\) for every selected section have relative density at least
\[
                              \prod_{(k,l)}(B/(8n))^r .                    \tag{2}
\]
The reduction test concerns a finite collection at a time and leaves the sizes of the rational factor degrees open.

<a id="diagonal"></a>
## A common alternating quotient forces a multiplicative relation

The [single-section joint action](Yin_C_G0345_v806.md#traces) gives independent alternating groups on each section over \(F\).
If a finite joint action over \(F\) is smaller than the product, the [simple subdirect product argument](Yin_C_G0345_v268.md#normal) identifies a diagonal between two factors of distinct sections.
The sizes agree by group orders, and their common degree \(d\) exceeds eight uniformly by the [degree cutoff](Yin_C_G0345_v837.md#height).
Every automorphism of their alternating group is induced by a relabeling.
After this relabeling the full rational action normalizes the diagonal and acts by identical permutations on its two sets of labels, because the centralizer of \(A_d\) in \(S_d\) is trivial.
The matched trace labels thus generate the same degree-\(d\) field.
Choose roots \(x,y\) over matched labels from directions \((k,l),(k',l')\), respectively, and set \(K=\mathbb Q(x,y)\), \(D=[K:\mathbb Q]\), \(u=\min(l,l')\), \(v=\max(l,l')\).
Each root is at most quadratic over its trace, hence \(D\le4d\ll u\).
The cutoff on the larger section gives \(d\gg v(\log\log v/\log v)^3\), so \(v\le u^2\) for all sufficiently large \(u\).
By the [individual height formula](Yin_C_G0345_v799.md#statement) both heights are \(O(1/u)\).
If the roots were multiplicatively independent, the [fixed-rank height bound](../audits/literature/AM16/Yin_U005.md#am16-u005) at rank two and \(\epsilon=1/4\) would give \(\max(h(x),h(y))\gg D^{-3/4}\), a contradiction as \(u\to\infty\).

<a id="poles"></a>
## Recovering both section coordinates from dependent roots

The subgroup generated by \(x,y\) modulo torsion is infinite cyclic, since both roots are nontorsion.
Choose a generator represented by a monomial \(w\in K\) in those roots.
After replacing either root by its inverse if necessary, write \(x=\xi w^a,\ y=\eta w^b\) with \(a,b>0\) and roots of unity \(\xi,\eta\in K\).
Reciprocity preserves the section equations under these choices.
The same fixed-rank bound at rank one and \(\epsilon=1\) gives \(h(w)\gg D^{-2}\).
Consequently \(a,b\ll u^2\), by \(h(x)=a h(w)\) and \(h(y)=b h(w)\).

Use the embedding of \(K\) that maximizes \(|w|\), and identify the roots with their images there.
This embedding simultaneously maximizes \(|x|\) and \(|y|\), with both values greater than one.
For each root its irreducible factor has exterior conjugates at both poles by the [pole allocation](Yin_C_G0345_v790.md#allocation).
Every exterior conjugate in the short coordinate is exponentially close to one of \(\beta_3<\beta_4\) by the [radial bound](Yin_C_G0345_v776.md#statement).
The maxima must therefore visit \(\beta_4\) for both sections.
Writing \(s_*=\min(l/k,l'/k')\ge(\log u)^2\), we obtain
\[
                   x^k=\beta_4+O(e^{-c s_*}),\qquad
                   y^{k'}=\beta_4+O(e^{-c s_*})
\]
with a common constant \(c>0\).
Since \(b\log|x|=a\log|y|\), it follows that
\[
            |b k'-a k|\log\beta_4\ll (b k'+a k)e^{-c s_*}=o(1),
\]
where \(k,k'\le v\le u^2\).
Integrality forces \(a k=b k'\), so \(x^k/y^{k'}\) is a root of unity in \(K\).
Its order \(q\) satisfies \(\varphi(q)\le D\) and \(q\le2D^2\), since \(\varphi(q)^2/q=\prod_{p^e\parallel q}p^{e-2}(p-1)^2\ge1/2\).
Any nonidentity root of order \(q\) has distance at least \(4/q\) from one.
The pole estimates put the ratio within \(O(e^{-c s_*})\) of one, hence \(U=x^k=y^{k'}\) exactly.

At this common \(U\), the two values \(x^l,y^{l'}\) both tend to infinity in absolute value, since \(|U|\to\beta_4>1\) and both slopes grow.
They solve the same quadratic \(A(U)V^2+U^2V+A^*(U)=0\).
If they were distinct, their reciprocal sum would obey \(A^*(U)(1/x^l+1/y^{l'})=-U^2\) by subtracting the equations for the reciprocals.
Boundedness of \(A^*(U)\) and the limit \(U^2\to\beta_4^2\) exclude this equality.
Thus \(V=x^l=y^{l'}\) as well.
The identities \(V^k=U^l\) and \(V^{k'}=U^{l'}\) imply \(lk'=l'k\) since \(|U|>1\).
Primitivity gives identical directions, contradicting the diagonal between distinct sections.
All the exclusions are uniform as \(u\to\infty\), which proves the single threshold and joint disjointness.

<a id="primes"></a>
## The same prime for a finite collection

Condition the Frobenius action in the compositum of the selected trace fields and the prescribed cyclotomic field on the required cyclotomic automorphism.
Its projection is uniform on a union of cosets of the full alternating product, since the cyclotomic field lies in \(F\).
Within each coset the permutations of the separate blocks are independent with their signs prescribed.
The [long-cycle count](Yin_C_G0345_v838.md#count) costs a factor of at most \(8n/B\) per block of that section, irrespective of its sign.
Multiplication across all blocks followed by the [Chebotarev density theorem](../audits/literature/KW22/Yin_U001.md#kw22-u001) gives (2), omitting the finitely many bad primes.
