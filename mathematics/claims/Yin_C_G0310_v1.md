### 2. A finite support-energy defect lemma

Let X be any integer d-by-b matrix of full row rank, Q=XX^T, S=X eta, p=Pr(S=0), and mu(z)=Pr(S=z).
Define the COMPLETE support energy

    M_X=max_{z:mu(z)>0} z^T Q^(-1)z.                    (1)

Every maximum is over the entire original support.
It is finite and at most b: if z=X eta, its minimum squared real preimage norm is z^T Q^(-1)z<=||eta||^2<=b.
The maximum may be much smaller than b, but no smaller general bound is presumed.
For any invertible real L,

    E_(LX)(Lz)=E_X(z), M_(LX)=M_X,                     (2)

where the transformed support is understood as the pushforward of the original finite law.
This follows either by the inverse congruence formula for L Q L^T or by equality of the real preimage constraints.
The transformed map need not be integral for the energy identity; integrality is used separately in the lattice summation below.

The already proved L251 relative estimate gives

    mu(z)<=p exp[-E_X(z)/2].

For clarity its optimization uses the full Gram Q: the fixed-endpoint exponential inequality gives mu(z) exp(t dot z)<=p product_j cosh(t dot X_j) <=p exp(t^T Q t/2); putting t=Q^(-1)z proves the displayed bound.
It is a relative estimate against the actual zero atom, with no substituted return lower bound.

Since a>1/2, all attained z therefore satisfy

    mu(z)<=C_X p exp[-a E_X(z)],
    C_X=exp[(a-1/2)M_X].                               (3)

Indeed -E/2=-aE+(a-1/2)E and E<=M_X.
Off the finite support mu is zero, so(3) remains valid on all integer z.
This is a DEFECT bound: it does not establish C1, which would require C_X=1.

Let 0<W<=I_d be real symmetric positive definite and put

    D=(W^(-1)+Q)^(-1),
    G(u)=exp(a u^T D u) E exp[-a(S+u)^T W(S+u)],
                    u in R^d.

Then the ENTIRE real-tail comparison is

    p<=G(0)<=sup_{u in R^d}G(u)
          <=C_X K_d det(W)^(-1/2) p,
    K_d=2^(d/4)(2pi/a)^(d/2).                         (4)

To prove it, substitute(3) into the complete finite expectation and complete the square.
With H=W+Q^(-1) and c=-H^(-1)W u,

    (z+u)^T W(z+u)+z^T Q^(-1)z

The constant identity is W-W H^(-1)W=(W^(-1)+Q)^(-1); multiplication or the elementary Schur complement verifies its order.

    G(u)<=C_X p sum_{z in Z^d}exp[-a(z-c)^T H(z-c)]

### 3. A prefix-independent support-energy constant for every fixed hyperbolic input

Fix any nonzero algebraic integer alpha with monic irreducible minimal polynomial P of degree d, and assume EVERY conjugate has modulus different from1.
Nonunits are allowed.

    X_(i,b)=[-M^(d+i)v,...,-M^(d+i+b-1)v].             (7)

Here are full fixed-input constants establishing a uniform bound in b.
Use the real Minkowski change H from the power basis, retaining both real coordinates of every nonreal conjugate pair.
Let A=H M H^(-1), block diagonal with scalar real blocks and rotation-dilation real2-by-2 blocks.
Write A=A_- direct-sum A_+ for stable and expanding blocks, and

    w=-A^d H v=(w_-,w_+),
    s=||w_-||, e=||w_+||,
    rho=max{||A_-||,||A_+^(-1)||} in(0,1),             (8)

using0 for the norm of an absent block.
An expanding block exists: otherwise the positive integer |P(0)|, the product of all root moduli, would be less than1.

For the complete original endpoint S_b=sum_(j<b)A^j w eta_j, set T_b=I_- direct-sum A_+^b and Y_b=T_b^(-1)S_b.

    ||Y_b||<=R0=(s+rho e)/(1-rho).                     (9)

The stable component uses the forward geometric series; the expanding component is the reverse sum of A_+^(-l)w_+ for1<=l<=b.
Adding the two component bounds gives(9).

Let Q_b be the full Gram of these b columns and Q'_b=T_b^(-1)Q_b T_b^(-T).
Its limit is the positive definite block diagonal matrix

    Sigma=Sigma_- direct-sum Sigma_+,
    Sigma_-=sum_(j>=0) A_-^j w_- w_-^T(A_-^j)^T,
    Sigma_+=sum_(l>=1) A_+^(-l)w_+ w_+^T(A_+^(-l))^T.

The absent block is omitted.
To verify strict positivity, a real vector annihilating the corresponding orbit vectors gives after complexification a linear combination of distinct conjugate powers with nonzero coefficient multipliers.
