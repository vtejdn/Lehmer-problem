## 2. Exact original-field identity and its finite coefficient form

Fix integers a>=6 and n>=2, and let P be ANY monic irreducible integer factor of

    F_n(t)=t^(2n)-t^(n+1)-a t^n-t^(n-1)+1.

Write alpha for a root of P, K=Q(alpha), D=deg P, and

    C_k(P)=abs Norm_(K/Q)(alpha^k-1),
    G_a(t)=t^2+(a-2)t+1.

The sparse-family argument excludes circle roots; consequently P is a nontorsion integer-unit polynomial and every C_k(P) is a nonzero integer.
It is not necessary that P be reciprocal, that F_n be irreducible, or that powers of alpha preserve degree.
All norms below remain in K with all D original embeddings.

Divide the defining identity F_n(alpha)=0 by alpha^n to obtain

    alpha^n+alpha^(-n)=a+alpha+alpha^(-1).

Subtract2, and use alpha!=0, to give the exact equality

    (alpha^n-1)^2/alpha^n
        =a-2+alpha+alpha^(-1)=G_a(alpha)/alpha.        (1)

Taking full K/Q norms and absolute values, the unit factors have norm of absolute value1.
Thus

    C_n(P)^2=abs Norm_(K/Q)(G_a(alpha))
             =abs Res(P,G_a).                         (2)

In particular the absolute resultant on the right is a PERFECT SQUARE for every actual factor in this domain.
The signed resultant is retained before taking its absolute value; its sign need not be silently fixed for a possibly nonreciprocal factor.
Equation(2) also gives, for EVERY rational prime ell,

    2 v_ell(C_n(P))=v_ell(abs Res(P,G_a)).             (3)

This equality includes bad primes, all depths and every original conjugate.
It does not identify the right side with a trace-field norm or divide the original degree by2.

There is a finite exact coefficient certificate requiring no factorization of the integer norm and no list of arithmetic primes.
Reduce the actual polynomial P modulo the monic quadratic G_a, obtaining

    P(t)=u+v t modulo G_a(t), u,v in Z.

If gamma1,gamma2 are the two roots of G_a, their sum is -(a-2) and their product is1.
Since both polynomials are monic and 2D is even, resultant symmetry gives

    Res(P,G_a)=Res(G_a,P)
               =(u+v gamma1)(u+v gamma2)
               =u^2-(a-2)uv+v^2.                    (4)

The remainder is obtained by ordinary monic long division, or the exact two-coordinate recurrence induced by t^2=-(a-2)t-1.
Every coefficient of the ORIGINAL P is used, and the degree remains D in the normalization of(2).
Equation(4) is a formula for a certificate, not a claim that any native evaluation has been executed.
Merely obtaining a nonzero square from an arbitrary polynomial does not prove that it divides F_n; the actual factor premise or its exact polynomial-divisibility certificate is essential.

    R^(-1)<=|alpha_i|<=R,

    |a-2+alpha_i+alpha_i^(-1)|
        >=a-2-|alpha_i|-|alpha_i|^(-1)>=delta.

    log C_n(P)/D >= (1/2)log delta >0.                (6)

## 4. The complete reducible polynomial has an exact second-order norm recurrence

There is a further exact consistency check which retains the whole original polynomial instead of pretending it is an irreducible input.
Let

    C_n(F_n)=abs Res(F_n,t^n-1).

At any n-th root of unity zeta,

    F_n(zeta)=2-a-zeta-zeta^(-1)=-G_a(zeta)/zeta.

Since the product of all n-th roots of unity has absolute value1, resultant symmetry gives

    C_n(F_n)=abs Res(G_a,t^n-1).                       (13)

Put s=a-2>=4 and let lambda>1 satisfy lambda+lambda^(-1)=s.
The two roots of G_a are -lambda and -lambda^(-1), so(13) is

    C_n(F_n)=lambda^n+lambda^(-n)-2(-1)^n.

Equivalently, the EXACT integer sequence U_0=2, U_1=s,

    U_(j+1)=s U_j-U_(j-1)

gives

    C_n(F_n)=U_n-2(-1)^n.                             (14)

This positive integer is not substituted for C_n(P).
If F_n=product_j P_j^(e_j) is its actual factorization, multiplicativity retains

    C_n(F_n)=product_j C_n(P_j)^(e_j).                (15)

No factor or multiplicity disappears, and(9) was proved separately for every P_j rather than inferred from a large product alone.
Equations(13)--(15) are a bounded explicit coefficient-recursion connection, with no scan or further source theorem.
