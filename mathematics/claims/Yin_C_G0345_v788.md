# Parities of signed integer relations and collisions of weighted line records

<a id="statement"></a>
## An exact weighted count for every mask

Let \(\alpha\) be a nontorsion algebraic unit of degree \(d\), let \(L\ge d\) and \(J\ge1\), and put \(n=L^J\), \(K=L-d\).
Use the [integer line records](Yin_C_G0345_v782.md#statement) on the grid \(\{0,\ldots,L-1\}^J\), recording \(\sum_j x_j\alpha^j\) on every coordinate line.
Fix a mask \(S\) of size \(s>0\), and choose independent fair bits there and zeros elsewhere.
Write \(Y\) for the tuple of all resulting records, \(Y'\) for an independent copy, and \(M_S\) for the largest number of binary arrays having one common record.
Reduce the records modulo \(2\mathbb Z[\alpha]\), and let \(C_S\) be the binary vector space of arrays supported on \(S\) with all reduced records zero.
For \(z\in C_S\), let \(w(z)\) be its Hamming weight and \(O(z)\) the number of sign assignments on its support whose signed array has all integer records zero.
In particular \(O(0)=1\).
Then
\[
       M_S\ \ge\ 2^s\Pr(Y=Y')
          =\sum_{z\in C_S} 2^{-w(z)}O(z).                              \tag{1}
\]
Write \(D_S=\dim_{\mathbb F_2}C_S\), \(q=n-K^J\).
The full-grid code has dimension \(K^J\), and \(D_S\ge s-q\).
A parity is called liftable here when \(O(z)>0\), equivalently when it has an integer lift of supremum norm at most one supported on the mask.
For any \(t\ge0\), if a fraction \(p>0\) of the code consists of liftable words of weight at most \(t\), then
\[
                         \log_2 M_S\ge D_S-t+\log_2 p.                   \tag{2}
\]

On the full grid with \(L\ge3d\), any uniformly chosen binary codeword has pairwise independent fair coordinates.
If a fraction bounded below by a positive constant lifts along a sequence with \(n\to\infty\), then
\[
                        \log_2 M_S \ge K^J-n/2-o(n).                    \tag{3}
\]
The same conclusion holds for a liftable fraction at least \(n^{-A}\) for any fixed \(A>0\) if \(L/d\to\infty\).
Consequently a positive exponential fiber rate follows under either lifting hypothesis when \((1-d/L)^J\ge1/2+\epsilon\) for fixed \(\epsilon>0\).
For \(J\to\infty\) and \(L/(Jd)\to c\) this inequality holds eventually with some \(\epsilon>0\) whenever \(c>1/\log2\), with natural logarithm in this threshold.

For arbitrary masks, if all words in \(C_S\) lift, then \(\log_2 M_S\ge D_S-s/2\ge s/2-q\).
If a fraction \(p>0\) lifts and \(D_S>0\), one always has
\[
       \log_2 M_S\ge \tfrac32 D_S-s
                   -\sqrt{D_S\log(2/p)/2}+\log_2(p/2).                 \tag{4}
\]
Thus at density \(s/n\ge\delta>0\), condition (4) gives a positive rate uniformly over masks if \(q/n\le\delta/3-\epsilon\) and the liftable fractions satisfy \(\log(1/p)=o(n)\) uniformly.
The all-words condition gives a positive rate already for \(q/n\le\delta/2-\epsilon\).
The [marginal entropy obstruction](Yin_C_G0345_v786.md#statement) leaves the joint fiber size open at \(L\asymp Jd\).
These conditions use simultaneous signed lifts at that scale.

<a id="kernel"></a>
## The two kernels and the collision probability

Use the power basis \(1,\ldots,\alpha^{d-1}\) of \(\mathbb Z[\alpha]\) and write \(A:\mathbb Z^L\to\mathbb Z^d\) for one line map.
The first \(d\) columns give the identity, so \(\mathbb Z^L=W\oplus E\), where \(W=\ker A\) is free of rank \(K\) and \(A\) maps \(E\) isomorphically to \(\mathbb Z^d\).
In the tensor expansion \((W\oplus E)^{\otimes J}\), vanishing of all records parallel to axis \(i\) requires exactly the vanishing of the components with \(E\) at position \(i\).
Thus the integer kernel of all records on the full grid is \(W^{\otimes J}\).
The splitting remains direct after reduction modulo two and gives the full-grid code \(\bar W^{\otimes J}\) over \(\mathbb F_2\).
Consequently every full-grid codeword has an unrestricted integer lift.
Intersecting the binary kernel with the \(s\)-dimensional coordinate subspace supported on \(S\) gives \(D_S\ge s-q\).

For two independent binary arrays the coordinate differences on the mask are independent, each taking \(0,1,-1\) with respective probabilities \(1/2,1/4,1/4\).
A prescribed signed difference supported on \(w\) sites has probability \(2^{-s-w}\).
Grouping the zero-record differences according to their binary supports gives the equality in (1).
The collision probability is at most the largest record probability \(M_S/2^s\), proving the inequality there.
Summing just the prescribed liftable words proves (2).

<a id="weight"></a>
## Independence of small sets of coordinates on the full grid

Over \(\mathbb F_2\), every consecutive block of \(d\) columns of \(A\) is a basis.
Indeed it is the first basis block multiplied in the quotient ring by a power of \(\alpha\), which is a unit since its minimal polynomial has constant coefficient \(\pm1\).
Every nonzero word in the row space of \(A\bmod2\) therefore meets each of the \(\lfloor L/d\rfloor\) disjoint full blocks.
Since this row space is the orthogonal complement of \(\bar W\), all sets of at most \(b=\lfloor L/d\rfloor-1\) coordinate functionals on \(\bar W\) are linearly independent.

Take at most \(b\) distinct grid sites and distinguish one of them.
For each axis choose a word of \(\bar W\) equal to one at the distinguished coordinate and zero at the other distinct coordinate values used on that axis.
The one-dimensional independence just proved permits this interpolation.
Their tensor product isolates the distinguished site on the chosen set.
Repeating this for each site shows that projection of the tensor code onto these sites is surjective, so their bits are independent and fair under its uniform measure.
In particular when \(L\ge3d\) the weight has mean \(n/2\) and variance \(n/4\).
Chebyshev's inequality at \(t=n/2+n^{3/4}\) in (2) proves (3) for a fixed positive liftable fraction.

For an even integer \(2r\le b\), the \(2r\)-th centered moment of the weight agrees with that of independent bits.
Expanding in their signs, nonzero terms must have even index multiplicities, hence can be paired in at most \((2r-1)!!\) ways with at most \(n^r\) index assignments for each pairing.
It follows that for \(x>0\)
\[
              \Pr(w>n/2+x)\le \left(\frac{rn}{2x^2}\right)^r .
\]
If \(p\ge n^{-A}\), fix an integer \(r>A\) and choose \((1+A/r)/2<\beta<1\).
Eventually \(2r\le b\) when \(L/d\to\infty\), and the tail at \(x=n^\beta\) is \(o(n^{-A})\).
Thus at least a fraction \(p/2\) lifts below this weight, proving (3).
The threshold in the statement follows from \((1-d/L)^J\to e^{-1/c}\).

<a id="masks"></a>
## Shortened codes and a parity with no signed lift

For a uniform word of \(C_S\), each coordinate is either zero identically or is a fair bit, giving \(\mathbb Ew\le s/2\).
If every class lifts, Jensen's inequality in (1) gives \(M_S\ge2^{D_S}\mathbb E 2^{-w}\ge2^{D_S-s/2}\).
For partial lifting take \(D_S\) coordinates on which the code projects bijectively to all binary patterns, by column independence in a generator matrix.
Their total \(T\) is a sum of \(D_S\) independent fair bits, and the full weight is at most \(T+s-D_S\).
The moment generating function of \(T-D_S/2\) is \(\cosh(u/2)^{D_S}\le \exp(D_Su^2/8)\) at \(u\ge0\), using \(\tanh v\le v\) for \(v\ge0\).
Exponential Markov and optimization give \(\Pr(T>D_S/2+x)\le\exp(-2x^2/D_S)\).
With \(x=\sqrt{D_S\log(2/p)/2}\), at least a fraction \(p/2\) lifts at weight at most \(s-D_S/2+x\), giving (4).

In general the full integer reduction can be surjective while a prescribed parity admits no signed lift.
In the finite ring \(\mathbb Z[\alpha]/2\mathbb Z[\alpha]\), let \(M\) be the multiplicative order of the class of \(\alpha\).
If \(L>M\), the binary word supported on \(\{0,M\}\) is in the reduced line kernel.
Neither choice of relative sign gives a zero integer record, since \(\alpha^M\ne\pm1\).
Its \(J\)-fold tensor product belongs to the full-grid code, and any signed lift would have to cancel these same two terms on each nonempty line.
For example the larger root of \(X^2-3X+1\) is a reciprocal nontorsion unit with \(M=3\).
This obstruction applies to one prescribed parity and supplies no estimate for the fraction or weights of the remaining liftable classes.
