# A characteristic window for common residues of bounded multiplicative order

<a id="statement"></a>
## The order alternative at comparable exponent primes

For integer \(L\ge2\), let \(p\) range through the primes of \([L,5L/4]\) and \(q\) through those of \([3L/2,2L]\).
Fix computable positive integer functions \(X(L)=o(L^2/\log L)\) and \(A(L)\to\infty\).
There are a computable width \(b(L)\to\infty\) and computable sets of prime pairs of relative size tending to one with the following property.
For all sufficiently large \(L\), on each retained pair suppose \(U,V\) pass the [common-row test](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#common) at this width with \(0<\eta=\log H/(p+q)\le10^{-3}\), where \(H=\max(\|U\|_1,\|V\|_1)\).
For each choice of qualifying seeds at the two primes, there is an integer \(N\) dividing the positive generator of \((U,V)\cap\mathbb Z\) with
\[
 \frac{\log N}{\log H}>\frac{1-1/b(L)}2\exp(T_\eta),\qquad
 T_\eta>\mathrm e,\quad T_\eta/\log T_\eta=(4\eta)^{-1/3}.
\]
Each rational prime \(\ell\mid N\) is distinct from \(p,q\), exceeds \(\lfloor\log_2 L\rfloor\), and admits a nonzero common root of both rows and both powered minima in a finite field, of degree at most the smaller seed degree over \(\mathbb F_\ell\).
For each such \(\ell\) outside the interval
\[
                     X(L)<\ell\le L^{A(L)},                         \tag{1}
\]
**every** common residue root of the two powered minima has multiplicative order greater than \(b(L)\).
Existence of rows with \(\eta\to0\) along these pairs is equivalent to vanishing of the unrestricted Lehmer infimum, and when it vanishes any sequence of retained pairs at scales tending to infinity admits such rows.
The statement allows all of \(N\) to be supported in (1).

<a id="incidence"></a>
## Possible exponent primes at one residue characteristic

Fix an integer \(d\ge1\) and two monic reciprocal nontorsion unit minima \(P_1,P_2\) of degrees \(e,f\) and log measures \(h_1,h_2\).
Their roots in a fixed common splitting field are \(a_i,c_j\).
Remove the finite set of rational primes dividing any of the nonzero rational integers
\[
       \operatorname{Res}(P_s,Y^u-1),\qquad s=1,2,\quad 1\le u\le d.
\]
At any remaining rational prime \(\ell\), fix **one** prime of the splitting field over \(\ell\).
All roots of both monic polynomials reduce there and exhaust their respective roots in an algebraic closure of the residue field, with multiplicities.
Their residues are units and each has order greater than \(d\).
The powered polynomials also split into the reductions of the powered roots at this same place.

If one powered node \(\bar a_i^p=\bar c_j^q\) has order \(t\le d\), the original residue orders satisfy
\[
                \operatorname{ord}(\bar a_i)=pt,\qquad
                \operatorname{ord}(\bar c_j)=qt.
\]
Indeed the order after taking a prime power \(p\) is the old order divided by its gcd with \(p\), and retaining the old order would contradict its strict lower bound.
When \(p,q>d\), the integer \(pt\) has \(p\) as its unique prime divisor greater than \(d\), and \(qt\) has the corresponding property for \(q\).
Thus each fixed first label permits at most one \(p\), and each second label at most one \(q\).
Over the entire rectangle a fixed \(\ell\) allows at most \(ef\) ordered pairs with a common node of order at most \(d\), and at a specified \(p\) it allows at most \(f\) partners.
These counts apply also when multiple labels coalesce in the residue field.

<a id="tails"></a>
## Counting collisions in the two ranges of characteristics

Write \(M,N_q\) for the two prime interval sizes, each of order \(L/\log L\) by the [prime interval estimate](../audits/literature/Ked07/Yin_U001.md#siegel-walfisz) at modulus one.
Above the removed finite set, the number of pairs with a bounded-order collision at a prime \(\ell\le X=X(L)\) is at most \(ef\pi(X)\).
Here \(\pi(X)=o(MN_q)\): for \(X\le L\) use \(\pi(X)\le L\), and otherwise the same prime estimate gives \(\pi(X)\ll X/\log X\le X/\log L\).

At a given \(p\), every rational prime supporting such a collision divides the positive integer
\[
           W_p=\prod_{u=1}^d |\operatorname{Res}(P_1,Y^{pu}-1)|.
\]
The bound \(|a_i^{pu}-1|\le2\max(1,|a_i|)^{pu}\) gives
\[
           \log W_p\le de\log2 + \tfrac12pd(d+1)h_1.
\]
There are at most \(\log W_p/(A(L)\log L)\) distinct prime factors exceeding \(L^{A(L)}\).
Each permits at most \(f\) partners by the preceding count, for large \(L\) when these primes exceed the fixed exceptional characteristics.
Summing over the first primes gives \(O_{P_1,P_2,d}(ML/(A(L)\log L))=o(MN_q)\) pairs.
These estimates count actual finite-field orders, irrespective of any higher local valuations at a witness prime.

<a id="calibration"></a>
## Simultaneous choice for all seeds and both row polynomials

Use the cutoff \(Z(L)=\lfloor\log_2 L\rfloor\) in the [finite calibration for the surviving integer](Yin_C_G0345_v508.md#width).
At trial width \(j\ge2\), keep its powered-degree, disjointness and exact inequality (5) for all ordered pairs of listed seeds of degree at most \(j\) and multiplicative measure at most \(1091/1000\).
Also require that no such ordered pair have a common powered node of order \(t\le j\) at any prime \(\ell>Z(L)\) outside (1).
For fixed \(j\) the cutoff eventually exceeds all fixed exceptional characteristics from the finite list, so the two counts at \(d=j\) remove only a proportion tending to zero.
The earlier tests likewise hold on a fraction tending to one for each fixed \(j\).

The additional test is finite and computable.
For the first seed factor the integers \(W_p\) at \(d=j\), retain only the indicated prime divisors, and test each \(1\le u\le j\) for a nonconstant simultaneous gcd of the two powered minima and \(Y^u-1\) over \(\mathbb F_\ell\).
Every forbidden collision is detected since its characteristic divides \(W_p\), even before \(Z(L)\) exceeds all the fixed exceptions.
Take the largest \(2\le j\le L\) for which at least \(1-1/j\) of all pairs pass all tests, and take those passing pairs at \(b(L)=j\).
Use width one and the empty set if the rectangle is empty or no trial passes.
Each fixed trial eventually passes the fraction threshold, so the chosen width diverges and the retained fraction tends to one.

The [row cost and contact bounds](Yin_C_G0345_v508.md#rows) place every permissible independent choice of seeds inside the tested list.
For those seeds take \(N=B_0^*\) of the preceding finite calibration, with the star removing both exponent primes and all primes up to \(Z(L)\).
The [local row and residue argument](Yin_C_G0345_v508.md#rows) gives its size, its divisibility in the row ideal and a common nonzero root at every prime factor, including when the two original labels agree.
At primes outside (1) the additional test gives the claimed order for every powered collision and hence for each such common row root.
Finally the [unrestricted common-row limit](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#gap) applies at the selected widths to any sequence of retained prime pairs.
The normalized costs can tend to zero exactly when the unrestricted infimum vanishes.
