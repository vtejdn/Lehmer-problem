# Field radical indices at polynomially parametrized Salem seeds

<a id="statement"></a>
## A uniform tail over all delays and both signs

Let \(F\in\mathbb Z[T]\) be nonconstant with positive leading coefficient, and put \(V=(F-1)F(F+1)\).
Take the parameter set \(\mathcal A\) to be either the rational primes, if every nonzero complex root of \(V\) is simple, or the positive integers, if all its complex roots are simple.
For \(a\ge3,\ n\ge7,\ \epsilon=\pm1\), let \(r_{a,n,\epsilon}>1\) denote the exterior Salem root of \(G_{a,n,\epsilon}(X)=X^n(X-a)+\epsilon(aX-1)\).
Let \(R_{a,n,\epsilon}\) be the largest positive integer \(h\) with \(r_{a,n,\epsilon}^{1/h}\in\mathbb Q(r_{a,n,\epsilon})\), using the positive real root.
Set \(\mathcal R(a)=\sup_{n,\epsilon}R_{a,n,\epsilon}\).
For each \(0<\theta<1\) there is a constant \(C_{F,\theta}\) such that for all \(B\ge1\),
\[
 \limsup_{X\to\infty}
 \frac{\#\{t\in\mathcal A:t\le X,\ F(t)\ge3,\ \mathcal R(F(t))>B\}}
      {\#\{t\in\mathcal A:t\le X\}}\le C_{F,\theta} B^{-\theta}.        \tag{1}
\]
In particular, for every positive function \(f(t)=o(\log t)\), a relative density one of parameters \(t\) have every noncyclotomic irreducible factor of every \(G_{F(t),n,\epsilon}(X^h)\), \(h\ge1\), of logarithmic Mahler measure greater than \(f(t)\).
The exceptional parameter set here may depend on \(f\).

The prime-parameter conclusion includes \(a=b p^k+c\) for all fixed integers \(b,k\ge1,\ c\in\mathbb Z\).
At \(a=p^k,\ k\ge2\), the earlier divisor bound has denominator at least \(p^{k-1}\), whereas the factor measures exceed any prescribed \(o(\log p)\) positive function in prime density.
The [exact full-measure result on an explicit set of prime squares](Yin_C_G0345_v729.md#statement) additionally identifies parameters with \(\mathcal R(p^2)=1\).

<a id="range"></a>
## Only small primes can occur in an index

Write \(H(a)=\operatorname{lcm}_{j=-1,0,1}( (a+j)/\operatorname{rad}(a+j))\), where \(\operatorname{rad}\) is the product of the distinct prime divisors.
By the [exceptional-seed count](Yin_C_G0345_v703.md#seeds), at most \(\exp(O_F(\sqrt{\log(X+1)}))\) parameters \(t\le X\) with \(a=F(t)\ge3\) can have \(R_{a,n,\epsilon}\nmid H(a)\) at any delay or sign.
Indeed \(F(t)\ll_F (X+1)^{\deg F}\), and every seed has at most \(\deg F\) preimages.
These parameters have relative density zero under either choice of \(\mathcal A\).
After removing them, \(\ell^j\mid R_{a,n,\epsilon}\) for a prime \(\ell\) and \(j\ge1\) implies \(\ell^{j+1}\mid V(t)\).
Independently the [prime-power bound](Yin_C_G0345_v701.md#statement) gives \(\ell^j\log(2\ell)\ll\log r_{a,n,\epsilon}\), and the [exterior root interval](Yin_C_G0345_v681.md#roots) gives \(a-1<r_{a,n,\epsilon}<a+1\).
Thus every prime dividing any index at \(t\le X\) is at most \(L=C_F\log(X+2)\) for a fixed \(C_F\).
At each fixed seed all the indices are bounded, since the same bound restricts each prime power separately.

For integer parameters set \(D=V\).
For prime parameters write \(V=T^\nu D\) with \(D(0)\ne0\) and discard the parameters \(p\le L\), whose relative proportion tends to zero.
In both cases \(D\in\mathbb Z[T]\) is squarefree over \(\mathbb Q\), and for every remaining parameter and every \(\ell\le L\) one has \(v_\ell(V(t))=v_\ell(D(t))\).
Hence, at all delays and both signs,
\[
 R_{a,n,\epsilon}\mid\prod_{\ell\le L,\ \ell\ {\rm prime}}
                    \ell^{(v_\ell(D(t))-1)_+},\qquad x_+=\max(x,0).    \tag{2}
\]

<a id="local"></a>
## The local root counts

Choose integer polynomials \(A_0,A_1\) and a nonzero integer \(m_0\) satisfying \(A_0D+A_1D'=m_0\) by the Euclidean algorithm over \(\mathbb Q\).
For \(\ell\nmid m_0\), the polynomial \(D\) has at most \(\deg D\) roots modulo \(\ell\), all simple.
Each lifts uniquely at every higher power, by solving one linear congruence at each step.
For any fixed \(\ell\mid m_0\), put \(v=v_\ell(m_0)\).
If \(D(x)\equiv0\bmod\ell^j\) with \(j>2v+1\), the identity above implies \(v_\ell(D'(x))\le v\).
For distinct integers \(x,y\) in the same class modulo \(\ell^{v+1}\), with both polynomial values zero modulo \(\ell^j\), Taylor expansion gives
\[
 v_\ell(D(y)-D(x))=v_\ell(y-x)+v_\ell(D'(x)).
\]
The linear term has strictly smaller valuation than every subsequent term.
Thus the roots in each such class lie in a single residue modulo \(\ell^{j-v}\), giving at most \(\ell^{2v+1}\) roots modulo \(\ell^j\) in total.
The same bound applies trivially at \(j\le2v+1\).

For fixed \(j\), let \(\delta_\ell(j)\) be the fraction of roots of \(D\) in the full residue system modulo \(\ell^j\) for integer parameters, or in its unit group for prime parameters.
Then \(\delta_\ell(j)\le 2\deg(D)\ell^{-j}\) for \(\ell\nmid m_0\), and \(\delta_\ell(j)\ll_{D,\ell}\ell^{-j}\) at each of the finitely many other primes.
Fixed residue conditions at distinct primes have their product frequencies, by the Chinese remainder theorem, using [prime equidistribution in reduced classes](../audits/literature/Ked07/Yin_U001.md#siegel-walfisz) for prime parameters.

<a id="density"></a>
## Truncation of the radical-prime product

Choose \(Y\) larger than every prime divisor of \(m_0\) and at least two.
The upper relative density of parameters for which some \(Y<\ell\le L\) satisfies \(\ell^2\mid D(t)\) is \(O_F(1/Y)\).
For integer parameters, each \(\ell\) excludes at most \(\deg(D)(X/\ell^2+1)\) integers.
For prime parameters, apply the [Siegel–Walfisz estimate](../audits/literature/Ked07/Yin_U001.md#siegel-walfisz) to each of the at most \(\deg(D)\) reduced residue classes modulo \(\ell^2\).
Its absolute error \(O(X/(\log X)^4)\) per class totals \(O_F(X/(\log X)^3)\), since \(L\ll_F\log X\).
The main terms divided by \(\pi(X)\sim\operatorname{li}(X)\) cost \(O_F(\sum_{\ell>Y}1/(\ell(\ell-1)))=O_F(1/Y)\).

For fixed integers \(Y,E\ge2\) set
\[
 W_{Y,E}(t)=\prod_{\ell\le Y,\ \ell\ {\rm prime}}
       \ell^{(\min(v_\ell(D(t)),E)-1)_+}.
\]
This variable is periodic with fixed modulus, with the fraction of exceptions \(t\) prime dividing the modulus tending to zero in the prime case.
Its limiting mean after raising to \(\theta\) is the product over \(\ell\le Y\) of the local means
\[
 1+\sum_{j=2}^E
  \big(\ell^{\theta(j-1)}-\ell^{\theta(j-2)}\big)\delta_\ell(j).
\]
For \(\ell\nmid m_0\) the sum is \(O_{F,\theta}(\ell^{\theta-2})\) by the geometric series, and at each other \(\ell\) the local mean is bounded in \(E\) by the same calculation with its fixed local constant.
Since \(\theta<1\), the product of the upper bounds converges, giving an upper bound \(C_{F,\theta}\) independent of \(Y,E\).
Markov's inequality gives limiting upper frequency at most \(C_{F,\theta}B^{-\theta}\) for \(W_{Y,E}>B\).

By (2), \(\mathcal R(F(t))\le W_{Y,E}(t)\) outside the previously discarded sets, the large-prime tail, and the parameters with \(v_\ell(D(t))>E\) for some \(\ell\le Y\).
The last set has upper density at most \(\sum_{\ell\le Y}\delta_\ell(E+1)\), which tends to zero at fixed \(Y\).
Letting \(E\to\infty\) and then \(Y\to\infty\) proves (1).

<a id="factors"></a>
## Growth of the factor measures

The [cofactor classification](Yin_C_G0345_v681.md#roots) gives \(G_{a,n,\epsilon}=P U\), with \(P\) the minimal polynomial of the exterior root and all roots of \(U\) in \(\{1,-1\}\).
The [least factor measure formula](Yin_C_G0345_v205.md#statement) gives a lower bound \(\log r_{a,n,\epsilon}/R_{a,n,\epsilon}\) for every noncyclotomic irreducible factor after every dilation.
For any fixed \(B\), the parameters with \(\mathcal R(F(t))\le B\) therefore give lower bound \(\log(F(t)-1)/B>f(t)\) for all sufficiently large \(t\), because \(\log(F(t)-1)\sim(\deg F)\log t\).
By (1) the upper density of exceptions is bounded by \(C_{F,\theta} B^{-\theta}\) for each \(B\), so it is zero.
For \(F=bT^k+c\), its derivative has no nonzero complex root and the three shifted polynomials have disjoint root sets.
Hence every nonzero root of \(V\) is simple, as required in the prime case.
For \(a=p^k\) the central term of \(H(a)\) equals \(p^{k-1}\), proving the comparison.
