# Local collision depths determine the interpolated row integer up to bounded powers

<a id="statement"></a>
## A denominator computed from the powered nodes

Let \(P,S,Q,J\) and the two integer rows \(U,V\) be as in the [critical-point interpolation](Yin_C_G0345_v518.md#rows).
Write \(B,E,E_2\) for the positive generators obtained by contracting \((U,V),(Q,Q'),(Q^2,Q')\), respectively, to \(\mathbb Z\).
Let \(D\) be the least positive integer for which \(D/Q'(z)\) is an algebraic integer at every root \(z\) of \(Q\).
Then
\[
 D\mid E\mid D^2,\qquad E\mid E_2\mid E^2,\qquad E_2\mid B\mid J E_2^4.
\]
In particular for any set \(\mathcal T\) of rational primes disjoint from the divisors of \(J\), write \(a_{\mathcal T}=\prod_{\ell\in\mathcal T}\ell^{v_\ell(a)}\).
Even when the retained set depends on the rows or their valuations,
\[
 \log D_{\mathcal T}\le\log B_{\mathcal T}\le16\log D_{\mathcal T}.
\]
The derivative denominator can be computed in any splitting field \(K\) of \(Q\).
For each rational prime \(\ell\), normalize every valuation \(v_{\mathfrak l}\) above it by \(v_{\mathfrak l}(\ell)=1\).
Then
\[
 v_\ell(D)=\left\lceil\max_{\mathfrak l\mid\ell,\ Q(z)=0}\;\sum_{w\ne z,\ Q(w)=0}v_{\mathfrak l}(z-w)\right\rceil.
\]
The formula follows by factoring \(Q'(z)\) and imposing the integrality condition separately at every prime ideal.
Only divisors of \(\operatorname{disc}Q\) can contribute.

<a id="comparison"></a>
## Integral interpolation and ideal inclusions

An identity for \(E\) evaluated at each \(z\) shows that \(E/Q'(z)\) is integral, so \(D\mid E\).
Conversely the polynomial
\[
 G(Y)=\sum_{Q(z)=0}\left(\frac{D}{Q'(z)}\right)^2\frac{Q(Y)}{Y-z}
\]
has Galois-invariant integral coefficients, hence belongs to \(\mathbb Z[Y]\).
Evaluating at each node gives \(G(z)Q'(z)=D^2\), so monic division gives \(D^2\in(Q,Q')\).
The inclusion \((Q^2,Q')\subseteq(Q,Q')\) and the square of an identity for \(E\) give \(E\mid E_2\mid E^2\).
Set \(W_0=YQ'\).
Since \(Q(0)=1\), \(Y\) is a unit modulo \(Q^2\), and the [row formulas](Yin_C_G0345_v518.md#rows) give
\[
 (U,V)\subseteq(U,W_0)=(Q^2,W_0)=(Q^2,Q').
\]
This yields \(E_2\mid B\), and taking the fourth power of an identity for \(E_2\in(U,W_0)\) gives \(E_2^4\in(U,W_0^4)\).
Now \(JW_0^4=Y^2V\) gives the asserted multiple of \(B\).

## Collision depths remaining after adaptive deletions

Let \(S\) consist of all primes in \([L,2L]\), set \(r=|S|,\ \Sigma=\sum_{p\in S}p\), and use the [calibrated width](Yin_C_G0345_v513.md#width) \(b=rj(L)\).
Take any monic reciprocal nontorsion unit minimum of degree \(e\le(j(L)-1)/4\) and measure \(h=\log M(P)\le(\log2)/5\).
All estimates below hold uniformly over these seeds for sufficiently large \(L\).
A collision of distinct conjugates under \(p\)-th powering puts a nontrivial \(p\)-th root of unity in the compositum of two fields of degree \(e\), forcing \(p-1\le e^2\).
Since \(j\ll(\log L)^{1/4}\), this is impossible on these intervals.
The row degree \(4er+1\) fits \(rj\), and the [height bound](Yin_C_G0345_v518.md#statement) yields \(\log H\le4h\Sigma+o(\Sigma)\le\Sigma\log2\).
Indeed \(er/\Sigma\le e/L\), \(\log J/\Sigma\le\log(2L)/L\), and \(\log(er)/\Sigma=o(1)\) uniformly since \(r\le2L\).

Let \(\mathcal N=\{p+q:p,q\in S,\ p<q\}\) and \(W=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N}\varphi(n)\).
Delete all rational primes up to \(L\sqrt{\log L}\) and any further \(\lfloor W/(16Le)\rfloor\) primes, chosen after inspecting the seed, rows or collision depths.
Let \(\mathcal T\) consist of the remaining primes dividing \(\operatorname{Res}(P,\Phi_n)\) for at least one \(n\in\mathcal N\) coprime to that prime.
The [many-prime row estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v513.md#remove) with multiplicity two gives \(|\mathcal T|\gg L/e\) and \(e\log B_{\mathcal T}\gg hW\).
The divisors of \(J\) have all been removed, so every one of those primes divides \(D\) and
\[
 e\log D_{\mathcal T}\gg hL^2,\qquad \frac{\log D_{\mathcal T}}{\Sigma}\gg j(L)\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Here \(W\asymp L^2\), \(W/\Sigma\asymp\log L\), and the [width calibration](Yin_C_G0345_v513.md#remove) gives \(h\log L/e\gg j(L)\).
The conclusions concern an integer depending only on the seed and the exponent set, even though the deletions may use both rows.
If the unrestricted Lehmer infimum vanishes, reciprocal seed measures can approach zero by the [reciprocal reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v408.md#lower).
Holding each such seed fixed until its degree fits the growing width and then taking successively smaller measures yields actual rows of normalized cost tending to zero together with these divergent retained collision depths.
Conversely any such sequence of rows of vanishing normalized cost implies zero unrestricted infimum by the [common graph lower bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#tangencies).
