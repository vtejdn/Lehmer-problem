# The exact minimum at conjugate argument rank three through degree forty-five

<a id="statement"></a>
## The minimum and its compressed representative

Let \(k(w)\) be the rational multiplicative rank modulo torsion of the arguments \(a/|a|\) of the algebraic conjugates of a nontorsion algebraic unit \(w\).
Among such units of degree at most 45 with \(k(w)=3\), the least multiplicative Mahler measure is the unique real root \(\lambda>1\) of
\[
                   X^8-X^5-X^4-X^3+1=0,\qquad
                   1.2806<\lambda<1.2807 .
\]
The same minimum holds for \(k(w)\le3\) in this degree range.
In fact, every such input of rank at most three with \(M(w)<13/10\) has \(M(w)=\lambda\), has rank three, and its torsion-class product has minimum polynomial \(P(X)\) or \(P(-X)\), where
\[
                         P(X)=X^8+X^5-X^4+X^3+1 .
\]
Conversely either compressed minimum gives these values for the original input.
The conclusions on the minimum also hold for nonzero nontorsion algebraic integers, since nonunits have measure at least two.
These statements concern the given degree range; no minimum in larger degrees is asserted.

<a id="cut"></a>
## From all inputs to eight primitive rows

It suffices to classify units of rank at most three with \(M(w)<13/10\), once an attaining unit below this cutoff has been exhibited.
Equality with \(13/10\) is impossible for a unit because its multiplicative measure is an algebraic integer.
Apply the [torsion-class compression](Yin_C_G0901_v68.md#degree).
It preserves total measure and the rational conjugate space with its conjugation action, hence preserves argument rank.
It yields an exponent-primitive polynomial since the compressed roots are distinct modulo roots of unity.
The [nonreciprocal lower bound](../audits/literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-statement-and-conventions) forces reciprocity, and the compressed degree is even, at most 44.
Thus the compressed input occurs up to \(X\mapsto-X\) in the [complete primitive catalogue](../audits/literature/Mos26/readme.md#complete-small-cores).

For compressed degree \(2n\), write \(u\) for the number of neutral inverse pairs, \(l=n-u\) for the exterior conjugate count, and \(b\) for the rational conjugate class rank.
The [signature inequality](Yin_C_G0901_v90.md#dimension) requires \(u\le3l\) and \(b\le\lfloor3n/u\rfloor\) for \(k(w)\le3\) whenever \(u>0\).
The compressed splitting group acts faithfully on these classes.
At every squarefree prime the least common multiple of the irreducible factor degrees of the full reciprocal polynomial therefore has to be the order of a finite-order rational matrix in dimension at most \(b\).
Such an order is a least common multiple of indices of distinct cyclotomic polynomials with total degree at most \(b\), by the semisimple minimal polynomial of the matrix.
The variable sign changes neither the signature nor the finite-field degree patterns.

The [rank-three exhaustion](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0191/rank_three.md#exhaustion) checks all 944 rows.
The signature inequality excludes 519; the others all have \(u>0\) and a hypothetical rank bound at most six.
At the primes \(2,3,5,11,13\), forbidden orders first exclude another \(369,40,6,1,1\) rows respectively.
This leaves the eight source lines \(152,158,159,162,164,165,166,193\).
The faithfulness used in these exclusions belongs to the hypothetical compressed witness.

<a id="signs"></a>
## Primitivity and signed Frobenius elements at seven rows

For each of the seven lines other than 152, use \(P_0=X^nQ(X+X^{-1})\) for its polynomial and trace.
The action on the \(n\) inverse-pair labels is transitive by the irreducibility of \(P_0\).
For the two degree-ten rows it is primitive since \(n=5\) is prime.
For the four degree-twelve rows and the degree-sixteen row the [modular certificates](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0191/rank_three.md#flips) give an \((n-1)\)-cycle with one fixed label on \(Q\).
Its fixed-label stabilizer is therefore transitive on all other labels.
Transitivity of the group then implies double transitivity, in particular primitivity.

At the specified flip prime on each row take a Frobenius element in the common splitting field of \(P_0\) and the trace roots.
Both polynomials have squarefree reduction.
Let \(e\) be the order of its permutation of the roots of \(Q\), computed from their factor degrees.
Its \(e\)th power lies in the sign kernel \(J\) of the action on inverse pairs.
On any full root cycle it moves all the roots precisely when that cycle length fails to divide \(e\), and otherwise it fixes them all.
Dividing the count of moved roots by two gives a sign change on strictly between zero and \(n\) labels on each of the seven rows.
For example on line 162 the trace factors modulo two have degrees \(2,4\); the full factors have degrees \(4,8\).
The fourth power fixes the trace labels and flips precisely four of the six pairs.

The coordinate sign characters of \(J\) define an invariant equivalence relation on the labels by equality of characters.
The exhibited nonconstant sign pattern rules out a single equivalence class.
Primitivity then forces all equivalence classes to be singletons.
Consequently the rational signed permutation module on the \(n\) oriented inverse pairs is irreducible: averaging over \(J\) against a single character projects onto its coordinate, and the full group acts transitively on these coordinate lines.
Its natural map to the class span of the actual roots is nonzero and surjective, so it is an isomorphism, as in the [signed-action criterion](Yin_C_G0901_v58.md#signs).
The fixed dimension of ordinary complex conjugation on this module is the number of real exterior conjugates plus the number of complex conjugate pairs of exterior conjugates.
Indeed a real inverse pair gives one fixed coordinate, a neutral pair none, and a signed swap of two remaining labels gives exactly one fixed dimension.
The complementary dimension is the argument rank.
Using the exact trace signatures gives ranks four on lines 158 and 159, five on lines 162, 164, 165 and 166, and six on line 193.

<a id="attainment"></a>
## The degree-eight Salem value

Line 152 has exactly the displayed polynomial \(P\).
Its [Sturm signature and root isolation](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0191/rank_three.md#winner) give one pair of reciprocal negative real roots, one exterior, and three neutral inverse pairs.
Thus \(M(P)\) is the absolute value of that sole exterior root, equivalently the unique real root \(>1\) of \(P(-X)\), at the stated rational interval.
With \(n=4,\ l=1\), the [radial dimension bound](Yin_C_G0901_v64.md#degree) gives \(4r\le b\), where \(r\ge1\) is the radial rank.
Also \(b\le4\) because four oriented representatives generate the class space.
Hence \(r=1,\ b=4\), and \(k=3\) by the [negative-eigenspace identity](Yin_C_G0901_v68.md#selection).
The polynomial is an irreducible row of the catalogue and its unit roots attain the value.
The sign substitution preserves all these properties.
The seven exclusions and the compression argument now give the classification and minimum through degree 45.
