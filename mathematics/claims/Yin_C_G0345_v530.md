# Exact unrestricted coefficient cost on prescribed exponent sets after one common dilation

<a id="statement"></a>
## One dilation for every fixed seed and every admissible width

Fix \(C>1\).
For integers \(L\to\infty\) supply any nonempty sets \(S_L\subset[L,CL]\) of distinct positive integers with \(r=|S_L|\to\infty\) and \(s=\sum_{p\in S_L}p\).
From \(L,S_L\) one can compute a power of three \(g=g_L\) with the following properties, independently of the width and the seed eventually used in the test.
One may impose any additional prescribed computable lower bound on \(g\).
Use the actual exponents \(gp\) with total \(\Sigma=gs\), and any integer widths \(b=b_L\) satisfying \(b/r\to\infty\).

Fix a monic reciprocal minimum \(P\) of a nontorsion unit such that distinct conjugates have nontorsion quotient, and write \(h=\log M(P)\).
For each \(L\), let \(H_P\) be the least \(\max(\|U\|_1,\|V\|_1)\) over coprime nonzero integer polynomials of degrees at most \(b\), with \(UU^\iota-VV^\iota\ne0\), \(\iota(Y)=Y^{-1}\), such that \(P^2\) divides every \(U(X^{gp})+XV(X^{gp})\).
Such rows exist eventually and
\[
                              \frac{\log H_P}{\Sigma}\longrightarrow h.       \tag{1}
\]
All exterior conjugates contribute, with arbitrary coincidences between their radial scales in different exponent slots.

Let \(\lambda\) be the unrestricted infimum of positive logarithmic Mahler measures.
In the same test allow any common reciprocal seed, including seeds outside the fixed class above, and minimize with fallback \(2^\Sigma\) and cutoff \(H\le2^\Sigma\).
The normalized logarithm of this finite minimum converges to \(\lambda\).
For the [prescribed odd blocks and their actual calibrated width](Yin_C_G0345_v524.md#width), one may simultaneously require every primitive rational direction of every qualifying pair to have logarithmic measure at least \(\Sigma/f(\Sigma)\) for any prescribed computable nondecreasing unbounded positive integer function \(f\).
The common dilation is selected for each base set before testing the rows.
No upper bound on the first passing dilation is required.

<a id="coincident"></a>
## All labels at a coincident radial scale

First hold a finite set \(T\) of distinct positive integers, the seed \(P\), and a nonnegative integer degree bound \(d\) fixed.
As \(g\to\infty\) through positive integers, every nonzero integral pair of degree at most \(d\) satisfying the homogeneous double contacts on \(gT\) has
\[
      \log H\ \ge\ gh\sum_{p\in T}p-o_{P,T,d}(g).                    \tag{2}
\]
If \(T\) is empty this is immediate.
For each positive radial scale \(\rho=p\log|c|\), collect all labels \((p,c)\) giving that scale, with \(c\) an exterior conjugate.
Put \(z_{p,c}(n)=(e^\rho/c^p)^n\).
Their bases are algebraic of modulus one, since \(e^\rho=|c|^p\).
The phases for distinct labels in a group are distinct for every positive integer \(n\).
Indeed \(c^p/d_0^q\) torsion for another conjugate \(d_0\) implies equality of the absolute logarithmic heights \(p h/\deg P=q h/\deg P\), then \(p=q\) and the seed hypothesis forces \(c=d_0\).

Suppose the group has \(m\) labels.
On two coefficient lists \(A,B\) of lengths \(m\), form the \(2m\) square equations with rows
\[
 A(z)-cB(z),\qquad Xp z(A'-cB')(z)+c B(z),
                       \qquad z=z_{p,c}(n).
\]
Let \(D(X,n)\) be the determinant.
At \(X=0\) the derivative rows force \(B\) to vanish at \(m\) distinct nodes, and the value rows then force the same for \(A\).
Consequently \(D(0,n)\ne0\) for every positive \(n\), even when the exponents \(p\) differ within this group.
Expansion gives \(D(X,n)=\sum_\beta P_\beta(X)\beta^n\) with finitely many algebraic unit-modulus bases and algebraic polynomial coefficients.
On each progression killing all torsion ratios, the grouped coefficient polynomials cannot all vanish identically, since their values at \(X=0\) sum to \(D(0,n_0)\ne0\) for a positive representative \(n_0\).
After substituting \(X=n\), the surviving bases on this progression have pairwise nontorsion quotients.
The [archimedean recurrence bound](../audits/literature/Xia24/Yin_U001.md#arch) therefore gives \(\log|D(g,g)|\ge-o(g)\) with eventual nonvanishing, by the [progression argument](Yin_C_G0345_v434.md#recurrences).
All cofactors grow at most polynomially in \(g\), so the inverse of the exact square system has norm at most \(\exp(o(g))\).

For completeness, suppose (2) fails along a sequence with bounded \((\log H)/g\).
Reverse at the actual maximum degree, change the sign of the second row, and pass to a fixed coefficient support and degree.
Let \(\gamma_j\ge0\) be the subsequential limits of \(g^{-1}\log\|(u_j,v_j)\|_\infty\) at the occupied indices; index zero is occupied.
For \(E(x)=\max_j(\gamma_j-jx)\), denote the first and last maximizers at \(x=\rho\) by \(a,b_0\).
The [reversed value and derivative equations](Yin_C_G0345_v487.md#exterior) on the block \(a,\ldots,b_0\), with coefficients weighted by \(\exp(-g\rho(j-a))\) and scaled to maximum norm one, give the displayed rows at \(X=n=g\) with errors \(O(e^{-\eta g})\).
Indeed every omitted term has a strictly smaller limiting exponent, \(p\) and the original degree bound are fixed, and shifting by \(a\) adds only a multiple of the value equation to the derivative equation.
If no terms are omitted the errors vanish.
If \(b_0-a<m\), pad this block by zeros to length \(m\) and apply the inverse bound, contradicting the normalization.
Thus the index jump at each distinct radial scale is at least its full group size.
The sum over positive breakpoints of their values times their index jumps equals \(E(0)-\gamma_0\) by summing the linear segments.
It is at least \(\sum_{p,c}p\log|c|=h\sum_{p\in T}p\), where \(c\) ranges over exterior conjugates.
This proves (2) even for pairs with common factors or zero reflected difference.

<a id="choice"></a>
## A finite test before selecting the width

Enumerate the reciprocal nontorsion unit minima of degree at most \(L\) and multiplicative Mahler measure at most \(2^L\) whose conjugates have no nontrivial torsion quotient.
The [finite coefficient box and power-degree test](Yin_C_G0345_v487.md#packets) are effective.
For this base set take \(d=rL\).
Test the full \(T=S_L\) and each \(T=S_L\setminus\{p\}\).
For every listed seed and every nonzero integral pair of degrees at most \(d\) with homogeneous double contacts on \(gT\), require
\[
                        2^g H\ \ge\ M(P)^{\,g\sum_{q\in T}q}.         \tag{3}
\]
For each proposed \(g\), only norms up to the right side need be searched, a finite calculation with integral polynomials and algebraic comparisons.
At fixed \(L\) all its finitely many tests eventually pass by (2).
The search over \(g=3^a\) therefore terminates and any lower bound can be imposed first.
Each fixed eligible seed lies on the list for all sufficiently large \(L\).
This construction certifies a loss \(g\log2\) on all the specified deletions without requiring an effective onset for the recurrence theorem.

<a id="sharp"></a>
## Recovering every prescribed slot and the unrestricted infimum

For the given widths set \(q=\min(\lfloor b/r\rfloor,\lfloor\sqrt L\rfloor,r)\), \(w=rq\) and \(t=2+\lceil r/q^{1/3}\rceil\) as in the [protected-slot construction](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#counts).
In particular \(q\to\infty,\ w\le rL,\ t\to\infty,\ t=o(r),\ r^2=o(wt)\) and \(w+r=o(tL)\).
All powered minima at the actual exponents preserve degree and have the respective measures \(gph\), hence are distinct.
The construction there uses the one-exterior assumption to supply the homogeneous separation for packing and the same separation after dropping one slot.
Here (3) supplies both, with error \(g\log2\) in log one-norm, for every solution and every required quotient within width \(w\).

More explicitly use the [integral Hasse jet count](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#counts) at scale \(gL\).
At all double slots use the coefficient box with \(\log B=h\Sigma+htgL\) up to rounding.
Its homogeneous fiber differences have sup separation at least \(\exp(h\Sigma-g\log2)/(w+1)\).
The log count exceeds the packing bound for a space of dimension \(w+1\) by \(whtgL-o(wtgL)\).
The box errors \(O_{P,C}(r\Sigma+rtgL+w(g+\log(2w))+r\log(2gLw))\) are \(o(wtgL)\) by the parameter bounds and \(\log(2gLw)/(gL)\to0\) uniformly in \(g\ge1\).
Polarization supplies a homogeneous pair with nonzero reflected difference.

For each designated \(p\), request contact of order \(t\) there and two elsewhere, at \(\log B=h\Sigma+3tgph/4\) up to rounding.
The [designated-slot count](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#protect) still gives a nonzero pair since its excess is at least \(wtgph/2-o(wtgL)\); the extra jet expense \(O_{P,C}((r+t^2)\log(2gLw))\) is \(o(wtgL)\).
If the common power of the minimum of \(\alpha^{gp}\) in its entries were \(\nu\ge t-1\), divide by this common power.
The remaining slots retain double contacts and (3) bounds the resulting log one-norm below by \(h(\Sigma-gp)-g\log2\).
The integral-factor upper bound \(w\log2+\log(w+1)+\log B-\nu gph\) would then give \((t/4-2)gph\le O(w+\log(2w)+g)\), contradicting \(w+g=o(tgL)\).
So the divided pair retains a double contact and is nonzero at its designated node.
The [finite grid argument](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#combine) combines all the pairs, preserves every node and removes their common polynomial factor with total additional log cost \(O(w+\log(2r))=o(\Sigma)\).
This proves the upper bound \(h\Sigma+o(\Sigma)\) for the fixed seed on the selected dilations, at every width in the statement.
The [tangency inequality](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v243.md#tangencies) supplies the matching lower bound.

For the finite minimum each actual pair has normalized cost at least \(\lambda\), as does the fallback.
The [reciprocal reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v408.md#lower) and [torsion-class product](Yin_C_G0345_v264.md#labels) give seeds with nontorsion conjugate quotients and measures arbitrarily close to \(\lambda<\log2\); the class products remain reciprocal units since inversion permutes the original classes.
Taking the upper limit in (1) for each fixed such seed, and then the infimum over these seeds, proves the limit \(\lambda\).
For the odd blocks impose also \(f(gs)\ge L^2\) in the search.
The [primitive pencil bound](Yin_C_G0345_v524.md#rate) then holds at the width \(rj(L,g)\), and the width ratio tends to infinity uniformly over \(g\).
This proves the two assertions simultaneously on the same prescribed blocks.
