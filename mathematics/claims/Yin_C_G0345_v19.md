# Uniform Pisot excess for both primitive cubic offset pairs

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact branch-two numerators and a prefix criterion

Let a positive-constant Pisot minimal polynomial have constant \(q\ge2\), exterior root \(\theta\), branch index \(j\), and [trace numerator](Yin_C_G0345_v7.md#trace) \(C\) of degree \(h\).
On branch \(j=2\) there are absolute constants \(c>0\) and \(Q<\infty\) such that \(\theta-q\ge c\) and \(q\le Q\) whenever
\[
 C(x)=2U_h(x/2)+aU_{h-md}(x/2)+bU_{h-3d}(x/2),
 \qquad m\in\{1,2\},\quad 1\le d,\quad 3d\le h,\quad a,b\in\mathbb Z .
\]
Here \(d\) is an integer and \(U_n\) is the Chebyshev polynomial of the second kind.
This includes both parities, all signs and all common multiples of the primitive offset pairs \((1,3),(2,3)\).
Together with the [quadratic case](Yin_C_G0345_v18.md#statement), it forces the upper offset divided by the gcd of the two offsets to be at least four eventually in every six-term branch-two sequence with \(q\to\infty\).

There is also a criterion on every branch \(j\ge1\).
Fix \(M<\infty,\ \eta>0\), write \(\Pi=(1-z^2)z^h C(z+z^{-1})\), and assume \(\|\Pi/j\|_\infty\le M\) on the unit circle.
Suppose the coefficients of \(\Pi/j\) through \(\lfloor\eta h\rfloor\) agree with \(G(z)S(z^d)\), where \(G\in1+z\mathbb Z[[z]]\) and \(d\) is an integer in \([1,h]\).
There exist \(\varepsilon,c'>0\), depending only on \(M,\eta\), such that \(\theta-q\ge c'\) in either of the following cases.

1. \(2S(w)=2+a w^m+b w^3\) with \(m\in\{1,2\}\), \(a,b\in\mathbb Z\), and this polynomial has no zero in the open unit disk.
2. \(S(w)=1+\lambda w+\mu w^2+\nu w^3\), and the real triple \((\lambda,\mu,\nu)\) has maximum-norm distance at most \(\varepsilon\) from the nonconstant coefficient triple of \(A(sw)/2\), where \(s=\pm1\) and
   \[
          A(w)\in\{\,2-w+w^3,\quad 2-2w+w^3,\quad 2+w^2+w^3\,\}.
   \]

The integral unit can vary with the input.
In the factorizations in case 1, one may also perturb the residual linear or quadratic factor within the ranges in the [factor argument](#factors), retaining the integral factor in \(G\).

## The three irreducible cubics

Suppose the neighborhood assertion fails for fixed \(M,\eta\).
Take inputs with excess tending to zero and coefficients tending to one of the six centers, using finiteness and shrinking neighborhoods.
Write \(S_0=A(sw)/2\) for this limit and \(S_i\) for the actual varying prefix factors.
The [inverse-measure estimates](Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#integrality) give \(h\to\infty\), an integer denominator \(B^\sharp=(\Pi/j)J_i\) with \(M(B^\sharp)>1\) tending to one, and a symmetric signed representing measure of mass one and vanishing negative mass for \(J_i\).
Here \(M(B^\sharp)\) denotes multiplicative Mahler measure.
The normalized prefix is integral below \(d\) as far as the given agreement extends, so the [sublinear first nondivisible index](Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#statement) forces \(d=o(h)\).
The formal division \(X=B^\sharp/G\) has integer coefficients and gives \(X=S_i(z^d)J_i\) throughout the common prefix.

For the three cubics in the displayed order the first fractional limiting coefficient occurs at \(d,3d,2d\), respectively.
In the middle case the coefficient at \(d\) tends to an integer, and the coefficient at \(2d\) tends to zero.
The prefix recurrence therefore puts the bounded coefficients of \(J_i\) within \(o(1)\) of integers through index \(3d-1\).
In the last case the coefficient at \(d\) tends to zero and its product with any of these bounded moments tends to zero.
At the first fractional index the coefficient is a signed half in all three cases.
Testing on either side of that index at shifts \(rt\), for \(t=o(d)\), gives the mixed moment integrality conditions of the [Haar marginal theorem](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#haar).
The resulting initial interval of vanishing coefficients and Bessel's inequality on blocks between the four offsets give bounded support and bounded absolute coefficient sum for \(X\) on each \([0,Ld]\) with \(L\) fixed.
For bounded \(d\) the latter conclusion follows directly from the bounded moments.
The respective prefix recurrences and block bounds are given for the [middle cubic](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v80.md#return) and the [two unit-coefficient cubics](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v81.md#return).

At scales \(d/t\to0,\ t/h\to0\), a limit of the inverse measures on \((v^d,v^t)\) is a probability.
Testing at multiples of \(d\) makes \(S_0J\) integral for its first marginal transform \(J\).
The [five profiles](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v80.md#profiles) for the middle cubic and the [eight profiles for each other cubic](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v81.md#profiles) exhaust these transforms, with rotation by \(s\).
At \(kt+ld\), for fixed \(k>0,\ l\in\mathbb Z\), the reflected weighted row has integer Fourier coefficients by the same prefix identity, since all four indices are positive and the coefficient perturbations multiply bounded moments.
The [row decomposition](Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#far-rows) gives continuous density \(H_k=S_0\sigma c_k\), where \(\sigma\) is twice the continuous marginal density and the \(c_k\) are moments of common conditional probabilities.
The cubic [middle-case envelope bounds](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v80.md#extinction) and [unit-coefficient envelope bounds](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v81.md#extinction) give finitely many integer shapes up to translation for \(H_k\), each of Mahler measure strictly less than two.
Irreducibility and \(M(A)=2\) prevent any nonzero shape from containing \(A(sw)\).
The resulting exterior pole in the phase quotient excludes nonzero real specialized moments at sufficiently large prime-order nodes, and the finite-valued periodic tails force all late rows to vanish.
At a last nonzero row, positivity after the corresponding power pushforward gives \(|c_k|\le1/2\), contradicting the envelope measure strictly below two.
Thus only the marginal atoms contribute to the mixed rows.
For the three cubics their orders divide \(4,2,2\), respectively, including the rotations.
The integer coefficients of \(X\) at \(t\) and \(t+4d\) consequently agree eventually along these intermediate scales.

By diagonal extraction the equality holds throughout \(Ld\le t\le h/L\) for some fixed \(L\) with \(1/L<\eta\), on the hypothetical sequence.
Put \(W=1-z^{4d}\) and truncate \(WX\) at \(\lfloor h/L\rfloor\) to form \(Q_0\).
It has degree \(O_L(d)\) and bounded absolute coefficient sum by the period and the initial support bound.
All roots of each limiting cubic lie strictly outside the unit disk, so \(1/S_i\) has uniformly exponentially decaying Taylor coefficients on this sequence.
The [progression expansion](Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#comparison) therefore represents the positive coefficients of \(J_*=Q_0/[W S_i(z^d)]\) by a measure of bounded variation.
The two series \(J_i,J_*\) agree through \(\lfloor h/L\rfloor\).
In the identity
\[
 S_i(z^d)W B^\sharp-Q_0\Pi/j
      =S_i(z^d)W(\Pi/j)(J_i-J_*),
\]
the left polynomial has degree \(O(h)\), and the polynomial multiplier on the right has bounded circle supremum.
The [two-cutoff argument](Yin_C_G0345_v18.md#far) bounds the left circle \(L^1\) norm.
Since \(Q_0\Pi/j\) is bounded and \(S_i\) has a uniform positive circle minimum, \(WB^\sharp\) has bounded \(L^1\) norm and hence [bounded integer support](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v50.md#window).
Its Mahler measure is \(M(B^\sharp)>1\) tending to one, contradicting the [fixed-support gap](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v32.md#boyd-return).
This proves case 2 uniformly for all six centers.

## Exact integer prefixes and perturbations after dividing out integral factors

Write \(P=2+a w^m+b w^3\) as in case 1.
If its nonconstant coefficients vanish, \(P/2=1\), so the [integral prefix bound](Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#statement) applies.
If there is just one nonconstant term \(c w^k\), the root-modulus condition gives \(|c|\le2\).
For \(|c|=2\) the entire prefix is integral, and for \(|c|=1\) the remaining factor is \(1\pm w^k/2\).
More generally the [linear prefix theorem](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v71.md#unit-prefix) permits replacing the latter by \(1+\lambda w^k\) for every fixed \(0<\alpha\le|\lambda|\le\beta<1\), with a bound depending only on \(\alpha,\beta,M,\eta\).
In particular \(1/4\le|\lambda|\le3/4\) gives neighborhoods of both signs with constants depending only on \(M,\eta\).
If the effective offset \(kd\) exceeds \(h\), the prefix through \(\lfloor\min(1,\eta)h\rfloor\) is already integral.

Now suppose \(ab\ne0\).
The product of the cubic root moduli is \(2/|b|\), so \(|b|\le2\).
At equality every root lies on the circle, making \(P\) equal up to sign to \(w^3P(1/w)\) by real conjugation.
Its support would then contain both \(m\) and \(3-m\), which are distinct.
Thus \(|b|=1\), and \(|a|\le3\) since otherwise the middle term strictly dominates on the circle and forces interior zeros by Rouché's theorem.
Factor \(P\) over the integers, choosing every factor to have positive constant term.
The leading coefficients have modulus one and precisely one irreducible factor \(F\) has constant two, with multiplicity one.
Each remaining factor has constant one, so its root moduli are all one and it is cyclotomic up to sign by Kronecker's theorem.
Their product \(E\), with its full multiplicities, has constant one.
Replacing \(G(z)\) by \(G(z)E(z^d)\) leaves the residual prefix \(F(z^d)/2\).

If \(P\) is reducible then \(\deg F\le2\), and the [quadratic factor analysis](Yin_C_G0345_v18.md#quadratic) applies.
A linear \(F\) or a quadratic with zero middle coefficient gives the preceding binomial criterion.
For \(F=2+u w+v w^2\) with \(u\ne0,\ |v|=1\), the same dominance argument gives \(|u|\le3\).
When \(v=-1,\ |u|\ge2\), evaluating at \(-\operatorname{sgn}u\) gives an interior zero by sign change.
The cases \(v=-1,\ |u|=1\) and \(v=1,\ |u|=3\) factor as \((1+s_1w)(2+s_2w)\) for signs \(s_1,s_2\), so cannot be an irreducible \(F\).
The remaining normalized quadratics have pairs \((\pm1/2,1/2)\) or \((\pm1,1/2)\).
After absorbing \(E\) these two coefficients may both vary in the neighborhoods of the [stable quadratic criterion](Yin_C_G0345_v18.md#statement).
The perturbations here are of the indicated residual factor, with the integral factor retained exactly in the common prefix.

For irreducible \(P\), rotating \(w\) by the sign of \(b\) reduces the leading coefficient to \(+1\).
For \(m=1\), the rotated \(a\ge2\) gives a sign change between \(-1\) and zero, and \(a=1,-3\) gives a root at \(-1,1\), respectively.
Thus \(a=-1,-2\).
For \(m=2\), \(a\le-2\) gives a sign change on the same interval, and \(a=-1\) gives a root at \(-1\).
If \(a\ge2\), the value at \(-2\) is \(4a-6>0\), so there is a root below \(-2\) and the product of the two remaining root moduli is less than one.
This leaves \(a=1\).
These are exactly the three [irreducible expanding cubics](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v77.md#cubic), so case 2 applies to either rotation.
Taking the binomial range \([1/4,3/4]\) for the modulus, all residual estimates use \(M,\eta\) and finitely many fixed coefficient neighborhoods, giving a common lower bound for case 1 and these perturbations.

## Exhaustion at upper primitive offset three

For the exact trace numerator in the statement set \(N=2h+2\), \(P=2+a w^m+b w^3\).
The lift is \(P(z^d)-z^N P(z^{-d})\), whose reflected terms begin above degree \(h\).
If \(a\) or \(b\) vanishes, the [four-term theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v15.md#statement) gives an absolute excess bound, including the binomial lift when both vanish.
If either uniform conclusion failed, the [fixed-branch identity](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#second) would yield a sequence with both \(q\to\infty\) and \(\theta-q\to0\).
In fact \(2=(\theta-q)(q+\theta^{-1}-H_q'(0))\) with \(|H_q'(0)|\le2\) on this branch.
On the remaining six-term sequence the [fixed-pair reduction](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v61.md#six) gives, after extraction, constant nonzero \(a,b\), \(|a|\le3,\ |b|=1\), and no zero of \(P\) in the open disk.
The normalized lift now has circle supremum at most \(2+|a|+|b|\le6\), with prefix \(P(z^d)/2\) through \(h\).
Case 1 at \(M=6,\eta=1,G=1\) contradicts the vanishing excess and proves both absolute bounds.
The same proof covers the rotated center by testing its actual coefficients, without requiring that rotation realize another trace input.
