# Argument ranks of all constituents and a Voutier bound throughout the divisible conjugate group

<a id="statement"></a>
## Representatives for individual constituents and for the least argument rank

Let \(w\) be a nontorsion algebraic unit, \(m(w)=[\mathbb Q(w):\mathbb Q]h(w)\) its total logarithmic Mahler measure and \(D(w)\) the divisible group generated by its conjugates and all roots of unity.
Write \(V_y\) for the rational multiplicative span of the conjugates of \(y\) modulo torsion inside \(\overline{\mathbb Q}^{\,\times}/\mu_\infty\), with its absolute Galois action.
For ordinary complex conjugation \(c\), the argument rank of \(y\) is \(k(y)=\dim V_y^{-c}\).
The following conclusions hold for all \(0<m(w)<\varepsilon\) with an absolute \(\varepsilon>0\).

There is a finite sequence \(z_1,\ldots,z_s\) of compressed reciprocal units in the ordinary multiplicative group of the conjugates of \(w\), with strictly decreasing degrees \(d_i\le\deg w\) and \(0<m(z_s)\le\cdots\le m(z_1)=m(w)\).
There are absolutely irreducible rational submodules \(W_1,\ldots,W_s\) of pairwise distinct dimensions such that
\[
            V_w=V_{z_1},\qquad V_{z_i}=\bigoplus_{h=i}^s W_h .
\]
These \(W_i\) are all the actual irreducible rational subspaces of \(V_w\).
For the \(u_i\) canonical blocks of \(z_i\), one has \(u_i\ll(\log(3d_i))^{212}\) and \(d_{i+1}\le2u_i\) whenever \(i<s\).
For each \(W_i\), its dimension \(t_i\) is \(d_i/2\) or \(d_i/2-u_i\), and
\[
     j_i=\dim W_i^{-c},\qquad
     j_i\le k(z_i)\le j_i+u_i,\qquad
     j_i>\sum_{h>i}j_h .                                                   \tag{1}
\]
The possible \(k(x)\) for nontorsion \(x\in D(w)\) are precisely the nonempty subset sums of the \(j_i\), all distinct.
In particular \(z_s\) attains the least of these ranks inside the ordinary conjugate group with no increase of total measure.

As \(m(w)\to0\), uniformly for every \(i\),
\[
       d_i\le4j_i+o\big(\sqrt{j_i\log j_i}\big),\qquad
       j_i\ge t_i/2-o\big(\sqrt{t_i\log t_i}\big).                         \tag{2}
\]
In particular, for each nontorsion \(x\in D(w)\) and each specified irreducible rational subspace \(W\subseteq V_x\), the chain gives \(z_i\) with \(W=W_i\subseteq V_{z_i}\), \(d_i\le4k(x)+o(\sqrt{k(x)\log k(x)})\), and \(k(z_i)\le k(x)+u_i\).
The same degree and argument bounds in terms of \(\dim W^{-c}\) as in (1) and (2) apply to any least-degree realization in \(D(w)\) under the budget \(m(z)\le m(w)\) and constraint \(W\subseteq V_z\), with \(u\) taken for that realization.
The argument rank of every such \(x\) is at least \(\dim V_x/2-o(\sqrt{\dim V_x\log \dim V_x})\) along shrinking sequences.

For all sufficiently large \(k=k(x)\), uniformly over nontorsion units \(w\) and nontorsion \(x\in D(w)\),
\[
 m(w)>\frac14\left(\frac{\log\log(5k)}{\log(5k)}\right)^3
      =\left(\frac14-o(1)\right)\left(\frac{\log\log k}{\log k}\right)^3.  \tag{3}
\]
In particular (3) applies to the least available argument rank along every positive shrinking-measure sequence.
The input degrees, the degree and total measure of \(x\), and the number of constituents are unrestricted.

## Successive block products and their class spans

Taking the product of the conjugates in one torsion class of \(w\) gives the first \(z_1\).
Its conjugates are distinct modulo torsion, have the same rational span as before and preserve total measure, since all classes have the same size and all members of each class have the same modulus. [Compression](Yin_C_G0901_v68.md#degree)
Every nontorsion unit of measure at most \(m(w)\) has degree tending uniformly to infinity as \(m(w)\to0\), by bounded-degree coefficient finiteness.
Take \(\varepsilon\le 1\) small enough that every such unit is reciprocal by [Smyth's bound](../audits/literature/Smy71/Yin_U001.md#smy71-statement-and-conventions), and its degree is beyond the threshold for the [canonical blocks](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement) at the fixed upper bound one.
For any compressed unit \(z\) encountered, write \(d=2n\), \(u\) for the number of blocks and \(b=n/u\) for their size.
We may require \(b\ge9\), and \(u\ll(\log(3d))^{212}\) with an absolute implied constant.

In the independent inversion case the full formal signed module on the \(n\) inverse pairs maps isomorphically to \(V_z\), by multiplicative independence.
The independent even flips distinguish all coordinate characters, so averaging isolates each coordinate line even over \(\mathbb C\); transitivity gives absolute irreducibility.
In this case take \(W=V_z\) and finish. [Inversion alternatives](Yin_C_G0345_v274.md#statement)

In the oriented case the formal module is the direct sum of the subspace \(U\) of vectors summing to zero in each block and the subspace of blockwise constants of dimension \(u\).
The supported alternating groups act separately by ordinary permutations within their blocks, and the full group transports blocks with a uniform orientation sign on each transported block.
Each block sum-zero space is irreducible over \(\mathbb C\) by double transitivity, and the different factors acting on different blocks make those representations inequivalent.
Transitivity on blocks makes \(U\) absolutely irreducible for the full group.
Its map to \(V_z\) is nonzero because two distinct oriented roots in the same block have distinct classes, so the map is injective there.
The image \(B\) of the blockwise constants has dimension at most \(u<\dim U=n-u\).
Its intersection with the irreducible image \(W\) of \(U\) is zero, giving \(V_z=W\oplus B\). [Supported lifts](Yin_C_G0345_v274.md#cycles) · [Oriented module](Yin_C_G0901_v89.md#oriented)

The space \(B\) is precisely \(V_\beta\) for the product \(\beta\) of one oriented half-block, because its conjugates run over the half-block products, with repetitions allowed.
The degree bound \(\deg\beta\le2u\) and height bound \(h(\beta)\le b h(z)\) give \(m(\beta)\le m(z)\). [Oriented norm](Yin_C_G0345_v274.md#fields)
If \(B=0\), finish.
Otherwise compress \(\beta\) to give the next unit, of degree at most \(2u<d\), with class span \(B\) and unchanged positive total measure \(m(\beta)\).
Each operation uses only products of actual conjugates of the current unit, so every resulting unit belongs to the ordinary multiplicative group of the original conjugates.
All the spaces and direct sums embed in the original class space with their absolute Galois action, since restriction to each rational splitting field is surjective.
Strict degree descent ends the construction.
The dimension of each \(W_i\) exceeds that of the entire remaining space, which is at most \(u_i\) if nonzero.
The resulting summands thus have strictly decreasing dimensions, remain irreducible over \(\mathbb C\), and are pairwise nonisomorphic.
By complete reducibility for the finite image, every rational submodule is the direct sum of a subset of these actual summands, since no isomorphism type occurs twice.

## Negative eigenspaces on the realized constituents

At one stage let \(a\) be the number of real exterior roots, \(q\) the number of neutral inverse pairs and \(s'\) the number of pairs of nonreal conjugate exterior roots, so \(n=q+a+2s'\).
Complex conjugation on formal signed coordinates contributes one negative dimension per neutral pair, zero per real inverse pair, and one per two labels interchanged by \(c\).
Thus the formal negative eigenspace dimension is \(q+s'=d/4+q/2-a/2\). [Signature count](Yin_C_G0901_v84.md#signature)
In the full signed case \(W\) retains all these dimensions.
In the oriented case passing to the formal sum-zero subspace loses at most the \(u\) blockwise constants.
Consequently, with \(j=\dim W^{-c}\),
\[
                j\ge d/4+q/2-a/2-u,\qquad
                0\le k(z)-j=\dim B^{-c}\le u,                              \tag{4}
\]
where \(B=0\) in the full signed case.
This calculation uses the formal module and permits a nonzero \(B\) in \(V_z\).

There are \(2a\) real conjugates.
When \(a>0\) the [real-place bound](Yin_C_G0901_v65.md#signature) gives \(4a^2<100m(z)d\log(8d/(2a))\).
Thus \(a\le5\sqrt{m(w)d\log(8d)}\) in all cases, and \(u=o(\sqrt{d\log d})\) by its fixed polylogarithmic bound.
The estimates are uniform across all stages because their smallest degree tends to infinity with \(m(w)\to0\).
Equation (4) yields \(j\ge d/4-o(\sqrt{d\log d})\).
After decreasing \(\varepsilon\), we have \(d\le5j\) and \(j>u\) at every stage, including the last.
In particular \(j_i>\dim V_{z_{i+1}}\) whenever \(i<s\), proving the strict inequality in (1).
Since \(j\le d/2\), the scales \(\sqrt{d\log d}\) and \(\sqrt{j\log j}\) are comparable, giving the first estimate in (2).
The second follows because \(d/2-u\le t\le d/2\).

<a id="realization"></a>
## Prescribed subspaces and the complete set of argument ranks

If \(x\in D(w)\) is nontorsion, some positive power represents a monomial in the original conjugates up to torsion, so \(V_x\) is a nonzero rational submodule of \(V_w\).
It is therefore a sum of a unique nonempty subset of the \(W_i\).
Conversely, choose a nonzero rational vector in each selected \(W_i\), form their sum and realize a common positive integer multiple as a monomial in the conjugates of \(w\).
The submodule this class generates has nonzero projection to each selected summand and lies in their direct sum, so is exactly that sum.
The negative eigenspace computes the [argument rank](Yin_C_G0901_v68.md#selection), proving the subset-sum description.
Two distinct subsets differ first at some \(i\), whose weight by (1) exceeds the sum of all later weights; hence their ranks differ.
The least weight is \(j_s=k(z_s)\).

For \(W=W_i\subseteq V_x\), one has \(j_i\le k(x)\).
The estimates for the chain representative in the statement follow directly from (1) and (2).
If \(i\) is the first selected summand, the bound \(\dim V_x\le t_i+u_i\), the second estimate of (2) and \(u_i=o(\sqrt{t_i\log t_i})\) give the claimed estimate for the entire \(V_x\).

One can instead minimize the degree among all nontorsion \(z\in D(w)\) with \(m(z)\le m(w)\) and the prescribed actual subspace \(W\subseteq V_z\).
This set contains \(w\), and compressing a minimizing \(z\) would preserve \(W\) while lowering the degree if it had a torsion collision.
In the decomposition of this compressed \(V_z\) above, \(W\) cannot lie in \(B\), since the block product would give a smaller realization.
The projection of \(W\) onto the leading irreducible summand is then an isomorphism; its projection to \(B\) vanishes since \(\dim B<\dim W\).
Hence \(W\) is that leading summand as an actual subspace, and (4) and (2) apply to this minimizing \(z\).
This argument minimizes degree subject to retaining the prescribed subspace, while allowing other summands in \(V_z\).

## A lower bound for the original total measure

For \(m(w)<\varepsilon\), take the last representative \(z_s\).
Every nontorsion \(x\in D(w)\) has \(k(x)\ge j_s\), and \(d_s\le5j_s\).
Take \(\varepsilon\) small enough that \(d_s>\exp(e)\).
The function \(D\mapsto\log\log D/\log D\) is then positive and decreasing.
The [Voutier estimate](../audits/literature/Vou96/Yin_U001.md#height) for this nontorsion \(z_s\) gives
\[
       m(w)\ge m(z_s)>\tfrac14
                  \left(\frac{\log\log d_s}{\log d_s}\right)^3
               \ge\tfrac14\left(\frac{\log\log(5k(x))}{\log(5k(x))}\right)^3 .
\]
For \(m(w)\ge\varepsilon\) the right side is smaller than \(\varepsilon\) for all sufficiently large \(k(x)\).
This proves (3), with \(1/4\) from Voutier's total-measure estimate and the factor four in (2) from the signed action of complex conjugation.
Bounded-degree finiteness and \(d_s\le5j_s\) show \(j_s\to\infty\) uniformly as \(m(w)\to0\).
This supplies a rate for the [bounded-rank gap throughout the divisible group](Yin_C_G0901_v73.md#arguments), with the degree of a representative for each prescribed constituent replacing the degree-record hypothesis in the earlier [constituent estimate](Yin_C_G0901_v80.md#records).
At a [strict degree record](Yin_C_G0901_v84.md#modules) the chain ends at its first term, since a further term would have smaller degree and at most the original measure.
