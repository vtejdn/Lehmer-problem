### 1. Actual graph and exact variational statement

The graph D is countable, finitely branching and irreducible through zero.
Distinct digits have distinct successors.

For a complete first-return word w let |w| be its length and p(w) the product of its original digit weights.
Write

    f_n=sum_(|w|=n) p(w),       F(z)=sum_(n>=1) f_n z^n,
    p_n=K^n(0,0),              U(z)=sum_(n>=0) p_n z^n,

Here h is the actual collision entropy, U=1/(1-F) within convergence, f_1=1/2 and 1<=R<=2.

    I(Q)=sum_x pi(x) sum_eta q_x(eta) log(q_x(eta)/p_eta),

    J(a)=inf{I(Q): Q admissible and pi(0)=a},                  (3)

with infinity for an empty class.

### 2. Complete-excursion entropy and actual canonical laws

### 3. Two realization lemmas retaining the original state space

#### Finite excursion Markovization

Let nu be any finitely supported probability on complete first-return words.
Let c_x and c_(x,eta) be the expected numbers of departures from x and departures using eta during a sampled word.
Put mu=sum_x c_x=E_nu |w|, pi(x)=c_x/mu and q_x(eta)=c_(x,eta)/c_x where c_x>0.
The union of the used closed paths is finite and communicates through zero.
Closed-word flow balance gives pi Q=pi; hence Q is irreducible positive recurrent on this finite union.
Each first-return word has exactly one departure from zero, including the length-one zero loop, so

    pi(0)=1/mu.                                              (9)

To bound information, expose a word according to its successive prefixes.
At every prefix its conditional next-digit row is a probability on the allowed original digits, and prefix probabilities give the stopped chain-rule identity

    sum_w nu(w)log(nu(w)/p(w))
       =sum_prefix Pr_nu(prefix) D(next_digit|prefix || p).

There are finitely many prefixes.
Average these history-dependent rows over all prefixes ending at the same state x.
Their occupation average is q_x; convexity of relative entropy, or the log-sum inequality, yields

    I(Q)<=mu^(-1) sum_w nu(w)log(nu(w)/p(w)).                 (10)

The constructed chain need not have excursion law nu.
No such identification is assumed.
In particular, when f_n>0, conditioning original first-return weights on length n produces a finite actual chain Q^(n) with

    pi^(n)(0)=1/n,            I(Q^(n))<=-log f_n/n.           (11)

These finite chains are the required long-excursion competitors; no unrelated abstract renewal law is substituted for the actual algebraic kernel.

    pi=lambda pi_A+(1-lambda)pi_B,

Flow balance gives an invariant probability.

An irreducible countable chain with an invariant probability is positive recurrent, as justified by the elementary return-occupation argument retained in L206.

### 4. Infinite first-return support: exact curve and affine branch
