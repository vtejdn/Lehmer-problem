## Statement

### Original law and the complete short-witness certificates

Fix P(t)=t^4-t^3-t^2-t+1, its exterior Salem root beta, a neutral conjugate zeta and O=Z[beta].
Put B=1/(beta-1), S=beta B and retain the complete actual core

    D={x in O: |x_+|<B, |x_-|<S}.

The chronological kernel K has X_(j+1)=beta X_j+eta_j, with ORIGINAL independent probabilities 1/2 at digit zero and 1/4 at each of +1 and -1, and is killed on exit from D.
For k>=0 let nu_k(x)=Pr(sum_(j=0)^(k-1)eta_j beta^j=x) be the COMPLETE AMBIENT original displacement law on all of O.
For core endpoints x in D, nu_k(x)=K^k(0,x); arbitrary displacement arguments outside D retain the ambient law and are not replaced by killed probabilities.
Put p_k=nu_k(0)=K^k(0,0)>0, p_0=1, c_k(x)=4^k nu_k(x), and rho(x)=|x(zeta)|.
Every c_k is a complete integer count with word weight 2^(number of zero digits).
Set

    kappa=1/|1-zeta^2|>0, gamma=4sqrt(BS),
    alpha_k(y)=K^k(y,0)/p_k,
    beta_k(c)=K^k(0,c)/p_k.

For every integer k>=0 and 0<=T<=k define the actual union probabilities

    C^+_(k,T)(c)=Pr_0[X_k=c and X_r=0 for SOME
                              r in {k-T,...,k}]/p_k,
    C^-_(k,T)(y)=Pr_y[X_k=0 and X_r=0 for SOME
                              r in {0,...,T}]/p_k.

The first contains precisely the complete k-step words obtainable by concatenating an arbitrary return of length k-ell with an arbitrary reaching word of length ell<=T.
The second contains precisely those obtained by an arbitrary continuation y->0 of length ell<=T followed by an arbitrary remaining return.
Multiple visits are allowed, but each complete word retains at most its original probability once in the union.
These are therefore maximal probability certificates in this zero-junction construction class.
Any subcollection, overlap-resolved union or random mixture respecting the original per-word probability budget is dominated by the corresponding certificate.

For EVERY actual c,y in D,

    0<=C^+_(k,T)(c)<=min{beta_k(c),
                  (T+1)exp[-rho(c)^2/(T+2kappa)]},
    0<=C^-_(k,T)(y)<=min{alpha_k(y),
                  (T+1)exp[-rho(y)^2/(T+2kappa)]}.        (SW1)

Both full endpoint ratios are at most one.
The certificates equal one at endpoint zero and vanish at nonzero endpoints when T=0, including all k=0 cases.
Every probability uses the same actual k; no future-time comparison or R=beta identity is used.

If 0<q<=1 and either certificate at endpoint x is at least q, necessarily

    rho(x)^2<=(T+2kappa)log((T+1)/q).                     (SW3)

More explicitly, for fixed a>0,b>=0, r=rho(x)>=max{1,b+2kappa} and T<=a r+b, both certificates obey

    C^+_(k,T)(x), C^-_(k,T)(x)
         <=(a+b+1)r exp[-r/(a+1)].                       (SW4)

This is smaller than every fixed reciprocal power of r as r tends to infinity.
Thus aggregation of every linear-length witness cannot yield a polynomially large same-time lower-count certificate at arbitrarily large neutral radius.
This statement makes no assumption that a positive amount of actual current probability lies at such radii.

### Complete current-mass consumer

For n>=5 put k=n-2 and use the ORIGINAL law pi_n on ALL returned length-2n words eta=(A,theta,C), with outside tails of length k and middle length four.
Explicitly pi_n(eta)=2^(number of zero digits in eta)/c_(2n)(0).
Let E_n be the whole zero-middle event theta=0.
Its labels are (y,c)=(beta^4c,c), and its actual midpoint has algebraic value beta^2c, hence neutral radius rho(c).
Put D_n=p_(2n)/p_n^2.
Take ANY subprobability a_n<=pi_n supported on E_n, of total mass delta_n, without selecting a single representative of a tail fiber.
The selection may be fractional, full-fiber or sign-symmetric; the bound needs only the stated domination.

Suppose 0<=T<=k, 0<q<=1, and at every selected label at least one of

    C^+_(k,T)(c)>=q or C^-_(k,T)(beta^4c)>=q

holds; requiring both is a stronger hypothesis.
Then the complete original mass satisfies

    delta_n D_n <=[1+gamma sqrt((T+2kappa)
                                      log((T+1)/q))]^2.  (SW6)

If delta_n D_n>1, the positive denominator permits the equivalent necessary inequality

    T+2kappa >=(sqrt(delta_n D_n)-1)^2
                         /[gamma^2 log((T+1)/q)].        (SW7)

For every fixed m>=0, if additionally q>=n^(-m), then

    (sqrt(delta_n D_n)-1)_+^2
       <=gamma^2(m+1)(T+2kappa)log n.                    (SW8)

Consequently any sequence with delta_n>=delta>0 and T=O(sqrt(n)) admitting these polynomially large certificates must satisfy

    D_n=O_(delta,m,quartic,T-budget)(sqrt(n)log n).        (SW9)

Conditionally, if D_n>=eta n along a sequence for some eta>0, the same mass and polynomial-certificate hypotheses force

    T+2kappa >=(sqrt(delta eta n)-1)^2
                         /[gamma^2(m+1)log n]

whenever delta eta n>1.
No such linear lower growth of D_n, all-late escape, or actual critical-mean branch is asserted.
On the hypothetical infinite-mean branch only C204's already established common subsequence D_n tending to infinity is used; SW6 then excludes bounded T and fixed q at positive retained mass along that subsequence.

C647's actual source mass, complete marginal conductances, exact own-source negative-word paths, short refreshes and n+2 digit-work price remain unchanged.
SW1--SW9 do not supply a better true conductance, an upper or lower true-load estimate, a uniform odd-energy coefficient or unrestricted Lehmer completion.
The entire obstruction concerns the specified zero-junction certificate class; longer witnesses and comparisons without such a junction remain possible and unproved here.

## Argument

### Exact reuse and original objects

Read the actual C645 endpoint profile, C647 same-current-time concatenation and conductances, and C673 displacement-copy boundary before selecting this question.
The local C/L search by short witnesses, last returns, endpoint Gaussian profiles and complete reaching words found the existing fixed-word injection and Gaussian profile, not the whole-short-witness consequence below.
Those existing mechanisms suffice for the direct calculation; no general new hitting theorem, external source or native experiment is used.

Fix the actual quartic P(t)=t^4-t^3-t^2-t+1, exterior root beta, neutral conjugate zeta, and order O=Z[beta].
The actual zero-to-zero core is D={x in O: |x_+|<B, |x_-|<S}, where B=1/(beta-1) and S=beta B.
Let X_(j+1)=beta X_j+eta_j, with ORIGINAL independent digit probabilities Pr(eta_j=0)=1/2 and Pr(eta_j=1)=Pr(eta_j=-1)=1/4, killed on leaving D.
Write K for this chronological killed kernel and nu_k(x)=Pr(sum_(j=0)^(k-1)eta_j beta^j=x) for the COMPLETE AMBIENT original displacement law on O.
For core endpoints x in D, nu_k(x)=K^k(0,x); for arbitrary displacement arguments outside D, nu_k remains the ambient law and is not redefined by a killed transition.
Put p_k=nu_k(0)=K^k(0,0)>0, p_0=1, and rho(x)=|x(zeta)|.
Set kappa=1/|1-zeta^2|>0 and gamma=4sqrt(BS).
The fixed accepted inputs are

    K^ell(y,0)=nu_ell(-beta^ell y),
    nu_ell(x)/p_ell<=exp[-rho(x)^2/(ell+2kappa)], ell>=1,
    p_(a+b)>=p_a p_b, a,b>=0,
    N(r):=card{x in D:rho(x)<=r}<=(1+gamma r)^2.

The endpoint identity retains every original word; core endpoints ensure every joining word has core intermediate states.
The Gaussian bound is C645 equation (1), not a lower profile or a conditional independence assumption.
The return inequality follows directly by concatenating all a-step and b-step original zero returns.
For ell=0 the Gaussian inequality also holds because nu_0(x)=1_(x=0) and p_0=1.
No true critical-radius identity is needed.

### Actual maximal short-witness subevents

For integers k>=0 and 0<=T<=k, define

    C^+_(k,T)(c)=Pr_0[X_k=c and X_r=0 for SOME
                              r in {k-T,...,k}]/p_k,
    C^-_(k,T)(y)=Pr_y[X_k=0 and X_r=0 for SOME
                              r in {0,...,T}]/p_k.

These are probabilities of actual unions of original chronological events.
Earlier or repeated zero visits are neither deleted nor counted more than once.
All words have their complete original weights, including arbitrary zero digits.
The right event contains every construction that concatenates a return of length k-ell with a reaching word of length ell<=T.
The left event contains every construction that concatenates a continuation y->0 of length ell<=T with a return of length k-ell.
Conversely every word in the corresponding union has at least one such split.
Thus these subevents are the MAXIMAL complete original probability certificates in this class.
Any smaller collection, probability-preserving random mixture, or overlap-resolved union of such constructions is bounded by C^+ or C^-.
An arithmetic sum that counts the same original word repeatedly is not a certificate for a lower original probability.

Put

    alpha_k(y)=K^k(y,0)/p_k,
    beta_k(c)=K^k(0,c)/p_k.

The complete original autocorrelation gives alpha_k,beta_k<=1.
For every actual y,c in D one has

    0<=C^+_(k,T)(c)<=min{beta_k(c),
               (T+1)exp[-rho(c)^2/(T+2kappa)]},
    0<=C^-_(k,T)(y)<=min{alpha_k(y),
               (T+1)exp[-rho(y)^2/(T+2kappa)]}.           (SW1)

Both certificates equal one at endpoint zero, including T=0 and k=0.
For nonzero endpoints and T=0 they vanish.
No future length k+T is used to bound a present k-step count.

For the right certificate, the union bound and the original Markov factorization give

    C^+_(k,T)(c)
      <=sum_(ell=0)^T p_(k-ell)nu_ell(c)/p_k
      <=sum_(ell=0)^T nu_ell(c)/p_ell
      <=(T+1)exp[-rho(c)^2/(T+2kappa)].                   (SW2)

The second line uses p_k>=p_(k-ell)p_ell, with the direction shown.
The first line may overcount visits, which is legitimate ONLY for this upper bound on the true union.
The Gaussian bound and ell<=T give the last line.
For the left certificate replace the summand in the first line by
K^ell(y,0)p_(k-ell)/p_k.
Its displacement has neutral modulus |zeta^ell y(zeta)|=rho(y), so the same calculation applies.
Every intermediate word and zero-return normalizer in this argument is original.

### Necessary time to obtain a specified count certificate

Let 0<q<=1.
If either short-witness certificate at endpoint x is at least q, then SW1 implies

    rho(x)^2<=(T+2kappa)log((T+1)/q).                    (SW3)

Consequently collecting ALL witnesses at chronological time T=O(rho) does not provide even a reciprocal-polynomial lower-count certificate at large neutral radius.
More explicitly, fix a>0 and b>=0, and suppose r=rho(x)>=max{1,b+2kappa}, T<=a r+b.
Then

    C^+_(k,T)(x), C^-_(k,T)(x)
        <=(a+b+1)r exp[-r/(a+1)].                       (SW4)

Indeed T+1<=(a+b+1)r and T+2kappa<=(a+1)r.
For every fixed m>=0 the right side is o(r^(-m)).
This limiting statement uses only the elementary exponential series: a fixed sufficiently high positive Taylor term dominates any prescribed power of r.
No existence of actual endpoint mass at radius comparable to sqrt(k) is assumed.
When such endpoints are under consideration, the assertion concerns their short-witness certificates regardless of the actual full endpoint probabilities.

For comparison, a lower certificate q at radius r requires exactly the necessary inequality SW3; in particular bounded T cannot support fixed q at unbounded radius.
Allowing witness times comparable to r^2, or contributions without a zero-return junction near the endpoint, lies outside the linear-time exclusion.
These alternatives are not proved successful.

### Current original mass and the complete midpoint price

Now take the ACTUAL returned-word law at n>=5, k=n-2, on all length-2n returned words eta=(A,theta,C), with middle length four.
Its law is pi_n(eta)=2^(number of zero digits)/c_(2n)(0), where c_m(x)=4^m nu_m(x).
Let E_n be the whole zero-middle event, theta=0.
For eta in E_n its current label is (y,c)=(beta^4c,c), and the actual chronological midpoint is beta^2c, with neutral radius rho(c).
The full original midpoint atom is at most 1/D_n, where D_n=p_(2n)/p_n^2.
Both this atom estimate and N(r) retain the ORIGINAL probability law, not uniform label or counting-l2 weights.

Take ANY subprobability a_n<=pi_n supported on E_n, including complete-fiber, fractional or sign-symmetric selections, and put delta_n=sum_eta a_n(eta).
Suppose, at every selected label, at least ONE of

    C^+_(k,T)(c)>=q, or C^-_(k,T)(beta^4c)>=q             (SW5)

holds, with fixed current parameters 0<=T<=k and 0<q<=1.
The alternative may depend on the label.
Requiring both certificates, as a common lower-tail-count comparison normally does, is a stronger hypothesis.
Since rho(beta^4c)=rho(c), SW3 confines all selected midpoint states to radius

    r_q=sqrt((T+2kappa)log((T+1)/q)).

The exact current-mass consequence is therefore

    delta_n D_n <=[1+gamma sqrt((T+2kappa)
                                      log((T+1)/q))]^2. (SW6)

The proof sums the true midpoint atoms, at most 1/D_n each, over at most N(r_q) actual states; restricting to E_n can only decrease those atoms.
It does not select one tail representation or replace a fiber by an unweighted point.

If delta_n D_n>1 then equivalently

    T+2kappa >=(sqrt(delta_n D_n)-1)^2
                       /[gamma^2 log((T+1)/q)].         (SW7)

If the denominator is zero, then T=0,q=1 and SW6 already forces delta_n D_n<=1, so SW7 is never used in that case.
For any fixed m>=0, a certificate q>=n^(-m) and T<=k=n-2 gives

    (sqrt(delta_n D_n)-1)_+^2
       <=gamma^2(m+1)(T+2kappa)log n.                   (SW8)

Here T+1<=n-1<n and log((T+1)/q)<=(m+1)log n.
Thus, on any sequence with delta_n>=delta>0 and T=O(sqrt(n)), such polynomially large short-witness certificates necessarily imply

    D_n=O_(delta,m,quartic,T-budget)(sqrt(n)log n).       (SW9)

If instead D_n>=eta n on a sequence for some eta>0, the same assumptions force

    T+2kappa >=(sqrt(delta eta n)-1)^2
                         /[gamma^2(m+1)log n]

whenever delta eta n>1; thus linear-in-radius O(sqrt(n)) witness times cannot supply these certificates there.
Neither the D_n>=eta n premise nor any all-late escape is claimed.
On the actual hypothetical infinite-mean branch, C204 supplies only its already established common subsequence D_n->infinity.
SW6 then excludes bounded T and fixed q on any selected mass bounded below along that subsequence, but does not determine a new growth rate for D_n or the actual mean.

### Exact construction boundary and costs retained

C647 already gives actual source mass at least 1/32, same-current-length sign paths, conductance at least 4^(-3T_n)/(131072D_n), and the full load at most 131072D_n 4^(3T_n)L_n^2 with its short-refresh price896*4^T_n included.
The changed aggregation above includes every possible short witness and all remaining-return words, so merely replacing its single witness by this whole union cannot give polynomial count certificates throughout a linearly growing witness-radius range.
It does not disprove a better actual endpoint comparison derived by another mechanism, and does not lower-bound the true load by reciprocating an upper bound on a lower certificate.
No path, source selection or complete marginal in C647 is changed.
The chronological k-step events above are not the returned-word resampling kernel K_n.
Any use of that original K_n still pays every actual update, full negative-word terminal condition, short refresh and the n+2 digit-work divisor/multiplier exactly as in C647.
No R=beta identity, uniform odd-energy coefficient, finite/infinite critical-mean conclusion, all-late diffusion lower bound or unrestricted Lehmer completion follows.
