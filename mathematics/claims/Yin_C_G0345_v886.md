# A uniform small-measure bound from coefficient height five

<a id="statement"></a>
## Entry into the measure window for all signatures

For integers \(q\ge5,\ p=q+1,\ |a|=q,\ |b|=p,\ \epsilon=\pm1\), let \(P(X)=a(1+\epsilon X^{u+v})+b(X^u+\epsilon X^v)\) with coprime \(0<u<v\).
If \(P(X^g)\) has a nontorsion unit root \(z\) for some integer \(g\ge1\), put \(\beta=z^g,\ D=[\mathbb Q(\beta):\mathbb Q]\) and let \(R\) be the largest modulus of a root of \(P\).
Then
\[
 R^u<13/10,\qquad M(\beta)<13/10,\qquad u<2D ,
\]
where \(M(\beta)\) is the multiplicative Mahler measure.
In fact the product of the moduli of all exterior roots of the primitive parent, counted with multiplicity, is below \(13/10\).
This applies to all signs and all signatures of the unit.

<a id="height"></a>
## Using both sides of the unit circle in the height inequality

The reciprocal or antireciprocal symmetry of \(P\) puts the modulus of each conjugate of \(\beta\) in \([1/R,R]\).
The unit norm gives \(\sum_\sigma\log|\sigma\beta|=0\), so twice the sum of the positive logarithms equals the sum of all absolute logarithms.
Consequently \(2h(\beta)\le\log R\), where \(h\) is absolute logarithmic Weil height.
Kronecker's theorem gives \(R>1\).
The [projective height comparison](Yin_C_G0345_v869.md#count) gives \(\log(p/2)\le(u+v)h(\beta)\).
Indeed \(a/b=-(\beta^u+\epsilon\beta^v)/(1+\epsilon\beta^{u+v})\), the denominator is nonzero, and the two coordinates have local maximum bounded by \(\max(1,|\beta|)^{u+v}\) times two at the infinite places and times one elsewhere.
Their projective height is \(\log p\) because the rational integers \(a,b\) are coprime.
Writing \(r=R^u,\ s=R^v,\ C=(p/2)^2\) gives \(rs\ge C\).

At a point of modulus \(R\), the [radial inequalities](Yin_C_G0345_v869.md#radius) give
\[
 \frac1q\ \ge\ \frac{(r-1)(s-1)}{r+s},\qquad
 r<\frac pq+\sqrt{(p/q)^2-1}.
\]
For \(q\ge5\) the second bound gives \(r<2\) while \(\sqrt C\ge3\).
The fraction in the first inequality increases with \(s\) for fixed \(r>1\).
Replacing \(s\) by \(C/r\) gives the lower bound \((C+1)/(r+C/r)-1\).
For \(1<r<\sqrt C\) this increases with \(r\), and at fixed \(r>1\) it also increases with \(C\).
If \(r\ge13/10\), its value would therefore be at least its value at \(r=13/10,\ C=9\), namely \(231/1069>1/5\).
This contradicts \(1/q\le1/5\).

The [winding count](Yin_C_G0345_v869.md#count) bounds the number of exterior roots by \(u\), so their product is at most \(R^u\).
For the monic unit factor of \(\beta\), its Mahler measure is exactly the product over its own exterior roots.
Finally the [house bound](Yin_C_G0345_v869.md#degree) gives \(\log R\ge\log(2)/(4D)\).
Thus \(u<4D\log(13/10)/\log2<2D\), since \((13/10)^2<2\).
