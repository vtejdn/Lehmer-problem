### 4. A finite input-dependent bound uniform over every tail translation

The convergent geometric-series bound for the inverse in(9) gives ||y_*||<=2R.

Now apply(2) at z_* and use maximality to obtain(8) for every tail-space x.
Every finite-n maximum, all n>=n0 and arbitrary tail magnitude are covered.
