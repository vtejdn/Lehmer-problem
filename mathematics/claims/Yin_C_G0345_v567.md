# Density of exponent sets admitting no ternary multiple

<a id="statement"></a>
## Two bounds for the density

Let \(P\) be the monic minimal polynomial of an algebraic integer \(\alpha\) of house \(\rho>1\), let \(m=\log M(P)\), and fix an integer \(k\ge1\).
Let \(D_k(P)\) be the limit, as \(N\to\infty\), of the largest fraction of \(\{0,\ldots,N-1\}\) occupied by a mask supporting no nonzero polynomial with coefficients in \(\{-1,0,1\}\) divisible by \(P^k\).
Equivalently, all binary subsets of this mask have distinct residues modulo \(P^k\).
The maximum cardinality is subadditive in the interval length, since restrictions to adjacent subintervals remain admissible after translation, so the limit exists.
For \(e_q=[\mathbb Q(\alpha):\mathbb Q(\alpha^q)]\), one has
\[
 \sup_{q\ge1:\,kq\log\rho>\log16}\frac{e_q}{q}
 \ \le\ D_k(P)\ \le\ \min\{1,km/\log2\}.                         \tag{1}
\]
Each density on the left is realized by a single periodic mask all of whose finite subsets satisfy the condition.
In particular, with \(u=k\log\rho\), one has \(D_k(P)\ge1/(1+\lfloor(\log16)/u\rfloor)\).
If at most \(s\) conjugates lie outside the unit circle, then \(m\le s\log\rho\), so
\[
 \frac{km}{s\log16+km}\ \le\ D_k(P)\ \le\ \min\{1,km/\log2\}.
\]
Thus for bounded exterior count the extremal density has order \(\min\{1,km\}\), with constants independent of the degree and multiplicity.
The lower bound in terms of the house can be much weaker when the exterior count grows.

<a id="disk"></a>
## A multiplicity bound at one exterior zero

Let \(G\ne0\) be a ternary polynomial having a zero of modulus \(R>1\) of multiplicity at least \(k\).
Reverse its coefficients and rotate the variable, obtaining a polynomial \(f\) with \(|f(0)|=1\), all coefficients of modulus at most one, and a zero at \(r=1/R\) of multiplicity at least \(k\).
The geometric series bound gives \(r\ge1/2\).
Take the circle of center \(a=1/4\) and radius \(b=3/4\).
At the center \(|f(a)|\ge 1-a/(1-a)=2/3\).
At \(z=a+b e^{it}\) with \(t\ne0\pmod {2\pi}\), one has \(1-|z|\ge ab(1-\cos t)\) by squaring the modulus and using \(1+|z|\le2\).
Consequently the circular mean of \(\log|f|\) is at most \(\log(2/(ab))\), by \(|f(z)|\le(1-|z|)^{-1}\) and the circular mean \(-\log2\) of \(\log(1-\cos t)\).
Jensen's formula for a polynomial holds also with zeros on the circle, which contribute zero to the Jensen sum.
The zero at \(r\) gives
\[
 k\log R\le k\log\frac b{r-a}\le\log\frac{2}{ab}+\log(3/2)=\log16,
\]
where the first inequality uses \(br\ge r-a>0\).
This estimate is uniform in the degree and the phase of the exterior zero.

<a id="period"></a>
## Independent residue classes for jets

Fix any positive \(q\), write \(Q\) for the minimum of \(\alpha^q\), and use the periodic mask of exponents congruent to \(0,\ldots,e_q-1\pmod q\).
These are distinct classes since \(e_q\le q\).
Every polynomial on the mask has the form \(F(X)=\sum_{j<e_q}X^j F_j(X^q)\), with \(0\le j<e_q\).
The powers \(1,\alpha,\ldots,\alpha^{e_q-1}\) form a basis over \(\mathbb Q(\alpha^q)\), so \(P\mid F\) forces \(Q\mid F_j\) for every \(j\).
Moreover \(Q(X^q)\) vanishes simply at \(\alpha\) by separability and \(\alpha\ne0\).
Dividing by this common factor and iterating gives the equivalence
\[
 P^k\mid F\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Q^k\mid F_j\ \text{for all }j.
\]
This extends the [direct coordinate argument for ordinary signed relations](Yin_C_G0279_v1.md#dissociated-colors-and-cost) to the powered field and every multiplicity.
For a ternary \(F\) each \(F_j\) is ternary, and \(Q\) has a conjugate of modulus \(\rho^q\).
The [disk bound](#disk) rules out every nonzero \(F_j\) when \(kq\log\rho>\log16\), proving the periodic lower bound.
Taking \(q=1+\lfloor(\log16)/u\rfloor\) gives the stated bound using \(e_q\ge1\).
For the upper bound, every density strictly larger than \(km/\log2\) forces a nonzero balanced relation on all sufficiently large masks by the [fixed-weight jet count](Yin_C_G0345_v560.md#density) with weight fraction one half.
This proves (1).
