## 4. A bounded-class lift in the normal closure, returned to the original norm

**Statement.**
Let alpha be a nontorsion algebraic integer unit, P its monic irreducible polynomial over Q, K=Q(alpha), and d=[K:Q].
No normality, reciprocity or unit-circle-root hypothesis is imposed.
Let L be its normal closure, G=Gal(L/Q), and H=Gal(L/K).
Choose a rational prime p, a prime ideal Pfrak of O_L above p, and an actual sigma in its decomposition group satisfying

    sigma(x)=x^p mod Pfrak for every x in O_L.            (11)

Let c=[G:C_G(sigma)] be its conjugacy-class size.
For any integer k>=c, put

    r0=ceil(log_p(k d)), n=p^(r0+2k-1).

Then the following bounds hold with the ORIGINAL polynomial degree and total logarithmic Mahler measure:

    m(P)>=d log(p^2/2)/[n(p+1)]
         >=log(p^2/2)/[k(p+1)p^(2k)]>0,
    n<k d p^(2k), pn<k d p^(2k+1).                     (12)

The supplied class-size bound, fixed p and fixed k are substantive conditional inputs.

### 4A. The small auxiliary field and its commuting embeddings

Set C=C_G(sigma), H'=H intersection C, and K'=L^(H').
Elementary Galois correspondence gives

    r=[K':K]=[H:H']<=[G:C]=c,
    d'=[K':Q]=r d<=c d<=k d.                            (13)

For the index bound, distinct H-cosets modulo H intersection C give distinct G-cosets modulo C; no normality of H or C is needed.
In particular K' need NOT be Galois over Q.
Since sigma commutes with every element of H', it normalizes H', and therefore restricts to an actual finite-order automorphism of K'.
Both alpha and sigma(alpha) belong to K'.

The embeddings K'->L are indexed by the left cosets tau H' in G/H'.
Exactly

    |C|/|H'|=d'/c                                      (14)

of them are represented by tau in C.
This is a count of embeddings of the degree-d' field; it is not |G| original embeddings counted without division.
For every selected tau, write a=tau(alpha) and b=tau(sigma(alpha))=sigma(tau(alpha)).
By(11), a^p/b is a local unit congruent to1 modulo Pfrak.
Moreover a^p/b belongs to the ACTUAL intermediate field tau(K'), not merely L.

Normalize the valuation v at Pfrak by v(p)=1.
For the restriction to tau(K'), its positive value group has step 1/e_tau, where e_tau is that number field's ramification index at the restricted prime.
The number-field degree identity sum e_i f_i=d' implies e_tau<=d'.
Consequently the selected embeddings obey

    v(a^p/b-1)>=1/e_tau>=1/d'>=1/(k d).                 (15)

The possible equality a^p/b=1 has infinite valuation and also satisfies the bound.

### 4B. Depth, nonvanishing and the complete auxiliary-field norm

Apply the elementary binomial estimate(6) to(15).

    beta=alpha^(pn)-sigma(alpha)^n in O_(K'),

all d'/c selected embeddings have v(tau beta)>=2k.

    v_p(|Norm_(K'/Q) beta|)>=2k d'/c>=2d',

    log|Norm_(K'/Q) beta|
