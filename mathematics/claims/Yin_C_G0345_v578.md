# Fourier coefficients at large conductors in all periodic unit quotients

<a id="statement"></a>
## A degree-independent obstruction at small measure

Let \(P\) be the minimum of any nontorsion algebraic unit \(\alpha\), put \(K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\), \(m=\log M(P)>0\), and write \(D=D_1(P)>0\) for the limiting maximum density of exponents with distinct binary subset sums.
Let \(h\) be the Shannon entropy rate in nats of the sums \(S_n=\sum_{i<n}\xi_i\alpha^i\) with independent fair binary digits.
On \(G_n=\mathcal O_K/(\alpha^n-1)\mathcal O_K\), write \(\mu_n\) for the law of the residue of \(S_n\).
For each additive character \(\chi\) of this group define the ideal
\[
 \mathfrak a_\chi=\{z\in\mathcal O_K:\chi(zy)=1\text{ for all }y\in\mathcal O_K\}.
\]
Its norm is the index in \(\mathcal O_K\).
Put \(A_n(\chi)=|\widehat\mu_n(\chi)|\) and \(g(x)=\big((1+x)\log(1+x)+(1-x)\log(1-x)\big)/2\) on \([0,1]\), with \(0\log0=0\).
Uniformly over the characters at each \(n\),
\[
 \frac{D}{m}\,\log N\mathfrak a_\chi\,g(A_n(\chi))
              \ \le\ n(m-h)+O_P(\log(2n)).
\]
In particular if \(\liminf_n \log N\mathfrak a_{\chi_n}/(nm)\ge\kappa>0\), then
\[
    \kappa D\,g(\limsup_n A_n(\chi_n))\ \le\ m-h\ \le\
    C(m),\qquad C(m)=\tfrac5{12}(e^{2m}-1)^2(1-e^{-4m}).
\]
These limit statements also hold along any unbounded subsequence.
For \(m\le1\) this gives \(\limsup_n A_n(\chi_n)\le16\sqrt{C(m)/(\kappa m)}\).
Thus along any shrinking-measure sequence the per-input limiting amplitudes at a fixed positive conductor fraction are \(O(m)\), uniformly in degree, including for Salem inputs.

<a id="quotient"></a>
## Entropy lost on reduction

Write \(J_n=(\alpha^n-1)\mathcal O_K\).
At each embedding with \(|\beta|>1\) the factor \(|\beta^n-1|\) is at most \(2|\beta|^n\) and at least \((1-|\beta|^{-1})|\beta|^n\).
At contracting embeddings it stays between positive input-dependent constants.
At any neutral embedding \(\beta\) is not torsion and the [linear-form bound for \(1-\beta^n\)](../audits/literature/Wus02/Yin_U001.md#wus02-u001) gives \(|1-\beta^n|^{-1}\le(2n)^{O_P(1)}\).
Consequently \(\log|G_n|=nm+O_P(\log(2n))\), with an \(O_P(1)\) upper error.

Two binary sums in the same fiber differ by \((\alpha^n-1)y\), \(y\in\mathcal O_K\).
Dividing the geometric series bounds at expanding and contracting embeddings shows that the corresponding conjugates of \(y\) are bounded in terms of \(P\).
At neutral embeddings their moduli are at most \(n/|\beta^n-1|\).
The image of \(\mathcal O_K\) is a fixed full lattice in its real and complex embeddings, so there are \((2n)^{O_P(1)}\) choices of \(y\).
Relative to a fixed member of the fiber each distinct unreduced value gives a different \(y\).
Thus \(H(\mu_n)\ge H(S_n)-O_P(\log(2n))\ge nh-O_P(\log(2n))\) by entropy subadditivity in the original independent blocks.
In particular
\[
       \log|G_n|-H(\mu_n)\le n(m-h)+O_P(\log(2n)).
\]

<a id="frequencies"></a>
## Dissociated characters and relative entropy

For a character \(\chi\), put \(\chi_j(y)=\chi(\alpha^j y)\).
The Fourier amplitudes at all \(\chi_j\) equal \(A_n(\chi)\), since the independent digit factors are permuted cyclically modulo \(J_n\).
The conductor contains \(J_n\), so \(\log N\mathfrak a_\chi\le nm+O_P(1)\).
A ternary polynomial \(F\) supported on \([0,L)\), \(L\le n\), has
\[
                 |N_{K/\mathbb Q} F(\alpha)|\le e^{Lm}(2n)^{O_P(1)}
\]
by the same geometric series and the bound \(n\) at neutral embeddings.
If \(F(\alpha)\ne0\) lies in \(\mathfrak a_\chi\), its absolute norm is at least \(N\mathfrak a_\chi\).
Choose \(L\in[0,n]\) by subtracting a sufficiently large \(P\)-dependent multiple of \(\log(2n)\) from \(\log N\mathfrak a_\chi/m\), taking the integer part and truncating below at zero and above at \(n\).
When \(L>0\) this ensures the norm upper bound is strictly below \(N\mathfrak a_\chi\), and always \(L\ge(\log N\mathfrak a_\chi)/m-O_P(\log(2n))\).

Take an admissible exponent set \(I\subseteq[0,L)\) of maximum size.
Subadditivity gives \(|I|\ge LD\).
A nonzero ternary combination of its character indices would be the trivial character exactly if \(F(\alpha)\in\mathfrak a_\chi\) for the corresponding ternary polynomial.
Admissibility gives \(F(\alpha)\ne0\) and the norm excludes this membership.
Thus the characters indexed by \(I\) are dissociated in the finite dual group.

Let \(U_n\) be uniform on \(G_n\) and multiply each \(\chi_j\) by a constant phase so that \(Y_j=\Re(e^{i\varphi_j}\chi_j)\) has mean \(A=A_n(\chi)\) under \(\mu_n\).
For \(0<t<1\), the product \(f=\prod_{j\in I}(1+tY_j)\) has mean one under \(U_n\).
Indeed expanding the factors gives only nontrivial characters apart from the constant term.
The relative entropy inequality gives \(\log|G_n|-H(\mu_n)\ge\mathbb E_{\mu_n}\log f\), by comparison with the probability \(fU_n\).
On \([-1,1]\), \(\log(1+ty)\) lies above its chord through the endpoints.
Each factor therefore contributes at least
\[
        \tfrac{1+A}2\log(1+t)+\tfrac{1-A}2\log(1-t).
\]
Taking \(t=A\), with the endpoint cases by limits, yields \(|I|g(A)\).
As \(g(A)\le\log2\), the estimate for \(L\) and the quotient entropy bound give the finite inequality.

The [repeated-digit entropy estimate](Yin_C_G0305_v1.md#statement) at one fair digit gives \(m-h\le C(m)\).
Passing between fair signs and bits is an affine bijection of the finite sums and preserves entropy.
Since \(g(x)\ge x^2/2\), \(D\ge m/128\) when \(m\le1\) by the [explicit density bound](Yin_C_G0345_v572.md#constant), and \(C(m)=O(m^3)\) at zero, the remaining estimates follow.

<a id="traces"></a>
## Bilateral products for hyperbolic units

For a hyperbolic input fix any \(b\in\mathcal O_K\setminus\{0\}\) and use the character \(y\mapsto\exp(2\pi i\,\operatorname{Tr}(by/(\alpha^n-1)))\).
If \(\mathfrak D^{-1}\) is the trace dual of \(\mathcal O_K\), then \(b\mathfrak a_\chi\subseteq J_n\mathfrak D^{-1}\).
Taking ideal norms gives \(N\mathfrak a_\chi\ge |G_n|/(N\mathfrak D\,|N_{K/\mathbb Q}b|)\).
Its limiting conductor fraction is one.
The limiting amplitude for fair bits is
\[
 c_b=\prod_{i\ge0}\left|\cos\!\left(\pi\sum_{|\beta|<1}\sigma_\beta(b)\beta^i\right)\right|
       \prod_{i\ge1}\left|\cos\!\left(\pi\sum_{|\beta|>1}\sigma_\beta(b)\beta^{-i}\right)\right| ,
\]
where \(\sigma_\beta(\alpha)=\beta\).
Indeed the finite product has arguments \(\pi\operatorname{Tr}(b\alpha^i/(\alpha^n-1))\), \(0\le i<n\).
For fixed \(i\) near zero the trace tends to the negative contracting sum, and for \(i=n-\ell\) with fixed \(\ell\ge1\) it tends to the expanding sum with exponent \(-\ell\).
Throughout the middle it is bounded in absolute value by \(C_{P,b}(\eta^i+\eta^{n-i})\) for some \(\eta<1\).
Using \(1-|\cos x|\le x^2/2\) controls the two discarded tails uniformly and proves convergence.
The partial traces are real algebraic integers and cannot be nonintegral half-integers, so the bilateral product is positive.
Consequently
\[
       D\,g\!\left(\sup_{0\ne b\in\mathcal O_K} c_b\right)
                          \le m-h\le C(m).
\]
Here each \(b\) is fixed before passage to \(n\); the supremum follows since the bound is independent of \(b\).
This gives a density-weighted quantitative form of the [strictness at hyperbolic units](../audits/literature/Str26/Yin_U001.md#str26-u001).
Taking the subset of multipliers \(2b\) gives the fair-sign products of the [integral trace estimate](Yin_C_G0298_v1.md#statement).
Their optimized amplitude is therefore \(O(m)\) along every hyperbolic shrinking sequence, irrespective of the number of exterior conjugates.
For a neutral embedding the finite conductor inequality applies directly through the periodic quotients.
