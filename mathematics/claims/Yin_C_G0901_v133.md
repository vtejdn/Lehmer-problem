# Delay one attains the least exterior root for every relative seed

<a id="statement"></a>
## A reduction valid before varying the coefficient field or the seed degree

Take any nondegenerate relative seed of the [delayed construction](Yin_C_G0901_v128.md#statement).
At the distinguished real embedding let \(P\) be its monic polynomial with unique simple exterior root \(\beta>1\), put \(Q=(P^\tau)^*\), and write \(R_{k,e}\) for the exterior root of \(X^kP+eQ\), where \(e\in\{1,-1\}\) and \(k\ge1\).
For each individual seed,
\[
      \inf_{k\ge1,\ e=\pm1} R_{k,e}
          =\min_{e=\pm1}R_{1,e}.
\]
If \(Q(\beta)\ne0\), the sign \(e\) with \(eQ(\beta)>0\) gives a strictly increasing sequence \(R_{k,e}<\beta\); the other sign gives a strictly decreasing sequence \(R_{k,-e}>\beta\).
Both converge to \(\beta\).
If \(Q(\beta)=0\), all these exterior roots equal \(\beta\).
In the two-sign family the least Mahler measure of the rational norm polynomials for a fixed seed is therefore exactly \(\min_e R_{1,e}^2\).
More generally if a sequence with a prescribed sign on each seed has \(R_{k,e}\to1\), then eventually \(1<R_{1,e}\le R_{k,e}\) for the same signs.
Thus deciding a gap for the entire nondegenerate relative construction, including unbounded seed degrees and coefficient fields, requires only delay one.
This reduction concerns the two-sign norm construction and does not assert that all unrestricted integer polynomials arise from it.

<a id="ordering"></a>
## Ordering the two exterior roots

At the marked place \(P(x)<0\) for \(1\le x<\beta\) and \(P(x)>0\) for \(x>\beta\), since its leading coefficient is positive and \(\beta\) is its only real zero in \((1,\infty)\), of multiplicity one.
By the [strict circle inequality and exterior root count](Yin_C_G0901_v128.md#delay), each \(F_{k,e}=X^kP+eQ\) is monic and has one simple exterior root \(R_{k,e}>1\).
It too is negative on \(1<x<R_{k,e}\) and positive on \(x>R_{k,e}\).
Because \(F_{k,e}(\beta)=eQ(\beta)\) is independent of \(k\), a positive value puts every \(R_{k,e}\) below \(\beta\), and a negative value puts every \(R_{k,e}\) above it.
At its root,
\[
    F_{k+1,e}(R_{k,e})=(R_{k,e}-1)R_{k,e}^kP(R_{k,e}).
\]
This is strictly negative on the lower side and strictly positive on the upper side, proving the two strict monotonicities.
For fixed \(x\) strictly between 1 and \(\beta\), \(F_{k,e}(x)\to-\infty\), and for fixed \(x>\beta\) it tends to \(+\infty\).
Thus the roots on either side converge to \(\beta\).
If \(Q(\beta)=0\), \(\beta\) itself is the only exterior root at both signs and every delay.
The real opposite embedding supplies the reciprocal roots, and the other places are neutral, so the rational norm measure is \(R_{k,e}^2\), including repeated factors. [Norm root count](Yin_C_G0901_v131.md#delays)
Finally the [integer norm bound](Yin_C_G0901_v128.md#seed) gives the uniform seed inequality \(\beta\ge B_2=\sqrt{(\sqrt{17}-1)/2}>1\).
On any shrinking sequence one consequently has \(R_{k,e}<\beta\) eventually, so the same sign at delay one gives a value no larger.
