## Statement

Work over F_2, with phi=X^2+X+1 and chi=Xphi^2.
Let x(t) be the formal inverse of chi at0, U=1+tF_2[[t]], and V the high-primary completed unit group at phi, viewed as the units of the rank-four algebra A_high over F_2[[t]], t=chi(X).
Let Uhat=U times V be the full completed low/high unit group.
For N>=1 define

    R_N=F_2[X]/chi(X)^N=F_2[X]/(X^Nphi^(2N)),
    Uhat_N=ker(Uhat -> R_N^*),
    k_N=2^floor(log_2 N), L_N=2k_N.

Thus L_N is the least power of2 strictly greater than N.
For odd n>1 let W_n be the complete primitive order-n trace polynomial, one trace per inverse pair, and B_n the complete trace polynomial of all nonidentity n-th roots, with B_1=1.
Let G_N be the subgroup of R_N^* generated by ALL W_n with n odd,n>1,n!=5, equivalently by finite nonnegative complete products.
All support orders and multiplicities are allowed.
Let Ghat be the closure of that group in Uhat.

Use the adopted C652 character ell with ell(1+t)=1, kernel consisting of h(t)^5/h(chi(t)), and ell(phi)=-1.
Use the adopted C654 surjective integral character

    Pi(u,v)=[ell(N_high(v))-(4/5)ell(u)]/2 in Z_2,
    Pi_N:R_N^* -> Z/k_N,
    G_N subset ker Pi_N, Ghat subset ker Pi.

### Optimal conductor and every-depth lifting

For every N>=1,

    Pi(Uhat_N)=k_N Z_2.                                  (K1)

An explicit witness is the completed pair

    w_L=(1,1+Xphi^(2L-1)), L a power of2 with L>=2,
    Pi(w_L)=L/2 modulo L.                                (K2)

For L=L_N this pair belongs to Uhat_N and its character has exact valuation log_2(k_N).
Thus Pi modulo k_N is determined by the finite class in R_N, but Pi modulo2k_N is NOT determined by that class.
This proves optimality among the power-of-two character moduli, including N=1.

Every finite kernel class has a full completed kernel lift, and every deeper finite kernel projects onto the shallower one:

    image(ker Pi -> R_N^*)=ker Pi_N,
    image(ker Pi_M -> R_N^*)=ker Pi_N for every M>=N.       (K3)

Here the second arrow is the natural quotient map.
The completed lift need not be a global polynomial or a complete factor product.

### What follows from the accepted finite image

## Argument

Use the completed unit group Uhat=U times V, the maps Pi and Pi_N, and G_N from C654.
For every N>=1 put k_N=2^floor(log_2 N) and L_N=2k_N, the least power of2 strictly greater than N.
Let Uhat_N be the kernel of Uhat->R_N^*.
The proposed uniform strengthening is

    Pi(Uhat_N)=k_N Z_2.                                  (KL1)

This is stronger than the containment already proved in C654.
It makes the finite modulus k_N maximal among powers of2 and makes the projection kerPi->kerPi_N surjective for EVERY N.

### An explicit high unit of exact character order

For L a dyadic integer>=2 set w_L=(1,1+Xphi^(2L-1)) in the completed low/high product.
It belongs to Uhat_N whenever N<L.
The exact claim is

    Pi(w_L)=L/2 modulo L.                                (KL2)

For L>=4 write its high component as1+t^(L-1)b, b=X^(2-L)phi.
Embed F_4 in the high algebra via the cube-one lift rho with residue X, and factor its rank4 polynomial over F_4[[t]] into two monic quadratic factors with reductions(X+rho)^2 and(X+rho^2)^2.
The global high quartic is

    F(X,t)=X^4+xX^3+(x^2+1)X^2+(x^3+x)X+(x^4+x^2+1),

with x=x(t)=t+O(t^3).
Hence F=phi^2+t(X^3+X) modulo t^2.
Writing the first quadratic as X^2+rho^2+t(aX+b_0) and its conjugate by squaring constants, coefficient comparison gives a=rho,b_0=0.
Explicitly a+a^2=1, b_0+b_0^2=0, a rho+a^2rho^2=1 and b_0rho+b_0^2rho^2=0.
The two possible a with trace1 are rho and rho^2, and only rho satisfies the X coefficient; b_0 is in F_2 and the constant equation forces0.
The two distinct quadratic factors modulo t are coprime, so their lifts exist uniquely by coefficientwise Bezout lifting.
The high algebra is the rank-two algebra of the first factor over its embedded constant field F_4; the other is its constant-field conjugate.
Therefore the relative rank2 trace and norm satisfy

    Tr_rel(X)=rho t modulo t^2,
    N_rel(X)=rho^2 modulo t^2.

Newton's elementary two-root identity Tr_rel(X^3)=Tr_rel(X)^3+N_rel(X)Tr_rel(X) gives t modulo t^2, while Tr_rel(X^2) and Tr_rel(X^4) vanish at that precision.
Thus Tr_rel(X^2phi)=t modulo t^2.
Because L>=4 is dyadic and X-rho lies in the high uniformizer ideal, X^(-L)=rho^(-L) modulo t^2.
Here phi=(X-rho)(X-rho^2), the second factor is a unit in this branch, and (t^2)=(phi^4), so raising X-rho to the Lth power gives exactly the required divisibility.
Taking the F_4/F_2 trace and using Tr_F4(rho^(-L))=1 yields

    Tr_high(X^(2-L)phi)=t modulo t^2.

The norm determinant now gives N_high(1+t^(L-1)b)=1+t^L modulo t^(L+1): its quadratic and higher terms have order at least2L-2>=L+1.
C652's finite ell rule then gives ell(N_high(w_L))=L modulo2L, proving(KL2).

At L=2, use w_2=1+Xphi^3=1+t phi.
The high quartic has Tr_high(X)=x and Tr_high(X^2)=x^2, so Tr_high(phi)=x+x^2=t modulo t^2.
The residue of multiplication by phi is nilpotent, hence its second characteristic coefficient vanishes modulo t.
Its norm is therefore1+t^2 modulo t^3, again giving Pi(w_2)=1 modulo2.
This handles N=1 explicitly.

### Every-depth lifting and what finite equality follows

C654 gives Pi(Uhat_N) subset k_N Z_2.
Taking L=L_N in(KL2) supplies an element of Uhat_N whose Pi value has exactly that valuation.
It is principal, so its2-adic powers converge and their Pi values cover all k_N Z_2, proving(KL1).
In detail Pi(w_(L_N))=k_N c for an odd c in Z_2, and a->w_(L_N)^a is continuous for a in Z_2 because its2^j-th powers tend to1.
For any desired value k_N d, the exponent a=d/c gives that exact character value.
Given any class in kerPi_N, choose a completed lift; its Pi value is a multiple of k_N.
Multiplying by a suitable2-adic power of w_(L_N) cancels that value exactly without changing the class modulo chi^N.
Thus kerPi projects onto kerPi_N, and kerPi_M->kerPi_N is surjective for every M>=N.
This completion proof supplies genuine finite representatives after projection, not an original polynomial factorization.

For N<=32, projection of the adopted G_32=kerPi_32 is exactly G_N on the generator side and exactly kerPi_N on the kernel side.
Thus G_N=kerPi_N for ALL1<=N<=32, by one lifting theorem rather than a new enumeration.
Their orders are3*2^(5N-3-floor(log_2 N)), because |R_N^*|=3*2^(5N-3).
No N>32 equality is inferred.
The finite image of Ghat is exactly G_N: continuity of the map to a finite group sends the closure of the complete products to their already closed finite image.
If Ghat=kerPi then(KL1) gives all finite kernel equalities.
Conversely, if G_N=kerPi_N for every N, any element of kerPi can be approximated at every chi-adic depth by complete products, hence belongs to Ghat.
Thus the all-finite and completed kernel assertions are equivalent, without either being proved here.

For dyadic N, the reduction kernel R_(2N)^*->R_N^* is the elementary abelian group1+chi^N R_N of F_2 dimension5N.
The restriction of Pi_(2N) maps it onto the order-two subgroup N Z/(2N), by w_(2N).
Its intersection with kerPi_(2N) therefore has dimension5N-1.
The known deep composition subgroup consists of1+chi^N a(chi), deg a<N, and has dimensionN.
Consequently a proof of G_(2N)=kerPi_(2N), assuming equality at N, still requires generation of the remaining4N-1 binary directions modulo those known composition directions.
The exact equivalence follows by comparing the two fibers over a given element of G_N=kerPi_N: both groups project onto that group, and equality of their intersections with the reduction kernel is necessary and sufficient for equality of the full groups.
The known composition intersection is exactly the displayed dimension-N space, because composition by chi is an automorphism of the low-X formal units and detects the first N base coefficients.
This is an exact missing generation lemma, not a claim that those directions have been supplied.

### Why the existing density basis does not solve that lemma

Let D_N be the subgroup of all h(chi) in R_N, of size2^(N-1).
Each e_j=Theta^j(B_3) lies in G_N, but e_(j+1)=e_j^5/e_j(chi) and e_j(chi) lies in D_N.
Therefore

    <D_N,e_0,e_1,...>=<D_N,B_3>.

This group's order is at most2^(N-1) times3*2^ceil(log_2(2N)), using the ambient exponent.
The composition map is injective on base units modulo t^N, so |D_N|=2^(N-1).
The full ambient exponent is3 times the least power of2 at least2N: the residue group is F_4^*, while the principal low/high nilpotency lengths are N and2N.
The quotient of the displayed subgroup by D_N is cyclic, so its order is bounded by this exponent.
It is strictly smaller than |kerPi_N| for every N>=1, so low density plus the SAME Theta iterates cannot prove kernel generation.
For N=1 the exponents are1<2; for N>=2 use ceil(log_2(2N))<=N and floor(log_2 N)<=N-1 to check the strict inequality directly.
This is a failure of that proposed bootstrap, not an additional obstruction to the full G_N.

In the full completion, quotienting by all h(chi) identifies a pair(u,v) with v/u(x(t)) in the high unit group.
The remaining coprime-five generators are therefore

    K_n(X)=B_n(X)/B_n(x(t)), t=chi(X), 5 not dividing n,
    ell(N_high(K_n))=0.

The full unanswered statement is that their closed generated subgroup equals ker(ell composed with N_high) in V.
The factor1/2 does not change this infinite kernel.
For completeness the quotient map is kappa(u,v)=v/u(x(t)); it is surjective onto V, and its kernel is exactly the full composition subgroup h(chi), h in U.
Indeed choose h=u composed with x to recover both components when kappa=1.
Its norm obeys ell(N_high(kappa(u,v)))=ell(N_high(v))-(4/5)ell(u)=2Pi(u,v), so it identifies the two asserted infinite kernels.
For coprime-five n, N_high(B_n)=B_n(t)/B_n(x(t)), and the scalar denominator B_n(x(t)) has high norm its fourth power.
Therefore N_high(K_n)=B_n(t)/B_n(x(t))^5, whose ell value is0 by the exact inverse-composition identity.
The complete composition subgroup lies in Ghat by C654, and all allowed factors are generated by coprime-five B_n and B_n(chi), by the full divisor induction.
Consequently kappa(Ghat) is exactly the closed subgroup generated by the K_n; closedness follows from compactness of Ghat and the continuous quotient map.
This proves the precise equivalence(K7), leaving its generation assertion open.
All higher five-order factors are already accounted for by the composition subgroup; none has been removed from the original complete support.
No realization, global completeness of an individual Y/H, actual q descent or change of the original C655 remaining range follows.
