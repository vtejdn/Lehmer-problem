# The infimum of coefficient rates with bounded multiplicities at roots of unity

<a id="statement"></a>
## Comparison with the exceptional-unit infimum

Let \(B\ne\varnothing\) be a finite set of positive orders and let \(c_b\) be fixed nonnegative integer caps.
Let \(P\) range over monic minima of nontorsion algebraic units and \(a\) over positive integers.
Define
\[
 g(B,c)=\inf_P\ \liminf_{a\to\infty}\frac1a
     \log\min\{\,\|P^a C\|_1: C\ne0,\quad
                    \operatorname{ord}_{\Phi_b} C\le c_b\ (b\in B)\,\}.
\]
Use either all integer cofactors or just monic cyclotomic products in this definition.
There is no degree cap in the minimum, which exists by integrality and the availability of \(C=1\).
Write \(\lambda_{\rm exc}=\inf m(\alpha)\) over nontorsion algebraic units for which \(1-\alpha\) is a unit.
For either cofactor class,
\[
 \max_{b\in B}\frac1{\varphi(b)}
               \min\left\{\lambda_{\rm exc},\frac{\log2}{c_b+1}\right\}
              \ \le\ g(B,c)\ \le\lambda_{\rm exc}.                       \tag{1}
\]
Consequently \(g(B,c)=0\) if and only if \(\lambda_{\rm exc}=0\).
If \(1\in B\) with \(c_1\le3\), or \(2\in B\) with \(c_2\le3\), then \(g(B,c)=\lambda_{\rm exc}\).
Other tested orders and their caps are arbitrary in these equalities.

<a id="twist"></a>
## The unit and nonunit values of a tested resultant

Fix \(P,b\) and a root \(\alpha\) of \(P\).
If \(|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_b,P)|=1\), then \(\Phi_b(\alpha)\) is a unit.
In a field containing all \(b\)-th roots of unity it is a product of algebraic integers \(\alpha-\zeta\) over primitive roots \(\zeta\), so every factor is a unit.
The unit \(\alpha/\zeta\) is nontorsion and exceptional, with \(m(\alpha/\zeta)\le\varphi(b)m(P)\) by [degree and Weil height under twisting](Yin_C_G0901_v17.md#lifting).
In this case the rate for \(P\) is at least \(m(P)\ge\lambda_{\rm exc}/\varphi(b)\).
Otherwise the absolute resultant is an integer at least two and the [unrestricted-degree multiplicity bound](Yin_C_G0901_v30.md#statement) gives a rate at least \((\log2)/(\varphi(b)(c_b+1))\).
Both estimates hold for arbitrary integer cofactors.
Taking the maximum of the resulting lower bounds and then the infimum over \(P\) proves the left inequality.

For the upper bound start with any exceptional nontorsion \(\alpha\).
Take a root \(\beta\) of \(\beta^N=\alpha\) for a common multiple \(N\) of every member of \(B\).
By the [unit-factor argument](Yin_C_G0901_v17.md#lifting) its minimum has only unit tested resultants, and \(0<m(\beta)\le m(\alpha)\).
The [finite avoidance formula](Yin_C_G0901_v29.md#statement) gives coefficient rate \(m(\beta)\) even for pure cyclotomic cofactors with all tested multiplicities zero.
This proves the right inequality.

Lehmer's [Salem unit with minimum \(L\)](Yin_C_G0901_v22.md#representatives) is exceptional because \(L(1)=-1\).
Its only exterior root is positive and is less than \(19/16\), since \(16^{10}L(19/16)=330753263801>0\).
Since \(19^4=130321<131072=2\cdot16^4\), this gives \(\lambda_{\rm exc}\le m(L)<(\log2)/4\).
Substitution in (1) at an indicated order with \(\varphi(b)=1\) proves the equalities.

<a id="diagonal"></a>
## Varying the input, multiplicity and tested orders together

Fix one order \(b\) and a nonnegative cap \(c\).
Suppose \(P_j\) are monic minima of nontorsion units, \(h_j=m(P_j)\), \(d_j=\deg P_j\), and integer \(F_j\ne0\) satisfy
\[
  a_j\to\infty,\qquad P_j^{a_j}\mid F_j,\qquad
  \operatorname{ord}_{\Phi_b}F_j\le c,\qquad
  \log(2+d_j/h_j)=o(a_j),\qquad \log\|F_j\|_1=o(a_j).
\]
Then \(|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_b,P_j)|=1\) for all sufficiently large \(j\), and twisting a root by a primitive \(b\)-th root gives exceptional units of positive measures tending to zero.
Indeed put \(H_j=\|F_j\|_1\), and use \(s_j\le H_j\) in the [explicit terminal degree bound](Yin_C_G0901_v30.md#gaps).
For \(K_j=2+(s_jd_j/h_j)(a_j+\log H_j)\), the hypotheses give \(\log K_j=o(a_j)\).
Every terminal piece has degree \(D\) with \(D+2\le4K_j\log(4K_j)\), hence \(\log(D+2)=o(a_j)\).
Choose a terminal piece of tested order at most \(c\).
The divided derivative bound gives
\[
  \frac{\log|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_b,P_j)|}{\varphi(b)}
            \le \frac{\log H_j+c\log(D+2)}{a_j}\longrightarrow0 .
\]
The fixed-order integer resultant must therefore have absolute value one eventually.
Also \(0<h_j\le(\log H_j)/a_j\to0\), so the degree factor \(\varphi(b)\) in the twist is harmless.

Conversely if \(\lambda_{\rm exc}=0\), for every sequence of finite sets \(B_j\) containing this \(b\) there are such \(P_j,a_j,F_j\) with \(F_j/P_j^{a_j}\) a pure cyclotomic product avoiding all orders in \(B_j\).
First choose an exceptional unit with measure at most \(1/j\); the upper-bound construction gives \(P_j\) with \(0<h_j\le1/j\) and exact avoidance rate \(h_j\) for the fixed data at this \(j\).
Choose \(a_j\ge j[1+\log(2+d_j/h_j)]\) large enough to attain normalized log norm at most \(h_j+1/j\).
The desired conditions follow with zero tested multiplicities.
The unrestricted infimum \(\mathfrak L\) may use inputs and multiples outside these conditions; its vanishing has not been shown to imply vanishing of \(\lambda_{\rm exc}\).
