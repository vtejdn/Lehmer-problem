# A uniform Mahler gap at every Salem construction delay of at least three

<a id="statement"></a>
## Two possible delays for a near-one sequence

Let \(f\in\mathbb Z[z]\) be the monic minimal polynomial of a Pisot number \(\theta>1\), with \(q=|f(0)|\) and \(f^*(z)=z^{\deg f}f(1/z)\).
For either sign \(\sigma=\pm1\) and any integer \(N\ge1\) put
\[
                    H_{N,\sigma}(z)=z^N f(z)-\sigma f^*(z),
           \qquad M_{N,\sigma}=M(H_{N,\sigma}).
\]
Here \(M\) is multiplicative Mahler measure.
There is an absolute \(c>0\) such that, simultaneously over all degrees, seeds, signs and \(N\ge3\),
\[
                       M_{N,\sigma}=1
                \quad\hbox{or}\quad M_{N,\sigma}\ge1+c .
\]
In particular every sequence in this construction with \(M_{N,\sigma}>1\) tending to one has \(N\in\{1,2\}\) eventually.
At delay two eventually \(q=1\) or \(q=2\).
On the unit subsequence every cluster value of \(\theta\) lies in \([2,1+\sqrt2]\), while on the norm-two subsequence \(20/9<\theta\le1+\sqrt3+o(1)\).
A second uniform assertion, valid even at delay one, is that for every fixed \(b<2\) the measures \(M_{N,\sigma}>1\) of these constructions with seeds \(1<\theta\le b\) are separated from one.
The gaps from the sparse-polynomial argument are qualitative.

The [fixed-delay enumeration of Boyd](../audits/literature/Boy78/Yin_U001.md#fixed-delay-enumeration) for his choice of sign reports the complete interval \((1,1.3]\) at delays four through eight, and the interval \([1.1,1.3]\) at delay three.
The argument below treats delay three down to the endpoint one without a new finite search.
The [delay-one representation](../audits/literature/Smy15/Yin_U002.md#closedness-and-delay-one) still includes every Salem number.

<a id="compact"></a>
## Compact seed intervals below two

Fix \(1<b<2\).
The [compact classification of Pisot numbers](../audits/literature/Laz78/Yin_U001.md#compact-pisot-families) and the [factorization of the regular defining polynomial](../audits/literature/Boy96/Yin_U001.md#regular-cofactor) give a uniform term bound for an integer cyclotomic multiple \(A=C f\) of each seed minimal polynomial with \(\theta\le b\).
Here \(C\) is a product of cyclotomic polynomials, possibly one.
For detail, if no term bound existed, choose distinct seeds whose smallest achievable term counts tend to infinity.
After passing to a subsequence their roots converge to a limit in \([1,b]\).
The classification below two gives within this subsequence a further sequence of regular defining polynomials \(z^{t_i}P(z)\pm Q(z)\) for fixed integer polynomials \(P,Q\), with the seed root outside the unit disk and its minimal polynomial as the only noncyclotomic factor.
Indeed the limiting data have \(|Q|\le|P|\) on the unit circle and \(P\) has just one exterior root.
Rouché with a factor less than one on \(Q\), followed by passage to one, gives at most one strict exterior root counted with multiplicity.
The polynomials are monic with nonzero constant for large \(t_i\), so every other irreducible factor has measure one and is cyclotomic by Kronecker's theorem.
Thus \(A\) can be taken to have at most \(\#\operatorname{supp}P+\#\operatorname{supp}Q\) terms on that subsequence, a contradiction.
The fixed seeds and the finitely many exceptions also have a finite term count using \(A=f\).

Since \(C^*=\tau C\) with \(\tau=\pm1\), we have the exact identity
\[
                  C H_{N,\sigma}=z^N A-\sigma\tau A^* .
\]
If \(A\) has at most \(k_b\) terms, this polynomial has at most \(2k_b\) terms, independently of \(N,\sigma\) and the degrees of \(C,f\).
It is nonzero and its measure is \(M_{N,\sigma}\) because \(M(C)=1\).
The [fixed-support Mahler gap](../audits/literature/DS17/Yin_U001.md#ds17-u001) proves the assertion for \(\theta\le b\) and every delay.

<a id="radial"></a>
## The remaining seeds at delays three and higher

For \(q\ge2\) the [explicit nonunit bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v113.md#nonunit) gives \(M_{N,\sigma}>3/2\) for \(N\ge3\), except for one cyclotomic output.
In brief the signed disk quotient with constant \(q\) gives
\(\theta-q\ge1/(q+\theta^{-1}+2)\) for nonlinear seeds.
In particular \(\theta>20/9\) for \(q=2\) and \(\theta\ge3\) for \(q\ge3\).
At \(r=3/2\) the coarse root-factor comparison \(r^3>(\theta r-1)/(\theta-r)\) holds whenever \(\theta>13/6\).
It gives a positive real root beyond \(r\) for all such seeds, since \(H_{N,\sigma}(r)<0\) and the leading coefficient is one.
The integer seed two at \(N\ge4\) obeys the same comparison with \(r^4\), and its two outputs at \(N=3\) are given in the explicit bound.

For unit seeds choose \(b=19/10\).
At every \(1<r<\theta\) the [norm-sensitive radial comparison](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v113.md#radial) gives
\[
       \left|\frac{f^*(r)}{f(r)}\right|
          \le D(r,\theta)
          :=\frac{\theta r-1}{\theta-r}\,\frac{\theta+r}{r\theta+1}.
\]
For fixed \(r>1\) both factors are decreasing in \(\theta>r\).
At \(\theta=b\) the derivative of \(D(r,b)\) in \(r\) at one is \(4b/(b^2-1)=760/261<3\) and \(D(1,b)=1\).
Fix \(r_0\in(1,b)\) sufficiently close to one that \(D(r_0,b)<r_0^3\).
For every \(\theta\ge b,\ N\ge3\) we then have \(r_0^N>|f^*(r_0)/f(r_0)|\).
As \(f(r_0)<0\) for \(1<r_0<\theta\), this inequality forces \(H_{N,\sigma}(r_0)<0\) for both signs, hence a positive root beyond \(r_0\).
Together with the compact interval below \(b\) and the nonunit bound, this proves the uniform assertion.

At \(N=2\), the [two-delay comparison](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v113.md#two) gives \(M_{N,\sigma}+M_{N,\sigma}^{-1}\ge\theta-q/\theta\).
The [nonunit exclusions and remaining bands](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v113.md#bands) give \(q\le2\) eventually and the stated upper limits, including the lower estimate for \(\theta\) when \(q=2\).
If \(\theta\) had a cluster value below two on the unit subsequence the compact-interval result would preclude vanishing excess there.
This proves the remaining restrictions and leaves open the existence of such a sequence at delay two.
