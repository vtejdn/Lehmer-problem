# A lower bound for the part of a reciprocal section index outside any fixed set of primes

<a id="statement"></a>
## Restrictions at the coefficient primes and at primes where the coefficients agree up to sign

Let \(P(X)=a(1+\epsilon X^{u+v})+bX^u(1+\epsilon X^{v-u})\), with \(0<u<v\) coprime, \(\epsilon=\pm1\), and \(a,b\) coprime nonzero integers.
Put \(H=|b|\) and \(\delta=|b/a|-1>0\).
Consider sequences with \(\delta\to0\) in which \(P(z^g)=0\) for a nontorsion algebraic unit \(z\) and a positive integer \(g\).
Write \(\beta=z^g\), \(D=[\mathbb Q(\beta):\mathbb Q]\), and fix a prime \(\ell\) and a nonempty finite set \(\mathcal A\) of \(r\) primes, possibly including \(\ell\).
For a positive integer \(n\), let \(n_{\mathcal A}=\prod_{p\in\mathcal A}p^{v_p(n)}\) and \(n_{\rm out}=n/n_{\mathcal A}\).
At each index satisfying one of the following conditions, use the corresponding \(N\).

| Condition | Index \(N\) |
| --- | --- |
| \(\ell\mid a\) | \(v-u\) |
| \(\ell\mid b\) | \(v+u\) |
| \(\ell\mid b^2-a^2\) | \(v\) |

Eventually the chosen index satisfies
\[
  N_{\mathcal A}\ll_{\ell,\mathcal A}D(\log H)^r,\qquad
  N_{\rm out}\gg_{\ell,\mathcal A}\frac{1}{\delta(\log H)^{r-1}} .
\]
These bounds allow every exponent of every prime in \(\mathcal A\) to vary, and impose no bound on the primes or exponents in \(N_{\rm out}\).

In particular, for \(|a|=q,\ |b|=q+1\), take \(N=v-u\) at even \(q\) and \(N=v+u\) at odd \(q\).
A nontorsion unit requires \(N_{\rm out}\gg_{\mathcal A} q/(\log q)^{r-1}\) for all sufficiently large \(q\).
Taking \(N=v\) gives the same necessary bound, with constant also depending on \(\ell\), whenever a fixed odd prime \(\ell\) divides \(2q+1\).
For example the latter applies when \(q\equiv1\pmod3\).
If the corresponding cofactor is \(o(q/(\log q)^{r-1})\), eventually all positive-measure integral factors have logarithmic Mahler measure at least \(\log2\) at every dilation.
In particular this excludes all mixed powers \(2^i3^j\) at the parity-selected endpoint for large \(q\).
The [fixed-coefficient endpoint gap](Yin_C_G0345_v562.md#statement) permits constants depending on both original coefficients, whereas the restrictions here apply as their magnitudes grow.

There is also an exact congruence restriction for the full, possibly composite coefficient modulus.
Eventually the order of \(\beta\) in \((\mathbb Z[\beta]/c\mathbb Z[\beta])^\times\) equals \(N\) for \(\epsilon=-1\) and \(2N\) for \(\epsilon=1\), simultaneously for \((c,N)=(|a|,v-u)\) and \((|b|,v+u)\).

<a id="reduction"></a>
## A single long binomial after retaining the short factor

Write \(S\) for the monic minimal polynomial of \(\beta\).
Its constant coefficient is a unit and its reduction \(\overline S\) at \(\ell\) divides the reduction of \(P\) with multiplicities.
Take \(M=2N\) in every row of the table.
In the first two rows set \(U=1\) and \(J=\overline S\).
The [endpoint reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v874.md#norm) gives \(J\mid X^M-1\).
In the last row the ratio \(s=b/a\) in the residue field is \(1\) or \(-1\), and \(P/a=(1+sX^u)(1+\epsilon sX^v)\) there.
Take \(U=\gcd(\overline S,1+sX^u)\) monic and \(J=\overline S/U\).
Comparing multiplicities gives \(J\mid X^M-1\) and \(U\mid X^{2u}-1\), even when the two binomials share roots.
No coprimality of \(U\) and \(J\) is needed.

The [primitive measure bound](Yin_C_G0345_v869.md#statement) and [individual height formula](Yin_C_G0345_v875.md#statement) give
\[
 D h(\beta)\le3\delta,\qquad u h(\beta)\to0,\qquad
 N h(\beta)=\log H+o(1)
\]
in all three rows, since \(N\) is \(v\) or \(v\pm u\) and the growing coprime magnitudes force \(H\to\infty\).
Consequently \(D/N\ll\delta/\log H\).

<a id="primary"></a>
## The part of a primary period supported on the selected primes

For any monic irreducible power \(f^e\) occurring in \(J\), let \(d=\deg f\) and \(t\) be the order of a root of \(f\) in the multiplicative group of its residue field.
The least period \(k_f\) with \(f^e\mid X^{k_f}-1\) is \(t\ell^{\lceil\log_\ell e\rceil}\), as in the [primary period calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v257.md#multiplicities).
In particular \(k_f\mid M\).
We need only
\[
 (k_f)_{\mathcal A}\le C_{\ell,\mathcal A}\,d e\le C_{\ell,\mathcal A} D ,
\]
with no restriction on the prime divisors of \(t\) outside \(\mathcal A\).
Indeed \(t\mid\ell^d-1\).
For each odd \(p\in\mathcal A\) different from \(\ell\), the \(p\)-valuation of \(\ell^d-1\) is zero unless \(\operatorname{ord}_p(\ell)\mid d\), and otherwise is \(v_p(\ell^{\operatorname{ord}_p(\ell)}-1)+v_p(d)\).
This follows by the binomial expansion at a unit congruent to one modulo \(p\).
For \(p=2\ne\ell\), odd \(d\) gives \(v_2(\ell-1)\), and even \(d\) gives \(v_2(\ell^2-1)+v_2(d)-1\) by repeated squaring.
The product of the variable factors \(p^{v_p(d)}\) divides \(d\).
The possible \(\ell\)-part of \(k_f\) is at most \(\ell e\), proving the bound.

<a id="cover"></a>
## A product of at most one binomial per selected prime

Choose \(\eta>0\), depending only on \(\ell,r\), such that every nontorsion unit \(\gamma\) of height at most \(\eta\) in any number field \(K\) satisfies
\[
 \frac{\log|N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\gamma-1)|}{[K:\mathbb Q]}
 \le\frac{\log\ell}{2(r+1)}.
\]
The [cyclic norm estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v730.md#statement) gives this choice explicitly by taking \(n\) large enough that \(\log n/(n-1)\le\log\ell/(4(r+1))\), then taking \(\eta=3\log\ell/(4(r+1)(n+1))\).

Put \(T=\max(2,Mh(\beta)/\eta)\).
For each \(p\in\mathcal A\) with \(p^{v_p(M)}\ge T\), take the least power \(b_p\) of \(p\) at least \(T\) and put \(n_p=M/b_p\).
Then \(h(\beta^{n_p})\le\eta\).
Eventually \(h(\beta^{2u})\le\eta\) as well.
Suppose every full primary factor \(f^e\) in \(J\) divides at least one \(X^{n_p}-1\).
Since different primary factors are coprime, \(J\) divides the product of these binomials modulo \(\ell\).
In the third row multiply the product by \(X^{2u}-1\) to cover \(U\), so that the resulting integral polynomial \(G\) satisfies \(\overline S\mid\overline G\) in all cases.
The empty product is one.
Then \(G(\beta)\in\ell\mathbb Z[\beta]\), and it is nonzero since \(\beta\) is nontorsion.
Its normalized log norm is at least \(\log\ell\).
On the other hand the norm bound for each of its at most \(r+1\) binomials gives an upper bound of \((\log\ell)/2\).
This contradiction proves that a primary factor \(f^e\) in \(J\) escapes every selected binomial, also when none is selected.

For this factor, \(k_f\mid M\) but \(k_f\nmid M/b_p\) for each selected \(p\), whence \(p^{v_p(M/k_f)}<b_p\le pT\).
Each unselected \(p\in\mathcal A\) contributes less than \(T\) to \((M/k_f)_{\mathcal A}\).
It follows that \(M_{\mathcal A}\ll_{\mathcal A} T^r(k_f)_{\mathcal A}\ll_{\ell,\mathcal A} T^r D\).
As \(N\mid M\) and \(T\ll_{\ell,r}\log H\), this gives the first inequality in the statement.
The estimate for \(D/N\) then gives the second one.
A lift to a common dilation has a nontorsion unit as its primitive power, so exclusion at the primitive index excludes such roots at every dilation.
The [integral divisor gap](Yin_C_G0345_v869.md#degree) follows once those units are excluded.

<a id="composite"></a>
## The exact period modulo each full coefficient

In \(\mathbb Z[\beta]/c\mathbb Z[\beta]\) the other coefficient and \(\beta\) are invertible, giving \(\beta^N=-\epsilon\) in both endpoint cases.
For large indices \(c>2\), and \(1\) and \(-1\) are distinct in this quotient, since \(\mathbb Z[\beta]\) has integral basis as a free additive group \(1,\ldots,\beta^{D-1}\).
Let \(j\) be the least positive index with \(\beta^j=-\epsilon\) there.
For \(\epsilon=-1\) this is the order and divides \(N\).
For \(\epsilon=1\), the cyclic group generated by \(\beta\) contains \(-1\) as its unique element of order two, so the order is \(2j\) and \(N\) is an odd multiple of \(j\).
The nonzero norm of \(\beta^j+\epsilon\) is divisible by \(c^D\) and its magnitude is at most \(2^D\exp(jDh(\beta))\).
Thus \(j h(\beta)\ge\log(c/2)\).
Since \(N h(\beta)=\log H+o(1)\) and \(\log c=\log H+o(1)\), this forces \(j>N/2\) eventually.
The divisibility \(j\mid N\) proves \(j=N\).
