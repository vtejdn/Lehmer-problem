# Local factors with a vanishing degree fraction and matching binomial norms

<a id="statement"></a>
## Matching the units after division by the full power of five

Fix positive integers \(a_2,a_3,a_5\) and a real number \(\eta>0\).
There exist an odd prime \(L>5\), a rational number \(0<\theta<\min(\eta,1)\) with denominator \(L\), and arbitrarily large pairs of primes \(r<q\) with \(g=q-r=O_L(\log r)\) and \(2L\mid g\) for which the following holds for both signs.
Put
\[
 Q=\frac{X^r(X^g-2)(X^g-3)+\varepsilon(1-2X^g)(1-3X^g)}{X+\varepsilon},
 \qquad d=\theta(q-1),\qquad u=\theta g/2.
\]
For each \(\ell=2,3,5\) there is a monic reciprocal divisor \(S_\ell\) of \(Q\) in \(\mathbb Z_\ell[X]\) of degree \(d\), with constant term one and coprime reduction to its complement.
Define \(N_j(F)=\operatorname{Res}(F,X^g-j)\) for \(j=2,3\).
All three local factors satisfy
\[
       S_\ell(\varepsilon)\equiv1,\qquad
       N_2(S_\ell)/5^u\equiv N_3(S_\ell)/5^u\equiv1
                                      \pmod{\ell^{a_\ell}}.                 \tag{1}
\]
The two ratios are units in the corresponding local ring.
At five this prescribes the exact valuations \(u\) and the first \(a_5\) digits of each unit part.
At two and three no selected root is exceptional, and at five the exceptional degree is \(2u\).
The exponents and \(\eta\) can be chosen before the parameters.
On these pairs any monic rational divisor of \(Q\) of degree \(d\) would have logarithmic Mahler measure \(\theta\log 6+O(g^2 e^{-cr/(2g+1)^2})\) with an absolute \(c>0\).
This gives local choices at every prescribed finite precision while leaving rational divisibility undecided.

<a id="parameters"></a>
## A prime with many equal-sized orbits

Let \(E\) be a positive multiple of four and of the exponents of the finite unit groups \((\mathbb Z/\ell^{a_\ell}\mathbb Z)^\times\) for \(\ell=2,3,5\).
Write \(G_\ell=((\mathbb Z/\ell^{a_\ell}\mathbb Z)^\times)^3\).
On a finite list labelled by \(G_\ell\), partition each label class into sets of size \(E\) with a remainder of fewer than \(E\) entries.
All these sets have product label one, and fewer than \(E|G_\ell|\) entries altogether are left over.
In particular a union of any prescribed number of the sets is available up to the number constructed.

Choose an integer \(M\) larger than \(4E\max_\ell |G_\ell|\) and \(2E/\eta\).
Choose an arbitrarily large prime \(L\) splitting completely in \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{2M},5^{1/M})\), using the [fixed-field density theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v218.md#prime-pairs) for this finite Galois extension.
Exclude divisors of \(10M\).
Then \(5\) is an \(M\)-th power modulo \(L\) and \((L-1)/M\) is even.
If \(t=\operatorname{lcm}(2,\operatorname{ord}_L(5))\) and \(J=(L-1)/t\), we have \(M\mid J\).
The nontrivial roots of \(Z^L+1\) other than \(-1\) in characteristic five are the \(-\zeta_L^i\) for \(1\le i<L\).
They split into \(J\) orbits of size \(t\) under Frobenius and inversion.
The product of the linear factors for each orbit is monic reciprocal and has constant term one by inverse pairing.
Define
\[
                           \theta=2Et/L .
\]
Since \(J\ge M\), this is smaller than both \(\eta\) and \(1/2\).
Increasing \(L\) also ensures \(L\nmid 2E\), \(L>4E\max_\ell|G_\ell|\), and \(t<L\), so its reduced denominator is exactly \(L\).

For this fixed \(L\), choose the [short prime pairs with large orders](Yin_C_G0345_v221.md#supply) with splitting parameter \(h=L\).
In particular \(b=g/L\) is even and \(u=Etb\) is divisible by \(E\).
At each characteristic the corresponding primitive grid has \(J_\ell\) orbits of the same size \(t_\ell\).
Splitting and the even quotients give \(L\mid J_\ell\), as in the [three-grid construction](Yin_C_G0345_v218.md#three), and thus \(J_\ell\ge L\).

<a id="exceptional"></a>
## Choosing the exceptional roots at five

For each of the \(J\) orbits in \(Z\), with polynomial \(A(Z)\), select its entire inverse image \(A(X^{2b})\) in \(X^{2g}+1\).
The \(J\) inverse images and the remaining \(X^{2b}+1\) are pairwise coprime over \(\mathbb F_5\).
Indeed \(Z^L+1\) is separable, and any polynomial Bezout identity stays an identity after substitution, even when \(5\mid b\).
These degree-\(2bt\) factors are disjoint from the primitive \(r\)-grid and avoid \(1,-1\).
Each lifts uniquely to a monic reciprocal factor \(F\) of \(Q\) over \(\mathbb Z_5\), with constant term one, by [coprime lifting and reversal](Yin_C_G0345_v216.md#lifts).
The lifts of distinct orbit factors can be used together, since the entire pairwise coprime factorization lifts and any two-factor grouping agrees by uniqueness.

The exceptional roots of \(F\) all satisfy \(X^g=2\) or \(3\) in the residue field; inversion exchanges these classes.
There are exactly \(bt\) roots in each class, counted with multiplicity.
The [individual valuation calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v219.md#sign) uses the root equation and disjointness from \(X^r+\varepsilon\); it gives valuation one for \(x^g-j\) on the class above \(j\) and zero on the other.
Consequently \(F(\varepsilon)\), \(5^{-bt}N_2(F)\), \(5^{-bt}N_3(F)\) are five-adic units.
Use their residues as the triple label in \(G_5\).
Since \(J>E|G_5|\), choose \(E\) distinct orbit factors with the same label.
Their product has all three labels one, and its degree is \(2Ebt=\theta g=2u\).
Both norms of this product have valuation \(u\).

<a id="regular"></a>
## Filling the primitive grid and determining the rational share

For \(\ell=2,3\) write \(p=q\), and for \(\ell=5\) write \(p=r\).
Lift every full Frobenius and inversion orbit on the simple primitive \(p\)-grid to its monic reciprocal factor \(F\) over \(\mathbb Z_\ell\).
The primitive roots avoid \(\varepsilon\).
Raising them to the even power \(g\), coprime to \(p\), gives elements of order \(p>5\), excluding the residues \(2,3\) in the corresponding prime field.
Thus \(F(\varepsilon),N_2(F),N_3(F)\) are units.
Use these three residues as labels in \(G_\ell\).
The required number \(m_\ell=\theta J_\ell=2Et(J_\ell/L)\) of orbits is an integer multiple of \(E\).
The inequality \(J_\ell-m_\ell> J_\ell/2>E|G_\ell|\) guarantees \(m_\ell/E\) of the product-one sets from the partition.
Their combined degree is \(\theta(p-1)\) and their three values are all one modulo \(\ell^{a_\ell}\).

At two and three use this factor alone; \(5^u\) is one modulo each prescribed power since \(E\mid u\).
At five multiply it by the chosen exceptional product.
This gives (1) with the required degree \(\theta(r-1)+\theta g=d\).
Each choice is coprime modulo \(\ell\) to its complement, and can be made independently for the two signs.
The resulting local factorizations give compatible coefficient factorizations by Chinese remaindering at all finite levels, with (1) asserted at the preassigned levels.

Finally let \(S\) be a monic rational divisor of degree \(d\).
At two, \(d/t_2=\theta J_2\) is integral, so the exceptional degree is a multiple of \(t_2\).
It is at most \(g<t_2\), hence zero.
The [exact degree identity](Yin_C_G0345_v221.md#statement) gives total exterior share \(s=\theta g\).
Sum the [individual measure estimates](Yin_C_G0345_v198.md#statement) over its at most \(g\) irreducible constituents to obtain the stated error.
Taking \(\eta\to0\) and choosing \(r\) sufficiently large at each step would give positive logarithmic measures tending to zero if such rational divisors existed.
