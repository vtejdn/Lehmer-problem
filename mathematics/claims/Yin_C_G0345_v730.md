# Cyclotomic norm bounds and Frobenius probabilities at small height

<a id="statement"></a>
## A simultaneous bound at the finite places

Let \(z\) be a nonzero algebraic number of infinite multiplicative order, with absolute logarithmic Weil height \(h\).
For a number field \(K\) containing it, use local weights \(d_v=[K_v:\mathbb Q_v]/[K:\mathbb Q]\), with \(|p|_v=p^{-1}\) above \(p\).
For \(t\ge1\) let \(\Phi_t\) be the cyclotomic polynomial and put
\[
 L_t(z)=\sum_{v\nmid\infty}d_v\big(\varphi(t)\log^+|z|_v-\log|\Phi_t(z)|_v\big).
\]
These quantities are nonnegative and independent of \(K\).
For an integer \(n\ge2\) write \(B_n=n(n-1)/2\), \(E_t(n)=\sum_{1\le j<n/t}(n-tj)\), and
\[
 U_n(h)=\frac{\log n}{n-1}+\frac{n+1}{3}h.
\]
Then
\[
                  \sum_{t<n}\frac{E_t(n)}{B_n}L_t(z)\le U_n(h).       \tag{1}
\]
In particular \(L_1(z)\le\inf_{n\ge2} U_n(h)\le(\sqrt{2/3}+o(1))\sqrt{h\log(1/h)}\) as \(h\downarrow0\).
For an algebraic unit \(\alpha\) of degree \(d\), \(z=\alpha^j\), \(j\ge1\), this bounds the complete cyclic norm
\(\log|\operatorname{N}_{\mathbb Q(\alpha)/\mathbb Q}(\alpha^j-1)|/d\) at \(h=jh(\alpha)\), including every powered-field multiplicity.
It improves the small-height envelope in the [complete cyclic-norm estimate](Yin_C_G0147_v5.md#statement).

If \(z\) is a unit and \(t_v\) is the multiplicative order of its residue at a place \(v\mid p\) of \(K\), (1) implies the joint restriction
\[
 \sum_{p}\ \sum_{\substack{v\mid p\\t_v\le n/2}}
      \frac{f_v\log p}{[K:\mathbb Q]\,t_v}\le 4U_n(h),                \tag{2}
\]
where \(f_v\) is the residue degree.
All residue characteristics and all indicated orders can be counted together.

<a id="cosets"></a>
## Application to conjugate embeddings of a base field

Let \(F\) be any number field, \(\alpha\ne0\) any algebraic number of infinite order, \(h=h(\alpha)\), and \(D=[F(\alpha):F]\).
At any prime \(p\), for any integer \(k\ge1\), put \(q=p^k\).
In the normal closure of \(F/\mathbb Q\), choose a uniformly random prime above \(p\) and a uniformly random lift there of the residue map \(x\mapsto x^q\).
Let \(w_{p,k}\) be the largest probability that the lift lies in a single right coset \(Hg\), with \(H\) the subgroup fixing \(F\).
Then
\[
           w_{p,k}\log p\le D\big(U_n((q+1)h)+2h\big)
                     \qquad(n\ge2).                                    \tag{3}
\]
For algebraic units the term \(2h\) may be dropped.
In particular for the degree-\(d/2\) trace field of a Salem number of degree \(d\) and \(m=\log\alpha\), all cosets at every fixed \(p,k\) have probability
\[
             O_{p,k}\!\left(\sqrt{(m/d)\log(d/m)}\right)
                    \quad\text{as }m/d\longrightarrow0.                \tag{4}
\]
This includes wildly ramified primes and trace fields of every permutation type.
For any fixed \(p,k\), a probability at least \(\eta\sqrt{\log d/d}\) for fixed \(\eta>0\) rules out \(m\to0\) on that class.
Indeed \(m\log(d/m)/\log d\to0\) when \(d\to\infty\) and \(m\to0\).
Thus the relevant comparison for the [relative Frobenius test](Yin_C_G0345_v728.md#statement) can involve probabilities tending to zero.

<a id="auxiliary"></a>
## The consecutive powers of one number

Use the integral auxiliary polynomial
\[
 A_n(X)=\prod_{j=1}^{n-1}(X^j-1)^{n-j}
       =\prod_{t<n}\Phi_t(X)^{E_t(n)},\qquad
 \deg A_n=n(n^2-1)/6 .
\]
On the unit circle its absolute value is that of the Vandermonde determinant on \(1,X,\ldots,X^{n-1}\).
Hadamard's inequality gives \(|A_n(X)|\le n^{n/2}\) there, including repeated nodes.
Apply the maximum principle to \(A_n(X)\) and its reversed polynomial in the unit disk to obtain
\(|A_n(z)|\le n^{n/2}\max(1,|z|)^{\deg A_n}\) at each archimedean embedding.
Each \(\Phi_t\) is monic integral with unit constant term, so every finite-place summand defining \(L_t\) is nonnegative and vanishes if \(|z|_v\ne1\).
The product formula at \(A_n(z)\ne0\), with the preceding archimedean bound, gives
\(\sum_{t<n}E_t(n)L_t(z)\le(n/2)\log n+(\deg A_n)h\).
Division by \(B_n\) proves (1).
Since \(E_1(n)=B_n\), take \(n\sim\sqrt{3\log(1/h)/(2h)}\) for the asserted asymptotic upper bound.
At a unit the finite-place sum for \(t=1\) equals its normalized logarithmic norm by the product formula.

For \(1\le t\le n/2\), the first \(\lfloor n/(2t)\rfloor\) terms of \(E_t(n)\) are each at least \(n/2\), and \(\lfloor n/(2t)\rfloor\ge n/(4t)\).
Hence \(E_t(n)/B_n\ge1/(4t)\).
At a place \(v\mid p\), \(t_v\) is prime to \(p\), so \(X^{t_v}-1\) is separable in the residue field and its root of exact order \(t_v\) vanishes in \(\Phi_{t_v}\).
Consequently \(-\log|\Phi_{t_v}(z)|_v\ge(\log p)/e_v\), with ramification index \(e_v\).
Weighting by \(d_v=e_v f_v/[K:\mathbb Q]\) in (1) gives (2).

<a id="ratio"></a>
## Relative cross differences and their ratios

Choose \(Hg\) attaining the stated maximum, extend \(g\) to an automorphism of the algebraic closure, and write \(a_i\) for the \(D\) relative conjugates of \(\alpha\), \(b_j=(g^{-1}a_j)^q\).
They are all nonzero.
Every ratio \(a_i/b_j\) is nontorsion, since \(h(b_j)=qh>h(a_i)=h\), and \(h(a_i/b_j)\le(q+1)h\).
Repeated entries among the \(b_j\) cause no problem.

Fix a prime \(P\) of the normal closure of \(F\) above \(p\), let \(v=P\cap F\), and suppose some lift \(x\in Hg\) at \(P\) induces the \(q\)-power residue map.
Write \(f\) for the monic relative minimal polynomial of \(\alpha\) and scale it by the inverse of a coefficient of largest \(v\)-absolute value to get \(f_0=cf\) of local Gauss norm one.
For each coefficient \(a\in F\) of \(f_0\), \(g^{-1}a=x^{-1}a\).
The Teichmüller representative of the residue of \(a\) belongs to \(F_v\), differs from \(a\) by an element of valuation at least \(1/e_v\), and \(x\) acts on the representative by its \(q\)-th power.
This gives \(v_p(a-(g^{-1}a)^q)\ge1/e_v\).
Modulo \(p\) all mixed terms of a \(q\)-th power vanish.
Thus the coefficients of \(f_0(X^q)-(g^{-1}f_0(X))^q\) have \(v_p\ge1/e_v\), in the normalization \(v_p(p)=1\).
Evaluation at each \(g^{-1}a_j\) and \(|c|_v^{-1}=\prod_i\max(1,|a_i|_v)\) show that at every extension place \(u\) above \(P\),
\[
 \sum_{i,j}\log\frac{\max(1,|a_i|_u)\max(1,|b_j|_u)}
                         {|a_i-b_j|_u}\ \ge\ D(\log p)/e_v .
\]
At every finite place all terms are nonnegative.
At fixed \(P\) the fraction of its lifts in \(Hg\) is \(1/e_v\) if the coset is hit and zero otherwise, by the inertia-coset count in the [nonnormal comparison](Yin_C_G0345_v728.md#weights), which uses only the size of the inertia orbit and thus also holds for \(q=p^k\).
Normalized weights above \(P\) sum to its weight and all these \(P\) have equal weight.
The sum over all finite places of the left side, weighted by their local degrees in a common normal field, is therefore at least \(Dw_{p,k}\log p\).

For each pair \(a,b\), the local logarithm on the left is the local summand defining \(L_1(a/b)\) before its weight, plus
\[
  \log\frac{\max(1,|a|_u)\max(1,|b|_u)}{\max(|a|_u,|b|_u)} .
\]
This last term vanishes for units at \(u\) and in general lies between zero and \(\big|\log|a|_u\big|\).
Its finite-place weighted sum is at most \(2h(a)\), by the product formula.
Use (1) on each ratio and sum over the \(D^2\) pairs to get (3).
The trace field of a Salem number has relative degree two, the number is a unit and \(h=m/d\), giving (4).
