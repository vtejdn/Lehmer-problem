# Bounded gaps between the degrees supporting narrow double contacts

<a id="statement"></a>
## A positive proportion in every moving interval

Let \(\alpha\) be a reciprocal nontorsion unit of even degree \(e\) with \(\mathbb Q(\alpha^n)=\mathbb Q(\alpha)\) for every positive \(n\).
Put \(h=m(\alpha)>0,\ c=5e/2-1\), and take \(B\) to be twice the product of the finitely many odd primes \(\ell\) for which \(\alpha\in\mathbb Q(\alpha)^\ell\).
At each prime \(p>3\) use the [two narrow-width tests](Yin_C_G0345_v473.md#statement) for common genuine double contacts at a reciprocal unit and its square at width \(b=5d/2-1\).
The individual budget requires \(\max(H_+,H_-)^8<2^p\); the transverse product budget requires \((H_+H_-)^4<2^p\) and nonzero determinant.
Here \(H_\pm\) are the coefficient one-norms.
One may additionally multiply the left side of either budget by \((2pb+1)^4\).

Suppose \(h\le(\log2)/120\).
For every sufficiently large prime \(p\), all but \(O_\alpha(1)\) of the nonzero residue classes of \(k\bmod p\) have the following property.
For every positive \(k\) in such a class with \(\gcd(k,B)=1\), the degree \(d=ek\) passes both coefficient-only tests, using the same seed minimum \(P_\alpha(X^k)\) and its square minimum for both tests.
The selected rows may differ between the two tests.
Within each pair the normalized span of the union of supports is at most \(c\) and the number of occupied positions in the union is at most \(c+1\).
Consequently each test admits at least
\[
          \frac{\varphi(B)}{eB}L-O_\alpha(L/p+1)                            \tag{1}
\]
distinct passing degrees in every real interval \([X,X+L)\) with \(X\ge1,\ L\ge1\).
In particular the gaps between passing degrees are bounded independently of \(p\) for all sufficiently large \(p\).
When the width cost is charged, (1) still holds along sequences with \(p\to\infty\) and \(\log(2p(X+L))/p\to0\).
The same assertions permit prescribed joint normalized span or support bounds tending to infinity with \(p\).

Thus if the proportion of passing degrees tends to zero along any such sequence of intervals with \(L\to\infty\), the unrestricted infimum \(\lambda\) of positive logarithmic Mahler measures satisfies \(\lambda\ge(\log2)/120\).
In particular, arbitrarily long empty intervals suffice.
At the original dyadic blocks this replaces the [previous \(o(D/p)\) condition](Yin_C_G0345_v468.md#budgets) by \(o(D)\) along a subsequence and removes the restriction \(p=o(D)\).
For the width charge one retains \(\log(2pD)/p\to0\).
The counts here concern all admissible placements, as in the [exact progression test](Yin_C_G0345_v476.md#statement).
The more restricted presentations using only an unshifted base and \(k\equiv1\bmod p\) have their original lower count.

<a id="rank"></a>
## Numerator rank at a fixed displacement

Write \(P_a=\min_{\mathbb Q}(\alpha^a)\) for \(a=1,2\), \(G=P_1P_2,\ m=2e,\ r=e/2\), so \(c=m+r-1\).
Fix a sign \(\varepsilon\) and an integer \(u\) with \(0<|u|<p\).
For \(B_0\) of degree at most \(c\) use the shifted graph \(B_0(X^p)+\varepsilon X^u B_0^{*c}(X^p)\), with \(B_0^{*c}(Y)=Y^c B_0(1/Y)\).
Negative exponents are allowed in the Laurent ring, since every target is a unit.
Choose one representative \(x\) of each inverse pair of roots of \(G\).
At each such site put \(y=x^p\) and \(D=Y\,d/dY\).
The two equations on the row are
\[
 \begin{split}
 B_0(y)+\varepsilon x^u y^c B_0(1/y)&=0,\\
 p(DB_0)(y)+\varepsilon x^u y^c
          \{(u+pc)B_0(1/y)-p(DB_0)(1/y)\}&=0 .
 \end{split}                                                              \tag{2}
\]
The double contact at the inverse follows by signed reciprocity.
For each target \(a\) append evaluation of the numerator \(B_0(y)\) at the \(r\) representatives belonging to \(P_a\).
The resulting square matrix has \(m+r=c+1\) rows.
Call its determinant \(D_{p,a,\varepsilon}(u)\).
If it is nonzero then no nonzero rational row of the shifted kernel vanishes at \(\alpha^{ap}\), since such vanishing holds on the whole conjugacy class.

For each fixed nonzero integer \(u\), these determinants are nonzero at every sufficiently large \(p\).
Here is how the [fixed-displacement rank proof](Yin_C_G0345_v462.md#rank) applies.
On each torsion class for the exponent replace \(p\) by a variable \(N\) and the nodes \(x^p\) by the torsion-labeled Laurent monomials \(y_x\) used in that proof.
Writing \(H(Y)=\prod_x(Y-y_x)\) over all \(m\) roots, the kernel at \(N=0\) has basis \(H(Y)Y^l\) for \(0\le l<r\), since \(u\ne0\).
As in the cited calculation, lift each basis element with the \(r\) highest coefficients held fixed.
The first variation at \(N=0\) of its numerator at a zero \(y_x\) is
\[
         (DH)(y_x)\bigl(y_x^l+\varepsilon x^u y_x^{r-1-l}\bigr)/u .
\]
The [leading-monomial minor argument](Yin_C_G0345_v462.md#minor) applies to these \(r\) target representatives.
The leading coefficients for paired columns involve differences of \(\varepsilon x_i^u\), which are nonzero because distinct root quotients are nontorsion.
A central column contributes \(1+\varepsilon x_i^u\ne0\).
Reorienting a representative as in that argument exchanges its numerator equation with a nonzero multiple on the kernel, by the first equation of (2).
Thus the augmented determinant is nonzero over the Laurent function field on every torsion class.
The [same nondegenerate recurrence specialization](Yin_C_G0345_v462.md#minor) gives eventual nonvanishing for this fixed \(u\).

<a id="exceptions"></a>
## Uniformly few exceptional displacements at each prime

All \(D_{p,a,\varepsilon}(u)\), considered as functions of the integer \(u\) with \(p\) fixed, lie in one fixed finite-dimensional space over the splitting field of \(G\).
Indeed the entries of (2) have their \(u\)-dependence in products of \(1,u,x^u\).
Expanding the determinants gives terms \(u^j t^u\), with \(j\le m\) and \(t\) from a finite set of algebraic units independent of \(p\).
The coefficients can vary with \(p\).

Choose any finite completion \(K_v\) of the splitting field.
There is a positive integer \(M\) such that every \(t^M\) is close enough to \(1\) at \(v\) for \(\exp(z\log(t^M))\) to be a convergent restricted power series on the entire valuation ring of \(K_v\).
This follows by first killing the finite residue orders and then raising to powers of the residue characteristic.
On each class \(u=j_0+Mz\), the terms interpolate as \((j_0+Mz)^j t^{j_0}\exp(z\log(t^M))\), also at negative integer arguments.
They span a fixed finite-dimensional space of convergent series over \(K_v\).

On such a space every nonzero series has at most a common finite number of zeros in the valuation ring.
Indeed, choose a basis and normalize a linear combination to have maximum coefficient modulus one in that basis.
The set of normalized coefficient tuples is compact since \(K_v\) is a locally compact field.
The maximum modulus of the power-series coefficients is continuous in the tuple and bounded away from zero, since the basis is independent.
The tails tend uniformly to zero over the normalized tuples, as they do for each of the finitely many basis elements.
Hence the last index attaining the maximum modulus is uniformly bounded.
The [closed-disk zero count](Yin_C_G0345_v422.md#disk), obtained by division at each zero, proves the assertion.

For each of the finitely many classes choose a single fixed nonzero \(u\) in it.
By the fixed-displacement argument all four determinants there are nonzero for every sufficiently large prime.
Thus their interpolating series on every class are then nonzero.
The uniform bound on zeros, summed over the classes and four determinants, shows that all but \(O_\alpha(1)\) integers in \((-p,p)\setminus\{0\}\) give full numerator rank for both targets and signs.
This bound uses no uniform zero estimate as the seed varies.

<a id="height"></a>
## A centered determinant estimate uniform over the full range of shifts

Fix a sufficiently large good prime for \(G\), and consider any displacement just retained.
At any good prime and \(p\nmid u\), the two integer kernels of (2) have rank \(r\), and reduce modulo \(p\) onto \(\bar G C_0\) for \(\deg C_0<r\), by the [shifted signed rank calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v465.md#shift).
It works for negative \(u\) by working with the same unit Laurent quotient.
The perfect pairing of the [two reduced quotients](Yin_C_G0345_v468.md#pairing) therefore applies at every such displacement, since the common factor \(X^u\) cancels from the determinant.
Every nonzero rational row of either lattice is genuine at both targets by the augmented determinants.

Let \(v_1\ge\cdots\ge v_{m/2}\) be the sorted absolute logarithms of the conjugate moduli at one representative of each inverse pair of \(G\).
Their sum is \(3h\) and \(v_1\le2h\).
In the signed matrix at an archimedean embedding put \(t_x=\log|x|\) at its oriented sites.
In column \(l\), the exponential part of the bound on either entry at that site is
\[
        \exp\!\left(\frac{pc+u}{2}t_x
                      +p\,\left|l-\frac{c+u/p}{2}\right|\,|t_x|\right).
\]
The remaining factors are \(O_c(p)\) uniformly in \(|u|<p\).
Each site occurs twice in a maximal minor.
The \(m\) largest distances of the integer columns \(0,\ldots,c\) from the shifted center occur in successive endpoint pairs.
Their \(j\)th pair consists of \((c-2j+2+|u|/p)/2\) and \((c-2j+2-|u|/p)/2\), since \(|u|/p<1\).
By decreasing rearrangement the two distances of this pair are charged to the same \(v_j\).
Their sum is independent of \(u\).
The signed linear term from the centers averages to zero over the embeddings by the unit product formula.
All entries are algebraic integers at finite places, including when \(u<0\).
The [primitive minor and successive-minimum bound](Yin_C_G0345_v462.md#norm) thus gives for either lattice
\[
 \log\prod_{i=1}^r\lambda_i
      \le p U_c+O_\alpha(\log(2p)),\qquad
 U_c=\sum_{j=1}^{m/2}(c-2j+2)v_j\le(3c-2)h ,
\]
where \(\lambda_i\) are its Euclidean successive minima.
The last inequality follows from \(\sum_{j\ge2}v_j\ge h\).
Each shortest row is genuine, and its one-norm costs at most \(\sqrt{c+1}\lambda_1\).
The [complementary minima estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v468.md#budgets) for the perfect pairing also supplies a genuine transverse pair whose product of one-norms is at most \((c+1)\bigl(\prod_{i=1}^r\lambda_i^+\lambda_i^-\bigr)^{1/r}\), using the successive minima of the two signed lattices.
Hence the individual rows can be taken with
\[
       \frac{\max(\log H_+,\log H_-)}p
            \le (15-10/e)h+O_\alpha(\log(2p)/p),
\]
and the transverse rows satisfy the same bound for \(\log(H_+H_-)/(2p)\).
At \(0<h\le(\log2)/120\) this gives a fixed strict margin at the stated budgets.

<a id="intervals"></a>
## Exact width and degree counts

For every \(k\ge1\) with \((k,Bp)=1\), the [radical substitution](Yin_C_G0345_v466.md#count) gives the distinct degree-\(ek\) minima \(P_1(X^k),P_2(X^k)\) of a unit and its square, with measures \(h,2h\).
Use the unique odd \(u\in(-p,p)\) congruent to \(k^{-1}\bmod p\), and take \(t=(ku-1)/p\).
The integer \(t\) is even and \(|t|\le k-1\) since \(|u|\le p-1\).
Put \(b=5ek/2-1=kc+k-1\) and \(w=(k-1-t)/2\).
Then \(0\le w\le k-1\).
From a shifted base row \(B_0\) form \(A(Y)=Y^w B_0(Y^k)\).
At width \(b\) its graph is the base graph at \(X^k\) times \(X^{pw}\), since \(1+p(b-2w-kc)=ku\).
The two numerators remain nonzero, and the common substitution and padding preserve the coefficient lists, double contacts and nonvanishing of the two-row determinant.
In fact the determinant is \(Y^{b-kc}\) times the base determinant at \(Y^k\).
The normalized span bound follows from the common affine map on the two supports.

At most \(O_\alpha(1)\) nonzero inverse classes encounter an exceptional displacement.
Each excluded class, including the multiples of \(p\), removes at most \(L/(ep)+1\) integers \(k\) from the interval \(X/e\le k<(X+L)/e\).
Before these exclusions the count with \((k,B)=1\) is \(\varphi(B)L/(eB)+O_B(1)\) by inclusion-exclusion.
The remaining degrees yield (1) with no comparison between \(p\) and \(L\).
For large \(p\) the leading coefficient in this lower bound stays uniformly positive, and a fixed sufficiently large \(L\) then guarantees at least one degree in every interval.
When the width cost is charged its normalized logarithm \(4\log(2pb+1)/p\) tends uniformly to zero under the displayed restriction on the interval, so the strict margin persists.
The conditional unrestricted conclusion follows because a positive measure below the threshold gives a fixed seed of at most that positive measure by the [irreducible reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#upper) and [all-power representative](Yin_C_G0345_v421.md#norm).

A collaborating agent for Sheng Yin developed the uniform displacement argument and the centered height and interval estimates while investigating exclusions across primes.
