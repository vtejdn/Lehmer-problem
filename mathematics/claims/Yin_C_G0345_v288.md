# The exact logarithmic discriminant deficit in the sub-plastic Salem family

<a id="statement"></a>
## Polynomial and trace discriminants

For the [Salem minimal polynomials](Yin_C_G0345_v286.md#statement)
\[
 P_h(Z)=\frac{Z^{60h+2}(Z^3-Z-1)-(1-Z^2-Z^3)}{Z^5-1},
 \qquad d=60h,\qquad h\to\infty,
\]
let \(\beta_h>1\) be the exterior root, let \(\theta>1\) be the plastic number, and write
\(P_h(Z)=Z^n S_h(Z+Z^{-1})\), \(n=d/2\).
Then
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \log|\operatorname{disc}P_h|
      &=(d-7)\log d+2d\log\theta+O(1),\\
 \log\operatorname{disc}S_h
      &=(n-4)\log(2n)+2n\log\theta+O(1).                           \tag{1}
 \end{aligned}
\]
Consequently
\[
          \frac{|\operatorname{disc}P_h|^{1/d}}d\longrightarrow\theta^2,
 \qquad
 d\log d+2(d-1)\log\beta_h-\log|\operatorname{disc}P_h|
                           =7\log d+O(1).                         \tag{2}
\]
These are polynomial discriminants.
Every subsequence satisfying the [unramified-prime and local congruence conditions](Yin_C_G0345_v287.md#statement) retains these asymptotics.
Thus the general Hadamard discriminant bound is asymptotically attained by actual integral Salem minimal polynomials with the absolute unramified windows constructed there.

<a id="phase"></a>
## The original completion has \(d+3\) unit-circle roots

Put \(f(Z)=Z^3-Z-1\), \(f^*(Z)=1-Z^2-Z^3\), and \(N=d+2\).
The cubic has two roots inside the unit disk and one at \(\theta>1\), and no unit-circle root.
Choose a smooth argument \(\gamma(t)\) of \(f(e^{it})\) on the real line.
The argument principle gives \(\gamma(t+2\pi)=\gamma(t)+4\pi\).
On the circle \(f^*(e^{it})=e^{3it}\overline{f(e^{it})}\), so the roots of the numerator \(Z^Nf-f^*\) are given there by
\[
                  (N-3)t+2\gamma(t)\in2\pi\mathbb Z.
\]
Its phase is \((N+1)t+b(t)\), where \(b(t)=2\gamma(t)-4t\) is a fixed smooth periodic function.
For all sufficiently large \(N\) the phase derivative is positive.
Its winding is \((N-3)+4=N+1=d+3\), giving exactly that many simple unit-circle roots.
After a cyclic labeling, their arguments differ from an equally spaced grid of \(d+3\) points by \(O(1/d)\).
For every fixed smooth function \(g\) on the circle, comparison to that grid and a Riemann sum therefore gives
\[
 \sum_{Z^Nf-f^*=0,\ |Z|=1}g(Z)
       =(d+3)\int_0^{2\pi}g(e^{it})\,\frac{dt}{2\pi}+O_g(1).        \tag{3}
\]
Removing the five fixed roots of \(Z^5-1\) leaves the \(d-2\) unit-circle roots of \(P_h\).

<a id="resultant"></a>
## Summing the degree-six resultant

Use the [exact discriminant formula](Yin_C_G0345_v287.md#discriminant), with
\[
 H_d=dff^*-V,\qquad V=2Z^6+3Z^5-5Z^3+3Z+2 .
\]
Uniformly on the unit circle,
\[
                     \log|H_d|=\log d+\log|ff^*|+O(1/d),
\]
since \(ff^*\) has no zero there.
The function \(g=\log|ff^*|\) is smooth, and its circle mean is \(2\log\theta\) by Jensen's formula.
Equation (3) and removal of the five fixed roots thus show
\[
       \sum_{P_h(z)=0,\ |z|=1}\log|H_d(z)|
                    =(d-2)\log d+2d\log\theta+O(1).                \tag{4}
\]

At the exterior root the completion equation is
\(f(\beta_h)=\beta_h^{-N}f^*(\beta_h)\).
The known monotone limit \(\beta_h\to\theta\), with \(\beta_h\ge\beta_1>1\), implies exponential convergence of \(f(\beta_h)\) to zero.
Since \(f'(\theta)\ne0\), the same holds for \(\theta-\beta_h\).
From \(W=ZV=(Z^2f)'(Z^5-1)-5Z^4(Z^2f)\) one gets
\[
                         V(\theta)=\theta f'(\theta)(\theta^5-1)>0 .
\]
Hence \(H_d(\beta_h)\to-V(\theta)\ne0\).
Reciprocity gives \(H_d(\beta_h^{-1})=\beta_h^{-6}H_d(\beta_h)\), so the two off-circle roots contribute only \(O(1)\) to the logarithmic resultant.
Combining with (4) yields
\[
            \log|\operatorname{Res}(P_h,H_d)|
                      =(d-2)\log d+2d\log\theta+O(1).
\]
The denominator in the exact formula has logarithm
\[
             \log(12h-1)+2\log(144h^2+36h+1)=5\log d+O(1).
\]
Subtraction proves the first line of (1).
The reciprocal discriminant identity and the exact endpoints give
\[
           |\operatorname{disc}P_h|
                     =(12h-1)(\operatorname{disc}S_h)^2.
\]
This proves the second line.
Finally \(d(\log\theta-\log\beta_h)\to0\) exponentially, which gives the Hadamard deficit in (2).
The coordinator proposed the discriminant saturation from the fixed-degree resultant, and the contributor derived the logarithmic deficit and trace form.
