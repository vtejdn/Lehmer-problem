## Complete argument

### LBX2. The full positive integer support and its block restriction

Write H=sum_(j=0)^h h_j z^j and let f_w in{0,1} be the binary prefix sum through offset w, with h_j=0 beyond h.
For h<=w<N one has f_w=1.
Use the actual integer polynomials

    F_N=sum_(w=0)^(N-1) f_w z^w,
    S_N^int=sum_(w=0)^(N-1)z^w,
    A_H=f_0+sum_(j=1)^h(f_j-f_(j-1))z^j.

Then A_Hbar=H and

    (1-z)F_N=A_H-z^N,
    (1-z)(S_N^int-F_N)=1-A_H.                 (LBX5)

Let E be the even block indices b with 0<=b<Q and binary digit 2^3 equal to zero.
The genuine integer positive-lift identity proved in C759 LB29 is

    W=sum_(b in E)z^(bN)[F_N+z^N(S_N^int-F_N)].

The unique binary-subset expansions of E give the additional INTEGER factorization

    (1-z)W=A_H(1-z^N)
             product_(j in{1,2,4,...,ell-1})(1+z^(2^j N)).
                                                  (LBX6)

No signs or coefficients of the lift have been inferred merely from a binary factorization: LBX6 follows by applying LBX5 to the displayed actual W.
At an arbitrary offset w, a supported block index is in E if f_w=1 and in E+1 if f_w=0.
Adding one to an even b in E produces no carry into its digit 2^3.
Consequently EVERY supported monomial of W has block index modulo16 in{0,1,...,7}, including the prefix and final partial blocks.
The same restriction holds for Z and for Z*, since W is reciprocal at ambient degree r.

### LBX3. Exact integer dyadic carry on every old root

Put

    Sigma_N=(z^N+1)/(z+1) in F_2[z].

This is the full squarefree polynomial of all nonidentity N-th roots and is coprime to z(z+1).
It is not an individual primitive cyclotomic factor.
The map z->z+z^(-1) has derivative 1+z^(-2), nonzero at every root of Sigma_N, so these old root valuations agree with their trace-primary valuations under substitution.
This includes every proper divisor order of a composite N.
The complete-row identity P_(B_N)=Sigma_N and gcd(B_N,Y_1 phi)=1 imply H is a unit at every one of these roots.

For an actual integer polynomial A define Delta_z(A)=[A(z^2)-A(z)^2]/2.
It is an integer polynomial and satisfies the binary product rule

    Delta_z(AB)bar=Abar^2 Delta_z(B)bar
                    +Bbar^2 Delta_z(A)bar.    (LBX7)

At any nonidentity N-th root zeta, the binary orders of the factors on the right side of LBX6 are 0,1 and 2^j, respectively; their sum is rho.
The integer calculations

    Delta_z(1+z^k)=-z^k,
    Delta_z(1-z^N)=z^N(1-z^N)

show that the product-rule term from the UNIQUE largest exponent 2^j=Q/2 has exact old order

    2(rho-Q/2)=Q-18.                          (LBX8)

Every smaller exponent gives strictly higher order; the A_H term has order at least2rho, and the 1-z^N term has order2rho-1.
The unique lowest term has nonzero leading coefficient, so there is no cancellation.
Applying LBX7 to (1-z)W, whose first factor is an old unit, shows

    ord_zeta Delta_z(W)bar=Q-18

at EVERY root of Sigma_N; the other product-rule term has order2rho.

Let P_+=W(1+z^(2n)) be the genuine positive reciprocal integer lift before any signs change.
Since gcd(n,N)=1, the factor 1+z^(2n) is a unit at every nonidentity N-th root, including roots of proper divisor order.
LBX7 therefore gives the exact, not merely lower, value

    ord_zeta Delta_z(P_+)bar=Q-18.            (LBX9)

This baseline lies strictly above30 and above ceil(rho/3).
It was obtained by the full integer support calculation, not by declaring Delta of an arbitrary lift to vanish.

### LBX4. Actual sign changes, monic remainders and exact old thresholds

Set R=Z+z^(2n)Z* as an actual integer polynomial, so P=P_+-2R.

    Delta_z(P)=Delta_z(P_+)-R(z^2)+2P_+R-2R^2,

    Delta_z(P)=z^(2d_out)Delta_T(z+z^(-1)),

    a_T=rem_T Delta_T.                        (LBX11)

The last equality is actual monic division over Z, using T(C_2)=TQ_2+2a_T, and is also the canonical trace interface of adopted C747 MP11--MP13.
Thus Delta_Tbar-a_Tbar is a multiple of U_out and has old order at least rho.
The unit z^(2d_out) and the unramified trace substitution preserve every order below rho, and preserve all old vanishing tests with threshold at most rho.
No divided identity in a binary quotient is being used.

As gcd(N,3)=gcd(N,5)=1, both maps zeta->zeta^3 and zeta->zeta^5 permute the full set of nonidentity N-th roots.
Hence B_N divides B_N(C_3bar) and B_N(C_5bar), and their rho-th powers retain the full multiplicity rho.

    b_Tbar=c_Tbar=0 modulo B_N^rho.

Since Q-18>ceil(rho/3), LBX9--LBX12 give the EXACT equivalences

       iff Sigma_N^ceil(rho/10) divides Rbar,

Indeed the first required alpha order is ceil(rho/5), the second is ceil(rho/3), and the sign term in LBX10 has twice the order of Rbar.

### LBX5. The finite cyclic quotient and its period16 error

Work in A_cyc=F_2[z]/(z^(16N)+1), put x=z^N and use x^16=1.
Every class has a unique representative

    sum_(w=0)^(N-1) z^w Z_w(x), deg Z_w<16.

The support conclusion in LBX2 gives deg Z_w<=7 for both the class of Zbar and that of Z*bar; repeated blocks may cancel but cannot introduce new residues.
We have N=1 modulo16, rho=7 modulo16, and

    0<Delta<N, N-Delta>=16,
    z^(2n)=z^Delta x^7 in A_cyc.             (LBX14)

For these inequalities, N-Delta=1+(2Bs-1)Delta and B>=120Q, s>=1, Delta>=1.
There is no asymptotic restriction beyond the already paid actual C759 threshold.
Write Z_w and Z_w^* for the two families of block polynomials.
The coefficient of offset w in Rbar is

    R_w=Z_w+x^7 Z^*_(w-Delta)       if Delta<=w<N,
    R_w=Z_w+x^8 Z^*_(w-Delta+N)     if 0<=w<Delta.
                                                  (LBX15)

The first line has degree at most14, so its x^15 coefficient is zero.

Assume Sigma_N^16 divides Rbar in F_2[z].
Because z^(16N)+1=(z+1)^16 Sigma_N^16, the canonical cyclic remainder is then uniquely of the form Sigma_N^16 E(z), deg E<16.
This assertion follows by dividing the multiplier of Sigma_N^16 by the monic (z+1)^16, so it does not cancel a zero divisor inside A_cyc.
In characteristic2,

    Sigma_N^16=sum_(j=0)^(N-1)z^(16j).

Thus the cyclic remainder's coefficient at z^j is e_(j mod16), where E=sum_(j=0)^15 e_j z^j: it is precisely16-periodic throughout degrees0 through16N-1.
For each w in[Delta,N-1], LBX15 makes the coefficient at z^(15N+w) zero.
Since N=1 modulo16 and this offset interval has length at least16, all16 values e_j vanish.
It follows that

    Rbar=0 in A_cyc.                          (LBX16)

This proves the required implication from the modulus Sigma_N^16 to the larger cyclic modulus; it does not confuse their root1 factors.

### LBX6. Reflection eliminates the cyclic sign correction

Equation LBX16 and the first line of LBX15, with both input polynomials of degree at most7, force Z_w to be supported only in degree7 and Z^*_(w-Delta) only in degree0.
For w<Delta, the low degrees0..7 and high degrees8..15 in LBX15 are disjoint, so both polynomials are zero.
Together, Zbar can occupy only block7 in A_cyc, and Z*bar only block0.
The latter block's nonzero offsets must be at most N-Delta-1, and the former block's offsets must be at least Delta.

Set c_r=h-1, so 0<=c_r<N and r=7N+c_r modulo16N.
Reflection of a block7 monomial z^(7N+w) is z^(c_r-w) in A_cyc.
If w<=c_r this lies in block0; if w>c_r it lies in block15 with offset N+c_r-w.
Reflection is a bijection on cyclic monomials and cannot cancel two distinct monomials.
Since Z*bar is confined to block0, Zbar's offsets must therefore satisfy w<=c_r.
The simultaneous requirement w>=Delta is impossible because Delta=c_r+2g+2>c_r.
Hence

    Zbar=Z*bar=0 in A_cyc.                    (LBX17)

This is only a cyclic congruence; the actual integer Z and Z* need not be zero.
Also Dbar=H(z)(z+1)^(rho-1)Sigma_N^rho is divisible by (z+1)^16 Sigma_N^16=z^(16N)+1, since rho-1>=16.
Finally the actual half identity S=W-Z-Z* gives

    Sbar=Dbar+Zbar+Z*bar=0 in A_cyc.

Combining this with LBX13 proves the general supported-sign necessary condition LBX3.

### LBX7. All eight C759 positions and simultaneous failure at one old primary

Let u in{0,1,2} and c_0 be exactly the fixed shift and constant of C759 LB3--LB5.

    (L_0-5)N+p_1, (L_0-5)N+p_1+1.           (LBX18)

    (rho-b-1)N+(N+h-1-p),

whose offset is again in[h,N-1].
Thus the four pairs of mask positions occupy respectively the block indices

    4, L_0-5=Q/2-10, rho-5=Q-14, L_0+5=Q/2.

Modulo16 these are 4,6,2,0, respectively.
Each block has two distinct adjacent offsets, and different displayed blocks remain different cyclic blocks.
Therefore all EIGHT mask monomials are distinct in A_cyc and in particular its block4 coefficient is nonzero.
The specified integer signs in C759 LB5 do not alter this binary mask.
Consequently Sbar is nonzero in A_cyc for every one of its free-sign outputs, contradicting LBX3 if either full old test were to pass.

For the sharper simultaneous conclusion, the same contradiction shows Sigma_N^16 does not divide Rbar.
At some root zeta of this full squarefree polynomial one thus has ord_zeta Rbar<=15.
The exact baseline Q-18>30 in LBX9 and the square term in LBX10 give

    ord_zeta Delta_z(P)bar=2ord_zeta Rbar<=30.

Let psi be the irreducible trace factor containing zeta+zeta^(-1).
Its multiplicity in U_out is exactly rho, so the true monic remainder and unramified identity LBX11 give ord_psi a_Tbar<=30 exactly, not just an order of a discarded numerator.
Since 150<rho and90<rho, LBX12 gives ord_psi F_Tbar<=150 and ord_psi X_(epsilon,T)bar<=90, both strictly below rho.
This proves LBX4 at the SAME old primary and for both second-residual signs.

### LBX8. Actual raised families and scope of the negative result

For every dyadic q>=1, the raised polynomial in C759 is precisely P(z^q), with own trace T_q=T(C_q).
The actual canonical covariance used in [C724 complete canonical covariance argument](Yin_C_G0724_v1.md#complete-argument) applies to arbitrary monic integer T, without an irreducibility premise.
For clarity, substituting C_q into the true division T(C_j)=TQ_j+j a_j and using C_j(C_q)=C_q(C_j) preserves the divisor and gives a_j(T_q)=a_j(T)(C_q), since the substituted remainder still has degree below deg T_q.
The same actual monic-division argument gives F_(T_q)=F_T(C_q) and X_(epsilon,T_q)=X_(epsilon,T)(C_q).
As C_qbar=X^q, a nonzero binary residual remains nonzero under this composition, and every old order and the old multiplicity are multiplied by q.
Thus the coefficient valuations remain zero and the full old row B_N^(rho q) fails for all raised members as well.

The obstruction uses all supported signs and the actual fixed mask, with no flip-budget restriction.
C759's proved old-X-primary and added-B_n^2 fibers remain nonempty, its canonical G remains nonzero, and its exact resultant and fixed-flip Mahler statements remain valid in their stated scopes.
Their members do not satisfy the additional full-old first-bit residual tests.
This conclusion gives neither a Mahler upper estimate nor a Task-level impossibility result, and does not decide whether another mask, support block or source extension can satisfy the missing old-primary equations.
