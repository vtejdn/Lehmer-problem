# A double root forces pencil roots into a specified hyperbolic disk

<a id="statement"></a>
## A weighted count at a conjugate

Let \(p>r\ge1\) be integers and \(U,V\) be coprime nonzero complex polynomials.
Suppose \(F(x)=U(x^p)-x^r V(x^p)\) has a double zero at \(\alpha\) with \(0<|\alpha|<1\), allowing higher multiplicity.
Put \(b=\alpha^p,\ w=\alpha^r,\ u=-\log|\alpha|\).
For \(|z|,|b|<1\) write \(\delta(b,z)=|(z-b)/(1-\bar b z)|\).
For \(|s|<1,\ 0<R\le1\), define
\[
 J(b,R;s)=\int_{\mathbb T}\frac{1-|s|^2}{|t-s|^2}
   \sum_{\substack{U(z)-tV(z)=0\\ |z|<1,\ \delta(b,z)<R}}
           \log\frac{R}{\delta(b,z)}\,d\sigma(t),
\]
using normalized circle measure and counting roots with multiplicity.
The center cannot occur in the sum since \(U(b)/V(b)=w\) and \(|w|<1\).
Define
\[
       D_b=\frac rp\,\frac{\sinh(pu)}{\sinh(ru)}>1 .
\]
Then
\[
                        J(b,R;w)\ \ge\ \max(0,\log(RD_b)).               \tag{1}
\]
For each \(R>1/D_b\), a positive-measure set of phases has at least one root in \(\delta(b,z)<R\).
Without the Poisson weight the same root integral is at least \(\frac{1-|w|}{1+|w|}\max(0,\log(RD_b))\).
These statements apply to the rows with the specified exponent difference \(r\).
Individual row monomial shifts must be included when writing that equation.

<a id="jensen"></a>
## Averaging Jensen's formula with a disk change of coordinates

For any \(|s|<1\) set \(A=U-sV,\ B=V-\bar s U\).
Use \(z=\psi_b(y)=(b+y)/(1+\bar b y)\) and put \(f_s(z)=\log\max(|A(z)|,|B(z)|)\).
Then
\[
    J(b,R;s)=\int_{\mathbb T} f_s(\psi_b(R\zeta))\,d\sigma(\zeta)-f_s(b).       \tag{2}
\]
Indeed the logarithmic mean of \(|A-\xi B|\) over \(|\xi|=1\) is \(f_s\) at each point by Jensen's formula for a linear polynomial in \(\xi\).
Its zeros in \(z\) are the zeros of \(U-tV\), where \(t=(s+\xi)/(1+\bar s\xi)\).
Changing the circle variable from \(\xi\) to \(t\) gives the density \((1-|s|^2)/|t-s|^2\), by differentiating the inverse circle map.
Apply [Jensen's centered formula](../audits/literature/Hay59/Yin_U001.md#hay59-u001) in \(y\) to \((A-\xi B)(\psi_b(y))\), which is analytic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk.
Its center value is nonzero for all \(\xi\), and its zeros inside \(|y|<R\) contribute the displayed logarithms.
Zeros on the bounding circle contribute zero by continuity of the logarithmic mean, also at \(R=1\).
The rows \(A,B\) have no common zero because the linear transformation is nonsingular.
The positive part of the logarithm is bounded on the two circles, and its phase average is the bounded continuous function \(f_s\).
These bounds give integrability on the product of the circles.
This proves (2), with finite nonnegative \(J\).

<a id="jet"></a>
## The derivative and the interpolation radius

The rational function \(H=U/V\) is regular at \(b\), because a zero of \(V(b)\) would force \(U(b)=0\).
Vanishing of \(F,F'\) at \(\alpha\) gives
\[
                  H(b)=w,\qquad H'(b)=\frac{r w}{p b}.
\]
Take \(s=w\).
The function \(A(\psi_b(y))\) vanishes simply at zero and its derivative there is \((1-|b|^2)V(b)H'(b)\).
On \(|y|=R\) its modulus is bounded above by \(\exp f_w(\psi_b(y))\).
Divide by \(y\), use the submean inequality at zero, and subtract \(f_w(b)=\log|(1-|w|^2)V(b)|\).
Equation (2) gives
\[
 J(b,R;w)\ge\log\left(R\frac{1-|b|^2}{1-|w|^2}|H'(b)|\right)=\log(RD_b).
\]
The strict inequality \(D_b>1\) follows because \(\sinh(v)/v\) is strictly increasing for \(v>0\).
The assertions about phases and the unweighted count follow from positivity and the upper bound \((1+|w|)/(1-|w|)\) on the density.
In particular, a double-root representation of this form cannot have every circle pencil member supported only on the circle.

<a id="several"></a>
## Several nodes with the same target value

Let \(b_i\) be distinct nodes in the disk with \(V(b_i)\ne0\) and values \(w_i=H(b_i)\) of modulus less than one.
At a center \(b=b_i\) with \(w=w_i\), let \(s\ne w\) be in the open unit disk.
The same identity and Jensen's formula for \(A(\psi_b(y))\) give
\[
 J(b,R;s)\ge \log\delta(s,w)+
       \sum_{\substack{j:\ w_j=s\\\delta(b,b_j)<R}}
                  \log\frac R{\delta(b,b_j)} .                          \tag{3}
\]
Indeed the center ratio \(|A(b)/B(b)|=\delta(s,w)<1\), and one may retain just the indicated zeros in the nonnegative Jensen sum.
At \(s=w\), for a center with the specified double-root data, the sum over the *other* nodes with \(w_j=w\) can likewise be added to \(\log(RD_b)\) in (1), by applying Jensen after division by the central zero.
In particular, a pair of distinct target values at nodes closer in disk distance than their target values forces a positive full-disk count in (3) at \(R=1\).
The inequalities describe localization and logarithmic multiplicity.
A lower bound on the total Dirichlet energy at the scale of the [finite cofactor test](Yin_C_G0345_v379.md#cofactor) would require an additional estimate.
