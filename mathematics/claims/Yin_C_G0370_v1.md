### 1. Original law, complete embeddings and precise normalization

The orbit_research AI for Yin read the entire [L342 arbitrary-support theorem and review](../ledger/Yin_L_G0342_v1.md#arbitrary-block-barrier-fixed-scope), its original [L335 covariance/minorization interface](../ledger/Yin_L_G0335_v1.md#returned-block-convolution-minorization), and [L173's separated-digit and general-unit interfaces](../ledger/Yin_L_G0173_v1.md#collision-operator-fixed-scope).
The actual new issue is the use of ALL expanding and contracting embeddings when there need not be a real expanding root or reciprocal automorphism.
The local mechanisms are sufficient.
Positive total measure for a nonzero nontorsion algebraic integer is the already accepted Kronecker statement, with its exact irreducible scope in [Smy08-U001, printed322](../audits/literature/Smy08/Yin_U001.md); it is reused at that recorded statement/inspection depth, not newly proved or source-audited here.
No new source, program, eigenvalue computation or finite search is used.
Root supplied only the generalization question.

Let alpha be ANY fixed algebraic unit which is not a root of unity.
Its ACTUAL minimal polynomial P is monic irreducible of degree d, with constant coefficient+/-1.
Write its complete distinct conjugates as alpha_1,...,alpha_d, and r_i=|alpha_i|.
All occurrences in products and sums over i below include both members of every complex-conjugate pair.
Put

    m=sum_(r_i>1) log r_i>0,
    q=#{i:r_i=1},
    D_i=1/(r_i-1) if r_i>1,
    D_i=1/(1-r_i) if r_i<1,
    C=product_(r_i!=1) D_i.                       (1)

Because the absolute norm of alpha is1, sum_i log r_i=0 and sum_(r_i<1)(-log r_i)=m.
There is at least one expanding AND one contracting conjugate.
No reciprocal pairing, Salem hypothesis, reality of alpha, or equality m=log|alpha| is assumed.
Neutral real conjugates could only be+/-1, which by irreducibility would make alpha torsion; hence all neutral conjugates are nonreal and q=2s for some integer s>=0.

For covariance use the unweighted real Minkowski coordinates: one coordinate sigma(x) at each real embedding and the two coordinates Re sigma(x),Im sigma(x) at ONE representative of each complex-conjugate pair.
Denote this embedding map by Phi (extended real-linearly on K tensor_Q R when needed), and its neutral subvector by Z(x) in R^q.
Thus ||Z(x)||^2 is the sum of |sigma(x)|^2 over s neutral representatives, not the doubled sum over both roots.
Let V(x) be its nonneutral subvector and define

    C_non=sum_(nonneutral representatives i) D_i^2.             (2)

The field norm always counts complex embeddings TWICE, despite this unweighted covariance convention.
These two conventions are kept separate.
For q=0 the neutral vector is the zero-dimensional vector and its covariance trace is0.
The quartet convention in L342 is recovered exactly when s=1.

Fix N=2n,n>=1, Omega={0,...,2n-1}, with independent ORIGINAL digits eta_j having probabilities(1/4,1/2,1/4) at(-1,0,1).
For any I subset Omega let

    U_I=sum_(j in I) eta_j alpha^j,
    Y_I=-alpha^(-n) sum_(j in I,j<n) eta_j alpha^j,
    p_I=Pr(U_I=0), g(I)=min({n-1-j:j in I,j<n} union {j-n:j in I,j>=n}).
                                                               (3)

The last definition is for nonempty I.
Under U_I=0 the second, equally exact expression is Y_I=sum_(j in I,j>=n)eta_j alpha^(j-n), at EVERY embedding.
The unit assumption makes Y_I an algebraic integer even in its low-exponent expression.
Empty or one-sided sets have Y_I=0 on their own return.
Every p_I is positive by the all-zero word.

For the whole set, Y=Y_Omega conditioned on U_Omega=0 is exactly the ORIGINAL midpoint of the affine recursion x_(j+1)=alpha x_j+eta_j on a length2n zero-return word.
To see this without a reciprocal automorphism, reverse the labels of the independent digits: the chronological midpoint uses the high half of the final power polynomial, shifted down by alpha^n.
The high expression above is exactly that half.
The full return identifies it with the negative shifted low half.
Identical digit probabilities justify the relabeling; no conjugate is exchanged and no new conditioned law is introduced.
Simultaneous sign reversal centers each Phi(Y_I) on its own return and centers the full midpoint on its return.

For any collection of pairwise disjoint nonempty supports I, define

    C_n^0=sum_I p_I Cov(Phi(Y_I) | U_I=0).         (4)

The supports may be arbitrary, sparse, interlaced or depend on n.
This is a positive semidefinite LOWER certificate for the actual return-conditioned covariance, by the complete conditional-endpoint argument recalled in Section5.
The new theorem gives a fixed-input upper bound on THIS certificate, not an upper bound on the actual neutral covariance.

### 2. Sparse original atom bounds at a possibly nonreal place

Let r_* be the largest expanding modulus and choose the integer

    k=min{j>=1:r_*^j>2}, a=(log2)/k>0.            (5)

For a support of cardinality l>=1, select the first, (k+1)st, (2k+1)st,... positions in its increasing exponent list.
There are ceil(l/k) of them, with exponent gaps at least k.
Evaluate at a conjugate of modulus r_*, which may be nonreal.
If two selected binary sums differ, their largest differing exponent e gives a term of modulus r_*^e; all earlier terms together have modulus at most r_*^e/(r_*^k-1), strictly smaller.
The triangle inequality therefore proves injectivity.
Phases cannot invalidate this modulus dominance.
This is the exact changed-place extension of the already retained separated-digit method, not a real-order comparison in disguise.

Conditioning on all unselected fair bits bounds the maximum atom of the COMPLETE binary endpoint distribution by2^(-ceil(l/k)).
Averaging over those conditioned bits retains that bound.
The original ternary endpoint law is the difference of two independent copies of the binary endpoint, so its mass at zero is their collision probability, at most the largest binary atom.
Consequently

    p_I<=2^(-ceil(l/k))<=exp(-a l).               (6)

The selected binary subcube is injective; the full binary or ternary cube need not be.
All multiplicities in their actual endpoint fibers remain.
Empty supports separately have p_empty=1 and zero certificate contribution.
No entropy limit, stationary profile or real expanding root enters (6).

At a neutral representative, either half of the returned word represents the same Y_I with coefficients of modulus1, so |sigma(Y_I)|<=min(l_low,l_high)<=l/2.
Centering and the unweighted convention give

    tr Cov(Z(Y_I) | U_I=0)<=s l^2/4.              (7)

For s=0 this term is exactly0.
For positive s, (6)--(7) already pay the complete original probability as a function of cardinality, irrespective of span.

### 3. Full field norm and every nonneutral place

For g=g(I), the low representation at EVERY expanding conjugate gives

    |sigma_i(Y_I)|<=r_i^(-g)/(r_i-1)=D_i r_i^(-g).

The high representation at EVERY contracting conjugate gives |sigma_i(Y_I)|<=r_i^g/(1-r_i)=D_i r_i^g.
These follow by summing the full geometric tail, so missing positions only improve the bound; no real positivity or reciprocity is used.
Multiplying over ALL nonneutral roots gives C exp(-2mg) by(1).

If an internally returned word has Y_I!=0, its field norm is a nonzero integer.
The bound at each of the q neutral roots is l/2, with complex partners counted separately in the norm.
Hence

    1<=|Norm(Y_I)|<=C exp(-2mg) (l/2)^q.           (8)

There are two genuinely different cases.

If q>0, define c=C^(-1/q)>0 and rho=exp(2m/q)>1.
Then every contributing block must satisfy

    l>=2c rho^g.                                 (9)

A positive neutral covariance term means some returned word has a nonzero neutral image and therefore Y_I!=0, so(8) applies.
Conversely a nonzero field element cannot vanish under a field embedding.
This justifies the implication from a positive covariance contribution to(9); it is not a condition on all unrestricted words.

If q=0, a nonzero internally returned midpoint can exist only when C exp(-2mg)>=1.
If C<1 none exists at any closest distance.
If C>=1 it requires g<=floor(log C/(2m)).
This finite arithmetic cut-distance conclusion is independent of l.
It must not be obtained by dividing by q in(9).
Neutral covariance is absent in this case, while the full nonneutral observable has the deterministic bounds above.

In fact for the WHOLE returned word, g=0 and these same geometric estimates yield

    tr Cov(V(Y) | U_Omega=0)<=C_non.              (10)

Thus the actual nonneutral second moment itself is uniformly bounded at this fixed input.
This is not asserted for the neutral part.
It is useful below because every valid lower certificate has its nonneutral trace at most(10), regardless of conditioning amplification.

### 4. Complete fixed-input bound and explicit distant tail

**Theorem 1.** If q>0, every disjoint-support certificate(4) satisfies

    tr C_n^0 <= C_non + K(a),
    K(delta)= [8s/(delta^2 exp(2))]
               exp(-delta c)/(1-exp[-delta c(rho-1)]), delta>0. (11)

Its neutral contribution from supports with g(I)>=G, for any integer G>=0, is at most

    K_G(delta)= [8s/(delta^2 exp(2))]
       exp(-delta c rho^G)/(1-exp[-delta c rho^G(rho-1)]),
       evaluated at delta=a.                      (12)

In particular the neutral distant-support tail decreases doubly exponentially in G.
If q=0, the full certificate is instead bounded by C_non directly.
The same conclusions hold for convex averages of valid disjoint collections.
They do not authorize adding arbitrary overlapping inequalities without weights.

*Proof.* Assign each block one coordinate achieving its closest-cut distance, with a fixed rule for ties.
Disjoint blocks receive distinct coordinates, and at distance g only n-1-g and n+g are available.
Thus at most TWO blocks occur at each g, regardless of span, interlacing or cardinality.

By(6)--(7), a block's neutral trace contribution is at most s(l^2/4)exp(-a l).
For any delta>0 the elementary maximum of(l^2/4)exp(-delta l/2) over real l>=0 equals4/[delta^2 exp(2)], at l=4/delta.
Factoring the exponential into halves and using(9) therefore bounds each nonzero term by[4s/(a^2 exp(2))] exp(-a c rho^g).
Sum at most two per distance.
The elementary binomial inequality rho^g>=[1+g(rho-1)] gives exactly K(a).
Starting at G and writing g=G+h instead yields (12).
This proof includes all n, small l and absent distances, without any approximate constants or integrality of c rho^g.

For the nonneutral part, the positive-semidefinite lower-certificate inequality proved in Section5 implies that its trace is at most the actual nonneutral trace(10).
This introduces no circular dependence: that covariance inequality follows solely from independent original digit families conditioned on their endpoints, without using(11).
Adding the two diagonal-block traces gives the full real covariance trace.
Off-diagonal neutral/nonneutral entries do not alter trace and no claim that those entries vanish is made.
For q=0 there is only the already bounded nonneutral trace.
Convex averaging preserves every scalar bound.

If a full distant-support estimate is wanted, its nonneutral part has the additional explicit upper bound

    2 sum_(nonneutral representatives i)
       D_i^2 exp(-2|log r_i|G)/(1-exp[-2|log r_i|]).             (13)

Each conditional zero-block covariance is bounded by its deterministic squared geometric coordinate bound, its probability coefficient is at most1, and at most two blocks occur per g.
Summing gives(13).
Thus the FULL distant-support tail need only be exponential, while the neutral tail is doubly exponential.
The distinction is deliberate; the proof never calls the entire covariance tail doubly exponential in the presence of nonneutral coordinates.

All constants depend on the complete ACTUAL input: d, every r_i, m, q, the chosen k, and the denominators r_i-1 or1-r_i.
They can deteriorate as off-circle roots approach the circle, as m/q decreases or as k increases.
This theorem supplies fixed-input finiteness, not a degree-independent or near-one bound.

### 5. Exact conditioning and the necessary amplification cost

Let J=Omega\I and p=p_Omega.
The exact original block-return weight under the whole return is

    alpha_I=p_J/p,
    q_I=Pr(U_I=0 | U_Omega=0)=p_I alpha_I,
    1<=alpha_I<=2^l, 0<q_I<=1.                   (14)

The complement endpoint law is the binary autocorrelation on that PARTICULAR gapped support, so Cauchy--Schwarz makes its maximum occur at zero.
Convolution with the other endpoint law gives p<=p_J.
Conversely the subevent in which all I digits are zero and J returns has mass2^(-l)p_J, giving the other inequality.
No replacement by a chronological return p_(2n-l) or by a critical exponential approximation is made.

For any disjoint collection, the full law of conditional covariance gives

    Cov(Phi(Y) | U_Omega=0)>=C_n^q,
    C_n^q=sum_I q_I Cov(Phi(Y_I) | U_I=0)>=C_n^0.              (15)

Indeed condition on the endpoints of all blocks and of their complement.
The original digit families are independent; given their own endpoints their internal words remain independent.
Whole return is then already determined by these endpoints.
The conditional covariance of the sum is additive.
Average and keep in each term only the block's zero endpoint; all omitted covariance terms and the covariance of conditional means are positive semidefinite.
The exact weight of the kept term is q_I.
This proof does not assume the blocks remain independent under whole return alone.
Sign reversal centers their zero-conditioned observables, but other endpoint-conditioned means need not vanish.

**Theorem 2.** Suppose q>0 and constants A>=1,0<=gamma<a satisfy alpha_I<=A exp(gamma l) on EVERY contributing neutral block in a chosen collection sequence.
Put delta=a-gamma>0.
Then

    tr C_n^q <= C_non+A K(delta),                 (16)

uniformly in n, and its neutral g>=G tail is at most A K_G(delta).
Formula(13) still bounds the nonneutral distant tail without the factor A, because q_I<=1.
The proof is exactly the scalar envelope in Section4 with p_I alpha_I<=A exp(-delta l); every other norm/distance fact is unchanged.
For q=0, (15) and(10) already give tr C_n^q<=C_non for every possible amplification, with no hypothesis like(16).

Consequently if the traces of these refined zero-block certificates are unbounded at a fixed input, q must be positive and there must be contributing blocks with l tending to infinity and

    limsup log(alpha_I)/l >= a=(log2)/k.          (17)

Here the limsup is over arbitrarily long contributing blocks appearing in that sequence.
If it were smaller, choose one gamma<a bounding all sufficiently long blocks.
Bounded lengths are covered by one finite A using alpha_I<=2^l.
Equation(16) would give a contradiction.
If lengths were bounded from the start the same argument applies directly.
This is a necessary amplification payment, not a sufficient lower-moment criterion and not an assertion about every block or every n.

The actual whole-block choice I=Omega has alpha_I=1/p_(2n), q_I=1, and its refined certificate is exactly the UNKNOWN actual full midpoint covariance.
From(6), log alpha_I/(2n)>=a, and the retained fixed-input collision-entropy limit in L173 has h_2>=a.
Thus the threshold in(17) is already compatible with a tautological actual construction; crossing it alone yields no growth estimate.
No equality between the critical R, one conjugate modulus or exp(m) has been used.

For completeness the exact integer-fiber form remains available at every general unit.
For any support A, let c_A(0) be the sum of2^(number of zero digits) over ALL ternary words on A with U_A=0.
Let T_I be the same complete weighted sum of ||Z(Y_I)||^2 over I's zero fiber.
Then

    p_A=4^(-|A|) c_A(0),
    alpha_I=4^l c_J(0)/c_Omega(0),
    tr(neutral part of C_n^q)=
         sum_I c_J(0) T_I / c_Omega(0).            (18)

The first and last integer fibers include every original multiplicity.
The moment T_I is a nonnegative REAL algebraic number in the selected embedding convention, not generally a rational integer.
Formula(18) is exact, with no numerical approximation, surrogate walk or free conditioning factor.
It identifies a possible place for a genuinely stronger lower estimate, which has not been supplied here.
