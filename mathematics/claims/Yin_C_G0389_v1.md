## Trace regularity and the Galois field interface

The complete selected pair-kernel proof identifies V as the kernel of G on all inverse pairs and its fixed field as the splitting field of the trace polynomial.
For J=Gal(L/F), the stabilizer of the selected inverse pair is J and V is the kernel of the coset action on G/J.
The faithful quotient H=G/V acts regularly exactly when its point stabilizer J/V is trivial, that is, J=V.
This implies F=L^J=L^V is Galois over Q.
Conversely if F/Q is Galois, J is normal in G, so the kernel of the coset action is J itself and V=J; hence H is regular.
This spells out the stated fixed-interface equivalence without adding a split-extension or new arithmetic hypothesis.
