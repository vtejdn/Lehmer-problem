## Statement

Put phi=X^2+X+1 and S_N=F_2[X]/(phi^N).
For each odd n>1 let W_n be the monic product of all primitive n-th-root traces in an algebraic closure of F_2, one trace per inverse pair.
Let G_N be the multiplicative subgroup of S_N^* generated by every W_n with n!=5, and put E_N=<G_N,X>.
Every such W_n is a unit at phi.
The endpoint X is kept separately and is not one of the W_n generators.
Because these ambient groups are finite, their generated subgroup images are also exactly their images under products with nonnegative integer multiplicities.

On S_8^* retain the adopted character

    lambda_8(u)=[X^13](u'/u)+[X^15](u'/u),

where the quotient is reduced to degree below16.
On S_16^* put lambda_16=lambda_8 composed with reduction to S_8^*.
Then the following are exact assertions at the stated two precisions:

    G_8=ker lambda_8,      |G_8|=24576,
    G_16=ker lambda_16,    |G_16|=1610612736,
    E_8=S_8^*,            |E_8|=49152,
    E_16=S_16^*,          |E_16|=3221225472.       (FS1)

In particular these complete-group images have index2, with precisely the inherited character obstruction.
No assertion at every N follows from (FS1).

The square map Fr:S_8^* -> S_16^*, u -> u^2, is well-defined and injective.
One has

    Fr(S_8^*) subset G_16,
    [G_16 intersect Fr(S_8^*):Fr(G_8)]=2.       (FS2)

Thus the proposed complete-group square-saturation equality is false.
For the endpoint-extended groups the analogous equality holds because both are already entire unit groups.

There is an explicit monic complete-group polynomial Y of degree2813, prime to X phi and globally nonsquare, with

    Y=X^2 modulo phi^16,
    unique square root of [Y] in S_8^* = X,
    X not in G_8.                              (FS3)

Its exact nonnegative multiplicities, polynomial factor product and complete finite congruence certificate appear below.
It has endpoint exponent0; its local square root nevertheless has the nonzero endpoint character.
This is a local square-root failure inside the complete-group image, not a global square-root construction.

The same inherited character also kills every class 1+phi^4 h in S_8^*, so it descends to a character of S_4^*.
This character fact uses an exact derivative identity and no further finite quotient calculation.
No minimal-conductor assertion is made.

The result supplies neither an actual full normalized A_0>=4 gate seed nor an exclusion of every such seed.
It does not construct the same actual monic T, canonical R_0, complement B, Gamma modulo8, second residual, first-error compatibility or original five-layer integers required by C199/C603.

## Argument

### The all-order generator reduction

Let B_1=1 and, for odd n>1, let B_n=product_(d|n,d>1)W_d.
The full Laurent identity is

    B_n(z+z^(-1))=z^(-(n-1)/2)(z^n+1)/(z+1).

It gives B_3=X+1 and B_(n+2)=X B_n+B_(n-2), with the matrix A=[[X,1],[1,0]].
These are the exact whole-trace interfaces of [C608](Yin_C_G0608_v1.md#argument).
Direct multiplication gives

    A^5-I=phi[phi A+(X+1)I].

As all matrices in this identity commute, characteristic-two powering yields A^80-I divisible by phi^16 and A^160-I divisible by phi^32.
The sequence step advances the odd index n by2.
Consequently

    B_(n+160)=B_n modulo phi^16,
    B_(n+320)=B_n modulo phi^17.                (FS4)

For 5 not dividing n the first congruence covers all B_n by the64 odd residues 1<=n<160 with 5 not dividing n.
For 5 dividing n, distinct odd-order roots show B_n has exactly one complete order5 factor phi and no repeated irreducible factor.
Put D_n=B_n/phi, a polynomial unit at phi.
Dividing the SECOND congruence by phi before reduction gives D_(n+320)=D_n modulo phi^16.
Thus n=5,15,...,315 gives all32 divided classes.
No inverse of phi is used in a ring where it is nilpotent.

For every odd n divisible by5 one has D_n=product_(d|n,d>1,d!=5)W_d.
The96 displayed B and D classes are therefore products of the allowed W_n.
Conversely induction on the divisors of n isolates W_n from B_n when 5 does not divide n and from D_n otherwise, with W_5 omitted in the latter case.
This proves that their generated subgroup is EXACTLY G_16, including all orders divisible by arbitrarily high powers of5.
Projection proves the same96 classes generate exactly G_8.
This is an all-order periodicity reduction at two fixed precisions, not an order cutoff or numerical extrapolation.

### Filtered certificates and exact image size

The ring S_N has4^N elements and its unit group has3*4^(N-1) elements.
For N=8 or16, t=(X+1)^N has order3, belongs to G_N and maps onto the residue-field unit group.
Every remaining generator is normalized by a power of t to lie in 1+phi S_N.
The filtration quotients (1+phi^j S_N)/(1+phi^(j+1) S_N) are additive F_4 for 1<=j<N.
Use the residue basis1,X in this order, and label its two binary positions by2(j-1),2(j-1)+1.
A principal pivot has zero earlier bits and a1 in its indicated first bit.

J123 inserts the96 generators and, for the endpoint groups, X, using at most2(N-1) pivots.
Canceling the first available pivot by its inverse moves to a strictly later bit.
When a pivot is newly installed its square is queued once.
There are at most97+30 insertions and30 cancellations per insertion in each of the four runs.
There is no group-state enumeration.
Every retained pivot has a nonnegative exponent vector modulo48 in the original generators, since S_16^* has exponent48 and this exponent also kills S_8^*.

The retained square relations make binary pivot normal forms closed: the group is abelian and carrying an exponent2 replaces a pivot square by factors with later first bits.
Distinct binary products of the pivots are distinct because their earliest differing bit cannot cancel.

### The character descends and the actual gate remains

For every class h in S_8, the unit u=1+phi^4h satisfies

    u'/u=phi^4 h' modulo phi^8,

because (phi^4)'=0 and the next term in the inverse expansion carries phi^8.
A polynomial derivative over F_2 has only even powers ofX, and phi^4 also has only even powers.
Thus its X^13 and X^15 coefficients vanish.
The character kills the kernel of S_8^* -> S_4^*, proving the asserted descent without claiming a minimal conductor or a new all-depth image formula.

The complete [C199v4 actual gate](Yin_C_G0199_v4.md#normalized-common-frobenius-unit-gate) involves the same canonical H_0,R_0,V_0,C_00, the original q and actual five-layer multiplicities, not a freely chosen residue of V_0.
Its [full complement](Yin_C_G0199_v4.md#normalized-low-degree-complement) has deg B<2A_0 and may differ from the special A_0=2 complement; no fixed endpoint monomial is imposed here for A_0>=4.
The current counterexample refutes the proposed abstract complete-group square-saturation lemma and shows that at precisions8 and16 the character is the entire complete-group obstruction.
