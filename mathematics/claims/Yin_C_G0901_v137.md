# A uniform Mahler gap after adjoining trace-comparison square roots from infinitely many Salem fields

<a id="statement"></a>
## Simultaneous adjunction below the real endpoint

Write \(T=\mathbb Q^{\rm tr}\) for the field of totally real numbers and \(V=\mathbb Q^{\rm sol}\) for the rational solvable closure in a fixed algebraic closure inside \(\mathbb C\).
Fix \(M>0\) and take any family of pairwise unmatched real Salem numbers \(\alpha>1\) with \(\log\alpha\le M\).
Unmatched means that nonzero powers of conjugates cannot agree up to torsion.
The family may include all sufficiently large primitive positive Salem numbers of bounded measure satisfying the following field condition.
For each source write \(2n=\deg\alpha\), and take its \(u\) canonical blocks of \(b=n/u\) inverse-pair labels, at degrees sufficiently large in terms of \(M\).
Assume that the normal field of the permutation action on these \(u\) blocks is solvable over \(\mathbb Q\).
This includes the single-block case and permits unbounded degrees and numbers of sources.

For each source let \(x_i\) be one root from each inverse pair, and put \(t_{i,h}=x_i^h+x_i^{-h}\) for \(h\ge1\).
All these traces belong to \(T\).
For a sufficiently large absolute constant \(C\), set
\[
                      \Delta_\alpha=C\frac{1+M+u(\log(2b)+4)}b .
\]
Let \(W\) be the field generated over \(TV\) by all
\[
                \sqrt{t_{i,h}-\lambda},\qquad
     \lambda\in V\cap T,\qquad \max_\sigma\sigma\lambda\le2-h^2\Delta_\alpha ,
                   \qquad i=1,\ldots,n,\quad h\ge1,                      \tag{1}
\]
omitting zero radicands.
Then \(W\) has a uniform positive Mahler gap for all nonzero algebraic numbers of positive total logarithmic measure.
More precisely, for each \(N>0\) its algebraic units with \(0<m(w)\le N\) have bounded degree after taking the product over one conjugate class modulo roots of unity.
The degree bound depends only on \(M,N\), independently of the family.
In particular only finitely many positive unit measures up to \(N\) can occur, even across the different families at fixed \(M\).
The conclusion applies to the inputs themselves, with no assumption on their second iterates.

At \(h=1\), (1) permits the conjugates of all nontrivial cyclotomic traces of conductor \(3\le p\ll\sqrt{b/(1+M+u\log(2b))}\) with a sufficiently small absolute implied constant.
Here a trace of conductor \(p\) means \(\zeta+\zeta^{-1}\) for \(\zeta\) of exact order \(p\).
Thus even the simultaneous adjunction of the radicals giving the [full-occupancy rank formula](Yin_C_G0901_v129.md#statement) at these prime conductors preserves a gap.
The coefficient field \(TV\) and the conditions on the sources and thresholds are part of the conclusion; this gives no positive gap on all algebraic numbers without a field restriction.

<a id="signs"></a>
## Quadratic characters over the totally real field

The field \(W\) is rationally normal because (1) is stable under conjugation and both base fields are normal.
It is contained in the solvable closure of \(T\): the compositum with each finite normal solvable subfield of \(V\) is solvable over \(T\), and the other generators are square roots of elements of \(T\).
If \(d\in T^\times\) has a square root in \(W\), then
\[
        d=c\,s^2\prod_{j=1}^{k}a_j,qquad
          c\in(V\cap T)^\times,\quad s\in T^\times ,                    \tag{2}
\]
for finitely many of the radicands \(a_j\) in (1).
Indeed elementary multiquadratic theory over \(TV\) first gives \(z=d/\prod a_j\in T\) square in \(TV\).
Its square root belongs to \(TL\) for some finite rational normal solvable \(L\subset V\).
If this root is outside \(T\), the quadratic field it generates over \(T\) corresponds under restriction to a quadratic subextension of \(L/(L\cap T)\).
Writing that subextension as \((L\cap T)(\sqrt c)\) gives \(z/c\) square in \(T\); if \(z\) was already square take \(c=1\).
In particular the sign of \(g(d)\) for each rational absolute automorphism \(g\) is the sign of \(g(c\prod a_j)\), since every conjugate of \(s\) is nonzero and real.

Let \(K\) be \(V\) times the compositum of the full rational splitting fields of all sources.
Given a ceiling \(N\) on a target, one can uniformly ensure the following consequence of the [unmatched-field theorem](Yin_C_G0901_v106.md#statement).
For an unmatched target of sufficiently large compressed degree and measure at most \(N\), all the independently supported alternating groups of its label action lift to automorphisms fixing \(K\).
For a source of sufficiently large degree the same assertion holds on fixing \(V\) and all the *other* source splitting fields.
To check the thresholds, remove sources of degree below the source threshold of that theorem at bounds \(M,\max(M,N)\).
Absorb their splitting fields into the external base with \(V\).
For uniformity one may absorb all Salem inputs of these bounded degrees and bounded measure, a finite list by coefficient finiteness.
The remaining source block permutation fields lie in \(V\) by hypothesis.
The target label field cannot lie in this enlarged external base at sufficiently large target degree: the latter is a finite fixed Galois field times \(V\) and its finite normal quotients have nonabelian simple composition factors only from that fixed field.
The target label field has \(A_b\) as a composition factor with \(b\to\infty\) by the [canonical block theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#statement).
The last assertion of v106 therefore gives the desired lifts.
For a source target apply it to the family with this source omitted; all its other members are unmatched.
The same reasoning works for an empty remaining family.

<a id="source"></a>
## A comparison cannot distinguish the marked root from two neutral roots

Consider a sufficiently large source, and mark the label corresponding to the unique exterior root.
In its own block there are two other labels represented by upper neutral roots \(e^{i\theta}\) with
\[
                           0<\theta^2<\Delta_\alpha .                      \tag{3}
\]
For detail put \(v=1+M+u(\log(2b)+4)\) and choose \(r=C_1\sqrt{v/b}\) with a sufficiently large absolute constant \(C_1\).
The canonical upper bound on \(u\) implies \(r\to0\) uniformly at fixed \(M\).
Apply the [individual block disk estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v539.md#statement) to the disk of radius \(r\) about \(e^{3ir}\).
Its proportion of the \(2b\) actual roots is at least
\[
     \frac r{4\pi}-\frac1{2br}
                      -\sqrt{\frac{2M+u(\log(2b)+4)}{4b}}
                     \ \ge\ \frac r{8\pi}
\]
for \(C_1\) large.
The disk lies in the upper half-plane for small \(r\), and its circle points have angles between zero and \(5r\) by the chord formula.
It contains many distinct neutral labels, since \(br\to\infty\).
Taking \(C>25C_1^2\) proves (3).
The conductor statement follows from \(2-2\cos(2\pi/p)=4\sin^2(\pi/p)\gg1/p^2\).

Take a supported three-cycle on the marked label and two such neutral labels.
Lift it to an absolute automorphism \(g\) fixing \(V\) and every other source splitting field as above.
On all three labels, for every \(h\ge1\), the trace \(t_{i,h}\) is strictly above \(2-h^2\Delta_\alpha\): at the marked label it exceeds two and at the others use \(2\cos(h\theta)\ge2-h^2\theta^2\).
Consequently the three-cycle preserves the sign of every radicand (1) at the chosen real embedding.
It fixes all thresholds and scalars from \(V\), and leaves all the other label traces of this source fixed.
Yet \(d=(\alpha+\alpha^{-1})^2-4\) changes from positive to negative under this cycle.
Formula (2) excludes \(\sqrt d\) and hence \(\alpha\) from \(W\).
This comparison only needs to preserve signs of the radicands, not their values or their square classes separately.

The threshold restriction cannot simply be removed.
Choose a rational \(\lambda\) strictly between the largest neutral conjugate trace of this source and two.
The elements \(t_{1,1}-\lambda\) and \(d\) have identical signs at every embedding, so their quotient is totally positive and has a totally real square root.
Thus \(T(\sqrt{t_{1,1}-\lambda})=T(\alpha)\).
For this single targeted threshold the original Salem generator is recovered; this observation makes no assertion about an unrestricted sequence of conductors.

<a id="gap"></a>
## All small units in the resulting field

Fix \(N>0\) and take a nontorsion algebraic unit \(w\in W\) with \(m(w)\le N\).
Its [conjugate-class product](Yin_C_G0345_v264.md#labels) \(y\) belongs to \(W\) by normality, has the same total measure and has distinct conjugates modulo torsion.
Call its degree \(d\) and suppose it is sufficiently large depending on \(M,N\).
Let \(P_y\) be the rational label field.
Since \(W\subseteq T^{\rm sol}\), the [relative field criterion at level zero](Yin_C_G0901_v108.md#statement) gives \(P_y\subseteq T\) in this large-degree range at fixed \(N\).
Indeed the relative block-orbit size is always at least one, so the other alternative for level zero is impossible.
At the chosen absolute complex conjugation \(c_0\), each label is fixed since \(P_y\subseteq T\).
If a root \(z\) is nonreal, \(\bar z\ne z\) belongs to its label.
Their quotient cannot be torsion by compression, so their product must be torsion by definition of the inverse-pair label.
But \(z\bar z>0\), forcing \(z\bar z=1\).
Such a root and its inverse are actual conjugates, so its irreducible minimum is reciprocal.
If all the roots are real, the [real-conjugate height bound](Yin_C_G0901_v65.md#statement) gives \(m(y)>d/(100\log8)\), contradicting bounded measure at large degree.
This leaves a reciprocal target with only real nonneutral roots and totally real trace field.

If \(y\) matches a sufficiently large source, transport a matched pair to a conjugate of \(y\) of modulus greater than one.
Its matching source conjugate, having modulus different from one, is the real exterior or interior conjugate of that Salem source.
Both are real, so the nonzero power equality modulo torsion becomes an equality of powers up to rational sign.
Compression makes each nonzero power of \(y\) have full degree, and every nonzero power of the Salem source generates its field.
The two numbers would therefore generate the same real field, putting the prohibited source in \(W\).
Equality of stabilizers of proportional nonzero classes gives equality of compressed degrees for any match, so targets at unbounded \(d\) cannot match one of the discarded bounded-degree sources.

Now suppose \(y\) is unmatched.
For each inverse pair let \(v_i=y_i+y_i^{-1}\in T\).
Then \(d_i=v_i^2-4\ne0\) and \(\sqrt{d_i}\in W\), because either choice is up to sign the difference of the reciprocal roots in the normal field \(W\).
By (2) its sign cannot change under any automorphism fixing \(K\), since that field contains all radicands and the allowed scalar.
Every supported alternating permutation in the target block action lifts over \(K\).
Consequently the collection of real exterior inverse-pair labels, exactly those with positive discriminant at the chosen embedding, is a union of whole blocks.
It is nonempty, since otherwise the unit would be torsion by Kronecker.
At least one block of size \(b_y\) therefore consists of real inverse pairs.
Writing \(d=2u_y b_y\) the actual real-conjugate proportion is at least \(1/u_y\).
The same real-conjugate bound now gives
\[
       m(y)>\frac{d}{100u_y^2\log(8u_y)}\longrightarrow\infty
\]
as \(d\to\infty\), since \(u_y\ll_N(\log(3d))^{212}\).
This contradiction proves the uniform compressed-degree bound.

Bounded-degree coefficient finiteness for the compressed units gives the asserted finite spectrum.
For the unrestricted positive gap it suffices to apply the unit conclusion at any one positive ceiling: an algebraic number of positive measure below \(\log2\) is a unit by the extreme coefficients of its primitive integer minimum.
The new field thus excludes shrinking sequences inside this class even on the unbounded-second-iterate branch.
