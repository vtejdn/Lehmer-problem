# One finite set of exceptional torsion parameters for all common dilations

<a id="statement"></a>
## Dilation along a primitive direction

Let \(F\) be a primitive rationally irreducible integral Laurent polynomial with two-dimensional Newton polygon and let \(H=\mathbb Q(\mu_\infty)\).
Fix a signed primitive pair \((k,l)\), put \(n=\max(|k|,|l|)\), and let \(W_0\) be the width of that polygon in direction \((k,l)\).
For roots of unity \(\xi,\eta\) put \(t=\eta^k/\xi^l\) and let \(w\) be their joint order.
There is a finite set \(E_{F,k,l}\) of roots of unity such that, whenever \(t\notin E_{F,k,l}\), every integer \(g\ge1\) and every nonzero nontorsion root \(z\) of \(F(\xi z^{gk},\eta z^{gl})=0\) satisfy
\[
 [H(z):H]\ge\frac{gW_0}{cI},\qquad m(z)\ge b_F\varphi(w).
\]
Here \(c,I,b_F\) are the constants of the [fixed-exponent theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v859.md#statement), and \(m(z)\) is the logarithmic Mahler measure of the primitive minimal polynomial of \(z\).
The same set works for every common dilation and both twists realizing an invariant outside the set.
It may depend on the primitive direction, including when \(t=1\) is the invariant of interest.

Even at an exceptional invariant, there is an integer \(B_F(n)\ll_F 1+\log^4(2n)/(\log\log(5n))^3\) with the following property.
Set \(G=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,B_F(n))\).
For \(h=\gcd(g,G)\), raising each nontorsion root at dilation \(g\) to the power \(g/h\) gives a nontorsion root at dilation \(h\) with exactly the same logarithmic Mahler measure.
Every nontorsion root at \(h\) has such a lift, and if \(P\) is its rational minimal polynomial then \(P(X^{g/h})\) is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\).
In particular a common dilation all of whose prime factors exceed \(B_F(n)\) preserves each original positive factor measure at every invariant.
Every positive measure arising at any dilation also occurs at some dilation \(e\le B_F(n)\) of the same twisted primitive section.

<a id="radicals"></a>
## Divisibility of a primitive section root in its cyclotomic extension

Let \(\beta=z^g\), \(K=H(\beta)\), and \(D=[K:H]\).
The specialized Laurent polynomial in the primitive parameter cannot vanish identically, since its image in the two-torus would then be a coset component of \(F=0\), contrary to [positive area on each geometric component](Yin_C_G0345_v859.md#lattice).
Its width can only decrease under cancellation, so \(D\le W_0\).
The [individual height estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v769.md#statement) gives \(W_0 h(\beta)\ll_F 1\) at large \(n\), where \(h(\cdot)\) denotes absolute logarithmic height.
For bounded \(n\) the same bound holds after increasing the constant.
Indeed the coefficients of the specialized polynomial after removing a monomial are algebraic integers, and at every archimedean embedding their sum of absolute values is at most the sum \(L_F\) of the absolute coefficients of \(F\).
For a nonzero root the usual root bound at each place bounds \(\max(1,|\beta|_v)\) by the coefficient maximum divided by the absolute leading coefficient at finite places, and by twice that ratio at infinite places.
The product formula eliminates the leading coefficient, giving \(h(\beta)\le\log(2L_F)\), while the widths in bounded directions are bounded.

Let \(e_g\) be the largest divisor \(e\) of \(g\) such that \(\beta\in K^{\times e}\).
The [cyclic radical calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v775.md#compression) gives
\[
 [K(z):K]=g/e_g,\qquad \delta=z^{g/e_g}\in K,\qquad H(\delta)=K .
\]
Indeed the Galois group acts on \(z\) by the subgroup of roots of unity of some order \(j\mid g\), and \(z^j\in K\).
If \(\beta\in K^{\times e}\) with \(e\mid g\), then \(z^{g/e}\in K\) since it differs from any chosen \(e\)-th root in \(K\) by a root of unity.
Thus \(j\mid g/e\), and \(e_g=g/j\) is divisible by every admissible \(e\).

By the [relative height bound over the cyclotomic closure](../audits/literature/AD07/Yin_U001.md#rational-base),
\[
 \frac{h(\beta)}{e_g}=h(\delta)\gg
 \frac{(\log\log(5D))^3}{D(\log(2D))^4}.
\]
The function \(x(\log(2x))^4/(\log\log(5x))^3\) is eventually increasing, so its maximum through \(W_0\) is bounded by a constant times its value at \(W_0\).
The width \(W_0\) is comparable to \(n\) up to constants of the fixed polygon, since the width function is positive and continuous on the unit circle.
Using \(W_0 h(\beta)\ll_F1\) gives the stated bound for \(e_g\).
Choose a positive integer upper bound \(B_F(n)\) of that order valid for every twist, primitive direction of norm \(n\) and positive dilation.

<a id="compression"></a>
## Exact measures under removal of large common powers

For a fixed primitive root \(\beta\) and \(h=\gcd(g,G)\), the integer \(e_g\) divides \(h\) and \(\beta\in K^{\times e_g}\).
All divisors admissible at \(h\) are admissible at \(g\), so \(e_h=e_g\).
Put \(u=z^{g/h}\), which has \(u^h=\beta\).
Dividing the two relative degrees gives \([H(z):H(u)]=g/h\).
The rational degree \([\mathbb Q(z):\mathbb Q(u)]\) is at least this large by base change and at most this large by the power equation.
Consequently the product of degree and height is the same at \(u\) and \(z\), and the minimal polynomial of \(u\) stays irreducible after substitution of \(X^{g/h}\).
Every \(u\) at dilation \(h\) lifts simply by taking a \((g/h)\)-th root.
Taking \(h=e_g\) instead gives \(e_h=e_g\) for the same reason, so \(m(z)=m(\delta)\) already at a dilation at most \(B_F(n)\).

<a id="transfer"></a>
## Specializing a single finite dilation

Apply the [torsion specialization theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v859.md#statement) at the exponent pair \((Gk,Gl)\) and take its finite exceptional set for \(E_{F,k,l}\).
For any \(t\) outside this set, take any \(G\)-th root \(v\) of the primitive root \(\beta\).
It belongs to the section at dilation \(G\) with the original twists and satisfies
\[
          [H(v):H]/G=D/e_G\ \ge\ W_0/(cI).
\]
As \(e_g\mid G\) and \(\beta\in K^{\times e_g}\), one has \(e_g\mid e_G\).
Therefore \([H(z):H]/g=D/e_g\ge D/e_G\), simultaneously for every \(g\).
The [absolute degree and height argument](Yin_C_G0345_v859.md#measure) gives the stated \(b_F\varphi(w)\) with the same constants, since the degree fraction and the uniform height estimate are unchanged.

For the [two-pole curve](Yin_C_G0345_v794.md#isogenies), every torus isogeny pullback is geometrically irreducible.
In the [component proof of the fixed-exponent theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v859.md#covers) this gives one component and the full projected width at dilation \(G\).
For each primitive nonaxis direction we may therefore choose one finite exceptional set outside which every torsion-translated polynomial at every dilation is irreducible over \(H\) after clearing the lowest monomial.
Indeed those directions have unit corner coefficients and actual width \(gW_0\), and [the curve has no torsion point](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#torsion), so the full degree bound applies to every root.
