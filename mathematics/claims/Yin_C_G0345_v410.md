# Quartic Salem seeds in the coefficient boxes of row degrees four and five

<a id="statement"></a>
## An eventual exclusion and the quadratic left at the next degree

Take the [prime-indexed minimum](Yin_C_G0345_v408.md#statement) with row-degree bound \(b\) and coefficient cost \(H=\max(\|U\|_1,\|V\|_1)\).
At every sufficiently large odd prime \(p\) the minimum is \(2^p\) for every \(1\le b\le4\), and no genuine pair attains this cutoff.
For \(b=5\) at those primes any genuine pair of cost \(H\le2^p\) satisfies the following restriction.
After the simultaneous change \((U(Y),V(Y))\mapsto(U(-Y),-V(-Y))\) if needed, its seed is a quartic Salem minimum with positive exterior root \(\rho\).
Let \(Q\) be the minimum of \(\rho^p\), put \(D=U(Y)U(1/Y)-V(Y)V(1/Y)\), and write
\[
        Y^5D=Q^2\{c(Y^2+1)+dY\}.
\]
The integers \(c,d\) satisfy
\[
             c=u_5u_0-v_5v_0\ne0,\qquad
             2|c|+|d|\le4H^2/\rho^{2p},\qquad |c|\le H^2/\rho^{2p}.   \tag{1}
\]
Here the row coefficients are padded with zeros to degree five.
Only the two seeds in the [quartic trace classification](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v242.md#short) can occur.
The finite census separately excludes both of them at every width through five and every odd prime through \(1009\). [Exact computation](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0177/readme.md#census)
The asymptotic statement supplies no explicit onset.

<a id="quartic"></a>
## Lower cost at fixed seed

For a fixed quartic Salem seed the [collision estimate](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v246.md#collisions) for a signed reciprocal component \(A=\sum_{i=0}^{4}a_iY^i\) gives \(\log|a_4-a_0|\ge p\log\rho-o_P(p)\) whenever the integer difference is nonzero.
The estimate sums the finite-place collisions of the two powered inverse pairs and controls their overlap by the [fixed-field gcd bound for independent units](../audits/literature/CZ05/Yin_U001.md#s-unit-gcd).
Decomposing the row pair by reversal produces two signs.
At least one has a nonzero edge difference for every sufficiently large \(p\), by the [two derivative conditions](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v246.md#edges).
Evaluation at the exterior root gives \(|a_4-a_0|\ll_P pH/\rho^p\), so \(\log H\ge2p\log\rho-o_P(p)\).
This applies at all sufficiently large positive integer exponents and requires only double divisibility and \(D\ne0\).

At width five, if \(c=0\), the leading vectors of the original pair and its reversal are dependent.
Their entries are \(O_P(pH/\rho^p)\), so an integer combination of cost \(O_P(pH^2/\rho^p)\) cancels them.
When all four vanish one can use the original width-four pair directly.
In the other cases the surviving width-four pair has nonzero reflected determinant by the [exclusion of width-three graphs](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v247.md#three) and the [reversal computation](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v247.md#outer).
Consequently \(\log H\ge\tfrac32p\log\rho-o_P(p)\) at width five whenever \(c=0\).
The two eligible exterior roots both satisfy \(\rho^{3/2}>2\), so both lower rates exceed \(\log2\).
Taking the larger of the two seed thresholds proves the asserted uniformity over the finite seed list.
The sign change preserves the coefficient cost and double divisibility at every odd prime and preserves nonvanishing of the outer coefficient.

<a id="quadratic"></a>
## The remaining integer coefficients

Every qualifying pair recovers its original unit from its \(p\)-th power, and [reflected elimination](Yin_C_G0345_v408.md#lower) gives \(Q^2\mid Y^5D\).
The monic reciprocal polynomial \(Q\) has degree four and log measure \(p\log\rho\).
Ordinary monic division gives an integral quotient of degree at most two which is reciprocal with formal degree two, because \(Y^5D\) and \(Q^2\) are reciprocal at formal degrees ten and eight.
Its constant and leading coefficients equal the stated integer \(c\).
On the circle \(|D|\le H^2\), hence the quotient has log measure at most \(2\log H-2p\log\rho\).
The absolute leading coefficient of a nonzero integral polynomial is bounded by its multiplicative Mahler measure, and its one-norm is bounded by \(2^{\deg}\) times that measure.
Using \(c\ne0\) gives both bounds in (1).
At the full cutoff the only surviving reflected quotients thus have nonzero outer coefficient and \(2|c|+|d|\le4(2/\rho)^{2p}\).
Whether any of these quadratics comes from a qualifying pair for arbitrarily large primes remains open.
