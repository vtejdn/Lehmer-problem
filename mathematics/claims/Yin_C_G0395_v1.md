## 1. Complete structural extension

Define

    g_tr=product_(w outside[-2,2]) dist(w,[-2,2])/(|w|+2)>0,
    c_tr=2^(-r)g_tr,
    S(P)=sum_(P(zeta)=0,|zeta|=1)1/|P'(zeta)|.
