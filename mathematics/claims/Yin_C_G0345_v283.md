# Common base fields for constructing specified roots by radicals

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact cost of omitting any of the label fields

Take the inputs and independent alternating factors of the [finite compositum theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v279.md#statement), including its pairwise independence up to conjugacy, common measure bound, large compressed degrees and \(\min b_i>\max u_i\).
Increase the common degree threshold so all block sizes \(b_i\) are sufficiently large absolutely.
Choose the actual input roots \(x_i\) inside a fixed algebraic closure of the rationals.
In the label splitting field \(P_i\) let \(E_i\) be the field fixed by the stabilizer of the distinguished label of \(x_i\), and let \(F\) be the compositum of these \(E_i\).
For every subset \(I\) of the input indices, the minimum degree of a number field \(K\) over which all these particular roots are obtainable by radicals and for which \(E_i\not\subseteq K\) exactly for \(i\in I\) is
\[
                 [F:\mathbb Q]\prod_{i\in I}\frac{b_i-1}{2}.                 \tag{1}
\]
Here \(K\) may be nonnormal and need not be contained in the compositum of the original splitting fields.
The unique minimizing field when the choice of omissions is unrestricted is \(F\).
In particular for a single input the least base degree is \(v\) and the least degree of a base omitting its own label field is \(v(b-1)/2\).
For a compressed reciprocal unit the distinguished field is the trace field generated by \(x+x^{-1}\).

<a id="orbit"></a>
## The cost on the orbit of the chosen blocks

Write \(P\) for the compositum of the \(P_i\), \(\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(P/\mathbb Q)\) and \(D=\prod_i(A_{b_i})^{u_i}\).
The [independent factors](Yin_C_G0345_v279.md#socles) give \(D\) inside \(\Gamma\) in the stated action.
Let \(k\) be the size of the \(\Gamma\)-orbit of the tuple of distinguished blocks.
Over each block tuple the independent factors move the distinguished labels through every choice, giving \([F:\mathbb Q]=k\prod_i b_i\).

Let \(H\le\Gamma\) be the image on \(P\) of the absolute Galois group of \(K\), and set \(J=H\cap D\).
The fixed field of \(H\) in \(P\) is \(P\cap K\), so \([K:\mathbb Q]\ge[\Gamma:H]=[\Gamma:HD][D:J]\).
Write \(\ell\) for the size of the \(H\)-orbit of the block tuple and \(\ell_i\) for the size of the orbit of its \(i\)-th block.
Then \(\ell\le\prod_i\ell_i\), and \([\Gamma:HD]\ge k/\ell\) since \(D\) fixes every block and the \(\Gamma\)-orbit is covered by translates of the \(HD\)-orbit.

The action of \(H\) on the orbit of each distinguished label is solvable.
Indeed, the corresponding root lies in a finite radical tower over \(K\), whose normal closure after adjoining the required roots of unity is solvable over \(K\).
The label is determined equivariantly by the root, so the permutation action on its \(K\)-conjugates factors through that solvable group.
For each of the \(\ell_i\) reached blocks, the projection of \(J\) to its alternating factor is proper.
Otherwise \(J\) would act on all labels of this block as the full alternating group, a nonsolvable section of the action on the \(H\)-orbit of the distinguished label.
Every proper subgroup of \(A_b\) has index at least \(b\) for \(b\ge5\), by simplicity and the order bound for a faithful coset action.
The index \([D:J]\) is at least the product of the indices of all single-factor projections, even when \(J\) links different coordinates.
Thus coordinate \(i\) contributes at least \(b_i^{\ell_i}/\ell_i\) after allowing for the denominator \(\ell\).
For \(\ell_i\ge2\) this is at least \(b_i^2/2\), and the distinguished field is necessarily omitted.

<a id="one-block"></a>
## A label that moves in a fixed block

Suppose \(\ell_i=1\), write \(b=b_i\), and let \(s\) be the size of the \(H\)-orbit of the distinguished label.
For \(s=1\) the field \(E_i\) is contained in \(K\) and the lower bound \(b\) already suffices.
For \(2\le s\le b-2\), the \(J\)-projection preserves that \(s\)-set, so its index in \(A_b\) is at least \(\binom bs\ge\binom b2\).
For \(s=b-1\) or \(s=b\) the projection is itself solvable, since its faithful action is determined by the restriction to the \(s\)-set.
Inside \(A_b\) place \(\lfloor b/5\rfloor\) independent alternating groups on disjoint five-sets.
The intersection with the solvable projection has proper projection to each of these simple factors.
Its index in their product is at least \(5^{\lfloor b/5\rfloor}\), eventually greater than \(\binom b2\).
This also bounds from below the index of the solvable projection in \(A_b\).
Multiplying the separate contributions proves the lower bound in (1), since \(s>1\) in this case is equivalent to omission of \(E_i\).

<a id="pairs"></a>
## A base attaining each degree

For every \(i\in I\), choose another label in its distinguished block and replace the distinguished label by the unordered pair consisting of these two labels.
Keep every other distinguished label as a singleton.
The stabilizer of this tuple in \(\Gamma\) has fixed field \(K_0\) of degree \(k\prod_{i\notin I} b_i\prod_{i\in I}\binom{b_i}{2}\), since the tuple determines its block tuple and \(D\) is independently transitive on the indicated choices within each block.
For a pair, an even permutation supported on its block can swap its two members and two other labels and fix all other input coordinates.
Thus its distinguished label is not fixed by the stabilizer, whereas each singleton is fixed.
Over \(K_0\) each distinguished label has orbit of size at most two.
Consequently \(K_0 E_i/K_0\) is at most quadratic for every \(i\).
After these quadratic steps, the [construction over a single label](Yin_C_G0345_v281.md#attainment) gives all the original \(x_i\) by radicals.
This realizes (1).
Because every omission increases the degree for \(b_i>3\) and a base with no omissions contains \(F\), equality at the unrestricted minimum forces \(K=F\).
