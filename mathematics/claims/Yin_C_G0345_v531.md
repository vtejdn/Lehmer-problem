# Simultaneous exterior residues for growing sets of double contacts

<a id="statement"></a>
## A lower bound uniform in the polynomial degrees

Fix a monic reciprocal minimum \(P\) of a nontorsion unit, write \(h=\log M(P)\), fix \(C>1\), and take a nonempty set \(S\) of distinct positive integers in \([L,CL]\).
Set \(r=|S|\) and \(\Sigma=\sum_{p\in S}p\).
Index all pairs \(i=(c_i,p_i)\) consisting of an exterior conjugate of \(P\) and an exponent in \(S\), and set \(z_i=c_i^{-p_i}\).
Assume the nodes are distinct.
Partition the indices into singletons and optional blocks consisting of \((c,p),(\bar c,p)\) over any subset of slots for a fixed nonreal label \(c\).
Also allow a block consisting of two fixed real labels of opposite signs over any subset of even slots.
Suppose that indices in different blocks with different labels \(c_i\ne c_j\) satisfy
\[
       |\log|z_i|-\log|z_j||\ge 8\log(2L).                                      \tag{1}
\]
For every nonzero integral pair with \(P^2\mid U(X^p)+XV(X^p)\) for all \(p\in S\), the cost \(H=\max(\|U\|_1,\|V\|_1)\) satisfies, for sufficiently large \(L\),
\[
             \log H\ge h\Sigma-O_{P,C}(L+r).                                     \tag{2}
\]
This includes pairs of arbitrary degree, with a common divisor or with identically zero reflected difference.
The estimate also applies after removing any subset of slots, using their remaining sum.

If \(r\to\infty\) and \(b/r\to\infty\), the minimum normalized log cost among coprime pairs of degree at most \(b\) with these contacts and nonzero reflected difference tends to \(h\).
In particular, suppose each exterior modulus carries one real conjugate or one nonreal complex-conjugate pair, and the selected powers preserve the seed degree.
Choose \(C>1\) small enough that the intervals \([\log|c|, C\log|c|]\) for distinct exterior moduli are disjoint.
Then every growing set in \([L,CL]\) qualifies, including the entire interval of integers.
Indeed labels of different moduli are separated by a fixed positive multiple of \(L\), and each nonreal conjugate pair uses one block over the whole schedule.
This gives the limit for every comparable schedule at a seed with just one nonreal exterior pair and no other exterior conjugates whenever the selected powers preserve degree.
For exactly two real exterior labels of opposite signs, the same conclusion holds at any comparable schedule of even exponents preserving degree, without radial separation between the labels.
Indeed nodes from distinct exponents cannot coincide: if powers of two conjugates coincide, their absolute logarithmic heights are equal, whereas these are the respective exponents times the same positive height of the seed.

<a id="system"></a>
## Exact equations for the normalized residues

Reverse both polynomials at the largest occupied degree, obtaining a column \(w=(u,v)^t\) with a nonzero integral entry at zero.
Write \(\ell_i=(1,c_i)\) and \(k_i=(-c_i,1)^t\).
The [reversed double contact](Yin_C_G0345_v525.md#endpoints) gives \(\ell_i w(z_i)=0\) and \(p_i z_i\ell_i w'(z_i)=c_i v(z_i)\).
Put \(T(Y)=\prod_i(Y-z_i)\) and write the partial fractions
\[
      w(Y)/T(Y)=F(Y)+\sum_j z_j d_j k_j/(Y-z_j)
\]
with a polynomial column \(F\).
This form follows from the value equation, and \(v(z_i)=T'(z_i)z_i d_i\).
For \(|y|\le1/2\), the circle integral of \((w/T)(\zeta)/(\zeta-y)\) with measure \(d\zeta/(2\pi i)\) equals \(F(y)\), since each proper partial fraction contributes zero by its Laurent expansion on the circle.
Writing \(N\) for the number of nodes, eventually \(|T|\ge2^{-N}\) on the circle and \(\|F(y)\|_\infty\le2^{N+1}H\).
After applying \(\ell_i\), the pole at \(i\) disappears identically.
Differentiating the product by \(T\) at \(z_i\) therefore gives the linear system
\[
   \frac{c_i}{p_i}d_i-\sum_{j\ne i}\frac{(c_i-c_j)z_j}{z_i-z_j}d_j=\ell_i F(z_i).       \tag{3}
\]
In particular the off-diagonal interaction of two slots with the same label vanishes exactly, even at consecutive exponents.

<a id="inverse"></a>
## Uniform inversion on the separated blocks

Conjugate the matrix in (3) by the diagonal matrix \(D_{ii}=|z_i|^{1/2}\), sending the unknown to \(Dd\).
An entry between two different blocks has absolute value bounded by
\[
 |c_i-c_j|\frac{\sqrt{|z_i z_j|}}{\big||z_i|-|z_j|\big|}\ll_P (2L)^{-4}
\]
when its labels differ, by (1); for coincident labels the entry is zero.
The maximum absolute row sum of these entries is \(O_{P,C}(L^{-3})\) because \(N=O_{P,C}(L)\).
A singleton diagonal block has inverse norm \(O_{P,C}(L)\).
For a block with two conjugate labels write \(x_i=c^{-p_i}\), \(y_i=\bar x_i\), \(\rho_i=|x_i|\), indexing once per slot within that block.
Its two diagonal submatrices have norms \(O_P(1/L)\), since same-label off-diagonal entries vanish.
Its two off-diagonal submatrices are constant multiples of the weighted Cauchy matrix
\[
     K_{ij}=\sqrt{\rho_i\rho_j}/(x_i-y_j)
\]
and its complex conjugate, up to column factors of modulus one.
Both constant multipliers have modulus \(|c-\bar c|>0\).
To bound the inverse define
\[
 A_i=\frac{\prod_k|x_i-y_k|}{\sqrt{\rho_i}\prod_{k\ne i}|x_i-x_k|}.
\]
The Cauchy inverse has entries \(|(K^{-1})_{ji}|=A_i A_j/|x_i-y_j|\).
Indeed the rational function with poles at the \(y_k\) taking the Kronecker values \(\delta_{i\ell}\) at \(x_\ell\) is a constant times \(\prod_{k\ne i}(Y-x_k)/\prod_k(Y-y_k)\); its residues give the unweighted inverse column, and the weights divide its entries by \(\sqrt{\rho_i\rho_j}\).
Conjugation identifies the two absolute product factors with the displayed \(A_i,A_j\).
Writing \(R=|c|>1\), one has
\[
 A_i\le \frac{|x_i-y_i|}{\sqrt{\rho_i}}
       \prod_{d=1}^\infty\left(\frac{1+R^{-d}}{1-R^{-d}}\right)^2.
\]
Each exponent distance \(d\) occurs at most twice relative to a given slot, and the logarithm of the product converges by geometric decay.
Consequently diagonal inverse entries are bounded by a constant times \(|x_i-y_i|/\rho_i\le2\).
Off the diagonal, \(|(K^{-1})_{ji}|\ll_P \sqrt{\rho_i\rho_j}/|\rho_i-\rho_j|\ll_P R^{-|p_i-p_j|/2}\).
This summable bound controls the maximum row sum independently of the number and phases of the nodes.
The matrix formed from the two off-diagonal submatrices and zero diagonal submatrices thus has bounded inverse; the omitted diagonal entries have norm \(O_P(1/L)\), so a geometric series gives a bounded inverse for the whole block.
For an opposite-sign real block the nodes are positive at all its even slots.
The weighted cross terms have the symmetric entries \(-(c_i-c_j)\sqrt{z_i z_j}/(z_i-z_j)\), and the diagonal subblocks have only the diagonal entries \(c_i/p_i\).
Writing \(A\) for this block and \(J\) for the diagonal sign of its labels, the Hermitian part of \(JA\) is exactly \(\operatorname{diag}(|c_i|/p_i)\).
Thus \(\|Ax\|_2\|x\|_2\ge \operatorname{Re}(x^*JAx)\gg_{P,C}L^{-1}\|x\|_2^2\), regardless of the cross entries.
Conversion to maximum row sum gives \(\|A^{-1}\|_\infty\ll_{P,C}L\sqrt N\).
The block diagonal inverse times the off-block part has maximum row sum \(O_{P,C}(L^{-3/2})\), so the geometric series bounds the full conjugated inverse by \(O_{P,C}(L^{3/2})\).
Since \(\|D\|_\infty\le1\) and \(\|D^{-1}\|_\infty\le\exp(O_{P,C}(L))\), (3) yields \(\max_i|d_i|\le H\exp(O_{P,C}(L+N))\).
At zero, \(w(0)=T(0)(F(0)-\sum_i d_i k_i)\) and \(|T(0)|=\exp(-h\Sigma)\).
The nonzero integral coefficient proves (2).

<a id="width"></a>
## Recovering every slot at growing width

All powers in \(S\) preserve the seed degree: a collision of two conjugates at exponent \(p\) would remain a collision after applying a splitting-field automorphism carrying the first conjugate to an exterior one.
The second image would also be exterior, contradicting distinctness of the listed nodes.
The powered minima \(Q_p\) therefore have logarithmic measures \(ph\) and are distinct.
Use the [higher-jet count and width parameters](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#counts) \(q,w,t\), for which \(t\to\infty\), \(t=o(r)\), \(r^2=o(wt)\), and \(w+r+\log(2Lw)=o(tL)\).
In the packing step the minimum log distance in coefficient sup norm is at least \(h\Sigma-O_{P,C}(L+r+\log(2w))\) by (2).
Its error is \(o(tL)\); after multiplication by \(w+1\) it stays below the leading packing surplus \(whtL\) in order.
Thus the dimension and polarization argument yields the required short pair with nonzero reflected difference.
In the [designated-slot division](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#protect), (2) on the other slots gives the lower bound \(h(\Sigma-p)-O_{P,C}(L+r)\).
If the common power of \(Q_p\) were at least \(t-1\), the same factor estimate would force \((t/4-2)ph\le O_{P,C}(w+L+r+\log(2Lw))=o(tL)\), which is impossible.
The [integral combination and gcd removal](Yin_C_G0345_v529.md#combine) now preserve every slot with \(\log H\le h\Sigma+o(\Sigma)\).
The lower estimate (2) has error \(o(\Sigma)\) and gives the asserted limit.
