# Excluding all branch-two quadratic trace prefixes and their integral unit factors

<a id="statement"></a>
## The offsets \(d,2d\) and a stable prefix criterion

On the positive-constant Pisot branch \(j=2\), every trace numerator
\[
                C(x)=2U_h(x/2)+aU_{h-d}(x/2)+bU_{h-2d}(x/2),
            \qquad 1\le 2d\le h,\quad a,b\in\mathbb Z ,
\]
has \(q\) absolutely bounded and \(\theta-q\) bounded below by an absolute positive constant.
All allowed degrees, offsets, coefficients and both parities of the original polynomial are included.

More generally fix \(M<\infty,\eta>0\), let \(j\ge1\) vary, and let \(\Pi=(1-z^2)z^h C(z+z^{-1})\) be the trace lift of an actual positive-constant input with \(\|\Pi/j\|_\infty\le M\).
Suppose that through degree \(\lfloor\eta h\rfloor\) its normalized coefficients agree with
\[
       G(z) S_i(z^d),\qquad S_i(w)=1+\lambda_i w+\mu_i w^2,\qquad
                      G\in 1+z\mathbb Z[[z]],\quad 1\le d\le h .
\]
There exist \(\varepsilon,c>0\), depending only on \(M,\eta\), such that \(\theta-q\ge c\) whenever the real pair \((\lambda_i,\mu_i)\) is within \(\varepsilon\) in maximum norm of
\[
                       (\pm1/2,1/2),\qquad(\pm1,1/2).
\]
The formal series \(G\) can vary freely subject to its stated integrality and constant term.
The criterion covers arbitrary middle support and does not require a circle bound on \(G\).
The main researcher obtained this stability and its applications using the [unit-coefficient quadratic classification](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v74.md#profiles) and the [coefficient-two classification](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v76.md#profiles).

## Integrality at the first fractional coefficient after formal division

Otherwise take a sequence with excess tending to zero and the pairs tending to one of the four centers, with corresponding polynomial \(S\).
Write \(B^\sharp=(\Pi/j)J_i\) for the actual monic reciprocal denominator of degree \(2h+2\).
By the [varying-branch trace estimates](Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#integrality), \(h\to\infty\), \(M(B^\sharp)>1\) tends to one, and \(J_i\) has a symmetric signed representing measure \(\omega_i\) of mass one with vanishing negative mass.
Below \(d\) the given prefix is integral, so the [first nondivisible coefficient theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v13.md#statement) gives \(d=o(h)\).
Put \(X=B^\sharp/G=\sum x_nz^n\).
This is an integer formal series, and \(X=S_i(z^d)J_i\) on the entire common prefix.
Only these prefix coefficients of \(X\) will be used.

For each fixed \(L\) the interval \([0,Ld]\) contains boundedly many nonzero \(x_n\), all of bounded size along the sequence.
The size bound is immediate from the bounded coefficients of \(S_i\) and the uniform bound on the moments of \(\omega_i\).
For a support bound only the case \(d\to\infty\) needs proof.
Write \(j_n=[z^n]J_i\), with \(j_0=1\).
All \(j_n=x_n\) for \(0\le n<d\).
For any positive integer \(t=o(d)\), push \(\omega_i\) to \((v^t,v^D)\), where \(D=d\) if \(\lambda_i\to\pm1/2\) and \(D=2d\) if \(\lambda_i\to\pm1\).
The weak limit after extraction is a probability.
Its doubled first marginal has integral moments \(M(r,0)\) at all positive \(r\) by \(j_{rt}=x_{rt}\).
As in the [single fractional coefficient test](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#seam), the mixed row \(M(r,1)\) is integral for \(r<0\), half-odd-integral at zero, and \(M(r,1)+\tau M(r,0)/2\) is integral for \(r>0\), with \(\tau=\pm1\).

For \(D=d\) the term at \(2d\) is outside every tested index \(d+rt\), so these conditions follow directly by integrality of \(X\).
For \(D=2d\), all \(j_n\) with \(0\le n<2d\) have distance \(o(1)\) from integers uniformly: below \(d\) they are integral and above or at \(d\) they equal \(x_n-\lambda_i j_{n-d}\), with \(j_{n-d}\) a bounded integer.
At \(2d+rt\) the additional term \(\lambda_i j_{d+rt}\) likewise has distance \(o(1)\) from integers for every fixed \(r\), since \(\lambda_i\to\pm1\) and the raw coefficients are bounded.
The contribution from \(\mu_i\to1/2\) thus yields exactly the claimed conditions, including the Taylor constant at \(r=0\).
The [marginal classification for this mixed row](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#haar) forces the first marginal to be normalized arclength.
In particular \(j_t=x_t\) eventually vanishes.
Consequently there is a fixed \(c_0>0\) on the sequence such that \(x_n=0\) for \(1\le n\le c_0 d\) eventually, since otherwise one can extract nonzeros at \(t=o(d)\).

Partition \([0,Ld]\) into a bounded number of blocks of diameter at most \(c_0d\), splitting also at \(0,d,2d\) and counting those three coefficients separately.
In an open piece each \(x_n\) is a moment of twice the same bounded partial sum of \(S_i(v^{-d})\) against \(\omega_i\).
The Gram matrix of the characters at distinct indices in a block is the identity against the signed measure if \(c_0<1\).
For any fixed \(K\) selected indices the positive part changes this Gram norm by \(o(1)K\), and each original moment by \(o(1)\).
The [Bessel estimate](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v59.md#sparse) applied to the fixed multiplier on the piece bounds the number of nonzero integer \(x_n\), by taking \(K\) larger than a fixed bound for its squared norm.
This proves the local support assertion.

## Harmonic extinction for the limiting quadratic

Take any \(d\ll t\ll h\), meaning \(d/t\to0\) and \(t/h\to0\).
Push the positive parts of \(\omega_i\) to \((w,u)=(v^d,v^t)\) and take a weak probability limit.
The \(w\)-marginal has symmetric Herglotz transform \(J_0\) with \(S J_0\in\mathbb Z[[w]]\) by the exact prefix identity at each fixed multiple of \(d\).
Therefore it has one of the quadratic profiles in the [eleven-profile list](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v74.md#profiles) or the [nine-profile list](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v76.md#profiles), including their sign rotations.
In both cases all atoms lie on twelfth roots of unity.

For every \(k\ge1\), weight twice the limit by \(u^k\), project onto \(\bar w\), and multiply by \(S(w)\) in the reflected coordinate.
Every Fourier coefficient at \(l\in\mathbb Z\) is the limit of the integers \(x_{kt+ld}\), since all three indices in \(S_iJ_i\) are positive, inside the prefix, and the multiplier coefficients converge.
The [integer Fourier–Stieltjes decomposition](Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#far-rows) splits this measure into a torsion atomic part and a continuous integer Laurent polynomial density \(H_k=S\sigma c_k\).
Here \(\sigma\) is twice the continuous marginal density and the \(c_k\) are moments of a common family of conditional probabilities.
The [unit-coefficient extinction](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v74.md#harmonics) and the [coefficient-two extinction](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v76.md#harmonics) give \(H_k=0\) for every \(k\).
The surviving atoms of the weighted projection are supported on atoms of the marginal, so \(x_{t+12d}=x_t\) eventually by weak convergence and integrality.
This holds on every sequence of such indices by further extraction if necessary.

Choose an integer \(L\ge14\), with \(1/L<\eta\), sufficiently large that the relation holds simultaneously for all \(Ld\le t\le h/L\) eventually.
Failure for every \(L\) would give counterexample indices with \(d/t\to0\), \(t/h\to0\).
For \(U=1-z^{12d}\), let \(Q\) be the truncation of \(UX\) at \(H=\lfloor h/L\rfloor\).
Its degree is at most \((L+12)d\) and its coefficient sum in absolute value is bounded by the low-index result, including the translated coefficients.
The comparison \(J_*=Q/[U S_i(z^d)]\) agrees with \(J_i\) through \(H\).
For some radius \(R>1\) independent of \(i\), \(1/S_i(w)\) is uniformly bounded on \(|w|\le R\), since the limiting quadratic has both roots of modulus \(\sqrt2\).
Its coefficients \(\alpha_n\) thus have uniformly bounded \(\sum (1+n)|\alpha_n|\).
The [congruence-class expansion](Yin_C_G0345_v12.md#comparison) gives a Fourier measure of uniformly bounded variation for the positive coefficients of \(J_*\): each monomial of \(Q\) at index \(l=O(d)\), shifted by \(nd\), needs one residue class modulo \(12d\) and \(O(1+(l+nd)/(12d))\) singleton corrections.

On the actual input,
\[
       S_i(z^d)U B^\sharp - Q\Pi/j
                 =S_i(z^d)U(\Pi/j)(J_i-J_*).
\]
The polynomial on the left has degree \(O(h)\), the normalized multipliers on the right have uniformly bounded circle norms, and the difference of the Taylor series vanishes through \(H\).
At strictly positive indices its coefficients are moments of a measure of bounded variation.
The [two cutoff argument](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v51.md#window) therefore bounds the \(L^1\) norm of the left side.
Since \(Q\Pi/j\) is bounded and \(\min_{|w|=1}|S_i(w)|\) is bounded away from zero, \(UB^\sharp\) has bounded \(L^1\) norm.
The [integer support bound](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v50.md#window) contradicts \(M(UB^\sharp)=M(B^\sharp)>1\) tending to one by the [fixed-support gap](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v32.md#boyd-return).
This proves the stable prefix assertion.

## Exhaustion of integer quadratic prefixes

On \(j=2\), if either interior coefficient vanishes, the [four-term gap](Yin_C_G0345_v15.md#statement) applies.
Otherwise a sequence with \(q\to\infty\) has, after extraction, a fixed quadratic \(A(w)=2+aw+bw^2\), with \(|b|=1,\ |a|\le3\) and no open-disk zero by the [six-term reduction](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v61.md#six).
For \(b=1,\ |a|=1,2\), apply the stable prefix assertion with \(G=1\), since the reflected terms begin above \(h\) and the normalized lift has bounded norm.
When \(b=-1,\ |a|\ge2\), the values \(A(0)=2\) and \(A(-\operatorname{sgn}a)=1-|a|<0\) would give an open-disk zero.
For \(b=1,\ |a|=3\) and \(b=-1,\ |a|=1\), the lower half factors as \((1+s w)(2+t w)\), \(s,t=\pm1\), so the [factored-prefix gap](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v71.md#six-term) applies.
The [fixed-branch derivative identity](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v36.md#second) makes \(q\to\infty\) equivalent to \(\theta-q\to0\) on this branch, giving both bounds.

The same reasoning excludes a larger class of prefixes on branch two whenever the normalized lift is bounded and agreement holds on a fixed positive fraction of \(h\).
Suppose the lower prefix before normalization has the form \(G(z)A(z^d)\), with \(G\in1+z\mathbb Z[[z]]\) and \(A\) an integer polynomial of constant two, leading coefficient \(\pm1\), and no open-disk root.
If its irreducible noncyclotomic part has degree at most two, its factors normalized to constant one other than that part are integral cyclotomic products.
Indeed monic factors up to sign with absolute constant one and no interior roots have every root on the circle and are cyclotomic by Kronecker, and the product of all absolute constants is two.
After absorbing the cyclotomic factors into \(G\), the remaining factor has constant two and the form \(2\pm w\) or \(2+a'w\pm w^2\).
The linear case and \(a'=0\) follow from the [formal unit prefix theorem](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v71.md#unit-prefix), with offset \(d\) or \(2d\), respectively; if \(2d>h\), the integral prefix already occupies a positive fraction of \(h\) and the divisibility theorem applies.
For the quadratic case \(|a'|\le3\) follows from the middle term dominating the other two on the circle if \(|a'|>3\).
The two factored quadratics are also covered by the formal unit theorem after adjoining their integral linear factor to \(G\).
The remaining quadratics are exactly the centers of the stable prefix bound or have an open-disk zero.
In particular this excludes every reducible primitive cubic \(2+aw^m+bw^3\), \(m=1,2\), in an escaping six-term sequence: the preceding six-term reduction gives \(|b|=1\), bounded coefficients and no open-disk root, and reducibility in degree three leaves noncyclotomic degree at most two.
An irreducible cubic and a lower polynomial of larger noncyclotomic degree remain possible here.
