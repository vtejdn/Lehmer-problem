# Rational approximation at the six exceptional slopes

<a id="statement"></a>
## The part of a complementary resultant supported below twenty-three

Let \(k,l\) be coprime positive integers and let \(P=ST\) be a factorization of the [two-pole section](Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#statement) into monic reciprocal integer polynomials with nonzero resultant \(R=|\operatorname{Res}(S,T)|\).
There are two fixed slopes \(\alpha\in\mathbb Z_{13}\) and four in \(\mathbb Z_{19}\), given by the double roots of the [lifted critical system](Yin_C_G0345_v815.md#local).
For \(p=13,19\), if \(v_p(R)>2\), then \(p\nmid k\) and exactly one of the slopes at that prime satisfies
\[
                       v_p(R)=1+v_p(l/k-\alpha).
\]
Here the matching slope is identified by \(l/k\equiv\alpha\pmod {p^2}\).
Moreover \(13\) and \(19\) cannot both divide \(R\).

In any rectangle \(1\le k\le K,\ 1\le l\le L\) one may discard at most six primitive directions so that every remaining direction, simultaneously for all such factorizations, satisfies
\[
                 13^{v_{13}(R)}19^{v_{19}(R)}\le\max(19^2,19KL).
\]
If all irreducible factors of the section are reciprocal, its entire resultant part supported on primes below twenty-three is at most nine times this bound on the remaining directions.

<a id="local"></a>
## The valuation at a double residue root

Write \(s=l/k=s_0+pb\) on a surviving row and use the restricted series \(G(w,b)\) and residues \(a,c,h\) of the [local trace calculation](Yin_C_G0345_v815.md#local).
The series depends only on the fixed row, because its two torus arguments are \(u\exp(pw/B)\) and \(v\exp((s_0+pb)pw/B)\).
For each of the two exceptional second digits in that row let \((w_*,b_*)\) be its unique lifted solution to \(G=G_w=0\), and put \(\alpha=s_0+pb_*\).
Fix \(b\equiv b_*\pmod p\).
Hensel lifting of \(G_w=0\) gives a unique \(w_c\equiv w_*\pmod p\), since \(G_{ww}\equiv2a\) is a unit.
If \(b\ne b_*\) and \(t=v_p(b-b_*)\), uniqueness modulo \(p^t\) gives \(w_c\equiv w_*\pmod {p^t}\).
Taylor expansion about \((w_*,b_*)\) therefore gives \(v_p(G(w_c,b))=t\), since the linear term is \(G_b(w_*,b_*)(b-b_*)\) with unit coefficient and all remaining terms have valuation at least \(2t\).

Suppose the two roots in the matching disk lie in different reciprocal factors.
Their scaled trace coordinates then lie in \(\mathbb Q_p\), since the residue trace disk is defined there and each factor has exactly one trace root in that disk counted with multiplicity.
The trace isometry of the local calculation gives corresponding \(w_1,w_2\in\mathbb Z_p\), both congruent to \(w_c\) modulo \(p\).
Indeed that coordinate has unit linear coefficient and reduction of degree one, hence has an inverse on the closed unit disk by Hensel lifting.
Write \(G(w_c+y,b)=G(w_c,b)+y^2 U(y)\) with integral restricted \(U\) and \(U(0)\equiv a\ne0\pmod p\).
For \(y\in p\mathbb Z_p\), the map \(y\mapsto y\sqrt{U(y)/U(0)}\), taking the root congruent to one, preserves distances.
In fact the square-root factor is a unit and is 1-Lipschitz by subtracting its two squared values and dividing by the sum of the roots.
The transformed coordinates of the distinct roots are negatives of each other with valuation \(t/2\), and hence \(v_p(w_1-w_2)=t/2\) because \(p\) is odd.
If \(b=b_*\) the disk has just a double root, which cannot be shared when \(R\ne0\).
Returning to unscaled roots contributes \(1+t/2\) in the section disk and the same amount in its inverse disk, with all other differences units by the [critical residue census](../computations/programs/Yin_J_G0184/readme.md#sieve).
Thus the valuation when the exceptional disk is shared equals \(2+t=1+v_p(s-\alpha)\).
For other second digits the existing bound is two.
The order congruence at thirteen implies \(l\) even and the two at nineteen imply \(k\) even, proving the mutual exclusion by coprimality.

<a id="spacing"></a>
## Integer directions in a rectangle

Two different reduced slopes in the rectangle have nonzero integer determinant \(l_1k_2-l_2k_1\) of absolute value strictly smaller than \(KL\).
If both have unit denominators at \(p\) and lie within the congruence \(v_p(l_i/k_i-\alpha)>\log_p(KL)\), the determinant is divisible by a power of \(p\) greater than \(KL\), a contradiction.
Discard that one possible direction per center and use the valuation formula and mutual exclusion above.
The [bounds at the remaining small primes](Yin_C_G0345_v814.md#statement) supply the factor nine.

<a id="consequence"></a>
## Prime support required by a small rational factor

Suppose \(k\to\infty\), \(l/k\to\infty\), and \(S\) is rational irreducible of degree \(d=o(l)\), with \(T=P/S\).
Write \(R_{\ge23}=\prod_{p\ge23}p^{v_p(R)}\), the product taken over primes.
Apply the rectangle bound to the square of side \(2^j\) on the shell \(2^{j-1}<l\le2^j\), where eventually \(k\le l\).
Outside at most six primitive directions per shell,
\[
                     \log R_{\ge23}\ \ge\ (1-o(1))d\log(l/d).
\]
Indeed all factors are eventually reciprocal, and the [discriminant and Vandermonde bounds](Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#resultant) give \(\log R\ge d(\log(l/d)+O(1))\).
Here \(d\to\infty\) and \(d\log(l/d)/\log l\to\infty\), as is seen by splitting at \(d=\sqrt l\).
Thus the \(O(\log l)\) contribution from smaller primes is negligible on the indicated directions.

If primes below twenty-three supply at least \(\epsilon d\log(l/d)\) to \(\log R\) infinitely often for a fixed \(\epsilon>0\), some single center admits infinitely many distinct rational approximations with \(v_p(l/k-\alpha)\log p/\log l\to\infty\).
This forces that center to be transcendental over \(\mathbb Q\).
Indeed if an integral polynomial \(A\ne0\) of degree \(e\) vanished there, division by \(X-\alpha\) in \(\mathbb Z_p[X]\) would give \(v_p(A(l/k))\ge v_p(l/k-\alpha)\).
Except at the finitely many rational zeros of \(A\), the nonzero integer \(k^e A(l/k)\) has absolute value \(O_A(l^e)\), which bounds that valuation by \(e\log_p l+O_A(1)\).
These necessary conditions leave the arithmetic nature of the six centers and the placement of exceptional local roots in rational factors open.
