# Exact gap thresholds and consecutive measures in a fixed interval

<a id="statement"></a>
## Testing any threshold through the degree-ten example

Let \(\lambda\) be the unrestricted infimum of positive logarithmic Mahler measures of nonzero integer polynomials.
Choose a positive real algebraic \(Q\) with \(1<Q\le6/5\), given by exact algebraic data.
For each prime \(p>3\), choose computable integers \(2\le s_p\le p,\ 1\le C_p\le p\), both tending to infinity at any rates.
At degree \(d\) use the signed graphs and common genuine double contacts of the [transverse degree test](Yin_C_G0345_v468.md#statement) at the exact width \(b=s_p d-1\).
Bound the normalized span of the union of the two supports by \(C_p\).
Take both minima to have even degree \(d\), and require the two signs to have nonzero elimination determinant.
Choose \(\epsilon=0\) or \(1\), and impose
\[
                        2(2pb+1)^\epsilon H_+H_- < Q^{6p}.                 \tag{1}
\]
Let \(S_p(Q)\) consist of the distinct positive logarithmic measures of the initial units so represented, over all even degrees.
Each \(S_p(Q)\) is an effectively computable finite set, with a finite list of degree progressions for each measure, truncated by the width charge when present.
The exact lower bound satisfies
\[
 \lambda\ge\log Q
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S_p(Q)=\varnothing\ \text{for every }p>3
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S_p(Q)=\varnothing\ \text{for infinitely many primes}.       \tag{2}
\]
In fact every positive irreducible measure below \(\log Q\) occurs in \(S_p(Q)\) at every sufficiently large \(p\), and no value at or above the threshold occurs.
Choosing \(Q\) equal to the [measure of the degree-ten Lehmer polynomial](Yin_C_G0345_v408.md#lower) tests that specific lower bound with a strict comparison.
Choosing any computable sequence of algebraic \(Q>1\) decreasing to one allows (2) to test the existence of a positive gap.

<a id="enumeration"></a>
## All degrees at a variable width slope

Here \(s=s_p,\ C=C_p\) are fixed while enumerating a prime.
Repeat the [joint support compression](Yin_C_G0345_v474.md#compression) at \(b=sd-1\).
Write \(c\le C\) for the true joint normalized span, \(K\) for its spacing, \(w\) for the common translation, \(R=1+p(b-2w-Kc)\), \(g=\gcd(K,R)\), \(\ell=K/g\), and \(u=R/g\).
The two compressed graphs have rows \(B_\pm\) of common formal width \(c\), exponent \(p\ell\) and displacement \(u\).
For \(x=\gamma^g\) their powered minima retain the fields of \(x,x^2\) respectively.
As in the compression proof the derivative polynomial has degree at most \(2c\); the distinct powered minima divide it, so the degrees \(e_1,e_2\) of \(x,x^2\) satisfy \(e_1+e_2\le2c,\ d/g\le e_2\le c\).
These arguments use only the two genuine contacts and \(\gcd(p\ell,u)=1\).
Thus \(Kc\le b<sd\) gives \(\ell<s\), while \(|R|\le 1+pb\) gives \(|u|\le psc\).
The graph degrees after clearing monomials are at most \(2psc\).
Its coefficient box is finite by (1), using \(H_\pm\ge1\).

For the smaller row norm \(H\), the derivative polynomial has one-norm at most \(3pscH^2\).
The powered minima contribute at least \(2p\ell m(x)\) to its logarithmic measure, since \(m(x^2)=2(e_2/e_1)m(x)\ge m(x)\) and the powering by \(p\ell\) preserves both fields.
Since \(2H^2<Q^{6p}\) and \(s,c\le p\), one has
\[
                 m(x)<3\log(6/5)+\frac{\log(3p^3/2)}{2p}<2 .
\]
The last expression decreases for \(p\ge5\) in its second term and the bound follows at \(p=5\).
The [uniform radical modulus \(M_C\)](Yin_C_G0345_v475.md#uniform) therefore applies, as \(e_1\le2C\).

Use the [exact radical enumeration](Yin_C_G0345_v476.md#degrees) with this modulus.
For each \(m\mid M_C\) and each reciprocal root \(\beta^m=x\) of the same degree \(f\) as its square, write \(g=mk,\ d=fk\).
Take \(k\) odd with \(\gcd(k,M_C/m)=1\).
The new width gives \(A=sf-m\ell c\) and again \(2pw=1-p+k(pA-mu)\).
The congruence \(k(pA-mu)\equiv p-1\bmod 2p\) and inequalities \(k(pA\mp mu)\ge p\mp1\), respectively, express exactly \(w\in\mathbb Z\) and \(0\le w\le b-g\ell c\).
Thus the period in \(k\) divides \(2pM_C/m\) and the inequality cutoff, if feasible, is at most \(p+1\).
All surviving lifts have precisely the seed measure \(m(\beta)\).
The actual determinant, degree, numerator and budget checks survive the common lift exactly as in the cited enumeration.
Every nonempty progression has first degree at most \(8p C M_C\), including after monotone truncation by (1).
The set and the assigned measures can consequently be enumerated by algebraic operations; equality of positive multiplicative Mahler measures is decidable by their conjugate products.

<a id="spectrum"></a>
## Every strictly smaller irreducible measure appears eventually

The [determinant estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v468.md#statement) gives \(6p m(\gamma)\le\log(2H_+H_-)\), proving the pointwise strict upper cutoff for \(S_p(Q)\).
Conversely let \(h\) be any positive irreducible measure below \(\log Q\).
At this cutoff the [small-measure reduction](Yin_C_G0345_v420.md#upper) permits a reciprocal nontorsion unit, and the [relative norm construction](Yin_C_G0345_v421.md#norm) gives such a representative \(\alpha\) of degree \(e\) with every positive power generating the same field and \(m(\alpha)=h\).
The fixed-seed argument for the [limiting transverse height](Yin_C_G0345_v468.md#limit) supplies, for any prescribed excess \(\delta>0\), a fixed width with genuine transverse pairs at every sufficiently large prime and \(\log(2H_+H_-)/(6p)\le h+\delta\).
Choose the excess strictly smaller than \(\log Q-h\).
Pad both rows at the base degree to \(b=s_p e-1\) by \(Y^{\lfloor t/2\rfloor}(1+Y)^{t\bmod2}\), where \(t\) is the nonnegative width difference.
Both contacts and transversality survive, the norm cost per row is at most two, and the translated occupied positions have span at most the original fixed width plus one.
The increasing cap eventually includes them.
Also \(\log(2p b+1)/p\to0\), since \(s_p\le p\) and \(e\) is fixed.
This proves eventual occurrence at the original degree even with the width charge.
Any integer polynomial of positive measure smaller than \(\log Q\) has a positive irreducible factor at or below its measure, by multiplicativity.
This proves (2).

<a id="band"></a>
## Detecting zero infimum inside one interval bounded away from zero

Return to the single width \(5d/2-1\) and either of the [two original budgets](Yin_C_G0345_v477.md#statement).
Fix \(0<a<b_0<(\log2)/120\), with \(\exp(a),\exp(b_0)\) real algebraic given exactly.
For instance one can take \(\exp(a)=1001/1000,\ \exp(b_0)=501/500\).
At the same degree \(d\), require that for some nontorsion unit \(\eta\) of degree \(d\) and some \(t\ge1\), all of
\[
                \eta^t,\eta^{t+1},\ldots,\eta^{t+N-1}                      \tag{3}
\]
are initial units of genuine passing pairs and have measures in \((a,b_0)\), for a prescribed \(N\ge2\).
Each can use its own rows, and each of its degree-\(d\) square factors must satisfy the original graph conditions.
This requires algebraic compatibility between the recovered units.
The presence of \(N\) unrelated values in the interval does not impose (3).

If \(\lambda<(b_0-a)/N\), then for all sufficiently large primes, the degrees satisfying (3) occur at bounded intervals in the coefficient-only test.
More precisely they number at least \(c_0 L-O(L/p+1)\) in every interval \([X,X+L)\), \(X,L\ge1\), for some \(c_0>0\) depending on the fixed seed, interval of measures and \(N\).
The same conclusion holds along sequences with \(\log(2p(X+L))/p\to0\) in the width-charged test, and both permit joint span and support caps tending to infinity with \(p\).

Indeed choose an all-power reciprocal representative \(\alpha\) of positive measure \(h<(b_0-a)/N\).
Taking \(t=\lfloor a/h\rfloor+1\) puts all \((t+i)h\) for \(0\le i<N\) strictly inside the interval.
Each \(\alpha^{t+i}\) has the same degree \(e\) as \(\alpha\).
Apply the [uniform displacement and degree estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v477.md#intervals) to each of these finitely many bases.
Exclude every prime divisor of their finitely many forbidden odd power indices, those for \(\alpha\) itself, all primes dividing any \(t+i\), and the prime two, from the multipliers \(k\).
This gives a fixed squarefree integer \(B\) with the requirement \((k,B)=1\).
At a sufficiently large \(p\), at most a bounded number of additional \(k\bmod p\) classes are excluded, counting the union of the exceptional classes and zero.
For every remaining \(k\) and a root \(\eta^k=\alpha\), \(\eta\) has degree \(ek\) by the [odd binomial argument](Yin_C_G0345_v466.md#count).
The minimum of \(\eta^{t+i}\) is \(P_{\alpha^{t+i}}(T^k)\) by irreducibility of that substitution, and its measure is \((t+i)h\).
Its square has the same degree by the cited construction, which supplies both signs for this very minimum.
This gives (3) at \(d=ek\).
Inclusion-exclusion over the fixed \(B\), followed by deletion of the bounded number of \(k\bmod p\) classes, gives the estimate with \(c_0=\varphi(B)/(eB)\).

Conversely every recovered run satisfies \((N-1)m(\eta)<b_0-a\), because its terms and \(\eta\) have the same degree and therefore their logarithmic measures are \((t+i)m(\eta)\).
At a fixed \(d,p\) the condition (3) is decidable.
Factor the finitely many candidate graphs at that degree, list their eligible algebraic roots, and try all ordered choices \(y,z\) for the first two powers.
Test \(\eta=z/y\) for nontorsion, the unit condition and degree \(d\).
The bound \(t\,m(\eta)<b_0\) is finite and effective since \(m(\eta)>0\); a rational positive lower approximation suffices.
For each surviving \(t\) check all the actual powers and the interval endpoints exactly.

Consequently \(\lambda=0\) exactly when runs of arbitrarily large length can be recovered in this one fixed positive interval, even if only their existence at some prime and degree for each length is required.
For any fixed length \(N\), intervals of degrees of unbounded length with proportion of such runs tending to zero as \(p\to\infty\) imply \(\lambda\ge(b_0-a)/N\) under the appropriate width-cost condition.
This criterion still permits individual passing rows at every degree.
The existence of arbitrarily long runs or a uniform obstruction across primes is unresolved.
