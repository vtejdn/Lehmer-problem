### 1. The exact actual core and cyclic coordinate kernel

Let alpha be an algebraic unit of degree d, let q>=1, and suppose beta^q=alpha and [Q(beta):Q(alpha)]=q.
Write O_alpha=Z[alpha], O_beta=Z[beta].
Then

    O_beta = direct_sum_(j=0)^(q-1) beta^j O_alpha.       (1)

Spanning follows by division of each nonnegative exponent by q, and independence follows from the field-degree hypothesis.
Both elements are units; the minimal polynomial of beta is the complete P(t^q).
No maximal-order substitution is involved.

For x=sum beta^j y_j, an original digit step x -> beta*x+eta becomes exactly

    (y_0,...,y_(q-1)) -> (alpha*y_(q-1)+eta,y_0,...,y_(q-2)). (2)

Let D_alpha and D_beta be their actual original zero-to-zero cores, with probabilities p_0=1/2, p_1=p_(-1)=1/4.
Under(1),

    D_beta = (D_alpha)^q.                              (3)

For one inclusion, track each coordinate token along a beta closed word: a token is multiplied by alpha and receives one original digit each time it wraps from the last coordinate to the first.
Between wraps it is unchanged.
Its complete trajectory begins and ends at zero, so every encountered token value lies in D_alpha.
Thus every beta core state belongs to the product.
Conversely, each component of a product state has an alpha path from zero and a path back.
Pad the paths from zero by leading zero loops, and the return paths by trailing zero loops, to common lengths.
Interleave the q words through(2).
This supplies beta paths in both directions.
No bound on the component path lengths or assumption of real-strip completeness is used.

If K_alpha,K_beta denote the unchanged killed kernels on these actual cores, one complete cycle of q steps gives

    K_beta^q = K_alpha tensor ... tensor K_alpha        (4)

in the coordinates(1), because every token has been updated once, with independent original digits.
Intermediate states stay in the product exactly when the corresponding updated components stay in D_alpha.
Formula(4) is a genuine kernel equality, not just a diagonal-coefficient identity.
It applies to finite and infinite cores and to torsion units.

### 2. Critical recurrence and its first derivative are power-summability tests

Write p_n(alpha)=K_alpha^n(0,0), r=lim p_n(alpha)^(1/n), R=1/r, h=-log r, and

    a_n=R^n p_n(alpha),    n>=0.

L201 gives r=sup_n p_n^(1/n), hence0<a_n<=1 and a_0=1.
Reusing L5's exact independent digit decomposition, for n=q*l+s,0<=s<q,

    p_n(beta)=p_l(alpha)^(q-s) p_(l+1)(alpha)^s,
    h_2(beta)=h_2(alpha)=h,
    R^n p_n(beta)=a_l^(q-s) a_(l+1)^s.                 (5)

The limit is for each fixed q; it exchanges no varying-degree and time limits.
In particular the critical R is the same for both inputs.

Let U_beta(z)=sum p_n(beta)z^n and F_beta its complete first-return series.
Define extended nonnegative sums

    S_q=sum_(l>=0) a_l^q,      T_q=sum_(l>=0) l*a_l^q.

The complete critical Green value obeys

    S_q <= U_beta(R) <= q S_q.                         (6)

Indeed the s=0 terms give the lower bound.
Weighted arithmetic-geometric mean gives

    a_l^(q-s) a_(l+1)^s
      <= ((q-s)/q)a_l^q+(s/q)a_(l+1)^q.

Summing the finite s range and then all l proves the upper bound, with Tonelli valid also at infinity.
More exactly the resulting upper sum is q S_q-(q-1)/2 when S_q is finite.

For V_beta=sum_(n>=0) n R^n p_n(beta), the same estimate yields

    q T_q <= V_beta <= q^2(S_q+T_q).                  (7)

The upper bound uses q*l+s<=q(l+1), followed by the preceding weighted estimate and a shift of l.
Thus V_beta is finite exactly when T_q is finite; finiteness of T_q also gives S_q finite because a_0=1 and l>=1 in every other term.

Complete renewal, not a finite kernel truncation, now gives

    F_beta(R)<1  iff S_q<infinity,
    F_beta(R)=1  iff S_q=infinity.                    (8)

If S_q is finite, then

    F_beta(R)=1-1/U_beta(R),
    R F_beta'(R)=V_beta/U_beta(R)^2,                 (9)

where infinity is allowed in the second identity.
Differentiate the convergent series below R and pass monotonically; the denominator has a finite positive limit.
Therefore, in the transient critical case, the derivative is finite exactly when T_q is finite.

### 3. Attainment is preserved in both directions

The L201 stationary-information infimum is attained at beta if and only if it is attained at alpha.

    pi_beta=pi_alpha tensor ... tensor pi_alpha,

Here zero in the middle formula is the entire zero tuple.

First suppose the base infimum is attained.
L201 gives its unique full-core critical law Q_alpha, using every original allowed edge with positive probability, and stationary pi_alpha.

The product probability pi_alpha^q is invariant: the updated component remains distributed as pi_alpha and remains independent of the other components.

Interleave these paths.

For the converse, suppose Q is an attaining law at beta, with stationary pi.
