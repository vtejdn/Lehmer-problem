# Independent signed alternating groups on the steep quadratic sections

<a id="statement"></a>
## The joint splitting field and exterior products

Put \(A(U)=(U^2-3U-1)(U^2-4U-1)\), \(A^*(U)=U^4A(1/U)\), and \(P_{k,l}(X)=A(X^k)X^{2l}+X^{2k+l}+A^*(X^k)\).
Take coprime positive \(k,l\) with \(k\to\infty\) and \(s=l/k\to\infty\).
For all sufficiently large indices let \(S_i\) run through the distinct monic irreducible rational factors, write \(2n_i=\deg S_i\), and let \(L\) be their joint splitting field over \(\mathbb Q\).
All factors are reciprocal, and their roots form actual inverse pairs.
Set \(F=\mathbb Q^{\rm ab}\) and let \(V_i\le\mathbb F_2^{n_i}\) be the vectors of even coordinate sum acting by exchanges on the pairs of \(S_i\).
Then, in the natural joint action on roots,
\[
           {\rm Gal}(LF/F)=\prod_i (V_i\rtimes A_{n_i}).                 \tag{1}
\]
In particular one representative from each inverse pair of the entire section gives a multiplicatively independent set modulo torsion.
If \(H_i\) counts the exterior roots and \(\delta_i=M(S_i)\) is the multiplicative Mahler measure, then for arbitrary positive integers \(a_i\),
\[
 [F(\delta_i^{a_i}):F]=[\mathbb Q(\delta_i^{a_i}):\mathbb Q]
       =2^{H_i}\binom{n_i}{H_i}.
\]
The joint degree obtained by adjoining all these powers is the product of the individual degrees, both over \(F\) and over \(\mathbb Q\).

<a id="traces"></a>
## Independence across factors of equal degree

Write \(G={\rm Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)\), \(J=G'\), and order the inverse pairs separately in each factor.
By the [factor action](Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#factors), uniformly \(n_i\to\infty\), and the rational image on the pairs of \(S_i\) contains \(A_{n_i}\).
Thus the image of \(J\) on all the pairs is a subdirect product of the simple groups \(A_{n_i}\).
If it is proper, the [simple subdirect product argument](Yin_C_G0345_v268.md#normal) identifies two factors through a diagonal isomorphism.
The corresponding sizes agree by group orders, and for the common \(n\ge9\) the isomorphism is induced by a relabeling, as in the [natural alternating actions](Yin_C_G0345_v270.md#partition).
The simultaneous rational pair action normalizes the resulting diagonal \(A_n\) and therefore acts by the same permutation on both factors, since the natural \(A_n\) has trivial centralizer in \(S_n\).
For matched labels their traces generate the same field of degree \(n\), by their identical stabilizers.
Roots \(r,t\) above the two traces consequently generate a joint field of degree at most \(4n=O(l)\), as each is at most quadratic over its trace field.
Yet \(rt\) has degree \(l^{2+o(1)}\) uniformly by the [two-root degree theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v799.md#statement), since these reciprocal factors are distinct.
This excludes every diagonal identification, as in the [trace argument for the cubic family](../ledger/Yin_L_G0462_v177.md#product).
Hence the joint image of \(J\) on the pairs is the entire product of alternating groups.

<a id="signs"></a>
## Isolating signs by inertia and three-cycle lifts

Both total flip parity and pair permutation parity in each signed permutation group are homomorphisms to a group of order two, so \(J\subseteq\prod_i(V_i\rtimes A_{n_i})\).
Fix \(i\), let \(H\le J\) be the inverse image of the alternating group on that factor alone under the joint pair action, and set \(D=H'\).
Both groups are normal in \(G\), and \(D\) projects onto \(A_{n_i}\) by perfectness.
On every other factor \(H\) acts by commuting sign changes, so \(D\) fixes every root there.
If the kernel of \(D\) on its own pairs contains only constant vectors, the [four-letter lifting lemma](Yin_C_G0345_v384.md#four-letters) partitions the roots of \(S_i\) into two classes of size \(n_i\) preserved as an unordered pair by the normalizer of \(D\), hence by \(G\).
Any element swapping the classes moves all \(2n_i\) roots.
By the [finite-inertia bound and generation](Yin_C_G0345_v805.md#ramification), each finite inertia element moves at most 32 roots, and these inertia groups generate \(G\).
Thus for \(2n_i>32\) every element of \(G\) preserves both classes individually, contradicting irreducibility.

There is consequently a nonconstant kernel vector \(v\).
Choose a three-cycle \(c\) whose support meets both values of \(v\).
Conjugation by a lift of \(c\) and multiplication by \(v\) gives a weight-two kernel vector \(v+cv\).
By two-transitivity all weight-two vectors are in the kernel, and they span \(V_i\).
It follows that \(D=V_i\rtimes A_{n_i}\), supported only on \(S_i\), by projection and the parity bound.
Taking all \(i\) proves (1), since restriction from \(LF/F\) gives the commutator of \(G\).

<a id="products"></a>
## Multiplicative independence and degree of the Mahler products

For a monomial in the chosen representatives equal to a root of unity, take an exponent \(e_j\) at any label \(j\), and two other labels \(p,q\) of its factor.
Subtracting the transformed relation in the rational vector space of exponent relations modulo torsion from the given relation for the double flip \(jp\) leaves a relation with coefficients \(2e_j,2e_p\) at those two labels.
Applying the same difference for \(jq\) to this relation gives a relation supported at \(j\) alone with coefficient \(4e_j\).
The [torsion exclusion](Yin_C_G0345_v793.md#torsion) forces \(e_j=0\).
This proves independence simultaneously across the section.
The [uniform share of exterior roots](Yin_C_G0345_v794.md#growth) gives \(H_i\sim2n_i/s\), so \(1\le H_i<n_i\) eventually.
Each \(\delta_i\) differs from the exterior product only by a rational sign.
The alternating group realizes every support of cardinality \(H_i\), and the even flips realize every sign choice there by compensating on an unused label.
Independence distinguishes these signed supports even after taking positive powers.
No rational automorphism can take the product outside these possibilities, and (1) permits every choice independently on all factors, proving the degree formulas.
