# Open volume and modular returns for a rotation with many local twists

<a id="rotation"></a>
## A class with growing open support

Let \(N=2g+1\ge5\), let \(1\le s<N/2\) be coprime to \(N\), and let \(m\ge1\) satisfy \(m(s+1)\le N\).
Take nonzero integers \(c_1,\ldots,c_m\) and form
\[
 Q(Y)=\prod_{j=1}^m(Y+c_j),\qquad
 F(X)=X^{N-sm}Q(X^s)-Q^*(X^s),\qquad P=F/(X-1),
 \quad Q^*(Y)=Y^mQ(1/Y).
\]
Suppose \(P\) is irreducible, noncyclotomic and does not lie in \(\mathbb Z[X^a]\) for \(a>1\).
There is an orientable hyperbolic open surface bundle with two fixed boundary components, whole homological polynomial \(F\) and volume at most \(Ck\) for an absolute \(C\), where \(k=|\operatorname{supp}F|\).
Capping gives a closed hyperbolic bundle with whole polynomial \(P\).
The exponents of the twists and the closed support are unrestricted.

Use the [odd polygon rotation and edge classes](Yin_C_G0883_v10.md#four).
For \(u\) inverse to \(s\bmod N\), take the order-\(N\) rotation \(A=R^u\) and index the edge classes in rotation order as \(e_t=A^te_0\), with subscripts modulo \(N\).
Consecutive physical edges now correspond to index differences \(s,-s\).
Choose \(m\) centers \(a_j\) in cyclic order whose successive gaps \(\delta_j=a_{j+1}-a_j\bmod N\) all exceed \(s\), taking the gap to be \(N\) for a single center.
The assumed budget allows this by assigning \(s+1\) to each gap and adding the unused length to the last.
No two chosen edges are physical neighbors, so their lifted simple closed curves are pairwise disjoint.

Compose their twists with \(A\), applying the rotation last.
On the vector space of \(N\) formal edges, orient by rotation and choose the sign of each twist so its only change to the coefficient vector is \(y_{a_j}\mapsto y_{a_j}+c_j(y_{a_j-s}-y_{a_j+s})\).
This is the transvection of the lifted edge because its two nonzero pairings have opposite signs and absolute value one.
The twist actions do not alter one another's input coordinates.
All fix the vector of ones, and the quotient by this line is the closed first homology over \(\mathbb Q\).

<a id="determinant"></a>
## The characteristic polynomial across one full rotation

In the matrix \(XI-AT\), with \(T\) the total twist, every ordinary row is \(Xy_t-y_{t-1}\).
Row \(a_j+1\) has the additional term \(-c_j(y_{a_j-s}-y_{a_j+s})\).
Retain the variables \(v_j=y_{a_j+1}\) and the corresponding rows.
Eliminating the remaining \(N-m\) variables over \(\mathbb Q(X)\) contributes \(X^{N-m}\) to the determinant, since the ordinary rows break into triangular paths after the \(m\) cuts.
With cyclic subscripts, the remaining row is
\[
       (X+c_j X^{1-s})v_j-(1+c_j X^s)X^{-(\delta_{j-1}-1)}v_{j-1}.
\]
Indeed the gap bounds give \(y_{a_j-s}=X^s y_{a_j}\), \(y_{a_j+s}=X^{1-s}v_j\) and \(y_{a_j}=X^{-(\delta_{j-1}-1)}v_{j-1}\).
Taking the product of the diagonal entries minus the product of the predecessor coefficients, then multiplying by \(X^{N-m}\), gives \(F\).
For one retained variable the two terms occur in that single entry.
Removing the fixed line divides the determinant by \(X-1\), giving exactly \(P\).

Write \(b_Q=|\operatorname{supp}Q|\).
For \(s>1\) the two blocks of \(F\) lie in distinct residue classes modulo \(s\).
For \(s=1\), \(N\) is odd and \(2m\le N\), so their exponent intervals are disjoint.
Consequently \(k=2b_Q\).
Descartes' rule applied to \(Q(Y)\) and \(Q(-Y)\) gives \(m\le2(b_Q-1)\), since all \(m\) roots are real and nonzero, counted with multiplicity.
In particular \(m+2\le k\le2m+2\).

<a id="volume"></a>
## Retaining two fixed punctures

The [closed homological criterion](Yin_C_G0883_v2.md#mapping-tori) gives a pseudo-Anosov closed mapping class.
For \(s>1\), one has \(u\not\equiv\pm1\bmod N\).
Mark the origin of the polygon sphere and its two lifts, and project the twists to powers of half twists on the chosen polygon edges.
The [annular winding argument](Yin_C_G0883_v10.md#punctures) applies with this disjoint collection.
Indeed the permutation correction is a product of disjoint neighbor transpositions, so it moves each vertex by at most one physical sector.
No finite vertex is fixed after the rotation.
Each half twist has zero total winding about the origin because its support disk avoids the two poles.
The total winding of the composite is therefore \(u\bmod N\), excluding a reducing disk complementary to the poles.
As in the cited argument, closed pseudo-Anosov type excludes the other reductions before the extra marking, and any new reducing curve would have to enclose the origin and one fixed old mark.
The lifted marked map is pseudo-Anosov after filling the branch points and fixes the two lifts of the origin.

For \(s=1\), the rotation moves one sector.
Use [the fixed disk and separating twist](Yin_C_G0883_v10.md#neighbor) at that rotation step.
The two marks lie in the fixed disk away from the equatorial edge twists.
The additional separating curve still has a filling orbit since the closed map is pseudo-Anosov, and the curve separates the two marks.
Taking a sufficiently high power of its twist gives a pseudo-Anosov marked lift with the same closed homology.

Each original twist uses a bounded neighborhood of one physical polygon edge in the standard sector triangulation.
The additional twist in the one-sector case uses a bounded seam neighborhood, and may be placed at a different time from any edge twist.
The rotation is simplicial in the first marking and has bounded annular displacement in the second.
There are therefore \(O(1+m)\) drilled layers per period, independently of the twist powers.
The [cyclic-cover volume estimate](Yin_C_G0883_v9.md#layers) removes the initial \(O(N)\) collar, proving the asserted \(O(k)\) bound.
The [boundary module](Yin_C_G0883_v8.md#cycles) of two fixed punctures restores the factor \(X-1\).

<a id="returns"></a>
## A single prime sees at least half the degree up to the capping loss

Put \(l=\#\{j:c_j\text{ is odd}\}\) and \(H=N-sl\ge1\).
Then
\[
              \overline F=(X^s+1)^l(X^H-1)\quad\text{in }\mathbb F_2[X].
\]
The binomial contributes degree \(H\) at time \(H\).
When \(l\ge1\), the first factor contributes degree \(sl\) at time \(s2^{\lceil\log_2 l\rceil}\le2sl\), by the Frobenius identity with multiplicity.
Taking the larger degree and removing the one-dimensional boundary factor gives
\[
          \max_{1\le j\le 2N}\deg\gcd(\overline P,X^j-1)
                       \ \ge\ \lceil N/2\rceil-1=(N-1)/2 .
\]
The case \(l=0\) already has full return at \(N\).
This deduction needs no irreducibility assumption on \(P\).
On the irreducible branch the [return inequality](Yin_C_G0904_v2.md#return-ranks) gives a positive absolute logarithmic measure gap.
Thus the construction applies to growing open support but cannot furnish a shrinking sequence of strict records.

For explicit dense examples take any odd prime \(N\ge5\) and all \(c_j\) positive multiples of \(N^2\).
Then \(P(X)\equiv (X^N-1)/(X-1)\bmod N^2\), so \(P(X+1)\) is Eisenstein at \(N\).
For \(sm<N/2\), every coefficient of \(P\) is positive: the lower block contributes negative partial sums, and the higher positive block cancels their total for the first time at degree \(N\).
Thus \(P\) is dense and has no power substitution.
At the prime \(N\), it divides \(X^N-1\) modulo \(N\).
To exclude the cyclotomic case directly, \(P(0)=1\) and its middle coefficient is \(\prod_j(1+c_j)\).
Choosing \(c_j=N^2 4^N\) makes that coefficient exceed \(2^{N-1}\), the binomial bound for any degree-\((N-1)\) monic polynomial of measure one.
These choices give noncyclotomic realizations with \(k=2m+2\), since \(Q\) is positive in all degrees.
For instance take \(m=\lfloor N^{1/3}\rfloor,\ s=\lfloor N/(3m)\rfloor\) at sufficiently large primes.
Then \(k\to\infty\), \(k/(N-1)\to0\), and the span \(sm\) of an end block is asymptotic to \(N/3\).

<a id="trimming"></a>
## A wide central gap allows deletion of a boundary period

Let \(P\) be a monic irreducible noncyclotomic reciprocal polynomial with \(1<M(P)\le\lambda<\theta\), where \(\theta\) is the plastic number.
Suppose \(F=PC\) for an admissible nonempty boundary cofactor \(C\), so \((X-1)C=\prod_j(X^{h_j}-1)\).
Write \(F=X^n Q\pm Q^*\) with \(Q\) monic of degree \(r\), \(n>r\).
There is a constant \(A_\lambda\) such that if \(n>A_\lambda r\), then
\[
                      F=(X^h-1)G
\]
for a period \(h\) occurring exactly once, and \(G\) is an admissible multiple of the same \(P\) with exactly half the support of \(F\).
In particular every support-minimizing admissible completion of a strict record of degree at least ten has \(n=O(r)\) in every such two-block presentation.

Indeed remove \(X^v\) from \(Q\), changing \(n\) to \(h=n+v\), and denote the reduced block by \(Q_0\).
Its monic gcd \(G_0\) with \(Q_0^*\) lies in \(\mathbb Z[X]\) and is reciprocal up to sign with unit constant.
In fact the common nonzero roots and their multiplicities are inversion stable, so its constant has absolute value one and \(G_0^*=\pm G_0\).
Writing \(Q_0=G_0 T\) leaves \(T,T^*\) coprime.
If \(\deg T>0\), it has a nonreciprocal monic irreducible factor, hence \(M(T)\ge\theta\) by the [nonreciprocal measure bound](Yin_C_G0904_v7.md#width).
The [uniform comparison for both signs](Yin_C_G0904_v9.md#width) then gives \(M(X^h T\pm T^*)>\lambda\) for \(h>A_\lambda\deg T\) with \(A_\lambda\) sufficiently large.
This contradicts \(M(F)=M(P)\), by the factorization and \(M(G_0)\ge1\).
Therefore \(T=1\), and \(F=(X^h\pm1)G_0\) with \(\deg G_0<h\).

The number of boundary circles is \(\deg C+1=h+\deg G_0-\deg P+1<2h\).
The plus sign would require a boundary period divisible by \(2h\), because \(\Phi_{2h}\) would divide \(C\).
The minus sign requires a period divisible by \(h\), which by the same budget must be \(h\) and can occur only once.
Removing that period gives \(G_0=P(C/(X^h-1))\), with at least one boundary period remaining: an initial list consisting only of \(h\) would imply \(P=(X-1)G_0\).
The remaining cofactor is the boundary factor of that nonempty list.
The two translates of \(G_0\) have disjoint support, proving the exact halving.
The [degree-ten record ceiling](Yin_C_G0904_v5.md#record) permits the same fixed \(\lambda=19/16\) for the stated record consequence.
