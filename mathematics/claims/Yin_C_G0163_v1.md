That special case is not new coverage.
The new [C162 orbital criterion](Yin_C_G0162_v1.md#statement) is the accepted quantitative consumer premise.
The question is whether a NORMAL actual trace field, not assumed abelian, makes its valency and density explicit in the quotient Frobenius class, without charging the large full inversion kernel.
These existing finite-group and norm interfaces suffice; no classification or additional external theorem is needed.

## Candidate statement

Let alpha be a genuine Salem number of degree d=2t>=4, K=Q(alpha), F=Q(alpha+alpha^-1), and m=log alpha its ORIGINAL TOTAL measure.
Assume the ACTUAL degree-t trace field F is Galois over Q.
Let L be the normal closure of K and G=Gal(L/Q).
Choose a rational prime p, a prime of O_L above p and any actual residue-Frobenius lift sigma.
Put T=Gal(F/Q), bar_sigma=sigma|_F and kappa_T=|class_T(bar_sigma)|.
All ramification and every original root are retained.

There is a conjugate beta of alpha for which C162's actual orbital parameters obey

    s<=2, gamma/s>=1/(2 kappa_T).                     (1)

If bar_sigma is nonidentity, EVERY starting root beta has

    s=2, gamma=1/kappa_T.                             (2)

If bar_sigma is identity, one can choose beta in the larger fixed or inversion-matched set, with

    s=1, gamma>=1/2.                                 (3)

Thus every supplied integer k>=kappa_T gives

    m>H5(log p/(2k))/[2p(p+1)].                       (4)

Here H5 is the exact complete capped inverse in accepted C147v5.
For every integer A>=1 with A log p/(2k)>log2, one also has

    m>[A log p/(2k)-log2]/[2p^A(p+1)].                (5)

In particular p=2 and kappa_T=1 imply m>log2/96.
The full G-class of sigma need not be bounded.
This is a supplied prime/quotient-class criterion, not automatic control on all near-one inputs.
If p is unramified in L, the stronger original absolute-height conclusion is

    m/d >[A log p/(2k)-log2]/[p^(A-1)(p+1)],          (6)

so p=2,kappa_T=1 gives m/d>log2/24.
Equation (6) is not asserted at a ramified prime.

## Regular pair action and the complete inversion kernel

The t trace values are distinct, since equality of alpha_i+alpha_i^-1 forces the same inverse pair.
Because F=Q(alpha+alpha^-1) is Galois of degree t, it is the splitting field of the trace polynomial.
Thus G acts on the t inverse pairs through T, and this T action is regular: a trace value generates F, so its stabilizer is trivial.
Let V be the kernel, embedded in F2^t by the pair flips.
L162 proves V=F2^t for even t, and E_t contained in V for odd t, where E_t is the even-weight space.
This is the actual Salem kernel, not an abstract wreath assumption.
No splitting of G over T is required.

Fix a starting root beta in pair i and put H=G_beta.
Regularity of the quotient makes H exactly the subgroup of V with i-coordinate zero.
For any different pair j, H can flip j.
When t is even use e_j in V.
When t is odd, t>=3 and e_j+e_l works for a third pair l distinct from i,j.
The small t=2 case is already even.
Therefore H has an orbit of size two on the roots of every different pair and fixes each root of pair i separately.

Suppose c=bar_sigma is nonidentity.
Identify the regular pair set with T, with starting pair the identity and its image c.
The orbital of (beta,sigma beta) consists of all ordered root pairs whose underlying pair labels are

    (g,gc), g in T,

with all four choices of their two roots.
The stabilizer argument gives s=2 and the two target choices, and full transitivity gives both initial choices.
For a root in pair g, sigma sends its pair to cg.
It lies in the selected orbital exactly when cg=gc.
There are |C_T(c)| such pair labels and two original roots at each label, so

    b=2|C_T(c)|, d=2|T|, gamma=1/kappa_T.

Any pair flips in the lift do not change the count because both target roots occur in the orbital.
Changing the starting pair conjugates c and preserves its class size.
This proves (2) without assuming the extension splits.

If c is identity, sigma lies in V and either fixes or inverts every pair.
The fixed-root and inversion-matched root counts sum to d.
Choose beta in a set of size at least d/2.
In the fixed case the orbital is the diagonal; in the other case it is the inversion graph, since inversion commutes with G.
Both have valency one and compatible density equal to the chosen set's relative size.
This proves (3), including all-fixed and all-inverted cases, and hence (1).

## Quantitative consumption and original-field transport

For the selected beta, accepted C162 gives the actual bound

    m>H5(gamma log p/s)/[p(p+1)s].

The full H5 function is nondecreasing by its strictly increasing underlying Phi5 and its stated cap.
Using gamma/s>=1/(2k) and s<=2 gives (4), including the capped branch.
For (5), the exact direct-depth numerator is at least A log p/(2k)-log2>0 and its denominator is at most2p^A(p+1).
Strictness is retained.
At p=2,k=1,A=3, numerator(log2)/2 divided by denominator48 gives log2/96.
No finite inverse is evaluated.

If beta differs from alpha, its degree and total measure are unchanged.
An automorphism of L taking beta to alpha transports its actual nonzero integral norm witness from Q(beta) to O_K, preserving the complete rational norm, factor count and exponent expense.
No inexpensive representative in Z[alpha] is inferred.
Equivalently, the scalar inequalities concern the unchanged original minimal polynomial and do not depend on selecting its root.

The H5 branch uses s<=2, n<p s d<=2pd, pn<2p^2d and relative total exponent below4p^2d, as in C162.
The direct-depth branch has n_A<p^A s d<=2p^A d, with the corresponding pn_A and s pn_A costs.
A is bounded only when the supplied p,k criterion is bounded.
No full inversion-kernel field cost has been suppressed.

## Additional unramified height bound

Assume now that p is unramified in L.
Every compatible conjugate rho_i-1 has positive INTEGER valuation at the chosen normal-closure prime under v(p)=1.
Thus the initial depth is at least one; the degree-D amplification is unnecessary.
After A-1 p-powers, n=p^(A-1), every selected depth is at least A.
Keeping all ratio fibers gives the lower norm density A gamma log p/s.

For the actual nontorsion integral unit rho one has

    log|Norm(rho^n-1)|/D < log2+n h_abs(rho)
                              <=log2+n(p+1)m/d.

For the strict first inequality, some conjugate has modulus different from one.
Otherwise the accepted Kronecker unit interface would make rho torsion.
At that conjugate 1+|rho_i|^n<2max(1,|rho_i|^n), while every other factor satisfies the weak triangle estimate.
Combining the lower density with gamma/s>=1/(2k) proves (6).
For p=2,k=1,A=3, its positive numerator(log2)/2 divided by12 gives log2/24.
This branch retains actual unramifiedness in L and is not transported to ramified lifts.
It explains the large total measures of an unramified fixed-prime realization as d grows; no such realization is a premise here.
The exact exponent in this branch is n=p^(A-1), with p n=p^A and relative total exponent at most2p^A.
