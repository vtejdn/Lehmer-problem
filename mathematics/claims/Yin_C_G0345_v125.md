# The other conjugates at a smaller exterior Salem trace

<a id="statement"></a>
## Radial separation at integer levels

Let \(\sigma\) be a Salem number of degree \(2m\) with \(a=\log\sigma\le A\) for fixed \(A\).
Let \(P\) be its minimal polynomial, \(S\) its trace minimal polynomial, and \(T=\sigma+\sigma^{-1}\), so that \(P(z)=z^m S(z+z^{-1})\).
For nonzero integer \(b\), put \(G_b=P-bz^m\) and \(q=m^{-1}\log|b|\).
There is a constant \(C_A\) such that, with \(h=C_A\log(2m)/\sqrt m\), the following assertions hold for all sufficiently large \(m\) and all \(q\le a-2h\).

* There is exactly one root \(\tau_b\) of \(G_b\) of modulus greater than \(e^{q+h}\), with multiplicity one.
  It is real and positive, and \(\tau_b<\sigma\) for \(b<0\), while \(\tau_b>\sigma\) for \(b>0\).
  Apart from \(\tau_b,\tau_b^{-1}\), every root satisfies
  \[
          (q-h)_+\le|\log|\beta||\le q+h.                            \tag{1}
  \]
  In particular each negative level here gives a strictly smaller Perron unit.
  Different levels give different minimal polynomials.
* Write \(D=S'(T)\).
  Uniformly over these signed levels,
  \[
    \tau_b+\tau_b^{-1}-T=(1+o(1))b/D.                                \tag{2}
  \]
  In particular for each fixed \(0<\kappa<1\) there are at least \(\lfloor e^{\kappa ma}\rfloor\) distinct smaller Perron units of degree at most \(2m\).
  Their other conjugates, apart from a possible reciprocal, have modulus at most \(e^{\kappa a+h}\).
* Each noncyclotomic irreducible factor of \(G_b\) missing \(\tau_b\) has at least
  \[
           \gg \frac{1}{q+h}\left(\frac{\log\log(3m)}{\log(3m)}\right)^3 \tag{3}
  \]
  roots strictly outside the unit circle.
  This includes a factor that contains only the reciprocal of the distinguished root from the exceptional pair.

At \(b=-1\), let \(v\) be the number of local minima of \(S\) with critical value strictly greater than \(-1\).
There is precisely one real trace above two and none below minus two; the trace polynomial \(S+1\) has exactly \(2v\) nonreal roots counted with multiplicity.
If \(v=0\) the distinguished root gives a smaller Salem number or a reciprocal quadratic unit.
If \(v>0\) and the same level still gives a smaller Salem number, then
\[
        v\gg_A\frac{\sqrt m\,(\log\log(3m))^3}{\log(3m)^4}.           \tag{4}
\]
For \(v\) below a sufficiently small fixed multiple of the right-hand scale, the only noncyclotomic factor is the reciprocal minimal polynomial of \(\tau_{-1}\).
All the nonreal traces belong to that factor's trace polynomial.
In this range, for \(\sigma<(3+\sqrt5)/2\), the level gives a Salem descent exactly when \(v=0\).

<a id="radial"></a>
## Dominance on a circle between the two scales

Write \(N(z)=P(z)/((z-\sigma)(z-\sigma^{-1}))\) and \(\nu\) for the uniform probability on its \(2m-2\) unit roots, viewed as phases.
The [neutral Fourier energy estimate](Yin_C_G0345_v119.md#slope) gives
\(\sum_{1\le j\le2m}|\widehat\nu(j)|^2/j\ll_A\log(2m)/m\).
For \(h\le s\le A\), expand the logarithms of \(1-e^{-s+i\varphi}\) in the neutral product.
Cauchy–Schwarz up to \(2m\) and the absolute tail bound give, uniformly on \(|z|=e^s\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\log|N(z)|-(2m-2)s|
 &\ll_A \sqrt{m\log(2m)}
             \left(\sum_{j\ge1}e^{-2sj}/j\right)^{1/2}
       +m\frac{e^{-2ms}}{2m(1-e^{-s})}\\
 &\ll_A\sqrt m\log(2m).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The last bound holds already for \(s\ge\log(2m)/\sqrt m\).

Take \(s=q+h\le a-h\).
On this circle \(|z-\sigma|\ge e^a-e^s\ge h\), and \(|z-\sigma^{-1}|\ge e^s-e^{-a}\ge h\).
Consequently
\[
    \log|z^{-m}P(z)|\ge(m-2)s+2\log h-O_A(\sqrt m\log(2m))>mq
\]
by choosing \(C_A\) sufficiently large.
Rouché's theorem gives \(G_b\) exactly as many roots inside the circle as \(P\), namely \(2m-1\), with none on it.
At its positive real point \(z=e^s\) we have \(2<z+z^{-1}<T\), hence \(S(z+z^{-1})<-|b|\) by the sign and modulus.
Since \(G_b\) is monic and \(G_b(\sigma)=-b\sigma^m\), the unique exterior root is positive and lies on the stated side of \(\sigma\).
Reciprocity then gives the upper bound in (1).
For every root \(\beta\), evaluate \(P\) at the member \(w\) of \(\{\beta,\beta^{-1}\}\) inside or on the unit circle to obtain \(\log|b|\le m|\log|\beta||+\log\|P\|_{\mathbb T}\).
The [circle supremum bound](Yin_C_G0345_v73.md#determinant) at confluence order \(\lfloor\sqrt{2m}\rfloor\) gives \(\log\|P\|_{\mathbb T}\ll_A\sqrt m\log(2m)\), proving the lower bound in (1) on enlarging \(C_A\).

<a id="location"></a>
## Signed displacement near the source and distinct Perron units

Write \(x_j\) for the neutral traces, \(v_j=(T-x_j)^{-1}\), and \(W=\sum_j v_j\).
The [exterior slope and inverse-distance formulas](Yin_C_G0345_v119.md#slope) give
\(\log D=ma+O_A(\sqrt m\log(2m))\) and \(\log W=O_A(\log(2m))\), using the input Dobrowolski bound \(a\gg(\log(2m))^{-3}\).
Enlarge \(C_A\) so that \(W|b|/D\to0\) uniformly for \(q\le a-2h\).
For \(|x|\le 2|b|/D\) the identity
\[
         S(T+x)=xD\prod_j(1+xv_j)=(1+o(1))xD
\]
holds uniformly.
The two signed points \(x=b/(2D),\,2b/D\) bracket a solution.
It belongs to the distinguished root: its distance from \(T\) is \(O_A(\exp(-c\sqrt m\log(2m)))\) for some \(c>0\), whereas \(T-2\cosh(a-h)\ge h^2\) for \(a\ge2h\), by integration of \(2\sinh u\ge2u\).
This proves (2).
The unique dominant positive root of the monic unit polynomial \(G_b\) is a Perron unit.
A common conjugate at two distinct levels would be a nonzero root of their difference \((b'-b)z^m\), which is impossible.
Finally \(h=o(a)\) by the input degree bound, so the stated exponential range of negative levels is available.

<a id="factors"></a>
## Counting the exterior roots in each complementary factor

For every noncyclotomic monic unit factor \(F\) of degree at most \(2m\), the [Dobrowolski bound](../audits/literature/Smy08/Yin_U001.md#smy08-u001) gives \(\log M(F)\gg(\log\log(3m)/\log(3m))^3\).
Here the benchmark is eventually decreasing with degree; the finitely many smaller degrees have a uniform positive gap by bounded-measure coefficient finiteness.
If \(F(\tau_b)\ne0\), all its strictly exterior roots have logarithmic modulus at most \(q+h\), proving (3).

For \(b=-1\), consider the last negative lobe of \(S\) between its largest neutral root and \(T\).
One has \(S(2)\le-1\) by integrality, and \(S(2\cosh h)<-1\) by the circle estimate.
The unique critical point in that lobe is a strict minimum.
Thus there is exactly one solution of \(S=-1\) strictly above two, including the case \(S(2)=-1\), by monotonicity on the two sides of the minimum and positivity beyond \(T\).
On \(x<-2\) the product of distances to all original roots is strictly larger than \(|S(-2)|\ge1\), excluding a solution there.
Every local maximum is positive, and each negative local minimum above \(-1\) loses two real intersections with the level, counted with multiplicity.
Equality retains the double root at the minimum, by the simple interlacing of derivative roots.
Counting the monotone branches as in the [critical-value test](Yin_C_G0345_v115.md#descent) gives \(2v\) nonreal trace roots.
The full lift therefore has \(1+2v\) exterior roots, one for each nonreal trace and one for the positive exterior trace.

For \(v=0\), all the other conjugates of the distinguished trace are in \([-2,2]\).
A nonrational irreducible trace has a neutral conjugate in \((-2,2)\), where the reciprocal quadratic has negative discriminant in the totally real trace field.
Its lift is therefore an irreducible Salem polynomial.
A rational trace gives the reciprocal quadratic exception, and this requires an integer trace at least three, impossible when \(\sigma<(3+\sqrt5)/2\) since the distinguished trace is strictly smaller than \(T\).

If a smaller Salem factor exists at this level, its real exterior root must be \(\tau_{-1}\).
Any nonreal trace then belongs to the complement, so some noncyclotomic irreducible factor missing \(\tau_{-1}\) must account for at least the number in (3) of exterior roots when \(v>0\).
Since only \(2v\) exterior roots are left, this proves (4).
Conversely, if \(2v\) is smaller than the lower bound in (3), every complementary irreducible factor is cyclotomic.
The distinguished factor has multiplicity one, and it must be reciprocal, since its otherwise different reciprocal mate would also be noncyclotomic.
It therefore contains all nonreal trace roots after lifting.
This proves the stated criterion with the specified quadratic exception.
