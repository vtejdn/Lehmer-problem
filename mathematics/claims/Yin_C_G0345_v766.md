# A lower bound for the degree of every radical of a rational graph unit

<a id="statement"></a>
## Divisibility at arbitrary torsion and an almost linear bound in the dilation

Fix any nonzero \(R\in\overline{\mathbb Q}(Z)\).
There exist \(s_R>0\) and a positive integer \(H_R\) such that the following holds.
Let \(\beta\) be any nontorsion algebraic unit with \(\beta^v=R(\beta^u)\), \(u,v\ge1\) integers and \(v/u>s_R\).
Write \(F=\mathbb Q(\beta)\), \(d=[F:\mathbb Q]\), and for every \(g\ge1\) and any \(\alpha^g=\beta\) put
\[
       t=[F(\alpha):F],\qquad d_0=\gcd(g,t),\qquad
       r=g/d_0,\qquad W_0=|\{\zeta\in F:\zeta^{d_0}=1\}|.
\]
Then
\[
        r\mid H_R W_0,\qquad g\mid H_R d_0^2,\qquad
        t\ge\sqrt{g/H_R}.                                                \tag{1}
\]
Put \(G(d)=(\log\log(5d)/\log(2d))^3\) and \(J(d)=\log\log\log(100d)>0\).
In terms of the original degree and total logarithmic Mahler measure \(m(\beta)\) one has
\[
         r\ll_R\sqrt{\frac{m(\beta)J(d)}{G(d)}},\qquad
         m(\alpha)=\frac{t}{g}m(\beta)
                      \gg_R\sqrt{\frac{m(\beta)G(d)}{J(d)}}.              \tag{2}
\]
In particular \(m(\beta)\ll_R1\) and \(t\gg_R g\sqrt{G(d)/J(d)}\).
All branches and signatures are allowed, with no restrictions on \(\gcd(u,v)\) or the roots of unity of \(F\).
These estimates apply to every irreducible factor of \(P_\beta(X^g)\), where \(P_\beta\) is the minimal polynomial.
The first divisibility gives a constant linear in \(W_0\) in the [bounded-torsion comparison](Yin_C_G0345_v761.md#statement).

<a id="separation"></a>
## An integer polynomial separates prime roots even after a variable twist

Use the integer polynomial \(P=P_{u,v}\) obtained by multiplying the conjugate equations of \(R\) as in the [graph height and separation theorem](Yin_C_G0345_v758.md#separation).
At large slope it vanishes at \(\beta\), has length bounded by a constant \(L_R\ge1\), and satisfies \(\deg(P)h(\beta)\ll_R1\).
Here \(h\) denotes the absolute logarithmic Weil height.
At a single sufficiently large slope threshold, for every prime \(p\), every root of unity \(\zeta\), and every \(\gamma\) with \(\gamma^p=\zeta\beta\),
\[
                              P(\gamma/\zeta)\ne0.                        \tag{3}
\]
Indeed suppose failure occurred along a sequence with \(v/u\to\infty\).
Write \(x=\gamma/\zeta\); it is a nontorsion unit with \(h(x)=h(\beta)/p\).
Vanishing gives \(x^v=R^\tau(x^u)\) for one of finitely many coefficient conjugates, since coprimality excludes a zero denominator.
For these two sequences the [fixed-map height law](Yin_C_G0345_v758.md#height) and the positivity on actual solutions give
\[
                  vh(\beta)\longrightarrow H(R)>0,\qquad
                  vh(x)\longrightarrow H(R).
\]
In fact \(u h(\beta)\to0\) and \(u h(x)\le u h(\beta)/2\), and the map height is invariant under coefficient conjugation.
But \(v h(x)\le v h(\beta)/2\), a contradiction independent of the order and choice of \(\zeta\).

<a id="congruence"></a>
## Integer matrix power congruences with coefficients in a ramified cyclotomic field

Let \(\zeta\) have exact order \(p^c\), with \(c\ge0\), put \(E=\mathbb Q(\zeta)\), and let \(M\) be any square matrix over \(\mathcal O_E\).
For \(a>c\) the characteristic polynomials of \(M^{p^a}\) and \(M^{p^{a-1}}\) are coefficientwise congruent modulo \(p^{a-c}\mathcal O_E\).
For \(c=0\), \(E=\mathbb Q\) and this is the [integer matrix power congruence](Yin_C_G0345_v206.md#congruence), including \(p=2\).

For \(c\ge1\) there is one prime over \(p\) with residue field \(\mathbb F_p\), and its ramification degree is \(e=\varphi(p^c)=p^{c-1}(p-1)\).
Indeed \(\Phi_{p^c}(1+Z)\) is monic, equals \(Z^e\) modulo \(p\) and has constant coefficient \(p\), so is Eisenstein over \(\mathbb Q_p\) of the full global degree.
With the valuation \(w(p)=1\) the prime ideal thus has valuation \(1/e\).
For each \(x\in\mathcal O_E\) the residue field gives \(w(x^p-x)\ge1/e\).
For integral \(y,z\) with \(w(y-z)\ge b>0\), the binomial theorem gives
\[
                      w(y^p-z^p)\ge\min(pb,1+b).
\]
Iterating up to \(\ell=c\) gives \(w(x^{p^\ell}-x^{p^{\ell-1}})\ge p^{\ell-1}/e\) for \(1\le\ell\le c\).
At \(\ell=c\) the bound equals \(1/(p-1)\), and every succeeding iteration increases the bound by one.
In particular the difference is divisible by \(p^{\max(0,\ell-c)}\) in \(\mathcal O_E\).
This calculation includes \(p=2,c=1\), when \(E=\mathbb Q\), and uses only the weaker stated modulus in that case.

For an integral matrix \(T\) over \(E\), expand \(\operatorname{tr}(T^{p^a})\) over based closed walks on its index set with edge weights given by its entries.
A primitive cyclic walk of length \(p^j\), \(j\le a\), contributes \(p^j x^{p^{a-j}}\), where \(x\) is the product of its edge weights on one period.
A walk of least period \(p^a\) contributes a multiple of \(p^a\).
For \(j<a\) the difference of its contributions to the two successive traces is
\[
                p^j\big(x^{p^{a-j}}-x^{p^{a-j-1}}\big)
                       \ \in p^{a-c}\mathcal O_E ,
\]
since \(j+\max(0,a-j-c)\ge a-c\).
Summing proves the trace congruence.
Use \(T=\bigwedge^i M\) for each characteristic coefficient, since exterior powers commute with taking matrix powers and their traces give the signed coefficients.

<a id="norm"></a>
## Removing the prime-to-\(p\) part of the norm twist

The [relative norm and Bézout identity](Yin_C_G0345_v761.md#norm) give a unit \(\theta\in F\) and a root of unity \(\xi\in F\) with
\[
                      \theta^r=\xi\beta,\qquad \xi^{d_0}=1 .
\]
Write \(w_0=\operatorname{ord}\xi\), so \(w_0\mid W_0\mid d_0\).
For each full prime power \(p^a\mid r\), let \(c=v_p(w_0)\).
Write \(\xi=\zeta\xi'\) with \(\zeta\) of order \(p^c\) and \(\xi'\) of order prime to \(p\).
The \(p^a\)-th power map is an automorphism of \(\langle\xi'\rangle\), so dividing \(\theta^{r/p^a}\) by an appropriate element of this group gives \(\theta_0\in F\) with \(\theta_0^{p^a}=\zeta\beta\).
It suffices to treat \(a>c\).

Set \(E=\mathbb Q(\zeta)\), \(z=\zeta\beta\) and \(\gamma=\theta_0^{p^{a-1}}\).
Because \(E\subseteq F\) and \(\beta=z/\zeta\), one has
\[
                 E(z)=E(\gamma)=E(\theta_0)=F .
\]
The monic minimal polynomial of \(\theta_0\) over \(E\) has coefficients in \(\mathcal O_E\) since \(\theta_0\) is a unit.
Its companion matrix has powers \(p^a,p^{a-1}\) whose characteristic polynomials are the respective monic minimal polynomials \(S_z,S_\gamma\) over \(E\), since the relative degrees agree.
The [ramified congruence](#congruence) implies \(S_z(\gamma)\in p^{a-c}\mathcal O_F\).
The translated polynomial \(P(Y/\zeta)\in\mathcal O_E[Y]\) vanishes at \(z\) and hence is divisible by \(S_z\) in \(\mathcal O_E[Y]\) by monic division.
Its value at \(\gamma\) is therefore divisible by \(p^{a-c}\) and [nonzero](#separation).
Each complex embedding of \(F\) preserves the absolute values of the coefficients of this translated integer polynomial.
Taking absolute norms and then \(d\)-th roots gives
\[
           p^{a-c}\le L_R\exp(\deg(P) h(\gamma))
                    = L_R\exp(\deg(P)h(\beta)/p)\ll_R1 .
\]
Choose \(H_R\) to be the least common multiple of the positive integers up to a common upper bound for the right hand side, increased to at least one.
For every \(p\) dividing \(r\), its excess exponent over \(v_p(w_0)\) is at most \(v_p(H_R)\).
Thus \(r\mid H_R w_0\), proving (1) since \(W_0\mid d_0\) and \(d_0\le t\).

<a id="degree"></a>
## Relative height over the cyclotomic field of the original twist

The minimal polynomial \(P_\beta\) is monic integral and divides the integer graph test \(P\).
The nonzero integer quotient has nonnegative logarithmic Mahler measure, so \(m(\beta)\le m(P)\le\log L_R\).
Retain the original norm twist \(\xi\), and put \(E_0=\mathbb Q(\xi)\).
Since \(\beta=\theta^r/\xi\) one has \(E_0(\theta)=F\), whence \(D_0=[E_0(\theta):E_0]=d/\varphi(w_0)\) and \(h(\theta)=m(\beta)/(rd)>0\).
The [relative cubic height inequality](Yin_C_G0345_v727.md#statement) applies over the abelian field \(E_0\) with \(H=1\) and at most \(\log_2 w_0\) exceptional odd primes.
Indeed a prime not dividing \(w_0\) is unramified there, since \(Z^{w_0}-1\) has separable reduction and its roots lie in an unramified local extension by lifting the residue roots.
Every Frobenius conjugacy class in this abelian field has size one.
Also \(w_0\le 2\varphi(w_0)^2\le2d^2\), since in \(\varphi(w_0)^2/w_0\) the factor \(p^{a-2}(p-1)^2\) for each \(p^a\Vert w_0\) is at least one except for \(2^1\), where it equals \(1/2\).
Thus \(\sqrt{1+\log_2 w_0}\ll\sqrt{\log(2d)}\ll\log(2d)/\log\log(5d)\).
Likewise \(\log(2D_0)/\log\log(5D_0)\ll\log(2d)/\log\log(5d)\) since the ratio is eventually increasing and is bounded above and below by positive constants on every fixed initial interval.
The cubic inequality therefore gives \(D_0 h(\theta)\gg G(d)\), with absolute constant even for small \(D_0\).
Substitution now gives
\[
                         r\varphi(w_0)\ll\frac{m(\beta)}{G(d)}.          \tag{4}
\]
Since \(m(\beta)\ll_R1\) and \(w_0\le2\varphi(w_0)^2\), (4) implies \(w_0\ll_R\log^6(2d)\).
The [classical totient lower bound](../audits/literature/BV19b/Yin_U002.md#bv19b-collision-partitions) gives \(w_0/\varphi(w_0)\ll\log\log(5w_0)\ll_R J(d)\), including \(w_0=1\) after adjustment of the constants.
Therefore \(r\le H_Rw_0\) gives \(r^2\ll_R r\varphi(w_0)J(d)\).
Substitution of (4) proves the first bound in (2).
Finally \(t/g\ge1/r\) and \(m(\alpha)/m(\beta)=t/g\) by the absolute degree and height formulas.
Each irreducible factor of \(P_\beta(X^g)\) has a root above \(\beta\) by extending an embedding of the powered field.
This proves the remaining assertions.
