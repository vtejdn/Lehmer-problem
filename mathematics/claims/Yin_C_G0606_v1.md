## 1. Exhausting the actual short-bridge constructor

Keep P, beta, zeta, O, the strict core D and the original full returned-word law pi_n from the cited fixed inputs.
Put k=n-2, B=1/(beta-1), S_real=beta B and

    Z0={c in O: |c_+|<B beta^-4, |c_-|<S_real}.

The set Z0 is exactly the set of normalized right labels for which both c and beta^4 c belong to D.
Indeed the stronger expanding condition comes from beta^4 c, while the stronger stable condition comes from c.
Let E_n be the complete event of four zero middle digits, including every original tail representation.
For each word in E_n its normalized incidence edge is (c,y) with y=beta^4 c, and c belongs to Z0.
At a fixed current n only those labels realized by its actual positive tail counts occur.

A short bridge is a path of one or two positive transitions of the ORIGINAL K_n whose initial and final words belong to E_n.
The intermediate word, when present, can have any allowed middle digits and all original tail representations.
A restricted path is any finite concatenation of such bridges, all at the same n.
This includes arbitrarily many bridges and arbitrary changes inside conditional word fibers.

Under one conditional update the complete left tail or complete right tail is fixed, so respectively y or c is fixed.
If both endpoints of one update lie in E_n, the equation y=beta^4 c forces the other endpoint label to be fixed too.
Thus a one-update bridge does not change c.
Two updates fixing the same tail likewise do not change c between their zero-middle endpoints.
For two updates of different types, write theta for the middle polynomial of the single intermediate word.
If y is fixed first, then

    beta^4 c=beta^4 c'+theta(beta),
    c'-c=-beta^-4 theta(beta).

If c is fixed first, the same comparison gives c'-c=beta^-4 theta(beta).
Here theta has degree at most 3 and all four coefficients belong to {-1,0,1}, with the original ascending position convention.
All cases of the one- or two-update constructor have now been exhausted.
In particular no endpoint-only adjacency has been inserted in place of a positive original transition.

The two endpoint labels both belong to Z0, so their difference forces

    |theta(beta)|<2B,
    |theta(beta^-1)|<2S_real beta^-4.                 (1)

These are only NECESSARY conditions for a current-length bridge.
They do not assert that every retained pattern can occur from every zero-middle fiber, nor replace the finite tail-support conditions.
If theta*(t)=t^3 theta(t^-1) is its coefficient reversal, (1) is equivalent to

    |theta(beta)|(beta-1)<2,
    |theta*(beta)|(beta-1)<2.                         (2)

Thus exactly 81 finite patterns suffice to screen every possible bridge, independently of n.
